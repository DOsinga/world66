---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best window is **mid-December to mid-April**: hot, dry, with reliable trade winds and Atlantic water still warm enough to swim. Christmas and New Year are the peak; Easter is busy. **May to early June** is quieter and warmer, with the Pineapple Festival in Gregory Town in early June.

**July to October** is hot, humid, and inside the Atlantic hurricane season; many small hotels close in September. **November** is a gamble — usually fine, occasionally still stormy. Winds tend to be strongest in February and March, which is good for kiters but pushes Atlantic-side beaches around.
