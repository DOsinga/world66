The Exumas are a 200-kilometre chain of cays, and the famous sights are spread along it: the swimming pigs at Big Major Cay, **Thunderball Grotto**, the iguanas, the Land and Sea Park. From George Town on Great Exuma, a full day on a boat reaches most of them. From [Staniel Cay](/northamerica/bahamas/exumas/staniel_cay) they are minutes away. From Nassau there are fast day trips. Elizabeth Harbour, off George Town, has the diving and is the place to start a kayak trip down the cays.
