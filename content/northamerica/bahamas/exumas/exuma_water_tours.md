Captain Pat's boat tours up the Exuma Cays from Great Exuma. The half-day trip covers the nearer sights; the full day goes up to the **swimming pigs**, **Thunderball Grotto**, Starfish Bay and the rock iguanas.

The base is on Barraterre Road at Alexandra, north of George Town, which the map services do not place; the pin is on George Town.
