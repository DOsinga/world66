Staniel Cay is where the Exumas' set pieces are closest together, and this local company runs boats to all of them: the original **swimming pigs** on Big Major Cay, the snorkel through **Thunderball Grotto**, the sunken piano and the turtles, and further afield Norman's Cay and the Exuma Cays Land and Sea Park. Fishing trips too. Departures are from Staniel Cay itself, and also from Nassau, George Town and Florida for those not staying on the cay.
