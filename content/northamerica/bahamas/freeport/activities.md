Grand Bahama is flatter and quieter than New Providence, and its best outdoor trips are on the water or in the pine forest: kayaking to Peterson's Cay, the caves and mangroves of **Lucayan National Park**, and snorkelling on the reefs off Taino Beach. The [Dolphin Experience at UNEXSO](/northamerica/bahamas/freeport/dolphin_experience) is the island's best-known attraction.
