A watersports concession with booths on Taino Beach and at the Pirates Cove water park. The menu is broad: snorkelling at **Rainbow Reef**, a glass-bottom boat, guided wave-runner tours, sunset cruises, deep-sea fishing and a swimming-pig trip.
