The 57-hectare water park inside the Atlantis complex on Paradise Island, with eleven pools, twenty waterslides, a kilometre-long river ride through caves and lagoons, and a rope-and-plank cliff descent past shark-filled tanks. Among the best water parks anywhere — children love it; adults will too.

The **Power Tower** drops you through a near-vertical slide into a glass tunnel that runs through a shark lagoon. Day passes are expensive; the package is included if you stay at any Atlantis hotel. Bring water shoes. Best to arrive at opening to claim a lounger before the cruise excursion crowds arrive late morning.
