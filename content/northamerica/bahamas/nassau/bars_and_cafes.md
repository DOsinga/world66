title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Nassau drinks **Kalik** (the local lager, perfectly cold) and **Sands** (the rival, slightly more interesting). Bahamian rum is everywhere: try **Ricardo Burrows** for the local connoisseur's choice and **John Watling's Buena Vista** for a craft-distilled option. The standard rum cocktail is the **Bahama Mama** — golden rum, coconut rum, grenadine, pineapple — usually too sweet but a fixture.

For drinking with locals, **Arawak Cay** in the early evening is the obvious place. For the resort-bar experience, **Cable Beach** and the Atlantis lounges deliver. Downtown after dark gets quieter than it should — the cruise crowd leaves and many places close by 9 pm. The most reliable post-dinner scene is at Cable Beach.
