A coastal park at the southwest tip of New Providence, twenty-five minutes by car from downtown. The site combines several layers of Bahamian history — Lucayan rock art (small, hard to find without a guide), the ruins of a 19th-century slave plantation, and the cliff-top **Genesis** stone sculptures by Antonius Roberts, life-sized human figures cut into the limestone.

Trails wind down to a small swimming beach with good snorkelling around the rocks. The park is open daily for a small admission fee; weekends bring local families. Combine with **Love Beach** further north for a full day in western New Providence.
