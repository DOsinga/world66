The official residence of the Governor-General of the Bahamas, built in 1801 in the same pink Georgian colonial style as the parliament buildings. It sits at the top of the steep Mount Fitzwilliam, with a statue of Christopher Columbus at the foot of the front steps facing down toward the harbour.

The house itself is not normally open to the public, but the **Changing of the Guard ceremony** held every other Saturday at 10 am is worth catching for the police marching band in white pith helmets. The hilltop gives a fine view down to Bay Street. Most visitors stop in passing on the walk between Bay Street and the Queen's Staircase.
