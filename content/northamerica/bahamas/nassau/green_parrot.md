An open-air harbour-side bar and grill on East Bay Street, with a long wooden deck cantilevered over Nassau Harbour. The drinks are cheaper than the resort bars and the burgers are surprisingly good. The view across to Paradise Island is the reason to come; sunset is the time.

The Sandyport branch on the western side of town has the same formula in a marina setting. Both branches do food until late and stay open later than most downtown bars. Live music several nights a week.
