Small-group walks through old Nassau that mix history with tastings. **Rum Runner's Passage** is a three-hour tasting walk, and **Nassau Sites & Tastes** is a longer general introduction. The guides are certified under the Ministry of Tourism's Bahama Host programme.

The company publishes no street address, so the pin is on downtown Nassau. Its website shows more than one phone number; booking by email or online is the safe route.
