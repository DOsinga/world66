A long thin island just north of Nassau, connected to downtown by a pair of high-arched bridges. The eastern two thirds are dominated by the Atlantis resort and a string of luxury condominiums; the western tip is the more public, beachy end with Paradise Beach.

The island has the **best beaches** in the Nassau area (Cabbage, Paradise), the only **golf course** worth playing, and most of the resort hotels. There are taxis at every corner; the bridge toll is about a dollar each way. If you are staying in downtown Nassau, a day on Paradise Island is the obvious half-day excursion.
