The Exuma Cays are a long way from Nassau, and this is the fast way to get there and back in a day. Fast boats run across to the company's own private island in the Exuma Cays, with the **swimming pigs**, rock iguanas, stingray and shark feeding, and lunch on the beach. It has been running for more than twenty years.

The sales desk is at the Paradise Island ferry terminal; the office is in the Fred Ramsey Building on Shirley Street (+1 242 323 8888). The WhatsApp number is the after-hours line.
