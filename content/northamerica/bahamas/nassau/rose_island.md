A long, thin, mostly-uninhabited island stretching east from Paradise Island for fifteen kilometres. The main public draws are the white sand of **Sandy Toes Beach** near the western end and **Pirate Cove**, both reached by ferry-and-day-pass deals from Nassau marinas.

The Sandy Toes beach club includes ferry, lounger, lunch, and unlimited drinks for a fixed price — a reasonable package for a beach day without the Atlantis crowds. The far eastern end of the island has unspoiled beaches that you can only realistically reach by private boat charter.
