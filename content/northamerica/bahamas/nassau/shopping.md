---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Nassau's shopping is essentially the Bay Street duty-free strip — watches, jewellery, perfume, and cigars at supposedly tax-free prices. **John Bull** is the biggest of the watch-and-jewellery emporiums, with smaller competitors on either side. The savings on high-end Swiss watches are real; on lower-end goods the prices may not be much better than at home.

The **Straw Market** at the western end of Bay Street is the souvenir bazaar — straw work, T-shirts, conch jewellery, knock-off handbags, all with bargaining expected. For local crafts of better quality, try the **Plait Lady** on Bay Street or the **Doongalik Studios Art Gallery** on Village Road. **Bahamian rum** (John Watling's Buena Vista, Ricardo) and **Bahama Mama hot sauce** are the best edible souvenirs.
