The Bahamas' most established dive operator, on the southwest coast of New Providence about thirty minutes' drive from downtown. The signature trip is the **Shark Dive** — a circle of certified divers on the bottom while reef sharks are baited and circle above. Even non-divers can do **Shark Boat** snorkel trips that hover above the action.

Beyond sharks, the surrounding reefs offer some of the best wall diving in the Bahamas, with drops to over a hundred metres and reliably clear water. Wrecks include the *James Bond* movie set wreckage (the Vulcan bomber from *Thunderball*). Hotel pickups included; advance booking essential.
