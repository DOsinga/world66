A formal terraced garden on Paradise Island, originally laid out by the supermarket heir Huntington Hartford in the 1960s as the centrepiece of his transformation of Hog Island into a luxury destination. The terraces step down toward the harbour through clipped hedges and statuary; at the top sits a genuine **14th-century French cloister**, dismantled from a French monastery and reassembled stone by stone.

The gardens are free to wander and remain blessedly quiet despite being a short walk from Atlantis. Now part of the Ocean Club resort grounds; non-guests are welcome to walk through. Sunsets here look back over Nassau Harbour and are one of the underrated views in the Bahamas.
