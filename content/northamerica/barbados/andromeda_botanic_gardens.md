Andromeda Botanic Gardens is a hillside garden near Bathsheba, created by horticulturist Iris Bannochie and later given to the Barbados National Trust. Paths wind through palms, orchids, tropical trees, and flowering plants with views toward the east coast.

It is a good gentle stop when exploring Bathsheba and the wilder Atlantic side of Barbados.
