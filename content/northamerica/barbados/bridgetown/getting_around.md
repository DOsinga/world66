---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

**On foot.** The historic centre of Bridgetown is genuinely walkable — the main sights, the Parliament Buildings, the Careenage, the synagogue, and Broad Street are all within a fifteen-minute walk of each other. The Garrison area is about twenty minutes further south; Queen's Park is ten minutes east of the centre.

**ZR minibuses and route taxis.** The public transport system is built around ZR vans (privately operated minibuses) and yellow route taxis, both of which are cheap and cover most of Barbados. Fares within Bridgetown are a flat BBD 3.50. The main terminal is at Fairchild Street in the city centre; from here you can get to most parts of the island. ZRs are fast but can be crowded and the music is always loud.

**Taxis.** Conventional taxis are widely available and relatively affordable by Caribbean standards. They do not use meters — fix the price before you get in. The tourism authority publishes standard fare schedules; most drivers will quote within that range. Useful for getting to the Garrison, Carlisle Bay, or the airport.

**Car rental.** Available from the airport and several city locations. Traffic in Bridgetown is heavy in the morning and evening rush hours; outside rush hour the city is easy to drive. Note that Barbados drives on the left.
