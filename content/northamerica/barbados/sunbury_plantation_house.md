Sunbury Plantation House is one of Barbados's better surviving plantation houses, with a history stretching back to the 17th century. The interiors were rebuilt after a fire, but the house still gives a useful look at the island's sugar-era architecture and estate life.

It is a small site, so it works best as a stop while crossing the island rather than as a full half-day outing. Cruise groups can make it feel crowded; come outside peak tour hours if you want a quieter look around.
