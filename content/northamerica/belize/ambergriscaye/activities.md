The reef sits a few hundred metres off the eastern shore of the caye, close enough that the waves break in a line you can see from the beach, and everything here follows from that. **Hol Chan Marine Reserve** is a cut through the reef where the fish concentrate; **Shark Ray Alley** alongside it is where fishermen used to clean their catch, and the nurse sharks and southern stingrays that learned to gather for it still do.

Ambergris is also the main departure point for the **[Great Blue Hole](/northamerica/belize/great_blue_hole)** day — two hours each way, three dives, an early start — and for the atolls at Turneffe and Lighthouse Reef, which are the better diving by some distance.

Golf carts are the island's transport and the island's hazard; there is one road and everybody is on it.
