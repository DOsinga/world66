A dive centre on its own pier about a mile south of San Pedro town, working the reef off Ambergris — Hol Chan and Shark Ray Alley for the shallow stuff — and running the long day out to the [Great Blue Hole](/northamerica/belize/great_blue_hole) and Lighthouse Reef. They also do PADI courses, boat charters and fishing.
