The reef is what Ambergris Caye is about. Hol Chan Marine Reserve and Shark Ray Alley are among the most accessible and rewarding dive and snorkel sites in the Caribbean — close to shore, in calm water, and teeming with marine life. Beyond the reef, the island itself has a relaxed beach-town atmosphere centered on the sandy streets of San Pedro.
