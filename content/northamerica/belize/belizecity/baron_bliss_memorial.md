The Baron Bliss Memorial stands near the Fort George Lighthouse at the northern end of the city's peninsula. It marks the grave of Henry Bliss, a Portuguese-British nobleman who bequeathed his substantial fortune to the country of Belize despite never having set foot on shore — a story that ranks among the stranger acts of colonial-era philanthropy.

The memorial is a modest structure but the story behind it is anything but. The Bliss Institute of Performing Arts, one of Belize City's main cultural venues, was built with funds from the same bequest, as were several rural libraries and the Bliss School of Nursing. His generosity reshaped the infrastructure of the young nation.
