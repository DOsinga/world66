Belize Zoo is one of the easiest places in the country to see native wildlife
responsibly and at close range. Jaguars, tapirs, toucans, harpy eagles, and other
Belizean species are housed in a forested setting that grew from rescue and education
work rather than a conventional menagerie.

From Belmopan it makes a simple half-day trip, especially for families or travelers who
will not have time for long jungle excursions. The value is not only seeing animals, but
learning what lives in Belize's forests before you head into reserves where sightings
are far less predictable.
