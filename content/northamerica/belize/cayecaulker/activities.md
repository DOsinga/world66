Caye Caulker sells the reef and not much else, which is the point of it. The island is five streets of sand with golf carts and a sign telling you to go slow, and the day is structured around getting on a boat: the Hol Chan channel and Shark Ray Alley to the south, the seahorses and manatees in the shallows, and the longer runs out to Turneffe or the [Great Blue Hole](/northamerica/belize/great_blue_hole).

Prices barely differ between operators here, because the marine reserve fees are fixed and everyone visits the same three or four sites. What varies is the boat — skiff or catamaran — the group size, and whether lunch happens on a caye or on deck.

Almost every operator on the island runs on WhatsApp. Several publish a number and no email address at all, which is worth knowing before you try to book from home.
