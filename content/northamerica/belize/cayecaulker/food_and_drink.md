Caye Caulker is cheap by Caribbean standards and genuinely good for fresh seafood. Grilled fish, lobster (in season), conch fritters, and rice and beans are the staples. Most restaurants are open-air and unpretentious. Breakfast spots serving eggs, fry jacks, and fresh juice open early to catch divers heading out at dawn.

Belikin is the Belizean beer and is available everywhere, cold, for BZ$4–6. A few bars along Front Street and at the Split serve rum punches and mix cocktails. Nothing is expensive.
