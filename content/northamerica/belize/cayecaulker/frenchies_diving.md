Family-run on Caye Caulker since 1990 and one of the two shops on the island that make the long day out to the [Great Blue Hole](/northamerica/belize/great_blue_hole) and Lighthouse Reef. Closer to home they dive the reef off the island and the sites around Hol Chan, and run night dives, which on this reef means octopus and the tarpon that hunt in the boat lights.
