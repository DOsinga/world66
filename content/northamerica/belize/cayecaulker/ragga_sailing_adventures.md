The three-day sail south along the reef to [Placencia](/northamerica/belize/placencia) is the trip this island is known for: snorkelling the sites you cannot reach on a day boat, sleeping on uninhabited cayes, cooking on the beach. It runs in both directions and books out well ahead in the dry season.

They also do the shorter things — the Hol Chan snorkel sail and the sunset run — at prices they publish openly, which is rarer here than it should be. Note that the older raggamuffintours.com address now redirects here; it is the same operation trading under the Ragga name.
