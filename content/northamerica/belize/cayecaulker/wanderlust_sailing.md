Sailing and snorkelling out of Caye Caulker on a catamaran rather than a skiff, which makes the difference on the longer runs south to Hol Chan and Shark Ray Alley. The standard day takes in three or four stops — the channel, the rays, the seahorses in the shallows off the island — with rum punch on the way back, which is roughly the island's official religion.
