---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Corozal sits on the Northern Highway, the main road between Belize City and the Mexican border at Santa Elena (Chetumal). It is one of the easier Belizean towns to reach.

**By bus.** Frequent buses run from Belize City — three and a half to four hours via Orange Walk, with regular departures throughout the day. Buses arrive at the small Corozal terminal a few blocks back from the waterfront. The border at Santa Elena is 15 km north; minibuses and shared taxis run from Corozal to the crossing every half hour or so.

**From Mexico.** The Chetumal–Corozal hop is straightforward: combi minibuses run between Chetumal's central bus terminal and Corozal across the day, with a stop at the border for immigration formalities. The whole trip takes about 90 minutes including the border.

**By plane.** Tropic Air and Maya Island Air both run several daily flights between Corozal Municipal Airport and the cayes (San Pedro on Ambergris Caye), about 20 minutes each way — a popular short hop that avoids the long road and water transfer via Belize City.

**By boat.** The Thunderbolt fast boat used to run between Corozal and San Pedro daily; check current schedules locally as the service has been intermittent in recent years.
