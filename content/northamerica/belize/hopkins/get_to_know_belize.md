A licensed, locally owned family company in Hopkins, running the things this stretch of coast is for: snorkelling the southern reef and the cayes offshore, the **Cockscomb Basin Wildlife Sanctuary** inland — the world's first jaguar preserve, though what you actually see is the forest, the waterfalls and the tracks — cave tubing, and the Maya sites further south.

Hopkins is a Garífuna village, and the cultural side of what the operators here sell — drumming, cooking, the language — is not a performance laid on for coaches; it is a working village that takes visitors.
