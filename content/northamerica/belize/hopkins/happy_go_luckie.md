A Hopkins outfit covering the same ground from the other end of the village: snorkel trips out to the cayes, night paddles and river trips, the Cockscomb Basin, and the drive inland to the waterfalls at Mayflower Bocawina. Hopkins is small enough that both operators will know if the sea is too rough to go out, and will say so.
