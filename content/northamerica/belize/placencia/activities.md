Placencia is the southern reef's base: quieter diving than Ambergris, fewer boats, and cayes you stop at for lunch that are often empty. The signature trip is **Gladden Spit**, where whale sharks come in to feed on the spawn of snapper aggregations around the full moons from March to June. It depends on fish behaviour rather than a timetable, and any operator promising you a sighting is selling something they do not control.

The peninsula is also the jumping-off point for the Monkey River, the Cockscomb Basin jaguar preserve inland, and the Garífuna coast north towards [Hopkins](/northamerica/belize/hopkins) and [Dangriga](/northamerica/belize/dangriga).
