The Placencia Sidewalk is the village's main pedestrian thread, a narrow concrete path running past houses, bars, shops, and beach access points. It is more useful than monumental, but it gives Placencia its walkable, barefoot feel.

Follow it slowly rather than treating it as a sight to tick off. The point is the drift between sea, food, and village life.
