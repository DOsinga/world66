A PADI shop on Placencia's Main Street, diving the southern reef and the cayes off the peninsula. Its distinctive trip is **Gladden Spit**, where whale sharks come in to feed on the spawn of snapper aggregations around the full moons of March to June — an encounter that depends on fish behaviour rather than a schedule, so nobody can promise you one.

The southern reef is quieter than the diving off Ambergris: fewer boats, and the cayes you stop at for lunch are mostly empty.
