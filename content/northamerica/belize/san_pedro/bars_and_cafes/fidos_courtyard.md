Fido's Courtyard is the social hub of San Pedro's town centre — a large open-air bar, restaurant, and entertainment venue on the waterfront. The thatched roof, fairy lights, and broad deck are unpretentious in a way that feels right for a place this size. Belikin is on tap and the pours are generous.

Live music plays several nights a week, ranging from local bands to touring acts, and the crowd swells accordingly. It draws a tourist-heavy crowd but that is the nature of San Pedro, and the atmosphere on a busy evening is good. If you want to find someone you met on the reef, this is where they probably ended up.
