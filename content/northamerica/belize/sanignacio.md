San Ignacio (also called Cayo) is the main town in western Belize and the base for exploring the interior. It sits on the [Macal River](/northamerica/belize/sanignacio/macal_river) near the Guatemalan border, and the surrounding area packs in Maya ruins, jungle caves, waterfalls, and wildlife into a compact area.

The town itself is lively and unpretentious — the [Saturday market](/northamerica/belize/sanignacio/saturday_market) brings vendors from surrounding Maya and Mennonite communities, and there are good restaurants, guesthouses at all price levels, and plenty of tour operators on Burns Avenue. The [Hawkesworth Bridge](/northamerica/belize/sanignacio/hawkesworth_bridge) — Belize's first suspension bridge — crosses the Macal to Santa Elena. It's one of the most pleasant places in Belize to base yourself for several days.

The Maya site of [Cahal Pech](/northamerica/belize/sanignacio/cahal_pech) sits on a hill on the edge of town and is reachable on foot. The major day trips start a short drive out: [Xunantunich](/northamerica/belize/sanignacio/xunantunich) — a large Maya site with a 40-metre pyramid and panoramic views — is 12 km away, reached by a hand-cranked ferry across the Mopan River. [Caracol](/northamerica/belize/sanignacio/caracol), deeper in the Chiquibul jungle, is a longer trip but one of the most impressive Maya sites in the region.

[Actun Tunichil Muknal](/northamerica/belize/sanignacio/atm_cave) requires a tour with a licensed guide — it's a jungle hike, river swim, and cave exploration, with Maya skeletal remains and ceramics still in situ. It's one of the most memorable experiences in Belize, though increasingly popular; book ahead. The Macal and Mopan rivers offer tubing, kayaking, and canoeing — multi-day river trips are available. The [Belize Botanic Gardens](/northamerica/belize/sanignacio/belize_botanic_gardens) at duPlooy's downstream make a slower-paced afternoon.

La Loma Luz Hospital in Santa Elena (across the bridge from San Ignacio) is one of the two best private hospitals in Belize.
