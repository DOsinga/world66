Green Hills Butterfly Ranch is a small nature stop in the hills outside San Ignacio, focused on Belize's butterflies and the plants that support them. It gives a gentler wildlife experience than caves, ruins, or jungle hikes, and the setting makes the drive part of the outing.

It is best for travellers who enjoy slow observation: wings, flowers, humidity, and the small mechanics of a butterfly house. Combine it with other Cayo sights rather than making it your only plan for the day.
