Tropical Wings Nature Center is a small family-friendly stop near San Jose Succotz, west of San Ignacio. It combines a butterfly farm, a modest educational museum, and outdoor activities in a green setting.

It pairs best with nearby Xunantunich or the Belize Botanic Gardens rather than as a full-day destination.
