The Bermuda Historical Society Museum is a small museum in central Hamilton covering the island's past, with particular attention to Bermuda's period as a British colony. It is the kind of stop that helps put the capital's pastel streets and civic buildings into context.

The museum is best combined with nearby Front Street, the Cathedral of the Most Holy Trinity, and the Sessions House rather than treated as a long stand-alone visit.
