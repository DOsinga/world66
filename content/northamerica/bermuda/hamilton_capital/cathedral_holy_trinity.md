The Cathedral of the Most Holy Trinity on Church Street is the Anglican cathedral of Bermuda and the island's most prominent church. The Gothic Revival building in local limestone was consecrated in 1911 after decades of construction following the destruction of its predecessor, and the tower is visible from much of the city.

The interior holds memorial windows, military plaques, and a cedar-panelled nave that reflects Bermuda's traditional building material. Visitors are welcome outside service times. The tower can be climbed for a fee, giving a bird's-eye view over Hamilton's rooftops and the harbour — a good alternative to Fort Hamilton if you prefer church architecture to military history.
