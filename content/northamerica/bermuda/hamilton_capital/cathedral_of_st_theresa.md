The Cathedral of St Theresa is Hamilton's Roman Catholic cathedral, close enough to the city's better-known Anglican cathedral to make a useful architectural comparison. It is quieter and less showy, but its pale stone, tower, and calm interior add another layer to the capital's religious landscape.

A visit is brief, and that is fine. Step in while walking central Hamilton, especially if you are tracing the city's churches, government buildings, and harbour streets rather than heading straight for shops or ferries.
