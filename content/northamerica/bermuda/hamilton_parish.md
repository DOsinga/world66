Hamilton Parish occupies Bermuda's north-eastern middle, wrapped around Harrington Sound and split between quiet residential lanes, caves, small bays, and Flatts Village. It should not be confused with the City of Hamilton; this is a parish, not the capital.

The strongest reason to come is the cluster around Bailey's Bay and Flatts. [Crystal and Fantasy Caves](/northamerica/bermuda/hamilton_parish/crystal_and_fantasy_caves) are among Bermuda's classic sights, with clear underground pools and limestone formations. Flatts Village adds the [Bermuda Aquarium, Museum and Zoo](/northamerica/bermuda/hamilton_parish/bermuda_aquarium_museum_and_zoo), a tidal inlet where water races under the bridge, and easy bus access between Hamilton and St. George's.

The parish works well as a half-day loop: caves, aquarium, a look at Harrington Sound, then a beach or the Railway Trail. It is not a place for nightlife or big-town energy, but it gives Bermuda a quieter, greener, more local-feeling day away from the cruise ship routes.
