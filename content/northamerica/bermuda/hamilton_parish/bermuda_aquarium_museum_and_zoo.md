The Bermuda Aquarium, Museum and Zoo sits in Flatts Village and is one of the island's easiest family stops. Its centrepiece is a large living coral reef tank focused on Bermuda's marine life, backed by natural-history exhibits and a small zoo.

It is compact enough for an hour or two, which suits Bermuda's scale. Pair it with Flatts Bridge and a meal nearby rather than treating it as an all-day attraction.
