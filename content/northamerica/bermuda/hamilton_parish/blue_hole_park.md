Blue Hole Park is a small, lush pocket of Hamilton Parish with caves, ponds, and dense vegetation close to the airport road. It is less formal than the commercial caves nearby, which makes it useful when you want a short walk rather than a guided attraction.

Bring shoes that can handle uneven ground. The park is at its best as part of a Bailey's Bay loop with Crystal and Fantasy Caves.
