Devil's Hole is a water-filled sinkhole near John Smith's Bay. The opening is small but memorable, with deep water below and strange sounds as the water level rises and falls.

It is a short stop rather than a major attraction, best paired with the nearby beaches, Flatts, or the Bermuda Aquarium, Museum and Zoo.
