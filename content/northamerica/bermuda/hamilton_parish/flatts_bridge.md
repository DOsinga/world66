Flatts Bridge spans the narrow channel between Harrington Sound and the open Atlantic, where tidal water can rush through with surprising speed. It is a small sight, but a very Bermudian one: colourful village buildings, clear water, fish below, and the occasional spotted eagle ray if you are lucky.

It is not worth a special trip by itself. It works because it sits beside the aquarium and the restaurants of Flatts Village, turning a quick look into a pleasant stop.
