The Bermuda Railway Trail cuts through Hamilton Parish on the route of the old island railway. Around Bailey's Bay and Coney Island it gives walkers and cyclists ocean views without requiring them to share Bermuda's narrow roads.

Use it as a short scenic section rather than trying to cover the whole island in one go. In this parish, it pairs neatly with the caves or Blue Hole Park.
