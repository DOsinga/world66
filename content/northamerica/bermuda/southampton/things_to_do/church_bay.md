A small, sheltered beach known as one of the best shore-snorkelling spots on the island. The shallow reef just offshore is dense with fish and coral, making it a favourite with beginners and families. The bay gets its name from the small church on the headland above it. Facilities are minimal — bring your own gear. Access is down a short path from South Shore Road.
