  When it was built in 1846, Gibbs Hill was one of the world's first cast-iron
  lighthouses. The technology had only just become viable, and the prefabricated
  sections were shipped from England and assembled on the hilltop. The light
  still operates today, guiding ships through the same reefs it was built to
  warn against 180 years ago.
---

The oldest cast-iron lighthouse in the world still in operation, standing 36 metres tall on the highest point in Southampton. The 185 steps to the top reward the climb with a 360-degree panorama across the parish rooftops, the Great Sound, and the open Atlantic — on a clear day you can see the entire length of the island. The lighthouse has been in continuous operation since 1846. A small tea room at the base serves breakfast and lunch.
