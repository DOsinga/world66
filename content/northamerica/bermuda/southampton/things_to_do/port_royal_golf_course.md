Bermuda's premier public golf course, designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr and carved into the clifftops along the north-west shore of Southampton. Several holes play directly above the ocean. The course hosted the PGA Grand Slam of Golf for many years and offers some of the most dramatic golf scenery in the Atlantic. Green fees are reasonable by Bermuda standards and the course is open to all.
