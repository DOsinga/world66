Clearwater Beach, Turtle Beach, Long Bay, Well Bay, and Soldier Bay form the beach cluster around Cooper's Island and the former U.S. air base lands near St. George's. The area has calm water, open sand, basic facilities, and good space for families.

Come for an easy beach stop after seeing historic St. George's, or continue into Cooper's Island Nature Reserve for a quieter walk and sea views.
