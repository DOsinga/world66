The beaches around Cooper's Island run along Bermuda's quiet eastern end, with Clearwater Beach, Turtle Bay, Long Bay, Well Bay, and Soldier Bay all close together. The former US air base land now feels open and low-key, with summer lifeguards and easy facilities at Clearwater.
