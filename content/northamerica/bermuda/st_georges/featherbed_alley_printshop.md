Featherbed Alley Printshop is a small museum devoted to Bermuda's early printing trade and the story of the Bermuda Gazette. Its replica Gutenberg press helps make the old craft feel concrete rather than just a line in a history panel.

It is a short stop, but a useful one in St. George's, where much of the pleasure is in piecing together the old town's maritime and colonial past.
