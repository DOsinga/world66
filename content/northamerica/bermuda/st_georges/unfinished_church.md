The Unfinished Church sits above town as a roofless stone shell, begun in the 19th century and never completed. It could have become a replacement for St. Peter's; instead it became one of Bermuda's most memorable ruins.

Its open arches and grass floor make a good contrast with the lived-in weight of St. Peter's below.
