Cow Wreck Beach is at the western end of Anegada's north shore — quieter than Loblolly Bay and requiring a longer drive on rough tracks from The Settlement. The reward is a wide beach with clear water and, on most days, very few other people.

The bar and restaurant here has been a fixture for decades, serving the standard Anegada menu: lobster, fresh fish, and cold rum punch. The snorkelling is decent close to shore. This is the kind of beach where you anchor a sailboat, row ashore, and spend the afternoon.
