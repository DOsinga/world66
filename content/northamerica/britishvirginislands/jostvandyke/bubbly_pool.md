Bubbly Pool is a small natural rock pool on the northeastern side of Jost Van Dyke. When the swell is right, waves force water through the rocks and turn the pool into a foaming saltwater bath.

It is an easy walk from the Diamond Cay area and makes a good low-key outing away from the island's beach bars. Conditions matter: without enough swell it is more of a quiet tide pool than a show.
