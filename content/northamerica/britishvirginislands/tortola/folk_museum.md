A small folk and history museum on Main Street in Road Town, housed in a traditional wooden West Indian building. The collection is modest but well-curated, covering pre-Columbian Arawak and Carib artefacts, the plantation era, and a section on the 1867 wreck of the RMS Rhone — including objects salvaged from the wreck site off Salt Island.

Worth half an hour as part of a Road Town walk. Free entry. Hours can be irregular outside the cruise-ship season; check before making a special trip.
