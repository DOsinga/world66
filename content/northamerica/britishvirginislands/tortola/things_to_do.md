Tortola's best beaches are on the north coast. Use the filter below to pick by category — beaches, historic sights, hiking and nature, or museums and rum.

For practical day-trip planning, Diving and snorkelling are excellent across the BVI: wrecks (most famously the **RMS Rhone**, off nearby Salt Island), shallow reefs, and dozens of anchorages along the Sir Francis Drake Channel. Day boats from Road Town and West End run snorkel and dive trips to **Norman Island, the Caves**, **The Indians**, and **The Baths** on Virgin Gorda — Tortola is the natural base for all of them.
