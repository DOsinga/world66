---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The high season is **December through April** — warm, sunny, dry, and the busiest months for sailing and cruise visits. Prices for accommodation and yacht charters peak around Christmas, New Year, and Easter; book well ahead.

The shoulder months of **May, June, and November** are excellent value, with prices dropping and the weather still mostly fine. Hurricane season officially runs from **June through November**, with peak risk in August and September. Tortola was hit hard by Hurricane Irma in September 2017 and the territory has been rebuilding since; check current conditions before booking in late summer.

The annual **BVI Spring Regatta and Sailing Festival** in late March/early April is the territory's biggest event, drawing yachts and crews from across the world to Road Town and Nanny Cay. The **August Emancipation Festival** in Road Town marks the abolition of slavery with a week of parades, music, and food.
