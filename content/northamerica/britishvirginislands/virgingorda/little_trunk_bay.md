Little Trunk Bay is a quiet beach just north of Spring Bay, reached by a short trail. It has the same granite-boulder scenery that makes this corner of Virgin Gorda famous, but usually with fewer people. A rougher path continues from the north end toward Big Trunk Bay, helped by a rope on the steepest section.
