South Sound is a quiet reef-protected bay on Virgin Gorda, shallow inside the reef and richer in coral and fish outside it. It is less developed than the island's better-known beach stops, which is the appeal: clear water, a sandy bottom, and a sheltered feel.

The opening in the reef makes access possible, but this is still a place to treat with care. Conditions matter, and snorkellers should ask locally before heading through or around the reef.
