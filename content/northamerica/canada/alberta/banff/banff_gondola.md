An eight-minute gondola ride to the 2,281-metre summit ridge of Sulphur Mountain, where a boardwalk connects two summits with panoramic views over Banff town, the Bow Valley, and the surrounding peaks. The upper terminal has a restaurant and interpretive centre. For those who want to earn the views, a hiking trail climbs the mountain from the hot springs below — about five kilometres and 700 metres of gain. You can take the gondola back down. The Banff Upper Hot Springs pool sits at the base of the gondola; both can be combined in a half-day visit.
