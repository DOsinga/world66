The best restaurant on Bear Street and one of the most consistent in the town. The menu focuses on Alberta and Canadian ingredients — bison, elk, Alberta beef, local trout, seasonal foraged items — cooked in a straightforward style that lets the produce speak. The bison short rib and the Rocky Mountain trout are reliable orders. The room is warm and wood-panelled, with a long bar that serves good Canadian whisky. Book ahead in season.

**Address:** 211 Bear Street, Banff.
