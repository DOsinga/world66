Not set in Banff, but Richler is the defining novelist of mid-century Canadian life, and his comic masterpiece about a young Jewish Montrealer scrambling for success in postwar Canada is essential reading for understanding the country you are traveling in. Several of Richler's novels touch on the tension between Montreal and the vast, indifferent Canadian landscape — which is what Banff, in a way, represents to most Canadians: something immense and beautiful that most of them experience only briefly, on a trip west.
