The standard reference for hiking in Banff, Jasper, Yoho, Kootenay, and the other Rockies parks, now in its ninth edition. Covers more than 200 trails with detailed descriptions, distances, elevation profiles, and maps. Written by two people who have actually walked all of these trails, not assembled from other sources. Not exactly literature, but indispensable if you are spending any serious time hiking in the park.
