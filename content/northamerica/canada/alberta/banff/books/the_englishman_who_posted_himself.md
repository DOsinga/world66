A short, strange, and celebrated Canadian novel about a librarian sent to catalogue a collection at a remote northern Ontario estate, where she begins a relationship with the resident bear. It won the Governor General's Award in 1976 and remains a touchstone of Canadian literature precisely because of how seriously it takes the idea of the non-human Canadian wilderness as a presence, a force, something that cannot be tamed or understood on human terms. Relevant reading for any trip to bear country.
