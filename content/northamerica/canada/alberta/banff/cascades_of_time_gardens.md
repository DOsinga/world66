Cascades of Time Gardens is a landscaped garden in front of Banff's Administration Building. Laid out in the 1930s, it covers about four acres with terraces, paths, flower beds, and rustic timber-and-stone pavilions.

The gardens are an easy walk from central Banff and make a calm break from the busier viewpoints. The pavilions are recognized federal heritage buildings, and the site has good views back toward the town and surrounding peaks.
