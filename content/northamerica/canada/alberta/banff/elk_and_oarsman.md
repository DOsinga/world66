The classic Banff pub, operating since 1983 on Banff Avenue. Two floors, multiple bars, a rooftop terrace that is always packed in summer, and a menu of burgers and nachos that fuel the après-hiking crowd. The beer selection is heavy on Alberta craft options — Wild Rose, Big Rock, and Banff Ave Brewing all feature. It gets loud and crowded on weekends, but there's no more reliably atmospheric spot for a post-trail pint in town.

**Address:** 119 Banff Avenue, Banff.
