---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car:** Banff is about 130 kilometres west of Calgary on the Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1). The drive takes about an hour and a half in normal conditions. All vehicles entering Banff National Park require a park pass — day passes are available at the park gates. Annual Discovery Passes are worth buying if you plan to visit multiple national parks.

**By bus and shuttle:** Banff Airporter and Brewster Express run regular shuttle services between Calgary International Airport and Banff, with the trip taking about two hours. Several companies also offer services connecting Banff to Jasper along the Icefields Parkway. The on-demand Roam Transit system serves Banff town and connects to Lake Louise.

**By rail:** VIA Rail's Canadian (Toronto–Vancouver) passes through nearby Canmore and Banff, though the train runs only twice weekly in each direction. Rocky Mountaineer operates luxury rail journeys between Vancouver and Banff (and onward to Jasper), running April to October. These are scenic experiences rather than practical transportation.

**By air:** Calgary International Airport (YYC) is the practical gateway, with direct flights from most major Canadian cities and many international hubs. Edmonton International Airport is about 4 hours away and less convenient.
