An intensely turquoise glacier lake backed by the Valley of the Ten Peaks, considered by many to be the most beautiful lake in the Canadian Rockies. It appeared on the Canadian twenty-dollar bill in the 1970s. The lake is reached via a 14 km side road from Lake Louise village. In peak summer season (June to October) Moraine Lake Road is closed to private vehicles; access is only by reservation-based Parks Canada shuttle or on foot and bicycle. The Rockpile trail gives the classic elevated view over the lake in about 20 minutes. The lakeshore trail is flat and easy; canoe rentals are available from the lodge boathouse.

story: >
  The lake's extraordinary colour comes from glacial rock flour — fine particles ground from the rock by glaciers above — suspended in the meltwater. The particles refract light in the blue-green spectrum, producing the signature turquoise that shifts in intensity with the season and the angle of the light.
