Sulphur Mountain Trail is the walking version of Banff's famous gondola view. The trail climbs steadily through forest to the summit area, where the boardwalk opens onto big Bow Valley and surrounding peak views.

It is a real climb, not a casual stroll, so bring water and layers. Many hikers take the gondola down when operating, but check current rules and schedules before relying on that plan.
