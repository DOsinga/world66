---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Banff is fundamentally an outdoors destination. The town itself offers a Whyte Museum of the Canadian Rockies, the Banff Park Museum (one of the oldest natural history museums in western Canada), the Cave and Basin National Historic Site, and the Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity. But the reason most people come is the landscape beyond town: Lake Louise, Moraine Lake, Johnston Canyon, the Icefields Parkway. The ski season runs roughly December to May at three resorts; summer hiking and cycling take over from June through September.
