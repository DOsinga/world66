The only public hot springs in Banff open for bathing (the original Cave and Basin closed to swimmers in 1993). The outdoor pool is fed by natural sulphur springs at around 37 to 40 degrees Celsius — hot enough that the steam rises visibly in winter, when soaking in the mineral water surrounded by snowfields is a memorable experience. The site has operated as a bathhouse since 1886. Basic towel and suit rental is available. Sitting at 1,585 metres with views of the surrounding peaks, it is one of the better excuses to do nothing for an hour.
