---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Banff has two distinct peak seasons separated by brief shoulder periods that are the best times to visit.

**Winter (December to March)** is ski season. The three resorts — Lake Louise, Sunshine Village, and Mt. Norquay — draw skiers and snowboarders from around the world. The town is busy, especially over Christmas and in February. January tends to be quieter and often cold enough for good snow conditions.

**Summer (June to August)** is the other peak. Hiking, cycling, and driving the Icefields Parkway draw millions of visitors, and the main roads and attractions can be very crowded by July. Moraine Lake Road closes to private vehicles in peak season; Parks Canada reservation systems and shuttle buses manage access.

**Shoulder seasons (April-May and September-October)** are the best for avoiding crowds. Late September brings reliable golden larch season in the high alpine areas — the larch trees above Moraine Lake and on the Plain of Six Glaciers turn brilliant yellow in late September, one of the finest autumn displays in Canada.

**The Banff Mountain Film and Book Festival** runs every autumn (usually late October to early November), drawing outdoor film and adventure travel content from around the world. The Banff Springs Hotel is booked solid during this period.
