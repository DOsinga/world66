The best coffee in Banff, in a small, well-designed space on Bear Street. Espresso drinks are made carefully, the pastries are excellent, and the staff knows what they are doing. Popular with the local guide community for early-morning meetings before heading into the mountains. Also has a second location in Lake Louise village.

**Address:** 401 Banff Avenue, Banff.
