The central cultural institution in Banff, with collections focused on the art and history of the Canadian Rockies. The gallery holds paintings by the Group of Seven and other artists who came to document the mountain landscape from the 1880s onward. The archives hold one of the most comprehensive collections of historical photographs, films, and documents related to the Rockies. The museum also maintains four historic homes on the property, including that of Peter and Catharine Whyte, the artists who founded the museum in 1968. Worth a visit on a rainy afternoon or for anyone seriously interested in the human history of the park.
