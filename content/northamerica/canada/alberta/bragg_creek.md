Bragg Creek sits where Calgary's western edge gives way to ranch country, spruce woods, and the first serious folds of the Rockies foothills. It is small, but it has a clear job: an easy day out for Calgarians and a softer entry to Kananaskis Country than Banff's busy main corridor.

The hamlet itself has cafes, galleries, and outfitters, but the day is really made by the outdoors around it. [Bragg Creek Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/alberta/bragg_creek/bragg_creek_provincial_park) gives you the Elbow River close to town, [West Bragg Creek Trails](/northamerica/canada/alberta/bragg_creek/west_bragg_creek_trails) supplies the year-round trail network, and [Elbow Falls](/northamerica/canada/alberta/bragg_creek/elbow_falls) is the obvious scenic stop farther along Highway 66.

This is a useful World66 location because it works as a real spare-day choice from Calgary. Come for a walk, a waterfall, and a meal in the hamlet, not for a checklist of museums.
