West Bragg Creek is the serious trailhead near the hamlet. The network spreads across forest and foothills west of town, with routes used for hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding, snowshoeing, and cross-country skiing depending on the season.

Pick a route before you go rather than arriving vaguely. The trail system is large enough to be confusing, and winter or mud-season conditions can change what is enjoyable on the day.
