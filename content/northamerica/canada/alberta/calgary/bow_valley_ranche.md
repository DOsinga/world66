Bow Valley Ranche is a historic ranch site in Fish Creek Provincial Park, on the south side of Calgary. The old ranch house and surrounding grounds recall the city's cattle-ranching period, before Calgary grew around the Bow and Elbow river valleys.

The area has interpretive walks, gardens, and a quiet park setting that makes it more than a quick look at an old building. It works well as part of a longer visit to Fish Creek.
