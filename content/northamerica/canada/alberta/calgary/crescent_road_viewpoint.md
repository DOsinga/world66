Crescent Road Viewpoint sits on the ridge above the Bow River and gives one of the easiest free views of downtown Calgary. From here the towers rise above Prince's Island Park, with the river valley and pathways laid out below.

Follow the path along the ridge for changing angles, or take the stairs down the hillside if you want to turn the view into a short walk. It is best in clear weather, especially near sunset.
