A low-rise shopping complex at the south end of the Glenmore Reservoir, on the corner of 14 Street and 90 Avenue SW. Around sixty shops and services — bakery, coffee, fashion, a small gallery — aimed mostly at locals. Worth a stop if you are out walking or cycling the reservoir paths.
