A restaurant and lounge attached to the Holiday Inn on Macleod Trail South. Buffets at breakfast, lunch and dinner, plus a standard steak-and-seafood menu and a Sunday brunch. The sort of place that makes sense if you are staying on Macleod Trail; otherwise skip it for the better food downtown.
