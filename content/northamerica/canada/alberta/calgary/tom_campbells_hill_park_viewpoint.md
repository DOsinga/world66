Tom Campbell's Hill Park has open views over the confluence of the Bow River and Nose Creek, with downtown Calgary's towers to the southwest.

It is a simple viewpoint rather than a long visit, but it pairs well with Inglewood, the river paths and nearby Fort Calgary.
