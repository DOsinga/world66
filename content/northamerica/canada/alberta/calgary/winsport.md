WinSport occupies the former Canada Olympic Park, built for the 1988 Winter Olympics. Visitors can tour the ski jump area for a view over Calgary, visit Canada's Sports Hall of Fame, or use the site's seasonal activities, from skiing and snowboarding in winter to luge-style rides and ziplines in summer.
