Camrose is a small city southeast of Edmonton, where Alberta's prairie starts to soften into aspen parkland. It grew as a railway hub and farm-service centre, and it still feels calm and practical, but the old downtown, museums, campus, and Mirror Lake give it more texture than a routine highway stop.

The best visit starts with [Bailey Theatre](/northamerica/canada/alberta/camrose/bailey_theatre), a surviving vaudeville house, then moves through the [Railway Museum](/northamerica/canada/alberta/camrose/camrose_railway_museum) and [Centennial Museum](/northamerica/canada/alberta/camrose/camrose_centennial_museum). [Mirror Lake Park](/northamerica/canada/alberta/camrose/mirror_lake_park) is the easy walk that ties the city together.

Camrose becomes a different place during the [Big Valley Jamboree](/northamerica/canada/alberta/camrose/big_valley_jamboree), when country music crowds fill the exhibition grounds. Outside festival time, it is still a worthwhile half-day or slow day if you are already in east-central Alberta and like towns with visible railway bones.
