Bailey Theatre is the most characterful building in downtown Camrose. Opened in the early 20th century and restored as a community performance space, it carries the old vaudeville-town atmosphere that many prairie main streets have lost.

It is worth checking the programme before you go. Even without a show, the facade and lobby give downtown Camrose a real historical anchor.
