The Camrose and District Centennial Museum is a practical local-history stop rather than a grand museum. Its value is in the collection of buildings and artefacts that explain the farm settlements, services, and institutions around Camrose.

It pairs well with the railway museum. Together they show why this became the service centre for a wider rural district.
