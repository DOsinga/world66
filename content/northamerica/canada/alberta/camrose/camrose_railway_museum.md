Camrose Railway Museum occupies a 1911 Canadian Northern Railway depot, a good setting for a town whose early growth depended on rail lines. Displays cover local railway history, with archives, small exhibits, and period details inside the station.

It is a quiet stop, but a useful one. Camrose makes more sense when you see how the rails connected it to Edmonton, Calgary, and the smaller farm towns around it.
