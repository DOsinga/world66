Mirror Lake Park is Camrose's simplest pleasure: a central lake, walking paths, waterfowl, and enough open space to slow the town down. In winter, skating on the lake is part of the local rhythm when conditions allow.

It is not a destination by itself, but it rounds out a Camrose visit nicely after the museums and downtown theatre.
