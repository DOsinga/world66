---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Canmore's main draw is the trails — dozens of them, starting at the edge of town and ranging from easy riverside walks to serious alpine scrambles. Ha Ling Peak and Grassi Lakes are the perennial favourites; the Canmore Nordic Centre has a trail network usable year-round. For time off the trail, the town's Main Street has galleries, gear shops, and cafes worth an afternoon.
