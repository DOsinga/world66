The Carriage House Theatre is a small Cardston landmark, built in 1912 and later restored for films, community theatre, and summer performances. It seats about 350, which gives shows here a local, intimate feel.

It is not the town's main reason to visit, but it pairs naturally with the Remington Carriage Museum and the historic buildings around central Cardston.
