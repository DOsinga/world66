Clark's is another Lakeshore Drive fixture — a casual restaurant with lake views and a menu focused on straightforward Canadian comfort food. It draws a mixed crowd of military families, oil workers, and locals who have been coming for years. Portions are generous and the atmosphere is relaxed. Worth booking on summer weekends when the lakeside tables fill up quickly.
