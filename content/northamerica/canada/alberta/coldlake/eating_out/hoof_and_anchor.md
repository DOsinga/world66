One of the better-regarded spots on Lakeshore Drive, the Hoof and Anchor is a pub-style restaurant with solid Alberta beef and reliable pub food. The location near the lake makes it a popular spot on summer evenings, and the patio, when the weather allows, is hard to beat. Service is friendly in the way that small-city locals-only restaurants tend to be — nobody is performing for out-of-towners.
