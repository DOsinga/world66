---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cold Lake punches well above its weight for outdoor recreation. The lake itself — one of Alberta's largest — anchors almost everything worth doing here, from summer fishing and beach days to winter ice activities and snowmobile trails through the surrounding boreal forest. The Canadian Forces Base adds a military-aviation angle that few cities anywhere can match. Between the provincial park, the ski hill, and the base museum, there's more than enough to fill a long weekend.
