---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Cold Lake is a year-round destination in the sense that people here make the most of every season — but the two peak periods are summer (July–August) and ski season (January–March).

**Summer** is when the lake is the centre of everything. Kinosoo Beach fills up on hot days, boaters are out in force, fishing is at its most social, and the provincial park campground is busy. Temperatures in July average around 20°C but can push into the mid-20s on good days. Book accommodation ahead if you're coming on a summer long weekend.

**Winter** is longer than many visitors expect — snow arrives in October and can linger into April. But locals treat it as an opportunity: Kinosoo Ridge opens for skiing, the lake freezes for ice fishing and snowmobiling, and the snowmobile trail network that fans out from the city into the boreal forest is extensive. The **JJ Parr Centre** keeps indoor recreation going year-round.

**Spring** (May–June) brings **Exercise Maple Flag** to CFB Cold Lake — the sky above town fills with fighter jets from NATO nations participating in what is routinely called the world's most demanding air combat exercise. It's a spectacle that's completely unplanned for most visitors and unforgettable if you happen to be there.

**Autumn** (September–October) is quieter, with foliage colour in the boreal forest, good fishing, and the crowds gone. A good time to visit if you want space.
