Bellevue Underground Mine is the most direct way to feel the pass's coal-mining past. Guided tours take visitors into a former mine, with helmets, lamps, and enough darkness to make the work feel real.

It is not just a display of tools. The tunnel itself explains why these communities existed and why mining shaped so much of the Crowsnest story.
