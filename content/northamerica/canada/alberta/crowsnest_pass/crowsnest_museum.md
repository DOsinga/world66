The Crowsnest Museum in Coleman is useful because the pass is scattered. Its exhibits and walking-tour material help connect the separate communities, mine sites, disasters, and old main streets into one story.

Stop here if you have more than a quick look at Frank Slide. It gives names and context to what otherwise passes by as a series of roadside towns.
