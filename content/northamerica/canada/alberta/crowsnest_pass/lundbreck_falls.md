Lundbreck Falls sits just east of the pass, where the Crowsnest River drops into a small limestone canyon. It is quick, photogenic, and easy to reach from Highway 3.

It is not the reason to come to Crowsnest Pass, but it is a good final stop if you are driving east toward Pincher Creek or Waterton.
