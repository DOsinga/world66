The Hoodoos Trail is a short loop to a cluster of sandstone pillars capped by harder rock. The formations are not huge, but they are strange and delicate, and they give a clear look at the erosion that shapes the Drumheller Valley.

Stay on the marked trail. The hoodoos damage easily, and the best view is from a little distance rather than from scrambling around their bases.
