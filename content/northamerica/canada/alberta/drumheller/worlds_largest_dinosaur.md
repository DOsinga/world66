The World's Largest Dinosaur is pure roadside theatre: a huge Tyrannosaurus rex standing by the visitor centre near the river bridge. You can climb inside to a viewpoint in the mouth, which is silly, quick, and very Drumheller.

It is not the reason to come to town, but it makes a useful first stop for maps, orientation, and the obligatory dinosaur photo.
