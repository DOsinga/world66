The East Coulee School Museum tells the part of mining history that heavy machinery leaves out. The old schoolhouse has classrooms, photographs, domestic objects, and stories from the years when East Coulee was a busy coal community rather than a near-ghost town.

It is a good companion to Atlas Coal Mine. See the mine first for the industry, then the school for the lives built around it.
