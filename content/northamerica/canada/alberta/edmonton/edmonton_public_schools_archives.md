The Edmonton Public Schools Archives occupy the historic McKay Avenue School and tell the story of public education in the city. The displays are modest, but the old school building, photographs, documents, and classroom material make it a useful stop for visitors interested in local history.

It pairs well with a walk through central Edmonton rather than as a long standalone museum visit.
