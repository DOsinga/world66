Four Rooms is a downtown Edmonton restaurant serving an eclectic tapas-style menu in a setting divided into four distinctly decorated dining spaces. The menu runs to international small plates: satay skewers, parmesan chicken, salmon, and a range of combinations that work well for sharing. The wine list is substantial and the bar programme is strong.

The four rooms allow for different atmospheres within a single venue — a good choice if you're dining with people who want different things from an evening out. Open for lunch on weekdays and dinner daily; reservations recommended on weekends. Located at Edmonton Centre downtown.
