John Janzen Nature Centre is a family-oriented nature centre in Edmonton's river valley. Exhibits and programs introduce local plants, animals, and habitats, with a strong focus on hands-on learning for children.

It is a useful stop for families already visiting the west side of the river valley or Fort Edmonton Park. Adults without children may find it brief, but it gives younger visitors an easy indoor-outdoor nature break.
