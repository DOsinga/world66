Rutherford House is a restored historic house museum on the University of Alberta campus. It was the home of Alexander Cameron Rutherford, Alberta's first premier, and shows the domestic life of an upper-middle-class Edmonton household in the early 20th century.

The house is a useful stop for visitors interested in provincial history, especially when combined with nearby campus and Old Strathcona sights.
