Valley Zoo is a compact animal park in Edmonton's river valley, with a mix of mammals, birds, reptiles, and small family-friendly exhibits. It is quieter and easier to cover than a big-city zoo, so it works well if you want a low-key afternoon rather than a full-day attraction.
