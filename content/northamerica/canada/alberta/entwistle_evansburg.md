Entwistle and Evansburg are small neighbouring communities west of [Edmonton](/northamerica/canada/alberta/edmonton), where the Yellowhead Highway meets Cowboy Trail on the eastern edge of the Rocky Mountain foothills. They are not major destinations, but they make a useful and pleasant stop on the long drive toward Jasper.

The Pembina River separates the two hamlets and gives the area its main appeal. Around the river gorge you can camp, walk, golf, or float through the valley in summer, with wooded banks and sandstone cliffs breaking up the ranchland around town.

The local character is cheerfully odd. Entwistle once called itself Canada's diamond capital after a diamond was found nearby, while Evansburg is famous for electing a yearly "Town Grouch" during Pembina Valley Daze. That mix of roadside humour, river scenery, and practical highway services is the reason to pause here.
