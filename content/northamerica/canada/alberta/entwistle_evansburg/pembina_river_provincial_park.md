Pembina River Provincial Park protects a pretty stretch of the Pembina River gorge north of Entwistle and Evansburg. The valley cuts through the foothills with steep banks, forest, and enough wildlife to make it feel wilder than its position beside Highway 16 suggests.

Most visitors come for camping, short walks, and river access. It is an easy overnight stop if you are driving between Edmonton and Jasper and want a quieter place than a highway motel.
