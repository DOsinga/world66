Pembina River Tubing turns the local river into an easy summer outing, renting tubes and life jackets and running a shuttle back to the start point. The float is gentle rather than wild, which is exactly the point on a hot Alberta afternoon.

Book ahead in peak season and pay attention to river conditions. Water levels and weather matter, even on a relaxed float.
