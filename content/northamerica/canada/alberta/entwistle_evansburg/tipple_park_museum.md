Tipple Park Museum is the area's local history stop, with exhibits on railways, farming, and the coal mines that once shaped this part of west-central Alberta. Its name comes from the mine tipple, the structure used to load coal.

It is a small museum, but a useful one if you want the highway stop to mean more than fuel and coffee. Check seasonal hours before making a special detour.
