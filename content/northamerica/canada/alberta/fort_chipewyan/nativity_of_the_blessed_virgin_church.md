The Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Roman Catholic Church dates from 1909, though the Catholic mission in Fort Chipewyan began in the mid-19th century. The church stands on high ground overlooking the community and Lake Athabasca, a reminder of how mission, trade, and Indigenous life met here in complicated ways.

Its setting matters as much as the building. From the ridge, the lake makes clear why this remote settlement became a northern crossroads.
