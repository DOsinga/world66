The Empress Theatre is the most photogenic single building on Fort Macleod's main street. Built during the town's early twentieth-century boom, it still works as a cinema and performance venue rather than just a preserved facade.

Check the schedule if you are overnighting nearby. Even if you only see it from the street, the theatre helps the downtown feel like a living place instead of an outdoor museum.
