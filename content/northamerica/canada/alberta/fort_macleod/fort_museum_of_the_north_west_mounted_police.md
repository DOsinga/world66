The Fort Museum tells the story of the North-West Mounted Police post that gave Fort Macleod its name. It is a recreated fort rather than the original buildings, but the exhibits, period rooms, and First Nations interpretation give useful context before or after Head-Smashed-In.

In season, the Musical Ride demonstrations are the draw. If you miss those, the museum is still worth a stop for the way it ties policing, settlement, and Blackfoot country together.
