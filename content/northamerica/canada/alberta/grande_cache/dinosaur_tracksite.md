type: poi

The Grande Cache Dinosaur Tracksite preserves Cretaceous dinosaur footprints in the former No. 9 Mine area north of town. It is a reminder that these mountains and coal seams hold more than industrial history.

Access can be limited and conditions change, so treat this as a specialist stop to research locally rather than a casual roadside viewpoint.
