Labyrinth Park is a small, quiet stop near 104 Street and 97 Avenue, built around an 11-circuit walking labyrinth. With the Rockies behind it, the place works better than it sounds: a pause point before or after the bigger outdoor trips.

It will not make a trip by itself, but it gives Grande Cache a local, human-scale landmark between the highway and the hills.
