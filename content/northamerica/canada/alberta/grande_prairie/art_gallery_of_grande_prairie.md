The Art Gallery of Grande Prairie is the city's main visual-art institution, showing contemporary and regional work in the restored Montrose cultural centre. It gives Grande Prairie a cultural stop that is not tied only to pioneer history, dinosaurs, or parks.

Exhibitions change, so the gallery is strongest when you treat it as a current snapshot of northern Alberta art rather than a fixed collection. It is central, manageable in size, and easy to pair with the nearby museum or downtown errands.
