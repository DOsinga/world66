---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Grande Prairie eats like a working oilfield town with a steadily improving food scene. Steakhouses and pub menus still dominate, but a handful of independents on and around Resources Road and downtown 100 Avenue have raised the bar — particularly for craft beer and brunch. Asian options are surprisingly strong for a city this size, reflecting the diverse workforce drawn here by the oil and gas industry.
