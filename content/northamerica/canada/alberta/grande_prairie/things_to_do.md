---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Grande Prairie's sights are spread across a wide, flat landscape rather than concentrated in a walkable downtown. Muskoseepi Park threads along Bear Creek through the heart of the city and is the obvious place to start — a long green corridor with trails, the heritage village, the museum, and the historic playhouse all clustered around it. Beyond the park, the real reason to detour here is the [Philip J. Currie Dinosaur Museum](/northamerica/canada/alberta/grande_prairie/philip_j_currie_dinosaur_museum) in nearby Wembley, built around the extraordinary Pipestone Creek bonebed. Add a stop at [Saskatoon Island Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/alberta/grande_prairie/saskatoon_island_provincial_park) for the trumpeter swans and you have a respectable day out from the Alaska Highway.
