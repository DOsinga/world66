Hinton is a practical mountain gateway on the Yellowhead Highway, about an hour east of [Jasper](/northamerica/canada/alberta/jasper). It is not as pretty as the national park towns, and it does not pretend to be. It gives travellers fuel, food, cheaper beds, and a useful pause before or after the Rockies.

The town sits in the Athabasca River valley, with forested foothills close by and industry never entirely out of sight. That mix is part of the place: pulp mill, rail history, highway services, and quick access to trails and lakes. The Beaver Boardwalk is the best small surprise, a wetland walk that turns a driving break into something memorable.

Hinton works best if you have a car and are moving between Edmonton, Jasper, and the northern Rockies. Stay for the services, then use the town as a jumping-off point for Switzer Provincial Park, Brule, and the quieter country east of Jasper National Park.
