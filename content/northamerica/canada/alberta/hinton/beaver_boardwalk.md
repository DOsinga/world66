The Beaver Boardwalk is Hinton's best short walk. Timber paths and gravel trails loop around Maxwell Lake through wetland habitat, with visible dams and lodges and a good chance of seeing wildlife if you come early or late. It is easy, close to town, and much more pleasant than a quick highway stop usually deserves.
