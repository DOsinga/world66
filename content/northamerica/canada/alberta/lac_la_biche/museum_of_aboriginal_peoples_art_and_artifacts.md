The Museum of Aboriginal Peoples' Art and Artifacts sits inside Portage College and holds more than 2,000 traditional and contemporary works by First Nations, Metis, and Inuit artists. Its collection includes the Professional Native Indian Artists Inc., often called the Indigenous Group of Seven.

It is a quiet museum, but a useful one. Pair it with Lac La Biche Mission if you want the day's history to include Indigenous art and voice, not only mission-era buildings.
