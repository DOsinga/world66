Sir Winston Churchill Provincial Park protects the islands in the east basin of Lac la Biche. Big Island and Long Island are linked to the shore by a causeway, so you can reach the forest, beaches, picnic areas, and trails without a boat.

The park is the easiest outdoor day from town. Come for swimming and paddling in summer, cross-country skiing and snowshoeing in winter, or birdwatching almost any time the lake is open. The mix of island forest and open water makes it feel more distinctive than a standard roadside campground.
