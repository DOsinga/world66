The Lake Louise Summer Gondola uses the ski-area lifts to give non-skiers a high, open view over the Bow Valley and surrounding peaks. It is one of the easier ways to get above the forest without committing to a long hike.

Go early for clearer air and a better chance of seeing wildlife on the slopes below. It is a paid attraction and weather-dependent, but for travellers with limited mobility or limited time it delivers a lot of mountain scenery quickly.
