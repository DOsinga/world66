---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Legal is about 45 km north of Edmonton on Highway 651. By car from Edmonton, take the Yellowhead Trail (Highway 16) west and then Highway 651 north — the drive takes around 45 minutes in normal traffic. There is no regular bus service to Legal; a car is effectively required. The nearest major services are in St. Albert, about 25 km to the south.
