title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lethbridge's main sights cluster around the Oldman River valley and downtown. The [High Level Bridge](/northamerica/canada/alberta/lethbridge/high_level_bridge) is the city's signature landmark, best seen from Indian Battle Park below. [Fort Whoop-Up](/northamerica/canada/alberta/lethbridge/fort_whoop_up) reconstructs the whisky trading post that brought chaos to the prairies in the 1870s, while the [Galt Museum](/northamerica/canada/alberta/lethbridge/galt_museum) covers the region's coal and settler history. The [Nikka Yuko Japanese Garden](/northamerica/canada/alberta/lethbridge/nikka_yuko_japanese_garden) is genuinely beautiful and a legacy of the Japanese-Canadian community that farmed the surrounding landscape.
