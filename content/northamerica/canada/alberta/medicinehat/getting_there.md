---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Medicine Hat sits on the **Trans-Canada Highway** (Highway 1), roughly halfway between [Calgary](/northamerica/canada/alberta/calgary) (290 km west) and [Regina](/northamerica/canada/saskatchewan/regina) (430 km east). The drive from Calgary is around three hours on good prairie road; from Regina, four to four and a half.

By rail, **Via Rail's Canadian** stops at the historic CPR station three times a week in each direction — a way to arrive that fits the city's CPR-era character, though the schedule is inconvenient and the trip is long.

The local **Medicine Hat Airport (YXH)** has limited scheduled service; most fly-in visitors come via Calgary or Lethbridge. There is no inter-city bus service of note since the closure of Greyhound's western Canadian routes.
