The Canadian Grain Elevator Discovery Centre preserves the kind of wooden elevators that once marked almost every prairie railway town. They are big, plain structures, but they explain a whole economy: wheat, rail sidings, local co-ops, and the way small towns were tied to distant markets.

For travellers crossing Alberta by road, this is a useful stop because it turns a familiar prairie silhouette into something legible. It pairs well with Nanton's aviation museum, giving the town both wartime and farming history in one compact visit.
