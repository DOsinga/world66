The Coutts Centre for Western Canadian Heritage sits east of Nanton, away from the highway, and gives the area a softer stop after the town museums. Its gardens, walking paths, sculptures, and reused historic buildings make it feel more like a prairie estate than a formal museum.

It is best in the growing season, when the gardens justify the detour. Outside that window, check before going; the value here depends more on access and season than Nanton's indoor sights do.
