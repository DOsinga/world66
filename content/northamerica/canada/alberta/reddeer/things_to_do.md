---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Red Deer's strongest suit is its outdoor life. The Waskasoo Park trail system runs through the heart of the city along the river valley, connecting a series of parks, nature reserves, and historic sites in a green corridor that most cities much larger than Red Deer would envy.

The [Kerry Wood Nature Centre](/northamerica/canada/alberta/reddeer/kerry_wood_nature_centre) is the best introduction to the valley's ecology, and the adjacent [Gaetz Lakes Sanctuary](/northamerica/canada/alberta/reddeer/gaetz_lakes_sanctuary) is one of the oldest migratory bird sanctuaries in Canada. For history, [Fort Normandeau](/northamerica/canada/alberta/reddeer/fort_normandeau) reconstructs a significant site from the 1885 North-West Rebellion just west of the city. The [Red Deer Museum and Art Gallery](/northamerica/canada/alberta/reddeer/red_deer_museum) downtown covers the city's past and present with rotating art exhibitions alongside regional history.

[Bower Ponds](/northamerica/canada/alberta/reddeer/bower_ponds) is the most popular gathering spot in the valley — paddle boats in summer, skating in winter, and year-round trails along the river. For sport, the [Marchant Crane Centrium](/northamerica/canada/alberta/reddeer/centrium) is home to the Red Deer Rebels hockey team and hosts concerts and events. [Westerner Park](/northamerica/canada/alberta/reddeer/westerner_park) to the south puts on the city's biggest annual event, Westerner Days, a rodeo and fair in early July.
