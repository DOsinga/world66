The Boreal Centre is a practical first stop in the provincial park. It combines visitor information, exhibits, education programs, and advice on where to walk or watch birds around the lake.

It is especially useful if you are not already a birder. Staff can point you toward trails and seasonal activity, and the centre gives the surrounding forest more meaning before you head outside.
