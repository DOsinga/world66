The Lesser Slave Lake Bird Observatory monitors migratory birds in the boreal forest, a setting that makes it more interesting than a casual lake lookout. In season, visitors may be able to learn about bird banding and the research behind the conservation work.

Check current access and tour details before going. The experience depends on season, weather, and research schedules.
