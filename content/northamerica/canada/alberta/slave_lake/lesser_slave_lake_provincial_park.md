Lesser Slave Lake Provincial Park is the reason Slave Lake earns a place on an itinerary. The park protects shore, dunes, wetlands, and boreal forest along one of Alberta's largest lakes, with access to beaches, camping, walking trails, and birding areas.

It is a good northern counterpoint to Alberta's mountain parks: flatter, quieter, and more about water, forest, and migration than alpine drama.
