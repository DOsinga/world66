Stettler is a small town in east-central Alberta, about 100 km east of Red Deer. It calls itself the Heart of Alberta, and on an ordinary day it feels like a farm-service town with a wide main street, grain-country horizons, and a few good reasons to stop.

The reason to plan around Stettler is [Alberta Prairie Railway Excursions](/northamerica/canada/alberta/stettler/alberta_prairie_railway_excursions), a half-day steam or diesel train trip through the prairie. In town, the [Town and Country Museum](/northamerica/canada/alberta/stettler/town_and_country_museum) has a village-like collection of historic buildings, and the [Downtown Historic Walking Tour](/northamerica/canada/alberta/stettler/downtown_historic_walking_tour) adds context to the old commercial core.

Stettler is not a place to cross the province for on its own, but it clears the one-day test if you are nearby and can time the railway trip.
