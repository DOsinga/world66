Red Brick Common, formerly the Multicultural Heritage Centre, is Stony Plain's indoor culture stop. The old brick school houses a living museum, public art gallery, programs, and a cafe, so it works well before or after a mural walk.

It is small, but it gives the town's arts-and-heritage identity a real address rather than leaving it scattered across downtown walls.
