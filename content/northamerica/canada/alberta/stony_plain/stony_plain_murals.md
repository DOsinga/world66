The murals are Stony Plain's signature. More than 40 public artworks are spread through downtown and nearby streets, turning local stories, settlers, railways, agriculture, and community characters into a walkable outdoor gallery.

Pick up the town's mural guide or use the online map rather than wandering at random. The paintings are at their best when you read the plaques and let the town explain itself one wall at a time.
