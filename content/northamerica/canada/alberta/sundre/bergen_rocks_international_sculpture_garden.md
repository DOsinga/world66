Bergen Rocks International Sculpture Garden is the area's most unexpected stop: large stone sculptures set in a forested garden south of Sundre. The works were created by artists from several countries, giving the place more character than a normal roadside art stop.

It is a short detour by car, and it pairs well with a Sundre museum visit or a drive along the Cowboy Trail.
