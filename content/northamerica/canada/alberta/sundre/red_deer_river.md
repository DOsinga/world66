The Red Deer River is the reason Sundre feels like a foothills town rather than just a highway stop. Local access points are used for kayaking, fishing, and relaxed riverside time, while rafting operators run trips farther west where the water has more bite.

Conditions change with season and weather, so ask locally before putting in. Even without a boat, the river gives the town its best picnic and camping feel.
