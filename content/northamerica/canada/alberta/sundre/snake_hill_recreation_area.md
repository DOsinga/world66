Snake Hill Recreation Area is Sundre's easiest outdoor escape. The trails climb and loop through woods just north of the main street, close enough to use without turning the day into a full backcountry outing.

Walk or bike in summer. In winter, the same trail network is used for snowshoeing and cross-country skiing when conditions allow.
