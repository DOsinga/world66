Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village is an open-air provincial historic site east of Edmonton, near Elk Island National Park. More than 30 historic buildings tell the story of Ukrainian settlement in east-central Alberta, including churches, a grain elevator, a blacksmith shop, and a sod house.

Costumed interpreters bring the village to life, especially on weekends and event days. It is one of the stronger cultural stops in the Beaver Hills area and pairs well with a visit to Elk Island.
