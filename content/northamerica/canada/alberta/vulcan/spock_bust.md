The Spock bust is the downtown counterpart to the highway starship. Leonard Nimoy visited Vulcan in 2010 for the unveiling, and his bronze handprint, making the Vulcan salute, sits with the bust.

It is a small stop, but it gives the town's Star Trek identity a surprisingly direct link to the actor who made the name famous. Look for it near 2 Avenue South, just off Centre Street.
