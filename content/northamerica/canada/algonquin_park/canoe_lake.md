Canoe Lake is one of Algonquin Park's best-known lakes on the Highway 60 corridor. It is busy by backcountry standards, but it gives easy access to canoe routes, park facilities, and classic shield-country scenery.

The lake also has a place in Canadian art history through Tom Thomson, who worked and painted in the area. Paddlers can use it as a practical starting point or as a short outing without committing to a long wilderness route.
