108 Mile Ranch Heritage Site preserves buildings and artifacts tied to the Cariboo wagon road, ranching, and roadhouse travel in British Columbia's interior. The setting helps explain how distance, horses, freight, and settlement shaped this part of the province.

It is an easy heritage stop for travellers driving Highway 97. The site works best as a leg-stretch with historical substance, especially for anyone following Gold Rush or ranching routes through the Cariboo.
