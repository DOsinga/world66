Lac La Hache Provincial Park is a straightforward but pleasant Cariboo stop, set on a long lake north of 100 Mile House. Its value is practical: water views, picnic space, swimming in season, and a break from highway driving in a region where distances stretch out quickly.

It is not a wilderness showpiece, but it fits the way many travelers experience the Cariboo, as a chain of lakes, ranch country, and road stops. Use it for a relaxed pause, a family swim, or an easy overnight if your route through central British Columbia needs a slower rhythm.
