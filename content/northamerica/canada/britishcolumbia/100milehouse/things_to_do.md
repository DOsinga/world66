100 Mile House is an outdoor destination. The main draws are cross-country skiing in winter and lake fishing in summer, with the [100 Mile Marsh](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/100milehouse/mile_house_marsh) offering birdwatching year-round. The surrounding Cariboo countryside — wide valleys, ponderosa pines, and hundreds of small lakes — rewards slow exploration by car or on horseback.
