The World's Largest Cross-Country Skis are a cheerful roadside monument to 100 Mile House's winter-sports identity. The oversized blue skis stand just over 10 m tall and were built as a community project.

It is a brief photo stop, not a major attraction, but it is distinctive enough to mark the town's link with cross-country skiing.
