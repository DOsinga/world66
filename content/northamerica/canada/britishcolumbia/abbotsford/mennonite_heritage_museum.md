The Mennonite Heritage Museum documents Mennonite migration, faith, and daily life in the Fraser Valley and across Canada. Exhibits use artifacts, photographs, and family stories to explain a community that has shaped Abbotsford's history.

It is a focused local-history stop, especially worthwhile for visitors interested in immigration, religious communities, or the Fraser Valley's farming culture.
