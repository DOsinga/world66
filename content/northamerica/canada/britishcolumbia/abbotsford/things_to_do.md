title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Abbotsford's best stops are spread out, so it works better by car than by transit. The strongest day combines the [Gur Sikh Temple](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/abbotsford/gur_sikh_temple), the heritage streets of [Clayburn Village](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/abbotsford/clayburn_village), and either an easy walk around [Mill Lake Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/abbotsford/mill_lake_park) or a longer hike in [Sumas Mountain Regional Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/abbotsford/sumas_mountain_regional_park).
