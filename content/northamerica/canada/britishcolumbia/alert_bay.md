Alert Bay is a small village on Cormorant Island, a short ferry ride from Port McNeill on northern Vancouver Island. It is not a generic coastal stop. The island is deeply tied to the 'Namgis First Nation and the wider Kwakwaka'wakw world, and the best visit is one that treats that culture as living community, not decoration.

The central reason to come is the [U'mista Cultural Centre](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/alert_bay/umista_cultural_centre), whose Potlatch Collection tells a hard and important story of government seizure, cultural survival, and return. The ['Namgis Big House](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/alert_bay/namgis_big_house) and the village's totem poles add outdoor context, while the [Alert Bay Ecological Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/alert_bay/alert_bay_ecological_park) gives a quiet walk through marsh and forest.

Alert Bay works best as a deliberate day from Port McNeill or as a slow overnight on the North Island route. Check opening hours and cultural-event schedules before crossing, and remember that some places are ceremonial or community spaces first.
