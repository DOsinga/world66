Alert Bay Ecological Park protects a small marsh and forest area created after a cannery dammed a stream in the 19th century. The trails are short, but the birdlife and still water make it a good counterpoint to the village waterfront.

Pick up a walking map before you go; the loops are simple, but the map helps tie the park into a wider stroll around the island.
