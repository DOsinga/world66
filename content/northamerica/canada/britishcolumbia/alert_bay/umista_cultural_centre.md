U'mista is the reason many travelers cross to Alert Bay. The centre holds the repatriated Potlatch Collection: masks, regalia, and ceremonial objects taken during Canada's potlatch ban and returned after decades of work by Kwakwaka'wakw people.

Go slowly. This is not just an art display; it is a place about law, loss, survival, and cultural continuity.
