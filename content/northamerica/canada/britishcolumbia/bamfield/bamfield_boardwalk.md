The Bamfield Boardwalk is the village's best introduction. It runs along West Bamfield's waterfront, linking docks and homes above the inlet, with boats moving across the narrow water and forest pressing in behind.

It is not a grand promenade, and that is the charm. Take the water taxi across, walk slowly, and use it as the start of the short route toward Brady's Beach.
