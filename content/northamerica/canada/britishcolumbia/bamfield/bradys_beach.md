Brady's Beach is the easy wild-coast payoff from West Bamfield. A short walk from the boardwalk brings you to a rocky, driftwood-strewn beach with sea stacks, tide pools, heavy surf, and a blowhole at the south end.

It is a place to linger rather than tick off. Watch the tide, wear shoes that can handle wet rock, and give yourself time for the walk back to the dock.
