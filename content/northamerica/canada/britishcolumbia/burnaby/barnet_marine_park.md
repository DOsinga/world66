Barnet Marine Park gives Burnaby a real waterfront escape, with beach access, picnic lawns, forested edges, and broad views across Burrard Inlet. It is more relaxed than the city’s mountain viewpoints and works well for a slow afternoon outdoors.

The park is strongest in good weather, when locals come for swimming, paddling, and shoreline walks. For travellers staying in Burnaby or exploring beyond central Vancouver, it shows a quieter inlet landscape that is easy to miss from SkyTrain corridors and shopping districts.
