Burnaby Village Museum recreates a small British Columbia town from the early 20th century, with heritage buildings, working shops, costumed interpreters, and a preserved interurban streetcar. The museum shares the site with the restored C. W. Parker Carousel, making it one of Burnaby's best family-friendly history stops.
