Burnaby is often overlooked in favour of its famous neighbour, but it offers a different Vancouver experience: parkland and lakes instead of the downtown crowds, a world-class art gallery in a heritage house overlooking Deer Lake, and Simon Fraser University on its dramatic mountain perch. The city's Italian community has shaped a distinct neighbourhood feel in the Heights, and the views from Burnaby Mountain are among the best in the Lower Mainland.
