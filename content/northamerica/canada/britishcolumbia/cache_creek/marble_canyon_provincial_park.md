Marble Canyon Provincial Park is 40 km northwest of Cache Creek on Highway 99, set among the pale limestone slopes of the Pavilion Mountain Range. The canyon walls and small lakes make it a more scenic stop than its modest size suggests.

BC Parks describes access by the Crown Lake campground entrance. In season, it is useful for camping, fishing, birding, and short walks; out of season, check services before relying on it as more than a viewpoint and picnic stop.
