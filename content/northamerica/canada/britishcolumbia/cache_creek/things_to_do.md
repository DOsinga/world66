title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cache Creek's worthwhile stops are mostly outside the village, so treat the town as a base rather than the attraction itself. [Historic Hat Creek](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/cache_creek/historic_hat_creek) is the headline, with original roadhouse buildings from the Cariboo Wagon Road era. The [McAbee Fossil Beds](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/cache_creek/mcabee_fossil_beds) add deep-time geology, while [Juniper Beach](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/cache_creek/juniper_beach_provincial_park) and [Marble Canyon](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/cache_creek/marble_canyon_provincial_park) show off the dry Thompson landscape in very different ways.
