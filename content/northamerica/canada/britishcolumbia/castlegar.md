Castlegar sits where the Kootenay River meets the Columbia, in a green pocket of the West Kootenays. It is not as pretty or as bohemian as nearby [Nelson](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/nelson), but it has a more specific story than most small highway towns: Doukhobor settlement, railway history, river paths, and a downtown that has turned itself into an outdoor sculpture gallery.

The best reason to stop is the mix of culture and landscape. [Castlegar Sculpturewalk](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/castlegar/castlegar_sculpturewalk) changes the mood of downtown from ordinary to curious, while the [Doukhobor Discovery Centre](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/castlegar/doukhobor_discovery_centre) explains the pacifist Russian religious community that shaped much of the area. [Zuckerberg Island](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/castlegar/zuckerberg_island) gives you an easy walk through cottonwood and cedar at the river confluence.

Castlegar works best as a half-day or full-day stop between Nelson, Trail, and the Boundary country. Come for the heritage sites, add a river walk or the old Brilliant Suspension Bridge, and save the deeper mountain adventures for travellers with more time and a car.
