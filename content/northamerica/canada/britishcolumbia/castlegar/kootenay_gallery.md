Kootenay Gallery is a regional visual arts gallery in Castlegar, with rotating exhibitions and a small shop. It is a useful stop if you want a quick look at the West Kootenay art scene, especially when paired with the nearby Doukhobor Discovery Centre or Castlegar's public sculpture walk.
