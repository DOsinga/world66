Cawston is tiny, but it has a clear reason to exist on a travel map. The village sits in the lower Similkameen Valley, surrounded by orchards, vineyards, fruit stands, and organic farms. Local tourism calls it Canada's organic farming capital, and for once the slogan explains the place rather well.

This is not a town for monuments. The day is about slow roads, farm produce, cider, and tastings at small wineries such as [Seven Stones Winery](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/cawston/seven_stones_winery), [Orofino Vineyards](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/cawston/orofino_vineyards), and [Crowsnest Vineyards](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/cawston/crowsnest_vineyards). The dry valley air, mountain edges, and fruit-growing economy make it feel distinct from the larger Okanagan towns.

Cawston passes the spare-day test if you are already in the South Okanagan or crossing Highway 3. Come hungry, keep the pace loose, and treat it as a farm-and-wine village rather than a conventional sightseeing stop.
