Crowsnest Vineyards is a practical and pleasant stop because it pairs wine with food rather than treating tasting as the whole experience. The bakery and casual meals make it useful in a village where the best day is built from small stops.

It is a good place to pause between tastings, especially if you are cycling or driving the flat roads around Cawston and Keremeos.
