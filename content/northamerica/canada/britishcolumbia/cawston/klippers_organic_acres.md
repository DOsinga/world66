Klippers Organic Acres gives Cawston's organic-farming claim a concrete stop. The farm grows fruit and vegetables in the Similkameen Valley and has become known for produce, cider, and farm-to-table food.

Use it as the food anchor for a Cawston day. The point is not a formal tour; it is seeing how the valley's farms, orchards, and restaurants connect.
