Orofino Vineyards is a good example of why Cawston is worth slowing down for: small-scale, local, and rooted in the dry Similkameen benchlands. Its straw-bale tasting room has become part of the winery's identity.

The visit is straightforward, but the broader setting is the point. You are tasting in a valley where orchards, organic farms, and vineyards sit almost on top of each other.
