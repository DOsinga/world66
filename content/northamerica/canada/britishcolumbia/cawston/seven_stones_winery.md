Seven Stones Winery is one of Cawston's more memorable tasting stops. The draw is not only the wine but the setting: a patio above the Similkameen Valley and man-made caves used for tours and events.

Book ahead if you specifically want the cave tour. Otherwise, it works as an easy tasting stop on a slow Cawston and Keremeos wine loop.
