Bridal Veil Falls is Chilliwack's simplest win: leave Highway 1, walk a short forest trail, and stand below a high waterfall that spreads over the rock face in a pale curtain. It is an easy stop, but it feels much wilder than the access suggests.

Go after rain or in spring if you want the falls at their strongest. In dry late summer the flow can shrink, though the shaded walk and mountain setting still make the detour worthwhile.
