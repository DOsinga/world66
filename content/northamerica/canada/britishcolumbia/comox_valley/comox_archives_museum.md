Comox Archives & Museum is a small local-history museum covering the town of Comox, early settlers, the old wharf, the 102nd Battalion's departure for the First World War, and the long military presence at nearby Goose Spit.

It is a modest stop, but useful if you are already in Comox and want a grounded sense of the town's past.
