---
title: "Festivals"
type: section
---

Three festivals are worth shaping a trip around. The **Vancouver Island MusicFest** in mid-July (Comox Valley Fairgrounds) is the island's biggest folk and roots music gathering, drawing 10,000 people for three days of camping and concerts. The **Filberg Festival** in early August fills the lodge gardens with 140 juried artists from across western Canada — exceptional ceramics, glass, and woodwork. The **Comox Valley Shellfish Festival** in mid-June celebrates the local oyster and clam industry with chef demos, raw bars, and a competition for the fastest shucker.

Smaller but worth catching: the Cumberland Wild Brewfest in July, the Hornby Festival of concerts in August, and the World Community Film Festival in February.
