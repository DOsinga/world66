---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Comox Valley is built around outdoor pursuits, and the geography lets you stack them: ski on Mount Washington in the morning, kayak from Goose Spit in the afternoon. Day-trip out to Hornby and Denman islands for galleries and beach time; head into Strathcona Park for the alpine. Cumberland's compact downtown is the place for mountain biking and coffee.
