Como Lake Park is a gentle neighbourhood escape built around a small lake in southwest Coquitlam. A flat loop path, lawns, docks and mountain glimpses make it easy to use, especially for families or travellers who want a quiet walk without committing to a backcountry trail.

It is not as event-heavy as Town Centre Park, and that is part of its value. Come for a picnic, a short stroll or a pause between suburban errands; in a fast-growing city, Como Lake still feels pleasantly local.
