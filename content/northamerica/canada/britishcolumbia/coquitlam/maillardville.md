Maillardville is Coquitlam's historic Francophone neighbourhood, founded around French Canadian workers who came west for the lumber mills. Street names, heritage markers, churches, and community memory give the area a distinct identity within suburban Metro Vancouver.

It is best explored as a short neighbourhood walk rather than a single attraction. Look for heritage interpretation and time a visit with local festivals if you want the French Canadian story to feel more present.
