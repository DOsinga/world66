Pinecone Burke Provincial Park protects a large mountain-and-forest landscape north of Coquitlam and Port Coquitlam. It is not an urban stroll; the draw is rugged hiking, backcountry lakes, forest, and views toward the Coast Mountains.

For most travellers it is a fair-weather outdoor objective rather than a casual city stop. Go prepared, choose a route that fits your ability, and do not treat the park like a groomed municipal trail system.
