[Minnekhada Regional Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/coquitlam/minnekhada_regional_park) is the most rewarding piece of countryside in Coquitlam — a Metro Vancouver park on the Pitt River floodplain with a network of trails through wetland, forest and across the dyked marshes, with viewing platforms over flooded fields where wintering raptors and waterfowl gather in numbers.

[Burke Mountain](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/coquitlam/burke_mountain) rises 1,200 metres above the eastern edge of the city. The summit trail from Harper Road is a steady 7-hour return walk through old-growth Douglas fir to subalpine meadows — the most accessible serious hike from central Vancouver.

[Town Centre Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/coquitlam/town_centre_park) is the heart of civic Coquitlam, with [Lafarge Lake](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/coquitlam/lafarge_lake) — a five-hectare former gravel pit, now landscaped — at its centre. The lake hosts the city's free Lights at Lafarge winter festival (mid-November to mid-January, the largest free light display in Metro Vancouver) and an outdoor concert series in summer.

The [Evergreen Cultural Centre](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/coquitlam/evergreen_cultural_centre) at the south edge of the park is the city's main theatre and visual arts venue, with a 257-seat auditorium and a regular programme of music, theatre, dance and exhibitions. The free public art trail through the Town Centre area is worth following — substantial commissioned sculptures and installations punctuate the walkways.
