ƛ̓éxətəm Regional Park is a restored natural area shared by Coquitlam and Port Coquitlam. It has about 8.5 km of trails and is known locally for birdwatching, with roughly 150 bird species recorded in the park.

The park is best for a low-key walk, especially if you want a quieter green space than the more central city parks.
