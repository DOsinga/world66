Comox Valley Art Gallery is Courtenay's main contemporary art space, showing work by Vancouver Island artists alongside exhibitions from elsewhere in Canada. The scale is modest, but the programming is serious and often tied closely to the landscape, communities, and politics of the island.

It is an easy stop in downtown Courtenay, especially paired with the Courtenay and District Museum nearby.
