The Courtenay Riverway Heritage Walk is an easy, rewarding path along the Courtenay River and estuary, giving the town a natural corridor that is useful for both orientation and fresh air. The route mixes river views, birdlife, and glimpses of the Comox Valley's working waterfront.

It is a good first walk after arriving in Courtenay, especially if you want something low-effort before heading farther into Strathcona, Comox, or the coast. The path is flat and accessible, so it works for a short leg-stretcher or a longer out-and-back depending on time and weather.
