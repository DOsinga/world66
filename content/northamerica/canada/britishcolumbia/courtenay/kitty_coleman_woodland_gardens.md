Kitty Coleman Woodland Gardens is a hand-built woodland garden north of Courtenay, known for its rhododendrons, shaded paths, water features, and native plants. It makes a quiet half-day stop for garden lovers and anyone looking for an easy walk away from the busier Comox Valley sights.
