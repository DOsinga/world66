Nymph Falls Regional Park is one of the most rewarding nature stops near Courtenay, with forest trails leading to rocky river shelves and small falls on the Puntledge River. The landscape feels wilder than its easy access suggests.

It works well for a short hike, picnic, or summer river stop, and it is a useful alternative to driving all the way into the mountains. Stay attentive near the water, as the falls and rocks are part of the appeal but also the main hazard.
