Cranbrook Community Forest is the easy local escape, with a large network of trails east of town. Wikivoyage notes more than 100 km of hiking trails, and mountain biking is common here too.

It is a good choice when you want the Kootenay landscape without committing to a full mountain day. Carry water; the country around Cranbrook can feel drier than visitors expect.
