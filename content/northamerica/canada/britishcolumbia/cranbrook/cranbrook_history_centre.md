The Cranbrook History Centre is the city's strongest indoor attraction. Its best-known collection is rail travel: restored passenger cars, model railway displays, and exhibits tied to the lines that made Cranbrook matter.

The museum also covers local history and fossils, so it is not only for train obsessives. Still, anyone with even mild railway interest should put it first.
