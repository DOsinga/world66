Fort Steele Heritage Town is a short drive northeast of Cranbrook and preserves the old settlement that lost out when the railway chose Cranbrook. The restored buildings and period interpretation make the shift in regional history easy to picture.

It is touristy in the good sense: clear, physical, and easy to understand. Pair it with the Cranbrook History Centre for a full history day.
