Columbia Brewery is the home of Kokanee, the beer brand that made Creston familiar to many Canadians long before they could point to it on a map. The brewery sits on Erickson Road on the east side of town, surrounded by the agricultural valley that feeds Creston's fruit-stand reputation.

Tours and visitor access have varied over time, so check locally before planning around it. Even from outside, it is a useful stop because it ties Creston's working-town identity to one of British Columbia's better-known labels.
