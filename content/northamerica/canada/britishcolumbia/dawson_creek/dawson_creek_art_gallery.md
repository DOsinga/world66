Dawson Creek Art Gallery occupies a renovated annex of a prairie grain elevator in Northern Alberta Railway Park. The building gives the gallery a strong sense of place before you even step inside.

Exhibitions rotate through local, regional, and touring art, so it works best as a compact cultural stop near the Mile Zero and railway history sights.
