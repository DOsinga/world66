Northern Alberta Railway Park gathers Dawson Creek's rail history around the old station area. Before the Alaska Highway, the railway was the city's main link to the wider region, and the park helps explain why the town became the highway's starting point.

The station museum is the most useful stop here. It gives enough local context to make the Mile Zero marker feel less like a roadside sign and more like the start of a much bigger story.
