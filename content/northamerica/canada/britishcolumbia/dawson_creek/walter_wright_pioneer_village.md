Walter Wright Pioneer Village looks at Dawson Creek before the Alaska Highway era, with historic buildings and local displays from the Peace Country's farming and settlement years. It is a useful counterweight to the city's Mile Zero identity.

The village is best for travellers who like open-air museums and regional history. It pairs naturally with Mile Zero Park, which sits in the same general area.
