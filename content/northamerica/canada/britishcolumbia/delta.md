Delta is a municipality on the Fraser River delta, about 22km south of Vancouver, made up of three distinct communities: Ladner, Tsawwassen, and North Delta. For most travellers, Delta comes up because of Tsawwassen, where BC Ferries runs frequent services to Victoria and Nanaimo on Vancouver Island.

Ladner is the oldest part and has a quiet village feel. The wetlands and estuary marshes surrounding the area are exceptional for birdwatching — the [George C. Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/delta/reifel_bird_sanctuary) on Westham Island draws serious birdwatchers from across the region, particularly in autumn when snow geese arrive in vast numbers. [Burns Bog](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/delta/burns_bog), the largest raised peat bog in North America, occupies a quarter of the municipality and is home to black bears, bald eagles, and carnivorous plants more typical of the subarctic.
