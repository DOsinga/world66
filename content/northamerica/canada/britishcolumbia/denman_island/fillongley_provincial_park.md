Fillongley Provincial Park covers 96 hectares on Denman Island's eastern shore and offers the island's best forest walking. A network of trails winds through old-growth Douglas fir and cedar, past a small lake and heritage farm buildings, out to a pebble beach with views across Baynes Sound to Vancouver Island. The forest is genuinely beautiful — some of the trees are several hundred years old.

The beach access is the main draw in summer: calm, sheltered water, the sound of herons, and minimal crowds. The campground is small (15 sites) and popular; book ahead for summer visits. The heritage buildings — a barn, farmhouse remnants, and the old orchard — give the park an additional historical dimension that most provincial parks lack.
