Kinsol Trestle is one of the Cowichan Valley's signature engineering landmarks: a high, curved wooden railway trestle restored for walkers and cyclists on the regional trail network. Its scale is the point of the visit, with the timber structure spanning the forested Koksilah River valley in a way that feels both delicate and massive.

The trestle is outside central Duncan, but it clears the threshold as a destination because it is a distinctive heritage structure and an easy outdoor excursion. Bring time for the approach walk or ride, not just the bridge crossing, since the surrounding trail is part of the appeal.
