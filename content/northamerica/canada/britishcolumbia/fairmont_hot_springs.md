Fairmont Hot Springs is a small resort community in the Columbia Valley, between Invermere and Canal Flats. The name tells the truth: the hot mineral pools are the headline, but the setting is what makes the stop work, with dry hoodoo cliffs, big valley views, golf fairways, a small ski hill, and Columbia Lake just down the road.

This is not a deep town. Most services cluster around Highway 93/95 and the resort. But a traveler already moving through the East Kootenays can make a good spare day here: soak in the [hot springs](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/fairmont_hot_springs/hot_springs), hike the [Dutch Creek Hoodoos](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/fairmont_hot_springs/dutch_creek_hoodoos), and look south toward the headwaters country around [Columbia Lake Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/fairmont_hot_springs/columbia_lake_provincial_park).

Fairmont is best treated as a low-key outdoors stop rather than a polished mountain village. Come for warm water and easy scenery, then keep moving through the valley.
