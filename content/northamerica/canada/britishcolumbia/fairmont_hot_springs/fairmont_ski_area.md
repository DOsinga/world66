Fairmont's ski hill is modest by Canadian Rockies standards, which is exactly why families like it. The runs are short and manageable, the lift system is simple, and the hot pools are close enough to make skiing and soaking part of the same day.

Experienced skiers should keep expectations low. This is a gentle local hill, not a destination resort.
