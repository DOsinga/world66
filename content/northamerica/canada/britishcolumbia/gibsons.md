Gibsons is the first real town many travellers meet on British Columbia's Sunshine Coast, a harbour community reached by ferry from Horseshoe Bay rather than by road from Vancouver. That ferry separation matters. Even though the town is on the mainland, it feels like an island arrival: boats in Howe Sound, wooded hills behind, and a slower rhythm as soon as you step into Lower Gibsons.

Canada knows Gibsons as the setting of *The Beachcombers*, and the town still leans into that memory without being trapped by it. The old harbourfront has Molly's Reach, the restored tug *Persephone*, the [Sunshine Coast Museum & Archives](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/gibsons/sunshine_coast_museum), galleries, cafes, and a public market with a small marine education centre.

The best day here is simple: walk the harbour, climb [Soames Hill](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/gibsons/soames_hill) for the view over Howe Sound, then come back for seafood, a beer, or the market. Gibsons is not wild coast, but it is a good soft landing for the Sunshine Coast.
