Gibsons Public Market is the town's easy indoor gathering place, with produce, prepared food, local goods, and the marine education centre under the same roof. It is not huge, but it is one of the best practical stops in town: coffee, lunch, groceries, and a sense of the local scene.

Come before a picnic, a harbour walk, or the ferry back to Vancouver.
