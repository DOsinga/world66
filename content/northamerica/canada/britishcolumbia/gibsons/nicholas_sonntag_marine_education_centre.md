Inside the Gibsons Public Market, this small marine centre focuses on the life under Howe Sound: local fish, invertebrates, tide-pool creatures, and the habitats they need. It is modest, but it gives families and curious adults a good close look at the water they have just crossed by ferry.

Pair it with the market rather than treating it as a stand-alone museum. Together they make a useful rainy-hour stop near the harbour.
