Persephone Brewing is an 11-acre farm brewery north of Lower Gibsons, with a laid-back tasting room and a stronger sense of place than the average taproom. It is a good late-afternoon stop after Soames Hill, especially if you want something local without making dinner complicated.

Check opening hours outside summer, when Sunshine Coast schedules can shrink.
