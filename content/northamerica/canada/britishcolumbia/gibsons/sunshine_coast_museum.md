The Sunshine Coast Museum & Archives is small, local, and useful, exactly the kind of museum that helps a coastal town make sense. Exhibits cover Indigenous history, logging, fishing, settler life, and the television fame that came with *The Beachcombers*.

Start here if Gibsons feels too postcard-pretty at first glance. The museum adds the working-coast background behind the harbour.
