---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Golden sits on the Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1), which makes it straightforward to reach by car. It is approximately 262 kilometres west of Calgary (about 2.5 hours) and 713 kilometres east of Vancouver (about 7-8 hours). The drive from Calgary through Banff and over Kicking Horse Pass is spectacular.

The nearest airports with regular commercial service are **Calgary International Airport** to the east (2.5-3 hours) and **Kelowna International Airport** to the west (3.5 hours). Shuttle services operate seasonally between Calgary airport and Kicking Horse Mountain Resort during the ski season. **Rocky Mountaineer** rail service passes through Golden on its east-west route between Vancouver and the Rocky Mountains, though it operates as a touring train rather than a transit service.

Greyhound Canada discontinued its Golden stop, so independent travellers without a car face limited options outside the ski season. The Trans-Canada is a major truck route and hitchhiking is practiced, though not guaranteed.
