Northern Lights Wildlife Wolf Centre is a specialist wildlife education stop outside Golden. Its focus is wolves: how they live, why they matter ecologically, and how people in the Rockies have argued over them for generations.

This is not the same kind of draw as Yoho, Glacier, or Kicking Horse, and it should be judged on that smaller scale. For travellers interested in wildlife issues, especially families trying to add context to mountain drives, it can be a useful and memorable stop.
