---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Golden has two distinct peak seasons separated by shoulder periods that are worth considering for budget-conscious travellers.

**Winter (December to March)** is prime time for skiing at Kicking Horse Mountain Resort. The resort typically receives its best powder in January and February. Book accommodation well in advance for holiday periods and popular weekends. Spring skiing in March and April can offer excellent conditions with softer snow and longer daylight hours.

**Summer (June to September)** brings hiking, rafting, mountain biking, and wildlife viewing. July and August are the warmest months, with long daylight hours — essential for glacier hikes and long trail days. The Kicking Horse River is at its most powerful in late June when snowmelt peaks, which is when the most exhilarating rafting trips run. September is often underrated: the crowds thin out, the larches turn golden on the high slopes, and the weather remains stable.

**Shoulder season** (April-May and October-November) offers the lowest prices and quietest conditions, but the resort is closed and many summer attractions haven't opened yet. Snowpack on hiking trails can persist well into June at higher elevations.
