Pitt Lake is a long freshwater lake at the edge of the Fraser Valley, where the lowlands give way to steep forested mountains. The setting feels wilder than its distance from Metro Vancouver suggests, especially once you are out on the water.

Kayaking is the best reason to come. Conditions can change quickly, so treat it as an outdoor trip rather than a casual city-park paddle, and check local access and weather before setting out.
