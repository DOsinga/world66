---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (July to September) is peak season. Alouette Lake is warm enough to swim, the high trails are clear of snow, and all campgrounds are open. The trade-off is crowds: summer weekends bring large numbers from Metro Vancouver and parking areas fill by mid-morning.

Spring (May–June) is excellent for the falls and wildflowers, but snow remains on the upper ridges well into June. The Golden Ears trail is best left until July. Autumn brings fall colour to the valley and much quieter conditions — often the best time to visit for anyone focused on hiking rather than swimming. Winter is quiet and grey; the lower trails are walkable, but the mountains are in cloud most of the time.
