Alpine Taxidermy & Wildlife Museum is a small roadside wildlife museum east of Grand Forks, with preserved animals from British Columbia and beyond. The collection is old-fashioned, but families and wildlife-curious visitors may find it a worthwhile short stop.

Outside, there are gardens and a cedar maze for children. It is best treated as a quick add-on rather than the main reason to visit Grand Forks.
