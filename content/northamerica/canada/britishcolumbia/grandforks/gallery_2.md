Gallery 2 is Grand Forks' public art gallery, set in the town's old courthouse. The permanent collection and changing exhibitions lean toward regional artists and Boundary Country history, with enough local context to make it more than a small-town display room.

It is a worthwhile indoor stop while walking around downtown, especially if you are already tracing the Doukhobor and railway-era layers of Grand Forks.
