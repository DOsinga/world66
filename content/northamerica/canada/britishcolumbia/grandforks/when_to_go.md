---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June to early September) is the prime season — warm, dry days in the high twenties to mid-thirties Celsius, cool nights, and the rivers and Christina Lake at swimmable temperatures. The hill country immediately above town is also at its best for hiking and biking then.

Autumn brings clear days, cold nights, and good colour in the cottonwoods along the rivers. Winters are mild for British Columbia — generally above freezing, with some snow but rarely the deep accumulations of the higher Kootenay valleys. Spring is short and unpredictable, often wet.
