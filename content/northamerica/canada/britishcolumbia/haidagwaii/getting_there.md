---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By air**: Air Canada Jazz operates flights from Vancouver (YVR) to **Sandspit Airport** (YZP) on Moresby Island, and to **Masset Airport** on the north end of Graham Island. The flight to Sandspit takes about 1 hour 40 minutes; flight frequency varies by season. Book well ahead in summer.

**By BC Ferries**: BC Ferries operates year-round service on the **Inside Passage** route from Prince Rupert (on the mainland) to Skidegate on Graham Island. The crossing takes about 7 hours and is a journey in itself — the ferry passes through spectacular island scenery. Prince Rupert is 1.5 hours by air from Vancouver (direct flight) or 1-2 days by road plus ferry from Vancouver.

Getting **between Graham Island and Moresby Island** (where Sandspit and the Gwaii Haanas gateway are) requires BC Ferries' short Skidegate-Alliford Bay route (20 minutes, frequent daily sailings).

Getting around within Haida Gwaii is by rental car (available in Queen Charlotte and at Sandspit airport) or by pre-arranged tours. There is no public bus service on the islands.
