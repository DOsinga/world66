Sea kayaking through Gwaii Haanas is the best way to experience the southern archipelago — moving quietly between islands, camping on beaches, and arriving at abandoned village sites from the sea as the Haida themselves would have. Several operators based in Queen Charlotte offer guided multi-day kayak expeditions through the park, typically lasting 7 to 12 days and covering the highlights from Moresby Camp to SGang Gwaay.

The paddling is through relatively sheltered waters most of the time, but the outer coast can be exposed and conditions change quickly; no previous kayak experience is required on guided trips, though a reasonable level of fitness is essential. Independent paddling is possible but requires completing the registration and orientation process, carrying a VHF radio, and having genuine open-water experience. The wildlife is extraordinary: black bears on the beaches at low tide, bald eagles overhead, orca pods threading through the channels, and harbour porpoise in almost every passage.
