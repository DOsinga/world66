The Haida Heritage Centre at Kaay Llnagaay, in Skidegate on Graham Island, is the primary cultural institution of the Haida people — a large complex built in the form of a traditional winter village, with a main longhouse, carving shed, canoe house, and museum galleries. It opened in 2007 and is the result of years of community planning and fundraising by the Haida Nation.

The museum collection includes masterworks of Haida art: argillite carvings (a distinctive black stone found only on Haida Gwaii), silver and gold jewellery, woven hats and capes, ceremonial objects, and a comprehensive collection documenting the devastation of the 1862 smallpox epidemic and the subsequent cultural revival. The carving shed is where active artists work; you can watch contemporary carvers working on poles and discuss their work. The canoe house protects a 15-metre red cedar ocean-going canoe. This is the essential first stop for understanding Haida culture before visiting Gwaii Haanas.
