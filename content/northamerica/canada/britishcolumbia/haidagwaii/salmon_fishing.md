Haida Gwaii is one of the premier fishing destinations in British Columbia, with excellent runs of chinook, coho, and pink salmon, large halibut in the channels around Moresby Island, and abundant Dungeness crab. Several fishing lodges cater specifically to fly-out fishing expeditions; most are based in Queen Charlotte or operate floatplane services from Sandspit airport.

Freshwater fishing in the island's rivers is outstanding: the Tlell and Yakoun rivers are among the finest steelhead streams on the coast, and the salmon runs in autumn fill the rivers to the bank. A fishing licence is required for both freshwater and saltwater fishing; regulations change by season and species, so check with BC's provincial fisheries authority before your trip.
