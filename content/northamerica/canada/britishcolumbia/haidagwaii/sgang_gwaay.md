SGang Gwaay (also known by its English name Ninstints, or SGang Gwaay Llnagaay — "Red Cod Island Village") is a UNESCO World Heritage Site at the southern end of Gwaii Haanas, accessible only by boat or floatplane. It is the site of a Haida village that was abandoned in the late 19th century following the smallpox epidemic; the mortuary and memorial poles left standing in the forest are among the most powerful and moving sights in Canada.

Standing in front of the poles — their faces weathered to grey, tilting and splitting as the forest reclaims them — is an experience that refuses easy categorisation. The Haida Watchmen who are stationed here from May to September provide context and ensure that visitors treat the site with appropriate respect. No guides from outside the Haida community are permitted. Getting here requires booking through a registered tour operator (most operate from Queen Charlotte or Sandspit) and registering in advance with Parks Canada.
