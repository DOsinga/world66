---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The core experiences in Haida Gwaii are shaped by its remoteness and the depth of Haida culture. Visit Gwaii Haanas for the totem poles and the wilderness; fish the rivers for salmon and steelhead; kayak the outer coast; walk the old-growth forest trails. The Haida Heritage Centre at Skidegate is the starting point for understanding the culture before going anywhere else.
