---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Haida Gwaii is from **late May to September**. The days are long, the weather is most reliable, and Gwaii Haanas is accessible. July and August are the warmest months, though "warm" is relative — bring waterproof clothing at all times of year, and expect rain even in summer.

**Gwaii Haanas** (the national park and heritage site in the south) is accessible only from May to September, when the Haida Watchmen are stationed at the historic village sites and guided tours operate. Book registration early — the park is popular and visitor numbers are capped.

**Salmon fishing** peaks from July through September, with different species peaking at different times. Chinook are present from June; coho runs peak in August and September. Steelhead fishing on the rivers is best October to April (winter steelhead).

**Autumn** (September–October) is spectacular and less crowded: the forest turns gold, salmon fill the rivers, and the humpback whales that feed in the offshore waters are at their most visible. Winter brings storms and is for committed outdoors people only; the weather can be severe and the Hecate Strait rough.
