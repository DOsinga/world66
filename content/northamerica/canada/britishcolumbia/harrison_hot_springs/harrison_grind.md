The Harrison Grind is the local steep hike above Harrison Hot Springs. The round trip is about 7 km and climbs roughly 600 m, ending at a viewpoint over Harrison Lake and the village.

It is a proper workout rather than a gentle lakeside stroll, and the trail can be muddy after rain. Go for the climb and the view, then head back down to the hot pools or the lakefront.
