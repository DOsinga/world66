The Harrison Public Hot Pool is the straightforward way to use the hot springs if you are not staying at a resort. The facility is simple and indoors, with mineral water piped in from the local source.

It is not a wild spring and it is not fancy. Treat it as a practical soak after a walk or paddle, and it does exactly what the village name promises.
