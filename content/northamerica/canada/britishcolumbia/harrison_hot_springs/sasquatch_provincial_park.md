Sasquatch Provincial Park begins north of Harrison Hot Springs along the east side of the lake. It is the place to go when the village beach feels too tame: forest roads, picnic areas, small lakes, and mountain views.

Green Point gives an easy day-use stop on Harrison Lake, while Hicks Lake and Deer Lake add beaches, short walks, and paddling in a quieter setting. Road conditions vary, so check locally before treating the park as a quick drive-by.
