The Spirit Trail is a short forest loop south of Harrison Hot Springs, best known for the clay faces mounted on trees along the path. It is easy, flat, and a little odd in a good way.

Do it as a leg-stretcher rather than a major hike. The trail adds some local character to a village that can otherwise feel dominated by the beach and hot pools.
