title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Harrison Hot Springs is built around water. The public pool is the practical way to try the mineral springs without staying at a resort, while the lakefront gives you the main walk, beach, kayak launches, and mountain views.

For a fuller day, leave the village. [Sasquatch Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/harrison_hot_springs/sasquatch_provincial_park) has beaches, forest, and small lakes north of town, while the [Spirit Trail](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/harrison_hot_springs/spirit_trail) is a short, strange forest loop south of the village.
