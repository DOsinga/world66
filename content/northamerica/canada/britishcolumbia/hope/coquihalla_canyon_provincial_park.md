Coquihalla Canyon Provincial Park protects the dramatic gorge and former railway route
just outside Hope, including the famous Othello tunnels. Sheer rock walls, rushing
water, and old rail engineering make it one of the area's clearest short walks.

Conditions and access can change after storms or repairs, so check park notices before
going. When open, the route is an easy way to experience Hope's mountain-and-river
setting without committing to a long hike.
