Home Restaurant on Wallace Street is a straightforward diner: vinyl booths, laminate tables, a counter with stools, and a menu that covers the basics without complication. Breakfast runs until early afternoon and is the main reason to go — eggs done properly, thick-cut toast, and a reasonable cup of coffee at prices that make you remember small-town BC exists. The lunch menu adds sandwiches and burgers.

The clientele is a mix of locals and people passing through on the Trans-Canada who know to pull off here rather than settling for the chain options near the highway interchange. It fills up on weekend mornings, particularly in summer when camping traffic is heavy. The service is quick and unshowy. For a town this size, a reliable diner that has been operating continuously for decades is not nothing — it is the kind of place that keeps a community functional on a Tuesday morning in November.
