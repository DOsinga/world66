Skagit Valley Provincial Park protects a long, forested mountain valley southeast of
Hope, close to the United States border. It is quieter and rougher than the more famous
stops along the Fraser Canyon, with rivers, old logging roads, and trail access into
serious backcountry.

This is a place to plan, not improvise. Road conditions, seasonal access, and weather
matter, and services are limited once you leave Hope. For prepared travelers, the reward
is a valley that feels genuinely away from the highway.
