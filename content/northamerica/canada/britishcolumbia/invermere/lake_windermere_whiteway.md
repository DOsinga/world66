The Lake Windermere Whiteway is Invermere's winter signature. When conditions allow, the lake is groomed into a long loop with separate space for ice skating and classic cross-country skiing.

It connects Invermere and Windermere and gives the valley a rare, open-air winter route. Check current conditions before planning around it, because the Whiteway depends on lake ice and grooming.
