Pynelogs Art Gallery gives Invermere a small cultural anchor right beside Lake Windermere. The historic building hosts Columbia Valley art shows, workshops, concerts, and community events.

It is a good stop when you want a break from lake and mountain time. Check the current exhibition schedule, because the draw is what is on the walls and calendar rather than the building alone.
