---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Kamloops has a respectable craft-beer scene for a city its size — three downtown microbreweries (**Red Collar**, **Iron Road**, **Alchemy**) and a handful of well-stocked beer-focused bars along Victoria Street. The cafés are concentrated downtown too, with several good independent roasters; **Caffé Motivo** and **Hello Toast** are the local favourites for morning coffee.
