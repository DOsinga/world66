---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Kamloops is a good base for exploring the BC interior. The most rewarding day trips:

**Sun Peaks** (45 min north-east) — skiing in winter, mountain biking and golf in summer; see the dedicated POI.

**Adams River sockeye run** (90 min east, near Chase) — every four years, in early October, around two million sockeye salmon turn the Adams River bright red as they return to spawn. The **dominant run year** is the experience of a lifetime; even off-cycle years are worth the drive.

**Wells Gray Provincial Park** (2.5 hours north on Highway 5) — the "waterfall park", with **Helmcken Falls** (a 141-metre drop, four times the height of Niagara) and a number of other major cataracts, plus excellent canoeing on Clearwater Lake.

**Kamloops Lake** (30 min west, along the Trans-Canada) — a long fjord-like lake that the South Thompson opens into, with several beaches and viewpoints, plus the small wine region of the **Thompson Valley**.

**Cariboo Gold Rush trail** (north on Highway 97) — the road from Cache Creek up to historic **Barkerville** runs through striking dry country; a full long day or better an overnight.
