---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Kamloops has more dining choice than its size would suggest — the universities and the steady flow of through-traffic on the Trans-Canada keep the restaurants busy. The strongest concentration is along **Victoria Street** downtown, with Italian, Japanese, modern Canadian and a couple of good burger and brewpub options. The interior's stone-fruit and ranch beef show up on most local menus in summer. For groceries, the **Brock Shopping Centre** and the Saturday **Farmers' Market** cover most needs.
