---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By road.** Kamloops sits at the junction of the **Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1)** and the **Yellowhead Highway (Highway 5)** — about 350 km / four hours north-east of Vancouver, 550 km / six hours west of Calgary. The drive from Vancouver crosses the Coast Mountains and the Fraser Canyon and is one of the more spectacular routes in the country.

**By train.** **VIA Rail's Canadian** stops at Kamloops twice a week in each direction on its three-day run between Vancouver and Toronto. The luxury **Rocky Mountaineer** runs Vancouver–Banff via Kamloops with an overnight stop in the city.

**By air.** Kamloops Airport (YKA) has direct flights to Vancouver and Calgary several times a day on Air Canada and WestJet, plus seasonal service to a few other Canadian cities.

**By bus.** Long-distance bus options have shrunk since Greyhound left Western Canada; **Rider Express** and **Ebus** run Vancouver–Kamloops and Calgary–Kamloops services.
