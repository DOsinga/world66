**Victoria Street** is the spine of downtown Kamloops — a wide tree-lined boulevard running east–west for several blocks through the centre, lined with restored late-19th and early-20th-century brick storefronts. It is the city's most pleasant urban walking environment, with cafés, brewpubs, independent shops and a number of public art installations.

The **Kamloops Farmers' Market** takes over part of the street on Saturday mornings from spring to autumn, with stalls of stone fruit (the Thompson is one of the country's best peach- and cherry-growing regions), greens, baked goods, ranch beef and crafts. The Kamloops Art Gallery sits on the same stretch. A late-afternoon walk down Victoria Street with a stop at one of the brewpubs is the easiest way to take the city's pulse.
