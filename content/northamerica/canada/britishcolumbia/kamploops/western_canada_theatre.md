Western Canada Theatre is Kamloops' principal professional theatre company, staging drama, comedy, musicals, and touring work in the city centre. For visitors staying overnight, it can add a real local evening option beyond restaurants and pubs.

Its travel value depends on the season and the production, so check the current programme before building plans around it. When a show lines up, it is one of the better ways to see Kamloops as a working regional city rather than just a stop on the way to the mountains.
