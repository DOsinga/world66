The Kaslo River Trail is the village's best short walk, a gentle loop along both sides of the river with forest, water, and a covered bridge. It starts close enough to town that you can fold it into a lazy afternoon without planning much.

It is not wilderness, and that is the point. The trail gives Kaslo its quietest nearby landscape while keeping you tied to the village.
