H2O Adventure and Fitness Center is a large public indoor water park in Kelowna. It has an Olympic-length pool, wave pool, river run, waterslides, a children's water play area, and an indoor surf simulator.

It is most useful for families or for a bad-weather day, especially when lake swimming is not appealing.
