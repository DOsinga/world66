Mission Creek Greenway is one of Kelowna's best low-effort outdoor options, following the creek through a chain of parks and natural areas east of the city centre. The route is mostly gentle, making it accessible for walkers, runners, families, and casual cyclists.

The greenway is especially good when you want shade and water rather than another winery or beach. Interpretive signs, bridge crossings, and seasonal birdlife give the route enough variety for a short walk or a longer out-and-back ride.
