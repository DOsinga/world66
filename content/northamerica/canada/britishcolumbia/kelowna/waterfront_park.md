Waterfront Park is one of the easiest places to understand Kelownas appeal. Boardwalks, lawns, small beaches, public art, and views across Okanagan Lake put the citys resort mood right in the centre.

It is best at the edges of the day, when the lake light softens and locals are out walking, cycling, or meeting friends. Combine it with the downtown marina and cultural district for a relaxed introduction to Kelowna.
