Campbell Valley Regional Park is one of Langley's best outdoor escapes, spread across old farm fields, forest, wetlands, and the Little Campbell River corridor. It feels more rural than manicured, which is exactly the point: you come for long easy walks, birding, and space to breathe.

The trail network is gentle and varied, with boardwalks, equestrian paths, picnic areas, and traces of the area's agricultural past. It is a strong choice if Fort Langley has given you the history and you want a few hours outside.
