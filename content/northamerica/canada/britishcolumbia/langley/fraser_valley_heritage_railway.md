Fraser Valley Heritage Railway preserves the interurban rail history of the British Columbia Electric Railway. Its main draw is a restored interurban car, with speeders and velocipedes adding to the hands-on railway atmosphere.

It is best for rail enthusiasts and families looking for a compact heritage stop in the Fraser Valley. Check operating days before going.
