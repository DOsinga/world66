---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Langley's main draw is Fort Langley National Historic Site, but the surrounding area offers more than you might expect. The Canadian Museum of Flight sits at Langley Regional Airport with an impressive collection of vintage aircraft. The Township's agricultural land supports a handful of wineries and farm-gate operations worth exploring, and the Fraser River waterfront around Fort Langley village is pleasant to walk along.
