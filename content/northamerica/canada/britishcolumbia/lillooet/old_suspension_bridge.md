Lillooet's Old Suspension Bridge is a compact piece of canyon history, spanning the Fraser River where travel once meant hard crossings, steep grades, and a real sense of exposure. It is not a long visit, but the bridge gives a sharp visual reminder of why this valley mattered during the gold-rush era.

The setting does most of the work. Stop for the river views and the dry, rugged slopes around town, then fold it into a wider look at Lillooet's old roads, Indigenous history, and frontier-era landmarks.
