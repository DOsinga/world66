Furry Creek Golf & Country Club is one of the most scenic golf courses on the Sea to Sky route, laid out between forest, rock, and Howe Sound. Non-golfers may know it as a filming location from "Happy Gilmore", but the real draw is the setting.

It is a seasonal course and prices reflect the scenery. If golf is only a mild interest, the public parks nearby are better value; if you want a round with mountain-and-water views, this is the local choice.
