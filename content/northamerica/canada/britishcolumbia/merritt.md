Merritt sits in the dry Nicola Valley of British Columbia, where the Coquihalla and Okanagan routes meet between Hope, Kamloops, and Kelowna. Plenty of travellers know it only as a highway break, but the town has enough personality to reward a longer stop: ranch-country history, fishing lakes, mountain-bike trails, and a downtown identity built around country music.

The most visible local quirk is the [Merritt Walk of Stars](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/merritt/merritt_walk_of_stars), a self-guided circuit of country-music handprints and murals. For history, start at [Baillie House](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/merritt/baillie_house) and the [Nicola Valley Museum and Archives](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/merritt/nicola_valley_museum). Outside town, the dry hills and lake country make the place feel more Interior than Coast: open sky, sagebrush slopes, and backroads that quickly leave the highway mood behind.

Merritt is best with a car, a bike, or fishing gear. It is not a polished resort town, and that is part of the point. Give it a spare day if you want a practical base for the Nicola Valley rather than another quick fuel stop.
