Nanaimo River Regional Park protects a stretch of forest and river south of the city, giving Nanaimo an easy inland nature outing away from the harbour. Trails, riverbanks, and seasonal swimming spots make it popular when the weather is warm.

The park is best approached casually: walk, watch the water levels, and choose safe river access rather than treating it like a managed beach. It shows another side of the Nanaimo area, where forest and freshwater sit close behind the coast.
