---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Nanaimo's main draws are outdoors: the harbour, Newcastle Island, the waterfront walkway, and world-class cold-water diving at artificial reef sites in the strait. The Nanaimo Museum covers the city's coal-mining past and First Nations history. For the adventurous, WildPlay offers bungee jumping and ziplines above the Nanaimo River canyon.
