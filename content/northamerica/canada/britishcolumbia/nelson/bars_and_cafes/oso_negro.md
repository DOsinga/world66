Nelson's best-known coffee roaster, with a cafe on Baker Street that has been a meeting point for locals for years. The beans are roasted in-house and the espresso is serious. The room is large, the tables are communal, and the noise level is what you'd expect from a place that functions as the town's de facto living room. Arrive before 10am on weekends if you want a seat without a wait.

Bags of beans are available to take home — the dark roasts are particularly good.
