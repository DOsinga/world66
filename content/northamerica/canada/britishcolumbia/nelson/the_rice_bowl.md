Nelson's go-to for Asian food — a long-established restaurant on Baker Street serving reliable Chinese and pan-Asian dishes. The portions are generous, the prices reasonable, and the room fills up with locals most evenings. Nothing revelatory, but solid and consistent: the kind of place you're glad every small city has.
