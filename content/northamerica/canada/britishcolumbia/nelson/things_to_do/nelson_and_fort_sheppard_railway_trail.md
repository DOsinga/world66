The old Nelson & Fort Sheppard Railway right-of-way has been converted into a multi-use trail running south from Nelson toward Salmo. The grade is gentle — railways were built to avoid steep climbs — which makes it accessible to cyclists and walkers of all abilities. The trail runs through second-growth forest and alongside small creeks, with occasional views down to the valley below.

The section closest to Nelson is the most used; the trail gets wilder and less maintained the further south you go. Mountain bikes handle it easily; hybrid bikes are fine for the first stretch.
