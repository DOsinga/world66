New Denver is a small village on Slocan Lake in British Columbia's West Kootenays, quiet enough that it can look like a pause between more famous mountain towns. Stay a little longer and it becomes more distinct: lake shore, old mining country, Japanese Canadian internment history, and access to some fine Selkirk hiking.

The strongest stop is the [Nikkei Internment Memorial Centre](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/new_denver/nikkei_internment_memorial_centre), which tells one of Canada's harsher wartime stories in the place where it happened. The [Silvery Slocan Museum](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/new_denver/silvery_slocan_museum) fills in the mining and settlement history, while [Kohan Reflection Garden](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/new_denver/kohan_reflection_garden) brings that memory down to the lake edge.

The setting does the rest. [Galena Trail](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/new_denver/galena_trail) follows an old railway grade through forest and mining country, and [Idaho Peak](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/new_denver/idaho_peak) gives one of the great high views over Slocan Lake when the road is open.
