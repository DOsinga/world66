The Waterfront Esplanade is New Westminster's easiest river walk, following the Fraser with views of working water, bridges, and the changing face of the old royal city. It helps explain why the river still matters here, even as the waterfront has become more residential and recreational.

Use it as a connector as much as a destination. A stroll can tie together the quay, parks, public art, and nearby food stops, making it a simple way to get oriented in New Westminster without needing a car.
