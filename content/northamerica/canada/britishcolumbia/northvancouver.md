North Vancouver sits on the north shore of Burrard Inlet, about 8 km from downtown [Vancouver](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vancouver) and backed by the Coast Mountains. Access is via the Lions Gate Bridge, the Second Narrows Bridge, or the [SeaBus](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/seabus) passenger ferry from Waterfront Station — which is itself a pleasant crossing with views of the harbour and skyline.

Most visitors come for the mountain attractions. The [Capilano Suspension Bridge](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/capilano_suspension_bridge) draws crowds to its swaying span 70 metres above the Capilano River, while the nearby [Cleveland Dam](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/cleveland_dam) and salmon hatchery offer a free look at the same canyon and at returning chinook climbing the fish ladder. [Grouse Mountain](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/grouse_mountain) climbs to a summit with hiking trails, a grizzly bear refuge, the [Eye of the Wind](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/eye_of_the_wind) viewing platform inside a working wind turbine, and city views that stretch to Mount Baker on a clear day. The smaller and quieter [Lynn Canyon](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/lynn_canyon) park a few kilometres east has its own (free) suspension bridge and a network of forest trails. In winter, Grouse and neighbouring [Mount Seymour](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/mount_seymour) offer the closest skiing to a major Canadian city.

The [Lower Lonsdale](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/lower_lonsdale) waterfront area has developed into a destination in its own right, with the [Lonsdale Quay](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/lonsdale_quay_market) public market, the [Polygon Gallery](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/polygon_gallery) photography museum, and the redeveloped [Shipyards District](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/shipyards_district) along the waterfront. Out east, [Deep Cove](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/northvancouver/deep_cove) is the launching point for sea-kayaking up Indian Arm.
