---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

North Vancouver eats out predominantly in three areas: the **Lower Lonsdale waterfront**, **Edgemont Village** uphill, and the **Deep Cove** village out east.

In Lower Lonsdale, **Tap & Barrel** and **Black + Blue** have the harbour-front tables. **Burgoo Bistro** does extremely good slow-cooked comfort food (the pulled-pork sandwich, the duck poutine, French onion soup) at moderate prices. **Anatoli Souvlaki** in Central Lonsdale is the long-running excellent Greek restaurant; reservations needed on weekends. **The Beer Farmers** at the Pipe Shop is the brewpub of choice for craft beer and casual food.

For ramen, **Hapa Izakaya** at the Quay. For Vietnamese, **Pho Mai Tea House** at Central Lonsdale — a tiny, packed, perfect bowl of pho for under C$15. For sushi, **Ki Sushi** at Marine Drive. For brunch, **Cafe Orso** in Edgemont. For dim sum, **Cheese & Crackers Asian fusion** at the Quay.

In Deep Cove, **Honey Doughnuts** is the famous cinnamon-sugar donut stop (queues on weekends). **Café Orso** opens at 6:30 for early-morning kayakers. **The Raven Pub** is the classic dark-wood-and-pints lunch spot.
