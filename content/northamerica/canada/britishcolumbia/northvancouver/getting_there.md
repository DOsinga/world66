---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

North Vancouver is across Burrard Inlet from downtown [Vancouver](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vancouver). There are four obvious ways across.

**The SeaBus** is the easiest for visitors without a car: 12-minute passenger ferry every 12 minutes from Waterfront Station to Lonsdale Quay. The crossing is included in the standard transit fare.

**Lions Gate Bridge** from Stanley Park is the most scenic road crossing — three lanes that reverse direction with the rush-hour flow. Beware of long delays westbound in the morning and eastbound in the afternoon.

**Second Narrows Bridge** (officially the Ironworkers Memorial Crossing) at the east end of the inlet carries Highway 1; six lanes, no reversible centre lane. The standard option for traffic to and from the east.

**Bus** routes from downtown Vancouver run via both bridges. The 246 from Vancouver to Highlands uses Lions Gate; the 257 from downtown to Horseshoe Bay also uses Lions Gate; numerous 2xx routes serve the eastern side via Second Narrows.

The nearest airport, **YVR**, is about 45 minutes south of the city by car or SkyTrain plus SeaBus.
