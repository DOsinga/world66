Indian Arm is a long fjord reaching north from Deep Cove into the Coast Mountains. Say Nuth Khaw Yum Provincial Park protects much of the shoreline, with waterfalls, steep forested slopes, boat-in campsites, and popular kayaking routes from Deep Cove.
