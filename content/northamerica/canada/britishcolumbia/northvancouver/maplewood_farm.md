Maplewood Farm is an easy, low-key family stop on the North Shore, especially useful when younger travellers need something more hands-on than viewpoints and suspension bridges. The farm keeps goats, sheep, horses, rabbits, birds, and other domestic animals in a setting that still feels surprisingly rural for North Vancouver.

It is not a major sight for every visitor, but it earns its place because it fills a practical gap: an approachable half-day outing with children, close to transit and other North Vancouver stops. Pair it with nearby parks or the waterfront if you want a relaxed day away from downtown Vancouver.
