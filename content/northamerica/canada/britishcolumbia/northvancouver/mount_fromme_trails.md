Mount Fromme is one of the classic North Shore riding areas, where steep forest, ladders, roots, rock rolls, and loamy descents helped define Vancouver's mountain-bike reputation. The trail network starts above residential North Vancouver and quickly feels like deep coastal rainforest.

This is not a casual bike path. Pick routes that match your ability, wear proper protection, and expect wet wood and slippery roots after rain. Hikers also use parts of the mountain, but Fromme's real travel value is for riders who want to sample the technical style that made the North Shore famous.
