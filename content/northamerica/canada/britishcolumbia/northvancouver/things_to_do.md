---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

North Vancouver's sights cluster in three areas: the mountain attractions on the lower slopes of the Coast Mountains (Capilano Suspension Bridge, Grouse Mountain, the Cleveland Dam and Lynn Canyon), the developed Lower Lonsdale waterfront (Lonsdale Quay market, the Shipyards District, brewpubs), and a green ring of regional parks (Lynn Headwaters, Mount Seymour, the Capilano River) within a 20-minute drive of downtown. The combination of a quick SeaBus crossing from downtown Vancouver and immediate access to old-growth temperate rainforest is what makes this side of Burrard Inlet so popular.
