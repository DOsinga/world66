---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**June to September** is the comfortable stretch. Daytime temperatures sit around 20–25°C, the rain stops, the mountains are open, the harbour is busy. July and August are the busiest months — book accommodation early and arrive at popular trailheads (Quarry Rock, the Lynn Canyon parking lot, the Grouse Grind) before nine to find parking.

**October and November** are wet and dim. The forests are spectacular — fall colour through the cedar-and-maple stands of Lynn Canyon and the Capilano corridor — but rain is constant and the lower slopes can be unpleasant on a multi-day soggy spell.

**December to March** is the winter ski season. The North Shore mountains receive heavy snowfall — sometimes 12 metres in a season — and Grouse and Seymour both run night skiing. The downside is short daylight hours (sunset at 16:20 in December), wet roads at sea level, and the constant chance of rain that turns to snow at altitude.

**April and May** are the cherry-blossom and dogwood months. The mountains are still snow-capped, the lower slopes turn green, and the tourist crowds have not yet arrived. May is arguably the prettiest month.
