Peach Cliff is the landmark above Okanagan Falls. Its pale volcanic face catches the light from around Skaha Lake, and the slopes are habitat for mule deer and California bighorn sheep.

The best-known detail is Balancing Rock, a glacial erratic perched above the cliff. Even if you do not hike far, the cliff gives the village its shape.
