---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Osoyoos is a small town and the restaurant scene reflects that — expect a few solid options rather than a wide range. The better meals are often found at winery restaurants, where the kitchen has a captive audience and a clear incentive to perform. Spirit Ridge Resort, adjacent to the Nk'Mip cultural centre, has a restaurant with good desert views and a menu that leans into local produce. Several Italian-influenced spots in town are steady performers: Osoyoos's grape-growing heritage and the Italian-Canadian community that has shaped local wine culture carry over into the cooking.

In summer, the town fills with visitors and most places are busy on weekends. Outside summer, choices narrow considerably and some spots close entirely. The Saturday farmers market (late spring through autumn) has the best local fruit: Okanagan peaches, cherries, and apricots in July and August are among the best in Canada.
