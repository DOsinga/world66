Osoyoos Museum is a small local-history museum with a setting that deserves explanation: desert, lake, border, orchards, and Indigenous history all meet here. Exhibits cover settlement, agriculture, community life, and the unusual ecology of the south Okanagan.

It is a useful stop before heading to the lake, vineyards, or desert conservation areas. The museum helps Osoyoos feel less like a resort strip and more like a place with a complicated landscape behind it.
