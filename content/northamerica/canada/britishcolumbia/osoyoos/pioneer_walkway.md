Pioneer Walkway is the kind of simple local feature that matters in Osoyoos: a flat lakeside path, water views, pocket beaches, and quick access from town. It is not dramatic, but it gives structure to an easy morning or evening walk along Canada's warmest lake country.

Use it for orientation and downtime between wineries, desert walks, and swims. In summer, go early or late to avoid the strongest heat and to catch the softer light on Osoyoos Lake.
