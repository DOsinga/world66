Little Qualicum Cheeseworks and MooBerry Winery is a working farm stop near Parksville, good for travellers who want something more local than another beach walk. The draw is simple: cheese, farm animals, and a look at Vancouver Island agriculture at a visitor-friendly scale.

It is especially useful with children or as a rainy-day break. Tastings and farm-shop browsing are the core experience, while the rural setting makes it feel connected to the surrounding Oceanside countryside rather than just a roadside shop.
