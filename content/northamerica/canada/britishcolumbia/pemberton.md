Pemberton is the quieter valley beyond Whistler, where the Sea to Sky Highway leaves the resort crowds and opens into farmland below Mount Currie. It is a small town, but the setting is strong enough that it earns a stop of its own.

The easy anchor is [Nairn Falls](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/pemberton/nairn_falls_provincial_park), a short trail to a powerful waterfall on the Green River. In town, [One Mile Lake](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/pemberton/one_mile_lake) gives a gentle boardwalk loop and a good place to swim or picnic, while the [Pemberton Museum](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/pemberton/pemberton_museum) adds local history without demanding much time.

Pemberton is also the practical base for bigger mountain days, especially [Joffre Lakes](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/pemberton/joffre_lakes_provincial_park) east on the Duffey Lake Road. It is not a town of grand monuments. Its appeal is the combination of farms, glacier water, mountain walls, and a slower rhythm than Whistler.
