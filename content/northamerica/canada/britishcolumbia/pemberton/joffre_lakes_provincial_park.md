Joffre Lakes is the big-ticket hike from Pemberton. The trail climbs past three bright glacial lakes, with the lower lake reached quickly and the upper lakes requiring a more serious uphill walk.

It is popular enough that day-use passes are often required in season, and closures or Indigenous access periods can affect plans. Check BC Parks before driving out, then start early if you want any quiet.
