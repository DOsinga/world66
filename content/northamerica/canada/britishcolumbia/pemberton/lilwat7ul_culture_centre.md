Lil̓wat7úl Culture Centre presents Lil̓wat culture through cedar baskets, wood carvings, masks, buckskin clothing, stories, drum-making programs, and guided nature walks. The centre is also part of the Nation's administrative and educational work, which gives the visit more context than a simple display space.

It is a good cultural stop near Pemberton and Mount Currie, especially for travellers trying to understand the valley beyond its mountain scenery.
