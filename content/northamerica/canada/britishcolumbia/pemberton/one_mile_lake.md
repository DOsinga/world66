One Mile Lake is Pemberton's easy local walk. The loop is short, mostly flat, and good for a relaxed hour with views back toward the surrounding peaks.

In summer it becomes a swimming and picnic spot. It will not impress serious hikers on its own, but it shows the valley's softer side and pairs well with Nairn Falls.
