Sloquet Hotsprings is a remote set of natural pools in the forest along Sloquet Creek. Hot water comes out of the bank and feeds a small chain of bathing pools, from intimate upper pools to a larger lower pool.

This is a backcountry stop rather than a quick town attraction. Go prepared for rough access, changing pool temperatures, and a wilderness setting, but it is worth the trip if you are looking for a natural hot spring experience near Pemberton.
