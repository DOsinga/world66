Okanagan Lake Beach is the easy, central beach at the north end of Penticton. It is long, sandy, and close to downtown, with rentals, food, and shade never far away.

It gets busy in summer, but that is part of Penticton's rhythm. Come early for a swim or late in the day when the heat drops and the lakefront starts to feel social rather than crowded.
