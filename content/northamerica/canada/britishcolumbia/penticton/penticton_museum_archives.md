Penticton Museum & Archives tells the local story through pioneer material, photographs, documents and First Nations pieces. It is not the Okanagan's flashiest attraction, but it gives useful context for a town many visitors otherwise experience only through beaches, wine and lake views.

The museum shares a building with the regional library, which makes it an easy central stop. Go when you want a grounded sense of how Penticton developed between the two lakes.
