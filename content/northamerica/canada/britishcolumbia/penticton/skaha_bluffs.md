Skaha Bluffs is one of British Columbia's best-known rock-climbing areas, a landscape of cliffs, ponderosa pine, dry grass, and views over Skaha Lake. Climbers come for the sheer number of routes, but non-climbers can still enjoy the trails and the open, desert-like feel of the park.

Go early in summer, when the rock and trails heat up quickly. Spring and autumn are more comfortable and often better for walking.
