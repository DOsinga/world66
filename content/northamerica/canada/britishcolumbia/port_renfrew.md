Port Renfrew sits at the end of the road on southwestern Vancouver Island, where the forest runs down to a hard Pacific coast. It is tiny and weather-beaten, but the surroundings are strong enough to make it more than a logging-road outpost.

The headline is [Botanical Beach](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/port_renfrew/botanical_beach), one of the best tide-pool sites on the island and the western end of the [Juan de Fuca Marine Trail](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/port_renfrew/juan_de_fuca_marine_trail). Inland, [Avatar Grove](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/port_renfrew/avatar_grove) gives a short but memorable old-growth walk among giant cedars and Douglas firs.

This is not a place for polished comforts. Come for low tide, rain gear, big trees, and the sense that Victoria is much farther away than the map suggests.
