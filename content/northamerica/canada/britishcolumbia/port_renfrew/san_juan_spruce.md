The San Juan Spruce is one of Port Renfrew's great big-tree stops, a massive Sitka spruce beside the San Juan River. A lightning strike reduced its height and volume, but the trunk is still enormous, and the short visit fits well with Avatar Grove, Botanical Beach, and the other rainforest sights around town.
