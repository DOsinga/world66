*North America > Canada > British Columbia > Port Alberni*

Port Alberni sits at the head of a long saltwater inlet, folded into the middle of [Vancouver Island](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vancouverisland) between Mount Arrowsmith, Sproat Lake, and the road to the Pacific coast. It is still a working town, with a plainspoken logging and fishing past, but that is part of the appeal. This is not Tofino with better weather; it is the inland harbour that gives the island's west side some grit.

The best reason to stop is the mix of industry, water, and forest. [McLean Mill](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/portalberni/mclean_mill) preserves a steam-era sawmill, while the [Alberni Pacific Railway](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/portalberni/alberni_pacific_railway) keeps the old logging railway story alive when it is running. Down by the quay, the town opens to the inlet and to the small maritime museum built around a lighthouse lantern.

Nature is close and not ornamental. [Stamp River Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/portalberni/stamp_river_provincial_park) is the place to watch salmon fight their way upstream in season. [Sproat Lake](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/portalberni/sproat_lake_provincial_park) has warm summer swimming, forested shores, and one of the island's best-known petroglyph panels. If you are crossing the island between Nanaimo and Tofino, Port Alberni is worth more than a fuel stop.
