Kwinitsa Station Railway Museum tells the story of early Prince Rupert and the Grand Trunk Pacific Railway. The station was built in 1911 and later moved to the waterfront, where it now preserves railway artifacts and local history.

It is a small but worthwhile stop for visitors interested in how rail, port development, and northern British Columbia's coastal settlements grew together.
