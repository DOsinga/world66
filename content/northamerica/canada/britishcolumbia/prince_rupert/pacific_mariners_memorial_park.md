Pacific Mariners Memorial Park overlooks Prince Rupert's harbour and honours the mariners of the North Coast. Its main features are the bronze Mariner's statue, memorial walls, and a small Shinto shrine tied to a Japanese fishing boat that drifted across the Pacific.
