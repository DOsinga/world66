Huble Homestead is a restored early twentieth-century homestead and trading-post site
north of Prince George, near the old Giscome Portage route. Buildings, gardens, costumed
interpretation, and river-country setting make it an accessible introduction to the
region before highways dominated travel.

It is especially good for travelers interested in local history rather than big-ticket
sights. The site helps explain how people, goods, and news moved through central British
Columbia when rivers, portages, and horses mattered more than cars.
