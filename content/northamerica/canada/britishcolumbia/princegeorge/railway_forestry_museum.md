The Central BC Railway and Forestry Museum is an outdoor museum with over sixty pieces of rolling stock spread across an eight-acre site, including steam locomotives, diesel engines, a working 1906 steam engine undergoing restoration, and an eclectic collection of historical buildings. The museum reflects Prince George's identity as a city built by the railway and sustained by forestry — both industries are represented in detail.

The site takes a couple of hours to walk properly, and the volunteers who staff it are generally knowledgeable and enthusiastic. This is the kind of museum that rewards the patient visitor who reads the signs rather than the one looking for interactive exhibits. The collection of historic wooden railway cars is particularly good.
