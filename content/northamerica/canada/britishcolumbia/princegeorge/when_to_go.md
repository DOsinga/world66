---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Prince George has a continental subarctic climate with extreme temperature swings: summers can reach nearly 40°C, while winters have dropped to -50°C in recorded history. The most extreme cold is in January and February. July and August are the most comfortable months for visiting, with long days, warm temperatures, and the outdoor activities at their best.

Winter is long and dark but has its own appeal for cross-country skiers and those driving the Alaska Highway who need a comfortable overnight stop. The Tabor Mountain and Powder King ski areas are within reasonable driving distance for downhill skiing. Spring is muddy and often anticlimactic.
