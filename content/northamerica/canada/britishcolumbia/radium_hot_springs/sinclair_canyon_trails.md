The Sinclair Canyon Trails follow the lower part of Sinclair Creek, just below the cliffs that frame the highway entrance into Radium. They are short, close to town, and useful when you want a real walk without driving deeper into the park.

The trail system is also one of the easiest places to appreciate Radium's geography: a hot-spring village squeezed between mountain wall, creek, and highway.
