---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Revelstoke sits on the Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1), 560 km east of Vancouver and 290 km west of Calgary — roughly six hours from Vancouver and three and a half from Calgary by car. The highway through Rogers Pass is spectacular but requires care in winter; chains or winter tires are mandatory, and the pass is occasionally closed for avalanche control.

There is no commercial air service. The nearest airports are **Kelowna** (2.5 hours west), **Kamloops** (2 hours west), and **Calgary International** (3.5 hours east). Most visitors drive. Via Rail's Canadian stops at Revelstoke station on its transcontinental route (Toronto to Vancouver), but services are infrequent — typically three times per week in each direction.

Greyhound no longer serves the route, but private shuttle services run between Revelstoke and Kelowna, Kamloops, and Calgary, particularly during ski season.
