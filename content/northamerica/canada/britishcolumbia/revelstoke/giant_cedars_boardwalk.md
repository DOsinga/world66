Giant Cedars Boardwalk is a short interpretive trail through old-growth western red cedar and hemlock forest in Mount Revelstoke National Park. The 500-metre loop uses boardwalks to protect the wet inland rainforest floor, with signs explaining the trees and the unusual ecosystem around them.

It is one of the easiest worthwhile nature stops near Revelstoke, especially for families or anyone wanting a forest walk without committing to a longer hike.
