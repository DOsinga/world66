The Enchanted Forest is a deliberately odd roadside attraction west of Revelstoke, where
fairy-tale figures, small buildings, and boardwalks sit among wet Interior cedar forest.
It is whimsical rather than wild, but the setting keeps it from feeling like a generic
theme park.

It works best for families, road-trippers, or anyone who enjoys a little eccentricity
between mountain drives. Do not expect deep cultural meaning; expect a gentle walk, big
trees, and the kind of handmade roadside imagination that has mostly disappeared from
major highways.
