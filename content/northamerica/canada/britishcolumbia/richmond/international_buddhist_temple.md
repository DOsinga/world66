The International Buddhist Temple in south Richmond is one of the largest Chinese Buddhist temples in North America, built in traditional imperial Chinese style — curved yellow roofs, red columns, stone lions at the gate — in what is otherwise a quiet suburban neighbourhood. The contrast is striking.

The temple grounds include several halls, a pond with ornamental fish, and gardens. The main hall houses golden Buddhist statues and is open during visiting hours. Monastic life continues here; the monks are generally welcoming of respectful visitors. The temple is most active on Buddhist holidays and the Chinese New Year period, when it draws large crowds.
