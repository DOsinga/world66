No. 5 Road is one of Richmond's most unusual cultural corridors, with Buddhist temples, Sikh gurdwaras, mosques, churches, and other religious centres set close together. It is less a single monument than a visible expression of the city's immigrant communities.

The route works best by car, bike, or a carefully planned walk, and visitors should remember that these are active places of worship. Go for architecture and cultural context, not spectacle, and check individual sites before assuming public access.
