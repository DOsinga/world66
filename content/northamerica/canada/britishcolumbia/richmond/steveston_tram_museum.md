The Steveston Tram Museum preserves Car 1220, a 1913 interurban tram from the former British Columbia Electric Railway. The car is displayed in its own building near the Steveston waterfront, making it an easy add-on to the Gulf of Georgia Cannery or Britannia Shipyards. It is a small stop, but a useful one for understanding how Richmond and Vancouver's early suburbs were linked.
