**Rossland** is a small mountain city in the West Kootenays, high above Trail and close to the United States border. It began as a gold-mining boom town in the 1890s, and the old streets still climb steeply past brick shops, mining-era buildings, and views into the surrounding ranges.

Today the reason to come is the mountains. [Red Mountain Resort](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/rossland/red_mountain_resort) sits just outside town and has a serious local following for powder, tree skiing, and a relaxed ski-hill mood. In summer, Rossland turns into a mountain-bike base, with the [Seven Summits Trail](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/rossland/seven_summits_trail) as the big prize for strong riders.

Rossland is not polished in the way bigger resort towns can be. That is part of the appeal. You can spend the morning in the [Rossland Museum and Discovery Centre](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/rossland/rossland_museum_discovery_centre), walk the historic downtown in the afternoon, then end the day with a beer in a town where ski boots and bike mud are normal street wear.
