The Rossland Court House stands on a steep site above Columbia Avenue, a confident public building from the years when the mining boom made Rossland feel much bigger than it was. It was completed in 1901 and later designated a National Historic Site of Canada.

It is worth including on a downtown walk, along with the old post office, Miners' Hall, and other boom-town buildings nearby.
