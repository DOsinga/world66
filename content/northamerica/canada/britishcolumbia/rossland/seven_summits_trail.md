The Seven Summits Trail is the classic Rossland summer challenge: a long, high, point-to-point route along the Rossland Range. Mountain bikers come for the sustained singletrack and big views; strong hikers can also use sections of the route.

This is not a casual town path. Expect a demanding day, changing mountain weather, and logistics at both ends of the trail. Go when the high country is snow-free, and check local trail reports before committing.
