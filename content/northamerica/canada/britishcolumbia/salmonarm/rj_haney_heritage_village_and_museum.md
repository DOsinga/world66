R.J. Haney Heritage Village and Museum is Salmon Arm's main heritage attraction, with historic buildings, exhibits and grounds that recreate parts of the Shuswap's settler-era past. It gives the town a cultural stop beyond the lake and wharf.

The village is seasonal in feel, with programming and access varying through the year. It works best for families or travelers interested in local history, especially when combined with a walk at the waterfront.
