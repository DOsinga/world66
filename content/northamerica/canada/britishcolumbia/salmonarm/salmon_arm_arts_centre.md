Salmon Arm Arts Centre is a small downtown gallery and cultural space rather than a major museum, but it earns its place in Salmon Arm because the town's visitor rhythm is slow and local. Exhibitions change through the year, often drawing on Shuswap artists and regional themes.

It works well with the wharf, waterfront, and a coffee stop in the centre. Come here for a grounded look at the local arts scene, not for a large collection or big-city gallery polish.
