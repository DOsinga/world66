---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Sidney's location at the top of the Saanich Peninsula makes it an excellent base for day trips in several directions.

**Victoria** is 25 kilometres south along Highway 17 — under 30 minutes by car or about an hour on BC Transit. The provincial capital has excellent museums, a walkable inner harbour, and the famous afternoon tea tradition at the Empress Hotel.

**Butchart Gardens**, about 15 kilometres south of Sidney, is one of British Columbia's most-visited attractions. Fifty-five acres of landscaped gardens occupy a former limestone quarry; the gardens are particularly beautiful in summer when they're illuminated at night, and in winter during the Christmas season.

**The Gulf Islands** are a short ferry hop from the Swartz Bay terminal. Salt Spring Island, the largest and most accessible, has farms, artists' studios, a renowned Saturday market, and good cycling. Galiano and Mayne are quieter, with walking trails and dramatic coastal scenery.

**San Juan Islands, Washington** are reachable via the seasonal Anacortes ferry from Sidney. Friday Harbor on San Juan Island is the main destination — it has whale watching, local wineries, and the Pig War historical park commemorating the unusual 1859 boundary dispute between the US and Britain.
