---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sidney is about 25 kilometres north of Victoria on the Saanich Peninsula, easily reached by car or bus along the Patricia Bay Highway (Highway 17).

**By air:** Victoria International Airport is immediately adjacent to Sidney — it is one of the few towns in Canada where you can walk from the terminal to a town centre (about 20 minutes on foot or a short taxi ride).

**By ferry:** BC Ferries' Swartz Bay terminal, just north of Sidney, connects to Tsawwassen on the mainland (about 90 minutes, multiple daily sailings). The same terminal also serves the Gulf Islands — Salt Spring, Galiano, Mayne, Saturna, and the Penders. A seasonal foot-passenger ferry operated by Washington State Ferries (and connecting services) links Sidney to Anacortes, Washington, for travellers heading to the San Juan Islands or onwards into the United States.

**By bus:** BC Transit route 70 runs between Victoria's downtown and Sidney (and the Swartz Bay ferry terminal), making the journey without a car straightforward if not particularly fast.
