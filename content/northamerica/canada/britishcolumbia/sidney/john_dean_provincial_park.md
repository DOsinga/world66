John Dean Provincial Park sits on Mount Newton above the Saanich Peninsula, close enough to Sidney for a short detour but high enough to feel like a different landscape. The park protects a pocket of old coastal Douglas fir and arbutus, with trails climbing through mossy forest to viewpoints over the Gulf Islands and the fields around Saanichton.

It is a good choice when you want a land-based walk to balance Sidney's waterfront and island trips. The trails are short but steep in places, and the viewpoints reward clear weather. Come with walking shoes and a bit of time to linger; this is not a manicured garden, but a quiet local park with a fine sense of the peninsula's natural setting.
