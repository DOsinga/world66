The Shaw Centre for the Salish Sea is Sidney's most substantial indoor attraction. Its aquariums focus tightly on local waters, with rockfish, anemones, crabs, kelp-forest life, and the strange small animals that make tide pools worth lingering over.

It is not a big city aquarium, but it does its job well: it makes the sea outside the door more legible. Pair it with the waterfront walk or a ferry day, especially if weather has made the coast look grey and closed-off.
