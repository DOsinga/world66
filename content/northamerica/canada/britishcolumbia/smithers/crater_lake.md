Crater Lake is a mountain hike above Smithers with views toward the Telkwa Mountains. The trail starts from the Hudson Bay Mountain ski area and climbs through open, scenic country to the lake.

It is a good fair-weather walk for travellers who want a mountain outing without driving far from town. Check local trail and road conditions before setting out.
