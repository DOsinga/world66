Hudson Bay Mountain is the peak that gives Smithers its shape. In winter it is the local ski hill, with a friendly, northern feel rather than a polished mega-resort mood. In summer the same road and slopes lead toward hiking, horseback riding, and big views over the Bulkley Valley.

It is the first place to look if you came to Smithers for mountains rather than errands.
