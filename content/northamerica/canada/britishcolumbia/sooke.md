Sooke is a harbor town west of Victoria, useful as a base but strong enough for a day in its own right. It sits where southern Vancouver Island starts to feel rougher: rocky points, cold Pacific water, rainforest trails, and the road onward to Port Renfrew.

The town's anchors are natural rather than urban. [East Sooke Regional Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/sooke/east_sooke_regional_park) has some of the best coastal hiking near Victoria. [Sooke Potholes](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/sooke/sooke_potholes) draws swimmers to clear river pools in summer. [Whiffin Spit](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/sooke/whiffin_spit) gives an easy harbor walk with seals, birds, and good weather-watching.

Sooke is not a polished resort town, and that helps. Come for walking shoes, tide air, and a looser edge than Victoria. A car makes the visit much easier, though buses can get you from Victoria to town and near some trailheads.
