The Sea to Sky Gondola is the easy way to get the Squamish panorama: Howe Sound below, forested ridges around you, and mountains rising behind the town. At the top are walking trails, viewing platforms, and a suspension bridge.

Hikers can make it tougher by taking the Sea to Summit Trail up through the forest and riding the gondola down. For most visitors, the lift itself is the point.
