Shannon Falls drops 335 metres down the mountainside just south of Squamish, making it one of the easiest big-nature stops on the Sea to Sky Highway. The main viewpoint is only a short paved walk from the parking area.

It is popular and busy, but still worth the stop. The falls are especially good after rain, when the whole cliff seems to run with water.
