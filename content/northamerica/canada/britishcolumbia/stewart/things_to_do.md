---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Stewart is about glaciers, bears, and the strange border geography it shares with Hyder. The best day starts with Bear Glacier on the drive in, continues across the Alaska line to Fish Creek if the salmon are running, and then climbs the Salmon Glacier road if weather and road conditions allow.

In town, the estuary boardwalk is the easy walk. It gives Stewart a softer counterpoint to all the rock and ice: tidal flats, birds, grass, and mountains reflected in the water.
