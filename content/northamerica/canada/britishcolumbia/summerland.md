Summerland is a small Okanagan town on the west side of Okanagan Lake, between Kelowna and Penticton. It has the lake-and-vineyard pleasures of the valley, but with a quieter feel than its bigger neighbours.

The town spreads from the lakeshore up to a sunny bench of orchards, wineries, and dry hills. [Giant's Head Mountain](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/summerland/giants_head_mountain) rises almost in the middle of it all, giving a quick hike and a wide view over the lake. Below the hill, beaches and fruit stands carry the summer rhythm.

Summerland's best single attraction is the [Kettle Valley Steam Railway](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/summerland/kettle_valley_steam_railway), a short heritage ride on the old railway grade above Trout Creek. Add the [Trout Creek Trestle](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/summerland/trout_creek_trestle), the [Ornamental Gardens](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/summerland/summerland_ornamental_gardens), and a tasting or two on Bottleneck Drive, and it makes an easy half-day or full day if you are already in the South Okanagan.
