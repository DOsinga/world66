The Trout Creek Trestle was built for the Kettle Valley Railway in 1913 and still spans the canyon south of town. It is a striking piece of railway engineering, with the creek far below and open Okanagan hills on both sides.

The steam railway crosses it on scheduled trips, but you can also reach viewpoints by trail. It is best paired with the nearby Ornamental Gardens or the heritage train.
