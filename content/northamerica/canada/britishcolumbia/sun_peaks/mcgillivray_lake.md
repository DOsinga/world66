McGillivray Lake is the low-key counterweight to the lifts. It sits a short distance from the village and is used for canoe, kayak, and paddleboard rentals in summer.

It is not a wilderness epic, but it is a good reset if you have already skied, biked, or hiked hard and want water, trees, and an easier pace.
