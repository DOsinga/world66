The bike park is what keeps Sun Peaks from feeling like a winter-only resort. It has lift-access riding across two mountains, with enough flow trails and technical downhill lines for a full day.

Beginners can start in the progression areas, while stronger riders can use the lifts for repeated descents without turning the day into a grind.
