Sun Peaks Resort is the reason the village exists. The ski area spreads across Tod, Sundance, and Morrisey, with 144 trails, long cruising runs, and enough intermediate terrain to keep mixed-ability groups happy.

It is less dramatic than Whistler, but also easier. The village sits at the base of the lifts, so a ski day can stay simple: walk out, ride, return for lunch, and go back up.
