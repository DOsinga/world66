The summer hiking network gives Sun Peaks its best non-skiing argument. Chairlift access gets you high quickly, from where trails lead through alpine meadows, viewpoints, and routes toward Tod Lake.

The flower season is the prettiest time, but the practical appeal is simple: you can get a mountain walk without a long approach or rough backroad start.
