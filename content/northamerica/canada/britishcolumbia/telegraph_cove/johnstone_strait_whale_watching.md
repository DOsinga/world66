Whale watching is Telegraph Cove's headline trip. Boats leave the harbour for Johnstone Strait, where orcas, humpbacks, Pacific white-sided dolphins, sea lions, and bald eagles are all possible in season.

Book ahead in summer and dress for cold wind even on a warm day. The trip is not a zoo show, but this stretch of water has a strong reputation because the wildlife is genuinely concentrated.
