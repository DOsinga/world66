Bearhole Lake Provincial Park is a quiet wilderness park north of Tumbler Ridge, used for camping, fishing, canoeing, hiking, cross-country skiing, and wildlife viewing. It is a low-key stop for travellers who want lake and forest time away from the main dinosaur and waterfall sights.
