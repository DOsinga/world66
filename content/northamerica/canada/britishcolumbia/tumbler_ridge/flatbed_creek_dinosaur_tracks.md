Flatbed Creek is where Tumbler Ridge's modern dinosaur story began. A short trail from near town leads down to creek-bed track sites, including the Cabin Pool area, where guided tours can point out footprints that are easy to miss if you do not know the shapes.

Treat the site gently. These are real fossil surfaces, not a theme-park display, and water levels or seasonal conditions can affect what is visible.
