Kinuseo Falls is the showpiece waterfall of Monkman Provincial Park and the Tumbler Ridge UNESCO Global Geopark. The Murray River drops roughly 60 metres through a hard-edged northern canyon, with viewpoints reached by a long gravel-road drive south of town.

It is worth the effort, but check road and viewing-platform conditions before you set out. The falls are far enough from town that a casual mistake with fuel, weather, or timing becomes annoying fast.
