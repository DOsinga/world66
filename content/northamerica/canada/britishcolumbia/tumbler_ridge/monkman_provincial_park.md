Monkman Provincial Park is the wild mountain-and-river country south of Tumbler Ridge, best known for Kinuseo Falls on the Murray River. The falls are the headline sight, but the park also has backcountry trails, forest, and a remote northern Rockies feel.

This is not a casual town park. Roads and conditions can matter, so check access before going, especially outside the main summer season. For travellers making the effort to reach Tumbler Ridge, it is one of the area's strongest reasons to come.
