Quality Falls is a short, satisfying hike close to Tumbler Ridge, with a forest trail dropping to a narrow waterfall in a rock-walled creek. It is not as dramatic as Kinuseo Falls, but it is much easier to fit into a spare morning or evening.

The trail can be slippery after rain. Wear shoes with grip and save the sandals for town.
