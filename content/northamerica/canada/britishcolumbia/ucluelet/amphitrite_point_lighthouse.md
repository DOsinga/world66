Amphitrite Point Lighthouse is Ucluelet's classic photo stop: a low, red-and-white light station standing above some very serious Pacific water. The lighthouse itself is not open, but the headland and nearby trail viewpoints are the draw.

Stay off the rocks when the sea is up. The waves here are powerful, unpredictable, and best watched from the trail.
