Ucluelet Aquarium is a small waterfront aquarium focused on local marine life: sea stars, anemones, crabs, prawns, rockfish, and whatever else the season brings. It uses a catch-and-release model, so the displays change and remain tied to the coast outside.

It is especially good with children, but adults who have just walked the tide line will also get more out of the shore after seeing what lives below it.
