The Beaty Biodiversity Museum at the University of British Columbia is best known for Canada's largest blue whale skeleton. Its collections also include plant specimens, fossils, insects, fish, marine invertebrates, and other animal specimens, making it a worthwhile science stop on the UBC campus.
