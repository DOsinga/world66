The Bill Reid Gallery is a compact downtown museum devoted to Northwest Coast art, with a strong permanent collection by the Haida artist Bill Reid. Come for the jewellery, bronze work, carving, and rotating contemporary exhibitions; it is especially useful if you want context before seeing Indigenous art elsewhere in Vancouver.
