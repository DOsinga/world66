The Chinatown Millennium Gate marks the western entrance to Vancouver's Chinatown, with traditional tiled roofs and ornamental arches spanning Pender Street. It is a quick stop, but it sets the tone for a walk through one of the city's most historic immigrant neighbourhoods.
