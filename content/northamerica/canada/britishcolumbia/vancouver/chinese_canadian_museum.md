The Chinese Canadian Museum is in Vancouver's Chinatown and is Canada's first museum dedicated to Chinese-Canadian history. Exhibits cover migration, exclusion, community life, and the long role Chinese Canadians have played in shaping British Columbia and the country as a whole.
