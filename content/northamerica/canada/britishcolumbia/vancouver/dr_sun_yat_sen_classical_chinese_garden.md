This classical Chinese garden is a quiet, carefully composed corner of Chinatown, built by craftspeople from Suzhou using traditional materials and techniques. The pavilions, pond, plants, and curved paths make a small city block feel much larger, and guided tours help explain the symbolism built into the design.
