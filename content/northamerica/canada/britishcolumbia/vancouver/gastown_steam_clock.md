The Gastown Steam Clock is a small Vancouver landmark with outsized tourist pull. Every 15 minutes it whistles and vents steam, drawing cameras to a street corner that might otherwise be passed in a few seconds.

It is more charming than grand, and part of the appeal is the kitsch. Stop by while walking through Gastown rather than making a special trip just for the clock.
