The Jewish Museum and Archives of BC documents Jewish life in Vancouver and across British Columbia through photographs, oral histories, community records, and changing exhibits. It is a small museum, but it gives useful context for a community that shaped parts of the city's business, cultural, and neighbourhood history.

Look for special exhibitions or walking tours, which are often the most rewarding way to visit.
