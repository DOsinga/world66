Kitsilano Beach is Vancouver's classic summer hangout, with sand, volleyball courts, mountain views, and a crowd that makes people-watching part of the appeal. The huge saltwater pool beside the shore is the practical bonus: you can swim laps with the ocean and downtown skyline right next to you.
