The Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery is a small contemporary art gallery on the UBC campus, with changing exhibitions and a strong collection tied to Vancouver's art scene. The concrete "woodpile" outside is a sculpture, and the gallery is an easy add-on if you are already visiting the Museum of Anthropology.
