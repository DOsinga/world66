Nitobe Memorial Garden is a compact Japanese garden on the UBC campus, designed for slow looking rather than quick photos. Paths, water, stone lanterns, bridges, and carefully framed planting create one of Vancouver's calmest small spaces.

It combines naturally with the Museum of Anthropology and the wider Point Grey campus. Come when you have time to slow down; rushing through misses the point.
