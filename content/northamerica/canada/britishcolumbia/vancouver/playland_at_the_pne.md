Playland at the PNE is Vancouver's classic amusement park, with midway games, family rides, seasonal shows, and a wooden roller coaster that has become part of the city's summer memory. It sits in Hastings Park, away from the downtown waterfront sights but easy enough to reach for a half-day outing.

The park is most useful for families, groups, and anyone who wants a lighter break from museums and scenery. Opening dates and ride lineups vary by season, so check ahead before crossing town.
