Prospect Point is the main viewpoint at the north end of Stanley Park, looking across First Narrows to the Lions Gate Bridge and the North Shore mountains. It works well as a pause on a Stanley Park loop, especially if you are cycling or driving the park road. There are basic facilities nearby, but the reason to stop is the bridge-and-harbour view.
