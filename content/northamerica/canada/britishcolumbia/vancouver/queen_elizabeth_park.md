Queen Elizabeth Park turns an old quarry into one of Vancouver's loveliest public gardens. Terraced planting, ponds, lawns, and paths fill the former quarry bowl, while the higher ground gives wide views back toward downtown and the North Shore mountains.

It is an easy place to recommend even on a short Vancouver trip. Go for the city view, stay for the gardens, and give yourself time to wander rather than treating it as a quick photo stop.
