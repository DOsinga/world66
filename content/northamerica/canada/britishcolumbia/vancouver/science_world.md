Science World is Vancouver's hands-on science centre, set in the distinctive geodesic dome built for Expo 86 at the east end of False Creek. The exhibits are built for participation, which makes it one of the city's easiest museum choices with children.

The building itself is part of the draw. It sits by the Main Street-Science World SkyTrain station and is easy to combine with a walk along False Creek.
