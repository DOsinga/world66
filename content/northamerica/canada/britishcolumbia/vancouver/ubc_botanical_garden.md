UBC Botanical Garden is Canada's oldest continuously operating university garden, with more than 8,000 kinds of plants across designed landscapes and coastal forest. The Asian garden, alpine garden, and food garden are highlights, and its UBC location makes it quieter than Vancouver's more central gardens.
