The Vancouver Holocaust Education Centre is a teaching museum focused on the Holocaust, genocide, and anti-racism education. Its small temporary exhibitions are thoughtful rather than showy, with programming aimed at schools as well as the wider public.

Check what is on before making a special trip, as exhibits can change and summer may be used for preparing the next show. When open, it is a serious, worthwhile stop for modern history.
