The Vancouver Maritime Museum covers Pacific Northwest and Canadian maritime history, with ship models, navigation artifacts, and the preserved Arctic patrol vessel St. Roch. Its Kitsilano waterfront setting also makes it easy to combine with Vanier Park and nearby beaches.
