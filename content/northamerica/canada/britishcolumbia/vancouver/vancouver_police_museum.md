The Vancouver Police Museum is a compact, offbeat museum about the city's policing history, unusual confiscated weapons, and some of Vancouver's better-known crimes. It is more niche than the city's headline museums, but the subject matter is unusual and the exhibits give a different angle on local history.

Families may find it useful too, as the museum runs programs and activities aimed at children.
