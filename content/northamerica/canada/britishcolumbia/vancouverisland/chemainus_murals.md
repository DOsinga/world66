title: Chemainus Murals
type: poi

Chemainus reinvented itself around outdoor murals after its mill economy faltered, and the result is one of the island's stranger small-town success stories. The murals turn a quick highway stop into a walk through local history, logging, railways, and theatre posters.

It is not worth crossing the island for alone, but it is a good pause between Victoria and Nanaimo, especially if you like towns that have made an effort to survive creatively.
