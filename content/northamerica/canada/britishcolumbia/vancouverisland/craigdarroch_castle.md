Craigdarroch Castle is a Victorian mansion built for coal baron Robert Dunsmuir, all stained glass, carved wood, and upstairs-downstairs ambition. It is less a castle than a very confident house, but that confidence is revealing.

The visit works best if you are curious about Victoria's moneyed 19th century. The hilltop neighbourhood around it is also pleasant for a walk.
