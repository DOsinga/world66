Elk Falls Provincial Park near Campbell River is best known for its deep canyon, suspension bridge, and powerful waterfall. It is an easy way to add a forest-and-waterfall stop to a north-island drive.

The trails are short enough for a half-day visit, but the gorge feels properly dramatic. Salmon, hydro history, and Campbell River's fishing culture all meet around here.
