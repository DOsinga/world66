type: poi

Horne Lake Caves is the island's best-known caving area, with guided trips into limestone passages, crystal formations, and tight, muddy chambers. It is more active than ornamental: expect helmets, crawling, and cold rock.

Book ahead in summer. The park also works as a rainy-day adventure, which is useful on an island where rain is not a rare event.
