Long Beach is the broad, wind-brushed strand that made Tofino and Ucluelet famous with surfers and storm watchers. Even on grey days it feels grand, with low cloud, headlands, and a hard Pacific horizon.

Swimming is cold and rip currents are serious, so treat it as a walking, surfing, and watching beach rather than a casual dip. In winter, it is magnificent in bad weather from a safe distance.
