Mount Washington is Vancouver Island's main ski hill, rising above the Comox Valley with winter snow and summer access to alpine meadows. It is one of the island's best reminders that this is not just a coastal destination.

In winter, check road and chain conditions. In summer, combine the resort with nearby Strathcona Provincial Park for higher-country hiking.
