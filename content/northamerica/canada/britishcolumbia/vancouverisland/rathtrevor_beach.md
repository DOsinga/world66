Rathtrevor Beach near Parksville is the east coast at its most relaxed: broad sand flats, warmish shallow water at low tide, and big views across the Strait of Georgia. It is a strong family stop and a useful counterpoint to the wilder west coast.

Check the tide before planning your swim. At low tide the sea retreats far across the flats, which is great for wandering but less ideal if you expected deep water.
