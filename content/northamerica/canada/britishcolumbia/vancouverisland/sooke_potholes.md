type: poi

The Sooke Potholes are smooth rock pools carved into the Sooke River, popular for summer swimming and picnics west of Victoria. The water is cold, clear, and very welcome on a hot day.

Stay cautious around current and slippery rock. Outside swimming season, the river canyon still makes an easy nature break from the city.
