title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Vancouver Island is strongest outdoors: surf on the west coast, old-growth forest in the middle, beaches and small harbours on the east, and mountain hikes almost everywhere. The island also has a strong museum and garden tradition around Victoria.

Start with the island's big contrasts: [Butchart Gardens](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vancouverisland/butchart_gardens) and the [Royal BC Museum](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vancouverisland/royal_bc_museum) are polished and accessible, while [Cape Scott Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vancouverisland/cape_scott_provincial_park), the [West Coast Trail](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vancouverisland/west_coast_trail), and [Pacific Rim National Park Reserve](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vancouverisland/pacific_rim_national_park) are wilder commitments.
