The Wild Pacific Trail wraps around Ucluelet's rocky shore with lighthouse views, wave-watching platforms, and short forest sections. It gives you west-coast drama without needing backcountry gear.

The Lighthouse Loop is the easiest piece and works in almost any weather. Bring a waterproof shell, because the trail's best moods often arrive with wind and spray.
