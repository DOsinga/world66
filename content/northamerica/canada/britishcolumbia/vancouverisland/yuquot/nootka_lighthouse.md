Nootka Lighthouse stands above Friendly Cove, a short walk from the dock and cultural centre. The view takes in the entrance to Nootka Sound and the broken Pacific edge that made this coast both powerful and difficult to reach.

Canada has few manned lighthouses left, which gives the place an added pull. Even if you came for history, this is the walk that makes Yuquot's geography plain.
