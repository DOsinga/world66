Yuquot's former church, built in the 1950s and later decommissioned, now serves as a small cultural centre. Inside are replica totems, carvings, and material that help visitors understand the Mowachaht/Muchalaht story behind the site.

It is a modest building, but do not rush it. Yuquot's importance is cultural and historical before it is scenic, and this is the clearest place to start.
