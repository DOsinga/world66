---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Vanderhoof sits on Highway 16, the Yellowhead, roughly 100 km west of [Prince George](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/princegeorge) and the same distance east of Burns Lake. The drive from Prince George takes a little over an hour on a straight, well-maintained two-lane.

VIA Rail's *Jasper–Prince Rupert* train stops in Vanderhoof three times a week in each direction. The station is on the north side of the highway, near the river. The journey is more for the scenery than for efficiency — Prince George to Vanderhoof is about two hours by train, against an hour by car.

Vanderhoof Airport, 4 km north of town, handles general aviation and the airshow but no scheduled passenger flights. The nearest commercial airport is Prince George (YXS).
