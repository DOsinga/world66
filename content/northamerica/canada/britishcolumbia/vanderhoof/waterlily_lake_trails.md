The Waterlily Lake Trails form a 24-kilometre trail network around Homestead and Waterlily lakes east of Vanderhoof. The routes are used for hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding, snowshoeing, and cross-country skiing depending on the season.

The appeal is low-key northern British Columbia lake country: forest, quiet water, and room to move without much infrastructure. Check local trail conditions before setting out, especially in shoulder season.
