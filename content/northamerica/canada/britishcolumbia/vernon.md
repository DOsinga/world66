Vernon sits at the north end of Okanagan Lake in British Columbia's sun-drenched interior, surrounded by three lakes, rolling ranchland, and some of the province's best vineyards. It is less polished than [Kelowna](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/kelowna) to the south, which is part of the appeal — the beaches are quieter, the trails less crowded, and the pace noticeably slower.

In summer, [Kalamalka Lake](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vernon/kalamalka_lake) steals the show: the water shifts between vivid turquoise and deep blue depending on the light, earning it the nickname "Lake of a Thousand Colours." [Silver Star Mountain Resort](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vernon/silver_star_mountain), a short drive east of town, draws skiers in winter and mountain bikers in summer. The [historic downtown](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/vernon/historic_downtown) along 30th Avenue has a growing number of independent restaurants and craft breweries, and the weekly farmers' market is one of the best in the Okanagan.
