Historic O'Keefe Ranch preserves one of the Okanagan Valley's best-known ranching sites, with heritage buildings, agricultural displays, and living-history programming north of Vernon. It shows the valley before orchards, wineries, and lakefront development became the dominant visitor images.

The ranch is strongest for families and travellers interested in settler history, horses, and the working landscape that shaped the North Okanagan. It is outside central Vernon but close enough to combine with Swan Lake, downtown, or a drive toward Armstrong.
