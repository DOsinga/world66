One of Vernon's better sit-down restaurants, The Fig on 30th Avenue serves a Mediterranean-influenced menu with a strong emphasis on local Okanagan ingredients. The seasonal menu rotates with availability — in summer, expect dishes built around stone fruit, fresh vegetables, and regional cheeses. The wine list focuses on BC producers with a good selection from the Okanagan Valley. Booking advisable on weekends.

The room is comfortable and unhurried, the service friendly. It draws a mix of locals and visitors passing through on the wine trail. A solid choice for a proper dinner rather than a quick bite.
