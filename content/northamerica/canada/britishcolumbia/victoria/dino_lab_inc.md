Dino Lab is a small Victoria fossil-preparation studio where the attraction is seeing paleontology as work in progress. Rather than simply looking at finished skeletons behind glass, visitors can learn how fossils are cleaned, stabilised, and interpreted.

It is especially good with curious children or adults who like science explained at close range. Book or check hours ahead, since small specialist attractions often depend on tours and staff availability.
