The Galloping Goose Regional Trail turns an old railway corridor into a 55 km route from downtown Victoria through the West Shore and on toward Sooke. You can sample the harbour end on foot, rent a bike for a longer ride, or use it as a traffic-free way to stitch together urban Victoria, farmland, forest, and coastal scenery.
