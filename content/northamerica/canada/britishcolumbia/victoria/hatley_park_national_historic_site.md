Hatley Park National Historic Site is the Colwood estate built around Hatley Castle, a grand Edwardian mansion now used by Royal Roads University. Its stone exterior, formal gardens, and forested grounds make it one of the more distinctive historic sites in greater Victoria.

Film fans may recognise the castle as the screen version of Professor Xavier's School in the X-Men films, but the gardens are the better reason to come. The grounds are generally open during the day; interior access is limited and usually by seasonal tour.
