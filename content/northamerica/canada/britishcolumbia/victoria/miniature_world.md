Miniature World is a compact Victoria attraction filled with detailed model scenes, tiny cities, railways, fantasy displays, and working miniature machinery. The appeal is old-fashioned but strong, especially for families and anyone who likes models built with patience and humour.

It is very central, near the Inner Harbour and the Empress, so it is easy to fit between larger Victoria sights or use as a rainy-day stop.
