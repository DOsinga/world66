Oak Bay Village is a genteel east-side district of small shops, cafes, galleries, and older houses, with a more residential rhythm than downtown Victoria. It is useful if you want to see the city's British-influenced, garden-suburb side without making a formal museum stop.

Pair it with a walk toward the Oak Bay waterfront or nearby beaches. The village itself is compact, so it works best as part of a relaxed half-day rather than a standalone expedition.
