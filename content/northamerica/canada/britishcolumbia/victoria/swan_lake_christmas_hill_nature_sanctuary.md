Swan Lake Christmas Hill Nature Sanctuary is one of Victoria's best close-in nature breaks, combining marsh, lake, meadow, forest, and a Garry oak hilltop in a compact protected area. Boardwalks and trails make it easy to see birds, turtles, wildflowers, and seasonal wetland life.

It is not wilderness, but that is part of the appeal: you can get a real ecological reset without leaving the city. The nature house is useful for families and for understanding why these small habitats matter on southern Vancouver Island.
