Victoria Bug Zoo is a small, hands-on family attraction near the Inner Harbour, with live insects and other invertebrates shown by enthusiastic guides. It is best for curious children, rainy afternoons, or anyone who wants a compact stop between Victoria's larger museums and harbour sights.

The visit is short, but the close-up encounters and guided explanations make it more memorable than its size suggests.
