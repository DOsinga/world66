Willows Beach Park is a popular Oak Bay beach with shallow water, grass, picnic tables, and views toward Discovery Island and Mount Baker on clear days. It is more relaxed than the Inner Harbour sights and works well for a family break.

The beach is best in good weather, when it can be used as a gentle coastal stop alongside Oak Bay Village or nearby gardens.
