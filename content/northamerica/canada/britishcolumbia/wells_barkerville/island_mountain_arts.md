Island Mountain Arts is the best expression of Wells' second life as an arts town. The organization runs a public gallery, workshops, residencies, an international harp school, and the ArtsWells festival, making the little mining settlement feel more active than its population suggests.

Check what is on before you go. Outside events and exhibitions it is a modest stop, but it explains why Wells is not just Barkerville's service village.
