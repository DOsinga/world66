Whistler is a resort municipality in British Columbia, about 120 kilometres north of Vancouver along the Sea to Sky Highway. It is one of North America's premier ski destinations, with Whistler Mountain and Blackcomb Mountain together forming the continent's largest ski area — over 8,000 acres of runs, accessed by the PEAK 2 PEAK gondola that spans the two summits. Ski season typically runs November to April, with some glacier skiing persisting into summer.

The village is purpose-built but has settled into itself over the decades. Whistler Village and the surrounding developments have good restaurants, bars, and shops. The après-ski scene is well-established. In summer the mountain infrastructure turns to mountain biking, hiking, and zip-lining; the valley has three championship golf courses. June to August brings warm temperatures and long days.

Whistler's food scene punches above its weight for a resort town. [Araxi](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/whistler/araxi) on the Village Stroll is the top table in town, focused on Pacific Northwest produce. [The Edgewater](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/whistler/the_edgewater) offers fine dining with three-sided views of Green Lake from its peninsula. More casual options line the village — everything from quick-service bowls to pizza to pubs packed with skiers shedding their boots.
