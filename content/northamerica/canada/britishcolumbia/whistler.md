*North America > Canada > British Columbia > Whistler*

Whistler is one of the finest mountain resort towns in the world, and it knows it. Tucked into the Coast Mountains of British Columbia, about two hours north of Vancouver on the Sea to Sky Highway, it sits in a narrow valley flanked by Whistler Mountain and Blackcomb Mountain — together forming the largest ski area in North America. In 2010 it co-hosted the Winter Olympics with Vancouver, a moment that confirmed what skiers had been saying for decades.

The village itself was built from scratch in the 1980s, and the planning shows — in a good way. Whistler Village is a compact, car-free zone of restaurants, bars, and shops connected by pedestrian plazas to Whistler Village North and the Upper Village at the base of Blackcomb. It's walkable, lively, and well-maintained, with the gondola terminals sitting right in the centre of things. You can step off the slopes and be in a pub within five minutes.

Skiing and snowboarding dominate from November to April, but Whistler is genuinely four-season. Summers bring mountain biking on the [Whistler Mountain Bike Park](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/whistler/whistler_mountain_bike_park) — one of the top lift-accessed bike parks in the world — along with hiking, golf, kayaking on [Lost Lake](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/whistler/lost_lake), and paddling the [River of Golden Dreams](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/whistler/river_of_golden_dreams). The [Audain Art Museum](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/whistler/audain_art_museum) and the [Squamish Lil'wat Cultural Centre](/northamerica/canada/britishcolumbia/whistler/squamish_lilwat_cultural_centre) give the village some cultural weight beyond après-ski.

Prices are uniformly high. Whistler has always been an expensive destination, and the presence of the Olympic legacy and continued international investment has pushed costs further up. Budget travellers would do better heading to smaller BC mountain towns. But if you want world-class terrain with excellent infrastructure, a lively village, and easy access from Vancouver, Whistler delivers reliably.
