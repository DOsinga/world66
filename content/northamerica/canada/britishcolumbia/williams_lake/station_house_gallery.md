Station House Gallery occupies a restored BC Rail station near the centre of town. Exhibitions change monthly and focus on local, regional, and touring artists, with a shop selling work by Cariboo makers.

It is a modest stop, but a good one if you want Williams Lake to be more than gas, groceries, and the highway.
