Beckoning Hills Museum is Boissevain's local history museum, with material from the town and the surrounding Turtle Mountain district. It is the kind of small prairie museum where the value is in the local details: farm life, settlement stories, railway-era town growth, and community memory.

Opening is seasonal, so check before building a day around it. If you are already stopping for Tommy the Turtle, the museum is an easy add-on.
