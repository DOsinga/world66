---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Brandon is a car city. The distances between attractions are walkable in the downtown core but getting to the Keystone Centre, the college, or the airport requires either a vehicle or a taxi. Brandon Transit operates local bus routes covering most of the city, but schedules are infrequent and not geared to visitors.

Downtown is compact and easy to navigate on foot. Rosser Avenue is the main commercial street. Parking is free or metered and straightforward to find.
