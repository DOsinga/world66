Riverbank Discovery Centre is Brandon's main gateway to the Assiniboine River corridor. The centre,
grounds, gardens, and adjoining trails give visitors a practical way to understand the city's
relationship with the river, floodplain, and prairie-edge landscape.

It is not a high-drama attraction, but it is useful and pleasant: a place to stretch your legs, pick
up local information, and connect to walking routes without leaving town. For road-trippers crossing
western Manitoba, it makes a better break than another highway stop.
