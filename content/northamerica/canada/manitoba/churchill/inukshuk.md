Churchill's best-known inukshuk stands on the shore of Hudson Bay, where the stone figure is framed by open water, big sky, and tundra light. It is a simple landmark, but the setting makes it one of the town's most photographed spots.

Come at sunset or during aurora season if conditions are clear. The view is exposed and windy, so dress for the weather even if you are only stepping out briefly for photos.
