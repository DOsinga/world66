Canada's National Ukrainian Festival is the time when Dauphin most clearly becomes a destination. Held at Selo Ukraina on the northern slopes of Riding Mountain, it brings together dance, music, food, ceremonies, crafts, and a heritage village over the August long weekend.

The setting matters. Selo Ukraina is an outdoor amphitheatre and cultural site rather than a downtown hall, so the festival feels tied to the prairie and the edge of the park. Outside the event dates, check ahead before making the drive.
