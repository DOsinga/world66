Fort Dauphin Museum is a small heritage village rather than a single-room local museum. Its buildings include a church, school, trading post store, blacksmith shop, house, and outdoor machinery displays, all gathered to tell the settlement story of the Dauphin area.

It is best in summer, when the site is open and the outdoor pieces make sense. Pair it with the Ukrainian church and rail museum for a grounded look at how this prairie city grew.
