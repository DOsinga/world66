Bakers Narrows Provincial Park is the easy escape from Flin Flon into the lake country around it. The park sits by Lake Athapapuskow, with canoeing, fishing, camping, and a scenic tower that gives a broad look over the surrounding water and forest.

It is close enough for a half-day trip, but it changes the scale of the visit. After the compact, rock-built town centre, Bakers Narrows shows the larger boreal landscape that makes this part of Manitoba worth the long drive.
