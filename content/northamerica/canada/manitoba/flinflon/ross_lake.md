Ross Lake sits in the middle of Flin Flon, giving the town its best everyday scenery. The shoreline has rocky outcrops, water views, and access to Flinty's Boardwalk, a local walking route with interpretive panels on the area's geology.

This is the place to slow down between the museum and the more practical errands of town. In summer, the lake turns Flin Flon from a mining settlement into a northern water town.
