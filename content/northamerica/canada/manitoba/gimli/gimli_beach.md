Gimli Beach is the reason summer weekends get busy. The sand sits directly beside the harbour and the old town centre, so a swim, a picnic, ice cream, and a walk on the seawall all fit into one easy loop.

Lake Winnipeg is not a small lake pretending to be big. The horizon feels open, the wind can change quickly, and the water is shallow enough near shore to make the beach popular with families.
