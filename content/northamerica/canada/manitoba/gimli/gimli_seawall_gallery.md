The harbour wall in Gimli doubles as an outdoor gallery. Dozens of small murals line the seawall, showing Icelandic settlement, fishing life, lake scenes, and episodes from local history.

It is not grand art, and that is part of the charm. The murals make the harbour walk feel rooted in the town rather than just scenic.
