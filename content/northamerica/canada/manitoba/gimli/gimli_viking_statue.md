The Viking statue is Gimli's easiest photo stop and a blunt statement of the town's Icelandic roots. It stands near the lakefront, close enough to pair with the beach, seawall, and museum.

Come during the Icelandic Festival and the statue feels less like a roadside mascot and more like the town's unofficial master of ceremonies.
