The New Iceland Heritage Museum explains why this small Manitoba town has such a strong Icelandic identity. Exhibits cover the 1870s migration, the harsh first years on Lake Winnipeg, the fishing industry, and the wider natural history of the lake.

It is the right first stop in Gimli. After the museum, the Viking statue, harbour, and Icelandic Festival references around town make much more sense.
