Ship & Plough is Gimli's proper local tavern, a short walk from the harbour. It leans into Manitoba products and regional craft beer, with pub food, live music, comedy, open mics, and quiz nights depending on the week.

For visitors, it is a good place to feel the town after the beach crowd thins out.
