Morden sits in southern Manitoba's Pembina Valley, west of Winkler and close enough to Winnipeg for a long day trip. It is a prairie service centre first, but it has a stronger identity than many towns its size: Cretaceous sea monsters, Mennonite farm country, and a downtown that fills with corn, apple juice, and music each August.

The main reason to come is the [Canadian Fossil Discovery Centre](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/morden/canadian_fossil_discovery_centre), whose collection comes from the old Western Interior Seaway that once covered this part of the continent. Outside town, the [Pembina Threshermen's Museum](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/morden/pembina_threshermens_museum) keeps the machinery and buildings of prairie settlement in one place, while [Lake Minnewasta](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/morden/lake_minnewasta) gives Morden its easiest outdoor escape.

Morden is not a place to race through a checklist. It works best if you are already crossing southern Manitoba and want a town with a clear local story, a proper museum stop, and a few hours outside by the water or in the surrounding fields.
