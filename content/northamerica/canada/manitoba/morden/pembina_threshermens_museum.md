The Pembina Threshermen's Museum sits on Highway 3 between Morden and Winkler. It gathers old farm machines, historic buildings, and prairie settlement objects in the kind of open-air museum that makes most sense in grain country.

The appeal is practical rather than polished: threshers, tractors, schoolhouse memories, and the slow mechanics of how this part of Manitoba was farmed. It is a good pairing with the fossil centre because it brings Morden's story back from deep time to the immigrant farms of the last two centuries.
