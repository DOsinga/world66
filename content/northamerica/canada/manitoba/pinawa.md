Pinawa sits on the Winnipeg River at the edge of the Canadian Shield, about 90 minutes northeast of Winnipeg. It is a small planned town, but it has a stronger identity than its size suggests: river trails, old hydro ruins, a suspension bridge over the Pinawa Channel, and the legacy of Whiteshell Laboratories, the nuclear research site that brought the modern town into being.

The best visit is outdoors. Walk the [Pinawa Suspension Bridge and Channel Heritage Walk](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/pinawa/pinawa_suspension_bridge), swim or paddle from [Pinawa Beach](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/pinawa/pinawa_beach), then drive north to the broken concrete of [Pinawa Dam Provincial Heritage Park](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/pinawa/pinawa_dam_provincial_heritage_park). The scenery is not dramatic in a mountain sense, but the mix of granite, forest, water, and industrial ruins gives the town a memorable feel.

Pinawa works well as a day trip from Winnipeg or as a quiet base for Whiteshell country. It is not a resort town with big-ticket attractions. Its appeal is slower: a trail loop, a picnic by the river, and a bit of Manitoba engineering history in the woods.
