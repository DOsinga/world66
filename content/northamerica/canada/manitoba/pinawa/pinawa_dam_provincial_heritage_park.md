Pinawa Dam Provincial Heritage Park is the old Pinawa Generating Station site, now a set of concrete ruins beside the Winnipeg River. The station began operating in 1906 and helped open the river to large-scale hydroelectric development before it was shut down in 1951.

The ruin has real atmosphere. Broken spillways, stonework, and quiet pools sit among trees and picnic areas, so it feels half industrial archaeology and half riverside park. It is about eight kilometres north of Pinawa and is the best single stop in the area.
