The Pinawa Heritage Sundial is a five-metre-tall landmark near the marina and riverfront. It is not worth a special trip by itself, but it adds a bit of odd local character to a walk around town.

Stop here while moving between the beach, marina, and riverside paths. On a sunny day it does exactly what it promises, which is part of the charm.
