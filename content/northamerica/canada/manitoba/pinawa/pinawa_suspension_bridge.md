Pinawa's suspension bridge is a simple but satisfying crossing over the Pinawa Channel. It is 54 metres long and only a metre wide, with water, forest, and granite close on both sides.

Use it as part of a longer walk rather than just a photo stop. The Channel Heritage Walk connects from the bridge into a loop of interpretive trails, fishing spots, and cross-country ski routes in winter.
