Selkirk sits on the Red River just northeast of Winnipeg. It is close enough to the city for an easy day trip, but it has its own odd, useful identity: lake ships on land, catfish pride, a lift bridge, and major Red River history nearby.

The [Marine Museum of Manitoba](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/selkirk/marine_museum_of_manitoba) is the town's best reason to stop, with historic vessels that explain Selkirk's place between the Red River and Lake Winnipeg. South of town, [Lower Fort Garry](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/selkirk/lower_fort_garry) is one of Manitoba's strongest historic sites.

Add [Oak Hammock Marsh](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/selkirk/oak_hammock_marsh) if birds and wetlands interest you. Selkirk is not a polished destination, but it makes a good, varied day outside Winnipeg.
