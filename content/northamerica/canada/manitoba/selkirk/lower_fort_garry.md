Lower Fort Garry is the strongest historic site near Selkirk. The stone Hudson's Bay Company fort preserves a fur-trade outpost on the Red River, with restored buildings and costumed interpretation when the site is fully open.

It is the stop that lifts Selkirk from a quick roadside town into a proper day trip. Pair it with the Marine Museum for a clear river-and-trade thread.
