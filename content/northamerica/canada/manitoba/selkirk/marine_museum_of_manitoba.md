The Marine Museum of Manitoba is Selkirk's headline attraction. Its historic vessels sit at the edge of Selkirk Park, turning the story of Lake Winnipeg and Red River transport into something you can walk through rather than just read about.

The museum works best in summer, when boarding the ships gives the place its hands-on character. It is a niche stop, but a good one.
