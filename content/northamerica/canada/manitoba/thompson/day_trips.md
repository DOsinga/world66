For most travellers passing through Thompson, the destination is [Churchill](/northamerica/canada/manitoba/churchill), the polar-bear-and-beluga town on Hudson Bay reached only by air or by the Via Rail train that runs from Thompson north. Thompson is the closest road-accessible town to Churchill — a useful overnight either side of the train.
