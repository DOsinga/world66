A small log-built museum on Princeton Drive covering northern Manitoba — Cree and Dene history, the fur trade, the founding of Thompson as a company town by Inco in the 1950s, and the natural history of the boreal forest and tundra. Displays include trapping equipment, mining tools, and taxidermy of the region's signature species (lynx, wolverine, wolf).

It's the most useful single hour you can spend in Thompson if you want context for everything around the town. Open year-round; a small admission fee.
