---

A 13-metre cascade on the Grass River about 75 km south of Thompson on Highway 6, accessible by a short boardwalk from the parking lot. A small provincial park surrounds the falls with picnic tables and an interpretive trail.

A longer 22-km backcountry trail leads onward to Kwasitchewan Falls, the tallest in Manitoba; the round trip is a serious two- to three-day hike with bushcraft conditions. For a day visit, the Pisew boardwalk and short loop are enough.
