---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Winnipeg's bar scene is centred on **Osborne Village**, **Corydon Avenue**, and **the Exchange District**. The Exchange has become the city's creative nightlife neighbourhood, with breweries, cocktail bars, and music venues in restored heritage buildings. Several bars have live music on weekends.

The **craft beer scene** has grown significantly: Half Pints Brewing, Torque Brewing, and Little Brown Jug are local favourites. Most have taprooms open to the public.

Coffee culture is strong. **Parlour Coffee** (multiple locations, including a beautiful Exchange District spot) is the city's standout specialty roaster. **Forth** in the Exchange is a good all-day café with local food.

In summer, rooftop and patio culture takes over — the short season means Winnipeggers make the most of every warm evening.
