A 2,305-seat performing arts centre opened in 1968 as part of the Centennial Centre complex, which also includes the Manitoba Museum and the planetarium. Home to the **Winnipeg Symphony Orchestra** and a regular venue for the **Royal Winnipeg Ballet**, one of the oldest professional ballet companies in North America. Check the season schedule — performances run September through May.
