The Fire Fighters Museum of Winnipeg preserves the city's firefighting history in a former fire hall, with engines, uniforms, alarms, photographs, and equipment. The building and collection together explain how urban safety systems developed as Winnipeg grew.

It is a compact specialty museum, best for families, fire-service enthusiasts, or visitors exploring Exchange District heritage. Its appeal is strongest when seen as part of Winnipeg's broader civic-history landscape.
