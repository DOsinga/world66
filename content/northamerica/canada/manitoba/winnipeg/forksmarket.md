The indoor market hall at The Forks, housed in converted railway maintenance buildings from the 1880s. Dozens of stalls sell everything from fresh produce and perogies to Filipino street food and local charcuterie. A BeaverTail stand is a fixture near the river entrance. Several sit-down restaurants occupy the upper level. Busy year-round; the best casual lunch spot in central Winnipeg.
