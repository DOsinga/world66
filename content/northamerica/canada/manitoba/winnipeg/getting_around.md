---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

**On foot:** Downtown Winnipeg is compact and walkable in summer. The Forks, Exchange District, Centennial Centre, and The Bay are all within a 20-minute walk of each other. The **Winnipeg Walkway** (skyway system) connects many downtown buildings at second-floor level — useful in winter.

**By transit:** Winnipeg Transit operates buses across the city. The service is comprehensive but slow — routes radiate outward from downtown, and cross-town trips often require a transfer. The fare is a flat rate; day passes are available. The rapid transit route runs from downtown south along Pembina Highway toward the University of Manitoba.

**By bike:** Winnipeg has a growing network of protected bike lanes downtown and along the riverbanks. The **Forks to Osborne Village** route along the Red River is scenic. Bikes can be rented from several outfitters near The Forks in summer.

**By car:** Easy outside of rush hour. Parking is cheap and plentiful by Canadian city standards. Portage Avenue and Main Street are the two main downtown arteries; the intersection of the two is famously the windiest corner in Canada.

**By taxi/rideshare:** Cabs are available but rideshare (Uber, Lyft) is the default for most visitors.
