Winnipeg's Graffiti Gallery grew from street-art culture into a community arts space,
giving aerosol art, youth creativity, and urban visual culture a dedicated platform. It
is a different kind of gallery from the city's larger institutions, with more emphasis
on local voices and public art energy.

Check current exhibitions and hours before visiting, since programming matters here. It
is best for travellers interested in contemporary community arts, murals, and the
creative side of Winnipeg's inner-city neighbourhoods.
