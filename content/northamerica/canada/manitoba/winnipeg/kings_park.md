King's Park is a spacious south Winnipeg park beside the Red River, with walking paths, open lawns, prairie-style plantings, and a formal garden marked by a Chinese-style pagoda. It is calmer than the better-known Assiniboine Park and more local in feel.

The park is useful if you are staying near the University of Manitoba or exploring beyond central Winnipeg. Come for a walk, a picnic, or autumn colour; in a city of long winters, its green-season quiet is part of the appeal.
