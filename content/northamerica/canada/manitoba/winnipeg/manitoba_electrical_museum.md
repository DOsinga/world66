The Manitoba Electrical Museum is a compact, kid-friendly museum on the history of power in the province. Displays include an electric streetcar, a robot made from household electronics, and examples of consumer technology from different eras.

It is not a grand museum, but it is a good rainy-day stop for families or anyone curious about how electricity changed ordinary life in Winnipeg.
