Winnipeg's most eclectic neighbourhood, a ten-minute walk south of The Forks across the Osborne Bridge. The main strip along Osborne Street is dense with independent cafes, restaurants, bookshops, and bars in a mix of old houses and low-rise storefronts. Thai, Greek, Japanese, and North African restaurants sit alongside Ukrainian delis and vegan bakeries. The residential streets behind have fine examples of pre-war Winnipeg housing. Lively on weekends; the place Winnipeggers go when they don't feel like driving.
