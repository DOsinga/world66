Riel House is a small National Historic Site in south Winnipeg, but it carries more weight than its size suggests. The house interprets the family life of Louis Riel and the Metis community around the Red River, with domestic rooms used to tell a political and cultural story.

It is best visited with some interest in Canadian history, since the power here is in context rather than architecture. The stop helps connect Winnipeg to the conflicts, languages, and mixed communities that shaped Manitoba's entry into Confederation.
