The Royal Winnipeg Ballet is one of Canada's major performing-arts institutions and a distinctive part of the city's cultural identity. For visitors, the company matters most when a performance overlaps with a trip, turning an evening in Winnipeg into something more memorable than another restaurant or bar stop.

Check the schedule before planning around it, as the experience depends on the season and venue. When performances are available, the ballet pairs naturally with downtown cultural sights such as the Exchange District, theatres, and the Winnipeg Art Gallery.
