Springhill Winter Sports Park is not mountain skiing, and that is exactly the point: it is Winnipeg's accessible local hill for short ski, snowboard, and tubing sessions when the prairie winter is in full force. It works best for beginners, families, and anyone who wants snow play without a long drive.

Check operating dates and conditions before going, because the season depends on weather and snowmaking. For travellers in winter, it is a useful reminder that Winnipeg does not hibernate; it puts on layers and goes outside.
