Tinkertown Family Fun Park is a classic small amusement park on Winnipeg's eastern edge, aimed squarely at families with younger children. Rides, mini-golf, games, and summer snacks make it a light-hearted break from museums and historic sites.

It is not a reason to visit Winnipeg on its own, but it earns its place for family travel. Check the season and hours before going; the value is highest on warm days when children need space, movement, and a few uncomplicated thrills.
