The Uptown Theatre is a historic Winnipeg cinema building remembered for its role in the city's twentieth-century entertainment landscape. Even where the use has changed, the building remains part of the Osborne Village streetscape and local heritage record.

It is a quick architecture-and-history stop rather than a major attraction. Visitors interested in Winnipeg's old theatres can pair it with better-known performance venues downtown.
