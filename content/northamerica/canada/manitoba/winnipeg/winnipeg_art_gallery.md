The Winnipeg Art Gallery is one of the city's essential cultural institutions. Its collections range across Canadian and international art, but the museum is especially important for Indigenous and Inuit work, with Qaumajuq adding a major dedicated centre for Inuit art to the complex.

The building sits close to the Manitoba Legislature and downtown hotels, making it easy to work into a city walk. For visitors, the gallery is more than a rainy-day option: it is one of the best places in Winnipeg to understand the artistic conversations between the Prairies, the North, and the wider country.
