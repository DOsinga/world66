Cape Enrage is a dramatic headland on the outer shore of Albert County, with a lighthouse perched at the edge of cliffs above the bay. The cape got its name from the turbulent currents that swirl around the point as tidal water moves in and out of the bay — the surf and standing waves visible from the headland make the name apt. The lighthouse dates from 1838 and is now an adventure centre.

Rock climbing and rappelling operations use the vertical red sandstone cliffs below the lighthouse. The views from the cape over the Chignecto Channel and across to Nova Scotia are some of the finest on the New Brunswick shore of the Fundy.
