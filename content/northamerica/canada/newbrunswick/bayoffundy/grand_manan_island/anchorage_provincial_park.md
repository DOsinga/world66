Anchorage Provincial Park protects a quieter stretch of Grand Manan's southeast coast, with trails through woods, ponds, dunes, bog edges, and beach. It is one of the best places on the island for birding, tide watching, and an easy walk that still feels exposed to the Bay of Fundy. The park trails are short, so you can combine several in a single visit.
