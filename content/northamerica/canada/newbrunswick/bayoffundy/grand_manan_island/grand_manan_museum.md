Grand Manan Museum is the island's memory box. Its collections cover local geology, ferry and lighthouse history, shipwrecks, fishing, and the bird life that has long drawn naturalists to the archipelago. It is not flashy, but it gives useful depth to the island before or after you have seen the cliffs and harbours.
