Hole in the Wall is a natural rock arch on the coast near North Head. The walk is short, but the reward is pure Grand Manan: dark rock, surf, spruce, and a sea-carved opening that looks different with every tide and patch of fog. Pair it with Swallowtail Lighthouse if you are staying near the ferry end of the island.
