The Grand Manan Whale and Seabird Research Station is a small, useful stop near the ferry terminal. Its Gaskin Museum of Marine Life explains the whales, porpoises, seabirds, and other species that make the lower Bay of Fundy so rich. Go before a boat trip or cliff walk, and the water around the island becomes easier to read.
