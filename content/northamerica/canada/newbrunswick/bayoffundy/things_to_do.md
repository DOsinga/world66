The Bay of Fundy's defining experience is tidal, and everything should be timed around the tides. [Hopewell Rocks](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/bayoffundy/hopewell_rocks) is the iconic stop — walk the ocean floor at low tide, kayak around the sea stacks at high. [Fundy National Park](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/bayoffundy/fundy_national_park) offers coastal walks, waterfalls, and tidal pool exploration on the inner shore.

Wildlife watching is exceptional: [Mary's Point](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/bayoffundy/marys_point) for shorebird spectacle in July and August; [whale watching](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/bayoffundy/whale_watching) tours from Saint Andrews and Grand Manan for right whales, humpbacks, and finbacks.

[Saint Andrews](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/bayoffundy/saint_andrews) is the most historically interesting town on the shore, with a walkable Loyalist grid and access to the extraordinary tidal island at [Ministers Island](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/bayoffundy/ministers_island). Check the tide tables — Ministers Island is inaccessible at high water.
