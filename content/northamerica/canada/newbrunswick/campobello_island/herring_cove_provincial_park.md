Herring Cove Provincial Park covers forest, bog, cliffs, and a long beach on Campobello Island. Its trails include old carriage and logging routes, with chances to see shore life, bald eagles, osprey, and beavers.

The park is a good outdoor stop for visitors already on Campobello, especially as a contrast to the Roosevelt historic sites and the island's small villages.
