Roosevelt Campobello International Park preserves Franklin D. Roosevelt's summer home on Campobello Island. The 34-room cottage is kept much as the Roosevelt family used it, and the grounds include walking trails through spruce forest and along the Bay of Fundy shoreline.

The park is jointly administered by Canada and the United States, matching the island's unusual geography: Canadian land reached by bridge from Lubec, Maine.
