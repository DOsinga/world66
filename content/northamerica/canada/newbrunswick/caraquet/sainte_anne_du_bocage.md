Sainte-Anne-du-Bocage is a small Acadian pilgrimage site on the edge of Caraquet, with a chapel, Stations of the Cross, and paths through a wooded bluff above the bay. It is not grand, but it gives the town a sense of depth: a quiet religious landscape in a place better known for festival noise and seafood.

Come for a short walk and the view over the water. The site is especially atmospheric when the town is busy, because the contrast is so sharp.
