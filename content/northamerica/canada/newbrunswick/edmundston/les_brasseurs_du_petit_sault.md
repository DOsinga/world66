Les Brasseurs du Petit-Sault is the easiest place in Edmundston to feel the local identity without visiting another interpretive panel. The brewery's name and branding lean into Petit-Sault, Brayon culture, and the Republic of Madawaska story.

It is a good evening stop after the botanical garden or riverfront walk, and a useful reminder that small border cities can have a personality of their own.
