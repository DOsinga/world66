Madawaska Historical Museum covers the history and culture of the Madawaska region through rotating heritage exhibitions, a permanent antique collection, and the Colline Art Gallery.

It is a small museum, but a useful stop for understanding Edmundston's Acadian, borderland, and regional identity before heading to the riverfront or botanical garden.
