---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Fredericton Airport (YFC) is a small regional airport about 15 minutes southeast of the city, with direct flights to Toronto, Montreal, and Ottawa. Car rental is available at the airport. There is no bus service connecting the airport to downtown, so you will need a taxi or rental car.

By road, Fredericton sits on the Trans-Canada Highway, making it easily reachable from Moncton (about 180 km east) and from the Quebec border to the west. Maritime Bus provides coach service connecting Fredericton to Moncton, Saint John, and Halifax with several departures daily.
