King's Landing is an open-air history village west of Fredericton, with restored
buildings, interpreters, farm animals, workshops, and domestic scenes from nineteenth-
century New Brunswick. It is carefully staged, but at its best it feels active rather
than static.

The village is a strong family stop and also useful for adults who want to understand
Loyalist and rural settler life in the province. Allow more time than you think; the
appeal is in conversations, demonstrations, and small details rather than a single
centerpiece.
