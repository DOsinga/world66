The New Brunswick Sports Hall of Fame is a niche stop, but it earns its place in Fredericton because it sits right in the Historic Garrison District and tells a provincial story you will not get from the larger museums. The collection covers athletes, teams, coaches, and builders from across New Brunswick, with portraits, memorabilia, and interactive exhibits rather than just cases of trophies.

It works best as a short indoor visit while you are already downtown. The Clark Building is on Queen Street near City Hall, the Beaverbrook Art Gallery, and the riverfront, so it is easy to fold into a walk through the centre. Sports fans will get the most from it, but families may appreciate the hands-on exhibits and modest scale.
