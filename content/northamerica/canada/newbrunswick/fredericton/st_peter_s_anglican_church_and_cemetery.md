St Peter's Anglican Church and Cemetery is one of Fredericton's quieter historic stops, set away from the downtown cathedral circuit on the north side of the Saint John River. The wooden church and old burial ground speak to the Loyalist and Anglican roots of the city without the ceremonial weight of Christ Church Cathedral.

Come for a short, reflective wander rather than a major monument. The cemetery gives the place much of its atmosphere, with weathered stones, mature trees, and a sense of the older settlement pattern that existed before modern Fredericton spread across both banks of the river.
