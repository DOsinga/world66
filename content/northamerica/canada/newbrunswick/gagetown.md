Gagetown is a small village on the Saint John River between [Fredericton](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/fredericton) and [Saint John](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/saintjohn). It is easily confused with the nearby military base at Oromocto, but the village itself is quieter and older: riverfront lanes, Loyalist-era stories, and a cluster of heritage museums.

The town's strongest claim is Sir Samuel Leonard Tilley, a Father of Canadian Confederation, whose family home is now the [Tilley House Museum](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/gagetown/tilley_house_museum). Nearby, the [Queens County Court House Museum](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/gagetown/queens_county_court_house_museum) and [Loomcrofters Studio](/northamerica/canada/newbrunswick/gagetown/loomcrofters_studio) turn Gagetown into a compact heritage stop rather than just a pretty river village.

This is not a place to rush through with a long checklist. It works best as a slow half-day from Fredericton or Saint John: visit the museums, walk Front Street, look at the river, and let the village's low-key arts-and-history mood do the work.
