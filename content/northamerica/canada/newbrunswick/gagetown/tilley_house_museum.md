Tilley House Museum preserves the birthplace of Sir Samuel Leonard Tilley, one of the Fathers of Canadian Confederation. The house is also a Queens County history museum, with Loyalist and Victorian furniture, local art, Indigenous artifacts, agricultural tools, and period rooms. It is the village's most important stop.
