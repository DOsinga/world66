The Grand Falls Farmers Market fills a red-brick building across from the gorge on Saturday mornings from spring into autumn. It is a good place for breakfast, baked goods, preserves, crafts, and a sense of the potato-country farming belt around town.

The building stands on an old industrial site, so it also gives the riverfront walk a little more local context than the viewpoints alone.
