Zip Zag runs a pair of ziplines across the Saint John River gorge, close enough to the falls that you hear the water below you. It is short, seasonal, and very much a tourist activity, but the setting makes it more memorable than a standard zipline course.

Book ahead in peak summer and check opening dates outside July and August.
