St. Michael's Basilica stands on a hill in the former town of Chatham and is the most dramatic building in the Miramichi Valley. The neo-Gothic church dominates the old town from a distance.

Until Newfoundland joined Confederation in 1949, it was the largest church in Canada east of Quebec City. Even a short look gives Miramichi's scattered layout a clear landmark.
