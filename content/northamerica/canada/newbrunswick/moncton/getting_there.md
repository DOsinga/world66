---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

**By air**, the Greater Moncton Roméo LeBlanc International Airport (YQM) is a short drive from downtown, with daily flights to Toronto, Montreal, Halifax, and Ottawa.

**By car**, the Trans-Canada Highway (Route 2) passes through Moncton, putting the city about three hours from Halifax and 2.5 hours from Charlottetown via the Confederation Bridge.

**By train**, VIA Rail's Ocean stops at Moncton three times a week on its overnight Montreal–Halifax route. The station is on the south edge of downtown.

**By bus**, Maritime Bus runs regular routes connecting Moncton to Halifax, Charlottetown, Saint John, and points in between.
