Magnetic Hill Winery makes fruit wines from New Brunswick berries, rhubarb, apples, pears, maple, and local grapes. The setting is part of the appeal, with a hilltop view back toward Moncton and the Petitcodiac River.

It fits best as a short stop while visiting Magnetic Hill, especially if you want a local bottle rather than another roadside attraction.
