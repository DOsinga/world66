Magnetic Hill Zoo is Moncton's main family wildlife attraction, with hundreds of animals across native and exotic species. The grounds cover a broad park-like site near Magnetic Hill, so it fits naturally with the area's other family stops.

Plan for a relaxed half-day rather than a quick look. Opening hours vary by season, and some outdoor areas are less appealing in poor weather.
