The Moncton Acadian Museum is a key place to understand the Acadian story in southeastern New Brunswick. Its exhibitions cover identity, displacement, language, and cultural survival in a city where French and English Canada meet daily.

It is especially useful if your trip includes coastal Acadian communities or the wider Maritimes. Visit before heading out to smaller towns, and Moncton's bilingual character will make more sense.
