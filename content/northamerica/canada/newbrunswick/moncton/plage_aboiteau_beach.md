Plage Aboiteau Beach is a sandy beach on the Acadian coast near Cap-Pele, within easy day-trip range of Moncton. Like nearby Parlee, it benefits from the warmer waters of the Northumberland Strait, but it often feels a little less central to the Shediac crowds.

It is a summer beach stop first and foremost: swimming, walking, and lingering over the dune-and-strait views. Check seasonal services before going, especially outside the main holiday months.
