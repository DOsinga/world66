---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tantramar's best stops are spread between Sackville, Dorchester, and Aulac. The natural starting point is Sackville Waterfowl Park, then the Owens Art Gallery on the Mount Allison campus. If you have a car, continue to Dorchester's small museums and Fort Beausejour on the open ground near the Nova Scotia line.
