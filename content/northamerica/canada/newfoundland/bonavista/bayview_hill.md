Bayview Hill is one of Bonavista's best short viewpoints, with a 360-degree look over the town, harbour, coast, nearby hills, and Cape Bonavista Lighthouse. The top is reached by a brief scramble from a gravel road near the weather station.

It is a quick stop in clear weather and a useful way to understand how the town sits between open sea and sheltered water.
