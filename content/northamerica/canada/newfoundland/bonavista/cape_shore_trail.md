Cape Shore Trail is a short coastal walk from the edge of Bonavista toward the lighthouse, running about 3.5 km one way along open shoreline. Benches along the route make it easy to slow down and watch the sea, and the walk is a good way to turn the lighthouse visit into more than a quick drive-by.

Bring a wind layer even in good weather. This coast can feel exposed, which is part of the appeal.
