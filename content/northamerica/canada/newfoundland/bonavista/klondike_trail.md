Klondike Trail is a coastal walk between Elliston and Spillar's Cove on the Bonavista Peninsula. The 3.5-km route crosses low hills and open coastal ground, with broad views near Spillar's Cove.

It is a good short hike if you want a quieter landscape stop near Bonavista. Bring wind protection, as the exposed sections can feel raw even on clear days.
