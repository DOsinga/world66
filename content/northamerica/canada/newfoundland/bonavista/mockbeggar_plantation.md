Mockbeggar Plantation is a restored family home and historic site overlooking the water. It is tied to F. Gordon Bradley, Newfoundland's first Canadian senator, and the rooms are set up to show the house around the 1940s. The library and chapel are the memorable spaces, but the broader value is the glimpse into outport life when fishing, politics, church, and trade were all tightly bound together.
