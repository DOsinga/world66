Port Union is a rare planned town built around a fishermen's union, and it still feels different from the older outports along the Bonavista Peninsula. The district brings together the town museum, the old factory, and William Coaker's bungalow, making it a good stop if you want the labour and fishing history behind Newfoundland's coastal scenery.
