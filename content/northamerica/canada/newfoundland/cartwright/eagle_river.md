Eagle River is known for Atlantic salmon and fly fishing, with lodges and camps reached by boat or floatplane rather than road. It is the kind of Labrador side trip where logistics are part of the experience.

For most travellers it will be something to arrange through an outfitter, not an independent afternoon outing.
