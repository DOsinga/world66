---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Most visitors to Channel-Port aux Basques are in transit — catching the Marine Atlantic ferry to or from North Sydney, Nova Scotia. But the town rewards a few hours' exploration. The [Gulf Museum](gulf_museum) has a 17th-century Basque astrolabe and the story of the Basque whalers who named this harbour. The [Railway Heritage Centre](railway_heritage_centre) covers the Newfoundland narrow-gauge railway that was axed in 1988. At the [SS Caribou Memorial](ss_caribou_memorial), the town remembers the wartime tragedy that killed 137 people on the overnight crossing. [Cape Ray Beach](cape_ray_beach) has piping plovers, coastal scenery, and the ruins of a Marconi wireless station.
