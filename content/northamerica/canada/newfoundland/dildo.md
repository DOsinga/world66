Dildo is a small outport on Trinity Bay, about an hour west of [St. John's](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/stjohns), and it knows exactly why many travellers first stop the car. The name gets the laugh, but the town has done something useful with the attention: it has turned a pretty harbour, fishing history, and a proud local joke into a real day-trip destination.

The waterfront is the point. Boat tours head into Trinity Bay and around Dildo Island, mixing outport history with chances for whales, seabirds, icebergs in season, and cod-fishing stories. Dildo Brewing Company and Museum gives the town a lively centre, with beer, music, harbour views, and a small outport-history angle rather than just a souvenir stop.

Dildo is not trying to compete with Newfoundland's grand landscapes. Its charm is smaller and more social: painted houses, working-water views, a name locals have chosen to own, and enough sea air to make the detour from St. John's feel worthwhile.
