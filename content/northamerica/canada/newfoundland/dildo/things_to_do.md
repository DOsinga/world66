---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Dildo is best as a half-day or day trip from St. John's. Take a boat tour if the weather is kind; the Dildo Cove trips lean into the old make-and-break motor, fishing premises, Dildo Island, and Trinity Bay wildlife. Back on shore, Dildo Brewing Company and Museum is the town's natural gathering place, with harbour views and local music on busy days. Leave time simply to wander the waterfront, because the name is only half the story.
