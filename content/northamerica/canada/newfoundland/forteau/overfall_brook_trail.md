Overfall Brook Trail is Forteau's easy leg-stretcher, a short walk from the village along a brook toward small falls. It is not a wilderness expedition, but it gives a good sample of the Labrador Straits landscape: spruce, coastal air, running water, and open views when the trail breaks out.
