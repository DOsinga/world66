Fortune Head Park Reserve protects cliffs where rocks record the boundary between the Precambrian and Cambrian periods, about 540 million years ago. Fossils and exposed strata make the headland a serious stop for anyone interested in geology, not just a scenic coastal walk.

The nearby geological centre arranges guided tours, which are worth taking if you want the rocks explained rather than simply photographed. Bring wind protection, as the headland is exposed.
