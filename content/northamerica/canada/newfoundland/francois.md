Francois is a tiny south-coast Newfoundland outport at the head of a narrow fjord, cut off from the road system and reached by coastal ferry from Burgeo via Grey River. The name is usually said "Franceway", and the place feels as far from the island's highways as that pronunciation suggests.

This is not a casual stop. There are no car roads, only paths and local utility vehicles, and weather can make the ferry feel like the real boss of the itinerary. But the reward is rare: a working roadless village, steep hills rising straight behind the houses, and salt water pressed close on every side.

The best days here are simple and physical. Walk the harbour, climb toward the local lookouts, or arrange a boat or hiking trip toward [Devil Bay](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/francois/devil_bay) and [Chaleur Bay](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/francois/chaleur_bay). Francois is only worth the effort if you like remote places. If you do, it is one of Newfoundland's more memorable edges.
