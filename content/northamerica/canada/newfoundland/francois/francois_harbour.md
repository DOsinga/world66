Francois Harbour is the reason the village exists and the first thing you understand when the ferry arrives. Houses, wharves, boats, and footpaths sit tight against the water, with steep ground rising so quickly that the settlement seems folded into the fjord.

Walk it slowly. The pleasure is not a single viewpoint but the way daily life works without cars: freight, groceries, conversations, and weather all moving through the same small waterfront.
