Gander's airport terminal is more than a place to catch a flight. Its international lounge preserves the confidence of the jet-age North Atlantic, with modernist design, original furnishings, terrazzo, and a large Kenneth Lochhead mural.

Access can depend on airport operations, so check locally rather than assuming you can wander everywhere. Even a brief look helps connect the museum story to the actual building that once handled the world between continents.
