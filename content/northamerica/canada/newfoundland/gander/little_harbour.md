Little Harbour gives Gander a simple outdoor stop away from the highway and airport. The recreation area sits on Gander Lake, with a marina, picnic ground, boat launch, and freshwater views.

It is not a major attraction, but it rounds out a day in town nicely after the aviation sights. Bring food, watch the weather, and treat it as a low-key lake break.
