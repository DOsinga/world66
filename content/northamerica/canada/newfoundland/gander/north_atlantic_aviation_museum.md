The North Atlantic Aviation Museum is Gander's essential stop. It explains why this inland Newfoundland town mattered to the world, from wartime ferry routes and early transatlantic flying to the diverted aircraft of September 11, 2001.

The museum is compact, but the subject is unusually strong. If you only do one thing in Gander, do this before driving on.
