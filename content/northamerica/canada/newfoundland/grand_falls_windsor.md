*North America > Canada > Newfoundland > Grand Falls-Windsor*

Grand Falls-Windsor is the main town of central Newfoundland, built around the Exploits River and the paper-mill history that shaped the valley. It is not as dramatic as the island's national parks or old outports, but it has enough substance to reward a traveller crossing Newfoundland with a free day.

The river is the reason to stop. At the [Salmonid Interpretation Centre](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/grand_falls_windsor/salmonid_interpretation_centre), you can watch Atlantic salmon through underwater viewing windows as they move upstream near the falls. [Gorge Park](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/grand_falls_windsor/gorge_park) gives a simpler walk along the Exploits, while local outfitters run [rafting trips](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/grand_falls_windsor/rafting_newfoundland) when conditions suit.

The town's history adds another layer. The [Mary March Provincial Museum](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/grand_falls_windsor/mary_march_provincial_museum) covers the Beothuk, geology, rail, and industrial stories of the region, and [Harmsworth Hall](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/grand_falls_windsor/harmsworth_hall) is a reminder of the Anglo-Newfoundland Development Company that planned Grand Falls as a mill town. Time a visit for the Exploits Valley Salmon Festival in mid-July and the town is livelier than its highway-stop reputation suggests.
