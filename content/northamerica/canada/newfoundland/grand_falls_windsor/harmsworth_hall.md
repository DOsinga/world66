Harmsworth Hall was built in 1929 by the Anglo-Newfoundland Development Company and now houses town offices. Its brick exterior, arched windows, and peaked centre roof make it one of the clearer architectural reminders of Grand Falls-Windsor's planned mill-town origins.

It is a quick look rather than a destination, but it helps connect the modern town to the paper company that created Grand Falls.
