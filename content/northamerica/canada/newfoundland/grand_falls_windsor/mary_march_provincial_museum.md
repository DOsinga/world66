Mary March Provincial Museum is the main museum for the central Newfoundland region. Its exhibits cover the Beothuk people, local geology, regional industry, and natural history.

Look for the Newfoundland Railway locomotive outside. It is a useful reminder of how the island was tied together before the railway closed in 1988.
