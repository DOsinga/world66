The Salmonid Interpretation Centre is the best single reason to stop in Grand Falls-Windsor. Exhibits explain Atlantic salmon biology and habitat, while underwater windows let visitors watch fish moving upstream toward spawning grounds.

The setting matters too: the centre looks toward the falls and the Exploits River, with nesting gulls and river views adding life to the visit.
