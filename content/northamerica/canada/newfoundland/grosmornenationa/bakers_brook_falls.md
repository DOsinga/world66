Baker's Brook Falls is a moderate forest-and-boardwalk hike to a wide cascade north of Rocky Harbour. The trail crosses bog, tuckamore, and boreal forest before the falls appear.

It is less dramatic than Western Brook Pond but much easier to fit into a flexible day. Expect wet footing after rain.
