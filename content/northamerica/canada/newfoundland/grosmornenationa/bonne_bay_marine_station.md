Bonne Bay Marine Station in Norris Point is a small but worthwhile marine science centre with touch tanks and local interpretation. It is especially good for families or for anyone who wants names for the life under the cold water.

Pair it with a walk around Norris Point or a boat trip on Bonne Bay. The setting makes the science feel immediate rather than abstract.
