type: poi

Lobster Cove Head Lighthouse watches over the entrance to Bonne Bay, with short trails, coastal views, and exhibits on local life along the shore. It is an easy, atmospheric stop near Rocky Harbour.

Come near sunset if the weather cooperates. The headland catches the evening light beautifully, and the site gives a gentler counterpoint to the park's bigger hikes.
