The Tablelands look almost wrong for Newfoundland: orange-brown, bare, and harsh beside the surrounding green hills. The rock is peridotite from Earth's mantle, shoved upward during a continental collision.

The main trail is easy and rewarding, with Parks Canada interpreters often explaining the geology in summer. Stay on durable surfaces where asked; the thin plant life here has a hard enough time already.
