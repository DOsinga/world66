Gros Morne is built for walking, boat trips, and slow coastal drives. The headline sights are [Western Brook Pond](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/grosmornenationa/western_brook_pond), [The Tablelands](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/grosmornenationa/tablelands), [Gros Morne Mountain](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/grosmornenationa/gros_morne_mountain), and [Green Gardens](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/grosmornenationa/green_gardens), but smaller stops such as Lobster Cove Head and Baker's Brook Falls round out the park.

Weather changes quickly. Pack layers, expect wind, and do not underestimate exposed trails just because the elevations look modest on paper.
