The Lawrence O'Brien Arts Centre is Labrador's main performing arts venue, hosting theatre, concerts, films, and community events. In a remote town, that matters: this is where touring artists, local performers, and visiting audiences meet.

Check the programme rather than just dropping in. On the right night it gives a much better feel for contemporary Labrador than another hour in the car.
