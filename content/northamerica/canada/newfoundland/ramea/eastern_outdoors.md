Eastern Outdoors is the practical contact if you want to turn Ramea's coast into more than a harbour walk. Guided island tours and kayak outings make sense here: the water and weather are serious, and local knowledge matters.

For most visitors, this is a plan-ahead option rather than something to count on after stepping off the ferry. If the timing works, it is one of the better ways to see the Ramea Islands at their proper scale.
