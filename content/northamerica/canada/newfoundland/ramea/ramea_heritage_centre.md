The Ramea Heritage Centre is a small local museum in one of the island's older buildings. It is the place to look for the fishing, family, and everyday objects that explain Ramea better than a viewpoint alone can.

Do not expect a large interpretive centre. The value is in the local scale: photographs, tools, household pieces, and the sense of a community keeping its own record.
