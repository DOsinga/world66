---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Ramea is a walking and looking place. Follow the harbour first, then use local advice for the Man-O-War Hill and Northwest Head routes, where the views open across the Ramea Islands and toward White Bear Bay.

The **Ramea Heritage Centre** gives the outport story a local voice. The wind-diesel site is more unusual: not beautiful in the postcard sense, but a good reminder that remote island life depends on practical systems most travellers never think about.
