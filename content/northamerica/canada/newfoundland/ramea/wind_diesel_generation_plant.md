Ramea's wind-diesel generation plant is not a romantic attraction, but it is one of the island's most distinctive facts. The community was chosen for a wind-diesel demonstration project because remote Newfoundland outports depend on expensive, vulnerable diesel generation.

The turbines and related systems make a good stop while walking the island. They tell a practical story about life beyond the road network: power, weather, engineering, and the cost of keeping small communities supplied.
