---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Rencontre East is best treated as a slow day outside the road network. Walk the harbour first, then follow local advice toward the trails and ponds above town. If you have arranged a local-led outing, Rencontre Lake opens the larger landscape: fjord-like water, waterfalls, old mine traces, and the quiet remains of communities that once made this coast feel less empty.
