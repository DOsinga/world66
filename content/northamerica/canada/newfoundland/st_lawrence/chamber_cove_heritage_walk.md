Chamber Cove is the strongest reason to make time for St. Lawrence. The trail leads through rough coastal scenery to the place connected with the 1942 wrecks of the USS Truxtun and USS Pollux, when local miners and residents helped pull survivors from the freezing sea.

The walk is not long, but it has weight. Go for the Atlantic views, then stay with the story told by the memorials and the landscape itself.
