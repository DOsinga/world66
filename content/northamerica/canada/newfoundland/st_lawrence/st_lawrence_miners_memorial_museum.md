The Miner's Memorial Museum explains the fluorspar industry that made St. Lawrence more than a fishing outport. It is also useful background for the shipwreck story, since the miners' rescue work is central to the town's memory of 1942.

Look for the local fluorspar pieces if the workshop or shop is open. The stone is part of the town's identity, not just a souvenir.
