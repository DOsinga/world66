Admiralty House Museum in Mount Pearl occupies a former Marconi wireless station built during the First World War. The museum covers naval communications, local military history, and the role of wireless technology in Newfoundland's Atlantic connections.

Guided tours explain the building's wartime use and its later life as a community museum. It is a useful stop for visitors interested in communications history or looking beyond the central sights of St. John's.
