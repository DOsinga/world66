Eastern Edge is an artist-run contemporary gallery that helps explain St. John's as a working arts city rather than only a harbor of colorful houses. Exhibitions change regularly and often foreground Newfoundland and Labrador artists alongside wider contemporary practice.

It is a small, flexible stop, best for visitors already walking the downtown waterfront or interested in local creative scenes. The value is in seeing what artists are making now, not in a permanent collection.
