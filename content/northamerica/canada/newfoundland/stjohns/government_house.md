Government House is the official residence of Newfoundland and Labrador’s lieutenant governor, but it also gives St. John’s a quietly grand historic stop just east of the downtown core. The stone building and formal grounds speak to the city’s colonial administration, with a scale that feels dignified rather than showy.

Access depends on events and tours, so check ahead rather than assuming you can wander inside. Even from the grounds and surrounding streets it is worth pairing with nearby ecclesiastical and harbour-side sights, especially if you are tracing the older civic geography of St. John’s.
