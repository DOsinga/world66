Blue Hill is the highest road-access viewpoint in the park and a fine place to understand the maze of water, islands, and forest around Newman Sound. It is less of a hike than a scenic drive and look-around.

On a clear day the view reaches across Bonavista Bay. In fog, wait if you can; the landscape appears and disappears quickly here.
