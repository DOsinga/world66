type: poi

Dunphy's Pond Trail is a quieter forest walk to still water, bog plants, and boreal detail. It is not a headline attraction, but it is exactly the kind of small trail that makes Terra Nova pleasant.

Go when you want a break from driving or when the coast is windy. The reward is subtle: moss, water, birds, and silence.
