Malady Head is a forested bluff above Southwest Arm, reached from the campground area. The trail saves its coastal view for the end, which makes the climb feel more rewarding than the distance suggests.

It is a good evening walk if you are camping nearby. Expect roots, damp ground, and a quieter atmosphere than Ochre Hill.
