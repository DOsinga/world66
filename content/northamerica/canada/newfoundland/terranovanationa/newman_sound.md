Newman Sound is the sheltered marine heart of Terra Nova, a long inlet of coves, forested slopes, and calm paddling routes. Kayaking here is one of the best ways to feel the park's shape.

Weather still matters, even in protected water. Go with local advice or an outfitter if you are not used to cold North Atlantic conditions.
