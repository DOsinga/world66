The Outport Trail is one of Terra Nova's longer coastal walks, linking forest, shoreline, and traces of Newfoundland's outport history. It suits hikers who want more than a viewpoint but less than a backcountry expedition.

Check current trail conditions before committing. Wet sections and coastal weather can make the walk feel longer than its map distance.
