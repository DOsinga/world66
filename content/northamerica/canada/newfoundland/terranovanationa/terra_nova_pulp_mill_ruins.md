type: poi

Near Glovertown South, the Terra Nova Sulphite Company ruins are a strange industrial footnote: a pulp mill project that left imposing remains without ever becoming the boom its backers imagined. It is outside the national park proper but fits a Terra Nova loop.

Treat the site carefully and from safe public access. The appeal is the contrast between failed industry and the quiet coastal landscape around it.
