---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Terra Nova's best sights are modest but satisfying: viewpoints over Clode Sound, short forest hikes, sheltered paddling water, and quiet ponds. [Ochre Hill](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/terranovanationa/ochre_hill), [Blue Hill](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/terranovanationa/blue_hill), [Sandy Pond](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/terranovanationa/sandy_pond), and [Malady Head](/northamerica/canada/newfoundland/terranovanationa/malady_head) make a good first pass.

The park is especially good for travellers with a camper, families, or anyone who wants Newfoundland nature without long mountain hikes.
