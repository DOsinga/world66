title: Terra Nova Visitor Centre
type: poi

The visitor centre is the practical starting point for trail advice, maps, interpretation, and current conditions. It is also useful for understanding the park's mix of marine and boreal ecosystems.

Stop here before choosing hikes if the weather is unsettled. Staff advice can save you from picking a fogged-in viewpoint when a forest trail would be better.
