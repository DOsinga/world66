Fisherman's Wharf is the town at its most local. In season, the market brings together fish, baking, crafts, and neighbours along the waterfront, and it gives visitors a better feel for Hay River than another roadside stop would.

Even outside market hours, the channels and docks explain the town's identity. This is where Great Slave Lake feels practical rather than scenic: boats, fish, freight, and weather.
