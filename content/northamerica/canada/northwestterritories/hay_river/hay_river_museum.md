Hay River Museum is a modest but useful stop if you want the town to make sense. Exhibits cover early settlement, Hudson's Bay Company history, commercial fishing, the railway, and the role Hay River played as a southern gateway to the Mackenzie River system.

It is not a blockbuster museum, but it gives context to the wharf, the old town, and the lake traffic you see outside.
