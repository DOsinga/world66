Louise Falls is a tiered waterfall on the Hay River, reached on a short day hike downstream from Alexandra Falls. It is smaller than its famous neighbour, but the walk gives a better feel for the river canyon and northern forest.

The two falls work well as a pair if you are driving between Hay River and Enterprise. Wear sturdy shoes, as the trail can be rough or wet in places.
