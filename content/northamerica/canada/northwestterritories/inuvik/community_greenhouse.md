Inuvik's most surprising building is a converted hockey arena turned greenhouse. Outside, it looks like any 1980s rink — corrugated steel, low slung. Inside, the roof has been replaced with a clear dome and the floor with hundreds of raised beds growing tomatoes, peppers, herbs, lettuce, and flowers through the 56-day Arctic summer.

The greenhouse is run by a non-profit member-cooperative and is open to visitors most afternoons in summer. Tours run when staff are available — five dollars, very short, fascinating. It is one of the more practical demonstrations of life above the Arctic Circle and well worth half an hour.
