The Mackenzie Delta — visible from any high point in town — is a vast flat plain of channels, oxbow lakes, and willow scrub stretching to the horizon. It is one of the most important wildlife habitats in the Arctic: beluga whales calve in the offshore bays in July, caribou herds cross seasonally, and the delta is a critical staging ground for hundreds of thousands of migrating geese and ducks.

Tour operators in Inuvik (Tundra North Tours, Arctic Chalet) run **river boat tours** from the town dock in summer — half-day or full-day trips through the channels, sometimes including a stop at a Gwich'in fish camp where dryfish is still made the traditional way. In winter the delta is crossed by ice roads.
