The Midnight Sun Mosque — known locally as the "Little Mosque on the Tundra" — is a small green-and-white prefabricated wooden building on the north edge of town that serves Inuvik's roughly 100-strong Muslim community. It is the northernmost mosque in the Americas.

The building was constructed in Winnipeg, then trucked, barged, and re-assembled in Inuvik over the summer of 2010 — a logistical operation more interesting than the building itself, but the mosque is now a small element of the town's surprisingly varied religious geography (Catholic igloo, Anglican wooden church, mosque, Pentecostal hall). Visitors are welcome; remove shoes if you go inside.
