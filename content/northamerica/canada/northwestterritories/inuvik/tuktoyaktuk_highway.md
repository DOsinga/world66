The **Inuvik–Tuktoyaktuk Highway** opened in November 2017 and is North America's first all-season public road to the Arctic Ocean. It is 138 km of gravel, taking two and a half hours each way, mostly across treeless tundra dotted with thousands of small lakes.

**Tuktoyaktuk** itself ("Tuk") is an Inuvialuit community of about 900 on the Beaufort Sea, with grocery stores, a couple of guesthouses, the famous **pingos** (ice-cored hills) on the outskirts, and a beach where you can dip a toe in the Arctic Ocean and collect a certificate to prove it. Tour operators in Inuvik run day trips by van or by car. Doing it yourself by rental car is possible but expensive — windshields routinely crack on the gravel; check your insurance.
