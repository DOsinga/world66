Tuktoyaktuk Cemetery is a stark local example of Arctic coastal erosion and thawing permafrost. Rising seas and unstable ground have damaged parts of the shoreline, making the cemetery a visible reminder of how climate change is affecting the community.

Visit respectfully and keep to public areas; this is still an active place of memory for local families.
