The Annapolis Royal Historic Gardens are more than a pleasant stroll. The beds and themed areas move through local gardening styles and settlement periods, from Acadian plots to Victorian taste, making the town's history visible in soil, paths, and walls.

Come when you want a softer counterpoint to the forts. In season, the rose garden and broad lawns make this one of the best slow walks in town.
