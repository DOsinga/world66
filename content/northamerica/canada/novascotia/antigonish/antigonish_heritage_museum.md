The Antigonish Heritage Museum occupies the old railway station east of Main Street. It is small, but it gives useful context for the town's Mi'kmaq, Scottish, railway, church, and university stories.

Stop in if you like local history or want to put the Highland Games and StFX into a wider county setting.
