The Antigonish Highland Games are the town's best reason to plan around a date. For a week in July, Columbus Field and the surrounding streets fill with heavy events, piping, dancing, concerts, and a parade.

It is a local institution rather than a tourist show dropped into town for the weekend. If you are in Nova Scotia in early July, this is the moment when Antigonish feels most like itself.
