St. Francis Xavier University gives Antigonish much of its character. The campus is compact enough for an easy walk, with a mix of older stone buildings, newer academic blocks, lawns, and chapels.

It is not a museum piece, but it is the place where the town's academic, Catholic, and Maritime cooperative history meet.
