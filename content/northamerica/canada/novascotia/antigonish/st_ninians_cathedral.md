St. Ninian's Cathedral is the seat of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Antigonish, an unusually weighty role for a town this size. Its stone presence beside the StFX campus helps explain why Antigonish has long punched above its weight in Maritime church and education life.

The building is most rewarding as part of a campus walk rather than as a long standalone visit.
