Baddeck sits on the Bras d'Or Lake near the middle of [Cape Breton](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/capebreton), and it is much more than a fuel stop for the Cabot Trail. The village has a calm lakeside setting, a strong boating feel, and one first-rate museum tied to Alexander Graham Bell, who spent his later years across the water at Beinn Bhreagh.

The [Alexander Graham Bell National Historic Site](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/baddeck/alexander_graham_bell_national_historic_site) is the anchor. It covers Bell's experiments with flight, hydrofoils, sound, and education for the deaf, and its rooftop view over the lake helps explain why he stayed.

With a spare day, mix the museum with the Baddeck waterfront, [Kidston Island](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/baddeck/kidston_island), or the short drive to [Uisge Bàn Falls](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/baddeck/uisge_ban_falls). The village is also a natural pause before or after the Cabot Trail, but it deserves more than a quick pass-through.
