Kidston Island sits just offshore in Baddeck Harbour, close enough that it feels like part of the village. In summer, a small ferry carries visitors across to the island's beach, short trails, and lighthouse.

It is a gentle outing rather than a major sight, but it gives Baddeck its postcard foreground. Bring a swimsuit if the weather is warm and check the ferry season before planning around it.
