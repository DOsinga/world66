Uisge Bàn Falls is the best quick nature trip from Baddeck. A well-marked trail follows forest and stream into a granite gorge, ending at a 15-metre waterfall whose Gaelic name means white water.

The walk is short enough for most visitors but still feels like a proper Cape Breton forest outing. It is especially good after rain, when the falls have more force and the hardwood valley feels lush.
