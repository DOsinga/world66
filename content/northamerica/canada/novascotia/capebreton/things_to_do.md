Cape Breton's main draw is the [Cabot Trail](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/capebreton/cabot_trail), the 300km coastal loop that runs through [Cape Breton Highlands National Park](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/capebreton/cb_highlands_national_park). The [Fortress of Louisbourg](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/capebreton/fortress_of_louisbourg) on the southeastern coast is the largest historical reconstruction in Canada. The [Bras d'Or Lake](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/capebreton/bras_dor_lake) — a tidal inland sea — offers sailing, kayaking, and bald eagle sightings from almost any shore.
