Chéticamp is an Acadian fishing village on the west coast of [Cape Breton](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/capebreton), squeezed between the Gulf of St. Lawrence and the highlands. It is one of the best bases on the Cabot Trail because the village has its own culture, not just a convenient address near the park gate.

The Acadian character is visible in the language, music, food, and craft traditions. [Les Trois Pignons](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/cheticamp/les_trois_pignons) is the best place to start, especially for Chéticamp's hooked-rug tradition, which turns a practical household craft into serious folk art.

The landscape gives the village its other half. [Cape Breton Highlands National Park](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/capebreton/cb_highlands_national_park) begins just north of town, with the [Acadian Trail](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/cheticamp/acadian_trail) climbing to broad views over the coast and river valley. The short [Gypsum Mine & Quarry Trail](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/cheticamp/gypsum_mine_quarry_trail) and the quiet edges of [Chéticamp Island](/northamerica/canada/novascotia/cheticamp/cheticamp_island) round out a good day without needing to rush around the whole Cabot Trail.
