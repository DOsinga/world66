The Admiral Digby Museum tells the town's Loyalist, shipping, and domestic history from the Woodrow/Dakin house, one of Digby's oldest buildings. It is small, but the location and scale fit the town.

Come here to understand Digby as more than a ferry landing and a plate of scallops.
