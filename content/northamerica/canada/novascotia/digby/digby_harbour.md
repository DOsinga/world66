Digby Harbour is the town's main stage: scallop boats, gulls, ferry traffic, and the Bay of Fundy light all close together. It is not a grand promenade, but it is the place where Digby makes sense.

Walk it before dinner, then choose somewhere nearby for scallops. The harbour is also the right place to judge the weather before committing to a longer drive down Digby Neck.
