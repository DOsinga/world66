Glace Bay is a working Cape Breton town with a deeper story than its plain streets first suggest. Coal shaped the place for generations, and the Atlantic cliffs at nearby Table Head put it on the map again when Marconi used the coast for early wireless communication across the ocean.

This is not a polished resort. Come for the underground mining tour, the Marconi site, and a sense of industrial Cape Breton that is very different from the Cabot Trail's scenery. It makes the most sense as a day from Sydney or as a cultural stop before heading to Louisbourg.

The town's identity is strongest when you connect its sights: miners going underground, wireless signals crossing the sea, and local performances in the old Savoy Theatre. Together they give Glace Bay enough substance for a focused half-day or more.
