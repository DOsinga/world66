The Cape Breton Miners Museum is Glace Bay's strongest reason to stop. Its underground tour descends into the Ocean Deeps Colliery, where guides explain the cramped, dangerous world that powered the town for more than a century.

The surface exhibits add context, but the tour is the point. It is physical, memorable, and much more direct than reading about coal from a display case. Check seasonal hours before making the drive.
