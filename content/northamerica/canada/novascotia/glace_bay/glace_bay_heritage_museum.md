Glace Bay Heritage Museum is a small town museum with mining photographs, fishing artifacts, school yearbooks, and local memorabilia. A memorial to the town's coal miners stands on the grounds.

The Cape Breton Miners Museum is the stronger stop if you only have time for one, but this museum adds a more local layer to Glace Bay's story and is easy to combine with a walk through town.
