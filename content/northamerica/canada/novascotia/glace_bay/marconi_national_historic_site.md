Marconi National Historic Site sits at Table Head, facing the Atlantic. From here, Guglielmo Marconi's wireless station helped push radio communication beyond local experiment and toward transatlantic reality.

The site is modest, so do not expect a large museum. Its value is in the setting and the story: a windy Cape Breton cliff tied directly to the early global communications network.
