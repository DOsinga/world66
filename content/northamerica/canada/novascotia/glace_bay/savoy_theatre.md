The Savoy Theatre gives Glace Bay a cultural anchor beyond its mines and historic sites. The restored 1920s venue hosts concerts, comedy, theatre, and community events, often with the Cape Breton music thread running through the programme.

It is worth checking the calendar if you are staying nearby. A performance here gives a better feel for the town than another roadside dinner.
