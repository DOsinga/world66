The Art Gallery of Nova Scotia is Halifax's main public art museum. Its collection gives strong attention to Nova Scotian artists, including Maud Lewis and Alex Colville, alongside Mi'kmaq art and rotating exhibitions.

The gallery is compact enough for a short visit but substantial enough to anchor a rainy afternoon downtown. It is especially worthwhile for visitors who want a local view of Atlantic Canadian art.
