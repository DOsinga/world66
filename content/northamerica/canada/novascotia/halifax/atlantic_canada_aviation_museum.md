Atlantic Canada Aviation Museum sits near Halifax Stanfield airport, which is exactly
where an aviation museum should be. Its aircraft, engines, models, and archival displays
cover military, civilian, and regional flying across Atlantic Canada.

It is most rewarding for travelers with a spare hour around the airport or a specific
interest in aviation history. The museum is volunteer-driven and practical rather than
flashy, but that directness suits the subject: machines, routes, weather, and the people
who kept them flying.
