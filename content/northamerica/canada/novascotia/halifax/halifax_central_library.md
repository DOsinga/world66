Halifax Central Library is a bold modern building on Spring Garden Road, designed like a stack of shifted glass books. It opened in 2014 after years of planning and quickly became one of the city's best pieces of contemporary architecture.

Visitors can browse the local history section, stop at the cafes, or head up to the rooftop patio for free views toward Citadel Hill and Halifax Harbour.
