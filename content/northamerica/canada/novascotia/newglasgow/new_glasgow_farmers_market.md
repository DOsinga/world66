New Glasgow Farmers Market is the best casual food stop in town if you are here on market day. Vendors change with the season, but the mix usually leans toward local produce, baking, preserves, crafts and simple prepared food.

It is not a destination market on the scale of Halifax, but it gives a good read on Pictou County and makes a useful Saturday morning stop before heading to the beaches or Pictou.
