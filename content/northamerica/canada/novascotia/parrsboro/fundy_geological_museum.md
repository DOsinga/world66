The Fundy Geological Museum is Parrsboro's anchor sight. It explains the ancient rocks, minerals, tides, and dinosaur fossils of the Fundy shore, and it runs seasonal beach walks that make much more sense of the cliffs than wandering alone.

For families it is hands-on enough to work as a rainy-day museum. For adults, the appeal is the chance to read the coastline properly before heading out onto the beaches and headlands.
