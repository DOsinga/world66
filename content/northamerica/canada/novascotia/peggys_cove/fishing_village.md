The cove is small enough to cross in a few minutes, but it gives the lighthouse its context. Fishing boats, rope, sheds, and low houses sit tight against the water, making Peggy's Cove feel like a village rather than a scenic pull-off.

Most buildings are private, so wander gently and do not block lanes or driveways. Early morning is the best time for a quiet look.
