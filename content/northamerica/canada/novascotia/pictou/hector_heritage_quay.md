Hector Heritage Quay is the reason most travellers stop in Pictou. The full-size replica of the Hector recalls the 1773 voyage that brought Highland Scots to Nova Scotia, and the quay explains the hard crossing and the settlement that followed.

The site is small but central to the town's identity. Even if you do not go inside, the ship gives the waterfront its most memorable view.
