McCulloch House is quieter than the waterfront sights but worth the short walk if you like local history. It tells the story of Reverend Thomas McCulloch, an educator and satirist whose work helped shape early Nova Scotian public life.

The house also gives a useful sense of what respectable town life looked like in Pictou after the first rough settlement years.
