The Nova Scotia Historic Acadian Village is Pubnico's most useful cultural stop. Set on a site overlooking the harbour, it recreates Acadian life in the early 1900s through historic buildings, costumed interpretation, and everyday tools rather than grand monuments.

It is especially good if you want context before exploring the rest of the harbour communities. Check the season before going, since opening is aimed at the warmer travel months.
