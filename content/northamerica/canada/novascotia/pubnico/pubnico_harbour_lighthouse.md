Pubnico Harbour Lighthouse stands at Beach Point near the harbour entrance. It is a simple coastal marker rather than a major staffed attraction, but it gives a good reason to follow the road out toward the sea.

The point is best for a short drive, a photograph, and a look back toward the fishing harbour. Weather changes quickly on this coast, so bring a layer even on a bright day.
