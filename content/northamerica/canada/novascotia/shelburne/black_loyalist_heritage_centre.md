The Black Loyalist Heritage Centre is in Birchtown, a short drive west of Shelburne, and it is the most important historical stop in the area. It tells the story of Black Loyalists who left the American colonies after the Revolution and tried to build free lives in Nova Scotia under harsh conditions and broken promises.

Do not treat it as a side note. Without Birchtown, Shelburne's Loyalist history is incomplete, and the town's pretty waterfront loses much of its meaning.
