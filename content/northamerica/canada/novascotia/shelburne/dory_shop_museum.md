The Dory Shop Museum sits in a harbour-side building associated with Shelburne's boatbuilding trade. Dories were practical working boats, and the museum is strongest when it shows the tools, shapes, and routines behind that plain usefulness.

It is a small museum, but it fits the town perfectly. Shelburne's waterfront makes more sense after you have seen how boats were built and repaired here.
