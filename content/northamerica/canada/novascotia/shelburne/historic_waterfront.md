Shelburne's historic waterfront is low-key, which is part of its charm. Dock Street has old wooden buildings, museum sites, wharves, and harbour views without the heavy polish of better-known South Shore towns.

Walk it slowly before or after the museums. The street gives the history a setting: a sheltered harbour, a practical shoreline, and a town that still feels tied to the water.
