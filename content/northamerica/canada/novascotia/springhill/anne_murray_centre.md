The Anne Murray Centre is a fan museum, but it is a good one: gold and platinum records, stage clothes, video clips, photographs, and a clear line from Springhill childhood to international fame. If you know the songs, it lands warmly.

Even if Anne Murray is not your era, the centre helps explain how much pride a small town can attach to one of its own. Check the season before you go; it is not a year-round attraction.
