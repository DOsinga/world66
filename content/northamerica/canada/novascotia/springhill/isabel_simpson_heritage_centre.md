The Isabel Simpson Heritage Centre is the quieter companion to Springhill's two headline museums. It is the place to look for local photographs, family material, and the civic texture behind the larger mining story.

This is a worthwhile add-on, not a destination by itself. Pair it with the Miners Museum if you want Springhill to feel like a lived-in town rather than just a disaster headline.
