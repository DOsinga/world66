Lightfoot & Wolfville is one of the easiest places to understand why the Annapolis Valley has become Nova Scotia's wine centre. The vineyard sits just outside town, with rows running toward open Fundy views and a tasting room focused on cool-climate whites, sparkling wine, and pinot noir.

It is polished, but not stiff. Come for a tasting, stay for the view, and use it as a pleasant counterpoint to the heavier history at Grand-Pré.
