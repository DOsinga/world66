Kiluk is worth seeking out because it connects travel to local work rather than imported souvenir shelves. Seamstresses here make sealskin, fur, leather, and textile pieces, and the shop also carries arts and crafts by Nunavummiut makers.

Opening hours are limited, so check before you go. If you want to buy something in Arviat, this is the place where the money and the skill stay closest to the community.
