*North America > Canada > Nunavut > Auyuittuq National Park*

Auyuittuq National Park is one of Canada's great wilderness journeys, not a casual sightseeing stop. On southeastern Baffin Island, it protects the Akshayuk Pass, granite walls, glaciers, ice caps, and fjords in a landscape whose Inuktitut name is usually translated as "the land that never melts."

Most visits follow some version of the [Akshayuk Pass](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/auyuittuq/akshayuk_pass), travelling between Pangnirtung Fiord and the north side near Qikiqtarjuaq by foot, ski, snowmobile, or boat-supported access. The famous peaks — [Mount Thor](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/auyuittuq/mount_thor), [Mount Asgard](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/auyuittuq/mount_asgard), and their neighbours — are huge, cold, and indifferent.

This is a place for experienced, self-reliant travellers with local support, proper registration, and flexible plans. Weather, river crossings, sea ice, and evacuation logistics are not background details; they are the trip.
