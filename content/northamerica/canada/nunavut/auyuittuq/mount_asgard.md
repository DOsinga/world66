Mount Asgard's twin towers rise above the pass with a shape that seems designed for cinema and mountaineering posters. It is one of Auyuittuq's defining silhouettes.

Like Thor, it is mainly a sight for most travellers, not an objective. Climbs here require expedition-level preparation and a tolerance for waiting out weather.
