Mount Thor is the park's most famous peak, known for one of the greatest vertical drops on Earth. Its west face is so steep that it overhangs, which explains the mountain's near-mythic status among climbers.

Most visitors admire it from the pass rather than climb it. That is sensible: the mountain is remote, technical, and utterly serious.
