title: Pangnirtung Fiord
type: poi

Pangnirtung Fiord is the southern approach to many Auyuittuq journeys, a long Arctic inlet leading toward the park's interior valleys. The transition from community, boat travel, and mountains is unforgettable.

Conditions on the fiord decide access as much as trail plans do. Local outfitters and Parks Canada guidance are essential here.
