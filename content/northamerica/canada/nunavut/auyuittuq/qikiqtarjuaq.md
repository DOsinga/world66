Qikiqtarjuaq is one of the key community gateways for Auyuittuq, especially for northern access. It is a real Inuit community, not just a trailhead, and visitors should treat it with respect.

Flights, local transport, and weather windows often make time here part of the journey. Use that time to listen, prepare, and avoid rushing Arctic logistics.
