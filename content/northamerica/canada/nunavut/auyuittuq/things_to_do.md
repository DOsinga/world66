Auyuittuq is about one main experience done properly: moving through the Akshayuk Pass among glaciers, rivers, and granite peaks. [Mount Thor](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/auyuittuq/mount_thor), [Mount Asgard](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/auyuittuq/mount_asgard), [Summit Lake](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/auyuittuq/summit_lake), and [Pangnirtung Fiord](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/auyuittuq/pangnirtung_fiord) are the names travellers tend to remember.

Do not treat these as ordinary day sights. Access, safety registration, local transport, and weather windows are part of every plan.
