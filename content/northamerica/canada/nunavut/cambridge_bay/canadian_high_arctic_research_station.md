The Canadian High Arctic Research Station is the most striking modern building in Cambridge Bay and a reminder that the Arctic is not just a place of old exploration stories. Scientists work here on polar climate, technology, ecosystems, and northern knowledge.

Public access varies, but the main building is worth seeing even from outside. Its presence changes the feel of town: beside a small harbour community on Victoria Island is a serious national research campus looking straight at the future of the North.
