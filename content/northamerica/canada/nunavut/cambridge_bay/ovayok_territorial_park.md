Ovayok Territorial Park lies east of Cambridge Bay, where a group of rounded hills rises from the otherwise wide, low tundra. The main hill, Uvayuq or Mount Pelly, is only about 200 metres high, but in this landscape it feels like a landmark.

Come for a tundra walk, muskox watching if you are lucky, and a better sense of the open country around Cambridge Bay. Go with local advice on weather, transport, and wildlife; distances here are easy to underestimate from a map.
