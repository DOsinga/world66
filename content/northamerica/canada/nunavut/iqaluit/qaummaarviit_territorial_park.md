Qaummaarviit Territorial Park protects an island site in Frobisher Bay where the remains of Thule winter houses, tent rings, caches, and other archaeological traces sit in open Arctic landscape. The name means a place that shines, and the setting makes the past feel unusually immediate: stone outlines, sea, tundra, and sky with Iqaluit still nearby.

Access is seasonal and usually requires local transport by boat, snowmobile, or guided arrangement, so this is not a casual stroll from town. For travellers who can make the logistics work, it is one of the strongest cultural landscapes around Iqaluit and a meaningful companion to Sylvia Grinnell Territorial Park.
