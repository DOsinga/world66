The Auyuittuq National Park Office is not a scenic attraction in the usual sense, but for many visitors it is the most important stop in Pangnirtung. This is where you register, get orientation, confirm conditions, and understand what the park will demand of you.

Take the briefing seriously. Akshayuk Pass, fiord travel, river crossings, wind, cold, and rescue limits are not abstract risks here. The office turns a dream of Arctic trekking into a plan that has a chance of working.
