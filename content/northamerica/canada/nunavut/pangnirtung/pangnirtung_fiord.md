Pangnirtung Fiord is the landscape that makes the hamlet unforgettable. Mountains rise hard from the water, boats become part of the travel system, and the light can make the whole valley look larger than it should.

For Auyuittuq trips, the fiord is practical as well as beautiful: it is part of the southern approach toward the park. Even if you are not trekking, find time to walk the shore and let the scale settle in.
