Uqqurmiut Centre for Arts and Crafts is the cultural anchor in Pangnirtung. It brings together the print shop, tapestry studio, craft gallery, and working artists who have made the hamlet known far beyond Baffin Island.

This is the place to buy art if you can, but it is also worth visiting just to understand Pang as more than a park gateway. Check opening hours locally; in a small Arctic community, schedules can bend around weather, freight, and daily life.
