Rankin Inlet's inukshuk is a quick town-centre landmark rather than a major sight, but it is worth including on a short walk through the community. The human-like stone figure stands as a simple northern marker against the open Hudson Bay sky.

Pair it with the visitor centre and a look along the shoreline. In a place this exposed, even a short walk gives you a feel for scale and weather.
