The Kivalliq Regional Visitor Centre is a useful first stop in Rankin Inlet, especially if you have just arrived by air and need a sense of where you are. Displays cover local wildlife, arts, crafts, and the communities of the wider Kivalliq region.

It is also practical. Ask about current road, weather, park, and wildlife conditions before heading out toward the territorial park or the edge of town.
