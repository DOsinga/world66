Marble Island is a stark boat trip from Rankin Inlet, important in Inuit tradition and marked by stories of whaling ships, shipwrecks, and Arctic travel. The island's pale rock, low tundra flowers, and visible graves from the Knight Expedition make it one of the more memorable excursions in the area.
