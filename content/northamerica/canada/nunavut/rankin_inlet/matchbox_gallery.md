Matchbox Gallery is the place to understand Rankin Inlet's unusual art story. The gallery is closely associated with Inuit fine-art ceramics, a field in which Rankin has played a rare and important role since the late twentieth century.

Expect a small, working northern art space rather than a big museum. The reward is seeing how local artists turn clay, drawing, and printmaking into something rooted in Arctic life without feeling like souvenir production.
