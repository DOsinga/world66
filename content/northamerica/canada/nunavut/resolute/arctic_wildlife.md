The tundra and sea ice around Resolute support a range of iconic Arctic wildlife. Muskoxen graze on the frost-cracked tundra above the hamlet, especially in summer when there is enough vegetation to sustain them. Polar bears travel the sea ice between Cornwallis Island and the surrounding islands during spring and early summer, and beluga whales move through the channels when the ice breaks up. Arctic foxes are year-round residents, often seen around the hamlet.

Wildlife watching in the Resolute area is best done with a local Inuit guide, who will know the current locations of polar bear activity and can read sea ice conditions safely. The high Arctic summer (late June and July) brings the greatest diversity: migratory shorebirds and geese arrive to nest, jaegers hunt along the clifftops, and the tundra briefly turns green and flowers. Snowmobiles and dog sleds are used in winter for travel across the sea ice to more distant wildlife areas.
