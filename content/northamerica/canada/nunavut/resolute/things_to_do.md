---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Resolute is not a conventional tourist destination — it is a staging post for Arctic expeditions and a place to experience the High Arctic on its own terms. The [Polar Continental Shelf Program](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/resolute/polar_continental_shelf_program) base is the operational heart of Canada's Arctic research effort and an interesting insight into how scientists and explorers use this remote settlement as a launching point. [Devon Island day trips](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/resolute/devon_island) offer a chance to set foot on the world's largest uninhabited island, used by NASA as a Mars analogue site. [Arctic wildlife watching](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/resolute/arctic_wildlife) — muskoxen, polar bears, Arctic foxes, and beluga whales — is the main draw for most non-scientist visitors. The [midnight sun and northern lights](/northamerica/canada/nunavut/resolute/light_phenomena) offer extraordinary natural spectacles depending on the season.
