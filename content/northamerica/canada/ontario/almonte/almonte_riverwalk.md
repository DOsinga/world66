The Almonte Riverwalk is the easiest way to read the town on foot. It follows the Mississippi River through the old core, with views of the falls, stone mill buildings, bridges, and the backs of shops along the water.

It is short, so treat it as part of a wider wander rather than a hike. Pair it with the textile museum and Mill Street shops for the best sense of Almonte.
