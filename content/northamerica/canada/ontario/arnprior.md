Arnprior sits where the Madawaska River runs into the Ottawa, about 45 kilometres west of [Ottawa](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa). It is a small town, but not a blank one: lumber money left stone civic buildings, the rivers give it a pleasant edge, and Gillies Grove preserves the white pines that once made this valley rich.

The best reason to come is [Gillies Grove](/northamerica/canada/ontario/arnprior/gillies_grove), a rare stand of old-growth white pine within walking distance of town. Pair it with the riverside trails, the beach at [Robert Simpson Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/arnprior/robert_simpson_park), and the small [Arnprior and District Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/arnprior/arnprior_and_district_museum) under the clock tower.

Arnprior works best as a quiet half-day from Ottawa or as a stop on the road up the Ottawa Valley. It is not a big sightseeing town, but the forest, riverfront, and lumber-town bones make it worth more than a fuel stop.
