Aylmer is a small town in Elgin County, southwestern Ontario, surrounded by tobacco and dairy farmland. The town has a well-preserved Victorian main street, and the [Aylmer and District Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/aylmer/aylmer_and_district_museum) in the old town hall gives a good sense of the area's agricultural roots.

The main draw for visitors is the surrounding countryside. [Long Point](/northamerica/canada/ontario/aylmer/long_point_biosphere_reserve), a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve stretching 40 kilometres into Lake Erie, is about a half-hour drive south and offers some of the best birdwatching in Ontario — it sits on a major migration flyway. [Port Burwell Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/aylmer/port_burwell_provincial_park), on the lake shore, has a long sandy beach and the oldest wooden lighthouse on the Canadian side of the Great Lakes.
