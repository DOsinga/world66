Silent Lake Provincial Park protects a quiet, motor-free lake south of Bancroft, with canoe routes, swimming, and hiking trails through mixed forest and marsh. It is the best nearby place to turn Bancroft from a quick mineral stop into a proper outdoor day.

In winter, the park's groomed trails make it one of the more useful cross-country ski options in this part of Ontario.
