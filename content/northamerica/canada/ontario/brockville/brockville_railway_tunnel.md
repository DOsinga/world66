Built between 1854 and 1860, the Brockville Railway Tunnel carried rail traffic from the Ottawa Valley down to the St. Lawrence waterfront. Today you can walk the half-kilometre passage, with coloured lights picking out the stonework and mineral stains. It is odd, local, and genuinely memorable.
