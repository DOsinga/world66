Leeds and Grenville County Court House is a National Historic Site of Canada overlooking Court House Square in Brockville. The central block was built in 1842-1844, with later wings and a former gaoler's house added in the late 19th century. The building is still a working civic landmark, but its exterior and square are worth a short look.
