Lock 31 sits in Buckhorn village on the Trent-Severn Waterway, with mooring, summer boat traffic, and easy access to village shops and snacks. It is not dramatic engineering, but it is a pleasant way to watch the rhythm of the canal system.

Stop here when passing through Buckhorn, especially if you are following the waterway by car rather than boat.
