Warsaw Caves Conservation Area has seven small caves, about 15 kilometres of trails, a beach, and access to easy paddling on the Indian River. The caves are short but hands-on, so bring a headlamp and clothes that can get dirty.

It is one of the most active family-friendly stops in the area, especially when you want something more physical than another lakeside lookout.
