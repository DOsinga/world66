Burlington is a city of about 180,000 on the western shore of Lake Ontario, between Hamilton to the west and Oakville to the east, roughly 50 km from Toronto. It has a pleasant waterfront and a quieter character than its larger neighbours, with good parks along the lake and a strong community arts scene.

The lakefront centrepiece is [Spencer Smith Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/burlington/spencer_smith_park), a long waterfront greenway with walkways, a Discovery Centre, and a pond that becomes an outdoor skating rink in winter. The [Brant Street Pier](/northamerica/canada/ontario/burlington/brant_street_pier) reaches 137 metres out into Lake Ontario from the foot of the main downtown street. The [Art Gallery of Burlington](/northamerica/canada/ontario/burlington/art_gallery_of_burlington) focuses on Canadian ceramics and contemporary craft. The [Joseph Brant Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/burlington/joseph_brant_museum) commemorates the Mohawk leader who was granted land here by the Crown in the 18th century. The [Royal Botanical Gardens](/northamerica/canada/ontario/burlington/royal_botanical_gardens), straddling the Burlington-Hamilton border, is one of the largest botanical garden complexes in Canada, with the world's largest collection of lilacs.

The Niagara Escarpment rises to the north and the [Bruce Trail](/northamerica/canada/ontario/burlington/bruce_trail_burlington) passes through Burlington, providing access to escarpment forest walks; [Mount Nemo](/northamerica/canada/ontario/burlington/mount_nemo) is the closest dramatic cliff-top viewpoint. The summer is a long succession of waterfront festivals — Canada's largest [Ribfest](/northamerica/canada/ontario/burlington/ribfest) on the Labour Day weekend, the free [Sound of Music Festival](/northamerica/canada/ontario/burlington/sound_of_music_festival) in early June. Niagara Falls and Niagara-on-the-Lake are straightforward day trips to the south.
