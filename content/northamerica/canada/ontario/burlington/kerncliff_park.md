Kerncliff Park is a former quarry on the Niagara Escarpment, now used for short walks, birding, and views over Burlington and Lake Ontario. The exposed rock face, marsh boardwalk, and wooded trails give it more character than a simple neighbourhood park.

It works best as a brief nature stop while exploring Aldershot or the Royal Botanical Gardens area. Trails are short, but footwear with grip helps after rain.
