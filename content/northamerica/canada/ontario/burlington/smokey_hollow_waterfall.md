Smokey Hollow Waterfall, also known as Grindstone Falls, is a small but photogenic
waterfall in Waterdown, reachable from the Burlington-Hamilton edge. It gives the
Burlington area a quick Niagara Escarpment nature stop without needing a full hiking
day.

The waterfall is popular and the surrounding trails and parking can be pressured, so
follow posted access rules. It is best as part of a broader escarpment walk or scenic
drive rather than a standalone destination.
