Calabogie Highlands is the village golf stop, set near the lake with an 18-hole course and a shorter nine-hole layout. It is most useful for travellers already building an outdoors weekend around Calabogie rather than as a stand-alone reason to drive from Ottawa.

The course pairs naturally with a lake afternoon or dinner in the village. In a place this small, that kind of easy mix is the point.
