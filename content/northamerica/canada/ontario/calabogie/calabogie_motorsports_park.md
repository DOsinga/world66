Calabogie Motorsports Park is a five-kilometre road course east of the village, used for track days, racing schools, motorcycle events, and regional race weekends. It gives Calabogie a travel niche that has nothing to do with lakeside quiet.

Check the calendar before going. On event days the place has paddock energy, engine noise, and enough action to entertain even a casual fan; on a closed day it is just a private circuit behind a gate.
