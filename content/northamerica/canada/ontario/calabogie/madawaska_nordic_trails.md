The Madawaska Nordic Trails give Calabogie a quieter winter option than the downhill hill. The trailhead is near Crestview Drive, with cross-country ski routes through rolling forest when snow conditions are good.

It is a simple, local trail network rather than a staffed resort, which is part of the appeal. Bring what you need, check current conditions, and expect a low-key day in the woods.
