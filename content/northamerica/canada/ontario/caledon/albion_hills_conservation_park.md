Albion Hills Conservation Park is Caledon's more active outdoor stop. It has hiking and mountain-bike trails, a seasonal swimming area, and groomed cross-country ski trails in winter when conditions cooperate.

It is not as visually odd as Cheltenham Badlands or as classic as Forks of the Credit, but it is useful if you want to spend the day moving rather than just sightseeing.
