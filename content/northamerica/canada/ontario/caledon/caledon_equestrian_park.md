Caledon Equestrian Park was built for the 2015 Pan Am Games and remains a useful stop when competitions are on. It is a spectator venue rather than a general sightseeing attraction, so timing matters.

If horses are your thing, check the event calendar before visiting Caledon. On the right weekend it adds a different note to a day otherwise built around parks and villages.
