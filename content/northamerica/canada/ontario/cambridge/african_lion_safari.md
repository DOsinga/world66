African Lion Safari is the big family attraction near Cambridge, built around drive-through reserves where visitors see lions, giraffes, rhinos, zebras, and other animals from vehicles or park buses. It is a full-day outing rather than a quick zoo stop.

The ethics of safari parks are worth thinking about, but the place remains a major regional draw for families. Check seasonal opening dates, decide whether to use your own car, and expect summer weekends to be busy.
