Cambridge Arts Theatre is a small community theatre in Galt, used by local companies including Cambridge Community Players. It is not a blockbuster attraction, but it gives the old downtown a bit of evening life and is worth checking if you like local theatre.

The programme changes through the year, with most productions running for short stretches. Pair it with dinner in the historic core rather than treating it as a stand-alone day trip.
