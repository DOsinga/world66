McDougall Cottage is a small but worthwhile stop in old Galt, set in an 1858 limestone house beside the Grand River. The cottage focuses on the Scottish families who helped shape Cambridge, with domestic rooms, local history displays, and craft traditions that make the building feel lived-in rather than frozen as a showpiece.

It is best paired with a walk through the Galt historic core, since the scale is intimate and the story is local. Check opening times before crossing town; when the museum is running workshops or heritage events, it becomes a much better window into the community than a quick look at the exterior.
