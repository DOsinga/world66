Campbellford is a small town of about 3,500 on the Trent River in Northumberland County, Ontario, roughly midway between Toronto and Ottawa. It sits on the [Trent-Severn Waterway](/northamerica/canada/ontario/campbellford/trent_severn_waterway), the historic canal route that links Lake Ontario to Georgian Bay — a popular route for recreational boaters and canoeists. The town has a pleasant riverside character around [Old Mill Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/campbellford/old_mill_park), where a [27-foot replica of the Canadian toonie](/northamerica/canada/ontario/campbellford/giant_toonie) commemorates the local artist who designed the coin's polar bear.

The region is known as "Chocolate Town" for the [World's Finest Chocolate factory](/northamerica/canada/ontario/campbellford/worlds_finest_chocolate) outlet on Front Street, and culturally for [Westben](/northamerica/canada/ontario/campbellford/westben), a 400-seat barn theatre on a farm just outside town that draws name musicians from across Canada through the summer. [Ferris Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/campbellford/ferris_provincial_park), a few kilometres south, has a dramatic suspension footbridge over the Trent Gorge and good camping and hiking beside the river. Campbellford is a popular weekend destination for people from Toronto and Peterborough who want a slower pace without travelling too far.
