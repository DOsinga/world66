World's Finest Chocolate has operated a manufacturing plant in Campbellford since the 1990s, and the smell of cocoa on warm days is part of the town's everyday character. The factory itself does not run public tours, but the company outlet store on Front Street sells direct-from-the-factory chocolate at substantial discounts and is one of the busiest small shops in town.

The link to chocolate underpins Campbellford's self-styled nickname "Chocolate Town," and is reinforced by the annual Incredible Edibles Festival each summer. The shop stocks the company's classic bars, fundraising-style chocolate bars and the seasonal Easter and Christmas lines. A practical stop on a tour through town, and the closest thing Campbellford has to a souvenir industry.
