Cape Croker Park is a First Nations-operated campground and day-use area on a rocky point reaching out into Georgian Bay. The setting is genuinely dramatic — limestone bluffs drop to cold clear water on both sides, and the forest behind is dense and quiet.

The park has over 250 campsites, ranging from serviced sites with electricity to basic tent spots in the trees. Facilities are simple but well maintained. Day visitors can pay at the gate and use the swimming areas, picnic grounds, and trail access.

The water is clear and cold even in July, and the bluffs make for good scrambling and photography. Canoe rentals are available in summer. Reservations are advisable in July and August, when the park fills most weekends.
