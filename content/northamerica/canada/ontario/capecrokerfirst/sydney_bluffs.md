Sydney Bluffs is a Bruce Peninsula escarpment hike with wide views over Georgian Bay. The loop from Hope Bay or Sydney Bay is about 8 km, with stairs or ladder access depending on the trailhead.

The south side is rougher and hillier, while the north side is easier going and has the best open views from the top of the cliffs.
