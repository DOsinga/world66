---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cape Croker's natural setting is the draw — limestone bluffs tumbling into Georgian Bay, clear cold water, and forest trails with almost no one on them. The park is run by the Chippewas of Nawash First Nation, and the admission fees go directly to the community.

Swimming and canoeing are the main summer pursuits, with the bluffs giving the shoreline a drama rare in Ontario. The Bruce Trail passes through, giving hikers a taste of Niagara Escarpment scenery at its wildest.
