The Black Mecca Museum preserves the archives and exhibits of the Chatham-Kent Black Historical Society. Its focus is Chatham's African-Canadian history, including the people and institutions that made the town an important destination for Black settlement and the Underground Railroad story.

It is a focused research and history stop rather than a large museum, best paired with the wider Black heritage sites around Chatham-Kent.
