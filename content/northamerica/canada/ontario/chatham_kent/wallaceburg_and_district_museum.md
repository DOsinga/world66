Wallaceburg and District Museum covers the local history of northern Chatham-Kent, from the Baldoon Settlement of 1804 through the town's lumber, glass, and cooperage industries. It is a small museum, but useful if you are tracing the area's settlement and industrial story.

The museum works best as a local-history stop while passing through Wallaceburg rather than as a full-day destination.
