Cochrane is a small railway town in northeastern Ontario, far enough north that the landscape has shifted into boreal forest and long winter. It is not a polished resort town, but it has a clear identity: polar bears, hockey, and trains heading toward the edge of James Bay.

The town's best reason to stop is the [Cochrane Polar Bear Habitat](/northamerica/canada/ontario/cochrane/cochrane_polar_bear_habitat), a sanctuary for polar bears that cannot live in the wild. Next door, the [Heritage Village](/northamerica/canada/ontario/cochrane/heritage_village) gives the place some local history, with early-20th-century buildings and displays.

Cochrane is also the southern end of the [Polar Bear Express](/northamerica/canada/ontario/cochrane/polar_bear_express), Ontario Northland's train to Moosonee. If you have the time, that rail trip is the real northern adventure. If you only have a few hours, the town still makes a useful and memorable stop on the long road across northern Ontario.
