Heritage Village sits beside the Polar Bear Habitat and recreates Cochrane life in the early 1900s. The walk-through village includes replica buildings such as a doctor's office, train station, hardware store, and blacksmith shop, with displays focused on local settlement and work.

It is not large, but it rounds out a visit nicely. Pair it with the Polar Bear Habitat rather than treating it as a stand-alone museum.
