The Tim Horton Events Centre is mainly a community recreation complex, but it matters to travellers because Cochrane was the birthplace of Tim Horton, the hockey player whose name became part of Canadian coffee culture. Inside, a small museum display covers his career and local roots.

This is a short stop rather than a major attraction. It is most worthwhile for hockey fans, Canadians on a northern road trip, or anyone curious about how deeply hockey runs through small-town Ontario.
