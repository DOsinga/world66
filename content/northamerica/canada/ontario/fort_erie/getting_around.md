---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

**By car:** Fort Erie is most easily explored by car. Garrison Road (Provincial Route 3) runs east-west through town. The Queen Elizabeth Way (QEW) connects to Niagara Falls, Hamilton, and Toronto. The Niagara Parkway follows the river northward and is a scenic leisure drive.

**From the USA:** The Peace Bridge connects Fort Erie directly to Buffalo, New York. US speeds are in mph; Canada uses km/h throughout.

**Local transit:** Fort Erie Transit runs limited service on weekdays, connecting the town centre to Crystal Beach. Service is infrequent — about every 90 minutes — so a car is practical for getting around.

**To Toronto:** The QEW provides direct highway access. Toronto is approximately 130 kilometres north, about 90 minutes without traffic.
