Point Abino Light Tower is a refined early-20th-century lighthouse on a private peninsula at the east end of Lake Erie. Its white concrete tower and classical detailing make it more elegant than many Great Lakes lights.

Access is controlled, so check Fort Erie's seasonal tour schedule before going. If the timing works, it is a rewarding coastal detour from Old Fort Erie and the Niagara River sights.
