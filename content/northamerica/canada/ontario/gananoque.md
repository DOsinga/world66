Gananoque sits on the St. Lawrence River at the western end of the Thousand Islands. It is small, easy to walk, and busy in summer for one clear reason: this is one of the simplest places to get out among the islands without first detouring through [Kingston](/northamerica/canada/ontario/kingston).

The waterfront is the point of the visit. [Gananoque Boat Line](/northamerica/canada/ontario/gananoque/gananoque_boat_line) runs cruises into the maze of channels, cottages, granite islets, and cross-border river history that make the Thousand Islands more than a postcard view. Back on shore, the paired [1000 Islands History Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/gananoque/1000_islands_history_museum) and [Thousand Islands Boat Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/gananoque/thousand_islands_boat_museum) give the river some context.

Gananoque is not a grand destination in itself, but it is a useful one-day stop if you are already moving between Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal, or Kingston. Come for the river, stay for a waterfront performance at [1000 Islands Playhouse](/northamerica/canada/ontario/gananoque/1000_islands_playhouse), and use the old parkway for a quiet walk or bike ride when the cruise traffic has gone.
