The 1000 Islands Boat Museum tells the boating story of Gananoque and the Thousand Islands through exhibits, galleries, and in-water displays. It is small, but the river setting helps the subject feel immediate rather than abstract.

The museum is best paired with time on the waterfront or a boat trip through the islands. Short boat rides are sometimes available by reservation, which makes the visit more than a static collection.
