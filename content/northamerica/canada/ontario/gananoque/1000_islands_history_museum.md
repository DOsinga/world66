The 1000 Islands History Museum is a compact waterfront stop that makes the river easier to read. Exhibits cover the islands' natural setting, Indigenous and settler history, river transport, and the old railway that once tied Gananoque's waterfront to the main line.

It is especially useful before or after a boat tour. The view is pretty on its own, but the museum explains why this stretch of water mattered.
