Gananoque Boat Line is the practical reason many travellers stop in town. Its cruises head straight into the Thousand Islands, where the St. Lawrence breaks into channels, private island cottages, wooded shores, and border stories.

The shorter cruises work if you are passing through, but a longer trip gives the islands more room to unfold. Summer departures are busiest, so book ahead if your day is fixed.
