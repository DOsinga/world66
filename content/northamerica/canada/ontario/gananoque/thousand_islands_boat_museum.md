The Thousand Islands Boat Museum turns the river's boating culture into something tangible. Displays, in-water exhibits, and restoration work focus on the wooden boats, working craft, and pleasure boats that shaped summers along this part of the St. Lawrence.

It pairs naturally with the neighbouring history museum. If you like maritime craft, give it more time than its modest size suggests.
