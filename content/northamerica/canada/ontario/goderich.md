Goderich sits on the eastern shore of Lake Huron, where the Maitland River reaches the lake and the sunsets do most of the sales work. It is small, tidy, and a little proud of itself, with a courthouse square laid out like a wheel and a beach below town where freighters still come in for salt.

The town is more than a lakefront stop. The [Huron Historic Gaol](/northamerica/canada/ontario/goderich/huron_historic_gaol) is one of Ontario's stranger small-town museums, the [Huron County Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/goderich/huron_county_museum) is better than you might expect, and the [Goderich Lighthouse](/northamerica/canada/ontario/goderich/goderich_lighthouse) gives you the classic Lake Huron view.

Come in warm weather if you want the boardwalk and beaches at their best. Even outside beach season, Goderich works as a half-day or full-day stop on the Lake Huron shore, especially if you like compact towns with a visible working harbour and a bit of 19th-century Ontario still showing.
