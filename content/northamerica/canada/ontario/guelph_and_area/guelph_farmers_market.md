  The Guelph Farmers' Market has operated continuously since 1827 — the year the city was founded. It is one of the oldest continuously operating farmers' markets in Ontario, and it has been held in or near the same central location for its entire history. The surrounding Wellington County is some of the best agricultural land in the province, and the market reflects this: the produce quality is genuinely good.

The Guelph Farmers' Market, held Saturday mornings in the Baker Street market building in the heart of downtown, is one of the city's genuine pleasures. Wellington County's rich agricultural landscape means the market has real substance — local cheese, Mennonite baked goods, fresh vegetables, meat from nearby farms, and a wide range of prepared foods. It runs year-round, though it's most vibrant in summer when local produce is at its peak.

The market operates from 7 am to noon on Saturdays; arriving before 9 am gives you the best selection and the most atmosphere before the crowds peak. The building is also used for community events throughout the week. This is not a tourist market — it is where Guelph residents actually shop, which is its main virtue.
