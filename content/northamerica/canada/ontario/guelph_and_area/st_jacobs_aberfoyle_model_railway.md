St. Jacobs & Aberfoyle Model Railway is a large O-scale model railway showing southern Ontario in the 1950s. The appeal is the detail: towns, trains, rural scenes, and railway operations built at a scale big enough to reward slow looking.

It is a good family stop if you are already visiting St. Jacobs or the Mennonite country north of Guelph. Railway fans will get the most from it, but the craft is easy to appreciate even on a casual visit.
