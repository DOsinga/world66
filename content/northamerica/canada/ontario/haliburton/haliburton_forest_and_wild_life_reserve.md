Haliburton Forest and Wild Life Reserve is a large privately managed forest north of Haliburton, with hiking, lake trout fishing, canopy tours, and winter activities such as ice fishing and dog sledding.

The most distinctive stop is the wolf education centre, built around a resident wolf pack. It works well as a half-day nature visit, especially for families.
