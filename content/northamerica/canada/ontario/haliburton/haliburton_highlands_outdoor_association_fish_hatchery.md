The Haliburton Highlands Outdoor Association Fish Hatchery raises the genetically distinct Haliburton Gold Lake Trout before releasing them into local lakes. The story is tied to the region's glacial history and fishing culture.

Tours are offered on some Saturdays, with occasional events through the year. Check ahead before making the drive.
