Stanhope Heritage Museum focuses on local pioneer and logging history in the Haliburton Highlands. Its strongest draw is the restored Hawk Lake Log Chute, described locally as the only remaining log chute of its kind in Ontario.

The site also has a lookout over Maple, Beech, and Boshkung lakes, so it works as both a small museum stop and a landscape stop. It is best folded into a wider drive through the lakes north of Haliburton rather than treated as a standalone day.
