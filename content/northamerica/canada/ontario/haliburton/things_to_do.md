title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Haliburton is a base for the outdoors, not a town with attractions of its own. The main draws are the [Haliburton Sculpture Forest](/northamerica/canada/ontario/haliburton/haliburton_sculpture_forest) and the trail system in [Glebe Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/haliburton/glebe_park) around it. The Highlands themselves — the rocky lake country to the north and east — are best seen from a canoe, on a hike, or from the lookouts in [Haliburton Highlands Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/haliburton/haliburton_highlands_water_trails).
