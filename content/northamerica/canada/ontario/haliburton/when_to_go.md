---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Two short seasons matter for Haliburton. The cottage and canoe season is roughly mid-June through Labour Day weekend in early September, when the water is warm enough to swim, the lakes are busy, and almost everything is open. Autumn colour peaks from the last week of September through the second week of October — busy weekends, easy midweek. The town empties out in November, picks up again with snow for cross-country skiing and snowmobiling in January and February, and goes quiet through the spring melt and black-fly season in May and early June.
