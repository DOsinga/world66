Hamilton is a mid-sized city at the western end of Lake Ontario, about an hour from Toronto. It built its identity on steel — Stelco and Dofasco still dominate the eastern waterfront — but has diversified considerably since the industry's decline, with a growing arts scene centred on [James Street North](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/james_street_north), and a revitalized downtown built around the [Art Gallery of Hamilton](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/art_gallery), the [Hamilton Farmers' Market](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/hamilton_farmers_market), and the cafes of [Locke Street](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/locke_street).

The Niagara Escarpment runs through the city, creating the dramatic geography that gives Hamilton over 100 waterfalls within its city limits, more per capita than anywhere in the world. [Webster's Falls](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/webster_falls), [Albion Falls](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/albion_falls), and the geologically extraordinary [Devil's Punch Bowl](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/devils_punch_bowl) are the most photogenic. [Dundurn Castle](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/dundurn_castle), the [Canadian Warplane Heritage Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/canadian_warplane), and [HMCS Haida](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/hmcs_haida) are the main historic attractions; [Whitehern](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/whitehern), a three-generation McQuesten family home preserved exactly as left, is one of the best small-house museums in Canada. The [Royal Botanical Gardens](/northamerica/canada/ontario/hamilton/royal_botanical) at the Burlington border is worth a half-day, particularly in May for the world's largest lilac collection. The city is 70km from Niagara Falls.
