Crooks Hollow Historical Trail follows the remains of a former industrial community along Spencer Creek. Most buildings are gone, but the route passes ruins, old mill sites, and wooded creek scenery, and it links naturally with walks around Greensville, Webster's Falls, and Spencer Gorge.
