Gage Park Greenhouse gives Hamilton a warm, green indoor stop inside one of its best-loved city parks. The greenhouse holds tropical plants and seasonal displays, making it especially welcome in winter or on rainy days.

It is a modest attraction, but it pairs well with a walk through Gage Park's formal gardens, paths, and open lawns. Families and plant lovers will get the most out of the detour.
