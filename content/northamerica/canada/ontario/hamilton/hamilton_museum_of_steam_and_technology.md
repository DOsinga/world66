The Hamilton Museum of Steam and Technology preserves the 19th-century waterworks that helped supply the growing industrial city. Its great steam engines are the stars: huge, elegant machines that make Victorian engineering feel both practical and theatrical.

This is one of Hamilton's strongest heritage museums because it connects machinery, public health, and the city's industrial rise. It pairs well with other east-end and waterfront stops, especially for travellers who like working history rather than polished nostalgia.
