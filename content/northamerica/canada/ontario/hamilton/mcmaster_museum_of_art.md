McMaster Museum of Art is a small but serious university gallery on the McMaster campus. Its collection and temporary exhibitions give Hamilton visitors an indoor cultural stop beyond the city's industrial heritage and waterfront.

It is most useful if you are already near the university or interested in Canadian and international art in a quieter setting. Check current exhibitions first, because the strength of a visit depends heavily on what is on view.
