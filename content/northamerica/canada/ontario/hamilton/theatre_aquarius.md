Theatre Aquarius is Hamilton's main professional theatre, a useful evening option if you want culture after a day of waterfalls, museums, or harbour walks. Its programming typically mixes contemporary plays, musicals, and Canadian work rather than touring-only fare.

Check the schedule before building plans around it, because the attraction is the performance rather than the building alone. When something good is on, it gives downtown Hamilton a more rounded feel than the city's industrial reputation suggests.
