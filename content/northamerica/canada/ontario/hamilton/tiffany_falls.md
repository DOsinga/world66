Tiffany Falls is one of Hamilton’s classic escarpment waterfalls: easy to reach, pretty in most seasons, and framed by a short wooded walk rather than a long hike. The water drops in a broad ribbon over the Niagara Escarpment, with a viewing platform that keeps the fragile slope protected.

It is a strong choice if you want a waterfall stop without committing to a full conservation-area day. Go early on weekends, wear shoes that can handle mud or ice, and combine it with nearby Dundas Valley or other west-end escarpment sights.
