Westfield Heritage Village is an open-air museum that gathers historic buildings from around southern Ontario. Churches, houses, workshops, and public buildings are arranged as a walkable village, often animated by costumed interpreters and seasonal demonstrations.

It is outside central Hamilton, but it gives useful context for the rural and small-town history behind the modern industrial city. It is best with a car and enough time to wander slowly rather than rushing it as a quick photo stop.
