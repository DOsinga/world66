The Workers Arts and Heritage Centre is a small museum and cultural centre focused on labour history, working people's lives, and social justice movements. Exhibits connect Hamilton's industrial past with wider stories of unions, migration, activism, and everyday work.

It is a good counterpoint to Hamilton's waterfront and military museums, especially if you are interested in the city as a place shaped by factories, organizing, and working-class culture.
