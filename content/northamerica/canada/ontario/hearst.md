Hearst is a small town in Northern Ontario on Highway 11, deep in the boreal belt between Kapuskasing and Longlac. It calls itself the "Moose Capital of Canada", which tells you something about the scale of the surrounding forest and the town's sense of humour.

This is logging country. Sawmills and forestry still shape the local economy, and the town has a long history as a railway and highway stop across the north. Hearst is also one of Ontario's most strongly French-speaking communities, so travellers will hear French in shops, cafes, and on the street even though English is widely understood.

Most visitors arrive by car while crossing Northern Ontario, or come in winter for snowmobiling on the long trail network. In summer, nearby lakes and parks make Hearst a practical base for fishing, paddling, camping, and quiet northern road trips.
