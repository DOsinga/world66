Fushimi Lake Provincial Park is the main outdoor draw near Hearst. It has drive-in campsites, backcountry sites, swimming, fishing, and short hiking trails through the northern forest.

The park is not dramatic in the Lake Superior sense. Its appeal is quieter: a lake, forest, loons, and enough space to feel properly away from the highway.
