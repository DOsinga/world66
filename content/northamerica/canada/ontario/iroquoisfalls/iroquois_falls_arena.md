story: "Small northern Ontario towns measure themselves partly by their hockey arenas, and Iroquois Falls takes this seriously. The town's Senior A team, the Iroquois Falls Ice Dogs, plays here through the winter months, and games draw a loyal crowd from across the region. The arena has a certain old-fashioned northern Ontario character — wooden bench seating, a community canteen — that has become harder to find as rinks in larger centres get rebuilt."
---

The local arena in Iroquois Falls hosts Senior A hockey through the winter season, offering an authentic slice of small-town northern Ontario hockey culture. If you are passing through between November and March, a home game is worth attending for the atmosphere as much as the sport. The schedule is posted at the arena and on the town's community notice boards.
