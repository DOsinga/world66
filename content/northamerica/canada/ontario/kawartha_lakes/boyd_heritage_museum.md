The Boyd Heritage Museum is a small community museum in Bobcaygeon, focused on the Boyd family and their role in the area's lumber, farming, and civic history. It gives useful local context for a village that grew around the Trent-Severn Waterway.

The collection is modest, but it is worth a short stop if you are already exploring Bobcaygeon or want to understand how the Kawarthas developed beyond the cottage-country postcard.
