---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating in Kawartha Lakes is mostly an informal business — diners, lockside snack bars, an increasing number of bakeries and small breweries. The notable concentration is in **Bobcaygeon** and **Fenelon Falls**, where the lockside trade keeps a half-dozen restaurants busy through the summer. **Lindsay** has the only proper downtown dining, with a handful of better-than-average places along Kent Street.

Local specialities are predictable but reliable: pickerel from the lakes (the Ontario name for walleye, served pan-fried or in beer batter), butter tarts (the Kawarthas have a small running rivalry with the Muskokas over who makes the best ones), and Kawartha Dairy ice cream — the local creamery in Bobcaygeon has a cult following and a queue out the door on warm afternoons.
