Fenelon Falls is a small town 30 kilometres north of Lindsay where the Trent-Severn Waterway descends through a lock beside a genuine waterfall on the Fenelon River. The falls are modest — a few metres — but the combination of rushing water, lock machinery, and a pretty main street makes it one of the more appealing towns in the region.

The waterfront here is less busy than Bobcaygeon and the downtown has a handful of good cafes and art studios. Cameron Lake and Sturgeon Lake stretch away in either direction. This is a good base if you want to paddle or fish and prefer to avoid the more touristed spots.
