title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kawartha Lakes is a region rather than a city, and most of what you come for is on the water. The Trent-Severn Waterway runs north-south through the middle of the municipality, threading together a chain of large lakes — Pigeon, Sturgeon, Cameron, Balsam — through historic locks at **Bobcaygeon** and **Fenelon Falls**. The lockside villages are the social centres of the area in summer: ice cream, antique shops, lift bridges, and tied-up cruisers.

Inland, **Lindsay** is the largest town and has the only real downtown, with a small theatre, museum, and a busy farmer's market. The provincial park at **Balsam Lake** has the best public beach. For something more unusual, walk out across the deck of **Doube's Trestle Bridge**, a hundred-year-old railway viaduct now repurposed as part of the Victoria Rail Trail.
