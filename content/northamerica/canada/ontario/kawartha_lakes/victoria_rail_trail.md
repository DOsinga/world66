The Victoria Rail Trail follows an old CN rail corridor through Kawartha Lakes, running from Bethany through Lindsay, Fenelon Falls, and on toward Kinmount. It is used year-round for walking, cycling, horseback riding, snowshoeing, cross-country skiing, and snowmobiling.

For visitors, the most convenient taste is around Lindsay, where the trail links into Ken Reid Conservation Area and the broader regional trail network. It is long enough to plan a proper ride, but easy enough to sample in a short walk.
