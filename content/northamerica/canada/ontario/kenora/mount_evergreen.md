Mount Evergreen is Kenora's local ski hill and outdoor recreation area, set close enough to town to feel like a community facility rather than a remote resort. In winter it offers downhill skiing and snowboarding on a friendly scale.

Outside ski season, the area still has value for events and outdoor activity, but travellers should check what is operating before heading out. It is best for active visitors, families, or anyone spending more than a quick lakeside stop in Kenora.
