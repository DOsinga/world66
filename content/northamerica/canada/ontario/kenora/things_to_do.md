---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Kenora's centre is small and walkable, with the Harbourfront, Husky the Muskie at McLeod Park, and a couple of historic buildings worth a look. Everything else worth doing is on or near the water: fishing, boating, swimming off the docks, or taking the M.S. Kenora out for a Lake of the Woods cruise. In winter the same lake becomes a snowmobile and ice-fishing landscape.
