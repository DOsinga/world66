Point Clark Lighthouse is a mid-19th-century Lake Huron lighthouse south of Kincardine. Built in 1850, it is one of Ontario's imperial towers, a sturdy stone design meant to guide shipping along a rough stretch of coast.

The lighthouse operates as a museum in season. Climb the tower for a broad view over the lake and the small shoreline community around it.
