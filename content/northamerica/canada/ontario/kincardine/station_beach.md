Station Beach is Kincardine's easiest stretch of Lake Huron shore, a sandy beach beside the harbour with a boardwalk and room for a swim, picnic, or sunset walk. The lake can be cold and windy even in summer, but on a clear evening this is the place to understand why people make the detour.
