Frontenac County Schools Museum is a small, volunteer-flavoured museum about local school life, especially the one-room schoolhouse era. Desks, books, maps, and classroom objects make it a gentle nostalgia stop rather than a major institution.

It is worth a look if you enjoy local history or are travelling with children who can compare old classrooms with their own. Kingston's forts, university, and waterfront are bigger draws, but this museum adds a modest human detail to the city's past.
