MacLachlan Woodworking Museum focuses on tools, timber, craft, and the material culture of woodworking in Ontario. It is a niche museum, but the subject fits Kingston's old-building and lake-country surroundings well.

Because it sits outside the city centre, it is best for travellers with a car and a specific interest in craft or rural history. Check seasonal hours before driving out.
