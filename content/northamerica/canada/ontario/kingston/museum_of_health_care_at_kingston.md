The Museum of Health Care at Kingston is a small museum devoted to the history of medicine, with instruments, artefacts, and exhibits connected to hospital care and medical practice. It is most worthwhile for visitors with an interest in medical history, and pairs naturally with a walk around Queen's University and Kingston General Hospital.
