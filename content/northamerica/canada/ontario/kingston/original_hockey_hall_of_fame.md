The Original Hockey Hall of Fame in Kingston preserves the city's claim as one of hockey's early homes. Founded in 1943, it predates the better-known Toronto hall and keeps memorabilia tied to early organized hockey, including Kingston's 19th-century role in the sport.

It is most rewarding for hockey fans and sports-history travellers, but the local angle gives it more interest than a standard trophy room.
