The Princess of Wales' Own Regiment Military Museum is a small Kingston collection with a long local memory. Uniforms, medals, weapons, photographs, and regimental material connect the citys military presence to Canadian conflicts and community service.

It is a specialist stop, but Kingston is a military town as well as a university and waterfront town. Visit it with Fort Henry or the old armoury area if that side of the city interests you.
