Kingston's waterfront is one of the city's best casual walks, running past Queen's University, Kingston General Hospital, City Park, small beaches, lawns, and public sculpture. It is not a single blockbuster sight, but it gives you the lakefront version of Kingston and pairs easily with downtown or the university campus.
