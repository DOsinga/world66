---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

London is two hours by car from Toronto and Detroit on Highway 401, the country's busiest motorway. VIA Rail runs five trains a day from Toronto's Union Station (about two hours) and continues west to Sarnia and Windsor. London International Airport (YXU) handles short-haul flights from Toronto, Calgary, and a handful of US destinations.

Greyhound is gone, but Megabus and Flixbus run cheap buses several times a day to Toronto. The downtown VIA station on York Street is a five-minute walk from Victoria Park.
