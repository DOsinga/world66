The cathedral church of the Anglican Diocese of Huron, built in 1846 in pointed Gothic Revival yellow brick and stone, with a square crenellated tower and a high vaulted interior. The original wood-and-plaster ceiling was replaced after a 1922 fire but the rest is essentially the building Queen Victoria's contemporaries saw. Stained glass by Tiffany Studios of New York; concerts and choral evensong worth checking the schedule for.
