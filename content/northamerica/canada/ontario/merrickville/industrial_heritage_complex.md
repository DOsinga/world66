The Industrial Heritage Complex gathers old foundry tools, woollen mill machinery, household equipment, and power-generation gear from Merrickville's working past. It is small, but it helps explain why the village grew here before it became a day-trip destination.

Pair it with the lockstation and blockhouse. Together they turn Merrickville from a pretty canal village into a place where transport, defence, and local industry all overlapped.
