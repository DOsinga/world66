The Art Gallery of Mississauga is a compact public gallery in the city centre, with four gallery spaces and a rotating programme of contemporary Canadian and international art. Exhibitions change often, so check what is on before making it the focus of a trip.

It is an easy cultural stop if you are already around Celebration Square, the Civic Centre, or Square One, and it adds a quieter counterpoint to the area's malls and public events.
