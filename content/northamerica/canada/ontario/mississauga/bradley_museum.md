Bradley Museum is a small heritage complex in Mississauga with restored nineteenth-century buildings, domestic interiors, gardens, and exhibits on local settler history. It gives the sprawling modern suburb a rare tangible link to its village and farm past.

The museum is most worthwhile if you are already exploring the lakeshore communities or travelling with children. It is quiet and local, but that makes it a useful counterpoint to Mississauga's malls, highways, and condo skyline.
