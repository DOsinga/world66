Brueckner Rhododendron Gardens is a quiet Lake Ontario garden in Port Credit, planted with rhododendrons, azaleas, and companion shrubs that make their best show in late spring. Outside bloom season it still works as a gentle waterfront walk, but the colour is the reason to time a visit.

It pairs naturally with nearby Jack Darling Memorial Park and the Port Credit waterfront. Mississauga is not overburdened with delicate public gardens, so this one stands out precisely because it is small, local, and seasonal.
