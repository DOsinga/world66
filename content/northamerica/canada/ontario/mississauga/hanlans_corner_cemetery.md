A small, easy-to-miss pioneer cemetery on Derry Road in the north of the city, marooned between warehouses and the perimeter of Pearson airport. The stones — many of them weathered to the point of being illegible — record the families that farmed this land in the 19th century before Mississauga as a city even existed.

There is no entrance gate and no information panel. The Mississauga Heritage Foundation maintains the site, and it is locked behind a chain-link fence, but you can see most of it from the roadside. It is a particularly quiet kind of monument.
