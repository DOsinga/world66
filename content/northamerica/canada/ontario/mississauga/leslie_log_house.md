Leslie Log House is a restored 19th-century log building in Streetsville, preserving a rural layer of Mississauga that is easy to miss in the modern suburb. Its value lies in scale: a small domestic building that makes early settlement feel concrete.

The site is best for travellers already exploring Streetsville, the Credit River, or Mississauga's scattered heritage houses. Check opening times, as interiors and programming can vary, but the exterior and setting still make a worthwhile short stop.
