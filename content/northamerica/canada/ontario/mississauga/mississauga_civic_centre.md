The Civic Centre is Mississauga's city hall — a strikingly postmodern building from the late 1980s with a slim clock tower, a conical council chamber, and a great staircase running up through the entrance pavilion. The complex was one of the city's first attempts at giving itself a centre, before the towers and the Square One mall built up around it.

Inside, a wide public concourse is usually open during business hours. Outside, **Celebration Square** is the city's main public space — an open plaza with a fountain that turns into a skating rink in winter and a stage for outdoor concerts and a Friday night film series in summer.
