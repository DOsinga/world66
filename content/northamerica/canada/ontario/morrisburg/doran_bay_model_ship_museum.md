Doran Bay Model Ship Museum is a small private collection west of Morrisburg, with detailed ship models covering merchant sailing vessels, War of 1812-era craft, and later icons such as RMS Titanic. It is a specialist stop rather than a broad regional museum, but anyone interested in maritime history or careful model work will find more here than the size of the building suggests.
