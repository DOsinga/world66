Prehistoric World is a wooded outdoor park filled with dinosaur sculptures, just west of Upper Canada Village. It is old-fashioned, family-oriented, and a little eccentric, which is part of its charm.

This is not a scientific museum to plan a whole trip around. It is better as a fun add-on for children after the heavier history of the riverfront sites.
