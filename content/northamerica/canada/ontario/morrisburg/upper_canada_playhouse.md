Upper Canada Playhouse is Morrisburg's local theatre, set in a former factory building and running a regular season of comedies, musicals, and Canadian stage favourites. It gives the village a cultural stop after the daytime history sites close.

Check the schedule before planning around it. For travellers already staying along the Seaway, an evening performance can turn Morrisburg from a daytime stop into an overnight pause.
