The Resource Management Centre Trails are a wooded network near the Muskoka River, used for hiking and mountain biking in warm weather and groomed cross-country skiing and snowshoeing in winter. They are a good choice when you want an easy outdoor stop close to Bracebridge rather than a full backcountry outing.
