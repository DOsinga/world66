Wilson's Falls is the wilder middle stop on Bracebridge's waterfall circuit. The river bends and drops through a long rocky chute here, with rough side paths giving partial views through the trees.

The marked trail is not difficult, but it is less polished than the downtown river walks. Wear shoes with grip, especially when the rocks are wet, and take your time rather than trying to turn it into a quick roadside photo stop.
