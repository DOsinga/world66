The Gravenhurst Opera House has been the town's main stage since 1901. It is not a grand metropolitan theatre, but that is the charm: a compact heritage room with professional summer theatre, concerts, films, comedy, and community events.

Check the calendar before you come. Seeing the building is pleasant; catching a show is what makes it part of a good Gravenhurst evening.
