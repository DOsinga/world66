The Group of Seven Outdoor Gallery turns Huntsville and the wider Muskoka area into an open-air art walk. More than 90 mural replicas refer to the Group of Seven painters and Tom Thomson, whose northern landscapes helped shape Canada's idea of itself. Start by the Algonquin Theatre and follow the map as far as your time allows.
