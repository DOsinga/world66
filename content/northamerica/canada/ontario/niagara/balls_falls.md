Ball's Falls Conservation Area protects a pretty stretch of the Twenty Valley, with upper and lower waterfalls, short trails, and a small historic hamlet. It is a quieter escarpment stop than the Niagara River gorge and works well as a break between wineries.

The falls are strongest after rain and in spring. In dry spells they can shrink to a trickle, but the wooded valley and old stone buildings still make the detour worthwhile.
