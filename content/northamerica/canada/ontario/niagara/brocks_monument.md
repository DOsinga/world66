Brock's Monument rises above Queenston Heights, one of the key War of 1812 sites on the Niagara River. The column is austere and dramatic, and the park around it gives wide views toward the river, Lake Ontario, and the escarpment.

Climbing the monument is the memorable part when it is open, but the grounds are worth a stop even without the stairs. The battlefield trails give Niagara's border geography some historical weight beyond the usual falls-and-wine circuit.
