The Floral Clock is a cheerful Niagara Parkway stop north of the falls, with a large planted clock face whose design changes with the season. It is not profound, but it is very Niagara: tidy, photogenic, and oddly hard to dislike.

Stop here if you are driving between Niagara Falls and Niagara-on-the-Lake, especially in spring or summer when the bedding plants are fresh. The visit takes minutes rather than hours.
