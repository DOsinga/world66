The Bruce Trail follows the Niagara Escarpment across the region, linking conservation areas, vineyard country, waterfalls, and wooded ridges. Around Niagara it is a practical way to turn the escarpment from scenery into a proper walk.

You do not need to hike far to get the point. Pick a section near Ball's Falls, Short Hills, or the benchlands and you get limestone cliffs, forest, and broad views over one of Ontario's most distinctive landscapes.
