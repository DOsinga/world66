---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Niagara-on-the-Lake is not a late-night town, but it does well for daytime wine bars and historic pubs. The Olde Angel Inn on Regent Street is the most atmospheric — a 19th-century coaching inn with low ceilings and good local beer on tap, popular with theatre-goers and cyclists. The wineries on the Niagara Parkway offer tasting rooms that function as de facto afternoon bars, with local wines by the glass and cheese boards.
