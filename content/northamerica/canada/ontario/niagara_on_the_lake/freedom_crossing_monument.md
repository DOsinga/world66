The Freedom Crossing Monument stands by the Niagara River and depicts freedom seekers arriving in Canada on the Underground Railroad. The sculpture also remembers local stationmaster Josiah Tryon, who helped people cross the river from the American side.

It is a compact but meaningful stop near Queenston, best combined with the area's other War of 1812 and Niagara River history sites.
