Laura Secord Homestead is a restored house museum in Queenston, tied to one of the best-known civilian stories from the War of 1812. The visit explains Laura Secord's warning journey in the context of border warfare, local settlement, and Niagara's strategic position.

Although it sits outside Niagara-on-the-Lake's old town, it fits naturally with Queenston Heights, Fort George, and other War of 1812 sites. It is a good stop for travellers who want the Niagara region's history, not only wineries and scenery.
