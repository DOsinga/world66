The Living Water Wayside Chapel is a tiny white chapel on the Niagara Parkway, often promoted as one of the smallest chapels in the world. Whether or not the record claim holds up, the building is undeniably charming: a miniature roadside sanctuary with room for only a handful of people.

It is a quick stop, not a major attraction. Pull in if you are driving between Niagara-on-the-Lake wineries and the river road, take a respectful look, and move on. The chapel works because it is so slight: a small, odd pause in a region full of grander tourism machinery.
