Mackenzie Printery and Newspaper Museum preserves the world of hand presses, type, and early Canadian newspaper culture in Queenston. It is connected with William Lyon Mackenzie, the reformer and publisher whose politics helped shape nineteenth-century Canada.

The museum is small, but it is a good fit with Niagara-on-the-Lake's layered loyalist, military, and reform history. Pair it with Queenston Heights or other Niagara Parkway stops rather than making it your only outing.
