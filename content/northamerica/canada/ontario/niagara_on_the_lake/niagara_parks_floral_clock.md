The Niagara Parks Floral Clock is exactly what it sounds like: a large working clock face planted with thousands of bedding flowers and changed with the seasons. It sits along the Niagara Parkway, making it an easy pause between Niagara Falls, Queenston, and Niagara-on-the-Lake.

No one needs an hour here, but it is a classic Parkway stop and a pleasant piece of mid-century civic showmanship. It is especially worthwhile if you are already driving the river road and want a quick, photogenic break from forts, wineries, and waterfall crowds.
