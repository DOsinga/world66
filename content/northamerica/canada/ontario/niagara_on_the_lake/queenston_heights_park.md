Queenston Heights Park sits above the Niagara River on the escarpment, combining picnic lawns, formal gardens, and one of the key battlefields of the War of 1812. Brock's Monument gives the site its vertical landmark and its historical focus.

For Niagara-on-the-Lake travellers, it is a useful counterpoint to wineries and theatre. The views, monuments, and trails make it a strong stop on the route between the old town and Niagara Falls.
