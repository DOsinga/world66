St. Mark's Anglican Church is one of Niagara-on-the-Lake's oldest surviving buildings and the oldest Anglican church in Ontario. Construction began in 1800, and the church still carries the layered history of the War of 1812, when its roof was destroyed and later rebuilt.

It is a quiet stop rather than a grand one, but it fits naturally into a walk through the old town. The churchyard adds to the sense of early colonial Niagara.
