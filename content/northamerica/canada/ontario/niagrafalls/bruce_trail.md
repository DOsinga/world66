The Bruce Trail passes through the Niagara Escarpment landscape around Niagara Falls, offering a very different experience from the crowded viewpoints beside the water. It is a way to trade mist, souvenir shops, and traffic for woodland paths and limestone terrain.

You do not need to be a long-distance hiker to use it here. Pick a manageable section, check access points, and treat the trail as a reminder that Niagara is also an escarpment and river landscape, not just a single famous cascade.
