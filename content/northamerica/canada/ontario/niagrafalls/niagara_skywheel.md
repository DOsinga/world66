Niagara Skywheel is a large observation wheel on Clifton Hill, with enclosed climate-controlled gondolas and a short ride above the area's arcades and attractions. The main reason to go is the view over Niagara Falls and the surrounding skyline, especially after dark when the falls and strip are lit.
