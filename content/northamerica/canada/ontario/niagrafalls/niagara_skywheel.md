The Niagara Skywheel is a big observation wheel on Clifton Hill, with enclosed gondolas and broad views over the falls, the river, and the neon-heavy tourist district. The ride is short and commercial, but the height gives a useful sense of how the Canadian and American falls sit in the gorge.

It is best treated as a light add-on rather than a main attraction. Families, first-time visitors, and anyone wanting an easy evening view will get the most out of it.
