Niagara's Fury is a 360-degree audio-visual show run by Niagara Parks near the brink of Horseshoe Falls. The 45-minute presentation explains how the falls were formed, then turns into a light, mist, and motion-floor experience.

It is more theme-park interpretation than natural wonder, but it works well for families and for days when the weather makes the outdoor viewpoints less inviting.
