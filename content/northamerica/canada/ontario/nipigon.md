Nipigon is a small town on the north shore of Lake Superior, where the Trans-Canada Highway reaches one of its lonelier and more beautiful stretches. It sits near the mouth of the Nipigon River, between [Thunder Bay](/northamerica/canada/ontario/thunder_bay) and the scattered communities of the lake's north shore.

The town is practical first: fuel, food, a place to sleep, and a useful stop before the highway divides east and north. But it also has a real outdoors reason to linger. The [Nipigon River Recreational Trail](/northamerica/canada/ontario/nipigon/nipigon_river_recreational_trail) follows the river toward Red Rock with lookouts over forest, water, and the big sweep of Lake Superior country.

Nipigon's history is tied to fur trade routes, fishing, forestry, and the highway itself. The [Nipigon Historical Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/nipigon/nipigon_historical_museum) is small, but it helps explain why this modest village has long mattered as a crossing point in northern Ontario.
