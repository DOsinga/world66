The Nipigon River Recreational Trail runs about 10 kilometres one way between Nipigon and Red Rock. The first section from Nipigon Marina is relatively easy, while the full route has steeper stretches and several lookout points.

It is the best way to turn Nipigon from a highway stop into an actual Lake Superior experience. Wear proper shoes, bring water, and check daylight if you plan to walk the whole trail.
