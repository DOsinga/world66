---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Oakville's dining scene is concentrated along Lakeshore Road East in the Old Oakville area and around the Kerr Village strip to the west. The town has a good range of options, from fine-dining rooms with serious wine lists to casual spots near the harbour. Hexagon is the headline name, holding a Michelin star, but there is plenty to eat well without that price tag.
