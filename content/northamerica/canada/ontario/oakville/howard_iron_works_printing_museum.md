Howard Iron Works is a specialist museum and restoration workshop devoted to printing presses, type, and the machinery of the printed page. It is a niche subject, but the collection is unusually hands-on and industrial for Oakville.

This is most rewarding for travellers who like craft, design, engineering, or local industrial history. Confirm visiting arrangements before going, since workshop-style museums can have more limited public hours than civic museums.
