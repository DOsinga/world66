Sovereign House is a small historic house museum near Bronte Harbour, preserving one of Oakville's older lakeside buildings. It gives a modest but useful window into the community that grew around the harbour before modern suburban Oakville spread inland.

The house is best visited as part of a Bronte waterfront walk rather than as a standalone museum trip. Its scale is intimate, and opening times can be limited, so check before going.
