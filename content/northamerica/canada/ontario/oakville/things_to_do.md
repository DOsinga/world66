---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Oakville punches above its weight for a town of its size. The waterfront trail along Lake Ontario links the historic downtown to the quieter Bronte Harbour, and the Sixteen Mile Creek ravine provides an unexpected slice of nature in the middle of the suburbs. Glen Abbey is a pilgrimage for golfers, and the Oakville Museum keeps track of the town's surprisingly interesting past.
