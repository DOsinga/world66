Oshawa sits on the eastern edge of the Greater Toronto Area, close enough to be swallowed by the commuter map but still distinct in mood. Its identity was built around cars: the McLaughlin carriage and motor companies, General Motors Canada, and more than a century of industrial work.

That history gives the city its best traveler stops. The [Canadian Automotive Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/oshawa/canadian_automotive_museum) is the obvious place to start, and [Parkwood Estate](/northamerica/canada/ontario/oshawa/parkwood_estate) shows what automotive money bought in the early 20th century. North of downtown, the [Ontario Regiment Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/oshawa/ontario_regiment_museum) adds a surprisingly strong military-vehicle collection.

Oshawa is not Toronto's prettiest day trip, and it still has a plain industrial edge. But for travelers interested in Canadian car history, grand houses, or working-city Ontario, it clears the one-day test.
