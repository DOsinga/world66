The Canadian Automotive Museum is the place that makes Oshawa's car-town identity visible. It fills a former dealership with Canadian-built cars, early motoring oddities, and machines tied to the factories that shaped the city.

You do not need to be a mechanic to enjoy it. The museum works best as social history: a look at how cars changed work, suburbs, travel, and status in 20th-century Canada.
