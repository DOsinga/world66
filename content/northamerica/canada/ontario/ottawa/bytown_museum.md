Bytown Museum is a compact local-history museum at the foot of Parliament Hill, beside the Rideau Canal locks. Its exhibits explain Ottawa's early years as Bytown, before the lumber town became Canada's capital.

It is a useful stop if you are already visiting Parliament Hill, the canal, or the ByWard Market area and want the city's origin story in one place.
