The Canada Agriculture and Food Museum brings a working farm into the capital, with barns, animals, machinery, and exhibits about food systems. It is especially strong for families because the subject is tangible rather than abstract.

Its Central Experimental Farm setting makes it feel different from Ottawa's downtown national museums. Come for a slower, hands-on look at agriculture, and pair it with nearby gardens or cycling routes if the weather is good.
