---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

[Gatineau Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/gatineau_park) is the obvious half-day or full-day escape — 361 square kilometres of Canadian Shield wilderness beginning fifteen minutes from downtown. The **Ottawa River** west of the city offers some of the best whitewater rafting in North America — operators run day trips from spring through autumn out of the Foresters Falls area, about 90 minutes upriver. The [Diefenbunker](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/diefenbunker) at Carp, 35 kilometres west, fills a half-day on its own. Further afield, **Montreal** and **Kingston** are both reachable in two hours by car or train for a long day out.
