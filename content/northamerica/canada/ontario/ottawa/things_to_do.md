Ottawa's sights cluster in three groups. The political core sits on [Parliament Hill](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/parliament_hill) above the Ottawa River, with the [National War Memorial](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/national_war_memorial) and [Château Laurier](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/chateau_laurier) a short walk away. The national museums are spread across the city — the [National Gallery of Canada](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/national_gallery_of_canada), [Canadian Museum of History](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/canadian_museum_of_history), [Canadian War Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/canadian_war_museum), [Canadian Museum of Nature](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/canadian_museum_of_nature), and [Royal Canadian Mint](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/royal_canadian_mint) are all within walking distance or a short transit ride. The [Canada Aviation and Space Museum](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/canadian_aviation_space_museum) and [Diefenbunker](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/diefenbunker) are further out but well worth the trip. And then there are the neighbourhoods and parks: [Gatineau Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/gatineau_park) for wilderness, [Major's Hill Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/majors_hill_park) for views, and [Central Experimental Farm](/northamerica/canada/ontario/ottawa/central_experimental_farm) for the improbable pleasure of watching cows graze within sight of Parliament.
