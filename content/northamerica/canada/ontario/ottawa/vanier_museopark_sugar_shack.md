The Vanier Museopark Sugar Shack brings a classic eastern Canadian spring ritual into the city. Set in Richelieu Park, it is part of the Vanier Museopark and connects maple syrup traditions with the neighborhood's francophone history.

It is most rewarding in maple season, when meals and syrup-focused activities make the place feel lively. Outside that window, treat it as a seasonal heritage stop and confirm what is open before crossing town.
