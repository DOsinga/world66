The Bruce Trail gives Owen Sound direct access to the Niagara Escarpment, with side trails linking town paths to Inglis Falls and longer routes toward the Bruce Peninsula and the Blue Mountains. It is best treated as a flexible hiking option rather than a single viewpoint: choose a short escarpment walk if you are staying in town, or use Owen Sound as a staging point for a longer section hike.
