Inglis Falls is one of Owen Sound's best nearby natural stops, where the Sydenham River drops over the Niagara Escarpment into a wooded gorge. Several viewpoints make it easy to photograph the falls, and the surrounding conservation area connects to a trail network with walks of varying difficulty.

The falls are worth visiting in any season: autumn brings wide valley views, while winter can leave the rock face framed by ice.
