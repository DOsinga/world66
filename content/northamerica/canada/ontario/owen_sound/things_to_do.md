---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Owen Sound's best sights split neatly between the escarpment and the town. Inglis Falls is the headline natural stop, with trails, gorge views, and the Sydenham River falling over limestone. Harrison Park is gentler but very useful, with forest paths and a direct connection toward the Bruce Trail. In town, the Tom Thomson Art Gallery is the essential cultural stop, especially if you are travelling through the Georgian Bay landscapes that shaped Thomson's work.
