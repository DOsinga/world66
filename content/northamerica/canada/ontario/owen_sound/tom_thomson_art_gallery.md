The Tom Thomson Art Gallery is Owen Sound's key art stop, with work connected to Thomson, the Group of Seven, and contemporary artists from the region and across Canada. It is especially worthwhile if you are travelling through the Georgian Bay landscapes that shaped Thomson's painting.
