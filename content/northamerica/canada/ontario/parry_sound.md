Parry Sound is a Georgian Bay harbour town at the edge of the Thirty Thousand Islands, where pink granite, wind-bent pines, and cold blue water start to feel more northern than southern Ontario. The town itself is practical and modest, but the setting is first-rate.

The best day here gets you onto the water. A [Thirty Thousand Islands cruise](/northamerica/canada/ontario/parry_sound/thirty_thousand_islands_cruise) shows the broken-rock coast better than any roadside lookout, while the [Museum on Tower Hill](/northamerica/canada/ontario/parry_sound/museum_on_tower_hill) gives the town a good landward viewpoint.

Parry Sound also has one very Canadian claim: it is Bobby Orr's hometown. The [Bobby Orr Hall of Fame](/northamerica/canada/ontario/parry_sound/bobby_orr_hall_of_fame) will not convert everyone to hockey, but it adds a strong local story to a day of islands, harbour walks, and Shield scenery.
