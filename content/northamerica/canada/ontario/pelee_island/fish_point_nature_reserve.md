Fish Point is the southern tip of Pelee Island, and it feels like one: sand, scrub, water, and birds moving along the Lake Erie flyway. It is one of the island's strongest nature stops, especially in spring and autumn migration.

Trails and beach access vary with weather and lake levels. Bring binoculars if you have them, and give the place time; the point is not just the view but the movement of birds through it.
