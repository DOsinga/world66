Lighthouse Point protects the island's northern shore, where marsh, beach, and open lake give Pelee a different feel from the southern point. The old lighthouse area adds a maritime note to the birding and shoreline walk.

It pairs well with a slow bicycle loop around the island. Watch conditions, stay on marked access where required, and expect wind.
