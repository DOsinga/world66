The Heritage Centre is the best first stop if you want Pelee to make sense. Exhibits cover the island's natural habitats, archaeology, farming, shipwrecks, and the people who have lived with a ferry schedule and a very large lake.

It is compact, but it adds depth to a cycling day. Stop here before heading to Fish Point or Lighthouse Point.
