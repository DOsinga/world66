Pelee Island Winery gives the island's climate a taste. The company farms hundreds of acres of vines here, taking advantage of Lake Erie's warmth and the long growing season at Canada's southern edge.

The island pavilion is the relaxed version of the experience: tastings, bottles, casual food in season, and a reminder that Pelee is agricultural as much as scenic.
