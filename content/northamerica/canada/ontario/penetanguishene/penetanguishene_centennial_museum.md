The Penetanguishene Centennial Museum sits in an 1875 lumber office and general store. It is a modest local museum, but a useful one: the exhibits tie together the town's Indigenous, French, military, lumbering, and harbour stories.

Visit it before wandering the older streets. Penetanguishene makes more sense when you know why this small town has both garrison churches and a strong French-Ontarian thread.
