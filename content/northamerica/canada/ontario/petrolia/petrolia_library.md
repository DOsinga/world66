Petrolia's public library is one of the original Carnegie-funded libraries built across Canada in the early 20th century — opened in 1907 and still in continuous use as a library. The architecture is the typical Carnegie classical revival: a temple-front portico, broad steps, and large arched windows.

Inside, much of the original fabric survives. It's worth a few minutes' detour from Petrolia Line if you're walking the heritage district.
