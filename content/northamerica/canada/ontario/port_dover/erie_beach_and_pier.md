Erie Beach is Port Dover's main summer draw. It is not wild or remote, but it has the classic small-town Lake Erie mix of sand, casual food, families, anglers, and people wandering down to the pier for the view.

On a hot day, this is where Port Dover makes the most sense. Pair it with the lighthouse and a plate of local perch.
