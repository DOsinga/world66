Lake on the Mountain Provincial Park is a small picnic and viewpoint stop above the Bay of Quinte. Its oddity is the lake itself: a freshwater lake sitting high above nearby Lake Ontario, with no obvious source for its clean water.

There is no admission charge, and the stop is easy to combine with the Glenora ferry or a drive through Prince Edward County.
