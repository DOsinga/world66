Little Bluff is a quiet Lake Ontario lookout with high limestone cliffs, a stony beach, and broad views over the water. The shoreline is less developed than the county's busier beach stops, which makes it a good place for a picnic, a swim from the rock beach, or a simple view stop.

The cliffs are the main draw, so come for the lake panorama rather than facilities.
