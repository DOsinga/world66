Macaulay Heritage Park gathers a restored historic house, gardens, a cemetery, and a museum in a converted church on the edge of Picton. It is one of the better places in Prince Edward County to get beyond wineries and beaches and into the county's older Loyalist and farming history.

The house is especially atmospheric around Christmas, when it is decorated with antique ornaments and the fireplaces are part of the experience.
