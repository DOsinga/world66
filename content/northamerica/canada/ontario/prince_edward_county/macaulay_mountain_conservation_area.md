Macaulay Mountain Conservation Area is a wooded outdoor area near Picton with picnic spots, hiking and mountain-bike trails, birdwatching, and a winter toboggan hill. Its most memorable feature is Birdhouse City, a cluster of miniature buildings made as birdhouses, many of them modelled on local heritage structures.

It is a good low-key stop if you want a walk away from the county's beaches and wineries. Summer children's camps can make parts of the area busy, but the trails still give a useful dose of shade and quiet.
