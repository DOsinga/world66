Mariner's Park Museum covers Prince Edward County's maritime history, from fishing and shipbuilding to ice harvesting, rum running, and shipwrecks. The collection includes wreck material recovered by divers, the relocated False Duck Island lighthouse, Fort Kente, and a large rowboat.

There is also a picnic area on the grounds.
