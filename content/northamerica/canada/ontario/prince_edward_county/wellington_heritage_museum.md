Wellington Heritage Museum covers the local history of Prince Edward County, including the Quaker families who helped shape the area. Exhibits also explain the county's once-important canning industry, with displays and artefacts from local plants.

It is a small museum, but a good stop for context if you are spending time around Wellington or the county's wine route.
