Quinte West is a small city of about 45,000 at the western end of the Bay of Quinte, where the Trent River runs into Lake Ontario. It is a 2001 amalgamation of the old town of **Trenton** and the surrounding villages of Frankford, Sidney and Murray, and most travellers know it for two things: the southern terminus of the **Trent–Severn Waterway**, and **CFB Trenton**, the largest air base in the Canadian Forces.

The Trent–Severn is the reason to come. Boats begin (or end) the 386-kilometre canal-and-river journey from Lake Ontario to Lake Huron right in downtown Trenton at [Lock 1](/northamerica/canada/ontario/quintewest/trent_severn_lock_1), and the locks here are pleasant places to watch summer traffic. The waterfront has been tidied up with a park and a pier, and the [National Air Force Museum of Canada](/northamerica/canada/ontario/quintewest/national_air_force_museum) at the base is the country's largest aviation museum, with a hangar full of restored military aircraft and an outdoor air park you can wander through for free.

Otherwise Quinte West is a useful stop on the drive between Toronto and Kingston, or a base for visiting **Prince Edward County** wine country and the dunes at Sandbanks Provincial Park, both about half an hour to the south.
