---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Quinte West sits on Highway 401 between Toronto (175 km west) and Kingston (85 km east) — virtually every cross-province drive passes through or near it. The Trenton exit (exit 526) puts you in downtown within minutes.

**By train:** VIA Rail stops at Trenton Junction, a short distance outside the main town. Trains run between Toronto and Ottawa/Montréal, making Quinte West accessible by rail if you plan ahead — trains are not frequent, so check the VIA schedule.

**By bus:** Coach Canada and other regional operators serve the area. The Trenton bus terminal connects to Toronto, Ottawa, and Kingston.

**By car:** From Toronto, the 401 east takes about 1 hour 45 minutes in normal traffic. From Kingston, it is under an hour west.
