---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Quinte West's main draws are the **National Air Force Museum of Canada** and the start of the **Trent–Severn Waterway** — two things that reward a half-day stop even if you're just passing through on the 401. The waterfront park around Lock 1 is a relaxed spot for lunch and watching boats negotiate the first lift of a very long journey.
