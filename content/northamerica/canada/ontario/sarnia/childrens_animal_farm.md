The Children's Animal Farm is a small family attraction inside Canatara Park, close to Sarnia's Lake Huron waterfront. It is simple rather than flashy, with domestic animals and a park setting that works well for young children.

The farm is best treated as part of a Canatara Park visit, not as a standalone destination for adults. Families can combine it with the beach, playgrounds, picnic areas, and easy walks through the park.
