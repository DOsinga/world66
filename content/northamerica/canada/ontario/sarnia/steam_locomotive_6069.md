Steam Locomotive 6069 gives Sarnia a tangible historical marker: something you can stand in front of, walk through, or use to understand how the place changed over time. It is a compact stop, but a useful one when the surrounding streets need context.

Read the on-site interpretation if it is available, then take a few minutes to look around the immediate area. These smaller historic sites often work best when they are folded into a walk rather than treated as a destination on their own.
