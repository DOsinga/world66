A large, well-done aviation and forestry museum on the waterfront in downtown Sault Ste. Marie. The collection spans Curtiss HS-2L flying boats through to more recent water bombers, all restored and displayed in the cavernous heritage hangar where they once lived. The exhibits focus on how bush flying made Northern Ontario accessible and how aerial firefighting evolved from improvised beginnings.

Even if you have no particular interest in aircraft, the hands-on approach and the quality of the restoration work make this worth an hour or two. The location, right on the St. Marys River, adds to the atmosphere — you can see where the floatplanes once landed.
