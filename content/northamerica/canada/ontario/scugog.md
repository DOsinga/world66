Scugog is a rural township northeast of Toronto, best known through Port Perry, its lakeside service town on the southern shore of Lake Scugog. It is not a major destination in the Ontario sense; it is a good small day out when you want water, a heritage main street, and countryside without driving all the way into cottage country.

The spare-day case depends on keeping the visit focused. [Port Perry Downtown](/northamerica/canada/ontario/scugog/port_perry_downtown) has the brick storefronts, cafes, galleries, and brewery stops. [Palmer Park and the Lake Scugog Waterfront](/northamerica/canada/ontario/scugog/palmer_park_lake_scugog_waterfront) give you the marina and shoreline. [Scugog Shores Museum Village](/northamerica/canada/ontario/scugog/scugog_shores_museum_village) adds local history on Scugog Island, while [Nonquon Provincial Wildlife Area](/northamerica/canada/ontario/scugog/nonquon_provincial_wildlife_area) is the wilder edge of the township.

Scugog is not worth bending a whole Ontario itinerary around, but it clears the World66 bar as a relaxed local excursion from Toronto, Oshawa, or the Kawarthas. Come for a slow loop rather than a checklist.
