title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Scugog is strongest as a slow lunch, coffee, or brewery stop in Port Perry. The most useful approach is to walk the downtown first, then choose somewhere close to Queen Street or the waterfront.

[Old Flame Brewing Co.](/northamerica/canada/ontario/scugog/old_flame_brewing_co) is the most distinctive drink stop, set in a historic downtown building and close enough to the lake for an easy before-or-after walk.
