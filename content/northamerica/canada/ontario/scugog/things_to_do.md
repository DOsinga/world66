---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Most visitors should begin in [Port Perry Downtown](/northamerica/canada/ontario/scugog/port_perry_downtown), then walk down to [Palmer Park and the Lake Scugog Waterfront](/northamerica/canada/ontario/scugog/palmer_park_lake_scugog_waterfront). That gives the township its two strongest pleasures: a small heritage main street and open water.

If you have more time, cross to Scugog Island for [Scugog Shores Museum Village](/northamerica/canada/ontario/scugog/scugog_shores_museum_village), or head northwest to [Nonquon Provincial Wildlife Area](/northamerica/canada/ontario/scugog/nonquon_provincial_wildlife_area) for a quieter walk through wetland and forest.
