Heritage House Museum is a restored 19th-century home near Old Slys Lockstation, built for the Bates family when mills, canal traffic, and railways were shaping Smiths Falls. Its period rooms and exhibits cover local life rather than national drama, which is exactly the scale that works here.

The museum is worth adding to a canal walk if you want the domestic side of the town's transport history. It also gives a good excuse to continue east from the downtown locks toward the quieter Rideau waterfront.
