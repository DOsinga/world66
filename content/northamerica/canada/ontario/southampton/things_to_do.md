---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Southampton's main attraction is the beach and the lake. The long sandy strand on Lake Huron is one of the finest on the Ontario shore, facing west for the famous sunset views. The Chantry Island lighthouse, reached by summer boat tour, offers a combination of Lake Huron scenery and Victorian heritage architecture. The Saugeen River, which meets the lake at the north end of town, is popular for fishing and canoeing.
