Adcock's Woodland Gardens is a small, seasonal garden stop rather than a major botanical garden. Its appeal is the setting: beds and paths worked into the island's wooded landscape, with a slower, more personal feel than a public park.

It works well as a short break on a circuit of the island, especially if you are already here for fall colour or a quiet summer drive.
