AlgomaTrad gives St. Joseph Island a cultural stop beyond museums and scenery. The non-profit teaches and presents traditional music, dance, and crafts, with concerts and summer programs that can make a visit feel much less like a simple island drive.

Check the calendar before you go. Without an event or class it is mainly a point of interest; with one, it can be the reason to stay into the evening.
