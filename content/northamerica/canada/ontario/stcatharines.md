St. Catharines is the largest city in the Niagara region of [Ontario](/northamerica/canada/ontario), sitting on the shore of Lake Ontario at the northern end of the Welland Canal. Most travellers pass through on their way to [Niagara Falls](/northamerica/canada/ontario/niagrafalls), but the city makes a decent base for exploring the wider peninsula.

The [Welland Canal](/northamerica/canada/ontario/stcatharines/welland_canal_lock3) itself is worth a stop — the St. Catharines Museum and Lock 3 viewing platform let you watch enormous lake freighters rise and fall through the locks. The surrounding [Niagara wine country](/northamerica/canada/ontario/stcatharines/niagara_wineries), with its concentration of icewine producers, is within easy reach. [Port Dalhousie](/northamerica/canada/ontario/stcatharines/port_dalhousie) has a historic harbour, beach, and 1898 carousel; downtown clusters around the renewed [FirstOntario Performing Arts Centre](/northamerica/canada/ontario/stcatharines/firstontario_performing_arts) and the Frederick Olmsted-designed [Montebello Park](/northamerica/canada/ontario/stcatharines/montebello_park).
