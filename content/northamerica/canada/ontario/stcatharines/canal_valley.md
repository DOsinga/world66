Canal Valley is a linear green space in St. Catharines shaped by the city's canal history. Paths and open areas follow land once tied to the Welland Canal, giving walkers a mix of industrial memory, neighbourhood edges, and surprisingly quiet pockets of green.

It is not a blockbuster sight, but it helps explain the city better than another roadside stop would. Use it for a walk or cycle ride when you want to connect downtown St. Catharines with the waterways that made the place matter.
