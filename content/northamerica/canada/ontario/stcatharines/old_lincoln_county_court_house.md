The Old Lincoln County Court House is one of downtown St. Catharines' more distinctive 19th-century buildings. Built in 1848-49 and modelled partly on the town hall of Perugia, it has a clock tower, an octagonal cupola, and enough Italianate flourish to stand out from the surrounding streets. It is a quick architectural stop near the centre of town.
