---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

For a town of 33,000 Stratford has a serious food scene. The local culinary college — the Stratford Chefs School — has been training chefs since the early 1980s, and many of them have stayed on or come back to open their own places. Reservations during festival season are essential; midweek matinee days are easiest.
