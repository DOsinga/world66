The red-brick city hall on the central square is Stratford's most distinctive building — a 1899 Queen Anne pile with a clock tower, conical turrets, and the kind of civic confidence that small Ontario towns once had in abundance.

It still functions as the town hall and is best appreciated from the surrounding square, which doubles as a market space on Saturdays in summer.
