The local history museum sits in a converted farmhouse on the western edge of town. The permanent displays cover Perth County agriculture, the railway boom, and the early years of the festival. The big draw, surprisingly, is the Justin Bieber exhibition — a permanent gallery of memorabilia from the singer's Stratford childhood that pulls international fans.

Admission is modest. The grounds include a small heritage garden and a walking trail along the Avon.
