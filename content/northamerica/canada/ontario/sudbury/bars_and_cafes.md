---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Sudbury's nightlife clusters along Elgin Street downtown, with a mix of student-friendly pubs, a couple of craft beer bars, and the long-running Townehouse Tavern as the city's main live music venue. Coffee culture has caught up in recent years, with several independent roasters and cafes spread between the downtown and the Donovan neighbourhood.
