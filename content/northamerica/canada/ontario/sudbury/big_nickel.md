A 9-metre-tall stainless-steel replica of a 1951 Canadian five-cent piece, standing on a low hill at the edge of [Dynamic Earth](/northamerica/canada/ontario/sudbury/dynamic_earth). The world's largest coin in any reasonable sense of the term, and an unabashed monument to the mineral that built the city.

You can walk right up to it — there is no admission for the coin itself — and the wider grounds include other oversized coin replicas. Best visited together with Dynamic Earth, but worth a separate stop if you are just passing through and want a photo.
