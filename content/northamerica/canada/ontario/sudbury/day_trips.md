The best day trip from Sudbury is **Killarney Provincial Park**, about 90 km southwest. The white quartzite ridges of the La Cloche Mountains were a favourite painting subject for the Group of Seven, and the park's lakes — Topaz, OSA, and Killarney itself — are some of the most beautiful canoe waters in Ontario.

**Manitoulin Island**, the world's largest freshwater island, lies south via Espanola and the small swing bridge at Little Current — about a 2-hour drive. The drive itself is worth it, through the rocky shield country and across the bridge with the boats backed up either side.

**[North Bay](/northamerica/canada/ontario/north_bay)**, on Lake Nipissing two hours east, makes a longer day out if you want a contrasting Northern Ontario town.

For a shorter trip, the French River — birthplace of the voyageur fur-trade route — is an hour south and offers boat tours and short trails along the gorge.
