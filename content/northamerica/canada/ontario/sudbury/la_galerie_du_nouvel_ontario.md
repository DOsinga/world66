La Galerie du Nouvel Ontario presents contemporary and experimental Canadian art, with a particular focus on French Canadian artists. The gallery is small, but it adds a sharper contemporary edge to Sudbury's cultural scene.

It is most worthwhile if you are already downtown or are interested in northern Ontario's Francophone arts community.
