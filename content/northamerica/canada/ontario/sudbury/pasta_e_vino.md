A long-running downtown Italian restaurant with a wood-fired oven turning out solid thin-crust pizzas and a pasta menu that leans homemade. The room is warm and busy, with hanging copper pans and a chalkboard of daily specials.

Family-run, mid-priced, and a good first-night stop. Reservations advisable on weekends, particularly when there is a show at the [Sudbury Community Arena](/northamerica/canada/ontario/sudbury/sudbury_community_arena).
