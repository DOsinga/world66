Science North is Northern Ontario's main science museum and consistently rated one of the best in Canada. Spread across two seven-storey hexagonal buildings cantilevered over Ramsey Lake, it has the usual hands-on physics and biology galleries, a butterfly room and beaver pond, a digital planetarium and IMAX cinema, and a tornado simulator that genuinely soaks you.

What lifts it above other regional science museums is the staff. "Bluecoat" scientists — including university researchers — wander the floor and will happily talk through the exhibits at any level. Aim for a full half-day; combined tickets with [Dynamic Earth](/northamerica/canada/ontario/sudbury/dynamic_earth) save money.
