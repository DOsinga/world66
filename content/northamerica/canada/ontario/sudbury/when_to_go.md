---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Sudbury has a continental climate with four very distinct seasons. Summers are warm and dry, with highs of 25 °C and the lakes warm enough for swimming from late June into early September. Winters are long, snowy, and properly cold — January averages around -13 °C, with -30 °C cold snaps once or twice a year.

**June to September** is the obvious window for outdoor activities — paddling, hiking, festivals, and patio life. Northern Lights Festival Boréal takes place in early July, Up Here urban arts festival in mid-August.

**Late September to mid-October** brings excellent fall colour across the surrounding bush. The summer crowds have gone and the air is sharp.

**December to March** is cold but Sudbury embraces it — cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, ice fishing, and Wolves hockey games all peak here. Ramsey Lake freezes over and the city looks completely different.

**April and November** are the in-between months — too late for snow sports, too early for hiking. Avoid unless you have business in town.
