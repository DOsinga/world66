Neys Provincial Park protects a fine sand beach and wild Lake Superior shoreline east of Terrace Bay. Trails lead through boreal forest and lakeside habitats, while the visitor centre explains the area's Second World War prisoner-of-war camp and its connection to Group of Seven painter Lawren Harris.
