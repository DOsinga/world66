Eagle Canyon is a private nature attraction east of Thunder Bay, known for its suspension bridges over a narrow forested canyon. The bridges are the headline, but the appeal is broader: cliffs, boreal forest, short trails, and the sense of entering rougher Canadian Shield country.

It is a day trip rather than a city sight, so plan around opening dates and driving time. In the right season it offers a more dramatic outing than a simple roadside lookout, especially for travellers who want a controlled but still memorable taste of the landscape beyond Thunder Bay.
