A red-brick monument to Thunder Bay's Finnish heritage, the Finnish Labour Temple on Bay Street is a Heritage Canada property and the most visible reminder of the city's deep Finnish roots. The building has hosted political rallies, cultural performances, and the legendary Hoito Restaurant in its basement — for decades the city's mandatory Finnish-pancake breakfast spot, until it closed in 2020.

Efforts to reopen the Hoito in some form continue. The Labour Temple itself remains a working community building and is worth a walk past in a city tour that includes the Bay and Algoma district.
