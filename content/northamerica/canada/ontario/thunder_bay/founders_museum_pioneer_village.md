Founders' Museum and Pioneer Village is an open-air heritage site outside central Thunder Bay, with relocated and reconstructed buildings that evoke the region's early settler period. The village format makes the material more tangible than a room of cases: school, shop, church, and domestic interiors show how people lived and worked.

It is best for travellers who have time beyond the waterfront and major natural sights. The location is outside the core, so combine it with other east-side or rural stops rather than making a special trip for a short visit.
