Big Tub Lighthouse stands at the mouth of Big Tub Harbour, close to the wreck sites that made Tobermory famous with divers. The lighthouse itself is modest, but the harbour setting is classic Bruce Peninsula: clear water, rock ledges, and boats moving between town and the islands.
