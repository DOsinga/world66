The Bruce Trail Cairn marks the northern end of the 900-kilometre Bruce Trail. Even if you are not starting or finishing the whole route, it is a satisfying marker: the Niagara Escarpment has run all the way here, ending at Georgian Bay in a harbour town built around rock and water.

From town, short side trails lead toward Dunks Point and Georgian Bay viewpoints.
