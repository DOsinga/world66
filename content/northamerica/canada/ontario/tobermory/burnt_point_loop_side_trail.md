Burnt Point Loop Side Trail is a short Bruce Peninsula walk near Tobermory, starting near the Bruce Trail cairn and looping toward the northeast edge of the peninsula. The route is about 3.5 km and has several lookouts over Georgian Bay.

It is a good choice when you want a coastal walk that is shorter than the longer national park hikes, with enough limestone shoreline and open water views to feel distinct from the quick roadside lookouts.
