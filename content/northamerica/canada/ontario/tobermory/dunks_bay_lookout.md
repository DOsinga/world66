Dunks Bay Lookout gives a short, easy view over the rocky edge of the Bruce Peninsula near Tobermory. It is close to the park visitor centre, so it works well as a quick first stop before longer walks, boat trips, or lighthouse visits.

The appeal is simple: limestone shoreline, clear water, and a broad view across the bay. It is not a long hike, but it is a useful orientation point for the peninsula's landscape.
