Flowerpot Island is the boat trip most visitors have in mind when they picture Tobermory. The island's sea stacks rise from the Georgian Bay shore like carved limestone towers, with trails linking them to caves, beaches, and the old light station.

Access is by boat only, and the island is part of Fathom Five National Marine Park. Go early or outside peak weekends if you want the rock formations without the heaviest crowds.
