Aga Khan Museum is a major Toronto museum devoted to Islamic art, Iranian art, and Muslim cultures. Its collection includes manuscripts, ceramics, metalwork, paintings, and other objects from across a wide geographic span.

The building and surrounding grounds are part of the visit, so allow time for both the galleries and the calm exterior spaces.
