This preserved Banksy mural shows a policeman and dog on concrete slabs protected in a glass case. The work was salvaged from a demolished building and is the only surviving Toronto piece from Banksy's 2010 visit, making it a brief but worthwhile stop for street-art fans near the waterfront.
