Biidaasige Park sits at the mouth of the Don River, where new wetlands and walkways have reshaped a once-industrial edge of Toronto's waterfront. It is a good place to see how the city is trying to give the river a softer landing into Lake Ontario.

There are launch points for kayaks, canoes, and stand-up paddleboards, and the paths make an easy short walk if you are exploring the eastern harbour area.
