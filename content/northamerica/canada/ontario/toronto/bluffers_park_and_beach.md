Bluffer's Park and Beach sits below the Scarborough Bluffs, the pale cliffs that run along this part of Lake Ontario. The draw is the combination of beach, marina, lake views, and cliff scenery, especially when the waves are up.

Parking is limited on summer weekends, and the road down to the park is steep if you arrive on foot. It is still one of Toronto's strongest natural viewpoints and worth the trip east from the centre.
