Campbell House is one of Toronto's oldest surviving buildings and a rare downtown example of Georgian architecture.

The house was saved from demolition in the 1970s and moved to its current site. Today it works as a compact historic-house museum, useful for seeing an older layer of Toronto beside the city's civic and theatre district.
