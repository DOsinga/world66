Cloud Gardens is a small downtown park with a waterfall wall and a compact greenhouse planted with palms, tree ferns, and other tropical species. A walkway climbs from the lower entrance toward the upper exit, making the little space feel larger than it is.

It is not a destination on the scale of Allan Gardens, but it is a useful green pause in the Financial District and a good short stop if you are walking between downtown theatres, offices, and shopping streets.
