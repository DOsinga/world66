Edwards Gardens is a landscaped ravine park in North York, with flower beds, lawns, bridges, and walking paths that feel far removed from downtown Toronto. The adjoining Toronto Botanical Garden adds themed plantings and seasonal displays, so it works well as a slower, greener stop between the city's bigger museums and waterfront sights.
