Franklin Children's Gardens is a small themed garden on the Toronto Islands, based on the Franklin the Turtle books. Bronze figures, a turtle pond, planting beds, benches, and short paths make it especially useful for families with young children.

It is best treated as part of a Toronto Islands outing rather than a stand-alone trip from the city.
