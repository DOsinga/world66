Glen Stewart Ravine is a short wooded escape in the Beaches, with a stream, boardwalks, footbridges, and a trail that feels surprisingly wild for this part of Toronto. The route is only about 600 metres long, but the steep northern section and thick tree cover make it feel more like a ravine walk than a city shortcut.

It is best folded into a Beaches wander: Queen Street, Kew Gardens, the boardwalk, and this shaded ravine give the neighbourhood more variety than the lakefront alone.
