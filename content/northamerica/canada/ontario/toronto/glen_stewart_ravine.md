Glen Stewart Ravine is a short wooded ravine walk in The Beaches, with a stream, wooden footbridges, and a steep gravel trail tucked behind the residential streets north of Queen Street East.

The trail is only about 600 metres long, but it gives a quick change of pace from the lakefront and shopping streets. It fits well with Kew Gardens, Woodbine Beach, or a wider walk through the neighbourhood.
