Graffiti Alley is a run of back lanes near Queen Street West covered with murals, tags, and large-scale street art. The best-known stretch is around Rush Lane, where the walls change often and make a good short walk for photographers.

It is not a polished outdoor gallery, which is part of the appeal. Go in daylight and combine it with Queen West or Kensington Market.
