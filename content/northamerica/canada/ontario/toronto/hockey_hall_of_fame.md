The Hockey Hall of Fame is the central Toronto stop for anyone who cares about Canada's national winter obsession. Exhibits cover great players, teams, trophies, and the mythology of the game, with enough interactive material to keep non-experts from drifting off.

The Stanley Cup is usually here, which is the main draw for many visitors. If seeing it matters, check ahead, because the Cup still travels.
