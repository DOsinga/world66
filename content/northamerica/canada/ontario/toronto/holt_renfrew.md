The flagship of Canada's grande dame of luxury retail, anchoring the stretch of Bloor Street West known as the Mink Mile — block for block among the most expensive shopping in the country. The store carries the designer houses you would expect across several floors of fashion, accessories, and beauty, with a café and a personal-shopping operation aimed at its well-heeled clientele.

Even without buying, it is the natural starting point for a walk along the Mink Mile, where the international luxury brands line both sides of Bloor between Yonge and Avenue Road, a block south of the Yorkville boutiques.
