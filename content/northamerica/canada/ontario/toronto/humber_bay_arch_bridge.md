Humber Bay Arch Bridge carries pedestrians and cyclists over the mouth of the Humber River, linking the western waterfront trail with Humber Bay Shores. Its pale steel arch is one of the more graceful pieces of Toronto's lakefront infrastructure, and the south side gives a long view back toward the CN Tower and downtown skyline.

It works best as part of a waterfront walk or bike ride rather than as a stand-alone trip. From here you can continue east toward Sunnyside and High Park, or west along the Humber Bay parkland.
