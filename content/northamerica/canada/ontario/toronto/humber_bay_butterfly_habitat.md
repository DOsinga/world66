Humber Bay Butterfly Habitat is a restored lakefront meadow planted with wildflowers, tall grasses, shrubs, and other butterfly-friendly plants. The best sightings depend on weather: butterflies are less active in wind, rain, and the hottest part of the day.

Even without many butterflies, it is a pleasant nature stop on the Etobicoke waterfront. Pair it with a walk through Humber Bay Park if you want a softer, greener break from central Toronto.
