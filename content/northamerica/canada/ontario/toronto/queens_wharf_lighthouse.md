Queen's Wharf Lighthouse is a small survivor from Toronto's working waterfront. Built in 1861, decommissioned in 1911, and moved to this park site in 1929, it now sits well inland from the harbour it once guarded.

It is a quick stop rather than a destination in itself, but the little white lighthouse adds a useful piece of history to a walk through the Fort York and Exhibition Place area.
