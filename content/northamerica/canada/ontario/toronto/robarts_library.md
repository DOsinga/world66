Robarts Library is a landmark of Brutalist architecture on the University of Toronto campus. Opened in 1973, it rises as a fourteen-storey concrete complex of raised podia, sharp triangular forms, and cantilevered mass.

The building divides opinion, which is part of the fun. Architecture-minded visitors should pair it with a walk through the older university grounds nearby, where Gothic revival colleges make the library look even stranger.
