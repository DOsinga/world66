Rosetta McClain Gardens is a formal garden above the Scarborough Bluffs, with rose beds, a rock fountain, pergolas, and broad views over Lake Ontario. It is a quieter stop than the busier bluff parks nearby.

The gardens work best as a slow walk or picnic stop, especially when the roses are in bloom or the lake view is clear.
