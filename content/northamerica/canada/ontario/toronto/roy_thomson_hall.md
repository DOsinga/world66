Roy Thomson Hall is the glass-walled concert hall that serves as home to the Toronto Symphony Orchestra. Its round form stands out among the towers of the Entertainment District, and the building is a useful landmark even for visitors not attending a performance.

The hall hosts classical concerts and other music events through the year. If you are interested in Toronto's performing arts scene, check the schedule alongside nearby venues such as the Four Seasons Centre and Massey Hall.
