St. Basil's Church is a compact Gothic Revival church on the University of St. Michael's College campus. Consecrated in 1856, it is the oldest University of Toronto building still in continuous academic use.

It is not one of Toronto's headline sights, but it is a worthwhile stop if you are already walking around the university or Bloor-Yorkville. The church gives the campus a pocket of 19th-century calm just off busy Bay Street.
