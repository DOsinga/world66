The Textile Museum of Canada is a compact museum devoted to historic fabrics, global textile traditions, and contemporary fibre art. Its exhibitions are strongest when they show how everyday cloth carries stories about trade, identity, ritual, and labour.

The museum sits close to City Hall and the Eaton Centre, making it an easy cultural stop in downtown Toronto.
