TIFF Bell Lightbox is the year-round home of the Toronto International Film Festival, with cinemas, galleries, talks, and film programming that runs well beyond festival season. The building anchors Toronto's film culture in the Entertainment District and is one of the better places in the city to catch repertory screenings or international releases.

The smaller gallery spaces are often free, while major exhibitions and screenings need tickets.
