Todmorden Mills Heritage Site preserves a small cluster of historic buildings in the Don Valley, including restored houses with period furnishings. It gives a compact look at the mills, settlement, and early industry that once lined this part of the river.

Behind the buildings, a short walking path loops through a former river bend and wetland. The mix of local history and green space makes it a worthwhile stop if you are exploring Toronto's east side.
