Toronto Fire Station 311, also known as Balmoral Fire Hall, is a 1911 Queen Anne-style fire station built for the horse-drawn era. Look for the double vehicle doors, the old hay-hoist opening, and the hose-drying tower at the rear. It is a short architecture stop rather than a museum, but a good one if you are walking through Deer Park.
