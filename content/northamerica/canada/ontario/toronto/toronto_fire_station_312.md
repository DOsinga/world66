Toronto Fire Station 312 is a small but charming Yorkville landmark, built in 1878 before the neighbourhood was absorbed into Toronto. Its Victorian Gothic brickwork, clock tower, and old Yorkville coat of arms make it one of the more characterful active fire halls in the city.

This is a look-from-the-street stop rather than a destination. Add it to a Yorkville walk with the Reference Library, Village of Yorkville Park, and the old residential lanes nearby.
