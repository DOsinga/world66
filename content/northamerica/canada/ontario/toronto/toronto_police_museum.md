The Toronto Police Museum & Discovery Centre is a small specialist museum inside Toronto Police Headquarters. Its exhibits cover the history and traditions of policing in the city, with displays that give a different angle on Toronto's civic history.

Because the museum is inside an active police building, visitors should allow time for security screening before entering.
