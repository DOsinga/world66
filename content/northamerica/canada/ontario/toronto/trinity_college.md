Trinity College is one of the University of Toronto's most handsome college precincts. Its main building has Jacobethan Tudor lines, while the chapel brings in a more English Gothic note, with sandstone outside and pale limestone within.

It is a quiet architectural stop rather than a headline attraction. Fold it into a walk through the university grounds, the Royal Ontario Museum area, or nearby Bloor-Yorkville.
