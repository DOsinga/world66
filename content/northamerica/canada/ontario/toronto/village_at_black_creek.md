The Village at Black Creek is a living-history museum built around restored 19th-century homes, workshops, shops, and gardens. It presents the kind of rural crossroads community that once surrounded Toronto.

It is especially useful for families and history-minded visitors who want a hands-on view of daily life before the modern city grew around the area.
