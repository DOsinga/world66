Winchester Street is one of Cabbagetown's handsome Victorian residential streets, linking Parliament Street with Riverdale Farm. The walk is lined with restored houses and front gardens, with the former St Enoch's Presbyterian Church as the main architectural marker.

It is not a major sight on its own, but it makes a good route between Cabbagetown's shops, Toronto Necropolis, and Riverdale Farm.
