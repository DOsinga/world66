title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Welland's identity is inseparable from the canal that cuts through the center of town, and watching ocean-going ships navigate the locks is the city's signature experience. Beyond the waterway, Welland has made a virtue of its industrial heritage through a celebrated mural program that has transformed canal-side walls into an outdoor gallery. The former canal channel also provides one of Canada's premier flatwater paddling and rowing venues.
