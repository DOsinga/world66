The Algonquin Way Cultural Centre focuses on Algonquin culture in the upper Ottawa Valley, with exhibits and programs around craft, canoe building, dance, drumming, and community history. It is the kind of small regional stop that helps explain whose land this is, not just what the scenery looks like.

Check current opening times before making a special detour, as programming can matter more here than the static displays.
