The Polish Kashub Heritage Museum and Skansen is the village's key stop. Its displays, log farmhouse, furniture, tools, and community objects tell the story of Kashubian families who settled this part of the Madawaska Valley from the 1850s onward.

It is small, seasonal, and personal rather than slick. That suits Wilno: the point is not a big museum day, but a clear sense of how a distinct community took root here.
