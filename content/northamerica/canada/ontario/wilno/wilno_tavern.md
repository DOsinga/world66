Wilno Tavern is the village's social stop, serving Polish and Kashubian comfort food such as pierogies, sausage, cabbage rolls, and other old-settler dishes. It is also where many travellers first realise Wilno is not just a sign on Highway 60.

Come for lunch rather than polish. The tavern works because it is part restaurant, part community room, and part cultural shorthand.
