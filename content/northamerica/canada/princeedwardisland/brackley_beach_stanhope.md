Brackley Beach-Stanhope is a loose north-shore stretch of Prince Edward Island rather than a single town. Brackley, Stanhope, Covehead and the lanes between them sit behind dunes, cottages, small farms and the protected beach country of Prince Edward Island National Park.

The reason to come is simple: beach time with a little more texture than a resort strip. [Brackley Beach](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/brackley_beach_stanhope/brackley_beach) has the broad sand and facilities, [Stanhope Beach](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/brackley_beach_stanhope/stanhope_beach) is usually calmer, and [Covehead Harbour Lighthouse](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/brackley_beach_stanhope/covehead_harbour_lighthouse) gives the area its postcard marker.

It is seasonal, and outside summer the place can feel half-asleep. In July and August, though, this is one of the easiest ways to get from [Charlottetown](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/charlottetown) to dunes, warm Gulf water, lobster rolls and a classic drive-in night.
