Brackley Drive-In is a summer-night throwback: park the car, tune the radio, and watch a double feature under a big PEI sky. It is not high culture, and that is exactly the point.

Bring layers and something comfortable to sit on if you plan to watch from outside the car. Mosquitoes can be part of the programme.
