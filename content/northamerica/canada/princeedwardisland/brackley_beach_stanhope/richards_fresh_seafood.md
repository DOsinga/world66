Richard's is the classic Covehead Wharf seafood stop: informal, busy in season, and best approached with beach clothes rather than ceremony. Lobster rolls, fish and chips and fried clams are the point.

Eat outside if the weather allows. The harbour setting does half the work, especially after a swim or a lighthouse stop.
