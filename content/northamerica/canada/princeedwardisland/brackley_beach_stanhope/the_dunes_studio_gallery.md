The Dunes is part gallery, part shop, part cafe, and more interesting than the average beach-road retail stop. Local art, ceramics, textiles and imported pieces fill the rooms, with gardens outside when the season is in full swing.

It works well on a cloudy beach day or as a lunch stop before heading into the park. Like most things here, it is strongly seasonal.
