Green Gables Heritage Place is the farmhouse and landscape that readers around the world associate with Anne of Green Gables. L.M. Montgomery knew the farm through relatives and used it as the model for the fictional home of Marilla, Matthew, and Anne.

The house is restored with period rooms, but the site is more than a set piece. Walk the short Haunted Wood and Balsam Hollow trails to get a better feel for the Cavendish landscape Montgomery turned into fiction. In peak summer, arrive early or late to avoid the tour-bus crush.
