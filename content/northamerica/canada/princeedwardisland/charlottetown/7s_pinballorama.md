Pinballorama on Prince Street is part arcade and part informal museum — a collection of vintage and modern pinball machines spanning decades of the form, from the electromechanical tables of the 1970s to the digital machines of the 2000s. The owner maintains them all in working order, which is the difficult part: vintage pinball machines require constant adjustment to stay playable.

It is a small venue and an unusual one — a serious collection in a city that doesn't typically attract this kind of niche preservation project. Good for an afternoon rainy-day diversion or for anyone who wants to play a working Williams machine from 1978. Pay per game, open most afternoons and evenings; hours vary by season.
