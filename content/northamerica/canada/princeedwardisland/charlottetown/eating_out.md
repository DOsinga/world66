---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Charlottetown eats well for a city its size. PEI oysters, lobster, and mussels are on every seafood menu and are excellent — the cold water and clean tidal flats produce some of the best shellfish in North America. Prices are reasonable compared to mainland cities. Victoria Row is the social hub in summer, with restaurants spilling out onto the closed street.
