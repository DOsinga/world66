Victoria Row is the stretch of Richmond Street between Queen and Great George, closed to vehicles in summer and lined with restaurants whose patios fill the road. It is the most social block in Charlottetown from June through September — a combination of good food, outdoor seating, and a density of restaurants that gives you options from seafood to pizza to upscale Canadian without walking further.

The buildings are late Victorian brick, the atmosphere is genuinely lively rather than forced, and the street fills up fast on summer evenings. It is the obvious answer to "where should we eat tonight?" in Charlottetown, and not just a tourist trap — locals use it too. In shoulder season it quiets down but the restaurants keep going year-round.
