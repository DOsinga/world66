Souris is a small, friendly town at the eastern tip of Prince Edward Island, about 60 km from Charlottetown. The town has clean sandy beaches along the Northumberland Strait, and the nearby [Basin Head Provincial Park](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/souris/basin_head_beach) — known for its "singing sands" — is a popular summer destination. Drag your foot across dry sand here and it squeaks and sings; the high quartz content makes Basin Head one of the finest examples of this phenomenon in Canada.

Souris is also the departure point for the [ferry to the Magdalen Islands](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/souris/magdalen_ferry) (Îles-de-la-Madeleine) in Quebec. The crossing takes five hours and the islands — remote red-sandstone archipelago in the Gulf of St Lawrence — are one of eastern Canada's most distinctive destinations. [Souris Beach](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/souris/souris_beach) Gateway Park, just west of town, has the characteristic red-tinged sand and warm Northumberland Strait swimming that eastern PEI is known for.

The local fishing economy is reflected in a good selection of seafood restaurants in town. East PEI is quieter and less developed than the western part of the island — visiting Souris means experiencing a PEI that hasn't much changed since the lobster boats have been working these waters.
