Summerside is Prince Edward Island's second city, a low-key harbour town on the island's south shore. It does not have Charlottetown's political drama or Cavendish's Anne crowds, but it has a strong local story: shipbuilding, silver fox farming, harness racing, and a waterfront that has been pulled back into public life.

The best reason to stop is the cluster of small museums and heritage sites around the old core. The [Summerside Interpretive Centre and PEI Fox Museum](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/summerside/summerside_interpretive_centre) explains the silver fox industry that once made this one of the world's centres of fur farming. The [Wyatt Historic House Museum](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/summerside/wyatt_historic_house) is more intimate, full of the objects and family stories of an upper-class Island household.

Down by the harbour, the [Baywalk Boardwalk](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/summerside/baywalk_boardwalk) and [Spinnakers' Landing](/northamerica/canada/princeedwardisland/summerside/spinnakers_landing) give Summerside its easiest summer rhythm: a walk along the water, seafood or ice cream, then a slow loop through the downtown streets. If you are here in July, harness racing and the Lobster Carnival pull the town into a louder, more Island kind of party.
