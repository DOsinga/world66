The Baywalk is Summerside at its simplest: water on one side, town on the other, and enough length for a proper walk or easy cycle. It follows the harbour past parks, wharf views, and pieces of the old industrial waterfront.

It is especially good near sunset, when the south-shore light softens and the working edges of the harbour look less ordinary.
