Eptek is a small waterfront gallery with rotating exhibitions and a permanent connection to Prince Edward Island art. It is not a blockbuster museum, but it works well as part of a harbour walk.

Look for local and regional shows rather than expecting a fixed collection. On a rainy day, it is one of Summerside's easiest cultural stops.
