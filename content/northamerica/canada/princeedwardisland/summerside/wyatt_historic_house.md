The Wyatt House was built in 1867, the year Canada became a country, and still feels more like a home than a staged period room. The collection is dense with furniture, clothing, books, and household objects kept by the Wyatt family over generations.

Go with a guided tour if one is available. The point is not just the house, but the stories of a well-connected Island family and the town that grew around them.
