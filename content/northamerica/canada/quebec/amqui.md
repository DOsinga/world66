Amqui is the main town of the Matapedia Valley, a long green corridor between the St Lawrence and the Gaspé Peninsula. It is not a big destination in the [Quebec](/northamerica/canada/quebec) sense of old stone streets and grand museums, but it is a useful and pleasant base if you are crossing eastern Quebec slowly.

The town sits where the Matapedia and Humqui rivers meet, surrounded by forested hills, farms, and salmon rivers. Its best-known sights are its covered bridges, especially [Pont Beauséjour](/northamerica/canada/quebec/amqui/pont_beausejour), which now links riverside parks for walkers and cyclists.

Amqui works best by car. Highway 132 runs through town, with [Montreal](/northamerica/canada/quebec/montreal) far to the west and the Gaspé beyond. In winter the valley turns toward skiing, snowmobiling, and cross-country trails; in summer it is about fishing, cycling, and unhurried drives between small villages.
