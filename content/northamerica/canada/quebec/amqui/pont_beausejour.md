Pont Beauséjour is the easiest place to start in Amqui. The red covered bridge crosses the Amqui River in Parc des Soeurs and gives the town a proper Quebec postcard moment without pretending to be grander than it is.

The bridge was built in the 1930s, later moved from the Rimouski area, and adapted for pedestrians and cyclists. It is a short stop, but a good one, especially if you are following the Matapedia Valley's covered-bridge route.
