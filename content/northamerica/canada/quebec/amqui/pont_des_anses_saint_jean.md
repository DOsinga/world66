Pont des Anses-Saint-Jean is a working covered bridge on the north side of Amqui. Built in 1931, it is narrow, practical, and still part of everyday rural traffic rather than a museum piece.

Pause before driving through, as there is only one lane. The detour is worthwhile if you like the older roadscape of eastern Quebec: timber bridges, farm clearings, and forest close at hand.
