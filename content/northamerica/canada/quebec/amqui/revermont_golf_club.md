Revermont Golf Club is a low-key summer stop just outside town. Its two nine-hole courses sit in different landscapes, with the Matapedia River helping to break up the round.

This is not a reason to cross Quebec, but it is a pleasant afternoon if you are based in Amqui and want an easy outdoor activity with valley views.
