Aylmer is a historic neighbourhood in the western part of [Gatineau](/northamerica/canada/quebec/gatineau), across the Ottawa River from Canada's capital. It was an independent town until 2002, when it was merged into Gatineau, but it still keeps its own character — quieter and more anglophone than the rest of the city, with tree-lined residential streets and a distinct small-town feel despite being minutes from downtown Ottawa.

The waterfront along Lac Deschênes is the main attraction. The [Aylmer Marina](/northamerica/canada/quebec/aylmer/aylmer_marina) and the beach at [Parc des Cèdres](/northamerica/canada/quebec/aylmer/parc_des_cedres) are popular in summer, and the [Voyageurs cycling path](/northamerica/canada/quebec/aylmer/voyageurs_cycling_path) along the Ottawa River connects through to Gatineau Park. The old main street has a handful of restaurants and shops, and the [Symmes Inn](/northamerica/canada/quebec/aylmer/symmes_inn), a stone building dating to 1831, now operates as a local history museum.
