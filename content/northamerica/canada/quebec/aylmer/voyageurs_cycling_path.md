The Voyageurs cycling path (Corridor des Voyageurs) runs along the Ottawa River from downtown Gatineau through Aylmer and on toward Gatineau Park. Aylmer is the middle section of this route, with flat, well-maintained trail passing through the marina area and Parc des Cèdres before climbing gently toward the park entrance.

The trail is multi-use and busy on summer weekends with cyclists, inline skaters, and joggers. The Aylmer section has views of Lac Deschênes for much of its length, with benches and rest areas at regular intervals. The full ride from Hull to the Gatineau Park visitor centre is about 20 km one way and is manageable in a day.
