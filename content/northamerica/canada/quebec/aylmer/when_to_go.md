---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer (June to August) is the obvious time to visit Aylmer if you want the beach and the water. The cycling paths are at their best, the marina is active, and the long evenings make the waterfront especially pleasant. Expect the beach to be crowded on weekends.

Late spring and early autumn are excellent for cycling without the crowds. Winter is cold — Ottawa and Gatineau regularly see temperatures below -20°C — but the trails stay usable for cross-country skiing and snowshoeing when there is enough snow. If you are based in Ottawa, Aylmer works as a half-day trip in any season.
