Lac Salé lies just inland from the village on the Piashti River system. It is a quiet water stop rather than a dramatic sight, but it helps explain why Baie-Johan-Beetz is more than a roadside settlement: rivers, lakes, bays, and the Gulf all meet here.

Use it for fishing, paddling, or a slow look at the village's backcountry side. It is best paired with the coastal walks rather than treated as a destination on its own.
