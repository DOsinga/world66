Cran de Lamant is a shore viewpoint near Baie-Saint-Paul, looking over the bay and its broad tidal flats. At low tide the sandstone and exposed foreshore can stretch far out from the municipal beach.

It is a short, simple stop rather than a major attraction, but it gives a good sense of the Charlevoix coast and the scale of the tides.
