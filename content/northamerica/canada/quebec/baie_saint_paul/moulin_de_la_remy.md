Moulin de la Remy is a historic flour mill associated with Charlevoix's rural heritage. It has been part of the local landscape since the 19th century and is now maintained as a heritage site rather than a working industrial stop.

It is most useful if you are exploring the countryside around Baie-Saint-Paul by car. The mill adds a small, concrete piece of agricultural history to a region better known for river views, galleries, and village streets.
