Baie-Saint-Paul's contemporary art museum anchors the town's gallery culture. It presents modern and contemporary work, runs exhibitions through the year, and is tied to the international art symposium that helped make the town known beyond Charlevoix.

It is the best first stop if you want to understand why this small place has such a strong art identity.
