Route du Fleuve is the scenic road east from Baie-Saint-Paul toward La Malbaie, with river views, old villages, and Charlevoix farms along the north shore of the St. Lawrence. It is broader than the town, but Baie-Saint-Paul is one of its natural starting points.

Use it if you have a car and want the landscape version of a Baie-Saint-Paul day. Even a short out-and-back gives you the river, hills, and light that define this part of Quebec.
