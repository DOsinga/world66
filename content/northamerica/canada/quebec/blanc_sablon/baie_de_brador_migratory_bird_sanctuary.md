Baie de Brador Migratory Bird Sanctuary protects islands and surrounding waters off Blanc-Sablon and Brador. The sanctuary is especially important for nesting seabirds, including Atlantic puffins, and it gives the coast a wild edge beyond the ferry wharf.

Access depends on season, weather, and local boat options. Even if you do not land anywhere, ask locally about viewpoints and respectful ways to watch birds without disturbing colonies.
