Mont Parent rises over the peninsula between Blanc-Sablon Bay and Brador Bay. It is not a big mountain, but in this low coastal landscape it gives a clear look at the villages, the strait, and the shape of the shore.

Use local trail information before setting out, especially in fog or rough weather. The reward is simple: a view that explains why this coast is all bays, boats, weather, and patience.
