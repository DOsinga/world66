Bioparc de la Gaspésie is a small wildlife park near the mouth of the Bonaventure River. The focus is on native species from Gaspé and Quebec, with a walking trail through habitats designed for close, easy viewing.

It is especially good with children, but adults get something from it too: a compact look at the animals and landscapes that shape the peninsula beyond the road.
