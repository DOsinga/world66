Cime Aventures is the practical way to get onto the Bonaventure River, whose clear water is one of the pleasures of this part of Gaspé. The outfitter runs canoeing, kayaking, tubing, stand-up paddleboarding, and other summer activities.

For most travelers, this is the stop that turns Bonaventure from a cultural pause into a real day outside. Book ahead in the high season, especially if you want a longer river descent.
