The Green Mountains Nature Reserve is the serious hiking landscape around Eastman and Sutton. It protects more than 78 square kilometres of Appalachian forest and links into one of southern Quebec's important wildlife corridors.

For travellers, the draw is simple: trail walking in a protected forest with better views and a wilder feel than the village parks. Check the current trail map before going, because the reserve is spread out and access points vary by sector.
