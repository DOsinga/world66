Les Correspondances d'Eastman is the event that gives the village its most distinctive cultural note. The festival is built around books, authors, public conversations, writing workshops, and the old-fashioned pleasure of sending letters.

It is worth planning around if you like French-language literary culture. Outside the festival dates there is no single sight to visit, but the event explains why this small village has a more thoughtful, bookish identity than many resort towns of the Eastern Townships.
