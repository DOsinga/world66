Parc Missisquoi-Nord is Eastman's easiest outdoor anchor: a 50-hectare municipal nature park at the edge of the village. Trails move between forest cover, more open patches, and the Missisquoi North River valley.

The main trail is short and approachable, so this is a good choice if you want a proper walk without committing to a mountain hike. It is also a useful first stop because the village, the river, and the wooded hills all make more sense once you have walked here.
