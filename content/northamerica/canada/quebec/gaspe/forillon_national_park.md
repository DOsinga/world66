Forillon protects the outermost cliffs, coves, forests, and fishing villages of the Gaspé Peninsula. The park is compact enough for a day trip but varied enough to feel wild: walk the Les Graves trail toward Land's End, watch for seals and whales offshore, or visit the Grande-Grave heritage sector for the human story of the coast.

Go early if you want to hike. The best viewpoints ask for real walking, and the weather can change quickly where the Gulf winds hit the cliffs.
