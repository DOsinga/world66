The Gespeg Mi'kmaq Interpretive Site introduces the Mi'kmaq history of the Gaspé Peninsula before and after European arrival. The centre uses exhibits, guided interpretation, and reconstructed spaces to explain seasonal life, tools, trade, and the community's continuing ties to the coast.

It is one of the better cultural stops around Gaspé if you want the peninsula to feel like more than cliffs, lighthouses, and sea views. Allow about an hour, more if you join a guided visit.
