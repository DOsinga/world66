Hyman & Sons General Store is a restored historic store at Grande-Grave in Forillon National Park. The buildings recall the fishing, trading, and community life that shaped this part of the Gaspe coast in the 19th and early 20th centuries.

It is most worthwhile as part of a broader visit to Forillon, especially for travellers interested in coastal history rather than only trails and sea views.
