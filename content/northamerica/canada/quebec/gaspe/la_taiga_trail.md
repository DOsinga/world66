La Taiga Trail crosses the flat sand spit of La Penouille through one of the southernmost patches of boreal forest in the world. The walk is short and easy, but the lichen, low conifers, and coastal light make it feel like a pocket of the far north dropped beside the Gulf of St. Lawrence.
