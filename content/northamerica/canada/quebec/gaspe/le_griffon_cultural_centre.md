Le Griffon Cultural Centre occupies an old clapboard cold-storage building by the harbour at L'Anse-au-Griffon. It mixes a seasonal cafe, local crafts, and the Claude Cote Gallery and Workshop, making it a good short stop for art and Gaspesian coastal atmosphere on the way to or from Forillon.
