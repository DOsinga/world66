Marie-Josée Gagnon Art Gallery is a small Pointe-Jaune gallery focused on colourful Gaspé landscapes, seascapes, and flower paintings. It is a good local-culture stop if you are driving the coast and want to see how regional artists interpret the peninsula's light and weather.
