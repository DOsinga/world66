Our Lady of Pointe-Navarre Shrine is a Catholic retreat and pilgrimage site several kilometres from central Gaspe. The complex includes a church, the Chapel of Remembrance, Father Jean-Marie Watier's tomb, and hillside paths with views over Gaspe Bay.

It is a quiet stop for travellers interested in religious sites or local history, and a peaceful short detour when driving between Gaspe and Forillon.
