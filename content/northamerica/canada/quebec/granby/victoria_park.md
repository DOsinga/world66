Victoria Park is Granby's old central garden, bought by the town in 1889 and landscaped around the turn of the 20th century. It is a small, easy-going park rather than a destination in itself, but it gives the centre of town a pleasant green pause.

In warm months the gazebo sometimes hosts free concerts, which is when the park feels most alive. At other times it is simply a good place to sit between the museum, cafes, and the rest of downtown Granby.
