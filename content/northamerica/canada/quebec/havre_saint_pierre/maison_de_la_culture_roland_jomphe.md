Maison de la Culture Roland-Jomphe is the small cultural centre that gives the town some context before you head for the islands. Its exhibitions and local programming cover Minganie history, coastal life, and the people who shaped Havre-Saint-Pierre.

It is not a grand museum, but it is the right scale for the place. Pair it with a harbour walk and you will leave with a clearer idea of why this remote settlement became the main visitor base for the archipelago.
