Hemmingford is a small village and township south of Montreal, close to the New York border. The village itself is quiet, but the surrounding countryside has a clear day-trip identity: apple orchards, ice cider, a big family safari park, and outdoor trails at Saint-Bernard.

The place clears the bar because its attractions are rural rather than civic. [Parc Safari](/northamerica/canada/quebec/hemmingford/parc_safari) is the obvious family draw, while [Verger Hemmingford](/northamerica/canada/quebec/hemmingford/verger_hemmingford), [Vignoble le Chat Botte](/northamerica/canada/quebec/hemmingford/vignoble_le_chat_botte), and [Parc Regional Saint-Bernard](/northamerica/canada/quebec/hemmingford/parc_regional_saint_bernard) make the area more than a one-attraction stop.

Come by car and think of Hemmingford as orchard country, not as a town to wander for hours. It is at its best in late summer and autumn, when cider, fruit, and country roads do most of the work.
