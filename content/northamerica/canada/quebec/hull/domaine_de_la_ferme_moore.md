Domaine de la ferme Moore is a heritage farm landscape in Gatineau, offering trails, gardens, and community-oriented green space near the urban edge. It provides a softer local stop than the region's federal museums and big Gatineau Park viewpoints.

Use it for a short walk, seasonal programming, or a quiet break if you are already on the Hull/Gatineau side of the river. Check current access and activities before making it the focus of a trip.
