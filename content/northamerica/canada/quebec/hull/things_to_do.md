title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Gatineau's main draw is the [Canadian Museum of History](/northamerica/canada/quebec/hull/canadian_museum_of_history), one of the most important museums in Canada, set in a stunning Douglas Cardinal–designed building on the Ottawa River with views across to Parliament Hill. [Gatineau Park](/northamerica/canada/quebec/hull/gatineau_park), just north of the city, offers 361 square kilometres of forests, lakes, and trails — including the [Mackenzie King Estate](/northamerica/canada/quebec/hull/mackenzie_king_estate), the former summer retreat of Canada's longest-serving prime minister. In the evening, the [Casino du Lac-Leamy](/northamerica/canada/quebec/hull/casino_du_lac_leamy) is the region's major entertainment and music venue.
