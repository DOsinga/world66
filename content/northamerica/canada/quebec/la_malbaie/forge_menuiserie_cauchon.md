Forge-menuiserie Cauchon is a working forge and woodworking site by the Malbaie River. Guided visits explain the craft work and the local setting, and the riverside picnic area makes it an easy low-key stop outside central La Malbaie.

It is most interesting for visitors who like small heritage workshops and traditional trades.
