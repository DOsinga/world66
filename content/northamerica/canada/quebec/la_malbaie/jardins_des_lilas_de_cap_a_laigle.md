The lilac gardens at Cap-à-l'Aigle give La Malbaie a softer, village-scale stop. The site is known for its large lilac collection, but it is also pleasant as a quiet walk through one of the town's most attractive sectors.

Timing matters. The garden is most rewarding around bloom season; outside that window, treat it as a gentle stroll rather than a headline attraction.
