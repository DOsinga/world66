The Musée de Charlevoix is the most useful cultural stop in La Malbaie. Its exhibitions connect folk art, local history, and the St. Lawrence landscape, which helps explain why this corner of Québec has such a distinct identity.

It is a good first stop on a mixed-weather day, or a calm counterweight to the bigger scenery inland.
