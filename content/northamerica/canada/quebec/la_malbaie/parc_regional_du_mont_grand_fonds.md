Parc regional du Mont Grand-Fonds is La Malbaie's mountain day out, with forest trails in summer and downhill and cross-country skiing in winter. The main warm-weather reason to come is the climb toward Lac Gravel and the summit area, where the views open across the Charlevoix hills and the St. Lawrence.
