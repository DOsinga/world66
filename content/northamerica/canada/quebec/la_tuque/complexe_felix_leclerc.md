Complexe Félix-Leclerc gives La Tuque a cultural stop between outdoor outings. It hosts performances and local programming, and its name nods to one of Québec's great singer-poets.

Check the schedule before building plans around it. On a short visit, it is best paired with the waterfall park and a walk through the centre rather than treated as a major museum.
