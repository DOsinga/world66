Parc des Trois Soeurs is a riverside municipal park south of La Tuque, with about 11 km of trails on the west bank of the Saint-Maurice River. The paths climb enough to give a real walk rather than a short stroll, and the setting works well for visitors looking for a quiet outdoor stop near town.
