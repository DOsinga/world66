La Tuque's real travel value lies beyond the street grid. The Upper Saint-Maurice region has dozens of outfitters and several ZECs, opening access to canoeing, kayaking, fishing, hunting, snowmobiling, snowshoeing, and backcountry skiing.

This is not a single attraction with a ticket booth. Treat it as the reason to plan ahead, talk to local operators, and choose a lake or river trip that matches your time, vehicle, and comfort with remote country.
