Douglass Beach is the town's main public swimming spot, close to Knowlton and set under mature trees on Brome Lake. It has changing rooms, picnic tables, lake access for small non-motorized boats, and a view that explains why people keep coming back to this corner of the Townships.

It is simple, practical, and best on a hot afternoon.
