Vignoble Courville sits on slopes facing Brome Lake, with tastings built around cold-climate Quebec wines: Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Riesling, Vidal, Maréchal Foch, St-Pépin, sparkling wine, and late-harvest bottles. The draw is not only the wine but the view over the lake valley.

It is a good final stop if you have been looping the back roads around Lac-Brome.
