Mont Gosford is the highest summit in southern Quebec, with hiking and cycling trails through a forested borderland landscape. The climb is the main draw, ending with broad views over the surrounding mountains.

It is a deliberate outdoor stop from the Lac-Megantic area rather than a quick roadside viewpoint.
