Sainte-Agnès Church is a large historic greystone church in Lac-Mégantic, built in the early 20th century on a Latin-cross plan. Its pale stone, gold-toned brickwork, and mature trees make it one of the town's most visible landmarks.

It is worth a short look while walking or driving through central Lac-Mégantic, especially if you are already visiting the waterfront.
