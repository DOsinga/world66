Église Saint-Louis is the parish church of L'Isle-aux-Coudres, built in 1885. Its main interest is inside, where 15 frescoes illustrate the life of Saint Louis.

The church is an easy cultural stop while circling the island, especially if you are already visiting the mills or following the Chemin des Coudriers.
