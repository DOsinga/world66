Longue-Pointe-de-Mingan is a small village on Quebec's far North Shore, stretched along a sandy point facing the Gulf of St Lawrence. It is quieter than [Havre-Saint-Pierre](/northamerica/canada/quebec/havre_saint_pierre), but it has its own reason to stop: whales, puffins, beaches, and the western end of the Mingan island world.

The strongest outing is the boat trip to [Île aux Perroquets](/northamerica/canada/quebec/longue_pointe_de_mingan/ile_aux_perroquets), where a lighthouse station, seabirds, and open Gulf weather make the coast feel properly remote. In the village, the [Mingan Island Cetacean Study Research Station](/northamerica/canada/quebec/longue_pointe_de_mingan/mingan_island_cetacean_study_research_station) gives the whale story real scientific depth.

This is not a place for a long checklist. Walk the [Parc des Paspayas boardwalk](/northamerica/canada/quebec/longue_pointe_de_mingan/parc_des_paspayas_boardwalk), watch the water, and use the village as a slower western doorway to the [Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve](/northamerica/canada/quebec/havre_saint_pierre/mingan_archipelago_national_park_reserve).
