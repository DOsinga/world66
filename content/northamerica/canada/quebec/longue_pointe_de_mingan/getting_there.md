---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Longue-Pointe-de-Mingan is on Route 138, about 170 km east of Sept-Îles and a short drive west of Havre-Saint-Pierre. Intercity bus service along the North Shore is limited, so check schedules carefully.

Most travellers arrive by car as part of a Côte-Nord road trip. Distances are long, services thin out, and weather can change quickly along the Gulf, so keep fuel and timing conservative.
