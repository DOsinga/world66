Longueuil sits on the south shore of the St. Lawrence River, directly across from [Montréal](/northamerica/canada/quebec/montreal). Connected to the island by the Jacques-Cartier Bridge and a metro line, it functions as both a bedroom community and a destination in its own right.

The historic district of [Vieux-Longueuil](/northamerica/canada/quebec/longueuil/vieux_longueuil) along Rue Saint-Charles has a strip of restaurants and cafes worth exploring, and the waterfront [Parc Marie-Victorin](/northamerica/canada/quebec/longueuil/parc_marie_victorin) offers sweeping views of the Montréal skyline. [Parc Michel-Chartrand](/northamerica/canada/quebec/longueuil/parc_michel_chartrand) to the north is one of the largest urban forest parks in the region. For most visitors, Longueuil serves as a quieter, more affordable base for exploring the Montréal area — the metro gets you downtown in about fifteen minutes.
