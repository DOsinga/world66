---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Longueuil is directly across the St. Lawrence from [Montréal](/northamerica/canada/quebec/montreal) and is one of the most accessible cities in Québec.

**By Metro**

The Montréal Metro's Yellow Line crosses the river to Longueuil–Université-de-Sherbrooke station, the main metro terminus on the south shore. The journey from Berri-UQAM takes about 15 minutes. From the metro station, city buses connect to most parts of Longueuil.

**By Car**

The Jacques-Cartier Bridge links the island of Montréal directly to Longueuil. The Champlain Bridge (and the newer Samuel de Champlain Bridge) connects to the southwestern part of the city. Traffic on both bridges can be heavy during rush hours.

**By Bus**

RTL (Réseau de transport de Longueuil) operates bus services throughout the city. The main interchange hub is at the Longueuil–Université-de-Sherbrooke metro station.
