  The park is named for Brother Marie-Victorin, a Québécois botanist and Christian Brothers

Parc Marie-Victorin runs along the St. Lawrence riverfront and offers sweeping views of the Montréal skyline across the water — one of the best angles from which to appreciate the city, particularly at dusk when the light hits the skyscrapers and the Jacques-Cartier Bridge. The park is popular with cyclists, joggers, and anyone wanting to watch the river traffic.

The waterfront promenade is the park's main feature: a well-maintained path that runs for several kilometres with benches, picnic areas, and unobstructed river views throughout. In summer, the park hosts outdoor events and is a favourite spot for Longueuil residents on warm evenings. In winter, when the river freezes, the landscape becomes something quite different and dramatic.
