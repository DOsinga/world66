Le Vieux Clocher is Magog's best-known performance venue, set in a converted old church near the centre of town. Its programme leans toward Quebec chanson, comedy, and touring stage shows, and it gives evenings in Magog more shape than the usual resort-town dinner stroll.

Check the schedule before you come. Even if your French is limited, a concert here can be one of the better ways to feel the local side of the Eastern Townships.
