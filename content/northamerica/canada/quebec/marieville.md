Marieville is a small Montérégie town east of [Montreal](/northamerica/canada/quebec/montreal), set in the flat St. Lawrence plain between the Richelieu valley and the orchard country around Rougemont. It is more local than touristic, but the surrounding rural roads give a clear picture of southern Quebec's farming landscape.

The town centre is marked by the twin towers of [Saint-Nom-de-Marie Church](/northamerica/canada/quebec/marieville/saint_nom_de_marie_church), a handsome early 20th-century landmark that still gives Marieville its skyline. Around it are modest brick houses, local shops, parks and the municipal buildings that keep the town's community life busy.

Marieville works best as a small stop on a Montérégie drive. Pair it with Rougemont's orchards, Mont-Saint-Grégoire's sugar shacks, or the Richelieu River towns if you want a day outside Montreal that is more agricultural than polished.
