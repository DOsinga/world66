Parc Sainte-Marie-de-Monnoir is one of Marieville's main community spaces. It hosts local events, including Noctambule in winter, when the park fills with skating, outdoor activities and family programming.

Outside event days it is a simple local park rather than a destination in itself, but it gives the best feel for Marieville as a lived-in town rather than a roadside name.
