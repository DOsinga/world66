Saint-Nom-de-Marie Church is Marieville's main landmark, a double-spired Catholic church built between 1918 and 1920. It stands near the presbytery and municipal buildings, so it naturally anchors a short walk through the town centre.

The building is most rewarding from the outside, where the towers rise above the low streets and fields around them. It was designated a historic place in 2003, recognition less for grandeur than for its role in the town's identity.
