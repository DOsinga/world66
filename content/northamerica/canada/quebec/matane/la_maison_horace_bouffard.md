La maison Horace-Bouffard is a preserved country house near Matane, kept for its early 20th-century atmosphere and local family story. The house gives a modest but useful look at rural life on this stretch of the Gaspé coast.

It is best treated as a small heritage stop, not a major museum. Pair it with other Matane sights if you are following the coast road.
