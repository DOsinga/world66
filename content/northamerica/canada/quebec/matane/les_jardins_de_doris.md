Les Jardins de Doris is a community garden attraction near the Matane River, with flower displays, small animals, playful installations, and a social mission tied to youth reintegration. It is gentle rather than grand, but it adds a softer stop to a town otherwise defined by river, sea, and road.

Use it as a relaxed walk after the lighthouse or salmon centre. Garden lovers will get the most from it; everyone else should treat it as a pleasant local extra.
