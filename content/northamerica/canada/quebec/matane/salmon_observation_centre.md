The Salmon Observation Centre sits by the Mathieu-D'Amours dam, where Atlantic salmon move through the Matane River fishway. Windows and interpretation panels let you watch the run without needing a rod or local fishing knowledge.

This is seasonal by nature. When the salmon are moving, it is one of the clearest ways to understand why Matane is so tied to its river.
