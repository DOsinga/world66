---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The resort village has a handful of après-ski bars that get lively in the late afternoon when the lifts close, and quieter wine bars and pubs for the evenings. The Québécois craft beer scene has reached Tremblant, and most bars carry a reasonable selection of local breweries. The village can be crowded on Saturday nights in peak ski season, so arrive early if you want a good seat.
