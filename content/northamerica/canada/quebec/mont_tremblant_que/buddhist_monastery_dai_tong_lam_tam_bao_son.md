Dai Tong Lam Tam Bao Son is a Vietnamese Buddhist monastery in the Laurentians, set on quiet grounds with temple buildings, statues, and forested slopes. It is not a conventional tourist attraction, but respectful visitors come for the architecture, the hillside setting, and the unexpected sense of calm within reach of the Mont-Tremblant region.

The monastery should be approached as an active religious site rather than a theme park. Dress modestly, keep noise low, and avoid interrupting ceremonies or private areas. For travelers driving between Montreal, Mont-Tremblant, and the smaller Laurentian towns, it makes a distinctive cultural stop if opening conditions allow visitors that day.
