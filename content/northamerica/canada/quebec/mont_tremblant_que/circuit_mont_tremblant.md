Circuit Mont-Tremblant is a historic road racing circuit located about 8 km south of the ski resort, in a forested setting that makes it one of the more scenic in North America. The 4.26 km track was built in 1964 and became famous for its demanding layout and the thick Laurentian forest that frames nearly every corner.

The circuit hosts vintage racing events, club racing weekends, and track days throughout the summer — if you're visiting in the warmer months, check the schedule. Even outside of race weekends, the facility sometimes allows driving experiences. The paddock and pit lane can feel time-warped, with infrastructure that dates back to the early years of Canadian road racing. For motorsport enthusiasts, it's a genuinely interesting detour from the ski village.
