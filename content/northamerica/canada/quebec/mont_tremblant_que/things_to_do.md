---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Mont-Tremblant divides neatly between mountain activities and village life. In winter, the mountain itself dominates everything — skiing, snowboarding, snowshoeing, and skating on the resort's outdoor rink. In summer the same terrain becomes a destination for hiking, mountain biking, and watersports on the surrounding lakes. The pedestrian village at the base has enough restaurants, bars, and shops to keep you occupied on a rest day in any season.
