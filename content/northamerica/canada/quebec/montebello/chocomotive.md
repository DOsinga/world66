ChocoMotive turns the old Montebello railway station into a small chocolate workshop, shop, and economuseum. It is a modest stop, but a good one: you can see the craft process, taste fair-trade chocolate, and give the village walk a sweet pause.

It works best paired with Manoir Papineau or a riverfront stroll rather than treated as a destination on its own.
