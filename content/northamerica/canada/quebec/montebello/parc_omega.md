Parc Omega is Montebello's biggest draw, a drive-through wildlife park where deer, elk, bison, wild boar, and other animals move through large natural enclosures. It is not wilderness, but it gives visitors an unusually close look at Canadian wildlife without leaving the road.

The wolves, bears, and interpretive areas add enough depth to make it more than a simple animal-feeding loop. Go early if you can; the experience is calmer before the busiest family traffic arrives.
