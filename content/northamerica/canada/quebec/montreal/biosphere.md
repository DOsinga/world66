The Biosphere on Île Sainte-Hélène is a 76-metre geodesic dome designed by Buckminster Fuller as the American pavilion for Expo 67. After a fire destroyed its transparent acrylic skin in 1976, the bare steel lattice stood for years as a skeletal landmark before being enclosed and converted into an environmental museum. Today the dome houses interactive exhibits on water, ecosystems, and climate — the museum focuses on the St. Lawrence ecosystem, the Great Lakes, and Canada's changing environment.

The exhibits are solid and well-suited to families, but the building itself is the real draw. Standing inside the lattice structure and looking up through the curved steel geometry is genuinely memorable. The Biosphere is easily reached from the Jean-Drapeau metro station, and the surrounding park is excellent for walking and cycling.
