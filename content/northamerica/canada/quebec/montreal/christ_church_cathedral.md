Christ Church Cathedral is a compact Anglican cathedral in downtown Montreal, set above the shops of the Promenades Cathédrale. The Gothic Revival church is colourful inside, with stained glass, carved woodwork, and an organ whose pipes are split between the rear gallery and the front of the nave.

It is an easy stop between the Underground City, McGill, and the busy shopping streets around Sainte-Catherine. Step inside for a quiet break from the city centre rather than expecting a vast cathedral on the scale of Quebec City or Europe.
