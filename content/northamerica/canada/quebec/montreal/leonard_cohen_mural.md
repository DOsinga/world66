On the corner of rue Crescent and boulevard de Maisonneuve, a ten-storey mural portrait of Leonard Cohen looks down at the street. Cohen was born in Montreal in 1934 and grew up in the Westmount neighbourhood; he spent much of his life elsewhere but always identified with the city. The mural was painted in 2017 following his death in 2016 and has become one of the city's most photographed spots.

The image is based on a 1988 photograph and shows Cohen in his characteristic fedora and suit. The surrounding block of rue Crescent — historically an English-speaking nightlife strip — has been somewhat gentrified but still has a concentration of bars and restaurants. Cohen's childhood home at 599 avenue Belmont in Westmount can also be visited (it's a private residence, exterior only), and the Musée des Beaux-Arts has permanent materials related to his life and work.
