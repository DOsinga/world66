The 234-metre hill that gives Montreal its name, now a 200-hectare park designed by Frederick Law Olmsted (who also did Central Park). The Belvédère Kondiaronk lookout at the summit gives an unobstructed view of the downtown skyline and the St Lawrence beyond. In summer, Sunday tam-tams drum circles draw thousands to the base of the monument; in winter, the cross-country ski trails through the forest are free. Walk up from the McGill campus in about 30 minutes.
