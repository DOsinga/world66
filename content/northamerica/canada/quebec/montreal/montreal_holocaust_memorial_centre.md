The Montreal Holocaust Memorial Centre is a serious, carefully presented museum founded by Holocaust survivors in 1979. Its exhibits use testimony, documents, and personal objects to explain the genocide of European Jews and the wider machinery of Nazi persecution.

It is not a light stop, but it is a valuable one, especially in a city with a large Jewish community and a long history of immigration. Allow time to move slowly through the displays.
