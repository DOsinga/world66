Musee d'art contemporain de Montreal is the city's main contemporary art museum, with a large collection focused on Quebec, Canadian, and international artists. It sits in the Quartier des spectacles, close to Place des Arts and several of downtown's main performance venues.

The museum is strongest for modern and contemporary work rather than old masters. Check the current exhibition schedule before going, since temporary shows often shape the visit more than the permanent collection.
