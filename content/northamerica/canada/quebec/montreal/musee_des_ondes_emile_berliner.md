The Musee des ondes Emile Berliner tells the story of sound recording, radio, and Canadian audio technology from inside Montreal's former RCA Victor industrial complex. The exhibits connect Emile Berliner's gramophone work with later local contributions to broadcasting, satellites, and the Canadarm.

It is a small, specialist museum, but a rewarding one if you are interested in technology, industrial history, or how recorded sound became part of daily life.
