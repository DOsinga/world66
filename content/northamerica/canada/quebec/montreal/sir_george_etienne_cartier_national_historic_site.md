This national historic site preserves two 19th-century houses tied to Sir George-Étienne Cartier, one of the key political figures behind Canadian Confederation. The restored interiors show the domestic world of Montreal's French-Canadian elite in the decades before and after Confederation.

It is a good small stop while walking the eastern edge of Old Montréal. The appeal is quieter than the basilica or the port, but the houses add useful political and social context to the old city.
