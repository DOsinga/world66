Les Galets is Natashquan's most evocative place: a row of old fishing sheds by the Gulf, where cod was once salted and dried before shipping. Only a dozen of the original buildings survive, but that is enough to make the old economy visible.

Walk here for the buildings, the sea air, and the sense of distance. The site explains Natashquan better than a plaque-heavy museum ever could.
