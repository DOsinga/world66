Maison Gilles-Vigneault is the family heritage site associated with Natashquan's most famous son. Vigneault's songs and poems carried the village name far beyond the Lower North Shore, and the house gives that cultural fame a physical anchor.

Even if you know only a little Québec music, the stop matters because it shows how strongly Natashquan's identity is tied to memory, language, and stories.
