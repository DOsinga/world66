The Natashquan quay is a simple but telling stop, jutting out between the harbour and the Gulf. Cargo-passenger boats still connect communities along the Lower North Shore, and watching the quay at work gives the village's isolation a practical shape.

Come for a walk and the open water rather than a formal attraction. The point is the edge-of-the-map feeling.
