Cap-Blanc Lighthouse is a small red-and-white lighthouse south of Perce, built in 1915 to replace an earlier light on White Head. It is no longer active, but its octagonal form and coastal setting make it a photogenic short stop.

The lighthouse stands on campground land, so treat access as a brief look or photo stop rather than a formal attraction.
