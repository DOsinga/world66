The Historic L'Anse-a-Beaufils General Store preserves a 1928 village shop that once served as a social and commercial hub on the Gaspe coast. Shelves of old dry goods, farm equipment, photographs, and household objects turn the building into a small living-history museum.

Guided visits use period details and local stories to explain how fishing, farming, and trade shaped the community. It is close enough to Perce to combine with the coast's bigger sights when you want a quieter piece of local history.
