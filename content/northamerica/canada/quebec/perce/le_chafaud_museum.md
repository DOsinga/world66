Le Chafaud Museum occupies a 19th-century waterfront warehouse in Perce and focuses on the artists drawn to the village's cliffs, sea light, and famous rock. The setting is part of the appeal: an old fishing-era building repurposed for local culture.

It is a compact museum, best used as a calm indoor stop between the geopark, the waterfront, and boat trips to Bonaventure Island.
