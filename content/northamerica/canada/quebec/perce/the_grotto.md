The Grotto is not really a cave, but a small waterfall tucked between Mont Sainte-Anne and Mont Blanc behind Perce. A stream drops over red rock into a shallow pool inside a natural amphitheatre, with religious statues nearby reflecting the area's pilgrimage history. It works best as a short detour while exploring the Perce Geopark trails.
