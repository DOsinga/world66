Artillery Park preserves part of Québec City's military past, with surviving buildings from a site used for fortifications, barracks, and later a munitions factory. The former foundry houses exhibits on the city's history, while the arsenal building includes a weapons display.

It sits just inside the old defensive line, so it pairs well with a walk along the city walls.
