---
title: "Bars and Cafes in Québec City"
type: section
---

The [Grande Allée](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/grande_allee) is Québec City's main bar strip, a long boulevard lined with bars and terasses just outside the old city walls. In summer it gets very busy on weekend evenings; in winter the action moves indoors. The rue Saint-Jean, inside the old walls, has more neighbourhood pubs and a slightly younger crowd. [Pub Saint-Alexandre](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/bars_and_cafes/pub_saint_alexandre) on rue Saint-Jean is the best beer bar in the Old City, with over 250 varieties.

The [Saint-Jean-Baptiste](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/saint_jean_baptiste) neighbourhood outside the walls has LGBTQ+-oriented bars, including [Bar Le Drague](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/bars_and_cafes/bar_le_drague). [Café Temporel](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/bars_and_cafes/cafe_temporel) on rue Couillard is the best neighbourhood café in the Old City for an unhurried morning coffee.

Bars close at 3am throughout Québec.
