Québec City is well placed for day trips into the surrounding region. The three most useful are covered as POIs on the Things to Do page: [Montmorency Falls](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/montmorency_falls) (15 minutes east), [Île d'Orléans](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/ile_dorleans) (20 minutes east), and [Mont-Sainte-Anne](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/mont_sainte_anne) (45 minutes east).

**Baie-Saint-Paul** (90 minutes northeast on Route 138) is an attractive art town in the Charlevoix region, with a strong gallery scene and the beginning of the spectacular scenery that unfolds as you drive up the north shore of the St. Lawrence. The Charlevoix coast — dramatic cliffs, whales in the estuary, and a distinct local culture — is worth several days of exploration if time allows.

**Grosse Île** (50 km east, accessible by ferry from Berthier-sur-Mer) was the Irish quarantine station for immigrants arriving in Canada in the 19th century; the Memorial to the Irish Famine of 1847-48, when thousands died here, is one of the most moving historical sites in Canada.
