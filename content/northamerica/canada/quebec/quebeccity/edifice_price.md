Edifice Price is the slim Art Deco tower that rises above the rooftops inside Old Quebec's walls. Built in the 1930s, it remains one of the old city's most distinctive vertical landmarks and now includes the official residence of Quebec's premier. You mainly admire it from the surrounding streets rather than tour the interior.
