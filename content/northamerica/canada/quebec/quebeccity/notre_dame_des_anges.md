Notre-Dame-des-Anges is a tiny independent parish municipality surrounded by Quebec City. Its historic interest comes from the Augustinian monastery, the General Hospital of Quebec, its cemetery, and related buildings, which together form one of the city's older religious and medical heritage sites.
