---
title: "Things to Do in Québec City"
type: section
---

Québec City's main appeal is the Old City itself — walking the fortification walls, exploring the narrow streets of Vieux-Québec, and taking in the view from Dufferin Terrace. Most of what you'd want to see is within easy walking distance in the Upper and Lower Towns.

The [Vieux-Québec](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/vieux_quebec) walled city is the starting point for everything. Walk the [fortification walls](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/fortification_walls) (about an hour, free), stroll the [Dufferin Terrace](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/dufferin_terrace) for the panorama over the St. Lawrence, and take the [Funiculaire](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/funiculaire) down to the [Quartier Petit-Champlain](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/quartier_petit_champlain) and [Place Royale](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/place_royale) in the Lower Town. The [Château Frontenac](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/chateau_frontenac) is the unmissable landmark — even if you're not staying there, the guided tour of its history is worthwhile.

The [Plains of Abraham](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/plains_of_abraham) west of the walls are the site of the 1759 battle that ended French rule in Canada; the [Citadelle](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/citadelle) on the bluff above runs daily guided tours and a Changing of the Guard in summer.

For museums: the [Musée de la civilisation](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/musee_de_la_civilisation) in the Lower Town is among the best in Canada; the [Musée national des beaux-arts](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/musee_national_des_beaux_arts) covers Québécois visual art from the colonial period to the present; the [Ursulines Convent](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/ursulines_convent) contains Montcalm's skull and the history of North America's first educational institution for women; the [Morrin Centre](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/morrin_centre) is a former colonial prison turned Victorian library.

Day trips from the city: [Montmorency Falls](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/montmorency_falls) (15 minutes east, taller than Niagara), [Île d'Orléans](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/ile_dorleans) (20 minutes, farm island with excellent food), and [Mont-Sainte-Anne](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/mont_sainte_anne) (45 minutes, major ski area).

In winter, the [Hôtel de Glace](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/ice_hotel) at Valcartier and the [Carnaval de Québec](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/carnaval_de_quebec) in February are among the best cold-weather experiences in North America. In summer, the [Festival d'été](/northamerica/canada/quebec/quebeccity/things_to_do/festival_ete) in early July draws major international acts to the Plains of Abraham.
