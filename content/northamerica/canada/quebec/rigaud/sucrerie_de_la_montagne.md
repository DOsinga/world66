Sucrerie de la Montagne is a year-round sugar shack on the east side of Mont Rigaud. The classic season is March and early April, when the maple sap runs and Quebec's cabane a sucre meals are at their richest.

Reserve ahead if you want the full sugaring-off meal. Outside maple season, it still works as a good way to understand one of rural Quebec's defining rituals.
