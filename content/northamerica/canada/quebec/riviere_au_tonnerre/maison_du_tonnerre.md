Maison du Tonnerre is a small local heritage stop rather than a major museum. Its value is in the old photographs and village memory: fishing families, isolation, the late arrival of the road, and the practical history of life on this stretch of the North Shore.

Drop in if it is open while you are passing through. It pairs well with the church because both explain how remote this community was before Route 138 changed the coast.
