Saint-Hippolyte Church is the village's standout cultural sight. Built of wood in the early 20th century, it is often described as Norman in style, with a tall interior vault and hand-carved decoration made by local volunteers.

It is worth going inside if the church is open. The scale is modest, but the craftsmanship feels intensely local, which makes it more memorable than many grander churches.
