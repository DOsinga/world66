Île aux Lièvres sits offshore in the St. Lawrence, far enough from town to feel like a small expedition. Seasonal boats from the marina bring visitors to a network of trails through forest, shoreline, and bird habitat.

It is not a casual winter option, and schedules matter. In season, though, it gives Rivière-du-Loup a wilder side than the highway view suggests.
