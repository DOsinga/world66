L'Innocent is a useful central table on Rue Lafontaine, with the kind of mixed menu that works when a group cannot agree on one cuisine. It is one of the easier places in town for vegetarian and gluten-free choices.

Stop here before or after Parc des Chutes; the walk between the two is short.
