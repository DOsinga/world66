Lac Osisko gives Rouyn-Noranda its most visible natural landmark. The lake sits close to the centre, with walking areas and festival spaces around its shores, and it keeps the city from feeling only industrial.

Come for a simple walk, especially in summer when local events use the waterfront. The lake is also a good way to orient yourself before heading deeper into town.
