Fjord cruises from La Baie are the easiest way to see why the Saguenay is more than a river. The route puts you below dark cliffs and forested slopes that are hard to grasp from the road, with seasonal departures linking La Baie to smaller fjord communities.

This is the thing to do if you have one clear half-day and want the landscape rather than another museum.
