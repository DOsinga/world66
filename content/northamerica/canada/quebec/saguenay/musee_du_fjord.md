The Musee du Fjord in La Baie explains the Saguenay Fjord as a living environment: deep cold water, steep rock walls, unusual marine life, and a human history tied to shipping and settlement. It mixes exhibitions with an aquarium, making it a useful stop before taking a boat trip or driving farther along the fjord.

It is especially good in poor weather, when the landscape outside is still there but less inviting.
