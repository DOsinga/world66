Saint-Édouard-de-Maskinongé is a small parish municipality in the lower Mauricie, northeast of [Montréal](/northamerica/canada/quebec/montreal). It is rural Québec at an unhurried pace: farms near the village, forest roads farther out, and the Maskinongé River curving through the countryside.

This is not a town of grand monuments. Its appeal is local and outdoorsy, with camping, riding, cycling, fishing, snowmobile trails in winter, and a few family attractions around the village. The roads through Saint-Édouard are more useful if you are wandering between the St. Lawrence plain and the wooded country north of Louiseville than if you are trying to tick off famous sights.

[Chute du Poste](/northamerica/canada/quebec/saint_edouard_de_maskinonge/chute_du_poste) is the most interesting natural stop, a small fall on the Maskinongé River where the water loops through the trees before continuing toward Sainte-Ursule. Families may also appreciate [Zoo Animalia](/northamerica/canada/quebec/saint_edouard_de_maskinonge/zoo_animalia), a modest animal refuge and educational zoo near the village.
