Chute du Poste is a quiet fall on the Maskinongé River south of the main village. It is not a dramatic canyon or a developed park, but it gives you a good look at the river that shapes this part of the municipality.

The setting is the reason to come: wooded banks, moving water, and a rural road approach that feels far from the St. Lawrence corridor even though it is only a short drive away.
