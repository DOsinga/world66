Zoo Animalia is a small animal refuge near the village, with native and exotic animals, rehabilitation work, and educational visits. It is a family stop rather than a major zoo, but it fits Saint-Édouard's outdoors-and-country character.

Check opening times before driving out, especially outside the main summer season.
