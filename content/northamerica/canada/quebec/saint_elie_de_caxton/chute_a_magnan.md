Chute a Magnan is a waterfall on the Loup River near Saint-Elie-de-Caxton. It is one of several falls along the river, with Chutes des Chaudieres and Chute des Trembles upstream.

It is a short nature stop rather than a major excursion, but it fits well with a local drive through the Mauricie countryside.
