The Garage de la Culture is the village's cultural anchor, a small performance and exhibition space that gives Saint-Elie-de-Caxton more bite than its size would suggest. It hosts talks, concerts, theatre, workshops, and the visitor information point.

Check the programme before coming. Without an event it is still useful as a starting point, but with one it becomes the clearest reason to make the detour.
