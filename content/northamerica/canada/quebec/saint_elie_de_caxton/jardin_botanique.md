The village botanical garden is a small public garden with walking paths, flowers, succulents, water features, and interpretive signs for the Maskinonge region. It is not a destination on its own, but it fits the pace of Saint-Elie-de-Caxton well.

Use it as a quiet pause between the church, the Calvary hill, and the Culture Garage.
