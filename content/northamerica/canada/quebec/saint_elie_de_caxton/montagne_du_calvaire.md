Montagne du Calvaire is the village's old devotional hill, with Stations of the Cross and an illuminated summit cross. The wider Calvary ensemble, including the church, presbytery, cemetery, and hill, has recognised heritage value in Quebec.

It is a modest climb rather than a mountain outing, but it gives Saint-Elie-de-Caxton a useful sense of age and place. Go for the village view and the quiet, not for drama.
