Saint-Elie-de-Caxton's most distinctive sight is not one building but the way the village has turned local storytelling into a walking experience. Fred Pellerin's tales made the place known across French-speaking Quebec, and visitors come looking for the village behind those stories.

Start at the Culture Garage and ask what route or guided visit is currently running. The point is not to tick off monuments; it is to let a small rural place explain itself through folklore, humour, and local pride.
