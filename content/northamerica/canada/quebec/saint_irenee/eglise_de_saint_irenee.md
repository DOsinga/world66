The Saint-Irénée church has been folded into Domaine Forget's cultural life and now hosts concerts, workshops, and master classes in summer. That makes it more useful to visitors than a locked heritage building: it is still a village landmark, but also an active room for music.

If you are here outside event times, it is still worth pausing for the setting. The church stands above the river road, with views back toward the St. Lawrence and the village clustered below.
