Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu sits south of [Montreal](/northamerica/canada/quebec/montreal), on the Richelieu River near the route between the St. Lawrence and Lake Champlain. It is close enough to the city to be easy, but it has its own identity: military history, canal paths, old river streets, and summer balloons.

The key historic stop is [Fort Saint-Jean Museum](/northamerica/canada/quebec/saint_jean_sur_richelieu/fort_saint_jean_museum), on a site used by Indigenous, French, British, American, and Canadian forces. The [Chambly Canal](/northamerica/canada/quebec/saint_jean_sur_richelieu/chambly_canal) gives the town a slower outdoor rhythm, with cycling and walking along the old waterway.

In August, the city becomes one of Quebec's most recognisable festival towns during the [International Balloon Festival](/northamerica/canada/quebec/saint_jean_sur_richelieu/international_balloon_festival). Outside festival dates, it still works as a Montérégie day trip if you like river towns with real history.
