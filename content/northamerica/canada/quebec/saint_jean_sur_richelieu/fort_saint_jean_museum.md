Fort Saint-Jean Museum stands on one of the town's most important sites, now part of the Royal Military College Saint-Jean. The museum uses maps, models, uniforms, weapons, and archival material to explain the fort's long role on the Richelieu corridor.

It is the best first stop if you want to understand why this river town mattered: the route linked the St. Lawrence world to Lake Champlain and the American frontier.
