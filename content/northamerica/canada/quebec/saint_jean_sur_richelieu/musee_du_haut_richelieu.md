Musee du Haut-Richelieu is a small regional museum with a useful local focus: the history of the Richelieu valley and the ceramics industry that once made Saint-Jean and Iberville known as a pottery centre.

It pairs naturally with Fort Saint-Jean Museum and a walk through Old Saint-Jean, giving the town more depth than its suburban edges suggest.
