Canyon Sainte-Anne is the most dramatic stop around Saint-Joachim. Trails lead to viewpoints over the gorge, where the Sainte-Anne River drops through old Canadian Shield rock. The suspension bridges are the hook, especially the high crossing above the canyon, but the short walks and constant sound of the falls make the visit work even if you skip the adventure activities.
