Chute Jean-Larose is a forested waterfall walk near Mont-Sainte-Anne. The upper viewpoint is an easy short walk, while the full descent involves roughly 350 steps to reach the base.

It is a good leg-stretcher if you are already in the Beaupre coast area and want a quick nature stop away from the main road.
