Saint-Mathieu-du-Parc is a small Mauricie village with lakes, forest, and a useful position on the southwest side of La Mauricie National Park. It is not a big destination by itself, but it has more than gateway value: the village mixes park access with Indigenous interpretation, family outdoor attractions, and a bit of rural Quebec heritage.

The best visit uses the village as a soft landing before or after the park. [Mokotakan](/northamerica/canada/quebec/saint_mathieu_du_parc/mokotakan) is the strongest local stop, while [Labyrinthe Coureur des Bois](/northamerica/canada/quebec/saint_mathieu_du_parc/labyrinthe_coureur_des_bois) gives families something more playful than another lookout or trailhead.

If you are already in Shawinigan, Trois-Rivieres, or the Mauricie countryside, Saint-Mathieu-du-Parc is worth a spare day when you want forest roads, lakes, and a village that still feels tied to the land around it.
