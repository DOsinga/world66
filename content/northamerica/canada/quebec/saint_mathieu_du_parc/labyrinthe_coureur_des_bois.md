Labyrinthe Coureur des Bois is a cedar maze and activity site built around the world of the 17th-century coureurs des bois. It is playful rather than scholarly, with challenges, animation, and a family-friendly rhythm that suits a half-day in the countryside.

It is a good choice when the weather or mood does not call for a serious hike. The theme also fits the Mauricie setting, where fur trade, forest travel, and river routes are never far from the surface.
