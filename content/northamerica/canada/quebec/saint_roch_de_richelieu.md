Saint-Roch-de-Richelieu is a small municipality on the west bank of the Richelieu River in Montérégie, northeast of [Montréal](/northamerica/canada/quebec/montreal). It is rural, French-speaking and low-key, with farms and riverside houses spread along the water.

The river is the reason to know the place. A small seasonal ferry links Saint-Roch-de-Richelieu with Saint-Ours on the opposite bank, saving a long detour to the bridges north or south. For travellers following the Richelieu valley, that crossing is more memorable than the road distance suggests.

Do not come expecting a major sightseeing town. Come if you are tracing the Richelieu, cycling the countryside, or want a quiet stop between Sorel-Tracy and the villages farther south.
