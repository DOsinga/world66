Baie-des-Rochers is the strongest outdoor stop around Saint-Siméon: a small bay cut into the Charlevoix shore, with forest paths, rocky coves, and views over the St. Lawrence. It feels wilder than the village and rewards a slow walk.

Trails vary from short viewpoints to longer loops. Wear real shoes, especially after rain, and give yourself time to sit by the water rather than treating it as a quick photo stop.
