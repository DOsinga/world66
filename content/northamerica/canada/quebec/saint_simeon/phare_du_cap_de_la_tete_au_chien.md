Phare du Cap de la Tete au Chien is a historic lighthouse site on a rocky summit above the St. Lawrence River near Port-aux-Quilles. The draw is the exposed riverside setting, with broad views over the water and the Charlevoix shoreline.

It is best treated as a short scenic detour while travelling around Saint-Simeon, especially for visitors following lighthouse or river-view stops along the coast.
