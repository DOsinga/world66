Lac Saint-Tite is a small lake east of the village, reached by local roads and a short forest approach. In summer it is a simple swimming and picnic spot, backed by wooded slopes rather than resort development.

It is not a grand attraction, but it rounds out a Saint-Tite-des-Caps day if you want something gentler after the Sentier des Caps trails.
