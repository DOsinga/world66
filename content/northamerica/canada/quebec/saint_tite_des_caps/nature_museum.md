The Nature Museum is a small local museum on avenue Royale, with displays of naturalized animals, shells, insects, birds, mammals, fish, and other specimens. It is modest, but it fits the village's outdoor character and can be useful on a wet or very cold day.

Treat it as a short stop before or after the trails, not the main reason to come.
