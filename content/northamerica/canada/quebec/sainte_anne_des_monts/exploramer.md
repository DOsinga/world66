Exploramer is the town's marine museum and aquarium on the St. Lawrence waterfront. Its tanks, exhibits, and seasonal sea excursions focus on the cold-water life of the estuary rather than tropical spectacle.

It is especially useful if weather closes in on the mountains. The museum gives Sainte-Anne-des-Monts a local anchor, and the quay outside is one of the easiest places in town to feel the scale of the river.
