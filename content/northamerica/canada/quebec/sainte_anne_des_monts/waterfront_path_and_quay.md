The waterfront path and quay along 1re Avenue are Sainte-Anne-des-Monts at its most immediate: fishing-town streets, open water, and the long horizon of the St. Lawrence. It is an easy walk, not a major sight, but it helps the town make sense.

Go near sunset if the weather is clear. The view from the quay by Exploramer is one of the simplest pleasures in town.
