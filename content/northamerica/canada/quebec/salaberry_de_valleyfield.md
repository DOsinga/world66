Salaberry-de-Valleyfield sits southwest of [Montreal](/northamerica/canada/quebec/montreal), where canals, bays, and the St Lawrence system give the downtown an unusually watery shape. It is not a grand old city, but it has enough nautical life, industrial history, and summer swimming to make a good Montérégie day trip.

The centre turns around the Old Beauharnois Canal and the marina, with boats tied up close to restaurants and the commercial streets. [Parc Delpha-Sauvé](/northamerica/canada/quebec/salaberry_de_valleyfield/parc_delpha_sauve) gives the downtown its green edge, while the [MUSO](/northamerica/canada/quebec/salaberry_de_valleyfield/muso) explains how textile mills shaped the town.

If you have a car or a bike, widen the day to include [Îles-de-Saint-Timothée Regional Park](/northamerica/canada/quebec/salaberry_de_valleyfield/iles_de_saint_timothee_regional_park) or [Coteau-du-Lac National Historic Site](/northamerica/canada/quebec/salaberry_de_valleyfield/coteau_du_lac_national_historic_site). Valleyfield is best in warm weather, when the water is part of the city rather than just scenery.
