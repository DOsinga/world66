Beauharnois-Salaberry Regional Park follows both banks of the Beauharnois Canal through several towns and villages. Its main draw is a flat paved cycling route of about 72 km, with canal views and marshland sections around Grande-Île.

It is most useful for travellers with bikes or time for a longer waterfront walk, rather than as a single-point park visit.
