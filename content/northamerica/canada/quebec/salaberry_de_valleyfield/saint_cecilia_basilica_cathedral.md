Saint Cecilia Basilica-Cathedral is the landmark church of Salaberry-de-Valleyfield, completed in 1934 in a confident Gothic Revival style. Its scale and detailing make it one of the town's clearest architectural statements, and it is an easy stop near the old canal and waterfront.
