The Old Canal is the reason Valleyfield feels more like a boating town than a suburb. The marina sits right by the centre, with slips, launch points, and a promenade that follows the water toward Parc Delpha-Sauvé and the restaurant streets.

Come for a walk even if you are not arriving by boat. The canal makes the city legible: industry, leisure, and St Lawrence navigation all meet in a few compact blocks.
