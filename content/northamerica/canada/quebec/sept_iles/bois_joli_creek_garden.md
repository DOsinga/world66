Bois-Joli Creek Garden is a botanical garden on the north side of town, with several kilometres of trails and plantings arranged into themed areas. The sea-winds trail is the section to look for if you want the garden to lead naturally back toward the bay.

It is gentle rather than dramatic, but it gives a good feel for the North Shore's short growing season and hardy coastal plants.
