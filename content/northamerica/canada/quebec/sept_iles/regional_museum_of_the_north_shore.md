The Regional Museum of the North Shore gives Sept-Îles some context beyond its port and highway. The permanent exhibition covers the Côte-Nord's history, and temporary shows usually keep a cultural or artistic angle.

It is a practical first stop on a rainy day, and a useful companion to the Shaputuan Museum if you want to understand the wider coast.
