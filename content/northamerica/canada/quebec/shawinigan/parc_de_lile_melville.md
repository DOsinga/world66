Parc de l'Île Melville sits beside the Saint-Maurice River and Shawinigan's old power landscape. Trails, viewpoints, and outdoor activities make it the easiest way to add fresh air to a visit focused on La Cité de l'énergie.

It is especially useful if you do not have time to reach La Mauricie National Park. The park keeps the river present and gives Shawinigan a greener edge.
