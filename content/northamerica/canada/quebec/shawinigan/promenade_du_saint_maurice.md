The Promenade du Saint-Maurice is Shawinigan's easiest walk. It follows the river and helps connect the downtown to the landscape that made the city: water, falls, mills, and power.

Use it as a low-effort orientation route before or after La Cité de l'énergie. Shawinigan is not a postcard town, but the promenade shows its best angle.
