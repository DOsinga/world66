Stanstead is a border town in Quebec's [Eastern Townships](/northamerica/canada/quebec), made up of Stanstead, Rock Island, and Beebe Plain. Its identity is unusually literal: the United States line cuts through streets, old houses, and the famous Haskell Free Library and Opera House. You do not come for a long list of sights. You come because the border has shaped the town in ways you can see on the pavement.

The [Haskell Free Library and Opera House](/northamerica/canada/quebec/stanstead/haskell_free_library_and_opera_house) is the reason to stop. It was built to serve both sides of the line, with the border marked inside the building. Nearby, [Colby-Curtis Museum](/northamerica/canada/quebec/stanstead/colby_curtis_museum) fills a preserved granite villa with the story of an English-speaking border family, while [Granit Central](/northamerica/canada/quebec/stanstead/granit_central) explains the grey granite industry that shaped local buildings and work.

Stanstead is small, but it has a stronger place identity than many larger towns. Check current border rules before visiting the Haskell, and do not treat Canusa Street as a casual crossing point. The oddness here is real, and so are the rules.
