The Tomifobia Nature Trail follows a former railway corridor through the Eastern Townships landscape north of Stanstead. It is used by walkers and cyclists and links the town to a wider network of quiet country roads and old rail alignments.

Do not expect mountain drama. This is a gentle border-country ride or walk, best when you want fresh air after the museums and a sense of the farmland and woods around town.
