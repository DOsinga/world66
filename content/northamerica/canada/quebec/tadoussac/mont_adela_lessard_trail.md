The Mont Adela-Lessard Trail gives a higher view over the Saguenay Fjord and the inlets west of Tadoussac. It is a good choice when you want a land-based break from boats and boardwalks.

The climb is short enough to fit into a loose day in the village, but the payoff is real: forest, water, and the fjord opening out below.
