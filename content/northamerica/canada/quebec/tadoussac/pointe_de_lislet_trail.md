The Pointe-de-l'Islet Trail is the easiest walk in Tadoussac to recommend. The short route starts near the wharf and follows boardwalks and rock along the Saguenay side of the bay.

The view is the whole point: the fjord mouth, the ferry traffic, the St. Lawrence beyond, and sometimes marine life moving through the water below.
