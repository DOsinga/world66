Guided sea kayaking trips leave from Tadoussac Beach for the edge of the Saguenay Fjord and the St. Lawrence Estuary. On calm days it is a slower, quieter way to feel the scale of the water than a cruise boat.

Go with a guide unless you already know cold-water sea kayaking. Tides, wind, and boat traffic make this a serious stretch of river.
