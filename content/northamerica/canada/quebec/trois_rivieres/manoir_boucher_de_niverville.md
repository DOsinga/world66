Manoir Boucher de Niverville is one of the oldest buildings in Trois-Rivieres, dating from the French colonial period. Exhibits use the house to explain daily life, politics, manners, and society in New France.

It is a compact museum, but the age of the building gives it more presence than a standard local-history display.
