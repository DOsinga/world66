Parc de l'île Saint-Quentin sits where the Saint-Maurice meets the St. Lawrence, close enough to downtown Trois-Rivières to work as an easy outdoor break. In winter, flooded park paths become skating trails, with rentals for skates, sleds, and helmets.

It is a simple place, but a good one if you want fresh air without leaving the city behind.
