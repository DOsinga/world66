Parc de l'ile Saint-Quentin is an island recreation area near the meeting of the Saint-Maurice and Saint Lawrence rivers. Trails, river views, and seasonal activities make it a useful outdoor break from the historic centre of Trois-Rivieres.

In winter, parts of the trail network are turned into skating routes, with rentals available on site.
