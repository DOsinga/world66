The Quebec Museum of Popular Culture presents changing exhibitions on everyday culture, identity, and social history in Quebec. The tone is more playful and contemporary than a traditional history museum.

It is next to the Old Prison of Trois-Rivieres, making the two sights easy to combine. The museum's open storage areas are a useful bonus for visitors interested in material culture and local collecting.
