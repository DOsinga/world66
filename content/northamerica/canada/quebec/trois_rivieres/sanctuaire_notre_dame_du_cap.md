Sanctuaire Notre-Dame-du-Cap is one of North America's major Marian pilgrimage sites. The basilica, sanctuary museum, Roselets Bridge, and illuminated Stations of the Cross make the site a significant Catholic landmark as well as a major visitor destination in Trois-Rivieres.
