La Cité de l'Or is Val-d'Or's essential stop. The site uses the former Lamaque Mine to show how gold mining worked here, including an underground visit that makes the city's origin story physical rather than abstract.

It is the kind of industrial-history sight that can win over people who do not normally seek out museums. Bring a layer for the mine tour and give yourself time to connect it with Bourlamaque next door.
