The old Canora station is a neat piece of prairie railway history. Built in 1904, it belongs to the Canadian Northern Railway story that gave the town its name: Ca-No-Ra, from Canadian Northern Railway.

The station now holds local railway material, early settler artefacts, art, and visitor information. It is not a major museum, but paired with the Ukrainian sites it helps explain why this little town exists where it does.
