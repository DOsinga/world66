The Ukrainian Heritage Museum is the best single stop in Canora for understanding why this town feels different from many others on the Saskatchewan grid. Its collection covers costumes, embroidery, pysanky, pioneer tools, cookery, books, and the wider Ukrainian block settlement that took root here from the 1890s.

It is a modest volunteer-run museum, so check hours before making a special trip. The value is in the local texture: you see how prairie settlement, faith, language, and domestic craft were carried west and made practical in a new country.
