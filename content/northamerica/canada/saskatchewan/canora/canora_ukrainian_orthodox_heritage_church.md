This restored 1928 Ukrainian Orthodox church is Canora's most handsome heritage building. Its Kyivan-influenced form, icons, and stained glass tell the same settlement story as the museum, but in a quieter and more atmospheric way.

The church is a municipal heritage property and is usually best visited in summer, when occasional services and openings make it easier to see the interior. Even from outside, it gives the town a visible link to the first Ukrainian families who built religious and social life here.
