Nut Point Trail is the local hike that gives La Ronge its day-outdoor credibility. The route follows a long, forested peninsula in Lac La Ronge Provincial Park, with Shield rock underfoot and lake views opening through the trees.

It is a real hike rather than a town stroll. Start early, carry water and insect protection, and check conditions locally before committing to the full out-and-back.
