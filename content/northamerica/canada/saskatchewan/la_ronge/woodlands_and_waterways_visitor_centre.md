The Woodlands and Waterways Visitor Centre in Air Ronge is more useful than a standard brochure rack. It carries local maps, advice on canoe routes and day trips, and a small selection of regional arts, crafts, and preserves.

Stop here before setting out on the lake or north toward Stanley Mission. In a place where weather, road conditions, and local knowledge matter, a few minutes of current advice can save a lot of guessing.
