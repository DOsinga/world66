Lumsden sits in the Qu'Appelle Valley northwest of [Regina](/northamerica/canada/saskatchewan/regina), close enough for an easy afternoon but different enough to feel like you have left the city behind. The prairie drops into a green river valley here, and that shift in landscape is the town's best asset.

This is a modest place, but it clears the day-trip bar. You can pair the small [Lumsden Historical Museum](/northamerica/canada/saskatchewan/lumsden/lumsden_historical_museum) with prairie fruit at [Over the Hill Orchards and Winery](/northamerica/canada/saskatchewan/lumsden/over_the_hill_orchards_and_winery), then continue toward [Last Mountain House Provincial Historic Park](/northamerica/canada/saskatchewan/lumsden/last_mountain_house_provincial_historic_park) and [Valeport Marsh](/northamerica/canada/saskatchewan/lumsden/valeport_marsh). None is a blockbuster alone; together they make a good Saskatchewan valley day.

Lumsden is also a useful reminder that the province is not all flat highway and grain elevator. The Qu'Appelle country has bends, slopes, cottonwoods, wetlands, and small-town artists, and that is enough reason to turn off Highway 11 if you are nearby.
