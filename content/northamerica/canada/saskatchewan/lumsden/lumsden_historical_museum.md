Lumsden Historical Museum gathers relocated pioneer buildings, household objects, photographs, sports memorabilia, and farm machinery from the surrounding area. It is small and seasonal, but it gives the valley day a human scale before you head out to the wider countryside.

The museum is strongest if you like local history rather than polished interpretation. Check summer hours before making it the centre of your plan.
