Over the Hill Orchards and Winery grows prairie fruit above the Qu'Appelle Valley and turns it into wines, tastings, and seasonal food events. It is not a traditional vineyard stop, and that is the charm: Saskatchewan fruit, valley views, and a farm that makes the most of a short growing season.

Hours and public access change by season, so check ahead before driving out.
