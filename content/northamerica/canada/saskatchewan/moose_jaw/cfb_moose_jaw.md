CFB Moose Jaw, south of the city, is the training base associated with the Snowbirds, Canada's military aerobatic team. The base itself is not a casual tourist attraction, but the annual summer air show makes it part of Moose Jaw's visitor identity.

Check dates before building plans around it; outside event days, treat it as background rather than a sightseeing stop.
