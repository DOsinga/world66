Mac the Moose is a ten-metre concrete moose beside the visitor centre near the Trans-Canada Highway. It is silly, photogenic, and taken seriously enough by Moose Jaw to have become a civic mascot.

You do not plan a trip around Mac, but you will probably stop anyway.
