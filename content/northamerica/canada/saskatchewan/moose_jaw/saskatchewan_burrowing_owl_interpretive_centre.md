This seasonal interpretive centre focuses on burrowing owls and the prairie habitat they depend on. The birds are small, expressive, and threatened, which makes the centre a useful stop for families and anyone interested in grassland wildlife.

It pairs well with the nearby visitor centre and Mac the Moose.
