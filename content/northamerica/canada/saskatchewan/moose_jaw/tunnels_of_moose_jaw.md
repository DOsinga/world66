The Tunnels of Moose Jaw turn the city's underground passages into two theatrical guided tours. One follows the bootlegging and Al Capone legends of the 1920s; the other tells the harder story of Chinese immigrants and the head tax.

It is staged, not a dry museum, but the tours are the city's signature experience and should be booked ahead in busy periods.
