Moose Jaw's branch of the Western Development Museum tells Saskatchewan's transport story, from railways and farm machinery to early aviation. It is one of the better places in the province to understand how distance, grain, and machines shaped prairie life.

The scale of the exhibits makes it a strong stop even for travelers who do not usually seek out local museums.
