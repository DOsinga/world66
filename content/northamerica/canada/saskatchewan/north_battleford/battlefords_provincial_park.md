story: Battlefords Provincial Park sits on the south shore of Jackfish Lake, about 40 km north of North Battleford. The park has 4 km of sand beach — long, soft, and on warm summer afternoons crowded with day visitors from across northwestern Saskatchewan, all the way out to Lloydminster on the Alberta border.
---

The park is the obvious summer day-trip from the Battlefords: a swim, a paddle, a picnic, and a walk through the boreal-edge forest. There are tent and RV campgrounds, an 18-hole golf course, fish (walleye, perch, northern pike) in the lake, and well-marked hiking trails through the surrounding woods.

Winter brings cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, and ice fishing on Jackfish Lake. It is busy in July and August but never overwhelmed; arrive early on hot weekends to claim a beach spot.
