The Battlefords Wildlife Federation Nature Centre introduces the plants and animals of the North Saskatchewan River Valley and the surrounding Aspen Parkland. It is a small nature-focused stop close to North Battleford and Battleford.

The nearby Battlefords Goose Project adds more bird and wetland interest, making the area useful for travellers looking for an easy nature break rather than a formal museum visit.
