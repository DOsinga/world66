The Chapel Gallery is a public art gallery in North Battleford. It hosts exhibitions, workshops, and community arts programming, with a focus on regional and Saskatchewan art.

It is a useful companion stop to the Allen Sapp Gallery for visitors interested in the city's cultural side.
