Dekker's is the kind of family-owned diner every small prairie city has somewhere, with a substantial menu that runs from breakfasts the size of small countries through burgers, perogies, schnitzel, and Greek dishes left over from a previous owner. The room is straightforward, the staff know the regulars, and the coffee is bottomless.

It is the default North Battleford lunch stop for anyone who has been here more than once. Bring an appetite; do not arrive in a hurry.
