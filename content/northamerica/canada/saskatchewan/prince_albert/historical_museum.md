The Historical Museum in Prince Albert occupies the city's former fire hall and tells the local
story through photographs, rooms, tools, and everyday objects. Its collections touch on river trade,
settlement, Indigenous and newcomer histories, and the growth of a northern Saskatchewan service
centre.

It is a modest museum, but useful if Prince Albert is more than a fuel stop on your way to the
national park. The building itself gives it character, and the exhibits help explain the city’s role
between prairie farms and the boreal north.
