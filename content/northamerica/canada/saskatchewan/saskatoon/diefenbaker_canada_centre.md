The Diefenbaker Canada Centre is a museum and archive devoted to former Canadian prime minister John G. Diefenbaker. Its exhibits include 1950s-style replicas of the prime minister's office and cabinet room, along with displays on Canadian political history.

It is on the University of Saskatchewan campus, close enough to combine with a riverbank walk along the Meewasin Valley.
