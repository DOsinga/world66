Lucky Bastard Distillers is a Saskatoon craft distillery making vodka, gin, whisky-style spirits, liqueurs, and seasonal bottles with a prairie sense of place. It is a practical stop if you enjoy seeing how a local drinks scene has developed beyond beer.

The appeal is in tastings, tours, and the bottle shop rather than architecture. Check opening times before going, and treat it as an indoor afternoon stop rather than a must-see city landmark.
