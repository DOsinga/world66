Playland at Kinsmen Park is Saskatoon's small family amusement area, with child-friendly rides such as a miniature train, Ferris wheel, and merry-go-round. The surrounding park also has free play areas, including sand, water, and climbing features.

It is mainly useful for families with young children, but it is central enough to combine with a riverbank walk or a broader stop in Kinsmen Park.
