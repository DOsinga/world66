The Prairie Lily runs seasonal cruises on the South Saskatchewan River, giving Saskatoon a relaxed view from the water. It is a simple pleasure: bridges, riverbank parks, the Bessborough, and the wide prairie sky sliding past at slow speed.

Book ahead in summer, especially for evening sailings. The cruise works best after you have walked the Meewasin Valley Trail, because it lets you see the same river corridor from a different angle.
