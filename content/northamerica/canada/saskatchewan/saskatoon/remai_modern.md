Remai Modern is Saskatoon's clearest contemporary cultural landmark, a low, angular building looking over the South Saskatchewan River. The museum is best known for its Picasso linocuts, but the wider programme ranges across Canadian, Indigenous, and international modern and contemporary art.

The architecture is part of the visit: broad stairs, river views, and generous public spaces make it feel more ambitious than a regional gallery. Pair it with a walk along the Meewasin Valley trail or the nearby Broadway Bridge, so the museum sits inside the city's strongest riverside landscape.
