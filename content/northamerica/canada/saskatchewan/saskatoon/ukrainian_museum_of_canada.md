The Ukrainian Museum of Canada in Saskatoon holds one of the largest collections of Ukrainian art, textiles, and cultural artifacts outside Ukraine itself. The permanent collection includes over 35,000 items: embroidered clothing, decorated Easter eggs (pysanka), ceramics, woodwork, and archival photographs documenting the immigrant experience on the prairies.

The collection traces the Ukrainian diaspora in Canada from the earliest settlers of the 1890s through the twentieth century, with particular attention to the community's cultural resilience during times of hardship — including the internment of Ukrainian Canadians during World War I, an often-overlooked chapter in Canadian history. The museum is small enough to be seen in an hour or two, but the quality of the textile collection in particular makes it worth the visit.
