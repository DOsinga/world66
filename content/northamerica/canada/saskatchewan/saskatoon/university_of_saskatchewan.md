The University of Saskatchewan campus is centred on a broad open area known as the Bowl, with stone academic buildings that give it more architectural presence than many Canadian campuses. Several buildings hold free public exhibits, including natural history displays and antiquities collections.

It is not a single museum visit, but it is a worthwhile walk if you are interested in campus architecture, small collections, or the riverbank area east of downtown Saskatoon.
