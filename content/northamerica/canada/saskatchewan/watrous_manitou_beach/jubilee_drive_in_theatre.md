Jubilee Drive-In Theatre is a nostalgic add-on rather than a daytime sight, but it helps explain why Manitou Beach still feels like a throwback resort village. In summer, a film under the prairie sky is a better evening than driving straight back to Saskatoon.

Confirm the programme locally before building a trip around it, since drive-in schedules are seasonal and weather-sensitive.
