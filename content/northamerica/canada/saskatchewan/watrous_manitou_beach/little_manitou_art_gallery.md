Little Manitou Art Gallery is a cluster of pavilion galleries near the lake, with works by local and regional artists. It is small, informal, and best treated as part of a wander through Manitou Beach rather than a museum outing on its own.

Stop in after the beach or before an evening at Danceland. The setting matters as much as the art.
