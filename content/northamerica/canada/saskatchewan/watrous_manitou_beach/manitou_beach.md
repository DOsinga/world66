Manitou Beach is the simple, essential stop: a sandy lakefront on Little Manitou Lake. The water is famous for its salinity, so you float higher than you expect, a prairie version of the Dead Sea experience.

Bring a towel and a sense of humour. The lake is the sort of odd local pleasure that sounds overpromised until you try it.
