Godfrey Dean Art Gallery is small, but it gives Yorkton a real cultural anchor. The gallery presents changing exhibitions of local, provincial, and national contemporary artists, with workshops and public programs tied to the wider region.

It is worth checking what is on before you go. When the exhibition is strong, this is the stop that keeps Yorkton from feeling like only a practical service centre.
