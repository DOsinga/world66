---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Yorkton's useful sights are close together and mostly tell the same larger story: settlement, agriculture, railways, and the way a prairie service town built a civic life around them. The Western Development Museum is the headline stop, while the Brick Flour Mill and courthouse bring the town's early industrial and public architecture into focus.

For a break from buildings, the Ravine Ecological Preserve gives Yorkton a short green walk through grassland, marsh, and aspen parkland on the west side of town.
