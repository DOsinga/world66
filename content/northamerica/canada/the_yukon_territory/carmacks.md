Carmacks is a small Yukon River village where the North Klondike Highway meets the Robert Campbell Highway. It is an easy place to treat as a fuel stop, but the river, the Little Salmon/Carmacks First Nation, and the old route north give it more substance than its size suggests.

The best day here starts with the local story. [Tagé Cho Hudän Interpretive Centre](/northamerica/canada/the_yukon_territory/carmacks/tage_cho_hudan_interpretive_centre) introduces the Northern Tutchone culture of the Little Salmon/Carmacks First Nation, whose ties to this stretch of the Yukon River go back far beyond the Klondike roads. In town, the [Carmacks Riverfront Boardwalk](/northamerica/canada/the_yukon_territory/carmacks/carmacks_riverfront_boardwalk) gives a slow, open look at the river that made the community possible.

The headline stop is north of town at [Five Finger Rapids](/northamerica/canada/the_yukon_territory/carmacks/five_finger_rapids), where rock islands split the Yukon River into channels. It is a strong pullout even if you are just driving between Whitehorse and Dawson City, and a better one if you have time to walk down from the viewpoint.
