Tagé Cho Hudän Interpretive Centre is the key cultural stop in Carmacks. It focuses on the Little Salmon/Carmacks First Nation and the Northern Tutchone people, with exhibits on traditional clothing, tools, river life, hunting, and the wider cultural landscape around the Yukon River.

The centre is small, but it gives the village context that a highway stop cannot. Go before you walk the riverfront; the stories make the water, the old routes, and the surrounding country easier to read.
