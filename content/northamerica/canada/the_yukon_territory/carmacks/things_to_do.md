title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Carmacks works best as a short Yukon River stop with one real excursion. Start at [Tagé Cho Hudän Interpretive Centre](/northamerica/canada/the_yukon_territory/carmacks/tage_cho_hudan_interpretive_centre), walk the [riverfront boardwalk](/northamerica/canada/the_yukon_territory/carmacks/carmacks_riverfront_boardwalk), then drive north to [Five Finger Rapids](/northamerica/canada/the_yukon_territory/carmacks/five_finger_rapids) if the weather is clear.
