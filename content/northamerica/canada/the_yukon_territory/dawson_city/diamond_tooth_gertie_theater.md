Diamond Tooth Gertie Theater recreates the atmosphere of Dawson City's gold rush entertainment halls, with staged cancan-style shows, music, and period character. It is more performance venue than museum, but the appeal is tied directly to the town's Klondike history.

It works best as an evening stop after seeing Dawson's daytime heritage sites, including the historic townscape and local museums.
