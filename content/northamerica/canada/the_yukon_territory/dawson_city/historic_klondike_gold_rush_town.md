Parks Canada preserves a group of Dawson's most important gold-rush buildings, including the Palace Grand Theatre, the Commissioner's Residence, and the Robert Service Cabin. Together with the boardwalks and false-front storefronts, they make Dawson feel less like a normal small town and more like a surviving fragment of 1898.

Guided walks are the best way to make sense of the streets. Without context, the buildings can look merely quaint; with the stories attached, they become a map of boomtown wealth, hurry, performance, and collapse.
