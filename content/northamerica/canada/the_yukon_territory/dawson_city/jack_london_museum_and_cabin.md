Jack London came north during the Klondike stampede and left with material that later fed stories such as *The Call of the Wild* and *White Fang*. The cabin associated with his time in the region was moved to Dawson, where it now forms a small museum.

It is a modest stop, but a good one if you have read London or want to understand how quickly the Klondike turned into myth.
