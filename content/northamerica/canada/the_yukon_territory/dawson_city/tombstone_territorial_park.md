Tombstone Territorial Park protects a stark landscape of tundra, permafrost landforms, dark peaks, and wildlife north of Dawson City. The park is one of the Yukon's great backcountry areas, with access from the Dempster Highway and summer interpretation at the visitor centre.

Come prepared for remote conditions: weather changes quickly, trails can be rough, and even short walks feel far from town. Travellers with time and proper gear can continue into designated backcountry camping areas.
