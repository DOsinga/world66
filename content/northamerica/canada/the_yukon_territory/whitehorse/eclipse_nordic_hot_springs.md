Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs is one of the easiest ways to feel the Yukon in your bones: hot mineral water, cold air, forest, and steam. The springs sit north of Whitehorse and are especially satisfying in winter, when the contrast between pool and snow is the whole point.

It is polished rather than rustic, so expect a managed spa experience with Yukon scenery attached. Book ahead in busy periods, bring a hat for cold nights, and leave time for the drive back to town.
