The Kwanlin Dün Cultural Centre sits on the Yukon River waterfront and serves as both a community gathering place for the Kwanlin Dün First Nation and a cultural education facility for visitors. The architecture is deliberately impressive — a modern building with Indigenous design elements facing the river from which the Kwanlin Dün have fished for thousands of years.

Exhibits cover the history and culture of the Southern Tutchone people, including traditional knowledge, artwork, and the disruption caused by the Gold Rush and subsequent settlement. The centre hosts events, performances, and workshops throughout the year. If you want to understand whose land Whitehorse is built on and what that means in practice today, this is the best place to start.
