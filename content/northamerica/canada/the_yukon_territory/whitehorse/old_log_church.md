The Old Log Church Museum is one of downtown Whitehorse's most approachable historic buildings. The small log church preserves stories of Anglican missions, Yukon communities, and frontier life during the gold-rush and early territorial periods.

Its scale is modest, but that is part of the appeal: you can get a focused sense of local history without leaving the centre. It pairs well with the MacBride Museum, the riverfront, and other short stops around old Whitehorse.
