Whitehorse Fishway was built to help migrating fish move past the hydroelectric dam on the Yukon River. For visitors, it is a compact but distinctive nature stop, with interpretive material explaining salmon migration, river ecology, and the engineered ladder itself.

The best visits are seasonal, when fish are actually moving through the system, but the site is still useful for understanding how the city relates to the river. It pairs easily with Miles Canyon or a riverside walk south of downtown.
