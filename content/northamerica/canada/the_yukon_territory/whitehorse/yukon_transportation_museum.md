The Yukon Transportation Museum covers the machinery that opened and connected the territory: aircraft, riverboats, road vehicles, and equipment built for long distances and hard weather. Its best-known landmark is a DC-3 mounted outside as a giant wind vane.

It pairs well with the nearby Yukon Beringia Interpretive Centre, especially for visitors arriving by road or air who want the story behind travel in the North.
