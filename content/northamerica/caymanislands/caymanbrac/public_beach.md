The Public Beach on the south-west coast is the longest stretch of white sand on Cayman Brac, a quiet curve of beach near the airport with shade trees, picnic shelters and shallow turquoise water. By Caribbean standards it is unfussy — no bars, no jet skis, no chair rental, and most days almost empty.

The beach faces the dominant wind so there is a small chop most of the year; it is best for swimming and floating rather than serious snorkelling, though the snorkelling improves further along the coast toward the Brac Reef hotel. Bring everything you need; there are no facilities beyond a couple of shelters and a long-drop toilet.
