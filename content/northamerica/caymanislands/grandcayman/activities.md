Grand Cayman's diving is the best-organised in the Caribbean, and it shows in the prices as well as the standards. The **North Wall** and the drop-offs off Seven Mile Beach are the staple; the ***Kittiwake***, a US Navy submarine rescue ship sunk deliberately in 2011, lies in shallow water off the beach and is one of the few wrecks that works for snorkellers too. Shore diving is unusually good here — the ironshore on the west and north gives deep water within a swim of the car park.

**Stingray City** is the other thing everyone does: a sandbar in the North Sound where southern stingrays have gathered since fishermen cleaned their catch there, and where you stand in waist-deep water among them. It is busy, it is undeniably a set piece, and it is still remarkable.

Beyond the water: the **Mastic Trail** through old-growth forest in the interior, the blue iguana programme at the botanic park, and the Crystal Caves inland.
