One of the broadest operations on the island: shore diving straight off the ironshore at the north-west point, where the wall starts close in and you can do a proper deep dive without a boat, plus boat trips, freediving, rebreather and technical training.

Grand Cayman's diving is built on the **North Wall** and the drop-offs off Seven Mile Beach, with the *Kittiwake* — a US Navy submarine rescue vessel sunk as an artificial reef in 2011 — sitting in shallow water off the beach and accessible to snorkellers as well as divers.
