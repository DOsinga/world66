Tree of Life is a tropical garden and wildlife rescue centre near Cahuita, with palms, heliconias, bromeliads, and shaded paths through dense Caribbean greenery. It gives you a close look at rainforest plants and rescued animals without needing a long trek.

The paths are easy to manage, so it works well for families or anyone who wants a gentler nature stop near town. It is best treated as a quiet garden visit rather than a substitute for Cahuita National Park.
