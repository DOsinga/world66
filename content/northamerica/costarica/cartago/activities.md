Cartago's own sights — the basilica and the ruined parish church — need no guide and no ticket. What gets booked here is the country around it: [Irazú](/northamerica/costarica/irazu) up the mountain and the [Orosi valley](/northamerica/costarica/orosi) south, usually combined into one day.
