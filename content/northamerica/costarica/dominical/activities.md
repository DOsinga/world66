The beach break here is powerful with a serious rip, and a lesson buys judgement about where to be as much as instruction. Schools move to Dominicalito next door when the main beach is too big.

Inland, the Nauyaca waterfalls are the other reason to stop — a two-tier fall about ten kilometres up the road to San Isidro, reached on foot, on horseback or in a truck.
