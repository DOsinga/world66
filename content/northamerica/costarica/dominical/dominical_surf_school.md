Dominical's beach break is powerful and has a serious rip, which is exactly why a lesson here is worth more than one somewhere gentler. This school has taught on it since 2005, capped at two students per instructor, and moves to Dominicalito next door when the main beach is too much.

They also run multi-day camps that fold in the Nauyaca waterfalls up the road.
