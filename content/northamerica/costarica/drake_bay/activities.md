Nothing here is done without a boat or a guide. Corcovado requires a certified guide by park rule, and the operators below all use local ICT-certified ones; the choice is mainly between the San Pedrillo station, an hour up the coast, and Sirena, further and much better for animals.

The other trip is Caño Island, forty minutes offshore, for the snorkelling and diving. Whale and dolphin trips run in season, and the night walk in the forest behind the village is worth a evening.
