A 2,800-hectare reserve covering the steep, forested ridge that rises directly behind Golfito town. The refuge protects one of the wettest stretches of Pacific lowland rainforest in Central America, with rainfall exceeding 5 metres a year and a bird list of over 230 species.

A network of dirt roads and short trails climbs through the forest from town. The track behind the airport leads up to a series of viewpoints over the gulf. Squirrel monkeys, scarlet macaws, and toucans are common; jaguars are present but very rarely seen. A guide is strongly recommended — local outfitters operate from Golfito hotels.
