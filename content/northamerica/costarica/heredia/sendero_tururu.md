Sendero Tururu is a short local trail north of central Heredia, restored by the local government in 2023. It drops toward the Segundo River and follows the water upstream to a small waterfall.

The walk is a simple outdoors detour rather than a wilderness hike. The river receives runoff from farms and nearby residential areas, so treat it as a place for a walk and a look at the falls, not for swimming.
