title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Heredia's compact historic centre around the [Parque Central](/northamerica/costarica/heredia/parque_central) repays a relaxed afternoon of exploration. The [Iglesia de la Inmaculada Concepción](/northamerica/costarica/heredia/iglesia_inmaculada) is the most significant colonial building in the Central Valley still standing intact, and [El Fortín](/northamerica/costarica/heredia/el_fortin) is an unusual military curiosity that appeared on the country's currency. The [National University of Costa Rica](/northamerica/costarica/heredia/national_university) campus gives the city its youthful energy and is pleasant to walk through.

Beyond the centre, the surrounding hills are the main draw. Coffee plantation tours are easy to arrange and give genuine insight into the crop that shaped Costa Rica's modern economy. The [Barva Volcano](/northamerica/costarica/heredia/barva_volcano) in Braulio Carrillo National Park offers hiking through cloud forest to a flooded crater, and the [Sibu Chocolate](/northamerica/costarica/heredia/sibu_chocolate) facility runs excellent tours showing cacao production from bean to bar.
