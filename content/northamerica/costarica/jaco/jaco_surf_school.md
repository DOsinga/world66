Lessons on the Jacó beach break at every level from a first stand-up to one-to-one coaching, plus board hire by the day or the week — soft tops through to shortboards.

Jacó's wave is forgiving and close to San José, which is why so many people learn here; if you already surf, the hire counter matters more than the lesson, and Playa Hermosa is ten minutes south.
