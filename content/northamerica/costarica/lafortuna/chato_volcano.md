Cerro Chato is the dormant volcano adjacent to Arenal, its squat profile visible to the southeast of Arenal's perfect cone. Unlike Arenal, Chato can be climbed — the summit crater holds a beautiful green lake, one of the more unexpected sights in the region. The hike starts from the El Silencio trailhead near the national park and climbs through dense rainforest for about 2.5 km, gaining 500 metres of elevation.

The trail is steep in places and can be very muddy; hiking poles and waterproof boots are strongly recommended. The reward at the top is the crater lake, surrounded by forest and usually shrouded in mist, with Arenal's cone visible over the rim on clear days. Allow 4–5 hours round trip. The hike is independent — no guide required, though the trail can be confusing in mist. The national park entrance fee applies.
