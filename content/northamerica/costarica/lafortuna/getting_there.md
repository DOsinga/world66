---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

San José is the main gateway. By bus, the journey takes about 4 hours via Ciudad Quesada (San Carlos); direct buses run from San José's Terminal 7-10. By car the drive is 3–3.5 hours on the InterAmericana Highway north to Naranjo, then northeast to La Fortuna; or via the northern route through Ciudad Quesada.

The popular route from Monteverde is the boat-and-van option: a shared taxi takes you down to Lake Arenal, a boat crosses to the other side, and another taxi takes you to La Fortuna. The whole trip takes about 3 hours and costs around $25–30. It is scenic and much shorter than the road route (which adds another 2+ hours of winding mountain driving).

La Fortuna Arenal Airport (IATA: FON) receives small charter flights. No scheduled international service.
