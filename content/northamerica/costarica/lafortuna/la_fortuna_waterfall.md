The Catarata de La Fortuna is a 75-metre waterfall about 6 km from town, dropping from a basalt cliff into a deep pool of intensely coloured water. It is one of the most spectacular waterfalls in Costa Rica and the most accessible from La Fortuna. A path of around 500 steep steps leads down from the trailhead to the pool — the descent takes about 20 minutes; the ascent back up takes longer.

Swimming in the pool at the base is allowed and popular. The water is cold, fed from mountain streams, and the setting is dramatic — the falls thunder down above you and the surrounding forest comes to the cliff edge on both sides. Arrive early to avoid peak crowds. Admission covers the national park entry and there is a small cafe and changing facilities at the top. Tours from La Fortuna typically combine the waterfall with a visit to the national park.
