Lake Arenal is the largest lake in Costa Rica, a reservoir created by a dam in 1979 that flooded the original town of Arenal (the residents were relocated). It stretches 33 km along the base of the Tilarán mountain range and is used for kayaking, windsurfing, and sport fishing. The road along its northern shore between La Fortuna and Tilarán passes through some of the finest scenery in the region.

Windsurfing and kitesurfing are particularly good at the western end of the lake near Nuevo Arenal and Tilawa, where the trade winds funnel through the mountains from December to April. Kayaking on the eastern end near La Fortuna is gentler and good for birdwatching. Rainbow bass (guapote) and tilapia fishing tours can be arranged from La Fortuna. The boat-and-van route between La Fortuna and Monteverde crosses the lake at its midpoint — a more scenic alternative to the road journey that also saves time.
