The Mistico Hanging Bridges park offers a network of trails through primary and secondary rainforest, connected by a series of suspension bridges that cross ravines and tree canopies at heights of up to 45 metres. It is one of the better alternatives to zip-lining for those who prefer a slower, quieter way to experience the forest canopy — the bridges sway gently and offer good views of the surrounding forest without the speed.

The park is about 18 km from La Fortuna on the road toward Arenal Lake. The trail system covers 3 km and combines six hanging bridges with several ground-level paths. Birding is excellent in the early morning, with possibilities for toucans, trogons, motmots, and various hummingbirds. Self-guided entry is available; guided tours with a naturalist guide are more productive for wildlife. Comfortable closed shoes are recommended — the trails are well-maintained but can be muddy after rain.
