Tabacón is the most famous hot spring resort near La Fortuna, located 12 km northwest of town along the road to Nuevo Arenal. The thermal pools here are fed by water naturally heated deep in the Arenal volcanic system, ranging in temperature from warm to genuinely hot. The resort has several pools at different temperatures, waterfalls, and a swimup bar, with the volcano looming on the hillside above.

The entry price is substantial — one of the higher admissions in Costa Rica — but includes use of all pools, changing facilities, and a meal credit. Cheaper alternatives exist: Baldi Hot Springs is 7 km from town and costs less, and El Choyin near the river is a basic natural pool with minimal facilities and very low cost. The evening hours at Tabacón, with the pools lit and the volcano visible in the darkness, are particularly atmospheric. Day-visit and dinner packages are both available.
