---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

La Fortuna is wet by any standard — the Caribbean slope of the continental divide receives rainfall throughout the year. The drier months are February through April, when clear mornings are more frequent and the volcano is more often visible. Even in the dry season, cloud builds around Arenal by noon on most days. Rain gear is useful year-round.

The wetter months (May–November) bring daily afternoon downpours but also lusher forest and better wildlife. The waterfalls run higher and the hot springs feel better in the rain. This is also the quieter period with lower prices and fewer visitors. The week between Christmas and New Year is the busiest period of the year.
