La Fortuna Rafting runs half-day trips on the Balsa and Toro rivers close to town — class II and III, suitable for a first time on whitewater — and organises the longer runs on the Sarapiquí and the Pacuare for people who want more.

Half a day here is the easiest way to get on a river in Costa Rica without giving up a whole day to driving.
