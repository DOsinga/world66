Liberia is an airport and a junction rather than a destination, and most of what is sold here happens somewhere else: [Rincón de la Vieja](/northamerica/costarica/rincon_de_la_vieja) an hour north, the Río Celeste, Palo Verde on the Tempisque, and the beaches west.

Guanacaste distances are longer than they look, so an agency with a van is worth more here than in most of the country.
