A Guanacaste agency covering the day-trip circuit from Liberia and the northern beaches: [Rincón de la Vieja](/northamerica/costarica/rincon_de_la_vieja), the Río Celeste, Palo Verde on the Tempisque, Monteverde and the Arenal volcano, plus transfers between them.

Useful because Guanacaste's distances are longer than they look on the map and almost everything worth seeing is a drive.
