Rincón de la Vieja is the volcanic day trip that makes Liberia more than an airport town. The national park protects an active massif in the Guanacaste Cordillera, with dry forest on the lower slopes and a geothermal landscape of fumaroles, bubbling mud, hot springs, and sulphur vents. It is one of the easiest places in Costa Rica to see volcanic activity without a long expedition.

The Las Pailas sector is the usual focus, with trails that loop past steam vents and mineral-stained ground before reaching forest and waterfall options. Start early, carry water, and check current trail restrictions, because volcanic activity and weather can close routes. Even with closures, the mix of heat, forest, and restless ground is memorable.
