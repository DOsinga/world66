Limón's tourism runs on cruise calls, and the operators here sell the day out from the dock: the Tortuguero canals, [Cahuita](/northamerica/costarica/cahuita)'s national park, banana plantations and the sloth sanctuary.

Arriving overland, the same circuit is worth buying for the transport alone — the sights are spread over sixty kilometres of coast with no practical bus between them.
