title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Limón's cooking is Afro-Caribbean and unlike anything you will find in the Costa Rican highlands. The staples are **rice and beans cooked in coconut milk** (one word here, distinct from the gallo pinto served further inland), **rondón** (a slow-cooked seafood and coconut stew), **patí** (a Jamaican-style spiced beef pastry), and **pan bon**, a heavy fruit-and-cheese bread eaten at Easter and saved year-round in bakeries that know what they are doing.

For the cheapest and most authentic meals, head straight for the cooked-food counters at the Mercado Central. For sit-down evening dining, the simple sodas along the malecón and around Parque Vargas serve fresh fish and the full Caribbean menu.
