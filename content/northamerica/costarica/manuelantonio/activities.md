The national park is the draw and needs no operator — but it does reward a guide, because the sloths, and most of the birds, are found by people with a telescope who know which branch to check. Guides wait at the entrance; park tickets must be bought online in advance and the daily quota does sell out.

Off the water, the catamaran trips down the coast leave from the marina at [Quepos](/northamerica/costarica/quepos) next door.
