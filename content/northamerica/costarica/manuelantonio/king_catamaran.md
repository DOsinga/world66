The King Catamaran runs two trips a day out of Marina Pez Vela — a morning sail with snorkelling stops along the Manuel Antonio coast, and an afternoon sunset cruise — both with food and drinks included, which is most of what people want from a catamaran day.

Dolphins are common on the crossing, and humpbacks pass between July and November.
