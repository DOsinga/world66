Monteverde is one of [Costa Rica](/northamerica/costarica)'s most visited destinations, and for good reason. The cloud forest reserve here protects some of the most biodiverse habitat on earth — orchids, bromeliads, mosses, and hundreds of bird species including the resplendent quetzal.

The area sits at around 1,400 metres in the Tilarán Mountains, which means cool, misty weather year-round. The two main reserves are the [Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/monteverde_cloud_forest_reserve) and the [Santa Elena Cloud Forest Reserve](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/santa_elena_cloud_forest_reserve), both offering well-maintained trail systems through dense, dripping forest. The smaller [Curi Cancha](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/curi_cancha_reserve) reserve is one of the best places in the country to see the resplendent quetzal.

The town of [Santa Elena](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/santa_elena_village) is the commercial centre, with restaurants, tour agencies, and hostels. The surrounding area has a strong Quaker community that settled here in the 1950s, and their [cheese factory](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/monteverde_cheese_factory) is still operating. Canopy tours, hanging bridges, and night walks are the main organised activities, but the real draw is simply being in the forest.
