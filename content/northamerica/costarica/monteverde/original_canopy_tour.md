This is the zipline that started the industry: a canopy tour built in Monteverde in 1994 for researchers to move through the treetops, which turned into the activity now sold in every tropical country on earth.

Ten cables, a Tarzan swing, a rappel and a climb up the inside of a hollow strangler fig — and it is still in the cloud forest rather than over a cleared hillside, which is the difference from most of its imitators. From eighty dollars, with combinations that add the night walk, a coffee mill or the hanging bridges.
