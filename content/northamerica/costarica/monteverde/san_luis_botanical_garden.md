San Luis Botanical Garden is a quieter nature stop near Monteverde, with arboretum trails and a collection of medicinal plants. Resident naturalists lead walks that make the forest and garden more useful than a simple wander.

It works best for travellers who want a slower, more educational outing after the cloud forest reserves. The nearby San Luis waterfall can be combined with the garden for a fuller half-day.
