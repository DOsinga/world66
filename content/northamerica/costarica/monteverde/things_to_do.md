title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The two big draws are the [Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/monteverde_cloud_forest_reserve) and the smaller, quieter [Santa Elena Cloud Forest Reserve](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/santa_elena_cloud_forest_reserve). For a better chance of spotting the resplendent quetzal, the private [Curi Cancha Wildlife Reserve](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/curi_cancha_reserve) is widely considered the best bet.

For the canopy itself, [Selvatura Park](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/selvatura_park) and [Sky Adventures](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/sky_walk_sky_trek) run the area's best networks of hanging bridges and ziplines. A guided [night walk](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/night_walk) is the easiest way to see sloths, kinkajous, and vipers. Smaller, easy-going stops include the [Monteverde Butterfly Garden](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/monteverde_butterfly_garden), the [Serpentario](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/serpentario_de_monteverde), the [Ranario frog pond](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/ranario_de_monteverde), the [Bat Jungle](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/bat_jungle), and the [Orchid Garden](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/orchid_garden).

The [Monteverde Cheese Factory](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/monteverde_cheese_factory) is the Quaker community's original economic backbone and still produces good semi-hard cheeses. The [Don Juan Coffee Tour](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/coffee_tour) walks you through coffee, cacao and cane production on a working finca. Off the road between Santa Elena and the main reserve, you can climb up the inside of the hollow [Ficus La Raiz strangler fig](/northamerica/costarica/monteverde/ficus_la_raiz).
