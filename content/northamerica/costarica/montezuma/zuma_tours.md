Zuma Tours runs the snorkelling and island trips out of Montezuma — Isla Tortuga and the Cabo Blanco coast — and the water taxi across the Gulf of Nicoya to Jacó, which turns a five-hour drive round the gulf into an hour on a boat.

They also cover [Santa Teresa](/northamerica/costarica/santa_teresa) over the hill, and the Montezuma waterfall, which you can also simply walk to for nothing.
