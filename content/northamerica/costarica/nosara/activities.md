Playa Guiones is the reason people come: a long sand-bottomed break that works most of the year and is about as forgiving a place to learn as Costa Rica has. Schools line the road behind it and all teach on the same wave.

The other thing to time is the turtles. Ostional, twenty minutes north, gets olive ridley *arribadas* — mass nestings of tens of thousands of animals over a few days, usually around the last quarter of the moon between July and December — and visits are guided by the village association.
