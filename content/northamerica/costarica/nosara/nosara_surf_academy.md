Playa Guiones is one of the most reliable beginner waves in Costa Rica — a long sand-bottomed break that works most of the year — and Nosara Surf Academy teaches on it, from first lessons to coaching for people who already stand up.

The beach has no lighting and no hotel frontage by local ordinance, so lessons run to the tide rather than the clock.
