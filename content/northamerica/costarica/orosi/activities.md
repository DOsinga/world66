---

The valley loop is a drive, and the stops on it are ticketed individually rather than sold as tours: the thermal pools, the 1743 church in Orosi village, the ruins at Ujarrás and the Lankester gardens nearer Cartago.

A car makes this easy and its absence makes it hard; the alternative is a day tour from San José or [Cartago](/northamerica/costarica/cartago) that takes in Irazú as well.
