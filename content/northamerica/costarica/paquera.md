Paquera is not the Nicoya Peninsula's prettiest town, but it is one of its most useful. Ferries from Puntarenas land nearby, buses fan out toward Montezuma and Santa Teresa, and the sheltered Gulf of Nicoya sits close enough for island trips, kayaking, and quiet beach afternoons.

Most travellers pass through too quickly. Stay a night and Paquera becomes a practical base for [Curú National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/costarica/cur_national_par), boat trips to [Isla Tortuga](/northamerica/costarica/paquera/isla_tortuga), and the calm water around [Playa Órganos](/northamerica/costarica/paquera/playa_organos). It is cheaper and less performative than the peninsula's surf towns, with more local life and fewer sunset cocktails.

The trade-off is simple: Paquera is spread out, hot, and quiet after dark. Come for access rather than nightlife, and keep your expectations grounded.
