Parismina is the Caribbean end of the Camino de Costa Rica, the long-distance route that crosses the country through villages, farms, mountains, and protected areas toward the Pacific near Quepos. For most visitors, that is trivia; for walkers, it gives the village a satisfying edge-of-continent feeling.

Do not improvise the full trail from here without planning. The route takes about two weeks for many hikers, needs lodging coordination, and is best treated as a supported rural route rather than a wilderness path.
