Parismina has a long reputation among anglers for tarpon and snook. The river mouth, canals, and nearshore Caribbean waters give local guides several options depending on season, weather, and water conditions.

This is not casual pier fishing. Arrange a local boat, check what tackle and licenses are needed, and be honest about your experience level before heading out through river-mouth surf.
