Playa Herradura is a dark-sand beach 6 km north of Jacó on the Pacific coast. The bay is calm and well-protected, making it better for swimming than many nearby beaches. The [Los Sueños marina](/northamerica/costarica/playa_herradura/los_suenos_marina) development dominates the north end of the bay; the marina is the largest on Costa Rica's Pacific coast and a major hub for sport fishing charters.

The town itself is small and quiet — a contrast to the busier resort of Jacó to the south. It is a good base for visiting [Carara National Park](/northamerica/costarica/playa_herradura/carara_national_park), about 15 km north on the coastal highway. Carara is one of the most accessible parks on the Pacific coast and reliably good for scarlet macaw sightings. The [beach](/northamerica/costarica/playa_herradura/playa_herradura_beach) itself is sheltered and swimmable, with dark volcanic sand characteristic of this stretch of the Pacific.
