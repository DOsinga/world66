Coco is a dive town. The boats run out to the Catalinas for manta and mobula rays between December and April, and to the Bat Islands in Santa Rosa park for bull sharks — an advanced dive with current and a long crossing, and the operators will ask about your logbook.

Sport fishing goes from the same beach, and the town is a cheaper base than the Papagayo resorts twenty minutes north.
