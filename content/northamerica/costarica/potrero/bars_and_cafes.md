---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Potrero's bar scene is low-key and centred on the beachfront. A few open-air spots overlook the bay and do steady business through the evenings with cold Imperial beer, cocktails made with local guaro or rum, and the kind of unhurried service that suits the place. The crowd is a relaxed mix of long-term expat residents, visiting surfers, and fishing charter clients celebrating or commiserating after a day on the water.
