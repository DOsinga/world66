---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Guanacaste has one of the driest climates in Costa Rica, which makes it the most reliably sunny part of the country. The dry season runs from December to April — hot, blue-skied days, calm seas, and the best conditions for sport fishing and offshore boat trips. This is peak tourist season and prices are correspondingly higher; December through March in particular sees the most visitors.

The wet season (May–November) brings afternoon rains, lower prices, fewer crowds, and lush green countryside. The rains rarely last all day and mornings are often clear. The roads around Potrero can get rough in heavy rain, and a 4WD becomes genuinely useful rather than merely comfortable. Surf is generally better in the wet season, and fishing remains good through the year.

The shoulder months of May and November offer a reasonable compromise: decent weather, lower rates, and fewer people. Water temperature stays warm (around 27–29°C) year-round.
