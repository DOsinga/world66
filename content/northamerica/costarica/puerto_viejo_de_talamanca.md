Puerto Viejo de Talamanca is the beating heart of Costa Rica's South Caribbean coast, a small, sandy-streeted town where reggae drifts from beach bars and the pace of life matches the slow roll of the waves. The atmosphere is relaxed and multicultural — Afro-Caribbean, indigenous Bribri, and expat communities all mix here.

Surfers come for the [Salsa Brava](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca/salsa_brava), a powerful reef break that is one of the best waves in Central America when conditions are right. Everyone else comes for the beaches stretching south toward [Manzanillo](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca/manzanillo) — [Playa Cocles](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca/playa_cocles), [Playa Chiquita](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca/playa_chiquita), and [Punta Uva](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca/punta_uva) are all within cycling distance. The [Gandoca-Manzanillo Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca/gandoca_manzanillo_refuge) protects a stunning stretch of coastline where you can hike through coastal forest, snorkel coral reefs, and spot howler monkeys.

In October and November, the great raptor migration passes overhead at [Kekoldi](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca/kekoldi_reserve), with thousands of hawks and vultures funnelling along the Caribbean slope. Marine turtles nest at nearby [Playa Negra](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca/playa_negra) and Gandoca. Further afield, trips to the Bribri indigenous community at [Yorkin](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca/yorkin) offer a glimpse of traditional life along the river.
