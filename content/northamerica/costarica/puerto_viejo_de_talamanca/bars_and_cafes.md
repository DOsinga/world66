---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Puerto Viejo's nightlife is famous on the Costa Rican backpacker circuit — reggae, salsa, and the steady soundtrack of waves. Most of the action concentrates on the main beachfront strip and a few side streets running back from the sand. Bars open late, close late, and stay loose.

**Lazy Mon** (on the seafront) and **Salsa Brava** (across from the wave) are the perennial favourites for sundowners and live music. The cheap Imperial beers come in tall bottles; rum or guaro is what to order if you want to fit in. Cafés serving good coffee and breakfasts have multiplied in recent years — the **Bread & Chocolate** café-bakery is a long-standing morning fixture.
