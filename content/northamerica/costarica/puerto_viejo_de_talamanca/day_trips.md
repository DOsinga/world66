The natural day trip is **Cahuita**, half an hour up the coast, where the national park has a beautiful coastal hiking trail and one of Costa Rica's only living coral reefs. The Aviarios Sloth Sanctuary on the way is a worthwhile stop.

In the other direction, hop a dugout up to the Bribri village of **Yorkin** on the Panamanian border for a day with the Stibrawpa women's cooperative. The town of **Bribri** itself is worth a look for the busy Saturday market and a hint of the indigenous Talamanca canton beyond the tourist coast. Across the border, the islands of **Bocas del Toro** in Panama are a day trip if you want a passport stamp but really deserve at least an overnight.
