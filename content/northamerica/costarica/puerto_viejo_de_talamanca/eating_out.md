---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Puerto Viejo's food scene punches above its weight. Afro-Caribbean cooking dominates — rice and beans cooked in coconut milk, patacones, rondón fish stew, jerk chicken, and the famous **pati** (a spicy beef pastry sold from street stalls). You'll also find Italian, Israeli, Lebanese, Argentine and fusion restaurants up and down the main road — the town has drawn a multinational expat community for decades.

Most places are open-air, service is unhurried, and the best seats are the ones closest to the beach. **Tamara** for traditional Caribbean dishes, **Stashu's con Fusion** for fusion plates, **Como en mi Casa** for Argentine-flavoured comfort food, and **Lazy Mon** for casual bar food and live music. Local cacao is worth seeking out — try a hot chocolate at **Caribeans Coffee & Chocolate**, which works directly with Bribri growers.
