---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Puerto Viejo sits about 220 km southeast of San José on Costa Rica's Caribbean coast. The most common way to arrive is by **bus**: regular services run from San José's Caribbean terminal (MEPE) to Puerto Viejo, taking around 4–5 hours via Limón. The trip is comfortable and inexpensive.

Faster but pricier are the **shared shuttle vans** that pick up at hotels in San José, La Fortuna and Monteverde; popular operators include Caribe Shuttle and Interbus. **Driving** is straightforward — a rental car gives you the freedom to explore the beaches south to Manzanillo — but watch for potholes and slow-moving trucks on the road from Limón.

From Panama, several daily buses run from **Bocas del Toro** and **Almirante** via the Sixaola border, taking 4–5 hours total including the crossing.
