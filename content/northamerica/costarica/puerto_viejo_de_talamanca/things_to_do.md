---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Puerto Viejo is the kind of place where the activities all involve being outside, in the water, or both. Rent a bike from one of the dozens of shops along the main street and ride south through coastal forest to the beaches of **Playa Cocles**, **Playa Chiquita**, and **Punta Uva** — a string of golden- and dark-sand bays separated by rocky headlands and reef.

Inland, the **Jaguar Rescue Center** and the **Kekoldi Indigenous Reserve** are easy half-day trips, and the **Gandoca-Manzanillo Wildlife Refuge** at the southern end of the coast road is the place for serious wildlife. Surfers go straight to the **Salsa Brava** wave just offshore from town; the more cautious paddle out at Playa Cocles, where lessons are cheap and the waves more forgiving.
