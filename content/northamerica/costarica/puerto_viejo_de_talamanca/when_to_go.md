---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Caribbean coast does its own thing weather-wise — when the rest of Costa Rica is in dry season, Puerto Viejo can be wet, and vice versa. The two best windows are **September–October** and **February–April**, which both tend to be drier and sunnier than the rest of the year.

**December and January** see big surf for Salsa Brava and lots of visitors; book ahead. **May, June and November** are the wettest months — heavy afternoon downpours, lush forests, and far fewer tourists. **Turtle nesting** runs from February to July; **raptor migration** is mid-October to mid-November. The temperature stays around 25–30°C all year.
