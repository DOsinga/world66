Yorkin is a small Bribri community at the upper reaches of the Yorkin river, on the Costa Rica–Panama border. The community runs **Stibrawpa**, a women-led tourism cooperative that offers overnight stays — cooking, planting, weaving, traditional Bribri stories — in a way that supports the local economy directly.

Getting there is half the experience: a poled dugout canoe ride up the river through dense forest, sometimes with caimans on the banks. Day trips are possible but rushed; an overnight gives time to take part in a chocolate workshop (the Bribri have grown cacao on the Talamanca side for centuries), see the village at dawn, and learn a little of the Bribri language.
