Puerto Jiménez is the main service town on the eastern side of the Osa Peninsula, facing the calm Golfo Dulce rather than the open Pacific. It is dusty, humid, practical, and far more useful than pretty: the place where hikers find guides, boats cross to Golfito, and Corcovado trips become real.

That usefulness is exactly why travellers end up staying. From town you can organise entry to [Corcovado National Park](/northamerica/costarica/corcovado), watch scarlet macaws over the streets, walk to [Playa Platanares](/northamerica/costarica/puertojimenez/playa_platanares), or explore mangroves and forest edge around the [Preciosa-Platanares Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/costarica/puertojimenez/preciosa_platanares_refuge). The [Golfo Dulce](/northamerica/costarica/puertojimenez/golfo_dulce) gives the town a quieter, more sheltered feel than the surf coast.

Puerto Jiménez is not a resort, and that is a strength. Come prepared for heat, mud, early starts, and logistics. If you want polished beach life, look elsewhere; if you want a working rainforest gateway with macaws in the almond trees, this is the right side of the peninsula.
