Osa Interactive Gardens is a small garden project in Puerto Jiménez, useful for easing into the peninsula's plants and wildlife before heading deeper into the forest. It is informal, close to town, and best approached with modest expectations.

The appeal is not grand landscaping but proximity: butterflies, tropical plants, birds, and shaded paths within easy reach of guesthouses and restaurants. It works well on an arrival afternoon when a full expedition would be too much.
