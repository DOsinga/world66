Puntarenas is a port city on [Costa Rica](/northamerica/costarica)'s Pacific coast, built on a narrow sandy peninsula that juts out into the Gulf of Nicoya. For decades it was the country's main Pacific port and a popular weekend beach destination for families from the Central Valley. The port has since moved to nearby Caldera, and Puntarenas has settled into a quieter rhythm — a working town with a long waterfront promenade, fish markets, and the slightly faded charm of a place that has seen busier days.

The city is the main departure point for [ferries to the Nicoya Peninsula](/northamerica/costarica/puntarenas/nicoya_ferry), and many travelers pass through on their way to the beaches of Montezuma, Santa Teresa, or Tambor. The [Paseo de los Turistas](/northamerica/costarica/puntarenas/paseo_de_los_turistas) along the waterfront is pleasant for an evening stroll, and the seafood is fresh and cheap — try a ceviche at one of the open-air stands near the ferry terminal. Cruise ships call here occasionally, and the nearby [Isla San Lucas](/northamerica/costarica/puntarenas/isla_san_lucas), a former penal colony, has been converted into a wildlife refuge that makes for a worthwhile day trip by boat.
