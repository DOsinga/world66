Puntarenas is a port on a sandspit, and what leaves it is the ferry to the Nicoya Peninsula and the day boats to [Isla Tortuga](/northamerica/costarica/tortugaisland) across the Gulf of Nicoya.

The island cruise is the classic San José day at the sea: an hour and a half each way, lunch on the beach, snorkelling, and the crossing past the gulf islands, which is half of the point.
