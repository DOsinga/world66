Quepos is a billfish port — one of the best in the world — and the Marina Pez Vela is where everything leaves from. Offshore charters go for sailfish, marlin and tuna; inshore days are cheaper, shorter and better for roosterfish.

The same marina sends out the catamarans along the [Manuel Antonio](/northamerica/costarica/manuelantonio) coast, and the town is where most people staying near the park actually eat and sleep.
