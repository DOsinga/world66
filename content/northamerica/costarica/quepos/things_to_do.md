---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Quepos itself is a working fishing town, and the things to do here are practical rather than monumental: a stroll around the harbour, a meal in a fuselage cantilevered over the rainforest, a half-day on a sport-fishing boat, or an early start for [Manuel Antonio National Park](/northamerica/costarica/manuelantonio) a few kilometres up the road. The marina is the smartest part of town; downtown, the streets near the old pier are scruffier and more interesting.
