There is no public transport to the park gates and the last stretch of road is rough, so unless you have a vehicle with clearance a tour is the practical way in.

Two sectors are open. Las Pailas has the geothermal loop — fumaroles, boiling mud, a small volcanic vent that sounds like a washing machine — and the trails to the Cangreja and Escondidas waterfalls. Santa María has the hot springs and the quieter walking. Río Negro's mud baths are on the flank between them.
