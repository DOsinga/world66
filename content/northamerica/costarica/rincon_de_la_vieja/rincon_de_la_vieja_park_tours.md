The operator for the Río Negro hot springs and mud baths on the volcano's flank, along with guided hikes on the Las Pailas loop past the fumaroles and boiling mud, and the waterfall trails.

There is no public transport to the park gates and the last stretch of road is rough, so a tour is the practical way in unless you have a vehicle with clearance.
