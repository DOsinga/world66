Samara is a small beach town on the Nicoya Peninsula in Guanacaste province. [Playa Samara](/northamerica/costarica/samara/playa_samara) curves gently around a protected bay, making it calmer for swimming than many Pacific beaches — which is why it's popular with families. The surrounding waters are good for snorkeling and kayaking, and the reef just offshore breaks much of the wave energy, keeping the bay unusually tranquil.

All roads from San José to Samara are now paved, making it a comfortable 4-hour drive. It remains quieter and less developed than Nosara or Tamarindo up the coast — the main street has a handful of surf schools, sodas, and small guesthouses, and the pace of life matches the setting.

[Isla Chora](/northamerica/costarica/samara/isla_chora), a small wildlife refuge island about 3 km south, is reachable by boat for snorkelling. North of town, the [Flying Crocodile](/northamerica/costarica/samara/flying_crocodile) airfield offers ultralight flights over the coast — an unusual way to understand the geography of the southern Nicoya Peninsula.
