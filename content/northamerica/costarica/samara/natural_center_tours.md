Sámara's reef keeps the bay calm, which makes it one of the easier places on this coast to get in a kayak, and Natural Center runs the paddle out to Isla Chora with snorkelling on the way.

They also do the night turtle tour at Camaronal, horse riding, river kayaking and the long transfers to Arenal and Monteverde.
