Nature Tours works the wildlife side of Sámara rather than the beach side: birdwatching walks, the turtle beaches in season, kayak and snorkel trips out to Isla Chora, coffee farms and the longer runs to the national parks inland.

Guanacaste's dry forest is easier birding than the rainforest — the trees lose their leaves in the dry season, and things are simply easier to see.
