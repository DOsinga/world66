San Isidro de Pérez Zeledón, often called San Isidro de El General, is the working city of Costa Rica's southern mountains. It sits in the El General Valley below the Talamanca range, with coffee slopes, cattle country, and the road to Chirripó all feeding into its markets and bus terminals.

This is not a polished tourist town. It is useful, busy, and very Costa Rican: farmers in from the hills, buses heading toward the Pacific, shops clustered around the cathedral, and enough services to make it the sensible staging point for [Chirripó](/northamerica/costarica/chirripo). The [Catedral San Isidro Labrador](/northamerica/costarica/sanisidrodeprezzeledn/catedral_san_isidro_labrador) and [Mercado Municipal](/northamerica/costarica/sanisidrodeprezzeledn/mercado_municipal) anchor the centre.

The best reasons to linger are outside the grid of streets. Birders should make time for [Los Cusingos](/northamerica/costarica/sanisidrodeprezzeledn/los_cusingos), while waterfall hunters often detour toward [Nauyaca Falls](/northamerica/costarica/sanisidrodeprezzeledn/nauyaca_falls) on the road toward Dominical. Treat San Isidro as a base and supply stop, and it makes a lot of sense.
