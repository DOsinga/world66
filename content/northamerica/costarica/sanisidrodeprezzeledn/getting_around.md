---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The central streets are walkable, though traffic can be noisy and pavements uneven. Local buses reach nearby villages, but a taxi or rental car is easier for Los Cusingos, trailheads, and waterfall detours.

If you are using San Isidro before a Chirripó hike, sort out cash, food, and transport here. Smaller mountain villages have fewer services and less flexibility.
