The Museo de Arte y Diseño Contemporáneo, usually shortened to MADC, is San José's main contemporary art and design museum. It occupies part of the old National Liquor Factory complex, giving the galleries an industrial setting close to the National Museum and Parque España.

Exhibitions change often and focus on current Costa Rican and Central American artists, design, video, and installation work. Check the current programme before going, since the visit depends heavily on what is on show.
