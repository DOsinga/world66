The Museum of Insects displays Costa Rica's rich insect life, including butterflies, bees, beetles, and other specimens. It is a small university museum, but the subject fits a country known for biodiversity.

It is best for visitors with a specific interest in nature, science, or family-friendly indoor stops around San Pedro.
