title: Activities
type: section
---

Two things get booked here: surf lessons and something with an engine. The beach break runs the length of the town and is more powerful than the Guanacaste beaches, so a lesson is worth it even if you have surfed before.

The roads are dirt and the distances awkward, which is why almost everyone ends up on a quad or a scooter — and why the hire places also run guided trips out to the waterfalls and down to the Cabo Blanco reserve at the tip of the peninsula.
