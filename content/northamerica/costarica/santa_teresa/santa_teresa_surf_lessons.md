Certified lessons on the Santa Teresa beach break with bilingual Costa Rican instructors, taking beginners from five years upwards and running coaching for people who already surf.

The wave here is more powerful than Tamarindo's and the northern end near Playa Carmen is the forgiving bit; a lesson is worth it even for improvers, simply to be put in the right place on the beach.
