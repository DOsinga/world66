The Sarapiquí is the beginner's whitewater river: a class II–III section that families run, and a class IV stretch below it when the water is up. The banks are forest the whole way, which is the difference from most rafting.

The other trips here are gentler — wildlife floats for caiman, sloths and birds, and the private reserves like La Selva, which takes day visitors with a guide.
