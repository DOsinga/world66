---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

From San José, the main road south runs via Cartago and Turrialba over the mountains to the Caribbean lowlands, reaching Limón after about four hours — or up to six in heavy traffic or rain. Public buses from San José's Terminal del Caribe run several times a day to Puerto Viejo de Talamanca (journey time 5–6 hours), passing through Cahuita on the way. There is no need to change in Limón.

By air, Nature Air and Sansa operate small-plane services to the airstrip at Limón. The drive from Limón south along the coast to Puerto Viejo takes about 90 minutes.

The border crossing at Sixaola connects with Almirante in Panama — a straightforward crossing used mainly by travellers continuing to Bocas del Toro. Buses from Puerto Viejo to Sixaola run several times a day. No vehicular ferries; you cross on foot.
