Talamanca's appeal is the combination of Caribbean beaches, indigenous territory and some of the richest rainforest in Central America, and almost all of it is reached from one of the two coastal towns.

[Cahuita](/northamerica/costarica/cahuita) has the national park, where a flat trail runs the length of a forested headland beside a coral reef. [Puerto Viejo de Talamanca](/northamerica/costarica/puerto_viejo_de_talamanca) has the surf, the beaches south towards Manzanillo, the Gandoca-Manzanillo refuge, and the trips inland — the Kékoldi hawk watch on the ridge above town, and the dugout journey up the river to the Bribri community at Yorkín.
