---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Caribbean coast follows a different rain pattern from the rest of Costa Rica. While the Pacific coast dries out from December to April, the Caribbean can receive heavy rain at any time of year — the driest spells tend to fall in September–October and February–March, which are the best months to visit if sunshine is the goal.

March to July is the leatherback turtle nesting season at Gandoca, worth timing a visit around if wildlife is the priority. September and October bring the hawk migration over Kékoldi — three million birds in two months, with the peak in late October.

The festival calendar is thin but the town of Puerto Viejo comes alive for Carnival in October, a raucous week of Caribbean music, dancing, and food that draws participants from up and down the coast.
