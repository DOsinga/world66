Yorkin is a Bribri indigenous community accessible only by dugout canoe up the Yorkin River, which forms the border with Panama. The journey from Bambú takes about 45 minutes through flat river valley forest, passing other small communities and occasional caiman sunning on the banks.

The visit typically includes a walk through cacao and banana plantations, a demonstration of traditional chocolate preparation (from pod to cup), a cooked lunch, and time to wander the village. The community has received travellers for over two decades and handles it with easy confidence — this is not a performance but a working farm community that has chosen to share itself. Overnight stays are possible with advance arrangement. Most visits are booked through ATEC (the Talamanca Ecotourism Association) in Puerto Viejo, which coordinates transport and guides.
