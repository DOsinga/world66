Iguana Surf has been on this beach since 1989, which in Tamarindo makes it close to an institution. It hires boards, teaches beginners on the break directly in front of the shop, and books the wider set of tours around Guanacaste.

Board hire here is the practical reason to come: a big rack, honest advice about what you should actually be riding, and the beach thirty seconds away.
