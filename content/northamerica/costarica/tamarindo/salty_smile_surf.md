Salty Smile teaches on the Tamarindo beach break, which is about as forgiving a wave as Costa Rica offers — a sand bottom, a long ride and a gentle shoulder at the river end — and runs multi-day camps as well as single lessons.

Tamarindo has more surf schools per metre of sand than anywhere else in the country, so the difference is instructor ratio and whether they move you along the beach as the tide shifts.
