---
title: Things to Do in Costa Rica
type: section
---

Costa Rica has built its reputation on outdoor activity, and the country earns it. In a compact territory it packs together volcanoes, cloud forests, tropical dry forest, Caribbean rainforest, Pacific beaches, and highland plateaus — each with its own set of things to do. Travelers come for [canopy zip-lining](/northamerica/costarica/things_to_do/canopy) in the cloud forests of Monteverde, [surfing](/northamerica/costarica/things_to_do/surfing) the Pacific coast breaks from Tamarindo to Pavones, [whitewater kayaking](/northamerica/costarica/things_to_do/kayaking) on the Pacuare, and [trekking](/northamerica/costarica/things_to_do/trekking) to the summit of Chirripó or through Corcovado's lowland jungle.

The country has been deliberately building its ecotourism infrastructure since the 1980s, and it shows: guides are licensed, national parks are well maintained, and operators across most activity types follow genuine safety standards. You can be confident booking through reputable outfitters and your accommodation. The dry season (December through April) is the most comfortable for most activities, though the rainy season opens up less-visited trails and keeps prices lower.
