Everything here is a boat. Marino Ballena trips run from the park entrance at Playa Uvita and from Bahía Ballena, taking in the humpbacks in season, the dolphins most of the year, and a snorkelling stop at Isla Ballena or the Tres Hermanas rocks.

Two humpback seasons overlap on this coast — southern animals roughly July to November, northern ones December to March — which is a combination almost nowhere else offers. At low tide you can walk the whale's tail sandbar out from the beach for nothing.
