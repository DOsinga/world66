Bahía Aventuras has run boats out of Uvita since 2007 and is wholly Costa Rican–owned, with the widest range on this stretch of coast: Marino Ballena for the humpbacks, Caño Island for snorkelling and diving, and the long day down to [Corcovado](/northamerica/costarica/corcovado) at San Pedrillo.

The Caño Island trip is the one worth the money — the visibility is better than anywhere on the mainland coast, and the crossing usually turns up dolphins.
