A three-hour boat trip from the Marino Ballena entrance for humpbacks and dolphins, with a snorkelling stop on the reef, sold as a straightforward single product rather than a menu.

Boats leave from the beach at Playa Uvita, and at low tide you can walk the whale's tail sandbar out from the same point afterwards.
