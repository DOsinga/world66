Camara Oscura gives a live 360-degree view over Old Havana using a large optical device rather than a normal lookout platform. The attraction is small, but it is a memorable way to read the rooftops, plazas, and harbour edge of Habana Vieja from above.

It fits naturally into a walk around Plaza Vieja and the surrounding old town streets.
