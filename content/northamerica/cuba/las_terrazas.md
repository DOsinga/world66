Las Terrazas is a small planned community in the Sierra del Rosario, west of [Havana](/northamerica/cuba/havana). It was built around reforestation, terraced hillsides, lakes, and tourism rather than around a colonial plaza, which makes it feel unlike most Cuban villages. Concrete apartment blocks stand above green water and forested slopes, while paths and narrow roads step up and down the valley.

The setting is the real draw. Las Terrazas sits inside the Sierra del Rosario Biosphere Reserve, recognised by UNESCO for its mix of restored forest, rural settlement, and protected mountain habitat. It is popular as a day trip from Havana, so weekends can feel busy and local rather than hushed.

The best visit combines [Cafetal Buenavista](/northamerica/cuba/las_terrazas/cafetal_buenavista), the lake and river swimming spots, and slow walking through the community. It is not a grand monument town, but the mix of Cuban social planning, mountain forest, coffee history, and water gives it a strong identity.
