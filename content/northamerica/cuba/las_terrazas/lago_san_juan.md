Lago San Juan is the calm centre of Las Terrazas, with water, reeds, forest, and the village rising around it. Rowboats and kayaks make it a simple, low-key stop rather than a major adventure.

It is best treated as part of the place's rhythm: paddle a little, sit by the water, and watch how the community is built into the slope.
