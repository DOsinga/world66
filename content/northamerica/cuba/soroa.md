Soroa is a thin village strung along the road into the Sierra del Rosario, west of Havana. It is not a town of plazas and museums. Its pull is greener and quieter: orchids, wet forest, a waterfall, and the feeling of being in the hills after the heat of the plain.

The two main anchors are close together at the upper end of the village. The [Orquideario](/northamerica/cuba/soroa/orquideario_de_soroa) is a serious garden rather than a roadside flower stop, and [Salto de Soroa](/northamerica/cuba/soroa/salto_de_soroa) gives you a short walk, water, and a swim when conditions are good.

Soroa is worth adding to a western Cuba trip if you want a soft nature day without going all the way to Vinales. Transport can be awkward, so it is best with a taxi, rental car, or a casa that can help arrange rides.
