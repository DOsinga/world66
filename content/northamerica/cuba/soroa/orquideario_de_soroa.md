Soroa's orchid garden is the village's best-known sight, with hundreds of species planted on a hillside below the forest. It is a calm place to wander, especially in the morning before the day turns heavy.

Paths continue above the garden toward old estate ruins and valley views. Even if you are not especially interested in orchids, the setting makes the stop worthwhile.
