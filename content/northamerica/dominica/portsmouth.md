Portsmouth is [Dominica's](/northamerica/dominica) second town, a quiet settlement on [Prince Rupert Bay](/northamerica/dominica/portsmouth/prince_rupert_bay) in the north of the island. It serves as the base for visiting [Cabrits National Park](/northamerica/dominica/portsmouth/cabrits_national_park), where the restored [Fort Shirley](/northamerica/dominica/portsmouth/fort_shirley) — an 18th-century British garrison — sits on a volcanic headland between two beaches.

A boat trip through the [Indian River](/northamerica/dominica/portsmouth/indian_river) mangrove lagoons is one of Dominica's most popular excursions, and the guides who pole you through the tunnels of vegetation are excellent. The scenic coastal road between Portsmouth and [Roseau](/northamerica/dominica/roseau) passes through small villages, with overlooks and roadside restaurants along the way. Look out for the Macoucherie Rum factory near Salisbury, where an old waterwheel still powers the distillery.
