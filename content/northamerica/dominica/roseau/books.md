**Wide Sargasso Sea** by Jean Rhys. Rhys was born in Roseau in 1890 — her father was the town doctor — and her novel about a Creole heiress in nineteenth-century Jamaica draws on the world of plantation Dominica she knew as a child. The single most important book for understanding Roseau's colonial undertow.

**The Orchid House** by Phyllis Shand Allfrey. Allfrey was a white Dominican who served as a minister in the brief West Indies Federation. Her 1953 novel traces the decline of a planter family on the island, narrated by their black housekeeper. Still the best fiction about Dominica.

**Smile Please** by Jean Rhys. Rhys's unfinished autobiography, half of it set in the Roseau of her childhood — funerals, gossip, the colour bar, the priests, the carnival. Wonderful sense of the town.
