A small fishing village on the south-east coast that became the diving centre of the Dominican Republic, and the place almost every boat to [Isla Saona](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/isla_saona) leaves from.

The diving is the best in the country: wrecks sunk deliberately as reefs, notably the *St George*, a 73-metre freighter lying upright in 40 metres, and the shallow coral inside the **Parque Nacional del Este**, whose reefs are in better shape than most in the region because the park has kept the fishing out. There are caves too — the flooded cenotes inland and the caverns on Saona with Taíno pictographs on the walls.

The village keeps enough of itself to be pleasant: a working beach with boats pulled up on it, a handful of restaurants, and none of the scale of the resorts along the road to Punta Cana. Boats to Saona leave in the morning and come back mid-afternoon; going on a weekday makes a considerable difference to how many of them there are.
