Bayahibe is the country's dive capital, and what sets it apart is the **Parque Nacional del Este**: the reefs inside the park have been protected from fishing for long enough to be in noticeably better condition than most Caribbean coral, and there is active restoration work going on.

The wrecks are the draw for many — the ***St George***, a 73-metre freighter sunk upright in 40 metres, and the shallower *Atlantic Princess* — and the flooded caves inland are a speciality for divers with the right certification.

The other business here is the boat to **[Isla Saona](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/isla_saona)**, which nearly every visitor takes. It is a long, loud catamaran day in high season; going midweek, or taking a speedboat and arriving before the crowd, changes it completely.
