A PADI instructor-development centre in the village, diving the sites that make Bayahibe the country's best base: the ***St George***, a 73-metre freighter sunk upright in 40 metres, the shallow coral inside the **Parque Nacional del Este**, and the caverns on and around Isla Saona.

Their instructors include marine biologists, and the shop makes a point of the reef restoration work going on in this park — which is the reason the coral here is in better shape than most of the Caribbean.
