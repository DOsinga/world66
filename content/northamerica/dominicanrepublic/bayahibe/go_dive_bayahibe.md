A dive centre in the village, running courses from beginner upwards and daily boats to the wrecks and the reefs inside the Parque Nacional del Este. Bayahibe's diving is close in — most sites are fifteen to thirty minutes out — so two-tank mornings are the norm rather than full-day trips.
