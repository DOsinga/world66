An IKO-certified school on Kite Beach, which is the strip a kilometre west of town where the wind is cleanest and where most of the kiting happens. Lessons run from first-time through to independent riding, with gear hire for people who already know what they are doing.

The wind here is a daily pattern rather than a matter of luck: the trades build with the thermals through the morning and are usually solid by early afternoon, which is why lessons are booked by the time of day rather than the hope of conditions.
