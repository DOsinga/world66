---

Jarabacoa is the Dominican Republic's mountain-sports town, and the only place in the Caribbean with genuine whitewater. The **Río Yaque del Norte** runs class II–III on the standard descent — short, busy, and considerably livelier from May to November when the river is up.

The waterfalls are the other business: **Salto de Jimenoa Uno**, over a suspension bridge above the gorge, and **Salto de Baiguate**, where the canyoning trips abseil and jump their way down.

**Pico Duarte**, at 3,098 metres the highest peak in the Caribbean, is climbed from La Ciénaga half an hour away. It is two to three days up and back, with mules for the gear, huts at Compartición and La Laguna, and a compulsory guide arranged through the park office. Nights drop close to freezing and people regularly underestimate it.
