A local company in Jarabacoa running the rafting on the **Río Yaque del Norte** — the only whitewater in the Caribbean, class II–III on the standard descent and livelier in the wet season — along with canyoning at Salto de Baiguate, tubing, and the mountain-bike routes in the hills.

They also arrange the **Pico Duarte** trek, which needs a guide and a park permit and takes two to three days from La Ciénaga up to 3,098 metres, the highest point in the Caribbean.
