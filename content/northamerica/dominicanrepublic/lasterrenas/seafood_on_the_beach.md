---
title: Beach Seafood Restaurants
type: poi
tags: [eating_out, restaurant]
---

The strip of seafood restaurants along Playa Las Terrenas is the simplest and most reliable eating experience in town: tables on or immediately above the sand, fishing boats visible at the eastern end of the beach, and a menu built entirely around what came in that morning. Dorado, lobster, red snapper, and shrimp appear in various preparations — grilled, fried, in a creole sauce, or simply with lime and tostones. The setting at sunset, with the water turning orange and the first stars appearing over the palm trees, is the Las Terrenas version of a formal dining experience.

Most of the beach restaurants operate on a similar model: you choose your fish or lobster from what is displayed, agree on a price, and wait for it to be prepared. The lobster is the regional speciality and is invariably fresh; the price varies by size but is considerably lower than you would pay for equivalent quality in Europe or North America. Order enough for two people from a single whole lobster and you have the defining Las Terrenas meal.

The restaurants are open from around noon, pick up for lunch, quiet in the mid-afternoon, and busy again from sunset. Competition for tables is rarely fierce except on holidays and during the January-February peak season. There is no reservation system; you simply show up. The best tables are the ones closest to the water.
