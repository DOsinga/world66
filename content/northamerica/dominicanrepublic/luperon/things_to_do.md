---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Luperon is not a resort town. There are no water parks, no all-inclusive buffets, and no animation staff. What it has is a deep, sheltered harbour that sailors consider one of the safest hurricane holes in the Caribbean, a beautiful beach nearby, and an easy pace of life that rewards slow travel.

The harbour is the social centre. Long-term cruisers anchor here for weeks or months — the marina community is its own small world — and local fishermen work the docks in the early mornings. The [Malecón](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/luperon/malecon) at dusk is when the town comes alive, with locals gathering to watch the sun set over the bay.

Nearby, the ruins of [La Isabela](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/luperon/la_isabela) — Christopher Columbus's first permanent settlement in the Americas — are one of the most historically significant sites in the entire Caribbean, and they draw surprisingly few visitors.
