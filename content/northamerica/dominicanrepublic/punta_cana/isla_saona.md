Isla Saona is the largest island off the southeastern Dominican Republic coast and one of the most popular day-trip destinations in the Caribbean. Catamaran tours leave from Bayahibe, two hours west of Punta Cana, stopping at Natural Pool — a shallow sandbar in the middle of the ocean, knee-deep and kilometres long — before reaching the island.

The beaches are clean and uncrowded relative to their reputation, partly because they are so spread out. Starfish are visible in the shallows at several points; dolphins are occasionally spotted near the catamarans in transit. The interior is a dry tropical forest protected as part of the Cotubanama National Park. There is a small village on the island where locals live; visits are respectful rather than intrusive. Come on a weekday if possible — the weekend catamaran crowd can be large.
