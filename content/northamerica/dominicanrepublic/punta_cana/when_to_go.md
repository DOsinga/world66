---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Punta Cana has warm weather year-round, with average temperatures between 24°C and 30°C. The main distinction is between the dry season (December to April) and the wet season (May to November), with the peak of the hurricane season running from August to October.

The dry season is the main tourist season — prices are higher, resorts are fuller, and the weather is reliably sunny. January through March is the sweet spot: comfortable rather than hot, low humidity, and long clear days on the beach. Summer brings more clouds and occasional heavy afternoon showers, but the water temperature is at its best and rates are considerably lower.

The Atlantic hurricane season runs through the autumn months. Punta Cana sits far enough east to be more exposed than the western Dominican Republic; serious hurricanes hit the region periodically, though not every year. If you are visiting in September or October, buy travel insurance that covers weather cancellations.
