---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The whale-watching season runs from mid-January to mid-March — if seeing humpbacks in the bay is the point of your trip, this is when to come. The town fills up and operators are at full capacity, so book ahead. The weather in these months is also comfortably warm and mostly dry.

Outside whale season, the rest of the year offers a more relaxed experience. April and May are good shoulder months with quieter beaches and decent prices. The hurricane season runs from June to November, with the highest risk in August and September. December has good weather but rising prices toward Christmas. The peninsula is greenest after the spring rains, which sharpens the colours of the inland scenery.
