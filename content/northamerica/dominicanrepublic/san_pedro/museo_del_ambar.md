The Baseball Museum on the Malecón documents San Pedro's extraordinary contribution to Major League Baseball — a story that stretches from the 1950s through to today and includes hundreds of players who made it to the big leagues from this one city.

Exhibits cover the history of Dominican professional baseball, the winter league, and individual player stories, with photographs, memorabilia, and equipment spanning several decades. It's a small but genuine museum, not a tourist gimmick, and it makes the city's obsession with the sport make a lot more sense.
