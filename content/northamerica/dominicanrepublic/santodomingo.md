Santo Domingo is the oldest European-founded city in the Americas, and it wears its history proudly. The [Zona Colonial](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/zona_colonial) — the historic heart — is packed with firsts: the oldest street in the New World ([Calle de las Damas](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/calle_de_las_damas)), the oldest cathedral ([Catedral Santa María la Menor](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/catedral_santa_maria)), the first hospital in the Americas (the ruins of San Nicolás de Bari), and the oldest European fortress in the hemisphere ([Fortaleza Ozama](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/fortaleza_ozama)). The entire colonial district is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Beyond the colonial core, the city has real substance: the [Jardín Botánico Nacional](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/jardin_botanico_nacional) is one of the finest botanical gardens in the Caribbean, [Los Tres Ojos](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/tres_ojos) is a system of underground lakes that was sacred to the Taíno people, and the [Plaza de la Cultura](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/plaza_de_la_cultura) houses four museums including the excellent [Museo del Hombre Dominicano](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/museo_del_hombre_dominicano). The [Parque Mirador del Sur](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/parque_mirador_del_sur) runs for 8 km along southern cliffs above the Caribbean.

The city's baseball culture is deep and genuine — catching a game at the [Estadio Quisqueya](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/estadio_quisqueya) during the winter season is one of the most authentic experiences Santo Domingo offers. The nightlife in the Zona Colonial and [Piantini](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/piantini) runs late. And [La Caleta Underwater National Park](/northamerica/dominicanrepublic/santodomingo/la_caleta) is 30 minutes east — two shipwrecks, reef fish, and the clearest water within reach of the city.
