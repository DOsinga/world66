Iberoamerica Park is a useful green space in Santo Domingo, popular with families, walkers, and people looking for a break from traffic. It is not one of the capital's colonial showpieces, but it gives a good sense of everyday city recreation.

The park is most appealing in the cooler parts of the day or when events bring extra life to the grounds. Combine it with nearby cultural stops if you want a Santo Domingo day that goes beyond the Zona Colonial.
