The Museum of Dominican Rum is a small Zona Colonial attraction devoted to the history and culture of Dominican rum. It sits somewhere between museum, tasting room, and themed visitor stop, so expectations matter.

It can be worthwhile if you are interested in rum production or want a short indoor break while walking the colonial city. Treat it as a light cultural stop rather than one of Santo Domingo's core historic museums.
