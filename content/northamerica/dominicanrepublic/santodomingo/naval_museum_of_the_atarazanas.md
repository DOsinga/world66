The Naval Museum of the Atarazanas occupies part of the old royal shipyard complex in Santo Domingo's Colonial Zone. The stone buildings are among the oldest industrial structures in the Americas, and they suit a museum focused on navigation, shipwrecks, colonial trade, and the sea routes that made the city strategically important.

Even if the displays vary in depth, the setting gives the museum weight. It sits near the Ozama River and the Alcazar de Colon, so it fits neatly into a walking route through the riverfront side of the old city, where military, mercantile, and domestic colonial architecture sit close together.
