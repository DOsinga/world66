The Wax Museum Juan Pablo Duarte is a small museum in Santo Domingo focused on Juan Pablo Duarte, one of the founders of the Dominican Republic. It mixes wax figures, artifacts, and historical displays about Duarte and the country's independence story.

It is a short stop rather than a major museum, but it fits well into a walk around the Colonial Zone for visitors interested in Dominican history.
