---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Santo Domingo is tropical — hot and humid year-round, with a rainy season and a dry season rather than the four-season pattern of temperate climates.

**December through April** is the dry season and the most comfortable time to visit. Temperatures hover around 27–30°C with lower humidity and blue skies most days. This is when the Caribbean tourism season peaks, prices are higher, and the city is at its most vibrant.

**May through November** is the wet season. Rain comes in short sharp afternoon downpours rather than all-day drizzle; mornings are typically clear. August and September bring the most heat and humidity. This is also the hurricane season — the Dominican Republic is in the hurricane belt, and while direct hits are relatively rare, tropical storms bring heavy rain and disruption. Prices are lower during this period.

**Carnival in February** is the best single reason to time a trip to Santo Domingo. The Dominican Carnival is one of the most energetic in the Caribbean, with spectacular costumes, merengue music that goes all night, and street processions that take over the Malecón waterfront. The Zona Colonial is particularly festive during Carnival weekend.

The **merengue festival** in late July fills the Malecón with free outdoor concerts for several days and is worth timing a visit around if you are in Santo Domingo in summer.
