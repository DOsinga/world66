Cafe Albania is a coffee estate turned leisure stop on the edge of Apaneca. It is not subtle: people come for the rainbow slide, zipline-style activities, photos, and the view over the green folds of the Ruta de las Flores.

Treat it as a playful stop rather than a deep cultural experience. The coffee-country setting is lovely, and it works well with families or groups who want something more active than another town square.
