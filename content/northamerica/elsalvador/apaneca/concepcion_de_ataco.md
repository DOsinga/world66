Concepcion de Ataco is the easiest side trip from Apaneca and one of the most photogenic towns on the Ruta de las Flores. Murals cover many of the walls, craft shops cluster around the centre, and the surrounding slopes are thick with coffee.

It is busier and more consciously visitor-facing than Apaneca, especially on weekends. That makes it good for browsing and lunch, while Apaneca remains the calmer place to sleep.
