Germinal Museo de la Imprenta is a small museum devoted to printing presses and print history. It is a niche stop, but worthwhile if you like old machinery, typography, or the practical craft behind books and newspapers.

The museum sits near the town plaza, so it is easy to fold into a short walk around Apaneca.
