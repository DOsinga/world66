Laberinto de Apaneca is an outdoor adventure park best known for its hedge mazes, with ziplines, zip-bikes, and other light adventure activities around the grounds. It is a family-friendly stop on the Ruta de las Flores, especially if you want something more active than cafe-hopping and viewpoints.
