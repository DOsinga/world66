Los Ausoles is a geothermal area north of Ahuachapan, close enough to combine with Apaneca if you have a car or driver. Steam rises from vents and hot mud pools, a reminder that the pleasant coffee highlands sit on restless volcanic ground.

It is more rough-edged than polished, so go for the odd landscape rather than facilities. Wear shoes that can handle mud, keep to obvious paths, and do not test the pools by hand; the ground can be hotter than it looks.
