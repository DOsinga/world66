Santa Teresa Hot Springs is a mineral-water bathing stop outside Apaneca. Visitors come for the warm pools, mud masks, and spa-style treatments, usually as a slow break from the Ruta de las Flores towns.

It is more of a relaxation stop than a cultural sight, but it is one of the area's clearer visitor-facing hot-spring options.
