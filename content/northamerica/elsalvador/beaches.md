---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

El Salvador has a long Pacific coastline with a mix of surf beaches, black-sand shores, and quieter stretches popular with families. The black volcanic sand is a constant — these are not white-sand Caribbean beaches, but the waves, the seafood, and the atmosphere more than compensate.

The most visited beaches cluster around La Libertad to the west of San Salvador, where surf culture has taken hold. To the east, around Usulutan, the coast is quieter and less developed. Playa El Espino is typical of the eastern beaches — long, dark-sanded, and relaxed, with fresh fish and ceviche from the comedores that line the shore.
