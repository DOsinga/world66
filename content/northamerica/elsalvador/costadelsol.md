Costa del Sol is El Salvador's easy central-coast escape: a long, developed beach strip in La Paz department, close enough to San Salvador and the international airport for a quick break. It is not a lonely surf beach. It is family houses, beach clubs, seafood restaurants, warm water, and weekend traffic.

The best part is the double coast. On one side you have [Playa Costa del Sol](/northamerica/elsalvador/costadelsol/playa_costa_del_sol), a broad Pacific beach made for swimming, walking, and sunset. On the other side, the [Estero de Jaltepeque](/northamerica/elsalvador/costadelsol/estero_de_jaltepeque) spreads into mangrove channels, fishing villages, and birdlife.

If you want wild drama, go elsewhere. If you want a practical beach base with boat trips, fresh fish, and day-trip distance from the capital, Costa del Sol makes sense. [Isla Tasajera](/northamerica/elsalvador/costadelsol/isla_tasajera) and [Bocana El Cordoncillo](/northamerica/elsalvador/costadelsol/bocana_el_cordoncillo) give the area its quieter edge.
