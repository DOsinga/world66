La Puntilla is the practical end of many Costa del Sol outings: a place to eat seafood, find boats, and look across the water toward the estuary and islands. It is less about a single sight than about access.

Use it as a jumping-off point for Jaltepeque trips or a meal after the beach. As with most coastal restaurant areas, prices and quality vary; look for places with local families and fresh turnover.
