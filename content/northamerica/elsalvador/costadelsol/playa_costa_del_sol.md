Playa Costa del Sol is the main beach, a long strip of dark-golden sand facing the Pacific. The sea is usually more forgiving than the exposed surf beaches farther west, which makes it popular with families, though currents can still be strong.

Come early if you want a quieter walk. By midday on weekends, restaurants and beach houses fill up, music starts drifting across the sand, and the place becomes more social than serene.
