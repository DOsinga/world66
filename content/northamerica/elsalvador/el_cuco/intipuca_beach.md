Intipuca Beach sits farther along the same eastern shoreline, where river water, mangroves, and the Pacific meet. It is less about a single postcard view than the feeling of the coast loosening up beyond El Cuco.

Stand-up paddling and short beach trips are the usual reasons to come. Check locally before setting out, as transport and conditions change more than on the main village beach.
