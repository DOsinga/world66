El Tunco exists for the surf. The wave in front of town is a right-hand point over rock and cobble, working most of the year and best from mid to high tide; the beach break at the western end is where lessons happen. Boards rent by the hour from half a dozen shops on the two streets, and an instructor for a morning costs less than a good dinner in most countries.

This is also the easiest base in El Salvador for everything inland. The country is small enough that the Santa Ana crater, the Ruta de las Flores and Suchitoto are all day trips from the beach, and the operators here sell exactly that.

The serious wave is Punta Roca at [La Libertad](/northamerica/elsalvador/lalibertad), fifteen minutes east — a long, fast right that draws competition surfers, over a shallow rock shelf. It is not the place to take a first lesson.
