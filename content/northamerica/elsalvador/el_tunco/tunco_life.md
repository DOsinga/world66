A small locally owned operator based on the coast road at El Tunco, running day trips to most of what the country offers from a beach base: the Santa Ana crater, the waterfalls in the coffee hills, [Suchitoto](/northamerica/elsalvador/suchitoto), the Ruta de las Flores. Guides are bilingual and pickup from where you are staying is included, which matters here because the trailheads are awkward without a car.

They also run the San Salvador airport transfer, which is the other thing everyone arriving at El Tunco needs.
