---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

El Imposible sits deep enough in western El Salvador that day trips require planning and your own transport, but the surrounding area rewards the effort.

The most compelling excursion is [Cara Sucia](/northamerica/elsalvador/elimposiblenationalpark/cara_sucia), a pre-Columbian archaeological site on the coastal plain to the southwest. It is low-key by comparison with Tazumal — no polished visitor infrastructure, limited on-site interpretation — but it pairs naturally with a descent to the Pacific slope and rounds out a trip that might otherwise be all forest trails and highland towns.
