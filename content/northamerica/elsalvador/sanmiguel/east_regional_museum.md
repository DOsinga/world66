East Regional Museum is the main museum stop for understanding San Miguel and eastern El Salvador. Exhibits cover regional history, archaeology, culture, and civic identity, giving context to a city that many travelers otherwise experience only in transit.

It is a compact, locally focused museum rather than a blockbuster attraction. That makes it useful if you are already in San Miguel and want a grounded introduction before heading to nearby volcanoes, beaches, or lakes.
