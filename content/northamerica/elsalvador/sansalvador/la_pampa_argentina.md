A well-regarded steakhouse in the Colonia Escalón area, La Pampa Argentina serves Argentine-style grilled meats in a reliable mid-range setting. El Salvador produces good beef and the Argentine grill tradition translates well here. The menu also covers pasta and other South American standards. Popular with business diners and families.

Reservations recommended for dinner, particularly on weekends. Located on Boulevard El Hipódromo near the zona rosa.
