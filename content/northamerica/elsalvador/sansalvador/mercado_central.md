The Central Market of San Salvador is a sprawling, chaotic, genuinely commercial market — not a tourist market. It covers several city blocks in the historic centre and sells everything from fresh produce and street food to electronics, clothing, and traditional crafts. The pupusas (thick corn tortillas filled with cheese, beans, or chicharrón) sold by vendors here are some of the best in the city.

Go in the morning for the best atmosphere and the freshest produce. Pickpockets operate in crowded areas; keep valuables secure. The market is a good place to try local food at local prices — look for the comedor stalls serving the day's specials to market workers.
