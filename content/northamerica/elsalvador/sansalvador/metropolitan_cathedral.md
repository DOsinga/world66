The Metropolitan Cathedral anchors San Salvador's historic centre and is one of the country's most important churches. Its main draw for many visitors is the tomb of Saint Óscar Romero, the archbishop murdered in 1980 after speaking out against state violence.

Enter from the side to reach Romero's tomb and give yourself time to take in the cathedral's role in modern Salvadoran history. It pairs naturally with Plaza Barrios, Iglesia El Rosario, and the nearby markets.
