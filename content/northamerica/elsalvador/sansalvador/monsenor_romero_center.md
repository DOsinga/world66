The Monsenor Romero Center at the Central American University is one of San Salvador's most important memory sites. It is tied to Archbishop Oscar Romero, the Jesuit martyrs, and the country's painful civil-war history, giving visitors a direct encounter with faith, politics, and human rights.

This is not light sightseeing, but it is essential context for understanding modern El Salvador. Go respectfully, allow time to read, and expect the visit to stay with you longer than many more polished attractions.
