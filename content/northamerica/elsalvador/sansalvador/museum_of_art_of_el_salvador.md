The Museum of Art of El Salvador, usually known as MARTE, is one of San Salvador's essential cultural stops. Its galleries trace Salvadoran art through modern and contemporary work, giving visual form to the country's political, social, and creative history.

It pairs well with the nearby anthropology museum because the two institutions tell different halves of the national story. MARTE is the better choice for travelers interested in living culture and artistic interpretation rather than archaeology alone.
