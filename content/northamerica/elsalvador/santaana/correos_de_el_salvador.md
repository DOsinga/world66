Santa Ana's post-office building is a small but worthwhile detail in the city's historic centre. It sits close to the cathedral, theatre, and main square, so it is easy to fold into a walk that focuses on the city's late-19th- and early-20th-century civic architecture.

Do not expect a major attraction or a museum. The value is in the facade and the way the building rounds out Santa Ana's urban texture: public institutions, arcaded streets, and old commercial blocks still forming the everyday centre of the city.
