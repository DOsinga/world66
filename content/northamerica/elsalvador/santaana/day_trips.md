Santa Ana sits among some of El Salvador's finest natural and cultural day trips, most within an hour by car or bus.

[Lake Coatepeque](/northamerica/elsalvador/lakecoatepeque), a stunning blue crater lake about 25 km south, is the easiest escape — good for a swim and a lakeside fish lunch. The [Ruta de las Flores](/northamerica/elsalvador/rutadelasflores), the country's most popular tourist circuit, threads through coffee towns west of the city and is best on a weekend for its food festivals and craft markets.

For more active outings, the hike to the crater rim of the **Santa Ana Volcano (Ilamatepec)** from the Cerro Verde visitor centre is the most dramatic in the country, with the stark cone of **Izalco Volcano** visible across the park. West of the city, the **Tazumal ruins** at Chalchuapa are the best-preserved pre-Columbian site in El Salvador.
