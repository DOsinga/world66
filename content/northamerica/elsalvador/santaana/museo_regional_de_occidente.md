Museo Regional de Occidente occupies a former Banco Central de Reserva building in Santa Ana's historic centre. Its exhibits cover the history of Salvadoran money, including coins and banknotes displayed in the old strong room, with additional art displays upstairs.

It is a compact museum that fits well with the cathedral, theatre, and Parque Libertad on a walk through central Santa Ana.
