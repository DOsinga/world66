---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

San Vicente sits on the central corridor between [San Salvador](/northamerica/elsalvador/sansalvador) and eastern El Salvador. Buses from the capital are frequent, and onward connections continue toward [San Miguel](/northamerica/elsalvador/sanmiguel) and smaller towns in the department.

The city is also a useful stop if you are travelling between the coast near Zacatecoluca and the interior. For the volcano, lakes, and water parks, having a car or arranging a local taxi saves a great deal of time.
