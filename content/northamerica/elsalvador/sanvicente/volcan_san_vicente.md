Volcán San Vicente, or Chichontepec, rises south of the city in a twin-summit mass that dominates the central valley. It is one of El Salvador's major volcanoes and gives San Vicente its landscape: almost every view out of town eventually finds the mountain.

Hiking is possible but should be treated as a real mountain outing, not a casual city stroll. Trails, access, and safety conditions vary, so arrange a local guide and start early. Even if you do not climb, the volcano is the defining natural sight of the area.
