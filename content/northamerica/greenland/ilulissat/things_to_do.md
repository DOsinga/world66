---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ilulissat's main draw is the icefjord, and rightly so — but there is more to the town than staring at ice. The Knud Rasmussen Museum tells the story of the Arctic's most celebrated explorer, the Icefjord Centre explains the science behind what you're seeing, and summer boat trips and winter dog sledding round out a destination that rewards a few days rather than a quick stop.
