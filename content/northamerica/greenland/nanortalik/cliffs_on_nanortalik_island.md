The east side of Nanortalik Island has steep coastal cliffs that are especially striking from the water. They are close to town, but the terrain is rough and exposed, so footing matters if you approach on land.

The cliffs are best treated as a scenic viewpoint or boat-trip feature rather than a built attraction.
