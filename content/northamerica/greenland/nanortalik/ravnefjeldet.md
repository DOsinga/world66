Ravnefjeldet is the mountain immediately above Nanortalik, accessible on foot from the town in a half-day hike. The trail starts at the edge of town and climbs steadily through open tundra to the summit at 308 metres — not a significant altitude, but the views are disproportionate to the effort. The entire Nanortalik archipelago spreads out below, with the fjords, skerries, and granite peaks visible in every direction.

The hike is manageable for anyone in reasonable fitness with appropriate footwear; the terrain is tundra and rock rather than technical scrambling. Bring extra layers regardless of the weather forecast — Arctic conditions change quickly and the summit is exposed. The walk back down to town takes about 90 minutes.
