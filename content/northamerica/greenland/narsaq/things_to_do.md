title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Narsaq is a small place with a specific set of attractions — a brewery, a museum, a craft workshop, and easy access to fjord and mountain landscapes. The kayak harbour and the climb to Kvanefjeld are the main reasons to spend more than a few hours here. The village is close to several Norse settlement sites and to the sheep farming country of South Greenland.
