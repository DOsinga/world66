Aasivissuit-Nipisat is a UNESCO-listed cultural landscape north-east of Sisimiut, preserving traces of Inuit hunting life between the inland ice and the sea. The site is not a single monument so much as a sweep of terrain marked by seasonal movement, camps, graves, and hunting places used over thousands of years.

It rewards travellers who are already in Sisimiut and interested in Greenland beyond the town itself. Go with local guidance and realistic expectations: the value is in the landscape and its long human story, not in museum-style presentation.
