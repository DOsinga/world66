High North Peak is Carriacou's high point, a short but worthwhile climb when the weather is clear. From the top you can read the island properly: bays, dry hills, reefs, and the chain of smaller Grenadine islands beyond.

Go early to avoid the heat and take water. The trail is not a wilderness expedition, but it gives a welcome active counterpoint to Carriacou's beaches and boat trips.
