North Point is the kind of simple coastal stop that suits Carriacou: not developed, not busy, and most rewarding when you are exploring the island slowly. At the northern end of the island, the land feels more exposed, with wide views over the sea and neighbouring islets.

Go for the sense of edge rather than facilities. Bring water, expect rougher tracks or quiet roads, and combine it with Windward, Anse La Roche, or other northern Carriacou stops. It is a good reminder that the island's appeal is as much space and sea as beaches.
