White Island is a small offshore island near Carriacou, known for pale sand, clear water, and a castaway feel when conditions are calm. It is the kind of place that makes the Grenadines feel like a chain of reachable, low-key escapes.

Visits depend on boats, weather, and sea conditions, so arrange locally and go prepared for little infrastructure. The reward is simple: swimming, beach time, and a different angle on Carriacou's surrounding cays.
