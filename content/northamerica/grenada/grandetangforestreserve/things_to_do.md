---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Start at the [Grand Etang Visitor Centre](/northamerica/grenada/grandetangforestreserve/visitor_centre) overlooking the [crater lake](/northamerica/grenada/grandetangforestreserve/grand_etang_lake). Easy walks include the [Morne LaBaye Trail](/northamerica/grenada/grandetangforestreserve/morne_labaye_trail) and the lake circuit; for a half-day with views, climb [Mt Qua Qua](/northamerica/grenada/grandetangforestreserve/mt_qua_qua); for a full day on a good trail, hike down to the [Seven Sisters Falls](/northamerica/grenada/grandetangforestreserve/seven_sisters_falls) or take the longer route through to [Concord Falls](/northamerica/grenada/grandetangforestreserve/concord_falls). The [Annandale Falls](/northamerica/grenada/grandetangforestreserve/annandale_falls) on the west side of the reserve are a quick roadside stop on the way back to St George's.
