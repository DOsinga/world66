Place de la Victoire is the central square of Pointe-à-Pitre, shaded by old plane trees and flanked by colonial-era buildings painted in faded yellows and greens. The cafes and bars around its edges are where the city slows down in the late afternoon: ti' punch before dinner, cold Corsaire beer, and a view of the square's steady foot traffic.

The square is not glamorous — Pointe-à-Pitre is a working port city, not a resort town — but it has an authentic urban energy that the beach towns on the south coast lack. The bars here are simple places: plastic chairs, rum poured generously, conversation in Creole. It is the best place in Grande-Terre to drink alongside people who live here rather than visit.
