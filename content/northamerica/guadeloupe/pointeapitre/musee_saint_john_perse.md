The Musée Saint-John Perse is housed in a late nineteenth-century metal-and-brick Creole building on Rue de Nozières. The architecture is as important as the collection: painted shutters, iron framing, and a compact urban scale that recalls Pointe-à-Pitre before modern concrete took over.

Inside, the museum looks at the life of Saint-John Perse, the Guadeloupe-born poet and diplomat who won the Nobel Prize in Literature, alongside period furnishings and exhibits on colonial domestic life. It is a small museum, but it gives texture to the old merchant city.
