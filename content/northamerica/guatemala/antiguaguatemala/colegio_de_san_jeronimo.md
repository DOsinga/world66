Colegio de San Jerónimo is one of Antigua's colonial ruins, built in the 1750s and badly damaged by the 1773 earthquake. Its courtyard gardens, old dormitory arches, and upper-level views toward Volcán de Agua make it a worthwhile stop beyond the busiest central ruins.
