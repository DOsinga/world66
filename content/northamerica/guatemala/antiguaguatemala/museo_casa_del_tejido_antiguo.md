Museo Casa del Tejido Antiguo is a small museum about Guatemalan Maya weaving traditions. Guided visits explain the history, tools, and techniques behind traditional textiles, then continue into working spaces where artisans make garments and woven pieces.

The museum is useful before shopping for textiles in Antigua, because it gives context for regional patterns and handwork. Exhibits are mainly in Spanish, so ask ahead if you need an English-speaking guide.
