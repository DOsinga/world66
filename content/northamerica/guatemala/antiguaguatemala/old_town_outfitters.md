Based in Antigua but most useful for [Lake Atitlán](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan): the day trip paddles across the lake under the volcanoes and walks between the Kaqchikel villages on the shore, which is a better way to see them than the public boats. They also run mountain biking through the valleys around Antigua and multi-day combinations of the two. The coordinates are the town centre rather than the doorstep.
