Valhalla Macadamia Farm is a small farm outside Antigua that has become a popular half-day detour for travelers who want something more rural than another ruin or cafe. Visits usually center on macadamia cultivation, tastings, and the farm's low-key setting.

It is not a formal museum, and that is part of the appeal: the stop feels personal and informal. Combine it with a village outing or a slow morning, and confirm current access before setting out because transport and opening details can change.
