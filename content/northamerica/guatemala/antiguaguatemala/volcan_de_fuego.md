Volcán de Fuego is the restless volcano that gives Antigua's skyline its edge. Its name is literal enough: Fuego is one of Central America's most active volcanoes, and on clear nights travellers on Acatenango often see orange bursts, ash plumes, and glowing rockfall from a safe distance.

For most visitors, Fuego is a sight to watch, not a summit to casually climb. Guided Acatenango treks are the standard way to experience it, with camps positioned for views across the saddle toward the eruptive cone. Conditions, closures, and volcanic activity change, so use reputable guides and treat official warnings seriously.
