Chichicastenango Market is the town's great set-piece, spreading through the streets around Santo Tomas church on market days. Stalls sell textiles, masks, flowers, candles, vegetables, incense, and household goods in a dense mix of commerce and ritual life.

It is famous with travellers, but it is not just a tourist market. Go early, keep your bearings in the crowds, and give time to the church steps, where Maya and Catholic practices meet in plain view.
