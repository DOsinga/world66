Iglesia Santo Tomás is the anchor of Chichicastenango, standing above the market on a broad stairway that is often smoky with incense and busy with flower sellers. The building is Catholic, but the life around it is deeply syncretic, with Maya ritual practice visible in the candles, offerings, and prayers on the steps.

Visit respectfully, especially during ceremonies and market days. The church is not just a sight to be consumed; it is one of the most public places where the town's religious layers meet, and that is what makes it essential.
