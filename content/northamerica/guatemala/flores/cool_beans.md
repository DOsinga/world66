A traveller cafe on Calle 15 de Septiembre with a long shaded garden running down to the lake — the most pleasant of the western-side cafes, with strong coffee, decent breakfasts, and a book exchange. Wi-fi is reliable enough to be the unofficial coworking spot for the small itinerant crowd in Flores.

Open from breakfast through dinner. Lunch and the late afternoon are the quietest hours; mornings are competitive for the lake-edge tables.
