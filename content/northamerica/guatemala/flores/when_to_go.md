---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Peten has two seasons: dry from late November to May, and wet from June to October — with a smaller second peak of rain in September. Dry season is the obvious window for both Flores and the Maya sites: trails are firm, mornings are crisp, mosquitoes are tolerable.

The shoulder months either side of the wet season — late October, late May — can be excellent: green landscape, fewer crowds, mostly clear afternoons. Avoid August and September if you can. December and Easter week are crowded with Guatemalan domestic travellers; book a few days ahead.
