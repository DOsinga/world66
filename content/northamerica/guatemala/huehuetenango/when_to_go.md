---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Huehuetenango is a highland city at around 1,900 metres, so the climate is mild year-round — warm by day and cool at night. The dry season (November to April) is the standard travel season: clear skies, dusty roads, and good visibility for the mountain views. The rainy season (May to October) brings afternoon showers that clear quickly, and the mountains turn an almost implausibly vivid green.

The most important event is the **Todos Santos All Saints' Day horse race** on November 1st, in the mountain village of Todos Santos Cuchumatán (two hours by road). It is one of the most extraordinary festivals in Central America and worth planning a trip around. Book accommodation in advance if you plan to attend.
