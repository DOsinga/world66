Mirador Rey Tepepul is a high roadside viewpoint above Santiago Atitlán, with one side looking back across the lake basin and the other dropping toward Guatemala's Pacific slope. Go in the morning for clearer light, and avoid lingering around dusk; birding trips into the surrounding forest usually need a guide.
