Mostly a transport company, and that is what makes it useful: the shuttle network out of Panajachel to Antigua, Xela, Chichicastenango, the Mexican border at La Mesilla and on to Flores is how most travellers actually move around Guatemala, and this is one of the established operators running it. They have a second office in Antigua, and sell the lake and volcano tours alongside.
