Across the lake from [Panajachel](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/panajachel), San Pedro la Laguna is a small pueblo that captures the essence of Guatemala in one place. It sits at the base of [Volcán San Pedro](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/volcan_san_pedro_hike) on the southwestern shore of [Lake Atitlán](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan), and attracts the kind of traveller who comes for a week and stays for a month.

The draw is the mix: indigenous Tz'utujil Maya culture alongside a revolving cast of internationals, a concentration of [Spanish schools](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/spanish_schools) that undercut Antigua on price, outdoor activities from [volcano climbing](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/volcan_san_pedro_hike) to [kayaking](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/kayaking_lake_atitlan) to [scuba diving](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/scuba_diving), and [painting](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/painting_classes) and [weaving workshops](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/weaving_workshops) run by local Tz'utujil artists. Full moon parties, live music, and religious fiestas fill the calendar — all at prices that remain remarkably low.

San Pedro is less polished than Panajachel and quieter than San Marcos, which suits longer-stay travellers. The social life concentrates in the [Zona Viva](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/zona_viva), the strip of lanes between the two docks where bars, hostels, and tour operators cluster. Beyond it, the [Iglesia San Pedro Apóstol](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/iglesia_san_pedro_apostol) and the [municipal market](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/mercado_municipal) belong to the indigenous community that has lived here for centuries. The [neighbouring village of San Juan La Laguna](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeatitlan/san_pedro_la_laguna/san_juan_la_laguna) is an easy half-day addition.
