---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

San Pedro's restaurant scene is remarkably international for a small lakeside town. You'll find Israeli, Italian, Indian, and Mexican food alongside Guatemalan staples, mostly run by long-term expats and return visitors who stayed and opened a place. Prices are low. The local staple is a set lunch (plato del día) for a dollar or two — rice, beans, chicken or egg, tortillas.
