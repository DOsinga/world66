---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

San Pedro is one of the best-value adventure bases in Central America. The volcano above town is the most accessible of the three Atitlán volcanoes. The lake is clean and swimmable, with kayaking, stand-up paddle, and scuba diving offered by several operators. Spanish schools are cheaper here than almost anywhere in Guatemala, making extended stays practical. Weaving and painting classes run alongside yoga retreats and fire-spinning on full moon nights.
