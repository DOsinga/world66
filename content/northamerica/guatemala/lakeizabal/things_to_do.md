---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lake Izabal and the Río Dulce offer one of the best combinations of history, wildlife, and scenery in Central America. The 17th-century [Castillo de San Felipe](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeizabal/castillo_de_san_felipe) guards the lake's eastern outlet. The [Río Dulce gorge](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeizabal/rio_dulce_gorge) — jungle-clad limestone cliffs above deep green water — is best seen by lancha on the two-hour trip to Livingston. The [Biotopo Chocón Machacas](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeizabal/biotopo_chochon_machacas) reserve protects freshwater manatees and excellent birdwatching. [Hot springs](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeizabal/aguas_calientes) on the south shore make for a fine half-day by boat, and [Denny's Beach](/northamerica/guatemala/lakeizabal/horseback_riding) offers horseback riding into the Sierra de las Minas foothills.
