---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Livingston's food scene is built around Garífuna cooking — tapado, hudut (fish in coconut milk with green plantain mash), johnnycakes, and fresh fish. Prices are low and most restaurants are informal. The cluster of places near the boat dock is the most reliable area; venture a block or two inland for smaller, even cheaper spots used by locals.
