Tortugario Monterrico is the local turtle conservation anchor, tied to the Pacific coast's nesting beaches and mangrove habitat. In season, visitors may be able to watch hatchlings released toward the sea, which is easily the most memorable activity in town.

The timing depends on nesting and hatching, so do not treat it like a guaranteed show. Ask locally when you arrive, and go with the understanding that the conservation work matters more than the photo.
