Calle Santander is the commercial spine of Panajachel, running toward the lake through a long corridor of textile stalls, cafes, souvenir shops, tour agencies, and tuk-tuks. It is not subtle, but it is useful, lively, and hard to avoid.

Come here to compare shuttle prices, buy a woven bag, change money, or simply get your bearings. The street works best when treated as part market, part transit route, and part theatre: everyone on the lake seems to pass through at some point.
