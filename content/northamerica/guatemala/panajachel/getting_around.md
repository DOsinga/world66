---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The centre of Panajachel is walkable. Calle Santander runs between the highway side of town and the lakefront, with most shops, cafes, and agencies clustered along or near it.

Tuk-tuks are useful for short hops to the dock, the old church, or the nature reserve entrance. For villages around the lake, use lanchas rather than the slow, twisting roads unless you specifically want to travel overland.
