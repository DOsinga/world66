---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tourist shuttles connect Panajachel with Antigua, Guatemala City, Quetzaltenango, and Chichicastenango. They are not glamorous, but they are the simplest option with luggage. Public buses are cheaper and more interesting; expect changes at Sololá or Los Encuentros depending on where you start.

From Panajachel, shared lanchas run across Lake Atitlán all day. Boats leave when they have enough passengers, with the busiest routes serving Santa Cruz, San Marcos, San Juan, San Pedro, and Santiago Atitlán. Morning crossings are usually smoother than afternoon ones.
