Jardín de América is a small shopping arcade rather than a major sight, but it is a handy place to browse textiles, small gifts, and everyday items. Prices vary, so take your time and compare before buying.

It is most useful if Calle Santander feels too hectic. The selection is still aimed at visitors, but the setting is calmer and close to the older part of town.
