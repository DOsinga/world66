The Museo Lacustre Atitlán is small, but it helps make sense of the lake beyond the view. Exhibits cover the volcanic formation of the basin, local archaeology, and the communities that have lived around the water for centuries.

It is especially useful on a rainy afternoon or before visiting the other villages. Do not expect a major museum; expect a compact introduction that gives the landscape and its Maya history more context.
