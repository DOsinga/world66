Panajachel's public beach is less about swimming than about the view. The shore opens wide to Lake Atitlán, with Volcán San Pedro, Tolimán, and Atitlán arranged across the water like a stage set.

Morning is the best time, before haze and wind build. You will see lanchas loading passengers, vendors setting up, and families strolling the waterfront; it is the simplest place in town to understand why people keep calling this lake one of the most beautiful in the world.
