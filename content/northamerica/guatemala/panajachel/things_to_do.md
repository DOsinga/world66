---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Panajachel is best understood as a launch pad for the lake, but it has enough to occupy a relaxed day. Start with the waterfront views, then use Calle Santander for orientation, shopping, and food. Casa Cakchiquel and the small lake museum are worthwhile when you want context for the Maya villages and the volcanic basin around you.

The strongest sight near town is the Atitlán Nature Reserve, a forested ravine with trails, bridges, monkeys, coatis, and lake viewpoints. If you have your own transport or a taxi, the Mirador San Jorge above the road to Sololá gives one of the clearest looks back over Panajachel and the volcano-framed water.
