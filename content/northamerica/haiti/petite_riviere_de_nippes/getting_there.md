---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Petite Rivière de Nippes is in the Nippes department on Haiti's southern coast. The nearest town of any size is Miragoâne, about 20 km to the east, which is accessible from Port-au-Prince (around 90 km by road). From Miragoâne, tap-taps (shared minibuses) and motos run to the village. The journey from Port-au-Prince can take several hours depending on road conditions, which are variable throughout the region.

Travel in this part of Haiti is for experienced independent travelers comfortable with improvisation and minimal facilities.
