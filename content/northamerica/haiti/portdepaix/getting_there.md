---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Port-de-Paix is awkward to reach from anywhere. The road from Cap-Haïtien — about 110 km — has improved in recent years but is still slow, with a mountainous middle section. Allow three to four hours by car. Public tap-taps run the route but are uncomfortable and unpredictable.

The faster option is to fly. Sunrise Airways operates short hops between Port-au-Prince and [Port-de-Paix Airport](/northamerica/haiti/portdepaix/port_de_paix_airport) when there is enough demand; check schedules close to the date.

To reach [Tortuga Island](/northamerica/haiti/portdepaix/tortuga_island), boats leave from the jetty at the western end of town when they have enough passengers. The crossing takes 30 to 60 minutes and is open to the swell — not for nervous travellers.
