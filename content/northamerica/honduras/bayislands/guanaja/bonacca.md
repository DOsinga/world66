Bonacca, also called The Cay, is the compact island town just off Guanaja. Houses, shops, docks, and churches sit shoulder to shoulder over the water, with boats replacing streets for much of daily movement.

It is the best place to feel Guanaja's working rhythm: supplies arriving, water taxis loading, children heading to school, and fishers moving between the cay and the main island. Come with curiosity rather than a checklist.
