Dunbar Rock is a tiny islet off Guanaja, visually striking because it rises abruptly from the water like a natural watchtower. It is best known in the dive community, but even from a boat it is one of the island's more memorable shapes.

Access depends on operators and conditions. Treat it as a dive or boat-trip sight rather than a place to wander independently.
