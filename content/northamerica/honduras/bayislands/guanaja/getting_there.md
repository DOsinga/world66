---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Guanaja has a small airport with domestic connections that vary by season and operator. Most travellers reach the Bay Islands through Roatán or La Ceiba, then continue by small plane or boat depending on schedules.

Always confirm transport locally before building a tight itinerary. Weather can disrupt boats and flights, and Guanaja rewards travellers who leave room for delays rather than treating it like a simple ferry hop.
