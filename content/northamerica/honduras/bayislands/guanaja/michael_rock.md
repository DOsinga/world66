Michael Rock is a small west-side settlement and landmark, more of a local reference point than a polished sight. Its value is in the views, the boat ride, and the way it shows Guanaja breaking into little inhabited edges.

If you are exploring by water taxi, it is worth asking whether a route can include this side of the island. Late afternoon gives the coast a warm, open look.
