Playa El Soldado is a quiet beach area where Guanaja's appeal is easiest to understand: clear water, little noise, and not much built between you and the shore. It is not a serviced beach in the resort sense.

Bring what you need and check access locally. Like much of Guanaja, the right boatman and the right weather matter more than any printed timetable.
