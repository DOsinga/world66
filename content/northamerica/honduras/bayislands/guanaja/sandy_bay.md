Sandy Bay is one of Guanaja's calmer coastal settlements, close to small beaches, cays, and dive sites. The appeal is the lack of fuss: boats pulled up, clear water nearby, and fewer signs of packaged tourism.

It works best as a base for people who already know they want a slow island stay. Bring patience and expect arrangements to be informal.
