Savannah Bight sits toward the east of Guanaja, where the island feels more remote and less visited. It is not a conventional attraction, but it gives a good sense of how scattered and maritime the island really is.

Use it as a waypoint for exploring the eastern shore or arranging a boat trip. Facilities are limited, so bring water and confirm your return ride before wandering too far.
