---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Drinking happens in West End. The strip has six or seven bars and the kind of small island ecosystem where you will run into the same people three nights running. Sundowners is the standard sunset spot; **Booga's** for the live music and the late-night crowd; **The Argentinian Bar** for cocktails; **Hip Hippo** for the dance floor when one is needed. In Sandy Bay and West Bay the resort bars are pleasant but the scene ends earlier.
