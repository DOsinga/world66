---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Roatán's beaches are the postcard product — white sand, calm reef-protected water, palms. The famous ones are **West Bay** and **Tabyana** at the southwest tip; **Half Moon Bay** in West End is smaller but convenient. Quieter sand can be found at East Harbour, Camp Bay, and along the north coast east of Sandy Bay. Reef-shoes are useful most places — most beaches drop quickly into coral.
