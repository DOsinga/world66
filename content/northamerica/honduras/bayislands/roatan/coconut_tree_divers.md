A long-standing West End operation at the Half Moon Bay end of the strip, running trips to more than thirty sites along the reef — walls, channels and shallow coral gardens — with up to four dives a day. It is a PADI 5 Star IDC centre and, by its own account, the only TDI centre in West End, so it takes both first-timers doing an Open Water course and divers going on to technical training.
