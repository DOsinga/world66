French Harbour is the island's main fishing port, on the south coast about midway along Roatán, and increasingly a base for upmarket resort and residential development. The town itself is working Honduras — fish processing plants, supermarkets, a busy commercial main street — but the surrounding area has the **Iguana Wildlife Park**, several large resorts, and access to dive sites less visited than those off West End.

The **Coral Cay** area east of the port has good snorkelling from the shore. The harbour itself is the place to arrange a sport-fishing charter — Roatán is a serious billfish destination from March through July.
