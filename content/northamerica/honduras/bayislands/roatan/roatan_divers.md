A PADI 5 Star IDC and Tec centre in the middle of West End that keeps its groups small — the pitch is boutique diving rather than boatloads. Courses run from Open Water up to instructor development and technical training, and there is a second shop along the coast in Sandy Bay. The day starts early: the office opens at a quarter to eight.
