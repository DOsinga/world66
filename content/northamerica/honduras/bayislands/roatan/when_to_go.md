---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Roatán's climate is Caribbean tropical: warm year round, with two seasons that matter to visitors.

**The dry season**, roughly **February to early May**, has the best weather — sunny, low humidity, water visibility for diving at its best (often 30 metres). This is also high season; resort prices peak.

**The wet season**, **September through January**, has the cheapest prices and the lowest visitor numbers. Rain is mostly heavy short showers rather than days-on-end of grey; visibility drops to around 15 metres but the diving is still good. The risk of a hurricane is real from August to October — Hurricane Mitch did serious damage in 1998 — but direct hits are uncommon.

The shoulder months — **late May and June, and February** — are the sweet spot if you can pick. Cruise-ship traffic peaks November to April; for less-crowded beaches, go in September or October.
