One of the island's long-running shops, with access to more than sixty dive sites and courses from beginner to professional. Utila's draw is the whale sharks: they pass close to the north shore in the two seasons — roughly February to April, then October to December — often within a mile of the coast, and boats go out looking for the feeding "boils" when the birds give them away.
