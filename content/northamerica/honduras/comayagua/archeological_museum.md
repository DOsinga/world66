Comayagua's Archeological Museum gives a useful introduction to the Lenca civilization and the wider history of Honduras. The collection includes pre-Columbian artifacts, pottery, and exhibits that place the city in a longer regional story. It is a worthwhile stop before exploring Comayagua's churches and colonial centre.
