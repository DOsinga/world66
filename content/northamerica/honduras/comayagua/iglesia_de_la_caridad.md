Iglesia de la Caridad is a historic church in Comayagua, adding another layer to a city best known for its cathedral, clock, and compact colonial centre. Its value is in the cumulative rhythm of Comayagua's churches rather than a single spectacular feature.

It is worth a short stop while walking the old town, especially if you are tracing religious architecture and neighborhood plazas. The church helps show why Comayagua remains one of Honduras's strongest colonial-city visits.
