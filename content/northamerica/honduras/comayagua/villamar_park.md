Villamar Park is a local recreation stop rather than a historic sight, but it earns its place if you are in Comayagua with children or need a lazy afternoon away from the colonial centre. The draw is straightforward: swimming pools, lawns, play areas, and food stalls in a green compound on the edge of town.

Weekends are the liveliest time, when families from Comayagua come out for the water and shade. Go expecting a Honduran leisure park, not a polished resort; the appeal is that it shows ordinary local downtime and gives you a practical break from churches, museums, and hot streets.
