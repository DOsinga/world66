Part of the Belgian ViaVia network of traveller cafés, and the easiest place in town to arrange something that is not the ruins. Horseback rides go up into the hills behind Copán; the farm tours take in coffee and cardamom with the growers, and finish with a meal cooked from what the farm produces. The café itself sits a block and a half off the central park.
