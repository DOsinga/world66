---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Gracias is connected to the rest of Honduras by a winding mountain road through the Lempira Department. Direct buses run from **San Pedro Sula** (roughly 5–6 hours, with departures in the early morning) and from **Santa Rosa de Copán** (about 2–3 hours), which serves as a regional hub. The road from Santa Rosa is the most reliable approach and passes through attractive highland scenery.

A small regional airstrip (Celaque Airport) exists near town but sees minimal scheduled service. The vast majority of travellers arrive by bus. From **Copán Ruinas**, expect a connection through Santa Rosa — allow a full day of travel. There is no direct international access.
