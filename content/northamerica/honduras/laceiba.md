La Ceiba is Honduras's third-largest city, on the Caribbean coast at the base of the Cordillera Nombre de Dios mountains. It's primarily known as a transit hub — the main departure point for ferries to the Bay Islands of Roatán and Útila — but also has genuine character of its own.

The city has a reputation for nightlife, and the Zona Viva strip along Avenida 15 has the densest concentration of bars and clubs in the country. The [Feria Internacional de La Ceiba](/northamerica/honduras/laceiba/feria_internacional) in May is one of Central America's larger street festivals, drawing roughly half a million visitors for the carnival parade.

[Pico Bonito National Park](/northamerica/honduras/laceiba/pico_bonito) begins almost at the city limits — a cloud forest reserve with waterfalls, rivers, and good birding. Rafting on the [Río Cangrejal](/northamerica/honduras/laceiba/rio_cangrejal), which runs through the park's lower reaches, is a popular day trip. In the opposite direction, the [Cuero y Salado Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/honduras/laceiba/cuero_y_salado) protects a large mangrove wetland that is one of Honduras's best places to see manatees.
