The first rafting company on the Río Cangrejal, running since 1992 and still owned by the German kayaker who started it — a former member of the national rafting and kayaking team. The river drops through the Nombre de Dios range beside Pico Bonito, twenty minutes from La Ceiba, and the trips range from a half-day on class II–III water to harder sections. They also run kayaking, waterfall walks, horses and multi-day trips further into the rainforest.
