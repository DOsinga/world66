---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ocotepeque rewards low-key exploration. The main square and its colonial church are a pleasant morning stroll, and the surrounding highlands — pine forest, cool air, and views across three countries — are worth a day if you have transport. Most visitors pass through on their way to or from the El Poy or Agua Caliente border crossings, but the cloud forest reserve to the northwest justifies a longer stay.
