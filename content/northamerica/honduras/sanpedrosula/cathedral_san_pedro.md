The Metropolitan Cathedral of St. Peter the Apostle stands on the central Parque Central, the traditional heart of the city. It is the seat of the Archdiocese of San Pedro Sula and the most prominent religious building in northern Honduras. The exterior is a relatively plain neo-colonial design, but the interior is well-maintained and cool — a welcome break from the heat outside.

The Parque Central around it is the main public square of the city, ringed by benches, vendors, and the municipal buildings. It is busiest on Sunday mornings and during the Feria Juniana celebrations in June. For all its limited architectural distinction, the cathedral and its square give the city a recognisable centre and are worth at least a passing visit.
