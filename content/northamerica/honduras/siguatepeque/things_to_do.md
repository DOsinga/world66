title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Siguatepeque's pleasures are mostly low-key and unhurried. The [Parque Central](/northamerica/honduras/siguatepeque/parque_central) is the natural gathering point — a pleasant square shaded by trees, with the [Iglesia San Pablo](/northamerica/honduras/siguatepeque/iglesia_san_pablo) occupying one side. Sit on a bench and watch the town go about its business.

Calle 21 de Agosto — known to everyone as "Veinte-uno" — is the main commercial street, lined with comedores, shoe shops and hardware stores. It's a working Honduran high street rather than a tourist attraction, but that's exactly why it's worth an hour of your time. The [Sunday market](/northamerica/honduras/siguatepeque/mercado_dominical) draws farmers from the surrounding mountain villages with fresh produce, and it starts early.

The town sits at around 1,100 metres, which makes the climate noticeably cooler than the lowlands — the surrounding pine forests are a real draw. For a longer excursion, the [Parque Nacional Montaña de Comayagua](/northamerica/honduras/siguatepeque/parque_nacional_montana_de_comayagua) begins just outside town and offers serious hiking through cloud forest.
