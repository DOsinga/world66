The Basílica de Suyapa, about 5 km east of the historic centre, is the most important Catholic pilgrimage site in Central America. It houses the tiny Virgin of Suyapa, a 7 cm carved cedar figure discovered in 1747 that has accumulated a extraordinary reputation for miracles among Hondurans and Central Americans.

The basilica itself is a large modern church built to accommodate the crowds of pilgrims who arrive, particularly during the February festival (2 February, Feast of the Virgen de Suyapa) when hundreds of thousands of devotees walk here from across Honduras and neighbouring countries. The older, smaller colonial church adjacent to the basilica is more architecturally interesting. The complex is easily reachable by taxi from the centre and is worth visiting for anyone interested in living religious traditions in Central America.
