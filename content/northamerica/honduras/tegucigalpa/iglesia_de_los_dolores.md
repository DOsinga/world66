Iglesia de Los Dolores is one of Tegucigalpa's most characterful historic churches, standing close to the old market streets north of the cathedral. Its facade is the main draw, packed with carved details, religious figures, and the warm irregularity of a building that has been part of the city for centuries.

The church also gives a useful reason to walk beyond Parque Central into a busier, more everyday part of the old centre. Go in daylight, look closely at the stonework, and combine it with the cathedral and nearby plazas for a compact introduction to colonial Tegucigalpa.
