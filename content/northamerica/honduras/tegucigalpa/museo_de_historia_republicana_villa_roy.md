Museo de Historia Republicana Villa Roy occupies a historic residence in Tegucigalpa and focuses on Honduras after independence. It is a useful stop for travellers who want context for the capital's monuments, institutions, and often turbulent political life.

The museum is more about national memory than spectacle. Go expecting documents, objects, rooms, and atmosphere, then combine it with the cathedral, Parque La Leona, or the National Gallery of Art for a fuller picture of the old centre.
