Museo para la Identidad Nacional is one of Tegucigalpas most useful cultural stops. Set in a restored historic building, it uses archaeology, art, history, and multimedia exhibits to tell a broad story of Honduras and its people.

For travelers, it gives context that the capital does not always reveal easily on the street. Go early in your visit if you can, then walk the surrounding historic centre with a sharper eye.
