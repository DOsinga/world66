Museo y Balneario Rufino Galan is a small, local-scale stop in Trujillo, combining a modest museum element with a bathing area. It is not a polished national institution, but it gives some texture to a town where history, beach life, and local memory sit close together.

Go with modest expectations and it can be worthwhile, especially if you are spending more than a quick night in Trujillo. The appeal is the mix: a little local history, a little water, and a very Honduran sense of making a leisure spot out of what is close at hand.
