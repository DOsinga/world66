---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Caribbean coast of Honduras has a pronounced wet/dry split. **February to May** is the driest period and the easiest for travel — sunny days, calm sea, the road from La Ceiba mostly passable. April and May are also pleasantly warm without yet being oppressive.

**June to October** is the wet season, with afternoon downpours and the occasional tropical storm or hurricane (the Caribbean coast is exposed). Roads, especially east of La Ceiba, deteriorate sharply; some bridges wash out. Plan extra time and check conditions before travelling.

**November to January** is unpredictable — usually drier than the worst of the rainy season but cooler, with the so-called *norte* winds bringing several days of grey skies and rough sea. Hotel prices are at their lowest. The Garifuna village celebrations around Easter (Semana Santa) are worth timing a visit around.
