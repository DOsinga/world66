A long-running spot on the main road serving generous breakfasts, sandwiches, and decent coffee. The garden seating out the back is a good place to read or catch up on email after a morning dive.

Prices are higher than at the strictly local places but the portions are large, the bread is baked on site, and the wi-fi works.
