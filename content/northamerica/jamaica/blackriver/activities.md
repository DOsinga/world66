Three things sit within half an hour of each other here, and together they make the best day on the south coast.

The **Black River Safari** goes four miles up the Great Morass among the mangroves, looking for American crocodiles — there are a few hundred left in the system and the boats find them most mornings. Go early: better light, better wildlife, fewer boats.

**YS Falls**, twenty minutes inland, is the waterfall worth swimming at — seven cascades on a working farm, with pools, a rope swing and a fraction of the crowd at Dunn's River. **Appleton Estate** is up the same valley, distilling rum since 1749, and runs tours with rather more tasting than most people expect at eleven in the morning.

Offshore, the **Pelican Bar** stands on a sandbar a kilometre out: a shack of driftwood and thatch selling Red Stripe and fried fish, reached by boat from here or from [Treasure Beach](/northamerica/jamaica/treasurebeach). Agree the return time with your captain before you get out.
