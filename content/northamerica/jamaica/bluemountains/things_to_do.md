---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Blue Mountains offer some of the best hiking in the Caribbean. The signature experience is the overnight climb to Blue Mountain Peak, but the mountains reward slower exploration too — birding, coffee farm visits, and forest walks through cloud forest hung with moss and tree ferns.

The [Holywell Recreational Area](/northamerica/jamaica/bluemountains/holywell_recreational_area) is the easiest entry point, with well-maintained trails and cabins for overnight stays. For the serious hiker, the [Blue Mountain Peak](/northamerica/jamaica/bluemountains/blue_mountain_peak) trail is one of the great Caribbean treks. And no visit is complete without understanding [Blue Mountain coffee](/northamerica/jamaica/bluemountains/blue_mountain_coffee) — the crop that has shaped this landscape for two centuries.
