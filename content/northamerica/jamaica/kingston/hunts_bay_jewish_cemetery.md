Hunts Bay Jewish Cemetery is a rare trace of Jamaica's early Jewish community, with graves dating back to the 17th century. Some stones carry Hebrew inscriptions alongside skull-and-crossbones imagery, a striking reminder of the island's trading, seafaring, and pirate-era history.
