The Frame Centre Gallery on Tangerine Place in New Kingston is one of Kingston's most active commercial galleries for contemporary Jamaican art — a programme-driven space that shows both established names and emerging artists, with an emphasis on painting, sculpture, and mixed media. The gallery has been operating for decades and has a track record for identifying significant Jamaican talent early.

The exhibitions change regularly — every four to six weeks — so the specific show available during a visit is unpredictable, but the quality level is consistently high. The gallery also holds an art shop with prints and smaller works at accessible prices, and the knowledgeable staff are happy to discuss the Jamaican art scene in depth. Combined with the Mutual Gallery or a visit to the National Gallery Downtown, this provides a good overview of where Jamaican contemporary art currently sits.
