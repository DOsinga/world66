Track and Records at Marketplace on Constant Spring Road is the Usain Bolt-branded sports bar and restaurant that has become one of Kingston's most visited casual dining spots — partly for the association, partly because the food is genuinely good by sports bar standards. The menu covers Jamaican staples — jerk, patties, oxtail — alongside burgers and chicken wings, done with more care than the branding might suggest.

The walls display Bolt memorabilia, the TVs show sports, and the atmosphere on match days or during championship athletics meetings becomes something you'd have to be in Kingston to fully appreciate. The bar is well stocked, the service is fast, and the location in the Marketplace shopping complex makes it easily accessible. Not a serious restaurant but a reliable, enjoyable one.
