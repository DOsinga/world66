Trenchtown Culture Yard Museum preserves one of Kingston's most important music addresses, the yard where Bob Marley lived and where reggae's social world can be understood at street level. The site connects songs, community life and the hard history of Trench Town rather than treating reggae as a beach soundtrack.

A guided visit is the best way to understand the place. It is a meaningful stop for music travellers, but also one that asks for respect: this is a living neighbourhood with a famous cultural legacy, not a theme set.
