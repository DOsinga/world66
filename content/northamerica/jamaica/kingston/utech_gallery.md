The University of Technology (UTech) on Papine Road in eastern Kingston operates one of the city's more active contemporary art galleries — a university-based space that hosts regular exhibitions of student and faculty work alongside external shows. The quality is variable but the space is serious, the hanging is professional, and the work tends to be more experimental than what the established commercial galleries show.

The UTech campus sits in the area between New Kingston and the University of the West Indies (UWI) campus in Mona — a zone of institutional buildings at the foot of the Blue Mountains that also contains the Cultural Training Centre. For visitors making a circuit of Kingston's cultural institutions, UTech's gallery is worth checking for current programming. The campus itself is open and the surrounding Papine area, though not a tourist neighbourhood, is relaxed during daytime.
