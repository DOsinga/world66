William Grant Park, better known locally as Parade, is one of downtown Kingston's old civic spaces. It is not polished in the way a resort-town plaza might be, but it sits at the centre of the city's commercial, transport, and street-life rhythms.

The park is most interesting as urban context: vendors, buses, colonial-era street patterns, and the daily movement of central Kingston. Visit in daylight and with normal city awareness, using it as part of a broader look at downtown rather than a quiet garden escape.
