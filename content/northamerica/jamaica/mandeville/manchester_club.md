The Manchester Club sits on the edge of town and runs a tidy nine-hole golf course, the oldest in Jamaica. Greens fees are very modest by Caribbean standards and the course is rarely crowded; you can usually walk on. The fairways are short and tight, the greens slow, and the views toward the surrounding hills surprisingly fine.

Tennis courts and a small clubhouse round out the facilities. Drop in for lunch even if you don't play — the club terrace is a pleasant lunch spot with a view, and a small reminder of the British Jamaica that has otherwise mostly vanished.
