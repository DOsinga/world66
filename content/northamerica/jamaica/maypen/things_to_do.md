---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

May Pen itself is a market town that most travellers pass through, but the surrounding parish of Clarendon hides some genuinely worthwhile detours: a 17th-century plantation house, the country's longest river, and the radioactive Milk River mineral baths. Time it for a Saturday morning market or, better, the late-July Denbigh Agricultural Show and the town becomes a destination in its own right.
