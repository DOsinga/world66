[Falmouth](/northamerica/jamaica/falmouth), 35 km east along the coast, is the standout day trip — one of the best-preserved Georgian towns in the Caribbean and far more interesting than anything in MoBay itself. The bamboo rafting trips down the **Martha Brae River** start just inland from Falmouth and pair well with a walking tour of the old port.

Closer to home, the **Green Grotto Caves** (between Falmouth and Ocho Rios) and the **Belvedere Estate** in the hills above Reading make easy half-day excursions. Negril's seven-mile beach is about 90 minutes west and can be done in a day, but it deserves an overnight.
