---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Sangster International Airport** (MBJ) is Jamaica's main tourist gateway. Direct flights connect to most major US east-coast cities (New York, Miami, Atlanta, Charlotte, Boston, Philadelphia), Toronto, Montreal, London, Manchester, and a long list of Caribbean and Latin American hubs.

The terminal is five minutes' taxi from the Hip Strip — agree a fare with a JUTA driver at the rank or use a hotel transfer. Avoid unmarked taxis. Public buses (route 10) run cheaply between the airport and downtown but are not designed for travellers with luggage.

**Cruise ships** dock at the Freeport pier, west of the centre. Most tour buses go straight to the plantations and beaches; if you have a free morning you can take a taxi into town to see Sam Sharpe Square.

**Long-distance buses** to Kingston, Negril, Ocho Rios, and Port Antonio leave from the depot on Barnett Street. Knutsford Express runs an air-conditioned coach service on the main routes — book ahead.
