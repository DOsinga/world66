---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Montego Bay's tourist axis is the **Hip Strip** on Gloucester Avenue, lined with bars, restaurants, and the entrance to **Doctor's Cave Beach**. Behind the strip lies the real town: **Sam Sharpe Square** at the heart of downtown, the bustling Harbour Street, and a string of imposing Georgian-era buildings. East of the city are two of the great plantation houses of the Caribbean — **Rose Hall** and **Greenwood** — easily combined as a half-day trip.
