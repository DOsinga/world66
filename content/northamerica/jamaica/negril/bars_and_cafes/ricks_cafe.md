Rick's Café is the most famous bar in Jamaica and one of the most recognisable in the Caribbean. It sits on the edge of the West End cliffs and draws a crowd every evening for the sunset and the cliff jumpers. Locals and tourists of varying bravery go off ledges at 3, 5, and 10 metres into the deep water below; the professional divers who go off the highest point are worth watching. The drinks are tourist-priced and the place fills quickly — arrive an hour before sunset to get a cliffside position.
