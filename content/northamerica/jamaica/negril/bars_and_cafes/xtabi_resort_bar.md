A quieter alternative to Rick's just up the cliff road. Xtabi sits above a natural cove where the sea pushes in between the rocks; you can swim from the cove or simply sit at a cliffside table with a rum punch and watch the sun go down at your own pace. The crowd here is smaller and the atmosphere more relaxed than at Rick's. A good choice if you want the West End sunset experience without the organised spectacle.
