---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Ocho Rios has a lively after-dark scene by Jamaican north coast standards, though much of it is concentrated in a few areas near the waterfront and along Main Street. Rum punches, Red Stripe, and freshly blended fruit drinks are the staples. A handful of bars double as live music venues, with reggae and dancehall most evenings at weekends. The cruise ship crowd thins after sunset, which means the evening atmosphere is more local than the daytime.
