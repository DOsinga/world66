Dolphin Cove is a marine attraction just west of Ocho Rios where visitors can swim with dolphins, interact with sharks and stingrays in shallow pools, and walk through a small jungle path with birds and other animals. It's a well-organised commercial operation, popular with families and cruise passengers.

The dolphin encounters are the main draw — you can choose from different packages ranging from a briefer interaction to full swims where the dolphins push you through the water by your feet. Booking in advance is recommended, especially when cruise ships are in port. Views over the bay from the facility are good, and there are changing rooms and a restaurant on site. Animal welfare standards at marine attractions vary; research according to your own comfort level before booking.
