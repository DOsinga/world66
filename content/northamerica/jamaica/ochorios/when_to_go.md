---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Jamaica's north coast has a warm tropical climate year-round, with temperatures rarely dropping below 20°C even at night. The driest and most reliable period is December through April — the tourist high season, when prices rise accordingly and Ocho Rios fills with cruise passengers and winter escapees from North America and Europe. Rain can fall in any month, but the main wet periods are May–June and September–November.

If you want to visit when the crowds thin, June and July are a reasonable compromise: the rains are easing off, the cruise traffic is lighter, and many prices drop for the shoulder season. The one thing to watch is hurricane season, which runs June through November. Most years pass without incident, but if you're visiting between September and October, keep an eye on weather forecasts and ensure your travel insurance covers weather disruptions.
