---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Options are limited. The beach bar at James Bond Beach is the most obvious place for a drink — well-positioned for the afternoon light on the bay, serving the standard Jamaican roster of Red Stripe, rum punch, and fresh coconut water. In town, there are local bars, but they are not set up for passing visitors. The GoldenEye resort bar is available to non-guests but priced accordingly.
