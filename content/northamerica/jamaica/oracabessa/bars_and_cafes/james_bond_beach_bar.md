The beach bar at James Bond Beach is the natural place to end an afternoon in Oracabessa. Red Stripe, rum punch, and fresh coconut water are the staples. The position is good — facing west across the bay so the afternoon light that gives the town its name falls directly on the water in front of you. Not a destination in itself, but exactly the right place to sit after snorkelling or a swim.
