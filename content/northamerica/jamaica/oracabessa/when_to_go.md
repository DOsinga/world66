---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season, November to April, is the best time to visit. Skies are clearer, seas calmer, and snorkelling visibility in the marine reserve is at its best. December and January are the busiest months, when the GoldenEye resort fills with guests escaping northern winters and prices rise accordingly.

The rainy season runs May to October. The weather is warmer, rain comes in afternoon showers rather than sustained downpours, and rates are lower — making it a reasonable budget option if you are not set on perfect snorkelling conditions. September and October are peak hurricane season and the period to avoid if possible: the north coast is not as exposed as the south, but storms can still cause days of rough weather and closed beaches.
