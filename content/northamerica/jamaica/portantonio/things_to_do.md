---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Port Antonio's pleasures are mostly natural. Rio Grande rafting, the Blue Lagoon, Frenchman's Cove, and the lush hills of the Blue Mountains to the south make it one of the most scenically varied corners of Jamaica. The pace is slower than Montego Bay or Negril, the crowds are thinner, and the landscape more dramatic. The Folly Peninsula east of town has ruins, a lighthouse, and good swimming.
