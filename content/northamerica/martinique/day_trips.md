Martinique is compact enough to explore fully in a day from any base. The most popular excursion by far is [Les Fonds Blancs](/northamerica/thecaribbean/martinique/les_fonds_blancs), the area of shallow sandbars off the east coast between Le François and Le Vauclin. Boat trips run from the Baie du Simon most mornings, giving you a few hours wading in waist-deep turquoise water in the middle of the open sea, including a stop at the Baignoire de Joséphine sandbar. A Creole lunch on board is usually included.

The southern beaches at Sainte-Anne and the offshore Diamond Rock make for easy half-day trips from Fort-de-France. The Caravelle Peninsula on the northeast coast — a headland protected as part of the Regional Nature Park — rewards a longer outing, with a clifftop walk to the ruins of Château Dubuc taking around two hours.
