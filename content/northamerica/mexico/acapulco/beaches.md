---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Acapulco Bay has a string of beaches running along its inner curve, each with a different character. All the main beaches are safe for swimming — the bay is sheltered from the open Pacific swell, keeping the water calm and warm year-round.

**Playa Caleta and Playa Caletilla** in the old town are the most local and characterful. These two small coves are separated by a rocky point, and they fill with Mexican families on weekends. The glass-bottom boats to [Isla La Roqueta](/northamerica/mexico/acapulco/isla_la_roqueta) depart from here.

**[Playa Condesa](/northamerica/mexico/acapulco/playa_condesa)** is the most developed beach — long, lively, backed by hotels, with parasailing and jet ski rentals. Good for people-watching.

**Playa Hornos and Playa Hornitos** sit between Condesa and the old town and attract a mostly local crowd. Less organised than Condesa, more relaxed.

Beyond the bay, [Playa Pie de la Cuesta](/northamerica/mexico/acapulco/playa_pie_de_la_cuesta) to the west is a wild Pacific beach with thundering surf — not for swimming, but spectacular for a walk at sunset.
