title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Acapulco is a beach town, and the beach is why you come. But the city has more layers than the resort strip suggests.

The most iconic sight is [La Quebrada](/northamerica/mexico/acapulco/la_quebrada) — the 35-metre cliff off which local divers plunge five times a day into a narrow inlet, timing their jumps to the waves. The evening show with torchlight is the one to see. The [Fort of San Diego](/northamerica/mexico/acapulco/fort_san_diego) above the harbour is worth an hour for its account of the Manila Galleon trade that enriched the city for two centuries. The [Zócalo](/northamerica/mexico/acapulco/thezocalo) is the social heart of the old town, especially in the evenings.

West of the city, [Coyuca Lagoon](/northamerica/mexico/acapulco/coyuca_lagoon) is an 18-kilometre expanse of water important for migratory birds, reachable by boat from Pie de la Cuesta. [Isla La Roqueta](/northamerica/mexico/acapulco/isla_la_roqueta) is a short glass-bottom boat ride from Playa Caleta, with snorkelling and an underwater Virgin of Guadalupe.

For views over the whole bay, the [Capilla de la Paz](/northamerica/mexico/acapulco/capilla_de_la_paz) on its hilltop gives the best panorama in the city. The 40-metre cross beside it is visible from ships entering the harbour.
