Centro Deportivo Ejidal Ojocaliente is a practical place to try the hot spring water that gave Aguascalientes its name. The complex has thermal pools, steam rooms, saunas, and a few sports facilities, so it works best as a relaxed local bath stop rather than a polished spa.

It is on the east side of the city and is most useful if you want a low-key soak with local families.
