Aguascalientes' Contemporary Art Museum is a small central art stop with an exterior that is almost as interesting as the exhibitions inside. The facade is broken up by inset views and angles that make the building feel like part of the display.

It pairs well with the nearby National Museum of Death and other museums around the historic centre.
