Museo Espacio is a contemporary art museum in a former railway workshop. The industrial setting is part of the draw: the conversion keeps traces of the old locomotive repair complex, including sections where glass floors reveal the original tracks below.

Come for large-scale exhibitions and for the architecture itself, which gives Aguascalientes a very different museum experience from its historic centre collections.
