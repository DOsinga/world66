The Museum of Aguascalientes is an art museum in a pink-quarry building, with a strong focus on 20th-century Mexican work. It is especially useful for seeing pieces by Saturnino Herran, the Aguascalientes-born painter whose portraits helped shape a modern Mexican visual style.
