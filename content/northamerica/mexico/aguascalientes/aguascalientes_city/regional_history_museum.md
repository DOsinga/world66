The Regional History Museum Aguascalientes gives useful background on the area's precolonial and colonial history. It is a good early stop in the city, especially before visiting churches, plazas, and older neighbourhoods whose stories are easier to read with some context.

Expect a compact museum rather than a blockbuster one. Its value is in tying together the people and periods that shaped Aguascalientes.
