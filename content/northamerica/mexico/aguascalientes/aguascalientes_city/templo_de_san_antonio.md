Templo de San Antonio is the architectural show-off of Aguascalientes. Its warm stone, central dome, corner towers, and mix of styles make it more memorable than the city's larger cathedral.

The church sits a short walk from the core historic centre. Go for the exterior first, then step inside if it is open; the building is best appreciated slowly, from several angles.
