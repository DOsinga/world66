The Parroquia del Senor del Salitre anchors Calvillo's centre. Its scale is the first thing you notice from the plaza, and its towers give the town its clearest skyline.

Step inside if the doors are open, then circle the square for sweets shops and small-town street life. This is the natural starting point for a Calvillo visit.
