The Santuario de Guadalupe sits above the flatter town centre and gives Calvillo a second church stop beyond the main parish. The appeal is partly the building and partly the view back across town.

Go in the cooler part of the day if you are walking. The short climb helps you understand how Calvillo sits between orchards, hills, and the road back toward Aguascalientes.
