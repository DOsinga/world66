The Discover Interactive Museum of Science and Technology is Aguascalientes' hands-on science museum, aimed mostly at families and school-age visitors. Exhibits cover science and technology through interactive displays, with outdoor pieces that include dinosaurs, aircraft, and Day of the Dead figures.

It is strongest as a family stop or rainy-day option rather than a formal museum visit. The IMAX theatre adds another reason to check the current programme before going.
