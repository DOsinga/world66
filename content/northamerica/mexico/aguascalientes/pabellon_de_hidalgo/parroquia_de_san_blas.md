The Parroquia de San Blas is the town's main church and part of the old hacienda landscape. Its baroque tower, buttressed side walls, and period religious images make it the most visible landmark around the central plaza.

It is not a major church by Mexican standards, but it matters here because the town's identity is so tightly tied to San Blas and the independence-era hacienda.
