The Plaza Principal is Pabellón de Hidalgo's front room: a tidy garden of palms, paths, benches, and slow local movement beside the parish church. It is the place to pause between the museum and the surrounding hacienda fragments.

Come on a Sunday if you want the plaza at its most lived-in, with families and vendors giving the small town centre some weight.
