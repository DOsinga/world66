Presa San Blas is the easiest landscape break from the town centre. The reservoir sits west of Pabellón de Hidalgo, with old dam and aqueduct remains nearby and enough open space for walking, fishing, picnicking, or a simple look back toward town.

It is modest, but useful: after the tight historical core, the water and dry hills make the place feel less like a single-museum stop.
