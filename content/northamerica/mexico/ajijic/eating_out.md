---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ajijic's restaurant scene reflects its mixed Mexican-expat character: good traditional Mexican food alongside American breakfasts, Italian restaurants, and international cooking aimed at retiree palates. Prices are higher than in most Mexican towns of this size but still very affordable by North American standards. The best Mexican cooking is at the simple family-run places on the side streets rather than the restaurant-row establishments facing the lake.
