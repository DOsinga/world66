Akumal Bay is a wide, sandy-bottomed half-moon protected by a long reef that breaks most of the Caribbean swell. The water is shallow — much of it waist-deep — and the snorkelling starts a few metres from the beach. Sea turtles, rays, parrotfish, and the occasional small barracuda all show up regularly.

Since 2017, swimming with the turtles requires a permit, a guide, and a strict no-touching rule — buy the wristband from the village information point or one of the dive shops. The rest of the bay, beyond the marked turtle zone, is free to enter and still has excellent snorkelling along the reef. Bring reef-safe sunscreen; chemical-based blockers are banned.
