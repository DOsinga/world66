Akumal sits in the middle of the Riviera Maya, with major sights twenty to forty minutes away in both directions. The ruined Mayan cliff city of [Tulum](/northamerica/mexico/tulum) is the closest day trip. [Playa del Carmen](/northamerica/mexico/playadelcarmen) is the nearest proper town, good for shopping and nightlife. Inland, the jungle ruins at Cobá — about an hour's drive — are larger and more atmospheric than Tulum, and you can still climb to the top of the main pyramid for a view over the canopy.
