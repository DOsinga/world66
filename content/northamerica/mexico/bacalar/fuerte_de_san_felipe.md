The Fuerte de San Felipe is Bacalar's main historical sight, a compact stone fort overlooking the lagoon from the centre of town. Its walls, cannons, and small museum explain why this peaceful-looking waterway once mattered strategically.

The fort is also one of the best viewpoints in Bacalar. Go late in the afternoon for softer light across the lagoon, then walk to the plaza for food or a drink.
