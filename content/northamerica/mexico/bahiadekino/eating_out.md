---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bahia de Kino is not a restaurant town — options are limited and modest. The best eating is fresh seafood, either at small taco stands in Kino Viejo or the handful of simple restaurants along the beach road in Kino Nuevo. Shrimp from the Sea of Cortez is outstanding: grilled, in tacos, or as cocktail (ceviche served in a tall glass). Budget on paying very little and getting very good fish if you're willing to eat where the locals eat.
