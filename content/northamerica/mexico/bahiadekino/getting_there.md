---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Coastal Bus (Transportes del Pacifico) runs several times daily from [Hermosillo](/northamerica/mexico/hermosillo) to Bahia de Kino, a journey of roughly two hours on a mostly two-lane highway through the Sonoran Desert. Most visitors arrive by car. There is no commercial airport; the nearest is Hermosillo International, which has connections to Phoenix and several Mexican cities.
