Centro Estatal de las Artes is Mexicali's state arts center, with changing exhibitions, concerts, recitals, and other cultural events. It is most useful for visitors who want to see what the city's current arts calendar looks like.

Check the program before going, since the value of the stop depends on what is on. When there is an exhibition or performance, it pairs well with Mexicali's nearby museums and civic sights.
