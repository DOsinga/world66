---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Tijuana's food scene punches well above its border-town reputation. This is where the Caesar salad was born, and where Baja Med cuisine — a fusion of Mexican, Mediterranean, and Asian influences — was invented. The city's proximity to Baja California wine country and Pacific seafood means exceptional ingredients. From market tacos to sit-down fine dining, the range is wide and the quality is consistently high.
