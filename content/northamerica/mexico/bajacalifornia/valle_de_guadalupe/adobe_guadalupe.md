Adobe Guadalupe is one of the valley's better-known estate wineries, with a hacienda-style setting that makes a tasting feel like part of the landscape rather than a transaction. It works well for travelers who want a polished but still recognisably Baja stop.

Reservations are wise, especially on weekends and during harvest season.
