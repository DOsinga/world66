Bodegas de Santo Tomas is one of Baja California's oldest wine names, with a tasting room at San Antonio de las Minas near the Valle de Guadalupe wine route. The cellar pours a broad range of reds and whites, including Chardonnay and other regional varietals.

It is a practical stop for travellers using the valley as a wine destination rather than a single scenic attraction. Come for a tasting, then combine it with nearby wineries or the Wine Museum.
