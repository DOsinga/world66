Bodegas Magoni is one of Valle de Guadalupe's established tasting stops, run by Camillo Magoni, a long-time winemaker who helped shape Baja's modern wine scene before opening his own cellar. The vineyard grows an unusually wide range of grapes, so tastings can feel more experimental than at the valley's showier estates.

It is a good stop if you want context for the region rather than just a polished terrace. Book ahead when the valley is busy, especially on weekends.
