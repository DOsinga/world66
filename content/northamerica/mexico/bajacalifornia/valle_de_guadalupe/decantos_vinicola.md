Decantos Vinicola is a contemporary winery in Valle de Guadalupe, known for its modern architecture, cellar, art-filled spaces, and polished tasting experience. It is one of the valley's more visually striking wineries.

The winery produces Chardonnay, Tempranillo, Nebbiolo, and blends, and is a good fit for visitors who want a design-forward stop as part of a Valle de Guadalupe tasting route.
