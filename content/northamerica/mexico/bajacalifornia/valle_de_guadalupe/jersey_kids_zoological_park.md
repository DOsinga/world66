Jersey Kids Zoological Park is a family-focused stop in Valle de Guadalupe, with animals such as camels, tigers, and flamingoes alongside pools, a splash pad, picnic areas, and play space. It is not the valley's wine-country image, but it can be useful if you are travelling with children and need a break from tasting rooms.

Plan it as a half-day family outing rather than a quick roadside look.
