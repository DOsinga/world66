Founded in 1988 and generally credited with starting serious Mexican wine — before Monte Xanic the valley made bulk wine and brandy, and after it the boutique producers followed.

The tasting runs all week for between two and ten people and includes a walk through the winery and a cheese board. Book ahead; this is not a drop-in.

Their office number is a Mexican freephone line; the WhatsApp number reaches the Mexico City office.
