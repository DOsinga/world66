Rondo del Valle is a modern Valle de Guadalupe winery with a tasting room wrapped in stone, glass, and steel. During harvest, the production line becomes part of the visit: grapes move through the de-stemming equipment while tastings continue nearby.

The winery is best for travelers who like seeing how the valley's newer, more design-minded estates work, not just sampling the finished bottles.
