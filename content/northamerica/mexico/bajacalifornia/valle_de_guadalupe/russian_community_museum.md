This small community museum tells the story of the Russian Molokan settlers who arrived in the Guadalupe Valley in the early 1900s. They came looking for religious freedom, farmed wheat, vegetables, and grapes, and left a thread of history that still surfaces in family names and old ranches.

It is not a big museum, but it gives the valley a human story beyond tastings and restaurants.
