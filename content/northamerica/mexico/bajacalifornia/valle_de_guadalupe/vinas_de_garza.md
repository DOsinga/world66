Viñas de Garza is a family-run winery in Valle de Guadalupe, known for its hilltop tasting area, landscaped grounds, and owner-led tastings. The setting is open and breezy, with views over the valley and a focus on the wines rather than a restaurant scene.

Tastings usually cover a range of Baja reds and whites, including blends based on grapes such as Grenache, Tempranillo, Syrah, and Mourvèdre.
