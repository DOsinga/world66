A dive shop in the village holding a national park authorisation, which every boat going into the reserve is required to have.

The sites are a short ride out: El Bajo, Los Morros, El Islote, and the pinnacles where the bigeye jacks school. Snorkelling trips go to the shallower fingers of reef and to the sea lion colony at Los Frailes, the headland at the south end of the park.
