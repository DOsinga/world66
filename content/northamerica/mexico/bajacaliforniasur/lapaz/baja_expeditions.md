One of the oldest outfitters in Baja, working out of El Manglito in La Paz and running the trips that need a boat for several days rather than an afternoon.

That means **liveaboards to Socorro** in the Revillagigedo archipelago for the giant oceanic mantas, multi-day sailings around Espíritu Santo, the **grey whale camps** on the Pacific side in winter, and ocean safaris through the Midriff Islands. They also sell day trips in the Bay of La Paz for whale sharks and sea lions.

A Canadian line, **+1 604 241 1918**, and a US freephone number reach the same office.
