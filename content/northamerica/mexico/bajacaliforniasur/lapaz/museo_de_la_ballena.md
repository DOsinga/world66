The Museo de la Ballena focuses on the whales and marine mammals of Baja California and the Gulf of California. For travellers planning whale watching, whale-shark outings, or island trips, it gives useful biological context.

It is especially worthwhile with children or on a hot afternoon when the waterfront glare becomes too much. Exhibits can vary, so check opening details locally.
