Balandra is the beach people picture when they decide to come to La Paz: pale sand, shallow turquoise water, mangroves, and desert hills folding around a protected bay. At low tide you can wade far out without the water reaching your waist.

Access is managed and can be capacity-limited, so check current rules before going. Bring water, shade, and patience; the beauty is real, but so is the pressure on the place.
