A small operator working off the malecón with a cap of ten guests per boat and marine biologists rather than boatmen as guides.

The Bay of La Paz is the reason. **Whale sharks** feed in the shallow bay between roughly October and April, close enough in that the trip is a short ride. **Mobula rays** mass in the thousands in spring and sometimes leap clear of the water. **Orcas** pass through the Cerralvo channel. **Sea lions** haul out year-round at Los Islotes off Espíritu Santo. None of it needs a certification — it is all snorkelling.

Whale shark trips are permit-controlled in a protected zone: a fixed number of boats, a swim time limit, no touching, no fins near the animal. Operators who go outside the zone to beat the queue are breaking the rules, not finding a secret spot.
