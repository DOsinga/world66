In the centre of Loreto, two streets from the mission, covering the Bay of Loreto national park — five islands, a marine protected area, and some of the quietest water in the Sea of Cortez.

They run boat trips to **Coronado and Danzante**, kayaking and paddleboarding along the island shores, diving, and in winter the long day across the peninsula to **Magdalena Bay** for grey whales, which calve there between January and March and are famous for approaching boats to be touched.
