The Malecon de Loreto is the town's easiest pleasure: a waterfront walk with fishing boats, benches, statues, and open views across the Sea of Cortez. It is best early in the morning or at sunset, when the heat softens and the islands stand out across the water.

It is not a flashy boardwalk. That is its charm. Pair it with the mission plaza and dinner in the old centre.
