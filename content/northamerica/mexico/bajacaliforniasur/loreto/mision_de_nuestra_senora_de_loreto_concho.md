Mision de Nuestra Senora de Loreto Concho is the historic core of Loreto. Founded by Jesuits in 1697, it became the starting point for the mission chain that eventually stretched north through Baja California and into Alta California.

The church is not large, but its position on the plaza gives it weight. Visit with the nearby mission museum if you want the broader story of Jesuit movement, conflict, and survival in the desert.
