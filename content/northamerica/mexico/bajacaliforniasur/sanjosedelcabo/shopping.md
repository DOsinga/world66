---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The art galleries on Calle Álvaro Obregón are the most interesting retail in San José, particularly on Thursday evenings when the weekly art walk takes place. The galleries represent local and national Mexican artists, with prices varying from affordable prints to serious canvases. The weekend organic market on Calle Coronado (Saturdays, morning only) is the best food shopping in the Los Cabos area — local mezcal, Baja wines, cheese, and fresh produce from farms in the sierra.

For handicrafts, the stalls around Plaza Mijares sell typical Mexican tourist goods, but the better specialist shops on Boulevard Mijares have higher quality Talavera ceramics, silver jewellery, and handwoven textiles. The Las Tiendas de Palmilla shopping centre on the tourist corridor is upscale and air-conditioned; it handles jewellery and designer clothing for the resort crowd.
