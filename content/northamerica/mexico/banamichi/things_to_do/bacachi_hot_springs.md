Natural warm springs in the hills north of Banamichi, reached by a short drive on a dirt road. The water emerges at a comfortable temperature year-round and pools into bathing areas that are popular with locals on weekends and largely empty on weekdays. Bring everything you need — there are no facilities — and combine the trip with a look at the surrounding scrub landscape, which is good for birdwatching in the early morning.
