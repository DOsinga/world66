Barra de Navidad is a good place to eat well cheaply. The seafood is genuinely fresh — most of what is on the menu came out of the lagoon or the bay that morning. The town has a small but solid cluster of restaurants ranging from beach-sand tables to sit-down seafood spots, and prices are low by any standard.
