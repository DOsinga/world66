The church anchors Bernal's main square and gives the village its classic postcard view: pale stone, low arcades, and the Peña rising behind everything. It was built in the colonial period and is still the natural starting point for a slow walk through the centre.

The interior is modest, but the setting is lovely. Pause here before heading into the lanes that climb toward the rock.
