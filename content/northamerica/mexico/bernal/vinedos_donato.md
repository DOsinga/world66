Viñedos Donato is a vineyard near Bernal with tastings, red wines built around grapes such as Merlot, Syrah, Nebbiolo, and Cabernet Sauvignon, and a restaurant overlooking the grounds. It is one of the easier wine-country stops to fold into a visit to Peña de Bernal.
