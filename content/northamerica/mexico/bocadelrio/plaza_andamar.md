An upscale outdoor shopping centre opened in 2005, with palm-shaded courtyards and a large central fountain. The mix is mostly Mexican boutiques and chain stores rather than international luxury, but the layout — an open garden-style mall rather than an enclosed box — makes it more pleasant to wander than the standard mall offerings nearby.

There are several decent restaurants here for an evening when you don't feel like the malecón crowd, including a long-standing branch of La Mansión for steaks. Cinema at the back of the complex.
