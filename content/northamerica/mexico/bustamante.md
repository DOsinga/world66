Bustamante is a small Pueblo Magico north of Monterrey, tucked against pale limestone hills in a dry corner of Nuevo Leon. It is not polished, and that is part of the point: the town works best if you want caves, cold spring water, a quiet plaza, and a slower weekend than the city gives you.

The headline sight is [Grutas de Bustamante](/northamerica/mexico/bustamante/grutas_de_bustamante), a cave system in the mountains just outside town. Back in the centre, [Parroquia de San Miguel Arcangel](/northamerica/mexico/bustamante/parroquia_de_san_miguel_arcangel) faces the plaza with the plain, sturdy confidence of a northern Mexican mission town, while the [Museo de las Cosas Simples](/northamerica/mexico/bustamante/museo_de_las_cosas_simples) keeps the local memory small-scale and personal.

The other reason to come is water. In a dry landscape, [Parque El Molino](/northamerica/mexico/bustamante/parque_el_molino) and the Ojo de Agua area feel almost extravagant: pools, shade, families, and spring-fed relief from the heat. Bustamante is a good day or overnight trip from Monterrey if you have a car and a taste for places that still feel local.
