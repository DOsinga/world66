---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Cabo's nightlife is heavily oriented toward the American spring-break crowd and runs on tequila shots, loud music, and themed bars along the waterfront. Cabo Wabo, the bar and restaurant founded by Sammy Hagar of Van Halen fame, is the most famous name in town — a rock-and-roll institution with its own tequila brand and a clientele that skews older than the strip bars nearby. For something quieter, the restaurants along the Marina Boulevard have outdoor seating with views of the boats and a more relaxed pace in the evenings.
