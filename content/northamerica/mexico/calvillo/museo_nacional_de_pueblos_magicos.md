The Museo Nacional de Pueblos Magicos is a useful stop if Calvillo is part of a wider trip through Mexico's smaller heritage towns. It presents the Pueblo Magico idea through displays linked to different regions and traditions.

It is not a large museum, but it helps explain why Calvillo markets itself through craft, food, churches, and local identity rather than one blockbuster monument.
