Running Calakmul as a guided day with transport, based in Mérida rather than in Campeche, which suits travellers already on the Yucatán side.

It is a long day from anywhere — the site is sixty kilometres down a spur road off a highway that is itself hours from the nearest city — so a trip that includes the driving is a reasonable way to see it without hiring a car for the purpose.
