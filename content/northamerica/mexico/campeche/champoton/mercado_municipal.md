Mercado Municipal is where Champotón feels most like a working town. Stalls sell fruit, vegetables, meat, and local seafood, and the morning food stands are the useful part for travellers.

Go early if you want the market at its liveliest. Look for simple Campeche dishes and seafood snacks rather than a polished food-hall experience.
