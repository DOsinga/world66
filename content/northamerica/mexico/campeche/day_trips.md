**Edzná** (40 km southeast) is the most accessible Maya site from Campeche — a compact, well-excavated complex dominated by the Temple of Five Stories, an unusual pyramid with galleries on each level. Colectivo taxis and organised tours run from Campeche in the morning; allow half a day.

**Calakmul** (370 km southeast, near the Guatemalan border) is one of the great Maya cities — massive in scale, deep in the jungle, and dramatically under-visited given its importance. The site rivals Tikal in size and was Tikal's greatest rival in the Classic period. The journey requires an early start and ideally an overnight in the biosphere reserve. Tours can be arranged from Campeche or from the small town of Xpujil nearby. The Fuerte de San Miguel museum in Campeche displays jade masks recovered from the royal tombs here.
