---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The seafood in Campeche is among the best on the Gulf Coast. Pan de cazón — layers of tortillas with dogfish (a small shark), black beans, and tomato sauce — is the local dish to try; it sounds unlikely and tastes excellent. Coconut shrimp, crab, and various preparations of regional fish are equally good. The market on Calle 10, a few blocks from the Plaza Principal, is the best place for cheap, local lunches.

The restaurants around the Plaza Principal cater to tourists but maintain reasonable quality. More interesting options are found on the side streets inside the walled centre.
