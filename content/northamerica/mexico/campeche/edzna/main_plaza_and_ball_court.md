The main plaza gives Edzna its shape. Long platforms edge the open space, and the ball court sits close by, a reminder that Maya public life mixed sport, ritual, politics, and spectacle in ways that are hard to separate now.

Spend a few minutes here after climbing the acropolis. The plaza is where the site feels least like a collection of buildings and most like a city.
