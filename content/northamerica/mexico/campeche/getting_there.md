---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Campeche International Airport has flights to Mexico City and a few other domestic destinations. Most travellers arrive by bus: ADO operates first-class services from Mérida (2.5 hours), Mexico City (18 hours), and Villahermosa (4 hours), terminating at the modern bus station about 2 km from the historic centre.

By road, Campeche is 200 km southwest of Mérida on Highway 180 (the coastal route) or Highway 261 (faster, via the interior). If arriving from the Yucatán, the drive along the coast through Champotón offers views of the Gulf of Mexico.
