---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Campeche's historic centre is compact and walkable — the restored colonial core is enclosed within hexagonal ramparts that you can walk in an hour. The pastel-painted streets, the cathedral on the main plaza, and the well-maintained bastions and forts give the city a coherence that most Mexican colonial centres have lost. Start at the Plaza Principal and work outward.

The real prize is the Fuerte de San Miguel, a hilltop fort south of the city that houses one of the finest pre-Columbian collections in Mexico — including six jade funeral masks from the royal tombs of Calakmul. The combination of well-preserved military architecture and world-class archaeological objects in a single visit is hard to beat anywhere in Mexico.
