Mexico's largest Caribbean island, 75 km south of Cancun (90 minutes by car to Playa del Carmen, then a 45-minute ferry). Cozumel sits along the second-longest barrier reef in the world, and is regularly ranked among the top three dive destinations on Earth — Palancar, Santa Rosa Wall, and Columbia are the headline sites. Snorkellers do well at the same reefs in shallow versions.

The island's only town, San Miguel, has a small Mayan museum, a cluster of waterfront restaurants, and a busy cruise-ship terminal. The eastern (windward) side is undeveloped — mostly empty beach with a handful of palapa restaurants and the Punta Sur lighthouse. The Mayan pilgrimage site of San Gervasio, in the centre of the island, was where pre-Columbian women travelled to consult the goddess Ixchel. A full day from Cancun is feasible but Cozumel rewards an overnight stay.
