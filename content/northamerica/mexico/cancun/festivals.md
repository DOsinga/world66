---
title: "Festivals"
type: section
---

The biggest annual event is the **Spring Equinox at Chichén Itzá** (around 21 March), when the late-afternoon sun casts a serpent-shaped shadow down the steps of the Kukulkán pyramid — book your day trip well in advance. The **Autumn Equinox** in September produces the same effect for smaller crowds. **Day of the Dead** (1-2 November) is observed across the Yucatán with altars and processions; the Xcaret eco-park stages a particularly elaborate Mayan-influenced version. **Hanal Pixán**, the Mayan Day of the Dead celebrated in late October in Mérida and across the peninsula, predates the Catholic version by centuries. Cancun itself hosts the **Cancun International Film Festival** in November and an annual **Riviera Maya Jazz Festival** in late November-December in Playa del Carmen.
