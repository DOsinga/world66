---
title: "Nightlife and Entertainment"
type: section
---

Cancun's club scene concentrates at Punta Cancun in the Hotel Zone, where Coco Bongo, Mandala, The City, and Palazzo cluster within a few hundred metres. Most operate on an open-bar admission system — pay around US$70-90 at the door, drink as much as you want until closing. Coco Bongo is the spectacle option, with acrobatic performers, themed shows, and tribute acts to Michael Jackson, Beetlejuice, and Queen. Downtown (Cancun centro) has cheaper, more locally oriented bars and live music venues along Avenida Yaxchilán and around Parque Las Palapas.
