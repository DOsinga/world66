---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The Hotel Zone has the polished malls: **La Isla** (open-air, with a small aquarium, on a man-made canal), **Kukulcán Plaza** (the upscale option with luxury brands), and **Plaza Flamingo** (more affordable). Downtown is where locals shop — **Plaza Las Américas** is the giant mall, and the **Mercado 28** is the classic Mexican tourist market, with hundreds of small stalls selling silver, hammocks, leather, ceramics, and souvenirs. Haggling is expected at Mercado 28; fixed prices everywhere else. The artisan market **Coral Negro** in the Hotel Zone is more tourist-priced.
