title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Hotel Zone packs beaches, water sports, and a handful of legitimate sights into one 22-kilometre barrier island. The headline non-beach attractions are the underwater sculpture park [MUSA](/northamerica/mexico/cancun/musa), the two in-zone Mayan sites at [El Rey](/northamerica/mexico/cancun/el_rey) and [El Meco](/northamerica/mexico/cancun/el_meco), and the [Mayan Museum](/northamerica/mexico/cancun/archaeological_museum) which combines a strong indoor collection with the open-air San Miguelito ruins. Downtown adds the workaday markets and parks of a real Mexican city. Most visitors quickly default to the bigger draws beyond the city: see the [day trips](/northamerica/mexico/cancun/day_trips) section.
