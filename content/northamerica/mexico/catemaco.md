Catemaco sits on a wide volcanic lake in southern Veracruz, in the wet green country of Los Tuxtlas. It is a little scruffy around the edges, but the setting is excellent: rainforest hills behind town, fishing boats on the lake, and Gulf coast lagoons and mangroves within day-trip range.

The town is best known in Mexico for brujeria, or witchcraft. That reputation can feel theatrical, especially around the annual March gathering, but it gives Catemaco a sharper identity than most lakeside towns. The more reliable pleasure is getting onto the water, where boatmen head for small islands, birdlife, and forested shorelines.

Catemaco works as a base for [Los Tuxtlas](/northamerica/mexico/san_andres_tuxtla) if you want nature without staying in a resort. [Lake Catemaco](/northamerica/mexico/catemaco/lake_catemaco) and [Nanciyaga Ecological Reserve](/northamerica/mexico/catemaco/nanciyaga_ecological_reserve) are the headline anchors, while [Laguna de Sontecomapan](/northamerica/mexico/catemaco/laguna_de_sontecomapan) adds mangroves and quiet coastal water. [Salto de Eyipantla](/northamerica/mexico/san_andres_tuxtla/salto_de_eyipantla), closer to San Andrés Tuxtla, is an easy add-on if you have a car or driver.
