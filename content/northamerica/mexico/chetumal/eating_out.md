---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Chetumal sits at the crossroads of Yucatecan, Belizean and Caribbean kitchens, and the food reflects it. The seafood is excellent — *pescadillas* (fried fish tacos) and *ceviche de caracol* (conch ceviche) are the local specialities. Yucatecan staples turn up everywhere: *cochinita pibil*, *panuchos*, *sopa de lima*. From across the Belize border come influences like *rice and beans with coconut milk* and Caribbean-style stewed chicken.

The [Mercado Lázaro Cárdenas](/northamerica/mexico/chetumal/mercado_lazaro_cardenas) has the best cheap fondas in town. For sit-down meals, the small restaurants along Boulevard Bahía near the Maqueta serve grilled fish at sunset, and the streets just south of the Museo de la Cultura Maya have a cluster of Yucatecan cocinas económicas. Avoid the over-priced chains around the bus station.
