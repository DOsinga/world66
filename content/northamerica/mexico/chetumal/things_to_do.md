---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Chetumal's standout sight is the [Museo de la Cultura Maya](/northamerica/mexico/chetumal/museo_de_la_cultura_maya), worth a half-day on its own. Add a slow walk along the [Boulevard Bahía](/northamerica/mexico/chetumal/boulevard_bahia) at sunset, a peek at the [Maqueta Payo Obispo](/northamerica/mexico/chetumal/maqueta_payo_obispo) and a look at the [murals inside the state Congress](/northamerica/mexico/chetumal/congress_murals).

For a quieter morning, the [Museo de la Ciudad](/northamerica/mexico/chetumal/museo_de_la_ciudad) fills in the city's own history. Trips out onto the [Bahía de Chetumal](/northamerica/mexico/chetumal/bay_swimming) from the suburb of Calderitas offer a chance to look for manatees — one of the few places in Mexico where they still feed in their natural habitat.
