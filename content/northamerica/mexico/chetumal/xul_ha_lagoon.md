Xul-Ha is the low-key freshwater stop on the road between Chetumal and Bacalar. The village sits on clear, pale-blue water that feels more like a natural swimming pool than a conventional lake, with small balnearios, kayak rentals, and simple waterfront restaurants rather than big resort infrastructure. It is a useful escape if Chetumal feels too hot and flat by midday.

Come for a few hours, not for a checklist of sights. The appeal is the water itself: shallow edges, deeper blue channels, and enough space to float or paddle without the busier scene around Bacalar's main lagoon. Weekdays are quiet; weekends bring local families, music, and a more festive atmosphere.
