Catazajá has added a zipline over part of the lagoon, giving the town a more active option alongside boat trips and fishing. Treat it as a bonus rather than the main reason to come: operating hours and availability can change, and the wildlife and water are still the better draw.

If it is running, the ride gives a quick high view over the lagoon and waterfront. Check locally before making plans around it.
