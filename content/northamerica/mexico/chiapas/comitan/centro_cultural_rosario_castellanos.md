This cultural centre keeps Rosario Castellanos close to the town that formed much of her imagination. Exhibitions and events change, but the name matters: Castellanos wrote with unusual force about gender, class, and indigenous life in Chiapas.

Check the current programme before going. Even when there is no major exhibition, it is worth noting as part of Comitán's identity as a town of writers and political memory.
