Museo de Arte Hermila Dominguez shows modern and contemporary art in central Comitan. The collection includes work linked to major Mexican artists such as Rufino Tamayo, Francisco Toledo, Jose Luis Cuevas, Gunther Gerzso, and Jose Guadalupe Posada, alongside temporary exhibitions.
