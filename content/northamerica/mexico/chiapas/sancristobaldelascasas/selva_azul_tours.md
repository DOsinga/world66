On Real de Guadalupe, the pedestrian street that is where most of San Cristóbal's tourism is bought and sold, open from half past seven in the morning until half past nine at night.

They cover the standard circuit — Sumidero, Chamula and Zinacantán, Palenque with Agua Azul and Misol-Ha, Montebello, Yaxchilán and Bonampak — and multi-day combinations through Chiapas and into Tabasco.
