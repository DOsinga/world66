Parque Miguel Hidalgo is the city's front room. The plaza is shaded, busy, and ringed by the old and new municipal buildings, the cultural centre, and the open-air theatre where marimba music sometimes drifts across the square.

It will not change your life, but it helps Tapachula make sense. Sit here in the cooler part of the day and the city feels less like a bus connection and more like the commercial capital of a border region.
