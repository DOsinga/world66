The Temple of Kukulcan, better known to many visitors as El Castillo, is the image most people carry in their heads before arriving at Chichen Itza. The stepped pyramid dominates the main plaza and expresses the site's mix of Maya and central Mexican influences with extraordinary clarity.

You can no longer climb it, which is a good thing for both preservation and sanity. Walk the plaza instead, look at how the pyramid commands the surrounding buildings, and arrive early if you want even a short spell before the tour groups fill the open ground.
