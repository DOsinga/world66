An agency in Chihuahua city putting together Sierra Tarahumara trips as complete packages — the **Chepe** ticket, the transfers, the nights in Creel or Divisadero and the days in the canyon, which is a reasonable way to do it if you do not want to assemble a train journey across the Sierra Madre yourself.

The office works Monday to Friday, nine until one.
