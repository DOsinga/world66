A local operator covering the whole Sierra Tarahumara circuit: day trips around Creel, Divisadero and the canyon rim, the long descent to **Batopilas**, Cerocahui, the Basaseachi waterfall, and transfers to and from the Chepe at Creel, Divisadero, El Fuerte and Los Mochis.

Being based in Creel rather than in Chihuahua city matters for the longer trips — the roads here are slow and the weather changes, and a company whose vehicles are already in the sierra can rearrange a day that a company sending one up from the city cannot.

No street address is published, so this is pinned on the town.
