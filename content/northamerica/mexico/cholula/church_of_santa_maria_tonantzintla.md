Santa María Tonantzintla, south of Cholula, has one of the most astonishing church interiors in central Mexico. The outside is relatively restrained; inside, plasterwork, colour, saints, angels, fruits, and Indigenous motifs crowd almost every visible surface.

It is a short taxi ride from Cholula and pairs well with San Francisco Acatepec if you have extra time. Photography rules can be strict, so go to look rather than to collect images.
