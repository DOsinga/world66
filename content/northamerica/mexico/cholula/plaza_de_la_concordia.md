Plaza de la Concordia is the civic heart of San Pedro Cholula, a wide square framed by arcades, churches, municipal buildings, and everyday foot traffic. It is a good place to pause between the convent and the pyramid.

Come in the evening if you can. Families stroll, food vendors appear, and the city feels less like a day-trip stop and more like a lived-in Puebla neighbour.
