The Regional Museum of Cholula sits near the pyramid complex and fills in context that the ruins alone cannot provide. Exhibits cover pre-Hispanic Cholula, colonial history, local crafts, and the wider Puebla region.

It is a sensible stop before or after the pyramid, especially if you want more than the standard hill-and-church photograph. Allow an hour rather than treating it as a quick corridor.
