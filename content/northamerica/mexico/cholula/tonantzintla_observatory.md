Tonantzintla Observatory is a specialist stop south of Cholula, connected with Mexico's astronomical research history and the scientific institutions in the area. It is not a casual museum-style attraction in the way the pyramid is.

It is worth noting if you are interested in science history or already visiting Santa María Tonantzintla nearby. Check access before making a dedicated trip.
