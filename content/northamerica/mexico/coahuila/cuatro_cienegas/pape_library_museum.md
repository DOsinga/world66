Pape Library Museum is a modern museum-library with art galleries and an important bibliographic collection. Its permanent rooms and temporary exhibitions add a cultural stop to Cuatro Ciénegas, a town better known for desert pools and gypsum dunes.

It is a good indoor counterpoint to the natural sights around town. Stop in if you want local art, books, and a quieter look at regional cultural life before heading back into the desert landscape.
