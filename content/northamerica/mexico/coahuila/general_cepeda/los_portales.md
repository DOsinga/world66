Los Portales is the easiest place to turn a General Cepeda stop into a proper lunch. It sits by the central plaza in an old market building and serves regional dishes, sweets, and local products.

The food is part of the town's slow pace rather than a destination on its own. Use it as the natural pause between the church and the paleontological route.
