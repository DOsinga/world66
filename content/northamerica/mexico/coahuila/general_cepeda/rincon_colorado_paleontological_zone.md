Rincon Colorado is the main reason to make the trip to General Cepeda. Walkways cross a dry, rocky landscape where hadrosaur remains, fossil traces, and old shoreline sediments tell the story of a much wetter Cretaceous world.

It is modest as a visitor site, but the setting gives it force. You are not just looking at a display case; you are standing in the ground where the fossils were found.
