Jardín Escultórico Juan Soriano is a sculpture garden near Comala with modern works by Mexican and international artists. The garden is spread out enough to feel calm, with sculptures placed among lawns and trees rather than packed into a formal gallery setting.

It is a good quiet stop if you are combining Colima with Comala or the nearby archaeological sites. The appeal is the mix of public art, shade, and views toward the surrounding volcanic landscape.
