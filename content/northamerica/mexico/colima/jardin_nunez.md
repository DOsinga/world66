Jardín Núñez is one of central Colima's pleasant public squares, a shaded place to sit, watch the day move, and take the pulse of the city away from the more formal main plaza. Trees, benches, food vendors, and surrounding streets make it useful rather than spectacular.

Its value is local texture. Stop here while walking the historic centre, especially in the late afternoon when the heat eases and the square becomes more social. It is a good reminder that Colima's charm is often found in small, everyday spaces.
