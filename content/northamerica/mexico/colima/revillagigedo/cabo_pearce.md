A long finger of lava running out from the eastern side of Socorro island into deep water, with a cleaning station along it. Mantas work the ridge, and hammerheads school off the end in the blue.

What Cabo Pearce is known for is the **bottlenose dolphins**. A resident pod approaches divers of its own accord, circles, and in a well-attested pattern brings along juveniles — behaviour divers describe as being shown the young rather than merely encountering them.

Current along the ridge can be strong and the site is usually dived as a drift.
