A seamount off the northern tip of San Benedicto, rising to about twenty metres, and the most reliable place in the world to meet **giant oceanic mantas**. They come to be cleaned by clarion angelfish, and while they wait they approach divers — repeatedly, deliberately, and close enough that the usual advice is to keep still and let them pass rather than reach out.

The behaviour is well documented and not sentimentality: the animals appear to seek out exhaust bubbles, hovering over divers for whole dives. Individuals are recognisable by their markings and some have been recorded here for decades.

Hammerheads, silvertips and bottlenose dolphins pass the same pinnacle, and from January to March humpback song carries through the water.
