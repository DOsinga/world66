Roca Partida is the smallest of the Revillagigedo islands and barely an island at all — a white-streaked rock about a hundred metres long rising thirty metres out of three thousand metres of water, four hours by boat from San Benedicto.

Because it is the only structure for a very long way, everything gathers at it. Whitetip reef sharks stack into ledges on the walls, sometimes a dozen deep; silvertips and Galápagos sharks patrol the drop-offs; tuna and wahoo come through in numbers. Divers circle the rock in the current and wait, which is the entire technique.

The site is deep, exposed and frequently rough on the surface. Visibility is usually excellent and the current can be strong enough to make the circuit one-way.
