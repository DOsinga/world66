Comitán is a Chiapas highland city south of [San Cristóbal de las Casas](/northamerica/mexico/sancristobaldelascasas), close to the Guatemalan border and the lakes of Montebello. It has clean colonial streets, old churches, a strong local pride, and a literary memory tied to Rosario Castellanos, who spent her childhood here.

For travellers, Comitán works best as a quieter base in eastern Chiapas. The centre has enough architecture and street life for a slow afternoon, while [Tenam Puente](/northamerica/mexico/comitan/tenam_puente) gives the area a real archaeological anchor just outside town. The [Centro Cultural Rosario Castellanos](/northamerica/mexico/comitan/centro_cultural_rosario_castellanos) adds a more local kind of culture than the usual plaza-and-cathedral stop.

The city also sits within reach of [Lagunas de Montebello](/northamerica/mexico/comitan/lagunas_de_montebello), one of Chiapas's great natural sights. That does not make Comitán only a gateway; it is a good place to slow down between the more visited highlands and the wilder border country.
