The Archaeological Museum of Comitán covers the pre-Hispanic cultures of Los Altos de Chiapas and the wider Maya world. Its collection looks at trade routes, city-states, hunter-gatherer groups, and the archaeological sites that shaped the region.

It is a useful stop before or after visiting nearby ruins such as Tenam Puente. The museum also hosts temporary exhibitions, so the exact mix of objects can change.
