---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Cozumel has two seasons: a dry, breezy winter from November through April, and a wet, sticky summer from May through October. The dry season is when most people visit and prices peak around Christmas, New Year, and US spring break.

**November to April** brings warm sunny days (24–28°C), low humidity, and steady trade winds. Water temperatures hover around 25–26°C — cool enough for a 3mm wetsuit on long dives. This is the high season; book accommodation well in advance.

**May to October** is hotter (28–33°C), more humid, and prone to afternoon thunderstorms. Water temperatures climb to 28–29°C and visibility is often excellent. Hurricane season runs June through November, with the highest risk in September and October — Cozumel lies directly in the Caribbean hurricane belt and storms have closed the island for days at a time, as Wilma did in 2005.

**Festivals** worth timing a visit around: **Carnaval**, in the week before Ash Wednesday (usually February), is the island's biggest party, with parades through San Miguel and music in the main square. **El Cedral**, in late April or early May, is a five-day festival of bullfights, dances, and processions in the inland village of the same name — one of the oldest festivals in Mexico, dating from the 19th-century cholera epidemic.
