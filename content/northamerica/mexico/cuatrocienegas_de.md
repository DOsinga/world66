Cuatrocienegas sits in a desert valley in the state of Coahuila, surrounded by mountains and home to one of Mexico's most remarkable natural areas. The Cuatrocienegas Protected Area contains hundreds of spring-fed pools — pozas — with startlingly blue water in the middle of the Chihuahuan Desert. The pools support endemic species found nowhere else on earth, including unique turtles and stromatolites, living rock formations similar to some of the earliest life on the planet.

The town itself is small and quiet, the birthplace of Venustiano Carranza, one of the leaders of the Mexican Revolution — his [birthplace museum](/northamerica/mexico/cuatrocienegas_de/casa_carranza) is in the town centre. The main draw is the desert landscape: the [Poza Azul](/northamerica/mexico/cuatrocienegas_de/poza_azul) and the [Dunas de Yeso](/northamerica/mexico/cuatrocienegas_de/dunas_de_yeso) (gypsum dunes) are the most visited spots, both easily reached from town. Bring plenty of water — this is deep desert country, and summer temperatures are extreme.
