Museo Genesis is a small interactive science museum focused on earth science, climate change, and fragile environments. It fits Cuatro Cienegas well, because the surrounding wetlands and gypsum dunes are part of one of Mexico's most unusual natural landscapes.

Go before or after Poza Azul and the gypsum dunes if you want context for why this desert basin matters.
