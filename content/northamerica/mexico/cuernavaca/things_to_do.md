Cuernavaca packs an impressive range of sights into a compact centre. The colonial buildings around the zócalo — the [Palacio de Cortés](/northamerica/mexico/cuernavaca/palacio_de_cortes), the [Cathedral](/northamerica/mexico/cuernavaca/cuernavaca_cathedral), and the [Jardín Borda](/northamerica/mexico/cuernavaca/jardin_borda) — form the core. The [Museo Robert Brady](/northamerica/mexico/cuernavaca/museo_robert_brady) is the city's hidden gem, a personal art collection of extraordinary range. Beyond the centre, [Xochicalco](/northamerica/mexico/cuernavaca/xochicalco) — a UNESCO World Heritage hilltop fortress — is one of the finest pre-Columbian sites in central Mexico and easily reached on a half-day trip.
