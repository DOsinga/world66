DIFOCUR is one of Culiacan's main cultural complexes, with exhibition spaces, small museums, and a regular programme of events. It sits close to the Centro de Ciencias de Sinaloa, so the two can be combined easily.

The appeal depends on what is showing, but it is worth checking if you want a cultural stop beyond the city's cathedral, art museum, and botanical garden.
