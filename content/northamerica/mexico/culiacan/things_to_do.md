---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Culiacan is not a city overflowing with tourist sights, but what it has is worth your time. The [Museo de Arte de Sinaloa](/northamerica/mexico/culiacan/museo_de_arte_de_sinaloa) is genuinely impressive for a regional collection, the [Jardin Botanico](/northamerica/mexico/culiacan/jardin_botanico_de_culiacan) offers a green escape from the heat, and the historic center has enough character for a pleasant morning walk around the [cathedral](/northamerica/mexico/culiacan/catedral_de_culiacan) and [river promenade](/northamerica/mexico/culiacan/malacon_del_rio). If the [Tomateros](/northamerica/mexico/culiacan/estadio_tomateros) are playing during your visit, a baseball game is the most memorable evening in the city.
