---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Culiacan is from November to March, when the weather is dry, temperatures are mild (20–28°C during the day), and the Tomateros baseball season is in full swing. This is also harvest season in the Sinaloa valley, and the markets overflow with tomatoes, peppers, and tropical fruit.

Summer (June through September) is genuinely oppressive — temperatures regularly exceed 40°C and humidity from the rainy season makes the heat feel worse. Rainfall is concentrated in July and August, with afternoon thunderstorms common. If you must visit in summer, plan activities for early morning and late evening. The rainy season also brings green to the surrounding hills, which can otherwise look parched and brown.

December brings Christmas decorations and posadas to the center, and the malecon is particularly lively in the evenings around the holiday period.
