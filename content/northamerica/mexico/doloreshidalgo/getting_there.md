---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Dolores Hidalgo sits between [San Miguel de Allende](/northamerica/mexico/sanmigueldeallende) (about 40 minutes south) and [Guanajuato](/northamerica/mexico/guanajuato) (about an hour south-west) on a paved highway that is in good condition. The closest airports are Bajío International (BJX), about 90 minutes away, and Querétaro International (QRO), about two hours. Most visitors arrive on a day trip from San Miguel.

Frequent buses connect Dolores Hidalgo with San Miguel, Guanajuato, and León. The bus station is on the south-eastern edge of town, about a 15-minute walk from the Jardín or a short taxi ride. Driving in central Mexico has improved enormously over the last twenty years; the toll roads through Guanajuato state are safe and fast.
