The Parish Church of Our Lady of Sorrows dominates Dolores Hidalgo's main square and carries national weight far beyond its size. It is traditionally associated with Miguel Hidalgo's 1810 call to rebellion, the Grito de Dolores, which helped launch Mexico's War of Independence.

The facade mixes local stonework with the drama of a civic monument, and the plaza around it is the town's natural starting point. Visit early or late in the day, when the church and square feel less like a stop and more like the centre of the story.
