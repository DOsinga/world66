El Fuerte Train Station is plain but important: it is where many travelers join the Chepe railway toward the Copper Canyon. The station sits several kilometres south of town, with shaded waiting areas but limited services.

Treat it as logistics, not a sight. Arrange a taxi from town and check current train schedules before building a day around it.
