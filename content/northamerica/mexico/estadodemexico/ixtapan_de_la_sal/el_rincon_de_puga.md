El Rincón de Puga is a comfortable choice when you want a sit-down meal rather than market food. The draw is traditional Mexican cooking in a restored old building with an open courtyard.

It is especially useful at breakfast, when handmade tortillas and fresh juices make a better start than a rushed snack.
