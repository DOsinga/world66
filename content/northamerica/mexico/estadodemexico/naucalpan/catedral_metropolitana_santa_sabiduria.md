Catedral Metropolitana de la Santa Sabiduría de Dios is a Greek Orthodox church in Naucalpan. Inside, the icons, murals, and screens create a rich Byzantine atmosphere that feels quite different from the Catholic churches most visitors expect around Mexico City.

It is a worthwhile detour for travelers interested in religious art or the city's less obvious communities.
