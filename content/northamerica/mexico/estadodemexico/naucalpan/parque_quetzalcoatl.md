Parque Quetzalcóatl is a surreal sculpture park designed by architect Javier Senosiain in a natural ravine. Its winding paths, cave-like spaces, and brightly tiled forms riff on the feathered serpent of Aztec tradition. Visits are by advance reservation, usually as guided tours.
