Based in San Martín de las Pirámides, the village on the northern side of the site, with bilingual pilots holding commercial AFAC licences and flights every day of the year.

Weather cancels balloons more often than people expect — wind, low cloud, rain — and a company that flies anyway is one to avoid. Ask what happens if the flight is scrubbed before you pay for it.
