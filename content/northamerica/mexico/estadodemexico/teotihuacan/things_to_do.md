title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Teotihuacan is laid out along a single great axis, the [Avenue of the Dead](/northamerica/mexico/estadodemexico/teotihuacan/avenue_of_the_dead), which runs four kilometres from north to south. At the north end stands the [Pyramid of the Moon](/northamerica/mexico/estadodemexico/teotihuacan/pyramid_of_the_moon), facing the Avenue and the sacred mountain of Cerro Gordo behind. Halfway along, the [Pyramid of the Sun](/northamerica/mexico/estadodemexico/teotihuacan/pyramid_of_the_sun) — the largest in Mesoamerica — looms to the east. At the south end is the great walled enclosure of the Ciudadela, with the [Temple of the Feathered Serpent](/northamerica/mexico/estadodemexico/teotihuacan/temple_feathered_serpent) inside it.

The big pyramids are unmissable but the city's residential and palace compounds are where the murals and decoration survive. The [Palace of Quetzalpapálotl](/northamerica/mexico/estadodemexico/teotihuacan/quetzalpapalotl_palace) by the Pyramid of the Moon, the [Tepantitla compound](/northamerica/mexico/estadodemexico/teotihuacan/tepantitla) with its great paintings, and the apartment complexes of [Atetelco](/northamerica/mexico/estadodemexico/teotihuacan/atetelco) and [Tetitla](/northamerica/mexico/estadodemexico/teotihuacan/tetitla) reward an hour each. The site museum is small but excellent.

Start early, bring water and a hat, and budget a full day. Climbing the Pyramid of the Sun is currently restricted following recent stabilisation work — check on arrival.
