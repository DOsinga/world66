Sixteen years flying the Teotihuacán valley, booked from an office in the Juárez neighbourhood of Mexico City, with transport from the capital included on the early-morning run.

They also fly San Miguel de Allende and Tequisquiapan, which are the other two places in central Mexico where balloons go up regularly.
