Cascada Velo de Novia is the prettiest easy nature stop around Valle de Bravo. The waterfall drops about 35 metres through pine and oak forest near Avandaro, with short paths, viewpoints, picnic areas, and horses for rent around the park.

It can be busy, especially on weekends, but the setting is still worth the trip. Wear shoes with grip if it has rained; the paths and rocks around the falls can get slick.
