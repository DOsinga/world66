Parroquia San Francisco de Asis is Valle de Bravo's main parish church, with roots in the 17th century and a layout that once separated Indigenous and Spanish worshippers. The present building includes later additions and religious artworks, including Italian oil paintings, making it one of the town centre's main historic stops.
