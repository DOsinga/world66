The Ágora José González Echeverría is Fresnillo's best cultural stop, set in a former mining school near Mina Proaño. Its galleries and museum spaces pull together mining history with local figures from music, painting, and public life.

Visit it with the mine rather than as a separate errand. Together they explain why Fresnillo feels like a working silver city instead of just another town on the highway.
