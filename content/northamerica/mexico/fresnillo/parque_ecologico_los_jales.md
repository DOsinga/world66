Parque Ecológico Los Jales is a useful counterpoint to Fresnillo's mine story: land shaped by mining, later remade as public open space. It has paths, water, and wide views near the Proaño area.

Come if you are already visiting the mine or Ágora. It is not the city's headline sight, but it shows how Fresnillo is still negotiating the legacy of silver extraction.
