General Cepeda is a small town in southern [Coahuila](/northamerica/mexico), about an hour west of Saltillo. Its streets are quiet and low-slung, but the desert around it is unusually rich: Cretaceous fossils, dinosaur footprints, petroglyph-covered rocks, and the remains of one of northern Mexico's great old haciendas all sit within reach.

The town itself is a short walk around the plaza, the portales, and the church of San Francisco de Asis. The reason to come is the wider landscape. [Rincon Colorado](/northamerica/mexico/general_cepeda/rincon_colorado_paleontological_zone) is Mexico's first paleontological zone opened to the public, while [Narigua](/northamerica/mexico/general_cepeda/narigua_petroglyphs) spreads thousands of rock carvings across a rough hillside.

General Cepeda works best with a car, water, and a tolerance for sun and dust. Treat it as a compact fossil-and-desert day from Saltillo, not as a polished resort town.
