Narigua is a rough archaeological zone rather than a polished museum. The draw is the scale: hundreds of rocks hold thousands of carved figures spread across an open hillside.

Go prepared for heat, loose stone, and little shade. The lack of heavy visitor infrastructure is part of the appeal, but it also means you need to bring water and take the dirt-road access seriously.
