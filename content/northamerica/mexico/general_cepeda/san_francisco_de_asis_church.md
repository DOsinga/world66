The church of San Francisco de Asis gives the town centre its age and shape. It stands on the plaza of the old San Francisco de los Patos settlement, with a sober stone front and the calm air of a northern Mexican parish church.

It is not a grand cathedral, but it matters because it ties the town's fossil country to its colonial story. Stop here before heading out to the desert sites.
