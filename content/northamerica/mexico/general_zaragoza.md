General Zaragoza is a small mountain town in the far south-east of Nuevo León, much closer in spirit to the Sierra Madre than to metropolitan [Monterrey](/northamerica/mexico/monterrey). It is remote, cool by regional standards, and framed by wooded slopes, rivers, and waterfalls.

The reason to come is [Parque El Salto](/northamerica/mexico/general_zaragoza/parque_el_salto), where the Río Blanco drops through forested country and the Velo de Novia waterfall gives the town its main travel anchor. The broader setting matters too: Cerro Viejo is visible from town, and official tourism material sells Zaragoza as a place of misty forest, orchids, mountain biking, and outdoor cabins.

This is not a polished city break. It is a rural escape for travellers already moving through southern Nuevo León or northern San Luis Potosí, and it rewards the effort only if you want mountain air, cold water, and a quiet town at the end of the road.
