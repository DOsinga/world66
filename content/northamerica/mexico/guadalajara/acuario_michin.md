Acuario Michin is Guadalajara's main aquarium, a polished indoor attraction with marine, freshwater,
and conservation-themed displays. It gives families a reliable half-day option away from the plazas,
churches, and museums that dominate the historic-centre itinerary.

The aquarium is strongest as a weather-proof, child-friendly stop rather than as a reason to visit
Guadalajara on its own. Still, in a large inland city, the range of aquatic exhibits and hands-on
programming adds variety to the usual cultural circuit.
