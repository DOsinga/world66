A mezcal bar in Colonia Americana with one of the better selections of artisanal mezcal in Guadalajara — over 100 varieties from small producers across Jalisco, Oaxaca, and Guerrero. The staff know their product and are happy to guide choices based on what you have tried before. Prices are reasonable for the quality, and the kitchen turns out good tacos and botanas.

The courtyard setting, with tables under a canopy of plants and string lights, is one of the more pleasant drinking environments in the city.
