The Paraninfo (assembly hall) of the University of Guadalajara contains a cycle of murals by José Clemente Orozco painted in 1936, among his most politically pointed work. The central ceiling mural — "The People and Its False Leaders" — shows demagogues, clerics, and soldiers manipulating a passive crowd, surrounded by the symbols of modern power. Orozco painted it with Franco, Mussolini, and Hitler in mind.

The Paraninfo is on the Plaza Guadalajara, adjacent to the Cathedral. It is open to the public; access is free and the space is used for public events. Combined with the Hospicio Cabañas murals, it gives a comprehensive picture of Orozco's range and ambition.
