Teatro Diana is a large modern performing arts venue south of Guadalajara's historic centre. It hosts concerts, ballet, opera, comedy, and touring shows in a comfortable auditorium of about 2,400 seats.

It is more useful for checking the current programme than for casual sightseeing, but a good booking here gives you a polished night out beyond the older Teatro Degollado.
