The Alfredo Duges Natural History Museum is a small university museum with an old-fashioned collection of animal specimens, taxidermy, and preserved anatomical material. It is a little grisly in places, which is part of the appeal if you like cabinets of curiosities.

It is not a must-see in Guanajuato, but it makes a good short detour near the university. Pair it with the grand stairway and surrounding streets rather than crossing town only for the museum.
