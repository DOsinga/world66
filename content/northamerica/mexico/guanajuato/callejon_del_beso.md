---

A famously narrow alley a short walk from the Plaza San Roque, where the upper balconies on either side are only 69 cm apart — close enough for two people to kiss between them. The legend has made it a busy spot for tourists and engaged couples; a small fee allows you to stand on the third step and pose with a partner for the obligatory photograph. The alley is crowded by midday and quietest first thing in the morning.
