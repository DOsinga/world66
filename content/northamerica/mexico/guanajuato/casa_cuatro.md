A boutique restaurant inside a restored colonial house on Calle de Allende — a converted central courtyard with a wood-fired pizza oven on the ground floor and a more formal Mexican-Mediterranean restaurant upstairs. Service is slow but the room is one of the most atmospheric in the city. Good for an unrushed evening.
