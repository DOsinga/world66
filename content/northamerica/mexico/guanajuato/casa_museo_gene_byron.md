Casa Museo Gene Byron sits outside Guanajuato's dense historic centre, which gives it a calmer rhythm than the city's plazas, tunnels, and churches. The museum is associated with Canadian-born artist Gene Byron and presents a mix of artwork, craft, architecture, and garden spaces.

It is a good choice for travellers who have already seen the central museums and want a quieter cultural detour. The setting matters as much as the collection: the house, grounds, and studio atmosphere make the visit feel personal rather than institutional.
