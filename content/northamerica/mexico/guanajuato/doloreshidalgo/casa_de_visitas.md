Casa de Visitas faces the heart of Dolores Hidalgo with the formal confidence of a town that knows its place in Mexican independence history. The building has served civic and ceremonial roles, and its architecture helps frame the main square around the parish church and museums.

It is a short stop, but a useful one while walking the centre. Dolores Hidalgo works best when you let the buildings around the plaza build a shared story rather than rushing only to the headline museum.
