A short funicular running from beside the Teatro Juárez up to the Pípila monument — about two minutes one way, a few pesos a ticket. Saves the steep walk up the alley behind the theatre and gives a moving photograph of the city through the cabin window. Buy a return ticket and walk down through the alleys.
