A long-running music bar off Plaza San Fernando, named for the Spanish band and known for live rock, blues, and ska from local groups most evenings. Inexpensive beer, a covered terrace, and a younger crowd than the Jardín cafés. A reliable late-night destination after the formal venues have closed.
