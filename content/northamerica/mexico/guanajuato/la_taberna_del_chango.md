A small student bar a few streets from the Jardín, dark-panelled and decorated with vinyl record sleeves, mezcal bottles, and Frida Kahlo prints. Strong on mezcal — over forty varieties from Oaxaca and Guerrero — and on the local trova songs played by guitar duos through the evening. Lively until late.
