León's Museum of Art and History is the main museum anchor of the Forum Cultural Guanajuato complex. Its exhibitions connect regional history, visual art, and the wider cultural identity of Guanajuato state in a polished modern setting.

For travelers who know León mainly for leather shopping and the feria, the museum gives the city a stronger cultural dimension. It is easy to combine with the nearby theatre, public spaces, and other Forum Cultural venues.
