Our Lady of Angeles Church is an 18th-century church near the centre of Leon, built in a Churrigueresque style. It is smaller than the cathedral but has enough historic character to justify a short detour if you are already walking the old centre.

The church is best visited for its ornate design rather than as a major landmark. Combine it with the nearby cathedral and central streets.
