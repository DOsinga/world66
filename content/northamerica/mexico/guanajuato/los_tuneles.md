Guanajuato's network of subterranean roads runs beneath the entire old town, carved into former river beds and mine workings — the city's solution to having no flat land for cars. Many roads simply disappear into a hillside and emerge somewhere else, often a kilometre away, with junctions, traffic lights, and even bus stops underground.

The tunnels are not technically a tourist attraction, but every visitor will end up driving through them eventually. A taxi ride from the bus station to your hotel will almost certainly take you through several. Pedestrian routes through the tunnels exist for short distances and are signed.
