A small contemporary-Mexican restaurant on Calle Positos near the Alhóndiga, drawing on the same farm-to-table approach now common in Oaxaca but rare in the colonial cities of the Bajío. The menu is short, the wine list is mostly Mexican, and the room is plain. Bookings recommended at the weekend.
