A small museum at the Santa Paula Cemetery on the western edge of the city, displaying about a hundred naturally mummified bodies. The collection includes adults, children, and what is billed as the world's smallest mummy — a foetus extracted from a woman who died in childbirth. Some of the mummies still have the clothes they were buried in; the expressions on a few are notoriously distressing.

The exhibit is presented with a slightly old-fashioned, semi-ghoulish tone. Children's entry should be considered carefully — some find it fascinating, others terrifying.
