A museum of Mexican colonial and modern art housed in the seventeenth-century mansion of a silver-mining family, on Calle Positos near the Alhóndiga. The collection is broad — colonial religious painting, eighteenth-century portraits, Mexican Modernism — and the building itself, with its painted ceilings and inner courtyard, is part of the appeal. Includes a notable miniature collection on the upper floor.
