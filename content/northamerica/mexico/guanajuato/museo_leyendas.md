A small museum near the Plazuela San Roque dedicated to the city's many folk legends and ghost stories, told through miniature dioramas built in the 1980s. Each scene takes a familiar tale — the Alley of the Kiss, La Llorona of the tunnels, the Pípila at the Alhóndiga — and renders it in painted plaster figures with audio narration. Cheesy but well-made, and good for children.
