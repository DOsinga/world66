The former house and studio of the muralist José Chávez Morado and his wife, the German-born painter Olga Costa, in the southern suburb of Pastita. The couple lived here from the 1960s and bequeathed the house, garden, and their personal collections to the state of Guanajuato. Worth the short taxi ride for the intimate sense of a working artists' household, plus rotating exhibitions of their paintings.
