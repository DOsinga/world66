The triangular plaza at the geographical heart of the colonial city, dominated by the yellow façade of the Basílica de Nuestra Señora and surrounded by the eighteenth-century mansions of the silver-mining elite, most now banks, hotels, and government offices. The eponymous "Peace" monument at the centre commemorates the peace of 1903 that ended the Reform Wars. A pleasant place to sit for an evening coffee.
