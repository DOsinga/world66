A small triangular plaza off the main route between the basilica and the Mercado Hidalgo, ringed with cafés, art galleries, and one of the city's busier night-time scenes. The plaza is quieter than the Jardín during the day and lively after dark. Outdoor seating fills up; the prices are slightly lower than the Jardín.
