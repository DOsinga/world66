Two minutes from the Jardín on Relox, open seven in the morning until eleven at night, selling the outdoor side of San Miguel.

The headline is the **sunrise balloon flight** over the town and the surrounding ranch country — the same physics as Teotihuacán, so it goes at first light or not at all, and weather scrubs it often. They also run horse riding, bicycles, and the trips out to the hot springs at La Gruta and Escondido Place.

They publish no email address; the number takes calls and WhatsApp.
