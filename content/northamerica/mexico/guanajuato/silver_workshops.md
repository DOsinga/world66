Although the great silver mines have mostly closed, several family workshops in the centre and at La Valenciana still work the metal into jewellery, decorative objects, and tableware. Calle Sopeña and the lanes around La Valenciana church have the best concentration. Quality and prices vary widely; serious shoppers should compare a few before buying. Beware of plated pieces sold as solid silver — look for the "925" hallmark.
