The most ornate building in central Guanajuato, an 1873–1903 confection of doric columns, bronze muses, and pink-and-gold cantera stone tucked between the Jardín de la Unión and the church of San Diego. The interior is a small Moorish-inflected horseshoe auditorium with red velvet and gilt, a vivid trompe-l'œil ceiling, and a Tiffany-style lobby. Guided tours run most days when there is no performance. The Festival Cervantino opens here every October.
