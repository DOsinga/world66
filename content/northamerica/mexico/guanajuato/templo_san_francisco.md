A small late-eighteenth-century Franciscan church on a plaza of the same name, with a richly carved cantera façade and a quiet single-nave interior. Often overlooked by visitors going from the basilica to the Jardín de la Unión, but worth ducking into. The neighbouring Plaza de San Francisco has good shaded benches.
