An institution near the Basílica that has been serving home-style Mexican food at low prices since the 1980s. The dining room is small, dark, and panelled in wood; the menu is short and changes daily, written on a blackboard. The set lunch — soup, a main, dessert, and coffee — is one of the best value meals in central Mexico. Cash only.
