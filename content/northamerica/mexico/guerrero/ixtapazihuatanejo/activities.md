Ixtapa and Zihuatanejo are two places that share an airport. Ixtapa is a planned resort strip on a long open beach; Zihuatanejo is the fishing town around a sheltered bay six kilometres south, and it is the better base for everything on the water.

**Diving and snorkelling** work the rocky points and offshore pinnacles — Los Morros de Potosí is the serious one. Visibility is Pacific rather than Caribbean, so expect ten to thirty metres and schooling fish rather than coral. **Humpback whales** pass between December and March. **Sport fishing** out of Zihuatanejo is excellent and long-established, with sailfish the usual target.

**Playa Las Gatas**, across the bay and reachable only by water taxi from the municipal pier, has a stone breakwater said to have been built on the orders of a Tarascan king and now shelters the calmest swimming here. **Playa La Ropa** is the long sandy one; **Playa Linda** up at Ixtapa has the boat to **Isla Ixtapa**.

Turtle releases run on the beaches east of town in the nesting season, and the crocodile lagoon behind Playa Linda is worth a look.
