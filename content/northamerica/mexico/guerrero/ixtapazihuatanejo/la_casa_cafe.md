An inexpensive tropical courtyard patio a block back from Playa La Madera, serving organic house coffee, cappuccino, latte, espresso and frappés alongside an extensive menu of both American and Mexican food. The wait staff and owners know the area well and are happy to say which beach is worth the walk that day.

The old cafe website now redirects to a hotel-booking page, so check locally that it is still open before making the trip.
