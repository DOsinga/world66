Museo Arqueologico de la Costa Grande is a compact museum near the waterfront in Zihuatanejo. Its pottery, stone pieces, and local finds give useful context for the coast before the beach resorts arrived.

It is not a large museum, but it is a good hour out of the sun and one of the better cultural stops in Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo. Pair it with a walk around central Zihuatanejo or the municipal beach.
