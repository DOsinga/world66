Playa Los Escalleros is a rock-strewn beach north of Ixtapa's main resort strip. It is better for walking, ocean views, and looking back along the curve of golden sand than for an easy swim.

Come for a quieter coastal stop rather than full beach facilities. Footwear helps on the rocks, and the surf can be rough.
