Hermosillo's best day trips head west to the coast. Bahía Kino is the most accessible option; the Seri-controlled Isla Tiburón is reachable by boat from there. The La Pintada rock art canyon to the southeast requires a car but rewards the effort. The colonial silver city of Álamos, five hours south, makes a longer excursion.
