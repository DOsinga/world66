Most people come for the water. The Valle del Mezquital is dry Otomí country, but it sits on hot springs, and the [Grutas de Tolantongo](/northamerica/mexico/hidalgo/ixmiquilpan/grutas_de_tolantongo) — a warm turquoise river running out of a cave at the bottom of a canyon — is one of the great day trips from Mexico City. The park is run by the local ejido and you simply pay at the gate; the official site is *grutastolantongo.com.mx*, and it warns that other sites using the name sell fake all-inclusive packages.

Go on a weekday if at all possible. At weekends and holidays Tolantongo fills to bursting. Microbuses run from Ixmiquilpan, about fifty minutes away.

[EcoAlberto](/northamerica/mexico/hidalgo/ixmiquilpan/parque_ecoalberto), run by the Hñähñu community of El Alberto, has thermal pools, kayaking and rappel in the canyon of the Río Tula.
