The Capilla de Nuestra Señora del Carmen is a small but richly decorated 18th-century church in Ixmiquilpan. Its churrigueresque interior, gilded altars, and painted biblical scenes make it more than a quick look from the street.

It pairs naturally with the Templo y Ex Convento de San Miguel Arcangel, giving a compact sense of the town's colonial religious architecture.
