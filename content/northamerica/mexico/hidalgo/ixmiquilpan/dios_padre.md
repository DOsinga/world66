Dios Padre is one of the big thermal water parks that make Ixmiquilpan a weekend escape for families from Hidalgo, Queretaro, and Mexico City. It has warm-water pools, slides, children's areas, and the practical facilities you need for a long soak.

It is not wild or quiet. Come for an easy local day in mineral water, not for the canyon drama of Tolantongo or EcoAlberto.
