Museo de la Cultura Hñähñú is a small museum devoted to the Hñähñú people of the Mezquital Valley. Its galleries cover religious objects, household items, rituals, calendars, and the relationship between local culture and the dry valley landscape.

There is also a botanical garden with native plants from the Mezquital River Valley.
