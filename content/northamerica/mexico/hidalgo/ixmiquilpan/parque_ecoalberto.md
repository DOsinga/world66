Parque EcoAlberto is a community-run park in canyon country south of Ixmiquilpan. It mixes the easy pleasures of pools and family recreation with rougher outdoor activities such as kayaking, ziplines, and canyon walks.

Its best-known activity is the Caminata Nocturna, a staged night walk tied to migration stories from the region. Check schedules before building a trip around it, because some activities run only with minimum group numbers.
