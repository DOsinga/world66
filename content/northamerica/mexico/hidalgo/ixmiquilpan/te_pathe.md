Te-Pathé is a large aquatic park near Ixmiquilpan, with a wave pool, lazy river, giant slides, swimming pools, picnic areas, and sports fields. It is set up for a full family day rather than a quick look.

The park also has restaurants and camping facilities, making it one of the area's easier hot-weather outings if you want pools and slides instead of another church or market.
