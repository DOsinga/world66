Cascada de Rio del Milagro is a small waterfall northwest of town, reached by a short path from the road. The fall is modest, but the walk is pleasant, with cool air, old mine traces, and a suspended footbridge adding to the mood.

It is best in the rainy season. In dry months, treat it as a short forest walk rather than a waterfall expedition.
