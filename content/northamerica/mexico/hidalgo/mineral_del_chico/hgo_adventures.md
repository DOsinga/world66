An adventure park in the community of La Presa, twenty minutes from the centre of Mineral del Chico, working the rock towers and pine forest of El Chico national park.

The headline is the **via ferrata** up La Tanda, about 200 metres of iron rungs and cables over three hours. They also run canyoning — a basic descent and the longer "Tres Maravillas" — a single 180-metre rappel, a three-line zip circuit, the El Contadero labyrinth trail among the rock formations, day and night hikes, and camping packages that bundle several of these.

They are members of AMTAVE, the Mexican adventure-tourism association.
