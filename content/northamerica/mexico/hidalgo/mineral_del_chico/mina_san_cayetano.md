Mina San Cayetano is the mine to choose if you want the visit to feel like an outing rather than a museum stop. The route can involve more uneven ground and tighter spaces than the easiest town mines, so it suits travelers who are comfortable underground.

Ask locally about current access before walking out. Mine tours in Mineral del Chico can change hours, especially outside weekends and holidays.
