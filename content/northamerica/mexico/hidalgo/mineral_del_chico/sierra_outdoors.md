A Mineral del Chico outfit of local guides, based in the village itself, splitting its trips between the vertical and the gentle.

On the adventure side: rappel and canyoning at **Salto de las Palomas** and Las Brujitas. On the quieter side: a sunrise walk to a viewpoint, a guided walk round the Pueblo Mágico, panoramic hikes, a sunset in the forest and the trail to **Las Monjas**, the line of rock pinnacles the park is known for. Prices include the guide, gear, park fees, a snack and medical insurance.

They publish no phone line other than WhatsApp.
