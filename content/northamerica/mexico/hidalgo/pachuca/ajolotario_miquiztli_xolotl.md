Ajolotario Miquiztli Xolotl is a small private museum and breeding facility focused on the endangered axolotl. The animal is famous for keeping juvenile traits into adulthood and for its unusual ability to regenerate limbs and organs.

This is a small, specialist stop rather than a conventional museum. Call ahead before going, because visits depend on limited opening hours and staff availability.
