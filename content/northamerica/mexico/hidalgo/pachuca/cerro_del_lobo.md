Cerro del Lobo is a steep public park and viewpoint above Pachuca. Trails and cobblestone paths climb to a mirador with wide views over the city.

The park is best for the sunset view. Conditions can be rough in places, so it is more suited to visitors who do not mind a basic urban hill walk.
