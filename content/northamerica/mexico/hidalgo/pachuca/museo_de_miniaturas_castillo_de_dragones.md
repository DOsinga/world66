Museo de Miniaturas Castillo de Dragones is a small toy and miniature museum in a castle-like building decorated with dragons. It is playful and a little kitschy, but that is part of the appeal, especially for families or anyone looking for an offbeat stop outside Pachuca's main historic sights.

The museum is best treated as a short visit rather than a major museum stop.
