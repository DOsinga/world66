Museo El Rehilete is Pachuca's hands-on children's museum, with interactive exhibits aimed at making science and learning approachable for families. The complex also includes attractions such as a planetarium, botanical garden, and dinosaur-themed area.

It is most useful for travellers with children or anyone needing an indoor break from the city centre. Check the current schedule before going, since the planetarium and special activities may run at set times.
