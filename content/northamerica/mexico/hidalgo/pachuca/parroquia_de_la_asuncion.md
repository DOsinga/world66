Parroquia de la Asuncion is a 16th-century church near Pachuca's Plaza de la Constitucion. Inside are period oil paintings and unusual murals of heaven and the stars, while the parish's links to independence-era priest Mariano Matamoros add a layer of Mexican history to the visit.
