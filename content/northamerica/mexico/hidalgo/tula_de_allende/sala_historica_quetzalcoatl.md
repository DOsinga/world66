Sala Historica Quetzalcoatl is a small museum in the centre of Tula de Allende. It is useful if you have time before a bus or want a little local context beyond the archaeological zone.

Keep expectations modest. The ruins carry the trip; this is a short town stop that rounds out the visit rather than a reason to come by itself.
