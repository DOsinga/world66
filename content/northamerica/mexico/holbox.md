Holbox is a long, low island off the north coast of the Yucatan Peninsula, separated from the mainland by lagoon and mangrove. It is often sold as a beach escape, but the better version of Holbox is wilder than that: shallow sandbars, frigatebirds overhead, golf carts instead of heavy traffic, and a horizon that stays flat and bright for kilometres.

The island's strongest experiences are tied to water and wildlife. You can walk the pale shallows toward [Punta Mosquito](/northamerica/mexico/holbox/punta_mosquito), watch sunset at [Punta Cocos](/northamerica/mexico/holbox/punta_cocos), take a seasonal [whale shark trip](/northamerica/mexico/holbox/whale_shark_trips), or head by boat toward [Cabo Catoche](/northamerica/mexico/holbox/cabo_catoche) where the Gulf and Caribbean meet.

Holbox has become more famous and more expensive, so do not expect a secret island. It still earns the trip if you come for the open beaches, birds, and boat days rather than resort polish.
