Huichapan's archaeology and history museum is a compact local museum near the centre. Its exhibits connect the area to pre-Hispanic settlement, the colonial period, and the revolutionary history that still shapes the town's civic identity.

Use it as a short context stop rather than a major museum visit. It pairs well with El Chapitel and the central churches.
