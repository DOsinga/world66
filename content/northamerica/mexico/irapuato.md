Irapuato is a working city in [Guanajuato](/northamerica/mexico/guanajuato) state, sitting on the Bajio plain between Leon, Salamanca, and the colonial highlands. It is best known in Mexico for strawberries, a crop so tied to the city that Irapuato calls itself the strawberry capital.

This is not the showpiece that nearby Guanajuato or San Miguel de Allende is. The centre is lower-key and more local, with churches, fountains, plazas, and a few museums rather than a long list of famous sights. That can make it useful if you want a less polished stop in the Bajio or need a base between the region's better-known cities.

The most pleasant visit is compact: walk the historic centre, look for the old fountains, try something strawberry-based, then use Irapuato as a practical jumping-off point for the wider state.
