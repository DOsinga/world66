Irapuato Cathedral is the main architectural sight in the centre, a Spanish Baroque church with 16th-century roots. It gives the city its clearest colonial note, even if Irapuato is less ornate than nearby Guanajuato.

The cathedral is easiest to see as part of a short walk through the central plazas and fountains. Go in the morning or late afternoon, when the centre is cooler and busier with local life.
