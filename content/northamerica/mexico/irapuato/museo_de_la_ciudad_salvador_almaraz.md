The Museo de la Ciudad Salvador Almaraz is Irapuato's main local history museum. It is small, but it gives useful context for the city and the surrounding Bajio region, including pre-Hispanic and colonial material.

It works best as a short stop while walking the centre. Do not expect a blockbuster museum; the value is in getting a clearer sense of Irapuato beyond the strawberry nickname.
