Parque Zoologico de Irapuato is a large, modern zoo on the southeast side of the city. It is most useful for families or anyone spending more than a quick stop in Irapuato.

The collection includes big cats, giraffes, reptiles, and birds, with extra activities such as a small train and zip line. It is not the city's most distinctive sight, but it is one of its fuller half-day outings.
