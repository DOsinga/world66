The Faro Museo is Isla Aguada's clearest landmark: a two-storey lightkeeper's house topped by an 18-metre tower. The small museum covers local maritime history and underwater archaeology, and the outside grounds are often more relaxed than the word "museum" suggests. It is a short, worthwhile stop before or after a boat trip on the lagoon.
