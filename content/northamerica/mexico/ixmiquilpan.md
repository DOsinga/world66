Ixmiquilpan is the main town of the Mezquital Valley, a dry, bright part of Hidalgo where hot water, canyon walls, and Otomi culture give the area more personality than its highway approach suggests. It is not a polished colonial showpiece. It is a working regional town with a very good reason to stop.

The centre has one serious cultural anchor: the [Templo y Ex Convento de San Miguel Arcangel](/northamerica/mexico/ixmiquilpan/templo_y_ex_convento_de_san_miguel_arcangel), whose 16th-century murals mix Christian space with indigenous battle imagery. Around town, the draw shifts to hot springs and water parks, especially [Dios Padre](/northamerica/mexico/ixmiquilpan/dios_padre).

Ixmiquilpan also works as the practical base for the wilder attractions nearby. [Parque EcoAlberto](/northamerica/mexico/ixmiquilpan/parque_ecoalberto) sits in canyon country south of town, while the famous Grutas Tolantongo hot-spring gorge is farther east in Cardonal. Tolantongo is not in town, but Ixmiquilpan is where many travellers pass through, buy supplies, and connect onward.

## Destinations in and around Ixmiquilpan

- [Templo y Ex Convento de San Miguel Arcangel](/northamerica/mexico/ixmiquilpan/templo_y_ex_convento_de_san_miguel_arcangel) - fortified 16th-century church with unusual indigenous murals
- [Parque EcoAlberto](/northamerica/mexico/ixmiquilpan/parque_ecoalberto) - canyon ecotourism park run by the El Alberto community
- [Dios Padre](/northamerica/mexico/ixmiquilpan/dios_padre) - large thermal water park on the edge of town
