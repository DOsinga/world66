Te-Pathé is a big, practical water park for a hot day in the Mezquital Valley. The appeal is simple: wave pool, lazy river, slides, swimming pools, picnic areas, and enough food and camping facilities to make it an easy family outing. Check the weekday schedule before going, as not every pool always operates.
