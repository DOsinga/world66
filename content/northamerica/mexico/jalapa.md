Xalapa (also spelled Jalapa) is the capital of Veracruz state and one of Mexico's most pleasant surprises. Set at 1,400 metres in the cloud forest zone of the Sierra Madre Oriental, with the snow-capped Pico de Orizaba visible on clear days, it has a cool, misty climate that feels nothing like the sweltering coast below.

The city is best known for the [Museo de Antropología de Xalapa](/northamerica/mexico/jalapa/museo_antropologia), one of the finest archaeological museums in Mexico. Its collection of Olmec, Totonac, and Huastec artefacts — including massive Olmec heads — rivals anything in Mexico City. The university gives Xalapa a youthful, cultural energy, with good cafes, bookshops, and live music.

The colonial centre is small and walkable, focused on [Parque Juárez](/northamerica/mexico/jalapa/parque_juarez), the [cathedral](/northamerica/mexico/jalapa/cathedral) and the [Palacio de Gobierno](/northamerica/mexico/jalapa/palacio_de_gobierno), with steep cobbled alleys like the [Callejón Jesús Te Ampare](/northamerica/mexico/jalapa/callejon_jesus_te_ampare) running between them. Greenery is everywhere — the [Paseo de los Lagos](/northamerica/mexico/jalapa/paseo_de_los_lagos), the cone of [Macuiltépetl](/northamerica/mexico/jalapa/macuiltepetl) on the north side, and the [Jardín Botánico Clavijero](/northamerica/mexico/jalapa/jardin_botanico) on the southern edge.

The surrounding countryside produces some of Mexico's best coffee, and the area around Coatepec and Xico, both short trips from the city, is worth exploring for waterfalls, coffee fincas, and excellent food.
