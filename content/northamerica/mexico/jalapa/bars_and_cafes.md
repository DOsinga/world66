---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Xalapa is a coffee town in a coffee-growing region — the surrounding municipalities of Coatepec, Xico and Teocelo produce some of Mexico's best altitude coffee — and the café culture in town is strong. Independent cafés outnumber chains in the centre, often serving beans roasted that week from nearby fincas. The university gives the bar scene a young, slightly bohemian feel.
