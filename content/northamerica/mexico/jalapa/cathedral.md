The cathedral dominates the south side of [Parque Juárez](/northamerica/mexico/jalapa/parque_juarez), a heavy, slightly sober eighteenth-century building of yellow stone with a Neoclassical facade. It became a cathedral only in 1864 when Xalapa was made a diocese; before that it was the parish church of the colonial town.

The interior is large, calm, mostly Neoclassical, with a striking marble baldachin over the high altar. The unfinished south tower is the building's signature feature, the result of money running out in the 1770s. Open all day for visits when no Mass is in progress.
