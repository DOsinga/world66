---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Xalapa sits at the foot of two volcanoes and at the top of the coffee belt, and a day in any direction takes you somewhere worthwhile. The closest essentials are the colonial coffee towns of Coatepec and Xico, the Texolo waterfalls below Xico, and the archaeological site of Cempoala on the coast. Slightly further afield are the port of Veracruz, the mountain town of Perote, and the great pyramid complex of El Tajín in the Totonac country to the north.
