---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Xalapa eats well. The state of Veracruz has one of Mexico's most distinctive regional cuisines — Gulf seafood, indigenous ingredients like *acuyo* (hoja santa) and *xonequi*, the chiltepin chile from the mountains, and a long-standing love of coffee. The university town gives Xalapa a denser concentration of good casual places than the population of half a million would suggest.

Try *molotes* (stuffed fried plantain dumplings), *mole xiqueño* (the dark complex mole of nearby Xico), *picadas* (small fried tortillas with salsa), and the seafood specialties that come up from Veracruz city — *huachinango a la veracruzana* above all. Coffee is excellent and cheap; do not order a frappuccino in Veracruz state, life is too short.
