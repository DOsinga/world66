---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Xalapa is a comfortable bus ride from most of central Mexico. ADO and ADO GL run frequent first-class services from Mexico City TAPO (5 hours), Veracruz (1.5 hours), Puebla (3 hours) and Oaxaca (about 8 hours). The CAXA bus terminal is east of the centre — taxi or city bus into town.

The El Lencero airport (JAL) just outside the city handles only a couple of daily flights from Mexico City; most fliers use Veracruz airport (VER) on the coast, about 90 minutes away, with much wider connections. The road in from the coast — Federal 140 — climbs sharply through cloud forest and is one of the more scenic approaches to a Mexican capital.
