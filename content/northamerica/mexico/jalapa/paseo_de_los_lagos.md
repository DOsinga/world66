A short walk south of the centre brings you to the Paseo de los Lagos, a chain of three small lakes set in a long, narrow park along a stream valley. The paths are shaded by tall cedars and bamboo and the lakes are stocked with carp; on weekends families come to feed the ducks and rent rowing boats.

The walk along the lakes connects to the Estadio Xalapeño and continues into a string of small green spaces behind the university quarter. A useful escape from the steeper streets of the centre on a hot afternoon — though "hot" in Xalapa rarely means more than the low 70s F.
