---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Xalapa's sights divide neatly between one outstanding museum (the Museo de Antropología, one of the great archaeological collections in the Americas), the colonial centre clustered around the cathedral and Parque Juárez, and the green spaces that the cool climate and abundant rain make so easy to fill. Add a coffee finca or a waterfall on a day trip and you have a long weekend's worth.
