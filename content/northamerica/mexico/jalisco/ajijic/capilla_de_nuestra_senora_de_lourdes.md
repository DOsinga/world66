Capilla de Nuestra Señora de Lourdes is a small historic chapel on a hill above Chapala. Its appeal is quiet rather than grand: a simple colonial-era chapel, gardens, and a grotto with a statue of the Virgin.

It is best treated as a short reflective stop while exploring Chapala and the Lake Chapala shore, not as a major monument on its own.
