Hacienda Tequila Patron is the most visible premium-tequila operation around Atotonilco El Alto, with production, hospitality, and tasting experiences set on the western side of town. Patron is now a global brand, but the hacienda still matters because it shows the scale and polish of modern Los Altos tequila.

Visits should be planned in advance, usually through the hacienda or a specialist tequila tour. It is not the place for a casual drop-in drink; come because you want to see how a high-end brand presents agave, fermentation, barrel ageing, and tasting.
