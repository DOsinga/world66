title: Getting Around
type: section
tags: [getting_around]
---

Barra de Navidad is tiny and almost everything is walkable within ten to fifteen minutes. The town sits on a narrow sandbar with the beach on one side and the lagoon on the other, so it is essentially one long street — Avenida Veracruz — plus a few cross streets.

**Pangas** (small motorised boats) run from the malecón to Isla Navidad and the village of Colimilla on the far shore of the lagoon. The crossing takes five minutes and drivers charge a few pesos per person; agree a price before you get in. These boats also function as water taxis for the lagoon anchorage, ferrying sailors to and from their boats.

**Taxis** are available and can take you to San Patricio (Melaque), the larger neighbouring town about 4 km west, or further afield to Manzanillo. There is no local bus within Barra itself, but frequent second-class buses run along Highway 200 to Manzanillo, Guadalajara, and Puerto Vallarta from the bus stop on the main road.

**Golf carts** are popular among visitors for getting around the lagoon side and out to Isla Navidad, and can be rented locally.
