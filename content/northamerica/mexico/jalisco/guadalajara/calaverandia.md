Calaverandia is Guadalajara's seasonal Day of the Dead theme park, an evening attraction built around light installations, altars, music, food, and theatrical nods to calavera imagery. It is commercial and theatrical, but it taps into one of Jalisco's most vivid cultural seasons with real flair.

Because it is seasonal, check dates before counting on it. The park is best approached as a night out rather than a heritage lesson: buy tickets ahead, expect crowds, and enjoy the spectacle. It can be a memorable October or November addition to Guadalajara if your timing lines up.
