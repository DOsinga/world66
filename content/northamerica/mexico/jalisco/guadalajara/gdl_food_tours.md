Walking food tours of Guadalajara since 2013. They publish no address, so the pin is on the historic centre.

The **GDL Taste** walk spends two and a half hours eating the city's own dishes — the **torta ahogada** drowned in tomato and chilli sauce, **carne en su jugo**, and tequila at a mariachi house to finish. The evening **pub crawl** works the bars of Colonia Americana with drinks and snacks included. Private weekday tours are by email.
