A daily air-conditioned van from Guadalajara to Tequila, for 490 pesos a head. A second pick-up point is on Mexicaltzingo in the centre.

The day takes in a **jima** demonstration in the agave fields, an artisanal distillery with an underground cellar and a blanco-reposado-añejo tasting, a stop for **cantaritos** in Amatitán, and free time in the town of Tequila. A small deposit holds a seat.
