Private and small-group tours with local guides, all with their own transport and hotel pick-up, from an office in Colonia Americana.

In town, a three-and-a-half-hour **history and murals** walk covers the historic centre. Out of town: a private **Tequila** day at a small artisanal distillery, the potters of Tonalá and Tlaquepaque, a full day in the mountain village of **Tapalpa**, **Lake Chapala** with a boat to Mezcala Island and a stop in Ajijic, and the round pyramids of **Guachimontones**.
