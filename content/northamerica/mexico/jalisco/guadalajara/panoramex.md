A Guadalajara tour company that claims more than fifty years in the business, with an office high in the Punto São Paulo tower in Providencia.

**Tequila** goes every day, in two versions: one built around the José Cuervo distillery, the other with cantaritos and a premium distillery. They also run **Lake Chapala and Ajijic**, Casa Herradura with Tlaquepaque, the mountain town of **Mazamitla** on Fridays and Sundays, and private tours to order. They also run a second site, *gdltours.com*.
