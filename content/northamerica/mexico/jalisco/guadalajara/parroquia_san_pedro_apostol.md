Parroquia San Pedro Apóstol anchors the centre of Tlaquepaque, facing Jardín Hidalgo and the surrounding craft streets. The parish dates to the 19th century and mixes Baroque and Byzantine touches in a way that gives the square a stronger historic centre than the shops alone would manage.

It is an easy look-in while walking Tlaquepaque. The exterior is the main draw, but step inside if the church is open and quiet.
