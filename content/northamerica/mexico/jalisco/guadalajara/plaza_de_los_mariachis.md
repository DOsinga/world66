Plaza de los Mariachis, officially Plaza Pepe Guizar, is a small triangular square known for the mariachi bands who gather to play for visitors. It is closely tied to Guadalajara's music culture and the Jarabe Tapatio, though the area is best visited with normal city caution, especially after dark.
