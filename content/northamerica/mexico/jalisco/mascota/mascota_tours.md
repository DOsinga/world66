Guided tours from an office in the centre of Mascota, in English, French and Spanish.

The list covers the old mining country of the Sierra: the **volcanoes and their crater**, a walk round Mascota itself, the **hot springs**, an "Indiana Jones" tour, ATVs, hikes, El Peñasco Mágico and the Vista Mascota viewpoint. Two-day packages pair Mascota with **San Sebastián del Oeste** and hotel nights, and they run longer road trips into the Sierra Madre and Nayarit.
