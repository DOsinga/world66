An adventure park in the pine woods on the highway outside Mazamitla, open every day from nine to six. The pin is on the town.

There are **zip lines**, a suspension bridge, a rainbow slide, a giant swing, paintball, ATVs and **horse rides**, plus tours of the area. A second WhatsApp number on their site, +52 33 3018 2136, is for the tours.
