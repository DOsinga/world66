El Eden de Mismaloya is a jungle recreation area inland from Mismaloya, with canopy zip lines, a river, and natural swimming pools. It works best as a half-day stop for travellers who want a more active outing than the beach, especially if they are already exploring the coast south of Puerto Vallarta.
