The largest operator on Banderas Bay, with desks in Marina Vallarta, the northern hotel zone and Nuevo Nayarit, and a programme that covers most of what the bay sells: **humpback whale watching** from December to March, the **Islas Marietas**, diving at Los Arcos, canopy tours and zip lines in the sierra behind the city, and sailing days to Yelapa and Las Caletas.

Scale is the trade here — the boats are bigger and the trips are more scripted than a small operator's, but the licences and the safety record are in order, and on a whale trip the extra deck height is not a disadvantage.
