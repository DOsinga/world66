A flying club on the road below Tapalpa whose pilots have flown here since 1993, selling **tandem paragliding** with certified instructors every day. If the weather stops a flight they rebook it for free. The pin is on Tapalpa; the launch site is out on the Amacueca road.

At the end of October they host **CalacaFest**, a paragliding competition. They also have a pizza palapa for after the flight.
