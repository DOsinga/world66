The town of Tequila is the whole point, and the distilleries in and around it are the reason to come. The big houses — Cuervo and Sauza — have their distilleries in the centre and run tours several times a day; smaller ones such as Fortaleza and El Llano take visitors by appointment, and are worth the effort for anyone who cares how tequila is made.

Most visits include the **jima**, the harvesting of the agave heart with a long flat blade, out in the blue fields that are a UNESCO World Heritage landscape. Book ahead at weekends, and do not drive afterwards.
