The Sauza family distillery in Tequila, open for tours from Tuesday to Sunday.

The short **Perseverancia** tour is an hour round the distillery. The two-hour **Casa Sauza** tour adds the agave botanical garden and a demonstration of the jima. The **Hornitos** tour runs three hours with a grill lunch, and **Tres Generaciones** spends four hours on a professional tasting and a three-course meal.

The phone number they publish carries a Monterrey area code.
