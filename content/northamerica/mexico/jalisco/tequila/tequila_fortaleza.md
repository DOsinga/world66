La Fortaleza is a small, traditional distillery in the town of Tequila, admired by tequila people for doing things the slow way — the cooked agave is still crushed under a stone **tahona**. They publish no street address, so the pin is on the town.

Tours and private events are by **appointment only**, booked at least 36 hours ahead through their website; the distillery shop opens late morning, also by appointment. A second number is +52 374 742 0032.
