Activities based in Yelapa itself, the village on the south shore of the Bay of Banderas that you reach by boat. They publish no street address, so the pin is on the village.

The easy one is the **waterfall hike**, two and a half hours. Others: a mule ride up to an ATV escape in the hills, paddleboard and kayak hire, jet skis, zip lines and a tandem flight. By boat they go to surf lessons at Quimixto, snorkelling at Majahuitas, the Marietas Islands, sport fishing, and a two-day beach scuba camp. They publish no phone; write by email.
