Jalpan, usually written in full as Jalpan de Serra, is the main town of Queretaro's Sierra Gorda. It sits deep in green mountain country, far enough from the state capital that the road in feels like part of the visit.

The town's strongest reason to come is the Franciscan mission trail. [Mision Santiago Apostol](/northamerica/mexico/jalpan/mision_santiago_apostol) stands close to the centre, while [Nuestra Senora de la Luz de Tancoyol](/northamerica/mexico/jalpan/nuestra_senora_de_la_luz_de_tancoyol) is a longer excursion into the hills. Both belong to the UNESCO-listed Sierra Gorda missions, where European Catholic symbols and Indigenous imagery share the same carved facades.

Jalpan is more than church architecture. The [Museum of the Sierra Gorda](/northamerica/mexico/jalpan/museum_of_the_sierra_gorda) gives context to the region, and [Tancama](/northamerica/mexico/jalpan/tancama) adds a pre-Hispanic layer in the nearby hills. It is a strong base for travellers who prefer mountain towns to colonial showpieces polished for weekend crowds.
