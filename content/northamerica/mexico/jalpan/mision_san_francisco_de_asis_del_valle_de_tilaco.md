Mision San Francisco de Asis del Valle de Tilaco is one of the Franciscan missions of the Sierra Gorda. Built in the eighteenth century, it has an especially ornate facade with angels, corn, mermaids, and local decorative motifs, making it a strong stop on a mission route from Jalpan.
