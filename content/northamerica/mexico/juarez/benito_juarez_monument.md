The Benito Juárez Monument is a civic marker in the city that carries his name, honouring the 19th-century president associated with reform, republicanism, and resistance to foreign intervention. It is a brief stop, but it gives useful symbolic weight to a border city often discussed only through migration, industry, or violence.

See it as part of a central walk rather than a stand-alone destination. Combined with the cathedral, old mission, and Avenida Juárez, the monument helps restore a little historical depth to a place many travelers otherwise experience only in transit.
