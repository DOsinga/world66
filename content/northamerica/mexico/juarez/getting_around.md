---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The historic centre and the commercial streets around Avenida Juárez are walkable from the Puente Paso del Norte bridge. Taxis are widely available — agree on a price before getting in, or use an app-based service, which is generally safer. Public buses cover the city but routes can be confusing for visitors. If you are venturing beyond the historic centre, a taxi or rideshare is the practical choice. Keep a low profile, avoid displaying valuables, and exercise the same common sense you would in any large city with an uneven security situation.
