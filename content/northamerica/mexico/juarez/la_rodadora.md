La Rodadora, often described as the Tumbleweed Interactive Space, is one of Juarez's strongest family attractions. It mixes hands-on science, regional culture, environmental exhibits, and play in a city that does not have many large visitor museums.

The best reason to go is the local framing: the Chihuahuan Desert, border life, and northern Mexican context give the interactive displays a sense of place. It is particularly good with children, but curious adults will find enough to justify the trip.
