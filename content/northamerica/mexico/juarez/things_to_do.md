---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Juárez rewards visitors who come curious rather than cautious. The historic core around Plaza Principal is calm, genuine, and largely unhurried. The old mission is older than most of Mexico's other northern cities. The revolutionary history is real and worth understanding. Stay aware, stick to the established areas near the bridges and the centre, and you will find a city that has a lot more to show than its reputation suggests.
