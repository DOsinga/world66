The Catedral de Nuestra Señora de La Paz anchors the old centre beside Jardín Velasco. It is modest compared with Mexico's grand colonial cathedrals, but its calm facade and shaded plaza help define the city's civic core.

Step inside if it is open, then use the square as a starting point for a short walk through central La Paz. The appeal is quiet proportion rather than spectacle.
