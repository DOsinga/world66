The Museo de Arte de Baja California Sur gives central La Paz a useful indoor counterpoint to all that sun and sea. Its exhibitions focus on modern and contemporary art, with a regional emphasis when programming allows.

It is a good stop in the hottest part of the day or before an evening walk on the malecón. Check current exhibitions; the building is compact enough for a focused visit.
