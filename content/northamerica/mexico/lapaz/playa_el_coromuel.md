El Coromuel is the easiest beach from central La Paz, just beyond the main malecón. It has facilities, shallow water, and enough convenience to make it useful when you do not want to commit to the northern beaches.

It is not as beautiful as Balandra, but that is not its job. Use it for a quick swim, a family stop, or a relaxed hour near town.
