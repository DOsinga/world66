León is the largest city in the state of [Guanajuato](/northamerica/mexico/guanajuato) and the fifth-largest in Mexico. Known as the shoe capital of the world, the city produces around a third of all footwear made in the country, and the [leather goods](/northamerica/mexico/len/leather_market) on sale here are genuinely good value.

The city sits about 40 minutes from Guanajuato's colonial capital and makes a practical base for exploring the region. The centro histórico has a handsome [cathedral](/northamerica/mexico/len/cathedral) and the [Arco de la Calzada](/northamerica/mexico/len/arco_calzada), a triumphal arch marking the entrance to a long pedestrian boulevard lined with shops and restaurants. León also hosts the annual [Feria de León](/northamerica/mexico/len/feria_de_leon), one of Mexico's biggest state fairs, running from January into February.
