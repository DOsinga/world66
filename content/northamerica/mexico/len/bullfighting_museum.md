Leon's Bullfighting Museum preserves a tradition that has shaped parts of the city's popular culture, even as bullfighting itself remains controversial. Exhibits typically focus on matadors, costumes, posters, photographs, and the ritual world around the ring.

It is a specialist stop rather than a universal recommendation. Travellers interested in Mexican regional culture will find it useful; others may prefer to spend more time with Leon's churches, plazas, and leather markets.
