Explora Science Center Museum is an interactive science museum and park near Leon's fairgrounds. Its main halls cover themes such as the human body, water, communication, and movement, with hands-on exhibits aimed mostly at families.

The complex also has an IMAX theatre, workshops, classrooms, and temporary exhibitions. It pairs well with the zoo or the Forum Cultural area if you are planning a child-friendly day in Leon.
