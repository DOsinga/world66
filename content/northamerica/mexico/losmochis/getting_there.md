---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---
Los Mochis has a small international airport (LMM) with connections to major Mexican cities. Long-distance buses run frequently along the Pacific coast from Mazatlán, Culiacán, and Nogales. The Chepe train departs Los Mochis early in the morning for Chihuahua; book ahead during peak season. From the port of Topolobampo, 24 km southwest, Baja Ferries operates overnight sailings to La Paz in Baja California Sur — a popular way to loop between the mainland and the peninsula.
