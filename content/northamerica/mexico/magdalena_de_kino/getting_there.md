---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Magdalena de Kino sits on **Federal Highway 15**, the main road between Nogales on the Arizona border and [Hermosillo](/northamerica/mexico/hermosillo) — 80 km south of Nogales and 200 km north of Hermosillo. The toll motorway (Highway 15D) bypasses the town slightly; take the free road to enter the centre.

Long-distance buses (Tufesa, TAP, Estrella Blanca) stop in Magdalena en route between the border and Hermosillo. By car the town is a natural lunch and refuel stop on a road trip south. The nearest commercial airports are Hermosillo (200 km south) and Tucson, Arizona (140 km north across the border).
