Magdalena de Kino is a pilgrimage town wrapped around a single square. The Plaza Monumental, with the [crypt of Padre Kino](/northamerica/mexico/magdalena_de_kino/cripta_padre_kino) at its centre and the [Templo de Santa María](/northamerica/mexico/magdalena_de_kino/templo_santa_maria) on its north side, can be explored in an hour but rewards a slower wander.

For something less obvious, the **Palacio Municipal** was once the seasonal seat of Sonora's state government when officials fled the summer heat of Hermosillo; it is still in use and the courtyard is open during working hours. The **Mausoleo Luis Donaldo Colosio** honours the assassinated 1994 presidential candidate, a native son who is still mourned across Mexico.
