---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Manzanillo's coastline offers a range of beaches around its two bays. La Boquita at the northern tip of Santiago Bay is popular with locals for its mild surf and seafood stands. [Playa La Audiencia](/northamerica/mexico/manzanillo/playa_la_audiencia) on the Santiago Peninsula is the most famous, sheltered and good for swimming and snorkelling. Further out, Peña Blanca is a quieter stretch with palm trees and ATV riding (closed during turtle nesting season). Club de Yates, accessible via the Las Hadas road, has a protected cove excellent for snorkelling and diving. For surf, head north to [Playa Olas Altas](/northamerica/mexico/manzanillo/playa_olas_altas) on the open Pacific side.
