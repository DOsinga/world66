---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Manzanillo eats well. The local style runs to seafood — *huachinango* (red snapper) baked whole, *aguachile* (raw shrimp in a tart green chilli broth), ceviche of mahi-mahi or sailfish, and *pescado zarandeado* (whole fish butterflied and grilled over coals). The cheapest and often best places are the *palapas* on the beaches and the family *fondas* in the old town centre.
