Los Naranjos, a few blocks behind the central plaza, is one of the longer-running family seafood places in Manzanillo. The dining room is plain — plastic chairs, tile floor, an open kitchen — but the shellfish soup, the *aguachile*, and the whole grilled snapper are reliably excellent and reasonably priced.

It is busy at lunch with workers from the port and quiet by mid-evening. No reservations, no nonsense.
