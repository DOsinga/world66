Mascota's main church anchors the old centre. Construction began in the 18th century, and the building has the solid, bright, provincial confidence of a town that mattered to the surrounding valley.

It is not a grand cathedral, but it gives the plaza its shape. Step inside if the doors are open, then take a slow loop around the square.
