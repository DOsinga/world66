Mazamitla is Jalisco's mountain escape, a white-walled, red-roofed Pueblo Magico set among pine and oak forest south of Lake Chapala. Guadalajara comes here for cabins, cool air, fireplaces, horseback rides, and a weekend mood that feels much closer to the sierra than to the city.

The town centre is compact and photogenic, anchored by [Parroquia de San Cristobal](/northamerica/mexico/mazamitla/parroquia_de_san_cristobal) and a tidy plaza. The better reason to stay overnight is outside town, where [Cascada El Salto](/northamerica/mexico/mazamitla/cascada_el_salto) and the Sierra del Tigre trails pull you into the forest.

Mazamitla is easy to like and easy to overpackage. Skip the faux-alpine romance if that is not your thing; come for the cool climate, the walking, and the simple pleasure of being in a Mexican mountain town after dark.
