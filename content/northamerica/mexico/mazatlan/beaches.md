---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Mazatlan has a long string of beaches running north from the old town along the Malecon and into the hotel zone. **Olas Altas** is the original tourist beach, small and tucked under the headland; **Playa Norte** runs the longest stretch and is where joggers go in the morning; **Playa Gaviotas** and **Playa Sabalo** front the Zona Dorada and are where most resort guests end up. The water is the open Pacific — usually swimmable, but the surf can build and there are no lifeguards on most stretches.

**Stone Island** (Isla de la Piedra), reached by a five-minute panga ride from the south end of town, has the longest and quietest beach in the area — palm-fringed, far less developed than the city beaches, and lined with simple seafood palapas.
