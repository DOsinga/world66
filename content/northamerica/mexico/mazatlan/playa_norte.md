Playa Norte is the long, working stretch of beach north of Mazatlan's old centre, where the malecon runs beside fishing boats, seafood restaurants, and city monuments. It is less polished than the Zona Dorada beaches, but it feels more like Mazatlan itself.

The southern end is still used by fishermen, while other sections are good for a swim when the surf is calm. Come in the morning for a walk, or late in the day when the light drops over the Pacific and the malecon starts to fill.
