---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mazatlan is most comfortable from **November to April**, when daytime temperatures sit in the high 20s°C, humidity is low, and the rain holds off. This is also the busy season — flights and hotels fill, and Carnaval in late February or early March is the biggest party on the Mexican Pacific. Book months in advance for Carnaval week.

**May and June** are hot but dry; **July to October** is the rainy season — humid, with afternoon thunderstorms and an outside chance of a hurricane. Hotel rates are lowest then, and locals will tell you the city is at its greenest after the rains. Sport-fishing for marlin and sailfish peaks from May to October.
