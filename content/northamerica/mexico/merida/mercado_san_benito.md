Mercado San Benito is one of Mérida's big central market halls, a busy multi-level place where fresh food dominates and the daily rhythm of the city is easy to see.

It is best visited together with the surrounding market streets and nearby Mercado Lucas de Gálvez. Go for produce, local snacks, and the feel of a working Yucatán market rather than a polished shopping stop.
