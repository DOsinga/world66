Museo de Arte Popular de Yucatán fills an early 20th-century mansion with folk art, textiles, masks, pottery, and household objects from Yucatán and the rest of Mexico. It is a good small museum if you want a clearer sense of the craft traditions behind the region's markets and festival costumes.
