Museo Interactivo Sol del Niño is Mexicali's main hands-on science and children's museum. Exhibits focus on play, basic science, technology, and environmental themes, making it more interactive than the city's traditional museums.

It is most useful for families or as an indoor break from the heat. Travellers without children may find it optional, but it fills a real gap in Mexicali's things-to-do mix.
