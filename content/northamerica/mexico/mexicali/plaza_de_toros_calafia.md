Plaza de Toros Calafia is Mexicali's bullring and a recognizable circular landmark near the city centre. Even for visitors who are not attending an event, the building reflects a particular strand of northern Mexican public entertainment and urban architecture.

Its travel value depends heavily on what is scheduled, so check current events before making a special trip. As a landmark, it is a short stop; as a venue, it can be part of a fuller evening in Mexicali.
