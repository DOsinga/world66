Salon de la Fama de Mexicali is a local sports hall of fame dedicated to athletes, teams, and
sporting milestones from the city and Baja California. It is a specialist stop, but useful in a city
where baseball, boxing, and cross-border sporting culture have real local resonance.

The attraction is best for visitors already interested in Mexican sports history or looking beyond
Mexicali's food and border-city identity. It is not a long visit, but it adds a civic layer to the
usual downtown and La Chinesca circuit.
