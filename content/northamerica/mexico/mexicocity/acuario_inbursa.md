Acuario Inbursa is a polished, modern aquarium in Polanco, spread through several underground levels near Museo Soumaya and Museo Jumex. Its coral reefs, penguin habitat, and shark tanks make it a good family stop, especially on a rainy afternoon or when you want a lighter break between museums.
