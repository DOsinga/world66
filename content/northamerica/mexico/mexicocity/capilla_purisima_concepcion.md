Capilla de la Purísima Concepción is a small colonial chapel in Coyoacán, traditionally linked to the earliest years of Spanish rule in Mexico City. Its value is historical rather than grand: it is a brief but worthwhile stop while walking between Coyoacán's plazas and museums.
