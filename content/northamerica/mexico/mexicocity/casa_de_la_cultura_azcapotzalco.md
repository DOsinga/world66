Casa de la Cultura Azcapotzalco occupies a colonial-era building with a garden courtyard that is especially pretty when the jacarandas bloom. The centre hosts cultural exhibits on Azcapotzalco's Tepanec history and local figures, and the stained-glass ceiling panels are the architectural highlight.
