Casa de la Malinche is one of Coyoacan's more charged historic buildings. Tradition links it to Hernan Cortes and Malintzin, the Nahua interpreter and political figure better known as La Malinche.

The house is not open to visitors and is not heavily marked, which says something about how uncomfortable the story remains in Mexico. It is still worth pausing outside if you are walking Coyoacan for its colonial architecture and tangled early history.
