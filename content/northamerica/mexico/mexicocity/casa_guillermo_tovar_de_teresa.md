Casa Guillermo Tovar de Teresa is a preserved early-20th-century home associated with the historian and cultural chronicler Guillermo Tovar de Teresa. Its rooms, furnishings, books, and art give a close view of elite domestic life and collecting in Mexico City.

It is a compact but rewarding stop for visitors interested in historic houses, Mexican intellectual history, or quieter museums near Roma and Condesa.
