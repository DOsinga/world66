Casa Museo Leonora Carrington preserves the Condesa home and studio where the British-born surrealist artist lived for about 60 years. The house gives a more intimate view of Carrington than a conventional gallery: domestic rooms, working spaces, and the setting where much of her Mexican life unfolded.
