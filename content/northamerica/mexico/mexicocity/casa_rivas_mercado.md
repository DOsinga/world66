Casa Rivas Mercado is a restored Porfiriato-era mansion northwest of Alameda Central. The house is known for its neoclassical architecture, period furnishings, and the story of the Rivas Mercado family, including architect Antonio Rivas Mercado.

Guided visits are usually in Spanish, but the building is worthwhile for architecture-minded visitors exploring the western edge of the Centro Historico.
