Open since 1935, El Moro is Mexico City's most famous churrería and one of the few restaurants open 24 hours a day. The churros are fried to order — crisp, not soggy — and served with four varieties of hot chocolate: Spanish (thick), French (medium), Mexican (with cinnamon), and sugar-free. The original branch is in the historic centre on Eje Central, across from the Palacio de Bellas Artes. Go at 2am after a night out for the full effect.
