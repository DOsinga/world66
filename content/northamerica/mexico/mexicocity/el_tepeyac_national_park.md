El Tepeyac National Park covers Tepeyac Hill, the hill tied to the apparition story of Our Lady of Guadalupe. Pilgrims usually focus on the basilica below, but the hill and park add context to the shrine district.

The paths give a different view over northern Mexico City and make a good extension to a visit to the Basilica of Guadalupe.
