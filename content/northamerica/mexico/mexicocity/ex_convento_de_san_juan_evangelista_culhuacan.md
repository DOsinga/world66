The Ex-Convento de San Juan Evangelista Culhuacán is one of the strongest colonial sites in Mexico City's southeast, founded by Augustinian friars in the 16th century on much older Indigenous ground. The complex includes the former monastery, the church, quiet gardens, and a small museum explaining Culhuacán's role before and after the Spanish conquest.

It sits well outside the usual Roma-Condesa-Centro circuit, which is part of the point. Come if you want a less familiar piece of the city and are already heading toward Iztapalapa, Xochimilco, or the southern neighbourhoods.
