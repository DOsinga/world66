Ex-Fabrica de Pólvora is a former gunpowder factory built by the Spanish military near the end of the colonial period. The old industrial complex now hosts cultural exhibits.

It is a focused stop for travellers interested in Mexico City's lesser-known colonial and military history rather than a first-time essential.
