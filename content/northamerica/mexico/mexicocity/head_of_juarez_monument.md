The Head of Juarez Monument is a huge stylised bust of Benito Juarez, shaped with an echo of Olmec colossal heads. It is one of Iztapalapa's more distinctive landmarks and stands out for its scale as much as for the figure it commemorates.

A small museum at the site gives historical context, while the surrounding paths and benches make it a reasonable stop if you are exploring this part of eastern Mexico City. It is not a first-time highlight, but it is a real local monument rather than a routine statue.
