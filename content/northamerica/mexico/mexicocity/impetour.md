A registered Mexico City agency (RNT 04090064163) whose best-known product is the weekend day trip to the **Grutas de Tolantongo** in Hidalgo: an early coach out of the city around a quarter past five, a day in the thermal river, cave and pools, and back around ten at night. Park entry, insurance and a coordinator are included, and a two-day version with a night at the canyon is also sold.

Departures need at least ten people, with pick-up points around the city including Metro General Anaya and Boulevard Puerto Aéreo.
