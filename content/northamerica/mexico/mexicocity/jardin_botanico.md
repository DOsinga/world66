Jardin Botanico is a compact botanical garden in Chapultepec, focused on native Mexican plant life. It is a calm, low-key stop among the area's larger museums and park sights.

The garden works well as a short break while moving between Chapultepec Castle, the anthropology museum, and the Tamayo museum.
