The Jardín Botánico is a calm pocket of Chapultepec Park, planted with hundreds of Mexican native species. It is compact, but useful as a slower counterpoint to the park's big museums and crowds.

Stop in while crossing Chapultepec rather than making a special trip. The garden is best for travelers who like plants, dryland landscapes, and a quieter look at Mexico's biodiversity.
