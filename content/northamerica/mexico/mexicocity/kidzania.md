KidZania is an indoor role-play city for children, where younger visitors try scaled-down versions of adult jobs and services. It is structured, commercial, and very much aimed at families, but it can be useful on a rainy day or as a break from museums and ruins.

It sits in the south of Mexico City, so treat it as a planned family outing rather than something to add casually to a Centro Historico day.
