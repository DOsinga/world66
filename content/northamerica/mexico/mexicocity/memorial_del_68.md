Memorial del 68, inside UNAM's cultural centre at Tlatelolco, covers the 1968 student movement and the massacre that took place here before the Mexico City Olympics. It is one of the most serious museum stops in the city centre; most interpretation is in Spanish, so bring a translation app if you need one.
