Museo Casa Kahlo preserves the family home where several generations of the Kahlo family lived, including photographer Guillermo Kahlo. It is a quieter Coyoacan stop than the nearby Frida Kahlo Museum, with more focus on family history and the household context that shaped Frida Kahlo's early life.
