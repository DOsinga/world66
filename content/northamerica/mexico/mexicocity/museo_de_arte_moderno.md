Museo de Arte Moderno is one of Chapultepec's main art museums, with a collection focused on Mexican modernism and 20th-century art. Works by major muralist-era artists sit alongside later painting, drawing, sculpture, and temporary exhibitions.

It pairs naturally with Museo Tamayo, Chapultepec Castle, and the National Museum of Anthropology. The museum is a good stop for understanding how Mexican art moved from revolutionary public muralism into modern and contemporary forms.
