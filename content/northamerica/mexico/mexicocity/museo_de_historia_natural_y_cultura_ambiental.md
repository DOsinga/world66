The Museo de Historia Natural y Cultura Ambiental covers evolution, fossils, animals, plants, insects, viruses, and the environmental systems that tie them together. Its broad, hands-on subject matter makes it especially useful for families visiting Chapultepec.

It is not Mexico City's grandest museum, but it adds a different angle to a park better known for art, archaeology, and the castle.
