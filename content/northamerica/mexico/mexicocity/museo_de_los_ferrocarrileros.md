Museo de los Ferrocarrileros is a railway museum in northern Mexico City, close to the Basilica of Guadalupe. Its collection focuses on the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, when railways reshaped Mexico's movement of people, goods, and ideas.

The most tangible draw is the rolling stock, including locomotives and railway equipment. It is a good add-on if you are already visiting the Guadalupe area and have an interest in transport history.
