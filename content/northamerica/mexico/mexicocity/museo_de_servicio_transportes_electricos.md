The Museo de Servicio Transportes Electricos is a small collection of historic trams and trolleybuses, many from the early 20th century. The vehicles are displayed in a shaded courtyard beside an operating transit facility.

Visitors can sit in some of the old cars, and Spanish-speaking guides may be available to explain the history of electric transport in Mexico City.
