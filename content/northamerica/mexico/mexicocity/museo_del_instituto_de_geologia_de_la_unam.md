The Museo del Instituto de Geologia de la UNAM fills a handsome 1906 Porfirian building in Santa Maria la Ribera with minerals, fossils, geological specimens, and old scientific displays. Highlights include a mammoth skeleton, dinosaur material, and paintings by Jose Maria Velasco.

It pairs well with a walk through Santa Maria la Ribera and the nearby Kiosco Morisco. The museum is especially good if you like old-school science museums where the building is part of the pleasure.
