Museo del Objeto, usually shortened to MODO, is a small Roma museum built around everyday objects: packaging, toys, advertising, tools, souvenirs, and the strange things people keep. The exhibitions change often, so the museum can feel completely different from one visit to the next.

It is a good fit if you like design, popular culture, or museums that notice ordinary life. Check the current exhibition before crossing town, as the museum may close between shows.
