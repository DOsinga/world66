Museo Nacional de Acuarela Alfredo Guati Rojo is dedicated to watercolor painting, with Mexican and international works in a former private residence in Coyoacan. The permanent collection runs from pre-Hispanic themes to modern work, and temporary exhibitions make it a worthwhile quieter stop near the better-known Coyoacan museums.
