This Coyoacán museum focuses on Mexico's Indigenous and popular cultures through craft, music, food traditions, temporary exhibitions, and festival programming. It is a good stop if you want context beyond the famous Frida Kahlo and Trotsky houses nearby.

The bookshop and craft sales are part of the appeal, with work from artisans around Mexico. Combine it with Mercado de Coyoacán and Jardín Centenario for an easy half-day in the neighbourhood.
