The Museo Nacional de Energía y Tecnología is a hands-on technology museum west of Chapultepec. Its exhibits range from industrial-era machinery to transport, robotics, and energy displays, making it a useful stop for families and visitors interested in how machines work.

It is especially good with teenagers or anyone who wants a break from Mexico City's art and archaeology circuit without leaving the central museum belt.
