Museo Tezozomoc is an interactive science and technology museum in northwestern Mexico City, run by the Instituto Politecnico Nacional. The exhibits are aimed mainly at school-age children and teenagers, with hands-on displays rather than formal galleries.

It is not a first stop for most visitors to Mexico City, but it is useful for families staying in the north of the city or anyone looking for a child-friendly science museum away from the central museum belt.
