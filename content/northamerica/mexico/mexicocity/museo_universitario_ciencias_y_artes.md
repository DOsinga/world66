Museo Universitario Ciencias y Artes, usually shortened to MUCA, is an interdisciplinary exhibition space on the UNAM campus. Shows change by season and often sit somewhere between art, design, science and public culture, so it is worth checking the programme before crossing the city.

It works best as part of a Ciudad Universitaria visit, paired with the campus murals, the sculpture space or UNAM's larger contemporary art museum nearby.
