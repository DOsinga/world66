Museo Vivo del Muralismo fills a former 18th-century convent and government education complex with one of Mexico City's great mural cycles. Diego Rivera painted more than a hundred panels here in the 1920s, turning corridors, stairways, and courtyards into a walk through post-revolutionary Mexican ideals, workers, teachers, and everyday life.
