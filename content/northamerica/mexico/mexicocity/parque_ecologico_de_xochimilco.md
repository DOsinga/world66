Parque Ecologico de Xochimilco is a large wetland park of canals, lakes, gardens, and open green space on the edge of the famous Xochimilco chinampa landscape. It is quieter and more spread out than the busiest trajinera docks, with room for walking, picnics, playgrounds, and paddleboats.

Come here if you want a calmer look at Xochimilco's watery side. It is especially useful for families or anyone who wants the canals without turning the visit into a full boat-party outing.
