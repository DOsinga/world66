Parque Rio de Janeiro is one of Roma Norte's prettiest small squares, ringed by early 20th-century mansions and shaded by mature trees. A replica of Michelangelo's *David* stands in the central fountain, giving the park a slightly theatrical air. It is a good pause between cafes, galleries, and a walk through the surrounding streets.
