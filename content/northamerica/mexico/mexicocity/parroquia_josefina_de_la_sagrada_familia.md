Parroquia Josefina de la Sagrada Familia is a late 19th-century church in Santa Maria la Ribera. Its Byzantine-influenced design, stained glass, murals, and woodwork make it one of the neighbourhood's more rewarding architectural stops, especially as part of a walk beyond the main Centro Historico sights.
