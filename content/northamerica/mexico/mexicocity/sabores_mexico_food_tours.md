Food walks from a base on Ámsterdam, the ring-shaped street in Condesa that was a racetrack until 1924.

Their routes cover the Roma and Condesa eating, the markets, a taco-and-mezcal evening and a chocolate walk. Mexico City's street food is the best argument for the city and also the thing travellers are most nervous about; a guide who knows which stall has been there thirty years is the fastest way past that.
