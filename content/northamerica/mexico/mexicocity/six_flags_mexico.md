Six Flags Mexico City is the capital's biggest amusement park, set in the southern part of the city. It is a full-day family stop, with roller coasters, thrill rides, shows, and gentler rides for younger children.

It is not part of the historic centre circuit, so treat it as a deliberate outing rather than something to squeeze between museums. Check opening days and ride closures before heading south.
