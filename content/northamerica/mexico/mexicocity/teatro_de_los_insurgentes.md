Teatro de los Insurgentes is one of Mexico City's best-known modern theatres, opened in 1953 on Avenida Insurgentes. Even if you are not seeing a performance, the facade is worth a look for Diego Rivera's large mosaic, which mixes Mexican history, theatre, and political satire in a style that is unmistakably his.
