Templo de la Enseñanza is a small 18th-century church tucked into the Centro Historico. Its narrow Churrigueresque facade is easy to miss, but the interior is unusually rich, with nine gilded retablos that make it worth a short detour while exploring the streets around the cathedral and Templo Mayor.
