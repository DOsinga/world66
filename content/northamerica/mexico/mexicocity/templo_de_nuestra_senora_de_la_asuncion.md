Templo de Nuestra Señora de la Asunción is a 16th-century church in Milpa Alta. Its plain exterior gives little away, but the nave has high arches, colorful domes, Talavera tile, and viceregal-era artworks that make it one of the more rewarding historic churches in Mexico City's outer boroughs.
