Teuhtli Volcano is a small inactive volcano in the Milpa Alta area of southern Mexico City. The hike to the summit gives wide views over nopal fields, cornfields, and the edge of the capital's rural south.

It is a better fit for visitors who want an outdoor day away from the central museums and neighborhoods, and who are comfortable with a more local, less polished excursion.
