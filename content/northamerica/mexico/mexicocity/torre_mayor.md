Torre Mayor is one of the defining modern towers on Paseo de la Reforma, rising 225 metres beside Chapultepec. Its height and slim profile make it a useful landmark when moving between the park, Roma, Condesa, and the Reforma hotel strip.

Most travellers see it from the street rather than going inside. Look up as you walk Reforma and it helps show how Mexico City's business corridor has pushed west from the historic centre.
