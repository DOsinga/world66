The main campus of the National Autonomous University of Mexico, declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2007 for its extraordinary collection of 1950s Modernist architecture and murals. The Central Library, its exterior covered by a mosaic mural by Juan O'Gorman representing the history of pre-Hispanic and colonial Mexico, is one of the most photographed buildings in the country. Diego Rivera's mural on the Rectory Tower is equally spectacular. Free to enter and worth half a day.
