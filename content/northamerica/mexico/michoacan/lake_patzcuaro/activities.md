The boats to **Janitzio** and the other islands leave from the Muelle General outside Pátzcuaro and need no booking — buy a ticket at the jetty. A guide earns their fee in the villages around the shore, where each one keeps its own craft, and above all on the **Day of the Dead**, when the night of 1 November turns the lake into one of the most visited places in Mexico.
