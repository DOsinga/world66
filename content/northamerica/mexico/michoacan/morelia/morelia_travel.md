A travel agency in the historic centre whose Michoacán tours all leave from Morelia with transport and a certified guide, on published daily departures.

The list covers most of the state: colonial **Morelia** itself, **Pátzcuaro and Janitzio**, the copper town of Santa Clara del Cobre, **Uruapan**, **Paricutín**, **Tlalpujahua**, Zamora, and the **monarch sanctuaries** at El Rosario and Sierra Chincua in season. On the nights of 1 and 2 November they run Day of the Dead tours to the lake.
