A Morelia agency that sells beach and international packages too, but runs a long list of Michoacán day trips out of the city.

Besides the usual Pátzcuaro day (Quiroga, Tzintzuntzan, Janitzio) and the monarchs at Angangueo or Sierra Chincua, the list reaches places the others skip: the hot springs at **Los Azufres**, Zamora with Lake Camécuaro, Santa Clara del Cobre with Lake **Zirahuén**, and Paricutín via the buried church and Angahuan. In season they combine the monarchs with the Christmas bauble fair at Tlalpujahua.
