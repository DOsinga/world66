Museo de Arte Contemporáneo Alfredo Zalce gives Morelia a small but worthwhile modern-art counterpoint to its colonial centre. Named for the Michoacán artist Alfredo Zalce, it focuses on contemporary and regional work rather than the city’s more familiar stone architecture.

The museum is best for travellers who have already spent time around the cathedral, aqueduct, and old streets and want a different cultural register. Exhibitions change, so the experience depends on the current show, but the institution adds useful depth to Morelia’s arts scene.
