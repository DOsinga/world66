Plaza Villalongin is a small city square at the foot of Morelia's aqueduct, with a fountain and an easy view of the arches. It is not a reason to cross town on its own, but it fits neatly into an evening walk along the aqueduct, especially if you want a quieter pause near the cafes and bars around the eastern side of the centre.
