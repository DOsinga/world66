Paracho's guitar workshops are the town's main reason to visit. The densest cluster sits around Avenida 20 de Noviembre and nearby streets, where small shops sell everything from beginner instruments to carefully built classical and flamenco guitars.

Some workshops are simple retail spaces; others are working luthier shops where you may be able to see necks, soundboards, and bodies in progress. Be polite before photographing or handling instruments, and ask questions if you are serious about buying.
