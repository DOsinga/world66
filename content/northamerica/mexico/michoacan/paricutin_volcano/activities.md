Paricutín is a long day from anywhere but Angahuan. The usual approach is from the P'urhépecha village of **Angahuan**, where men with horses wait at the edge of town: the ride across the lava field to the buried church of San Juan Parangaricutiro takes an hour or so each way, and the full trip to the crater is most of a day — a horse to the foot of the cone, then a steep scramble up loose cinder for the last part.

Agree the price and the route before you set off, and take water, a hat and real shoes; the lava is sharp and the sun high.
