Parroquia El Sagrario is a handsome old church beside Casa de los Once Patios. Its long nave, stone arches, exterior buttresses, and weathered grey walls give it a much older feel than many buildings around it.

The adjoining cemetery, with iron gates and old headstones, makes this one of Patzcuaro's quieter historic corners.
