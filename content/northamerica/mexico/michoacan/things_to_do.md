title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Monarch Butterfly Biosphere Reserve](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/monarch_butterfly_reserve) is Michoacán's signature experience — visiting during the wintering season (November to March) to see millions of butterflies clustered on the oyamel firs is one of the most extraordinary natural spectacles in the Americas. [Paricutín Volcano](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/paricutin_volcano), which erupted from a farmer's field in 1943 and buried a church to its bell tower in lava, is reached on horseback from [Angahuan](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/angahuan), the P'urhépecha village 25 km above Uruapan. [Lake Pátzcuaro](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/lake_patzcuaro) and its islands — particularly Janitzio, with its famous Day of the Dead observances — are central to Purépecha culture. The nearby ruins at [Tzintzuntzan](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/tzintzuntzan), former capital of the P'urhépecha empire, stand on a terrace above the lake with views across the water.

The state capital [Morelia](/northamerica/mexico/morelia) rewards a day or two of colonial architecture walking, centred on the [Cathedral](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/morelia_cathedral) and the pink-stone streets of the historic centre. For nature, the [Barranca del Cupatitzio](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/barranca_del_cupatitzio) national park in Uruapan has waterfalls and lush cloud forest; the [Pacific coast](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/pacific_coast) around Caleta de Campos is rugged and largely untouristed.
