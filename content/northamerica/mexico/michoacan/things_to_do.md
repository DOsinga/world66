---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [Monarch Butterfly Biosphere Reserve](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/monarch_butterfly_reserve) is Michoacán's signature experience — visiting during the wintering season (November to March) to see millions of butterflies clustered on the oyamel firs is one of the most extraordinary natural spectacles in the Americas. [Paricutín Volcano](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/paricutin_volcano), which erupted from a farmer's field in 1943 and buried a church to its bell tower in lava, is reached on horseback from Angahuan and makes a memorable day trip from [Uruapan](/northamerica/mexico/uruapan). [Lake Pátzcuaro](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/lake_patzcuaro) and its islands — particularly Janitzio, with its famous Day of the Dead observances — are central to Purépecha culture. The state capital [Morelia](/northamerica/mexico/morelia) rewards a day or two of colonial architecture walking. For nature, the Barranca del Cupatitzio national park near Uruapan has waterfalls and cloud forest; the [Pacific coast](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/pacific_coast) around Caleta de Campos is rugged and largely untouristed.
