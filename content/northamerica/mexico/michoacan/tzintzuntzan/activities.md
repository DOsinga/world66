Tzintzuntzan is easy to see alone — the Yácatas, the old Franciscan convent and its olive trees, the craft market on the main road. Most guided trips fold it into a Pátzcuaro day, and on the night of 1 November its cemetery is one of the great sights of the Day of the Dead.
