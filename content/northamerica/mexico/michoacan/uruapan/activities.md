Uruapan is the town for [Paricutín](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/paricutin_volcano), forty minutes away by road to Angahuan. In town, the [Barranca del Cupatitzio](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/uruapan/parque_nacional_barranca_del_cupatitzio) national park and the falls at [Tzaráracua](/northamerica/mexico/michoacan/uruapan/tzararacua_falls) need nobody's help.
