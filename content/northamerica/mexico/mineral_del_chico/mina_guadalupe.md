Mina Guadalupe is one of the most accessible mine visits in town. Tours lead a short way into the old workings and give useful context for why this small mountain settlement exists at all.

It is a good first mine if you are curious rather than keen on ladders and tight spaces. Wear shoes that can handle damp stone.
