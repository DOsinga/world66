Museo de Los Tigres del Norte tells the story of the long-running norteño group whose roots are in Mocorito. The museum covers the band's rise, recordings, awards, films, and place in Mexican popular music.

It is a specialist stop, but a good one if you know the music or want a more local angle on Mocorito beyond the plaza, church, and regional history museum.
