The Regional History Museum is a compact stop for Mocorito's deeper past. Exhibits include archaeological and paleontological material from the region, including mammoth remains, stone tools, pottery, paintings, and everyday historical objects.

It is not a major museum, but it adds useful depth to the town. Pair it with the church and House of Culture rather than making a special trip only for the collection.
