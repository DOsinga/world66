The Cervecería Cuauhtémoc Moctezuma is one of Mexico's most important historic breweries, founded in 1890 and now producing some of the country's best-known beer brands including Dos Equis, Tecate, and Sol. Tours of the facility are available, taking you through the historic brewing halls, the bottling lines, and the company museum.

The old beer garden under massive oak trees, dating from the brewery's early days, is open to the public and makes a pleasant afternoon stop regardless of whether you take the tour. The on-site sports complex — built for brewery employees and still functioning — includes a baseball stadium where you can sometimes catch a game. The brewery is in the north of the city, easily reached by metro.
