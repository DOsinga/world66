The Museo de Historia Mexicana is one of the better history museums in northern Mexico, covering the full sweep of Mexican history from pre-Columbian times through the colonial period and independence to the modern era. It sits on the Macroplaza, making it easy to combine with a visit to MARCO or the cathedral.

The collections include significant pre-Columbian archaeological material — Olmec, Aztec, Maya, Toltec — alongside colonial-era religious art and artefacts from the 19th and 20th centuries. The coverage of the Mexican-American War and the Reform period is particularly strong, reflecting Monterrey's own complex relationship with the United States. Well labelled in both Spanish and English.
