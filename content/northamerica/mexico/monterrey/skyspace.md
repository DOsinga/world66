Skyspace is James Turrell's light installation on the Tecnologico de Monterrey campus. Like his other Skyspaces, it frames the changing colour of the sky, turning sunset into a quiet, slow-moving artwork.

The experience works only if you give it time and silence. Sunset visits may require advance registration, and the atmosphere is closer to a meditation room than a casual photo stop.
