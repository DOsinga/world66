Templo Expiatorio de San Luis Gonzalez is a late-19th-century Neo-Gothic church in central Monterrey. Its unusual claim is structural as much as religious: it was built entirely of concrete and cement, rather than stone or wood, which gives a familiar Gothic shape a distinctly modern material story.
