Museo de Historia Natural, often shortened to MUNAH, is a university-run natural history museum in
Morelia. Its collections introduce fossils, minerals, animals, and the biodiversity of Michoacán,
giving visitors a science-minded break from the city's colonial architecture.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop if you are traveling with children or want to understand the
landscapes around Morelia a little better. Pair it with a walk through nearby green spaces or the
university quarter rather than treating it as a whole-day museum.
