The Basilica de Nuestra Senora de los Remedios crowns a hill in Naucalpan, above the wooded slopes of Los Remedios National Park. It is an important pilgrimage church, tied to one of the oldest Marian devotions in the Valley of Mexico, and its hilltop position gives it a presence that is hard to miss.

For travellers, the basilica is most rewarding as a combined history-and-viewpoint stop. The approach makes clear how colonial religion, older sacred landscapes, and the modern metropolitan edge all overlap here. Go in daylight and pair it with the park rather than treating it as a quick roadside church.
