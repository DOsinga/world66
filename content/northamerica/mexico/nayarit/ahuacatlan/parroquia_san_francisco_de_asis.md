Parroquia San Francisco de Asis faces the main plaza and gives Ahuacatlán its clearest architectural landmark. The church dates from the colonial period and has a pink stone facade, sculptural details, and six small belfries.

The interior is modest but worth a look if the doors are open. Its ceiling mural and religious sculpture add depth to what would otherwise be a quick plaza stop.
