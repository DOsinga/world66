Plaza Central is the island's simple zocalo, with benches, trees, and the compact grid of Mexcaltitán gathered around it. It is not grand, but on an island this small the square is the natural place to pause and watch the daily rhythm.

Pair it with the nearby churches and a loop around the waterfront. The scale is the attraction: everything is only a few blocks away, and there are no cars to break the spell.
