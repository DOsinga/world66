---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

**Tepic** is the nearest city — 75 km east through the mountains. It is more a necessary hub than a destination, but it has a decent regional museum (Museo Regional de Nayarit) covering pre-Hispanic Huichol and Cora cultures, and a pleasant colonial centre around the cathedral.

**Santiago Ixcuintla** lies 40 km north and is the main town of the Mexicaltitán area. Mexicaltitán itself — a tiny island village reached by a short boat ride — claims to be the mythical Aztlán, the ancestral homeland of the Aztecs. The claim is disputed by historians but the village is genuinely atmospheric, half-flooded in the rainy season and surrounded by shrimp-filled lagoons.

**San Cayetano** and the **Singayta lagoon** area can be reached on a half-day excursion from town. The Singayta estuary is one of the best birding spots in the region and sees less tourist traffic than La Tovara.

**Puerto Vallarta** is about 170 km south and makes for a long day trip by car — feasible if you want a contrast of beach resort to quiet colonial backwater, but two to three hours each way makes it a tiring round trip.
