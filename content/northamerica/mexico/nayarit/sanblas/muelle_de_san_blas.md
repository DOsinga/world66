The Muelle de San Blas is the town's renovated pier, with concrete walkways, shade trees, benches, and a pavilion holding a small collection of surrealist modern sculptures. Vendors and boat traffic keep it from feeling too polished.

It is an easy waterfront stroll and a useful orientation point for trips toward islands and the mangrove channels around San Blas.
