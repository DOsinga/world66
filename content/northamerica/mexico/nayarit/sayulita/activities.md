Sayulita is a surf town, and its main break is the reason: a right-hander that peels over sand in front of the village, soft enough that the whole beach is full of first-timers on soft boards most mornings. The crowd is the hazard, not the wave.

For something less busy, **La Lancha** towards Punta Mita is a long point break through the jungle, and **San Pancho** up the coast is the same wave with fewer people and more rip. Several Sayulita schools shuttle to both.

Off the water, the **Islas Marietas** are an hour by boat: an uninhabited national park with the hidden beach inside a collapsed sea cave, blue-footed boobies, and a strict daily visitor cap with permits issued in advance — check your operator holds one.

**Humpback whales** are in Banderas Bay from December to March, close enough inshore to be seen from the beach. The jungle behind the village has waterfalls, and the rivers are worth a walk in the wet season.
