A family-run school a couple of streets back from Sayulita's beach, teaching all levels and running surf trips up and down the Nayarit coast when the town beach is crowded — which, in high season, it is.

Sayulita's main break is a gentle, forgiving right that breaks over sand and is about as good a place to stand up for the first time as exists. The crowd is the only hazard.
