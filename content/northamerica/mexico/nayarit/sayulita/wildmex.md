Lessons, camps and board hire from a Sayulita base and a second office at **Punta Mita**, next to the Pemex on the La Cruz highway — the Punta Mita side exists because of **La Lancha**, a long, soft right-hand point break reached through the jungle that is one of the best learner-to-intermediate waves in Mexico. They run a shuttle to it from Sayulita and San Pancho.

Three hundred boards in stock means you are not learning on whatever is left. Besides surf they run jungle hikes, snorkelling and the Marietas trip.

No street address is published for the Sayulita shop; this is pinned on the town.
