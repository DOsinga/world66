Casa Museo Juan Escutia is a small museum devoted to the Nayarit-born cadet remembered as one of Mexico's Niños Héroes. The displays focus on his life, the Battle of Chapultepec, and the patriotic story that made him a national figure.

It is a useful stop if you are already exploring central Tepic's museums and want local context beyond the larger regional collection nearby.
