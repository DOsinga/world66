This small culture centre sits across from the regional museum and is worth pairing with it. Exhibits focus on the popular and indigenous cultures of Nayarit, especially the highland communities whose art and ceremonies are still central to the state's identity.

It is not a blockbuster museum, but it gives useful context before heading into the Sierra or looking at Huichol beadwork and yarn paintings in local shops.
