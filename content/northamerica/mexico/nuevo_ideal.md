Nuevo Ideal is a dry highland town in Durango, sitting at roughly 2,000 metres in a long valley northwest of the city of Durango. It is a practical, Spanish-speaking town with little tourist polish and almost no English spoken.

The town is useful if you are travelling through rural Durango rather than following Mexico's better-known colonial or beach routes. Streets are laid out simply, distances are manageable, and Highway 23 links Nuevo Ideal with the other valley towns and the state capital.

The main natural reason to pause is [Laguna de Santiaguillo](/northamerica/mexico/nuevo_ideal/laguna_de_santiaguillo), a broad lake northeast of town that attracts birds and gives the valley its strongest landscape feature.
