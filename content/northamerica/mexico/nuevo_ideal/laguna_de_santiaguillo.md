Laguna de Santiaguillo is the main landscape feature near Nuevo Ideal, a several-mile-long lake set in the dry Durango highlands. It is close enough to town for a short detour and gives the area a sense of space that the street grid cannot.

Birdlife is the main draw. Bring binoculars if you have them, and do not expect much visitor infrastructure around the shore.
