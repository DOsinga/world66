---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Nuevo Laredo has a semi-arid climate with hot summers and mild winters. Summers (June to August) are brutally hot, with temperatures regularly exceeding 40°C — not comfortable for walking around. Winter (December to February) is the most pleasant season, with mild daytime temperatures and cool evenings.

Spring and autumn are acceptable, though spring can bring strong winds. Rain falls mainly in summer, usually as brief but intense thunderstorms.

Security conditions change and should be checked before any visit — consult current travel advisories from your government regardless of when you plan to travel.
