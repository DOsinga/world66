Nuevo León is the wealthiest state in Mexico and the least like the picture-postcard version of the country: industry, business schools, and mountains.

[Monterrey](/northamerica/mexico/nuevoleon/monterrey) is the third city and the richest, ringed by the Sierra Madre Oriental with the **Cerro de la Silla** — a saddle-shaped peak — as its emblem. The old foundry has been turned into **Parque Fundidora**, blast furnaces left standing in a public park, and the Macroplaza is among the largest city squares in the world. The **MARCO** contemporary art museum is the best in northern Mexico.

What makes the city unusual is what is twenty minutes from it. **La Huasteca** canyon is a world-class rock-climbing area inside the metropolitan area; **Chipinque** puts cloud forest on the skyline; and the **Cola de Caballo** waterfall and the Potrero Chico walls draw climbers from across the Americas.

[Bustamante](/northamerica/mexico/nuevoleon/bustamante) north of the city has a large cave system and a nut-paste sweet trade. [General Zaragoza](/northamerica/mexico/nuevoleon/general_zaragoza) sits high in the south of the state near the Tamaulipas border.

*Cabrito* — kid goat roasted over coals — is the regional dish and is taken seriously.
