Most visitors reach Bustamante on a long day out of Monterrey, two hours away. The [caves](/northamerica/mexico/nuevoleon/bustamante/grutas_de_bustamante) involve a stiff climb up the mountainside before you go underground, so wear proper shoes and start early, before the heat.
