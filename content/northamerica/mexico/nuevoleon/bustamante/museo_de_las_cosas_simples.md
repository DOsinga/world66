The Museo de las Cosas Simples is exactly the kind of modest local museum that fits Bustamante. Its collections lean toward ordinary things: domestic objects, regional dance costumes, and small pieces of daily life rather than heroic national history.

It is best treated as a short stop while walking the centre. The museum gives the town a human scale, and that matters here: Bustamante's appeal is not one giant monument, but the way caves, springs, parish life, and family memory sit close together.
