Las Presitas La Chingada is a modest local swimming area on the way toward El Salto. Its appeal is simple: shallow green water, walking paths, camping space, and a cooler place to sit when the town is warm.

Treat it as a supporting stop rather than the main reason to travel here. It works best when combined with Parque El Salto.
