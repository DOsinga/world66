Basilica de Nuestra Senora de Guadalupe is a major Catholic landmark in Guadalupe, just east of Monterrey. Pilgrims come for the shrine to the Virgin of Guadalupe, and the church is a worthwhile stop when exploring the older center of Guadalupe.
