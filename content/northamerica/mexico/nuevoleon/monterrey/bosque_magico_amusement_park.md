Bosque Magico is a family amusement park near La Pastora Park in Guadalupe, within the Monterrey metro area. Expect a mix of fairground rides, themed attractions, and kid-friendly entertainment rather than a polished destination park.

It is most useful for families who want a break from museums and city sightseeing.
