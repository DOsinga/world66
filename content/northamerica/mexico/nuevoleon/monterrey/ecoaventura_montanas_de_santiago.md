An outdoor activity centre at the entrance to Santiago, half an hour from Monterrey. It sells kayak outings on La Boca lake, bamboo-raft trips on the same water, a walk to the Cola de Caballo falls, a five-stop route around Santiago, quad bikes, horse riding, mountain biking and temazcal. Its campfire camp includes a rappel workshop. The site gives a WhatsApp number for bookings.
