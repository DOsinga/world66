Mark Grundon, an AMGA-certified rock guide, works the limestone of **El Potrero Chico** from a base in the village of Hidalgo, an hour north of Monterrey.

The speciality is the step up to **multi-pitch** — the Potrero's long bolted routes are where many climbers do their first big wall — along with beginner classes and self-rescue courses. The longest days go up 600-metre lines such as Time Wave Zero. He can arrange lodging and pick-up from Monterrey airport.

The phone is a US (Vermont) number; email is the surer route.
