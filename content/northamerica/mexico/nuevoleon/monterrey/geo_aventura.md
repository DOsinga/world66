Running adventure trips in the Sierra Madre since 1998, GEO Aventura guides the Matacanes, Chipitín and Hidrofobia canyons and the Ruta Vértigo and Ruta Ignis via ferratas. Its mountain base at Potrero Redondo, the usual start for Matacanes, has zip lines, a ropes course and rappelling. Guides carry a camera on canyon trips and share the photos afterwards at no extra cost. The site gives a WhatsApp number for bookings. The pin is on the city, not an office.
