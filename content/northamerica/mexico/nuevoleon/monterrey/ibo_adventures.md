An adventure park beside the **Cola de Caballo** waterfall: bungee, a roller zip line, a pendulum swing, hanging bridges, a canopy course and a children's zone. They publish no street address, so the pin is on the falls.

They also run the canyons — **Matacanes**, Chipitín and Hidrofobia — with Matacanes leaving from El Cercado on an eight-hour day that includes wetsuit, meals, insurance and photos. Further out they sell paragliding, paratrike and hot-air balloon flights at Montemorelos.

They publish no email and no WhatsApp; a second number, +52 81 8362 8566, sits behind the call button on their site.
