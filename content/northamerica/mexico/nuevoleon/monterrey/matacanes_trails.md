The company that explored and opened up the **Matacanes**, Hidrofobia and Chipitín canyons in the Sierra de Santiago, guiding them since 1997.

Matacanes comes three ways: the same-day descent, a version with a night at their lodge first, and a private group. The basic route has two rappels (27 and 15 metres), two underground rivers and the long run of jumps into turquoise pools that the canyon is known for; most trips start up at Potrero Redondo. They also sell the Hidrofobia and Chipitín canyons, two- and three-canyon packs, El Calabozo canyon at La Estanzuela, the Ruta Vértigo via ferrata, kayaking on La Boca lake, and canyoning courses from beginner up.

Their own base, **Aldea Matacanes**, has cabins, tipis and glamping, which makes the early start a lot easier than driving out of Monterrey before dawn.
