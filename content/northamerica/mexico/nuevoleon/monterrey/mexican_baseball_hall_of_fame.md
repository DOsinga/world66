The Mexican Baseball Hall of Fame is a modern museum in Parque Fundidora that tells the story of baseball in Mexico and its links with the major leagues in the United States. Exhibits focus on the players, teams, and moments that shaped the sport nationally.

It is an easy add-on to a Fundidora visit, especially for baseball fans already planning to see Museo del Acero or walk the park.
