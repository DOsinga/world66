Papalote Children's Museum is Monterrey's hands-on museum for younger travellers, with interactive exhibits built around science, play, and discovery. It is more practical than grand, but it fills a useful gap if you are travelling with children.

It sits close to Parque Fundidora and the city's central museum circuit, so it can be folded into a family day without much extra travel.
