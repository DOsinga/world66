Trading online as Aventureros Extremos en Serio, this outfit sells a long list of trips around Monterrey: Matacanes, Hidrofobia, Chipitín and smaller canyons, via ferratas, cave trips, hikes up the Cerro de la Silla and Chipinque, paragliding at Santiago and diving at Media Luna. There are also canyoning courses, Pico de Orizaba climbs, and kayaking on La Boca lake, including trips adapted for people with disabilities. The site gives a WhatsApp number for bookings.
