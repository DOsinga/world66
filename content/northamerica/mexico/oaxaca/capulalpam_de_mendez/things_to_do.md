---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Capulalpam is small, but it has a real day of travel in it. The parish church anchors the village, the Traditional Medicine Center gives it a distinctive cultural pull, and Los Sabinos brings in the Sierra Norte landscape.

Do not race through. The village is at its best when you combine one or two planned stops with time on the zócalo and a walk toward the edge of town for mountain views.
