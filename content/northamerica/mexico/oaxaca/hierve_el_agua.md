Mineral-rich springs that have been slowly depositing calcium carbonate over millennia, creating formations that look like frozen waterfalls pouring over a cliff edge. At the top, natural pools filled with the mineral water offer a swim with a view over the valley. It's 70km from Oaxaca city and requires a car or organised tour — the road becomes rough near the end. Go early, take sunscreen, and allow time for the walk down to the main cascade. A geological wonder that draws little of the attention it deserves.
