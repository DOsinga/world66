Cacaluta lies inside the Huatulco national park and has nothing built on it: a curve of sand with an island off its mouth, scrub forest behind, and no services of any kind. Bring water.

It is reached by boat from Santa Cruz, or on foot along a dirt track from the road — about forty minutes, and worth starting early because there is no shade at the far end.

The bay stood in for the beach in the Mexican film *Y Tu Mamá También*, which is why its name carries weight with Mexican visitors. Snorkelling is good around the rocks at either end; the centre of the bay can have surf and a pull.
