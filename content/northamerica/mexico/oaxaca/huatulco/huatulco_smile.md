A shopfront in Plaza Oaxaca on Flamboyán, open seven in the morning until ten at night, every day of the week.

Besides the standard bay tour they run the **sea turtle release** — olive ridley hatchlings, in season, at dusk, under the supervision of the camp that collected the nest — and the Ventanilla lagoon trip for crocodiles and birds. Turtle releases are regulated and the hatchlings must go in at the right hour; anyone offering one at midday is not running a real programme.
