La Entrega is the bay most people snorkel at Huatulco, and the reason is convenience: the coral starts a few metres from the sand along the right-hand side of the beach, the water is calm, and you can hire a mask on the spot.

The reef is shallow and in reasonable condition — parrotfish, angelfish, puffers, the occasional turtle — and it is a good first snorkel for anyone nervous about boats or depth.

Behind the beach is a line of palapa restaurants serving grilled fish; it gets crowded at weekends and in Semana Santa. Boats from Santa Cruz take ten minutes, or it is a short taxi ride by road.

The name — "the delivery" — comes from the handover of Vicente Guerrero to his executioners here in 1831.
