La Casa de la Ciudad is a small urban culture centre near Oaxaca's historic core, with changing exhibitions on architecture, city history, photography, and public space. It is not a blockbuster museum, but it is worth a look if you are interested in how Oaxaca has grown and how the old city is being read, mapped, and argued over today.
