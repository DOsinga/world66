Llano Grande is one of the Sierra Norte villages northeast of Oaxaca that developed community-run ecotourism around cloud forest, pine woods, and highland trails. The draw is not a single monument but the chance to walk through a cooler mountain landscape with local guides.

It works best for travelers who can give the excursion a full day or more, especially if connecting it with other Pueblos Mancomunados villages. Weather changes quickly at altitude, so bring layers even when Oaxaca city is hot.
