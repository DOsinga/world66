The principal covered market in downtown Oaxaca, a block from the zócalo, with stalls selling produce, textiles, chocolate, grasshoppers (chapulines — try them), mole paste in seven varieties, and cooked food. The food section at the back serves the most authentic and affordable Oaxacan cooking in the city: tlayudas (large crisp tortillas with black beans), memelas, and bowls of mole negro. Come at lunchtime; the market is also a good place to buy artisan mezcal at the source.
