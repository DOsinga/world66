Museo del Palacio occupies a 19th-century building near Oaxaca's central square and hosts changing exhibitions on the city's history and culture. Its best-known permanent feature is a mural by Arturo Garcia Bustos, making it an easy add-on while exploring the Zocalo, markets, and nearby museums.
