The Museo de los Pintores Oaxaqueños is an art museum in a late 17th-century building near the centre of Oaxaca. Its two floors focus on historic and modern work by Oaxacan artists, making it a useful stop for visitors who want local art beyond the larger church and archaeology circuit.
