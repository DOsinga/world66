Museo Textil de Oaxaca fills a restored 18th-century mansion with exhibitions on Oaxacan textiles, from traditional weaving techniques to contemporary design. The museum is small, focused, and useful for understanding why textiles are central to Oaxaca's craft culture.

Temporary exhibitions, talks, and workshops change the emphasis, so it is worth checking what is on before visiting.
