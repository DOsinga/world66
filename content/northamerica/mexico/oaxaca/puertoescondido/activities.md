Puerto Escondido is a surf town, and the first thing to understand is which wave you are talking about.

**Zicatela**, the Mexican Pipeline, is a world-class beach break that closes out hard and shallow. It breaks big from roughly April to October, it has a serious injury record, and it is not a wave to learn on or to swim at. Lifeguards pull people out of it regularly.

**La Punta**, at the southern end of the same beach, is where the swell wraps around a point and becomes a long, forgiving left. That is where beginners go. **Carrizalillo**, a small cove reached by a long flight of steps, is gentler still and the usual venue for a first lesson.

Away from the surf, the **bioluminescence** in the Laguna de Manialtepec north of town is extraordinary on a moonless night, and the lagoon is also serious birdwatching. Boats run out at dawn to see **dolphins, turtles and — from December to March — humpback whales**. Sport fishing leaves from Puerto Angelito, and turtle releases happen on the beaches east of town in nesting season.
