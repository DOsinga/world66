At La Punta, the southern end of Zicatela where the wave wraps around the point and slows down enough to learn on. Lessons are on soft boards, which is the right equipment and not every school here uses it.

They run surf-and-stay packages out of a villa a short walk from the water, and teach in English, Spanish and Russian.
