Comedor Monte Sinai is the more homespun side of eating in San José del Pacífico. It is the sort of place to choose for tortillas, mole, coffee, and a view rather than a designed dining room.

That simplicity is the point. After a cold walk or a long van ride up the mountain, straightforward Oaxacan cooking is exactly what you want.
