Temazcal Yolitztli is one of the more concrete ways to experience the village's ritual side without treating it as a slogan. The setting is woodland rather than spa-glossy, which suits San José del Pacífico well.

Book ahead and ask plainly what is included. A temazcal can be a serious heat experience, so go hydrated and skip it if your body is telling you not to be brave.
