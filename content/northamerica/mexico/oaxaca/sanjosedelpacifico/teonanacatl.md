Teonanácatl is a good first meal in San José del Pacífico: casual, warm, and tied to the foods people expect in the mountains. Mushroom soup is the obvious order, especially when the clouds are sitting low.

The coffee matters too. This is the kind of town where a simple cafe de olla can feel like part of the landscape rather than just a drink.
