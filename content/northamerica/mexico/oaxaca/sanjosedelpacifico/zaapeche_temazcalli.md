Zaapeche Temazcalli is another option for visitors who come to San José for ritual, steam, and mountain quiet rather than just the view. It sits away from the tight centre, which gives the experience more breathing room.

As with any ceremony or mushroom-related offering, be careful about your host, your health, and your expectations. The right approach here is respect, not consumption.
