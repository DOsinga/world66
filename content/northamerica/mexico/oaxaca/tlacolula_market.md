The Sunday market in Tlacolula de Matamoros, 30 kilometres east of Oaxaca city, has been running for centuries and is one of the most authentic indigenous markets in Mexico. Zapotec and Mixtec traders come from villages across the valleys to sell produce, woven goods, pottery, and mezcal. The market fills the streets of the entire town by 9am and winds down by 2pm. Colectivos leave from the second-class bus terminal in Oaxaca city. The 16th-century church beside the market has a remarkable Baroque interior worth seeing.
