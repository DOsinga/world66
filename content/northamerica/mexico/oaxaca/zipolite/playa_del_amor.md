Playa del Amor is the small cove at the eastern end of Zipolite, separated from the main beach by rocks and steps. It feels more tucked away than the main strand, with cliffs around it and a good look back across the coast from the path above.

It is a good late-afternoon walk, especially if you want a quieter corner after the main beach. The cove has a strong naturist reputation, so arrive with the same easy manners you would use on the larger beach.
