Mina de Acosta is a historic mine that produced silver, gold, and lead for more than two centuries, from 1717 until 1985. Tours take you through the old industrial complex, including the superintendent's house, lifts, shafts, and the tall chimney known as the Chacuaco.

The most memorable part is the descent into the mine itself, with hard hats and boots provided. It gives Pachuca's mining history a physical setting rather than leaving it as a note in a museum case.
