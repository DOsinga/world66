Mina La Dificultad is a mining museum and interpretation centre in the hills above Pachuca. The visit combines original mine buildings, mineral specimens, and exhibits on the region's silver-mining industry.

It is a useful stop for understanding the mining towns around Pachuca and Real del Monte, especially if you want context before visiting the area's older shafts and Cornish-influenced mining heritage.
