Pénjamo sits in southwestern Guanajuato, away from the state's better-known colonial showpieces. Its claim is not polish but identity: Miguel Hidalgo y Costilla, the Father of Mexican Independence, was born at nearby Corralejo, and the surrounding hills hold one of the Bajío's more interesting archaeological sites.

The clear first stop is [Zona Arqueológica de Plazuelas](/northamerica/mexico/penjamo/zona_arqueologica_de_plazuelas), where pyramidal platforms, a ball court, and carved stones sit in dry hill country. [Corralejo Ex Hacienda](/northamerica/mexico/penjamo/corralejo_ex_hacienda) gives the city its national-history weight, while the [Jardín Principal](/northamerica/mexico/penjamo/jardin_principal) and churches make the centre worth a short wander.

Pénjamo also has a rougher outdoor side in the [Sierra de Pénjamo](/northamerica/mexico/penjamo/sierra_de_penjamo), with canyons, waterfalls, and hiking country nearby. It is a strong day for travellers already in the Bajío who want something less rehearsed than the famous colonial circuit.
