Mirador Cuatro Palos is a high viewpoint above Pinal de Amoles, reached by mountain roads and a short climb. It is one of the cleanest ways to understand the Sierra Gorda's abrupt changes: oak forest, dry slopes, and folded ridges all visible in one sweep.

Sunrise is the classic time to come, when low cloud sometimes sits in the valleys. Even later in the day, the view gives Pinal de Amoles a scale that the town centre cannot.
