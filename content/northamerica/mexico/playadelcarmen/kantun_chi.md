Kantun Chi is a Riviera Maya cenote park where clear freshwater pools sit in low jungle south of Playa del Carmen. The experience is more organised than finding a roadside cenote on your own, but the sequence of open pools, limestone edges and underground river sections is still beautiful.

It works well for travellers who want a structured half-day of swimming and snorkelling without committing to a full theme-park day. Bring water shoes, arrive early if you can, and expect the best light in the open cenotes before the afternoon crowds build.
