Crabster is one of the more comfortable seafood choices on Progreso's waterfront. It is not the cheapest way to eat fish in town, but the location and broad menu make it useful when you want a sit-down meal after the beach.

Order seafood rather than trying to make the place into something it is not. Progreso is a fishing port first, and the Gulf is the kitchen's main advantage.
