Nuestra Señora de los Remedios is the yellow church that makes Cholula's skyline unforgettable. It stands on top of the pyramid hill, reached by a steady climb that pays off with views across the city, Puebla, and the volcanoes.

The church itself is small compared with the platform beneath it, but the setting is extraordinary. Visit after the archaeological zone so the climb feels like the final layer rather than just a viewpoint.
