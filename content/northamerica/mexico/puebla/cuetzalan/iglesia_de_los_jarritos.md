Iglesia de los Jarritos is a narrow neo-Gothic church whose tall tower is decorated with the small clay jars that give it its name. The church is reached through its cemetery, which adds to the atmosphere when fog settles over Cuetzalan.

It is a short walk from the centre and pairs well with the parish church and the town's steep market streets.
