Las Brisas is one of the easiest waterfall outings from Cuetzalan. The path drops through humid vegetation to a pool and cascade, with enough drama to justify the climb back out.

Wear shoes that can get wet and ask locally about current access, especially after heavy rain. The falls are close to town, but the terrain is still mountain country.
