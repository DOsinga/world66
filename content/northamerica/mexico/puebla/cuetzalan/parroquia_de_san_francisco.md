The parish church anchors Cuetzalan's centre. Its pale facade and high tower rise above the market streets, giving the town its most recognisable silhouette.

The church is best seen as part of the square rather than as a standalone monument. Come when the market is active and the steps, stalls, and surrounding lanes are full of local life.
