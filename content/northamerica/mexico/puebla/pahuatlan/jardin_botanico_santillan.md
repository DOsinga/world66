Jardín Botánico Santillan is a modest private garden rather than a grand botanical institution. Its value is local: endemic plants, fruit orchards, and tastings of wines and liqueurs made from what grows here.

It is most worthwhile if you are already in town for the craft villages and want a quiet hour with the region's plants.
