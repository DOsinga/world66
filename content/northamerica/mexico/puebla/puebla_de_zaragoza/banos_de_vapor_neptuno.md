Baños de Vapor Neptuno is a traditional steam bath in Puebla, with private sauna rooms as well as a common sauna. It is a practical stop if you want a local, low-key bathhouse rather than a hotel spa.

The appeal is simple: hot steam, modest prices, and a very everyday side of the city. Check current opening hours before going, as bathhouse schedules can change.
