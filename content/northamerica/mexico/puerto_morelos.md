Puerto Morelos sits between [Cancun](/northamerica/mexico/cancun) and [Playa del Carmen](/northamerica/mexico/playadelcarmen) on the Riviera Maya, but feels about a world away from either. It is a small fishing town that has grown into a low-key traveller base — still centred on a modest town square fronting the beach, with a lighthouse that leans slightly to one side thanks to a long-ago hurricane and never got fixed.

The reef offshore is part of the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef, the second largest in the world, and it is protected as a national maritime park. No motorised watersports are allowed, which keeps it quiet and healthy. Several dive shops work out of the town. The **Yaax Che Botanical Garden** on the highway south of town is one of the better places on the coast to see the native flora of the Yucatan, and the **Ruta de Cenotes** — a string of freshwater sinkholes — begins just inland.

The town square has a good spread of restaurants for its size, ranging from no-frills Mexican holes in the wall to fine dining that draws visitors from the resort strip. It is the right size to walk everywhere, and traffic is light enough that the horse carriage offering rides around the square is not just decorative.
