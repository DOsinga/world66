  bladder, which sold for tens of thousands of dollars on Chinese markets. The

At the southern edge of Puerto Peñasco, the estuary of Estero Morua is part of the Pinacate and Gran Desierto de Altar UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Where the town's resort development ends, the tidal flats begin: an expanse of mudflat, salt marsh, and shallow lagoon that at low tide extends for kilometres. It is one of the most important shorebird habitats in the region.

The CEDO (Intercultural Center for the Study of Deserts and Oceans) runs programmes from a station on the estuary edge and leads guided tide-pool walks during low tides. These walks — exploring the pools for sea stars, crabs, urchins, and the small fish stranded by the retreating water — are some of the best nature experiences available in Rocky Point and are particularly good with children. Check the CEDO website for timetables, as they only run at the low tides that expose the rocky reef.
