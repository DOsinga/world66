---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The marina strip has the largest concentration of restaurants, mostly aimed at the international tourist market. Quality is generally decent; prices are higher than you'd pay in a Mexican town. A few places stand out from the generic run.

**Café Ole International** sits right on the marina and serves straightforward Mexican and international food — grilled fish, quesadillas, breakfasts — at reasonable prices for the setting. It is a reliable choice when you want to eat with a view of the boats without being gouged.

**Papaya Republic** is a small garden restaurant a short walk from the marina that does better-than-average tacos and fresh ceviches. The outdoor setting is pleasant in the evening when the heat drops.

**John Gray's Kitchen** in nearby [Playa del Carmen](/northamerica/mexico/playadelcarmen) is worth the short drive if you want a genuinely good dinner — but for something special without leaving Puerto Aventuras, the marina restaurants serve well enough for a resort setting.
