---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The nearest international airport is [Cancún](/northamerica/mexico/cancun), about 100 km north. From the airport, take the ADO bus to [Playa del Carmen](/northamerica/mexico/playadelcarmen) and then a colectivo (shared minivan) south along Highway 307 — ask to be dropped at the Puerto Aventuras entrance. The whole journey takes around two hours. Taxis from Cancún airport are available and faster but considerably more expensive.

By car, Puerto Aventuras is directly off Highway 307 about 20 km south of Playa del Carmen. The entrance is well signed. Renting a car in Cancún or Playa del Carmen is the most flexible way to explore the area, especially if you plan day trips to [Tulum](/northamerica/mexico/tulum) or the inland cenotes.

Colectivos running between Playa del Carmen and Tulum pass the Puerto Aventuras turnoff every 20 minutes or so and are cheap. The walk from the highway to the marina is about 1 km.
