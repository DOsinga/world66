---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Puerto Aventuras offers a compact but varied menu of activities, most of them centred on the water. The marina is the social and practical hub — fishing charters, dive operators, and dolphin programmes all depart from here.

The [CEDAM Shipwreck Museum](/northamerica/mexico/puertoaventuras/cedam_museum) is a small but genuine collection of artefacts recovered from Spanish galleons and colonial-era wrecks off the Yucatán coast. It is surprisingly absorbing and easy to overlook.

Offshore, the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef — the second longest in the world — runs within easy reach. Dive and snorkel trips into the open reef are straightforward to organise from the marina. But the area's real diving speciality is the cenotes: [Cenote Kantun-Chi](/northamerica/mexico/puertoaventuras/cenote_kantun_chi), a short drive inland, offers easy access to a network of flooded cave passages with clear water and dramatic light.

[Dolphin Discovery](/northamerica/mexico/puertoaventuras/dolphin_discovery) operates one of its larger Riviera Maya facilities here, with a range of programmes from shallow-water encounters to full swim experiences. It is a polished operation and popular with families.

For something wilder, [Aktun-Chen Natural Park](/northamerica/mexico/puertoaventuras/aktun_chen) — about 7 km south — is a privately managed nature reserve with a dry cave system, a cenote, and a zip-line circuit through the jungle canopy.
