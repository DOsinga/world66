The mountainous coast and the Sierra Madre behind it offer some of the best day-trip options on the Mexican Pacific. The south-shore beach villages of [Las Ánimas](/northamerica/mexico/lasanimas) and [Yelapa](/northamerica/mexico/yelapa) are reached by water taxi from Los Muertos Pier or Boca de Tomatlán; the [Marietas Islands](/northamerica/mexico/marietasislands) snorkelling trip leaves from Punta Mita. Inland, the colonial mining town of [San Sebastián del Oeste](/northamerica/mexico/sansebastiandeloeste) is a 90-minute drive into the mountains. Up the coast, surf-town [Sayulita](/northamerica/mexico/sayulita) and the Four Seasons peninsula at [Punta Mita](/northamerica/mexico/puntamita) round out the standard rotation.
