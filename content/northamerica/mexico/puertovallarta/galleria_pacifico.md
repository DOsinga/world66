Gallería Pacifico is a long-running Puerto Vallarta gallery, open since the late 1980s and focused on Mexican and regional artists. It is small enough for a casual stop, but useful if you want to put names and context to the art scene behind the city's public sculptures and studio signs.

The gallery is also associated with the seasonal Public Sculpture Walking Tour on the Malecón. If you are already exploring central Vallarta, it is an easy cultural detour rather than a destination that needs half a day.
