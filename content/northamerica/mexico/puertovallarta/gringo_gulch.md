Gringo Gulch is a steep residential pocket above central Puerto Vallarta where Elizabeth Taylor, Richard Burton, and other Hollywood names bought homes in the 1950s and 1960s. It is best treated as a short wander for views, hillside lanes, and flashes of mid-century glamour rather than a formal sight.
