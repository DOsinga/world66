A small beach village on the southern shore of Banderas Bay, accessible only by boat. The beach itself is a long stretch of soft white sand backed by palapa restaurants serving grilled fish and cold beer. The water is calm enough for kids and there is good snorkelling at the rocky points at either end of the bay.

Most visitors come on a day trip from Puerto Vallarta — water taxis leave Boca de Tomatlán roughly every half hour from morning until late afternoon — and the village fills up around midday with the lunch crowd. Stay past 3 p.m. and most of them clear out, leaving you with the beach almost to yourself for the last hour before the return boats. A jungle trail connects Las Ánimas with Quimixto, 30 minutes' walk further south, where there is another waterfall to reward the hike.
