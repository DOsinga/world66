The narrow peninsula at the northern tip of Banderas Bay, about 45 minutes north of central Puerto Vallarta. The point itself was a humble fishing village called Corral del Risco until the 1990s, when most of the surrounding land was sold off to luxury resorts (the Four Seasons, the St Regis) and gated golf communities. The original village still exists at the eastern edge, with palapa restaurants serving the fishermen's catch.

For day visitors the draw is the Marietas Islands offshore — a small national park where Jacques Cousteau filmed in the 1960s and where the famous "hidden beach" (Playa del Amor) sits inside a collapsed cavern. Permit numbers are now strictly limited to protect the reef; tours must be booked through licensed operators. The two Jack Nicklaus golf courses are open only to resort guests but the public beaches at Anclote and Sayulita just up the coast are excellent.
