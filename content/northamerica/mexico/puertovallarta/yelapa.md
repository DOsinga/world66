A small fishing village 45 minutes by boat south of Puerto Vallarta, tucked into a sheltered cove on the southern arm of Banderas Bay. The setting is the draw: a wide crescent beach lined with palapa restaurants, jungle-covered cliffs rising directly behind, and two waterfalls within a short hike of the village. The upper waterfall, Cascada del Cielo, is a 100-metre plunge that requires a guided horseback trip; the lower one is an easy 20-minute walk from the beach.

Several restaurants serve the day-tripper crowd; the better experience is to stay overnight in one of the small inns or rented casitas, when the boats leave and the village empties out. Boats run from Los Muertos Pier and from Boca de Tomatlán several times daily. The local women still hawk homemade coconut and lime pies along the beach — they are excellent.
