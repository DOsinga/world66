A Bernal outfit whose signature is an hour in an open safari vehicle around the base of the **Peña de Bernal** with a guide, stopping at the Cueva de la Calavera; it runs every day from half past eight. They publish no address, so the pin is on the village.

They also sell a guided walk, a village tour, night tours, temazcal, balloon flights and the cheese-and-wine route out to the Freixenet cellars. Climbing is on the list at a price on request — ask which face, since the state closed the south face and the summit in 2024.

WhatsApp is the only contact they publish, apart from a form.
