Freixenet's Mexican estate is one of the best-known wineries in central Mexico, opened by the Spanish sparkling-wine company in 1979. Tours focus on the cellars and production, but the real pleasure is tasting how broad the local range is, from dry sparkling wines to sweeter bottles rarely seen outside Mexico.
