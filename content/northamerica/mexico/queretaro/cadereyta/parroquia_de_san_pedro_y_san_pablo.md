The parish of San Pedro and San Pablo is the natural starting point in Cadereyta's centre. It gives the town its colonial frame: plaza, church front, side streets, and the slow pace of a small Queretaro market town.

Do not expect a blockbuster church. Its value is in setting the scene before you head to the cactus gardens or onward into the wider municipality.
