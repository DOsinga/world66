Quinta Fernando Schmoll is a specialist cactus and succulent nursery rather than a polished museum. That is what makes it interesting. Founded in 1920, it has spent generations growing, studying, and showing desert plants from Mexico and beyond.

Pair it with the regional botanical garden if you want Cadereyta to make sense. One gives the semi-desert context; the other shows the collector's obsession up close.
