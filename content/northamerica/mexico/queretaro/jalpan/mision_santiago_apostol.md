Mision Santiago Apostol is Jalpan's essential sight. Built in the eighteenth century under the Franciscan mission campaign led by Junipero Serra, it belongs to the UNESCO-listed group of Sierra Gorda missions and has one of the most expressive church facades in the region.

Look closely at the surface rather than treating it as a quick photo stop. The facade is dense with saints, foliage, Franciscan symbols, and local visual language, a carved argument between conversion, craft, and mountain culture. Inside, the church is quieter, but the exterior is the reason to come.
