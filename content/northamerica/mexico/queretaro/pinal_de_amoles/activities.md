Pinal de Amoles is the high, cold gateway to the Sierra Gorda. The [Cuatro Palos](/northamerica/mexico/queretaro/pinal_de_amoles/mirador_cuatro_palos) viewpoint and the [El Chuveje](/northamerica/mexico/queretaro/pinal_de_amoles/cascada_el_chuveje) waterfall are both near town. [Puente de Dios](/northamerica/mexico/queretaro/pinal_de_amoles/puente_de_dios), its best-known waterfall and natural bridge, was reported closed to the public in 2026 — check locally before planning around it.
