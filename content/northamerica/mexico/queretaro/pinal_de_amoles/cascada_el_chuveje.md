Cascada El Chuveje is a popular walk through forest and along clear water to a tall single-drop waterfall. The pool below is cold and inviting after the dusty road in.

It pairs naturally with Puente de Dios if you have a long day and private transport, but do not rush both if the weather is poor. The best season is after rain, when the waterfall has real force and the surrounding forest is green.
