Parroquia San Jose is the main church on Pinal de Amoles' central plaza. The 18th-century building has a simple facade, clean lines, and a colorful exterior that stands out in the mountain town center.

It is an easy stop while walking around the plaza before heading out to the area's waterfalls and viewpoints.
