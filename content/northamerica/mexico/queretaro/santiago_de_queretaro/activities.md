---

Querétaro's historic centre is a UNESCO site and best walked, but two things are worth booking: the **legends walk** at night, with actors in costume, and a day out on the **cheese and wine route** to the dairies, vineyards and cellars between [Tequisquiapan](/northamerica/mexico/queretaro/tequisquiapan) and Bernal — which, with tastings involved, is better done without driving.

Further afield, the Peña de Bernal is an hour away and the Sierra Gorda missions a long day or, better, an overnight trip.
