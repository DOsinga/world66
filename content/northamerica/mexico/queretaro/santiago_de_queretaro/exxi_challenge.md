Founded by climbers in 2008 and working out of Los Olvera, on the south side of the Querétaro metro area.

The one-day **canyoning at Vaqueros** has slides, jumps and a 30-metre waterfall rappel. The **Sierra Gorda** adventure packs mountain biking, a 45-metre waterfall rappel, tubing, canyoning and the hike through the Cañón del Paraíso into one trip. They also run a bike-and-caves tour with a local community, team-building events and road trips around Mexico.
