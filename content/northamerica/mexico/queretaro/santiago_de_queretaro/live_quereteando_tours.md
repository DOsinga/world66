A centre-of-town operator with a long menu. In the city: the tram and the Querebus, horse-drawn carriages, a legends walk and an open-top Skyline bus. Out of it, all leaving from Querétaro: the **cheese and wine** route, Amealco and its Otomí dolls, Tequisquiapan's opals and vineyards, the **Sierra Gorda**, the Independence route, and a day by road to San Joaquín for its caves and forest.
