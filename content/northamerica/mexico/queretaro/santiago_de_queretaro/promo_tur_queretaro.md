A destination company of nearly thirty years that created two of the state's set pieces: the route through the Franciscan **missions of the Sierra Gorda**, and **Querétaro y sus Leyendas**, the night walk through the historic centre with actors in costume, which the UN's tourism organisation singled out as good practice in 2013.

They also run historic-centre walks, Sierra Gorda and Huasteca trips, and conference logistics.
