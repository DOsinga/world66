A tour and transport company of more than twenty years, selling day trips out of Querétaro at published prices. They publish no address, so the pin is on the city.

The **cheese and wine** day goes down into the Freixenet cellars, 25 metres underground. Others go to the Peña de **Bernal** for half a day, the Independence towns, Amealco, and the long day north into the **Sierra Gorda**; they also book balloon flights and the city's trams, and run private transfers.
