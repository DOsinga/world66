Tequisquiapan — Tequis to nearly everyone — is a small colonial town in the dry hills of southern Querétaro, built around a broad plaza and the pink-and-white church of Santa María de la Asunción. Its streets are cobbled and arcaded, and on a weekday it is calm in the way that the bigger Pueblos Mágicos seldom are.

It is also the natural base for the **Ruta del Arte, Queso y Vino**. The vineyards, sparkling-wine cellars and cheese dairies of the region are scattered within half an hour of town, between here and the Peña de [Bernal](/northamerica/mexico/queretaro/bernal), and Tequis has the hotels, restaurants and tour offices to go with them. The other draw is the sky: hot-air balloons go up most mornings at sunrise over the valley.

Come for a couple of nights. Querétaro city is about an hour away, and [San Juan del Río](/northamerica/mexico/queretaro/san_juan_del_rio) is just down the road. The wine and cheese fair at the end of May and the beginning of June is the busiest time of the year.
