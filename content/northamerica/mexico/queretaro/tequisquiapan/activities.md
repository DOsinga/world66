Two things are worth booking here. **Balloon flights** go up at sunrise over the town and the valley, so this means an early start, and weather cancels flights often — ask the cancellation policy before paying. And the **cheese and wine route**: the dairies, vineyards and the Freixenet cellars are spread out along country roads, and with tastings at every stop it is far better done with a driver than without.
