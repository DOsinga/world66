**Sunrise balloon flights** over Tequisquiapan and the vineyards around it. The company has built balloons since 1982, flies a fleet of more than twenty, and trains its pilots with instructors authorised by Mexico's civil aviation authority. The pin is on the town; their office is in Pedregal de Hacienda Grande.

Flights last 45 minutes to an hour and include the transfer to the launch field and a safety briefing. You can fly shared, as a couple, or with a private basket for a group, and there is a package that adds a horse ride after landing. As everywhere, the flight goes at first light or not at all, and weather cancels some mornings.
