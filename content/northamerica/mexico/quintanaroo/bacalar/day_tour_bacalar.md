A small lakefront operation selling the sailboat trip across the lagoon, shared or private, along with pontoon charters for larger groups.

The office works Thursday to Tuesday, ten until three, which is worth knowing: Wednesday is closed and the lagoon is best in the morning anyway, before the afternoon wind flattens the colour out.
