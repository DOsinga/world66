---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bacalar's strongest experiences are on or beside the lagoon. Kayak early, swim where access is allowed, and avoid stepping on stromatolites, the living mineral formations that help make parts of the shoreline so unusual.

In town, the fort and central plaza are enough for a short wander. The better days are slow ones: a morning paddle, a long swim, lunch in town, and sunset over the water.
