Cenote Choo-Ha is one of the cave cenotes a short drive from the ruins, with clear water under a low limestone ceiling. It feels very different from open-air sinkholes on the coast: darker, quieter, and more like swimming inside the geology of the peninsula.

Bring cash and a towel. If you still have energy, nearby Tamcach-Ha and Multum-Ha can be combined with it.
