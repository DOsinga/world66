Cozumel diving is drift diving: the current runs north along the island's leeward side and you go with it, over walls that drop into the channel. **Palancar**, **Santa Rosa** and **Columbia** are the names everyone knows, and the whole reef has been a national marine park since 1996, which is why it still looks the way it does.

This is a small Cozumel shop running those sites daily. The island's reefs suffered badly from stony coral tissue loss disease after 2018, and some sites are noticeably changed from older photographs — worth knowing before you compare what you see with what you were sold.
