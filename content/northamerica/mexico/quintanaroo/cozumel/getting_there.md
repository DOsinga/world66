---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** Cozumel International Airport (CZM) sits a few kilometres north of San Miguel and handles direct flights from a dozen US cities, plus Mexico City, Cancún, and seasonal European charters. A taxi to town is about US$15; airport collectivos and hotel shuttles also run.

**By ferry.** Two competing ferry lines — Ultramar and Winjet — run roughly hourly between Playa del Carmen on the mainland and the San Miguel passenger pier. The crossing takes 45 minutes and costs around US$15–20 each way. It is straightforward, comfortable, and the best option if you are combining Cozumel with the Riviera Maya.

**Car ferry** from Calica (south of Playa del Carmen) takes vehicles to Cozumel four times a day. Cars are not strictly necessary on the island — taxis and scooter rentals are plentiful — and the rental will probably be cheaper in San Miguel than driving your own across.
