Punta Cocos is the island's easy sunset ritual. The beach is shallow and relaxed, with hammocks in the water and a westward view that makes it a natural place to end the day.

On dark, calm nights, tours also come here and to nearby southern beaches for bioluminescence. It is a natural phenomenon, not a guaranteed show, so keep expectations flexible.
