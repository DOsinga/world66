Five kilometres south of Mahahual on the coast road towards Xcalak, which is far enough from the cruise-ship pier to make the difference on a day when a ship is in.

The reef here is the Mesoamerican barrier running south towards Belize, and the diving off this stretch is shallow, healthy and almost empty. Boats go from the shop's own beach. No WhatsApp number is published.
