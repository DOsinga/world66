A shop built into the Hacienda Morelos on the seafront, running the Puerto Morelos reef, the cenotes inland and the usual course ladder, every day from eight until five.

The reef park's rules are strict and worth knowing before you arrive: licensed guides only, no sunscreen in the water, no touching, and a cap on boats at each mooring. They are the reason the coral is still here.
