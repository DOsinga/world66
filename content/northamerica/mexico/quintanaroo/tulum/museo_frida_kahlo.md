Museo Frida Kahlo is an immersive museum near the Tulum archaeological zone, built around Frida Kahlo's life, image, and influence on later women artists. The presentation is interactive rather than a traditional collection of original works.

It can make a compact add-on before or after the ruins, especially for visitors interested in Mexican art and pop-cultural interpretations of Kahlo.
