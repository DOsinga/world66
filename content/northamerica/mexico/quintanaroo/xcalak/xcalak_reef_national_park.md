Xcalak Reef National Park protects the nearshore water that makes the village worth the trip. The reef sits close to land, with shallow, clear sections suited to snorkelling as well as deeper areas for diving.

The park also includes mangroves and coastal lagoon habitat, so it is more than a pretty strip of coral. Go with a local operator and be patient with conditions; wind can decide what kind of day you get.
