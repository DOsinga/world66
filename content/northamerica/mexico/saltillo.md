Saltillo is the capital of Coahuila state in northern Mexico, sitting at about 1,600 metres in a high desert basin surrounded by mountains. It's one of Mexico's most important industrial cities — the automotive cluster here produces a significant share of Mexico's vehicles — but also has genuine colonial heritage at its centre. The [baroque cathedral](/northamerica/mexico/saltillo/saltillo_cathedral) on the [Plaza de Armas](/northamerica/mexico/saltillo/plaza_de_armas) is considered one of the finest in northern Mexico.

The city has a cooler climate than the lowland north thanks to its altitude, and the surrounding desert and mountain landscape is scenic. The [Sarape de Saltillo](/northamerica/mexico/saltillo/sarape_market), a distinctive striped blanket, is the city's most famous craft product and is still woven here. The [Museo del Desierto](/northamerica/mexico/saltillo/museo_del_desierto) is the best museum in the region, with excellent dinosaur fossils from Coahuila. From [Cristo de las Noas](/northamerica/mexico/saltillo/cristo_de_las_noas), a giant hilltop Christ statue on the city's edge, you get fine views across the desert basin. Saltillo is not a major tourist destination but is a worthwhile stop for those crossing northern Mexico.
