The Cristo de las Noas is a giant white statue of Christ on outstretched arms, erected on a rocky hilltop — the cerro de las Noas — that rises dramatically above the city's southwest side. The approach by a winding road or a long flight of steps offers increasingly expansive views over Saltillo and the high desert landscape surrounding it.

At the hilltop there is a small chapel and a broad terrace. The statue is illuminated at night and visible from across the city. It is a popular destination for local families on weekends and for anyone wanting a panoramic view of Saltillo.
