---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Saltillo has a distinctive northern Mexican food culture — hearty, meat-centred, and proud of local products. Machaca (dried shredded beef), cabrito (roast kid goat), and flour tortillas are local staples. The city's restaurant scene is more varied than its tourist traffic suggests, with good traditional restaurants in the centre and upscale dining in the newer districts.
