---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Saltillo has an international airport, Plan de Guadalupe Airport (SLW), about 14 km northeast of the city centre. Aeromexico and Volaris operate regular flights from Mexico City, Monterrey, and several US cities. Monterrey, 85 km north, is the nearest major hub with more international connections.

By road, Saltillo sits on the main highway corridor between Monterrey and Mexico City (via Matehuala and San Luis Potosí). Omnibus de Mexico and other first-class bus companies connect the city well with the rest of the country; the Central de Autobuses is the main bus terminal. The drive from Monterrey takes about an hour.
