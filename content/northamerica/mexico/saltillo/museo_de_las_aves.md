The Museo de las Aves de México (Museum of the Birds of Mexico) is a pleasantly surprising institution for bird enthusiasts, housed in a converted colonial building near the city centre. The collection covers the extraordinary diversity of Mexican birdlife, with taxidermy specimens, interactive displays, and information on migration routes and threatened species.

Mexico is one of the world's most biodiverse countries for birds, and the museum puts this in context with exhibits covering the different ecological zones — tropical forests, deserts, wetlands, and highland forests. The Chihuahuan Desert region around Saltillo is an important area for migrating raptors and sparrows. The museum is small but well laid-out, and admission is modest.
