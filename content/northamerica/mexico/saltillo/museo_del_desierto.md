The Museo del Desierto is one of the best natural history museums in Mexico, dedicated to the ecology and palaeontology of the Chihuahuan Desert — the largest desert in North America. The museum occupies a large modern building on the edge of the city and covers everything from dinosaur fossils to living desert ecosystems, with excellent displays on the plants and animals that survive in arid Coahuila.

The fossil collection is outstanding, drawing on rich Mesozoic rock beds in the region. There are life-size dinosaur reconstructions, a botanical garden of Chihuahuan Desert plants, and vivarium sections with living reptiles and other desert species. Admission around 130 pesos. Open Tuesday to Sunday.
