The Recinto de Juárez is a small museum and memorial housed in the building where President Benito Juárez operated his government during the French Intervention (1864–1867), when he was forced out of Mexico City by imperial forces. The museum documents this turbulent period in Mexican history and Saltillo's role as a base for the republican cause.

The building retains much of its nineteenth-century character and contains period furniture, documents, and portraits. Entry is free and the museum provides good historical context for understanding Saltillo's political significance. Staff can provide guided tours on request.
