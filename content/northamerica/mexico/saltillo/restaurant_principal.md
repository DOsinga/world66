Restaurant Principal is one of the oldest and most respected traditional restaurants in Saltillo's historic centre, serving northern Mexican staples in a comfortable setting near the Plaza de Armas. The menu focuses on cabrito (roast kid goat in various preparations), machaca with eggs, and other regional classics. The handmade flour tortillas are excellent.

The restaurant draws a mix of locals, business travellers, and the occasional tourist, which is usually a good sign. The service is professional and the pricing is reasonable for the quality. A full meal with drinks runs about 200–300 pesos per person.
