Saltillo is famous throughout Mexico for the Sarape de Saltillo, a richly coloured woven blanket with a distinctive diamond-centred pattern that has been produced here since the colonial era. The city has several active workshops where you can watch weavers at traditional wooden looms and buy directly from the producers. The most established are in the Colonia Republica neighbourhood east of the centre.

The genuine article uses hand-spun wool or cotton and takes weeks to produce; prices reflect the labour. Cheaper machine-made imitations are widely sold as souvenirs across Mexico, but the hand-woven Saltillo sarape has a different weight and texture that experienced eyes quickly distinguish. Workshop visits are usually free and weavers are generally happy to demonstrate their techniques.
