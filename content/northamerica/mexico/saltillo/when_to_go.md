---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Saltillo's high altitude (about 1,600 metres) gives it a significantly cooler and more pleasant climate than the lowland north of Mexico. Summers are warm but not brutal — highs around 28°C — and winters can be genuinely cold, with occasional frosts and even snow. Rain falls mostly between June and September.

The best time to visit is spring (March to May) or autumn (September to November), when temperatures are comfortable for walking the centre and the surrounding sierra is green from summer rains. The city's main cultural events include the Festival de las Artes Escénicas in October and various celebrations around the national holidays in September.
