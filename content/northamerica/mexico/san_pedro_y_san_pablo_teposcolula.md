San Pedro y San Pablo Teposcolula, usually shortened to Teposcolula, is a small Mixtec town in the mountains northwest of [Oaxaca](/northamerica/mexico/oaxaca). It is quiet by Mexican Pueblo Magico standards, but it has one exceptional reason to come: a huge 16th-century Dominican complex with one of the finest open chapels in Oaxaca.

The town's interest is compact and old. The [convent church](/northamerica/mexico/san_pedro_y_san_pablo_teposcolula/convento_de_san_pedro_y_san_pablo) faces the centre, the [Casa de la Cacica](/northamerica/mexico/san_pedro_y_san_pablo_teposcolula/casa_de_la_cacica) preserves a rare Mixtec-Spanish noble house, and [Yucundaa](/northamerica/mexico/san_pedro_y_san_pablo_teposcolula/yucundaa) marks the pre-Hispanic settlement that the Spanish town replaced.

This is not a quick add-on from Oaxaca city unless you have a car and an early start. It works best for travellers already exploring the Mixteca Alta, or for anyone who likes colonial architecture with indigenous history still visible in the stones.
