Museo de los Altos de Chiapas occupies the former Santo Domingo convent and gives useful context for the history and cultures of the Chiapas highlands. Its exhibits cover regional history, art, and daily life.

The same complex includes the Centro de Textiles del Mundo Maya, with a strong collection of historic and contemporary Maya textiles from Chiapas and Guatemala. It pairs naturally with the Santo Domingo church and nearby market.
