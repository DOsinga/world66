---
title: "Festivals"
type: section
---

**Fiestas de San José** take place in mid-March around the feast day of the town's patron saint, Joseph. The celebrations centre on Plaza Mijares with music, dancing, food stalls, and a procession. This is the main local festival of the year and gives the town a much more authentic energy than the usual tourist season activities.

**Día de los Muertos** (1–2 November) is observed here with particular sincerity — the cemetery on the edge of town fills with families on the evening of 1 November bringing marigolds, food offerings, and candles to the graves of relatives. The altars (ofrendas) in private homes and some restaurants are elaborate and worth asking to see.

**Carnival** in February is smaller than in cities like La Paz or Mazatlán but still lively, with parades and music in the streets around the plaza in the days before Lent.
