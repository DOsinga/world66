San José del Pacífico is a small mountain village high in Oaxaca's Sierra Sur, where clouds slide through pine forest and the road seems to spend more time turning than going forward. It used to be a natural overnight pause between Oaxaca City and the coast; now that faster routes bypass it, the people who still come usually mean to be here.

The identity is clear: mist, mushrooms, temazcal, forest trails, and long views toward the Pacific when the weather opens. The town is famous for psychedelic mushrooms, but it is better treated as a mountain retreat than a novelty stop. Go carefully, use local guides or respected hosts for any ceremony, and do not confuse legality with wisdom.

For a simple day, walk the ridges above town, book a traditional temazcal such as [Temazcal Yolitztli](/northamerica/mexico/sanjosedelpacifico/temazcal_yolitztli), and eat something warming at [Teonanácatl](/northamerica/mexico/sanjosedelpacifico/teonanacatl) or [Comedor Monte Sinai](/northamerica/mexico/sanjosedelpacifico/comedor_monte_sinai). San José is not polished, but on a clear evening above the clouds it has a mood few Oaxacan towns can match.
