Ex-Hacienda Dolores Trompeta stands near the entrance to the Ogarrio Tunnel, the narrow passage that leads into Real de Catorce. The old hacienda buildings make a scenic stop before entering town, with stonework and mountain views that fit the area's mining history.

It is a quick visit, but worthwhile if you are arriving by road and want a sense of the landscape around the tunnel.
