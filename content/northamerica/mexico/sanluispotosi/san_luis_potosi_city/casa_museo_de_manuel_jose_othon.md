Casa Museo de Manuel Jose Othon preserves the birthplace of one of San Luis Potosi's best-known poets. The rooms are furnished with 19th-century decorative arts, period furniture, and personal objects connected to Othon's life and work.

It is a focused, literary house museum rather than a broad city museum. Visit if you like small historic interiors, or combine it with the churches, plazas, and art museums in the centre.
