Museo Federico Silva is a contemporary sculpture museum focused on the work of Federico Silva, whose pieces often mix modernist and cubist forms with pre-Hispanic influence. Temporary exhibitions bring in other Mexican and international artists, so the museum is not just a single-artist shrine.

It is one of San Luis Potosí's better cultural stops, especially if you want something beyond colonial churches and plazas.
