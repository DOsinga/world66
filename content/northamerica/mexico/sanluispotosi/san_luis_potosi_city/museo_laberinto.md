Museo Laberinto is San Luis Potosí's big modern museum for science, art, and hands-on learning. Its architecture and location near Parque Tangamanga make it feel different from the historic centre's churches, plazas, and colonial museums.

It is especially useful for families, but adults with a taste for interactive museums will not feel out of place. Use it as a change of pace after the centre, or as a good bad-weather option when the city's outdoor plazas are less appealing.
