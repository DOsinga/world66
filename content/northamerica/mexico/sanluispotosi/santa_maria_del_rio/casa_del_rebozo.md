Casa del Rebozo is the town's key stop. It treats the rebozo not as a souvenir but as skilled textile work, with displays of finished shawls and a look at how materials, patterns, and fringe work are handled.

If classes or demonstrations are underway, take the time to watch. The whole town makes more sense once you have seen the work behind the fabric hanging in the shops.
