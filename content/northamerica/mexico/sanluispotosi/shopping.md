title: Shopping
type: section
---

The one essential purchase in San Luis Potosí is a **Santa María rebozo** — the silk-and-cotton shawl woven in the nearby town of Santa María del Río to a fineness that lets the cloth pass through a wedding ring. The [Mercado Hidalgo](/northamerica/mexico/sanluispotosi/mercado_hidalgo) carries them, as does the government craft store **La Casa del Artesano** on Jardín Colón. Also local: **queso de tuna** (the prickly pear candy) and **Constanzo chocolates**, a city institution with shops throughout the historic centre.

For general Mexican crafts — talavera pottery, textiles, hammered tin — **Fonart** on Calle Morelos is a reliable government-run shop with fixed and fair prices. The streets around the Plaza de Armas have independent craft and jewellery shops; the city's proximity to silver-mining country means silverwork is abundant, though quality varies.

The [Mercado República](/northamerica/mexico/sanluispotosi/mercado_republica_fondas) is a working market rather than a craft showcase but has stalls selling clothing, household goods, and inexpensive local produce.
