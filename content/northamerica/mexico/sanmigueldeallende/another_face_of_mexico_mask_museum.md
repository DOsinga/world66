Another Face of Mexico Mask Museum is a small but memorable counterpoint to San Miguel de Allende's galleries and colonial streets. Its collection focuses on ceremonial and festival masks, making visible the regional traditions, dances, and ritual figures that rarely fit into a standard city walk.

The museum is private and focused, so check visiting arrangements before going. If you enjoy folk art, it adds welcome texture to San Miguel, a town that can otherwise feel dominated by architecture, shopping, and expatriate life.
