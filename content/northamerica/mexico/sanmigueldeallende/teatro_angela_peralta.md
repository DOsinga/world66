Teatro Angela Peralta is one of San Miguel de Allende's principal performance venues, named for the Mexican opera singer who inaugurated it in 1873.

It is generally visited for concerts and events rather than casual daytime touring, but the theatre is a useful cultural landmark in the historic centre.
