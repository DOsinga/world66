The Templo de Nuestra Señora de la Salud is one of San Miguel de Allende's most distinctive smaller churches. Its facade is easy to pick out: pink stone, a carved shell over the entrance, and a compact presence that feels more local than the grand parish church on the main square.

It stands close to the market and the old Oratorio, so it fits naturally into a walk through the busier, less polished side of the centre. Step inside if the doors are open, but also look back at the facade after dark, when the lighting brings out the stonework and the church becomes a quiet landmark in the surrounding streets.
