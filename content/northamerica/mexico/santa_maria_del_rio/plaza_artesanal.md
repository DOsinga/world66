Plaza Artesanal is the simple shopping stop that makes sense after Casa del Rebozo. Several vendors sell rebozos and other local crafts, so you can compare colours, fringe work, and prices without walking all over town.

Buy carefully and ask questions. The good part of shopping here is not just the object, but learning what separates a cheap shawl from work that took real skill.
