Capilla de Jesús Malverde is Culiacán's best-known folk shrine. Visitors come to see the plaques, offerings, and local devotion around Jesús Malverde, a bandit executed in 1909 who became a folk saint associated with migrants, difficult journeys, and Sinaloa's complicated modern identity.
