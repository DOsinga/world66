Mansión Orrantia is a good choice when you want El Fuerte's colonial setting to carry into dinner. Tables sit under porticoes in an old house near the plaza, and the menu leans into regional northwestern Mexican cooking.

Order seafood or shrimp if available, but come mainly for the combination of food and setting. It is more polished than the town's simple birria counters without feeling out of place.
