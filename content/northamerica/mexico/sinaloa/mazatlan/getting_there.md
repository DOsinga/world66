---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**General Rafael Buelna International Airport (MZT)** is 20 km south of the city. There are daily flights from Mexico City, Guadalajara, and Tijuana, plus seasonal direct flights from US and Canadian cities — Phoenix, Los Angeles, Houston, Dallas, Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto. A taxi to the Zona Dorada costs about 350–450 pesos; pre-paid taxis at the airport are the simplest option.

By road, Mazatlan sits on Mexico Federal Highway 15D. The drive from **Mazatlan to Durango** over the Sierra Madre crosses the spectacular Baluarte Bridge — at the time of opening the highest cable-stayed bridge in the world. Buses run frequently to Guadalajara (six hours), Tepic, and Culiacán.

The **Mazatlan–La Paz ferry** crosses the Sea of Cortez to Baja California Sur. The crossing takes about twelve hours overnight and is one of the more interesting overland routes in Mexico.
