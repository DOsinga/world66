Campo de Girasoles is a seasonal sunflower field near Mocorito, planted on a scale that turns the edge of town into a bright yellow photo stop. It is set up for visitors, with simple viewpoints and family-friendly rides rather than a wild countryside feel.

Go when the flowers are in bloom and treat it as a cheerful add-on to Mocorito's historic centre, not as a reason to travel across Sinaloa by itself.
