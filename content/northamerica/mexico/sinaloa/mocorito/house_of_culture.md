Mocorito's House of Culture occupies a 19th-century building beside the church. Its main draw is Alonso Enriquez's mural "Mocorito Through the Centuries", a large interior work that tells the town's story from its ancient roots through mission history and modern civic life.

The building also hosts exhibitions, workshops, and local events. Even a short visit helps explain why Mocorito leans so hard into culture rather than just pretty-street tourism.
