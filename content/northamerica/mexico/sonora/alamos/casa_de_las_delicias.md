Casa de las Delicias is an old manor on the edge of the historic centre, kept just tidy enough to visit and just worn enough to feel haunted. Local stories attach ghosts and family tragedy to the house; even without them, the empty rooms and gardens show the melancholy side of Álamos's old wealth.
