The Plaza de Armas is the heart of Álamos and the easiest place to understand the town's appeal. Arcades, trees, benches, the church, and old civic buildings sit close together, making it a natural base for a slow walk through the historic centre.

Come early or late in the day, when the stone and adobe facades are softer and the square is at its most local.
