The reason to come is across the water: **Isla Tiburón**, Mexico's largest island, the territory of the Comcáac (Seri) and a protected reserve. You cannot simply go; trips are arranged with the community at Punta Chueca, half an hour north of Kino. The Seri also sell their ironwood carvings and baskets in the village.
