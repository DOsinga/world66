Ecotourism run by the **Comcáac (Seri)** of Punta Chueca, the village north of Bahía de Kino that faces Isla Tiburón across the Canal del Infiernillo. Tiburón is the Seri's own island and a protected reserve, and going with the community is the proper way to visit.

Every package includes the boat to the island. The short one adds a night in a cabin in the village, face painting in the traditional patterns and a meal; the two-day trip adds kayaks and a hike to the Piedra de Gigante; the three-day one kayaks across the channel and camps on the island. WhatsApp is the only contact they publish.
