---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bahia de Kino's pleasures are simple: beach, sea, desert, and silence. The main draw is Isla Tiburón, the largest island in Mexico and a federally protected biosphere reserve managed by the Seri people. Beyond the island, the Seri Museum in Kino Viejo, the pelicans at the harbour, and the wildlife of the surrounding desert and coast keep slow-paced visitors happily occupied.
