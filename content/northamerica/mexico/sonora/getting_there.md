---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Hermosillo's General Ignacio Pesqueira García International Airport has direct flights from Mexico City, Guadalajara, and several US cities including Los Angeles, Phoenix, and Las Vegas. US travellers from Arizona can drive across the border at Nogales — a straightforward crossing used by thousands of Arizonans every week.

**By road from the US:** The main border crossing is Nogales, Arizona / Nogales, Sonora, connected to Hermosillo by a fast toll road (about 270 km, three hours). The Santa Ana / Altar crossing is used by travellers heading directly to the coast.

**By road within Mexico:** Federal Highway 15 runs the length of Sonora from the US border to Sinaloa. Federal Highway 16 connects Hermosillo to the Chihuahua border, crossing the Sierra Madre.

**By bus:** Long-distance buses connect Hermosillo to Mexico City, Guadalajara, and US border cities. The bus station is in the south of the city.
