A small Sonoran operator, running since 2017 with a permit from CONANP, the national parks agency, for the **El Pinacate y Gran Desierto de Altar** biosphere reserve. Tours leave Hermosillo at six in the morning. The pin is on the city.

The core trips are overnight camps in the reserve — a night under the stars among the giant craters, a moonlit night in the dunes of the Gran Desierto — with a four-day "Sonora Salvaje" circuit for those with longer. They also run day trips to **Isla Tiburón** and San Carlos, and pick up in Puerto Peñasco on the dunes trip. Guiding is in Spanish only.

WhatsApp is the only number they publish; a bot answers first.
