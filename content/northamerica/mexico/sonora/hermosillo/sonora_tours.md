A registered inbound operator (RNT 35260300030) of fifteen years, and the broadest in the state. The pin is on the city; their office is in Colonia Centenario.

The best trip goes to **Isla Tiburón** through Punta Chueca, with a welcome from the Comcáac (Seri) community and Bahía de Kino on the way back. The **Ruta del Río Sonora** runs one to three days through the old valley villages — Ures, Banámichi, Arizpe — with a bacanora tour on the side; their "Ruta del Novillo" ecotour won an award at the 2024 Tianguis Turístico. They also go to Álamos, Magdalena de Kino, the San Nicolás dunes, the Pinacate and San Carlos.
