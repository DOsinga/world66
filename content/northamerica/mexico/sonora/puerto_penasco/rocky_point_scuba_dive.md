A small dive operation run by Lea and Nathan Kesler, next door to Manny's Beach Club on the waterfront. The pin is on the town.

They teach NAUI courses from a try-dive to Divemaster and dive locally from the shore and the boat. The big trip is **Isla San Jorge** — Bird Island — where a colony of several thousand sea lions comes out to meet divers; they run it as a private charter or put divers on a shared boat. There is also a two-tank day down the coast at Puerto Lobos.

The phone is an Arizona number.
