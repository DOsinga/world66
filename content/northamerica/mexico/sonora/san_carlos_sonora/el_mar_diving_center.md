The San Carlos outpost of an Arizona dive shop, teaching SSI courses from a try-dive to Advanced Open Water and specialties, with air fills and gear hire.

Their boat, the 36-foot *Piesquita*, runs two-tank morning dives, afternoon dives and night dives, plus freediving, spearfishing and fishing charters. They also rent single and double **kayaks** by the day for the coves around the bay.
