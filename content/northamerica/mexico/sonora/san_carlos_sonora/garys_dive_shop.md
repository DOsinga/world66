A full-service dive shop on the main boulevard through San Carlos, open every day from eight to five. The pin is on the town; they give their address as kilometre 10 of the boulevard.

On Saturdays the boat goes out to **San Pedro Nolasco**, the island offshore where sea lions come to play with divers; Sundays are local dives. They teach PADI from a first try-dive up to Master Scuba Diver. Non-divers get **Donna's Nature Tours**, a two-hour narrated boat trip on the geology, the boobies, the dolphins and in season the whales, as well as scenic bay cruises and fishing trips.

They also publish a US number for WhatsApp, +1 520 370 7187.
