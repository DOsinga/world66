---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sonora's appeal is spread across a large territory. The coast of the Sea of Cortez is the main draw — beach towns, whale watching, diving, and some of the best sport fishing in Mexico. Inland, the colonial silver-mining town of Álamos and the capital Hermosillo offer a different kind of interest. The Sonoran Desert, which extends across the centre of the state, has its own stark beauty.
