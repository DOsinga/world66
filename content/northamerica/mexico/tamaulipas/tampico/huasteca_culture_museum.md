The Huasteca Culture Museum covers the archaeology and ethnography of the Huasteca region, with a collection of regional objects and interpretive displays.

It is a useful indoor stop in Tampico for visitors who want context before or after seeing the area's plazas, waterfront, and archaeological sites.
