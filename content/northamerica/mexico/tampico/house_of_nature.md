House of Nature is an Art Deco building used for nature-themed photography exhibitions. It is a small stop rather than a headline sight, but the architecture and changing displays make it worth checking if you are exploring the Tampico area slowly.

It works best paired with the lagoon, port and old customs-house sights rather than as a stand-alone trip across town.
