The Huasteca Culture Museum introduces the archaeology and living traditions of the Huastec region around Tampico. Its collection covers ceramics, sculpture, ritual objects, and ethnographic material, giving useful context before visiting smaller archaeological sites such as Las Flores.

It is a focused museum rather than a large one, but it fills an important gap in a city whose history is often told through its port and oil boom.
