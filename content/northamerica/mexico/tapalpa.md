Tapalpa is a highland town in southern Jalisco, about two hours from [Guadalajara](/northamerica/mexico/guadalajara). It is one of Mexico's Pueblos Magicos, and the appeal is easy to read: white facades, red tile roofs, cold mountain evenings, and pine forest all around.

The centre is small and good for wandering. The church, old fountains, market, and simple restaurants give Tapalpa a weekend-town rhythm, especially when visitors from Guadalajara arrive for cabins and grilled meat.

The best reasons to come are just outside town. [Las Piedrotas](/northamerica/mexico/tapalpa/las_piedrotas) is a field of huge rounded monoliths where people hike, ride horses, and zip-line. [Salto del Nogal](/northamerica/mexico/tapalpa/salto_del_nogal), south of town, is Jalisco's tallest waterfall and a real hike rather than a roadside viewpoint. For a more controlled dose of mountain air, [Parque Aventuras La Ceja](/northamerica/mexico/tapalpa/parque_aventuras_la_ceja) has paragliding, rappelling, and wide views over the valley.

Tapalpa is not a major city stop. It works best as a mountain break from Guadalajara or as part of a slow Jalisco trip, with one full day for the town and nearby countryside.
