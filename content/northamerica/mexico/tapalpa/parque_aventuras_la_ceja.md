Parque Aventuras La Ceja sits outside Tapalpa on a high viewpoint above the valley. The main draw is paragliding, but the park also offers rappelling, mountain biking, climbing, and guided outdoor activities.

Come for the view even if you are not planning to jump off anything. For paragliding, check wind and operating days before you make the trip, since mountain weather decides what is possible.
