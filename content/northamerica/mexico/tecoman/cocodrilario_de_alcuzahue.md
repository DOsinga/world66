Cocodrilario de Alcuzahue is a small wildlife stop near Laguna de Alcuzahue, east of Tecomán. It focuses on crocodiles, with protected viewing areas that make the animals easier to see than they would be in open lagoon water.

This is not a slick zoo, and that is mostly fine. It works best as part of a short loop through the agricultural land and lagoon country around Tecomán, especially for families or anyone curious about the wetlands behind the beaches.
