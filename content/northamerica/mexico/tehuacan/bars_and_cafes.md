---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Tehuacan's cafe and bar scene is modest and unhurried, centred on the portales around the zócalo. The local drink to try is a tepache — a fermented pineapple drink sold by market vendors — or agua de Jamaica, the ubiquitous hibiscus cooler found throughout the region. The cantina culture here is traditional: dark rooms, cold beer, and botanas (small snacks brought automatically with drinks).
