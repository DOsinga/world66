Tepatitlán, often shortened to Tepa, is a city in Los Altos de Jalisco, east of Guadalajara. It is cattle country, agave country, and church country, with a proud alteño identity that feels different from the big city just down the highway.

The historic centre is the best place to start. The [Parroquia de San Francisco de Asís](/northamerica/mexico/tepatitlan/parroquia_de_san_francisco_de_asis) gives the main square its skyline, while the [Santuario del Señor de la Misericordia](/northamerica/mexico/tepatitlan/santuario_del_senor_de_la_misericordia) and the [Museo de la Ciudad](/northamerica/mexico/tepatitlan/museo_de_la_ciudad) add enough substance for a good walk.

Tepa is not a first-time Mexico essential, but it earns a day if you are already in Jalisco and want something more local than Guadalajara's usual side trips. Come for the churches, the market food, the agave fields, and the matter-of-fact confidence of a town that knows exactly what it is.
