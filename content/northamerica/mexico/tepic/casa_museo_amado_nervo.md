Casa Museo Amado Nervo is a small museum dedicated to the poet born in Tepic in 1870. It is more intimate than essential: a local literary house rather than a major national museum.

For travelers interested in Mexican literature, it adds a human scale to the city. For everyone else, it works as a short stop while walking the centre.
