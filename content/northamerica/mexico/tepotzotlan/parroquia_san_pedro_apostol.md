Parroquia San Pedro Apóstol stands beside the larger museum complex and still works as a parish church. It is quieter than the museum church, with a more local rhythm and a graceful doorway facing the old centre.

Visit it before or after the museum rather than treating it as a separate expedition. The contrast between parish life and museum-piece baroque is part of the town's appeal.
