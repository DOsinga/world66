The former Palacio Jai Alai is one of the most striking buildings on Avenida Revolución — a 1940s Art Deco structure with a distinctive facade that once held one of the most elaborate sporting venues on the Pacific coast.

Jai alai, the Basque ball game played at extraordinary speeds in a three-walled court called a fronton, was wildly popular in Tijuana during the mid-20th century. The palace attracted thousands of American bettors who crossed the border specifically for the spectacle. When jai alai's American popularity declined, the palace closed and sat dormant for years.

The building has been reinvented as **El Foro**, a live music and events venue. The fronton court makes for an unusual concert space — the high walls and hard surfaces create exceptional acoustics. Check the venue's programme for concerts, which range from local bands to regional touring acts. The building's exterior is worth seeing regardless of whether anything is on.
