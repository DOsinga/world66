---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

There is essentially one reason a traveller pulls off the road in Tingambato, and that is the archaeological site at the western edge of the village. The Tarascan ruins are small enough to walk in under an hour, but they hold real surprises — including a ball court and a deep tomb that yielded one of the most important burial finds in pre-Hispanic Michoacán. The village plaza and its eighteenth-century church are worth a quick wander while you are here.
