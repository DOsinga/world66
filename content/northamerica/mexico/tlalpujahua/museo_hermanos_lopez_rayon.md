The Museo Hermanos López Rayón is a modest but worthwhile stop for the town's independence history. The Rayón brothers were born in Tlalpujahua and became important figures in the early Mexican War of Independence, giving the town a story that reaches beyond mining.

Do not expect a major museum. It works best as a short companion to the church and central streets, adding local context before you head out to the mine.
