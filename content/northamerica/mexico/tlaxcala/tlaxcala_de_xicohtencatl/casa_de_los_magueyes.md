Casa de los Magueyes leans into the food and drink that make Tlaxcala feel local: regional dishes, maguey culture, and pulque. It is close to Plaza Xicohténcatl, so it fits neatly into a centre walk.

The pulque tasting board is the reason to choose it over a generic plaza restaurant.
