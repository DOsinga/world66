The Museo de la Madera is a small museum, but it helps explain one of Tlaxco's real local trades. Inside are hand tools, workshop equipment, and examples of the furniture and carved pieces made around town.

It is not a grand museum; think of it as context for the craft shops and market stalls. If you like seeing how a place makes things, it is worth the short stop.
