---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Toluca's nightlife is more about cafes than clubs — the altitude is high, the evenings cool, and locals drink coffee or *atole* under the Portales until late. The cafes under the arcades on the main plaza are the obvious meeting point; **Café Casa Toluca** and **Cafetería Italiana** both do reliable coffee with a slice of pastry.

For drinks, a handful of bars cluster on the streets behind the cathedral. **La Vaquería Distrito** is a respectable craft-beer place. The hotel bars on the western end of town are reliable for a quiet drink. Avoid the larger, more anonymous discos out toward the highway unless you have a local guide.
