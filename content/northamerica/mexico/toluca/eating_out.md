---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Toluca's food has two specialities you should not miss: **chorizo verde**, a fresh pork sausage made with green herbs and chiles that has no real equivalent anywhere else in Mexico, and **tacos de plaza**, dripping carnitas tacos wrapped in pink paper. Both are best eaten standing up at a market stall or under the Portales.

For sit-down meals, **Hostería Las Ramblas** on the Portales does well-regarded regional cooking in a colonial space. **La Vaquita Negra del Portal** is a small, busy taqueria famous specifically for its chorizo-verde tacos. **Mercado Juárez** upstairs has dozens of cooked-food stalls for cheap, fresh, traditional food at any time of day.

**Moscos** — the small bottles of orange-flavoured liqueur that you see for sale under the arcades — are a Toluca tradition worth a bottle as a souvenir, or as a digestif after dinner. The drink is sweet and strong.
