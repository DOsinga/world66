Mercado Juárez is Toluca's main covered market, a few blocks south of the Portales — a sprawling, two-storey building that draws thousands of shoppers from across the State of Mexico, especially on Friday, the traditional market day. The ground floor is mostly produce, meat, and fish; the upper floor is full of cooked-food stalls.

It is the natural place to try the city's signature dishes: **chorizo verde** (green chorizo, coloured with cilantro and chile), **carnitas**, and the **tacos de plaza** that vendors sell in dripping foil parcels. The market also has good stalls for the Mazahua and Otomí textiles produced in surrounding villages — embroidered blouses, woven sashes, and shawls.
