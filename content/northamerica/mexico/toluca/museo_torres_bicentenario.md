Museo Torres Bicentenario is a modern landmark on Toluca's Paseo Tollocan, built around twin towers that are hard to miss. The site was created for Mexico's bicentennial commemorations and works as both monument and exhibition space.

It is not as essential as Cosmovitral, but it adds a contemporary civic note to Toluca's museum mix. Stop if you are already moving along the main avenue or want a quick look at the city's newer symbolism.
