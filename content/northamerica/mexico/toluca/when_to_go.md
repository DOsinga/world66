---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Toluca's altitude makes it the coldest of Mexico's state capitals — temperatures rarely climb above the low twenties Celsius and nights can drop close to freezing in December and January. Pack a sweater whenever you visit.

**November to April** is the dry season, the best window for getting up the Nevado de Toluca volcano (where the access road is more often open) and for clear views of the surrounding mountains. **May to October** brings afternoon thunderstorms; mornings are generally clear, evenings often wet.

The two great festivals are the **Feria del Alfeñique** (the sugar-skull fair, in the second half of October running into the Day of the Dead) and the **Las Mañanitas** celebrations for the patron saint in early November — both fill the Portales with vendors and music for several days.
