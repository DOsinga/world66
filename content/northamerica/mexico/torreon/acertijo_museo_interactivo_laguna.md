Acertijo Museo Interactivo Laguna is a hands-on science museum for children and families, with interactive exhibits, workshops, and changing temporary displays. It is a practical rainy-day or heat-of-the-day stop in the Torreon-Gomez Palacio area.

The museum is best for families rather than visitors looking for a traditional gallery. Check current hours before going, as workshop schedules and special exhibits can change.
