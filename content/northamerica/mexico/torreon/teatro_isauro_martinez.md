Teatro Isauro Martinez is Torreon's most beautiful performance hall and one of the best reasons to linger in the city centre. Opened in 1930, it mixes Moorish, Byzantine, and Art Deco decoration in a way that feels proudly theatrical before the curtain even rises. The facade is handsome, but the interior is the real prize, with painted ceilings, balconies, and ornament that survived the city's more practical modern growth.

Check the programme if you are in town, because seeing a concert or play here is far better than just admiring the building from the street. Even without a performance, it is worth seeking out as a cultural landmark: Torreon can feel workaday at first glance, and this theatre shows a more elegant civic ambition.
