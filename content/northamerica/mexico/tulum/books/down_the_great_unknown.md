A vivid and accessible introduction to the worldview of the ancient Maya — kingship, cosmology, the cycles of time. Particularly good on the meaning of cenotes (entrances to the underworld) and on coastal cities like Tulum that connected the Maya to trade routes across the Caribbean. The authors mix archaeological synthesis with ethnographic fieldwork among modern Maya communities, which keeps the book grounded rather than purely speculative.
