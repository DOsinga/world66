type: section
---

Uruapan's two unmissable attractions sit at opposite ends of the nature-adventure spectrum. The [Parque Nacional Barranca del Cupatitzio](/northamerica/mexico/uruapan/parque_nacional_barranca_del_cupatitzio), right at the edge of town, is one of the finest urban parks in Mexico — a spring-fed river gushing through mossy tropical forest. The journey to [Paricutín](/northamerica/mexico/uruapan/paricutin), the volcano that erupted in a cornfield in 1943 and buried the village of San Juan Parangaricutiro, is one of the genuinely unforgettable experiences in all of Mexico.

In town, the [Mercado de Antojitos](/northamerica/mexico/uruapan/mercado_de_antojitos) on the main plaza is the best place to sample Michoacán food, and the city's lacquerware crafts — sold throughout the market area — have been made here for centuries. The [Casa de la Cultura](/northamerica/mexico/uruapan/casa_de_la_cultura) hosts exhibits on the region's Purépecha heritage.
