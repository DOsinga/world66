An ecological reserve on the northern shore of Lake Catemaco, in the last substantial patch of lowland rainforest at this latitude anywhere on the American Pacific side — Los Tuxtlas is the northernmost tropical rainforest in the Americas, and this is a piece of it.

What they run is a guided walk through the forest with the mineral spring, the volcanic **mud bath**, a **temazcal** steam ceremony, and a boat across the lake. The site has a slightly famous second life as the location for *Apocalypto* and for a long tradition of curanderos; Catemaco is Mexico's witchcraft town and the reserve sits in the middle of that.
