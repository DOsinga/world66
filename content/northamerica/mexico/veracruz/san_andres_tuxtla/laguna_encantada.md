Laguna Encantada is a small lake on the edge of San Andrés Tuxtla, close enough to treat as a short side trip rather than a separate excursion. The setting is simple: still water, green slopes, and the humid volcanic feel that defines Los Tuxtlas.

It is not a blockbuster sight, but it adds an easy natural pause to a day otherwise built around Salto de Eyipantla and the town centre.
