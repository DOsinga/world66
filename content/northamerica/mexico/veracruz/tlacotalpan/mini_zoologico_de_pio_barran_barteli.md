Mini Zoologico de Pio Barran Barteli is a small, eccentric stop that mixes local history displays with rescued animals. It feels more like a personal collection and community curiosity than a conventional museum.

That oddness is the reason to go. Expect old objects, local stories, and a few animals rather than a polished attraction.
