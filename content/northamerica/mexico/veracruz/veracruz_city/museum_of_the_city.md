The Museum of the City traces Veracruz's long port history through dioramas, exhibits, and local material culture. It is not a huge museum, but it helps make sense of the colonial walls, plazas, music, and Gulf trade that shaped the city.

The building also serves as a performance space for Veracruz arts, so it is worth checking whether anything is on while you are in the centre.
