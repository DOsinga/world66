Playa Villa del Mar is one of the most accessible beaches in Veracruz city, close to the aquarium and the busy coastal hotel strip. It is not remote or pristine, but it gives the port city an easy urban beach option without leaving town.

Come for a short swim, a walk, or a break from museums and the malecón rather than a full resort day. Conditions vary with weather and crowds, and the beach is at its best when treated as part of Veracruz's lively waterfront rather than a secluded escape.
