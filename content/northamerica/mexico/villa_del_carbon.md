Villa del Carbón sits in the cool high country northwest of [Mexico City](/northamerica/mexico/mexicocity), close enough for a long day out but far enough to feel like a different rhythm. It is a small Pueblo Mágico of cobbled streets, tile roofs, leather workshops, and weekend charreada culture, with pine-covered hills and reservoirs just outside town.

The centre is easy to wander. The [Parroquia de la Virgen de la Peña de Francia](/northamerica/mexico/villa_del_carbon/parroquia_virgen_de_la_pena_de_francia) anchors the main square, while the [Casa de Cultura Jorge Jiménez Cantú](/northamerica/mexico/villa_del_carbon/casa_de_cultura_jorge_jimenez_cantu) gives a small dose of local history and Otomí material culture. Villa del Carbón is also one of the better places near the capital to browse handmade leather boots, belts, jackets, saddles, and charro gear without turning the outing into a mall trip.

The best reason to come is the mix of town and countryside. [Presa Taxhimay](/northamerica/mexico/villa_del_carbon/presa_taxhimay) is the strange one, a reservoir where the old San Luis de las Peras church tower still rises from the water. [Presa del Llano](/northamerica/mexico/villa_del_carbon/presa_del_llano) is gentler: forested slopes, boat rentals, picnic ground, and cool air. Together they make Villa del Carbón a worthwhile escape when the valley heat and traffic are wearing thin.
