Planetario Tabasco 2000 is a family-friendly science stop in Villahermosa's modern Tabasco 2000 district. Its dome shows and astronomy programming offer a useful indoor break in a city where heat and rain can shape the day as much as sightseeing plans do.

It is not as essential as La Venta Park Museum, but it rounds out the Tabasco 2000 area and can work well for travellers with children. Check show times before heading over, since a planetarium without a scheduled projection is mostly a building with potential.
