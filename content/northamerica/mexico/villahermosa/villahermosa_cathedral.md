The Cathedral of the Lord (Catedral del Señor) stands at the edge of the Zona Luz and is the principal Catholic church of Villahermosa. Its current form is relatively modest — the building has been rebuilt and restored several times following damage from flooding and earthquake — but it serves as a landmark and gathering point for the city's Catholic community.

The interior is cool and ornate, a welcome escape from the heat outside. Masses are held throughout the day; Sunday morning is busiest, when the square in front fills with worshippers and vendors selling religious items.
