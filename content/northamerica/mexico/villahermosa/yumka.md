Yumká is an ecological park outside Villahermosa that combines wildlife exhibits with recreated regional habitats. It is one of the stronger family outings in the area, especially for visitors who want nature without organizing a more remote excursion into Tabasco's wetlands or rainforest.

The park is spread out enough that it feels like more than a small zoo, with walking routes and habitat zones giving context to the animals. It pairs well with Villahermosa's La Venta park-museum for a broader day of nature and regional culture.
