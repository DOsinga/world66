Xalapa is a good base for day trips into the surrounding region. The pre-Columbian ruins at El Tajin, a Totonac ceremonial centre with its remarkable Pyramid of Niches, are about three hours away by bus via Papantla — a full day trip. White-water rafting on the Rio Pescados near Jalcomulco (about 45 minutes from Xalapa) is one of Mexico's best rafting experiences and can be arranged through operators in the city. The coast at [Veracruz](/northamerica/mexico/veracruz) is about 90 minutes away and makes an easy day trip if you want sea and sand after the highland coolness.

Coffee farm visits (fincas) in the hills around Coatepec and Xico — towns just outside Xalapa — are worth a morning. Both towns are picturesque, and Xico is famous for its mole and its waterfall, Cascada de Texolo.
