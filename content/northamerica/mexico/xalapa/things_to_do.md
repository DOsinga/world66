---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Xalapa rewards the traveler who stays more than a day. The Museo de Antropologia alone justifies the trip, but the city's steep streets, university life, and surrounding cloud forest make for a richer experience than a single museum visit. The coffee culture, bookshops, and weekend markets fill the gaps between sights, and the hills above the city offer easy walks with views when the clouds lift.
