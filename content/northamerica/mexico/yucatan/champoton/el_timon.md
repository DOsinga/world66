El Timón is the kind of restaurant Champotón does well: casual, waterside, and focused on seafood. The setting is the draw as much as the menu, with a deck facing the Gulf and the road south of town close by.

Order shrimp or fish rather than trying to make the meal complicated. If champotonero shrimp is available, that is the local dish to ask about.
