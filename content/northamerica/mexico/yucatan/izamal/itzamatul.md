Itzamatul is one of the smaller Maya sites folded into modern Izamal. It does not have the drama of Kinich Kakmó, but it helps show how much of the old sacred city survives as platforms, mounds, and stone edges inside the present town.

Go if you enjoy archaeology in context. The value is not a single perfect monument, but the sense that Izamal's ancient plan is still underfoot.
