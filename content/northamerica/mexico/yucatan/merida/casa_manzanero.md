Casa Manzanero is a small museum devoted to Armando Manzanero, the Merida-born composer known for his boleros. Interactive exhibits cover his life and music, with hands-on touches such as a marimba and karaoke room.

It is a good short stop for visitors interested in Yucatecan music or Mexican popular culture.
