The Museo de Arte Contemporáneo Ateneo de Yucatán sits in the former bishop's palace beside Mérida's cathedral, so it is easy to fold into a walk around Plaza Mayor. The collection focuses on modern and contemporary artists from Yucatán, with sculpture, painting, and changing local exhibitions.

It is a useful counterweight to the city's colonial churches and Maya archaeology: small enough for a short visit, but rooted in Mérida's own cultural scene.
