Palacio de la Música is a modern museum in central Mérida devoted to Mexican music, from pre-Hispanic traditions to regional and popular styles. Exhibits include instruments, recordings, and multimedia displays, though much of the interpretation is in Spanish.

Its central location makes it easy to combine with the cathedral, Plaza Grande, and nearby cultural sights. It is especially useful for visitors interested in Yucatecan song traditions and Mexico's wider musical history.
