---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Valladolid's food is distinctively Yucatecan and worth treating as one of the reasons to be here. The signature dishes — **lomitos de Valladolid** (pork loin in a tomato-and-pepper sauce), **longaniza** (a spiced, lightly smoked sausage), **pollo en escabeche** (pickled chicken with sour orange and oregano), and **cochinita pibil** (pork slow-roasted in banana leaves with annatto) — are easier to find here, and better cooked, than in most of the country.

The cheapest, most reliable eating is at the **Mercado Municipal** (north-east of the plaza) and the food stalls inside the **Bazar Municipal** on the north side of the square — both packed at lunchtime, both excellent. For a proper sit-down meal, **Conato 1910** on Calle 40 and **Yerbabuena del Sisal** on Calzada de los Frailes are reliable; **Taberna de los Frailes**, set in a colonial garden, is the most atmospheric for an evening. Save room for a **marquesita** from the plaza vendors at night.
