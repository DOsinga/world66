An agency in the centre of Valladolid running day trips across eastern Yucatán: **Ek Balam**, the Maya city half an hour north whose main pyramid you may still climb and whose stucco frieze is among the best-preserved anywhere; **Río Lagartos** for the flamingos; Chichén Itzá, Izamal, Cobá and the Punta Laguna monkey reserve.

Valladolid's advantage over Cancún or Mérida as a base is distance: Chichén Itzá is forty minutes away, so you can be at the gate when it opens rather than arriving with the coaches at eleven.
