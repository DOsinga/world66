Valladolid is a small colonial city halfway between [Mérida](/northamerica/mexico/merida) and [Cancún](/northamerica/mexico/cancun), and one of the most pleasant stops on the [Yucatán](/northamerica/mexico/yucatan) peninsula. The pace is slow, the architecture is handsome, and the town has not yet been overwhelmed by tourism.

The [main plaza](/northamerica/mexico/yucatan/valladolid_1/parque_principal) is the heart of the city — shaded, sociable, and ringed by colonial buildings including the [Cathedral of San Gervasio](/northamerica/mexico/yucatan/valladolid_1/cathedral_san_gervasio). Mayan women sell hand-embroidered huipiles and blouses on the benches opposite the church. The shops **Artesanías del Parque** and **Ek Balam** on the square carry unusual handcrafts at good prices.

Valladolid played a pivotal role in Yucatán history: the Caste War of 1847 began here. Seven neighbourhood churches from the colonial period are scattered through the town, and the [Convento de San Bernardino de Siena](/northamerica/mexico/yucatan/valladolid_1/convento_san_bernardino), founded in 1552, is one of the oldest religious buildings on the peninsula. The [Palacio Municipal](/northamerica/mexico/yucatan/valladolid_1/palacio_municipal) has impressive murals depicting Mexican history, and the [San Roque Museum](/northamerica/mexico/yucatan/valladolid_1/san_roque_museum) covers the town's pre-Hispanic and colonial past.

The two cenotes are not to be missed. [Cenote Zací](/northamerica/mexico/yucatan/valladolid_1/cenote_zaci), right in the town centre, is a huge open sinkhole. [Cenote Dzitnup](/northamerica/mexico/yucatan/valladolid_1/cenote_xkeken) (also called X'kekén), a few kilometres west, is a cathedral-like underground pool — you descend through a narrow passage into a cavern lit by a single shaft of daylight, then swim in the clear blue water.

The local cuisine is distinctive: **lomitos de Valladolid** (pork loin in tomato sauce), **longaniza** (spiced sausage), and **pollo en escabeche** (pickled chicken) are all worth seeking out.
