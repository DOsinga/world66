---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Valladolid sits roughly halfway between [Mérida](/northamerica/mexico/merida) (160 km west) and [Cancún](/northamerica/mexico/cancun) (160 km east) on the toll motorway 180D. Frequent buses with **ADO** and the second-class **Oriente** line connect both cities; the bus terminal is on Calle 39 a few blocks west of the plaza. From Cancún the trip takes about 2 hours; from Mérida about 2 hours 15 minutes.

The new **Tren Maya** stops at Valladolid (the station is north of the centre, accessible by short taxi), with services to Cancún, Mérida, Tulum, and beyond. Driving is also straightforward — the 180D is well-maintained but has tolls; the parallel *libre* road is slower and goes through villages.
