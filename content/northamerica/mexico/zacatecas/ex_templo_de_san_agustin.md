Ex Templo de San Agustin is a former 17th-century monastery church in the historic centre of Zacatecas. The complex no longer functions as a regular church; its main nave is used for conferences and cultural events, while other parts remain church offices.

The draw is the building itself and its place in Zacatecas's dense colonial core. It works well as part of a short walk between the cathedral, Santo Domingo, and the city's central museums.
