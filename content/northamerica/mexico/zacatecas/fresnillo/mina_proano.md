Mina Proaño is Fresnillo's defining industrial landmark. Silver has been worked in this district for centuries, and the visitor route gives a controlled look at underground tunnels before ending at a viewpoint over the city.

It is not a romantic ruin. The value is seeing how closely Fresnillo's identity is tied to mining that still matters, not just to a preserved colonial centre.
