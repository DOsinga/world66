Santuario de Plateros is the most powerful visitor draw around Fresnillo. The church is tied to devotion to the Santo Niño de Atocha, and INAH traces the settlement's roots to the San Demetrio silver mines and the rise of Real de San Demetrio de los Plateros.

The sanctuary's carved stone facade is worth seeing, but the real force is devotional. Pilgrims, votive offerings, and the steady movement around the church make Plateros feel distinct from Fresnillo even though it sits only a short ride away.
