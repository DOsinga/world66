---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Juchipila's main draws are its festival calendar and the natural landscape of the canyon. The thermal springs at the Hotel Paraíso Caxcán just north of town on Highway 54 offer a relaxing visit, with warm mineral pools set against canyon views. The town plaza fills with intense energy during the Fiesta de Santo Santiago in late July and again for the January fair with its jaripeos and bullfights — these are not tourist events but genuine regional celebrations.
