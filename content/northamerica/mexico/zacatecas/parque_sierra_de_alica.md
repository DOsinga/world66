Parque Sierra de Alica is a green pocket near the historic centre, useful for a short rest or a less formal look at local life. It is especially welcome in a city where many streets are narrow, steep, and paved in heat-holding stone.

Travellers do not need to make a special trip, but it works as a breather between the centre and the southern side of town.
