  Zacatecas was one of the great silver engines of New Spain, and El Edén was part of that underground world. The mine tour is theatrical now, but the tunnels still make clear how much of the city's wealth came from labour carried out in darkness.

Mina El Edén is a former silver mine converted into one of Zacatecas' most popular sights. A small train and guided route take visitors into the tunnels, past mineral displays and old workings that hint at the scale of the mining economy.

It is touristy, but worthwhile. Pair it with the Teleférico station nearby so you move from underground Zacatecas to the aerial view in one neat loop.
