Zipolite is a small beach village on the Oaxacan coast, between [Puerto Escondido](/northamerica/mexico/puertoescondido) and Huatulco. It is famous for one thing above all: [Playa Zipolite](/northamerica/mexico/zipolite/playa_zipolite), Mexico's best-known clothing-optional beach, where the old backpacker and naturist mood has survived better than it has in most beach towns.

This is not a polished resort. The main strip is a sandy, low-rise run of palapa restaurants, simple guesthouses, small bars, yoga signs, and people who have stayed longer than they planned. The beach is beautiful but serious: the surf and undertow can be dangerous, and swimming should be done with care.

Zipolite works best if you want a slow coastal stop with a strong identity. Spend the morning on the beach, climb over to [Playa del Amor](/northamerica/mexico/zipolite/playa_del_amor), watch sunset from the Roca Blanca end, and leave the big excursions to nearby Mazunte or Puerto Angel. Come for the freedom and the sunsets, not for a checklist.
