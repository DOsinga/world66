Playa Zipolite is the reason the village exists for travelers: a long, open Pacific beach with golden sand, strong waves, and a free-spirited culture that still feels unusual in Mexico. Clothing is optional, but the point is not shock value; the beach works because the mood is relaxed and people mostly leave one another alone.

The sea deserves respect. The surf can be rough, currents can be fierce, and this is a better beach for sun, walking, watching the water, and experienced surfing than for casual swimming. Check the flags and stay close to watched areas if you get in.
