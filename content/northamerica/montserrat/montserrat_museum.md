Montserrat Museum gives essential context for an island whose modern story is inseparable from the Soufrière Hills eruptions and the loss of Plymouth. Exhibits cover local history, culture, colonial life, and the changes forced by volcanic disaster.

It is small, but it helps visitors understand what they are seeing elsewhere on the island. Go early in a Montserrat trip if you can, before visiting viewpoints over the exclusion zone or the buried capital.
