---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Almost everything you do on Montserrat is shaped by the volcano. The Soufrière Hills eruptions since 1995 have closed the southern half of the island, turned Plymouth into a buried ghost town, and left the safer northern half of the island as the inhabited zone — a quieter, greener place with forested ridges, a few black-sand beaches, and the ruins of a famously rock-and-roll recording studio. The island is small enough to see in two or three days; the volcano viewpoints, a hike or two through the Centre Hills, and a Plymouth boat tour are the standard programme.
