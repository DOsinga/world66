The Netherlands Antilles was a constituent country of the Kingdom of the Netherlands until 2010, when it was dissolved and its six islands reorganised. The ABC islands — Aruba (now a separate constituent country), Bonaire, and Curaçao — sit close to the Venezuelan coast; the SSS islands — Saba, Sint Eustatius, and Sint Maarten — are in the northern Leeward Islands.

Today Curaçao and Sint Maarten are separate constituent countries of the Kingdom, while Bonaire, Saba, and Sint Eustatius are special municipalities of the Netherlands. The old name "Netherlands Antilles" is no longer officially in use, but it remains in common reference for the Dutch Caribbean as a whole. Curaçao and its colourful capital Willemstad (a UNESCO World Heritage Site) are the most visited of the islands.
