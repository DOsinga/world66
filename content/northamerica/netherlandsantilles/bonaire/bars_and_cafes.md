Kralendijk is sociable rather than wild after dark. Waterfront terraces fill around sunset, then thin out as people with early dive plans go home. **Karel's Beach Bar**, built over the water on a central pier, is the long-running institution and an easy place to watch the harbour.

For local drinks, **La Cantina** combines a beer café with a courtyard restaurant, while **Sugar Thief** serves beer, mead, kombucha, coffee, and soft drinks made on Bonaire. Opening days change more often than guidebooks do, so check on the day rather than planning a night around an old schedule. If you are driving, remember that almost every trip home leaves town on a dark road shared with donkeys and cyclists.
