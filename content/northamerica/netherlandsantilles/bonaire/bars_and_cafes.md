---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Most evenings on Bonaire end up on a beach bar somewhere. Waterfront places in Kralendijk fill at sunset and stay open until the early hours. The **Spice Beach Club** at the south end has a sand floor and a big rum list. **Karel's Beach Bar** on the central pier is the local institution, open since the 1980s, with live music two nights a week.

Bonaire brews its own beer — **Brewery Bonaire** in Belnem makes the **Karambola** lager and a passable IPA, both available everywhere.
