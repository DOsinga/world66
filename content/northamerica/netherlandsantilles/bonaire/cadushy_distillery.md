Cadushy Distillery produces cactus liqueur and Caribbean spirits in Rincon's old Cinelandia complex. A short self-guided visit explains the process, offers tastings, and leaves time to sit in the shaded garden, where loras and hummingbirds are sometimes seen among the trees.

This is an easy, sociable stop rather than a detailed factory tour. Check the current opening days and nominate a driver if you plan to taste more than a token amount.
