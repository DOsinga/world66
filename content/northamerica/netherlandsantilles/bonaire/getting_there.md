**By air**

Flamingo International Airport (BON) is just south of Kralendijk. Nonstop routes vary by season, but regular links connect Bonaire with Curaçao and Aruba, while long-haul and North American services operate on selected days. Check the operating day before building an itinerary around a particular connection; an overnight in Curaçao is sometimes the sensible fallback.

**By sea**

There is no dependable scheduled passenger ferry linking Bonaire with Curaçao or Aruba. Cruise ships call at Kralendijk, but they are not useful inter-island transport.
