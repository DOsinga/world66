The Hilma Hooker lies on its starboard side off Bonaire's southwest coast, with the upper hull around 18 metres and the mast descending towards 30 metres. The structure rises clearly from the sand at the start of the double-reef system, attracting fish and giving qualified divers a large, legible wreck to circle.

It can be reached from shore, but depth, current, navigation, and the swim out make this a poor place to overestimate your experience. Penetration requires the right training and equipment. Check conditions with a local operator and stay outside the wreck if you are not specifically prepared for overhead environments.
