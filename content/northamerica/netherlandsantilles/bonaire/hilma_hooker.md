The 73-metre cargo ship lies on her starboard side in 30 metres of water just off the south-western coast, between the dive sites **Angel City** and **Hilma Hooker**. The wreck is intact, the masts and superstructure rising clear of the sand, and the holds and cabins are penetrable for trained divers. Schools of horse-eye jacks circulate over the deck, with tarpon hovering in the shadow of the bow.

The wreck is shore-accessible from the road and well within recreational depth limits, making it one of the easiest big-ship wrecks to dive anywhere. Currents can pick up; do not dive it without checking conditions.
