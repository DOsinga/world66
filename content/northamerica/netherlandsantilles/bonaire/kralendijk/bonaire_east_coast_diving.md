The east coast takes the full Atlantic swell and cannot be shore-dived, so it stays far less visited than the west. This operator runs Zodiac boat dives out of the fishermen's pier at Sorobon on Lac Bay to sites such as **Turtle City**, where turtles and eagle rays are the draw. Two-tank trips go in the morning and a single tank at midday. Bring your own gear.

Bookings go through the contact form on its website; it publishes no phone number or email address.
