The local tour company for a first look round the island. The seven-hour island tour covers the pink salt flats and the slave huts in the south, flamingos at the Pekelmeer, and the **1000 Steps** on the north road. It also runs a food-and-bar walk through Kralendijk and an adventure day that adds landsailing.
