**Lac Bay** is one of the classic windsurf spots of the Caribbean: a shallow, reef-sheltered lagoon on the east coast where the trade wind blows hard and the water stays knee-deep for a long way out. This school sits on the beach at Sorobon and teaches windsurfing and wingfoiling, with rental for those who already sail. Open 365 days, 9:00 to 17:00.

Its only published number is a Dutch mobile, which it gives as its WhatsApp line.
