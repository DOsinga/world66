Bonaire is built for shore diving: rent a pickup, load it with tanks, and drive to the yellow-painted stones that mark the sites along the west coast. Dive Friends is set up exactly for that, with seven locations, several of them at dive resorts, where you can swap tanks at any branch, and unlimited air or nitrox packages. It also arranges truck packages with partner rental firms.

The company publishes no single street address, so the pin is on Kralendijk. Its phone numbers are Dutch and US lines; the WhatsApp number is local.
