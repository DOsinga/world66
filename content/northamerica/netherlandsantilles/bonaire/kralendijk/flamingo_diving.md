A PADI 5 Star centre on the water at Belnem, south of town. Unlimited air, small guided groups and private "caddy" dives with an instructor carrying your gear. Its speciality is the **ostracod night dive**, timed for the few nights after a full moon when tiny crustaceans put on a display of blue bioluminescence.
