The Lac Bay mangroves can only be entered with a certified guide, and this centre has been running the tours since 2002. One- and two-hour kayak trips go through the channels, where juvenile reef fish shelter among the roots, and a night paddle shows off the bioluminescence. It shares a phone number with Outdoor Bonaire, the cave-tour company.
