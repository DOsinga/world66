Ground handler since 1995 with a desk at the Plaza Resort. Its **Washington Slagbaai** safari takes about four hours on the park's rough tracks to the park's blowhole, its lighthouse and the hidden bays of the north, which saves renting a high-clearance car yourself. It also runs a shorter south-island tour and a cave snorkel trip.
