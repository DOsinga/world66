Mangazina di Rei centres on the former government storehouse from which provisions were once distributed to people living around Rincon. Exhibitions and guided activities use the building, gardens, plants, food, music, and craft to explain the practical life and cultural traditions of rural Bonaire.

The site is strongest when staff or a guide can add context, so check the programme and opening hours before setting out. Pair it with Rincon rather than rushing through on the way to Washington-Slagbaai.
