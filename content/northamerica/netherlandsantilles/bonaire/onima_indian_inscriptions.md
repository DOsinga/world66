Red pictographs survive beneath a shallow limestone overhang near Onima on Bonaire's northeast side. They are among the island's clearest traces of Indigenous life before European colonisation, though their precise age and meaning are not securely known.

The site is small and lightly interpreted. Look upward under the ledge, do not touch the pigment or limestone, and combine the stop with Rincon or the northern driving loop. The rough coast nearby is for looking, not swimming.
