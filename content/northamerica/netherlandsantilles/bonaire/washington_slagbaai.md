The whole northwestern third of Bonaire is a national park — a wilderness of cactus, divi-divi trees, and limestone outcrops with **Mount Brandaris**, the island's 241 metre high point, in the middle. Two dirt-track loops cover the park: a 24 kilometre short route and a 34 kilometre long route. You drive yourself in your own car (4WD recommended for the long route), stop at viewpoints, beaches, salt pans, and the flamingo lagoon at Goto, and return to the gate before 5pm.

The park is one of the great wildlife destinations of the Caribbean: flamingos at Goto Meer, yellow-shouldered Amazon parrots in the wooded valleys, iguanas everywhere, and the snorkelling at **Wayaka II** and **Slagbaai Bay** is excellent. Bring a lunch, plenty of water, and book ahead for entry tickets in high season.
