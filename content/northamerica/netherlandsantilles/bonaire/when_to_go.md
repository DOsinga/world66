---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Bonaire's climate is reliable to the point of monotonous. Temperatures sit at 27–30 °C all year, the trade winds blow constantly, and the rainfall is the lowest in the southern Caribbean at around 500 mm a year — and most of that falls in short showers between October and January.

**The peak season is December to April**, when North Americans and Europeans escape winter. Prices for hotels and dive packages are highest. **May to August** is the shoulder season — quieter and just as warm, with the best windsurfing conditions at Lac Bay. **September to November** is the quietest period; some restaurants close.

The island sits below the hurricane belt and has not had a direct hit in living memory. Mid-week rainshowers are short and refresh the dust.
