Bandabou is Curaçao's western half, where Willemstad's suburbs give way to low cactus hills, old plantation land, fishing villages, and a string of small beaches cut into the limestone coast. This is the side of the island to keep a rental car for. Buses are too infrequent for a satisfying beach-hopping day.

The coast supplies the easy pleasures. [Playa Kenepa Grandi](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/playa_kenepa_grandi) has the grand view from above and [Playa Lagun](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/playa_lagun) is a narrow fishermen's cove with good snorkelling. [Cas Abao Beach](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/cas_abao_beach), [Kokomo Beach](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/kokomo_beach), and [Playa Porto Mari](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/playa_porto_mari) have more facilities without losing the clear water and easy shore access that make the west coast so appealing. Bring your own snorkel if you have one; you will use it repeatedly.

Inland and on the north coast, Bandabou becomes rougher. [Christoffel National Park](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/christoffel_national_park) protects the island's highest ground and best hiking, while [Shete Boka National Park](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/shete_boka_national_park) follows a coast of caves, inlets, and violent surf. Start early, carry more water than you think you need, and treat the west as a full day rather than a quick loop from Willemstad.

The west also holds much of Curaçao's rural history. [Museo Tula](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/museo_tula) occupies the plantation where the 1795 revolt against slavery began, while [Kas di Pal'i Maishi](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/kas_di_pali_maishi) preserves the domestic life and traditions of the island's post-emancipation countryside. These small sites add essential context to the landhouses and salt pans passed on the drive.
