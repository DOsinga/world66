Blue Bay is a broad, managed beach northwest of Willemstad, with a gently sloping seabed, plenty of shade, and reef access from shore. Loungers, showers, food, a playground, and a water-sports centre make it one of the easier beaches for families or first-time snorkellers.

The beach sits inside a gated resort and charges admission. Come for convenience rather than seclusion; on busy days it feels more like a resort facility than a west-coast cove.
