Daaibooi is a relaxed public beach near Sint Willibrordus, set in a small bay with shade shelters and a simple snack bar. The sand gives way to rock along both sides, where the snorkelling becomes more interesting.

A short trail climbs into the dry countryside west of the bay. Daaibooi has enough facilities for an easy beach stop but remains less organised and commercial than Cas Abao or Blue Bay.
