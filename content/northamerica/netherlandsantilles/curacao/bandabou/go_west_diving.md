A dive shop on the beach at Playa Kalki, at the far western tip of the island, where the reef starts a short swim from the sand. **Alice in Wonderland**, the house reef, is one of the best shore dives on Curaçao; boat dives reach the rest of the west-end coast. Unlimited shore-air packages make sense if you are staying out here for a week.
