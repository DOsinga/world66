Hòfi Mango is a landscaped nature and heritage park on former plantation land near Santa Cruz. Trails pass old mango trees, cactus and palm gardens, viewpoints, and the remains of a sugar mill and distillery.

The park mixes history, gardens, art installations, and paid activities, so it feels more curated than Curaçao's national parks. Check the current map and admission options before visiting; an early walk is more comfortable than the exposed middle of the day.
