Playa Jeremi is a narrow, undeveloped cove reached by uneven steps from the coast road. Its clear water, rocky sides, and west-facing position make it a good late-afternoon swim and sunset stop.

There is little shade and no dependable food or equipment rental, which is part of the appeal. Bring what you need, wear water shoes if you dislike a rough entry, and leave before the unlit steps become awkward after sunset.
