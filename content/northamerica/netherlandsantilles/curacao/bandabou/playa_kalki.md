Playa Kalki is a small Westpunt beach best known as the shore entry for the Alice in Wonderland dive site. Steps descend to a narrow strip of sand and coral rubble, with clear water and a reef that rewards both divers and confident snorkellers.

Facilities are tied to the adjoining resort and dive operation, and access arrangements can change. Check locally before setting out and bring water shoes for the entry. The underwater setting is the draw; travellers looking mainly for sand have better choices nearby.
