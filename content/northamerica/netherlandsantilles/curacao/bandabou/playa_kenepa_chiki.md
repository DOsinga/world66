Playa Kenepa Chiki, commonly called Kleine Knip, is the smaller and quieter neighbour of Grote Knip. The compact public beach sits between rocky headlands, with clear water for swimming and snorkelling and fewer facilities than its famous sibling.

There is little reason to choose between the two when driving this far west: stop at Grote Knip for the grand view, then use Kleine Knip when you want a calmer patch of sand. Bring food, water, and shade rather than relying on vendors.
