Playa Santa Cruz is a wide, low-key beach backed by mangroves near Soto. The bay attracts birds and has some shade, food, and basic facilities, but runoff from the mangroves can make the water less clear than at Curaçao's best snorkelling beaches.

The beach is also the usual starting point for boat trips or the coastal route towards Blue Room Cave. Treat it as a practical base and quiet local beach rather than a must-see stretch of sand in its own right.
