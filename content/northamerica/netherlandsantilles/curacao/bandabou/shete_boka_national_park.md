Shete Boka National Park follows almost ten kilometres of Curaçao's exposed north coast. Waves drive into narrow inlets and limestone caves with tremendous force, most dramatically at Boka Tabla, where a short path descends to a viewing platform beside the sea cave.

A driving loop and walking trails connect several bokas. Sea turtles nest on some of the pocket beaches, so barriers and closed areas matter. There is little shade and absolutely no safe swimming along this coast; bring water and keep well back when the surf is high.
