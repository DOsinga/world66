---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bandabou combines Curaçao's strongest beaches with its two headline nature parks and several small museums in former plantation country. A good day pairs one early walk or cultural stop with two beaches, rather than trying to collect every cove along the road. Playa Piskadó is the best-known wildlife encounter, while the Blue Room requires a swim or boat trip and deserves more planning than an ordinary beach stop.
