A small SSI school run by a Dutch family on the Caracasbaai road at Jan Thiel. It keeps groups deliberately small and offers private introductory dives at **Tugboat** and Caracasbaai, two of the easiest and most rewarding shallow sites on the island. Lessons are in Dutch or English.
