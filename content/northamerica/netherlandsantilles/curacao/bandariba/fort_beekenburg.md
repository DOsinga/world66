Fort Beekenburg has guarded the entrance to Caracas Bay since the early 18th century. Its round stone tower and lower gun battery defended one of Curaçao's best natural harbours, and the fort remains the island's best-preserved outer-bay stronghold.

You can climb through the open compound for views across the bay and Spanish Water. Nearby stand the atmospheric remains of a 19th-century quarantine station. The ground is rough and there is little interpretation, so sturdy shoes and a little background reading improve the visit.
