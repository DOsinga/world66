The island's windsurf school, also trading as Windsurfing Curaçao, on the sheltered water off the Caracasbaai road where the trade wind blows steady and the sea stays flat. Lessons and rental for windsurfing, wingfoiling and stand-up paddle, and a guided SUP tour through the mangroves of **Spaanse Water**. The WhatsApp number is not the same as the phone line.
