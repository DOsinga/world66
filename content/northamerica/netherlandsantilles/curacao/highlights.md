## Give Willemstad more than a photo stop

[Willemstad](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/willemstad) has the Caribbean's most distinctive city centre. Walk across the floating [Queen Emma Bridge](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/willemstad/queen_emma_bridge), look back at the [Handelskade](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/willemstad/handelskade), then continue into Otrobanda rather than turning back with the cruise crowd. The [Mikvé Israel-Emanuel Synagogue](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/willemstad/mikve_israel_synagogue) and [Kura Hulanda Museum](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/willemstad/kura_hulanda_museum) give the beauty some necessary historical weight.

## Beach-hop through Bandabou

The west coast holds Curaçao's best beaches. [Bandabou](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou) strings together compact coves with pale sand, limestone cliffs, and clear water. [Playa Kenepa Grandi](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/playa_kenepa_grandi) is the grand postcard view; [Playa Lagun](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/playa_lagun) is smaller and better for slipping straight into the water with a snorkel. Go early, bring water and shade, and do not leave valuables in the car.

## Walk into the reef

Much of Curaçao's diving is wonderfully simple. At many south-coast sites you can gear up on shore, wade in, and swim out to the reef without a boat. Snorkellers benefit from the same easy access. Conditions vary, so ask a local dive shop about currents and entry points rather than assuming every pretty cove is beginner-friendly.

## See the island's rough side

[Christoffel National Park](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/christoffel_national_park) protects the island's highest ground, old plantation landscapes, and native dry forest. The summit walk is best started early, before the heat takes over. Nearby [Shete Boka National Park](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou/shete_boka_national_park) follows a very different coast, where waves explode into caves and narrow inlets. Stay behind the barriers: this shore is for watching, not swimming.

## Make an eastern loop

[Bandariba](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandariba) makes a compact counterpoint to the long western beach day. Climb [Fort Beekenburg](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandariba/fort_beekenburg), snorkel over the shallow [Tugboat wreck](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandariba/tugboat), and stop at [Den Paradera](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandariba/den_paradera) to understand how island plants became food, medicine, and household knowledge.

## Eat the island's history

Curaçao's cooking makes sense of its mixed past. Try *kabritu stobá* (goat stew), *sòpi kadushi* (cactus soup), *funchi* (cornmeal), and *keshi yená*, a baked cheese shell filled with seasoned meat. In Willemstad, the stalls at [Marshe Bieu](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/willemstad/marshe_bieu) are the best place to begin.
