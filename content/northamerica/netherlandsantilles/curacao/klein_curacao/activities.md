Nobody lives on Klein Curaçao, so the only way to see it is on a day boat. The crossing from the east end takes about two hours and can be rough on the way out. Boats usually stop at the island's reef for snorkelling, and turtles are common; you then have a few hours on the long white beach below the abandoned lighthouse. All the operators below leave from Banda Riba early in the morning.
