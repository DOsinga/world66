Curaçao opens up in two directions from Willemstad. [Bandabou](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandabou) to the west is the stronger full-day trip, combining the island's best cove beaches with Christoffel and Shete Boka national parks. Leave early, particularly if you plan to hike.

[Bandariba](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/bandariba) to the east is easier to explore in a half-day. Fort Beekenburg, the Tugboat wreck, and Director's Bay form a compact cluster around Caracas Bay; Den Paradera and the ostrich farm can extend the loop into the countryside.

[Klein Curaçao](/northamerica/netherlandsantilles/curacao/klein_curacao) is the full-day boat option: a long white beach, clear water, an abandoned lighthouse, and wrecks on an otherwise uninhabited island. Compare boats and included facilities carefully, and prepare for a rough outward crossing.

A rental car is the practical choice for either road trip. Public transport reaches some settlements but does not make a flexible sightseeing circuit possible.
