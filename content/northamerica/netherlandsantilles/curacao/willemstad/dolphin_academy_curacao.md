Dolphin Academy operates beside the Curaçao Sea Aquarium, with dolphins kept in seawater lagoons connected to the sea. Booked programmes range from dockside encounters to swimming and diving sessions, while some dolphin presentations can be seen during a Sea Aquarium visit.

This is a close-contact captive-animal attraction, so it will not suit every traveller. If it does interest you, reserve ahead and read the current programme details carefully: the age, swimming ability, and diving requirements differ by activity.
