---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Willemstad's food scene reflects Curaçao's multicultural history. Indonesian, Surinamese, Dutch, and Caribbean flavours appear on menus throughout the city. Local specialities include Kadushi cactus soup, stoba (a slow-cooked meat stew), and keshi yena — a whole gouda cheese stuffed with spiced meat and baked. Fresh fish is excellent throughout.
