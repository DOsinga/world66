Landhuis Bloemhof is an 18th-century country house turned cultural centre, with changing exhibitions, archives, sculpture studios, and a garden of native dry-country plants. It offers a useful look at Curaçao's contemporary art scene in a historic setting rather than treating the landhuis as a frozen period interior.

The garden's centrepiece is Herman van Bergen's Cathedral of Thorns, a building-sized labyrinth assembled from thorny sumpiña branches and filled with work by other artists. It can be visited during Bloemhof's daytime opening, while occasional evening events illuminate the structure and add performances. Check the current programme before travelling out from central Willemstad.
