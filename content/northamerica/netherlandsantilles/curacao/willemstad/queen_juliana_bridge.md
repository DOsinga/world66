The Queen Juliana Bridge carries road traffic high above Sint Annabaai, allowing large ships to enter the harbour beneath it. Opened in 1974, its sweeping arch has become a defining part of Willemstad's skyline and gives drivers a brief panorama across Punda, Otrobanda, and the Schottegat.

This is a working motorway bridge, not a pedestrian attraction: stopping, walking, and photography from the roadway are unsafe and prohibited. Admire the structure from the waterfront or take in the view while someone else drives.
