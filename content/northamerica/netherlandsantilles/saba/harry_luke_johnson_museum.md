Harry Luke Johnson Museum is a small Saba history museum in an 1840 house built for a Dutch captain. Much of the original furniture and household material remains, giving the rooms a lived-in feel.

It is a modest stop, but a worthwhile one if you are in Windwardside and want context for the island's Dutch Caribbean history before heading back to the trails or viewpoints.
