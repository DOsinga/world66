Saba rises straight out of deep water, and the diving is on volcanic spires and walls rather than the gentle fringing reefs of the islands to the south. Sea Saba has dived the marine park since 1985, from the island's only harbour at Fort Bay, using more than thirty moored sites. The best known are the **Pinnacles**, deep seamounts that suit experienced divers. The old Saba Deep website now points here.
