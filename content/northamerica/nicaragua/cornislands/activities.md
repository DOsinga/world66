The reef here sees a fraction of the traffic of Belize or Roatán and is correspondingly intact — walls, coral heads, nurse sharks and eagle rays, with visibility up to thirty metres when the water is calm.

February to May is the settled season. Big Corn has the airstrip and the larger shop; Little Corn, half an hour away by panga, has no cars and is the better island to stay on.
