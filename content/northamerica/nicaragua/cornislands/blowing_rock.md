The most celebrated dive site in Nicaragua. A volcanic pinnacle breaks the surface roughly 25 kilometres southwest of Big Corn, rising abruptly from a deep sandy bottom. Visibility is regularly 25 metres or more, and the structure attracts large schools of jacks and snapper, nurse sharks, turtles, and the occasional hammerhead in season.

It is an advanced dive — open ocean, exposed conditions, and a long boat ride to reach — so trips run only when the weather is right. Both Big Corn and Little Corn dive operators offer it as a special two-tank day, typically for around USD $80–110.
