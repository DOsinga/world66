Little Corn has no cars, no paved roads and a single generator-fed grid, and Dolphin Dive is the locally owned shop on it — courses from open water upwards, daily boats to the reef, and night dives.

Getting here is a panga ride of about half an hour from Big Corn, which is rough when the wind is up and a good part of the reason the island stays quiet.
