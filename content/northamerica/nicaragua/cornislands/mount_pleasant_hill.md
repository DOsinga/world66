A short, signposted walk inland from the road around Big Corn leads up to the island's highest point, around 100 metres above the sea. The path is rough and can be muddy after rain — closed shoes are sensible — but the climb takes only fifteen or twenty minutes from the bottom.

The reward is a 360-degree view across the whole island: the airstrip at Brig Bay, the reefs visible as dark patches in the turquoise shallows, and the coast curving away on both sides. Mornings are best for clarity; afternoons can bring haze and the occasional shower.
