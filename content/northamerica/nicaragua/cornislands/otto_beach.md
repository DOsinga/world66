Otto Beach is the postcard beach at the north end of Little Corn — a curving stretch of white sand backed by coconut palms, with a reef close enough to swim out and snorkel from the shore. A handful of small eco-lodges sit just back from the sand, but the beach itself is wide and empty enough that you can usually find a stretch to yourself.

To get here you walk across the island from the village: a flat 20-minute path through coconut groves and patches of jungle. There are no shops on this side, so bring water and any snacks; the lodge restaurants will serve food and cold drinks if you stop in.
