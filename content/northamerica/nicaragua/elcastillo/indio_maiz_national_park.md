Indio Maiz National Park protects one of Nicaragua's major rainforest areas, stretching from near El Castillo toward the Caribbean lowlands. It is visited for wildlife, river travel, and dense tropical forest rather than for built sights.

Many travellers use El Castillo as the practical base for guided day trips into the park.
