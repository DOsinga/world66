Granada's tours are the [isletas](/northamerica/nicaragua/granada/isletas), [Mombacho](/northamerica/nicaragua/granada/mombacho) and the volcanoes within an hour. Kayaking the isletas is better than the motor-launch version — quieter, closer to the birds, and you can get into channels the boats cannot.

[Masaya](/northamerica/nicaragua/masaya) after dark, when you can see the lava lake glowing in the crater, is the standout day trip and is usually sold as an evening run from here.
