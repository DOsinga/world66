The Cathedral of Our Lady of the Assumption stands on the eastern side of Parque Central and is the most recognisable landmark in Granada. Its yellow neoclassical facade, twin bell towers, and ochre interior make it one of the handsomest colonial churches in Central America.

The interior is cool and dimly lit, with painted vaults and wooden pews worn smooth by generations of worshippers. If the towers are open, climb them for a panoramic view over the red-tiled rooftops of the city and out toward Lago de Nicaragua. Early morning or late afternoon light is best for photographs of the exterior.
