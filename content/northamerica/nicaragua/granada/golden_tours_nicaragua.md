The [isletas](/northamerica/nicaragua/granada/isletas) — 365 small islands thrown into the lake when Mombacho collapsed — are best seen from a kayak rather than a motor launch, which is what this outfit does: a couple of hours paddling the channels between them, with herons, kingfishers and the odd howler monkey.

They also run the wider Granada circuit: [Mombacho](/northamerica/nicaragua/granada/mombacho)'s cloud forest, the [Masaya](/northamerica/nicaragua/masaya) volcano at night, and [Laguna de Apoyo](/northamerica/nicaragua/laguna_de_apoyo).
