Mi Museo is a small, quiet stop near the centre of Granada, built around a private collection of pre-Columbian ceramics from Nicaragua. It is not as architecturally impressive as the old San Francisco convent, but the focus is sharper: pots, figures, and fragments that show how sophisticated local pottery traditions were long before the colonial city existed.

The museum works best as a short cultural pause between Granada's churches and streets. The colonial house and courtyard keep the visit relaxed, and the collection gives useful context for the islands, lakeshore settlements, and indigenous cultures that are otherwise easy to flatten into background history.
