Two volcanoes rising out of a freshwater lake, joined by an isthmus: that is the island, and it is also the list of things to do. **Concepción** is the perfect cone on the northern half, an eight-to-ten-hour return climb, hot and exposed near the top. **Maderas** on the southern half is lower, wetter and harder underfoot — cloud forest, deep mud, and a crater lagoon at the top you can wade into. Guides are compulsory on both, and worth having on Maderas whatever the rule says.

Between them the **Istián** channel runs through mangrove and reed, and paddling it at dawn is the quietest thing on the island: caimans, turtles, and more birds than anywhere else in Nicaragua outside the Río San Juan.

The rest is gentler — the spring-fed pools at **Ojo de Agua**, the petroglyphs on the Maderas slopes, and plantation roads that are ideal on a bicycle and punishing on a scooter.
