A self-guided nature trail of about 2.5 km on a small hill in Sintiope, on the Concepción side of the island, with viewpoints over both volcanoes and Lake Nicaragua. The highest viewpoint is around 200 metres up. The trail is private: international visitors pay US$5, at the office of [Volcancito](/northamerica/nicaragua/isla_ometepe/volcancito) next to the trail entrance. It is an easy alternative for anyone who doesn't want to climb a volcano.
