Ometepe is built for the active traveller. The two volcanoes anchor every itinerary — Concepción for the punishing summit climb, Maderas for a forested hike to a crater lagoon. Around them are petroglyphs in farmyards, freshwater swimming holes, kayak routes through mangrove channels, and viewpoints that look out across Lake Nicaragua to the mainland.
