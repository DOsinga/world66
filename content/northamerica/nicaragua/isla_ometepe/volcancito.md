A local outfit in Sintiope on the Maderas side, guiding the two volcano climbs, the kayak through the Istián channel between them, the San Ramón waterfall and walks out to the petroglyphs. Both volcanoes legally require a guide, and on Maderas that rule earns its keep: the top half is mud, roots and cloud forest, and people come down it slower than they went up.

The address is a church in a village rather than a street number, which is how Ometepe works; the pin here is the island rather than the door.
