A locally run tourism business in Sintiope, on the Concepción side of the island, next to the Evangelical church. Volcancito guides hikes on both volcanoes, kayaking on the Istián River, visits to the San Ramón waterfall and trips to the island's petroglyphs. Both volcano climbs legally require a guide, and on Maderas that rule earns its keep: the top half is mud, roots and cloud forest, and people come down it slower than they went up.

Volcancito also runs [Little Concepcion](/northamerica/nicaragua/isla_ometepe/little_concepcion), a short self-guided trail that starts at its office. The site gives a WhatsApp number for bookings.
