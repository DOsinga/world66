Jinotega sits at nearly 1,000 metres in the northern Nicaraguan highlands, ringed by mountains that rise to 1,500 metres and kept cool by altitude — a welcome contrast to the sweltering lowlands. Known as the "City of Mists" for the clouds that roll through its valleys, it is the heart of Nicaragua's coffee country and a base for exploring some of the most dramatic highland scenery in Central America.

The town itself is small and unhurried, centred on the [Cathedral of San Juan](/northamerica/nicaragua/jinotega/cathedral_san_juan) and a grid of streets where the pace of life is distinctly slower than Managua. The [Mercado Municipal](/northamerica/nicaragua/jinotega/mercado_municipal) is the social hub, worth a morning visit for the highland produce and cheap breakfasts. The surrounding countryside is the real draw: [coffee fincas](/northamerica/nicaragua/jinotega/coffee_finca_tours) climb the hillsides, [Lake Apanás](/northamerica/nicaragua/jinotega/lake_apanas) shimmers to the north, and the [Cerro Datanlí-El Diablo Nature Reserve](/northamerica/nicaragua/jinotega/cerro_datanli) harbours cloud forest and the resplendent quetzal. You can reach Jinotega in about three hours by express bus from Managua's Mercado Mayoreo, or in an hour from [Matagalpa](/northamerica/nicaragua/matagalpa) along a road with spectacular views over the valleys and mountains of the northern sierras.
