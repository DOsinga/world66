Reserva El Jaguar is a private coffee farm and cloud-forest reserve in the mountains outside Jinotega. Its strongest appeal is birdlife: this is the sort of cool, green Nicaraguan highland where coffee, forest conservation, and early-morning binoculars all belong in the same sentence.

It is not a casual town-centre sight, so arrange transport and check visiting conditions before setting off. For travellers already interested in Jinotega’s coffee country, though, El Jaguar gives the landscape a sharper focus than a generic finca tour: conservation, shade-grown coffee, and mountain quiet all in one place.
