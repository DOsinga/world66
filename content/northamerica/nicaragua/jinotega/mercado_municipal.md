The Mercado Municipal at the centre of Jinotega is the practical and social hub of the surrounding highland region — farmers come in from villages across the mountains to sell produce, buy supplies, and meet. The market runs daily but is at its busiest on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday mornings.

The food section is worth exploring: simple comedores serve gallo pinto, cuajada cheese, and coffee for breakfast at prices well below what any restaurant would charge. The craft section has limited but authentic textiles and bags woven by local cooperatives. The market is not especially touristic — it functions primarily as a commercial space for local people — which is exactly what makes wandering through it worthwhile.
