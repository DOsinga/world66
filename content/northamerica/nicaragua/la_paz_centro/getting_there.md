---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

La Paz Centro sits on the busy highway between [León](/northamerica/nicaragua/leon) (about 30 km north-west) and [Managua](/northamerica/nicaragua/managua) (about 55 km south-east). Any bus running between the two stops at the entrance to town — buses leave both cities every 15-20 minutes during the day, and the fare is around 25 córdobas from either direction.

The drop-off is on the highway; the centre, the pottery workshops, and the quesillo market are a five-minute walk inland. A small taxi rank at the highway stop will run you to specific addresses for 20-30 córdobas.
