---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Most travellers see Lake Nicaragua from one of two bases: [Granada](/northamerica/nicaragua/granada) on the northern shore or [Ometepe Island](/northamerica/nicaragua/isla_ometepe) in the middle. From Granada the easiest day trip is a panga boat through the [Isletas de Granada](/northamerica/nicaragua/lakenicaragua/isletas_de_granada). With more time, head out to the quieter [Zapatera Island](/northamerica/nicaragua/lakenicaragua/zapatera_island).

On Ometepe, the two volcanoes — [Concepción](/northamerica/nicaragua/lakenicaragua/concepcion_volcano) and [Maderas](/northamerica/nicaragua/lakenicaragua/maderas_volcano) — are the headline acts, both serious all-day climbs. Easier days are spent at [Charco Verde lagoon](/northamerica/nicaragua/lakenicaragua/charco_verde) or seeking out the island's [pre-Columbian petroglyphs](/northamerica/nicaragua/lakenicaragua/petroglyphs_ometepe).

The lake has two further oddities worth knowing about: its [bull sharks](/northamerica/nicaragua/lakenicaragua/bull_sharks), which still swim up the [Río San Juan](/northamerica/nicaragua/lakenicaragua/rio_san_juan) from the Caribbean, and the remote [Solentiname Archipelago](/northamerica/nicaragua/solentiname_islands) at the southern end of the lake, a long boat ride from anywhere but worth the effort for its naive art tradition.
