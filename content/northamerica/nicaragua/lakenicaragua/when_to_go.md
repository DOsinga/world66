---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season runs roughly from December to April, with sunshine, low humidity and a steady breeze across the lake. This is when the volcano climbs are realistic — the trails dry out, the views from the summit are clear, and the cool evenings make Ometepe particularly pleasant. It is also the busiest period, especially around Christmas, New Year and Easter (Semana Santa), when Nicaraguan families take over Granada and the lakeside beaches.

The rainy season — May to November — is greener, cheaper and more humid. Afternoon downpours are short but intense, and the cloud forest on Maderas drips constantly; serious volcano hikes are best avoided. October is statistically the wettest month. Hurricanes occasionally affect the Caribbean side and push moisture across to the lake, but the Pacific shore is mostly shielded.
