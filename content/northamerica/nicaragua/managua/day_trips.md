---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Managua is more useful as a base than as a destination, and almost everything worth seeing is within two hours of it.

[Granada](/northamerica/nicaragua/granada), the colonial city on Lake Nicaragua, is an hour south and the most common day out; [Masaya](/northamerica/nicaragua/masaya) is closer still, with a craft market and [a volcano you can drive to the rim of](/northamerica/nicaragua/masaya/volcan_masaya) and look down into a live lava lake — one of very few places on earth where that is possible from a car park.

Northwest, [León](/northamerica/nicaragua/leon) is about ninety minutes, with the ruins of [León Viejo](/northamerica/nicaragua/leon_viejo) and the cone of [Momotombo](/northamerica/nicaragua/la_paz_centro/momotombo) on the way, and [Cerro Negro](/northamerica/nicaragua/leon/cerro_negro) beyond it for the volcano boarding.

[Laguna de Apoyo](/northamerica/nicaragua/laguna_de_apoyo), a crater lake with clear warm water, is forty minutes away and the best swim near the capital. [Isla Ometepe](/northamerica/nicaragua/isla_ometepe) is possible in a day but deserves two or three.
