The Malecón is a lakefront promenade along the southern shore of Lake Managua, stretching from the old city centre westward past the Rubén Darío theatre toward Puerto Salvador Allende. It is the most pleasant place in central Managua for an evening walk, with views across the water to the twin volcanoes Momotombo and Momotombito on the far shore.

The promenade was upgraded in the 2010s with lighting, benches, and a series of sculptures. Several outdoor restaurants operate along the strip, and on weekends vendors set up food stalls with quesillos, grilled corn, and cold drinks. Lake Managua (Xolotlán) is heavily polluted — the city has discharged wastewater into it for decades — but from the malecón it looks beautiful, particularly in the golden light of late afternoon. The juxtaposition of the lake's visual appeal and its environmental condition is a recurring theme of Managua.
