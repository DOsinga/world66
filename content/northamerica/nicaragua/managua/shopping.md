---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

For handicrafts, the best place in Managua is the **Roberto Huembes Market**, where you will find hammocks, pottery, wooden carvings, leather goods, and embroidered textiles from across Nicaragua. The prices are good and the selection is wide — this is a more practical shopping experience than the tourist-oriented stalls in Granada. Bargaining is expected.

The **Mercado Oriental** is the largest market in Central America by area and a genuine sensory experience — a vast, dense, mostly covered labyrinth selling everything from car parts to fresh mangoes. It is not particularly aimed at tourists but is fascinating to walk through; keep a firm grip on your belongings.

For more comfortable shopping, the **Metrocentro** and **Galerías Santo Domingo** malls have international chain stores, supermarkets, and food courts. They function as social centres for the middle class and are air-conditioned, which is welcome in Managua's heat.
