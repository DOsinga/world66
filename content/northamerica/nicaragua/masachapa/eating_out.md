---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating is the best thing to do in Masachapa. A row of simple comedores lines the beachfront, each serving the same essential menu: whole fried fish, grilled shrimp, occasionally lobster, served with rice, beans, and tostones. Prices are low by any standard. Order a cold Toña beer, pull a plastic chair to the water's edge, and eat with your hands.
