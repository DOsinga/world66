title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Masaya's two main draws are the [Volcán Masaya National Park](/northamerica/nicaragua/masaya/volcan_masaya) and the [Mercado de Artesanías](/northamerica/nicaragua/masaya/mercado_artesanias). The active volcano with its glowing lava lake is one of the few places in the world where you can drive to a crater rim and look directly into a lava lake below. The market — housed in a gothic fortress-like building in the city centre — is the best place in Nicaragua to buy crafts. Thursday evenings bring the [Jueves de Verbena](/northamerica/nicaragua/masaya/jueves_de_verbena) street festival.
