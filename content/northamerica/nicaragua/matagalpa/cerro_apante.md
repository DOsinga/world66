Cerro Apante Nature Reserve rises directly from the southern edge of Matagalpa — a cloud forest within walking distance of the city centre, though most visitors take a taxi or moto-taxi up to the trailheads. The reserve protects a fragment of the highland forests that once covered much of this part of Nicaragua, and it holds a surprisingly good bird list given its proximity to the city.

Trails wind through misty forest and up to viewpoints over the Matagalpa valley. The reserve is managed for both conservation and watershed protection — the streams that run off Cerro Apante feed the city's water supply. Entrance is minimal. The hike to the summit takes about three hours return and gives panoramic views over the coffee highlands.
