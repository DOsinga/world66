Matagalpa eats well for a highland city its size. The influx of coffee-sector travelers and NGO workers over the decades has created a reasonable restaurant scene beyond the standard Nicaraguan staples. You'll eat rice, beans, and cheese regardless — they're inescapable and generally good — but there are options for more varied meals. Coffee is, predictably, excellent throughout the city.
