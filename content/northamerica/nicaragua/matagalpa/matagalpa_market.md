Matagalpa's main market is a large, sprawling covered and open-air affair a few blocks from the central plaza. It is a working market first — produce from the surrounding highland farms, hardware, clothing, food stalls — and it smells and sounds like the real thing.

The coffee sold here is some of the best value in Nicaragua. You can buy freshly roasted beans from the surrounding fincas at prices far below what you'd pay anywhere else. The food section has excellent cheap lunches: rice, beans, chicken, cheese, all served in proper portions. The market is most animated on Saturday morning when farmers come in from the villages.
