La Vita è Bella is a reliable Italian-influenced restaurant in the city centre that has been a traveler staple in Matagalpa for years. The menu covers pasta, pizza, and some Nicaraguan dishes, with consistently good quality and reasonable prices. The coffee is excellent, as you'd expect this close to the source.

The setting is pleasant — a courtyard or covered dining room depending on the weather — and the atmosphere is easy. Good for a full dinner or just coffee and cake in the afternoon.
