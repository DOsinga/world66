---
title: When to Go
type: section
tags: [when_to_go]
---

Matagalpa's highland climate makes it pleasant year-round by Nicaraguan standards — the 700-metre elevation keeps temperatures in a comfortable 18–26°C range even in the dry season. Nights can be cool enough for a light jacket, which is a relief after the lowland heat.

The dry season (November to April) is the easiest time to travel, with clear days and no rain. This is also harvest season for coffee (October–January), when the fincas are at their most active — if you want to see or participate in harvesting, come between October and January. The rainy season (May–October) makes the highlands green and atmospheric, but roads to some rural fincas can become difficult. The rains usually come in the afternoon, leaving mornings clear.
