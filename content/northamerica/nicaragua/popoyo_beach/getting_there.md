---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Popoyo is roughly two hours south of Managua by road. Take the Pan-American Highway south to Rivas, then head west toward Tola. From Tola, a paved road leads to the coast — follow signs for Popoyo or Guasacate. A taxi from Rivas to Popoyo costs around $20–30.

There is no reliable public transport directly to Popoyo. The most practical option from Managua is to rent a car or take a taxi-share (colectivo) to Tola and arrange a taxi from there for the final stretch. From [Granada](/northamerica/nicaragua/granada), the drive is about 90 minutes via Rivas.

[San Juan del Sur](/northamerica/nicaragua/sanjuandelsur) is about an hour south along the coast and better connected — if arriving by long-distance bus, consider stopping there and arranging onward transport.
