Rivas is the town on the isthmus between Lake Nicaragua and the Pacific, and almost every traveller in southern Nicaragua passes through it without stopping: the ferry to [Isla Ometepe](/northamerica/nicaragua/isla_ometepe) leaves from San Jorge five minutes away, [San Juan del Sur](/northamerica/nicaragua/sanjuandelsur) is half an hour west, and the Costa Rican border at Peñas Blancas is forty minutes south.

If you are stuck here, the church on the central park has a dome painted with a naval battle in which Catholic ships defeat Protestant ones, which is not something you see every day, and the market is a good one.
