San Carlos sits where Lake Nicaragua empties into the Río San Juan, and it is the junction for everything in the southeast: boats downriver to [El Castillo](/northamerica/nicaragua/elcastillo) and the Indio Maíz reserve, out to the [Solentiname islands](/northamerica/nicaragua/solentiname_islands), across to Costa Rica at Los Chiles, and the long ferry back up the lake to Granada.

The town is a working port rather than an attraction — a fort ruin above the waterfront, a malecón, and a market — and most people arrive, sleep once and leave on the morning boat. The ferry from Granada takes around fourteen hours and is an experience in its own right.
