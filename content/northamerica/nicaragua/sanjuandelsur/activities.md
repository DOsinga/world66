---
title: Activities
type: section
---

The town beach barely breaks, so surfing here means getting to one of the beaches north or south — Remanso and Maderas for learning, Hermosa and Colorado when it is bigger. Schools include the transport, which is the main reason to use one rather than hiring a board.

Boat trips run up the coast for fishing and snorkelling, and the Sunday Funday pool crawl is a fixture that you will either seek out or carefully avoid.
