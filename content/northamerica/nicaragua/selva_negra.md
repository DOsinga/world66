Selva Negra is a coffee estate in the mountains north of [Matagalpa](/northamerica/nicaragua/matagalpa), founded by German settlers in the 1890s and still run by their descendants, with cloud forest above the plantation and around twenty kilometres of trails through it.

The forest is the reason to come: quetzals between February and May, howler monkeys, three-wattled bellbirds, and orchids in quantity. The estate runs coffee tours through the whole process from seedling to cup, and is unusually serious about the ecology of it — the farm is shade-grown, organic, and generates its own power.

It is about half an hour above Matagalpa on the road to Jinotega, and the temperature drops noticeably as you climb.
