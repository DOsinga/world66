Mirador Cerro Gaital is a lookout on the northern ridge above Anton Valley. The signposted trail climbs to a platform with a broad view over the valley, and the route is short enough to fit into a half-day outing.

The trail can be slippery after rain, so wear shoes with grip and avoid rushing the descent. You will need a ride to the trailhead, as it sits away from the village centre.
