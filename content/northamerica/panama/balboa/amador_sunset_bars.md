The western-facing stretch of the Amador Causeway catches the last light of the day over the Pacific entrance to the Canal, and several open-air bars capitalise on this. The format is consistent: plastic tables on a terrace, cold Panamanian beer (Balboa and Atlas are the local lagers), and a view of anchored container ships going orange in the evening light.

It's casual and popular with a local crowd on weekends. Come an hour before sunset for a table, order a beer and a plate of fried food, and watch the ships. A pleasant end to a day in Balboa.
