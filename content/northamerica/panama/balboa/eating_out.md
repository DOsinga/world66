---
title: "Eating Out in Balboa"
type: section
---

Balboa itself has limited dining options — the area is primarily residential and administrative. The best eating near here is along the [Calzada de Amador](/northamerica/panama/balboa/calzada_de_amador), where a string of restaurants lines the causeway with views across the Canal's Pacific approach. Most are casual, aimed at locals and tourists alike, and the seafood is reliable.

The [Miraflores Locks](/northamerica/panama/balboa/miraflores_locks) visitor centre has a restaurant on an upper floor with a direct view of ships transiting through the locks — a novelty that justifies an otherwise ordinary lunch.
