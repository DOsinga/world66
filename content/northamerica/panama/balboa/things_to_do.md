title: "Things to Do in Balboa"
type: section
---

Balboa's main draw is the [Miraflores Locks](/northamerica/panama/balboa/things_to_do/miraflores_locks), a few kilometres from the town centre, where you can watch ships be lifted or lowered between the Pacific Ocean and Miraflores Lake. It's one of the great engineering spectacles in the world. Beyond this, the [Calzada de Amador](/northamerica/panama/balboa/things_to_do/calzada_de_amador) waterfront, the [Goethals Monument](/northamerica/panama/balboa/things_to_do/goethals_monument), and [Ancon Hill](/northamerica/panama/balboa/things_to_do/ancon_hill) give the area several hours of content.

The [Smithsonian Tropical Research Institute](/northamerica/panama/balboa/things_to_do/smithsonian_tropical_research_institute) maintains research facilities here and runs public visitor programmes on Barro Colorado Island, accessible by boat — one of the best ways to see tropical rainforest wildlife near Panama City.
