The Bocas del Toro Archipelago sits close to the border with [Costa Rica](/northamerica/costarica), a scatter of islands and islets off Panama's Caribbean coast that have become one of the country's most visited destinations. The appeal is simple: luminous turquoise water, coral reefs thick with fish and turtles, and a laid-back Caribbean rhythm that is unlike anything in Panama City or the Pacific coast.

The base for most visitors is Bocas Town on Isla Colón, a colourful grid of wooden houses on stilts, restaurants, and dive shops connected to the world by a small airstrip and a water taxi dock. From here, water taxis spread out to the other islands. [Red Frog Beach](/northamerica/panama/bocasdeltoro/red_frog_beach) on Isla Bastimentos is the archipelago's most celebrated beach — a long strand of dark sand backed by jungle where the Strawberry poison dart frog, vivid red with blue-black legs, lives in the undergrowth. The [Parque Nacional Marino Isla Bastimentos](/northamerica/panama/bocasdeltoro/bastimentos_national_park), Panama's oldest marine park, covers southern Bastimentos and protects nesting beaches for all four Caribbean sea turtle species. [Starfish Beach](/northamerica/panama/bocasdeltoro/starfish_beach) on the western side of Isla Colón offers calm, shallow water carpeted with enormous orange starfish.

Underwater, [Hospital Point](/northamerica/panama/bocasdeltoro/hospital_point) is the standout site — a coral reef off the southern tip of Isla Colón where eagle rays, nurse sharks, and large turtles are routine sightings. The [Smithsonian Tropical Research Station](/northamerica/panama/bocasdeltoro/smithsonian_research_station) on Isla Colón offers occasional public programmes and a pier excellent for night snorkelling. Inland, [La Gruta Cave](/northamerica/panama/bocasdeltoro/la_gruta_cave) in the centre of the island is worth a wet walk-through to see the bat colonies overhead.

The Caribbean architecture, the local cuisine blending indigenous, Afro-Caribbean, and Panamanian influences, and the unpretentious bar scene of Bocas Town make the islands a place people tend to extend their stays in.
