type: section
---

Bocas runs on boat taxis, and almost every activity here is a day on the water: Dolphin Bay, the coral at Cayo Zapatilla and Hospital Point, the surf breaks off Isla Colón, and Red Frog and Starfish beaches. Boatmen tout the standard circuit along the Bocas town waterfront for a fixed price per head.

What the operators below add is sailing rather than outboards, and trips that go where the shared boats do not.
