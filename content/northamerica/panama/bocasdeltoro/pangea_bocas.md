Pangea runs the archipelago by boat: island-hopping days with snorkelling at Cayo Zapatilla and Hospital Point, Dolphin Bay, fishing, sunset and bioluminescence trips after dark, and private charters for people who would rather set their own route.

They are at the upper end of the price range here — day trips start around two hundred dollars a head — and correspondingly less crowded than the shared boats touting along the waterfront.
