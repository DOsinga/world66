---
title: Activities
type: section
---

Boquete has more operators per resident than anywhere else in Panama, and they sell much the same list: coffee fincas, the Quetzal trail, the Barú summit on foot or by four-wheel-drive, rafting on the Chiriquí Viejo, ziplines and hot springs.

Rafting is seasonal — the river runs hardest from roughly May to December — and the volcano is a night walk or a pre-dawn drive, so both need booking a day ahead rather than on the morning.
