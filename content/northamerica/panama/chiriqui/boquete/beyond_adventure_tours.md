Beyond Adventure Tours drives the service road up Volcán Barú in a 4x4, which is the way to the summit for anyone who does not fancy the thirteen-kilometre overnight walk. They leave in the dark to be at the top for sunrise, and on a clear morning that means both oceans from one place.

They also run cloud-forest walks and the usual Boquete circuit of coffee and hot springs.
