Boquete Outdoor Adventures runs the Chiriquí Viejo rafting, guided hikes including the Quetzal trail and the Barú summit, coffee tours, and trips out to the Gulf of Chiriquí islands — and it also assembles longer itineraries across the country for people who would rather not do the arranging.

It is the largest operator in town and the one most guesthouses hand you to, which makes it reliable and not the cheapest.
