The Pianista Trail climbs into the cloud forest above Boquete and is one of the area's more straightforward independent hikes. It is less developed than the better-known Sendero Los Quetzales, so bring a proper map, allow for mud and sudden weather changes, and turn back before the route becomes hard to follow.
