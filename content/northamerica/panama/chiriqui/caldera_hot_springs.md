About 30 minutes south of Boquete on a rough road, the Caldera hot springs are four open-air pools fed by mineralised water at around 40°C from underground sources connected to Volcán Barú. The setting is informal — a cleared patch of riverbank with low concrete-and-stone enclosures around each pool — and the surrounding river runs cold for contrast plunges.

Entry is a couple of dollars, paid to the family that maintains the site. There are simple changing rooms but little else; bring food and water. Weekends draw small crowds of Panamanian families; midweek you may have the pools to yourself. The road in is unpaved and tricky after heavy rain.
