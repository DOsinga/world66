Cangilones de Gualaca is a narrow rock channel and swimming spot near Gualaca. Visitors come to swim, cool off in the shade, and jump from the low cliffs when conditions are safe.

Bring water, sun protection, and anything you need for a simple outdoor stop. The appeal is the rock formation and clear stream, not built-up facilities.
