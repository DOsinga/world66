Finca Drácula is a private botanical garden near Cerro Punta, best known for its orchids. The cool highland climate suits a huge range of species, and the collection is far more specialised than a casual flower garden.

Parts of the grounds can be visited independently, while other areas require a guide. Arrange the visit ahead if orchids are the reason you are coming; this is remote Chiriquí, and turning up casually is not always the best plan.
