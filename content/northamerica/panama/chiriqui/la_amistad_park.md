La Amistad — Friendship — is the largest protected area in Central America, straddling the Panama-Costa Rica border at over 4,000 square kilometres and recognised by UNESCO as a World Heritage Site. The Panamanian side is administered from the ranger station at Las Nubes, twelve kilometres above [Cerro Punta](/northamerica/panama/chiriqui/cerro_punta).

The park is mostly trackless cloud forest and Talamanca mountain ridges, with very few visitor facilities. Three short trails leave from Las Nubes — the Vereda La Cascada to a waterfall, the Sendero El Retoño through bamboo and old-growth oaks, and the longer Mirador trail up to a viewpoint over Costa Rica. Wildlife includes resplendent quetzals, three-wattled bellbirds, and the rarely-seen Baird's tapir.

Longer trips into the interior require a guide and permits. Pack rain gear; the cloud forest here lives up to the name and you should expect to be wet for at least part of any day.
