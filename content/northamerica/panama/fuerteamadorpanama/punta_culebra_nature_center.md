Punta Culebra Nature Center is a Smithsonian-run marine education centre on Culebra Island, along the Amador Causeway. Exhibits focus on Panama's coastal and marine life, with tanks for Pacific, Caribbean, and freshwater species.

It is a good family stop while visiting Fuerte Amador, especially when combined with a walk or bike ride along the causeway.
