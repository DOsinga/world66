The largest shopping mall in Central America and second-largest in Latin America, attached to the city's main bus terminal on the western edge of town. Albrook is a vast indoor city of 700+ stores, food courts, cinemas, and chain restaurants — most of the recognisable American brands plus a long roster of Latin American ones.

It is not what anyone comes to Panama for, but it is a useful place for practical shopping (electronics, clothes for the climate, a SIM card, a missing piece of trekking gear) and for navigating the bus system on the way in or out of the city. The animal sculptures (each section is named for one) are surprisingly helpful as landmarks in what is otherwise a featureless concrete maze.
