A 6 km causeway connecting four small islands — Naos, Perico, Culebra, and Flamenco — to the mainland at the Pacific entrance to the canal. The causeway was built using rock excavated from the canal's **Culebra Cut**, which is a satisfying piece of geography to know while walking it. From the four islands you get one of the city's best views: the skyline of the new city across the bay, the Bridge of the Americas spanning the canal mouth, and a constant procession of container ships waiting to lock through.

The causeway is the place locals come to walk, jog, cycle, and eat seafood. Restaurants, marinas, the **Biomuseo**, the **Punta Culebra Nature Center** (a Smithsonian outpost), and a small handicrafts market are spread along its length. Sunset is the time to be here. Rent a bike or take a taxi to Flamenco and walk back as the lights of the financial district come on across the water.
