A 199-metre hill on the western edge of the city, draped in jungle and topped by the largest Panamanian flag in the country. Ancon Hill (Cerro Ancón) is the most accessible piece of nature in central Panama City — a 30-minute walk from Casco Viejo to the trailhead, then another 30-minute climb up a paved road to the summit. The view from the top covers the entire city, the canal entrance, the Bridge of the Americas, and the green wedge of Soberanía National Park stretching north.

Wildlife is the bonus: sloths in the trees right by the path, agoutis on the grass verges, toucans calling in the early morning. The road up is closed to most vehicles. Walk, or take a taxi to the lower gate and walk the last stretch. Go before 9am to avoid the heat.
