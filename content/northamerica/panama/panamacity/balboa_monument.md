A 1924 bronze-and-marble monument on the Avenida Balboa waterfront, between Casco Viejo and the financial district. The statue commemorates **Vasco Núñez de Balboa** — the conquistador who in 1513 crossed the isthmus from the Caribbean coast and became the first European to see the Pacific from the Americas, claiming the whole ocean for the Spanish crown.

The monument is a useful navigation landmark and a popular sunset photo spot, with the Cinta Costera spreading out on both sides. The Panama Bay glints in the background when the tide is in; when it is out, you see the mudflats that have historically defined the city's relationship with the sea.
