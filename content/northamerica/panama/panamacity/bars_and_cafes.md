---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Casco Viejo is where everyone ends up at night. Rooftops with views of the skyline across the bay, mezcal bars in restored colonial townhouses, a few legitimately good cocktail rooms. The scene is small enough that you can walk between most places.

For something less polished, Calle Uruguay in El Cangrejo has the bigger nightclubs (and the worse music). Cafe culture is strongest in Casco Viejo and along Vía Argentina, where Panama's specialty-coffee crowd grinds Boquete-grown geisha at international prices.
