Bella Vista runs along the bay between Casco Viejo and Punta Paitilla, and is the closest thing Panama City has to a centre. The neighbourhood is a mix of mid-century apartment buildings, embassies, hospitals, the **Multicentro** mall, and a strip of restaurants and bars along **Calle Uruguay** that bleeds into El Cangrejo.

For most travellers Bella Vista is where they pass through rather than stay — it's the link between the historic centre and the financial-district hotels — but it has some good independent restaurants and the quieter end of the nightlife scene. The seafront **Cinta Costera** here is the easiest stretch of waterfront to walk or cycle.
