Panama's National Library, on the eastern edge of Parque Omar — a sprawling postmodern building from 1991 with curved pink-and-white facades that have aged in interesting ways. The library holds the country's biggest collection of Panamanian literature, historical newspapers, and government records.

It is more an architectural curiosity than a tourist site, but free to enter and worth a quick walk through if you're already at Parque Omar. The reading rooms are air-conditioned and quiet, with views over the park. Visiting tourists need ID at the entrance.
