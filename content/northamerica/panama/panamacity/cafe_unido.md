The cafe arm of one of Panama's most-respected specialty coffee roasters, with the flagship outlet on Avenida A in Casco Viejo (inside the **American Trade Hotel**) and several branches across the city. Unido sources from farms in the Chiriquí highlands — particularly the celebrated geisha varietals from Boquete — and roasts in small batches.

The coffee is excellent, and the menu is the full pour-over and espresso programme that has become standard in third-wave cafes worldwide. The Casco Viejo branch is the best space — high ceilings, marble counters, in the old American Trade Bank building. Skip the food and stay for the coffee.
