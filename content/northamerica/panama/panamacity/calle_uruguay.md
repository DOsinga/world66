The nightclub strip of Panama City: a stretch of Calle Uruguay between Calle 47 and Avenida Federico Boyd in Bella Vista, lined with bars, clubs, sushi restaurants, and a steady weekend traffic of taxis and rideshare cars. The music is mostly Latin pop, reggaeton, and house, and the crowd is mostly Panamanian rather than touristic — which makes it more honest than the more polished Casco Viejo rooftops but also tougher to navigate without Spanish.

It is the place to come if you want to dance until 4am on a Saturday. Cover charges at the bigger venues, drinks are not cheap by Panamanian standards. Go in a group, take Ubers, and watch your wallet.
