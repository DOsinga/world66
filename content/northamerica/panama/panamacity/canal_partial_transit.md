If watching the canal from a viewing platform is not enough, you can transit it yourself. Tour operators run partial transits — Pacific entrance, through Miraflores Locks, Pedro Miguel Lock, across Miraflores Lake, into the Culebra Cut, and ending at Gamboa — most Saturdays, with full ocean-to-ocean transits roughly monthly.

The half-day partial transit (about 4–5 hours on the boat, plus the bus return) is far more practical than the full one and gives you the most interesting stretches of the canal: the locks, the cut, and the railway-and-jungle scenery on either side. Operators are **Panama Marine Adventures** and **Canal & Bay Tours**; book a few days ahead. Boats leave from the Flamenco Marina on the Causeway.
