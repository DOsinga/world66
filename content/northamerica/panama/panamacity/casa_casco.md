A four-floor complex of bars and restaurants in a restored colonial mansion on Calle 4a in Casco Viejo. Each floor has its own concept — a sushi bar, a steakhouse, a cocktail lounge — but the reason most people come is the rooftop, **Casa Bruja**, with one of the most theatrical bay views in the city.

The cocktails are good and not cheap; the rooftop pizza is better than it has any right to be. It is one of the most reliable places in Casco Viejo to find a seat with a view and live (or DJ-led) music. Weekends are packed; weekday evenings are calmer.
