The City Hall of Panama City, on the west side of Plaza de la Independencia in Casco Viejo. The Palacio Municipal is a small neo-classical building from 1908 — built shortly after independence as a symbol of the new republic's institutions — with a colonnaded facade and a clock tower visible from across the plaza.

The ground floor houses the **Museum of History of Panama**; the upper floors are still the working city hall. Worth looking at from the outside and stepping into the museum.
