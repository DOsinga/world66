The southernmost and largest of the four islands linked by the Amador Causeway. Flamenco was a U.S. military base until 1999 — the gun emplacements and tunnels are still visible — and was redeveloped after the canal handover as a marina, shopping arcade, and ferry terminal. Boats to **Taboga Island** and other Panama Bay destinations leave from the dock here.

The eastern side of the island has a small open shopping centre with seafood restaurants, ice-cream parlours, and bars; the western tip has a viewpoint over the Pacific and the entrance to the canal. The marina is busy with yachts waiting to transit the canal, sport-fishing boats, and the occasional cruise ship. A good place for a sunset drink or to wait for the Taboga ferry.
