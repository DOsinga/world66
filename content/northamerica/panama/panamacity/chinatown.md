A small but lively Chinatown immediately northeast of Casco Viejo, with two ceremonial Chinese gates (one near Plaza Santa Ana, another on Calle Salsipuedes) marking its entrance. The neighbourhood is the oldest Chinatown in Latin America — Chinese workers settled here in the 1850s and 60s.

The streets are crammed with small Cantonese and Hakka restaurants, herbalists, tea shops, and grocers selling ingredients you won't find elsewhere in Central America. The food is best at lunchtime; **Restaurante Macao** and **Sin Hai** are reliable. Walk through during the day; like much of the surrounding area, it is best avoided after dark.
