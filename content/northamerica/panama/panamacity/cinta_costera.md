A 6 km strip of reclaimed waterfront park that runs around the bay from Casco Viejo to the financial district, opened in stages between 2009 and 2014. The Cinta Costera includes wide cycle and jogging paths, basketball courts, children's playgrounds, a man-made beach (the **Playita**), and the only continuous walking route along the bay. The third phase — a controversial elevated highway extension that loops out into the sea around Casco Viejo — was built over the objections of UNESCO and very nearly got Casco Viejo struck off the World Heritage list.

It is the most popular outdoor space in the city and the best vantage point for the skyline. Locals come at dawn and at dusk for the cooler hours; midday is brutal. Renting a bike at one of the kiosks and riding the full length from Casco Viejo to Punta Paitilla is one of the better afternoons in the city.
