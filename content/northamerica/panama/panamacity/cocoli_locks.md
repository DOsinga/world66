The Pacific-side expansion locks of the Panama Canal, built between 2007 and 2016 to take the largest modern container ships. The Cocolí Locks have a visitor centre with a viewing platform — newer, less crowded, and less polished than the one at Miraflores but with the advantage of seeing the bigger ships transit. The locks themselves use a different design from the originals: instead of mitre gates they have rolling caisson gates, and they recycle 60% of the water with side basins.

The visitor centre has a small museum on the expansion project and a restaurant with a decent view of the locks. Less frequently visited than Miraflores; combine with that visit on the same day. About 20 minutes by taxi from downtown.
