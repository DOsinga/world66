Panama City is small enough that you can be out of it in under an hour. The most obvious day trip is the **Panama Canal** itself — the Miraflores Locks visitor centre is 20 minutes away and is the easiest way to watch a ship lock through. **Soberanía National Park**, in the same direction, has the legendary Pipeline Road for birding. **Taboga Island** is a 30-minute ferry from the Amador Causeway and offers beaches, a pretty fishing village, and a break from the city. For a longer day, **Portobelo** on the Caribbean side has crumbling Spanish forts and a famous Black Christ shrine.

The **San Blas islands** are technically a day trip but really deserve at least one overnight — the boat ride from the highway turnoff alone is half a day each way.
