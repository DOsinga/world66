A long-running, unpretentious traditional Panamanian restaurant in El Cangrejo, decorated with the masks of the **diablicos sucios** dancers of the Azuero peninsula. Diablicos is the place to eat the country's classic dishes if you only have one meal: **sancocho de gallina** (chicken soup with corn, yuca, and culantro), **arroz con guandú y coco** (rice with pigeon peas and coconut), **ropa vieja**, and **carimañolas** (fried yuca stuffed with meat).

Live folklore music several nights a week — drumming, accordion, traditional dance. Prices are middle-range; portions are generous. Touristy in the best sense: locals come here too. Reservations not usually necessary except on weekend nights.
