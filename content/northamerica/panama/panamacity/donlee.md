A tiny tasting-menu restaurant in Casco Viejo run by chef **José Olmedo Carles**, who returned to Panama after training under René Redzepi at Noma in Copenhagen. Donde José seats sixteen people across two sittings a night and offers a single fixed menu of nine or ten courses built entirely around Panamanian produce — corvina from the Pacific, palmito from the Darién, cacao from Bocas, salt from the Azuero peninsula.

It is the most ambitious restaurant in the country and has held a place on Latin America's 50 Best list for years. Book at least three months ahead through their website; the experience is not cheap by Panamanian standards but very modest by international tasting-menu standards. The room is austere and the focus is entirely on the food.
