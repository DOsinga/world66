title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Panama City punches well above its weight for food. The country's geography — two oceans, a tropical interior, immigrants from China, India, Lebanon, the Caribbean, Spain — shows up on every menu. The serious end of the scene is in **Casco Viejo**: a cluster of restaurants run by Panamanian chefs who trained abroad and came back. Outside the old quarter, the gold is in fondas and seafood stalls.

Three dishes to chase: **sancocho** (chicken soup with culantro, the national hangover cure), **ceviche** (best at the Mercado de Mariscos, where it costs a couple of dollars in a plastic cup), and **ropa vieja** (shredded beef in tomato sauce, often with patacones).
