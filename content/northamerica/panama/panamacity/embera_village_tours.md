Emberá Village Tours is an Emberá-owned company taking visitors to villages in the Chagres and Soberanía national parks — upriver by dugout, with the day hosted by the community rather than arranged around it.

They publish a US number for English and a Panamanian mobile for Spanish, and no WhatsApp, so telephone or e-mail is the way in.
