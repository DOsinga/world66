A bright, casual sister restaurant to Donde José, in the same Casco Viejo block, opened by chef José Olmedo Carles as a more democratic counterpart to the tasting-menu room. The name — "the diner of whatever there is" — sets the tone: a short, daily-changing menu of Panamanian comfort food, executed with the same obsessive sourcing as the upmarket original.

Expect dishes like grilled corvina with patacones and aji chombo, slow-cooked beef tongue with criollo sauce, or a long-cooked corn stew, depending on what arrived from the markets that day. Walk-in only; lunches are best. Less expensive than Donde José but still mid-range for Panama City.
