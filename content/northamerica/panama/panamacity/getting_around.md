---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Panama City has the only **metro system** in Central America: two lines, clean, cheap (35 cents a ride with a rechargeable Metrobus card), and air-conditioned. Line 1 runs north–south from Albrook to San Isidro and connects Casco Viejo (5 de Mayo station), Calidonia, El Cangrejo, and Vía Argentina. Line 2 is east-west through the suburbs and useful mostly for the airport extension that opened to Tocumen in 2024.

**Buses** (Metrobus) cover everywhere the metro doesn't. They are slow in traffic — and traffic is awful — but very cheap. **Taxis** are everywhere, almost never use meters, and require negotiating the fare before getting in; expect 3–6 dollars for in-town rides. **Uber** runs city-wide and is usually cheaper and more honest, especially to and from the airport.

Casco Viejo is best walked. The neighbourhood is compact, the streets are narrow, and most of what you want to see is within ten minutes of the cathedral. Between Casco Viejo and the financial district (Bella Vista, El Cangrejo) the bay-front **Cinta Costera** runs for several kilometres with a cycle lane — pleasant in the cooler hours, brutal in the midday sun.
