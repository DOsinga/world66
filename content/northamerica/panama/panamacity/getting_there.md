---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**Tocumen International Airport** (PTY), 25 km east of the city, is the Americas hub of **Copa Airlines** and the busiest airport in Central America. Direct flights connect Panama to most of Latin America, much of the United States and Canada, several European cities (Madrid, Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Istanbul, Lisbon), and a handful of Asian connections via Europe. Layovers in Panama City are a major part of Copa's business model — if you're flying within the Americas, you'll probably end up here whether you mean to or not.

From the airport, a metered taxi to Casco Viejo or El Cangrejo costs around 30 dollars; Uber is cheaper and easier. The **Metrobus 9-30** runs from outside the terminal to Albrook bus terminal for about a dollar, but it takes over an hour. **Albrook Bus Terminal**, on the west side of the city, is the country's main bus hub — comfortable, modern, and well-organised, with buses to David (the western terminus, near the Costa Rica border), Bocas del Toro, Santiago, Las Tablas, Colón, and across the border to San José, Costa Rica on Tica Bus and Expreso Panama.

Arriving by sea is unusual but possible: cruise ships call at the Amador and Colón cruise terminals, and a small number of sailboats run the legendary five-day passage to Cartagena via the San Blas islands.
