A French-style ice cream and sorbet shop on Avenida Central in Casco Viejo. Granclément was founded by a French expat in 2005 and uses Panamanian fruit, milk, and cacao to make perhaps thirty rotating flavours, including a few that you will not find easily elsewhere: **guanábana** (soursop), **maracuyá** (passion fruit), **tamarindo**, **mamey**, and a dark-chocolate sorbet made with Bocas del Toro cocoa.

It is a small, bright space with a couple of tables on the street, and at any time after about 4pm there will be a queue. Worth joining. The Cinta Costera and the Causeway have other Granclément outlets if Casco Viejo is too busy.
