A small church on Calle 4a in Casco Viejo, founded in 1684 and one of the few buildings in the new city to retain substantial parts of its original colonial structure. The exterior is austerely beautiful: stone walls, a single bell-cot above the entrance, and the patina of three centuries of tropical weather.

The church is rarely open outside of Mass times but the exterior alone — sitting on a quiet residential corner away from the tourist crowds of Plaza de la Independencia — is worth the five-minute detour. Walk past on the way between Plaza Herrera and the central plazas.
