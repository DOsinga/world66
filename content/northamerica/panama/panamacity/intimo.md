A modern Panamanian tasting-menu room on Calle 4a in Casco Viejo, run by chef **Carlos Alba**. Íntimo opens for 28 guests across two seatings and runs a menu of around twelve small courses that change every few months — the focus is on lesser-known Panamanian ingredients (the *guandú* bean, *chumico* fruit, *culantro* in unexpected places) cooked with technique borrowed from the chef's stints in Spain.

The room is calm and unfussy — bare brick walls, exposed beams — and the wine list emphasises lesser-known Latin American producers. Book ahead. With Donde José and **Maito**, it is part of the small cluster of restaurants that have put Panama City on the Latin American fine-dining map in the last decade.
