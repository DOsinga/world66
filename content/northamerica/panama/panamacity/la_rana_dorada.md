A Panamanian craft-beer brewpub with several outlets across the city; the original is on Avenida Eloy Alfaro in Casco Viejo. La Rana Dorada (the Golden Frog) brews about eight beers on-site at any given time — an IPA, a porter, a wheat beer, a Belgian-style blonde — most of them above local supermarket beer in quality but below international craft standards. The branded glassware and consistent pricing make it a reliable choice.

The food is bar food — pizzas, wings, burgers, sausages — and unremarkable. The strength is the beer and the location: in Casco Viejo the bar opens onto a quiet stretch of street and has outdoor tables; other branches in El Cangrejo and at the airport are utilitarian. Good place for a quick first stop before dinner.
