The vaulted brick chambers built into the seaward fortifications of Casco Viejo, on the western side of Plaza de France. There are nine bóvedas; once dungeons and store-rooms, they now house an art gallery, the French embassy's cultural centre (with a small library of French books), and a restaurant.

Above them runs the **Paseo Esteban Huertas**, a bougainvillea-draped walkway along the top of the seawall that is the single most photographed strip in Casco Viejo — and rightly so. Walk it at sunset for the best light on the modern skyline across the bay. Free; always open.
