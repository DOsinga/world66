The **Museo de Arte Contemporáneo Panamá** (MAC) is the country's only museum of modern and contemporary art, housed in a former Masonic lodge on Avenida San Blas in the Ancón neighbourhood. The permanent collection covers Panamanian and broader Latin American artists from the 1950s to the present — strong on the abstract painters of the 1960s and 70s and the politically engaged work of the post-Noriega period.

Temporary exhibitions are typically more interesting than the permanent collection and rotate every few months. The museum is small (you can do it in an hour) and the location is awkward — a long walk from Casco Viejo or El Cangrejo — but worth the taxi if you have any interest in Latin American art.
