A handsome Spanish-Panamanian restaurant in Casco Viejo run by Spanish chef **Andrés Madrigal** (one Michelin star in his Madrid days). The kitchen draws on Madrid taberna classics — Iberian ham, gambas al ajillo, pulpo a la gallega — but rebuilds them with Panamanian ingredients (Bocas chocolate, Pacific octopus, Darién palmito).

The dining room is one of the prettiest in Casco Viejo: a high-ceilinged colonial townhouse with original wooden beams, period tiles, and a small upstairs balcony. Good for a long lunch or a wine-heavy dinner. The bar is also worth visiting independently for tapas and very serious gin.
