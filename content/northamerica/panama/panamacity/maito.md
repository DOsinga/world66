Probably the most internationally recognised restaurant in Panama. **Maito**, in the quiet residential neighbourhood of Coco del Mar, is run by chef **Mario Castrellón** and has ranked on Latin America's 50 Best list every year since 2016 (peaking inside the top 20).

The food is contemporary Panamanian — produce from across the country reinterpreted with influences from Castrellón's Spanish, French, and Asian training. Dishes change seasonally but tend to include something with octopus, something with cacao, something with smoked plantain. The room is bright, modern, and surprisingly relaxed for a restaurant of this calibre. Two tasting menus and an à la carte; book a week ahead.
