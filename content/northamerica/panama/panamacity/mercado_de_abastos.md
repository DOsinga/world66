The vast wholesale market that supplies the country with fruit, vegetables, and grains. The **Mercado de Abastos**, on the western edge of the city in Curundú, takes up a city block and is at its most active before dawn, when trucks arrive from the Chiriquí highlands, the Azuero peninsula, and the Caribbean coast and small vendors load up for resale across the city. By 10am the action is mostly over.

It is not a tourist site in the conventional sense — there is no museum, no English signage — but for anyone interested in food it is the most vivid place in the city. Pineapples, plantains, root vegetables you have never seen, mountains of fresh chillies, every kind of bean. Bring small change and an empty stomach; the loading docks have fondas serving 3-dollar breakfasts that are some of the best in Panama. Take a taxi rather than the bus; it's not somewhere to wander after dark.
