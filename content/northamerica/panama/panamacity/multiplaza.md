The upmarket end of the city's mall scene, in Punta Pacífica next to the financial district. Multiplaza is where Panama's wealthy shop and where Latin American luxury brands (along with the global ones) keep their flagship stores. The food court is one of the better ones in the city, and the cinemas show first-run Hollywood films in English.

Of more practical interest: the basement-level grocery stores (**Riba Smith** and **Felipe Motta**) carry imported goods that are hard to find elsewhere in the city, and the pharmacies are open late. Functional rather than fun.
