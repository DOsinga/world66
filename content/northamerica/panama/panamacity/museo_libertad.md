Museo Libertad is a modern museum devoted to freedom, democracy and human rights, using Panama's own history as a starting point for broader questions about civic life. Its exhibitions are more reflective than monumental, with multimedia displays and temporary programming aimed at local students as much as foreign visitors.

The location near the Amador Causeway makes it easy to combine with Biomuseo, canal views or a waterfront walk. It is a good choice when you want Panama City context beyond the canal, skyscrapers and Casco Viejo.
