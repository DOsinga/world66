A small but well-curated history museum in the Palacio Municipal, on the western side of Plaza de la Independencia. The collection runs from pre-Columbian gold ornaments through the colonial period, the failed Scottish colony at Darién (the Darien scheme), independence from Spain in 1821, separation from Colombia in 1903, and on to the canal-era 20th century.

The strength of the museum is its primary documents: the 1821 act of independence, original 1903 papers from Theodore Roosevelt and Philippe Bunau-Varilla, and a copy of the Torrijos-Carter treaty. The labels are entirely in Spanish, which limits the experience for non-readers; an audio guide helps. Allow 45 minutes.
