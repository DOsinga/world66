The National Assembly of Panama meets in the **Palacio Justo Arosemena**, a 1956 Beaux-Arts-meets-Bauhaus building on the eastern edge of Casco Viejo. The exterior is monumental — a colonnaded portico, a wide flight of stone steps — and is a useful landmark for navigating between Casco Viejo and the rest of the city.

Not normally open to casual visitors, but worth walking past. The building stands on the site of the old American Trade Bank, demolished in the 1950s. The square in front sometimes hosts political demonstrations and during election season fills with banners and crowds.
