A small 19th-century theatre on Plaza de Francia, originally built as part of the colonial bóvedas complex. The Teatro Anita Villalaz (named for the Panamanian actress and theatre director) seats about 250 and hosts smaller productions — chamber music, contemporary dance, experimental theatre — that wouldn't fill the Teatro Nacional.

It is more interesting for the venue itself than for any particular performance: the building is one of the few surviving pieces of the original fortifications, and the small intermission terrace overlooks the bay. Check the city's cultural listings; tickets are typically modest.
