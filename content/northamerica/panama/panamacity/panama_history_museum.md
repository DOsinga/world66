Panama History Museum is one of the central Casco Viejo stops for understanding the country behind the canal, skyline, and colonial streets. Its displays focus on national history, civic symbols, and the political story of Panama's emergence as a republic.

The museum is most valuable when paired with the surrounding old town, cathedral square, and waterfront walks. It is a compact context stop rather than a full-day museum, but it helps make sense of the historic centre.
