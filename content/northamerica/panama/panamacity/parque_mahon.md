A small landscaped park in Bella Vista popular for evening walks and weekend family gatherings. Parque Andrés Bello — named for the 19th-century Venezuelan philosopher — has a children's playground, an exercise circuit, and a bronze statue of its namesake.

Of more practical interest: the park is the only patch of meaningful shade for several blocks in either direction, and the surrounding streets have a few good neighbourhood restaurants. Pass through if you are walking between Vía España and the Cinta Costera.
