Parque Natural Metropolitano is Panama City's great urban-nature surprise: a protected tropical forest reserve within sight of the skyline. Trails climb through dry and semi-humid forest where visitors may spot sloths, tamarins, birds, and leafcutter ants.

Start early for cooler weather and better wildlife chances, and bring water even though the hikes are short by wilderness standards. The viewpoints make the park especially rewarding because they show how abruptly forest, canal infrastructure, and high-rise city meet.
