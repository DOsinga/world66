The largest urban park in Panama City — 55 hectares of green space, mature trees, jogging tracks, a library, an outdoor swimming pool, basketball and volleyball courts, and a public golf course. Parque Omar (named for the former dictator Omar Torrijos) is the city's main weekend gathering place: families picnic, runners do laps, and on Sunday morning the central avenue closes to cars for a city-wide ciclovía that uses the park as its base.

It is more functional than scenic — no especially striking architecture — but in a city short on parks, it is the obvious place to come for an hour of green and shade. The **Biblioteca Nacional** (National Library) is at the entrance and worth a quick look for its modernist 1990s architecture.
