A small but well-loved park in Bella Vista with a softball diamond at its heart. Parque Urraca — named for a Ngäbe-Buglé Indigenous chief who fought the Spanish in the 1530s — is one of the few green spaces in the older heart of the city, with mature mango and palm trees, benches, and a steady stream of office workers eating lunch under the shade.

The softball games — a Panamanian obsession — run most evenings and weekends and have their own culture: shouted ranking, beers from the corner shop, neighbourhood pride. Even without baseball there is a children's playground, exercise equipment, and a couple of food trucks. Worth a wander if you are walking between Casco Viejo and El Cangrejo.
