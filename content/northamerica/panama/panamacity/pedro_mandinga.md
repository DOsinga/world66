A serious rum bar on Calle 8a in Casco Viejo that pours small-batch and aged rums from across the Caribbean, with a particular focus on the local distillery (also called Pedro Mandinga) that produces an excellent unaged white rum from Panamanian molasses.

The list runs to 100-plus rums, the cocktails are well-made, and the room — exposed brick, low light, vintage maps on the walls — is one of the more atmospheric in Casco Viejo. There is a small food menu of Caribbean-inflected bar snacks. Best on a weeknight when you can talk to the bartender. The distillery itself sometimes runs tours from the back of the bar.
