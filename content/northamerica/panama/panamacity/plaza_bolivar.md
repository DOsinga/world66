A leafy square in the centre of Casco Viejo dedicated to Simón Bolívar, who convened the **1826 Amphictyonic Congress** here — the first attempt to form a union of independent Latin American republics. The congress was held in the Franciscan convent on the east side of the square, now home to the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs** and a small **Bolívar Museum** (Sala Bolivariana).

The plaza is one of the prettiest in Casco Viejo: a small bronze Bolívar on horseback at the centre, surrounded by mature trees, benches, and the kind of restaurant terraces that fill up after sunset. The **Iglesia de San Francisco de Asís** dominates the east side. Worth ten minutes of sitting, an hour with a drink at one of the surrounding cafes.
