The main square of Casco Viejo, dominated by the white twin towers of the Metropolitan Cathedral on its east side and ringed by the Canal Museum, the Palacio Municipal (Museum of History), and a string of restored colonial buildings. Panama's independence from Colombia was declared here on 3 November 1903 — hence the name.

The plaza is the centre of gravity for tourists in Casco Viejo. Street musicians, ice-cream vendors, and Guna women selling molas spread out around the central fountain. The buildings around the perimeter contain three of the city's best museums and a couple of good restaurants with outdoor seating. Best visited in the early morning (when it is empty) or after dark (when the cathedral is lit).
