The ruined Dominican church and convent on Avenida A, abandoned after a 1756 fire. The roof never went back on, the walls are heavily weathered, and the site sits open to the sky in the middle of the otherwise restored Casco Viejo — one of the most photogenic ruins in the city.

The famous **Arco Chato** (the Flat Arch) inside the ruins is the reason this site matters: an extraordinarily flat brick arch that stood for centuries without buttresses, used in 1903 as evidence in the canal-route debate. The arch finally collapsed in 2003 under its own weight and was subsequently rebuilt. The adjacent small **Religious Art Museum** has a few colonial paintings and silverwork.
