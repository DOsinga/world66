---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Two reasons to shop in Panama City: handicrafts (real ones, from the Indigenous communities) and a long-standing duty-free tradition that puts electronics, perfumes, and watches at notably lower prices than elsewhere in the Americas.

For handicrafts, skip the airport stalls and go to the **Mercado Artesanal de Balboa** at the Causeway or the small Kuna-run stalls in Casco Viejo for **molas** — the layered, reverse-appliqué textiles made by Guna women in the San Blas islands. For electronics, the giant indoor malls — Multiplaza, Albrook, Multicentro — are the obvious destinations; Albrook is the largest in the country and Latin America's biggest by store count.
