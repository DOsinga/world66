The Latin American headquarters of the Smithsonian Institution, on the slopes of Ancon Hill. STRI runs scientific research stations across Panama — Barro Colorado Island in Gatun Lake is the most famous — and the headquarters in Panama City has a public library, a small visitor centre, and occasional public lectures (in English).

It is more an institution than an attraction, but worth knowing about: STRI is the reason Panama has more concentrated tropical biology research than almost anywhere else in the world. The headquarters offers research-station tours to Barro Colorado Island a few days a week — book well in advance through STRI's website. Combine a visit to the headquarters with a walk up Ancon Hill.
