A bright, all-day deli on Avenida A in the heart of Casco Viejo. Super Gourmet is the kind of place travellers find on day two and keep coming back to: counter-service breakfasts (eggs benedict, breakfast burritos), enormous sandwiches at lunch, a small case of pastries and cakes, very good coffee, and a list of craft beers from Panama's growing microbreweries.

The interior is air-conditioned and packed with North American and European expats, which can either be a plus or minus depending on what you're after. Prices are higher than at the surrounding fondas but very reasonable by international standards. A good place to hide from the midday heat with a sandwich and an iced coffee.
