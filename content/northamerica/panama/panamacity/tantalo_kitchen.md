A boutique-hotel restaurant and rooftop bar on Calle 8a in Casco Viejo that has been one of the neighbourhood's anchor venues since 2012. The ground-floor restaurant does Latin-American small plates — ceviches, anticuchos, slow-braised short rib — and is decent if unsurprising; the reason to come is the rooftop, where the view is among the best in the city, and a DJ keeps the place full from 8pm onward most nights.

The roof gets very busy on weekends — get there before sunset for a spot at the railing. Drinks are pricier than the city average but still cheap by international standards. Daytime, the roof is open for lunch with the same view and far fewer people.
