title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Three things define what visitors actually do in Panama City: walk the **Casco Viejo**, see the **canal**, and explore the patch of rainforest that sits surprisingly close to the skyline. Beyond that, the city rewards a few days of slower wandering — across the **Amador Causeway** at sunset, through the ruins of **Panamá Viejo**, into the **Biomuseo** at the canal mouth.
