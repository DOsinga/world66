The most reliable place in central Panama City to buy handicrafts directly from the country's Indigenous communities. The YMCA (Mercado de Artesanías Panameñas) is a long, low building on Avenida Balboa near 5 de Mayo metro station, with around 90 small stalls run mostly by Guna, Emberá, and Wounaan vendors.

The main goods: **molas** (the appliqué textile panels made by Guna women in San Blas), Emberá basketry made from chunga palm fibre, Wounaan tagua-nut carvings (the "vegetable ivory" of the rainforest), Ngäbe-Buglé chácara woven bags, and the *pollera* dresses of the Azuero region. Prices are negotiable but reasonable. A better place to spend a couple of hours than any mall.
