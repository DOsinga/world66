Guna Yala is governed by the Guna General Congress, and tourism runs on its terms: operators need its authorisation, you pay a comarca entry fee on arrival, and photographs of people are asked for rather than taken. The operators below are Guna-owned, which is the arrangement worth supporting here.

Almost everything starts at the Cartí port, reached by four-wheel-drive over the cordillera from Panama City in a little under three hours. Day trips are long days; two or three nights on an island is the better shape of a visit.
