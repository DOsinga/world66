The Cayos Limones (Lemon Cays) are a group of small coral islands in the central part of the San Blas archipelago, known for their particularly clear water and healthy coral reefs. The islands are among the more accessible from Cartí — about 30 to 40 minutes by motorized panga — and several island operators are based here. Basic accommodation in thatched huts over the water or on the beach is available through Guna families.

The snorkelling off the Cayos Limones is excellent, with colourful reef fish, sea turtles, and occasional rays in the shallower areas. Lobster is the main food on offer; meals are cooked fresh by the island's family and served simply with coconut rice.
