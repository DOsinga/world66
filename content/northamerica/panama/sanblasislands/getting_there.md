title: Getting There
type: section
---

Getting to the San Blas Islands requires effort, which is part of what keeps them special. There are two main options:

**By 4x4 and boat:** The most common approach is to drive or take a shared 4x4 from Panama City to the coast at Cartí (El Llano-Cartí Road), a rough 2.5-hour journey — often only passable in a high-clearance vehicle. At Cartí, small motorized pangas take visitors out to their island, a journey of 5 to 30 minutes depending on destination. Most island operators arrange transport from Panama City as part of their packages, picking up from hotels in the capital. The road has improved in recent years but can be impassable after heavy rain.

**By sailboat from Cartagena:** A popular route between Cartagena, Colombia, and Panama City runs through the San Blas over four to five days, typically stopping for two or three days in the islands. Boats depart Cartagena regularly; costs run around $550–700 per person including food and basic accommodation, but not the Panama immigration fee or island entry. This is the most atmospheric way to arrive.

**Entry:** All visitors pay a $20 comarca entry fee at the border (there are two checkpoints, one at Cartí and one at the road). The Guna congress controls access; at busy periods they may limit the number of visitors in specific areas.
