Guna Yala Panama Travel is a Guna-owned company working out of Cartí, the mainland port where the road from Panama City ends and the boats begin. They sell day trips to the islands and multi-day stays, and the guides are from the comarca rather than brought in.

Getting to Cartí is a four-wheel-drive run over the cordillera and takes a little under three hours from the city; operators either include the transfer or tell you where to meet.
