Soberanía has no operator inside it — the park is free to walk into, and Pipeline Road needs nothing but an early start and, if you want to know what you are hearing, a guide.

What is sold here leaves from Gamboa at the park's edge: boats onto Gatún Lake, birding mornings on Pipeline Road, and the dugout trips up the Chagres to the Emberá villages, most of which are booked from [Panama City](/northamerica/panama/panamacity).
