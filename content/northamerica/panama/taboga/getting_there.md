---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Taboga is reached by ferry from [Panama City](/northamerica/panama/panamacity). The most common departure point is the Calzada de Amador (Amador Causeway) at the Pacific entrance to the canal, where two operators run scheduled crossings daily — Taboga Express and Calypso Queen are the regulars. The crossing takes between 30 minutes and an hour depending on the boat. Buy tickets online or at the dock; book ahead on weekends, when the boats sell out.

There is no bridge and no airport. The boat is the only way on or off the island.
