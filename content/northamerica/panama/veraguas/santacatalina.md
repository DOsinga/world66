Santa Catalina is a fishing village on the Pacific coast of Veraguas that happens to sit beside the best surf break in Panama. La Punta, the right-hand point break off the headland, works on a reef at mid to high tide and holds a serious wave on a southern swell; Playa Estero next door is where everyone learns. The swell season runs roughly April to October, and the village fills accordingly.

It is also the main departure point for [Isla Coiba](/northamerica/panama/veraguas/coiba), an hour and a half offshore, and most dive shops and boat operators in town sell both. The village itself is one dirt road, a handful of restaurants, and a slow rhythm that people arrive intending to stay a week in and often do not leave.

Getting here takes commitment: Santiago on the Interamericana, then a bus or a drive of about two and a half hours south. The road is paved now, which locals will tell you changed everything.
