title: Activities
type: section
---

Everything in Santa Catalina is sold on the one street, and almost all of it is diving. The Coiba day trip is the standard product — two dives in the park, an hour and a half each way by boat — and the shops differ mainly in boat size and how far out they will take you. Park entry is extra and the permit is arranged for you.

Surf lessons and board hire are sold from the same street, and boatmen will run you to Playa Estero or round to the breaks for a negotiated fare.
