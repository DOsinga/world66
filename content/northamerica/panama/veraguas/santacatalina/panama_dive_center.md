Panama Dive Center is the five-star PADI resort in Santa Catalina, on the main road through the village, running daily dive trips into Coiba National Park and teaching the full course ladder from first dive to instructor.

The number they publish is a US one; they have two WhatsApp buttons, a US number and a Panamanian one, and label neither, so ask which to use when you book rather than guessing.
