Casa Pueblo is part museum, part community centre, and part environmental movement. It grew out of the campaign against open-pit mining in the mountains and now tells a wider story about forest protection, solar power, and local culture. It is the best first stop in Adjuntas because it explains why this small mountain town matters beyond its scenery.
