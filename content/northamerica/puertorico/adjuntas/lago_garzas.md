Lago Garzas is one of the reservoirs that give Adjuntas its "land of lakes" nickname. The lake is a calm counterpoint to the steep roads and forest trails, with fishing, views, and a slower rural feel. It is most rewarding as part of a loop through the Garzas area rather than as a single stop.
