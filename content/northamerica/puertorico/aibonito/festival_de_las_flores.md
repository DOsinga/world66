The Festival de las Flores is Aibonito's signature event, held in summer when nurseries, growers, food stalls, crafts, and live music take over the town's flower identity. It is not a delicate botanical show; it is a practical, crowded, local celebration of the industry that gives Aibonito its nickname.

If you are in Puerto Rico while it is running, this is the best time to see the town make sense.
