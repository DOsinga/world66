Arroyo is a small seaside town on Puerto Rico's south coast, just east of Guayama. It is not a polished resort town, and that is part of its appeal. The waterfront is local, the beaches are low-key, and the town's history turns up in odd places: an old customs house, sugar ruins, and a monument to Samuel Morse, who helped introduce the telegraph to Puerto Rico here in the 1850s.

The best short visit links the town centre, [Old U.S. Customs House](/northamerica/puertorico/arroyo/old_us_customs_house), [Malecon Paseo Las Americas](/northamerica/puertorico/arroyo/malecon_paseo_las_americas), and the beaches near Punta Guilarte. [Punta de las Figuras Lighthouse](/northamerica/puertorico/arroyo/punta_de_las_figuras_lighthouse) is the most atmospheric sight, a Spanish colonial lighthouse near the shore, damaged and not open inside but still worth seeing from outside.

Arroyo works best if you are already exploring southern Puerto Rico and want a quiet coastal day with history around the edges. It is not worth skipping [Ponce](/northamerica/puertorico/ponce) for, but it is a good antidote to busier beach towns.
