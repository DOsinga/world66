---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bayamón's food scene is local, casual, and good value. Look for lechoneras (roast-pork specialists), kiosks serving Puerto Rican comida criolla, and a few destination spots that pull in residents from across the metropolitan area. The classic Bayamón dish to seek out is the chicharrón — fried pork rind, sold from street stalls along the main roads.
