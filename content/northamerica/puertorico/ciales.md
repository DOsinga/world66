Ciales sits in Puerto Rico's interior mountains, where the northern karst begins to fold into the Cordillera Central. It is small, green, and slower than the coast, with coffee culture in town and winding roads that climb toward waterfalls and cloud forest.

The spare-day case here is not a single blockbuster sight. It is the combination of the [Coffee Museum](/northamerica/puertorico/ciales/museo_del_cafe), the viewpoints and mountain roads around town, [Las Delicias](/northamerica/puertorico/ciales/cascada_las_delicias), and the edge of [Toro Negro](/northamerica/puertorico/ciales/toro_negro_state_forest), Puerto Rico's highest forest country.

Ciales works best if you are already crossing the island's centre, or if you want a quieter counterweight to San Juan and the beaches. Roads are narrow and weather changes quickly in the hills, so give yourself daylight and patience.
