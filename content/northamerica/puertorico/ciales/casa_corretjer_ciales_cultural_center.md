Casa Corretjer is a small cultural center and literary arts stop near the center of Ciales. It is dedicated to Juan Antonio Corretjer Montes, the Puerto Rican poet, journalist, and political figure.

Visit for local cultural context rather than a long museum day. It pairs well with the nearby coffee museum and a walk around the town center.
