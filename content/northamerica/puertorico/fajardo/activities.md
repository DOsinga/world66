Two things happen here: boats and bioluminescence.

**Laguna Grande** is the bio bay, and the only one of Puerto Rico's three you reach by kayak through a mangrove channel — twenty minutes of paddling in the dark each way. The glow depends on the moon: near the new moon it is extraordinary, at full moon it is barely visible, and the honest operators will tell you that when you book rather than after. Rain and recent storms dull it too.

The **cays offshore** — Icacos, Palomino, Palominito — are the day-sail standard, with snorkelling on the reef and a good deal of rum. Boats leave from the marinas at Puerto del Rey and Las Croabas.

Fajardo is also where the **ferries** to [Vieques](/northamerica/puertorico/vieques) and [Culebra](/northamerica/puertorico/culebra) leave, from the terminal at Ceiba these days rather than the town dock. Book online ahead; residents get priority and the sailings fill.
