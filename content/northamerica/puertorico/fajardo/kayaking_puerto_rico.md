Based at Las Croabas, the fishing village at the Fajardo end of the road, and running the bio bay trip that is the reason most people come: a paddle through the mangrove channel into **Laguna Grande** after dark, where the dinoflagellates light up around the blade.

They also run daytime snorkelling out to the cays — Icacos and Palominito — and trips over to [Culebra](/northamerica/puertorico/culebra). Book for a date near the new moon if you can: a full moon washes the bioluminescence out, and no operator can do anything about it.
