Chorro de Dona Juana is the easy waterfall stop in Orocovis: a white drop beside PR-149, with a natural pool below and dense green slopes around it. It is not a manicured park, which is part of the appeal.

Parking is limited and the road is curvy, so pull over carefully. After heavy rain, treat the rocks and water with respect.
