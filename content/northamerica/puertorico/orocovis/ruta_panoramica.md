The Ruta Panoramica through Orocovis is a drive rather than a single sight, climbing through the Cordillera Central on tight roads with sudden views, forest edges, and small mountain communities.

The [Mirador Villalba-Orocovis](/northamerica/puertorico/orocovis/mirador_villalba_orocovis) is the obvious place to stop, but the route is best when you allow time for pauses and do not treat it as a shortcut.
