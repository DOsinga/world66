Toro Verde is Orocovis' headline attraction, a mountain adventure park where ziplines run over forested ridges and open views. The biggest rides are the reason many visitors make the trip inland, but the setting matters as much as the machinery.

Book ahead, check weather, and leave extra time for the drive. The approach roads are scenic but slow.
