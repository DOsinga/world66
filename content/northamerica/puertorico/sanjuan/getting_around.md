---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

**Old San Juan is walking territory.** The historic core is small enough that you cover it on foot, with the city's free trolley filling in when your legs give out — look for "Parada" stops on every other corner.

**Beyond the old city, ride-share is the default.** Uber works well everywhere in metro San Juan and is the easiest way to move between Old San Juan, Condado, Ocean Park, Isla Verde, and Santurce. Set a budget of 15–25 dollars for most cross-city rides. Tourist taxis (white, with a sentry-box logo) charge fixed rates between the airport, Old San Juan, and the resort strip — they are convenient but more expensive than Uber.

**Public buses (AMA)** are cheap (a few dollars) and run on most major routes but are unreliable and slow. Skip them unless you have time to spare. The **Tren Urbano** elevated metro is useful for trips to Hato Rey (Plaza Las Américas) and Bayamón, but does not reach Old San Juan or the beaches.

**The Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU)** is 15 minutes by taxi or Uber from Old San Juan, about 10 from Isla Verde. **Driving** is fine for excursions out of the city — Puerto Rico has US-standard signage, your home driving license is valid, but expect aggressive city traffic.
