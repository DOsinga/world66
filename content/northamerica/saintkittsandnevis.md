Saint Kitts and Nevis is one of the smallest nations in the Western Hemisphere, a federation of two small volcanic islands in the northern Leeward Islands. Saint Kitts is the larger, with the capital Basseterre and the UNESCO-listed Brimstone Hill Fortress — one of the best-preserved British colonial fortifications in the Caribbean. Nevis is quieter, rounder, and almost permanently wrapped in cloud at the peak; Alexander Hamilton was born here in 1755.

The islands attract a mix of yachters, history buffs, and beach visitors. Saint Kitts closed its sugar industry in 2005 after 350 years; the old Scenic Railway that served the cane fields now carries tourists on a narrated loop around the island.
