Nevis Peak rises to around 985 metres at the centre of the island and dominates every view. The climb is serious — the upper sections are steep, muddy, and involve rope-assisted scrambles through thick cloud forest. It should not be attempted without a guide, and most guides recommend leaving Charlestown by 5 or 6am to reach the summit before cloud cover closes in. The round trip takes 4 to 6 hours depending on fitness.

The lower slopes are covered in secondary tropical forest that transitions through banana and heliconia to tree ferns, bromeliads, and silver palms near the top. On clear days, the summit offers views of Saint Kitts, Montserrat, Redonda, and Antigua. Guides can be arranged through the Nevis Tourism Authority or through several operators in Charlestown — expect to pay around US$50 per person. Bring water, good boots, and clothing for the cool, wet conditions near the summit.
