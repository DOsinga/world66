title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Nevis is small enough to drive around in under an hour, and most activity centres on its beaches, plantation inns, and the climb up [Nevis Peak](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/nevis/nevis_peak). The west-coast beaches — [Pinney's Beach](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/nevis/pinneys_beach) in particular — are the most accessible and swimmable.

Historical sights include the [Alexander Hamilton Birthplace Museum](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/nevis/alexander_hamilton_birthplace) in Charlestown, the [Bath Hotel and Spring House](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/nevis/bath_hotel) (18th-century hot springs spa), and the atmospheric ruins of [Eden Brown Estate](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/nevis/eden_brown_estate) on the Atlantic coast. The [Nevis Botanical Gardens](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/nevis/botanical_gardens) make a pleasant half-day. Diving and snorkelling are good around the southern tip of the island where coral is still healthy.
