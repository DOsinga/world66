St. Kitts is the larger of the two islands that make up the Federation of Saint Kitts and [Nevis](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/nevis), a volcanic peak rising steeply from the Caribbean Sea. The island was one of the first in the Caribbean to be colonised — by both the British and the French, who fought over it for centuries — and the massive [Brimstone Hill Fortress](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/st_kitts/brimstone_hill_fortress), a UNESCO World Heritage Site perched on a volcanic outcrop, is the most dramatic reminder of that history.

The capital, [Basseterre](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/st_kitts/basseterre), is a small, walkable port town with colourful colonial buildings around [the Circus](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/st_kitts/the_circus) and [Independence Square](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/st_kitts/independence_square). The island's interior is dominated by [Mount Liamuiga](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/st_kitts/mount_liamuiga), a dormant volcano whose crater can be reached by a steep jungle hike — one of the best walks in the eastern Caribbean. The [southeastern peninsula](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/st_kitts/southeast_peninsula), connected to the rest of the island by a single road, has the best beaches: [Frigate Bay](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/st_kitts/frigate_bay) on the Atlantic side for surf, the calmer [South Friars Bay](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/st_kitts/south_friars_bay) on the Caribbean side.

St. Kitts is quieter and less developed than most Caribbean destinations. The old sugar railway has been converted into a [scenic tourist train](/northamerica/saintkittsandnevis/st_kitts/scenic_railway) that loops the island, passing through former plantation estates and along the coast. If you want a Caribbean island that still feels unhurried, this is a good choice.
