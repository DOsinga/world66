The obvious day trip is to **Nevis**, the smaller, quieter sister island just two miles across the Narrows. Ferries leave several times daily from Basseterre to Charlestown (about 45 minutes); a faster water-taxi runs from Cockleshell Bay on the southeast peninsula to Oualie Beach on Nevis in around ten minutes.
