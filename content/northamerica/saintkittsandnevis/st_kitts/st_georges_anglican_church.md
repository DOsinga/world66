The oldest Anglican parish on the island and one of the oldest churches in the eastern Caribbean. The squat stone tower and yellow stuccoed nave stand at the eastern edge of central Basseterre, on Cayon Street.

Inside, the church is plain in the West Indian style: white walls, dark timber pews, and memorial tablets to British colonial administrators and planters from the 18th and 19th centuries. The little graveyard outside is shaded by tropical trees and worth a slow walk for the inscriptions alone.
