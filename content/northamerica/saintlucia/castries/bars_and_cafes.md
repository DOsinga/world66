---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The local beer is **Piton**, brewed at Vieux Fort and named after the volcanic peaks on the bottle, and you will find it everywhere. The local rum is **Bounty** or, at the top end, **Chairman's Reserve**, both made by St. Lucia Distillers a short drive south of town. Most bars stock both.

In the city itself, **Pinky's** off Bridge Street is a friendly old-school rum-shop that gets going after work. The bars around the **Vigie Marina** are more polished and look out over yachts and the harbour. Friday nights, the locals head out to **Gros Islet** for the famous street party — sound systems, fried fish, and rum stalls on a closed-off road — about thirty minutes north of the city.
