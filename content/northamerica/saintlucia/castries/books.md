**Omeros** by Derek Walcott — Saint Lucia's Nobel laureate retells the Odyssey on the streets of Castries and the fishing villages of the west coast. Difficult, beautiful, and full of recognisable places — read it before you walk through Derek Walcott Square.

**Another Life** by Derek Walcott — A long autobiographical poem about Walcott's childhood and apprenticeship in Castries in the 1940s and 50s. More accessible than Omeros and the best evocation in print of mid-century Saint Lucian life.

**Sir Arthur Lewis: A Biography** by Robert L. Tignor — The other Nobel laureate. Lewis grew up in Castries and went on to win the 1979 Nobel Prize in Economics. The biography is short on Saint Lucia but useful context.

**The Star-Apple Kingdom** by Derek Walcott — An earlier collection of poems with the long title piece on the Caribbean's colonial inheritance. Read on the ferry between islands.
