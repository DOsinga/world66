Castries is the obvious base for trips into the rest of Saint Lucia. South to **Soufriere** is the must-do — the Pitons, the sulphur springs, the rainforest, the cocoa estates, all in a single, dramatic landscape. North to **Pigeon Island**, the eighteenth-century British naval base, makes a lazy half-day with a swim attached. **Marigot Bay**, halfway down the west coast, is reckoned by many to be the prettiest natural anchorage in the Caribbean.
