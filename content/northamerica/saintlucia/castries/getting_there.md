---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

International flights land at **Hewanorra International Airport** on the southern tip of the island, about 90 minutes by road from Castries. There are direct flights from the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and a handful of European cities, plus regional connections through Barbados and Trinidad.

The much smaller **George F. L. Charles Airport** on the Vigie peninsula handles regional flights to the other eastern Caribbean islands — Barbados, Martinique, Antigua, Trinidad, and so on. Inter-island ferries also call at Castries: the L'Express des Iles runs to Martinique, Guadeloupe, and Dominica.

Cruise ships dock at **Pointe Seraphine** on the north side of the harbour or at the older terminal in town.
