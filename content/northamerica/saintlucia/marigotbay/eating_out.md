---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The handful of restaurants in Marigot Bay are mostly waterfront operations serving fresh seafood, grilled fish, and Creole dishes to yacht crews and day-trippers. The setting — open-sided structures overlooking the bay — is hard to beat. Lunch on the water on a clear day, with flying fish and a rum punch, is a standard Marigot Bay experience done right.
