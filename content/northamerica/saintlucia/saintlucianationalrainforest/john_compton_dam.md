John Compton Dam is not a romantic rainforest sight, but it explains something practical about Saint Lucia: the wet interior stores the water that makes life on the coast possible. The reservoir sits in the central hills, surrounded by green slopes.

Most travellers will only pass near it en route to hikes or birding trails. Treat it as context rather than a destination, and do not assume access to operational areas.
