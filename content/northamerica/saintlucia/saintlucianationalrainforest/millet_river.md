The Millet River runs through part of Saint Lucia's central rainforest landscape, close to the bird sanctuary trail. It is not a standalone attraction in the usual sense, but it is part of the wet valley system that makes this interior so lush.

Look for it as part of a guided forest walk rather than as a separate outing. In heavy rain, small rivers here can rise quickly, so take local advice seriously.
