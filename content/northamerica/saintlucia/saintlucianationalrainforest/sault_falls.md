Sault Falls sits on the eastern side of Saint Lucia, away from the island's more polished west-coast visitor circuit. It is a modest waterfall rather than a thundering spectacle, but the surrounding vegetation and quieter setting make it a worthwhile stop if you are already exploring the interior roads.

Ask locally about access before setting out. Rain, road conditions, and maintenance change the experience more than any brochure will admit.
