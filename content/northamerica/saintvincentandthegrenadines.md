Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is a chain of islands strung between Saint Lucia and Grenada in the southern Caribbean. Saint Vincent itself is volcanic, mountainous, and largely agricultural — the slopes of La Soufrière volcano (which erupted in 2021) grow bananas and arrowroot, and the island is known for its hiking and waterfalls rather than beaches.

The Grenadines — a string of 32 islands and cays stretching south toward Grenada — are another matter: Bequia, Mustique, Canouan, Union Island, and the [Tobago Cays](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/mayreau) are among the most beautiful sailing waters in the world. Mustique is the private retreat of the wealthy; the Tobago Cays marine park has some of the Caribbean's finest snorkelling over pristine coral. Bequia is the most accessible and most visited, with a good anchorage and a small-boat-building tradition.
