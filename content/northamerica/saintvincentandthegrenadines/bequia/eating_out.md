---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Most of Bequia's restaurants are strung along the Belmont Walkway around Admiralty Bay, with a few more in Port Elizabeth and on Lower Bay beach. Local cuisine leans on fresh fish (mahi-mahi, snapper, kingfish), conch, lobster in season (September–April), and the traditional one-pot dishes of pelau and callaloo. Most places are casual; reservations are a good idea in high season (December–April).
