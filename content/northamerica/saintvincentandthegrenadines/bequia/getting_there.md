---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

The main approach is by ferry from Kingstown on **St Vincent**. The Bequia Express and the Admiral I run several times a day on the one-hour crossing — comfortable, air-conditioned, with seating on multiple decks. Schedules tighten at weekends, so check ahead.

Bequia has its own small airport (J F Mitchell Airport, code BQU) on the south-western side of the island, served by Mustique Airways, SVG Air, and Grenadine Airways with short flights from St Vincent, Barbados, and St Lucia. The runway is short and the approach over the hills is dramatic.

Yachts arrive constantly from Mustique, Union Island, and the wider Grenadines chain — chartering a yacht or hopping on a day-sail from another island is one of the great Caribbean experiences. Cruise ships call occasionally but the island has no big cruise pier.
