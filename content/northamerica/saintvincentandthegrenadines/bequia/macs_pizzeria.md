Mac's Pizzeria, on the Belmont Walkway, has been a Bequia institution since 1980 — an open-sided restaurant on a raised wooden deck looking straight out over Admiralty Bay. The pizza is the main draw and is genuinely good (the lobster pizza in season is justifiably famous), but the wider menu of pastas, salads, and grilled fish is reliable.

Sunset is the obvious time to come — every table on the deck has a clear view across to the anchored yachts and the sky turning pink behind them. Reservations essential during high season. Cash and credit cards accepted.
