---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bequia is the kind of island where doing very little is the point — long mornings on the beach, an afternoon snorkel, a sundowner overlooking Admiralty Bay. There is no resort district and no big-ticket attraction; the pleasure is in the slow rhythm of the place. What few sights there are reflect Bequia's particular character as a Caribbean island built around boats and the sea.
