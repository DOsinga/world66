The Whaleboner is one of the more distinctive bars in the Caribbean. Built around the literal bones of whales killed under Bequia's aboriginal subsistence quota, the small bar and restaurant sits on the Belmont Walkway looking out over Admiralty Bay. The atmosphere is unpolished, friendly, and authentically local.

Drinks are cold and reasonably priced; the kitchen does simple seafood, burgers, and rotis. It is a sundowner spot as much as a dinner one, and a good base for getting a feel for the harbour-front nightlife. Live music on some evenings.
