Basil's Bar is a wooden structure on a jetty jutting into Britannia Bay, and it has been the social hub of Mustique since 1977. The owner, Basil Charles, is one of the island's true characters — known personally to most long-term visitors and many famous guests, and present most evenings to make introductions and pour rum punches.

The bar serves food throughout the day: breakfasts, grilled fish lunches, dinners that lean toward seafood and Caribbean staples. What makes it worth visiting at any hour is the setting — the deck over the water, the light in the bay, the relative informality of a place where rock stars and royalty have sat on the same barstools as sailors and dive instructors. On Wednesday evenings during high season, the bar hosts a jump-up that occasionally features impromptu performances by whoever happens to be on the island.
