The highest point on Mustique commands views across the Grenadines chain on a clear day — Canouan to the south, Bequia to the north, and St Vincent itself visible beyond the horizon haze. The hillside vegetation gives way to more open scrub near the summit, and from up here the scale of the island becomes apparent: small enough to see both coasts simultaneously, large enough to hide its villas in the folds of the landscape.

The climb is short but steep in places, and a golf buggy track gets most of the way. The summit area is one of the few places on Mustique that feels genuinely wild rather than managed — the vegetation is denser and the wildlife more audible. A pair of binoculars is worth bringing for the seabirds.
