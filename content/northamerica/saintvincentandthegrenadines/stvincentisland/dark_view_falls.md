Dark View Falls is a twin-stage waterfall on the leeward coast, reached by a short walk and a wobbly bamboo suspension bridge over the Richmond River. The lower fall is a wide veil dropping into a clear pool deep enough to swim in; the upper fall is reached by a steeper, slightly scrambly path another fifteen minutes uphill.

It is one of the easiest waterfalls on the island to reach — about an hour's drive from Kingstown, and well signposted. There is a small entrance fee and a shed where you can change. The site is run by a community cooperative from the surrounding villages, and bringing a small purchase from one of the stalls at the entrance is a polite way to support it.
