Fort Duvernette is the smaller, stranger sibling of Fort Charlotte: a tiny British fortress perched on top of a near-vertical rock pillar just off Young Island. Two hundred and fifty steps cut into the rock spiral around the side and bring you out at a flat summit ringed by ramparts, with the original cannons still in place pointing south and east toward the open sea.

To get there you arrange a boat at Villa Beach — five minutes across the channel and a few East Caribbean dollars each way. The climb is steep but short, and the view from the top across to Bequia and back at the mainland coast is worth the effort. There are no facilities and no signage; bring water and a hat.
