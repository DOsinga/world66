A short walk inland from the village of Layou on the leeward coast leads to a set of pre-Columbian rock carvings on a single large stone face beside the Layou River. The largest panel shows a stylised human face and several geometric figures, all pecked into a vertical wall of basalt. A small viewing platform has been built in front of the rock and a local family who own the land collect a small entrance fee.

The site is unremarkable in scale but the petroglyphs are genuine and rarely visited — most days you'll have them to yourself. Combine the stop with a swim in the river pool downstream and a lunch break in Layou if you're working your way up the leeward coast.
