The Mesopotamia Valley — universally called Mespo by locals — is the agricultural bowl behind Kingstown, walled in by green ridges and visible from the road at a series of viewpoints on the way to Vigie Highway. It is one of the most photographed landscapes on the island: terraced plots of dasheen, bananas, breadfruit, cocoa and nutmeg climbing the valley walls in patchwork shades of green.

The Montreal Gardens, near the head of the valley, are a private 12-acre tropical garden open to the public on weekdays — anthuriums, heliconias, orchids, and views down the length of the valley. The drive in itself is the attraction; rent a car or hire a taxi for half a day to combine it with a stop at the Vermont Nature Trails on the way back.
