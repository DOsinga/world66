St Vincent rewards effort. The marquee outings are the all-day hike up [La Soufrière](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/stvincentisland/la_soufriere) and the rainforest walks of the [Vermont Nature Trails](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/stvincentisland/vermont_nature_trails). Kingstown is small enough to cover in a morning — the [Botanical Garden](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/stvincentisland/botanical_gardens), [Fort Charlotte](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/stvincentisland/fort_charlotte) and the [market](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/stvincentisland/kingstown_market) cover the essentials. The leeward coast drive past Barrouallie and Chateaubelair to the [Wallilabou Bay](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/stvincentisland/wallilabou_bay) Pirates of the Caribbean film set is a standard half-day, and waterfalls — [Trinity Falls](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/stvincentisland/trinity_falls), Dark View, [Falls of Baleine](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/stvincentisland/falls_of_baleine) — fill another day or two.
