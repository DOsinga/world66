Young Island is a 13-hectare private island a couple of hundred metres off the south coast at Villa, occupied entirely by a single small resort. A tiny ferry runs back and forth across the channel on demand for guests and for the few day visitors who book in to lunch at the open-air restaurant. The setting is what you'd expect — palm trees, a white-sand beach, hibiscus and bougainvillea — and the cooking is reliable Caribbean.

Behind Young Island, the steep rock pillar of [Fort Duvernette](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/stvincentisland/fort_duvernette) rises 60 metres straight out of the sea. You can pay a boatman at Villa to drop you at its foot, climb the 250 cut steps, and look down on the cannons that were dragged up here in the 1790s to defend the anchorage.
