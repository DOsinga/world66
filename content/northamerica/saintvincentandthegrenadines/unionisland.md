Union Island is a small, mountainous island at the southern end of the Grenadines chain, part of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. It serves as the jumping-off point for the [Tobago Cays](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/unionisland/tobago_cays), one of the Caribbean's most spectacular sailing and snorkelling destinations, and for the private island of Palm Island nearby.

The island has a rugged, undeveloped feel compared to its neighbours. [Clifton Harbour](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/unionisland/clifton_harbour), the main town, has a small harbour, a few guesthouses, and a handful of bars where the sailing crowd gathers. The beaches are quiet — [Chatham Bay](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/unionisland/chatham_bay) on the west coast is a long crescent of sand backed by hills — and a hike up [Mount Taboi](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/unionisland/mount_taboi) gives panoramic views across the southern Grenadines. The northeast shore draws kitesurfers to [Kite Beach](/northamerica/saintvincentandthegrenadines/unionisland/kite_beach), where the Grenadines trade winds are among the most consistent in the Caribbean. If you are island-hopping through the Grenadines by boat or ferry, Union Island is a natural stop.
