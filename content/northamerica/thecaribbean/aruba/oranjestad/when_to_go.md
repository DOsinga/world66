---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Oranjestad's climate is the same as the island's — hot, dry, and breezy year-round. The temperature sits around 28°C (82°F) in most months, and the northeast trade winds keep it comfortable. Aruba sits below the hurricane belt, so the June–November storm season that closes options elsewhere in the Caribbean is much less of a concern here.

High season runs from mid-December to mid-April, when the city is busiest and prices are highest. Carnival — celebrated from January through early March — brings colour, noise, and energy to Oranjestad, with street parades and competitions that are among the best in the Dutch Caribbean. If you want to see Carnival, book accommodation months ahead.

The shoulder months of May to July offer better value with the same reliable weather. August to November is the quietest period and the cheapest: occasional showers, slightly higher humidity, but nothing like the rains elsewhere in the tropics. Cruise-ship traffic peaks between November and April, so if you want Oranjestad's streets and museums without the crowds, the May–August window is the best bet.
