Bridgetown is the capital of [Barbados](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados), a compact Caribbean city whose historic centre — the [Careenage](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/the_careenage) waterfront and the streets around it — was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2011. It is small enough to walk across in an hour, but dense enough with history that you can spend a satisfying few days here without running out of things to see.

The [historic centre](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/historic_centre) clusters around [National Heroes Square](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/national_heroes_square), where the [Parliament Buildings](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/parliament_buildings) — one of the oldest continuous legislatures in the Western Hemisphere — face the waterfront. [Broad Street](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/broad_street) runs west from the square, loud with commerce; the quieter streets behind it hold the [Nidhe Israel Synagogue](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/nidhe_israel_synagogue) and museum, one of the oldest Jewish congregations in the Americas, and [St. Michael's Cathedral](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/st_michaels_cathedral), where George Washington attended services during his only trip outside North America.

South of the centre, [the Garrison](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/garrison) is a British colonial military complex that now holds the [Barbados Museum](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/barbados_museum) — the best on the island — and a racecourse that doubles as a social institution. [Queen's Park](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/queens_park), east of the centre, is worth visiting for the thousand-year-old baobab tree alone.

[Carlisle Bay](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/carlisle_bay), just south of the city, offers the nearest good swimming: calm turquoise water, sandy bottom, sea turtles, and five shipwrecks for snorkellers and divers. Cricket fills the rest of the calendar — [Kensington Oval](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/kensington_oval) is one of the legendary grounds in the West Indies, and a Test match here is an occasion worth planning a trip around. For rum, the [Mount Gay Visitor Centre](/northamerica/thecaribbean/barbados/bridgetown/mount_gay_visitor_centre) does justice to the world's oldest commercial rum brand.
