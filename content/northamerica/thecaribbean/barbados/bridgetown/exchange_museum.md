The Exchange Museum sits in historic Bridgetown, close to the city's old commercial and civic heart. Its exhibits look at money, trade, education, business, and institutions, themes that suit a capital shaped by empire, sugar, ports, and finance.

It is a good indoor stop when walking Bridgetown's UNESCO-listed core. The museum is not large, but it helps connect the surrounding streets to the economic systems that made Barbados so important in the Caribbean.
