type: section
---

Christ Church is primarily a beach parish, but its history runs deeper than the shoreline suggests. Between watersports sessions and evening fish fries, there is a handful of genuinely compelling sites: a Ramsar wetland teeming with birds, one of the Caribbean's most significant slave burial grounds, and a colonial-era church whose vault spawned a Victorian mystery that has never been conclusively solved.
