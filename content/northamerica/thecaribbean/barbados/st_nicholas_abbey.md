St Nicholas Abbey is one of Barbados' great historic plantation houses, built in 1658 and preserved as a rare Jacobean mansion in the Caribbean. Despite the name it was never a religious house; today it combines house tours, rum production, and landscaped grounds.

The interiors give a clear sense of plantation wealth, while the working distillery keeps the visit from feeling purely static. The optional heritage railway adds views across the estate and makes the site a strong inland stop away from the beaches.
