The Lighthouse Footpath is the short hiking trail along the easternmost tip of the Bluff, from the end of the road out to the automated lighthouse on the cliff edge. The walk is only 20 minutes one way over uneven karst limestone, but it feels much further: the path threads between scrub and cactus on the very edge of a 43-metre vertical drop into the sea.

The lighthouse itself is a small steel tower, more functional than picturesque, but the cliff edge a few metres beyond it is what people come for. On the right day you can see Little Cayman 8 km away. Brown boobies and tropicbirds nest in the cliff face below, and there's a small raised platform for safer viewing. Wear closed shoes — the limestone is sharp — and stay back from the edge in wind.
