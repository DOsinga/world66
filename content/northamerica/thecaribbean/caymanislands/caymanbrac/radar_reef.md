Radar Reef is an easy shore-entry spot for snorkellers and divers on Cayman Brac's north side. Stairs lead down near the boat launch, and the reef can be reached by following the old telephone-cable line west from the entry point.

It is not the island's grandest dive, but it is useful when you want a simple water stop without arranging a boat. Check the sea state first, as north-shore entries can change quickly with wind and swell.
