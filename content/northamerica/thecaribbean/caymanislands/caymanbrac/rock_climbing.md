Cayman Brac has the most developed sport-climbing scene in the Caribbean — somewhere around 80 bolted routes on the south side of the Bluff, ranging from beginner 5.7s to overhanging 5.13s. The routes were established mostly by visiting American climbers in the 1990s and early 2000s; the rock is solid limestone, often pocketed, with good friction once it has dried after rain.

The main concentration of routes is near the Spot Bay area on top of the Bluff, with approach paths from the road. Bring a 70-metre rope and quickdraws — there is no climbing shop on the island and equipment cannot be rented. The Brac Reef Beach Resort sometimes hosts climbing weeks with visiting guides; otherwise it is a do-it-yourself destination. A guidebook is available online at caymanbracclimbing.com or from longer-staying climbers.
