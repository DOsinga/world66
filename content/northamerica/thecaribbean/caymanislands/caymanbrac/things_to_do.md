---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Brac is built for outdoor low-key days. Divers come for [the wreck of the Captain Keith Tibbetts](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/caymanbrac/captain_keith_tibbetts_wreck) and the wall dives off both coasts; non-divers walk the trails on top of [the Bluff](/northamerica/thecaymanislands/caymanbrac/the_bluff) past the [parrot reserve](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/caymanbrac/brac_parrot_reserve) to the lighthouse, rock-climb on the cliffs, or explore the [caves](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/caymanbrac/peter_cave) used historically as hurricane shelters. The [Heritage House museum](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/caymanbrac/heritage_house) at Stake Bay covers the human history in an hour.
