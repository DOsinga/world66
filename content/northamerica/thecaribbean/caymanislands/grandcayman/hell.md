Hell is a cluster of jagged black limestone formations in West Bay, named for the way the rocks look in the heat and glare. The stop is openly kitschy, with Hell-themed souvenirs and postcards that can be postmarked from the local post office.

It is not a long visit, but it is one of Grand Cayman's stranger roadside sights and an easy add-on if you are already heading toward the north end of Seven Mile Beach or the West Bay area.
