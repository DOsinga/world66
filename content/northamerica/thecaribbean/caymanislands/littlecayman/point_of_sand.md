Point of Sand is a long sand spit at the eastern end of Little Cayman — the closest point to Cayman Brac, 8 kilometres across the channel. The beach is wide, the sand fine and pink in patches from crushed coral, and the water shallow and clear for a long way out. Most days you will be the only person there.

The drive to the end of the island takes about 25 minutes from anywhere on the developed western side and the last stretch is unpaved. There are no facilities at all; bring everything. The snorkelling close to shore is patchy but the channel itself is good for spotting eagle rays in the deeper water. On a calm day you can see across to the cliffs of Cayman Brac.
