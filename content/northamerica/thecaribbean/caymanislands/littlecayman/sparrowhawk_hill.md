Sparrowhawk Hill is the highest point on Little Cayman — a modest 12 metres above sea level. On an island this flat, that is enough to qualify as a viewpoint, and it gives a wider sense of the island's shape and the surrounding reef pattern than anywhere at sea level.

It is reached by a short footpath from the road near the centre of the island, signposted by the National Trust. The hill is dry forest, dense with cactus and silver thatch palm, and you might spot iguanas on the path. The summit has a small clearing with views across the lagoon system and out to the north reef. Take ten minutes, see the view, return.
