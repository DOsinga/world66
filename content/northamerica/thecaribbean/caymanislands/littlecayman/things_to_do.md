title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The diving is the reason to come — [Bloody Bay Wall](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/littlecayman/bloody_bay_wall) and [Jackson's Bight](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/littlecayman/jacksons_bight) are world-class and easily reached by any of the island's three dive operators. Above water there is the [Booby Pond](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/littlecayman/booby_pond_nature_reserve) with its red-footed booby colony, the [iguana population](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/littlecayman/rock_iguanas) ambling along the road and tracks, a couple of [beach walks](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/littlecayman/owen_island), and the marine research labs at the [Central Caribbean Marine Institute](/northamerica/thecaribbean/caymanislands/littlecayman/ccmi). Most days you will see the same handful of people twice.
