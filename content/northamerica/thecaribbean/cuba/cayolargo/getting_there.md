---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Cayo Largo is reached only by air. **Vilo Acuña International Airport (CYO)** on the island handles charter flights from Havana, Varadero, and a number of European cities — most visitors arrive on package tours from Canada, Italy, Spain, and Germany. There is no ferry service for tourists.

From within Cuba, daily small-aircraft flights run from **Havana** (about 50 minutes) and **Varadero** (around an hour). Aerogaviota and Cubana operate most of these. Day trips by air from Havana are sold as packages by many Havana tour agencies and include a beach transfer, lunch, and a catamaran ride.

There is no independent way to reach the island; you must come either as part of a package or via the day-trip charters. There is no equivalent to walking up and buying a ferry ticket.
