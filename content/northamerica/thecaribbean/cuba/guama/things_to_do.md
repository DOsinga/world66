title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The set piece is the boat to the Taíno reconstruction on the [Laguna del Tesoro](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/guama/laguna_del_tesoro), with its Rita Longa sculptures and lakeside cabins. Combine with the [crocodile breeding farm](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/guama/crocodile_farm) at Boca de Guamá and a serious naturalist outing into the [Zapata Swamp National Park](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/guama/zapata_swamp) for the endemic birds. The drive south continues to the [Bay of Pigs](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/guama/bay_of_pigs) — Playa Larga and Playa Girón — for snorkelling, shore-diving and the surprisingly good [Cueva de los Peces](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/guama/cueva_de_los_peces) cenote.
