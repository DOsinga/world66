Centro Habana has a rougher and more local bar scene than Old Havana — places where Cubans drink rather than tourist bars. The corner bars near the Malecón serve cheap rum and beer to neighbourhood regulars, often with a small television showing baseball or telenovelas in the corner. These bars do not require tourist dollars and are more interesting for that.

The area around San Rafael and Galiano is the most active for this kind of informal drinking. The bars are not well-signed and may look from the outside like private homes or empty commercial spaces. Walking in and asking for a drink is generally sufficient.
