Centro Habana has some of Havana's best private restaurants (paladares), particularly concentrated on Neptuno and the surrounding streets. Because the neighbourhood is less touristed than Old Havana, prices tend to be lower and the experience less staged. Doña Eutimia (actually just inside the Old Havana border), O'Reilly 304, and several unnamed family operations have built loyal followings.

The best paladares in Centro Habana are in former ground-floor apartments with no more than five or six tables. The menus are short, handwritten, and change with what is available in the market that morning. Look for chalkboard signs or ask local residents — the best places rarely have obvious signage.
