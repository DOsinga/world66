Centro Habana has several small music venues and casas de la trova that predate the tourist economy and cater primarily to Cuban audiences. The music — son, bolero, trova — is played with the kind of ease that comes from playing the same songs since childhood, and the atmosphere is genuinely different from the tourist-oriented shows in Old Havana.

Some of these venues are open during the day for informal sessions; others operate as evening clubs. Finding them requires local knowledge or willingness to follow music down a side street. The Tropical beer hall has regular live music in a cavernous space that packs in a local crowd.
