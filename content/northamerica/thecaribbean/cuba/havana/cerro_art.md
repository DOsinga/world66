El Cerro has become one of Havana's emerging areas for street art and community murals, partly because the crumbling facades of the old houses provide large unpoliced canvases. Some of the work is commissioned by neighbourhood associations or cultural programmes; some is informal. The quality ranges from political slogans to genuinely skilled large-scale murals.

The concentration is highest on the streets around the Estadio Latinoamericano and along Calzada del Cerro. A walking tour of the neighbourhood in the morning, before the heat builds, takes in both the architecture and the contemporary art that is gradually reclaiming the walls.
