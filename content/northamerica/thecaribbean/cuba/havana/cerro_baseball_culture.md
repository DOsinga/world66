Around the Estadio Latinoamericano on game days, the streets fill with vendors, touts, and fans — a whole ecosystem of informal commerce that makes the approach to the stadium as interesting as the game itself. Men discuss statistics and player form with the intensity reserved in other countries for football or cricket. Children re-enact plays in the surrounding streets.

Even on non-game days, the baseball culture of El Cerro is visible in the small parks and open spaces where pickup games continue throughout the day. Baseball is genuinely embedded in daily life here in a way that is unusual for professional sport anywhere in the world.
