Several small cigar workshops (talleres) operate in El Cerro, where torcedores (cigar rollers) produce handmade cigars in the traditional manner. Some are informal operations visible from the street; others are small factories that welcome visitors. The neighbourhood has long been associated with the cigar trade because of its working-class character and the tradition of skilled manual work that has persisted here.

Cigars bought directly from workshop torcedores are typically cheaper than those at official government stores (Casa del Habano), though provenance cannot always be verified. The craft itself — watching someone roll a cigar with practiced precision — is worth seeing regardless of whether you smoke.
