El Cerro has several casas de la trova and informal music venues where son, timba, and rumba are played for local audiences rather than tourists. The music in these spaces is less polished and more genuinely embedded in the neighbourhood's social life than what you find on the tourist circuit in Old Havana. Improvised sessions and neighbourhood dances happen on weekend evenings.

Finding these venues requires some local knowledge or willingness to wander — they do not advertise to tourists and venues change. The area around the stadium has the most activity on match evenings.
