El Floridita has been on the corner of Obispo and Monserrate since 1817, and Ernest Hemingway drank double daiquiris here most days he was in Havana. A bronze statue of him leans on the end of the bar. The daiquiris are excellent — frozen, sharp, without ice crystals. Yes, it's a tourist bar; no, that doesn't mean the drinks are any worse. The mojito was invented at La Bodeguita del Medio two blocks away; the daiquiri was perfected here.
