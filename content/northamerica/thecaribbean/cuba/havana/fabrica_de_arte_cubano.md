A converted cooking oil factory in Vedado that has operated as a hybrid arts space and nightclub since 2014. Gallery exhibitions, live music, film screenings, and installations run simultaneously across the vast interior, and the admission is a single card that logs your drinks. Thursday to Sunday from 8pm; the line outside fills early. The brainchild of musician X Alfonso and one of the few places in Cuba that genuinely rewards a night out.
