The coastal road east from the harbour tunnel through Habana del Este, past Cojímar and the fishing villages, to Playas del Este is one of the most pleasant drives or cycle rides in the Havana area. The road hugs the coast and alternates between small fishing communities, rocky outcrops, and sandy beaches. The traffic is lighter than within the city and the views out over the Florida Strait are clear.

Several small restaurants and kiosks along the road sell fresh fish and beer. The road makes for a good day trip from Havana, with a swim at the Playas del Este as the endpoint.
