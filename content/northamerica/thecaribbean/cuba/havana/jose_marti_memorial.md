José Martí International Airport serves as the main gateway to Cuba, named after the poet and independence hero whose monument stands in the Plaza de la Revolución in Vedado. The airport itself is in Nuevo Vedado, south of the city centre. The road from the airport into the city passes through some of Nuevo Vedado's Soviet-era residential blocks before entering the older urban fabric of Vedado.

The airport area has little of independent tourist interest, but understanding its location and the route into the city is useful for arriving visitors. The journey from Terminal 2 or 3 to Old Havana by taxi takes 25–40 minutes depending on traffic.
