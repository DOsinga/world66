Miramar has the most active nightlife scene in Havana for Cubans with some access to money — whether earned in the tourist economy or through remittances. The neighbourhood's large houses, converted to bars and clubs, offer space that the cramped streets of Old Havana cannot. Some clubs are Cuban-only, some are mixed tourist-Cuban, and some cater primarily to Havana's diplomatic and business community.

Casa de la Música Miramar is the best-known venue — a house where Cuban son and salsa bands play most nights, with dancing from around 11pm. The crowd is mixed and the music is genuine rather than staged. Tickets are sold at the door and prices are modest.
