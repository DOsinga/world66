Miramar hosts the majority of Havana's foreign embassies, concentrated on and around Quinta Avenida and the parallel streets. The embassy buildings occupy former mansions — each country's diplomatic mission housed in a different architectural style reflecting when the building was constructed and who commissioned it. Walking the street amounts to a tour through early 20th-century Havana's social geography.

The American embassy (technically the US Interests Section of the Swiss Embassy, now the US Embassy again) is a notable building on the seafront, surrounded by flagpoles that Cuba has planted to block the LED news ticker that the US once ran facing the street. The compound and its surrounding area has an odd, frontier atmosphere.
