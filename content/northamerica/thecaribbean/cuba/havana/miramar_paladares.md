Miramar has become Havana's best neighbourhood for upscale paladares. The large houses with their former dining rooms, gardens, and multiple spaces make ideal restaurant venues, and the relatively affluent clientele of diplomats and visiting business travellers supports higher prices and more ambitious cooking. La Guarida (technically in Centro Habana but associated with this circuit) set the template; Miramar now has several of its own.

Restaurants to look for include those on Calle 16 and in the streets around Quinta Avenida. Reservations are increasingly necessary for the best places. Cuban fusion food — combining local ingredients with international technique — is what distinguishes these restaurants from the simpler paladares of the centre.
