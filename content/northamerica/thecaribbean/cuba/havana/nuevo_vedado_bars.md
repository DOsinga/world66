Nuevo Vedado has several neighbourhood bars and small venues that serve a local residential clientele. These are not the tourist bars of Old Havana or the upscale cocktail bars of Miramar, but the kind of local drinking establishments that operate wherever Cuban residential density is high enough to support them — small, informal, with beer and rum at subsidised prices.

The area around Avenida 26 has the most activity. On weekends when there is a match at the Estadio Latinoamericano (nearby in El Cerro), bars throughout the area fill with fans watching on television.
