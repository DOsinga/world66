Cine Chaplin on Calle 23 in Nuevo Vedado is one of Havana's main art-house cinemas and the home of the annual Festival Internacional del Nuevo Cine Latinoamericano, held each December. The cinema screens Cuban films, international titles, and retrospectives throughout the year at prices accessible to locals — rarely more than a few pesos.

The building itself is a well-preserved modernist structure from the 1960s. Arriving early lets you see the lobby fill with a genuine cross-section of Havana residents rather than tourists, making it one of the more authentic ways to spend an evening in this part of the city. Check the programme board outside or ask at the box office — advance booking is rarely necessary outside festival season.
