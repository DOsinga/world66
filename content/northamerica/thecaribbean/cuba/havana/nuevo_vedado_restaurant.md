Nuevo Vedado has developed a small cluster of paladares in the 2010s that serve the airport-adjacent business traveller market — better-equipped and more reliable than the paladares of the historic centre, with menus in English and some wine options. The food is Cuban fusion — international technique applied to local ingredients. A solid option for a dinner before an early-morning flight.

The neighbourhood is quieter and more residential than central Havana, and the paladares here tend to have more space and parking. Best accessed by taxi from Old Havana or Vedado.
