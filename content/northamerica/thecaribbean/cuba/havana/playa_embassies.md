The Playa municipality stretches along the coast west of Miramar and contains a mix of residential streets, state enterprises, and some of Havana's most interesting institutional architecture from the 1950s and 1960s. The area around Ciudad Libertad — a former military base converted into a school complex after the revolution — contains several significant modernist buildings.

The seafront along Playa has a rougher, less developed character than the Miramar waterfront, with more actual beach access and fewer tourist facilities. It is a popular spot for Havanans who want a beach without travelling to Playas del Este. The residential streets inland have a suburban quietness that contrasts with the density of central Havana.
