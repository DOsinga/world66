The western suburbs of Playa and Miramar have developed the most sophisticated restaurant scene outside the tourist circuit, catering to the diplomatic community and Cubans working in the joint ventures. Several paladares here serve modern Cuban food with international wine lists and full menus in multiple languages. La Fontana on Avenida 46 is one of the most consistently praised in this category.

The quality-to-price ratio in this neighbourhood tends to be better than Old Havana tourist restaurants because the clientele is more demanding and repeat business matters more. Reservations recommended for dinner.
