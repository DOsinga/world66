Playa has a scattered but genuine nightlife centred around a few bars and music venues that serve both local residents and visitors staying in the western suburbs. The scene here lacks the concentration of Old Havana's tourist-oriented music spots but compensates with a more authentic atmosphere — Cubans going out for an ordinary Saturday night rather than performing Cuban culture for foreign visitors.

Several venues in Playa host regular live music from Cuban bands playing son, bolero, and timba — the local rhythmic development of salsa that has largely replaced older styles in Havana's clubs. Getting information on current venues requires asking locals, as the scene shifts constantly with new paladares opening and venues changing programmes. A taxi driver or casa particular host will know where the music is on any given night.
