Playa has developed a quieter, more local dining scene than the tourist-oriented paladares of Miramar. The neighbourhood attracts Cuban professionals and expatriate residents who want good food without the central Havana premium. Several paladares here specialise in traditional Cuban cooking — ropa vieja, rice and black beans, fresh seafood — in converted houses with shaded gardens.

The area around the seafront and the streets behind it has the highest concentration of restaurants. The pace here is slower than central Havana, tables tend to be less rushed, and the prices reflect a clientele that is partly local. For a longer evening meal in Havana away from the tourist circuit, Playa is worth considering.
