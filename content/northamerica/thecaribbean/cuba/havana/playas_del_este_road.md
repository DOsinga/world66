The road east from Havana through the harbour tunnel and along the coast to the Playas del Este passes through the working-class municipalities of Habana del Este and San Miguel del Padrón before reaching the beaches. The journey — whether by taxi, bus, or the occasional tourist shuttle — is interesting in itself: you pass Soviet-era residential blocks, small farms, fishing villages, and the increasingly rural character of eastern Havana province.

The journey by public bus (the P-1 and connections) is the most economical and gives the most contact with how Habaneros actually get around the city. Taxis are faster but more expensive. Both options depart from Parque Central or the bus station.
