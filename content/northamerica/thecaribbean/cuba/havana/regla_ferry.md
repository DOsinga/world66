The ferry from Muelle de Luz in Old Havana to Regla crosses one of the finest harbour approaches in the Caribbean — the entrance to a deep natural bay surrounded by fortifications, waterfront warehouses, and the Havana skyline. The crossing takes about eight minutes and costs a few Cuban pesos, making it the cheapest waterway experience in the city.

From the Regla ferry dock the view back across the harbour to Havana is exceptional, particularly at dusk when the light turns the neoclassical facades golden. The dock itself has been a landing point for pilgrims visiting the Virgen de Regla since colonial times — fishermen would touch the statue before crossing — and the area around the Regla church retains a devotional atmosphere that is distinctly different from tourist Havana.
