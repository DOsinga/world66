Regla has several small paladares and state restaurants that serve straightforward Cuban food at prices significantly lower than Old Havana. The quality is simple but honest — rice, beans, pork or fish, fresh fruit — prepared for local rather than tourist customers. The lack of tourist pressure means the food here is what Cubans actually eat rather than what they think foreigners want.

The area near the church has the highest concentration of food options. Most places are open at lunchtime; fewer operate in the evenings. Finding them requires wandering and looking for the small signs on doorways that indicate a paladar is in operation.
