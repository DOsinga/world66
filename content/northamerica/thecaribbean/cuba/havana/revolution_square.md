The main political and ceremonial square of revolutionary Cuba, dominated by the José Martí Memorial (a 109-metre obelisk with a museum inside) and flanked by the Ministry of the Interior building, with its famous steel mesh portrait of Che Guevara and the words "Hasta la Victoria Siempre." Fidel Castro gave his speeches here to crowds of a million people. On ordinary days it's a large, hot, mostly empty square; the monument has a viewing platform at the top with a clear view over the city.
