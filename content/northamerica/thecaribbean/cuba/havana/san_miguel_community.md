San Miguel del Padrón is one of Havana's most ordinary municipalities — neither architecturally significant nor tourist-oriented, simply a large residential district where Habaneros live and work. Visiting here is an exercise in seeing Cuban daily life outside the contexts prepared for tourists. The local markets, schools, and neighbourhood centres function without reference to foreign visitors.

The area around the main bus terminus has the most activity — informal vendors, taxis colectivos, and the constant movement of people going about their business. For anyone interested in how the Cuban urban economy actually operates, San Miguel del Padrón is more instructive than the curated streets of Old Havana.
