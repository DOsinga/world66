Along the Via Blanca as it passes through San Miguel del Padrón, roadside stalls sell produce, cooked food, and household goods serving the local population. These informal markets — more concentrated around the intersections at Diezmero and Cotorro — operate on a different rhythm from the tourist paladares of central Havana: the food is simpler, cheaper, and catered entirely to residents rather than visitors.

For travellers passing through on the way to the Playas del Este, a stop here offers grilled pork, rice dishes, fruit, and refreshments at local prices. The stalls are most active in the mornings and early afternoons. This is practical travel rather than a destination in itself, but it offers a clear sense of everyday commercial life in Havana's southern suburbs.
