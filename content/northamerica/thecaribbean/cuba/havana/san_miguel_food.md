San Miguel del Padrón has the cheapest food in the Havana metropolitan area — small private restaurants (paladares) and state cafeterias serving traditional Cuban food without any tourist premium. The cooking is plain: rice and beans, roast pork, fried plantains, occasional fish — but honestly done and priced for Cuban wages.

For visitors on a very tight budget, the bus journey from central Havana to San Miguel is worth making for lunch. The journey also passes through several working-class neighbourhoods that give a different perspective on the city.
