San Miguel del Padrón has a low-key neighbourhood bar scene that serves its own residents with no orientation towards tourists whatsoever. The bars here offer subsidised beer and rum at state prices, and weekend evenings bring out a local crowd that dances to whatever music is playing — often regguetón or timba from a sound system rather than a live band.

This is not a destination for visitors seeking a curated night out, but for those willing to find their own way, drinking in San Miguel del Padrón gives a more authentic experience of how Havanans socialise than the managed music venues of Old Havana. Ask at a casa particular in the neighbourhood for current recommendations — the scene changes constantly.
