San Miguel del Padrón is a mainly residential municipality in eastern Havana, less visited than the historic centre but useful as a transit point for reaching Havana's eastern beaches (Playas del Este). The area has a more suburban feel than central Havana — lower buildings, more open space, fewer crumbling colonnades.

The Playas del Este, about 20km east of Havana, are the best beaches within reach of the city: several kilometres of white sand with cleaner water than the harbour beaches. They are particularly popular with Habaneros on weekends and fill up considerably in July and August.
