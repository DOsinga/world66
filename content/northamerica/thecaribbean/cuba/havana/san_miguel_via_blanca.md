The Via Blanca is the main highway connecting Havana to the beaches of Playas del Este and running through the southern part of San Miguel del Padrón. The road passes through a landscape of light industry, repair workshops, and the everyday commerce of outer Havana — a working landscape that contrasts with the monumental city centre.

The Via Blanca is also the route by which Havanans reach the eastern beaches on weekends, and the traffic of classic American cars, horse-drawn carts, bicycles, and occasional trucks gives a sense of how Cubans move around their city. Catching a shared taxi (colectivo) along this route from Old Havana is an affordable way to reach the beaches while passing through neighbourhoods that organised tours never visit.
