Vedado contains the finest collection of 20th-century architecture in Cuba — a range of styles from Beaux-Arts to Art Deco to 1950s modernism that reflects the neighbourhood's development as Havana's wealthy residential and commercial district before the revolution. The streets around Paseo and Fifth Avenue (23rd to 17th Streets) are the richest for architecture.

The FOCSA Building (1956) was at its completion the tallest reinforced concrete building in the world — a 39-floor tower that still dominates the Vedado skyline. The Hotel Nacional (1930) is the finest Art Deco hotel in Havana. The various private mansions along the broad tree-lined avenues show the full range of Cuban elite architectural ambition from 1900 to 1959.
