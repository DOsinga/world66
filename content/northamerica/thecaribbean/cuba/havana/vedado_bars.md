Vedado has the most established bar scene in Havana, concentrated around La Rampa (23rd Street) and the streets around the Hotel Nacional and Habana Libre. The rooftop bar of the Hotel Habana Libre has views over the city and a mixed local-tourist crowd. The Café Madrigal in Vedado is a good cocktail bar in a renovated mansion.

The neighbourhood's bars generally operate later into the night than Old Havana and attract a more Cuban professional and diplomatic clientele than the tourist bars of the historic centre. La Rampa is particularly lively on weekend evenings when it fills with young Cubans.
