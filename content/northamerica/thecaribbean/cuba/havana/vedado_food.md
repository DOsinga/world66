Vedado has developed a strong paladar scene in the 2010s, anchored by the concentration of diplomats, Cuban professionals, and higher-spending tourists who live and stay in the neighbourhood. La Guarida — in Centro Habana but close to the Vedado border — remains the most celebrated, but the neighbourhood itself now has several excellent private restaurants. 

El Cocinero, in a converted oil factory chimney tower behind the Fábrica de Arte Cubano, is the best-designed restaurant space in the city — a rooftop bar and restaurant with good food and panoramic views. The Fábrica de Arte Cubano itself (a major cultural venue and gallery in a converted oil factory) is next door.
