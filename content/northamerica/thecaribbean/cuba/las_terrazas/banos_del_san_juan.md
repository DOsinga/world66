Baños del San Juan is the swimming stop most often paired with Las Terrazas. The San Juan River forms clear pools below the village, shaded enough to feel like a real break from the heat.

It is a social place, especially on weekends and holidays, not a secluded wilderness pool. Come early if you want the water before the day-trip mood takes over.
