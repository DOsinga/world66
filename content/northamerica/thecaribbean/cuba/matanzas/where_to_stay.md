---
title: "Where to Stay"
type: section
---

Matanzas has a growing stock of casas particulares — private home-stays — mostly in the older streets near the Plaza de la Vigía and along the riverbanks. These offer a more genuine stay than the resort hotels in nearby Varadero, and most hosts can advise on transport, local restaurants, and cultural events.

Booking ahead is advisable in high season. Most casas are listed on booking platforms but can also be arranged on arrival if you come outside peak months. There are no large international hotels in the city centre; Varadero, 30 km east, handles most of the resort accommodation for the region.
