---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pinar del Río city is compact and easy to explore in a day. The main sights are clustered around [Calle Martí](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/pinardelrio/calle_marti), the arcaded central boulevard where the [Cathedral of San Rosendo](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/pinardelrio/cathedral_san_rosendo) and the [Museo Provincial de Historia](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/pinardelrio/museo_provincial) face each other across the street. The [Fábrica de Tabacos Francisco Donatién](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/pinardelrio/fabrica_de_tabacos) offers one of the better cigar factory tours outside Havana and sells properly certified export cigars.

But the real draw is outside the city. The [Vuelta Abajo tobacco region](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/pinardelrio/vuelta_abajo_tobacco) stretches south and west — red-earth fields and thatched drying barns that produce what many consider the world's finest cigar tobacco. A drive through San Juan y Martínez lets you visit working farms and watch the leaf being prepared. And [Viñales](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/vinales), 25 kilometres north, is one of the most beautiful valleys in Cuba — worth at least a full day.
