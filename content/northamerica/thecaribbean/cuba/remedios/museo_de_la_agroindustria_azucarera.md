The Museo de la Agroindustria Azucarera tells the story of the sugar economy that shaped this part of Villa Clara. Set near the old Marcelo Salado mill, it is a good place to understand why railways, estates, labour, and cane fields mattered so much to towns like Remedios and Caibarien.

The machinery is the attraction: locomotives, industrial remains, and the scale of a business that once tied rural Cuba to the wider Caribbean economy. Check locally before setting out, since transport and opening hours around Cuban provincial museums can be irregular.
