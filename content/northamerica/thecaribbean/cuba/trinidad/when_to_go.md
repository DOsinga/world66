---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Trinidad's climate is tropical, hot and humid most of the year, with a wet season from June through October that brings afternoon showers and the occasional hurricane. The most comfortable months for visiting are November through April: warm (25–30°C), drier, with reliable sunshine.

December and January are the peak tourist months; the town is busy with foreign visitors but not overwhelming — it is small enough that the crowds disperse quickly across the streets. February and March are often cited as the sweet spot: good weather, slightly fewer visitors, and the colonial streets and plazas at their most atmospheric.

Carnival in July and August brings local festivity but also intense heat and the possibility of heavy rain. If you time a visit for this period, come for the celebration specifically rather than comfort. The worst months for pure weather are September and October — hurricane risk is real, rainfall is heavy, and the humidity is oppressive.
