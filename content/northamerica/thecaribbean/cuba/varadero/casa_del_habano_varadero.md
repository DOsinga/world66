Cuba's most famous export is the cigar, and Casa del Habano in Varadero is the best place to buy them. The shop stocks the full range of Habanos brands — Cohiba, Montecristo, Romeo y Julieta, Partagás — in a humidified environment with knowledgeable staff. You can smoke on the premises and get a sense of what you're buying before committing. Prices are fixed and clearly marked; the cigars are genuine.
