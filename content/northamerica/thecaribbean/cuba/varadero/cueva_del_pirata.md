Varadero's most distinctive nightlife venue is set in a genuine limestone cave on the south side of the peninsula — a natural cavity that has been fitted out as a disco and cabaret club. The combination of stalactites overhead and salsa music at volume is bizarre, and it works. There's a cover charge that includes a drink. Shows start around 9pm and run until the early hours.
