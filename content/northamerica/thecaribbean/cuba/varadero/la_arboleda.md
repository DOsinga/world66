A tiny, perpetually busy bar in Varadero town that operates around the clock. The space is small — barely room for a bar and a band — but the music is genuine Cuban: son, bolero, and salsa played by local musicians who mingle with the crowd between sets. It draws both Cuban residents and tourists who have found their way off the resort strip, and the mix makes for a better night than most of the purpose-built entertainment venues.
