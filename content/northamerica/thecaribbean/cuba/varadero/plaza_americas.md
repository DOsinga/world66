The main shopping mall on the Hicacos Peninsula, located at the resort end of the strip. It has a reasonable selection of international brands, a supermarket, a post office, and several restaurants and bars under one roof. It's not particularly Cuban in character — it could be in any resort town — but it's convenient if you need something specific and don't want to travel into Matanzas or Cárdenas.
