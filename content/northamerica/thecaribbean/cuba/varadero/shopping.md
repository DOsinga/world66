---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Shopping in Varadero is mostly resort-style: souvenir stalls, rum and cigar shops, and the [Plaza Americas](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/varadero/plaza_americas) mall at the resort end of the strip. The best single purchase is Cuban cigars from [Casa del Habano](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/varadero/casa_del_habano_varadero), where the selection is genuine and the staff can advise. Rum is available everywhere and is considerably cheaper than abroad — Ron Santiago and Havana Club are the main brands worth taking home.
