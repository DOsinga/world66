---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Varadero's main activity is the [beach](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/varadero/varadero_beach) — 20 km of white sand with calm Caribbean water — and most visitors don't need much else. Beyond the sun and sea, the [Varahicacos Ecological Reserve](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/varadero/varahicacos_ecological_reserve) at the eastern tip of the peninsula has pre-Columbian cave paintings and nature trails. [Parque Josone](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/varadero/parque_josone) in the centre of town is a pleasant shaded garden with a lake and rowing boats. The [Mansión Xanadu](/northamerica/thecaribbean/cuba/varadero/mansion_xanadu) at the far end of the resort strip is worth visiting for its architecture and the view from the terrace. Water sports — scuba diving, kayaking, catamaran tours, dolphin encounters — operate from beach concession points along the strip.
