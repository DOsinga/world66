---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Varadero has a sub-tropical climate and is warm year-round. The dry season runs from November through April — this is the peak tourist season, with lower humidity, less rain, and temperatures around 24–28°C (75–82°F). January is the coolest month but still pleasant for the beach. The wet season runs from May through October, bringing heavier rain, higher humidity, and the risk of hurricanes from August through October. July and August are busy with domestic Cuban tourism despite the heat. The driest and most comfortable months for international visitors are December through March.
