Sandy Island is the postcard version of Carriacou: a narrow strip of pale sand and palms in clear water just offshore from Paradise Beach. It is small enough to feel fragile, but memorable enough that many visitors treat it as the island's signature boat trip.

Come for swimming, snorkeling, and a few unhurried hours rather than facilities. Conditions and access depend on weather and local boat arrangements, so it works best as a flexible outing from Carriacou rather than a rigidly scheduled excursion.
