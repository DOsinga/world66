Belmont Estate is a 300-year-old working estate best known for cacao. The most interesting time to visit is around harvest, when you can follow the process from pods growing on the tree to fermented and dried beans ready for chocolate making.

It is one of Grenada's better stops for understanding how agriculture, food, and island history still connect.
