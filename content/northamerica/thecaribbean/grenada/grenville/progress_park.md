Progress Park is Grenville's main sports ground, used for cricket and football. It is not a formal tourist sight, but it is a good place to see the town at ease if a local match or school event is happening.

Cricket followers may recognise the name from regional fixtures hosted here. For most visitors, the appeal is simpler: a patch of Grenadian public life just north of the market streets, where the east coast gathers for something other than shopping or transport.
