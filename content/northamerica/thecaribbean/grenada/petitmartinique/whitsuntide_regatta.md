The Whitsuntide Regatta is Petite Martinique's signature event, built around open-boat racing, shore activities, music, and the island's unusually strong boatbuilding identity. If you want to see the island lively rather than sleepy, this is the weekend to aim for.

Expect crowds by local standards, improvised logistics, and a much bigger social scene than the island usually supports. Arrange transport and lodging on Carriacou well ahead if you are planning around the event.
