St. George's is one of the most attractive capitals in the Caribbean. It sits around the Carenage, a horseshoe-shaped inner harbour lined with Georgian warehouses painted in faded pastels, small fishing boats, and the occasional cruise ship tender. The city climbs the hills above the harbour in a jumble of steep lanes, red-roofed buildings, and old churches.

[Fort George](/northamerica/thecaribbean/grenada/stgeorges/fort_george) crowns the headland above [the Carenage](/northamerica/thecaribbean/grenada/stgeorges/the_carenage) and gives excellent views over both the inner and outer harbour. It was here in 1983 that Prime Minister Maurice Bishop and several of his ministers were executed during the coup that preceded the US invasion. A plaque marks the spot.

Below the fort, [Market Square](/northamerica/thecaribbean/grenada/stgeorges/market_square) in the centre of town is the place to go on Saturday morning, when vendors spread out spices, fresh produce, and handmade goods. The spice stalls are the highlight: nutmeg, mace, cinnamon, bay leaves, and dried turmeric piled in open sacks.

The [Grenada National Museum](/northamerica/thecaribbean/grenada/stgeorges/grenada_national_museum) on Young Street holds a modest but worthwhile collection covering Arawak and Carib history, the colonial period, and the 1983 revolution. Fort Frederick on Richmond Hill to the east is less visited than Fort George but has good views and interesting history.

The Anglican Church on Church Street dates from 1825, the [Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception](/northamerica/thecaribbean/grenada/stgeorges/immaculate_conception_cathedral) from 1818, and St. Andrew's Presbyterian Church from 1830 — a compressed history of colonial-era religious architecture within a few blocks of each other.

The [Botanical Gardens](/northamerica/thecaribbean/grenada/stgeorges/botanical_gardens) behind the roundabout at Lowthers Lane are a quiet place to sit out the midday heat. They were founded in 1887 and, though no longer at their colonial peak, are green and well maintained.
