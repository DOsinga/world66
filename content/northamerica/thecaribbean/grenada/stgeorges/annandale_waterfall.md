Annandale Waterfall is one of the easiest nature trips from St George's, tucked into green hills a short drive inland. The fall drops into a pool framed by tropical vegetation, giving a quick taste of Grenada's interior without a demanding hike.

It is popular, and sometimes theatrical, so do not expect solitude. Still, the cool air, dense greenery, and easy access make it a good first waterfall stop before pushing farther into Grand Etang or the island's rougher trails.
