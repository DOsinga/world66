Fort Louis is a modest colonial fort site on Grande-Terre, part of the defensive network that once guarded Guadeloupe's coast and anchorages. It is less complete than the island's better-known forts, but the setting gives it a clear travel value for visitors tracing the coastline.

Treat it as a short historical stop rather than a major destination. It works best when paired with nearby beaches, Pointe-à-Pitre sights, or other fortifications around the bay.
