---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season runs from December through April and is the most comfortable time to visit. January through March offers the clearest skies, the calmest seas, and the lowest humidity — good conditions for snorkelling and for walking the cliffs without arriving soaked. The trade winds blow steadily through this period, keeping temperatures pleasant even in the middle of the day.

The rainy season runs from June through November. Rain arrives in heavy short showers rather than all-day drizzle, and the island remains green and warm. The risk of tropical storms is real from August through October, and occasionally a season produces something more serious — check forecasts if you are travelling in this window. Seas can be rougher, which affects the ferry crossing and snorkelling conditions.

La Désirade is never overwhelmed by visitors, but Easter weekend and French school holiday periods (February half-term, July and August) bring the most day-trippers from Guadeloupe. Even then, the island absorbs the numbers without feeling crowded. If you want it to yourself, November, early December, or any time in January or February outside of public holidays will do it.
