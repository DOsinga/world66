---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating well in Les Saintes means finding the right balance between the tourist restaurants on the waterfront and the smaller local places tucked into the village streets. The local speciality is tourment d'amour — a small coconut tart found in every bakery — which you should eat at least once. Seafood is consistently good; accras (salt cod fritters) and langouste (spiny lobster) are the things to order.
