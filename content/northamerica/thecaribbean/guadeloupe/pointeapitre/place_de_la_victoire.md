Place de la Victoire is Pointe-à-Pitre's old civic centre, a broad square by the water with royal palms, colonial-era traces, and enough shade to make it useful as well as symbolic. It is the natural starting point for a walk through the city.

The square has been through fires, hurricanes, and political renamings, yet it still functions as a public room. From here you can drift toward the waterfront, the cathedral, the markets, or the museum streets without needing a plan more elaborate than curiosity and comfortable shoes.
