---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sunrise Airways runs short flights from Port-au-Prince to [Jérémie Airport](/northamerica/thecaribbean/haiti/jeremie/jeremie_airport), about 6 km west of town — these have historically been the most reliable way in. Overland from the capital is theoretically possible via Léogâne, Petit-Goâve, and Les Cayes, but the road past Les Cayes is rough and the trip a long, slow day. Coastal boats and ferries also serve the harbour from time to time. Check current security and infrastructure conditions before travelling overland anywhere in Haiti.
