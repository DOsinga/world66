title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Jérémie's draw is the surrounding peninsula, not the town itself. The [St. Louis King of France Cathedral](/northamerica/thecaribbean/haiti/jeremie/cathedral) on the central square is the main landmark in town, while a short walk away [Place Dumas](/northamerica/thecaribbean/haiti/jeremie/place_dumas) recalls the city's most famous family connection. The real reason to come, though, is the coast: [Anse d'Azur](/northamerica/thecaribbean/haiti/jeremie/anse_dazur) just outside town is the closest beach, with [Anse du Clerc](/northamerica/thecaribbean/haiti/jeremie/anse_du_clerc) further west the prettier escape. The road on to [Abricots](/northamerica/thecaribbean/haiti/jeremie/abricots) is one of the most scenic drives in Haiti.
