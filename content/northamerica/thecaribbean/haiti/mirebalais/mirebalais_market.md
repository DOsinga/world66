Mirebalais has long served as a market town for the Central Plateau, drawing produce, animals, sugar products, and everyday goods from surrounding rural communities. It is dense, hot, and functional rather than picturesque.

For a traveller, the market is the best window into why Mirebalais matters. It shows the town as a hinge between farms, roads, hospitals, schools, and the wider economy of the interior. In the current security climate, it should not be treated as a casual outing.
