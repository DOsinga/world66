---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Martinique is compact enough to explore fully in a day from any base. The Les Fonds Blancs sandbars off the east coast near Le François are the most popular excursion — boat trips run from the Baie du Simon for a morning swim in waist-deep turquoise water. The southern beaches at Sainte-Anne and the Diamond Rock offshore make for easy half-day trips from Fort-de-France.
