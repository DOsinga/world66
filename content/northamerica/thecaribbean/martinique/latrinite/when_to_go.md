---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Martinique has a tropical climate, warm year-round. The dry season (carême) runs roughly from December to May — lower humidity, clear skies, and the strong trade winds that make the bay ideal for windsurfing and kiteboarding. The rainy season (hivernage) runs June to November, bringing afternoon showers, higher humidity, and the occasional tropical storm or hurricane, with peak risk in August and September.

For hiking on the Caravelle Peninsula and watersports, December to April is the best window. The trails dry out quickly after rain but can be slippery in wet season. Temperatures stay between 24°C and 30°C year-round, with coastal breezes keeping the Atlantic coast slightly cooler than the sheltered west coast.
