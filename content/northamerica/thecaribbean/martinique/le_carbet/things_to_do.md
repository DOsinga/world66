---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Le Carbet's main draws are concentrated along the waterfront and just north of town. The [Musée Gauguin](/northamerica/thecaribbean/martinique/le_carbet/musee_gauguin) marks the painter's brief but significant stay in 1887. Just north of town, the [Botanical Gardens at Anse Latouche](/northamerica/thecaribbean/martinique/le_carbet/botanical_gardens_anse_latouche) occupy the ruins of a 17th-century sugar plantation with an impressive collection of tropical plants.

The [Columbus Landing Marker](/northamerica/thecaribbean/martinique/le_carbet/columbus_landing_marker) on the pier commemorates the Spanish explorer's 1502 landing — the first European contact with Martinique. The [Église Saint-Jacques](/northamerica/thecaribbean/martinique/le_carbet/eglise_saint_jacques), dating to 1645, is one of the oldest churches in the Caribbean. For more active visitors, the [Carbet Waterfalls](/northamerica/thecaribbean/martinique/le_carbet/carbet_falls) — three tiers of falls in the Carbet Mountains above the town — are accessible on foot.
