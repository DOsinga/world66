type: section
---

Saint-Pierre's defining experience is the 1902 eruption of Mont Pelée and its aftermath. The ruins of the old town are scattered through the new one, the volcano looms visibly overhead, and the harbour holds a fleet of shipwrecks that make this one of the Caribbean's best dive destinations. Everything here connects back to that single morning when a superheated pyroclastic surge ended the life of 30,000 people in minutes.
