Schoelcher's appeal is primarily its beaches and its proximity to Fort-de-France. The small bays along the coast are calm and swimmable, with views across the bay to the capital. The Regional Natural Park headquarters here is the starting point for information on hiking the island's interior. The Victor Schoelcher statue at the town centre is worth a quick look — an unusual commemoration of an abolitionist by the people freed by his campaigning.
