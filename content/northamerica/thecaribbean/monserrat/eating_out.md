---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Montserrat's restaurant scene is tiny — a handful of places, all in the north of the island — but the food itself is excellent: Creole cooking, fresh fish, the national dish of *goat water* (a heavily spiced goat stew thickened with breadfruit) and slow-cooked oxtail with peas and rice.

**Tina's Restaurant** in Brades is the locally beloved Creole spot — goat water on Saturdays, fried fish lunches, conch fritters, fresh juices. Cash only, very generous portions. **Soca Cabana** at Little Bay does grilled fish and lobster on the waterfront and has live music on Friday and Saturday evenings. **Olveston House** above Salem — the late George Martin's former Caribbean home, now a small hotel — serves a more European menu in a beautiful garden setting; reservations needed.

For breakfast, the **People's Place** in Brades does the classic Caribbean salt fish, bakes (fried bread), and bush tea in the mornings. Various small roadside stands across the north sell *johnny cakes* and grilled corn.

Pizza is available at one or two casual spots near Little Bay. Self-catering supplies are at the Ram's supermarket in Brades.
