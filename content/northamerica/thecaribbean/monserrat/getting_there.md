---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Montserrat is reached only via Antigua, an hour or so to the north.

**By plane**, FlyMontserrat and SVG Air run small twin-engined Britten-Norman Islanders on the 20-minute hop from VC Bird International (ANU) in Antigua to John A. Osborne Airport (MNI) in the north of Montserrat. Several flights a day in each direction. The airport is small, the plane carries about nine passengers, and the strict 30-kilo total luggage allowance is enforced.

**By ferry**, the catamaran ferry between Antigua's Heritage Quay in St John's and Montserrat's Little Bay runs several times a week (the schedule changes seasonally — check before you travel). The crossing takes about 90 minutes; in calm weather it is a smooth ride, in trade-wind swell it is not.

There are no direct flights from outside the region. All visitors connect via Antigua, which is reached from London, the eastern US, Toronto, and intra-Caribbean hubs.

Note that the original Bramble Airport on the east coast was buried by pyroclastic flows in 1997 and is now in the exclusion zone — the current Osborne Airport in the north was built in 2005 as a replacement.
