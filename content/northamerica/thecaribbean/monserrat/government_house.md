The official residence of the Governor of Montserrat — the territory's British-appointed head of government — sits in Woodlands, in the safe northern zone. The building is a modest pink-and-white painted colonial house with a long shaded verandah and a small garden, considerably less grand than the equivalents on other Caribbean islands.

The original Government House in Plymouth was abandoned in 1997 with the rest of the capital; the Woodlands building was repurposed as the new official residence. It is open for tours by appointment and the gardens are sometimes accessible during public events.

For most visitors the interest is more as a visual marker of how an entire seat of colonial-era government had to be relocated north of the volcanic exclusion zone — an architectural footnote to one of the most disruptive natural disasters of the modern Caribbean.
