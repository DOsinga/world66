Little Bay is the small port on the north coast that took over from Plymouth as Montserrat's effective capital after the eruptions. It is where the ferry from Antigua docks, where the inter-island cargo ships unload, and where Brades — the seat of government a couple of kilometres uphill — has its waterfront.

The bay itself is sheltered and good for swimming, with a curve of dark grey sand and a couple of bars and restaurants along the back. The cruise dock and the small harbour give it slightly more bustle than anywhere else on the island, which is to say that there are sometimes a few dozen people at the bar at once instead of a handful.

A new town centre is being developed inland behind the bay as Montserrat's permanent capital replacement for Plymouth — at the moment it remains very much a work in progress. The annual St Patrick's Festival (in March) is anchored here, with stages and parades on the waterfront.
