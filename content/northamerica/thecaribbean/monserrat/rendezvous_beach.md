Rendezvous Beach is the only white-sand beach on Montserrat — the rest are dark grey or black, the volcanic island's default. It lies on the north-west coast, hidden behind a low headland, and is reachable only by a 45-minute hike over the bluff from Little Bay or by boat from the same harbour. There is no road in.

The walk over from Little Bay is short but moderately steep on a rough track; bring water and proper shoes. The reward is a quiet horseshoe of white sand with calm clear water, decent snorkelling along the rocks at either end, and almost certainly the place to yourself. No facilities; pack out everything you bring in.

Most island operators run a "Rendezvous swim" boat trip from Little Bay that adds snorkelling and a picnic for a fixed price; this is the easier option if you don't fancy the hike or want to combine it with a swim along the cliffs.
