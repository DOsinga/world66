---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**December to April** is the dry season and the peak. Temperatures sit around 26–28°C, the trade winds keep the heat manageable, and the rainfall is modest. The St Patrick's Festival in mid-March is the most colourful event in the island calendar and the busiest week of the year — book well in advance if you want to coincide with it.

**May, June, October, and November** are the shoulder months. The rain picks up — short, sharp showers that pass quickly — but the heat is bearable, the island is less busy, and prices come down.

**July to September** is officially hurricane season; in practice the worst-affected months tend to be August and September. The island has been hit several times in recent decades (Hurricane Hugo in 1989 was the most destructive). Most visitors avoid this stretch; the locals carry on, and the volcano viewing is just as good if the rain holds off.

The volcano is monitored continuously and zone access changes day to day. Check the current Daytime/Maritime Exclusion Zone status with the Montserrat Volcano Observatory before planning any tour into the south.
