Woodlands is the most accessible and consistently calm beach on Montserrat's safe northern coast — a curve of dark grey volcanic sand backed by low cliffs, with a small picnic shelter and a stairway down from the parking area at the top of the cliff. The water is usually flat and excellent for snorkelling; the offshore reef has good fish, including the occasional turtle.

It is rarely busy. On weekdays you might be alone; on Sundays a few Montserratian families come down for the afternoon. There are no facilities to speak of — bring water, sunscreen, and shade if you want it.

The drive down to the beach access from the main road is short but the lane is steep and narrow; rentable Suzuki Jimnys handle it fine. Bioluminescent plankton are sometimes visible in the water after dark in late summer.
