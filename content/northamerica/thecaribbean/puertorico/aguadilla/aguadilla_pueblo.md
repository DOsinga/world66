Aguadilla Pueblo is the old civic heart of the city, centred on its Spanish-style plaza, Catholic church, municipal buildings, and surrounding streets. It is a compact place to walk and see the layout that shaped daily life in Puerto Rican towns.

Pair it with nearby historic stops such as El Parterre, Casa de Piedra, or the old fort remains. The area works best as a short stroll rather than a full outing on its own.
