---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Aguadilla mixes surf culture, Spanish colonial history, and a handful of genuinely offbeat attractions. The old town has several well-preserved 19th-century buildings, a formal park, and churches that have outlasted earthquakes. Outside the centre, the former Ramey Air Force Base has been converted into a community hub, and the coastline offers some of the best surfing on the island. The town has a relaxed pace that makes it easy to spend a day or two beyond the beach.
