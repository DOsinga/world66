Punta Guilarte Beach is Arroyo's practical beach stop, with parking, picnic areas, and calmer family energy than the island's famous resort strands. It sits near the lighthouse area and works well as the lazy half of a history-and-waterfront day.

Check local conditions before swimming, especially after storms. On a calm day it is a straightforward place to cool off without making the whole day about the beach.
