---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Tren Urbano.** The easiest way to reach Bayamón from San Juan is by the Tren Urbano metro, which has its western terminus at Bayamón station — a 30-minute ride from Sagrado Corazón in Santurce. From there, the historic centre is a 10-minute walk south. Stations also serve the Coliseo Rubén Rodríguez (Deportivo) and points further along the line.

**By car.** PR-22 (Expreso José de Diego) is the main highway connecting Bayamón to San Juan; the drive takes 25 to 40 minutes depending on traffic, which can be heavy at rush hour.

**By bus.** AMA buses run from San Juan to Bayamón but are slow and unreliable; the Tren Urbano is usually a better choice.
