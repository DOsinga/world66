Puerto Rico National Cemetery is a solemn, ordered landscape in Bayamon, with rows of white markers tracing generations of Puerto Rican military service. It is not a casual sightseeing stop, but it is a meaningful place if you are interested in the island's relationship with the United States.

The visit is quiet and reflective. Go respectfully, allow time to walk rather than rush, and treat it as a memorial landscape rather than a conventional attraction.
