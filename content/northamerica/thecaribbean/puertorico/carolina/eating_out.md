---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The best eating in Carolina happens informally, at the kiosks of [Piñones](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/carolina/pinones) along the coast east of Isla Verde. This is where San Juan families come on weekends for alcapurrias, bacalaítos, and cold beer — honest Puerto Rican beach food at low prices. The Isla Verde strip has plenty of hotel restaurants and chain establishments catering to resort visitors, but Piñones is the reason to venture east.
