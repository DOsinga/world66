Isla Verde is the beach resort area that technically falls within Carolina's municipal limits, though most visitors think of it as an extension of San Juan. The beach itself is excellent: a wide arc of fine pale sand with clear, warm Caribbean water, backed by a dense line of resort hotels, restaurants, and beach bars. The snorkelling near the rocks at the eastern end is decent, and the surf break at Pine Grove draws local surfers.

The strip is unambiguously a resort zone — there is nothing subtle or undeveloped about it — but the beach is genuinely one of the best in the metro area. The hotel options range from large international chains to smaller, cheaper guesthouses a block or two back from the water. For travelers flying in or out of Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport, which is immediately adjacent, staying in Isla Verde makes early departures painless and beach access effortless.
