Guanica is a small town on the southern coast of [Puerto Rico](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico), best known for the [Guanica State Forest](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/guanica/guanica_state_forest) — a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve with 48 rare tree species and excellent birdwatching. The subtropical dry forest is one of the best-preserved examples in the world and makes for good hiking.

The town is also the [site of the first US troop landing](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/guanica/spanish_american_war_landing) during the Spanish-American War in 1898. The coastline around Guanica has some of Puerto Rico's finest beaches. [Playa Caña Gorda](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/guanica/playa_cana_gorda) is a calm, family-friendly stretch with clear water and good facilities. [Playa Ballena](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/guanica/playa_ballena) nearby has bigger waves and a wilder feel — keep an eye on children in the water. Boat rentals are available for exploring the coast, including the mangrove [Cayos de Caña Gorda](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/guanica/cayos_de_cana_gorda) just offshore.
