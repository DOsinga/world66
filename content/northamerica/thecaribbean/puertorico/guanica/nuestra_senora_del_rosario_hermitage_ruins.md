These are the preserved ruins of the former Hermitage of Our Lady of the Rosary of Yauco, built by the Spanish near the site of Yauco's first settlement. The municipality maintains the ruins as Parque de la Fundación.

It is a compact historic stop, best combined with other Yauco and Guanica-area sites rather than treated as a long outing on its own.
