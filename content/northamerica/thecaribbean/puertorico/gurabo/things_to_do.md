title: "Things To Do"
type: section
---

Gurabo rewards visitors who slow down. The town's identity is tied to the pasillo, a traditional Puerto Rican musical form with roots in 19th-century salon music — the town celebrates this heritage and it lends the place a cultural dignity you don't find in tourist towns. The central plaza anchors daily life, a shaded square beside the colonial church where old men play dominoes and kids chase pigeons on weekend afternoons.

Beyond the plaza, the appeal is in the landscape. The Sierra de Cayey rises to the south and east, with informal trails offering views across the central mountain range and glimpses of the coast on clear days. The Río Gurabo runs through the valley floor, surrounded by farmland and tropical vegetation. The Universidad del Turabo campus brings some youthful energy to town and occasionally hosts public events.

This is not a place for a packed itinerary. A half-day is enough to walk the plaza, eat a proper Puerto Rican lunch, and get a feel for mountain-town life in the interior.
