The Río Gurabo runs through the valley that gives the municipality its name. It's not a dramatic river but it is a pleasant one — clear enough in the dry season, lined with riparian vegetation, and surrounded by the kind of working farmland and tropical greenery that characterises Puerto Rico's interior.

Walking the valley floor, especially in the quieter areas outside the town centre, gives a sense of the agricultural character of this part of the island. Coffee, plantains, and tropical fruit are grown nearby. If you have a car, driving the rural roads along the valley is a good way to see the landscape without a specific destination — look for farm tracks leading toward the river and the hills.
