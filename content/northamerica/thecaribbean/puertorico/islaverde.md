Isla Verde is the easternmost beach neighborhood in the San Juan metropolitan area, sitting right next to Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport. [Isla Verde Beach](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/islaverde/isla_verde_beach) is arguably the best in the city — a wide, palm-lined stretch of golden sand with clear water and enough wave action to keep things interesting without being dangerous. The big resort hotels (Ritz-Carlton, Intercontinental) line the waterfront, but the beach itself is public and accessible. At the eastern end, [Pine Grove Beach](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/islaverde/pine_grove_beach) draws the local surf crowd.

The neighborhood is more spread out and less walkable than Condado, organized along Avenida Isla Verde with strip malls, fast-food joints, and car dealerships mixed in with the resort properties. It lacks the urban charm of Old San Juan or the scene of Condado, but the beach quality makes up for the setting. If your priority is sand and sun with easy airport access, Isla Verde is hard to beat — you can be on the beach within fifteen minutes of landing. There are good casual restaurants in the area, particularly for seafood, and the [kiosks at Piñones](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/islaverde/pinones_kiosks), a short drive east along the coast road, serve some of the best fried snacks in Puerto Rico.
