The Parguera dining scene is short — a handful of waterfront places, mostly serving fresh fish, conch, octopus salad and the local *frituras* (fritters of *bacalao*, *empanadillas* of crab). La Casita and El Karacol are the long-running standbys for full sit-down seafood, both right on the water. For something quicker, the kiosks along the boardwalk do *alcapurrias*, *pinchos* and cold *Medalla* through the evening.

Expect a markup over inland Lajas — you are paying for the view and the dockside location — and expect crowds and a wait on weekend nights. Reservations help in high season.
