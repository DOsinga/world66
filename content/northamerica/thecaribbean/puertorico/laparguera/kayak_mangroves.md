title: Kayaking the Mangroves
type: poi
---

Kayaking is the quietest and most rewarding way to see the Parguera lagoon. Two or three outfits in the village rent sit-on-top kayaks by the hour or the half-day, and you can paddle straight out through the boat channel and into the mangrove cays within fifteen minutes.

The narrow channels between Cayo Caracoles and Cayo Enrique are the highlight — too tight for boats, full of juvenile fish, and shaded enough that you can paddle in the heat of the day. Stick to the marked routes, watch for shallow seagrass (sit-on-top kayaks scrape less than canoes), and head back well before dusk.
