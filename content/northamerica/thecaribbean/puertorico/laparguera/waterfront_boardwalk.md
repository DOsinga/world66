title: Waterfront Boardwalk
type: poi
---

La Parguera's *malecón* — the waterfront boardwalk along the village dock — is where everything happens. Boat captains tout bio-bay and cay trips by day; food stands and bars take over at night. On Friday and Saturday evenings the whole strip fills with families and groups from across the island for the *vellonera* scene, a Caribbean tradition of cars, jukebox music and beer on the boardwalk.

If you are after quiet, come midweek — Tuesday and Wednesday nights the village is almost empty. The waterfront has all the boat ticket booths in one place, which makes comparing prices on bio-bay tours easy.
