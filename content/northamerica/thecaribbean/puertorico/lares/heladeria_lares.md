Heladeria Lares is the town's beloved ice-cream stop, known for flavours that run far beyond vanilla and coconut. Corn, avocado, garlic, and rice-and-beans ice cream are part of the fun, though there are safer choices too.

It is not a grand attraction, but it is a very Lares thing to do: local, odd, and easy to remember.
