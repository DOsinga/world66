Plaza de la Revolucion is the symbolic heart of Lares, marking the 1868 uprising against Spanish colonial rule. The rebellion was short-lived, but it became one of the founding stories of Puerto Rican nationalism.

Come for the monument, the town-centre setting, and the sense that Lares remembers itself through this event. Around September 23, the anniversary gives the plaza extra life.
