A small, restored nineteenth-century townhouse a couple of blocks west of Plaza Colón, the childhood home of the woman whose son became one of the great cellists of the twentieth century. The house now operates as a small museum dedicated to Casals and to the musical life of Mayagüez.

Worth a stop for anyone interested in classical music or the city's quieter cultural history. Hours are limited — check before going.
