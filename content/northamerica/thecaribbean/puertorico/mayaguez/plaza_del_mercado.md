The Plaza del Mercado is the old central market of Mayagüez, a single-storey concrete-and-tile building a few blocks west of Plaza Colón. It is smaller than the great Caribbean markets of Port-of-Spain or Pointe-à-Pitre, but it does the same job: fish, plantains, root vegetables, hot peppers, fresh juice stalls, and a couple of food counters where you can sit and have *mofongo* with whatever stew is going that day.

Best in the morning, when the produce is fresh and the local fishermen drop off the day's catch from Mayagüez Bay. By mid-afternoon things slow down.
