The Mayagüez softball stadium, named for the Puerto Rican sportsman Santiago "Tatito" Llorens. It is a compact concrete bowl seating around 2,500 fans, home to the Mayagüez women's softball team. Softball in Puerto Rico is a serious business — the national women's team has competed for World Cup titles for decades — and a Friday-night game here is a low-cost, high-energy way to spend an evening.

Tickets are sold at the gate. The food stands behind the stands sell good *pinchos* and beer.
