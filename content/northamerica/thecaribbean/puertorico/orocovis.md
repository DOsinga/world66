Orocovis sits high in Puerto Rico's Cordillera Central, far enough from the coast that the island feels different: cooler air, tight mountain roads, roadside waterfalls, and small restaurants built around longaniza and slow afternoons.

The town has one very loud draw, [Toro Verde](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/orocovis/toro_verde), where the ziplines turn the ridges into a playground. But Orocovis is more than an adventure stop. [Chorro de Dona Juana](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/orocovis/chorro_de_dona_juana) drops beside the road in Toro Negro country, the [Ruta Panoramica](/northamerica/thecaribbean/puertorico/orocovis/ruta_panoramica) gives you some of the island's best mountain driving, and the town plaza still feels like the centre of a working interior municipality.

Come with daylight, patience, and a driver who is comfortable with hairpins. The roads are part of the trip, but they are narrow, steep, and not something to rush after rain.
