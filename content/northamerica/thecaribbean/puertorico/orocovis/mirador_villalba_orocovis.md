Mirador Villalba-Orocovis is the best formal lookout on the mountain road, set on PR-143 near the boundary with Villalba. On a clear day the view reaches across the folded interior, with the rare chance of seeing toward both the Atlantic and Caribbean sides of Puerto Rico.

Cloud can close the view quickly. If the weather is clear when you pass, stop then.
