Museo Orocoveno Celestino Aviles Melendez is a modest but useful stop if you want Orocovis to be more than scenery and ziplines. Its strongest angle is santo carving, the Puerto Rican tradition of hand-carved wooden saints.

Check locally before counting on fixed hours. Small municipal museums in the mountains can be informal.
