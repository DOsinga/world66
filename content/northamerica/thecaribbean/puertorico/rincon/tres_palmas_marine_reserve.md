Tres Palmas Marine Reserve protects a stretch of coast north of the lighthouse where elkhorn coral reef has survived in unusually good condition. The reserve was established in 2005 after local surfer activism; fishing and anchoring are prohibited within the reserve boundaries. In winter, the reef produces one of the Caribbean's most powerful surf breaks — experienced surfers only when the big swells arrive.

For non-surfers, the reef offers excellent snorkelling at calmer times of year, with good coral coverage, tropical fish, and occasional sea turtles. The beach here is narrow and rocky in places; access is easier from the Domes side. A good pair of reef shoes is useful.
