Piri Thomas's 1967 autobiography of growing up Puerto Rican and Black in Spanish Harlem during the 1940s and 1950s. The book is not set in San Juan, but no work has done more to define the Nuyorican identity that has shaped half of modern Puerto Rican culture — including the music, the art, and the food scene you encounter in Santurce.

A brutal and lyrical memoir of street violence, drug addiction, prison, and eventual redemption. Read it before going to Santurce, where the wall murals and the spoken-word scene continue the lineage Thomas opened up.
