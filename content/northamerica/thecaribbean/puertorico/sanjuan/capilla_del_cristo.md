The little Chapel of Christ sits at the southern end of Calle del Cristo, perched right at the city wall. It is a single room of polished wood and silver, with a small altar to El Cristo de la Salud — Christ of Health. The altar is plated in silver milagros, miniature votive offerings left by people seeking cures.

The chapel is small and almost always closed except for Tuesdays, when it opens for a few hours and a queue forms. Whether you go inside or not, the spot itself is one of the most photographed in the old city — the wrought-iron gate, the chapel against the wall, and the Parque de las Palomas just behind, where you can sit and feed pigeons under a fig tree.
