Puerto Rico is small enough that almost anywhere on the main island is a day trip from San Juan. **El Yunque** (the only tropical rainforest in the US National Forest system) is 45 minutes east — the most popular excursion. **Fajardo**, an hour east, is the launching point for the offshore islands of Vieques and Culebra and for the bioluminescent bay at Laguna Grande. **Ponce**, the second city, is a long day-trip on the south coast and worth it for the Museo de Arte de Ponce. **Arecibo**, on the north coast, used to host the world's largest radio telescope (which collapsed in 2020 but the site is still open as a memorial visitor centre).

**Vieques** and **Culebra**, the two inhabited offshore islands, deserve overnight stays rather than day trips — but if you have only a day and want a perfect Caribbean beach, the ferry-and-public-van combination from Fajardo to Culebra's Flamenco Beach is feasible.
