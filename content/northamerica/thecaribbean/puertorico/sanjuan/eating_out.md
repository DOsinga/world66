---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

San Juan's food scene splits into three. **Old San Juan** is where the upscale and tourist-facing restaurants concentrate — good but expensive, with strong contemporary Puerto Rican cooking at the top end. **Santurce and Ocean Park** is where you find the most interesting newer kitchens, often in converted houses or warehouse spaces, along Calle Loíza and around La Placita de Santurce. **Condado and Isla Verde** lean towards hotel restaurants and steakhouses.

The signature dishes to try: mofongo (mashed green plantain with garlic and pork crackling, served with shrimp, chicken, or beef), arroz con gandules (rice with pigeon peas), lechón asado (whole spit-roasted pork), and a tripleta sandwich — three meats (steak, ham, pork), cheese, lettuce, tomato, and a Puerto Rican sauce, on a soft long roll. For breakfast, ask for mallorca de queso, a sweet butter-and-cheese pressed roll dusted with powdered sugar.
