Isla de Cabras is a small peninsula across the bay from Old San Juan, with a recreational park, views toward El Morro, and the ruins of a former leper colony. The appeal is the mix of harbour scenery and rough-edged history.

It works best for travellers with a car or extra time who want a different angle on San Juan Bay.
