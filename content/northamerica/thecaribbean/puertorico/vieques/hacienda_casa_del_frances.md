The Frenchman's House — a two-storey wooden plantation house with deep verandas and a tin roof — sits in old gardens just north of Esperanza. Built in 1910 at the tail end of the sugar era, it is the most prominent surviving piece of plantation architecture on Vieques and is on the US National Register of Historic Places.

The property has had a long life as a guesthouse and is best seen from the road, where the proportions of the house and the avenue of old palms still convey what wealthy hacienda life looked like. Combine with a stroll round Esperanza or use as a landmark on the drive between the airport and the south coast.
