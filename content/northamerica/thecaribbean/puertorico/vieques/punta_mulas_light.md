A neat little 1896 Spanish-built lighthouse on the north shore at the entrance to Isabel Segunda harbour. The cylindrical tower and attached keeper's house are now restored and used for occasional cultural events. It is not normally open inside, but the seafront walk around the point gives the best view of arriving ferries and the open Atlantic to the north.

A short walk from the town centre. Combine with the fort for a leisurely morning before catching a taxi south to the beaches.
