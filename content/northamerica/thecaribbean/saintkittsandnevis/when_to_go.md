---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Saint Kitts and Nevis share the standard eastern Caribbean tropical rhythm: warm and breezy year-round, with a clear dry season and a riskier wet/hurricane season.

**Dry season (December–April)** is the peak time. Daytime temperatures hover around 27–29°C, the trade winds keep things comfortable, and the chance of rain on any given day is low. This is also the cruise-ship and high-season window — book hotels well ahead and expect higher prices.

**Shoulder (May–June and November)** offers similar weather at noticeably lower prices, with brief afternoon showers more likely.

**Wet/hurricane season (July–November)** brings heavier rain and the genuine — though statistically low — possibility of a hurricane. Direct hits to the islands are infrequent but real; September is the riskiest month. Travel insurance with weather provisions is worth the price.

**St Kitts Music Festival** in late June draws regional acts and is the islands' biggest annual event. **Culturama** on Nevis runs late July into the first weekend of August, with parades and pageants.

Whale-watching off the Narrows between the two islands is best **February–April** when humpbacks pass through.
