---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

The island has more beaches than most visitors expect, and they vary considerably in character. The French side tends toward quieter, less developed strands; the Dutch side has the more commercial beach scene. Given the island's small size, it is possible to visit several in a single day.

Orient Bay is the liveliest — long, windy, backed by restaurants and beach bars, and partly clothing-optional. It draws a crowd in high season but is large enough to absorb it. Baie Longue on the western shore is the opposite: a long, mostly empty sweep of white sand with calm water and almost no commercial development.

Maho Beach on the Dutch side is in a category of its own — the planes are the attraction, not the sand.
