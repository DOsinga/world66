Philipsburg is the capital of the Dutch side, sitting on a narrow strip of land between the sea and the Great Salt Pond. It is the island's commercial centre and its cruise ship hub: on days when two or three ships are in dock, the population of Front Street roughly doubles. The duty-free strip offers electronics, liquor, jewellery, and the full range of Caribbean souvenir merchandise. Prices on imported goods can be genuinely competitive given the free port status.

When the ships are not in, Philipsburg is a quieter town with some Dutch colonial-era architecture along the waterfront, a few decent restaurants, and the Great Bay Beach running along its southern edge. The beach is accessible and pleasant, though not in the same class as Baie Longue or Orient Bay.
