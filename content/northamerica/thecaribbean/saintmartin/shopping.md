---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The Dutch side is the island's shopping hub. Sint Maarten is a free port with no sales tax, which makes it popular with cruise ship passengers hunting for duty-free electronics, jewellery, liquor, and perfume. Philipsburg's Front Street is lined almost entirely with duty-free shops and souvenir stalls. Prices can be genuinely good on spirits and branded goods; the atmosphere is commercial and crowds can be thick when ships are in.

Marigot on the French side has a more relaxed shopping scene: boutiques selling French cosmetics, beachwear, and jewellery, plus the waterfront market where local crafts, spices, and Caribbean produce are sold. The Wednesday and Saturday morning markets are the best attended.

The **Marigot Marina** area has several boutiques and galleries that carry local artwork and Caribbean craft. Worth a browse if you want something that isn't a bottle of rum.
