---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Saint-Martin packs an extraordinary amount into its 37 square miles. The French side is built for beaches and eating; the Dutch side adds commercial energy and a few genuine spectacles. Most visitors cover both without noticing the transition — there is no border to cross, just a road sign.

The island's single most famous experience needs no effort to access: stand on Maho Beach at the right time and a jumbo jet will pass close enough overhead to make your heart stop. On the French side, the ruined fort above Marigot and the hiking trail to Pic du Paradis reward those willing to leave the beach.
