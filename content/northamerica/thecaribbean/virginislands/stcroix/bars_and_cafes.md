---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Christiansted's bar scene is concentrated on the Boardwalk and in the courtyards of the old warehouse district. Rum is the dominant spirit — St. Croix is home to Cruzan Rum, one of the Caribbean's best-known distilleries, and the island takes its rum seriously. The beach bars along the north shore cater to a different crowd: divers, surfers, and people who drove across the island specifically to drink with their feet in the sand.
