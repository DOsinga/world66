---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

St. Croix rewards visitors who are willing to go beyond the beach. The Danish colonial core of Christiansted is compact and walkable, with a National Historic Site covering the fort, the customs house, and the old slave market. Outside town, the island offers some of the most accessible reef diving in the Caribbean, a confirmed Columbus landfall site, and at its easternmost tip, the point where each new day in the United States begins.
