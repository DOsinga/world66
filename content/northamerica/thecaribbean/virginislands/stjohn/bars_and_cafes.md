---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Cruz Bay has a lively bar scene that punches well above its weight for a town of its size. The sailing crowd — yachties making the crossing from St. Thomas or coming up from the BVI — mix with expats, resort guests, and day-trippers, giving the waterfront bars an easy, international feel. Rum cocktails are the order of the day, and the vibe is reliably laid-back.
