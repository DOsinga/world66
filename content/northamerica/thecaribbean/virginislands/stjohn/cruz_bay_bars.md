Cruz Bay's waterfront bars stay lively well into the evening. Pusser's and similar spots serve rum cocktails and cold beer, attracting a mix of sailors, expats, and tourists in roughly equal measure. The atmosphere is relaxed and unpretentious — nobody is dressed up, nobody is trying too hard. Happy hour typically runs late afternoon, which is well-timed for the ferry crowd coming back from the beaches.
