The beach bars at Cinnamon Bay and the other main national park beaches serve cold drinks and simple food — burgers, fish sandwiches, grilled chicken — and are the obvious place for a post-snorkel lunch. Nothing fancy, but exactly what you want after a morning in the water. The Cinnamon Bay Beach Bar is the largest; smaller operations exist at other beaches. Opening hours depend on the season and visitor numbers.
