Tektite Trail is a short but moderately strenuous hike from Lameshur Road in Virgin Islands National Park. The trail climbs through dry coastal terrain to viewpoints over the south shore cliffs, making it a good choice for hikers who want something quieter than the better-known Reef Bay route.
