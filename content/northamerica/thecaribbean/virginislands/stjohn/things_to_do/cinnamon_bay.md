---
title: "Cinnamon Bay"
type: poi
latitude: 18.347
longitude: -64.748
---

A long, curved beach within the national park, calmer and less crowded than Trunk Bay. The snorkelling at the headlands is excellent — particularly off the rocks at either end of the bay — and the water is generally calmer. Camping is available at the Cinnamon Bay campsite, one of the few places to stay within the park, and watersport equipment (kayaks, windsurfers) can be rented on site.
