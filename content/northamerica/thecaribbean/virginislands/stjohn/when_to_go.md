---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

December through April is the dry season and the best time to visit. Water visibility for snorkelling is at its clearest, temperatures are cooler (mid-20s Celsius), and trade winds keep things comfortable. This is also high season: prices are higher, the beaches are busier, and accommodation fills up quickly — particularly over Christmas and New Year.

May and June are a good compromise: the high season crowds have gone, prices drop, and the weather is still generally fine before the rainy season fully takes hold. July through November is hurricane season, with September and October carrying the greatest risk. Rain comes in short sharp showers rather than all-day downpours for most of this period, but the threat of a serious storm is real, and some services operate reduced hours.
