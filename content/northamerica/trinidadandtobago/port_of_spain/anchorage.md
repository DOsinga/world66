The Anchorage restaurant on the Western Main Road in Carenage, west of St Clair, has been one of Port of Spain's most established seafood restaurants for decades. The setting is right on the water — tables on a deck looking out over the Gulf of Paria toward Venezuela — and the fish and seafood are as fresh as the location suggests.

The menu is built around the catch of the day supplemented by consistent local favourites: flying fish, kingfish, carite (Spanish mackerel), crab and callaloo, and whatever is running well that week. The bar is excellent and the rum selection comprehensive. It is a reliable choice for a special meal, particularly for visitors who want to sit on the water with a cocktail and watch the boats in the Gulf. Reservations recommended for dinner.
