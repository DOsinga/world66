Ariapita Avenue in Woodbrook is the main entertainment strip of Port of Spain — a long street of restaurants, bars, rum shops, and casual eating places that comes alive at night, particularly on weekends. The avenue is colloquially known as "the Avenue" and is the standard answer to the question "where should I eat in Port of Spain."

The range of food on offer reflects Trinidad's culinary diversity: Indo-Trinidadian curry houses, Chinese restaurants that have been operating for three or four generations, modern Caribbean fusion places, and any number of casual spots serving grilled meats and cold beer. The rum shops at the less glamorous end of the avenue are authentically local — low-key neighbourhood bars where the Carib beer is cold and conversation with strangers is normal. The more polished restaurant end is a better bet for an evening out. The whole strip gets busiest from Thursday through Saturday after about 9pm.
