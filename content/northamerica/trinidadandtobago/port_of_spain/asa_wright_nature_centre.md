The Asa Wright Nature Centre in the Arima Valley, about 90 minutes from Port of Spain, is one of the great birdwatching destinations in the Americas. Trinidad sits just off the South American mainland and has bird species from both the Caribbean and South American ecosystems; the Arima Valley alone has recorded over 160 species. The Centre is a working bird sanctuary and guesthouse on the site of an old cocoa and coffee estate, with a famous veranda from which hummingbirds, tanagers, and other species can be observed at close range.

The site is particularly famous for the Oilbird, a cave-dwelling species that navigates by echolocation and nests in a cave on the property. Guided night tours to the cave are one of the highlights of a visit. Day visitors are welcome for a fee; overnight stays are available. The Centre runs guided walks and birdwatching tours throughout the day.
