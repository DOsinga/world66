---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Port of Spain's nightlife is concentrated in Woodbrook, particularly on and around Ariapita Avenue, and in St James along the Western Main Road. Both areas have rum shops, bars, and clubs at various levels of formality. The Trinidadian approach to drinking is sociable and extended: an evening out often starts late (10pm is not unusual) and continues well past midnight.

Carib lager is the national beer and what you'll drink in most rum shops. Stag is a slightly stronger local lager. Rum punches — with the excellent Angostura rum, fresh lime, and grenadine — are the cocktail baseline. Solo is a local carbonated drink brand that produces a useful lime soda for mixing.

The Oval in Newtown, the Pelican Inn in Woodbrook, and the bars along Ariapita Avenue provide the widest range of options. Upscale hotel bars (the Kapok, the Hilton on Lady Young Road) are quieter and more expensive but useful for business meetings. During Carnival season, the fetes (ticketed all-inclusive parties with unlimited food and drink) are the primary social events; tickets sell out fast.
