The Chaguaramas Convention Centre, known as C3, is part of the larger Chaguaramas development on the northwestern peninsula. It hosts conferences, concerts, and major events throughout the year, and is occasionally used for large Carnival-season events. The facility sits on the waterfront of Chaguaramas Bay with good views of the Gulf and the neighbouring islands.

More interesting than the convention centre itself is the surrounding Chaguaramas area: the military museum, the yacht clubs, the beaches, and the general atmosphere of a peninsula that has been transformed from an off-limits military zone into a recreation and tourism destination. The food court near the marinas has several good seafood restaurants and casual eating places popular with the boating community.
