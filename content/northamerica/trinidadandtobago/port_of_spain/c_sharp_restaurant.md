C Sharp on Ariapita Avenue is one of the more dependable options on Port of Spain's main restaurant strip, serving well-executed Trinidadian food with some international dishes. The callaloo (a thick soup made from dasheen leaves, coconut milk, and okra) is reliably good, and the curried dishes reflect the Indo-Trinidadian side of the city's culinary heritage.

The atmosphere is unpretentious — a proper restaurant without the pretension of some of the newer places on the avenue — and the price point is reasonable. It is a sensible choice for a first dinner in the city, particularly if you want to try dishes like stewed chicken, rice and peas, fried plantain, and macaroni pie (a Trinidadian take on mac and cheese that is nothing like the American version).
