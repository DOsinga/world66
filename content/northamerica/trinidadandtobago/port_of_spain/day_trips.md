---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Trinidad's compact size makes day trips genuinely practical from Port of Spain. The north coast beaches (Maracas, Las Cuevas) are 45 minutes away; Asa Wright Nature Centre in the Arima Valley is 90 minutes; the Caroni Bird Sanctuary is 20 minutes south; the Chaguaramas peninsula is 30 minutes west.

For longer excursions: the Pitch Lake at La Brea (natural asphalt lake, the largest in the world) is about two hours south via the Uriah Butler Highway; Lopinot Historical Complex (restored cocoa estate) is 45 minutes east; the Pointe-à-Pierre Wildfowl Trust (conservation area within a working refinery) is 90 minutes south.

Tobago is a full-day ferry excursion or a 20-minute flight, and warrants at least an overnight stay to see the reef and beaches properly.
