Downtown Port of Spain is the dense commercial and governmental core of the capital: a grid of streets between the waterfront and Woodford Square, full of office buildings, shops, street vendors, and the kind of city noise that is both oppressive and energising. The streets are busy on weekday mornings with office workers, and some quieten dramatically after 5pm.

The key sights are clustered in a manageable area: the Red House, Woodford Square, Holy Trinity Cathedral, the Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception, Fort San Andres, and Independence Square can all be seen in a morning's walk. The covered galleries of many of the older commercial buildings provide shade. The area around Frederick Street and Charlotte Street is the main shopping zone, chaotic and crowded but functional. The Drag (Promenade) along Independence Square is the place to sit and watch the city's working life unfold.
