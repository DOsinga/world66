The Port of Spain waterfront along Wrightson Road was extensively redeveloped in the 2000s into the Government Campus Plaza complex, a large modern government precinct that has changed the character of the southern edge of the city. The development includes the International Waterfront Centre (a conference and convention facility) and several government ministry buildings.

The waterfront promenade provides a place to walk along the Gulf of Paria, with views of the gulf and the dry-season Venezuelan coast on clear days. The area is well maintained but feels somewhat formal and government-oriented rather than animated urban life. The ferry terminal for Tobago is at the western end of the waterfront; this is where the departure point for island ferries is located, making the area worth knowing if you're catching a boat to Scarborough.
