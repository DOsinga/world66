The Kapok Hotel on Cotton Hill in St Clair has long housed one of Port of Spain's better restaurants, Jaffa. It is a reliable choice for a more upmarket dinner — the menu focuses on Caribbean cuisine with modern presentation, using local ingredients including flying fish, crab, christophene (a local vegetable), and dasheen. The hotel rooftop bar is one of the better places in the city for a sunset drink, with views over the western part of the Savannah.

The Kapok is also used by visiting cricket teams and journalists during Test matches, which gives the bar a particular character during the Test season. The restaurant is busy on weekends; booking is advisable.
