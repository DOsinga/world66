Memorial Park is a small urban park on Frederick Street, opposite the National Museum. It was laid out as a memorial to Trinidadians who died in the First and Second World Wars, and a central cenotaph commemorates the fallen. The park is well shaded and maintained, providing a quiet respite from the surrounding city streets.

The park is a reasonable place to sit and read, and the surrounding area has some of Port of Spain's older commercial buildings. The National Museum is directly opposite and can be combined with a visit. The area is busy on weekdays with office workers on lunch breaks; it quiets considerably in the evenings.
