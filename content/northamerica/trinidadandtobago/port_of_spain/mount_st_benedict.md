Mount St Benedict is a Benedictine monastery perched on a hillside about 25 kilometres east of Port of Spain in the hills above Tunapuna. It is the largest monastery in the Caribbean and one of the oldest religious institutions in Trinidad, founded in 1912 by monks who came from Brazil. The drive up the hill alone is worth it — the views over the central plains of Trinidad and across to the Gulf of Paria are spectacular.

The monastery grounds are open to visitors, and the monks still live and work here. The monks operate a small guesthouse and a café — the Abbey Café — which serves excellent coffee made from Trinidadian cocoa and simple meals. Birdwatching on the monastery grounds is excellent: the hillside forest holds numerous species including the Bearded Bellbird, whose unusual call is one of the stranger sounds in the Caribbean. The site can be visited as a half-day excursion from Port of Spain.
