The National Academy for the Performing Arts (NAPA) is Port of Spain's main venue for classical music, theatre, and dance. Opened in 2009, the building was designed by Peter Minshall — the legendary Trinidadian Carnival artist and designer — and sits on a prominent site near the Queen's Park Savannah. The structure is shaped to suggest a large wave or sail, and its exterior glass fins shift colour with the light.

NAPA hosts concerts, theatrical productions, and Carnival-related performances throughout the year. The building is considered one of the most architecturally distinctive in the English-speaking Caribbean. Check the schedule in advance as performances sell out; the venue is also used for state functions.
