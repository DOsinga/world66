NAPA is Port of Spain's main concert hall and performing arts venue, opened in 2009 on the northeast corner of the Queen's Park Savannah. The building was designed by Berardo Borret, a Venezuelan architect, and its distinctive wave-shaped roof has made it one of the city's most recognizable modern landmarks, though its construction was controversial given the costs involved.

The main concert hall hosts classical music, jazz, theatre, and cultural performances throughout the year. The venue becomes especially important during Carnival season, when it is used for finals of the Calypso Monarch and other competitions. Check the schedule for what's on during your visit; Port of Spain's performing arts scene is more active than most visitors expect, and NAPA offers a chance to see Trinidadian performers in a proper concert setting.
