Newtown is the neighbourhood immediately east of Woodbrook and north of downtown, sandwiched between the commercial centre and the Savannah. It has more residential character than either neighbour — quieter streets of old wooden Trinidad houses (gingerbread houses with elaborate fretwork), small businesses, and a few restaurants that haven't been discovered by the Ariapita Avenue crowd.

Newtown is worth walking through for the architecture alone: the late Victorian and Edwardian-era houses here represent a vernacular building tradition specific to Trinidad, with their distinctive wooden galleries, decorative latticework, and painted timber facades. Many are in various states of disrepair, but others have been well maintained or restored. The neighbourhood is safe to walk during the day.
