The Northern Range, the chain of hills that forms the spine of northern Trinidad, provides excellent hiking directly accessible from Port of Spain. Several trails head into the forest from the suburbs on the city's northern edge, reaching ridge-top viewpoints and waterfalls within a couple of hours' walk. The forest is tropical and dense, and the birdwatching is exceptional — Trinidad has over 400 bird species, and the Northern Range holds most of them.

The most accessible trail starts from Maraval, about 15 minutes' drive from downtown, and climbs to the ridge above the city. The **Paria Falls** trail in the Blanchisseuse area, further east along the north coast road, leads to a waterfall and freshwater pool, and the Brasso Seco area offers guided forest walks and cocoa farm tours. A guide is recommended for any off-trail walking; trails can be overgrown and disorienting. Several local operators offer guided hikes.
