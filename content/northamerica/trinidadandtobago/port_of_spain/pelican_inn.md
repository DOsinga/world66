The Pelican Inn on Coblentz Avenue in Woodbrook is one of Port of Spain's most reliably good bars — a long-running, unpretentious place with cold Carib beer, a decent selection of rums, and a garden at the back that catches the evening breeze. It has been a fixture of the city's social life for decades and attracts a mixed crowd of locals, expats, and visiting Caribbean professionals.

The kitchen turns out solid bar food — wings, burgers, local dishes — without pretension. The atmosphere on a Thursday or Friday evening is warm and sociable, and it is a reliable place to find conversation with Trinidadians from all walks of life. Live music occasionally on weekends.
