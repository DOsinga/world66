The Pointe-à-Pierre Wildfowl Trust is one of the most unusual wildlife reserves in the Caribbean: a conservation area operating within the grounds of the state petroleum refinery, about 80 kilometres south of Port of Spain. The contrast between the industrial infrastructure and the lakes and forest within which it sits is genuinely strange, and the reserve is seriously important for conservation — it maintains captive breeding programmes for the scarlet ibis and other threatened Trinidadian bird species.

The reserve is open to visitors by appointment; advance booking is essential. It is a significant excursion from Port of Spain (90 minutes each way) and worth combining with a stop in San Fernando, Trinidad's second city. The birdwatching within the reserve is excellent, and the lake walks are peaceful in a way that defies the surroundings.
