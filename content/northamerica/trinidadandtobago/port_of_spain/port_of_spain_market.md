The Central Market on Charlotte Street is the working food market of Port of Spain, and one of the most vivid sensory experiences in the city. The covered halls and outdoor stalls sell fresh produce, ground provisions (root vegetables), spices, dried goods, and the full range of Trinidadian culinary ingredients — shadow beni (culantro), chadon beni seasoning, hot peppers of every variety, fresh coconuts, and mountains of tropical fruit.

The market is busiest on Friday and Saturday mornings, when vendors arrive early and shoppers come from across the city. It is a good place to buy provisions if you're cooking, and an excellent place to sample street food — the stalls around the market perimeter sell roti, doubles, and various cooked breakfasts to the workers and shoppers. The area around the market is dense and chaotic by urban Caribbean standards; keep your valuables secure.
