Queen's Royal College is one of the Magnificent Seven, the line of grand historic buildings facing Queen's Park Savannah. Its towers, gables, and warm stonework make it one of the most eye-catching pieces of Port of Spain's colonial-era architecture.

The building is still a school, so the visit is usually about the exterior and the wider Savannah walk. Go with the other Magnificent Seven in mind; together they give the city a more layered architectural story than the downtown streets alone.
