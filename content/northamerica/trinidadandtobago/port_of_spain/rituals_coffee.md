Rituals is Trinidad's own coffee chain, and while the concept is nothing unusual — espresso drinks, pastries, free WiFi — the fact that it uses Trinidadian-grown cocoa in some of its drinks makes it worth noting. Trinidad is historically significant in the world of chocolate: the island was a major producer of fine-flavour cocoa in the 19th century, and the Nacional and Trinitario cocoa varieties used in premium chocolate worldwide originated here.

The branches throughout Port of Spain are functional and air-conditioned; the Ariapita Avenue location is convenient to Woodbrook. For something more local, look for vendors selling fresh coconut water from the boot of their cars or at the Savannah food stalls — a cold green coconut is the best hot-weather drink in the city.
