The roti shop is the Indo-Trinidadian contribution to Port of Spain's food culture, and it is one of the most satisfying meals you can eat in the city for a few dollars. Roti — a large flatbread, either dhalpuri (stuffed with ground split peas) or paratha (plain, flaky) — is wrapped around a filling of curried potato, chickpeas, chicken, goat, duck, shrimp, or conch. The whole thing is folded into a package you eat with your hands.

The best roti shops have lines at lunchtime. Ali's on St James (northwest of downtown), Sauce Doubles near the Savannah, and the many shops along the Eastern Main Road are the places locals go. The coconut shrimp roti and the curry duck are the orders to judge a shop by. Unlike doubles, roti is a sit-down or takeaway meal — most shops have plastic tables and chairs, and eating at the counter is fine.
