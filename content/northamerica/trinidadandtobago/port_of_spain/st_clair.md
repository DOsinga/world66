St Clair is the upscale residential neighbourhood northwest of the Savannah, where many of Port of Spain's expatriates and wealthier residents live. The streets are wider, the houses are larger and better maintained, and the general atmosphere is more sedate than the rest of the city. It's not a destination in itself, but it contains some of the city's better hotels and several good restaurants.

The area around Nook Avenue and Stanmore Avenue has a concentration of restaurants popular with the business and diplomatic community. Anchorage on the Western Main Road, a bit further north, is one of the city's best-known seafood restaurants. The neighbourhood is pleasant for an evening walk after dinner, though after dark most movement is by car.
