The steelband "yards" — the headquarters and rehearsal spaces of Port of Spain's major steelbands — are concentrated in the East Dry River and Laventille areas east of downtown, the historically poor districts where the steel pan was invented in the 1930s and 1940s. In the months leading up to Carnival, the bands rehearse publicly in their yards for the Panorama competition, and anyone can walk in and listen.

The major bands include Phase II Pan Groove, Invaders, Renegades, and Exodus — each with a long history and a dedicated following. Panorama is the national steelband competition, and the rehearsal period (typically from December through the February competition) is when the bands work up their arrangements of a chosen piece of music for hundreds of players simultaneously. Walking into a rehearsal — the sound of 120 steel pans in unison is unlike anything else — is one of the more memorable experiences Port of Spain offers. Ask locally about which bands are rehearsing and when; most welcome respectful visitors.
