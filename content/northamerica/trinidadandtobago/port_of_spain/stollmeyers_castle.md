Stollmeyer's Castle, also known as Killarney, is one of the Magnificent Seven mansions facing Queen's Park Savannah. Built in the early 20th century with Scottish baronial inspiration, it adds a theatrical note to Port of Spain's most important historic boulevard.

The building is worth seeing as part of a Savannah walk, especially alongside the neighboring mansions. Access can vary, but the exterior alone helps explain the wealth, ambition, and imported architectural tastes of colonial-era St Clair.
