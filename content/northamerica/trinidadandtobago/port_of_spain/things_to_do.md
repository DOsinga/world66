title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Port of Spain rewards wandering. The downtown grid is compact and walkable, and the [Queen's Park Savannah](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/port_of_spain/queens_park_savannah) is the social heart of the city. The city has museums worth visiting, a historic quarter around [Woodford Square](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/port_of_spain/red_house), and a lively arts scene rooted in Carnival culture. The [National Museum and Art Gallery](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/port_of_spain/national_museum) is free and well worth an hour.
