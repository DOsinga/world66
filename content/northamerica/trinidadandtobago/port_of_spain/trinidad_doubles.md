Doubles is Trinidad's defining street food: two small pieces of fried bara (chickpea flour flatbread) filled with curried channa (chickpeas) and topped with any combination of pepper sauce, tamarind chutney, mango chutney, shadow beni sauce, and cucumber. It is eaten for breakfast, after late nights, and at any hour in between, from vendors at street corners and market stalls throughout Port of Spain.

A doubles costs between TT$5 and TT$12 depending on the extras, making it the cheapest substantial snack in the city. The best vendors are found near the market on Charlotte Street, at the Curepe Junction roundabout east of the city, and scattered along Ariapita Avenue. Each vendor has regulars who will argue for their preferred doubles man with the same conviction they bring to cricket or Carnival allegiances. The correct order — unless you genuinely want it spicy — is "slight" for the pepper: slight, medium, and heavy are the options, and Trinidadian "heavy" is not for the faint-hearted.
