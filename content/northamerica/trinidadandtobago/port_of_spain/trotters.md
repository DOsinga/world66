Trotters on Maraval Road in Woodbrook is the quintessential Port of Spain sports bar: large screens showing cricket, football, and whatever major sporting event is current, cold beer, and a crowd that ranges from after-work office drinkers to serious sports fans. It has been a fixture of the city's social life long enough that it now feels like an institution rather than a trend.

The food is pub fare — burgers, wings, fries, nachos — reliably adequate rather than distinguished. The beer is cold. The atmosphere on a West Indies match day or during the World Cup is genuinely electric. If you want to watch sport with Trinidadians rather than at your hotel, this is the place to do it.
