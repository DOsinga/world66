---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Trinidad has a tropical climate with two seasons: the dry season (roughly January to June) and the wet season (June to December). The dry season is the better time to visit for outdoor activities; the wet season brings heavy afternoon rain but remains warm and navigable.

**Carnival season** (January to February, ending with Ash Wednesday) is when Port of Spain is at its most intense — fetes every weekend, music everywhere, and an atmosphere unlike anywhere else. Carnival itself takes place on the Monday and Tuesday before Ash Wednesday, and the two weeks leading up to it are almost as much fun. Book accommodation and flights months in advance; prices are high and rooms scarce.

**Birdwatching** is excellent year-round, but the forest is particularly active in the January-May dry season. Asa Wright Nature Centre and the Northern Range are at their best when trails are dry.

**Avoiding** the peak of the wet season (August–October, when tropical disturbances can bring extended rain) is advisable if outdoor activity is the priority. The island rarely sees direct hurricane hits — it sits south of the main hurricane track — but heavy rainfall from passing storms can affect travel.
