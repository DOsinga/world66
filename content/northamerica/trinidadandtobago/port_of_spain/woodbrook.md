Woodbrook is the neighbourhood immediately west of the Queen's Park Savannah, and it is where Port of Spain's restaurant and nightlife scene lives. Ariapita Avenue is the main street — lined with bars, restaurants, rum shops, and casual eateries — and on Friday and Saturday nights it gets busy enough to feel like a proper street party, with music spilling out of bars and tables spreading onto the pavement.

The neighbourhood's tight grid of Victorian-era houses and small-scale commercial blocks has a more relaxed atmosphere than downtown, and many visitors base themselves here rather than in the more impersonal hotels near Independence Square. During the Carnival season, Woodbrook hosts fetes, J'ouvert parties, and the associated social chaos that makes Carnival both exhilarating and exhausting. Many of the mas camps — the workshops where Carnival costumes are built — operate out of Woodbrook yards.
