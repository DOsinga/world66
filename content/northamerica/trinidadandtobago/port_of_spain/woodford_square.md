Woodford Square is the civic heart of downtown Port of Spain, bordered by the Red House, the law courts, Holy Trinity Cathedral, and the national library. It is a good place to read the city at a glance: colonial institutions, political life, and everyday downtown traffic all meet around the same patch of shade.
