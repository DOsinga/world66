Pointe-à-Pierre Wild Fowl Trust protects wetland habitat for locally endangered birds in the unlikely setting of an oil refinery estate. The contrast is part of the interest: flares and industrial plant sit near ponds, trees, and wetland vegetation where wildfowl still thrive. Visits are by appointment.
