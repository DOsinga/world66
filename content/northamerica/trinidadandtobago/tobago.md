Tobago is the quieter, smaller sister island of Trinidad, about 30 km to the northeast. Where Trinidad is bustling and multicultural, Tobago is laidback and beach-focused — a proper Caribbean island with palm-fringed bays, coral reefs, and rainforest-covered hills.

The southwestern end around Crown Point has the airport, the best beaches (including [Pigeon Point](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/pigeon_point) and [Store Bay](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/store_bay)), and most of the tourist infrastructure. The northeastern end is wilder and less developed, with the [Main Ridge Forest Reserve](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/main_ridge_forest_reserve) — the oldest protected rainforest in the Western Hemisphere, established in 1776.

[Castara Bay](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/castara_bay) on the north coast is a beautiful, relatively undeveloped fishing village with good snorkelling. [Little Tobago](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/little_tobago), a small island off the east coast, is a bird sanctuary and one of the best places in the Caribbean to see seabirds up close. The diving and snorkelling around Tobago are excellent — the island sits where the Caribbean meets the Atlantic, creating nutrient-rich waters with abundant marine life. [Buccoo Reef](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/buccoo_reef) is the most accessible coral system, reachable by glass-bottom boat from Store Bay.

[Englishman's Bay](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/englishmans_bay) is widely regarded as one of the island's most beautiful beaches — a horseshoe cove backed by forest with calm, clear water. [Speyside](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/speyside) on the Atlantic coast is the centre of the island's dive scene, with manta rays, barracuda, and large schools of fish. [Arnos Vale](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/arnos_vale) on the Caribbean coast combines beach with a birding area and the ruins of an old sugar mill.
