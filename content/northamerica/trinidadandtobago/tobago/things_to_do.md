---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Tobago's activities revolve around its natural assets: beaches, coral reefs, rainforest, and birdlife. The southwestern end of the island has the most accessible sites; the northeast rewards those willing to make the drive.

The island has some of the best diving in the Eastern Caribbean. The waters off [Speyside](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/speyside) are famous for manta rays and enormous barrel sponges. [Buccoo Reef](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/buccoo_reef) near Crown Point is accessible by glass-bottom boat tour for non-divers. [Main Ridge Forest Reserve](/northamerica/trinidadandtobago/tobago/main_ridge_forest_reserve) is the oldest protected forest in the Western Hemisphere and excellent for birding.
