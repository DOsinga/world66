---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

A few low-key bars cluster along Duke Street and the waterfront. **Jack's Shack** at the cruise pier is the lively option when ships are in. **Sandbar** and the **Osprey Beach Hotel** bar pour rum punches at sunset on the west-facing beach.

Bobby Brown's Bar in Cockburn Town, and any beach bar at the cruise centre, are the right places to ask about local rake-and-scrape music nights — the Turks and Caicos folk genre played with accordion, a metal saw scraped with a screwdriver, and a goatskin drum.
