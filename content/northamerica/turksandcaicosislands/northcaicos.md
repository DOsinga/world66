North Caicos is the greenest island in the Turks and Caicos, with more rainfall than its neighbours and a lush, almost jungled interior. It is connected to [Middle Caicos](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/middlecaicos) by a causeway and reached by a short ferry from Providenciales.

The island has a handful of small resorts and guesthouses but nothing approaching the development of Provo. The beaches — particularly [Whitby Beach](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/northcaicos/whitby_beach) with Three Mary Cays just offshore for snorkelling — are excellent and often empty. Flamingos wade in the shallows at [Flamingo Pond](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/northcaicos/flamingo_pond), one of the easiest wildlife spots in the TCI. The [Wade's Green Plantation](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/northcaicos/wades_green_plantation) ruins are the best-preserved Loyalist plantation remains in the territory.

North Caicos is a good day trip from Providenciales or a quiet base for exploring the less-touristed side of the TCI. The causeway to [Middle Caicos](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/middlecaicos) opens up further exploration without backtracking to Provo.
