---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

North Caicos is reached from Providenciales either by ferry or by the small North Caicos Airport. The ferry operates from the dock at Walkin Marina on Provo; the crossing takes about 35 minutes and runs several times a day. North Caicos Airport (NCA) has occasional small-plane connections.

Once on the island, transport is limited. There are a handful of rental cars and golf carts available through local operators; contact your accommodation for current options. The island is small enough that the main sites — Wade's Green, Flamingo Pond, Whitby Beach — are all within 30 minutes of each other by road. The causeway connecting North Caicos to Middle Caicos allows onward exploration of the larger, even quieter island next door.
