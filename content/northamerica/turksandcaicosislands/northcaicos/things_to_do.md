---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

North Caicos rewards those who make the effort to get here with empty beaches, flamingos, and plantation ruins that get a fraction of the visitors that the main sights on Providenciales receive. The [Wade's Green Plantation](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/northcaicos/wades_green_plantation) ruins are the most substantial Loyalist plantation remains in the territory. [Whitby Beach](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/northcaicos/whitby_beach) and the cays around it offer excellent snorkelling. The [Flamingo Pond Overlook](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/northcaicos/flamingo_pond) is one of the easiest places in the TCI to see flamingos in the wild.
