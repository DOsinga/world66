Pine Cay is a small private island between [Providenciales](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/providenciales) and North Caicos, accessible only to guests of [The Meridian Club](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/pinecay/the_meridian_club), a low-key resort that has been operating here since the 1970s. There are no day-trippers, no day visitors, and no public ferry.

The island is covered in casuarina pines and ringed with an almost entirely deserted [beach](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/pinecay/pine_cay_beach). The surrounding waters sit on the [Caicos Barrier Reef](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/pinecay/caicos_barrier_reef) — the third-largest barrier reef in the world — and the snorkelling and diving are excellent. Flamingos visit regularly from North Caicos, feeding in the tidal ponds along the island's [nature trails](/northamerica/turksandcaicosislands/pinecay/nature_trails).

If you are not staying at the Meridian Club, Pine Cay is essentially off limits.
