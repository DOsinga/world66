The Meridian Club is the only accommodation on Pine Cay and the only way to visit the island. It has been running since the early 1970s in the style of an old Caribbean estate — no televisions, no air conditioning in the original cottages, a deliberate absence of the trappings of modern resorts. The handful of cottages and beachfront rooms are scattered through the pines close to the beach.

Rates are all-inclusive. Most guests stay for a week and come back year after year, which has kept the island almost entirely unknown to the wider travelling public. The small dining room serves good food using local seafood. There is a small pool, but most guests come for the beach.
