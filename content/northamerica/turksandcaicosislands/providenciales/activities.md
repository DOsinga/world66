title: Activities
type: section
---

Almost everything bookable in the Turks and Caicos starts on Provo. The operators cluster in three places: **Grace Bay**, for beach pickups and the dive shops on Grace Bay Road; **Turtle Cove Marina**, for fishing and parasailing; and **Leeward** at the eastern tip, for the eco tours and the boats to the outer cays. Long Bay, on the south-east shore, is the kiting beach. The diving is wall diving: Northwest Point, West Caicos and French Cay are boat trips from Provo, and there is no shore diving to speak of.
