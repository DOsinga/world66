Started in 2004 with one boat; it now runs nine. Group and private snorkel cruises, sunset cruises and fishing charters leave from Blue Haven Marina at Leeward. On Tuesdays the **North & Middle Caicos Experience** takes in the Conch Bar Caves, Mudjin Harbour and Flamingo Pond. The office is at 1 Princess Drive in The Bight.
