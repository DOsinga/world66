A sport-fishing outfit at Turtle Cove, in business for more than twenty years. Private deep-sea charters go out for wahoo, tuna and marlin. There is bottom fishing on the reef, sunset cruises, fish-and-snorkel combinations, and a shared four-hour trip on Wednesdays for those who can't fill a boat.
