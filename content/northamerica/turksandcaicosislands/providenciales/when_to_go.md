---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Turks and Caicos enjoy sunshine and warm water year-round. Peak season runs from December to April when the weather is reliably dry and the temperature on land is pleasant rather than hot. This is also the busiest and most expensive period.

Humpback whales pass through the Caicos channel between January and March — the Turks and Caicos Banks are one of the world's most important humpback breeding grounds, and whale-watching boat trips operate during these months. June through August is hot and humid but generally dry; this is when you get the best deals on accommodation.

Hurricane season runs from June to November. The islands have been hit, though not often. Travel insurance is strongly recommended for late summer bookings.
