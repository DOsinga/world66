The Harriott White House is the most imposing of Salt Cay's remaining colonial buildings — a grand Bermudian-style stone house with a distinctive coral stone exterior and large shuttered windows designed to catch the trade winds. It stands in Balfour Town near Deane's Dock and has become one of the island's most photographed landmarks.

The building is not currently a museum or open for interior visits, but the exterior and the surrounding neighbourhood of traditional Bermudian-style stone buildings give Balfour Town a character entirely unlike the resort architecture of Providenciales. Walking through the settlement — it takes about twenty minutes to cover the whole thing — gives the best picture of what Salt Cay was like at the height of the salt trade.
