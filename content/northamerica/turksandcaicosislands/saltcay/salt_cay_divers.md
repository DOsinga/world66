The island's dive shop since 1996, and the operator for Salt Cay's humpbacks. From January to March, two-and-a-half-hour boat trips go out into the passage where the whales migrate, and snorkellers can get in the water with them when conditions allow. The rest of the year it dives sixteen sites from two boats.

The pin is on Balfour Town; the dock is a short walk away.
