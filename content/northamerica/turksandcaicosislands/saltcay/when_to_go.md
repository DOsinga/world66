---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The peak season for Salt Cay is January through April, when humpback whales migrate through the Columbus Passage and the weather is at its most reliably pleasant. This is also high season for the Turks and Caicos generally, so book accommodation well in advance — options on the island are limited even in the off-season.

May through December is hotter and more humid, with a hurricane season running from June to November. Outside of that risk, the island is pleasant year-round for diving and beach time. The island is rarely crowded by Caribbean standards at any time of year.
