East Bay is one of South Caicos' broad, photogenic beaches: pale sand, shallow turquoise water, and very little development compared with the busier islands in the Turks and Caicos. It is the sort of beach where the space is the main luxury.

Bring what you need, because facilities are limited and shade can be scarce. The reward is a quiet swim and a better feel for why South Caicos appeals to travellers who want the islands without the resort bustle.
