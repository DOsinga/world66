---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating on South Caicos means **conch** and **lobster**, more or less in any direction. The catch is unloaded a few hundred metres from the kitchens that cook it, so freshness is not an issue.

The handful of small restaurants in **Cockburn Harbour** open and close with the seasons; the most reliable are usually the bar at the **Sailrock Resort** at the south end of the island and **Eastern Inn** on Front Street. For something cheap and unfussy, the **fish fry** held in the harbour on certain summer Friday evenings is a community event with whole fried snapper, conch fritters, peas and rice, and a few cold beers.

There are no full grocery stores; small island shops carry the basics and a weekly barge from Providenciales brings most of the fresh produce.
