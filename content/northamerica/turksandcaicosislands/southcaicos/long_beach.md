Long Beach is one of South Caicos' simple pleasures: pale sand, clear shallows, and the low-key emptiness that makes the island feel far removed from the busier resort Turks and Caicos. It is the kind of beach where the absence of infrastructure is part of the draw.

Bring what you need and do not expect a serviced strand. The reward is space, water, and a slow island pace, especially if you have come to South Caicos for diving, bonefishing, or a quieter version of the archipelago.
