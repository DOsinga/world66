---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Turks and Caicos sit in the trade wind belt and South Caicos is one of the breeziest islands in the chain. The dry, sunny months from **December to April** are the peak: water temperatures around 26°C, daytime air in the high 20s, and reliable sun. From late January to early April **humpback whales** pass through the Turks Island Passage just off the island's south end and can be heard underwater on dives.

**May to November** is the official Caribbean hurricane season; storms occasionally pass to the south of the islands, and afternoon showers are more frequent. The water is at its warmest in August and September. The off-season is cheaper, quieter, and good for divers willing to keep an eye on the tropical forecast.
