Alabama Splash Adventure is a combined amusement park and water park in Bessemer, on the western edge of the Birmingham area. Its main draws are summer water rides, family attractions, and the wooden Rampage roller coaster.

The park is seasonal and very weather-dependent, so check opening dates and ride status before making the trip. It is most useful for families looking for a full afternoon outside the city centre.
