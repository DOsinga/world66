The Alabama Sports Hall of Fame tells the state's sporting story through trophies, uniforms, photographs, and memorabilia connected with Alabama athletes and coaches. The collection is large, with thousands of objects across college football, baseball, boxing, motorsport, and Olympic sports.

It is a good stop for travellers who already understand how much sport shapes Alabama's public life. If you are only casually interested, pair it with nearby downtown museums rather than making it the centre of the day.
