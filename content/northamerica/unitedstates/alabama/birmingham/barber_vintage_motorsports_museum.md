The Barber Vintage Motorsports Museum, about 20 miles east of downtown Birmingham, is one of the great specialist museums in the United States — worth the detour even if you have only a passing interest in motorcycles. The collection is extraordinary in its breadth, its condition, and the quality of its display.

The museum sits adjacent to a road racing circuit that hosts major motorcycle racing events, including MotoAmerica rounds and the Honda Indy Grand Prix of Alabama. If a race weekend coincides with your visit, the combination of museum access and live racing is hard to beat. The museum also holds a substantial collection of racing cars, including several Formula One machines. Allow three to four hours.
