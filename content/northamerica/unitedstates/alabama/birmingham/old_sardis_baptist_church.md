Old Sardis Baptist Church is a historic African-American church with deep roots in Birmingham's civil rights movement. Under Rev. Robert Alford, the church became active in voter registration, economic organizing, and community leadership years before the better-known demonstrations of the 1960s.

It is most meaningful as part of a wider civil rights route through Birmingham.
