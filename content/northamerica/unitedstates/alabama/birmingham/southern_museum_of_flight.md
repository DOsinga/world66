The Southern Museum of Flight is Birmingham's main aviation museum, with aircraft, engines, models, military displays, and exhibits connected to Alabama flight history. It is a more specialised stop than the civil-rights district or the art museum, but it has enough substance for aviation-minded travellers.

The museum is away from the central sightseeing cluster, so it needs a deliberate trip. It works best for families, military-history readers, or anyone interested in the industrial and technical side of Birmingham's story.
