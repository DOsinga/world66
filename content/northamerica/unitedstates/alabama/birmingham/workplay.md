WorkPlay is one of Birmingham's better small venues, with concerts, comedy, and theatre in a Southside complex that also includes production spaces. It is not a sightseeing stop in daylight, but it can be a strong evening choice if the calendar lines up.

Check the schedule rather than wandering over cold. The venue works best for travellers who want to hear local or touring acts somewhere smaller than an arena and more purposeful than a bar stage.
