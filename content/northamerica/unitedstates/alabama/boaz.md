Boaz is a small town in the foothills of northeastern Alabama, best known as a factory outlet shopping destination that draws bargain hunters from across the region. With hundreds of outlet stores clustered along the main highway, Boaz bills itself as the "Outlet Capital of Alabama" — and for shoppers who make the trip, the prices often justify the claim.

The town sits near Lake Guntersville and Guntersville State Park, which offer fishing, hiking, and camping in a scenic stretch of the Tennessee River valley. If you are visiting the lake, the outlet stores make a convenient stop on the way. The historic core around [Julia Street Memorial United Methodist Church](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/boaz/julia_street_methodist_church) and [Snead State Community College](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/boaz/snead_state_community_college) gives a sense of the older, pre-outlet town beneath the commercial development.
