The Edward Fenns Whitman House is a historic residence listed on the National Register of Historic Places, one of the better-preserved examples of late 19th-century domestic architecture in Boaz. The house reflects the prosperity that came to the town after the arrival of the railroad in the 1880s, which transformed Boaz from a small agricultural settlement into a regional commercial centre.

The exterior can be seen from the street, though the interior is not open to the public. It is worth a look if you are walking the older part of town.
