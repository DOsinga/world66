The Julia Street Memorial United Methodist Church at 302 Thomas Avenue is a historic congregation and building listed on the National Register of Historic Places. The church dates from the early 20th century and is one of the landmarks of downtown Boaz's older residential neighbourhood.

The building is an active church rather than a museum, so visits are primarily a matter of viewing the exterior architecture, though Sunday services are open to the public in the usual Methodist tradition.
