---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Boaz is Alabama's self-styled outlet capital, and the outlet shopping is genuinely the main reason most people come. Hundreds of brand-name stores are clustered along Highway 431, selling clothing, footwear, housewares, and sporting goods at discounted prices. The main shopping strips are walkable and well-signposted.

The deals are best on weekdays when crowds are thinner. On weekends in good weather the parking lots fill up and the town takes on a carnival atmosphere.
