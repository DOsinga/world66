Snead State Community College is a public two-year college that has been part of Boaz since 1898, making it one of the oldest community colleges in Alabama. The campus is compact and sits just off the main commercial strip. It is not a major visitor destination, but it gives the town an educational anchor and contributes to the local economy.

The college offers associate degrees and transfer programmes, and its small size means it has a notably community-oriented character compared to the large state universities further south.
