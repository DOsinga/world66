Fort Gaines is a well-preserved pentagonal brick fort on the eastern tip of Dauphin Island, built in the 1820s and expanded through the Civil War. It guarded the main ship channel into Mobile Bay alongside Fort Morgan across the water. The two forts became famous in August 1864 when Union Admiral Farragut's fleet forced its way into the bay in one of the war's more dramatic naval engagements.

The fort is open to the public and contains the original cannon, a historic anchor from the CSS Hartford, Farragut's flagship, and exhibits on the Civil War naval campaign. The ramparts give good views over the ship channel where the battle was fought. A ferry runs from Dauphin Island to Fort Morgan on the far shore, where another preserved Civil War fortification can be visited — doing both in a day by ferry is a satisfying circuit. Allow a couple of hours for Fort Gaines itself.
