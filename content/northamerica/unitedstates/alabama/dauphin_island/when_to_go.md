---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Dauphin Island depends entirely on why you are coming.

**Spring (late March to May)** is peak birding season. The spring migration, when millions of songbirds cross the Gulf of Mexico from their wintering grounds in Central America and arrive exhausted on Dauphin Island's shores, makes this one of the great birdwatching events in North America. Late April is the peak, with late March and early May also excellent.

**Summer (June to August)** is beach season. The Gulf water is warm, the weather is hot and humid, and the island's campgrounds and rentals fill with families. Hurricane season runs June through November — the island is low-lying and vulnerable. Check forecasts if visiting in August or September.

**Autumn (September to November)** brings the fall migration (less dramatic than spring but still interesting for birders) and more comfortable temperatures. September is still hurricane season.

**Winter (December to March)** is quiet and uncrowded. Winter birding for shorebirds, waterfowl, and wintering songbirds can be excellent. Temperatures are mild by mainland standards (average January high around 60°F/16°C), though cool enough that beach swimming is not appealing.
