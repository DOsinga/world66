*North America > United States > Alabama > Dothan*

Dothan is the main city of Alabama's Wiregrass, a southeast corner of the state tied to peanuts, farms, highways, and the Florida Panhandle route. It is not a beautiful city in the usual travel-guide sense, and much of it feels like a regional service hub, but it has enough local identity to reward a spare day if you are already nearby.

The peanut theme is real rather than just a slogan. Dothan calls itself the Peanut Capital of the World, hosts the [National Peanut Festival](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/dothan/national_peanut_festival), and scatters painted peanut sculptures around town. More importantly, [Landmark Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/dothan/landmark_park) gives that agricultural story substance with a living-history farm, boardwalk, and museum.

Downtown adds a different layer: the [Wiregrass Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/dothan/wiregrass_museum_of_art), historic [Dothan Opera House](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/dothan/dothan_opera_house), murals, and the tiny oddity of the [World's Smallest City Block](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/dothan/worlds_smallest_city_block). Dothan just clears the World66 bar because the package is specific. Come for the Wiregrass story, not for generic city sightseeing.
