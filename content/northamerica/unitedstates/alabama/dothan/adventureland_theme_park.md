Adventureland Theme Park is a small, family-oriented amusement park on the south side of Dothan, useful when children need something more active than another museum stop. The setup is modest but varied: two miniature-golf courses with waterfalls and lagoons, go-karts, bumper boats, batting cages, an arcade, and a snack bar.

It is not a reason to come to Dothan, but it fills an afternoon well if you are already in town with kids.
