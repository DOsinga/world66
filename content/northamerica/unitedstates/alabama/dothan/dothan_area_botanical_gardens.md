Dothan Area Botanical Gardens is a calm outdoor stop, with cultivated gardens, wooded paths, camellias, roses, herbs, and seasonal planting that suits the humid Wiregrass climate. It is not a destination garden on the scale of a major city arboretum, but it is a pleasant way to slow down between Dothan's museums and roadside errands.

Go in the cooler part of the day. In summer, the shade matters.
