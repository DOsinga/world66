Landmark Park is Dothan's most useful sight because it explains the Wiregrass better than a slogan can. The park preserves an old farmstead, agricultural buildings, animals, tools, and exhibits, with nature trails and a boardwalk adding a bit of fresh air.

It is especially good with children, but adults interested in regional history will get something from it too. If you only do one thing in Dothan, make it this.
