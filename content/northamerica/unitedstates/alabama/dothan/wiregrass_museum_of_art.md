The Wiregrass Museum of Art gives downtown Dothan a real cultural anchor. It occupies the former municipal light and water plant, an industrial building from the city's early-20th-century growth, and runs changing exhibitions alongside its permanent collection.

It is a small museum, but the building and the regional ambition make it worth folding into a downtown walk.
