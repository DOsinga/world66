The World's Smallest City Block is a tiny triangular island at North Appletree, Museum, and Troy streets. It has a marker, signs, and just enough absurdity to be worth a glance while you are already downtown.

Do not cross town for it alone. As part of a mural-and-museum walk, it adds a little Dothan weirdness.
