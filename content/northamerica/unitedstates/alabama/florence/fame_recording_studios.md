FAME Recording Studios is the other essential stop on a Shoals music pilgrimage, and it is still a working studio rather than a preserved shell. Rick Hall founded FAME in Florence in 1959 and moved it to East Avalon Avenue in Muscle Shoals in the early 1960s, where the room became one of the engines behind the Muscle Shoals sound.

The studio's history runs through soul, country, and rock: Arthur Alexander, Aretha Franklin, Wilson Pickett, Etta James, the Osmonds, and plenty of later artists all passed through its rooms. Pair it with Muscle Shoals Sound Studio across the river in Sheffield; together they explain why this quiet corner of northwest Alabama became a place musicians still talk about with reverence.
