Wilson Dam is one of the Tennessee Valley Authority's big engineering landmarks on the Tennessee River, just outside Florence. Its lock has a lift of about 100 feet, making it the highest single-lift lock east of the Rockies, and the broad concrete dam gives a good sense of the scale of the TVA project in the Shoals.
