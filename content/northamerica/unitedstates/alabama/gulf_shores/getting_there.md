---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Most visitors arrive by car. Highway 59 runs south from I-10 and Foley straight to the public beach, while Highway 180 follows the Fort Morgan peninsula west of town.

The nearest larger airports are Pensacola and Mobile. Both are practical if you are renting a car, which is also the easiest way to reach Bon Secour, Fort Morgan, and the quieter beach access points.
