---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Downtown Huntsville has a compact and walkable bar district centered on the historic courthouse square and Clinton Avenue. Several craft breweries operate in converted industrial spaces, and the nightlife clusters tightly enough that you can walk from one to another without a car. The scene skews toward a professional crowd — engineers and researchers from Cummings Research Park and the Redstone Arsenal give the city a younger demographic than much of north Alabama.
