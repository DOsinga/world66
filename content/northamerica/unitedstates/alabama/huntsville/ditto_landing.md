Ditto Landing is Huntsville's main doorway to the Tennessee River, set south of the city where the water opens into a broad bend. The marina, boat launches, campground, and riverside paths make it more than a place to park a boat.

For travellers, it is best as a low-key outdoor break from museums, rockets, and downtown Huntsville. Come for a walk, a sunset over the river, or as a starting point if you have arranged time on the water.
