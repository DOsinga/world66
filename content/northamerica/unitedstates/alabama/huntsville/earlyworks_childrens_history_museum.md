EarlyWorks Children's History Museum is Huntsville's hands-on history museum for children, with exhibits built around play rather than glass cases. The big keelboat, oversized instruments, and storytelling displays make it an easy family stop downtown.

It is best for younger travellers and for parents who want a break from purely space-focused sightseeing. Adults without children will usually get more from Huntsville's other museums.
