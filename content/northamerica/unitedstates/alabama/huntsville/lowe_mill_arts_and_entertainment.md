Lowe Mill ARTS & Entertainment is a converted 1900s cotton mill in South Huntsville that has become the city's most dynamic cultural venue. The five-story building holds more than 150 working artists' studios, galleries, performance spaces, a cinema, restaurants, and craft producers. On weekends the studios are generally open for visitors to walk through, watch artists at work, and buy directly from the maker. The density of creative activity under one roof is genuinely unusual.

The complex anchors what has become a broader arts district in the Lowe Mill corridor — several other galleries and creative businesses have opened nearby, and the area sees steady foot traffic on weekend afternoons. Concerts and events run most weekends in the first-floor performance area; check the website for the current calendar. The food and drink options inside the building are reasonable for lunch or a snack. It is located about two miles south of downtown, easily reached by car or a short Uber ride.
