The North Alabama Railroad Museum preserves a small but engaging collection of locomotives, passenger cars, freight cars, and railroad equipment on the eastern side of Huntsville. It is not a polished big-city transport museum; its charm is in the working-yard atmosphere and the volunteer energy behind the place. For rail enthusiasts, that is exactly the point.

The museum is most rewarding when one of its excursion trains is running, especially for families or anyone who wants more than a static display. If your timing does not line up with a ride, it still makes a worthwhile short stop as part of a broader Huntsville itinerary, particularly after the city's better-known space and history museums.
