Bragg-Mitchell Mansion is a Greek Revival house museum west of downtown Mobile, built in the mid-19th century and set behind a line of oaks. Its columns, broad rooms, and period furnishings show the scale of the city's antebellum elite architecture.

The house is most useful with a guided tour, where the building's beauty can be set against the social and labor history that made such houses possible. It pairs well with Mobile's downtown historic districts for travelers interested in Gulf Coast architecture.
