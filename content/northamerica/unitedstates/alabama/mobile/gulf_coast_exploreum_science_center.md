The Gulf Coast Exploreum Science Center is Mobile's hands-on science museum, with interactive exhibits aimed at children and families. Its downtown location makes it easy to combine with Fort Conde, the Carnival Museum, and the historic streets around Government Plaza.

It is a useful stop on hot or rainy days, especially if you are traveling with children who need something more active than another old building.
