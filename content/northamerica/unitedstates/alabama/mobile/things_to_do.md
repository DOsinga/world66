---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mobile rewards slow exploration of its historic downtown. The Cathedral District around Bienville Square has the best concentration of antebellum architecture, and the side streets off Government Street hold some of the finest Greek Revival buildings in the South. For something harder to find elsewhere, the Mobile Carnival Museum explains why this city — not New Orleans — is the true home of American Mardi Gras.
