---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Mobile has a subtropical climate — hot and humid in summer, mild in winter. The best time to visit is from February through April, when temperatures are comfortable and the city is at its most festive. **Mardi Gras season** (January through Fat Tuesday) is the peak event and fills hotels fast; book well ahead if you want to see the parades.

Summer (June-August) is hot and oppressively humid, and the city occasionally takes direct hits from Gulf hurricanes. Winter is mild by most standards — temperatures rarely drop below freezing — but December and January can be grey and rainy. Spring and autumn offer the most pleasant weather.
