A small craft cocktail bar a block off the river in a converted historic building, Aviator does pre-Prohibition classics done properly — a real Old Fashioned, a proper Sazerac — and a short, rotating list of original drinks. Low light, dark wood, good bartenders.

A reliable post-dinner stop from any of the downtown restaurants. Quiet enough for conversation on weekday evenings.
