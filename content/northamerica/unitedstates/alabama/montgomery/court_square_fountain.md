The cast-iron fountain at the foot of Dexter Avenue is the natural orienting landmark of downtown Montgomery. Erected in 1885 on a centuries-old artesian well, it's topped by a bronze figure of Hebe, cup-bearer to the Greek gods.

This is the spot Rosa Parks boarded the bus on 1 December 1955. From here, Dexter Avenue runs straight uphill to the State Capitol — the same route the Selma marchers walked to in March 1965. Pause and orient yourself before tackling the museums.
