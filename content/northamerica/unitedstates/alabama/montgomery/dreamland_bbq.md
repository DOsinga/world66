The Montgomery outpost of the Tuscaloosa-born Dreamland chain — ribs cooked over hickory, white bread, sauce, banana pudding, no apologies and no fuss. The menu is short by design.

For a more local pit-barbecue alternative, ask anyone in town about Brenda's BBQ Pit or Martin's Restaurant — both are institutions, though Dreamland is the easiest walk from the downtown sights.
