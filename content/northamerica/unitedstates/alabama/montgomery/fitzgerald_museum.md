---

A small museum in the modest brown-shingled Felder Avenue house the Fitzgeralds rented in 1931 — the longest stretch they ever stayed in one place — set up in two ground-floor rooms with letters, manuscripts, Zelda's paintings, and rotating exhibits. The upstairs is now an artist's residency.

It's eccentric, low-budget, and a real find for Jazz Age readers; allow forty-five minutes to an hour. Donations encouraged. The Garden District neighbourhood it sits in is one of the prettier residential walks in town.
