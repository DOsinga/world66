Two blocks off Court Square, this small downtown museum houses one of the largest collections of Hank Williams memorabilia in the country: stage suits by Nudie Cohn, handwritten lyrics, the boots, the hats, the guitars — and the death car itself, kept exactly as it was the morning it pulled over in West Virginia.

The displays are old-school glass-case-and-typed-card, but the artefacts carry the visit. Allow an hour. Williams himself is buried at Oakwood Cemetery Annex, a short drive north, alongside his second wife Billie Jean.
