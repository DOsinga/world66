Montgomery Zoo is a straightforward family stop on the north side of the city, with shaded paths, animal habitats, and enough room to break up a history-heavy Montgomery itinerary. It is paired with the Mann Wildlife Learning Museum, which adds natural-history displays and taxidermy to the visit.

This is not the city's essential sight, but it earns its place for travellers with children or anyone needing a slower outdoor half-day. Summer heat matters here, so morning is the sensible time to go.
