The Town of Spectre is the preserved movie-set village from Tim Burton's *Big Fish*, on Jackson Lake Island north of Montgomery. The rustic false-front buildings remain scattered beside the water, making it an odd but memorable stop for film fans and families looking for something different from the city's museums and civil-rights sites.

Access is through the island property, which may charge a small day-use or camping fee.
