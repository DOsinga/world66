The Coastal Arts Center of Orange Beach sits on Wolf Bay, away from the louder beach-front strip. The campus includes a large gallery of Gulf Coast artists, a hot glass studio, a clay studio, and oak-shaded grounds that make the stop feel more local than commercial.

Drop in for the gallery if you only have half an hour. If you want the hands-on side, check class times ahead, since glass and clay sessions usually need booking.
