Phenix City sits on the Chattahoochee River in eastern [Alabama](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama), directly across the water from Columbus, Georgia. For most of the 20th century it had a notorious reputation as "Sin City" — a wide-open town of gambling, prostitution, and political corruption that culminated in the [assassination of a newly elected attorney general in 1954](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/phenix_city/patterson_office). The cleanup that followed transformed it into the quiet, suburban city it is today, though the history still makes for fascinating reading.

Modern Phenix City is primarily a gateway to the Columbus metropolitan area. Cross one of the bridges — the historic [Dillingham Street Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/phenix_city/dillingham_street_bridge) is now a pedestrian crossing — and you have easy access to Fort Moore (formerly Fort Benning), the National Infantry Museum, and the [Chattahoochee RiverWalk](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/phenix_city/riverwalk), a pleasant 22-mile paved path along the river. The [whitewater course](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/phenix_city/chattahoochee_whitewater) on the Chattahoochee, one of the longest urban whitewater runs in the world, is a genuine surprise — you can raft Class III and IV rapids with the Columbus skyline in view.

Callaway Gardens, about an hour's drive east into Georgia, is a popular family destination with gardens, lakes, a butterfly conservatory, and a beach. Closer to home, [Idle Hour Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/phenix_city/idle_hour_park) on the riverbank is a nice spot for a picnic and the [Brownville-Summerville Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/phenix_city/brownville_summerville_historic_district) preserves the late-19th-century neighbourhoods north of downtown. Phenix City has a growing food scene with several good barbecue joints and Southern restaurants, though for a wider selection of dining and nightlife you will want to cross into Columbus.
