---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Phenix City sits on the Alabama side of the Chattahoochee River, directly opposite Columbus, Georgia. The nearest commercial airport is **Columbus Airport (CSG)** with limited service, mostly Delta connections to Atlanta. **Atlanta Hartsfield (ATL)** is two hours north and is the practical choice for international or low-cost connections.

**By car** — Phenix City is on US-280 and US-431. From Atlanta, take I-85 south to LaGrange, then US-27 south (about 1 hour 45 minutes). From Montgomery, take I-85 east then US-80 east (about 1 hour 30 minutes).

**By bus** — Greyhound stops in Columbus across the river.

**Crossing the river** — Several bridges link the two cities: the modern J.R. Allen Parkway (US-80) for traffic, and the historic Dillingham Street and 14th Street bridges for pedestrians and cyclists.
