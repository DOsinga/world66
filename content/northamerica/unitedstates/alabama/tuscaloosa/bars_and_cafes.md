---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The Strip (University Boulevard) is the main student bar corridor, running west from campus toward downtown. It is reliably busy on football weekends and during the academic year, with a mix of college sports bars, live music venues, and casual restaurants.

**Innisfree Irish Pub** on the Strip has been a Tuscaloosa institution for decades — live music most nights, a reasonable selection of draft beer, and the kind of reliable atmosphere that makes it worth returning to. **The Houndstooth** is the choice for Alabama football watching, packed beyond reason on game days. For something quieter, the downtown area around Greensboro Avenue has cafes and bars that attract a slightly older crowd.

**Black Warrior Brewing** produces craft beer in the downtown area and has a taproom open most evenings. It is worth a visit if you have any interest in American craft brewing; the beers skew toward the hoppy and the amber.
