The Colbert County Courthouse Square Historic District forms the heart of Tuscumbia's small but well-preserved Victorian downtown. The courthouse itself is a classic Southern county courthouse in the Beaux-Arts style, surrounded by commercial buildings from the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, many of which house independent shops, restaurants, and professional offices.

The square retains much of its original character and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Tuscumbia's historic district is more compact and less touristy than some better-known Alabama downtowns, which gives it an authenticity that larger cities often lose. A leisurely walk around the square takes no more than thirty minutes.
