The Rattlesnake Saloon is a bar and restaurant built into a giant natural limestone cave on a hill outside Tuscumbia, about 15 kilometres north of the city. The cave creates a dramatic and unusual setting for a drinking establishment — the bar sits under an overhanging limestone ceiling with the rock walls on three sides, open to the ravine below. A wooden boardwalk leads up through the woods to the entrance.

The food is straightforward Southern bar food — burgers, wings, nachos — but the setting is what people come for. It's packed on weekends with locals from the Shoals area and visitors who stumbled across it. Live music on Friday and Saturday evenings adds to the atmosphere. Closed Monday and Tuesday.
