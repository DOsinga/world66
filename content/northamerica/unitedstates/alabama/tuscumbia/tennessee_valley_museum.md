The Tennessee Valley Museum of Art is the principal fine arts institution in the Shoals area, housed in a distinctive building in downtown Tuscumbia. The museum's permanent collection focuses on works by artists with connections to Alabama and the Tennessee Valley region, including nineteenth-century landscapes, folk art, and contemporary work.

The museum also organises temporary exhibitions drawing from regional and national collections. For a small city, it offers a respectable range of visual art and is worth a visit for those with an interest in Southern American art. Entry is free for members and modest for non-members.
