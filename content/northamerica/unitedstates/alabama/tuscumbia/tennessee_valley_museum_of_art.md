The Tennessee Valley Museum of Art gives Tuscumbia a cultural stop that is not tied only to music history or Helen Keller. Its exhibitions change through the year, mixing regional artists, touring shows, and work connected to the wider Tennessee Valley.

It is best treated as a compact, weatherproof addition to a Shoals itinerary. Check the current exhibition before going; when the programming lines up with your interests, the museum adds a quieter and more local counterpoint to the area's better-known heritage sites.
