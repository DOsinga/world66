title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tuscumbia's main draws are its Helen Keller connection and the broader Shoals music heritage shared with neighbouring Florence, Sheffield, and Muscle Shoals. The historic downtown is walkable, and the combination of Ivy Green, the Alabama Music Hall of Fame, and a day trip to FAME Studios and the Muscle Shoals Sound Studio makes for a rich cultural itinerary.
