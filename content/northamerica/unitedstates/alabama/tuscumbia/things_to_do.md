---

Tuscumbia's two main attractions — [Ivy Green](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/tuscumbia/ivy_green) and the [Alabama Music Hall of Fame](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/tuscumbia/alabama_music_hall_of_fame) — are both on or near US Highway 72 and can be covered in a half day. The Shoals area as a whole, including nearby [Florence](/northamerica/unitedstates/alabama/florence), rewards longer exploration for music history enthusiasts: FAME Studios and Muscle Shoals Sound Studio in nearby Muscle Shoals are pilgrimage sites for fans of soul and rock recordings.
