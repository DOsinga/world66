Alaska Aviation Museum is one of Anchorage’s most fitting museums, because aviation is not a side story in Alaska; it is how many communities connect to the rest of the state. The museum’s aircraft, engines, photographs, and restoration work sit beside Lake Hood, itself a major seaplane base.

The setting makes the visit better than a standard hangar museum. After looking at the collection, watch floatplanes moving on the lake and the whole Alaskan logic of distance, weather, and small aircraft becomes much easier to grasp.
