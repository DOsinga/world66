The Alaska Center for the Performing Arts anchors downtown Anchorage's civic core with three theatres under one roof. Opened in 1988, it hosts concerts, touring shows, theatre, dance, and local performances through the year.

It is most useful to visitors as an evening option after a day outside the city. Check the calendar before you go; the building is central, easy to reach, and close to downtown restaurants and bars.
