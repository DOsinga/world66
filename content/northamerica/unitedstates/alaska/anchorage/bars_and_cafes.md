---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Anchorage has a lively bar scene concentrated along Spenard Road and in the downtown core. [Chilkoot Charlie's](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/anchorage/chilkoot_charlies) on Spenard Road has been the city's most famous venue for decades — sprawling, loud, and distinctly Alaskan. [Humpy's](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/anchorage/humpys) on 6th Avenue draws a college crowd with a huge craft beer selection and live acoustic music. [Bear Tooth Theatrepub](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/anchorage/bear_tooth) on 27th is a good option for microbrews and wood-fired pizza alongside a second-run film. [49th State Brewing](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/anchorage/49th_state_brewing) has the best downtown rooftop, with views of Cook Inlet and twenty taps of house beer.
