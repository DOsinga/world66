The FOAST Law Enforcement Museum is a small downtown Anchorage museum focused on law enforcement memorabilia. Its collection is strongest on Alaska, but it also includes material from outside the state.

It is a niche stop, best for visitors with an interest in police history or travelers looking for a short indoor break near the centre of town.
