---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Anchorage works best as a base — the city has a few specific draws but most of what makes Alaska worth visiting lies just outside. Spend half a day on the downtown waterfront and museums, then use the rest of your time on day trips or to gear up for a longer trip into the interior.

The [Anchorage Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/anchorage/anchorage_museum) is the best single attraction in the city and the right introduction to the state. The [Tony Knowles Coastal Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/anchorage/tony_knowles_coastal_trail) is the best free thing to do — 11 paved miles along Cook Inlet with regular moose sightings and clear-day views of Denali. The [Alaska Native Heritage Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/anchorage/alaska_native_heritage_center) covers the Native cultures of the state at a depth no other museum attempts. For families, the [Alaska Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/anchorage/alaska_zoo) houses bears, lynx, and the only polar bears in the state on public display.
