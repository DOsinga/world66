Arctic Pizza is the local standby for an ordinary American meal in an extraordinary location: a working pizza joint a hundred metres from the frozen Arctic Ocean. The pizza is solid, the menu includes burgers, pasta, and fried fish for those who tire of it, and there is a view through the windows over the sea ice.

Prices reflect the cost of flying every ingredient four hundred kilometres from Anchorage — expect to pay double what you would in the Lower 48. No alcohol.
