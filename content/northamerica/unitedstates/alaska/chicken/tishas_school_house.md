The old schoolhouse is tied to Anne Hobbs Purdy, whose memoir *Tisha* turned her 1920s teaching years in remote Alaska into a much-loved frontier story. Summer tours usually pair the schoolhouse with the rest of the tiny settlement, giving Chicken a human story beyond fuel, signs, and souvenirs.
