Polychrome Pass is one of the classic viewpoints along the Denali Park Road, named for the multi-colored volcanic bluffs above the route. On clear days the overlook gives broad views across open tundra toward the Alaska Range.

Access depends on the park road and shuttle system, so check current road conditions before planning a visit. When open, it is a strong stop for scenery, geology, and wildlife watching.
