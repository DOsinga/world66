Savage River is a key stopping point inside Denali National Park, near the end of the road section open to most private vehicles. Trails leave from both sides of the parking area and follow the river through open tundra scenery.

Most visitors come for a short walk and the chance to look for Dall sheep on the slopes above the valley. It is also a useful place to feel the scale of Denali before continuing deeper into the park by shuttle or tour bus.
