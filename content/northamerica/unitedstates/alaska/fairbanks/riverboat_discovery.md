Riverboat Discovery is a polished Fairbanks excursion, but it earns its place because the Chena River is one of the easiest ways to understand the city's geography. The sternwheeler route combines riverbanks, floatplanes, dog-mushing demonstrations, and stops focused on Alaska Native culture and Interior Alaska life.

It is firmly on the organised-tour side of travel, so independent wanderers may find the pacing managed. Still, the trip covers a lot of local context in a few hours and works well for first-time visitors who want an overview before heading deeper into Fairbanks or the wider Interior.
