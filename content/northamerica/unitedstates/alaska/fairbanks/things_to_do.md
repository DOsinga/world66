---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Fairbanks rewards the curious. The city's best attractions lean into what makes the Interior unique: Arctic science, gold rush history, indigenous culture, and the extremes of subarctic life. The University of Alaska campus alone could fill a day between the Museum of the North and the Georgeson Botanical Garden, while downtown's Golden Heart Plaza and Pioneer Park anchor a walkable stretch along the Chena River.

Beyond the city, Fairbanks is a launching point for genuinely unusual experiences. The Permafrost Tunnel lets you walk through 40,000-year-old frozen ground. The Chatanika Gold Dredge sits rusting in the hills where industrial-scale mining reshaped the landscape. And in March, the World Ice Art Championships draw sculptors from around the world to carve massive blocks of Chena River ice into temporary masterpieces.
