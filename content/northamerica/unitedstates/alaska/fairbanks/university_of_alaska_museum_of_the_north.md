The Museum of the North is the best museum in Interior Alaska, and its building alone is worth the trip. Designed to evoke the sweeping lines of the Alaska Range and the aurora borealis, the white-and-glass structure sits on a ridge above the University of Alaska Fairbanks campus with views across the Tanana Valley.

Inside, the galleries cover Arctic natural history, Alaska Native cultures, and fine art in a single, well-curated flow. The Rose Berry Alaska Art Gallery has an outstanding collection of work by Alaska Native and contemporary Alaskan artists. The Gallery of the North devotes an entire room to the sounds of Alaska — bird calls, whale songs, ice cracking — that you can listen to through headphones.

The natural history collection includes dinosaur fossils from the North Slope, a complete bowhead whale skull, and extensive displays on permafrost, Arctic ecology, and climate change. Allow at least two hours.
