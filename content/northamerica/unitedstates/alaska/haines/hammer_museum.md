The Hammer Museum is exactly what it sounds like: a small museum devoted to hammers, from ancient tools to specialised trade implements. It would be a novelty anywhere, but in Haines it fits the town's independent streak.

Do not expect a major museum. Expect a strangely memorable half hour and a collection that takes an everyday object far more seriously than you thought possible.
