Homer Farmers Market is a summer market with local produce, prepared food, crafts, and occasional music. Saturday markets can include cooking demonstrations and a fuller mix of vendors.

It is worth checking when you are already in Homer, especially if you are stocking up for a cabin stay, picnic, or drive around Kachemak Bay.
