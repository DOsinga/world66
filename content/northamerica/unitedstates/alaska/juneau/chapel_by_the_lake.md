Chapel By The Lake is a quiet Presbyterian church set near Auke Lake, with forested slopes and Juneau's wet mountain light around it. Travellers usually come for the setting rather than formal sightseeing: the chapel sits in one of the more peaceful corners of the road system beyond downtown.

It is best treated as a small pause while visiting the Mendenhall Valley or the university area. Be respectful of services and community events; outside those times, the lake-and-mountain backdrop is the main reason to stop.
