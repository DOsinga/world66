The Last Chance Mining Museum preserves the remains of the gold mining operation that built modern Juneau. The star exhibit is the massive 1912 compressed-air plant — a building full of enormous machinery that once powered the drills working inside the mountain. Also on display are ore carts, mining tools, assay equipment, and photographic records of the operation at its height. The museum sits at the end of Basin Road, about a mile from downtown, at the base of the mountains that hide the old mine tunnels.

The walk along Basin Road from downtown is pleasant in itself, following Gold Creek through stands of Sitka spruce. The museum grounds include a picnic area. Entry is by donation; hours are limited, so call ahead or check current listings before making the trip.
