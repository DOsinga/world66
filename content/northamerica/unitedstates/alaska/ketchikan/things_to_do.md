---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Ketchikan's appeal is layered: indigenous culture and totem poles, rain-soaked temperate rainforest, salmon fishing, and the quirky historic grid of a frontier fishing town. The totem pole collections alone justify a stop — no museum diorama prepares you for the scale and presence of the real thing standing in the open air. Beyond the poles, the Southeast Alaska Discovery Center is one of the best introductions to the Tongass ecosystem and the region's human history, and Creek Street is genuinely interesting rather than merely photogenic.

For outdoor adventures, the Tongass Narrows and surrounding fjords reward kayakers and floatplane passengers alike. Misty Fjords National Monument, reachable by floatplane in under an hour, is a world-class wilderness experience most visitors skip entirely.
