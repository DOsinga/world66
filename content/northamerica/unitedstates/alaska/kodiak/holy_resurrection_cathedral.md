Holy Resurrection Cathedral is a small but important landmark in downtown Kodiak, carrying the Russian Orthodox story that began here in the late 18th century. The present building is not the original, but the parish is one of the historic Orthodox centres of Alaska.

Step inside respectfully if it is open. The icons, candles, and quiet interior give a different register to a town otherwise defined by boats, weather, and fish.
