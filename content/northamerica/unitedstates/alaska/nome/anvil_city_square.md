Anvil City Square is a small downtown park best known for Nome's oversized gold pan. It is not a major sight, but it is a quick and useful marker of the gold-rush story that still hangs over the city.

Use it as a short stop while walking Front Street between the museums, the sea wall, and the old commercial core.
