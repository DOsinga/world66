The Bering Land Bridge Heritage Center is the National Park Service foothold in Nome for Bering Land Bridge National Preserve. The preserve itself lies far north and is not a casual day trip, so this small centre is where most travellers get their first real explanation of Beringia, Ice Age animals, and the human migrations between Asia and North America.

It is worth visiting early in your stay, especially if you plan to drive out of town or fly onward into the Seward Peninsula.
