The Harding Icefield Trail is a demanding day hike from the Exit Glacier area into alpine terrain above Kenai Fjords National Park. The round trip is steep, exposed, and weather-dependent, but fit hikers are rewarded with sweeping views over Exit Glacier and the vast icefield beyond.
