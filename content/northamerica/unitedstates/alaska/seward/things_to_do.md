---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Seward's main draws are glaciers, wildlife, and water. The town itself is a pleasant few blocks on the shore of Resurrection Bay, with the SeaLife Center as the main in-town attraction. But most visitors come for the fjords — either by boat into Kenai Fjords National Park or on foot to Exit Glacier. The Alaska Railroad connection adds a scenic approach that's worth building into your itinerary.
