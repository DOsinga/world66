---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May to September** is the main visitor season. Summer days are long (up to 19 hours of daylight in June), the boat tours run daily, and Exit Glacier is accessible. Temperatures reach 15-20°C on good days. July 4th brings the Mount Marathon Race, which draws large crowds — book accommodation far in advance if you want to be here for it.

**October to April**: The boat tours mostly stop, Exit Glacier Road closes to vehicles, and snow covers the mountains. The town is quiet and services are limited. However, winter can be beautiful — the mountains are white, the occasional clear day brings spectacular cold-air clarity, and the harbor has a stark, working-port character that summer visitors miss.

The shoulder seasons — May and September — offer fewer crowds with most facilities still operating. September in particular can be excellent: summer temperatures, lower prices, and the salmon running in the streams.

Rain is common throughout summer; bring waterproofs regardless of the forecast. The Kenai Peninsula is notoriously changeable — a sunny morning can become a socked-in fog afternoon within an hour.
