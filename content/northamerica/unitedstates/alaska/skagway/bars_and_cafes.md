---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Skagway's bar scene is anchored by the gold rush storefronts of Broadway. The [Red Onion Saloon](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/skagway/red_onion_saloon) is the most historic and the most visited. Most of the eating and drinking establishments cater primarily to cruise ship passengers, which means the busiest hours are midday when ships are in port — evenings can feel surprisingly quiet by comparison. The Skagway Brewing Company at the corner of Broadway and 7th serves decent craft beer and a predictable menu in a warm room.
