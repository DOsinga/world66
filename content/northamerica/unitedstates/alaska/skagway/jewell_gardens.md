Jewell Gardens is a cultivated counterpoint to Skagway's gold-rush streets and mountain scenery. In
the brief Alaskan growing season, the gardens can feel surprisingly lush, with vegetables, flowers,
glass-art elements, and views back toward the surrounding peaks.

It is a gentle stop rather than an essential wilderness experience, but it suits visitors who want
something slower than the railway, hiking trails, or cruise-port rush. The appeal is seeing how
intensely a northern garden can grow when summer light is long and concentrated.
