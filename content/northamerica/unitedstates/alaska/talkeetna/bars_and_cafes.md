type: section
---

The [Fairview Inn](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/talkeetna/fairview_inn) on Main Street is Talkeetna's social hub and the place to spend an evening. It fills up in summer with a mix of climbers, pilots, and visitors, and the atmosphere can get lively when the weather is clear and everyone is in a good mood. There is not much competition -- this is a small town -- but there is rarely a reason to look for any.
