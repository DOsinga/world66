---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Talkeetna has a handful of places to eat, and the quality is higher than you might expect for a town of 900 people. The [Talkeetna Roadhouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/talkeetna/talkeetna_roadhouse) is the anchor, particularly for breakfast. A few other spots along Main Street cover pizza and burgers for evenings. Given the town's size, your choices are limited -- arrive hungry and keep expectations simple.
