title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Talkeetna punches well above its weight for a town this small. Flightseeing around Denali is the headliner -- operators like [Talkeetna Air Taxi](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/talkeetna/talkeetna_air_taxi) and [K2 Aviation](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/talkeetna/k2_aviation) run glacier landings and summit circuits that rank among Alaska's most spectacular experiences. On the ground, the town's history as a railroad stop and mountaineering base is preserved in a handful of log-cabin museums and landmarks. Walk Main Street to the [river confluence](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/talkeetna/river_confluence), stop at [Nagley's Store](/northamerica/unitedstates/alaska/talkeetna/nagleys_store), and keep your eyes on the sky -- there is almost always a bush plane coming or going.
