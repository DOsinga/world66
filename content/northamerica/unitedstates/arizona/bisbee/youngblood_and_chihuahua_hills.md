The hike up Youngblood and Chihuahua Hills is a short round trip above Old Bisbee, usually taking about an hour and a half. The route climbs to the hill marked with a large "B" and a cluster of small shrines, with good views over the old town, the surrounding desert hills, and the Lavender Pit.
