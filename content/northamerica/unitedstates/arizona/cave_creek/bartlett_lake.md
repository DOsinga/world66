Bartlett Lake is the big water escape east of Cave Creek, reached by a paved road through open Sonoran desert. It is a reservoir rather than a manicured park, so the appeal is space: ridgelines, saguaro country, bright water, and enough shoreline to make boating, paddling, fishing, and rough camping feel separate from Phoenix's suburban edge.

The Forest Service recreation areas are practical rather than polished, with vault toilets at some sites and little shade away from ramadas or your own vehicle. Bring water, sun protection, and a full tank of fuel. In cooler months the drive out is half the pleasure; in summer, the lake makes sense only if you are actually getting on or in the water.
