Cave Creek Museum is the town's clearest introduction to the desert history behind its western storefronts and trailhead culture. Exhibits cover mining, ranching, archaeology, pioneer settlement, and the practical adaptations that made life possible on the edge of the Sonoran Desert.

The museum is modest, but it is unusually useful in a place where atmosphere can otherwise blur into staged Old West style. Stop here before exploring the town and surrounding preserve areas; the mining equipment, historic buildings, and local stories give Cave Creek a firmer sense of place.
