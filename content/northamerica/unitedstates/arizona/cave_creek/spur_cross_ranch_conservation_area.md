Spur Cross Ranch Conservation Area protects Sonoran Desert foothills north of Cave Creek, with trails for hiking, mountain biking, and horseback riding. The landscape mixes cactus-studded slopes, riparian desert, and open views toward Tonto National Forest.

Several routes pass Hohokam rock art and old ranching traces, making it one of the better nature stops around Cave Creek.
