Beaver Street Brewery was Flagstaff's first brewpub when it opened in 1994 and remains one of the best places to eat and drink in the city. The wood-panelled interior is comfortable and unpretentious; the beer is genuinely good — the Railhead Red Ale is the flagship, but the seasonal offerings are worth trying. The food is solid pub fare with a few local and regional touches: decent burgers, good pizzas, reliable salads.

The location is right in the heart of downtown, a short walk from the Amtrak station and the historic hotel district. It fills up on weekends but handles the crowds well. If you are in Flagstaff overnight and looking for somewhere that the locals actually use rather than just the visitors, Beaver Street is a reliable choice.
