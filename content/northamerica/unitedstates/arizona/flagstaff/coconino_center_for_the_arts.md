Coconino Center for the Arts is Flagstaff's main community arts venue, hosting exhibitions, concerts, talks, and regional cultural events. It is a useful reminder that Flagstaff is not only a gateway to canyons, forests, and Route 66 nostalgia.

Check the calendar before going, since the experience depends on what is on. When the programme lines up, it offers a good local evening or a quieter gallery stop between outdoor days.
