Flagstaff's position in northern Arizona makes it one of the best bases for day trips in the American Southwest. The South Rim of the [Grand Canyon](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/grandcanyon) is about 80 miles north and takes under two hours to drive. [Sedona](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/sedona), with its famous red rock formations, is 45 minutes south. Page and Antelope Canyon are two and a half hours. Monument Valley, the Navajo Nation's iconic landscape, is about three hours east on Highway 160.
