Pickin' in the Pines is Flagstaff's notable bluegrass and acoustic music festival, usually associated with the wooded setting of Fort Tuthill County Park. It fits the city well: mountain air, camping culture, and a music scene that feels separate from the desert cities farther south.

As with any festival, it matters only if your dates align. If they do, it can be a strong reason to linger in Flagstaff rather than treating it only as a Grand Canyon or Route 66 stopover.
