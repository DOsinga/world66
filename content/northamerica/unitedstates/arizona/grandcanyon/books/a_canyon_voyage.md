The firsthand account of the 1871-72 Powell expedition down the Green and Colorado Rivers — the second survey voyage led by John Wesley Powell, who had lost his right arm at Shiloh. Dellenbaugh was seventeen years old and served as the expedition's artist and chronicler. The writing is clear, the adventure is real, and the canyon encountered in these pages has not yet been dammed, named, or photographed. An essential companion to anyone who wants to understand what the Colorado River corridor was like before the twentieth century intervened.
