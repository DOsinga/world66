Abbey worked as a park ranger at Arches National Monument in the late 1950s, and this is his record of two summers in the canyon country of the Colorado Plateau. Part nature writing, part polemic against industrial tourism, part elegy for a wilderness already being transformed by roads and development. Abbey is cantankerous, funny, and absolutely serious about what is being lost. The Grand Canyon passages are among the finest pieces of American nature writing. It will change how you look at the canyon, and possibly at every road built into it.
