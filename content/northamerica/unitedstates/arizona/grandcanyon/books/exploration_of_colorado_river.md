Powell led the first scientific expedition through the Grand Canyon in 1869 with nine men in four wooden boats, mapping nearly 1,000 miles of unknown river. His journal is a remarkable document: precise about geology and geography, honest about fear and hardship, and written with an eye for the spectacular. Three of his men quit mid-trip and climbed out of the canyon, only to be killed by Paiute or Mormon settlers on the plateau above. Powell went on to complete the journey. Reading this before your visit makes the canyon feel genuinely ancient and dangerous, which it is.
