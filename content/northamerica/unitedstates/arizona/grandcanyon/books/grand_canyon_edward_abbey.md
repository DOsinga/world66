Abbey's classic account of a season as a park ranger at Arches National Monument in Utah, with extended digressions on the canyon country of the Colorado Plateau — the same landscape that frames the Grand Canyon. Abbey was a passionate, funny, and infuriating writer who loved the desert more than almost anything and hated what motorised tourism was doing to it. Desert Solitaire is the book that made environmental writing angry and literary at the same time, and it remains the essential read for anyone traveling the American Southwest.
