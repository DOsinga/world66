A novel following a group of strangers on a two-week commercial rafting trip down the Colorado through the canyon. Hyde manages the ensemble cast — the ageing guide, the grieving widow, the difficult teenager, the newlyweds — with skill and without sentimentality. The canyon itself is a constant presence, transforming the passengers in ways they did not expect and could not have planned for. For anyone who has done or is about to do a river trip through the canyon, this is essential reading.
