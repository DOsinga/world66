A meticulous cataloguing of every recorded death at the Grand Canyon, organized by cause: falls, heat, flash floods, drowning, aircraft accidents. It reads as morbid but functions as the most practical safety guide in existence — the specific circumstances of each death reveal exactly how and why people get into fatal trouble here. Ghiglieri ran the Colorado for years as a guide; his analysis of river deaths is particularly authoritative. Read it before your hike and you will make better decisions below the rim.
