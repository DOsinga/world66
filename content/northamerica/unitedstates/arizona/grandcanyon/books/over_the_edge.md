The definitive catalogue of how people die at the Grand Canyon — falls, drowning, heat, lightning, aircraft crashes, murder — told in meticulous narrative detail for each case. It sounds grim, and it is, but it is also a deeply practical book that explains the canyon's genuine dangers without scaremongering. The section on heat deaths and dehydration is essential reading for anyone planning a below-rim hike. Ghiglieri and Myers are both experienced canyon veterans, and their authority shows.
