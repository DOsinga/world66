In the spring of 1983, a record flood tore through the Grand Canyon and Kenton Grua seized the opportunity to run the river faster than anyone ever had. Fedarko uses the story of this illegal speed run — in a 17-foot wooden dory called the Emerald Mile — to weave together the entire human history of the Colorado River, from John Wesley Powell to the dam-builders to the modern river-running culture. It is a gripping adventure story and an essential account of humanity's relationship with one of the world's great rivers.
