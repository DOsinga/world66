The story of the fastest dory run through the Grand Canyon ever recorded — three men in a wooden boat running the Colorado during the historic flood of 1983, racing a swollen river through the world's most dangerous whitewater while park rangers tried to stop them. Fedarko uses the story as a frame for the entire history of the canyon, the river, the dams that control it, and the culture of the river guides who have devoted their lives to it. The result is the best book about the Grand Canyon yet written.
