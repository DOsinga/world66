The first-person account of Powell's 1869 expedition — the first to navigate the entire Colorado River through the Grand Canyon by boat. Powell was a one-armed Civil War veteran who led ten men through unknown territory, navigating rapids, running out of food, and losing three crew members who chose to climb out and were never seen again. The prose is 19th-century formal but never dull, and the geological observations are still impressive. This is the book that opened the Grand Canyon to the world.
