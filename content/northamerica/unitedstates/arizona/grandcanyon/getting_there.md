---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Grand Canyon South Rim is 80 miles north of Flagstaff, Arizona, on Highway 180 and then Highway 64. From Las Vegas it is about 270 miles (four to five hours). From Phoenix it is about 220 miles (three and a half hours). The closest commercial airports are Flagstaff Pulliam Airport (small, limited routes) and Phoenix Sky Harbor (the practical choice for most international visitors, four hours away). Las Vegas is a common gateway for visitors combining the canyon with Las Vegas or the Utah parks.

There is no rail service to the canyon. Amtrak serves Flagstaff, and from there a shuttle or rental car is needed. Arizona Shuttle runs regular services between Flagstaff and the South Rim. Open Road Tours offers day trips from Sedona and Flagstaff.

Grand Canyon Airport, just south of Tusayan near the park entrance, serves small regional carriers and helicopter and airplane tour operators. This is a practical option for day trips from Las Vegas.

The North Rim is accessed from Page or Kanab via Highway 89A and 67. It is approximately 220 miles by road from the South Rim despite being only 10 miles across the canyon as the crow flies.
