---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

The Grand Canyon's South Rim is about 80 miles (130 km) north of Flagstaff and 230 miles (370 km) north of Phoenix. By car, take Highway 180 north from Flagstaff or Interstate 40 west from Flagstaff to Highway 64 north — the latter approach via Williams and Tusayan gives you the historic entrance road. Driving is the most flexible way to arrive.

Flagstaff has an Amtrak station (on the Los Angeles to Chicago Southwest Chief route) and a small airport. Arizona Shuttle and Groome Transportation run daily van services from Flagstaff and Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport to the South Rim.

The Grand Canyon Railway runs a historic steam and diesel train from Williams, Arizona, to the South Rim once daily. The trip takes two hours and twenty minutes each way, with entertainment aboard. It is a genuinely enjoyable way to arrive if you have the time.

There is no commercial airport at the South Rim, but Grand Canyon National Park Airport at Tusayan (just outside the south entrance) handles charter flights and helicopter tours. McCarran International Airport in Las Vegas is a common entry point for visitors approaching from the west, roughly 280 miles away.
