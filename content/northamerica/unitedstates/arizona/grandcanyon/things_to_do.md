title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Grand Canyon rewards both the casual visitor and the serious explorer. From the rim you can walk miles of paved trail between overlooks; below the rim the hiking ranges from challenging day trips to multi-day backcountry adventures. The scale of the place — 277 miles long, up to 18 miles wide, over a mile deep — only becomes clear once you are inside it.
