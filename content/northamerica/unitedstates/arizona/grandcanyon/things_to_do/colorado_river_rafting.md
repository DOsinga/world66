Running the Colorado River through the Grand Canyon is one of the great adventures in the United States. Full trips run 225 miles over two to three weeks; shorter motorized raft trips cover the upper section in three to four days. All commercial trips require permits and book out months to years in advance. Private trips through the park lottery can take years to arrange. The river corridor offers a completely different relationship with the canyon — the walls rise around you, the rapids are real, and the camps at the water's edge under a sky full of stars are unlike anything above the rim. The rapids at Lava Falls (mile 179) and Crystal (mile 98) are among the most challenging commercially run whitewater in North America.
