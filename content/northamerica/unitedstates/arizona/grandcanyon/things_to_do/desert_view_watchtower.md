A 70-foot stone tower at the eastern end of Desert View Drive, designed by architect Mary Colter in 1932 and modelled on Ancestral Puebloan watchtowers found elsewhere in the Southwest. Colter was the most important architect working for the Fred Harvey Company, and this is her masterpiece: a building that looks genuinely ancient from a distance, decorated internally with murals by Hopi artist Fred Kabotie. The tower offers some of the most dramatic panoramic views in the park — including the Colorado River far below and the Painted Desert stretching east toward the Navajo Nation. Free to enter.
