A historic photography studio and residence perched right at the Bright Angel trailhead on the South Rim. Built starting in 1904 by Emery and Ellsworth Kolb, who made their living photographing mule riders at the top of the trail, then running to a darkroom 4.5 miles below at Indian Garden (the only water source at the time) to develop the prints before the riders returned. The studio now hosts rotating art exhibitions and a bookshop. The building itself — clinging to the rim wall at the canyon's edge — is as interesting as the contents.
