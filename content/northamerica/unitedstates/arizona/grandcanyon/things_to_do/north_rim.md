A thousand feet higher than the South Rim and open only from mid-May to mid-October, the North Rim receives roughly a tenth of the South Rim's visitors despite being comparably spectacular. The drive from the South Rim is 220 miles and about four hours around the canyon. The Grand Lodge at Bright Angel Point offers views from its front porch that many consider the finest in the park — looking straight down into the canyon with no other building in sight. The North Kaibab Trail begins here and descends to the river and beyond to the South Rim, the route of the classic rim-to-rim hike.
