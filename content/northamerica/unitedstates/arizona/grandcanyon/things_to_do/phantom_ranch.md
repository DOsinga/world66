The only lodging and dining inside the canyon, at the bottom of the Bright Angel or South Kaibab trails beside Bright Angel Creek. Cabins and dormitories must be reserved through a lottery system that opens 15 months in advance and sells out immediately. If you cannot get a cabin, you can still hike down for a meal at the canteen — stew and cornbread for hikers, or the legendary beer served cold at the bottom of a mile-deep hole. The stone buildings were designed by Mary Colter in 1922. Mule trips from the South Rim also connect to Phantom Ranch; these too require advance booking. Even a day trip to the creek and back from the South Rim (via Bright Angel Trail) gives a taste of the canyon floor.
