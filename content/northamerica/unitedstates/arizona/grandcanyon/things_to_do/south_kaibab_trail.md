Steeper and more exposed than Bright Angel but rewarding with unobstructed views the entire way down. The ridge trail drops along a series of knife-edged spurs, so you can see both walls of the canyon simultaneously rather than one side at a time. The hike to Ooh Aah Point (1.8 miles round trip) or Cedar Ridge (3 miles round trip) makes an excellent short day trip. There is no water anywhere on this trail — carry more than you think you need. The Kaibab Trail transfers hikers to the North Rim via the Black Bridge or Silver Bridge over the Colorado River, the classic two-day rim-to-rim route.
