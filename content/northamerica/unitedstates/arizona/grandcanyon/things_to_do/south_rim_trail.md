A mostly paved trail running 13 miles along the South Rim between Hermits Rest and Desert View, passing every major overlook. You can walk as much or as little as you want, using the free shuttle buses to skip sections. Mather Point, near the Visitor Center, is where most people get their first view of the canyon and is very crowded; walk ten minutes in either direction and the crowds thin considerably. Hopi Point, accessible by shuttle on the Hermits Rest Route, is the best spot for sunset — it extends as a promontory into the canyon giving views both east and west. Pima Point, further along, offers the sound of the Colorado River rising from 3,200 feet below.
