Twenty-two miles east of Grand Canyon Village on Desert View Drive, a small but rewarding site covering Pueblo Indian life at the canyon 800 years ago. The museum holds artifacts from the excavation of the adjacent ruin — a compact Ancestral Puebloan settlement occupied around 1185 CE. A self-guided trail loops through the ruin itself, and rangers lead guided tours daily in season. It is one of nearly 4,000 archaeological sites within the park, and one of the few accessible to the public. Free admission.
