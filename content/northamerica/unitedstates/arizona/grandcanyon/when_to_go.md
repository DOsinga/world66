---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April to May) and autumn (September to October) are the best times to visit. Temperatures on the rim are comfortable — 50 to 75 degrees Fahrenheit — and the crowds, while substantial, are manageable compared to summer peaks. Below the rim, spring and fall are essential: summer temperatures at the canyon floor regularly exceed 110 degrees Fahrenheit, and the park service strongly discourages below-rim hiking from May through September except in the very early morning.

Summer (June to August) brings the largest crowds and the most difficult conditions below the rim. The rim itself is fine — at 7,000 feet it stays in the 70s — but parking becomes impossible by mid-morning, and the shuttle buses run at capacity. The North Rim gets significant thunderstorm activity in July and August (the Arizona monsoon season).

Winter (December to February) on the South Rim is cold but strikingly beautiful, with snow dusting the canyon walls and dramatically reduced crowds. The park remains open year-round, though some services and roads close. The North Rim is closed from mid-October to mid-May.

The most important annual events are the Phantom Ranch lottery (opens 15 months ahead and fills within minutes) and the rim-to-rim hiking season, which peaks in May and October.
