Jerome clings to Cleopatra Hill above the Verde Valley, north of [Prescott](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/prescott) and west of Sedona. It boomed on copper, almost emptied after the mines closed, then came back as a strange mix of ghost town, artists' village, mining museum, and day-trip stop.

The setting does half the work. Streets twist along the hillside, old brick buildings lean into the slope, and views open toward the red-rock country and the valley below. The mining story is still strong at [Jerome State Historic Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/jerome/jerome_state_historic_park), the [Mine Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/jerome/mine_museum), and the [Audrey Headframe](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/jerome/audrey_headframe).

Jerome is touristy, but it has not been smoothed flat. Come for a few hours of museums, galleries, old saloons, and odd bits of mining ruin, then leave time to simply walk the switchback streets.
