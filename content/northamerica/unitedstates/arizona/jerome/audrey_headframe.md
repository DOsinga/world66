The Audrey Headframe stands over one of Jerome's most dramatic mining relics: a shaft that drops about 1,900 feet into the mountain. When the park is open, visitors can look down through glass into the dark hole below.

Check current access before counting on it. Restoration closures happen, but even from outside the headframe helps make the scale of Jerome's mining past feel real.
