---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Kayenta's dining scene is limited but functional. The Hampton Inn's restaurant serves better food than you might expect, with Navajo dishes alongside standard American fare. The staff are knowledgeable about local history and the decor reflects Diné culture. The Amigo Café is a long-standing local favourite offering Navajo tacos — frybread topped with beans, meat, cheese, and salsa — and Southwestern staples. A Sonic and a pizza place handle fast-food needs. Stock up on groceries at Basha's if you're heading deeper into the Navajo Nation, where services become sparse.
