The pretty 1907 Mission Revival depot still serves as an Amtrak station on the daily Southwest Chief line between Chicago and Los Angeles. It is one of the few surviving early depots on the Santa Fe's transcontinental route, and one of the few still in use as a working passenger station.

You can walk through the small waiting room even when no train is due, and the platform is a good vantage for watching the long freight trains that roll through hourly. The building anchors the western end of the historic downtown stretch, a short walk from the Powerhouse.
