The 1915 Mohave County Courthouse and its attached jail occupy a full block at the top of the historic downtown, a handsome neoclassical pile in pale local stone. It is still the working seat of county government — the courtrooms and offices are open during business hours, and you can wander into the lobby to see the original tilework and the Andy Devine bronze bust just inside the door.

The most famous event ever held in the building was the 1939 marriage of Clark Gable and Carole Lombard, who eloped to Kingman from Hollywood for a discreet ceremony at the local Methodist church and stopped at the courthouse to file paperwork. The clerk's office still keeps a copy of the licence on the wall.
