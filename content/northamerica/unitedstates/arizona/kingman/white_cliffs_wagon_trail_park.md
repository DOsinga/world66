White Cliffs Wagon Trail Park preserves part of a 19th-century wagon route cut into bedrock by miners. The ruts and post holes are still visible, which makes the trail more than a simple desert walk.

It works well for visitors interested in Kingman's mining and Route 66-era landscape. Go in cooler hours, bring water, and expect an open desert trail rather than a landscaped city park.
