---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lake Havasu City is essentially a lake town — the lake is the attraction, and most visitors come to be on the water. The London Bridge provides the historic footnote, but the real draw is sun, warm water, and 60 kilometres of Colorado River reservoir flanked by red-rock desert. Most activities revolve around boats, jet skis, and beaches, with State Park trails providing a quieter alternative.
