Miami is a small copper mining town in Gila County, Arizona, sitting in the rugged hill country between Phoenix and the White Mountains. It is a classic Western boomtown — the copper mines that built it are largely dormant now, but the town retains a certain faded charm.

The old downtown has been partly renovated, with a handful of antique shops and galleries setting up in the historic storefronts along [Sullivan Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/miami/sullivan_street_historic_district). Miami and neighbouring Globe are often visited together, and the drive through the [Final Pass mining district](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/miami/final_pass_scenic_overlook) on US-60 is dramatic, with open-pit mines carved into the red and ochre hillsides. It makes a worthwhile stop on the scenic route between Phoenix and the eastern Arizona highlands.
