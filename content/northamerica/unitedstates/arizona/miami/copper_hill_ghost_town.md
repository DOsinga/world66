Copper Hill was founded in 1908 as a mining settlement and emptied out during the Depression. The town is gone, but foundations and scattered remains still mark the slope, giving a rough sense of how quickly Arizona mining camps could rise and disappear.

This is a stop for travelers already exploring the Globe-Miami mining country. Wear sturdy shoes and treat the ruins gently; there is no polished visitor infrastructure here.
