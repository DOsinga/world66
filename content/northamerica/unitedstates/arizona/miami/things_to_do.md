---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Miami is small but its mining heritage is visible at every turn. The [Bullion Plaza Cultural Center and Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/miami/bullion_plaza_museum) anchors the cultural life of the town, housed in a historic school building and telling the full story of copper mining in the Gila Valley. Downtown, [Sullivan Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/miami/sullivan_street_historic_district) preserves a row of early 20th-century commercial buildings — several now occupied by antique dealers and small galleries that make for an easy browse. The [Final Pass Scenic Overlook](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/miami/final_pass_scenic_overlook) on US-60 gives you the most dramatic view of open-pit copper mining you are likely to see from a public road — the scale is genuinely staggering. North of town, the [Inspiration Open Pit](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/miami/inspiration_open_pit) is the remnant of the old Inspiration Consolidated Copper Company workings and makes a worthwhile short detour.
