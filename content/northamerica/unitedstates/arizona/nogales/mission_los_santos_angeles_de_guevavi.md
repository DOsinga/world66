Mission Los Santos Angeles de Guevavi is one of the early Spanish mission sites of the Santa Cruz Valley. The remaining ruins are fragile and usually seen by guided tour, which makes the visit more deliberate but gives useful context to the frontier history around Nogales and Tubac.
