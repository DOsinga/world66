Built in 1904 from locally quarried stone, the old Santa Cruz County Courthouse is Nogales' most handsome civic building. It now houses the Nogales branch of Cochise College and the Arizona Ranger Museum.

The courthouse is a good anchor for a walk through the old downtown streets just north of the border.
