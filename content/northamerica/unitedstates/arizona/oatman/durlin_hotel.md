The Durlin Hotel, usually called the Oatman Hotel, is the town's main historic building. It stands on Main Street with the look of a small boomtown hotel that has survived by turning itself into a saloon, restaurant, and museum stop.

The National Park Service notes its place on Route 66 and its Clark Gable and Carole Lombard honeymoon story. Whether you come for the history, the dollar-covered saloon, or just a look inside, it anchors Oatman's old mining-town stage set.
