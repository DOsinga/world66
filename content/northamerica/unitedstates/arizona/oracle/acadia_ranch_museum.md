Acadia Ranch Museum is a small local history museum in a former guest ranch building. It covers Oracle's ranching, mining, resort, and health-retreat past, which helps explain why this quiet settlement has drawn such an odd mix of scientists, hikers, retirees, and desert eccentrics.

Check hours before going. It is best paired with Biosphere 2 or Oracle State Park, not treated as the main reason for the trip.
