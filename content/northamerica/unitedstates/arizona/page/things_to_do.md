---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Page is primarily a base for exploring the surrounding landscape rather than a destination in itself. Antelope Canyon and Horseshoe Bend are the two iconic sights; Lake Powell, Glen Canyon Dam, and the Vermilion Cliffs fill the rest of the itinerary. Plan at least two full days to do justice to the area, and book Antelope Canyon tours well in advance — they fill up weeks or months ahead in peak season.
