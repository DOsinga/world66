The Hualapai Cultural Center is a modest but important stop because it shifts Peach Springs from Route 66 scenery to Hualapai homeland. Exhibits introduce tribal history, language, and community life, which helps make sense of the permits, tours, and land access around town.

Visit before heading toward Diamond Creek or Grand Canyon West. Even a short stop gives better context for the canyon country than treating Peach Springs as just another roadside dot.
