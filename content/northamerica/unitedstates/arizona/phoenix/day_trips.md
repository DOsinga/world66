Phoenix is the natural base for exploring central Arizona. **Sedona**, two hours north, swaps the desert flats for red-rock canyons and a New Age scene that traders sometimes mock and sometimes embrace. **Prescott** offers a mile-high pine-forest climate and a Wild West main street that still feels alive after dark. **Scottsdale**, **Tempe**, and **Mesa** are part of the same metro and are easily reached by light rail or a short drive; each has its own distinct character.

For prehistory, **Casa Grande Ruins** preserves the largest Hohokam structure still standing, about an hour south on Interstate 10. Further afield, the **Grand Canyon** is a long but doable day trip — set out before dawn and you can be on the South Rim by mid-morning, with a few hours to take in the canyon before heading back.
