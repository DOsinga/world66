The Phoenix Space Museum is a small public space and STEM museum in north Phoenix. Exhibits have included a large Shuttle Atlantis model, deep-space displays, and hands-on material aimed at families, so it works best as a focused stop for children or space enthusiasts rather than a general city museum.
