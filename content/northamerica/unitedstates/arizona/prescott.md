Prescott (locals say PRESS-kit) is a former territorial capital in the mountains of central Arizona, about 100 miles north of Phoenix. At 1,600 metres it escapes the worst of the desert heat, and its pine-covered hills and mild summers have made it a popular retreat for Arizonans.

The town square — [Courthouse Plaza](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/prescott/courthouse_plaza) — is the heart of old Prescott, ringed by restaurants, shops, and the bars of [Whiskey Row](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/prescott/whiskey_row), which has been pouring since the 1860s. The [Sharlot Hall Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/prescott/sharlot_hall_museum) preserves Arizona's first government buildings from 1864 and tells the story of the territorial era in depth. The surrounding [Prescott National Forest](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/prescott/prescott_national_forest) offers excellent hiking and mountain biking, and nearby [Granite Dells](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/prescott/granite_dells) — a landscape of rounded granite boulders along Watson Lake — is one of the more striking geological features in the state.
