Courthouse Plaza is the heart of downtown Prescott — a square block of lawn, trees, and benches ringed by the historic buildings of the town's commercial district. On one side stands the Yavapai County Courthouse, a Romanesque Revival building from 1916 with a prominent clock tower. On the other sides, the arcaded storefronts of Whiskey Row and the adjacent streets give the plaza an appealing, walkable character.

The square hosts a farmers market on Tuesday and Saturday mornings, and is used for various community events throughout the year. Summer evenings bring concerts. The benches under the sycamores are a good place to take stock and people-watch before heading off to explore the surrounding streets.

The entire district is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, making this one of the better-preserved 19th-century downtown squares in the American Southwest.
