Heritage Park Zoological Sanctuary is a small non-profit wildlife sanctuary north of Prescott, with native and exotic animals in a quiet high-country setting. It is not a major city zoo, but it is a worthwhile family stop when you want something more relaxed than downtown museums or lake walks.

The sanctuary is especially useful for travellers with children, and it combines easily with the north-side lakes and open space around Prescott.
