Hi Jolly Monument is the one Quartzsite sight almost everyone should see. It stands in the pioneer section of the cemetery, marking the grave of Hadji Ali, better known in Arizona as Hi Jolly.

The monument is simple but wonderfully specific: a stone pyramid topped by a camel silhouette, surrounded by desert ground and pioneer graves. It turns an odd footnote of American military history into a physical stop you can understand in five minutes.
