Safford sits in Arizona's Gila Valley, below the Pinaleño Mountains and the long rise of Mount Graham. The town itself is practical and low-key, but the surrounding desert and mountains give it enough substance for a real stop between [Tucson](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/tucson), the White Mountains, and the New Mexico line.

The best days here are outdoors. [Gila Box Riparian National Conservation Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/safford/gila_box_riparian_national_conservation_area) protects a rare desert river corridor northeast of town, while [Hot Well Dunes Recreation Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/safford/hot_well_dunes_recreation_area) mixes off-road sand with hot soaking tubs in a very Arizona way.

Safford also has an astronomy angle. [Discovery Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/safford/discovery_park) is the visitor centre for Mount Graham International Observatory tours, giving travellers a public route into one of the state's more unusual scientific landscapes.

This is not a polished resort town. Come for desert water, mountain roads, hot springs, and a sky full of telescopes.
