Sanchez CCC Camp preserves the remains of a Depression-era Civilian Conservation Corps camp in the desert outside Safford. Several structures still stand, making it a worthwhile detour for travellers interested in New Deal history and remote Arizona sites.

The Bureau of Land Management manages the site. Roads can be rough, so check current access details and consider a high-clearance vehicle.
