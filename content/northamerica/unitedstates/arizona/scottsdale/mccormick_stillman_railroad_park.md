McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park is Scottsdale's most reliable family stop, built around miniature train rides, a vintage carousel, playgrounds, picnic lawns, and railway exhibits. It is playful rather than profound, but it is also unusually well kept.

Adults with a soft spot for rail history should look for the model railroad displays and preserved equipment. Everyone else can use it as a relaxed late-afternoon park, especially when desert heat starts to loosen its grip.
