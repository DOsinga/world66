Scottsdale Center for the Performing Arts anchors the city's civic arts district, just beside Scottsdale Museum of Contemporary Art and the lawns of Scottsdale Civic Center. Its calendar ranges from touring musicians and dance companies to lectures, comedy, and community festivals.

It is most useful for travellers who want an evening plan that is not another resort bar or Old Town gallery stroll. Check the schedule before you go; the building itself is restrained, but the programme can add a welcome cultural layer to a Scottsdale trip.
