Broken Arrow Trail is one of Sedona's signature red-rock routes, running through sculpted sandstone, juniper, and open desert views close to town. It is famous with hikers and high-clearance jeep tours alike, so the landscape can feel dramatic even when the trail is busy.

The walking route gives you more time to enjoy the rock formations than a fast vehicle tour, but bring water and start early in warm weather. If you want quieter Sedona, choose a less famous trail; if you want classic red-rock scenery, Broken Arrow earns its reputation.
