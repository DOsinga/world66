Devil's Bridge Trail leads to one of Sedona's most photographed sandstone arches, a broad natural bridge set high enough to open views across red-rock ridges and juniper country. The walk is not especially long, but the final approach is steep and exposed enough to feel earned.

Start early if you can. This is a popular trail, and the narrow bridge viewpoint can collect a queue of hikers waiting for the same photo. Good shoes, water, and patience make the experience far better.
