Honanki Heritage Site preserves cliff dwellings and rock art associated with the Sinagua people, set against the red-rock country west of Sedona. It is one of the area's most important archaeological sites and gives a deeper human timescale to a landscape many visitors otherwise experience mainly through scenery and hiking.

The site requires respect: stay on marked routes, do not touch rock art, and check current access conditions before driving out, as roads and management rules can change. For travellers who make the effort, Honanki offers a quieter and more serious counterpoint to Sedona's busiest viewpoints.
