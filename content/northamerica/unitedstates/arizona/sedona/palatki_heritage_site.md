Palatki Heritage Site preserves Sinagua cliff dwellings, pictographs, and petroglyphs west of Sedona. The setting in Coconino National Forest makes it both an archaeological visit and a red-rock landscape stop.

Access is managed to protect the site, so check current reservation rules before going. Stay on marked trails and avoid touching the rock art.
