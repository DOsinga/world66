---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The outdoor landscape is Sedona's main attraction. The red-rock formations define the hiking agenda, and there are trails for every level — from paved paths around the visitor centre to strenuous all-day scrambles. The vortex sites draw visitors with spiritual interests but work well as viewpoints for everyone. The art scene along Tlaquepaque adds a gentler counterpoint to all the trail dust.
