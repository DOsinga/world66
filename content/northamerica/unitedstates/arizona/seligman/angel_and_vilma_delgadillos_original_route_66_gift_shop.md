Angel and Vilma Delgadillo's shop is the stop that gives Seligman's Route 66 nostalgia some backbone. Angel ran a barber shop here, then helped found the Historic Route 66 Association of Arizona after the interstate drained traffic from town.

Today it is a gift shop and visitor stop, but the point is the story behind it. Without the local push that started here, the Seligman-to-Kingman stretch might have stayed a forgotten old road instead of becoming one of the best-known pieces of Route 66.
