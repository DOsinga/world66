Historic Seligman Sundries occupies one of the older commercial buildings on the town's Route 66 strip. It has served different roles over the years, including as a trading post and soda-fountain stop, and now mixes coffee, souvenirs, and bits of local road history.

It is not a museum in the formal sense. Treat it as a pleasant pause while you walk the main street and look at the old road's layers.
