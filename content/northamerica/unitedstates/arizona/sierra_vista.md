Sierra Vista sits in far southeastern Arizona, where the desert rises into the oak canyons of the Huachuca Mountains. It is not a pretty old mining town like [Bisbee](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/bisbee), but it has something rarer: easy access to some of the best birding country in the United States.

The strongest reason to stop is the landscape south and east of town. [Ramsey Canyon Preserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/sierra_vista/ramsey_canyon_preserve) is famous for hummingbirds and shaded trails, while the [San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/sierra_vista/san_pedro_riparian_national_conservation_area) protects a desert river corridor used by migrating birds and wildlife. The Huachucas give the city a real mountain edge, with hiking and canyon roads close enough for a spare afternoon.

Sierra Vista also has a military story. [Fort Huachuca](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/sierra_vista/fort_huachuca) began in the Apache Wars and remains an active Army post, so visits require a little planning. Put together, the fort, the canyons, and the San Pedro valley make Sierra Vista a worthwhile base for travelers already exploring Cochise County.
