Coronado National Memorial marks the route associated with Francisco Vásquez de Coronado's 16th-century expedition and protects a rugged stretch of the Huachuca Mountains near the Mexican border. Trails lead through desert grassland and oak woodland, with Coronado Peak offering wide views south into Mexico.

The memorial combines frontier history with some of the best short hikes near Sierra Vista.
