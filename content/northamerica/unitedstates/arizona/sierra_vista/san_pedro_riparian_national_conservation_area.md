The San Pedro Riparian National Conservation Area protects one of the last free-flowing desert rivers in the region. Cottonwoods, grasslands, and wetland patches make it a major migration route for birds, with quiet trails starting east of Sierra Vista.

It is a different experience from the mountain canyons: flatter, wider, and more open to the sky. Bring water, binoculars, and patience.
