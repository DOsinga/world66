The ASU Robert S. Dietz Museum of Geology is a compact university museum with minerals, gems, fossils, meteorites, and Arizona geology displays. The collection ranges from local fossil finds to a large amethyst geode and a Foucault pendulum.

It is especially good if you are already on the Arizona State University campus or travelling with someone who likes rocks, dinosaurs, or desert landscapes. The museum gives useful context for the geology behind the Grand Canyon, the Petrified Forest, and the state's mining history.
