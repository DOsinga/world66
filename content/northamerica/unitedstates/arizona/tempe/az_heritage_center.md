The AZ Heritage Center at Papago Park gives useful context for Tempe, Phoenix, and the wider Salt River Valley. Its exhibits lean toward twentieth-century Arizona: water, growth, technology, politics, and the way desert cities made themselves.

It is especially convenient if you are already visiting Papago Park, the Desert Botanical Garden, or the Phoenix Zoo. The museum is not flashy, but it helps make sense of the metro area's rapid expansion beyond the postcard version of cactus and red rock.
