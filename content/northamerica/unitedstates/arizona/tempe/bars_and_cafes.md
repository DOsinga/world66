---
title: "Bars and Cafes in Tempe"
type: section
---

Mill Avenue is Tempe's bar district, running from Arizona State University down to Tempe Town Lake. It gets rowdy on game nights and college weekends. For something quieter, head a few blocks east into the older neighbourhoods, where coffee roasters and tap rooms have taken over former storefronts. Tempe's craft beer scene is among the best in metro Phoenix.
