The Guadalupe Cemetery, in the Yaqui town of Guadalupe just south of Tempe, is a small, vivid burial ground belonging to the Pascua Yaqui tribe. Founded in the early 1900s when Yaqui refugees fleeing persecution in Mexico settled here, it remains in use by the community today.

What makes it remarkable is the colour: graves are dressed with bright plastic flowers, hand-painted crosses, family photographs, and personal mementos in a syncretic Yaqui-Catholic style. The cemetery is a living place and visitors should be respectful. It is at its most striking around Día de los Muertos in early November.
