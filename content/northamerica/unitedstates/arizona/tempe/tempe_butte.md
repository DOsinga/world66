Tempe Butte is a volcanic outcrop of dark andesite rock on the north edge of downtown, rising 100 metres above the Salt River. Most locals call it "A" Mountain after the enormous white letter A painted on its south face by Arizona State University students in 1918.

The summit trail is short — about fifteen minutes up from the parking lot near the lake — but steep enough to leave you breathing. The reward is a 360-degree view of Tempe Town Lake, the ASU campus, and the wider Valley of the Sun stretching out toward Camelback Mountain and the South Mountains. Go before sunset for the best light. The petroglyphs on the boulders near the base were carved by the ancient Hohokam.
