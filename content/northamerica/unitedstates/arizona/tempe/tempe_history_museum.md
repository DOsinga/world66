Tempe History Museum is a local-history museum with exhibits on the city's desert setting, Hohokam roots, university growth, and suburban development. It is not flashy, but it helps explain why Tempe feels distinct from Phoenix and Scottsdale.

The museum works best for visitors already spending time in Tempe or travelling with children who need an indoor stop. It is a supporting POI rather than a regional must-see.
