The best option for Mexican food in Tombstone, Don Teodoro's serves reliable enchiladas, tamales, and combination plates in a no-frills setting just off Allen Street. Given that Tombstone sits close to the Mexican border and that Mexican miners and workers were a significant part of the town's original population, Mexican food is historically appropriate here. The portions are large and the prices are reasonable by tourist-district standards.
