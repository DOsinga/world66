BK Tacos (B is for Benjamín, K is for the name of his original taco stand) is a Tucson institution for carne asada tacos, tortas, and Sonoran hot dogs. The grilled meats are smoky, the tortillas hand-made, and the salsa bar — green, red, guacamole, pickled vegetables — is well stocked.

The full plate is the carne asada with rice, beans and tortillas; the hot dogs come with bacon and the full Sonoran trimmings. Several locations across the city; cash and card; open late.
