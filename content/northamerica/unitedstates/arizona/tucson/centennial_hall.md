Centennial Hall is the University of Arizona's main performing arts venue, bringing touring classical music, dance, jazz, comedy, and Broadway-style shows to campus. It is most useful to visitors as an evening option in the University District.

Check the schedule before you go; the building matters less than what is on stage.
