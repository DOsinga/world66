Fiesta de los Vaqueros, the Tucson Rodeo, is one of the city's most distinctive annual events and a reminder that southern Arizona still has a strong ranching culture under the university-town surface. The rodeo brings professional events, school holidays, western wear, and a parade that has been part of Tucson life for generations.

It is worth planning around if you are in town in late winter. The arena events are the main ticket, but the wider rodeo week gives the city a different rhythm: horses on the streets, families in cowboy hats, and a public celebration that feels much more local than staged-for-tourists western nostalgia.
