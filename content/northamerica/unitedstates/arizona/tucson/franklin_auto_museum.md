A surprising small museum dedicated to the air-cooled cars built by the H.H. Franklin Manufacturing Company of Syracuse, New York, between 1902 and 1934. Around thirty restored Franklins are kept in original working condition — and most can be (and are) driven on the road.

Tours run by volunteer enthusiasts; you may see one of the cars warming up out front. Limited opening hours (typically Thursday through Saturday in season); closed for the summer.
