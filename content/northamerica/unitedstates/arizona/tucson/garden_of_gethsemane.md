The Garden of Gethsemane is a small concrete sculpture garden on the west side of downtown — Felix Lucero's hand-cast tableau of the Last Supper, Crucifixion, and Nativity, dating from the 1940s and 50s. Lucero was homeless when he started, working with materials he found along the Santa Cruz River bed nearby.

Tucked under cottonwoods, it is a strange, sincere little space. Free, always open during daylight.
