Philabaum Glass Gallery & Studio gives Tucson visitors a focused look at contemporary glass art in a working studio-gallery setting. It is a compact art stop, but the material and process make it distinct from the city's larger museums.

Visit if you enjoy craft, design, or want an indoor break while exploring south of downtown. Hours can be more limited than a museum, so confirm before going.
