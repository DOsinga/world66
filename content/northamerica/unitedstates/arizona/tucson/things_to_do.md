---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tucson sights split between the desert outside town — Saguaro National Park east and west, the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum, the trails of Sabino Canyon — and a compact downtown of historic buildings and barrios. The Mission San Xavier del Bac, a few miles south, is the single must-do.

For the city's neighbourhood character, walk the [Downtown](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/tucson/downtown), [University](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/tucson/university_district), [Fourth Avenue](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/tucson/fourth_avenue) and [Barrio Viejo](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/tucson/barrio_viejo) districts.
