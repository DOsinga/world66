The Tucson Rodeo — La Fiesta de los Vaqueros — has been running since 1925 and is one of the top professional rodeos on the PRCA circuit. Nine days in late February with bull riding, bareback bronc, steer wrestling, calf roping, and barrel racing.

The Tucson Rodeo Parade on the first morning is the largest non-motorised parade in the world: 150 horse-drawn wagons and carriages, mariachi bands, marching schools, and a couple of buffalo. The Rodeo Parade Museum (open during rodeo days) holds the wagon collection year-round.
