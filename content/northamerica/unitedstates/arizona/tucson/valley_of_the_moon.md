A surreal little folk-art wonderland on Tucson's north side — a half-acre of grottos, painted statues, miniature castles, and twisting paths built by an amateur visionary over fifty years. The site is now run by volunteers and only opens for occasional storytelling events and tours, mostly in autumn and around Halloween.

Check the website for open dates — it is hard to predict but worth catching if your visit lines up. Free admission, donations encouraged.
