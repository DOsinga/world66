Willcox Pioneer Cemetery is an atmospheric old burial ground with a direct link to Arizona frontier history. Warren Baxter Earp, the youngest of the Earp brothers of Tombstone fame, is buried here. Wear sturdy shoes and be alert for desert hazards around the abandoned plots.
