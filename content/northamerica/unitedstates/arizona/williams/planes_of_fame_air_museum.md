Planes of Fame Air Museum near Valle is a useful stop for aviation-minded travelers
driving between Williams and the Grand Canyon. Its collection focuses on historic
aircraft, with the dry Arizona setting giving the machines a frontier-airfield feel
rather than a polished urban museum atmosphere.

It is not essential for everyone, but it earns its place if your route already takes you
north from Williams. Allow enough time to look closely at the aircraft and interpretive
material; a rushed stop misses the mechanical detail that makes the place interesting.
