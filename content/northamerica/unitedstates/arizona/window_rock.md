Window Rock is the capital of the Navajo Nation, close to the Arizona-New Mexico line. It is small in population but large in meaning: a government centre, a cultural stop, and a place named for the red sandstone arch north of the civic buildings.

The best visit is compact and thoughtful. Start at [Window Rock Tribal Park and Veterans Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/window_rock/window_rock_tribal_park), then spend time at the [Navajo Nation Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/window_rock/navajo_nation_museum) and the [Navajo Nation Zoo and Botanical Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/window_rock/navajo_nation_zoo_and_botanical_park). The council chamber adds another layer: government architecture designed to sit in conversation with Navajo forms and the surrounding rock.

Window Rock is not a polished resort town, and that is the point. It works as a respectful half-day stop on a wider Navajo Nation trip, especially if you are moving between Gallup, Canyon de Chelly, Hubbell Trading Post, or the Four Corners country.
