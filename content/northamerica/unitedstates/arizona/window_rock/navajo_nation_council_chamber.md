The Navajo Nation Council Chamber is the symbolic heart of the capital campus. Built in the 1930s, it uses red sandstone, heavy timber, and a form that evokes a monumental hogan rather than a standard statehouse.

Access depends on tribal business and tour availability, but even from outside it helps explain why Window Rock matters. This is not just a town near a rock formation; it is a working capital.
