Homolovi State Park protects ancestral Hopi pueblo sites on the open desert north of Winslow. Short trails lead to archaeological areas, pottery scatters, and wide views that connect the modern town to a much older human landscape.

Treat the sites with care: stay on marked routes and leave pottery where it lies. The park turns Winslow from a Route 66 photo stop into a worthwhile northern Arizona detour.
