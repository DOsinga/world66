Old Trails Museum gives Winslow more weight than the corner photo. Its exhibits cover the Santa Fe Railway, the Harvey Girls, Route 66, ranching, and the town's links with Navajo and Hopi communities.

The museum sits in the historic district opposite Standin' on the Corner Park. It is small, free, and exactly the kind of local museum that makes a road-trip stop feel less disposable.
