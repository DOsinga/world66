Standin' on the Corner Park is Winslow's unavoidable stop: a statue, mural, flatbed Ford, and corner sign turning one line from "Take It Easy" into a small Route 66 pilgrimage. It is quick, kitschy, and more fun than it has any right to be.

Come early or late if you want the corner without a line of cameras. The Old Trails Museum is just across the street, so the photo can easily become a short walk through the town's deeper railroad and highway story.
