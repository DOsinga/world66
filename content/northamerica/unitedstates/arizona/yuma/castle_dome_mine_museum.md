Castle Dome Mine Museum is a desert ghost-town collection built around the old Castle
Dome mining district northeast of Yuma. Cabins, workshops, tools, signs, and salvaged
interiors create a rough, sun-bleached picture of Arizona mining life that feels more
tactile than a glass-case museum.

The distance from Yuma is part of the experience: you are heading into open desert, not
across town. Bring water, check seasonal hours, and allow time to wander. The site is
strongest for travelers who like frontier history, machinery, and places where the
landscape still does half the storytelling.
