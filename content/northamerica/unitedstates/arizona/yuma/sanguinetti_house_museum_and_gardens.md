Sanguinetti House Museum and Gardens occupies a historic adobe house dating from the 1870s. The exhibits cover Yuma and the lower Colorado River region, with material stretching from early Spanish exploration to the modern borderlands.

The house is a useful companion to Yuma Territorial Prison and Colorado River State Historic Park: smaller and quieter, but good for understanding the merchants, families and civic life that shaped old Yuma.
