The Silver Spur Rodeo is one of Yuma's better-known western events, bringing riding, roping, and arena spectacle into a city that still has strong desert ranching associations. It is a seasonal experience rather than a year-round attraction, but it gives visitors a direct taste of local cowboy culture.

If your timing matches the rodeo calendar, it can be more memorable than another indoor stop. Expect crowds, dust, announcers, and the practical pageantry of a community event that is as social as it is sporting.
