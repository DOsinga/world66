---

Yuma's appeal is rooted in its frontier history and its unlikely position at the junction of the Colorado River, two states, and Mexico. The [Yuma Territorial Prison](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/yuma/yuma_territorial_prison) is the obvious starting point, followed by the [Colorado River State Historic Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/yuma/colorado_river_state_historic_park) on the riverfront. Outside town, the [Kofa National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/arizona/yuma/kofa_national_wildlife_refuge) offers serious desert wilderness just 40 miles north.
