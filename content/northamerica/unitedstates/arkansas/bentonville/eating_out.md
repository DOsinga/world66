---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bentonville has a food scene that punches well above the weight of an Arkansas city its size. The Walton family investment in the area and the influx of professionals working for Walmart suppliers and cycling-sector businesses has created demand for good restaurants. Downtown has farm-to-table options, good barbecue, and several places with creative menus. The best concentration is within walking distance of the courthouse square.
