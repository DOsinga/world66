The restaurant inside Crystal Bridges has a menu that leans on Arkansas and Ozark food traditions — catfish, pork belly, local vegetables, cornbread — and takes them seriously. It is a full-service restaurant that can be visited without a museum ticket. Reservations are recommended for dinner. The setting inside the museum building with views of the wooded ravine is as much a reason to eat here as the food itself.
