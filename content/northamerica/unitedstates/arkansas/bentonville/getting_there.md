---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Northwest Arkansas Regional Airport (XNA) is the closest airport, about 25km south of Bentonville near Fayetteville. It has direct flights to many US hub airports including Dallas, Chicago, Atlanta, Denver, and New York. The airport is compact and straightforward. Car rental is the standard option from the airport — Bentonville has limited public transport and the region is spread across multiple cities.

Driving from Dallas takes about 5 hours, from Kansas City about 3.5 hours, and from Little Rock about 3 hours. The city is in the northwestern corner of Arkansas, close to the Oklahoma and Missouri borders.
