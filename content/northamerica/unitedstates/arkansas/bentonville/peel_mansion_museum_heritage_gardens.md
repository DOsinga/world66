Peel Mansion Museum & Heritage Gardens preserves an 1870s Italianate house built for Colonel Samuel West Peel, one of northwest Arkansas's prominent post-Civil War figures. The interiors, outbuildings, and landscaped gardens give Bentonville a historic-house stop distinct from its better-known contemporary art scene.

It is a useful pairing with Crystal Bridges because it shows the older civic and domestic history of the region. The gardens make the visit pleasant even if you only have time for a short walk around the grounds.
