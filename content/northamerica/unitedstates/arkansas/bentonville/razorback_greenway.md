The Razorback Greenway is a 38-mile paved trail that connects Bentonville to Fayetteville without touching a road. For cyclists it is the primary way to move around the Northwest Arkansas region — from Bentonville downtown you can reach Crystal Bridges, Walmart corporate headquarters, the University of Arkansas, and dozens of other destinations entirely on the trail. The surface is smooth, the grade is manageable, and bike rental is available in town for those without their own bikes.
