Scott Family Amazeum is a hands-on children's museum in Bentonville's museum district, close enough to pair with Crystal Bridges or the surrounding trails. Its exhibits are built around making, experimenting, and play, which gives families a strong alternative to a conventional art stop.

For adults travelling without children it may be a lower priority, but for family trips it clears the threshold easily. It is one of the places that makes Bentonville feel unusually well supplied with cultural attractions for a city of its size.
