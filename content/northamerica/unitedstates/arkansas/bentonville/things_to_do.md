title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bentonville has two anchor attractions — Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art and the Walmart Museum — that draw visitors from across the country, and a trail network that brings serious cyclists from further afield. The combination is unusual: an Ozark college town that has become a legitimate art destination and cycling hub, funded almost entirely by Walmart money. The downtown square is pleasant for a morning, and the surrounding hills give context to the cycling obsession.
