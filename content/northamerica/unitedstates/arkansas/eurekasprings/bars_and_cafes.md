---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Eureka Springs has a lively bar scene for a small Ozark town. The concentration of artists, musicians, and independent spirits in the community means there is always live music somewhere in the evenings, and the bars tend to be genuinely local rather than tourist-facing. The historic downtown has several good options within easy walking distance of each other, and the scene stays active well into the night on weekends. The town is welcoming and inclusive — Eureka Springs has a long tradition of being a safe and open community for LGBTQ+ visitors and residents.
