Chelsea's Corner is one of the most reliably good spots in Eureka Springs for live music and a drink. The bar occupies a Victorian corner storefront in the heart of the historic district and has been a community gathering point for years. The atmosphere is relaxed and unpretentious — local musicians play most evenings, ranging from Ozark folk and bluegrass to rock, and the crowd is a genuine mix of residents and visitors. The beer selection is solid, with several Arkansas craft beers on tap alongside the standards.

The corner location gives the bar good people-watching potential from its windows, and the building itself — original pressed-tin ceilings, worn wooden floors — is exactly the kind of space you hope to find in a Victorian hill town.
