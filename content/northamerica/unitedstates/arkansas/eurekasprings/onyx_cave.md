Onyx Cave is one of the oldest tour caves in Arkansas. The self-guided route uses a short-range FM receiver for interpretation and stays cool throughout the year.

The cave is an easy natural side trip from Eureka Springs, with formations such as the Amethyst Angel Wings making it especially popular for photos.
