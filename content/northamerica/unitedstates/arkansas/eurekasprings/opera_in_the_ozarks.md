Opera in the Ozarks is a summer opera program at Inspiration Point, a lookout above the White River Valley and Beaver Lake. Young singers perform a rotating season of operas here, making it one of the more unexpected cultural stops around Eureka Springs.

Even if opera is not the main reason you came to the Ozarks, the setting gives the evening a lift. Check the seasonal schedule before making the drive.
