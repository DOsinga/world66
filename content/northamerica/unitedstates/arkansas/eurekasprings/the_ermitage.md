The Ermitage is one of the most ambitious restaurants in Eureka Springs, occupying a converted Victorian house on Spring Street with a short, seasonally changing menu focused on Ozark ingredients. The kitchen sources locally — Ozark-raised beef, wild ramps in spring, local honey, Arkansas cheeses — and the cooking is contemporary without being precious. The dining room is candlelit and comfortable rather than formal, and the wine list is well considered for a town this size.

The restaurant is popular enough that reservations are essential on weekends and advisable any night in summer. If the main dining room is full, the bar often has space for walk-ins who want to order from the full menu.
