Fort Smith sits at the confluence of the Arkansas and Poteau rivers, right on the Oklahoma border. Arkansas's second-largest city has a frontier past that's hard to shake — this was the last outpost of law before Indian Territory, where "Hanging Judge" Isaac Parker held court and deputy marshals rode out after outlaws. That roughness has mellowed into a mid-sized city with a walkable downtown, a growing restaurant scene, and enough history to fill a solid weekend.

The [Fort Smith National Historic Site](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/fortsmith/fort_smith_national_historic_site) is the main draw, preserving the remains of two frontier-era forts and Judge Parker's courtroom and jail. The Trail of Tears passed through here, and the site does a thoughtful job covering that painful history alongside the marshals-and-outlaws story. Just down the river, the [United States Marshals Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/fortsmith/us_marshals_museum) opened in 2023 in a striking star-shaped building on the riverfront, tracing the history of America's oldest federal law enforcement agency. The [Belle Grove Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/fortsmith/belle_grove_historic_district) northwest of downtown is worth an afternoon on foot — 22 blocks of Victorian houses built by the merchants and lawyers who grew wealthy during the federal court era, including [Miss Laura's Social Club](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/fortsmith/miss_lauras), the only former brothel on the National Register of Historic Places. South Chaffee, just outside the city, is home to the [Chaffee Barbershop Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/fortsmith/chaffee_barbershop_museum), where Elvis Presley received his Army induction haircut in 1958.

The Arkansas River provides the city's outdoor anchor — the Riverfront Trail runs along the water between downtown and the [Janet Huckabee Arkansas River Valley Nature Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/fortsmith/janet_huckabee_nature_center), a free wildlife facility with native fish aquariums and river-access trails. The Garrison Avenue downtown strip has local restaurants, bars, and independent shops worth an evening's wander.
