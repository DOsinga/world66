---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Fort Smith Regional Airport (FSM) has connections to Dallas/Fort Worth and Chicago via American Eagle and United. Driving is the most common way to arrive: Fort Smith sits at the junction of I-40 and I-49, making it easy to reach from Little Rock (two hours east), Tulsa (two hours northwest), and Fayetteville (one hour north). Greyhound buses stop in Fort Smith on the Dallas–Fayetteville route.
