---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Fort Smith's main draw is its frontier history — the federal court where Judge Isaac Parker sent 79 men to the gallows, the U.S. Marshals Museum on the riverfront, and the Belle Grove Historic District with its grand Victorian homes. The Arkansas River offers walking and cycling on the Riverfront Trail, and the Janet Huckabee Nature Center is a fine place to see local wildlife.
