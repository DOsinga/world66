A small but well-curated museum on Central Avenue, dedicated to the era when Hot Springs functioned
as a national playground for organised crime. Exhibits walk through Owney Madden's quiet retirement
in town, the federal raids of the 1960s, and the way the springs themselves drew gangsters in for
medicinal soaks alongside the gambling.

Guided tours include working slot machines, vintage roulette wheels, and a recreated speakeasy. The
quality of the storytelling is well above what you would expect from a tourist-strip museum — the
guides know the local history and tell it with relish.
