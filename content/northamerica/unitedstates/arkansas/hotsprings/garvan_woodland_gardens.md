A 210-acre botanical garden draped over a peninsula on Lake Hamilton, about ten minutes south of
downtown. The setting is the main event — pine ridges, sandstone outcrops, and long views over the
water — but there is plenty of design too. The Anthony Chapel, a 57-foot all-glass wedding chapel
built among the trees, is the signature piece.

Best in spring for the azalea hillsides (over 160,000 plants), in autumn for the maples, and in
December for the holiday lights show. A network of bridges, the Children's Adventure Garden, and a
Japanese-influenced rock garden round out the visit. Allow at least two hours.
