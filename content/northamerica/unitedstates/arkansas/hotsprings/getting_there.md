---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Hot Springs sits about an hour southwest of **Little Rock**, the nearest major airport (LIT/Bill and
Hillary Clinton National). The drive in is straightforward on US-70 or Interstate 30 then US-270;
there's no train, and bus services are minimal. Most visitors arrive by car, and a car is essentially
required once you are there — the city is spread out along the lake corridors.

The small Memorial Field Airport (HOT) handles only general aviation. Renting a car at Little Rock
and driving is the standard approach.
