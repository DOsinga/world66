A steel observation tower on the summit of Hot Springs Mountain, reached by a short drive or a steep
walk up from Bathhouse Row. The current tower dates from 1983 — the third structure on the site since
the 1870s — and rises 216 feet above an already prominent ridge. On a clear day the view stretches
out over the entire Ouachita range, with the city laid out neatly below.

There's a small charge to take the elevator up. Two observation decks (one enclosed, one open) make
this an obvious stop for anyone wanting to understand the geography of the place — you can see how
the springs sit in a tight valley pinched between Hot Springs Mountain and West Mountain.
