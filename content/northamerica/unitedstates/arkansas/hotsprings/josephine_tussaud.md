A classic American roadside wax museum, opened in 1962 and run with the same matter-of-fact
showmanship ever since. Around 100 figures cover the predictable territory — presidents, movie
stars, and historical scenes — but there's a strong streak of the macabre, with grisly biblical
tableaux and torture scenes in the basement Chamber of Horrors.

It is not pretending to be art. The fun is in the slightly off-model faces, the dim lighting, and
the sense that nothing about the place has changed in decades. Pair it with the Gangster Museum and
Tiny Town for a tour of the Central Avenue tourist strip at its 1960s peak.
