A combined amusement and water park on the east side of Hot Springs, with around 15 mechanical rides
and a separate Crystal Falls water park. The headline coaster is the Big Bad John, a wooden-feeling
steel ride that loops out over the parking lot.

Operating roughly from spring break through October, with concerts on summer Saturday nights. It's
not Six Flags, but the lines are short and the place is well-shaded by oaks — a workable family
day if travelling with kids, especially when summer temperatures hit the upper 30s C.
