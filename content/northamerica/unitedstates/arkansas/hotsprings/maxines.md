A small, dark, dependable live-music room on the upper stretch of Central Avenue. The booking
lineup leans into roots music — Americana, blues, bluegrass, the occasional rockabilly act — and the
crowd is a friendly mix of locals and visitors. Beer prices are reasonable, the staff are quick.

If you're staying downtown for a couple of nights, this is the obvious bar to drop into for live
music without having to drive out to the lake.
