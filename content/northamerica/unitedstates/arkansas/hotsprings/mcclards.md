The Hot Springs barbecue institution — open since 1928, family-run, and one of Bill Clinton's
favourite places to eat when he was governor and after. The signature is the Tamale Spread: chili,
tamales, beans, chopped beef, Fritos, and cheese piled on a plate. Ribs and chopped pork sandwiches
are the other standards.

The dining room is plain, the service brisk, and there's almost always a line at lunch. Cash and
cards both work now, but the place still feels like 1955 inside. Closed Sunday and Monday.
