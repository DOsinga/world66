A serious hands-on science museum tucked into the woods west of town, with over 100 interactive
exhibits across natural science, physics, energy, and biology. The standout is the Bob Wheeler Tesla
Coil — the largest in any museum in the country, used in regular high-voltage demonstrations that
draw lightning bolts across the room.

The site also includes a tree canopy walk through 21 acres of forest, a digital dome theatre, and an
oversized walking-bridge sculpture. It's geared toward families with kids but is genuinely
interesting for adults too — a welcome counterweight to the wax museums and gift shops downtown.
