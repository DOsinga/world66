One of two bathhouses still operating on Bathhouse Row. Quapaw was restored and reopened in 2008 as
a public spa rather than a private bath suite — the four communal mineral pools are kept at staggered
temperatures from a comfortable 35°C up to a punishing 40°C. The Spanish tile dome over the lobby
dates to the 1922 original.

This is the easy choice for visitors who want to soak in the thermal water without committing to the
traditional ritual at Buckstaff. Walk-ins are usually possible, swimsuits are required, and you can
also book private tubs and massages. A small cafe and an excellent steam cave round out the visit.
