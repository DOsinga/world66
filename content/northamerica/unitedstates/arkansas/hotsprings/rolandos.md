A Cuban-Nicaraguan kitchen on Central Avenue that has stayed reliably good since the 1990s. The
upstairs dining room opens onto a vine-shaded courtyard with a small fountain — a welcome shift in
mood from the streetfront below. Plantains, churrasco steak, and roast pork are the standards;
specials lean Latin American across the map.

Margaritas and mojitos are the drinks to order. The vibe is casual but not cheap-feeling, and the
courtyard is a destination of its own on a warm evening.
