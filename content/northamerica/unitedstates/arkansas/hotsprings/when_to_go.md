---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (April–May) is the sweet spot — pleasant temperatures, full water in the Ouachita rivers and
waterfalls, the Garvan Woodland azaleas in full bloom, and the climax of the Oaklawn racing season.
The Arkansas Derby in mid-April is the busiest weekend of the year; book hotels well in advance.

Summer is hot and humid, with daytime highs in the low to mid 30s C. The lake-based half of the
visit (Hamilton, Catherine) makes sense in this season; soaking in 40°C bath water less so.
Autumn brings cooler air and decent foliage colour on the surrounding mountains, especially in late
October. Winter is mild compared to the rest of the country — daytime temperatures often hit 10–15°C
in January, ideal weather for a hot soak — but bathhouses and outdoor attractions run shorter hours.
