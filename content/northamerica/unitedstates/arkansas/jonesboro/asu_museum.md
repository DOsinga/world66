The Arkansas State University Museum is a useful indoor stop on the A-State campus. Its exhibits cover regional history, natural history, archaeology, and culture, with enough local focus to make it more than a generic college museum.

Admission is free, so it pairs well with the nearby Bradbury Art Museum.
