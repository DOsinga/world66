Bradbury Art Museum sits in the Fowler Center on the Arkansas State University campus. It focuses on contemporary art and changing exhibitions, so the value of a visit depends on what is currently installed.

Because admission is free and it is close to the ASU Museum, it is an easy add-on if you are already on campus.
