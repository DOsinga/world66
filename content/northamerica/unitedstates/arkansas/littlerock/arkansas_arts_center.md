The Arkansas Arts Center, which reopened in 2022 after a major renovation and expansion as the Arkansas Museum of Fine Arts, holds one of the country's stronger regional art collections. The works on paper collection is genuinely distinguished — over 10,000 drawings, watercolours, and prints spanning five centuries — and the painting galleries include works by Monet, Degas, and a solid representation of American modernism.

The building, redesigned by Studio Gang, adds a striking copper-roofed wing to the MacArthur Park complex. The museum sits in MacArthur Park, a historic neighbourhood just east of the State Capitol, named for General Douglas MacArthur who was born here. Admission to the permanent collection is free; temporary exhibitions carry a charge.
