The Arkansas Governor's Mansion is a formal Georgian Revival residence south of downtown Little Rock. It is not as essential as the Clinton Presidential Center or the State Capitol, but it adds useful context to the city's political geography and older residential streets.

Access is limited and security-conscious because it remains an official residence, so do not assume casual drop-in touring. Even from outside, the mansion works as part of a walk or drive through the Governor's Mansion Historic District.
