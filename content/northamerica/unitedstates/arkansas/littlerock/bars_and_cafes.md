---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Little Rock's bar scene is concentrated in two areas: the River Market District, which fills on weekend nights, and the Heights neighbourhood, which is more of a locals-after-work destination. The city has a solid craft brewery presence relative to its size.

White Water Tavern on West 7th Street is the city's best music venue — a dive bar that has hosted touring artists and local acts for decades, surviving fires and floods and the general entropy that closes similar places elsewhere. Lost Forty Brewing is the flagship of the local craft beer scene, with a large taproom in the River Market and a rotating selection of Arkansas-brewed beers. For coffee, Mylo Coffee on South Main is the neighbourhood standard, with good espresso and a room full of laptops.
