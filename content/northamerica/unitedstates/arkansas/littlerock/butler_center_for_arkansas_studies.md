The Butler Center is the research-minded stop for anyone who wants Arkansas history in more depth than a quick museum panel can give. It sits beside the main Central Arkansas Library, in a mix of late-19th-century brick buildings and newer library space.

The collections focus on manuscripts, maps, photographs, newspapers, and local archives, so this is best for travellers with a real interest in state history or family research. Exhibitions and public programs make it more accessible than a standard archive, but it is still a quieter, slower visit than the nearby Historic Arkansas Museum.
