---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Little Rock has a restaurant scene that punches above its weight for a city of its size. The River Market District and the South Main (SoMa) neighbourhood have the densest concentration of options. Arkansas has strong BBQ and soul food traditions, and the city also supports a range of upscale restaurants that draw from local ingredients.

South on Main is the anchor of the SoMa neighbourhood dining scene, serving Southern-inflected dishes in a converted building that also functions as a community arts space. Cheers in the Heights is a neighbourhood favourite for burgers and casual eating. For BBQ, Whole Hog Cafe has multiple locations and is a reliable standard-bearer for the local style — wet, vinegar-touched, slow-smoked. The Farmer's Table, in the River Market, does Arkansas produce-driven cooking worth seeking out for lunch.
