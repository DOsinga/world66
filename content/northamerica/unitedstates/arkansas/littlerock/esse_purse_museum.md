The Esse Purse Museum is exactly what it says: a museum dedicated to the history of women's purses, tracing the social, economic, and political history of women in America through the bags they carried. It sounds like a novelty, but the curators take the concept seriously and the result is surprisingly engaging — a handbag from the 1930s Depression era tells a different story than one from the 1970s women's liberation movement.

The collection spans from the early 20th century to the 1980s, with each decade getting its own room designed to evoke its era. Small, admission charged, and located in the River Market area. It tends to get better reviews from visitors who approach it sceptically than from those who think they already know what to expect.
