---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Little Rock Clinton National Airport (LIT) handles direct flights from about a dozen US cities including Atlanta, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, and Washington D.C. It is a 10-minute drive from downtown; taxis and ride-shares are readily available.

By car, Little Rock sits at the junction of I-30 and I-40, making it easily reachable from Dallas (about 5 hours), Memphis (2.5 hours), and Tulsa (4 hours). Amtrak's Texas Eagle service connects Little Rock to Chicago and to Dallas/San Antonio, running three times per week in each direction. The train station is downtown, within walking distance of the River Market.
