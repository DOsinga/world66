The Heifer International Center sits near the Clinton Presidential Center and brings a different kind of visitor stop to Little Rock's riverfront. The organization is known for anti-hunger and agricultural development work, while the headquarters building is often noted for its sustainable design.

It is a selective stop, best for travellers interested in development, green building, or social enterprise. Pair it with the nearby riverfront and Clinton complex rather than crossing town for it alone.
