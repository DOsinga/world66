The Historic Arkansas Museum preserves a cluster of antebellum buildings in the heart of downtown, including six original structures from the 1820s–1850s — among the oldest surviving buildings in the city. The museum focuses on Arkansas history and material culture, with particular strength in frontier-era craftsmanship: the collection of Arkansas-made knives, including Bowie knives, is one of the best in the country.

The restored buildings include two log structures, a townhouse, and a druggist's shop, all preserved in a courtyard setting that feels incongruous surrounded by downtown office towers. Admission to the museum galleries is free; the period buildings require a small fee. Worth an hour for anyone interested in early American frontier history.
