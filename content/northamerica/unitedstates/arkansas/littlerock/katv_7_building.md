The KATV-7 Building is a 1928 downtown landmark built for the W.B. Worthen Banking Corporation and later used by Little Rock's ABC television affiliate. It is mainly an exterior architecture stop, but its National Register status and layered banking-and-broadcast history make it worth noticing while walking between the River Market and the city's older civic buildings.
