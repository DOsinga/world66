The MacArthur Museum of Arkansas Military History occupies the Tower Building of the Little Rock Arsenal, the birthplace of General Douglas MacArthur. Exhibits cover Arkansas soldiers, the Civil War, world wars and the changing role of the arsenal grounds in the city.

The building is as important as the collection. Set in MacArthur Park, it pairs easily with the Arkansas Museum of Fine Arts and gives downtown Little Rock a deeper historical layer than the riverfront alone provides.
