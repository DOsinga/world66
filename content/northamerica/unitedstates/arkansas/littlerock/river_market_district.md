The River Market District runs along the Arkansas River waterfront east of downtown and contains Little Rock's best concentration of restaurants, bars, and live music venues. The area takes its name from the River Market Pavilion, a covered market hall where local farmers and vendors sell produce and food most days of the week.

The Riverfront Park alongside the market offers a riverwalk with views across to North Little Rock, where the Old Mill — the one seen in the opening credits of *Gone with the Wind* — sits in a small park. The district is walkable from the Clinton Presidential Center and the Old State House, making it a natural base for a day of sightseeing. Weekend nights bring live music and outdoor crowds along President Clinton Avenue.
