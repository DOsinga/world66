Trapnall Hall is a handsome Greek Revival house near the Arkansas Governor's Mansion and MacArthur Park, connected to Little Rock's nineteenth-century political and social life. It is smaller than the city's major museums, but it adds texture to the historic core.

The house is most useful for travellers already exploring the Quapaw Quarter, where architecture tells much of the story. Check access before going, as historic houses can have event schedules, but the exterior and setting still fit well into a downtown heritage walk.
