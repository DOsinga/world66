---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring (March–May) is the best time to visit Little Rock. Temperatures are mild — typically 15–25°C — the dogwoods and redbuds are blooming across the Quapaw Quarter, and the outdoor sculpture garden and riverfront are at their best. The Arkansas Riverfest, a large outdoor music and food festival, runs in late May or early June.

Summer is hot and humid, with temperatures regularly above 35°C in July and August, which makes the indoor museums more appealing and the outdoor sightseeing somewhat taxing. Fall (September–November) brings relief and pleasant walking weather. Winter is mild by northern standards — temperatures rarely drop below freezing — and the city is uncrowded.
