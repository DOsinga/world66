Wildwood Park for the Performing Arts combines gardens, woodland, a small lake and performance spaces on the western edge of Little Rock. It is a venue as much as a park, with concerts, theatre, festivals and community events shaping the calendar.

It is most rewarding when something is scheduled, but the grounds can also make a pleasant detour for travellers with a car. Check the programme before driving out; the difference between a quiet garden walk and a lively performance night is substantial.
