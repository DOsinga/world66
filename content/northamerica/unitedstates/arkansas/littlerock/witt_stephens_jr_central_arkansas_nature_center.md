The Witt Stephens Jr. Central Arkansas Nature Center introduces the state's rivers, forests, fish, and wildlife through aquariums, exhibits, and taxidermy displays. It is an easy, family-friendly stop in the River Market area, and it links neatly with a walk on the Arkansas River Trail.
