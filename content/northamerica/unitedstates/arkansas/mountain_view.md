Mountain View is a small town of about 3,000 people in the Ozark Mountains of north-central Arkansas, and it calls itself the "Folk Music Capital of the World" with some justification. Music is everywhere here -- on the [courthouse square](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/mountain_view/courthouse_square) on weekend evenings, at the [Ozark Folk Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/mountain_view/ozark_folk_center), and in impromptu jam sessions on front porches.

The [Ozark Folk Center State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/mountain_view/ozark_folk_center) is the main attraction, a living-history park dedicated to preserving Ozark mountain crafts and music. Artisans demonstrate traditional skills -- blacksmithing, broom-making, candle-dipping, lye soap -- and the concert hall hosts live folk and bluegrass performances most evenings. The park also has accommodation and a restaurant serving Ozark-style cooking.

The surrounding countryside is classic Ozark scenery: forested hills, clear spring-fed creeks, and limestone bluffs. [Blanchard Springs Caverns](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/mountain_view/blanchard_springs_caverns), a spectacular show cave managed by the Forest Service, is about fifteen miles north. The town sits on the banks of the White River watershed, and [float trips](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/mountain_view/white_river_float) on the nearby rivers are popular in summer.
