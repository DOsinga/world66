Blanchard Springs Caverns is one of the most spectacular show caves in the United States, set in the Ozark-St. Francis National Forest about fifteen miles north of Mountain View. The cave is on three levels and is still actively forming — water still flows through the lower chambers, stalactites and flowstone still grow. The Forest Service runs two main guided tours: the easier Dripstone Trail (about 700 feet of well-lit walkways) and the longer Discovery Trail (closer to a mile and a half, with stairs).

Above ground, the recreation area is a small Forest Service complex with a swimming hole, a campground, and a short trail to the spring itself — a turquoise pool that emerges from the limestone cliff face and is the source of Mirror Lake. Buy timed tour tickets at the visitor centre. Bring a light jacket — the cave is in the mid-50s Fahrenheit year-round.
