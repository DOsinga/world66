---
title: Things to Do in Pea Ridge
type: section
---

Pea Ridge is primarily a destination for Civil War history. The national military park is the main draw, and it's a good one — one of the best-preserved battlefields in the United States, with enough terrain and historical depth to keep a serious visitor engaged for half a day. The town itself is small; the surrounding Ozark foothills offer hiking and scenic driving if you want to extend your stay.
