Springdale is the fourth-largest city in [Arkansas](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas), located in the Ozark Mountains in the northwest corner of the state. It is the headquarters of Tyson Foods and often called the chicken capital of the world — the poultry industry dominates the local economy and has drawn a large Latino immigrant community, which in turn has given Springdale some of the best Mexican and Central American food in the region. The [Emma Avenue Latin food scene](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/springdale/latin_food_scene) is a genuine revelation: pupuserias, taquerias, and tamale shops that would be unremarkable in Los Angeles but are extraordinary in the Ozarks.

The town is part of the rapidly growing Northwest Arkansas metro area, alongside [Fayetteville](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/fayetteville), Rogers, and Bentonville. The [Arts Center of the Ozarks](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/springdale/arts_center_of_the_ozarks) hosts theater and gallery exhibitions, and the [Shiloh Museum of Ozark History](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/springdale/shiloh_museum) is a well-curated small museum covering the region's frontier and agricultural past. The [Arkansas and Missouri Railroad](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/springdale/arkansas_missouri_railroad) runs scenic excursion trains through the Ozark countryside — the route through the Boston Mountains is particularly beautiful in autumn.

The surrounding Ozarks countryside is the real draw. The [Razorback Regional Greenway](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/springdale/razorback_greenway), a 36-mile paved trail, connects Springdale to Fayetteville and Bentonville and is excellent for cycling. [Beaver Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/springdale/beaver_lake), about 30 minutes east, has swimming, fishing, and kayaking. The area's [mountain biking trails](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/springdale/mountain_biking), particularly those in the Slaughter Pen and Hobbs State Park systems nearby, are considered among the best in the country.
