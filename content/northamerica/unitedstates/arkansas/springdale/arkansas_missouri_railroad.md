The Arkansas and Missouri Railroad runs scenic excursion trains through the Ozark countryside on vintage diesel locomotives pulling historic passenger cars. The route from Springdale heads south through the Boston Mountains to Van Buren, passing through some of the most dramatic valley and ridge scenery in the Ozarks.

The fall foliage season — October through mid-November — is the most popular time, when the hardwood forests turn gold and red along the entire route. The line operates weekend excursions during the season and themed trips at other times of year. The Boston Mountains section is the highlight: deep valleys, steep grades, and forested ridges that give a very different perspective on the landscape than any road in the area.
