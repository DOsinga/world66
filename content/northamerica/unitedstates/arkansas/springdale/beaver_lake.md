Beaver Lake sits about 30 minutes east of Springdale in the Ozark National Forest, a large reservoir with over 480 miles of forested shoreline and excellent water quality. It is the primary outdoor recreation destination for Northwest Arkansas residents, offering swimming, powerboating, kayaking, sailing, and some of the best bass fishing in Arkansas.

The lake has multiple public access points; Rocky Branch Park and Prairie Creek Park on the western shore are closest to Springdale. The surrounding forest is excellent for hiking — the Ozark Highlands Trail passes near the lake, and the fall foliage here is among the best in the Ozarks. Houseboats are available to rent from marinas on the lake for multi-day trips.
