---
title: "Things to Do in Springdale"
type: section
---

Springdale offers a mix of Ozark outdoor activity, regional history, and the surprisingly diverse food culture that has grown alongside its large immigrant population. The Shiloh Museum of Ozark History is the best starting point for understanding the area's frontier past. The Razorback Greenway trail system connects the city to the rest of Northwest Arkansas, and the surrounding mountains offer excellent mountain biking, hiking, and lake access within thirty minutes in almost any direction.
