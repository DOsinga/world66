The Draughon-Moore Ace of Clubs House grounds also include a smaller secondary cottage and gardens that are part of the museum tour. The carriage house has been restored as an exhibit on Texarkana's Victorian-era boom, when the railroad junction made the town briefly very wealthy.

If you have already paid for the main house tour, the cottage is included; otherwise it is not worth a separate visit. The gardens, however, are open to the street and make a pleasant ten-minute detour from downtown.
