The Texarkana Federal Courthouse and Post Office sits directly on State Line Avenue, the only federal building in the country occupying two states. The exterior is a dignified Beaux-Arts structure, and the state line stripe painted across the pavement outside is the city's signature photo opportunity. The interior handles federal business for both Arkansas and Texas; the building genuinely functions as a single facility split by a state boundary.
