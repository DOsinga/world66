title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The [State Line Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/texarkana/state_line_courthouse) is the inevitable first stop — the only federal building in the United States that sits simultaneously in two states, with a painted stripe across the pavement for the obligatory two-states-at-once photograph. The [Perot Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/texarkana/perot_theatre), a restored 1924 movie palace funded by Texarkana-born Ross Perot, hosts touring productions and is worth a look for the ornate Atmospheric Theatre interior. The [Ace of Clubs House](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/texarkana/ace_of_clubs_house) is a genuinely odd 1885 mansion built in the shape of a playing card club symbol — three circular bays under a dome — now a furnished period museum. The [Museum of Regional History](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/texarkana/museum_of_regional_history) covers the Four States area including Scott Joplin's local connections, and the [Regional Arts Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/arkansas/texarkana/regional_arts_center) occupies a handsome WPA-era building with rotating art exhibitions.
