The Los Angeles County Arboretum and Botanic Garden occupies 127 acres of what was once Lucky Baldwin's Rancho Santa Anita. The collection is organised by geography — gardens of Australian, African, South American, and Asian plants, with extensive Mediterranean and Californian sections. A natural spring fills Baldwin Lake at the centre of the property, and free-ranging peacocks have run of the grounds.

The arboretum has been a steady film location since the 1930s; the giant tropical fronds in the Tantor Pool stood in for African jungle in the *Tarzan* films, and the Queen Anne Cottage appeared in the opening credits of *Fantasy Island*. Allow at least three hours; longer for a quiet day with a picnic.
