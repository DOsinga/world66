The Queen Anne Cottage and adjoining Coach Barn stand by Baldwin Lake inside the Arboretum. Built in 1885 for Elias Jackson "Lucky" Baldwin — the rancher, businessman, and gambling promoter who owned the surrounding 13,319-acre Rancho Santa Anita — the cottage is a model of Eastern Victorian taste airlifted into the California ranchlands. The exterior is gingerbread-trimmed clapboard painted white and trimmed in red.

The cottage is fully furnished in 1880s style and opens for guided tours on weekends; interiors are otherwise visible through the windows. Most visitors will recognise it from television: it was the opening shot of *Fantasy Island* throughout the show's run.
