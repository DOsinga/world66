In the San Lorenzo Valley, nestled in the redwoods, is Ben Lomond — named by an early Scottish immigrant who missed his homeland. Highway 9, the lifeblood of the valley, connects the three valley towns of [Felton](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/felton), Ben Lomond, and Boulder Creek with the outside world.

Ben Lomond is home to Park Hall, where the Mountain Community Theater puts on plays several times a year, and [Highlands Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/ben_lomond_califo/highlands_park), which hosts the annual Highland Games every spring — a tradition that goes back to the valley's Scottish founders. The [San Lorenzo River](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/ben_lomond_califo/san_lorenzo_river) runs through town and offers swimming holes in summer. A few kilometres away, the seasonal [Loch Lomond Reservoir](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/ben_lomond_califo/loch_lomond_reservoir) — also named for Scotland — is open for fishing, kayaking, and hiking from spring through summer.

Local colour is on display at establishments like Henflings Tavern, Ciao Bella, and The Tyrolean Inn, which hosts an annual Oktoberfest. All this in a scenic valley with a river running through it and Coast Redwood forest pressing in from every side.
