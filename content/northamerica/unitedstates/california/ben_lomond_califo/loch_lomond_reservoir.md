Loch Lomond Reservoir is a 175-acre body of water in the Santa Cruz Mountains, created in 1963 by damming Newell Creek, a tributary of the San Lorenzo River. The reservoir serves as part of Santa Cruz's water supply, but the surrounding Loch Lomond Recreation Area is open seasonally (April through September) for day use.

The area is popular for trout fishing, rowing, and kayaking on the reservoir — private powerboats are not permitted. Hiking trails ring the shoreline and climb into the surrounding redwood and mixed forest, offering quiet walks away from the summer crowds at better-known Santa Cruz County parks. The boat rental and snack shop operates on summer weekends.

Coordinates are approximate; access is via East Zayante Road from Ben Lomond.
