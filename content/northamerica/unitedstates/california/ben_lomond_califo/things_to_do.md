title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ben Lomond sits in the San Lorenzo Valley surrounded by second-growth Coast Redwood forest, and the outdoors is the main draw. [Loch Lomond Reservoir](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/ben_lomond_califo/loch_lomond_reservoir) — named, like the town, after the Scottish original — is a short drive away and open seasonally for fishing, boating, and hiking along the shore. The [San Lorenzo River](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/ben_lomond_califo/san_lorenzo_river) runs through the valley, offering swimming holes in summer.

The [Highlands Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/ben_lomond_califo/highlands_park) hosts the annual Ben Lomond Highland Games each spring — Scottish athletics, pipe bands, and clan gatherings that honour the valley's Scottish roots. The Mountain Community Theater at Park Hall puts on plays several times a year, drawing performers and audiences from across the Santa Cruz Mountains.

Highway 9 connects Ben Lomond with [Felton](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/felton) to the south — where the [Felton Covered Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felton_Covered_Bridge) and Henry Cowell Redwoods State Park are worth a visit — and Boulder Creek to the north.
