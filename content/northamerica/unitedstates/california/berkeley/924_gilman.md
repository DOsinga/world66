924 Gilman is Berkeley's legendary all-ages punk club, a plain warehouse-like venue with a long reach in underground music. Shows are usually on weekend evenings, and the no-alcohol, no-drugs policy is part of the place's stubbornly independent character.

It is worth checking the calendar if you want a loud, local night out rather than polished nightlife. Entry normally includes a small membership fee on top of the ticket.
