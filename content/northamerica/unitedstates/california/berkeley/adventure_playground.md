Adventure Playground at the Berkeley Marina is a rare surviving example of a hands-on, build-it-yourself playground, where children can climb, hammer, paint, and reshape parts of the play environment under supervision. It feels deliberately rougher and more creative than standard city playgrounds.

For families, it can be one of Berkeley's most memorable low-cost stops, especially when paired with marina views and a walk by the bay. Clothing may get dirty, and opening hours can be limited, so check the city schedule before promising it to children.
