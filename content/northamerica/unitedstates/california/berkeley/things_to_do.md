title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Berkeley rewards walking. The UC campus, [Telegraph Avenue](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/berkeley/telegraph_avenue), and the [Gourmet Ghetto](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/berkeley/gourmet_ghetto) on Shattuck are all within easy reach of each other. The [Campanile](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/berkeley/campanile) — Sather Tower — is visible from most of the city and offers a sweeping bay view from its observation deck. Above the city, [Tilden Regional Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/berkeley/tilden_regional_park) offers trails through the hills with views of the bay; [Indian Rock Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/berkeley/indian_rock_park) is the best viewpoint you can reach on foot from downtown. The [Lawrence Hall of Science](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/berkeley/lawrence_hall_of_science) is particularly good for families.
