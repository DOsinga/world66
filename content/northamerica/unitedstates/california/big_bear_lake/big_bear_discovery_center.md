Big Bear Discovery Center is the San Bernardino National Forest visitor center on the north shore of the lake. It is useful for trail information, Adventure Passes, maps, and ranger-led programmes, especially if you are using Big Bear as a base for hiking rather than just lakefront wandering.

The exhibits are modest, but families and first-time visitors get good orientation here before heading into the forest. It pairs naturally with the lakeshore paths and nearby picnic areas.
