Big Bear Mountain Resort combines the former Snow Summit and Bear Mountain ski areas into a single operation covering around 400 acres of terrain across two adjacent mountains. It is the most accessible ski destination from Los Angeles — about two hours on a clear day, though the drive can extend significantly in winter with traffic and chain requirements on the switchbacks of Highway 18.

The skiing is modest by the standards of Colorado or the Sierra Nevada — top elevation around 2,650 metres, mostly intermediate terrain — but the proximity to LA makes it crowd-friendly and the terrain park scene is strong, drawing serious snowboarders and freestyle skiers. The resort operates snow-making equipment that keeps runs open when natural snow is thin, which it often is in warmer years. Summer operations include mountain biking with lift access, zip-lining, and the Alpine Slide.
