Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park is one of Big Sur's defining stops, famous for the view of McWay Falls dropping toward the cove below the cliffs. The park's coastal side is the image most visitors come for, but the inland canyons and redwood habitat are just as important to its character.

Access and trail conditions can change after storms, fires, and road repairs, so check current state-park notices before making it the centre of a day. Even with limited time, the overlook gives a strong sense of why Big Sur's coastline is treated as a landscape to be protected rather than simply driven through.
