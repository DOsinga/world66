Ventana Wilderness is the wild inland half of Big Sur: steep ridges, redwood canyons, chaparral slopes, and trails that climb quickly away from Highway 1. It is a very different experience from the roadside viewpoints, rewarding hikers who want the coast's drama without staying beside the car.

Conditions matter here. Trails can be damaged by fire, storms, and slides, and water sources are not always reliable, so check current access before setting out. With the right preparation, the wilderness gives Big Sur its depth: ocean views on one side, rough Santa Lucia backcountry on the other.
