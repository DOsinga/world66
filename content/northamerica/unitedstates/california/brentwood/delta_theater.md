The Delta Theater on First Street is a handsomely preserved Art Deco cinema from the 1940s, now operating as a live music and events venue under the name The Delta. The original marquee and facade have been maintained, making it one of downtown Brentwood's most distinctive buildings. Events range from local bands and comedy nights to film screenings. Check the current programme before visiting — it is not a regular daily cinema but a community performance space with a varied schedule.
