Buena Park is well-placed as a base for visiting the rest of Orange County and southern Los Angeles. Disneyland in [Anaheim](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/anaheim) is fifteen minutes east; [Long Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach) and the Queen Mary are twenty minutes south-west; and downtown [Los Angeles](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/losangeles) is half an hour up the 5 freeway, traffic permitting.
