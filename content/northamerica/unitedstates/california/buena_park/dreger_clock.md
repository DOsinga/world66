The Dreger Clock is an extraordinary piece of folk engineering — an 18-foot Black Forest-style clock tower built by Buena Park resident Albert Dreger between 1940 and 1947 entirely from scrap parts. It has more than 250 moving figures, three organs, and chimes that play complete melodies on the hour.

The clock was displayed for years at the Movieland Wax Museum and was at risk of being scrapped when that closed in 2005. It is now preserved at the Buena Park Historical Society's Bacon House Museum on Beach Boulevard, where it operates on demonstration days. Worth checking the museum's website before visiting; opening hours are limited.
