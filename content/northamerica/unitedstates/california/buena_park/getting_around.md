---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Buena Park is a car town. Distances between attractions are short but pedestrian routes along Beach Boulevard are uninviting — six lanes of traffic and not much shade. The Knott's parking lot is paid; arrive early to get a spot near the gate.

OCTA buses run along Beach Boulevard and Lincoln Avenue, useful for cheap connections within Orange County. Disneyland is about 8 miles east in [Anaheim](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/anaheim) — a 15-minute drive, or 30 minutes by bus on the OCTA 38.
