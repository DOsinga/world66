The Source OC is an open-air Korean-themed lifestyle centre opened in 2015 on Beach Boulevard, with restaurants, shops, an H Mart Korean supermarket in the basement, and a CGV cinema upstairs. The architecture is striking — geometric concrete and glass arranged around a central plaza with water features — and the place is busy in the evenings, when Buena Park's Korean American population comes to eat.

For visitors it is the best place in town to try Korean food, with everything from Korean fried chicken at BBQ Chicken to barbecue at Genwa Korean BBQ and shaved ice at Sul & Beans. The H Mart food court alone is worth a stop.
