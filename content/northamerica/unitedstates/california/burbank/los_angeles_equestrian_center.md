The Los Angeles Equestrian Center sits on Burbank's edge near Griffith Park, a large horse facility that can feel oddly rural beside studio lots and freeway traffic. It hosts shows, competitions, training, and equestrian events rather than functioning as a conventional tourist attraction.

Its value depends on timing. If there is an event on, it gives Burbank a very different local scene from film studios and shopping streets; if not, it is mostly a working complex to note as part of the area's horse culture around the park.
