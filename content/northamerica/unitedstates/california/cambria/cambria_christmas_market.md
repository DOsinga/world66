The Cambria Christmas Market is a seasonal, German-inspired market held between Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve, with light displays, music, food stalls, and gift vendors. It is one of the few times sleepy Cambria feels genuinely busy after dark.

Parking is usually handled by shuttle from Coast Union High School, so do not expect to simply roll up to the entrance. It is best treated as an evening outing after a day on the coast.
