LEGOLAND California is the park that put Carlsbad on the family-travel map, and it delivers on its promise for children roughly between the ages of two and twelve. The rides are mild enough for toddlers but engaging enough for older kids, and the entire resort is built around the idea that children should be able to operate things themselves — kid-powered coasters, driving schools, and interactive water rides dominate.

The park's showpiece is Miniland USA, an outdoor section where famous American landmarks — the Washington Monument, the French Quarter, a functioning New York Harbour — have been recreated in obsessive detail from tens of millions of LEGO bricks. There is something quietly impressive about the scale and patience involved. Beyond Miniland, themed areas include Ninjago World, Star Wars Miniland, and a waterpark that operates in summer. The resort also has hotels, so families willing to spend the money can make a full weekend of it.
