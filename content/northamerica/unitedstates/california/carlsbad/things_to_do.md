---

Carlsbad's two flagship attractions draw visitors from across Southern California: [LEGOLAND California](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/carlsbad/legoland_california), one of the original LEGOLAND parks outside Denmark, and the [Carlsbad Flower Fields](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/carlsbad/carlsbad_flower_fields), where 50 acres of giant ranunculus bloom in vivid colour each spring (roughly March through May). Beyond those, the city has good beaches, the sheltered waters of [Batiquitos Lagoon](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/carlsbad/batiquitos_lagoon) for kayaking and birding, and access to excellent coastal surf at [Carlsbad State Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/carlsbad/carlsbad_state_beach).

The [Museum of Making Music](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/carlsbad/museum_of_making_music) near LEGOLAND is a genuine surprise for music lovers — hands-on exhibits trace a century of American popular music through the instruments that shaped it. East of town, [Leo Carrillo Ranch Historic Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/carlsbad/leo_carrillo_ranch) preserves a hacienda estate built by the Hollywood actor and conservationist, complete with wandering peacocks and original adobe buildings.
