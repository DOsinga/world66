Carmel River State Beach is the quieter, wilder counterpoint to Carmel's main white-sand beach. The river lagoon, dunes, and rocky coast create good birding habitat and a more natural shoreline at the south end of town.

It is better for walking, photography, and watching the surf than for casual swimming, since conditions can be rough. Combine it with the Scenic Road walk or a visit toward Point Lobos for a strong coastal half-day.
