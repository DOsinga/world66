Garland Ranch Regional Park protects a broad stretch of Carmel Valley, from the Carmel River up into the Santa Lucia foothills. Its trails are the reason to come, with hiking, biking, and horseback routes that climb from the valley floor to open views over the hills and, on clear days, toward the coast.
