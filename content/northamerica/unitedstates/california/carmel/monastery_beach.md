Monastery Beach, also called San Jose Creek Beach, is the southern unit of Carmel River State Beach, just north of Point Lobos. It is a handsome, steeply shelving cove backed by the Santa Lucia foothills, with clear water that draws experienced scuba divers and photographers.

The same shape that makes it beautiful also makes it dangerous. California State Parks warns against swimming, wading, or standing near the surf line here, because waves can surge hard up the steep beach and pull people into deep water. Come for the view, tide watching, diving if properly equipped, and the approach to Point Lobos, not for a casual swim.
