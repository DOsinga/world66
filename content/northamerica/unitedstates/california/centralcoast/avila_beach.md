Avila Beach is a small San Luis Obispo County beach town tucked into a south-facing cove about 10 miles from [San Luis Obispo](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/sanluisobispo). It is more sheltered than many Central Coast beaches, so the air is often warmer and the water a little less brutal than at nearby Pismo or Morro Bay.

The town is compact: a short beachfront, a pier, a promenade, seafood places, and hills rising quickly behind the shore. That would not be enough on its own, but Avila also has the [Point San Luis Lighthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/avila_beach/point_san_luis_lighthouse) on the headland, the paved [Bob Jones City to Sea Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/avila_beach/bob_jones_city_to_sea_trail) through San Luis Obispo Creek, and mineral pools at [Avila Hot Springs](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/avila_beach/avila_hot_springs).

Come for a slow day rather than a checklist. Walk the pier, book the lighthouse tour if it fits your schedule, ride or walk the creek trail, and leave time for the beach when the afternoon wind stays down.
