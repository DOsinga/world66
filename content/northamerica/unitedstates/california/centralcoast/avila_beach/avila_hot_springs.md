Avila Hot Springs is the old mineral-pool stop on Avila Beach Drive, close to Highway 101. The spring water comes up hot and is cooled before it reaches the small soaking pool.

It is not a refined spa, and that is the point. Pair it with the Bob Jones trail or use it as a warm-up after a windy beach afternoon.
