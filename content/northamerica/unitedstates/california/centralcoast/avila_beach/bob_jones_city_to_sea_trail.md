The Bob Jones City to Sea Trail is a flat, paved three-mile path along San Luis Obispo Creek. It follows an old Pacific Coast Railroad right-of-way from near Highway 101 toward the beach.

It is a good low-effort ride or walk, shaded in places and calm enough for families. Bike rentals are usually available near both ends.
