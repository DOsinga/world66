Central Coast Aquarium is a small, practical stop near the beach, with touch tanks and exhibits focused on the marine life of the Central Coast. It works best with children, or as a short break when the wind makes the sand less tempting.

Do not expect a Monterey-scale aquarium. The appeal is local, close-up, and easy to fit into a beach day.
