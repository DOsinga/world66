Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park is one of the anchor stops of Big Sur, protecting redwood groves and trails along the Big Sur River. It is less about ocean cliffs than the coast road's interior side: shaded paths, river pools, campground life and forested canyon scenery.

The park is useful both as a hiking stop and as a base for travelers staying overnight in Big Sur. Trails vary with storm damage and seasonal closures, so check current conditions before planning a long walk.
