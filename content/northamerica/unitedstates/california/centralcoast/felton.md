Felton is the southern gateway to the San Lorenzo Valley, where Highway 9 leaves Santa Cruz and slips into the redwoods. It is small, unpolished, and more useful than showy: a place for river walks, forest trails, and a steam train ride rather than a checklist of urban sights.

The big draw is [Henry Cowell Redwoods State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/felton/henry_cowell_redwoods_state_park), whose old-growth grove gives you the redwood experience without the long drive north. Across town, the [Felton Covered Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/felton/felton_covered_bridge) is a handsome 19th-century survivor over the San Lorenzo River, while [Roaring Camp Railroads](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/felton/roaring_camp_railroads) runs narrow-gauge steam trains through the forest.

Felton also makes sense as part of a wider valley day: [Ben Lomond](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/ben_lomond_califo) is just up the road, Santa Cruz beaches are close in the other direction, and the back roads into the Santa Cruz Mountains are slow but lovely. Come for trees and a slower mood, not nightlife or polish.
