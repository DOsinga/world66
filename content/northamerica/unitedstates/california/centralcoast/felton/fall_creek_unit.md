The Fall Creek unit of Henry Cowell feels wilder than the main redwood grove near Felton. Trails follow the creek through damp forest and past remnants of the old lime industry that once worked these hills.

It is a better choice for walkers who want quiet than for visitors checking off a famous sight. Bring decent shoes after rain; the shaded canyon can stay muddy long after the weather clears.
