The Felton Covered Bridge is the town's easiest landmark to like: red-painted timber, a quiet riverside setting, and enough history to give a short stop some weight. It sits in a small county park, so you can pair it with a picnic or a quick walk along the San Lorenzo River.

It is not a major detour by itself, but it is a good first stop before heading to Henry Cowell or Roaring Camp. Photographers should come early or late in the day, when the bridge looks less flat in the forest shade.
