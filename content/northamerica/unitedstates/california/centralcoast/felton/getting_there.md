---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Felton is reached by car on Highway 9 from Santa Cruz or from the San Lorenzo Valley towns to the north. The road is scenic but narrow in places, and weekend beach traffic can slow the approach from Santa Cruz.

Santa Cruz Metro buses run through the valley, but a car is far more useful if you want to combine Felton with Henry Cowell, Ben Lomond, and the mountain roads.
