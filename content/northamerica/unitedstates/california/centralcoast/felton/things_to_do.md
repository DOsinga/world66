---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Felton's best sights are clustered around the redwoods and the San Lorenzo River. Start with [Henry Cowell Redwoods State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/felton/henry_cowell_redwoods_state_park), then add the [Felton Covered Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/felton/felton_covered_bridge) and a ride on [Roaring Camp Railroads](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/felton/roaring_camp_railroads) if the train schedule works.

For a quieter afternoon, the Fall Creek unit north of town has shaded trails and old lime-kiln remains. The Bigfoot Discovery Museum is tiny and odd, but it fits the valley's eccentric side.
