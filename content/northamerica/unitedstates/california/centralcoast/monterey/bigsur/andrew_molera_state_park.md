Andrew Molera State Park is one of Big Sur's better choices for travelers who want to move through the landscape rather than only pull over for a view. Trails cross meadows, follow the Big Sur River, and continue toward a broad, wilder beach backed by coastal bluffs.

The park is less manicured than some nearby stops, which is part of its appeal. Bring shoes that can handle sand or seasonal river crossings, watch for changing trail conditions, and allow enough time for the walk to the beach instead of treating it as a roadside overlook.
