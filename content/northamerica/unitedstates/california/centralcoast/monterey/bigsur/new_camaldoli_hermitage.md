New Camaldoli Hermitage sits on a ridge above the Pacific, reached by a steep road that feels a long way from Highway 1 despite being close to it. The monastery is active, so the appeal is not a conventional attraction but the stillness: chapel, bookshop, retreat houses, and wide coastal views that explain why this stretch of Big Sur has drawn spiritual communities for decades.

Visit respectfully and quietly. Even a short stop gives you one of the calmer Big Sur viewpoints, especially when the marine layer is lifting off the cliffs. Road and fire conditions can change access, so check before driving up.
