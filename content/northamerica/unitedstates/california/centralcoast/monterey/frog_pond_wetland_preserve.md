Frog Pond Wetland Preserve is a small, quiet wetland on the inland side of the Monterey Peninsula. It is best for a low-key nature break rather than a major outing: short paths, frogs, deer, mallards, hummingbirds, and other birdlife make it a good stop if you have already seen the coast and want a softer corner of the peninsula.
