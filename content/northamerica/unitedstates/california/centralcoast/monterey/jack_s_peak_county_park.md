Jack's Peak County Park sits on the wooded ridge behind Monterey, high enough to give clear views across Monterey Bay, Carmel Valley, and the surrounding coast on a good day. The park protects one of the remaining natural stands of Monterey pine, so the trails feel distinct from the busier waterfront attractions below.

This is a practical half-day escape rather than a wilderness outing. A network of short loops and connector trails makes it easy to choose anything from a gentle walk to a longer ramble, and picnic areas near the road make it useful for families or travellers who want a quieter Monterey stop between the aquarium, Cannery Row, and Carmel.
