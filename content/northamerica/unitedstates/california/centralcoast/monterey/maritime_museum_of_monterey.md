The Maritime Museum of Monterey focuses on Monterey's working relationship with the sea: whaling, fishing, port history, and the commercial waterfront before Cannery Row became a visitor district.

It is a modest museum, but it makes sense if you are already walking between Fisherman's Wharf and old Monterey. For a broader historic walk, combine it with Monterey State Historic Park and Colton Hall.
