The Presidio of Monterey Museum covers the military history of the hill above Monterey, from the Spanish presidio through later American use. It is a modest museum, but it adds useful context to Monterey's older buildings, harbour, and strategic position on the bay. Pair it with Monterey State Historic Park rather than making it the day's main event.
