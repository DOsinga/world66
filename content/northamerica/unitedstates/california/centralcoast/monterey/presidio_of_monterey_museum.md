The Presidio of Monterey Museum covers the military history of Monterey from early settlement through the modern U.S. Army presence. It gives useful background to a town better known for its waterfront, adobes, and aquarium.

The museum is a compact stop near the old presidio area. It pairs naturally with Monterey State Historic Park and the Path of History.
