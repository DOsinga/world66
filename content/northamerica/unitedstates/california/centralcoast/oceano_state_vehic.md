Oceano State Vehicular Recreation Area is a sandy stretch of beach and dune area where motorists may cruise to their heart's delight. ATVs, 4x4, family campers and motorcycles live it up here, particularly on holiday weekends when State Parks limits access to avoid overcrowding.
