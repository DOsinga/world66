---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Oxnard is on the Pacific Coast Route (Amtrak Pacific Surfliner and Coast Starlight), with regular daily services from Los Angeles (about 75 minutes) and San Luis Obispo. The Oxnard Transit Center is the main hub, downtown, with connections to local buses. By car, Oxnard sits on US-101, roughly 60 miles northwest of central Los Angeles. Oxnard Airport (OXR) offers limited regional connections but most visitors use LAX or Burbank.
