---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pismo Beach is built around the shore, but the best day here mixes the obvious beach time with one of the places that gives the town real character. Start at the [pier and promenade](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/pismobeach/pismo_beach_pier), then choose the [Monarch Butterfly Grove](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/pismobeach/monarch_butterfly_grove) in winter, [Dinosaur Caves Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/pismobeach/dinosaur_caves_park) for an easy bluff walk, or the [Pismo Preserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/pismobeach/pismo_preserve) for a proper hike above the coast.
