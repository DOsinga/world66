William Randolph Hearst Memorial Beach sits in San Simeon Bay, below the road up to Hearst Castle. It is a good place to reset after a tour: walk the pier, watch the surf, or bring a picnic when the wind is behaving.

The water is cold and conditions can be rough, so think beach walk rather than lazy swim. The small bay setting is the attraction, with the hills rising immediately behind you.
