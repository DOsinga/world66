San Luis Obispo Children's Museum is a hands-on museum aimed at younger children, a short walk from Mission Plaza and downtown. The exhibits are built around learning through play, so it is most useful for families who need a child-focused stop between the mission, creek, and downtown sights.

It is not a major museum for adults, but for families it fills a clear gap in a very walkable part of San Luis Obispo.
