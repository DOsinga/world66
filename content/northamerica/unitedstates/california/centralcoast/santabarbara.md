Santa Barbara is one of those rare places that genuinely seems to have it all: a 200-year-old [Spanish Mission](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/old_mission) above the city, a working [harbour](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/sb_harbor) below it, a near-perfect Mediterranean climate, south-facing beaches, the [most beautiful courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/santa_barbara_courthouse) in America, and a wine country an hour's drive away. The locals call it the American Riviera, which is exactly the sort of self-congratulatory phrase travel writers should be suspicious of — except that, this once, the locals are mostly right.

The defining feature is the architecture. After a 1925 earthquake levelled much of downtown, the city adopted a strict Spanish Colonial Revival building code that has remained in force ever since. The result is a city of white walls, red-tile roofs, wrought-iron balconies, and hidden interior courtyards (*paseos*) all the way along [State Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/state_street). It looks like a deliberate setting, because it is one.

The other sights cluster in three areas. **Downtown**: the courthouse, Lobero and [Arlington](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/arlington_theatre) theatres, the [Presidio](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/presidio) and [Casa de la Guerra](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/casa_de_la_guerra) adobes, Museum of Art. **Waterfront**: [Stearns Wharf](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/stearns_wharf), the harbour, [East Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/east_beach), and the warehouse-converted [Funk Zone](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/funk_zone) where the Urban Wine Trail runs through. **Foothills and Montecito**: the Mission, the [Botanic Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/sb_botanic_garden), [Lotusland](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santabarbara/lotusland), and the celebrity neighbourhood that has produced more tabloid stories than is fair.

It is also a great food city — La Super-Rica Taqueria on Milpas Street, dependable seafood at Brophy Bros. in the harbour, and a strong showing of Funk Zone small plates. Beyond town, the [Santa Ynez](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santaynez) wine country, [Solvang](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/solvang), and the Channel Islands National Park are all within day-trip range. Most travellers stay two nights; three is better.
