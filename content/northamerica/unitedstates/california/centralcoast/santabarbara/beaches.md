---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Santa Barbara's coast actually faces south, not west — one of the only stretches of the West Coast where this is true — which means warmer water, gentler surf, and uninterrupted sunsets over the Pacific. The Channel Islands sit out to sea like a low blue wall on the southern horizon.

The main city beaches are **East Beach**, **West Beach**, and **Leadbetter Beach** — all easily walkable from downtown. Past the harbour, **Arroyo Burro (Hendry's) Beach** is the local-favourite cove for sunset and dog-walking. East of town, **Butterfly Beach** and **Miramar Beach** in Montecito are quieter and west-facing. For surfers, **Rincon Point** at the Ventura County line is one of the most famous right-hand point breaks on the West Coast.
