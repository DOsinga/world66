Cold Spring Tavern is a 1860s stagecoach stop tucked under oak trees on a back lane just off Highway 154, twenty minutes' drive from Santa Barbara. The kitchen does fire-grilled tri-tip sandwiches at lunch and a more serious dinner menu featuring rabbit chilli, venison, and roast quail in the evenings.

Sunday lunch is the famous time: bikers from the Santa Ynez Valley fill the parking lot, a live band plays under the trees, and the tri-tip is grilled outside on an oak fire. Cash and cards. The dirt road in and the lack of a sign means many people miss it; that is part of the charm.
