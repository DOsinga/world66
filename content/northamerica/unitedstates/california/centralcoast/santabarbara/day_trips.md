The most popular day trip is into the **Santa Ynez Valley** wine country over the San Marcos Pass — Highway 154 climbs through chaparral and oak savanna with viewpoints over the Pacific behind you. Stop at **Cold Spring Tavern** on the way over for lunch. The valley contains [Solvang](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/solvang), a slightly kitschy but real Danish-themed town founded by immigrant farmers in 1911, and [Santa Ynez](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santaynez) with its 100-plus wineries that featured in the film *Sideways*.

**The Channel Islands National Park** — particularly **Santa Cruz Island**, the closest — is the other major excursion: take Island Packers' boat from Ventura (45 minutes south) for a day or overnight on truly wild country. Bring everything you need; there are no shops on the island.

Further afield, **Hearst Castle** at San Simeon is a 2.5-hour drive north — William Randolph Hearst's improbable hilltop fantasy of a Mediterranean palace. **Los Angeles** is 90 minutes south on a clear weekday morning, longer most other times.
