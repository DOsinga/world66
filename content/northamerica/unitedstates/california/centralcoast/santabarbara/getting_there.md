---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Santa Barbara is on US Highway 101, 90 minutes north of Los Angeles and 5 hours south of San Francisco. Driving is the most common arrival, but Highway 101 between Los Angeles and Santa Barbara can be heavily congested on Friday afternoons and Sunday evenings.

**Amtrak's Pacific Surfliner** runs along the coast from San Diego, through Los Angeles, terminating in San Luis Obispo. The ride between LA Union Station and Santa Barbara takes about 2 hours 45 minutes, costs around $40, and runs almost entirely beside the Pacific — for many people, the most pleasant way to arrive in town.

**Santa Barbara Airport** (SBA) has direct flights from major western U.S. hubs (Denver, Seattle, San Francisco, Phoenix, Dallas). For most international travellers, fly into **Los Angeles (LAX)** and either rent a car, take Amtrak, or use the Santa Barbara Airbus shuttle.
