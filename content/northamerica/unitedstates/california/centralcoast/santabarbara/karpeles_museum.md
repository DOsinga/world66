Karpeles Museum displays rotating selections from a large private manuscript and document collection. Exhibits can include historic letters, maps, scientific papers, and other original documents.

Its central location makes it a worthwhile short stop for visitors interested in archives, rare documents, or an indoor break near Santa Barbara's downtown museums and courthouse.
