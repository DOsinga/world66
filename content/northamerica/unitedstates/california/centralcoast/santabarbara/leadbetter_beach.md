Leadbetter Beach sits between Santa Barbara Harbor and Shoreline Park, making it one of the easiest city beaches to fold into a day on the waterfront. The surf is usually gentler than at the more exposed breaks, and the grass, picnic areas, showers, and parking make it practical as well as pretty.

It is especially good for beginner surfers, windsurfers, and travellers who want a beach without leaving town. Walk up to Shoreline Park afterward for the broader view back over the sand, marina, and Santa Ynez Mountains.
