Lotusland is a 37-acre garden in Montecito, six kilometres east of Santa Barbara. Madame Ganna Walska, who lived on the estate from 1941 until her death in 1984, planted it as a sequence of themed gardens, each more eccentric than the last — a Blue Garden, a Cactus Garden, a Japanese Garden, an outdoor theatre.

Access is by **guided tour only**, with a strict reservation system; advance booking is essential, sometimes weeks ahead. Tours run February through November. The Blue Garden, with its silver-blue agaves and Atlas cedars, is the most photographed feature; the Aloe Garden, ringed by giant clam shells with an abalone-shell fountain, is the most surreal.
