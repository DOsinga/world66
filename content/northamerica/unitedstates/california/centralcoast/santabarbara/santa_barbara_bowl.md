The Santa Barbara Bowl is one of California’s more pleasant outdoor concert settings, tucked into the Riviera hillside above town. The venue is compact enough to feel intimate, but large enough to draw serious touring acts. On a clear evening, the walk up through the neighbourhood and the view back toward the city are part of the experience.

You come for a show, not for a daytime tour. Check the calendar before your trip, bring layers for the ocean-cooled night air, and allow time for the climb and post-concert traffic. When the booking is right, it is far more memorable than another generic indoor arena.
