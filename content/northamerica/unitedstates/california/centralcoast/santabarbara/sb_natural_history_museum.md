A short walk uphill from the Mission, this small natural-history museum is set in a oak-shaded creek campus above Mission Canyon Road. The galleries cover Chumash material culture, marine ecology, and the geology of the Santa Ynez Mountains. The blue whale skeleton suspended outside, partly visible from the entrance, is a landmark.

The associated **Sea Center** out on Stearns Wharf is a satellite for kids: touch-tanks, microscopes, and live animal exhibits. The main museum is the more substantial of the two.
