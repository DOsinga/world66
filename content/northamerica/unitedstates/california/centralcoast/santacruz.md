Santa Cruz sits where the redwood-covered mountains of the Coast Range meet the northern curve of Monterey Bay. It is a California beach town with a countercultural streak — part surf culture, part university town, part old-school boardwalk resort. The mix gives it a character quite unlike anywhere else on the coast.

The [Beach Boardwalk](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santacruz/beach_boardwalk) is the last surviving beachfront amusement park on the West Coast, with a 1924 wooden roller coaster that still draws crowds. Beyond it, the town fans out into a walkable downtown of independent shops, good restaurants, and coffee roasters. [Pacific Avenue](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santacruz/pacific_avenue) is the main drag, busy most evenings.

Surfing defines the town's identity as much as anything. [Steamer Lane](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santacruz/steamer_lane), just off [West Cliff Drive](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santacruz/west_cliff_drive), is one of the best-known surf breaks in California, and the water here is full of wetsuits year-round. The coastal path along West Cliff Drive is worth walking even if you never touch a board — the views stretch across the bay to Monterey on clear days, and [Natural Bridges State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santacruz/natural_bridges_state_park) at the western end has spectacular monarch butterfly aggregations in autumn.

Up the hill, the University of California Santa Cruz campus sprawls through redwood forest — the [Henry Cowell Redwoods State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santacruz/henry_cowell_redwoods) nearby has old-growth trees over 1,500 years old — which partly explains the town's youthful, progressive feel. The surrounding mountains offer good hiking and mountain biking at [Wilder Ranch](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralcoast/santacruz/wilder_ranch), and the farms of the Pajaro Valley supply the restaurants with some of the best produce in the state.
