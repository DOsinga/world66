The main commercial street of downtown Santa Cruz, a pedestrian-friendly strip of independent shops, bookstores, coffee roasters, and restaurants running north-south through the city centre. Pacific Avenue was rebuilt after the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake destroyed its previous Victorian commercial buildings, and the result is a walkable, lively street that serves both students from UC Santa Cruz and the broader community.

The Bookshop Santa Cruz — an independent bookstore that has occupied the same downtown block for decades — is one of the best general bookstores on the California coast. The Saturday farmers market at the nearby town clock draws a large crowd and an excellent selection of Monterey Bay-area produce. Pacific Avenue is busiest on Friday and Saturday evenings when the restaurants and bars fill up.
