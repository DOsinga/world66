The Seymour Marine Discovery Center is a marine science museum on the coast west of central Santa Cruz. Exhibits focus on Monterey Bay, ocean conservation, and local marine life, with tanks of native species and family-friendly hands-on displays. Guided tours usually add a look toward the dolphin research area, and the site has broad views over the bay.
