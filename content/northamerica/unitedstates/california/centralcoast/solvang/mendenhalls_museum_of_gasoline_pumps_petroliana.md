Mendenhall's Museum of Gasoline Pumps & Petroliana is a private museum devoted to vintage roadside culture. Its collection includes restored gasoline pumps, porcelain signs, oil-company globes, racing memorabilia, cars, and license plates.

Visits are usually by tour or arranged opening, so it is best treated as a planned stop rather than a casual walk-in museum.
