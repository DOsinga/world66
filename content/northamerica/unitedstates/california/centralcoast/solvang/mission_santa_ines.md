Mission Santa Inés is the spiritual origin of Solvang and one of the finest and most complete California missions. Founded in 1804, it is located directly adjacent to the town and remains an active Roman Catholic parish — mass is still held here — which gives it a living quality that many more famous missions have lost.

The church interior is beautifully preserved, with painted walls, carved wooden statuary, and the kind of intimate scale that makes the California missions so appealing as buildings. The adjacent museum houses vestments, religious objects, historical documents, and Chumash artefacts, along with a good account of the 1824 Chumash Revolt. The mission garden and the cemetery behind the church are quiet places to pause.

Missions Road, Solvang. Open daily; museum admission charged.
