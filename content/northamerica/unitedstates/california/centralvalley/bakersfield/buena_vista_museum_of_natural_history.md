The Buena Vista Museum of Natural History is Bakersfield's small but distinctive science museum, strongest on fossils, geology, and material connected to nearby Sharktooth Hill. That local fossil bed gives the museum a sharper identity than a generic natural history collection would have.

It is a good downtown stop for families or anyone curious about the Central Valley's deep past. Pair it with the Kern County Museum if you want both natural and human history in a single Bakersfield day.
