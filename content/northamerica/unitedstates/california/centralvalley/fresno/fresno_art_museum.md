Fresno Art Museum is the city's main art-museum stop, with changing exhibitions that often mix contemporary work, Mexican and Mexican-American artists, and pieces tied to the Central Valley. It gives Fresno a cultural anchor beyond the usual gateway-to-the-parks role.

The museum is compact enough for an unhurried hour or two. Check the exhibition schedule before making a special trip, because the value of a visit depends heavily on what is currently installed.
