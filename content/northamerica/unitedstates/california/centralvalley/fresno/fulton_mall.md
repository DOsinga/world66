Fulton Mall is a historic downtown Fresno corridor known for its public art and mid-20th-century pedestrian-mall legacy. Traffic has returned to parts of the street, but the area still holds one of the city's more unusual collections of outdoor sculpture and urban design.

It is worth a look if you are already exploring downtown Fresno, especially around the theaters, older commercial buildings, and civic landmarks nearby.
