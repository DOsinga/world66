Nonini Winery is a small Fresno-area wine stop that reflects the Central Valley's agricultural character. It is not a grand destination winery, but it gives visitors a local tasting option in a region better known for orchards, fields, and access to national parks.

This works best as a planned stop if tasting hours or events fit your schedule. For travellers staying in Fresno, it can add a low-key food-and-drink angle to a trip otherwise focused on city museums, gardens, or Yosemite approaches.
