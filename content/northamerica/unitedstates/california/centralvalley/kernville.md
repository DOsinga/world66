Kernville sits on the North Fork of the Kern River, just above [Isabella Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/kernville/isabella_lake) in the southern Sierra Nevada. The [Sequoia National Forest](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/kernville/sequoia_national_forest) rises to the north, and the whole area is laced with back roads that wind through granite peaks and pine forests.

In spring the river draws serious whitewater enthusiasts — the [Upper Kern](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/kernville/upper_kern_river) is one of the best rafting runs in California. The rest of the year it is a quiet base for camping, fishing, and exploring the mountains. Campgrounds line the river corridor both upstream and downstream. The little [historic district](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/kernville/kernville_historic_district) around Circle Park is the town's old-west centre — a couple of blocks of wooden facades, a small county museum, and the kind of hardware store you forget still exists.
