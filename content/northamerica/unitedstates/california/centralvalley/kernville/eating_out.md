---
title: "Eating Out in Kernville"
type: section
---

Kernville is a small town and the food scene is small to match — but a handful of places are worth knowing.

**Big Blue Bear Espresso** on Big Blue Road is the standard coffee-and-pastry stop, busy on weekend mornings with paddlers and hikers heading upstream. **Cheryl's Diner** does diner food well — biscuits and gravy, decent burgers, country-style breakfasts. **Kern River Brewing Company** is a craft brewery and gastropub with a wide patio overlooking the river: a serious tap list and burgers and pizza to soak it up. **That's Italian** is the dinner option of choice for pasta and old-school red-sauce dishes.
