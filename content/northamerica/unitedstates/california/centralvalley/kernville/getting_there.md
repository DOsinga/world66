---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kernville is about three hours from **Los Angeles** by car, four hours from **Las Vegas**, and an hour and a half from **Bakersfield**. The drive from LA is via Highway 178 east from Bakersfield, then north on Sierra Way along Lake Isabella — a scenic climb out of the Central Valley.

There is no public transport into Kernville and the **Kern Valley Airport** (3 miles south) handles only general aviation. The nearest commercial flights are at Bakersfield. A rental car is essentially required.

In winter, check road conditions: Highway 178 is sometimes closed by rockslides and the higher mountain roads to the north close for snow.
