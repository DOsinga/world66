Lake Isabella, just south of Kernville where the North and South Forks of the Kern River converge, is the largest freshwater lake in southern California — a 38,000-acre reservoir built behind a 1953 dam. It is a major summer playground for the southern Sierra, drawing fishing, boating and water-skiing crowds from across the Central Valley and Los Angeles.

The shoreline is open and varied, with sandy beaches at **Auxiliary Dam** and **Tillie Creek**, both with developed campgrounds. Boats can be rented at the **French Gulch Marina**. Water levels fluctuate with the snowpack — in dry years the lake retreats far from its high-water shoreline.
