---
title: "Things to do in Kernville"
type: section
---

Kernville is about the river and the mountains. Rafting and kayaking on the [Upper Kern](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/kernville/upper_kern_river) is the headline activity from April to July; the rest of the year it is a quiet base for hiking and camping in the [Sequoia National Forest](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/kernville/sequoia_national_forest) and for fishing and boating on [Lake Isabella](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/kernville/isabella_lake) just south of town.

Spend an hour in the [historic district](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/kernville/kernville_historic_district) for the old-west character, and a winter visit can be timed to the [Whiskey Flat Days](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/kernville/whiskey_flat_days) festival in February.
