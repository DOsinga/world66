---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Spring** (April–June) is whitewater season: the snowmelt fills the Kern River and the commercial rafting operations are in full swing. This is the busiest time of year and the only time most outfitters open.

**Summer** is hot and dry — temperatures often hit 38°C in the valley but stay cooler at altitude. Lake Isabella draws weekenders, and the higher trails in the Sequoia forest are accessible.

**Autumn** is the most pleasant season: warm days, cool nights, fewer crowds, and the Western Divide turns gold and red. **Winter** is quiet, with cold but rarely snowy weather in town; the higher passes close for snow and chains may be required on Highway 155 over to the western side.
