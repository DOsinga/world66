Merced is a small city in California's Central Valley, best known as the gateway to Yosemite National Park — the western entrance is about 80 miles northeast along Highway 140. The city is also home to [UC Merced](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/merced/uc_merced), the newest campus in the UC system, opened in 2005.

The town itself is not a major destination, but it has a few diversions worth knowing about. The [Castle Air Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/merced/castle_air_museum) in nearby Atwater displays 44 vintage military aircraft outdoors, from a B-52 Stratofortress to a SR-71 Blackbird. The [Grassland Wetlands](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/merced/grassland_wetlands) of Merced County, a 25-square-mile area including several national wildlife refuges, are excellent for birdwatching — hundreds of thousands of migratory waterfowl winter here on the Pacific Flyway. The [George Hicks Fancher Monument](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/merced/george_hicks_fancher_monument) — a 36-foot granite obelisk in the town centre — is a Victorian curiosity that rather exceeds the significance of the man it commemorates.

Most visitors use Merced as a base or transit stop on the way to Yosemite. There are regular Amtrak and YARTS bus connections to the park.
