The George Hicks Fancher Monument stands in a small park in central Merced: a 36-foot granite obelisk erected in the 1870s to commemorate an early California settler and philanthropist. It is one of the taller monuments in the Central Valley and stands out dramatically in a town of modest scale.

The monument is not a major destination in itself, but it is an unexpectedly imposing piece of Victoriana in an agricultural city that otherwise makes few concessions to grandeur. It sits in a neighbourhood of historic homes from the same period. For visitors exploring Merced beyond the castle museum and wetlands, the surrounding historic district has some well-preserved late 19th-century architecture worth a brief walk.
