The Merced County Courthouse Museum is the most substantial historic building in downtown Merced, an Italianate courthouse from 1875 now used to tell the county's story. Inside, exhibits cover settlement, agriculture, local families, and the wider Central Valley context that is easy to miss if you only pass through on the way to Yosemite.

It is a compact museum, but the building gives it weight. The old courtroom atmosphere, high ceilings, and civic architecture make the visit feel rooted in place rather than like a generic local display. Check hours before going; small county museums often keep limited schedules.
