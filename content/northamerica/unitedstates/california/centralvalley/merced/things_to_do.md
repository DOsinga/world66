---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Merced is primarily a gateway rather than a destination — most visitors are here en route to Yosemite. But a few stops are genuinely worth making: the Castle Air Museum for its remarkable aircraft collection, the Grassland Wetlands for migratory birds, and the university campus for a sense of California's newest UC institution. The town itself is compact and the pace is slow.
