---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Modesto's location at the centre of California's middle valley puts it within easy reach of some of the state's great destinations.

[Yosemite National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california) is two hours east on Highway 120 — close enough for a long day trip if you start early, although an overnight in a Sierra foothill town gives you a much better experience. The Gold Country towns of Sonora, Columbia, and Murphys are an hour and a half east. The Bay Area — [San Francisco](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfrancisco) and Oakland — is ninety minutes west, easy as a day trip in either direction.

Closer to home, the Hetch Hetchy reservoir, the [Stockton](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/stockton) waterfront, and the wine villages of Lodi to the north all make decent half-day excursions.
