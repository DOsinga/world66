Downtown Modesto runs along Tenth and Eleventh Streets and is the historic core of the city: red-brick warehouse buildings from the late 19th century, Spanish-revival civic buildings from the New Deal era, and the streets George Lucas cruised as a teenager in the late 1950s. It is compact enough to cover on foot in an afternoon.

The main draws are the [Modesto Arch](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/modesto/modesto_arch), the [Gallo Center for the Arts](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/modesto/gallo_center), the [State Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/modesto/state_theatre), and the [El Viejo Post Office](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/modesto/el_viejo_post_office). The Tenth Street Plaza area has the densest concentration of restaurants and bars. The downtown Modesto certified farmers' market runs on Saturday mornings in the warm half of the year.
