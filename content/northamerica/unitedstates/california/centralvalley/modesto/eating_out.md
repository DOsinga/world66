---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Modesto's food scene punches above the weight of a Central Valley working town. The mix of Mexican, Portuguese, Italian, Sikh, and Hmong influences from the agricultural workforce gives the city a deeper menu than its tract-housing reputation suggests.

For Mexican, **A&W La Morenita** on Crows Landing Road and **Tortilleria La Morenita** further out are the local choices for tacos and house-made tortillas. **Tresetti's World Caffe** on K Street is the place for a sit-down dinner — Italian-leaning, big wine list weighted toward Central Coast producers. **Surla's** on McHenry has been doing strong California cuisine for over twenty years.

Breakfast and lunch downtown: **Commonwealth** on 10th Street for sandwiches and craft coffee, **Brighter Side** on McHenry for diner standards, **Ralston's Goat** in Tenth Street Plaza for ambitious brunch. The Saturday-morning **farmers' market** on 16th Street, late spring through autumn, is excellent for stone fruit, almonds, and the valley's tomatoes and melons in season.
