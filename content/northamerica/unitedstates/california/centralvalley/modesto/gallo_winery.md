E. & J. Gallo Winery, headquartered in south Modesto, is the largest family-owned winery in the world and one of the largest wine companies of any kind. The brothers Ernest and Julio Gallo started it in 1933 and built it into a vast operation that today produces hundreds of brands, from supermarket bag-in-box to high-end Sonoma and Napa labels.

The main Modesto headquarters complex is not open to the public, but its presence is felt all over the city: the Gallo family has funded the [Gallo Center for the Arts](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/modesto/gallo_center), restored the [McHenry Mansion](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/modesto/mchenry_mansion), and supported the local hospital and university. For winery visits, the Gallo Vineyards experience in Healdsburg in Sonoma County is where tastings happen.
