A wrought-iron arch spanning Ninth Street at I Street in downtown Modesto, erected in 1912 to greet motorists arriving from the train depot. The slogan illuminated in neon along the top — "Water Wealth Contentment Health" — is one of California's most recognised pieces of small-city civic boosterism.

The arch was the model for the one in George Lucas's *American Graffiti*, although Lucas filmed in Petaluma. It has been restored several times, most recently in 2009, and still has its original lamps. The best photographs are at dusk when the neon comes on. The intersection beneath is a busy crossroads; take care with traffic if you stop in the middle for the perfect shot.
