---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Reedley is a small town and its downtown is entirely walkable — the Opera House, Mennonite Quilting Center, Reedley Museum, and most restaurants are within a few blocks of each other on G Street and 10th Street.

A car is essential for anything beyond the downtown core. The Kings River access parks, Watson's Farm Equipment Museum, and the surrounding farmland are not reachable on foot. Highway 180 connects Reedley to Fresno (25 miles northwest) and continues east toward Kings Canyon National Park.

There is no local bus service oriented toward visitors. Fresno Area Express (FAX) provides limited transit connections to Fresno but is not practical for sightseeing.
