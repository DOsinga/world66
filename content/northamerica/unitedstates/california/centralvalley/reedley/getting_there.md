---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Reedley is about 25 miles southeast of Fresno on Highway 180. Most visitors arrive by car — there is no passenger rail service to Reedley, and bus connections are limited. The drive from Fresno takes roughly 35 minutes. From Los Angeles, Reedley is about 230 miles north via Highway 99 (approximately 3.5 hours without traffic).

Fresno Yosemite International Airport (FAT) is the nearest commercial airport, with flights from major California cities and several national hubs. Car rental is available at the airport.

Reedley Municipal Airport (O32), a small general aviation field, sits about 5 miles south of downtown and handles private aircraft.
