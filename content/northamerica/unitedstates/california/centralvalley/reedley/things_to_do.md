---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Reedley's attractions are modest in number but genuine in character. The compact downtown along G Street holds most of them within easy walking distance. On the third Thursday of each month, the Kings River Arts Council runs ArtStop — a self-guided gallery hop along G Street where local businesses host artist receptions from 5 to 8pm. Outside of town, the Kings River is the main draw in summer.
