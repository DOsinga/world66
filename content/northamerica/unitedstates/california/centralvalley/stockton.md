Stockton is a large city in California's Central Valley, sitting at the eastern edge of the [Sacramento–San Joaquin River Delta](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/stockton/sacramento_san_joaquin_delta). It is not a tourist destination in the conventional sense, but the surrounding delta — a maze of waterways, levees, and small towns — is one of the most distinctive landscapes in the state.

The delta offers superb boating and fishing. Rent a houseboat and explore the waterways past the old towns of [Locke](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/stockton/locke) (a surviving Chinese-American settlement from the 1920s and the last of its kind in the country), Isleton, and Walnut Grove. Striped bass fishing is good year-round, and salmon and steelhead run in the autumn. The [Grizzly Island Wildlife Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/stockton/grizzly_island) near Suisun City is excellent for birdwatching.

Downtown Stockton has the [Haggin Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/stockton/haggin_museum) — a serious collection of French academic painting and Central Valley history that surprises most visitors. The revitalised [waterfront](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/stockton/stockton_waterfront) on the San Joaquin River, with its minor league ballpark and marina, is the city's most pleasant area for an evening walk. Stockton is also the birthplace of the Caterpillar tractor: Benjamin Holt invented the continuous-track vehicle here in 1904 to navigate the soft peat fields of the delta, and the [technology](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/stockton/caterpillar_origins) went on to shape both modern agriculture and WWI tank warfare.

Stockton's Miracle Mile district on Pacific Avenue has a cluster of restaurants, pubs, and small shops. The city has strong Mexican and Southeast Asian food scenes — look for Il Grullenese on Charter Way for Mexican, or explore the restaurants near downtown for Vietnamese, Cambodian, and Chinese.
