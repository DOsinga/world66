---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Visalia's main draws are its proximity to the national parks and a handful of genuinely interesting stops in and around the city. The [Tulare County Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/visalia/tulare_county_museum) is the standout — a solid local history collection inside [Mooney Grove Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/visalia/mooney_grove_park), which itself is a pleasant place to wander under ancient oaks. The [Kaweah Oaks Preserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/visalia/kaweah_oaks_preserve), seven miles east via Highway 198, offers a rare look at what the valley floor looked like before agriculture took over.

Downtown, the [Fox Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/visalia/fox_theatre) is a beautifully restored 1930s atmospheric theatre that now hosts live performances — the twinkling ceiling and ornate interior make it worth visiting even if you're not catching a show. The [Central California Chinese Cultural Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/centralvalley/visalia/central_california_chinese_cultural_center) documents the history of Chinese immigrants in the Valley, a story that rarely gets its due.
