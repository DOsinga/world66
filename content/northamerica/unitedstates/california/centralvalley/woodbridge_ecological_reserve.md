Woodbridge Ecological Reserve protects wetlands near Lodi and is best known for sandhill cranes. From September into March, large numbers of cranes use the area as a roost, and the evening fly-in can be one of the Central Valley's best wildlife spectacles.

This is a simple nature stop rather than a polished attraction. Bring binoculars, time your visit around dawn or dusk, and be patient; the reward is watching big birds move across a flat agricultural landscape that most travellers otherwise rush through.
