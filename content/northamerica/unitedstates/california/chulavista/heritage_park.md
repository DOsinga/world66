Heritage Park is a neighbourhood park in eastern Chula Vista with a small lake at its centre, lakeside gazebo, and a perimeter walking path of about half a mile. It is a pleasant, low-key green space well-suited to an early morning walk or a family picnic, with decent views over the lake and the hills behind.

The park covers just over ten acres and has a multi-use sports field, basketball courts, and children's play equipment alongside the more relaxed lakeside circuit. It is less crowded than the bigger parks and has a quiet, residential character. Located at 1381 E Palomar St in the Eastlake neighbourhood.
