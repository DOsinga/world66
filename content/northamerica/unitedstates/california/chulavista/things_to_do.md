---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Chula Vista's main draw for visitors is the [US Olympic and Paralympic Training Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/us_olympic_training_center), which offers free public tours of a working elite sports facility. The [Living Coast Discovery Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/living_coast_discovery_center) on the bayfront is a zoo and aquarium set inside a national wildlife refuge — an unusual combination that makes it well worth a visit. The [Chula Vista Bayfront](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/chula_vista_bayfront), now anchored by the new [Gaylord Pacific Resort](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/gaylord_pacific_resort), has been redeveloped with new parks, trails, and bay access. The [Sweetwater Marsh](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/sweetwater_marsh) is one of the few intact coastal wetlands remaining in Southern California, with exceptional birdwatching. The [Sweetwater River Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/sweetwater_river) follows the lower river through riparian woodland connecting the eastern hills to the bay.

Inland, [Otay Lakes County Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/otay_lakes_county_park) and [Otay Valley Regional Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/otay_valley_regional_park) offer hiking and cycling through native chaparral. [Heritage Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/heritage_park), [Rohr Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/rohr_park), and the planned community of [Eastlake](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/eastlake) offer quieter neighbourhood green spaces. The historic [Third Avenue Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/third_avenue_village) is the best place to understand Chula Vista as a real community, with the vintage [Vogue Theater](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/vogue_theater) on the main street. At the eastern edge of the city, the [Cerutti Mastodon site](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/cerutti_mastodon_site) is associated with one of the most controversial discoveries in New World archaeology.
