---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Chula Vista's main draw for visitors is the [US Olympic and Paralympic Training Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/us_olympic_training_center), which offers free public tours of a working elite sports facility. The [Living Coast Discovery Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/living_coast_discovery_center) on the bayfront is a zoo and aquarium set inside a national wildlife refuge — an unusual combination that makes it well worth a visit. The [Chula Vista Bayfront](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/chula_vista_bayfront) has been redeveloped with new parks, trails, and bay access. The [Sweetwater Marsh](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/sweetwater_marsh) is one of the few intact coastal wetlands remaining in Southern California, with exceptional birdwatching. Inland, [Otay Lakes County Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/otay_lakes_county_park) and [Otay Valley Regional Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/otay_valley_regional_park) offer hiking and cycling through native chaparral. [Heritage Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/heritage_park) and [Rohr Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/chulavista/rohr_park) are pleasant neighbourhood green spaces.
