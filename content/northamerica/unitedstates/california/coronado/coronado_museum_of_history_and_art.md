The Coronado Museum of History and Art is a compact introduction to how Coronado became a resort town, naval neighbour, and carefully preserved island community. Exhibits and local archives give useful background to the ferry landing, beach, Hotel del Coronado, and quiet residential streets.

It is not a large museum, but it is well placed for a Coronado walk. Visit early in the day if you want the rest of the island to make more sense, especially the way tourism, military presence, and small-town image overlap here.
