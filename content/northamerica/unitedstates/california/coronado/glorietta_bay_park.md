Glorietta Bay Park is Coronado at its gentlest: lawns, a playground, a small sand beach, and calm water on the bay side of the island. It is less dramatic than Coronado Beach, but better for families who want easy water access without surf.

The park also gives a different angle on San Diego Bay, with boats, the Coronado Bridge, and the Hotel del Coronado area close by. It is a practical picnic or breather stop while cycling or walking around the island.
