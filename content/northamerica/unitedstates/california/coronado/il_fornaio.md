The Hotel del Coronado's main sit-down restaurant offers Italian-American cooking in the hotel's historic ground-floor space. It is a reliable choice for a meal on-site — wood-fired pizzas, pasta, and grilled proteins — without the full cost of the hotel's more formal dining rooms. The terrace overlooks the pool and the beach beyond, and weekend brunch is a local ritual.
