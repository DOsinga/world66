Lamb's Players Theater is a professional theatre company based in Coronado's historic Spreckels Building. It stages a regular programme of plays and musicals, making it one of the better evening options on the island after the beach crowds thin out.

The theatre is small enough to feel local but serious enough to draw audiences from across the San Diego area. Check the current schedule if you are staying overnight in Coronado.
