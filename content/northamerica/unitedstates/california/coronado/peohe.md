Peohe's sits on the waterfront at the Coronado Ferry Landing, which means the view — looking back across the bay at the San Diego skyline — is the main attraction alongside the food. The menu centres on seafood: grilled fish, crab cakes, and a decent chowder. The outdoor terrace fills up quickly at sunset, so arrive early or book ahead if you want the prime tables. Prices are reasonable by waterfront-view standards.
