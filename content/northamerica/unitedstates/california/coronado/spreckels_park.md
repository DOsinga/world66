Spreckels Park is Coronado's civic lawn, a block-sized green space shaded by mature trees near Orange Avenue. It is the kind of park that explains the town's slower rhythm: families spread out on the grass, visitors pause between the ferry landing and the beach, and summer concerts turn it into a casual gathering place.

The park is not a blockbuster sight, but it is a useful anchor for exploring Coronado on foot. Pair it with the nearby library, small shops, and residential streets to see the island beyond the Hotel del Coronado postcard.
