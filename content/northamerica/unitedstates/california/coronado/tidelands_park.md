Tidelands Park sits on Coronado's northeast waterfront, looking across San Diego Bay toward the downtown skyline. It is not a blockbuster sight, but it is one of the easiest places on the island to slow down: lawns, picnic tables, a small beach edge, and a paved path that works for a gentle walk or bike ride.

Come here for the view more than for facilities. The bridge, the bay traffic, and the city towers all line up well in the late afternoon, and families use the open grass while cyclists roll past on the Bayshore Bikeway.
