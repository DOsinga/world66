Del Mar is a small, affluent beach community on the coast north of [San Diego](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego). The [wide sandy beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/delmar/del_mar_beach) draws sun-worshippers year-round, and in winter migrating California grey whales can be spotted from the bluffs.

[Del Mar Plaza](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/delmar/del_mar_plaza), perched above the ocean, has an eclectic collection of boutiques and restaurants with sea views. The [Del Mar Thoroughbred Club](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/delmar/del_mar_racetrack) hosts summer horse racing — "where the turf meets the surf" — and the annual San Diego County Fair is a major local event in June and July. Just south of town, [Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/delmar/torrey_pines_state_reserve) protects the rarest native pine in the United States on dramatic coastal bluffs.
