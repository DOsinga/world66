Jake's Del Mar has been on the beachfront at Coast Boulevard since the 1970s, and it remains one of the most reliably good restaurants on the San Diego coast. The setting is the main attraction — a window table at Jake's puts the Pacific Ocean directly in front of you — but the kitchen backs it up with well-executed seafood and Hawaiian-influenced dishes.

The Ahi tuna preparations are dependably excellent, and the fish tacos are among the better versions in a region where fish tacos are taken seriously. Sunset tables are the coveted spots; book well in advance for a Friday or Saturday evening between July and September. 1660 Coast Blvd.
