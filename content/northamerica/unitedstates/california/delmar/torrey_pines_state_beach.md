Torrey Pines State Beach runs below the cliffs of Torrey Pines, with surf, broad sand, and a salt marsh at the north end where the lagoon meets the ocean.

It pairs naturally with Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve above the bluffs. Come for a beach walk, surfing, or the classic view of sandstone cliffs dropping toward the Pacific.
