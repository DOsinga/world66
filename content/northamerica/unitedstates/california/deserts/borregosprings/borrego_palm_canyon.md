The signature hike of the park: a moderate three-mile round trip from the campground up a rocky wash to a hidden grove of California fan palms. The trail follows a seasonal stream — water in winter, dry in summer — past creosote, ocotillo, and the occasional barrel cactus, with the canyon walls closing in until you turn a corner and find a stand of forty-foot palms in a sudden green oasis.

Desert bighorn sheep are sometimes visible on the slopes above; mornings are the best time to spot them. Carry a litre of water per person even in winter, and start early to be back before midday in shoulder season. Park entrance fee at the trailhead applies.
