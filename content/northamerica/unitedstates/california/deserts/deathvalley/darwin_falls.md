Darwin Falls is a spring-fed waterfall reached by a short canyon hike west of Panamint Springs. The 15-foot fall is most striking in spring, and the walk offers a rare green pocket in the Death Valley area. Swimming is not allowed because the water supplies Panamint Springs.
