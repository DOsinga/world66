Devil's Golf Course is one of Death Valley's strangest salt landscapes: a rough field of jagged crystals left by repeated flooding, evaporation, and wind. It is not a place for a long hike, but the texture is extraordinary up close.

Step carefully and stay aware of the heat. The salt formations are fragile, slow to reform, and sharp enough to punish careless walking.
