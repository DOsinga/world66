The Eureka Sand Dunes are among the tallest dunes in the United States, rising in a remote basin in the northern part of Death Valley National Park. Their isolation is part of the appeal, with wide views toward the Last Chance and Saline ranges.

Reaching them requires a long drive on dirt roads, so they are best for travellers who have suitable time, supplies, and vehicle clearance.
