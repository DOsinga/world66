Natural Bridge is a rock span in a narrow canyon south of Furnace Creek, reached by a short dirt access road from Badwater Road. The bridge formed where erosion undercut the streambed and left a natural arch high above the canyon floor.

The walk from the parking area is short, but the canyon walls make it feel more enclosed than many of Death Valley's roadside stops. Go early or late in the day for better light and less heat.
