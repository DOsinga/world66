Racetrack Playa is one of Death Valley's strangest landscapes: a hard, flat lake bed where rocks have left long tracks across the desert floor. The science is now better understood than the old mystery suggested, but the sight still feels wonderfully improbable when you finally reach it.

Getting there is part of the commitment. The usual approach is a long rough road from Ubehebe Crater, so high-clearance preparation matters more here than at most Death Valley viewpoints. For travellers who like remote desert places, it is one of the park's great rewards.
