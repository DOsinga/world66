Cabot's Pueblo Museum is the strange, handmade house Cabot Yerxa built from reclaimed desert materials, inspired by Pueblo architecture and filled with art, artifacts, and local history. It is the one indoor sight in town that feels completely tied to Desert Hot Springs rather than the wider Palm Springs area.
