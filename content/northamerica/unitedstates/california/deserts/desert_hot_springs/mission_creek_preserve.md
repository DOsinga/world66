Mission Creek Preserve protects a wet desert canyon where Mojave and Sonoran habitats meet. Trails follow the creek past cottonwoods, palms, stone ranch ruins, and views toward San Gorgonio, making it one of the best natural escapes on the Desert Hot Springs side of the valley.
