Cholla Cactus Garden is one of Joshua Tree's most memorable short walks: a dense stand of teddy-bear cholla glowing against the desert flats. The loop is brief, but the concentration of cactus makes it feel almost planted.

Do not brush against the spines; they detach easily and are painful to remove. Early morning and late afternoon are the best times for light and heat.
