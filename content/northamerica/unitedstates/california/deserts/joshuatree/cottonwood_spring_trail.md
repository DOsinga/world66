Cottonwood Spring Trail leads to a fan-palm oasis in the southern part of Joshua Tree National Park. The short walk has shade, cottonwoods, palms, and good birdwatching, making it a useful contrast to the park's boulder fields and Joshua tree country farther north. It is especially worthwhile for travellers entering or leaving the park by the Cottonwood entrance.
