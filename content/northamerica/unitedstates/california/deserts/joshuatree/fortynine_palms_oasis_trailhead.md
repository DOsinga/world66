Fortynine Palms Oasis Trailhead is the starting point for a rugged hike to a fan palm oasis on the north side of Joshua Tree National Park. The route crosses exposed desert hills before dropping into a narrow oasis fed by natural water, a classic contrast of harsh desert and sudden green shade.

Start early in hot weather and carry plenty of water. The trail has little shade until the oasis.
