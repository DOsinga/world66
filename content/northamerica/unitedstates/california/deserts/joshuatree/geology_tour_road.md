Geology Tour Road is an 18-mile dirt route through Joshua Tree National Park's desert formations. It links scenic pullouts, trailheads, and climbing access points, with a closer look at the park's rock piles and open desert than the paved roads give.

Four-wheel drive is recommended, and conditions can change after storms. Check with rangers before setting out.
