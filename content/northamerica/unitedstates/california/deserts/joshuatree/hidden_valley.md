Hidden Valley is one of Joshua Tree National Park's classic short walks: a loop through a natural bowl of boulders, pinyon, juniper, and desert plants. The name comes from local stories of cattle rustlers using the enclosed valley as a hideout.

The trail is easy enough for most visitors and gives a quick, concentrated taste of the park's rock formations. It is especially good in late afternoon, when the boulders catch warm light and climbers are often still working the surrounding routes.
