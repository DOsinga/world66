Keys View is one of the easiest big-payoff viewpoints in Joshua Tree. The overlook sits at 5,185 feet and looks out across the Coachella Valley, the San Andreas Fault, and the desert ranges beyond.

Sunset is the classic time to come, when the valley lights start to appear below the mountains. On very clear days the view can stretch past the Salton Sea toward Signal Mountain in Mexico.
