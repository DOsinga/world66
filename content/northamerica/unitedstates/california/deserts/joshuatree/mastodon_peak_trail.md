Mastodon Peak Trail is a strenuous hike in the Cottonwood Spring area of Joshua Tree National Park. The route passes an old gold mine and climbs to broad views over the Eagle Mountains and the Salton Sea.

It is a good choice if you want a harder desert walk away from the park's busiest northern roads. Start early in warm weather and carry more water than you think you need.
