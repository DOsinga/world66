Ryan Mountain Trail is one of Joshua Tree's classic summit hikes, climbing to 5,461 feet with wide views over Lost Horse, Queen, and Pleasant Valleys. The route is strenuous but well maintained, with stone steps for much of the climb. Start early in hot weather and bring more water than the short distance suggests.
