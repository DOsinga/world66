The World Famous Crochet Museum is exactly the kind of tiny, eccentric stop that fits Joshua Tree. Housed in a small green roadside structure, it turns a collection of crocheted animals and objects into a folk-art landmark with more personality than size.

No one should plan a day around it, but that is not the point. It is a quick, memorable stop while exploring Joshua Tree's galleries, shops, and desert-edge oddities, and it captures the town's handmade spirit.
