The valley's main enclosed shopping mall, anchored by Macy's and JCPenney, with the usual mid-market chains in between. It is not a destination in itself, but it is the closest indoor air-conditioned space of any size in summer — locals walk laps inside on the worst days.

Record Alley, a long-running independent record shop, is tucked into the mall and worth a stop if you collect vinyl. There is also a 16-screen cinema attached. Address: 72840 Highway 111.
