---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Palm Desert punches above its weight for a resort town. The Living Desert is the standout — a genuinely impressive zoo and botanical garden, not just for kids. El Paseo offers galleries alongside the shops, and the McCallum Theatre brings in serious touring performers. Beyond the manicured fairways and patio brunches, the Santa Rosa foothills give you real desert hiking starting right at the edge of town.
