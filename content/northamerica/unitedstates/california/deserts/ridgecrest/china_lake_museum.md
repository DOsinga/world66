The China Lake Museum explains the research-and-testing culture that has shaped Ridgecrest since World War II. Displays focus on naval aviation weapons, desert test ranges, and the technical work carried out at the nearby base.

It is a specialist museum, best for travellers interested in aviation, military engineering, or Cold War history. Pair it with Maturango Museum for a fuller sense of why this remote valley became important.
