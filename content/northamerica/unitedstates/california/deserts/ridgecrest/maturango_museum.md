The Maturango Museum is Ridgecrest's best cultural stop and the place to begin if the desert feels empty at first glance. Exhibits cover local geology, wildlife, mining, Native history, and the rock art of the Coso Range.

Its gardens are worth a slow look too, especially if you are new to Mojave plants. The museum also helps travellers understand why this apparently spare landscape has drawn people for thousands of years.
