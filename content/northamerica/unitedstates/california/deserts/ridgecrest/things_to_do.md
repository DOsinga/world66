type: section
---

Ridgecrest's best stops explain the desert around it. The [Maturango Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/deserts/ridgecrest/maturango_museum) is the essential first call, followed by the [China Lake Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/deserts/ridgecrest/china_lake_museum) if aviation and weapons history interest you.

Outside town, the Indian Wells Valley opens into some stark country. The Coso Range is famous for rock art, while Trona Pinnacles and Red Rock Canyon make strong day trips if you have a car, water, and a realistic respect for desert distances.
