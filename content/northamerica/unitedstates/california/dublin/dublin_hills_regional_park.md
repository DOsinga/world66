Dublin Hills Regional Park protects rolling grassland and oak-lined canyons on the west side of Dublin. Its trails climb above suburban streets to open views across the Tri-Valley and toward the surrounding ridges.

The park is a good half-day choice for walkers who want local scenery without leaving the East Bay. Trails are exposed, so carry water and avoid the hottest part of summer days.
