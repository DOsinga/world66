Heritage Park Village is a collection of restored Victorian-era homes and buildings moved to a single site in downtown Escondido to preserve them from demolition. The oldest dates from the 1880s, when the city was founded as an agricultural settlement. Costumed docents interpret life in late-nineteenth-century Escondido, and the museum inside the park displays artefacts, photographs, and memorabilia from the city's early history.

The adjacent Heritage Walk is lined with examples of nineteenth-century California domestic architecture — Queen Anne cottages, Eastlake-style homes, and a working blacksmith's forge. It is a modest attraction, but gives the city a historical grounding that is easy to overlook when you are heading straight for the Safari Park.
