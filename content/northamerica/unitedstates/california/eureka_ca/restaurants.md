---
title: "Where to Eat"
type: section
---

Seafood is the foundation of Eureka's dining scene, and the proximity to Humboldt Bay means oysters, Dungeness crab, and fresh fish are reliably excellent. Old Town is the best area for restaurants — the streets around 2nd and 3rd have a concentration of independent places within easy walking distance of one another. The food quality is notably higher than the city's modest size might suggest, partly because the strong local culture and relative isolation from California's major cities have supported a genuine independent restaurant scene.

The Arcata farmers' market and the strength of local organic farming in Humboldt County means good local produce is available to restaurants year-round.
