The Calaveras County Fair and Jumping Frog Jubilee is Angels Camp's signature event, tying a county fair to the Mark Twain story that made the town famous. The frog-jumping contest is wonderfully specific: comic, competitive, and rooted in the Gold Country's habit of turning local oddities into public ritual.

It is seasonal, so it only belongs on your itinerary if the dates line up. When they do, the fair gives you a better feel for Angels Camp than another quick photo of a historic plaque: livestock, food stands, music, and one very strange sporting tradition.
