Mark Twain's Cabin is a small Gold Country literary stop connected to the Calaveras County stories that helped make Twain famous. The site is humble, but that humility suits the rough mining-camp world behind the tale of the celebrated jumping frog.

It works best as part of a drive through Angels Camp and the surrounding Gold Country rather than as a standalone pilgrimage. Stop for the marker, the setting, and the reminder that American literary history often grew out of very local stories.
