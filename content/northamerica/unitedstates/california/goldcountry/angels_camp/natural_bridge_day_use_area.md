Natural Bridge Day Use Area is a small but memorable outdoor stop near Angels Camp, reached by a short trail that drops to limestone caverns carved by Coyote Creek. In the right season the creek and rock openings make the place feel more adventurous than the modest mileage suggests.

It is a day-use site, so come prepared for heat, uneven footing, and limited parking. Spring is the easiest time to love it, with wildflowers on the approach and cooler weather for the climb back out.
