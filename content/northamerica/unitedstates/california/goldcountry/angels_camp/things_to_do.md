title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Angels Camp is Gold Rush country in miniature: the Main Street retains genuine 19th-century character, the museum tells the mining story well, and the Mark Twain connection gives the place a literary hook that most Gold Country towns lack. The surrounding countryside opens up toward Moaning Cavern and the Calaveras Big Trees, making it a useful base as well as a destination in itself.
