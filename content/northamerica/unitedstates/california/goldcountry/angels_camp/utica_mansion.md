Utica Mansion is an 1882 stone house tied to the Utica Mine, one of the mines that shaped Angels Camp during the Gold Country boom. Its Federal-style design and position near the old mill make it a useful stop for visitors following the town's mining history.

See it together with the Angels Camp Museum and the older commercial buildings along Main Street.
