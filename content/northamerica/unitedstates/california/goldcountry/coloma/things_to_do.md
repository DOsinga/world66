Coloma is essentially one site — [Marshall Gold Discovery State Historic Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/coloma/marshall_gold_discovery_park) — built around the place where James W. Marshall picked up the gold flake that launched California's Gold Rush in 1848. The park contains a working replica of [Sutter's Mill](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/coloma/sutters_mill), a Gold Rush museum, a small Chinese cemetery and the [James W. Marshall Monument](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/coloma/marshall_monument) on the hill above town.

Beyond history, the South Fork American River that runs through town is one of the best-known whitewater rafting stretches in California. Outfitters in the village run half-day and full-day trips on the Class II–III water.
