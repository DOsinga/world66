The Johnny Cash Trail is a paved multi-use path running about three kilometres along the American River Parkway near Folsom. It was named and developed to honour Cash's lasting connection with the city — specifically the two concerts he played at Folsom State Prison in 1968 that produced one of the most celebrated live albums in country music history.

Large outdoor murals at several points along the trail celebrate Cash's life and music, making this more than just a bike path. You can walk the trail in under an hour, or combine it with the wider American River Bike Trail network for a longer ride. The trailhead near Riley Street is the most convenient starting point from the historic downtown. Bring water; shade is limited in summer.
