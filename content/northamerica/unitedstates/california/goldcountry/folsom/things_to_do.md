---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Folsom punches above its weight for a small Gold Country city. The old town along Sutter Street gives you the Gold Rush atmosphere without the crowds of better-known towns further south. The Folsom Powerhouse, the Prison Museum, and the Johnny Cash Trail each tell a different chapter of the city's surprisingly layered history, while the American River and Folsom Lake offer genuine outdoor recreation within minutes of downtown.
