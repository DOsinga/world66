Grass Valley is a Gold Rush-era town in Nevada County in California's Sierra Nevada foothills, at an elevation of about 770 metres. It was one of the richest gold mining areas in California, and the [Empire Mine State Historic Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/grassvalley/empire_mine_state_historic_park) — which operated from 1850 until 1956 and produced nearly six million ounces of gold — is the best reason to visit. The park preserves the shaft house, stamp mill, and owner's "cottage" (in fact a substantial Victorian house), along with the underground shafts that once extended more than a mile below the surface.

What made Grass Valley different from other Gold Rush towns is that it stayed. The [discovery of quartz gold at Gold Hill](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/grassvalley/gold_hill) in 1850 proved that the real deposits were in deep underground veins, not river gravels — and deep mining required capital, machinery, and a permanent workforce. The Cornish miners who came to work the hard-rock mines brought their expertise from the tin mines of Cornwall, along with their pasties, their community organisations, and their stubborn self-reliance. That heritage is still visible: the [North Star Mine and Powerhouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/grassvalley/north_star_mine_and_powerhouse) museum displays one of the Pelton wheels that powered the mines, and [Marshall's Pasties](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/grassvalley/cornish_pasties) on Richardson Street still makes them the traditional way.

The Victorian downtown on Mill Street rewards an afternoon. The [Holbrooke Hotel](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/grassvalley/holbrooke_hotel) — the oldest continuously operating hotel in the Mother Lode — is where Ulysses Grant and Mark Twain slept. The [Home of Lola Montez](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/grassvalley/home_of_lola_montez) recalls the dancer whose affair with a Bavarian king brought her to California and set her up in this modest cottage on Mill Street. The [Del Oro Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/grassvalley/del_oro_theatre) still shows films under its Art Deco neon spire. The surrounding Nevada County has become a destination for artists and Bay Area transplants, and there is an unusually sophisticated food and wine culture for a town of this size. Nearby Nevada City is similarly charming and the two are naturally visited together.
