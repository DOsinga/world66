Across a small valley from the Kennedy Mine stand the headframe and stone ruins of the Argonaut Mine, Jackson's other great gold operation. Like the Kennedy, the Argonaut closed permanently in 1942 and was never reopened. The site is not formally a tourist attraction — the surface buildings are gated and the shafts long sealed — but the headframe is visible from Highway 49 and a memorial to the 47 miners killed in the 1922 fire stands on a small turnout above the road.
