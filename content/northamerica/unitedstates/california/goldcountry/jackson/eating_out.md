---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Jackson eats simply. The historic downtown has a row of low-key places: a brewpub, a couple of cafes, and the National Hotel's bar and dining room — operating since 1862 and one of the more atmospheric places to drink a beer in Gold Country. **Mel and Faye's Diner** on Highway 49 has been the locals' breakfast spot since 1956.

For something more ambitious, drive to nearby Sutter Creek or Amador City, where a number of small farm-to-table restaurants have appeared in recent years, riding the Shenandoah Valley wine boom. **Element Restaurant** in Sutter Creek and the **Imperial Hotel** in Amador City are both worth the short trip.
