Mariposa is a small Gold Rush town in the Sierra Nevada foothills, best known as the western gateway to Yosemite National Park — the valley is about an hour's drive east on Highway 140. The town makes a practical base for Yosemite visits, particularly in summer when accommodation inside the park fills up fast.

The [Mariposa County Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/mariposa/mariposa_county_courthouse), built in 1854, is the oldest continuously operating courthouse in California. The [California State Mining and Mineral Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/mariposa/california_state_mining_museum), at the county fairgrounds, houses an impressive collection of gems, minerals, and gold specimens including a 13.8-pound crystalline gold piece. The [Mariposa Museum and History Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/goldcountry/mariposa/mariposa_history_center) covers the local mining era with a miner's cabin, gold-panning demonstrations, and Native American exhibits.
