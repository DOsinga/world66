---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Murphys punches above its weight for a town this size. Mercer Caverns, a few miles north on a winding road, are genuinely impressive — not a tourist cave but a serious geological site with aragonite crystals and formations most visitors have never encountered before. Ironstone Vineyards, south of town, pairs Gold Rush history with a working winery and a rare crystalline gold specimen that draws serious attention. Back in town, the Murphys Hotel (1856) and the stone buildings along Main Street form one of the better-preserved Gold Rush streetscapes in the Sierra foothills — the kind of architecture that survived because it was built to survive. The Old Timers Museum fills in the human story with well-curated local history. You can cover the main sights in a full day, or stretch it to a weekend if you're working through the wine tasting rooms.
