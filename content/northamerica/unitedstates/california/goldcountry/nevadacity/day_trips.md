---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Nevada City makes a good base for the wider Gold Country. **Grass Valley**, five miles south, has its own historic downtown and the Empire Mine State Historic Park — California's oldest, largest, and richest gold mine, with 367 miles of underground tunnels. **Truckee and Lake Tahoe** are about 45 miles east on I-80, reachable in an hour. 

The **Malakoff Diggins** are the most compelling day trip from Nevada City itself — 26 miles north on a winding road, the hydraulic mining canyon is one of the strangest and most beautiful landscapes in the Sierra foothills. **South Yuba River State Park** at Bridgeport is an easier 15-mile drive south, good for swimming and the covered bridge.
