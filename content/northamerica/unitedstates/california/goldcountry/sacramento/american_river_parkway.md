The American River Parkway is one of the best urban greenways in California. A thousand acres of riparian woodland stretch along the south bank of the American River, threading through Sacramento from the confluence with the Sacramento River at Discovery Park all the way east to Folsom. The parkway has beaches, swimming holes, fishing spots, and an extensive trail network.

Bike rental shops near the river offer access to the Jededith Smith Memorial Trail, which runs 32 miles through the parkway without a stoplight. Salmon run up the river in autumn, visible from the banks. In summer the river beaches fill with families; spring brings wildflowers and nesting herons. This is where Sacramento residents actually spend their outdoor leisure time, as opposed to the tourist-oriented riverfront of Old Sacramento a few miles west.
