Sacramento's new science museum, opened in 2021 on the Old Sacramento waterfront. The Museum of Science and Curiosity (MOSAC) replaced several older science education facilities and consolidated them in a purpose-built building with hands-on exhibits for children and more substantive content for adults. The exhibits cover astronomy, natural history, and California ecology, with particular depth on the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta ecosystem.

The rooftop terrace offers some of the best views of the Sacramento River and the Tower Bridge. The building is striking from the river — a glass-and-steel structure that sits in deliberate contrast to the wooden storefronts of Old Sacramento behind it.
