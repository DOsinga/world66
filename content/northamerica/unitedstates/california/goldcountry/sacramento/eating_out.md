type: section
---

Sacramento declared itself America's Farm-to-Fork Capital in 2012, and the restaurants have made good on the claim. The Central Valley's extraordinary agricultural productivity — almonds, tomatoes, wine grapes, stone fruit, rice — translates directly into menus that change weekly with what is ripe. The best restaurants are concentrated in Midtown, with a secondary cluster in East Sacramento and along Broadway.
