---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sacramento packs a surprising amount into its flat, tree-lined grid. Old Sacramento preserves the Gold Rush years in cobbled streets and 1850s storefronts; the State Capitol anchors Midtown; and the Crocker Art Museum holds the best collection of 19th-century Californian painting you will find outside San Francisco. The American River Parkway gives the city a green corridor unlike anything most state capitals can claim.
