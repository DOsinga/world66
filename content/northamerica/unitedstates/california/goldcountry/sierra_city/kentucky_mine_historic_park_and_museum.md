Kentucky Mine is the clearest way to understand why Sierra City exists. The historic park preserves mining machinery, a stamp mill, mine buildings, and a small museum about the hard-rock gold mines that once worked the slopes around the Sierra Buttes.

In summer, guided tours usually demonstrate the stamp mill and explain how ore was crushed before the gold was recovered. It is a modest museum, but in this remote canyon the machinery feels close to the story.
