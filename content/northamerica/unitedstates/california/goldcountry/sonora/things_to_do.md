Sonora packs a surprising amount into a small downtown. The main draw is the Gold Rush heritage — historic buildings, a excellent county museum, and the preserved ghost town at Columbia a few miles away. Beyond history, the surrounding Sierra Nevada foothills offer hiking, white-water rafting on the Tuolumne River, and a springboard into Yosemite.
