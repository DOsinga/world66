Cowell Ranch State Beach is one of the quieter pieces of the Half Moon Bay coast, reached by a short trail through open fields before the land drops toward the Pacific. The approach is part of the pleasure: it feels rural and exposed, with bluff views, seabirds, and the sound of surf arriving before the beach itself appears.

Access to the sand can vary with conditions and closures, but the overlook alone is worthwhile. It is a good choice when the busier state beach lots feel crowded, and it pairs well with a drive or cycle along the coast south of town.
