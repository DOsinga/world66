The Strand is the best way to understand Hermosa Beach in motion. Cyclists, runners, skaters, dog walkers, and beach cruisers all use the oceanfront path, with volleyball courts on one side and beach houses on the other.

Rent a bike if you want to cover more ground, or simply walk a short stretch around the pier. The path connects easily to Manhattan Beach and Redondo Beach, making it a low-effort South Bay sampler.
