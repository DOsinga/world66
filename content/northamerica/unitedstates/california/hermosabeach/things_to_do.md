title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Hermosa Beach is simple: the beach, the pier, the Strand, and the streets around Pier Avenue. The [Hermosa Beach Pier](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/hermosabeach/hermosa_beach_pier) is the obvious landmark, while the [Strand](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/hermosabeach/the_strand) is the best way to stitch together Manhattan Beach, Hermosa, and Redondo on foot or by bike.

For something more local, look for live music at the [Lighthouse Cafe](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/hermosabeach/lighthouse_cafe), or time a visit around beach volleyball events when the sand becomes the centre of town.
