Bishop's murals form a self-guided public art walk through town, with works that show scenes from Bishop and the Owens Valley. Many were painted through the Bishop Mural Society's annual symposium.

Pick up a brochure locally or follow the murals along Main Street and nearby blocks. It is an easy way to add local history to a short stop in Bishop.
