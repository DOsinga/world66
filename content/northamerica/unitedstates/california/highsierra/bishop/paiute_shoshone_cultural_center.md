The Paiute-Shoshone Cultural Center on the Bishop Paiute Reservation documents the history and culture of the Owens Valley's indigenous people, who have lived in this valley for more than a thousand years. The museum holds basketry, tools, clothing, and photographs, along with exhibits on the traditional seasonal round of the Owens Valley Paiute — the movement between valley floor and mountain meadows that structured their year.

The center also has a gallery showing work by contemporary Native American artists from the region. It is a modest institution by big-city standards but informative and genuine, and visiting it puts the landscape of the Owens Valley in a longer context than the mining and irrigation history that usually dominates. Admission is charged; the center is closed on weekends.
