---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bridgeport is a small town with a short list of places to eat. Options concentrate along Main Street and cater mainly to fishermen, hikers, and road-trippers on US-395. Portions are large and prices are reasonable. Stock up on groceries if you are heading into the mountains for several days — the nearest supermarket is in Mammoth Lakes, 60 miles south.
