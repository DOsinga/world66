The restaurant attached to the Bridgeport Inn serves reliable American diner food — burgers, steaks, and chili — in a Western-themed dining room. It is one of the few places in town with a full dinner menu, which makes it the default choice after a long day on the road or trail. The bar next door is a convivial spot in the evenings.
