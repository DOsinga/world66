The best-preserved ghost town in the American West, Bodie sits on a high desert plateau 13 miles east of Bridgeport on an unpaved road. At its 1870s gold rush peak it was one of the largest towns in California; today around 100 of its original 2,000 buildings still stand, frozen in time with furniture, tools, and household goods still inside.

The park is open year-round, though the road may be impassable in winter without four-wheel drive. Summer mornings are the best time to visit — the light is good, the crowds are thinner before noon, and the high altitude (2,550m) keeps the air cool even in August. Allow at least two hours to walk the grid of streets and peer through the dusty windows.
