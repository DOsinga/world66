Roaring River Falls is a short, forceful waterfall where the Roaring River drops through a narrow granite chute. The viewing area is easy to reach and gives a close look at the power of Kings Canyon's side streams.

A trail connects the falls area with Zumwalt Meadow, about 1.8 miles away, making it a useful stop on a longer canyon-floor visit rather than just a roadside pullout.
