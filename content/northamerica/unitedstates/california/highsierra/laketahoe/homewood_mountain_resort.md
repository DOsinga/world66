Homewood Mountain Resort is the west-shore ski area with the lake right in front of you. Its terrain is smaller than the giant resorts around Tahoe, but that is part of the appeal: runs feel more local, the views over the water are superb, and the mountain is often less frantic than the big-name slopes around Olympic Valley or South Lake Tahoe.

The resort sits on Highway 89 in Homewood, a few miles south of Tahoe City. Winter operations, lift access, and redevelopment work have changed over time, so check the current season status before planning a ski day. When it is operating, Homewood is best for travellers who want Tahoe scenery and a quieter mountain rather than the biggest lift network.
