The Mono Basin National Forest Scenic Area Visitor Center is the best indoor stop before heading out to the lake. Exhibits explain the basin's volcanic geology, bird life, and the long fight over Los Angeles water diversions, which helps make the tufa towers and exposed shoreline mean more when you see them.

Docent-led talks and local permits are handled here, and the terrace has wide views across Mono Lake toward the Sierra. It is practical, but it is also a good short museum for understanding why this strange, salty lake matters.
