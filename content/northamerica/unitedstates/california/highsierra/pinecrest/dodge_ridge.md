Dodge Ridge is the winter anchor near Pinecrest. It is smaller and more local-feeling than Tahoe's big-name resorts, but that is part of its appeal for travelers already on the Highway 108 side of the Sierra.

Use Pinecrest as the quiet base and Dodge Ridge as the active day out. Conditions vary with Sierra storms, so check snow reports before committing the drive.
