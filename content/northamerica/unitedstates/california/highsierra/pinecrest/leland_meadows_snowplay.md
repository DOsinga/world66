Leland Meadows Snowplay is the easy winter alternative to a full ski day. Families come for tubing and snow play, which makes it useful when not everyone in the group wants lifts, lessons, and ski gear.

It is a seasonal stop rather than a deep destination. Pair it with Pinecrest's lake setting and the Highway 108 forest drive.
