---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Tahoe City has no commercial airport. The nearest airports are Reno-Tahoe International Airport (RNO) in Nevada, about 45 minutes east, and Sacramento International Airport (SMF) in California, about two hours west. San Francisco International Airport (SFO) is roughly three hours by car in normal conditions.

**By car:** Highway 89 runs through Tahoe City along the lake shore. From Sacramento and the Bay Area, I-80 brings you to Truckee, where CA-267 heads south to Kings Beach and CA-89 continues to Tahoe City. From Reno, take US-395 south to US-50 west, or I-80 west to CA-89. In winter, chains are frequently required on mountain passes; check Caltrans road conditions before you drive.

**By bus:** The Tahoe Area Regional Transit (TART) bus system connects Tahoe City to Truckee and along the north and west shores. From Truckee, Amtrak Thruway buses connect to the California Zephyr train. Getting to Tahoe City by public transit from the Bay Area or Sacramento is possible but slow.

**By train:** Amtrak's California Zephyr runs through Truckee (not Tahoe City). From Truckee station, a TART bus or taxi completes the journey to Tahoe City in about 20 minutes.
