---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Tahoe City has two peak seasons: summer (June to September) and ski season (December to March). Both are excellent, both are crowded and expensive. The shoulder seasons — May and October–November — offer the quietest experience, though May can be cold and wet and late October sees many businesses begin to close for the off-season.

**Summer** is warm, dry, and busy. Lake temperatures reach their peak in August (still cold by most standards — expect 68°F/20°C at the surface). Water activities, hiking, cycling, and camping are all at their best. Weekends bring large crowds from the Bay Area; if you are coming in high summer, arrive midweek if possible.

**Ski season** depends entirely on snowfall, which varies dramatically year to year. A good snow year in the Tahoe basin is extraordinary — hundreds of inches of Sierra cement that skiers love for its depth. A poor year can mean thin coverage until January. Check resort conditions in real-time; the snow can change quickly.

**Spring and autumn** are the quietest and often most beautiful times. Autumn colour in the Sierra is understated but real: aspens turn gold in October, the light is long and golden, and the lake is at its clearest. The road conditions on mountain passes can become difficult from October onward.
