---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Truckee's bar and cafe scene is concentrated around Commercial Row and the nearby streets, with a mix of mountain-town dive bars, craft breweries, and coffee shops that cater to skiers, hikers, and railroad workers' descendants. [FiftyFifty Brewing Company](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/highsierra/truckee/fiftyfifty_brewing) is the standout, known far beyond Truckee for its barrel-aged imperial stouts.
