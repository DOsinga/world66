title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Truckee area offers year-round outdoor activity in the High Sierra. In winter, [Northstar California](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/highsierra/truckee/northstar_california) and Palisades Tahoe (formerly Squaw Valley) are the largest resorts within 30 minutes. Summer brings hikers onto the Tahoe Rim Trail, swimmers and kayakers to the [Truckee River](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/highsierra/truckee/truckee_river), and mountain bikers to Northstar's extensive trail network.

The most important historical site is [Donner Memorial State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/highsierra/truckee/donner_memorial_state_park) on the shore of [Donner Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/highsierra/truckee/donner_lake) — the Emigrant Trail Museum tells the story of the Donner Party without flinching. Above the lake, the [Donner Pass Summit Tunnels](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/highsierra/truckee/donner_pass_summit_tunnels) from the original transcontinental railroad are open for hiking through. Downtown, [Commercial Row](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/highsierra/truckee/commercial_row) — the Victorian-era main street beside the train tracks — has good restaurants, shops, and a genuine sense of the railroad town Truckee used to be.
