Cathedral Lakes are among Yosemite's classic high-country hiking destinations. The trail climbs steadily from the Cathedral Lakes Trailhead toward Upper Cathedral Lake, with a spur to Lower Cathedral Lake near the top.

The reward is a granite-and-water alpine landscape below Cathedral Peak. Start early in summer, carry water, and expect the altitude to make the climb feel harder than the distance suggests.
