Chilnualna Fall is reached by a strenuous trail from Wawona, climbing past cascades toward the top of the waterfall. The hike is demanding, with steady elevation gain and some exposed stretches, but it gives you a quieter Yosemite waterfall day than the valley's busiest trails.

Treat it as a real hike, not a scenic pull-off. Start early, carry water, and turn around if the climb is more than you wanted; strong hikers can continue farther toward Crescent Lake.
