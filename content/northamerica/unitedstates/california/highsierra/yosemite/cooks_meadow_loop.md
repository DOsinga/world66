Cook's Meadow Loop is a short, flat walk in Yosemite Valley with some of the classic open views of Half Dome, Yosemite Falls, Glacier Point, and Sentinel Dome. The route follows meadow paths and bike paths near the visitor centre and Lower Yosemite Fall area.

It is one of the simplest ways to experience the valley floor without committing to a long hike. Go early or late in the day for better light and fewer people on the paths.
