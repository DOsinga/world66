Dog Lake and Lembert Dome can be combined into a rewarding Tuolumne Meadows hike. Dog Lake adds a quiet alpine lake, while the climb up Lembert Dome gives wide views over the high country. Doing the lake first makes the loop easier, saving the steeper dome section for when you know how much energy you have left.
