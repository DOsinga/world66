Glen Aulin is a Tuolumne Meadows trail destination reached by a moderate route that follows the gravel road behind Soda Springs before dropping toward the High Sierra camp. The walk is known for cascades along the Tuolumne River, especially Tuolumne Falls about four miles from the trailhead.
