Half Dome is Yosemite's great granite landmark, visible from across the valley and unforgettable from the trail. The full hike passes Vernal Fall and Nevada Fall before climbing through forest and open granite toward the summit.

The final cable section is steep, exposed, and serious; hikers need permits, fitness, water, and a clear weather window. Even if you never climb it, Half Dome shapes the whole Yosemite skyline.
