Lower Yosemite Fall is the easy valley-floor route to the base of Yosemite Falls. The paved loop is about a mile long and gives changing views of the waterfall through trees before reaching the viewing area below the lower drop.

The walk is accessible and popular, so expect crowds in peak season. Spring is the most dramatic time, when snowmelt makes the falls thunder; by late summer the flow can shrink sharply or disappear in dry years.
