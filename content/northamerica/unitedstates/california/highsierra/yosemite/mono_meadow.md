Mono Meadow is a quieter Yosemite hike starting from Glacier Point Road. The trail drops steeply at first to a wet meadow, then continues through forest and open country with wider views toward the park's high granite landscape.

Early summer can be muddy, so stay on the trail even where the path is wet. It suits hikers looking beyond the busiest Yosemite Valley routes.
