Ostrander Lake is a backcountry hiking destination south of Glacier Point Road. The trail starts gently, then climbs about 1,500 feet with views toward the Clark Range before reaching the lake. It is a full-day hike for fit walkers and a quieter alternative to Yosemite Valley's busiest trails.
