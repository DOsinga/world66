Poopenaut Valley is a steep trail near Hetch Hetchy that drops from the road toward the Tuolumne River below O'Shaughnessy Dam. The descent is quick, but the climb back out is real, with more than 1,200 feet of elevation gain on the return.

It is a good choice if you want a quieter Yosemite walk away from the valley crowds and are prepared for a hot, exposed uphill finish.
