Sentinel Dome is one of Yosemite's great short-hike viewpoints. The trail leads to the base of the granite dome, followed by a straightforward scramble up the northeast side to a wide 360-degree view of Yosemite Valley, the waterfalls, and the high country.

Many hikers combine it with Taft Point for a longer loop. The exposure is real near the rim, so stay well back from cliff edges and fissures.
