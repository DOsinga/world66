type: poi

Soda Springs is a small natural spring in Tuolumne Meadows where carbonated water bubbles from the ground inside a simple log enclosure. Nearby Parsons Memorial Lodge, built by the Sierra Club in 1915, is a stone landmark tied to early Yosemite conservation history.
