Taft Point is one of Yosemite's classic rim viewpoints, reached by a relatively easy trail from Glacier Point Road. Near the overlook, deep fissures split the granite and drop toward Yosemite Valley, while the railed viewpoint opens to a broad view of El Capitan and the valley walls.

The walk is short enough for many visitors, but the cliff edge is serious. Stay behind railings and keep well back from unprotected cracks, especially with children or in poor weather.
