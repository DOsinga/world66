Upper Yosemite Fall is reached by one of Yosemite's classic old trails, first built in the 1870s. The route climbs hard from the valley toward the top of Yosemite Falls, gaining around 2,700 feet on exposed switchbacks.

It is a demanding hike, especially in heat, but the reward is one of the park's great close-up waterfall experiences and broad views back across Yosemite Valley. Carry water and start early.
