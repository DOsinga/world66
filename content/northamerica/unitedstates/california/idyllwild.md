Idyllwild is a mile-high mountain village above the desert cities of Riverside County. It trades ski lifts and resort sprawl for pines, granite, trailheads, small galleries, and a compact centre where people still wander on foot.

The great shapes above town are Tahquitz Rock and Suicide Rock, long famous in Southern California climbing. Hikers use Idyllwild as a base for the San Jacinto high country, especially the trails from Humber Park and the state park wilderness beyond.

The village itself has enough texture to make the stop pleasant even if you are not climbing: a nature centre, a small local history museum, shops, cafes, and a strong arts-school presence. From Palm Springs or the Inland Empire, it is a real change of air.
