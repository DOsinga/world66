Tahquitz Rock is the granite face that gives Idyllwild its outdoor reputation. Humber Park below it is the practical trailhead for hikes toward Tahquitz Peak, Saddle Junction, and the San Jacinto wilderness; climbers come for the long, clean routes on the rock itself.
