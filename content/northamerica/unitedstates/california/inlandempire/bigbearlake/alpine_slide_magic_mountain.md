The Alpine Slide at Magic Mountain is a long-running Big Bear institution that has entertained families since the 1970s. Riders take a chairlift to the top of the slope, then descend in a sled along a track cut into the hillside. The track curves through pine trees and around banked turns, and riders control their own speed by pressing on a brake handle. The slower you go, the longer you can enjoy the view.

The complex also includes a water slide and an outdoor ice-skating rink (seasonal), making it a practical stop for families who want more than skiing. The slide operates year-round, conditions permitting. It's firmly in the category of classic, unpretentious fun rather than cutting-edge thrill-seeking — the kind of thing that has made it a nostalgic destination for Southern Californians who grew up coming to Big Bear.
