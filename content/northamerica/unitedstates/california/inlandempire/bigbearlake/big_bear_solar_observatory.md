The Big Bear Solar Observatory (BBSO) occupies a small island platform in the middle of the lake, operated by the New Jersey Institute of Technology. It houses the Goode Solar Telescope, with a 1.6-metre aperture the largest solar telescope in the world, capable of resolving features on the Sun's surface smaller than 50 kilometres across. Scientists here study sunspots, solar flares, and coronal activity, providing data used to predict space weather that can disrupt satellites and power grids.

The observatory is not routinely open to the public, but guided tours are occasionally offered. Even from shore, the white dome is visible sitting incongruously in the middle of the lake, a piece of serious science improbably planted in a recreational resort town. Worth a drive past even if a tour isn't available — and worth checking the BBSO website before you visit to see if a public event is scheduled.
