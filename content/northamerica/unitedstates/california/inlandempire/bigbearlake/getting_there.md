---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Big Bear Lake is about 100 miles east of Los Angeles, typically a two-hour drive in normal traffic — though on winter weekends when snow falls, Highway 18 (Rim of the World Drive) and Highway 38 both become heavily congested and CalTrans requires tyre chains at certain elevations. Check Caltrans road conditions (quickmap.dot.ca.gov) before leaving on a ski weekend.

There is no train or frequent bus service to Big Bear. The Mountain Transit Authority operates a limited bus service from San Bernardino, but most visitors drive. If you're staying more than a day, parking in the village is manageable; a single car is fine for getting around the lake.

From Los Angeles, take the I-10 east to Redlands, then Highway 30 and Highway 330 north to Running Springs, joining Highway 18 east to the village. Alternatively, Highway 38 from Redlands takes a longer but more scenic route up through the desert side of the mountains.
