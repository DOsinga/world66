---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Big Bear Lake's activities split neatly by season. In winter, two ski mountains — Snow Summit and Bear Mountain — pull in Southern California families who have never had to drive far to find snow. In summer the lake itself takes over: fishing, kayaking, paddleboarding, and boat rentals fill the marina, while hikers fan out across the surrounding San Bernardino National Forest. The wildlife centre, the solar observatory, and the old amusement slides round out a year-round offer that makes Big Bear genuinely versatile.
