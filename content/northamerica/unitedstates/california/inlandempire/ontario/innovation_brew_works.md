A small craft brewery and taproom run as a teaching operation by Cal Poly Pomona — the brewers are mostly students working under a professional head brewer, which gives the beer list an interesting rotating menu and the room a more academic feel than a typical taproom. Twelve beers on tap on a typical visit, with a leaning toward West Coast IPAs and Belgian-influenced ales.

A reasonable place to spend an hour before catching a show at Toyota Arena.
