A polished steakhouse on Holt Boulevard, Rok handles the special-occasion duty for the southern half of the city — Korean-American family-run, with proper aged steaks, a long sake list, and a small Korean barbecue menu that locals quietly prefer to the American beef. The dining room is dark wood and bottle-lined; the bar pulls a decent late-night cocktail crowd.

Reservations recommended on weekend nights.
