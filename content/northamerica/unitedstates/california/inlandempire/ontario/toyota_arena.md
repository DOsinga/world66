The 11,000-seat multipurpose arena (formerly Citizens Business Bank Arena) is the largest indoor venue in the Inland Empire. Home of the Ontario Reign minor-league hockey team — affiliates of the LA Kings — and the regular tour stop for mid-size touring concerts, comedy specials, and family shows that do not quite scale to the LA arenas.

A Reign game on a Friday night is one of the better-value sports tickets in Southern California. Easy parking, attached to the Toyota Center entertainment complex.
