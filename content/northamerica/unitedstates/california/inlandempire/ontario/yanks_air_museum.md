Yanks Air Museum is an aviation museum at Chino Airport with a large collection of American aircraft, including World War II fighters and dive bombers. The focus is on restoration, preservation, and close-up access to historic planes rather than broad general history.

It is especially worthwhile for aviation enthusiasts, and it pairs naturally with the nearby Planes of Fame Air Museum.
