Rancho Cucamonga sits at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains along historic Route 66. The city blends suburban living with echoes of its wine-country past — the [Cucamonga Valley](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/inlandempire/rancho_cucamonga/cucamonga_valley_ava) was once one of California's most productive grape-growing regions, larger than Napa and Sonoma combined at its 1940s peak.

Visit the [John Rains House](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/inlandempire/rancho_cucamonga/john_rains_house), an 1860s-era restored ranch headquarters, and the restored 1915 [Cucamonga Service Station](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/inlandempire/rancho_cucamonga/cucamonga_service_station) — the most photogenic surviving Route 66 site in the Inland Empire. [Victoria Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/inlandempire/rancho_cucamonga/victoria_gardens), an open-air shopping and dining destination, is the city's centerpiece for an afternoon out.
