Foothill Boulevard follows the original alignment of US Route 66 through Rancho Cucamonga, running east–west along the base of the San Gabriel Mountains. The stretch between Vineyard Avenue and Euclid Avenue preserves the most legible concentration of Route 66 commercial heritage in the Inland Empire: the 1915 Cucamonga Service Station, a 1920s Richfield eagle filling station, period motels converted to other uses, and a succession of stucco commercial buildings from the pre-freeway era.

The drive or walk from the Service Station east to the Cucamonga Winery site takes about 20 minutes on foot. The Route 66 Inland Empire visitors' centre at the Service Station distributes a heritage-corridor driving map. For context, the Mother Road originally ran from Chicago to Santa Monica; this stretch through the Cucamonga Valley was the route followed by the Dust Bowl migrants of the 1930s heading for California.
