North Etiwanda Preserve protects foothill habitat above Rancho Cucamonga, with chaparral, seasonal streams, and views back across the Inland Empire. The main trail network is short enough for a morning but still feels like a real escape from the suburban grid below.

The best-known walk heads toward Etiwanda Falls, which is strongest after winter and spring rain. Start early in hot weather, carry water, and stay on marked trails to protect the fragile slope habitat.
