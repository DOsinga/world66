The site of a small independent recording studio that operated in Cucamonga from 1957 to 1964 under Paul Buff, then briefly as Studio Z under Frank Zappa. The original building, on Archibald Avenue, has long since been demolished and replaced by a strip mall; a small plaque marks the location. The studio was an early home of surf-instrumental and proto-psychedelic recordings — much of the equipment Buff built himself.

For most visitors there is nothing to see. For music fans interested in the early-1960s southern California instrumental scene or Zappa's pre-Mothers years, the spot is a worthwhile two-minute pilgrimage.
