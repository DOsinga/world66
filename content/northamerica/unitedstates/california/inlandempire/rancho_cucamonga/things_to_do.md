---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Rancho Cucamonga's reasons to stop are mostly about Route 66 and the old Cucamonga wine country. The 1915 **Cucamonga Service Station** on Foothill Boulevard has been restored and is the most photogenic Route 66 site in the area; just north, the 1860 **John Rains House** is an early Anglo-American adobe surviving from the cattle-ranch era. The Cucamonga Valley was one of the largest wine regions in 19th-century California, and a few historic wineries — most famously **Galleano Winery** in nearby Mira Loma — still operate.

Most visitors today come for **Victoria Gardens**, a large open-air shopping district with a cultural centre and library that anchors the modern town. The San Gabriel Mountains rise directly behind the city; the trailheads at **Cucamonga Canyon** offer short hikes into the wilderness.
