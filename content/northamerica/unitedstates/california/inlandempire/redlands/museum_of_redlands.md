The Museum of Redlands gives context to a city whose orange groves, rail connections, university, and grand houses tell a distinctly Southern Californian story. Its exhibits focus on local history rather than broad spectacle, which is exactly what makes it useful.

Use it to frame a walk or drive through Redlands' historic neighbourhoods. The museum is best for travellers who like understanding why a small city looks the way it does, not for anyone seeking a major regional attraction.
