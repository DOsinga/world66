---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Redlands' charm is its late-Victorian architecture — the "Marmalade Mansions" built by citrus-boom families in the 1890s — and the unusually rich collection of small museums and civic buildings that the same generation left behind. Start with **Kimberly Crest**, the most famous of the mansions, a French chateau with formal Italian gardens. The **Lincoln Memorial Shrine** attached to the public library is the only Lincoln memorial west of the Mississippi. The downtown around Orange Street is walkable and well-preserved, and the **Smiley Park Historic District** a few blocks south is the residential heart of the old town. Out on Barton Road, the **San Bernardino Asistencia** — a 19th-century outpost of Mission San Gabriel — gives you a quiet pocket of early Californian history.
