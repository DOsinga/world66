Riverside has a small but growing craft beer scene around downtown, with a few taprooms within walking distance of the Mission Inn. The lineup of breweries changes as the scene matures, so it is worth asking locals or checking current listings rather than relying on a fixed list. Most taprooms are casual, welcome drop-ins, and stay open into the evening — a good option after an afternoon of sightseeing.
