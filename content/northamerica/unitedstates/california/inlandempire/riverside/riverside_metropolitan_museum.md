The Riverside Metropolitan Museum covers local history, geography, and culture through rotating exhibits and permanent displays. It is a compact museum rather than an all-day stop, but it gives useful context for Riverside and the Inland Empire.

Its downtown location makes it easy to combine with the Mission Inn, the Riverside Art Museum, and the historic streets around Main Street.
