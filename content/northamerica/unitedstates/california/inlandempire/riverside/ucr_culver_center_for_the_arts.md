UCR Culver Center for the Arts anchors the University of California, Riverside's downtown arts presence, sharing the Main Street complex with the California Museum of Photography. The programming changes often, but the focus is usually contemporary art, experimental film, photography, performance, and work that benefits from a university setting.

It is a useful counterweight to Riverside's mission-era architecture and citrus history. Check the calendar before going, since the value of a visit depends heavily on the current exhibition or screening, but the location makes it easy to combine with a walk through the historic downtown core.
