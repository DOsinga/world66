The Chapel of Memories is a small wooden Catholic church built in 1917 that has had two lives: first as St. Catherine's, the parish church for Temecula's pioneer families, and now as a popular wedding venue. The building is plain and white, with a single steeple — quintessential Southern California vernacular.

It is worth a look as you pass through. The grounds are usually open during the day; check the schedule if you want to peek inside.
