Meritage at Callaway Vineyard is the most reliable wine country restaurant for a proper sit-down dinner. The dining room runs along a glass wall facing rows of vines, so book a sunset table.

The menu is California-Mediterranean — short-rib, halibut, hand-cut pasta — and unsurprisingly the wine list leans hard on Callaway's own viognier and chardonnay. The desserts are above average. Reservations essential on weekends.
