---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Old Town's antique shops are the local specialty — a half-dozen browsable shops along Front and Main Streets, mostly trading in twentieth-century Americana, Western collectibles, and country furniture. The Saturday Farmers' Market on Sixth Street is the place for produce and prepared food.

For everything else, the [Promenade Temecula](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/inlandempire/temecula/promenade_temecula) on the north side of town handles the mall-style shopping.
