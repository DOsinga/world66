---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring and autumn are the obvious choices. March to May brings wildflowers and mild temperatures (highs around 25°C); September and October are warm but tolerable, with the wine harvest in full swing.

Summer is hot — inland-California hot, 35–40°C is normal in July and August — and the wineries are crowded. Winter is quiet, sometimes rainy, but the vineyards have their own austere beauty when the vines are bare.

Three big events worth timing a trip around: the **Temecula Valley Balloon and Wine Festival** in early June; the **Crush Festival** at harvest in September; and the **Old Town Bluegrass Festival** in March. All sell out hotel rooms; book ahead.
