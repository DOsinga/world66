The Swedish Coffee Pot Water Tower is Kingsburg's most immediately recognisable feature — a 1930s water tower painted to look like an enormous red Swedish coffee pot, complete with lid, spout, and handle. It looms over the town centre at around 40 metres and is visible for miles across the flat San Joaquin Valley.

The coffee pot identity is deliberate: the town adopted it as a civic emblem in the 1970s as part of a project to celebrate its Swedish heritage, and it has stuck ever since. The tower appears on local merchandise, town signs, and the letterhead of several businesses. It is the kind of thing that only small American towns do, and Kingsburg does it with genuine enthusiasm.
