---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kingsburg's main attraction is its Swedish heritage district downtown. The town spent decades leaning into its Scandinavian roots, styling buildings in Swedish architectural touches and installing a giant coffee pot water tower as its most recognisable landmark. The annual Swedish Festival in May brings the town's identity to life with folk dancing, traditional food, and crafts.

It's a pleasant stopover rather than a destination in its own right. The town is compact enough to walk through in an hour, with the best Swedish-styled buildings concentrated on Draper Street downtown.
