Ravenswood House is a Victorian-era estate on the edge of Livermore, set among lawns, old trees, and the quieter side of the valley's wine country. The house was built as a country retreat, and it still has that slightly removed feeling, even though modern Livermore has grown around it.

The site is best for travellers interested in local history, architecture, or a low-key stop between wineries. Public access depends on tours, events, and grounds hours, so check before making a special trip. When open, it gives a useful domestic counterpoint to Livermore's laboratories, vineyards, and downtown landmarks.
