Belmont Veterans Memorial Pier pushes out from the beach at the eastern end of Long Beach, giving Belmont Shore a proper oceanfront landmark. It is a simple pleasure: people fishing from the railings, beach cyclists passing below, and broad views back along the sand toward the city. The pier also gives you a better sense of Long Beach's scale than the denser downtown waterfront.

Use it as the turn-around point for a Belmont Shore walk or bike ride. The city lists fishing and strolling as the main uses, and that is about right. It is not a polished attraction, but it earns its place because it anchors one of Long Beach's most traveller-friendly beach districts.
