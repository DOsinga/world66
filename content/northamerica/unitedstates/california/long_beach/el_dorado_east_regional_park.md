El Dorado East Regional Park is Long Beach's big inland breathing space, with lakes,
picnic areas, trails, sports fields, and open lawns spread across the eastern side of
the city. It is the place to go when the waterfront is crowded or when you want shade
and room rather than sand.

The park works well for cycling, birding, family picnics, and low-key walks, and it sits
near the nature center for a more habitat-focused visit. Drivers should check entry fees
and gate hours before heading over.
