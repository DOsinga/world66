Long Beach Performing Arts Center anchors the city's downtown cultural calendar with theatre, concerts, dance and convention-adjacent events. The complex includes major performance spaces near the waterfront, making it useful for visitors staying around the Pike, Shoreline Village or convention centre.

It is event-dependent: the building matters most when something good is on. Check the schedule before you go, and use it as an evening plan after a waterfront day rather than a daytime sightseeing stop.
