The Museum of Latin American Art in the East Long Beach arts district is the only museum in the United States dedicated exclusively to modern and contemporary Latin American art. The permanent collection covers painting, sculpture, photography, and mixed media from across Latin America and Latino artists in the US, from the early twentieth century to the present.

The building itself was designed by Mexican architect Ricardo Legorreta and is distinctive for its bold, saturated colours. The outdoor sculpture garden is particularly well done and worth spending time in. MOLAA runs a busy programme of temporary exhibitions, films, and events, and the small café on site is genuinely good. Admission is modest and the museum is manageable in two to three hours.
