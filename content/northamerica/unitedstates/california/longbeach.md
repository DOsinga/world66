Long Beach sits where Los Angeles meets the Pacific, but it has never been content to play second fiddle to its famous neighbour. California's fifth-largest city has its own personality — part gritty port town, part beachside playground, part cultural hub with a surprisingly good arts scene.

The waterfront downtown area anchors everything. The [Queen Mary](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/queen_mary) looms at her permanent berth, an art deco ocean liner frozen in time. Across the harbour, the [Aquarium of the Pacific](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/aquarium_of_the_pacific) is one of the best aquariums on the West Coast. [Shoreline Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/shoreline_village) and [The Pike](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/pike_outlets) give the waterfront a boardwalk feel without too much tourist kitsch. Just offshore, the [Astronaut Islands](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/astronaut_islands) — four oil platforms disguised as tropical resort islands — are one of California's stranger sights.

Head inland along Pine Avenue and you hit the real downtown — restaurants, bars, and a growing arts district. East along the coast, [Belmont Shore](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/belmont_shore) has a neighbourhood feel with independent shops and restaurants along 2nd Street, plus the unexpected charm of gondola rides through the [Naples canals](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/naples_canals). Further out, the Cal State Long Beach campus hides the [Earl Burns Miller Japanese Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/earl_burns_miller_japanese_garden) and the [Rancho Los Alamitos](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/rancho_los_alamitos) historic ranch.

Long Beach is also a working port, one of the busiest in the country, and that industrial edge gives the city a grounded quality you don't find in LA's glossier neighbourhoods. The cultural diversity is genuine — the food scene ranges from Cambodian to Mexican to craft cocktails — and the Long Beach jazz festival draws serious crowds every summer.
