Signal Hill Hilltop Park offers one of the easiest wide views over Long Beach, the port, Palos Verdes and, on clear days, the mountains beyond Los Angeles. Interpretive panels point out the area's oil-field history as well as the modern skyline.

The park is small, but the viewpoint is useful at sunset or when you want to understand Long Beach's geography from above. It is best reached by car or rideshare and paired with other inland Long Beach stops.
