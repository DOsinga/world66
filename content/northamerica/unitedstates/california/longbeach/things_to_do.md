The [Queen Mary](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/queen_mary) and the [Aquarium of the Pacific](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/aquarium_of_the_pacific) anchor the waterfront, but Long Beach has more depth than those headline attractions suggest. [Belmont Shore](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/belmont_shore) is the neighbourhood for an afternoon walk and dinner, and [Naples](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/naples_canals) just to the south has the improbable canal system that gives it a character unlike anywhere else in LA County.

Off the harbour, the [Astronaut Islands](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/astronaut_islands) — four artificial oil platforms disguised as tropical resorts — are one of the stranger sights in California. The [Museum of Latin American Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/museum_of_latin_american_art), [Rancho Los Alamitos](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/rancho_los_alamitos), and the [Earl Burns Miller Japanese Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/longbeach/earl_burns_miller_japanese_garden) round out the cultural options.
