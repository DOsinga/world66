The American Merchant Marine Veterans Memorial stands on the San Pedro waterfront, honoring merchant mariners who served in wartime convoys and supply routes. Its dramatic figures make it more striking than a standard plaque or marker, and the harbor setting fits the subject.

It is a short, worthwhile stop while visiting San Pedro's waterfront, Point Fermin, or the port area.
