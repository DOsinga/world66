The Audubon Center at Debs Park is an urban nature center focused on the native habitats of the Los Angeles Basin. It is a useful starting point for short walks, birdwatching, and learning about local plants and wildlife.

The center works well as a quiet outdoor stop northeast of Downtown Los Angeles.
