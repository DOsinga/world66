Baldwin Hills Scenic Overlook rises more than 300 feet above the surrounding neighborhoods, with broad views across the Los Angeles basin. Visitors can drive to the top or climb the switchbacks and steps from Jefferson Boulevard; short nature trails and a visitor center add context on local history and wildlife.
