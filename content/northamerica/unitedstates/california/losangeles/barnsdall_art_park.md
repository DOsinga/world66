Barnsdall Art Park sits on a small East Hollywood hill with neighbourhood views and an arts complex at the top. The draw is a mix of architecture, local culture, and open space: galleries, classes, a community theatre, and the Frank Lloyd Wright-designed Hollyhock House nearby.
