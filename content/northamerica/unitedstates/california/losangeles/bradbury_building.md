A small office building on Broadway with one of the most extraordinary interiors in California — a five-storey atrium of glazed brick, wrought-iron stair rails, and open-cage elevators flooded with light from above. The architect, George Wyman, was an untrained draughtsman who claimed the design came from a spiritual consultation.

The building is still in commercial use, so public access is limited to the ground floor and the first landing of the stairs. Best seen on a sunny morning. The interior features in the original *Blade Runner* and dozens of other films.
