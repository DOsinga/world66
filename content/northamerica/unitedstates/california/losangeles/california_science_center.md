The California Science Center is one of Los Angeles' best family museums, with hands-on exhibits on technology, biology, space, and flight. The galleries include aircraft, touch tanks, a kelp forest aquarium, and plenty of displays aimed at curious children.

The headline exhibit is the Space Shuttle Endeavour. Timed reservations may be needed for shuttle access, and weekday afternoons are usually calmer than school-group-heavy mornings.
