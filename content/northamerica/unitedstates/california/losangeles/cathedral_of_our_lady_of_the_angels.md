The Cathedral of Our Lady of the Angels is the main cathedral of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Los Angeles. Opened in 2002, it replaced the earthquake-damaged Cathedral of Saint Vibiana and brought a strikingly modern religious building to the edge of downtown.

Its austere exterior divides opinion, but the scale, tapestries, alabaster windows, and calm interior make it worth stepping inside if you are already around Bunker Hill, Grand Park, or the Civic Center.
