Chinatown Central Plaza is the old heart of Los Angeles Chinatown, a pedestrian mall lined with pagoda-style buildings, lanterns, shops, and restaurants. Look for the wishing fountain and the Bruce Lee statue, then use the plaza as a starting point for a walk through the surrounding streets.
