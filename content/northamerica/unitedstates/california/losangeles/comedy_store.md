The Comedy Store occupies the old Ciro's nightclub building at 8433 Sunset Boulevard, and since Sammy Shore and Mitzi Shore opened it as a comedy club in 1972 it has been the most important stand-up room in the country. Richard Pryor, Robin Williams, David Letterman, and Jim Carrey all worked the stage here; the names of comics who have played the room are painted across the black exterior wall like a memorial.

There are three rooms — the Main Room, the Original Room, and the smaller Belly Room — running shows most nights of the week. Drop-in sets from major names are common and unannounced, part of the room's enduring mystique.
