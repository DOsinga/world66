The home of the Los Angeles Dodgers, opened in 1962 on a hilltop in Chavez Ravine just north of downtown. With more than 56,000 seats it is one of the largest baseball stadiums in the world, and its terraced design and panoramic views over the basin make it one of the most distinctive.

A regular season game is the easier ticket; even casual non-fans tend to enjoy the experience. Stadium tours run on most non-game days. Parking is famously fraught; ride-share or the dedicated Dodger Stadium Express bus from Union Station are simpler.
