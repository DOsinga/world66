Ennis House is one of Frank Lloyd Wright's Los Angeles textile-block houses, set high in Los Feliz below Griffith Park. Its patterned concrete blocks and stepped massing give it a monumental, almost ancient look that has made it a favourite of architecture fans and film location scouts.

Access changes, and the house is primarily appreciated from the outside, so check current visiting arrangements before going. Even as an exterior stop, it pairs well with Griffith Park, the Observatory, and other Wright sites around Los Angeles.
