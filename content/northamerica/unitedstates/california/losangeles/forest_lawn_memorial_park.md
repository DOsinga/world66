Forest Lawn Memorial Park in Glendale is both a cemetery and a Los Angeles cultural landmark. Visitors come for its landscaped grounds, memorial art, and the graves of many film, music, and entertainment figures, though the appeal is quiet wandering rather than sightseeing in a hurry.
