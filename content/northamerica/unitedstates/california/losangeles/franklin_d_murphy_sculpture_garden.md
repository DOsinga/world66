Franklin D. Murphy Sculpture Garden is an outdoor sculpture collection on the UCLA campus, with modern and contemporary works by artists including Alexander Calder, Henri Matisse, and Auguste Rodin.

It is best treated as a quiet art walk rather than a formal museum visit. The garden is especially worthwhile if you are already in Westwood or combining it with the nearby Getty Center.
