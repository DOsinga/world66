A 1935 Art Deco observatory on the south slope of Mount Hollywood, with planetarium shows, a Foucault pendulum, the original 12-inch Zeiss telescope, and the city's best free view over downtown to the Pacific. The building featured prominently in *La La Land* and *Rebel Without a Cause*.

Free admission to the building and the grounds; planetarium shows have a small charge. Parking is constrained — go on a weekday or take the DASH shuttle from the Vermont/Sunset metro stop. Best at sunset for the view.
