Heritage Square Museum gathers Victorian-era houses and other late-19th-century buildings that were moved here from around Los Angeles. Guided tours explain the architecture, domestic life, and preservation work, often with costumed interpreters adding period context.

The museum sits by the Arroyo Seco, north of downtown. It is best treated as a focused historic-house stop rather than a drop-in park; check tour times before going.
