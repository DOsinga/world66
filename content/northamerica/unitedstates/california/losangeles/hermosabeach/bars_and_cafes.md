---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Hermosa's bar scene is clustered around Pier Avenue and the beach, so it is easy to improvise after dinner. The [Lighthouse Cafe](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/losangeles/hermosabeach/lighthouse_cafe) is the standout because it has a real music history rather than just a good location.

Weekend nights can be loud and crowded. For a calmer drink, come earlier in the evening or stay a few blocks back from the pier.
