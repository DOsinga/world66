---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Hermosa's beach is wide, sandy, and active, with volleyball courts, surfers, runners, and cyclists all sharing the same narrow coastal strip. It is more social than secluded, especially near Pier Avenue.

If you want a longer outing, follow the Strand north to Manhattan Beach or south toward Redondo. Distances are short, flat, and far more pleasant by bike or on foot than by car.
