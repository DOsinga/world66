---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Hermosa Beach is southwest of central Los Angeles and south of LAX. Driving is the usual approach, but parking close to the beach can be tight on sunny weekends and summer evenings.

Cycling along the Strand is the most pleasant way to move between the South Bay beach towns once you are there. Rideshare trips work well for evenings when you do not want to deal with beach parking.
