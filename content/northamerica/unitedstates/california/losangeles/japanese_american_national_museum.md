The Japanese American National Museum in Little Tokyo tells the history of Japanese Americans, with particular strength on the World War II incarceration camps. Its collections include photographs, documents, personal objects, and art that make the story both national and intimate.

The museum is one of downtown Los Angeles's most important cultural stops and pairs naturally with a walk through Little Tokyo.
