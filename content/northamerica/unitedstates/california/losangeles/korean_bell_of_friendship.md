The Korean Bell of Friendship stands in a hilltop pavilion above the harbor in San Pedro, with wide views over the ocean, Catalina Channel, and the Port of Los Angeles. South Korea gave the bronze bell to Los Angeles in 1976, and it is still rung on a few ceremonial days each year.

Come for the views as much as the bell itself. It pairs well with nearby Point Fermin and the coastal paths around Angels Gate Park.
