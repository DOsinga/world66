The Self-Realization Fellowship Lake Shrine Temple is a peaceful 10-acre spiritual site in Pacific Palisades.

Its gardens, spring-fed lake, lotus flowers and Mahatma Gandhi World Peace Memorial make it a worthwhile quiet stop between the coast and the Getty Villa.
