Los Angeles Live Steamers Railroad Museum is a volunteer-run miniature railway museum in Griffith Park. Families come for the small-scale train rides, while railway fans can look at the historic cars and workshop displays.

The most notable exhibit is Walt Disney's former backyard workshop, moved here from his Holmby Hills home. Check operating days before going, since the rides depend on volunteer schedules.
