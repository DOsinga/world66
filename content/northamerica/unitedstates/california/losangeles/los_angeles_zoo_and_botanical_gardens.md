Los Angeles Zoo and Botanical Gardens fills the northern edge of Griffith Park with animal habitats, shaded paths, and planted grounds. The strongest exhibits include chimpanzees, gorillas, elephants, reptiles, and a children's zoo.

It is an easy family stop if you are already building a Griffith Park day around the Observatory, hiking trails, or the Autry Museum. Check the daily schedule for feedings and animal talks before you choose your route through the zoo.
