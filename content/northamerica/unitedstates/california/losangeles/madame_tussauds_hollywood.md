Madame Tussauds Hollywood is the local branch of the wax museum chain, set beside the busiest stretch of Hollywood Boulevard. The figures lean heavily toward film, television, and pop culture, so it fits naturally with the Chinese Theatre and Walk of Fame nearby.

It is polished, touristy, and easy to fold into a Hollywood walk, especially with children or film fans who want a light indoor stop.
