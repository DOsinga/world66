The Montebello Genocide Memorial is one of the most important Armenian memorials in the United States. Its stark stone form stands in Bicknell Park and commemorates the victims of the Armenian Genocide.

It is a quiet, reflective stop rather than a museum. The memorial is most meaningful if you are tracing Armenian history in Los Angeles, whose Armenian community is one of the largest outside Armenia.
