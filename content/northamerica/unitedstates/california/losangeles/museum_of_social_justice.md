The Museum of Social Justice occupies the 1920s La Plaza Methodist Church near El Pueblo and Union Station. It is a small museum, but the setting and subject matter make it a worthwhile add-on if you are already exploring the historic core of Los Angeles.

Exhibits change, drawing on the church's archives and on wider themes of immigration, labour, faith, and civil rights in Southern California. Check current hours before going, as small museums in this part of town can keep limited schedules.
