The Museum of Television and Radio is a media-focused museum in Beverly Hills, known for its archive of television and radio programming. Visitors can browse recordings from the collection, making it a useful stop for people interested in broadcasting history, classic shows, or American media culture.

The Richard Meier-designed building is part of the appeal, with a clean modernist look that stands out among the shops and offices nearby.
