The Museum of Tolerance focuses on the Holocaust, racism, and prejudice through immersive and interactive exhibits. Its strongest sections use survivor testimony, historical material, and staged choices to make visitors think about how hatred is built and how ordinary people respond to it.

The museum is serious in tone and is best treated as a half-day cultural stop, not a casual gallery visit.
