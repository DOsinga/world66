The Music Box Steps are a steep public staircase made famous by Laurel and Hardy's 1932 short film *The Music Box*. There is not much to do beyond climb them and smile at the absurdity of moving a piano here, but they make a good small stop for film fans exploring the hills north of downtown Los Angeles.
