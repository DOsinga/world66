The Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County is one of the city's major museums, with dinosaur halls, gems and minerals, dioramas, and exhibits on the natural and human history of Southern California. It dates to 1913 and remains one of the anchor attractions around Exposition Park.

Allow a couple of hours, more with children or if you also plan to visit the gardens and nearby museums.
