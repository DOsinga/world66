The Pacific Design Center is hard to miss: three enormous buildings sheathed in cobalt blue, green, and red glass, designed by Cesar Pelli and built in phases between 1975 and 2013. Angelenos nicknamed the first tower the Blue Whale. The complex holds more than a hundred interior-design and furnishing showrooms, most trade-oriented but several open to the public.

It anchors West Hollywood's Avenues of Art and Design, a cluster of galleries and showrooms along Melrose Avenue and Beverly Boulevard. The MOCA satellite gallery and a public plaza make it worth a look even if you are not shopping for a sofa.
