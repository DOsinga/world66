The Pantages Theatre is one of Hollywood's great Art Deco survivors, opened in 1930 and restored as a major venue for Broadway touring productions. Its lobby and auditorium are the reason to arrive early, even if you are here for the show.

Check the calendar before you go. Outside performance times, it is mainly an exterior architecture stop on Hollywood Boulevard.
