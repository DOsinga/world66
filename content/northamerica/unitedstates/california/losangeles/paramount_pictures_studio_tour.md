Paramount is the last major film studio still operating in Hollywood, with a backlot that has been in use since 1927. The guided tour takes you through a working production lot tied to films such as *Sunset Boulevard*, *Rear Window*, and *Breakfast at Tiffany's*.

It is best for travelers who care about film history, since the appeal is not just seeing sets but standing inside one of Hollywood's old studio empires.
