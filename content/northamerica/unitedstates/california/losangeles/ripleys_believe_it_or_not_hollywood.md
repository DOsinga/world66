Ripley's Believe It or Not Hollywood is an oddities museum on Hollywood Boulevard, close to the Chinese Theatre and Walk of Fame. The exhibits lean into optical illusions, strange collections, and sideshow-style curiosities.

It is best treated as a light family stop or a bad-weather break while walking the main Hollywood strip, not as one of Los Angeles' major museums.
