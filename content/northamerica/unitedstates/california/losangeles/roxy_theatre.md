The Roxy Theatre opened on the Sunset Strip in 1973, founded by record producer Lou Adler and club owner Elmer Valentine, and quickly became one of the defining venues of the LA rock scene. Neil Young, Bob Marley, and Bruce Springsteen played early West Coast shows here; the original American production of *The Rocky Horror Show* ran upstairs.

It remains a working club, a small standing room where you can still catch big names playing intimate sets. Above it, the On the Rox lounge was a notorious 1970s celebrity hideaway. Together with the Whisky a Go Go and the Roxy's neighbours, it anchors the Strip's live-music history.
