The Downtown Santa Monica Metro station opened in 2016 as the western end of the E Line (Expo Line), connecting the city to downtown Los Angeles and Culver City without a car. The station sits at 4th Street and Colorado Avenue, a short walk from the pier, Palisades Park, and Third Street Promenade.

The opening of rail access changed the dynamics of visiting Santa Monica noticeably: the parking situation, once a source of genuine frustration, became less critical, and the beach became meaningfully accessible from central LA without fighting traffic on the I-10. The station design — open-air, with views toward the ocean — makes the arrival feel like an event rather than just transport.
