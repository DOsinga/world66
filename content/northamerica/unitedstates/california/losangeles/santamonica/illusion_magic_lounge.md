The Illusion Magic Lounge is a dedicated magic theatre on Lincoln Boulevard, one of a small number of permanent magic venues in the United States. Founded by magician Steve Spill in 1998, it seats about 150 people and runs shows several nights a week. The format is traditional close-up and stage magic rather than Las Vegas-style spectacle — the emphasis is on technique and presentation rather than scale.

It is the kind of entertainment that works unusually well in person when done well, and the intimate scale makes the performance more engaging than a larger venue would. Check the schedule for visiting performers alongside the resident programme. Tickets are typically $30–50.
