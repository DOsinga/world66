The 2nd Street Tunnel runs beneath Bunker Hill and is one of downtown Los Angeles's most recognisable film locations. Its glossy white tiles and soft glow have appeared in films, music videos, and car commercials for decades.

It is still an active road tunnel, so this is a quick look rather than a place to linger. The best reason to seek it out is as part of a downtown architecture or film-location walk.
