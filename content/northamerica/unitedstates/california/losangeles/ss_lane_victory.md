The S.S. Lane Victory is a preserved World War II-era Merchant Marine vessel in San Pedro. Visitors can explore the ship and see museum displays in its cargo holds, which explain its wartime service and the role of cargo ships in the Allied effort.

It is a strong stop for maritime history fans and pairs naturally with other harbor-area sights.
