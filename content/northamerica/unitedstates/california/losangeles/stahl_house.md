The Stahl House is one of Los Angeles' best-known modernist houses, a glass-and-steel landmark perched above the city. Its dramatic setting and clean Case Study House design have made it a familiar image in architecture books, films, and photography.

Tours are limited and usually need to be reserved well ahead, but architecture fans will find it one of the area's strongest house visits.
