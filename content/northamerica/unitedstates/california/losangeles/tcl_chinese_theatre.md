Sid Grauman's 1927 Chinese-style movie palace on Hollywood Boulevard, with the famous forecourt of star handprints and footprints in concrete. It remains a working cinema, often used for premieres; the interior is one of the more elaborate surviving picture palaces in the country.

The forecourt is free to wander at any time. Tours of the auditorium run several times a day and are worth the half hour for the architecture.
