A free contemporary art museum opposite Disney Concert Hall, opened in 2015 to house the collection of Eli and Edythe Broad. The galleries are deliberately white and over-lit — partly architectural choice, partly because the works are so famous they need no introduction.

The Yayoi Kusama Infinity Mirror Room is the most coveted piece and requires a separate same-day timed ticket, picked up at the museum entrance. Free admission to the main galleries, but book online weeks ahead to avoid waiting.
