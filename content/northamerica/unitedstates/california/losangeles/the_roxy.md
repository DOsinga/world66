The Roxy opened on the Sunset Strip on 20 September 1973, backed by a partnership of Lou Adler, Elmer Valentine, David Geffen, Elliot Roberts, and Peter Asher, and immediately became one of the defining venues of the LA rock scene. Neil Young recorded *Tonight's the Night* here, Bruce Springsteen played a legendary early-career show, and the American premiere of *The Rocky Horror Show* ran upstairs.

It remains a working club with a capacity of around 500, similar to its neighbour the Whisky a Go Go, and still books big names into an intimate room. Above it, the On the Rox lounge was a notorious 1970s celebrity hideaway and occasionally opens to the public for events.
