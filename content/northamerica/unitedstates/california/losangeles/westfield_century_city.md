The outdoor shopping centre at the heart of Century City, rebuilt in 2017 into one of the more pleasant malls in the city: open-air walkways, a large Eataly food hall, an Equinox, and the usual run of upscale anchors and brand flagships. It serves as the de facto town centre of an otherwise corporate district of glass towers.

For most visitors it is a convenience rather than a destination — somewhere to eat, catch a film, or shop if you are already in Century City or neighbouring Beverly Hills. The food hall and the dining terraces are the main reason a traveller might seek it out.
