Leo Carrillo Beach is one of western Malibu's classic Pacific Coast Highway stops, with a wilder feel than the central Malibu beaches. Its coves, rock formations, and open shoreline have made it a frequent filming location, including appearances in *Grease*, *Gidget*, *The Karate Kid*, *Point Break*, and *Inception*.

Come for the beach first and the film associations second. It works well as part of a slow drive along the coast, especially if you want tide pools, cliffs, and a less built-up stretch of Malibu.
