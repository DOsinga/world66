Malibu Lagoon sits at the mouth of Malibu Creek, where brackish water, sandbars, and low coastal scrub make one of the easiest wildlife stops in town. It is part of Malibu Lagoon State Beach and lies beside Surfrider Beach, so a visit can move naturally from birdwatching to watching longboarders work the famous point break.

The lagoon is best approached slowly. Boardwalks and paths give views over the wetland without pushing into fragile habitat, and the Adamson House and Malibu Pier are close enough to make this a compact half-day on foot. Bring binoculars if you have them; even a short visit usually produces herons, shorebirds, and a sharper sense of how much ecology is tucked between Pacific Coast Highway and the ocean.
