Paramount Ranch is a former Hollywood filming location in the Santa Monica Mountains. Paramount Studios built it during the golden age of Hollywood, and the National Park Service later took over the site.

Visitors come for the film history, open landscape, and easy walks through a ranch setting that has appeared in westerns and television productions. Check current National Park Service access before going, as fire recovery and site work can affect what is open.
