Point Dume is one of Malibu's defining coastal landmarks, a bluff-backed headland with trails, beach access, and wide views over Santa Monica Bay. The cliffs, coves, and open ocean make it feel more rugged than many of the beaches along Pacific Coast Highway.

The short walks are easy to combine with swimming, tidepooling, or whale watching in season. Come early for parking and clearer light; by late afternoon the headland is especially beautiful, with the sun dropping across the water and the coast curving away toward Zuma and the Channel Islands.
