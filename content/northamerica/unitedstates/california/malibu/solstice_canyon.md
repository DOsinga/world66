Solstice Canyon is one of Malibu's best short hikes, climbing through chaparral and sycamores to stone ruins and a seasonal waterfall. It gives you the coastal Santa Monica Mountains without requiring a full backcountry day.

The trail is popular for good reason: it is close to the Pacific Coast Highway, scenic almost immediately, and varied enough for visitors who want more than a beach stop. Start early in warm weather, carry water, and watch for fire restrictions or trail closures after storms.
