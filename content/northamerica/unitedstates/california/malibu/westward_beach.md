Westward Beach is the broad, cliff-backed strand on the west side of Point Dume, less manicured than some Malibu beaches and often more dramatic. The sand runs below bluff faces and surf breaks, with the headland giving the whole place a strong sense of edge.

It is a good choice for a long beach walk, sunset, or a link with the Point Dume trails above. Conditions can be rougher than they look, so treat swimming with caution and come for the scenery first.
