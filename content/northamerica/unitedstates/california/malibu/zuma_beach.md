Zuma Beach is one of Malibu's most practical classic beaches: long, broad, scenic, and easier to use than many smaller coves along the Pacific Coast Highway. It is popular for swimming, surfing, sunbathing, and simply seeing the open sweep of the Malibu shoreline.

The beach is exposed to surf and rip currents, so conditions matter. It is at its best when you want a full beach stop with parking and space rather than a hidden cove or quick photo pullout.
