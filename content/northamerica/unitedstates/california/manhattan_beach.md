Manhattan Beach is one of the South Bay beach towns south of LAX, sandwiched between Hermosa Beach and El Segundo along the [California](/northamerica/unitedstates/california) coast. It has a quieter, more affluent feel than nearby Venice or Santa Monica — [the Strand](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/manhattan_beach/the_strand), a paved path running along the beach, is the social centre, busy with joggers, cyclists, and people walking dogs.

[The pier](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/manhattan_beach/manhattan_beach_pier) stretches out over good surf breaks, and the Roundhouse Marine Studies Lab at its end is a free aquarium. Downtown Manhattan Beach, a few blocks inland, has restaurants and shops along Manhattan Beach Boulevard. It is a pleasant, low-key beach town — not a destination in itself, but a good place to spend a day if you are in the LA area and want sand without the crowds.

One place worth knowing about is [Bruce's Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/manhattan_beach/bruces_beach) at the north end of town, where historical markers tell the story of a Black-owned beach resort seized by the city in the 1920s — a reminder that access to this now-exclusive coastline has not always been equal.
