Manhattan Beach Pier is the town's classic focal point, stretching from the end of Manhattan Beach Boulevard into the Pacific. The roundhouse at the end, the surfers below, and the long strand of sand on either side make it one of the easiest places to read the South Bay beach scene at a glance.

Come near sunset if you can. The pier is short enough for a casual stroll but visually strong, with views north toward Santa Monica Bay and south along the coast. It also works as a natural starting point for walking or cycling The Strand.
