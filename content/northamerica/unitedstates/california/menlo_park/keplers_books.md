Kepler's Books on El Camino Real is one of the Bay Area's best-loved independent bookstores and a genuine cultural institution rather than just a shop. It has been in continuous operation since 1955, with one near-fatal interruption in 2005 that ended with the community pulling the store back from the brink. The current location has a large floor area, an excellent selection across all genres, and an active events programme that brings authors and thinkers through on a regular basis.

The store is close to the Menlo Park train station and Cafe Barrone next door, making it a natural combined stop. The events programme spans talks, readings, and community discussions, and tends to attract a well-informed Peninsula audience. Signed editions of recent titles are stocked after author events. For anyone staying in the area, Kepler's is worth a dedicated visit rather than just a browse.
