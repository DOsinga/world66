Menlo Park Farmers Market fills Sunday morning with local produce, flowers, baked goods, nuts, seafood, and other Bay Area staples. It is the kind of stop that works best if you are staying nearby or want a low-key start before wandering Santa Cruz Avenue.

Expect a neighbourhood market rather than a destination food hall. Live music is common, and the stalls are useful for picnic supplies before heading toward Stanford, Palo Alto, or the nearby parks.
