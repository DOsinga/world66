The Stanford Linear Accelerator Center, usually called SLAC, is a major physics research site run by Stanford University for the U.S. Department of Energy. Its long accelerator building is one of the most distinctive science landmarks on the Peninsula.

This is an active research facility, so casual wandering is not the point. Visit only through public events or arranged tours, when the scale and purpose of the site can be properly explained.
