title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Menlo Park's sights cluster around two themes: Silicon Valley tech history and the quiet cultural enclave of the Allied Arts Guild and downtown. Sand Hill Road and the Meta campus represent the current commercial era; SRI International, the Google Garage, and the Mental Research Institute are for those interested in how the area's distinctive character was built. El Palo Alto, the ancient redwood on the Palo Alto border, is the most unexpected sight of all.
