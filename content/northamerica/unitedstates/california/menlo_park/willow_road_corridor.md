---

Willow Road and the surrounding streets east of US-101 form the core of what might be called the technology workers' Menlo Park — a zone of corporate campuses, shuttle bus stops, and lunch restaurants oriented entirely toward the tech workforce. The Meta campus at 1 Hacker Way is the anchor, but numerous smaller companies occupy the surrounding office parks.

Cycling or driving this area gives a direct sense of how Silicon Valley looks when you remove the mythology: flat, suburban, car-dependent, and physically indistinguishable from any other American business district. The contrast with the quiet tree-lined residential streets of the western part of Menlo Park is stark. The pedestrian and cycling paths along the bay shoreline, accessible from the eastern edge of this corridor, offer an escape into the marshy bay landscape.
