---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Castro Street has a decent cluster of bars and cafes that do brisk business on weekday evenings when the tech crowd knocks off. It's not a nightlife destination in the San Francisco sense, but there are enough good options that you won't struggle to find somewhere for a drink. The coffee culture is strong, as you would expect from Silicon Valley — multiple specialty roasters have outposts here.
