The Mountain View Center for the Performing Arts is the city's main indoor venue for theatre, dance, and music, with a 600-seat main stage and a smaller studio theatre. It anchors the southern end of Castro Street, and is home to the TheatreWorks Silicon Valley company — one of the Bay Area's most consistently good regional theatre groups, with a particular reputation for world premieres of new American musicals.

The building itself, designed by Robert A. M. Stern and opened in 1991, has an unusual presence for a suburban performing arts centre: a grand colonnade facing the street and an interior that feels genuinely theatrical even before any performance begins. If you're spending an evening in Mountain View, check the programme — the quality of what comes through here is well above what you might expect.

The box office is on Castro Street, and parking is available in the adjacent city garage. The Mountain View Farmers Market runs in the plaza outside on Sundays.
