Shoreline Park occupies a long strip of reclaimed baylands at the northern edge of Mountain View, with views across San Francisco Bay toward the East Bay hills. The park has twelve kilometres of paved and unpaved trails, a golf course, and Shoreline Lake — a 50-acre freshwater lake surrounded by wildlife habitat where windsurfing, kiteboarding, kayaking, and paddleboarding are all available for rent.

The lake is calm enough for beginners but windy enough to make it genuinely interesting for experienced windsurfers. The Shoreline Sailing School offers lessons from spring through autumn. Bird watching is also good along the bay-facing edges of the park, particularly for shorebirds and waterfowl during migration season. The surrounding wetlands restoration project has brought back species that had disappeared from the area for decades.

Rengstorff House sits at the south end of the park — it's one of the oldest surviving houses in San Mateo County and worth a short detour. The park is free to enter; equipment rental and sailing lessons have their own fees. Parking lots off Shoreline Boulevard.
