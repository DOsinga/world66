Mountain View is a mid-sized Silicon Valley city directly south of San Francisco, home to Google's main campus (Googleplex) and a long history of technology companies including NASA Ames Research Center at Moffett Field. Despite its corporate fame, the centre is unexpectedly human-scale: [Castro Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/mountainvieuw/castro_street) downtown is a lively strip of cafes, restaurants (heavy on Asian options), and bookshops.

The [Computer History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/mountainvieuw/computer_history_museum) near Shoreline Amphitheatre is one of the best technology museums in the world, with exhibits tracing computing from mechanical tabulation machines through to modern supercomputers. The nearby [Shoreline Amphitheatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/mountainvieuw/shoreline_amphitheatre) hosts major outdoor concerts in summer, and the adjacent [Shoreline Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/mountainvieuw/shoreline_park) on the San Francisco Bay has cycling trails, a lake, and kayaking. The bay salt marshes support significant birdlife.

Mountain View blurs into Palo Alto to the south and Sunnyvale to the east, so a visit typically covers several of these neighbouring cities. The CalTrain station provides direct trains to San Francisco (about 45 minutes).
