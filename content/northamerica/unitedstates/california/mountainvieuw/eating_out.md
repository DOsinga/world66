---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Mountain View's dining scene is driven by the tech workforce, which means it's cosmopolitan, competitive, and generally good. Castro Street has the highest density — Vietnamese, Indian, and Spanish restaurants occupy most of the blocks between the Caltrain station and the performing arts centre. The lunch crowds from nearby campuses keep standards up.

Chez TJ on Villa Street is the city's most serious restaurant, a French-influenced tasting-menu room in a Victorian cottage that has been running since 1982. At the casual end, the Vietnamese and Indian options on Castro offer some of the best value in Silicon Valley.
