---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The downtown core around Castro Street is walkable. Getting to Shoreline Park and the Computer History Museum requires either a car, a bike, or a bus (VTA route 40 runs along Shoreline). Cycling is practical — the city is flat and the bay trail is excellent.

The VTA light rail connects Mountain View to Sunnyvale and San Jose, but service is infrequent. For most visitors the combination of walking downtown and renting a bike for the Shoreline area works well.
