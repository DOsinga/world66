---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Mountain View is well-served by Caltrain, with a station on Castro Street in the downtown core. Trains run frequently to San Francisco (about 45 minutes to downtown) and south to San Jose and beyond. The station is a short walk from most of the city's restaurants and the performing arts centre.

By car, Mountain View sits along Highway 101, about 40 kilometres south of San Francisco and 20 kilometres north of San Jose. Traffic on 101 is heavy during commute hours. Norman Y. Mineta San Jose International Airport is the closest major airport, about 20 kilometres away; San Francisco International (SFO) is also usable, roughly 35 kilometres to the north.
