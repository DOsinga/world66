The Shoreline Amphitheatre is one of the best outdoor concert venues in the San Francisco Bay Area. With a capacity of around 22,000 — a covered pavilion section up front and a sloping lawn behind — it hosts major touring acts from May through October. The setting, at the edge of Shoreline Park with views toward the bay, makes even mediocre shows pleasant.

Getting in and out can be difficult on busy nights. The venue is not well-served by public transit for late-night shows, and traffic on Highway 101 backs up badly after large concerts. Plan accordingly.
