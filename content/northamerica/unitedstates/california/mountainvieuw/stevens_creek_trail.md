Stevens Creek Trail is one of Mountain View's best low-key pleasures: a paved route that lets you move through parks, creekside greenery, and the northern edge of Silicon Valley without dealing with traffic every minute. It works for walkers, runners, and cyclists.

The trail is especially useful if you want to connect everyday local life with the Bay-side landscape. It is not a wilderness route, but the contrast between tech campuses, open space, and shoreline air is exactly what makes it feel local.
