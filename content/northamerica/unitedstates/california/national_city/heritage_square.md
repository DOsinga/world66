Heritage Square preserves Brick Row, a terrace of ten attached red-brick houses designed by R.C. Ball and built in 1887 for senior Santa Fe Railroad executives. The terrace was modelled directly on the row houses of Philadelphia — Frank Kimball wanted the railroad brass to feel at home, having pulled most of them west from the east coast.

The houses are the only surviving row of their type in California and are unusual in San Diego County's largely detached-villa townscape. Several have been restored as small museums and event spaces; others are private. The square also takes in St Matthew's Episcopal Church, the Granger Music Hall (relocated here in 1983), and a small garden — a tight, walkable concentration of the Kimball-era city.
