Newhall is a community in the Santa Clarita Valley, about 35 miles north of downtown [Los Angeles](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/losangeles) via Interstate 5. It is the oldest settlement in the Santa Clarita area and has a historic Main Street that retains some of its Old West character — fitting, since this area was a major filming location for early Western movies. The [Walk of Western Stars](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/newhall/walk_of_western_stars), embedded in the sidewalks along Main Street, honours actors and filmmakers who shaped the genre.

The [William S. Hart Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/newhall/william_s_hart_museum), set in the former ranch estate of the silent-film cowboy star, sits on a hilltop above town and is free to visit. The grounds include a small herd of bison and offer views across the valley. Nearby, the [Melody Ranch Motion Picture Studio](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/newhall/melody_ranch_motion_picture_studio) is where countless Westerns were shot, from Gene Autry films to Deadwood and Django Unchained.

Newhall is not a destination in its own right, but if you are passing through the Santa Clarita Valley — perhaps on the way to Six Flags Magic Mountain or heading north toward the Central Valley — the historic downtown makes a pleasant stop. There are a few good Mexican restaurants and a growing craft brewery scene along Main Street.
