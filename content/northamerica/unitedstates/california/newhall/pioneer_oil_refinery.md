The Pioneer Oil Refinery stands on the southern edge of Newhall as a California Historical Landmark (No. 172), marking the site of the first commercial oil well in California and one of the earliest in the American West. The original refinery equipment — stills, tanks, and piping — has been partially preserved and is visible from the road, with interpretive signage explaining how the operation worked.

The site is not a full-scale museum but it is genuinely historic. The discovery of oil here in 1876 triggered California's first petroleum rush and helped establish the state's eventual dominance in American oil production. For anyone interested in industrial history or the early development of California, it is worth the short detour.
