---
title: "Things to Do in Newhall"
type: section
---

Newhall punches above its weight for a small community. The big draw is the William S. Hart Museum complex, which sits on a hilltop and offers an unexpectedly moving glimpse into the silent-film era. The historic Main Street corridor is genuine — not a theme-park reconstruction — and walking it is worth an hour of your time. The Melody Ranch studio is a working film set and tours are available when productions aren't in residence.
