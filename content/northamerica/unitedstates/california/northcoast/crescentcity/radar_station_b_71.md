Radar Station B-71 is a rare surviving Second World War early-warning radar station in Redwood National Park. Its buildings were disguised as ordinary farm structures, a simple camouflage trick meant to hide the station's military purpose from the air.

The site is a worthwhile stop if you are already exploring the coastal section of Redwood National and State Parks. It adds a small but memorable wartime layer to a landscape better known for redwoods, beaches, and river valleys.
