Tolowa Dunes State Park protects a long, windswept landscape of dunes, wetlands, beach, and coastal habitat north of Crescent City. It is quieter than the redwood parks nearby, but that openness is exactly its appeal.

The park is best for walkers, birders, and travelers interested in the meeting of natural and Indigenous cultural landscapes along the far northern California coast. Conditions can be exposed, so bring layers and treat it as a wild coastal outing.
