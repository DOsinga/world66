---
title: "Bars and Cafes in Eureka"
type: section
---

Eureka's bar scene is modest but genuine. The best options cluster in Old Town and include a craft brewery that has been producing reliably since the early 1990s. For livelier nightlife, [Arcata](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/arcata) — 8 miles north on the 101 — has more options and the energy of a college town.
