Fort Humboldt State Historic Site stands on a bluff above Eureka, where a 19th-century U.S. Army post once watched over Humboldt Bay and the surrounding settlements. The fort history is tied to the region's difficult colonial and Native history, including the violence that accompanied American expansion on the North Coast.

The park also interprets logging, with outdoor machinery and exhibits that connect Eureka to the redwood industry. It is not a large site, but the mix of bay views, frontier history, and industrial relics makes it one of the more rounded stops in town.
