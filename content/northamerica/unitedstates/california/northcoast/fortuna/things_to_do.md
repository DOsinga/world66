---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Fortuna is a small town, so the attractions are modest but specific. The **Fortuna Depot Museum**, set in the 1893 train depot in Rohner Park, holds an idiosyncratic local-history collection from the days when the Northwestern Pacific Railroad still ran. The **Fortuna Rodeo** is the main annual event. For the outdoors, the broad **Eel River** and the redwoods just south at Humboldt Redwoods State Park are within easy reach.
