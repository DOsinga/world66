---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

Garberville sits on US-101, 220 miles north of San Francisco and about 65 miles south of [Eureka](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/eureka). From San Francisco, allow around four hours with no stops. From Eureka, the drive is just over an hour south.

There is no rail service. Redwood Transit System runs a limited regional bus route along the 101 corridor connecting Garberville to Eureka, but schedules are sparse — this stretch of California is designed for cars. If you are driving, US-101 takes you directly into the center of town and is the only practical route from either direction.
