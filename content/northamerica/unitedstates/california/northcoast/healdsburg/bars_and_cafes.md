---
title: "Bars and Cafes in Healdsburg"
type: section
---

Healdsburg is not a late-night town — the clientele has typically driven here from San Francisco for a weekend of wine, and the rhythm of the place follows suit. Tasting rooms around the plaza double as afternoon drinking spots; the evening bar scene is modest.

**Portalupi Wine** on North Street has a welcoming tasting room open late into the afternoon. The **Healdsburg Bar & Grill** on the plaza itself serves local wines by the glass in an unpretentious setting and stays open for drinks after dinner. **Spoonbar** inside the h2hotel attracts a slightly more design-conscious crowd with good cocktails and a comfortable terrace.

For coffee, **Flying Goat Coffee** on Center Street is the local favourite — serious coffee, good pastries, and tables facing the street for watching the plaza come alive on weekend mornings.
