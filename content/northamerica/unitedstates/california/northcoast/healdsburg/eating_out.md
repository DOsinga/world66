---
title: "Eating Out in Healdsburg"
type: section
---

Healdsburg punches well above its size for food. The combination of Sonoma County's agricultural wealth — excellent produce, local lamb, Dungeness crab, farm eggs — and a clientele that arrives having already spent money on wine keeps standards high. The restaurants around the central plaza are genuinely good, and a few are exceptional.

[Barndiva](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/barndiva) is the most talked-about restaurant in town, with a farm-to-table menu that takes seasonality seriously and a garden setting unlike anything else in Sonoma wine country. [Scopa](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/scopa) is the go-to for Italian — small, informal, and consistently excellent. For a simpler lunch, the Healdsburg Bar & Grill on the plaza does solid sandwiches, burgers, and local wines by the glass.

Groceries and picnic supplies: the Healdsburg Farmers Market runs year-round on Saturdays on the plaza from May through November, and year-round inside in winter. The **Shed** food hall near the plaza sells local preserves, provisions, and good coffee.
