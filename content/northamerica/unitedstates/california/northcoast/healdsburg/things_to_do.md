---
title: "Things to Do in Healdsburg"
type: section
---

Healdsburg is built around its central plaza — a shaded square of mature trees with benches, a bandstand, and low-key foot traffic from wine-country visitors. Most tasting rooms are within a few blocks of this square, which is genuinely one of the most pleasant downtown areas in wine country. A brief stroll in any direction gets you into residential streets, vineyards, or along the Russian River.

Beyond the plaza, the best things to do involve getting in a car and driving the back roads. Dry Creek Valley Road winds through rolling Zinfandel country north of town; Westside Road follows the Russian River through stands of redwood. Cycling is a real option — the terrain in parts of Dry Creek Valley is gentle enough for casual riders.

The [Hand Fan Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/hand_fan_museum) and [Healdsburg Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/healdsburg_museum) give the town cultural depth beyond wine. The [Healdsburg Memorial Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/healdsburg_memorial_bridge) over the Russian River is a handsome piece of Depression-era infrastructure. The river itself has a popular swimming hole below the bridge in summer.
