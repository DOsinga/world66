---
title: "Things to Do in Healdsburg"
type: section
---

Healdsburg is built around its central plaza — a shaded square of mature trees with benches, a bandstand, and low-key foot traffic from wine-country visitors. Most tasting rooms are within a few blocks of this square, which is genuinely one of the most pleasant downtown areas in wine country. A brief stroll in any direction gets you into residential streets, vineyards, or along the Russian River.

Beyond the plaza, the best things to do involve getting in a car and driving the back roads. [Dry Creek Valley](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/dry_creek_valley) winds through rolling Zinfandel country north of town; [Russian River Valley](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/russian_river_valley) follows the river through stands of redwood toward the coast. Cycling is a real option — the terrain in parts of Dry Creek Valley is gentle enough for casual riders.

The [Hand Fan Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/hand_fan_museum) and [Healdsburg Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/healdsburg_museum) give the town cultural depth beyond wine. The [Healdsburg Memorial Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/healdsburg_memorial_bridge) over the Russian River is a handsome piece of Depression-era infrastructure, with a popular swimming hole below in summer. [Simi Winery](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/simi_winery), founded in 1876, is among the most atmospheric historic wineries within walking distance of the plaza.

The [Healdsburg Plaza](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/healdsburg_plaza) itself is worth time — shaded, well-maintained, and ringed with genuinely good shops and tasting rooms. The [Jimtown Store](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/healdsburg/things_to_do/jimtown_store) in Alexander Valley is a classic country general store that rewards a short drive east.
