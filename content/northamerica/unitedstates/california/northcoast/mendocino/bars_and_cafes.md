---
title: "Bars and Cafes in Mendocino"
type: section
---

Mendocino is a small town and the after-dark scene reflects that — a handful of good bars, a couple of wine tasting rooms, and cafes that close early. What's here tends to be warm and unpretentious: locals and visitors mix easily, and an evening that starts at the bar often ends with a conversation about hiking trails or where to find Dungeness crab.
