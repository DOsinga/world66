---
title: "Eating Out in Mendocino"
type: section
---

Mendocino punches well above its size for food. The restaurants here take local sourcing seriously — the Pacific fisheries, the farms of Anderson Valley, and the coastal foragers all feed directly into the menus. Most places are small, reservations are strongly advised on weekends and in summer, and the dining room windows often frame sea fog rolling in off the headlands.
