Twist is the best place in the village to work through the Anderson Valley wine list without committing to a full restaurant dinner. The selection is tightly curated around local and Northern California producers, with particular depth in the cool-climate Pinot Noirs and Alsatian-style whites that the Anderson Valley does better than almost anywhere in the country. The room is small and the atmosphere is relaxed; plates of local cheese and charcuterie pair well with the pours. On busy summer evenings it fills quickly — arriving early or making a reservation is wise.
