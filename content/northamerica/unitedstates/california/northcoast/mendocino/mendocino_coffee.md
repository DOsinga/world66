Mendocino Coffee Company is the practical first stop of the morning: good espresso, pastries, and a handful of indoor seats with views over the village rooftops. It opens earlier than most things in town and fills with a mix of local trades people and visitors planning the day. The coffee quality is well above what the tourist surroundings might suggest, and the staff are brisk and efficient — it is not a place to linger for hours, but it is exactly what you need before a headlands walk. The outdoor seating in good weather overlooks the Victorian streetscape.
