Patterson's is the closest thing Mendocino has to a true local pub: dark wood, a long bar, and a menu of reliable pub food — burgers, fish and chips, chowder — at prices that are kind compared to the fancier restaurants nearby. It opens earlier than most Mendocino establishments and stays open later, making it the default option when other kitchens are closed. The beer selection leans toward Northern California craft breweries, including North Coast Brewing from Fort Bragg. On weekend evenings it fills with a mix of locals and visitors and gets genuinely lively for a town this small.
