Helen Putnam Regional Park is a quiet 216-acre park southwest of downtown Petaluma. Its looped trail system lets you put together a short walk or a longer hike, with open hillsides, oak woodland, and good views across the Petaluma area.

It is a simple outdoors stop rather than a major destination, but it is one of the better places near town for an easy hike without driving deep into the North Bay countryside.
