Olompali State Historic Park preserves a long-used Coast Miwok site north of San Rafael, along with later ranch history. Reconstructed bark and tule reed houses help explain Indigenous life here, and the grinding rock behind the visitor centre is still visible.

The park also has easy walking trails and picnic space, so it is more than a roadside marker. It makes a worthwhile stop between Marin and Sonoma County.
