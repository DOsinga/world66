The Petaluma Wildlife & Natural History Museum is a small student-run museum focused on wildlife, conservation, and environmental education. It is not a polished big-city natural history museum, but the hands-on local energy is the point.

Families and curious kids will get the most from it, especially on a Petaluma visit that also includes the riverfront or Shollenberger Park.
