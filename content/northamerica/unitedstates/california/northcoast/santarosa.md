Santa Rosa is the biggest city in Sonoma County and the de facto capital of California's North Coast wine country. It's not a destination most tourists plan around — they come for the vineyards — but the city itself has more going on than people expect. A revitalized downtown with good restaurants, the legacy of Charles M. Schulz (who lived and worked here for decades), and easy access to the Russian River Valley make it worth more than a drive-through.

The heart of town is [Railroad Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/santarosa/railroad_square_historic_district), a cluster of restored stone buildings from the early 1900s with shops and restaurants. Fourth Street runs through the main downtown strip. Santa Rosa was rebuilt after major earthquake and fire damage, so the architecture is a mix of old survivors and modern fill — but the survivors include the [Church of One Tree](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/santarosa/church_built_from_one_tree) and the [Museum of Sonoma County](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/santarosa/museum_of_sonoma_county) in its 1909 federal post office. The [Charles M. Schulz Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/santarosa/charles_m_schulz_museum) in the north of the city is the main tourist draw, and [Snoopy's Home Ice](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/santarosa/snoopys_home_ice) next door is the arena Schulz himself built and skated at until the end of his life.

North of the city, the [Russian River Valley](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/santarosa/russian_river_valley) opens up with more than fifty wineries, hiking in Armstrong Redwoods State Reserve, and the small resort town of Guerneville. You can canoe past vineyards on the river itself. To the east, the Sonoma Valley wineries around Kenwood and Glen Ellen are a short drive away. [Luther Burbank's home and gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/northcoast/santarosa/luther_burbank_home_and_gardens) near downtown give a different view of the county's agricultural heritage.
