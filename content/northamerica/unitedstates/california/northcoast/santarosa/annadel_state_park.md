Annadel State Park is one of Santa Rosa’s best outdoor assets, a broad landscape of oak woodland, grassland, chaparral, and seasonal wildflowers on the city’s eastern edge. It feels close to town but quickly opens into proper Sonoma County hill country.

Hikers and mountain bikers come for the trail network, while spring visitors get some of the best colour. The park is also a useful counterbalance to winery-focused itineraries: bring water, start early in hot weather, and give yourself enough time to get beyond the first trail junctions.
