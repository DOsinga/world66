---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Santa Rosa's restaurant scene is better than you'd expect for a city that most wine tourists pass through without stopping. The downtown Fourth Street corridor and the Railroad Square area have the best concentration. Sonoma County produce — particularly in summer and autumn — is exceptional, and the restaurants that source locally show it. The wine is everywhere and the glass pours are well-chosen. A few spots near the Schulz Museum and the ice arena cater to families; the more interesting cooking is downtown.
