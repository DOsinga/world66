Spring Lake Regional Park is one of Santa Rosa's best outdoor spaces, with lakefront paths, oak woodland, picnic areas, seasonal swimming, boating, and camping. It links with nearby Howarth Park and Annadel State Park, creating a larger green corridor on the city's eastern side.

The park is useful because it works for many kinds of traveller: families, walkers, cyclists, and anyone wanting a break from wineries and downtown streets. Go early on warm weekends, when locals fill the lake area.
