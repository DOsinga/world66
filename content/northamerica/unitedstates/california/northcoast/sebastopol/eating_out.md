---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Sebastopol eats better than its size would suggest. The town's location in apple and wine country, combined with its countercultural streak, produces a food scene that values local sourcing and avoids the corporate sameness of larger Sonoma County towns. Downtown Sebastopol's Main Street has several solid options; the farmers market at Ragle Ranch on Sunday mornings is the best place to graze on local produce.
