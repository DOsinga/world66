Hallberg Butterfly Gardens is a small butterfly-focused garden in Graton, just outside Sebastopol. The plantings are managed for butterfly habitat, so the appeal is slower and more seasonal than a formal botanical garden.

Visits are usually by guided tour and appointment during the warmer months. It is worth considering for naturalists, families, or garden-minded travellers already spending time around Sebastopol and western Sonoma County.
