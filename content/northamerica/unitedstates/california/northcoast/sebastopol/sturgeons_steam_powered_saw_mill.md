Sturgeon's Steam Powered Saw Mill is a preserved working mill west of Sebastopol, where belts, boilers, and saws show how redwood-country lumber work looked before everything became diesel and electric. It is a hands-on piece of Sonoma County industrial history rather than a polished museum.

Visit only when demonstration days are running; that is when the machinery, volunteers, and smell of sawdust make the detour worthwhile. It pairs well with Sebastopol's farms and backroad wineries if you want a rural day with something more mechanical than tasting rooms.
