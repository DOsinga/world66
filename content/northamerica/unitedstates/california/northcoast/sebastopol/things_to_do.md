---

Sebastopol is a small Sonoma County town with a distinct character — part agricultural (the Gravenstein apple country), part countercultural, part wine-country adjacent. The surrounding apple orchards and Goldridge sandy soils produce some of Sonoma's best Pinot Noir alongside the apples that made this area famous. In town, a handful of genuinely unusual attractions — an experiment farm, carnivorous plants, monumental junk sculpture — reward unhurried exploration.
