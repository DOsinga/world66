Quarryhill Botanical Garden is a 25-acre garden in Sonoma Valley known for its large collection of documented Asian plants. It is a good change of pace from winery stops, with wooded paths, rare trees and shrubs, and strong birding.

The garden is near Glen Ellen, north of Sonoma. Check current opening arrangements before going, as special events and seasonal access can affect visits.
