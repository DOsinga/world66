Quarryhill Botanical Garden is a 25-acre hillside garden devoted to Asian plants, with winding paths, quiet benches, and a collection that feels far removed from Sonoma's tasting rooms. It is one of the better non-wine stops in the valley.

The garden is at its best when you slow down. Look for magnolias, maples, dogwoods, and other plants gathered from across East Asia, then leave time for the views over the surrounding hills.
