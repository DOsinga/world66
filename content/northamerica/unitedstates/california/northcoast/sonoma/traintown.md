TrainTown is a lighter, activity-led stop for travellers who want a break from conventional sightseeing. It is most useful for families, groups, or anyone building a day around entertainment rather than monuments. Its location makes it easy to combine with a walk through the surrounding part of Sonoma.

Check prices, opening season, and booking requirements before you commit. These places can be excellent on the right day and skippable on the wrong one, so treat it as an option to fit your mood, weather, and available transport.
