Little River State Beach is a quiet North Coast beach near Trinidad, with broad sand, surf, driftwood, and the river mouth shaping the landscape. It offers the raw Humboldt coast experience without much built-up infrastructure.

It is best for walking, beachcombing, photography, and watching conditions rather than swimming. Fog, wind, and surf are part of the setting, so bring layers and treat the ocean with respect.
