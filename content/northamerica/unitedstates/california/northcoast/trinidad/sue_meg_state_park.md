Sue-meg State Park, north of Trinidad, brings together redwood-country forest and a rugged Pacific shoreline. Trails pass through spruce, hemlock, fir, alder, and coastal redwoods, then open onto beaches, headlands, and viewpoints over the ocean.

The park is also home to a recreated Yurok village and a native plant garden, which add cultural context to the coastal scenery. It is one of the better half-day stops on this stretch of the North Coast, especially if you want both forest and beach in one place.
