The Yurok watched the ceremony of claiming the land for Spain from the shore, almost certainly without understanding its implications. Tsurai (also spelled Tsurau or Tschura) was a Yurok village on the southern shore of Trinidad Bay, occupied continuously for at least 600 years before European contact. The village site is now a California State Historical Landmark (No. 838) and is preserved as an open green area at the edge of the bay, visible from the harbour.

The Yurok were the most populous Native people in California at the time of European contact, living along the lower Klamath River and the coast. Tsurai was one of their most important coastal villages, known for its access to the abundant marine resources of Trinidad Bay. Interpretive panels at the site describe the village's history and the Yurok culture. The Yurok Tribe continues to have a presence in the Trinidad area and considers the site significant.
