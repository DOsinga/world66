---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Oakdale's appeal is its cowboy heritage and its setting on the edge of the Sierra foothills. Downtown is small enough to walk in twenty minutes — saddle shops, a cowboy museum, and the historic Oak Valley Community Bank on Yosemite Avenue. Beyond town the Stanislaus River carves a green corridor through the dry hills, with the historic settlement of Knights Ferry and its covered bridge a short drive upstream and Woodward Reservoir spreading out to the north.
