Oakland gets overshadowed by San Francisco across the bay, but that is part of its appeal. It is grittier, more affordable, and more diverse, with a cultural identity all its own. This is the city that gave the world the Black Panthers, the Hells Angels, and a fierce local pride that has only grown as the tech boom pushed artists, chefs, and musicians eastward across the bridge.

The heart of town is [Lake Merritt](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland/lakemerrittandgardens), a tidal lagoon ringed by parks and joggers just blocks from the downtown core. South of the lake, the [Oakland Museum of California](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland/oaklandmuseumofcalifornia) is one of the best regional museums in the country. Down at the waterfront, [Jack London Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland/jacklondonsquare) offers restaurants, a farmers' market, and ferries to San Francisco — plus Heinold's First and Last Chance Saloon, where a young Jack London reportedly came to read and drink. Head up into the hills and you will find redwood-lined trails, the [Chabot Space & Science Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland/chabotspaceandsciencecenter), and sweeping views back across the bay.

Oakland's food scene punches well above its weight. [Temescal](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland/temescal) along Telegraph Avenue is the epicenter, with Ethiopian, Korean, and Vietnamese restaurants packed into a walkable strip. The [Uptown](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland/uptown) district around the [Paramount Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland/paramounttheatre) is where the bar and live music scene concentrates.

The [African American Museum & Library](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland/africanamericanmuseumlibrary) documents the city's Black history from the Gold Rush through the Panther movement. The [16th Street Station](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland/sixteenth_street_station) is an abandoned Beaux Arts train terminal, half-ruin, and one of the most photographed buildings in the Bay Area. You can get here by BART in minutes from San Francisco — most visitors treat Oakland as an add-on, but it rewards a longer stay.
