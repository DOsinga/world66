The Cathedral of Christ the Light is one of Oakland's most distinctive contemporary buildings, using wood, glass, light, and curved forms to create a modern sacred space. Its location near Lake Merritt makes it easy to include in a central Oakland walk.

The architecture is the main reason to visit, even for non-religious travellers. Step inside quietly if open, since the changing light and interior volume are more impressive than the exterior alone.
