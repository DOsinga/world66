The Dunsmuir-Hellman Historic Estate is one of Oakland's standout historic-house sites, centered on a large late-19th-century mansion and landscaped grounds. Its scale gives a glimpse of Bay Area wealth and suburban estate culture before Oakland's modern urban expansion.

Tours and access can vary with events, so it is worth checking ahead. Even when approached mainly for the grounds, the estate adds architectural variety to an Oakland itinerary dominated by lakefront, museums, and neighborhoods.
