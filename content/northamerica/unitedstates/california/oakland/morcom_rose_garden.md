Morcom Rose Garden is a terraced public garden tucked into Oakland's Grand Lake area, with formal beds, steps, and seasonal blooms in a quiet residential bowl. It is one of the city's gentler short stops.

The garden is best during rose season and works well before or after time around Lake Merritt, Piedmont Avenue, or Grand Avenue. It is compact, so visit for atmosphere rather than a long botanical outing.
