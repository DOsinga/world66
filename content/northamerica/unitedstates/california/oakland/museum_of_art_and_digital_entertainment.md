The Museum of Art and Digital Entertainment treats video games as cultural artifacts, with playable systems, exhibitions, and preservation work. It is a niche museum, but a meaningful one in a region deeply tied to digital culture.

The appeal is strongest for game-history fans, families with older children, and anyone interested in interactive media. Because the experience can change with exhibits and events, check current opening details before going.
