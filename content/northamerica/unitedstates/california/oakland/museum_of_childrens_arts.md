The Museum of Children's Arts is a hands-on art space for kids, with workshops that move from drawing and illustration to sculpture, architecture, and wearable projects. It is useful if you are travelling with children and want something more active than a gallery visit.

Check the current workshop schedule before going, as the best reason to visit is joining a session rather than just dropping in.
