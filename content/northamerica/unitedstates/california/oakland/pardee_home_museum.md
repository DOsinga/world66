Pardee Home Museum preserves the home of one of Oaklands prominent nineteenth-century families, with rooms that feel dense, personal, and very Victorian. The house contains original furnishings, travel souvenirs, decorative objects, and stories tied to local politics and California history.

It is a good counterweight to downtown Oaklands larger civic buildings. Tours are the way to see it, so check hours before going rather than assuming drop-in access.
