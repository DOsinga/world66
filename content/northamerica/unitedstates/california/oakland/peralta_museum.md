The Peralta Museum of History and Culture is part of the Peralta Hacienda Historical Park, once connected to the Rancho San Antonio land grant. The site includes the 1870 Antonio Peralta House, interpretive exhibits, and a native plant garden, giving visitors a grounded look at Oakland's early layers of history.
