Boccali's is a long-running Ojai landmark for casual Italian food, with a big outdoor patio and an easy family feel. It is a little east of downtown, so it works well if you have a car and want a relaxed meal rather than a polished one.

The appeal is the setting as much as the food: tables outside, valley air, and a place that feels rooted in local routine.
