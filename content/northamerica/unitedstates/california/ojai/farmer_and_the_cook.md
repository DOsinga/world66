Farmer and the Cook is part vegetarian restaurant, part produce shop, and very Ojai in spirit. The menu leans organic and farm-driven, with salads, Mexican-influenced plates, and the sort of casual freshness that suits the valley.

It is a good lunch stop if you want something lighter than standard road-trip food.
