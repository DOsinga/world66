Ojai Valley Museum is the small local museum in the former St. Thomas Aquinas Chapel. It covers the valley's history, art, and landscape, and it is the best first stop if you want a little context before wandering downtown.

The building itself is part of the appeal: a modest, handsome chapel on Ojai Avenue rather than a generic museum box.
