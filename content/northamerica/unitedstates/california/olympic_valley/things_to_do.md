title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Olympic Valley's identity is built around the mountain. In winter that means skiing and snowboarding at Palisades Tahoe, which covers two linked peaks with terrain for every level. In summer the same mountain becomes a trail network, and the aerial tram remains the fastest way up. The village is compact and walkable, with the Olympic Museum tucked in as a reminder of the valley's 1960 moment in history.
