Honda Center is a 19,000-seat arena adjacent to Angel Stadium, home to the Anaheim Ducks NHL hockey team and a major concert venue. The Ducks, named after the Disney film when the franchise was founded in 1993, won the Stanley Cup in 2007 and remain competitive in the Pacific Division.

Attending a Ducks game in winter is a straightforward evening out: good transit connections from the resort area, a lively crowd, and tickets at a range of price points. The arena also hosts major touring acts throughout the year. Check the schedule if you are in Anaheim for several days — there is usually something on.
