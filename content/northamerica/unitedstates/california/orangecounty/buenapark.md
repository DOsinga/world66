Buena Park is a city in northwestern Orange County, best known as the home of [Knott's Berry Farm](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/buenapark/knotts_berry_farm) — the nation's oldest themed amusement park. What began in the 1920s as a roadside berry stand and ghost town attraction has grown into a full resort destination, drawing visitors from across Southern California and beyond.

The city sits about 25 miles southeast of downtown Los Angeles, making it an easy day trip or a convenient base for exploring both LA and Orange County. Beach Boulevard has evolved into a strip of family entertainment venues: [Medieval Times](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/buenapark/medieval_times) stages jousting tournaments over a four-course dinner, and [Pirates Dinner Adventure](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/buenapark/pirates_dinner_adventure) presents acrobatic swashbuckling aboard an indoor ship. In summer, [Knott's Soak City](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/buenapark/knotts_soak_city) waterpark opens next to the main theme park.

Beyond the entertainment cluster, Buena Park is a largely residential community with straightforward freeway access. The curious [Dreger Clock](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/buenapark/dreger_clock) near the civic centre — a 1953 tower tracking time in a dozen cities across the world — is a small reminder that the city had civic ambitions beyond hosting roller coasters.
