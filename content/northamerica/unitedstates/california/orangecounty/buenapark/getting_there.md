---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Buena Park sits at the junction of the 5 and 91 freeways in northern Orange County, about 25 miles southeast of downtown Los Angeles. By car it is a straightforward drive from anywhere in the LA basin, though the 5 freeway can be heavily congested during peak hours.

Metrolink's Orange County Line stops at Buena Park station on Raymond Avenue, with connections to Los Angeles Union Station and Oceanside. From the station, most of the Beach Boulevard attractions are about two miles west — too far to walk comfortably, but a rideshare takes five minutes. From Los Angeles International Airport, the drive takes 35 to 50 minutes depending on traffic; John Wayne Airport in nearby Santa Ana is closer at about 20 minutes.
