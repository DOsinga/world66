Knott's Soak City sits next to Knott's Berry Farm and shares its address, opening for the summer season typically from late May through early September. The park offers around 20 water slides ranging from mild to intense, a wave pool, a lazy river, and a large children's area. It is considerably less crowded than comparable waterparks in the region and well-priced for a full day.

Admission includes access to all rides and attractions. Combo tickets with Knott's Berry Farm are available and represent good value if you plan to visit both parks during your stay.
