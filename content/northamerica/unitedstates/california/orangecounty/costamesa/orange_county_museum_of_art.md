The Orange County Museum of Art brings contemporary art into Costa Mesa's Segerstrom
cultural district. Its modern building and rotating exhibitions make it a useful
counterpoint to the area's performing-arts venues and shopping-heavy reputation.

Expect a compact but serious museum rather than an encyclopedic collection. It is
easiest to fold into a half-day around the Segerstrom Center, South Coast Plaza, or
other Costa Mesa stops, especially if a current exhibition matches your interests.
