type: section
---

[South Coast Plaza](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/costamesa/south_coast_plaza) is the main event — one of America's largest shopping centres, home to virtually every luxury and designer brand. East 17th Street offers a more local shopping scene, anchored by Mothers Market (natural groceries) and Hi-Time Wine Cellars. On weekends, the Swap Meet at the [OC Fairgrounds](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/costamesa/oc_fairgrounds) draws bargain hunters. Bristol Street's [LAB Anti-Mall](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/costamesa/the_lab) and The Camp serve a different audience entirely.
