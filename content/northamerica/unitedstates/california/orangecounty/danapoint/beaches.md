---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Dana Point has a string of distinct beaches running about five miles along the coast. From north to south: **Strand Beach** with its inclined elevator down the cliffs, **Salt Creek Beach** below the Ritz-Carlton, **Dana Strand Beach** (mostly private hotel access), **Baby Beach** in the harbour (calm, family-friendly), **Doheny State Beach** just south of the harbour, and **Capistrano Beach** ('Capo Beach') further south on the San Clemente border.

Surfing conditions vary considerably. Doheny is the historical home of Orange County longboarding, with a gentle longshore break — gentler now than it was before the harbour breakwater cut off most of the swell. Salt Creek has more reliable shoulder-high beach break that suits intermediates. The harbour breakwater killed the famous 'Killer Dana' wave when it was built in 1966; surfers still mourn it.
