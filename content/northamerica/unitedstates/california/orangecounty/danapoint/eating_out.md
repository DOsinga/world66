---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The eating scene is split between harbour restaurants — almost all with patios and water views, varying widely in quality — and a small but improving Pacific Coast Highway strip in the Lantern District uptown.

For seafood, **Jon's Fish Market** at the harbour is a long-running counter-service spot for fish tacos, ceviche, and grilled-fish plates eaten outdoors on plastic chairs; it is the best honest-value meal in town. **Wind & Sea Restaurant** and **Proud Mary's** at the harbour cover the sit-down-by-the-water bracket. Up the hill in the Lantern District, **Brio Tuscany Grille** is a competent Italian and **Bear Coast Coffee** is the morning gathering spot. South Coast pizza shop **Pizza Lounge** has a small Capistrano Beach location worth knowing about.
