The most consistently good beach in Dana Point: a long crescent of sand backed by sandstone cliffs, with a reliable beach break for surfers, a good summer swimming area, and a grassy bluff-top park for picnics and watching the sunset.

The break here is shoulder-high to head-high on a normal swell, with several distinct peaks along the beach. Mornings are usually less crowded than afternoons. Parking on the bluff-top costs $1/hour and fills early on summer weekends. A paved path runs along the bluffs north to Strand Beach and the inclined elevator. Outdoor showers and restrooms are at the bottom of the access trail.
