---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Dana Point's southern California climate makes it pleasant nearly year-round, but seasons differ in useful ways.

**Late June to October** is peak: warm water (often 70°F / 21°C or above), reliable sunshine, and busy beaches and harbour. School-holiday weekends in July and August are crowded; midweek visits are easier. **September and early October** are arguably the best — water is still warm, the marine layer that often greys out June mornings has lifted, and crowds are thinner.

**November to April** is cool but mild (highs 60–70°F / 15–21°C). Water is too cold to swim without a wetsuit, but this is the prime **whale-watching season** — grey whales migrate close to the coast December through April, with peak sightings in February and March. Boats run year-round; in summer it is dolphin pods and blue whales further offshore. **May and June** can bring 'May grey' and 'June gloom' — overcast mornings that burn off by lunch, sometimes not at all.
