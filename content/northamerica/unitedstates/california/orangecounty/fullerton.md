Fullerton sits at the northern edge of Orange County, about 22 miles southeast of downtown Los Angeles — close enough to the sprawl but with its own distinct character. It's a college town at heart, home to California State University Fullerton, and that gives the downtown area a livelier, younger feel than most of its Orange County neighbors.

The historic downtown core along Harbor Boulevard and Commonwealth Avenue has genuine charm: old brick storefronts, a walkable strip of restaurants and bars, and a music scene that has quietly produced a disproportionate number of notable bands. The [Fullerton Transportation Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/fullerton/transportation_center) puts the rest of Southern California within reach by Amtrak and Metrolink.

The [Fullerton Arboretum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/fullerton/arboretum), tucked on the Cal State campus, is a genuine surprise — 26 acres of botanical gardens that feel nothing like suburban Orange County. The [Fullerton Museum Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/fullerton/museum_center) and the [Muckenthaler Cultural Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/fullerton/muckenthaler_cultural_center), a 1924 Italianate mansion repurposed as an arts venue, round out the cultural offerings.
