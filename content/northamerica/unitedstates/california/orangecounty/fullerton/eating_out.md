---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Fullerton's restaurant scene has improved sharply over the past decade — the streets around Harbor Boulevard and Commonwealth Avenue have a good mix of independent restaurants, brewpubs, and ethnic eateries that draw diners from across north Orange County.

The presence of California State University Fullerton, with its large student and academic population, keeps prices reasonable and the late-night options open. The city is particularly strong on Korean, Vietnamese, and Mexican food, reflecting the diverse population of the area. The **Elephant Packing House** complex on Santa Fe Avenue houses several of the better restaurants and a craft brewery, all in a converted 1920s citrus warehouse.
