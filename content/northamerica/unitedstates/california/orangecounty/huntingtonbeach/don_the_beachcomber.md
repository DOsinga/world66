Tiki bar in Sunset Beach in a long thatched-roof building on the inland side of PCH. It carries the name of Donn Beach's original 1933 Hollywood bar, which invented the genre. The current incarnation leans heavily into the dark-and-stormy era: bamboo, lanterns, flaming Scorpion bowls, a thirty-strong rum menu, and live music most nights.

The food (Polynesian-American: pupu platters, pork ribs, coconut shrimp) is incidental. Come for a Mai Tai at sundown and stay for the band.
