---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Huntington Beach is built for cars. Downtown, the pier area, and Pacific City are walkable; everything else — Bolsa Chica, Central Park, Sunset Beach — needs wheels.

**The beach bike path** runs nine miles along the back of the sand from the Santa Ana River north into Sunset Beach. It is the single most pleasant way to move along the coast — bike, skate, or e-scooter rentals are clustered along Main Street and at Pacific City. **OCTA bus 1** runs the length of PCH for those without a car. Rideshares are reliable.
