---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** The closest airport is **John Wayne / Orange County (SNA)**, 20 minutes inland — cheap shuttles and rideshares are easy. **Long Beach (LGB)** is about 25 minutes north on the 405. **LAX** is 45–90 minutes north depending on traffic.

**By car.** From LA, take the 405 south to Beach Boulevard (Highway 39) and exit south for ten minutes to the beach. From San Diego, the 5 north to the 405 north to Beach. Parking downtown is metered structures and street; long-stay use the Pacific City garage off PCH.

**By train and bus.** Amtrak's Pacific Surfliner stops at Anaheim and Santa Ana; both are 25 minutes from the beach by rideshare. OCTA bus 1 runs the length of PCH along the beach and is the most useful local route.
