A two-storey wooden building from 1903, combining a Main Street furniture store at the front with the owner's residence behind. It is the oldest surviving commercial building in Huntington Beach and the only nineteenth- or early-twentieth-century survivor on the downtown grid, listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

The store closed long ago, but the building has been restored and adapted as a coffee shop and small event space. It is a five-minute walk from the pier on 5th Street and easy to miss between the more recent buildings around it.
