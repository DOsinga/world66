Second-floor sports bar at the corner of Main and Walnut, half a block from the pier. Open balcony overlooking Main Street, dozens of screens, full bar, surprisingly competent burgers and tacos. It is loud, packed on weekends, and the closest thing downtown has to a default after-beach spot.
