---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Huntington Beach has the standard Southern California climate: dry and warm year-round, with most rain falling between December and March. Summer highs are typically 23–26°C (75–80°F), with afternoon onshore breezes; July and August are the busiest months. Ocean water peaks at about 21°C (70°F) in August and bottoms out around 14°C (57°F) in February — wetsuit weather most of the year.

**The US Open of Surfing**, held over nine days in late July, is the largest surf event in the world and turns the pier into a stadium. Streets are closed, the population doubles, and hotel rates spike — go on purpose or avoid on purpose. **Pacific Airshow** in early October brings the Blue Angels in low over the beach.

For mild crowds and good weather, **May, September, and October** are the best months. **Surf City Nights** runs every Tuesday year-round on Main Street.
