Tanaka Farms is a rare working-farm experience inside suburban Orange County. Seasonal wagon tours, produce picking, pumpkin patches, and farm events make it one of Irvine's more distinctive family stops.

It is not a wilderness escape, and that is part of its appeal: the farm shows how much agriculture still sits behind the county's planned-city surface. Check the calendar before going, because the best reasons to visit change with the season.
