---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Laguna Beach has a good restaurant scene for a town of its size — better than most of the Orange County coast outside downtown Santa Ana. Pacific Coast Highway and the streets off it in the village have a range of options from very cheap tacos to upscale California cuisine. Seafood is the obvious choice given the location. The town gets expensive in summer when demand peaks; some restaurants are significantly cheaper at lunch.
