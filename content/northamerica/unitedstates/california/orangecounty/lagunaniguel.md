Laguna Niguel is a planned suburban city in south Orange County, between Los Angeles and San Diego, incorporated in 1989. It is a quiet, well-kept residential community without a traditional downtown, but it has easy access to the beaches of [Laguna Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/lagunabeach) just to the west, the hills and trails of [Aliso and Wood Canyons Wilderness Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/lagunaniguel/aliso_and_wood_canyons), and the coastal paths along [Salt Creek Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/lagunaniguel/salt_creek_beach).

The city is primarily residential, but there are restaurants and shops clustered around the main commercial corridors — La Paz Road and Crown Valley Parkway. [Laguna Niguel Regional Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/lagunaniguel/laguna_niguel_regional_park) provides a recreational lake and trails in the city's interior. The most unusual landmark in the area is the [Ziggurat Building](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/lagunaniguel/ziggurat_building), an inverted pyramid federal office building visible from the freeway. Laguna Niguel is close enough to the ocean to catch sea breezes but far enough inland that property prices are lower than in neighboring Laguna Beach.
