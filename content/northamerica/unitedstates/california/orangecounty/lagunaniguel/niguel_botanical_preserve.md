Niguel Botanical Preserve is a community-built garden spreading over a sunny hillside in Crown Valley Community Park. Paths wind through drought-tolerant plantings, native and Mediterranean-climate species, and viewpoints that make the preserve feel larger than its acreage.

It is not a manicured show garden; part of the charm is that it feels local, volunteer-tended, and practical for Southern California's climate. Pair it with Laguna Niguel Regional Park if you want a relaxed outdoor afternoon away from the beach traffic.
