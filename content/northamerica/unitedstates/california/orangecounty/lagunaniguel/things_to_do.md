---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Laguna Niguel's main draws are outdoors: the wilderness park trails in Aliso and Wood Canyons, the regional park lake, and the easy access to Salt Creek Beach. The city itself has no historic downtown, but it sits at the intersection of the San Joaquin Hills and the coast, with hiking, cycling, and beach access more accessible than in denser Orange County communities.
