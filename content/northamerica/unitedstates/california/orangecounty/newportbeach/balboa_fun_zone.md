Balboa Fun Zone is a small waterfront amusement area beside Balboa Pavilion. Its Ferris wheel, arcade, rides, and harbor setting make it one of Newport Beach's classic family stops.

It works best as part of a Balboa Peninsula outing, with the ferry, pavilion, harborfront, and nearby beaches all within a short walk.
