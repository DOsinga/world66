Corona del Mar — usually shortened to CdM — is the higher, southern part of Newport Beach, sitting on the bluffs above Corona del Mar State Beach. The streets are leafy, the houses are large, and Pacific Coast Highway runs through the middle as a village high street with bistros, boutiques and the kind of small art galleries that survive on affluent locals.

The beach itself is a perfect crescent of sand at the harbour entrance, with high cliffs at one end and a tide-pool reserve at the other. The Sherman Library and Gardens — two acres of carefully tended subtropical garden a block off the highway — is a tranquil afternoon, especially the Tea Garden. Sunsets from the bluff at Inspiration Point are some of the best on the southern California coast.
