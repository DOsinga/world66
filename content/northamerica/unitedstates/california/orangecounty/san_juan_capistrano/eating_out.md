---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The historic downtown has several good options within walking distance of the mission. [El Adobe de Capistrano](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/san_juan_capistrano/el_adobe_de_capistrano) (31891 Camino Capistrano) is the most atmospheric choice — Mexican-American food in a genuine 1797 adobe building with a Nixon connection. **Sundried Tomato** (31731 Los Rios Street) in the historic district serves California-style breakfast and lunch in a rustic wooden building. For something more casual, the downtown area has several taco spots and cafes around Ortega Highway.
