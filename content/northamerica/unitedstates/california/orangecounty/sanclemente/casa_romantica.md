Casa Romantica is San Clemente's most graceful historic building, a white-walled Spanish Colonial Revival house above the ocean. Its gardens, tilework, and terraces make the city's founding fantasy visible in a way that a street walk alone does not.

Today it operates as a cultural centre, so check for exhibitions, concerts, and closures before visiting. Even a short stop is worthwhile for the views over the tracks, pier, and Pacific.
