San Clemente State Beach is the practical choice if you want more space than the pier area offers. The bluffs, campground, and beach access make it feel slightly removed from the compact town centre.

It is popular with families, campers, and surfers, especially in warmer months. Book camping well ahead if that is your plan; for a day visit, arrive early when the weather is good.
