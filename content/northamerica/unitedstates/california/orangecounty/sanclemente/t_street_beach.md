T-Street is one of San Clemente's classic local beaches, close enough to the pier for a walk but with a surf identity of its own. The name comes from the pedestrian overpass and street access that funnel people down to the sand.

It is better for watching surfers or joining them than for a quiet, empty beach day. Conditions vary, and swimmers should pay attention to surf and lifeguard advice.
