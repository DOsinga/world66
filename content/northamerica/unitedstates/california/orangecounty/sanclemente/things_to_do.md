title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

San Clemente's sights line up neatly along the coast and the bluff. Start at [Casa Romantica](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/sanclemente/casa_romantica), walk down toward the [San Clemente Pier](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/sanclemente/san_clemente_pier), and leave time for the beach trail if the weather is clear.

Surfers should look south to [Trestles](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/orangecounty/sanclemente/trestles) and San Onofre. Non-surfers still get a good coastal walk, train views, and a town centre that is more pleasant than many Orange County beach strips.
