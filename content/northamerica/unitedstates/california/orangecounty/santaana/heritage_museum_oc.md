A 12-acre outdoor history museum built around two Victorian-era houses — the 1898 Kellogg House and the 1895 Palmyra House — set in restored citrus groves that evoke Orange County's agricultural past before the freeways and suburbs arrived. The museum runs craft demonstrations, living history programmes, and seasonal events.

The setting is the real draw: old trees, period gardens, and the hum of the highway just far enough away to let the imagination run. Children's programmes here tend to be hands-on and old-fashioned in the best sense — blacksmithing, butter-churning, and the working kitchen garden. It is a quiet, unpretentious counterpoint to the louder attractions of the county.
