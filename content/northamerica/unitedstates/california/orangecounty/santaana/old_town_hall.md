Santa Ana's Old Town Hall is an Art Deco civic building in the downtown core. It now serves as a city museum, though access may require checking current opening or registration details before you go.

It is best visited as part of a downtown Santa Ana walk, especially if you are also seeing the Old Orange County Courthouse and nearby galleries.
