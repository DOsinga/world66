Palm Springs sits in the Coachella Valley, ringed by mountains on all sides and baked by desert sun for 354 days a year. It was a glamorous midcentury retreat — Frank Sinatra, Dean Martin, and the Rat Pack crowd all had homes here — and that era still defines the town's character. The [midcentury modern architecture](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/palm_springs/modernism_architecture) is the best-preserved collection in the United States, and Modernism Week each February draws architecture fans from around the world.

Today Palm Springs is a mix of retirees, weekenders from Los Angeles (it is about two hours east), and a large LGBTQ+ community that has helped keep the town culturally interesting. The downtown strip along Palm Canyon Drive has good restaurants, galleries, and a Thursday evening street market ([VillageFest](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/palm_springs/villagefest)).

The [Palm Springs Aerial Tramway](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/palm_springs/aerial_tramway) is the headline attraction: a rotating cable car that climbs from the desert floor to Mount San Jacinto at 2,596 metres, where the temperature can be 20°C cooler and the views stretch across the entire valley. In winter there is often snow at the top while it is warm enough for shorts below. The [Indian Canyons](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/palm_springs/indian_canyons), just south of town, offer hiking through fan palm oases on land still owned by the Agua Caliente Band of Cahuilla Indians. The [Palm Springs Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/palm_springs/palm_springs_art_museum) and the [Agua Caliente Cultural Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/palm_springs/agua_caliente_cultural_museum) are both worth an afternoon, and the small [Moorten Botanical Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/palm_springs/moorten_botanical_garden) is one of the more pleasantly eccentric gardens in California.
