The most dramatic thing you can do in Palm Springs is take the rotating cable car from the desert floor at 811 metres up to the Mountain Station at 2,596 metres on the shoulder of Mount San Jacinto. The 10-minute ride passes through five distinct ecological zones — from Sonoran Desert scrub to subalpine forest — and the temperature at the top is typically 15 to 20°C cooler than at the bottom.

At the top, a restaurant and observation decks offer views across the Coachella Valley to the Salton Sea and beyond. In winter there is often snow at the station while it is warm enough for shorts at the bottom. From the station you can hike into the San Jacinto Wilderness, which has trails ranging from easy walks to the full summit of San Jacinto Peak at 3,302 metres — one of the most dramatic climbs in southern California. The tram runs several times per hour throughout the day and into the evening.
