---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Palm Springs is 180 kilometres east of Los Angeles, about two hours by car on the I-10 freeway. From San Diego it is roughly the same distance going north and east. The drive from Los Angeles takes you through the San Bernardino Valley and then over the San Gorgonio Pass — watch for the famous wind turbines as you descend into the Coachella Valley.

**Palm Springs International Airport** (PSP) has direct flights from many US cities and some Canadian cities. It is a small, pleasant airport right at the edge of downtown. Car rental is available at the airport and is essentially necessary — public transport within the Coachella Valley is minimal. A taxi or rideshare from the airport to downtown takes about ten minutes.

**By bus**: Greyhound connects Palm Springs to Los Angeles and other California cities. The Amtrak Sunset Limited stops at Palm Springs (technically at North Palm Springs) but only three times per week each direction.
