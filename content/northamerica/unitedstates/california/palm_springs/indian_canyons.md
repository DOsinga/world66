Just south of downtown, Indian Canyons is tribal land of the Agua Caliente Band of Cahuilla Indians and one of the best places near Palm Springs for hiking through genuine desert wilderness. Three canyons are accessible: Palm Canyon, Murray Canyon, and Andreas Canyon.

Palm Canyon is the most popular, with a paved trail for the first section and a longer undeveloped route leading several miles up the canyon floor past hundreds of native fan palms. Murray Canyon is narrower and quieter, with a trail to a small waterfall in winter. Andreas Canyon has interesting rock formations carved by the creek. The canyons are open from autumn through spring and closed in summer due to heat and flash flood risk. An entry fee is charged, and the toll booth at the bottom of the canyon serves as the visitor centre.
