The Palm Springs Art Museum is a serious regional institution with a permanent collection that emphasises twentieth-century American art, glass sculpture, and the indigenous art of the American West. The collection of works by Cahuilla basket weavers is particularly strong, and the glass gallery — featuring Dale Chihuly and others — is one of the best in California.

The architecture building on nearby Palm Canyon Drive (the Edwards Harris Pavilion) is a separate gallery space dedicated to architecture and design exhibitions, which makes sense given Palm Springs's reputation as a showcase of midcentury modern design. The main museum runs regular travelling exhibitions alongside the permanent collection. Free on Thursday evenings.
