---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Palm Springs is a desert resort, which means weather matters more than almost anywhere else. In summer (June through September) temperatures regularly exceed 40°C and can reach 47°C in July and August. This is not a comfortable time to visit unless you plan to spend most of the day in a pool. Many restaurants and shops reduce their hours or close entirely, and hotels offer dramatically reduced rates to compensate for the heat.

The best time to visit is mid-October through April, when daytime temperatures are 20 to 30°C and the evenings are cool and pleasant. December through February is the peak season, when the population of the Coachella Valley roughly doubles with snowbirds from Canada and the northern United States, and hotel rates are at their highest.

**Modernism Week** (February) is the most culturally interesting time to visit, with home tours, architecture talks, and film screenings. The **Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival** (April) is held in Indio, 30 kilometres east — a logistical challenge but a reason to visit the region if you have tickets.
