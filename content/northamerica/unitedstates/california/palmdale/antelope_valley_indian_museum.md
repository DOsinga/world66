Antelope Valley Indian Museum is a small desert museum with collections from Indigenous peoples of California, the Great Basin, and the Southwest. The setting is part of the appeal: the museum sits among Joshua trees and rock formations, so the building and landscape feel tied to the objects inside.

It is worth pairing with other Antelope Valley desert stops, especially if you are already heading toward Saddleback Butte or the preserve.
