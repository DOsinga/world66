Blackbird Airpark is the most distinctive stop in Palmdale. The outdoor display focuses on the high-speed reconnaissance aircraft associated with the Air Force Flight Test Center and the Skunk Works tradition, including the SR-71 Blackbird, A-12, and U-2.
