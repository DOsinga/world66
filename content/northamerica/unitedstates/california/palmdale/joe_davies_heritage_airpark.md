Joe Davies Heritage Airpark sits next to Blackbird Airpark and broadens the story beyond spy planes. The collection is outdoors, informal, and very Palmdale: a lineup of aircraft connected to Plant 42, flight testing, and the aerospace industry that shaped the Antelope Valley economy.
