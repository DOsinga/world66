The Cantor Arts Center on the Stanford campus is free, excellent, and chronically undervisited by people who drive straight to San Francisco. Its permanent collection spans African, Native American, Asian, and European art, with particular strength in American painting and photography. The museum's architecture — a 19th-century building with a modern wing attached — works well, and the light in the Rodin Garden is best in the late afternoon.

The center is open Wednesday through Sunday. Admission is free. It sits just off Museum Way at the corner of Lomita Drive, a short walk from the main Oval.
