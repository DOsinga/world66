Coal Creek Open Space Preserve protects grassland and oak-dotted slopes in the Santa Cruz Mountains above Palo Alto. The trails are quieter than the Stanford Dish and give wide views across the Peninsula and Bay on clear days.

It is best for travellers with a car and proper walking shoes, especially in spring when the hills are green. Summer can be hot and exposed, so start early and carry water.
