---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Palo Alto is easily reached from San Francisco by Caltrain — the main train service running down the Peninsula. The Palo Alto station is on University Avenue, right in the centre of town, and trains run frequently throughout the day (journey time roughly 40–55 minutes from San Francisco's 4th and King station). From San José, Caltrain takes about 20 minutes.

The nearest major airport is San Francisco International (SFO), about 30 minutes by car or an hour by public transit (Caltrain to Millbrae, then BART to SFO). San José Mineta Airport (SJC) is slightly closer and often has cheaper fares for domestic travel.

By car, Palo Alto is on Highway 101 and the more scenic Highway 82 (El Camino Real). Parking downtown can be tight on weekdays; a better strategy is to park at a Caltrain station and take the train in.
