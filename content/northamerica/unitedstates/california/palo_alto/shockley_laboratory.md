Shockley Semiconductor Laboratory is not visually dramatic, but its historical weight is hard to overstate. William Shockley’s short-lived company helped gather the engineers who would become central to the early semiconductor industry, making this one of the seed sites in the Silicon Valley story.

Visit with the right expectations: this is a science-history stop, closer in spirit to the HP Garage than to a museum. The interest is in standing where a technical and business lineage began, then reading the surrounding office landscape differently afterward.
