The Anderson Collection is one of Stanford's strongest cultural stops, focused on postwar American art from the collection of Harry and Mary Margaret Anderson. Works associated with Abstract Expressionism, Bay Area Figuration, Color Field painting, and later movements make the museum feel concentrated and serious without being overwhelming.

It pairs naturally with the nearby Cantor Arts Center, and together they make Stanford worth more than a quick campus stroll. The Anderson is especially good if you want modern American painting in a setting that is quieter than a major city museum. Allow time for both the galleries and the surrounding campus.
