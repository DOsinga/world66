A small, slightly overgrown desert garden tucked behind the Stanford family mausoleum on the
northern edge of campus. Jane Stanford had it laid out by Rudolph Ulrich in the 1880s as part of
the family estate; it survived more or less by being forgotten when the original mansion was torn
down.

Roughly 500 species of cactus and succulent still grow here, planted in concentric circles around
a central palm. It's free, almost always empty, and a strange shock of Arizona desert in the
middle of a leafy peninsula campus. Best visited in spring when the prickly pears flower.
