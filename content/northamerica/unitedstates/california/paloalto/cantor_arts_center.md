Stanford's main art museum, free and surprisingly comprehensive — over 38,000 works covering 5,000
years, from Mediterranean antiquity to contemporary photography. The galleries upstairs hold strong
showings of European painting and Asian art; the photography and prints department punches above
the typical university museum.

The real signature is on the lawn outside, the **Rodin Sculpture Garden**: twenty bronze casts
including The Thinker, the Burghers of Calais, and the looming Gates of Hell. Free to wander any
time, day or night, but best on weekday mornings before tour groups arrive. The museum closes
Tuesdays.
