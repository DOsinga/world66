A small free museum in a craftsman house just off University Avenue, given over to mechanical
inventions of the pre-electronic era — typewriters, sewing machines, early radios, kitchen
appliances, surveying tools, and a wall of working antique telephones. The house itself dates
from 1907.

Volunteer docents are likely to demonstrate whichever machines you ask about. It's perfectly
weighted at about an hour. Closed Monday through Wednesday; donations welcomed at the door.
