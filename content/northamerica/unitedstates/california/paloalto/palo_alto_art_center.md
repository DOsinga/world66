Palo Alto Art Center is a community-focused visual arts center with changing exhibitions, classes, workshops, and art education programs. The emphasis is contemporary work, especially from the San Francisco Bay Area.

It is not a blockbuster museum, but it is a worthwhile stop if you want a local art angle between Stanford, downtown Palo Alto, and the nearby parks.
