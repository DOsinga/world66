A small, easily missed corner of campus that holds something genuinely surprising: dozens of
carved wooden poles, masks, and figures created on site in 1994 by ten New Guinean master carvers.
The pieces are arranged in a redwood grove, slowly weathering into the soil; the carvers chose
this fate deliberately.

It's free, never crowded, and almost no Stanford tour stops here. Find it by walking south from
the Cantor Arts Center past Roble Hall to the corner of Santa Teresa Street and Lomita Drive. The
contrast with the manicured arcades a hundred yards away is its own reward.
