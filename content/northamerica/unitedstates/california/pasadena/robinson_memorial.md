Robinson Memorial stands across from Pasadena City Hall and honours Jackie Robinson and his brother Mack Robinson, both of whom grew up in Pasadena. The bronze portrait sculptures mark Jackie Robinson's breaking of Major League Baseball's colour barrier and Mack Robinson's silver medal in the 1936 Berlin Olympics.

It is a quick but worthwhile stop when walking between City Hall, Old Pasadena, and the nearby museums.
