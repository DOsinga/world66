The Rose Bowl Flea Market fills the stadium grounds with thousands of vendors selling vintage clothing, antiques, jewelry, plants, collectibles, and odd finds. It is held on the second Sunday of each month and is a Pasadena fixture worth planning around if you enjoy browsing.
