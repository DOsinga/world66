Paso Robles sits in the northern half of San Luis Obispo County, tucked between the Santa Lucia Range and the Cholame Hills. The coastal mountains that separate it from the Pacific beaches — just 20 minutes west — also shield this inland valley from the marine layer, giving it long, hot summers and cool nights that are ideal for growing Zinfandel, Cabernet Sauvignon, and Rhône varieties.

The town itself has a pleasant [downtown square and city park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paso_robels/city_park) ringed with tasting rooms, restaurants, and cafes. It feels less polished than Napa but all the more likeable for it. More than 200 wineries operate in the region, ranging from small family operations to well-known names. [Tablas Creek](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paso_robels/tablas_creek_vineyard) and [JUSTIN](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paso_robels/justin_vineyards) are the Westside anchors; [Eberle](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paso_robels/eberle_winery) is the most visited on the Eastside. The Westside, with its limestone soils and the daily wind through the [Templeton Gap](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paso_robels/templeton_gap), tends to produce more structured reds; the Eastside is warmer and looser.

Beyond wine, the town has a good supply of reasons to linger. The [hot springs](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paso_robels/hot_springs) that gave Paso Robles its resort reputation in the 19th century still flow. The [Estrella Warbird Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paso_robels/estrella_warbird_museum) at the municipal airport is a serious collection of vintage military aircraft. And the [Sensorio](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paso_robels/sensorio) outdoor light installation is one of the more unusual art experiences on the Central Coast. About 30 kilometres east, the [James Dean Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paso_robels/james_dean_memorial) marks the spot where the actor died in 1955.
