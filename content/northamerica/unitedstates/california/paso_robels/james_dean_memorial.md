  Dean was driving his Porsche 550 Spyder, which he had nicknamed "Little Bastard," to a racing event in Salinas when he crashed at the junction of State Routes 46 and 41 on 30 September 1955. About 30 kilometres east of Paso Robles, at the junction of Highway 46 East and Highway 41, a stainless steel monument marks the spot where James Dean died in a car crash in 1955. It is a pilgrimage site for fans of the actor, who often stop here while driving through the region.

The Cholame area around the monument is striking in its own right — open ranch country, oak-dotted hills, and the sense that you are well off the tourist trail. The small Jack Ranch Café nearby has leaned into the Dean connection with memorabilia on the walls. Worth a short detour if you are driving east through wine country.
