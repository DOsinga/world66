A semi-private 18-hole golf course in north Placentia, set in the rolling foothills above the city with views toward the Chino Hills. The course is the kind of mid-century Orange County layout that does not get into national rankings but is well-loved by locals — narrow fairways, mature trees, and surprisingly tricky greens.

Tee times for non-members are limited, and prices are modest by the regional standard. The clubhouse restaurant is open to walk-ins for lunch with a view out over the course.
