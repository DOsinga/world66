---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Placentia's single compelling day-trip reason is sitting five miles to the southwest: the [Disneyland Resort](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/placentia/disneyland_day_trip) in Anaheim. In light traffic you can be at the park gates in fifteen minutes, which makes Placentia one of the more sensible and affordable bases for a theme-park visit. Many Placentia hotels run their own shuttles, and the fireworks are audible from much of the city on summer nights.
