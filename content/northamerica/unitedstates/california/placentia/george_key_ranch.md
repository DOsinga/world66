---

The George Key Ranch is the oldest standing house in Placentia and the centrepiece of a small county-run historic park. The two-storey wood-frame home is open for guided tours; outside, the surviving citrus trees, the rose garden, and the picnic lawn give a sense of the rural landscape that defined this corner of California a century ago.

Admission is free and tour times are limited — usually weekends only — so check the OC Parks calendar before driving over. It is a small site but it tells a big story about how Orange County got its name and its identity.
