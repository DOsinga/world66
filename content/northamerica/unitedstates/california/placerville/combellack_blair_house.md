The Combellack-Blair House is a small but worthwhile piece of Placerville’s historic streetscape. Its value is not spectacle; it is the way a surviving 19th-century residence helps connect Main Street’s Gold Rush mythology with the quieter domestic town that grew around it.

Seek it out if you are already walking Placerville’s old core and enjoy architecture with local context. It pairs best with the Fountain-Tallman Museum, Hangman’s Tree, and the other remnants that make Placerville more than a quick highway stop.
