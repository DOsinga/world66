---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Downtown San Jose has a small but lively bar scene clustered around San Pedro Square, South First Street (the SoFA district), and Santa Clara Street. The crowd skews younger than San Francisco and the drinks are usually cheaper. Cafes are easy to find in Japantown, the Rose Garden, and Willow Glen; the coffee culture is good but quieter than Berkeley's.
