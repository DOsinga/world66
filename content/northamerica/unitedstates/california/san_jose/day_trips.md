San Jose's central position in the Bay Area makes it a practical base for day trips. San Francisco is about an hour north by Caltrain and worth at least a full day. Santa Cruz, over the mountains on the Pacific coast, has the famous boardwalk and good beaches. Monterey and Carmel are a longer drive south but pair well into a single outing — especially with the 17-Mile Drive on the Monterey Peninsula. The wineries of the Santa Cruz Mountains, the redwoods at Henry Cowell and Big Basin, and the surf beaches at Half Moon Bay are all within an hour or so.
