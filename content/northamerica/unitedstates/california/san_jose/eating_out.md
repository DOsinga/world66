---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

San Jose has one of the most diverse restaurant scenes in California, reflecting a population that speaks over 50 languages. The Vietnamese restaurants along East Santa Clara Street and Story Road are some of the best in the Bay Area, and the city's large South Asian and Mexican populations support excellent options in those cuisines as well. Japantown has good ramen, curry, and Japanese-American comfort food. San Pedro Square Market is the easiest place to eat well without committing to a single cuisine.
