---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

San Jose Mineta International Airport (SJC) is one of three Bay Area airports and often the most convenient for visiting San Jose itself, with less traffic and easier access than San Francisco International. It handles most major US domestic carriers and a handful of international routes.

The Caltrain commuter rail connects San Jose's Diridon Station to San Francisco in about an hour and a half, with stops throughout the Peninsula. Diridon is also served by VTA light rail and Amtrak's Coast Starlight. BART does not yet reach central San Jose, though the Silicon Valley extension is under construction and will eventually connect to Diridon.
