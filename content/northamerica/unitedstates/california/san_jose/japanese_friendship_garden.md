San Jose's Japanese Friendship Garden sits in Kelley Park and was created as a gesture of friendship with Okayama, Japan. Koi ponds, bridges, stone lanterns, and careful planting make it one of the city's gentler outdoor stops.

The garden is especially pleasant when paired with History San Jose or other Kelley Park sights. It is not large, and drought or maintenance conditions can affect the look, but it remains a worthwhile pause in a city that often hides its quieter corners.
