San Jose's Japantown, centred on Jackson and Fifth streets about a mile north of downtown, is one of only three Japantowns left in the United States. The neighbourhood has been a Japanese American community since the 1890s and survived the upheavals of World War II internment largely intact. Today it is a lively commercial district with Japanese and Japanese American restaurants, bakeries, grocery stores, and cultural centres alongside newer businesses.

The Japantown Business District is compact and walkable. Good ramen, Japanese curry, and Japanese-American breakfast spots are the main draw for visitors. The neighbourhood also has several small temples and a Buddhist church, and hosts the Obon Festival in July — a Buddhist summer festival with taiko drumming and traditional dancing that draws the whole community together.
