The San Jose Museum of Art occupies a landmark 1892 building on downtown's Plaza de César Chávez and focuses on modern and contemporary art, with particular strength in works from the twentieth and twenty-first centuries. The permanent collection is solid; the temporary exhibitions change regularly and tend toward thoughtful programming rather than blockbuster crowd-pleasers.

The museum is a short walk from the Tech Interactive and the Cathedral Basilica of St. Joseph, making it a natural stop on a downtown loop. Admission is reasonably priced, and the first Friday evening of each month is free. The cafe inside is a pleasant spot for lunch.
