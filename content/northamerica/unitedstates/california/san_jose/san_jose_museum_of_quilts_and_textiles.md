The San Jose Museum of Quilts and Textiles is a specialist museum focused on quilts, textiles, and fiber art. In a city better known for technology museums and historic neighborhoods, it offers a quieter craft-and-design perspective.

It is best for visitors already interested in textiles, contemporary craft, or changing small-museum exhibitions. Pair it with the downtown arts district rather than treating it as a standalone San Jose must-see.
