Teatro Visión is a San Jose theatre company rooted in Chicano and Latine stories, performance, and community arts. It is not a sightseeing monument, but it belongs on the map for travelers interested in the cultural life of the South Bay beyond technology campuses and downtown museums.

The value depends on the calendar: go when a production, reading, or community event is scheduled. If the timing works, it offers a more local and politically aware evening than generic touring entertainment.
