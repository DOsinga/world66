Border Field State Park sits where the United States, Mexico, and the Pacific meet, with coastal habitat, border views, and access toward International Friendship Park. Conditions can be muddy and vehicle access is limited at times, so check before heading out and expect a wilder edge-of-the-map feel than a manicured beach park.
