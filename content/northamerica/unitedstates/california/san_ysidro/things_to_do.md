San Ysidro's main function is as a gateway to [Tijuana](/northamerica/mexico/tijuana). The [San Ysidro Port of Entry](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/san_ysidro/port_of_entry) is the busiest land border crossing in the world. [Las Americas Premium Outlets](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/san_ysidro/las_americas_outlets) sits just north of the border and draws shoppers from both sides.
