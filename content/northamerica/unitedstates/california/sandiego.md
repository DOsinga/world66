San Diego sits at the southwestern corner of the continental United States, against the Mexican border, and benefits from one of the most agreeable climates in the country — high 60s to mid 70s most of the year, long stretches without rain, and a Pacific that's swimmable from June through October. The city is more relaxed than [Los Angeles](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/losangeles), more compact than San Francisco, and built almost entirely around the outdoors.

Downtown is anchored by the [Gaslamp Quarter](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/gaslamp_quarter), sixteen blocks of restored late-Victorian buildings full of restaurants, bars, and music venues. Along the [Embarcadero](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/embarcadero), the [USS Midway](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/uss_midway) and the [Maritime Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/maritime_museum) sit moored side by side on the bay. Just across the water lies [Coronado](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/coronado), with its long white-sand beach and the storied [Hotel del Coronado](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/hotel_del_coronado), a Victorian beach resort that has been operating since 1888.

North of downtown, [Balboa Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/balboa_park) — 1,200 acres of Spanish-colonial pavilions from the 1915 Panama-California Exposition — contains the [San Diego Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/san_diego_zoo), a dozen museums, and the [Spreckels Organ Pavilion](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/balboa_park_organ) with its free Sunday concerts. The [San Diego Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/san_diego_museum_of_art), the [Natural History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/san_diego_natural_history_museum), and the [Fleet Science Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/fleet_science_center) all cluster around the park's central plaza.

The beach neighbourhoods run up the coast. [La Jolla](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/lajolla) is the upscale one, with the protected cove at [La Jolla Cove](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/la_jolla_cove), the [Birch Aquarium](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/birch_aquarium) on the bluffs above Scripps Institution, and the [Sunny Jim Sea Cave](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/sunny_jim_sea_cave) under the clifftops. [Mission Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/mission_beach) and [Pacific Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/pacific_beach) are the surf-and-boardwalk neighbourhoods. [Ocean Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/ocean_beach) is the hippie-holdout alternative with the longest wooden pier on the West Coast. The [Sunset Cliffs](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/sunset_cliffs) at the southern edge of [Point Loma](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/point_loma) catch the last light of the day better than anywhere else in the city. Inland, [North Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/north_park) has become the creative centre — the best craft breweries, farmers market, and independent restaurants outside the tourist corridor.

The food is excellent. [Fish tacos](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/fish_tacos) arrived from Ensenada and the border proximity keeps the standard high. The [craft beer scene](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/craft_beer) — 150 breweries in the county — is among the strongest in the country. South of downtown, [Barrio Logan](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/barrio_logan) and [Chicano Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/chicano_park) under the Coronado Bridge are essential. [Tijuana](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/tijuana_day_trip) is twenty minutes away and worth the crossing for street tacos alone.

The oddities are numerous: the [Whaley House](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/whaley_house) in Old Town (the most haunted building in America, according to the television industry), the [Spruce Street Suspension Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/spruce_street_suspension_bridge) bouncing over a eucalyptus canyon, and [Villa Montezuma](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/villa_montezuma) — an 1887 mansion built for a spiritualist pianist who left the city without explanation two years after moving in. The [Cabrillo National Monument](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/cabrillo_national_monument) at the tip of Point Loma marks where the first European set foot on the Pacific Coast in 1542.
