Casa de Estudillo is a restored adobe house in Old Town San Diego, showing the domestic architecture and courtyard layout of early Californio San Diego.

It is close to the Whaley House and Old Town San Diego State Historic Park, so it fits naturally into a short walking route through the historic district.
