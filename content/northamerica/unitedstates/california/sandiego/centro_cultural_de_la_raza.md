Centro Cultural de la Raza is a Chicano, Mexican, Indigenous, and Latino arts center in Balboa Park. The circular former water tank now holds a gallery with rotating exhibitions, murals, and cultural events.

It is a smaller stop than Balboa Park's major museums, but it adds a different voice to the park's museum cluster and is worth checking when an exhibition or performance is on.
