La Jolla Shores is one of San Diego's most approachable beaches, with gentle waves, a broad sandy strand, and easy access to kayaking, surfing lessons, and the water around Scripps Pier. It is less dramatic than La Jolla Cove, but better for a relaxed beach day.

Families like it for the calmer surf, while kayakers use it as a launch point for exploring the coast. It also works well before or after a visit to Birch Aquarium at Scripps.
