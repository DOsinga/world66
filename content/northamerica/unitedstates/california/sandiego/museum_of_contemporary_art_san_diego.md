The downtown branch of the Museum of Contemporary Art San Diego sits just north of Santa Fe Depot, making it an easy cultural stop near the waterfront and rail station. The museum draws from a collection of thousands of modern and contemporary works.

Check the current exhibition before going, as the downtown space is compact and its appeal depends heavily on what is on view.
