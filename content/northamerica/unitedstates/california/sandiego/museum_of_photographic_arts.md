The Museum of Photographic Arts focuses on photography, film, and visual culture in Balboa Park. Its exhibitions mix historic and contemporary photography, with work by major photographers alongside changing shows.

The museum is close to several other Balboa Park institutions, so it is easy to fold into a half-day of museum visits.
