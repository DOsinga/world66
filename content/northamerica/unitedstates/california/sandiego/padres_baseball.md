Petco Park is the San Diego Padres' home stadium in the East Village section of downtown, a 42,000-seat venue that opened in 2004 and is consistently rated among the best ballparks in Major League Baseball. The sight lines are excellent throughout; there is no bad seat in the stadium.

The Western Metal Supply Building incorporated into the left-field corner is the architectural set piece — a four-story 1909 brick warehouse whose roof is now a rooftop seating section with the best view in the park. The Park at the Park, a grassy slope beyond the left-center field fence, is the only spot in the stadium where you can watch the game while sitting on grass.

The Gaslamp Quarter is a three-minute walk from the stadium gate, meaning the postgame crowd disperses into restaurant and bar territory immediately. The weather for baseball in San Diego is essentially perfect: clear, 70 degrees, slight sea breeze, from April through October.
