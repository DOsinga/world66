San Diego Chinese Historical Museum tells the story of Chinese culture and Chinese American life in the city and beyond. Exhibits cover history, art, and community experience, making it a useful counterpoint to the nearby Gaslamp Quarter's better-known nightlife and Victorian streetscape.

It is compact, central, and best for visitors who want a more layered view of downtown San Diego.
