The San Diego Museum of Us is the anthropology museum beneath the California Tower in Balboa Park. Its Spanish Colonial Revival building is one of the park's landmarks, close to the Cabrillo Bridge and the central museum cluster.

The exhibits look at human culture, belief, identity, and archaeology, with changing shows alongside older collections. It is best combined with the San Diego Museum of Art, the gardens, and the other Balboa Park museums nearby.
