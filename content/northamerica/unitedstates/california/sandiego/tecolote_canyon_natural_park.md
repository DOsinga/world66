Tecolote Canyon Natural Park protects a long coastal canyon between Clairemont and Mission Bay. Its trails are used for walking, running, and mountain biking, with a quieter feel than San Diego's beach parks.

The visitor center near the lower canyon has exhibits on local plants and wildlife, making this a useful stop if you want a short nature break without leaving the city.
