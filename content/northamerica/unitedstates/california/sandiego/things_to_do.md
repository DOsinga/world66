title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

San Diego's headline attractions cluster in three places. [Balboa Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/balboa_park), just north of downtown, is a 1,200-acre cultural complex of Spanish-colonial pavilions built for the 1915 Panama-California Exposition. It contains the [San Diego Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/san_diego_museum_of_art), the [Natural History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/san_diego_natural_history_museum), the [Fleet Science Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/fleet_science_center), the [Air and Space Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/san_diego_air_and_space), the [San Diego Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/san_diego_zoo), and the [Spreckels Organ Pavilion](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/balboa_park_organ) with its free Sunday concerts. The park's [gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/balboa_park_gardens) and the [Old Globe Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/old_globe_theatre) round out what is genuinely one of the great urban parks in the US.

Downtown is anchored by the [Gaslamp Quarter](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/gaslamp_quarter) and the [Embarcadero](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/embarcadero) waterfront. The [USS Midway](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/uss_midway), a retired aircraft carrier moored at the harbour, is the standout attraction. The [Maritime Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/maritime_museum) nearby holds the 1863 tall ship Star of India and a Soviet submarine. The [Unconditional Surrender](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/unconditional_surrender) statue and [Seaport Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/seaport_village) fill the harbour walk. [Little Italy](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/little_italy) a few blocks north has the best Saturday farmers' market in the county.

[Old Town](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/old_town) preserves the Mexican-era settlement with adobe buildings and the [Whaley House](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/whaley_house) — California's most haunted building, according to the television industry. [El Campo Santo Cemetery](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/old_town_cemetery) next door has graves under the road surface from the 1889 streetcar construction. The [Mission San Diego de Alcalá](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/mission_san_diego) — the first of California's 21 missions — is in the Mission Valley nearby. [Cabrillo National Monument](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/cabrillo_national_monument) at the tip of [Point Loma](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/point_loma) marks where Europeans first landed on the Pacific Coast in 1542, and the views from 400 feet are the best in the city.

For outdoor San Diego: [Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/torrey_pines) has clifftop trails through a forest of one of the world's rarest pine trees. [Mission Trails Regional Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/mission_trails) is 7,000 acres of chaparral and canyon hiking ten minutes from downtown. [Sunset Cliffs](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/sunset_cliffs) at Point Loma are best at dusk. [La Jolla Cove](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/la_jolla_cove) is the finest snorkelling in the county. [Whale watching](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/whale_watching) boats depart from the Embarcadero from December through April.

The beach communities each have a distinct character: [Coronado](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/coronado) with the [Hotel del Coronado](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/hotel_del_coronado), [Mission Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/mission_beach), [Ocean Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/ocean_beach), and [Pacific Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/pacific_beach) each serve a different crowd. [Mission Bay](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/mission_bay) is engineered for watersports. [SeaWorld](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/seaworld) sits on the bay's south shore. For families, the [San Diego Zoo Safari Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/san_diego_zoo_safari_park) in Escondido is the zoo's open-range sister, with 1,800 acres of African savanna habitat.

The city's strong suits beyond the obvious: [fish tacos](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/fish_tacos), [craft beer](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/craft_beer) (over 150 breweries), [Chicano Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/chicano_park)'s mural collection under the Coronado Bridge, [Barrio Logan](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/barrio_logan) for the best taqueries, [North Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/north_park) for breweries and independent restaurants, and the [Spruce Street Suspension Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/spruce_street_suspension_bridge) — a bouncing 1912 footbridge over a eucalyptus canyon that barely anyone outside the neighbourhood knows about. A [Tijuana day trip](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sandiego/tijuana_day_trip) is twenty minutes south and worth the border crossing.
