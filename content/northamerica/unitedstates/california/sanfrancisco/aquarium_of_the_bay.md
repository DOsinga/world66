Aquarium of the Bay is a compact aquarium at Pier 39 focused on San Francisco Bay marine life. Its signature underwater tunnels pass through tanks where sharks, rays, and schooling fish swim overhead.

It is especially useful for families already visiting Fisherman's Wharf or Pier 39. The touch pools give children a close look at local sea creatures without needing to cross the city.
