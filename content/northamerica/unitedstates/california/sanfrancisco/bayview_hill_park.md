Bayview Hill Park rises above the city's southeast corner, with wide views over San Francisco Bay and the old Candlestick Point area. The park is rougher around the edges than the city's famous viewpoints, but the outlook is real.

Come for the panorama rather than polished landscaping. It works best as a short detour if you are already exploring Bayview or Hunters Point.
