---
title: 'Beaches'
type: section
---

San Francisco is not Los Angeles. The beaches here tend to be chilly, windy, and often shrouded in fog. The water is cold enough that wetsuits are standard for anyone going in past their ankles. But that should not deter you -- the coastal scenery is spectacular, with views of the Marin Headlands, the Golden Gate Bridge, and the rugged Pacific coastline.

[Ocean Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfrancisco/ocean_beach) is the big one, stretching five miles along the city's western edge. The smaller cove beaches like [Baker Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfrancisco/baker_beach) and [China Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfrancisco/china_beach) offer more intimate settings and dramatic clifftop views. [East Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfrancisco/east_beach) at Crissy Field is the only beach on the Bay side, calmer and family-friendly, and [Land's End Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfrancisco/lands_end_beach) is really a hike along bluffs with a rocky strand at the bottom.
