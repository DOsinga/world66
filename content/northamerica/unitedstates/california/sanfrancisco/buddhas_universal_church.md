Buddha's Universal Church is a five-storey Buddhist temple in Chinatown and one of the largest Buddhist churches in the United States. Its main hall is known for an altar shaped around the image of a Dharma ship, with teak-panelled walls suggesting sails.

The church is a good stop for visitors exploring the religious and community landmarks of Chinatown. Go respectfully, since it is an active place of worship as well as an architectural and cultural sight.
