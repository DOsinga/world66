The Coastal Bluffs on the western edge of the Presidio give San Francisco one of its wildest walks: wind-shaped cliffs, pocket beaches below, and open views along the Pacific coast. The Coastal Trail links the viewpoints, with easy side trips toward Baker Beach, China Beach, and the Golden Gate Bridge approaches.
