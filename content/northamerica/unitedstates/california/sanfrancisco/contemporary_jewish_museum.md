The Contemporary Jewish Museum presents exhibitions on Jewish history, art, ideas, and culture in a striking Daniel Libeskind-designed building joined to a 19th-century power station. It is a good SoMa stop for visitors interested in contemporary art, design, and Jewish cultural history.
