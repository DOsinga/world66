Fillmore Street cuts through some of San Francisco's most interesting neighbourhoods. The stretch between Jackson and California in Pacific Heights is lined with upscale boutiques, bakeries, and cafés — it feels like a small-town main street despite the city all around it. Further south, the Western Addition has a grittier, more diverse character and a deep connection to San Francisco's jazz and blues history.

The contrast between the two ends of Fillmore tells you a lot about San Francisco. Walk the whole length for a cross-section of the city's wealth gap and cultural richness.
