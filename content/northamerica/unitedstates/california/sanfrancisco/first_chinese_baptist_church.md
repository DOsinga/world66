First Chinese Baptist Church is one of San Francisco's oldest Chinese American churches, founded in 1880 and rebuilt after the 1906 earthquake. It is a modest but meaningful Chinatown landmark, especially for visitors tracing the neighbourhood's immigrant and religious history.
