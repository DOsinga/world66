The GLBT History Museum tells the Castro's story through protest posters, personal objects, photographs, and hard-won political history. It is small, but the exhibits give real depth to the neighbourhood's role in queer life, from Harvey Milk to transgender activism and the bars, bookstores, and bedrooms where community took shape.
