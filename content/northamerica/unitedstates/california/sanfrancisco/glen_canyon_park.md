Glen Canyon Park preserves a steep, rugged canyon in the middle of San Francisco, with trails, native plants, rock outcrops, and Islais Creek running through it. It feels more natural and less manicured than many city parks.

Come for a short hike, birdwatching, or a quiet break from denser neighborhoods. The canyon paths can feel surprisingly wild for the city, so wear shoes that can handle dirt and uneven ground.
