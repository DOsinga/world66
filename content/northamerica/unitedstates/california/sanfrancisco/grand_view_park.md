Grand View Park is a hilltop viewpoint above the Inner Sunset with wide views over Golden Gate Park, the Pacific, downtown San Francisco, Sutro Tower, and the Marin Headlands. It pairs well with the nearby 16th Avenue Tiled Steps, which climb toward the same ridge.
