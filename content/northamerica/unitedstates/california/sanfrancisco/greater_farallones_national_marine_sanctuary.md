The Greater Farallones National Marine Sanctuary Visitor Center sits in the old Coast Guard Station at the west end of Crissy Field. Its small exhibits explain the marine life, weather, and offshore islands beyond the Golden Gate, making it a useful add-on to a Presidio or waterfront walk.
