The Gregangelo Museum is an immersive, eccentric art environment created by artist and performer Gregangelo Herrera. It is a small, offbeat stop rather than a conventional museum, but the elaborate rooms and theatrical approach make it one of San Francisco's stranger visitor-facing art experiences.
