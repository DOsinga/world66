Heron's Head Park is a restored wetland on the Bayview waterfront, shaped by a narrow peninsula that reaches into San Francisco Bay. The 24-acre park is a quieter place for bay views, birding, and a short walk by the water.

It is most useful for visitors exploring the southeast waterfront or looking for an urban nature stop away from San Francisco's better-known parks.
