Infantry Row is a line of brick barracks in the Presidio, built for troops consolidated here after the Indian Wars. The buildings, numbered 101 through 105, were among the first brick barracks in the American West and signalled the Presidio's shift into a permanent military post.

They are best seen as part of a Presidio walk, with the Walt Disney Family Museum, Crissy Field, and the Golden Gate views nearby.
