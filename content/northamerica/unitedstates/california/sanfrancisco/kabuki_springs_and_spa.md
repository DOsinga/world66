Kabuki Springs & Spa is a Japanese-style public bathhouse in Japantown, with hot and cold pools, steam, sauna, and quiet bathing areas. It is more urban bathhouse than tourist spectacle, which is exactly the appeal after several days of San Francisco hills.

Check the schedule before going: bathing days are usually divided by gender, with limited co-ed sessions. Reservations are sensible at busy times.
