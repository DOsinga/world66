The Legion of Honor is an art museum in a grand Beaux-Arts building modeled on the Palais de la Legion d'Honneur in Paris. Its collection is strongest in European painting, sculpture, and decorative arts, with Rodin works among the highlights and sweeping views from its Lincoln Park setting.
