The Legion of Honor sits above the Pacific in a neoclassical building modelled on the Paris original, with Rodin's *The Thinker* greeting visitors at the entrance. Inside, the collection is strongest on European painting, sculpture, and decorative arts, from Renaissance and Baroque rooms through Impressionism and early modern work.

The setting is part of the appeal. Combine the museum with a walk along Land's End, where the cypress trees, ocean light, and Golden Gate views make this one of San Francisco's most dramatic museum trips.
