The LeRoy King Carousel is a restored 1906 merry-go-round beside the Children's Creativity Museum in Yerba Buena Gardens. It is a small stop, but a good one if you are travelling with children or like old fairground craft: painted horses, carved details, and a very San Francisco setting among museums, gardens, and downtown towers.
