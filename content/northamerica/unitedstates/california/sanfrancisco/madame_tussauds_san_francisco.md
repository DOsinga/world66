Madame Tussauds San Francisco is the Fisherman's Wharf branch of the wax museum chain, with celebrity, film, music, sports, and local-interest figures staged for photos. It is light and commercial rather than a traditional museum, but it can be useful for families or for a rainy break near Pier 39 and the waterfront museums.
