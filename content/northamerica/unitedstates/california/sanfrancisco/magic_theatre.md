Magic Theatre is one of Fort Mason's small, serious stages, best known for contemporary American plays and for its long association with Sam Shepard. It is a good choice when you want a San Francisco theatre night that is more intimate than the big downtown houses.

Check the current run before heading over. The setting at Fort Mason also makes it easy to pair a performance with a walk along the northern waterfront.
