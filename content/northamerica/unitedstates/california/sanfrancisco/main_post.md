The Main Post is the historic core of the Presidio, where the Spanish founded a garrison in 1776 and the U.S. Army later built its parade ground, barracks, and officers' buildings. It works best as part of a Presidio walk, with the Officers' Club, Walt Disney Family Museum, Crissy Field, and bridge views close by.
