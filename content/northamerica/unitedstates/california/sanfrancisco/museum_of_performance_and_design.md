The Museum of Performance & Design documents San Francisco's performing arts history, from nineteenth-century theatre to more recent dance, opera, and stage design. Its collections and programs are strongest for visitors with a specific interest in performance, costume, production design, or the city's cultural archives.

Check the current exhibition or event schedule before going, as the appeal depends on what is open to the public at the time.
