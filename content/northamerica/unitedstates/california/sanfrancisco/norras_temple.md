Norras Temple is a Buddhist temple in San Francisco's Chinatown, named for a Tibetan temple and known for its gilded wooden altar imported from China. It is a quiet, compact stop among Chinatown's better-known streets and markets.

Visitors should treat it as an active religious site, not just a decorative interior.
