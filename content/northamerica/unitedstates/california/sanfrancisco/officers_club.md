The Presidio Officers Club is one of San Francisco's oldest buildings, with adobe roots and a 1930s Spanish Colonial Revival shell. It now works as a cultural centre and small museum, useful for understanding the Presidio before walking on toward Crissy Field or the Golden Gate Bridge.
