Precita Eyes is a Mission District mural center rooted in the neighbourhood's long tradition of public art. Its best use for visitors is as a starting point for the murals around 24th Street, where storefronts, alleys, and walls carry bright political and community pieces.

It is less concentrated than [Balmy Alley](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfrancisco/balmy_alley), but it gives you a broader feel for the Mission's mural culture. Go in daylight and wander the surrounding blocks.
