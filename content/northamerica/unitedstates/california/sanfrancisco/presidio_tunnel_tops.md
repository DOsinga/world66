Presidio Tunnel Tops is a landscaped park built over the Presidio Parkway tunnels. Its lawns, picnic areas, playground, and food trucks make it one of the easiest Presidio stops for families, and clear weather brings views of the Golden Gate Bridge, the bay, and downtown San Francisco.
