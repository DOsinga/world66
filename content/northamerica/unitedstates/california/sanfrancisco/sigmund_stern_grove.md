Sigmund Stern Grove is a wooded outdoor amphitheatre best known for the Stern Grove Festival, a long-running summer series of free public concerts and performances.

Outside event days it is a quiet southwest San Francisco park, but it is most worth a deliberate trip when a performance is scheduled.
