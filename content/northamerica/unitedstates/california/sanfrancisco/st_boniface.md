St. Boniface is a Catholic church in the Tenderloin with an ornate interior dating from 1900. It is an active Franciscan parish and is also known locally for opening its pews to homeless people during weekday hours.

Step inside for a quiet look at the interior if you are nearby, and be respectful of people using the church for prayer or rest.
