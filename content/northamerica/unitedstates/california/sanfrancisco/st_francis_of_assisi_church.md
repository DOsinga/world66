St. Francis of Assisi Church is a Gold Rush-era church in North Beach that now serves as a national shrine to St. Francis of Assisi. It no longer functions as an active parish, but visitors can stop in for the historic interior and devotional art.

It pairs well with a North Beach walk, especially around Washington Square, City Lights, and the lanes leading toward Telegraph Hill.
