St. Francis of Assisi Church is a Gold Rush-era church in North Beach, now used as a national shrine rather than an active parish. Its appeal is quieter than the neighbourhood's cafes and Beat landmarks, but it adds a religious and immigrant-history layer to a walk around Columbus Avenue.

Stop in if you are already nearby; the church works best as part of North Beach rather than as a cross-town destination.
