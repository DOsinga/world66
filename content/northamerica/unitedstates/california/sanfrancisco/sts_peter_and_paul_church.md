Sts. Peter and Paul Church is the white neo-Gothic landmark facing Washington Square in North Beach. Its twin towers make it one of the neighborhood's clearest visual anchors, and it has a pop-culture footnote from Joe DiMaggio and Marilyn Monroe's post-wedding photos.
