The Truhlsen-Marmor Museum of the Eye is a small museum run by the American Academy of Ophthalmology. Exhibits cover eye anatomy, vision, eye disorders, treatments, and the history of ophthalmology, making it an unusual stop near Fisherman's Wharf.
