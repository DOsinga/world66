The USS San Francisco Memorial stands above the Coastal Trail near Land's End, using shell-damaged pieces of the heavy cruiser that fought in the Battle of Guadalcanal. The torn metal gives the memorial a physical force that a plaque alone could not carry.

It is a short stop, but a worthwhile one if you are walking between the Sutro Baths, the cliffs and the western edge of the city. The ocean setting suits the story better than almost anywhere else in San Francisco.
