The USS San Francisco Memorial stands above the Coastal Trail near Lands End. It incorporates damaged bridge wings from the cruiser USS San Francisco, which survived heavy action during the Naval Battle of Guadalcanal in 1942.

The memorial is a short stop, but its cliffside setting and wartime connection make it a meaningful addition to a Lands End or Sutro Baths walk.
