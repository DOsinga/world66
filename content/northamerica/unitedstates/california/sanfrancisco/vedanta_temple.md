Vedanta Temple is an unusual early-20th-century Hindu temple in Pacific Heights, built in 1905 and often described as the first Hindu temple in the West. Its towers mix several architectural styles, including Moorish, Edwardian, Queen Anne, and Oriental details, making the exterior worth a short detour.
