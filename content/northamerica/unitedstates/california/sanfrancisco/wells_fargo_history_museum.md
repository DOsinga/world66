The Wells Fargo History Museum is a small museum in San Francisco's Financial District, focused on the bank's role in western American history. Exhibits include stagecoach-era artifacts and material tied to the growth of banking and transport in California.

It is a compact indoor stop, easiest to fit into a walk between Chinatown, the Financial District, and the Embarcadero.
