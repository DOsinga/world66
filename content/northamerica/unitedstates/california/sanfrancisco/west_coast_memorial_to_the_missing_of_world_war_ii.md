The West Coast Memorial to the Missing of World War II stands in the Presidio, set among Monterey pine and cypress with the Pacific nearby. Its curved granite wall lists more than 400 service members who died or were buried at sea in the offshore Pacific waters.

It is a solemn, uncrowded stop rather than a major destination on its own. Combine it with a walk toward Battery Godfrey, Baker Beach, or the Golden Gate overlooks, where the military landscape of the Presidio makes the memorial feel less isolated.
