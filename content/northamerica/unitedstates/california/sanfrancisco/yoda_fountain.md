Yoda Fountain is a small, oddball stop outside the Letterman Digital Arts Center in the Presidio. The bronze Jedi master stands over a fountain in front of Lucasfilm offices, which makes it a quick pilgrimage for Star Wars fans.

It is not worth crossing the city for by itself, but it fits nicely with a Presidio walk, the Palace of Fine Arts, or the approach to the Golden Gate Bridge.
