The San Francisco Bay Area is not one city but a whole ring of them, wrapped around the bay and stitched together by bridges, ferries, BART trains, fog, money, food, and a persistent sense that the next big thing is being prototyped somewhere nearby. [San Francisco](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfrancisco) is the obvious star, with hills, cable cars, [Alcatraz](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfranciscobayarea/alcatraz_island), and the [Golden Gate Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfranciscobayarea/golden_gate_bridge), but the region gets more interesting when you cross the water.

The East Bay has [Oakland](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/oakland), [Berkeley](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/berkeley), Lake Merritt, old redwood parks, and a food scene with less performance and more soul. South along the peninsula, the mood shifts into Silicon Valley: [Palo Alto](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/paloalto), [San Jose](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanjose), Stanford, the [Computer History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfranciscobayarea/computer_history_museum), and enough campuses to make even the billboards sound ambitious.

North of the bridge, Marin opens into headlands, redwoods, ferry towns, and mountain roads. [Muir Woods](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfranciscobayarea/muir_woods) is the easy redwood fix; [Mount Tamalpais](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfranciscobayarea/mount_tamalpais) and [Point Reyes](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/sanfranciscobayarea/point_reyes_national_seashore) are wilder. Further north, [Napa](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/napa) and Sonoma turn the landscape into vineyards, tasting rooms, and old town squares.

The Bay Area rewards time and punishes over-scheduling. Public transport works well between the big nodes, but a car helps for Marin, the coast, and the wine country. Bring layers even in summer; the fog can make July feel like November, while September and October often deliver the clear, warm days you thought California had all year.
