The Golden State Model Railroad Museum is a good rainy-day stop if you have children or a weakness for miniature railways. Its large O, HO, and N scale layouts recreate parts of California's railroad landscape, with working trains, small landmarks, and enough detail to reward a slow look.

The museum is usually open on weekends, with the trains most likely to be running on Sundays.
