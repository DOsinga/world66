Gray Whale Cove State Beach is a pocket of coast below the cliffs south of Pacifica, with a sandy cove and a good chance of seeing gray whales offshore in season. It is also known locally for steep access paths and a clothing-optional tradition.

The stop works best as part of a slow drive along the San Mateo County coast. Bring layers, watch the surf, and do not expect much in the way of facilities.
