Mission San Jose was founded in 1797 as the 14th Spanish mission in California. The present church is a modern reconstruction of the 1809 adobe building, but the surviving monastery holds a small museum with mission-era artifacts, vestments, and local historical material.

The cemetery and museum make the stop more than a quick look at the chapel. It is one of the clearer places in the East Bay to trace the Spanish and Mexican layers of California history before the American period.
