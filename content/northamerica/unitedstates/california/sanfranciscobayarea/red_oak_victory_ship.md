The SS Red Oak Victory is a preserved World War II Victory ship at Richmond's old Kaiser Shipyards. Launched in 1944, it is the last surviving ship built in the Richmond yards and is being restored as a floating museum.

Visits focus on the ship itself: cargo holds, engine spaces, wartime shipbuilding, and the Bay Area's huge home-front role during the war. It pairs naturally with nearby Rosie the Riveter National Historical Park.
