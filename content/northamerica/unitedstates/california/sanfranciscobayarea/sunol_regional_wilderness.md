Sunol Regional Wilderness protects open hills, oak woodland, creek corridors, and rocky backcountry in the southeastern Bay Area. Trails leave from the visitor area and climb into the surrounding ridges, with spring wildflowers, winter waterfalls, and wide views toward the Diablo Range.

It is best treated as a real hike rather than a quick roadside stop. Bring water, check heat conditions in summer, and allow time for the drive through the rural edge of Alameda County.
