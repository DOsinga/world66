The Japanese American Museum of San Jose is a focused community museum in one of the United States' few surviving historic Japantowns. Its exhibitions cover immigration, farming communities, wartime incarceration, resettlement, and local family histories.

It is a strong stop for travellers who want San Jose to be more than tech campuses and downtown museums. Pair it with a walk through Japantown and give the stories enough time; the museum is small, but the subject matter is substantial.
