San Jose Municipal Rose Garden is a historic neighborhood park filled with rose beds, lawns, and a central fountain. In bloom, it is one of San Jose's prettiest low-key public spaces.

The garden is free, easy to visit, and best in spring or early summer when the roses are strongest. Pair it with nearby neighborhoods or use it as a quiet pause between Silicon Valley museums and downtown stops.
