The Tech Interactive is downtown San Jose's flagship hands-on science museum, and one of the few attractions that really fits the city's Silicon Valley identity. Exhibits focus on engineering, design, robotics, health, space, and problem-solving rather than passive display cases.

It is especially strong for families and curious adults who like to touch things, build things, and test ideas. Pair it with nearby museums or a walk through downtown to give San Jose more shape than office parks and freeways.
