---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

San Mateo has a food scene disproportionate to its size, shaped by its strong Japanese American community, its proximity to Silicon Valley money, and a general Bay Area tendency to take food seriously. Third and Fourth Avenues downtown are where most of the action is concentrated — a mix of long-running Japanese restaurants, newer California-cuisine spots, and a few places with serious culinary ambitions.
