Santa Barbara earns its nickname — the American Riviera — without too much strain. The city sits where the Santa Ynez Mountains meet the Pacific Ocean 160 km north of Los Angeles, in a microclimate warm enough to support bougainvillea, palm trees, and year-round outdoor life. The architecture is predominantly Spanish Colonial Revival — white stucco, red-tiled roofs — the result of strict building codes put in place after a 1925 earthquake, and the effect is unusually coherent for a California city.

The [Old Mission](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_barbara/old_mission), founded in 1786, is the architectural centrepiece and still an active parish church. State Street is the main commercial axis, running from the mountains down toward [Stearns Wharf](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_barbara/stearns_wharf) and the waterfront, where a working fishing harbour sits alongside five parks and a cycling path that stretches nearly eight kilometres. The beaches — [East Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_barbara/east_beach), West Beach, Leadbetter, [Butterfly Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_barbara/butterfly_beach), and several more — are wide, sandy, and sheltered from the worst Pacific swells by the Channel Islands offshore.

The Santa Ynez Valley wine country is 40 minutes north and contains some of California's finest Pinot Noir and Syrah vineyards. The city itself has a serious restaurant scene, strong [hiking in the mountains](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_barbara/santa_ynez_mountains) behind town, and a paragliding culture that takes advantage of the terrain. The downtown is anchored by the spectacular [County Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_barbara/county_courthouse), the reconstructed [Presidio](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_barbara/presidio), and the warehouse-turned-tasting-room district known as the [Funk Zone](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_barbara/funk_zone). It is a genuinely pleasant city to spend several days in, not just a day trip from Los Angeles.
