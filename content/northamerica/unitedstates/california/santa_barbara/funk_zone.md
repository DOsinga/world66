A few blocks of warehouses, fish-processing buildings, and light-industrial sheds between the highway and the harbour have, over the last 15 years, turned into Santa Barbara's most concentrated cluster of wine-tasting rooms, breweries, galleries, and casual restaurants. It is walking distance from the wharf and the train station, and is the obvious base for an afternoon of urban wine tasting without driving up to the valley.

Most of the tasting rooms belong to producers from the Santa Ynez or Santa Maria valleys, so a couple of hours in the Funk Zone gives a reasonable cross-section of the regional wine without leaving town. Friday evenings are particularly busy. The "Urban Wine Trail", a self-guided route map, links about two dozen tasting rooms.
