---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Santa Barbara is 95 miles up the coast from Los Angeles and 330 miles south of San Francisco. The drive from LAX takes about two hours in light traffic and considerably longer in rush hour; the Pacific Coast Highway via Malibu is far more scenic but slower.

**By train**

Amtrak's Pacific Surfliner runs from San Diego and Los Angeles up the coast to Santa Barbara, with several daily services. It is one of the most scenic train rides in the United States, hugging the ocean for much of the way, and arrives at a historic station right at the foot of State Street, three minutes' walk from the wharf. The Coast Starlight to San Francisco and Seattle also stops here.

**By air**

Santa Barbara Airport (SBA), in Goleta about 8 miles west of downtown, has flights to Denver, Dallas, Phoenix, Seattle, San Francisco, and a handful of other West Coast hubs. It is small, easy, and an excellent alternative to driving up from LAX for anyone with the option.

**By car**

Highway 101 runs through town. From the south, expect heavy traffic on weekends and during the summer; the bottleneck around Carpinteria can add an hour. Parking downtown is metered but generally available; the first 75 minutes in city parking lots are free.
