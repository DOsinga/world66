Stearns Wharf juts about a third of a mile out into the harbour from the foot of State Street and is the most-visited landmark in the city. The deck is wide enough to drive cars onto, and it carries restaurants, fish markets, a wine-tasting room, and the Ty Warner Sea Center, the Santa Barbara Museum of Natural History's small public aquarium with touch tanks and live local marine life.

At the end of the wharf, fishermen drop hand lines into the harbour and pelicans hang around the cleaning tables. The wharf is the launch point for whale-watching trips (grey whales pass close to shore from December to April, blue and humpback whales in summer) and for boats to the Channel Islands. Parking on the wharf itself is metered and limited; the easier approach is to walk down State Street.
