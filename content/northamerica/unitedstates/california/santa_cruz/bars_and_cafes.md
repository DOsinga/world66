---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

[Pacific Avenue](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_cruz/pacific_avenue) downtown is the centre of the drinking scene, with a mix of craft-beer bars, wine tasting rooms and laid-back cafes. The wharf area has a few spots with bay views. Santa Cruz leans casual — flip-flops are always acceptable.

[Verve Coffee Roasters](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_cruz/verve_coffee) on Seabright Avenue is the best cafe in town, started here before expanding to Los Angeles and Japan. For the evening, the Catalyst on Pacific Avenue is the main music venue with a good bar and touring acts most weekends.
