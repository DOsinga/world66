---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Santa Cruz has a good food scene for a city its size, with a strong emphasis on local and organic produce. [Pacific Avenue](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_cruz/pacific_avenue) and the streets around it have the highest concentration of restaurants, from taquerias and ramen shops to farm-to-table places. The [wharf](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_cruz/santa_cruz_wharf) has fresh seafood — clam chowder in a bread bowl is the classic boardwalk lunch.

[Laili](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_cruz/laili) on Cedar Street is the standout for a proper dinner — Afghan and Mediterranean food done with care. [Engfer Pizza Works](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/santa_cruz/engfers_pizza) near downtown does wood-fired pies worth seeking out.
