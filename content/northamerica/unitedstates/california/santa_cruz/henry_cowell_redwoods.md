  The redwood grove at the heart of the park includes trees that were already old when the Spanish founded the Santa Cruz mission in 1791. Henry Cowell Redwoods State Park sits in the forested hills about 6 miles north of Santa Cruz and protects a grove of old-growth coast redwoods alongside second-growth forest, chaparral, and the San Lorenzo River riparian corridor. The Redwood Loop Trail, about 1.5 miles, passes through the largest trees and is the essential walk.

The park also has longer trails leading up to Pine Ridge and a stretch of the Roaring Camp Railroad route through the trees. The Roaring Camp Railroads operates a narrow-gauge steam train from a depot just outside the park boundary — a legitimate way to see the redwoods if walking is not an option. From Santa Cruz, drive north on Highway 9.
