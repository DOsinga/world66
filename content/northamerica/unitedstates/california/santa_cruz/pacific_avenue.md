Pacific Avenue is the main commercial street through downtown Santa Cruz, running from the Farmers Market end near Cathcart Street south to Laurel. After it was largely destroyed by the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake, the street was rebuilt in a more pedestrian-friendly form and has since become one of the better small-city main streets in California — a mix of independent bookshops, clothing stores, cafes, record shops, and restaurants that has held off the complete chain-store takeover.

Bookshop Santa Cruz, at the north end, is one of the best independent bookshops in the state — strong on local history, California nature, and fiction. Several smaller boutiques and galleries have persisted for decades, giving the street an actual local character. The Catalyst music venue brings good touring acts through regularly.
