Steamer Lane is a surf break at the base of the cliff below Lighthouse Point, where natural rock formations create left-breaking waves that can hold a serious swell. On any day with surf, a crowd of spectators lines the cliff above, watching from behind the railing — it is one of the best places on the California coast to watch surfing from dry land, with the waves breaking directly below you.

The Santa Cruz Surfing Museum, housed in the Mark Abbott Memorial Lighthouse above the break, traces the history of surfing in Santa Cruz and Northern California. The museum is free and small — worth 30 minutes. The cliffs at Lighthouse Point are also good for sea otters and harbour seals.
