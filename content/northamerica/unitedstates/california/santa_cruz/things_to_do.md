---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Santa Cruz packs a lot of character into a small space. The Boardwalk is the obvious draw — a Victorian-era amusement park on the beach that has somehow survived into the 21st century. Beyond it, the surf breaks at Steamer Lane, the redwood forests above town, and the elephant seal colony at Ano Nuevo keep visitors busy for days. The counterculture streak is genuine, not performed — this is still a place where you might find a free jazz performance on Pacific Avenue at lunchtime.
