Verve Coffee Roasters started in Santa Cruz before opening locations in Los Angeles and Japan, and the original location on Seabright Avenue remains the best. The coffee is seriously good — single-origin espresso and filter coffees sourced with the kind of care that makes specialty coffee worth paying attention to, without the affectation that sometimes accompanies it.

The cafe is spacious by city standards, with good light and tables that are genuinely comfortable for lingering. It tends to be busy with university students and remote workers in the morning. Walk-in pastries from local bakeries are solid. If you drink coffee, this is the place to start your morning in Santa Cruz.
