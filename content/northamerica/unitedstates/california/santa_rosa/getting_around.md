---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

You will need a car for almost everything outside the downtown core — that is the bigger truth about Santa Rosa. Downtown itself, including Railroad Square and Old Courthouse Square, is walkable; the Schulz Museum, Luther Burbank Home, and the cemetery are all within twenty minutes' walk of each other and of the centre.

The **CityBus** network covers the rest of the urban area but is built for commuters rather than visitors. For the wineries and the coast, rent a car at the airport or downtown. Cycling along the Joe Rodota Trail (an old railroad converted into a paved path) is a pleasant way to reach Sebastopol; bike rental is available in Railroad Square.
