A standard regional mall that occupies several blocks at the western edge of downtown, Santa Rosa Plaza is bigger than you might expect for a city this size — it serves a large rural catchment. It is mainly useful if you need a Macy's or an Apple Store; the more interesting shopping in Santa Rosa is on the surrounding streets and in Railroad Square.

The plaza has a busy food court and a covered walkway across Third Street that links it directly to the Railroad Square SMART station.
