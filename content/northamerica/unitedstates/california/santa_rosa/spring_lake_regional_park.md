Spring Lake Regional Park is Santa Rosa's best all-purpose outdoor escape, a broad park of lake paths, oak woodland, picnic areas, campgrounds, and seasonal swimming. It connects with Annadel State Park and Howarth Park, so a short stroll can easily become a longer day outside.

It is especially useful for travellers with children or anyone wanting a low-key break from wineries and downtown. Bring water and sun protection in warm weather; the open stretches can feel much hotter than the shaded creekside paths.
