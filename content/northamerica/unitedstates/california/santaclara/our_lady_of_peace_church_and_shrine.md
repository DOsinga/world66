Our Lady of Peace Church and Shrine is hard to miss because of the tall stainless-steel statue of Mary rising beside the church. In a city better known for tech campuses and stadiums, the shrine is a very different landmark: devotional, roadside, and constantly active.

Visitors come for Mass, prayer, or simply to see the statue and grounds. Be respectful of worshippers and avoid treating the site as a novelty. As a short stop, it pairs well with nearby Mission Santa Clara for a look at the older and newer Catholic layers of the South Bay.
