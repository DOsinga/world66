The South Bay Historical Railroad Society keeps Santa Clara's railroad story alive
inside the old depot beside the tracks. Its model layouts, artifacts, signal equipment,
and preserved rolling-stock details make the station feel like a small working museum
rather than a static display.

It is a focused stop, best for rail fans, families, or anyone arriving by train who
wants to connect the modern Caltrain corridor with the older Southern Pacific world.
Hours are limited, so check before planning a special trip.
