The Gentle Barn is an animal sanctuary in Santa Clarita that turns rescued farm animals
into the focus of a gentle, hands-on visit. Rather than a conventional zoo, the
experience is built around stories of care, rehabilitation, and close contact with
animals such as cows, horses, pigs, goats, and turkeys.

It is best for families, animal lovers, and travellers who want a slow, values-driven
stop away from studio tours and theme parks. Public visiting days can be limited and
often require advance planning, so confirm the schedule before going.
