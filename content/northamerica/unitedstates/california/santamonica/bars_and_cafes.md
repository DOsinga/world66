---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Santa Monica's bar scene is split between the tourist strip around the pier and Third Street Promenade, and the better local bars on Main Street and in the Ocean Park neighbourhood to the south. The coffee culture is serious — this is Los Angeles, and people care — with independent cafes on Montana Avenue and Main Street worth seeking out over the chain options near the Promenade.
