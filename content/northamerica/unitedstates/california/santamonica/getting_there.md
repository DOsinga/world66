---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Santa Monica is reachable from downtown Los Angeles in 30–50 minutes depending on traffic. The Metro Expo Line (E Line) connects downtown LA to Downtown Santa Monica station at 4th Street and Colorado Avenue — a reliable option that avoids the frequently congested I-10. By car, the I-10 west is the direct route, though it can be slow during rush hour. From LAX, the Flyaway bus service runs direct to Santa Monica. Cycling is practical from nearby neighborhoods; the Marvin Braude Bike Trail (the Strand) connects to Venice and beyond.
