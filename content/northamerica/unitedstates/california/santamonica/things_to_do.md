title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Santa Monica's sights divide between the iconic (the pier, the beach, the promenade) and the genuinely interesting (Bergamot Station's galleries, the Art Deco architecture of downtown, the clifftop walk along Palisades Park). The beach is unavoidable and worth it; the rest of the list is what separates a day trip from an actual stay.
