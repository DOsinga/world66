---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Saratoga is in Santa Clara County, about 55 miles south of San Francisco and 10 miles southwest of downtown San Jose. The nearest major airport is San Jose Mineta International (SJC), about 15 minutes by car. San Francisco International (SFO) and Oakland (OAK) are both around 45–60 minutes away with normal traffic.

There is no direct public transit to Saratoga Village. The VTA bus network serves surrounding areas and the village has a stop, but most visitors arrive by car. A rental car or rideshare is the practical option. From Highway 85, take the Saratoga Avenue exit and head west toward the hills.
