Chico is a mid-sized city in the Sacramento Valley at the foot of the Sierra Nevada foothills, about 90 miles north of Sacramento. It is best known as a college town — California State University, Chico anchors the [downtown](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/chico/downtown_chico) — which gives the city an unpretentious energy and a lively bar and restaurant scene that belies its size.

[Bidwell Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/chico/bidwell_park) runs through the heart of the city and is one of the largest municipal parks in the United States, stretching for miles along Chico Creek into the oak-covered foothills. Swimming holes like One-Mile and Five-Mile attract locals on hot summer days, and the [upper park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/chico/upper_bidwell_park) offers hiking and mountain biking. The [Sierra Nevada Brewing Company](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/chico/sierra_nevada_brewery), one of the country's pioneering craft breweries, is based here and offers tours and a well-regarded brewpub.

The surrounding area produces almonds, walnuts, and olives, and the Saturday Farmers Market downtown is a good place to find local produce. The [Bidwell Mansion](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/chico/bidwell_mansion) tells the story of the city's remarkable founding family. Chico also hides an oddity: the [National Yo-Yo Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/chico/national_yoyo_museum), which contains the world's largest working wooden yo-yo. The city makes a reasonable base for exploring the upper Sacramento Valley or the volcanic landscapes of Lassen Volcanic National Park, about 90 minutes to the east.
