McCloud is a small historic lumber mill town at the base of Mount Shasta in northern California. It has reinvented itself as a quiet getaway with bed-and-breakfast inns and a handful of shops, set against the spectacular backdrop of the 4,300-metre volcano.

The McCloud River, running through forested canyon land south of town, has three waterfalls accessible from a single trail — Lower, Middle, and Upper [McCloud Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/mccloud/mccloud_falls). The hike is one of the best short walks in the Shasta region. In winter, the nearby [Mount Shasta Ski Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/mccloud/mount_shasta_ski_park) offers downhill and cross-country skiing.

Downtown is anchored by the [McCloud Mercantile](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/mccloud/mccloud_mercantile), a restored 1897 company-store building, and the old depot of the [McCloud Railway](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/mccloud/mccloud_railway), which still occasionally runs a heritage dinner train. East of town, half-hidden along the river, sits the Hearst family's secretive Bavarian retreat at [Wyntoon](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/mccloud/wyntoon).
