Mount Shasta City Park is a small place with an outsized sense of location. Its best-known feature is the headwaters area of the Sacramento River, where cold, clear water emerges below the mountain and immediately becomes part of California’s larger geography.

The park is easy to visit and works as a quiet pause between hikes, drives, and town errands. Come for the spring water, shade, picnic space, and the odd feeling that a major river begins in such an approachable corner of town.
