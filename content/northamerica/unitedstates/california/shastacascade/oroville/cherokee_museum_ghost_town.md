Cherokee Museum and Ghost Town sits in the old mining country above Oroville, where the Gold Rush left scattered settlements, hydraulic mining scars, and stories that are easy to miss from the valley floor. The museum and remaining town traces give a small but tangible link to that rougher 19th-century landscape.

It is a worthwhile detour if you are already exploring Oroville Dam, Table Mountain, or the Feather River area by car. Do not expect a polished theme-park ghost town; the appeal is quieter, with local history, remnants, and the sense of a once-busy place that the main road passed by.
