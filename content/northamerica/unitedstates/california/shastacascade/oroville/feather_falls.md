Feather Falls is the big waterfall hike in the hills east of Oroville, reached by a forest trail that leads to a viewpoint over a high, plunging cascade. The outing is long enough to feel earned, with canyon scenery and Sierra foothill forest along the way rather than a simple roadside overlook.

Allow several hours, carry water, and check current trail conditions before setting out, especially after storms or fire-related closures. For travellers using Oroville as a base, it is one of the strongest reasons to leave town early and spend the day outside.
