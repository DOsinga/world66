The Mother Orange Tree is a small sight with an outsized California story. Planted in the Gold Rush era, it became a symbol of how citrus could survive far north of the state's better-known orange country.

Do not expect spectacle; this is a historic tree, not a park full of attractions. It works best as a quick stop for travelers interested in Oroville's layered history, from mining and irrigation to the agricultural experiments that changed inland California.
