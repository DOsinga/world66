Quincy is a small mountain town in Plumas County in the northern Sierra Nevada, surrounded by some of California's most pristine wilderness. The county has more than 50 lakes and nearly 1,000 miles of streams, and the Pacific Crest Trail passes through the [Bucks Lake Wilderness](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/quincy/bucks_lake_wilderness) just to the south.

The [Plumas County Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/quincy/plumas_county_museum) (500 Jackson St) covers the region's logging, mining, and Maidu Indian history — a good introduction to the area before heading into the backcountry. [Lake Almanor](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/quincy/lake_almanor), about 30 miles southeast, offers fishing, swimming, and camping in an attractive mountain setting with views of Lassen Peak.

The drive east on Highway 70 through the [Feather River Canyon](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/quincy/feather_river_canyon) is one of the best in California — the gorge walls rise hundreds of feet, the river runs fast and clear, and the Union Pacific railroad tracks wind along the opposite bank through tunnels and across trestles. Even if you have no other agenda in Quincy, this drive is worth the trip.
