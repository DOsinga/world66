A popular breakfast and lunch spot in central Quincy. Morning Thunder does the kind of reliable diner food that sustains hikers, fishermen, and locals alike — large omelettes, good coffee, proper pancakes. It gets busy on summer weekends and often has Pacific Crest Trail thru-hikers resupplying at the nearby post office stopping in for a hot meal.

Opens early, closes mid-afternoon. Cash is king at many places in Quincy; check before you sit down.
