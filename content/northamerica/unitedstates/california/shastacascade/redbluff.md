Red Bluff sits on the Sacramento River at the southern edge of the Shasta Cascade region, about two hours north of Sacramento on I-5. It's a quiet agricultural town with a surprisingly intact [Victorian downtown](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/redbluff/historic_downtown) and easy access to the outdoors. The Sacramento River here offers good fishing, and the surrounding hills are good for hiking and cycling.

The town is worth a stop if you're driving north toward Redding or Lassen Volcanic National Park, with the [William B. Ide Adobe](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/redbluff/william_b_ide_adobe) and the [Kelly-Griggs House Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/shastacascade/redbluff/kelly_griggs_house) making for an easy half-day in town. Summers are very hot — regularly topping 100°F — so spring and fall are better times to visit.
