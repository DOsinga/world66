Pilgrim Congregational Church is a small but notable Redding landmark designed by Frank Lloyd Wright. It is away from the main visitor circuit, but architecture fans may want to make the detour for its angular form and unusual church design.

The building is best treated as a short stop rather than a long visit. Check ahead if you hope to see the interior, since it remains an active congregation rather than a museum.
