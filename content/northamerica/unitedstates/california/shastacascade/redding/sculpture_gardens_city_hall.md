Redding's City Hall grounds double as a small outdoor sculpture garden, with contemporary works placed among lawns, trees, and walkways near the civic centre. It is not a destination on the scale of Turtle Bay or the Sundial Bridge, but it gives downtown Redding a public-art stop that is easy to fold into a wider walk.

The garden is best treated as a short, free visit. Stop by in daylight, look for the pieces around the building and landscaped grounds, then continue toward the river trail or downtown. For travellers with a taste for civic art, it adds a pleasant local note to a city better known for bridges, water, and mountain access.
