Shasta State Historic Park preserves the brick ruins of Old Shasta, once a busy Gold Rush supply town on the road into the northern mines. Interpretive signs line the old townsite, and the restored county courthouse has museum displays on the settlement's short, intense boom.

It is an easy side trip from Redding and a good reminder that the Shasta Cascade was shaped as much by mining roads and failed towns as by mountains and rivers.
