A classic Western saloon on Main Street, serving cold beer and straightforward drinks to a mix of locals and visitors. The interior has the worn, comfortable feel of a bar that has been in continuous operation for a long time — pool table, neon signs, and regulars who know each other. A good place to ask about trail conditions on the Bizz Johnson or find out where the fishing has been running on Eagle Lake.
