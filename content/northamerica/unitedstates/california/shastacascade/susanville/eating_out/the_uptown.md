A downtown diner-style cafe on Main Street, the Uptown is the kind of place that has been feeding locals and passing travellers for decades. Breakfast and lunch are the strong suits — eggs, pancakes, burgers, sandwiches — served in a no-frills interior with quick turnover. It is a good stop before heading out on the Bizz Johnson Trail or the drive toward Lassen.
