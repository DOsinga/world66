---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Weaverville is not easy to reach, which is part of its character. There is no rail service and no scheduled bus service. A car is essentially required.

**By car from Redding**: The most common approach is Highway 299 west from Redding, about 45 miles and an hour's drive. The road is scenic but winding through the mountains; allow extra time in poor weather.

**By car from the coast**: From Eureka, Highway 299 runs east through Arcata and then inland to Weaverville — about 110 miles and two to two-and-a-half hours.

**By air**: The nearest commercial airport is **Redding Municipal Airport (RDD)**, served by United Express with connections to San Francisco and Los Angeles. From there it's the hour's drive on 299. San Francisco International (SFO) is a more flexible option if you're flying in from further away; the drive from SFO is about four hours, mostly on I-5 north and then 299 west from Redding.

Weaverville Airport (FAA: O54) is a small general aviation field with no scheduled service, used by private pilots and emergency medical flights.
