The most dependable restaurant in town and a genuine local institution. The breakfast is the main draw — generous portions, good coffee, and the kind of unhurried service that suits a mountain-town morning. Lunch is solid too; the menu leans toward American diner standards executed well rather than ambitious. Expect to share a table or wait during weekend mornings when hikers and kayakers are passing through. On Main Street; get there early if you want a seat.
