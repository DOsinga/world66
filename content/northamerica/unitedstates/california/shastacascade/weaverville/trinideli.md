A deli and sandwich shop that makes a good lunch stop before heading into the wilderness. The sandwiches are generously constructed and the ingredients are fresh; order one to take on the trail or eat at the small tables inside. It also stocks a reasonable selection of supplies — snacks, drinks, basic provisions — useful if you're heading somewhere that the supermarket's bulk section doesn't cover.
