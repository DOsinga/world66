East Jesus is a large off-grid art compound where broken machines, bottles, televisions, car parts, and desert junk have been turned into dense, funny, sometimes unsettling installations. The name is slang for the middle of nowhere, not a religious reference.

The site is run as a living art project, with volunteers, residents, and visiting artists adding to the work. Take a docent tour if one is offered; it gives the place more shape than simply walking through the sculpture garden with a camera.
