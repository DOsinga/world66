Salvation Mountain is the bright painted hillside that announces Slab City before you reach the settlement itself. Leonard Knight built it from adobe clay, straw, found objects, and layer after layer of paint, covering the mound with biblical phrases, flowers, hearts, and the repeated message "God is Love."

It is easy to treat the place as a photo stop, but it is more interesting as a decades-long act of folk devotion. Walk the approved paths, avoid climbing on closed areas, and leave a donation if volunteers are present.
