Belly Up Tavern is the rare small music club that has become a regional institution. It opened on Cedros Avenue in 1974 and still works as an intimate room for touring acts, local favourites, comedy, and the occasional artist who could fill a much larger venue but suits the low ceiling and close crowd here.

The point is the calendar, not the architecture. If there is a show that fits your taste, Belly Up gives Solana Beach an after-dark reason to linger beyond the beach and lagoon. Buy ahead for stronger names; the room is small enough that popular nights sell out.
