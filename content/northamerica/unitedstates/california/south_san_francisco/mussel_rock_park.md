Mussel Rock Park is a rugged coastal park on the bluffs south of San Francisco. Trails cross hilly open ground above the Pacific, with beach access, ocean views, and occasional whale watching.

The park feels surprisingly wild for such a built-up stretch of coast. Wear shoes with grip, stay back from unstable cliff edges, and expect wind even on clear days.
