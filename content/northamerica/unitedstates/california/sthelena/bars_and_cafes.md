---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

St. Helena's bar and cafe scene is small — this is not a late-night town — but the quality is high. The Main Street wine tasting rooms double as afternoon drinking spots, and several of the better restaurants have bar seats open for walk-ins when the dining room is booked solid.

For coffee, **Napa Valley Coffee Roasting Company** on Main Street is the town's main cafe, opening early and filling with locals before the visitors arrive. For wine by the glass in a low-key setting, the tasting room at **Charter Oak** operates as a neighbourhood wine bar in the evenings. The bar at **Press** on St. Helena Highway is the closest thing to a classic steakhouse bar in the valley, with a staggering Napa wine list and well-made cocktails.
