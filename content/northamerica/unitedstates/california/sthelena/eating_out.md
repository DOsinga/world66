---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

St. Helena holds an unusual concentration of serious restaurants for a town of 6,000. The valley's tradition of California-cuisine cooking — local produce, Mediterranean influences, careful sourcing — is on full display here. Reservations weeks ahead are standard at the better places, especially on weekends.

Notable kitchens in and around town include **The Restaurant at Charlie Palmer at the Harvest Inn**, **Cook St. Helena** for casual Italian, **Press** for steaks and an exhaustive Napa wine list, and the **Gatehouse Restaurant at the Culinary Institute** for student-cooked menus at lower prices. For a picnic, the deli at **V. Sattui Winery** or the **Sunshine Foods** grocer on Main both work well.
