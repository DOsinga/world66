---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The reason to come to St. Helena is wine. The town sits at the geographic and gastronomic centre of the Napa Valley, with several hundred tasting rooms within a fifteen-minute drive. The historic estates of **Charles Krug**, **Beringer**, and **Spottswoode** are within town limits or just outside; the **Culinary Institute of America at Greystone**, housed in a 19th-century stone winery, anchors the food side of the valley's reputation.

The town itself rewards an hour or two of walking. Main Street — a stretch of about eight blocks — has no chain businesses, only independent restaurants, wineries' tasting salons, the small **Robert Louis Stevenson Silverado Museum**, and a few good shops. The handsome stone **Pope Street Bridge** at the north end of town and the 1868 **Saint Helena Catholic Church** mark the older fabric.
