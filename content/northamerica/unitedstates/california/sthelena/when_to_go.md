---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Napa Valley is good to visit year-round, but the experience changes sharply with the season. **Spring (March–May)** brings green hillsides, wildflowers, mustard in the vineyard rows, and mild weather — many visitors' favourite time. **Summer (June–August)** is hot, often over 35°C in the afternoon, but mornings and evenings are pleasant and the long days are good for outdoor tastings. **Harvest (September–October)** is the busiest and most expensive season — vineyards are working, tasting rooms are full, hotel prices peak, and many wineries hold special events. **Winter (November–February)** is the quietest time, with cool damp weather, frequent rain in December and January, and the best chance to actually talk to a winemaker over a tasting.

A few practical notes. Avoid driving from San Francisco to Napa on Friday afternoons between 2pm and 7pm — traffic on Highway 29 and the Silverado Trail can add an hour or more. Many wineries close one day a week, usually Tuesday. The last two weeks of December and the first two of January see a wave of seasonal closures.
