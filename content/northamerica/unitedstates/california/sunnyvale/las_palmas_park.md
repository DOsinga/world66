A pleasant city park south of the centre, with a creek running through, mature oaks and redwoods, picnic tables, and a tennis complex. The Sunnyvale Tennis Center attached to the park has 16 courts and hosts the annual senior tennis tournament, the longest-running of its kind on the West Coast.

For a quick walk while based in central Sunnyvale, this is the best green space within walking distance. The disc golf course at the back of the park is a low-key favourite with locals.
