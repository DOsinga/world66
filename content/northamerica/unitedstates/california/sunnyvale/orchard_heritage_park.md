---

A working ten-acre orchard of around 800 apricot trees in a public park, preserved as a memorial to the Santa Clara Valley's century of fruit-growing. The trees are pruned and harvested each year; in March the orchard explodes into pale-pink blossom, in late June the apricots ripen and are picked and sold cheaply from the small farm shop.

The park is open dawn to dusk year-round; the picking weekends in late June are the best time to visit. The neighbouring Sunnyvale Heritage Park Museum, in the old Carnegie library building, fills in the history of the orchard era.
