Tahoe City is a small lakeside community on the northwestern shore of Lake Tahoe, the main hub for services and accommodation on the California side of the lake's north shore. The [Fanny Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/tahoe_city/fanny_bridge) over the Truckee River outlet is a popular stop for watching large trout holding in the current below. In winter, [Palisades Tahoe](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/tahoe_city/palisades_tahoe) (formerly Squaw Valley) and Alpine Meadows ski resorts are a short drive away.

In summer the town is a base for hiking, mountain biking, kayaking, and sailing on the lake. [Commons Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/tahoe_city/commons_beach) in the town centre has a small sandy beach with views across the famously clear blue water, and the paved [Truckee River Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/tahoe_city/truckee_river_trail) runs along the river through pine forest. The [Gatekeeper's Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/tahoe_city/gatekeepers_museum) at the lake outlet holds an outstanding collection of Washoe and Maidu basketry. Tahoe City has all the practical infrastructure — restaurants, rental outfitters, gear shops — and is the most convenient starting point for exploring the north shore.
