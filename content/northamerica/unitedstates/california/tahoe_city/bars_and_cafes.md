---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

After-ski and after-paddle drinking in Tahoe City revolves around a small handful of places. Bridgetender Tavern by Fanny Bridge has been the dive of choice for locals for decades — burgers, beer, and a riverside deck. Jake's on the Lake has a more polished lakefront cocktail bar. For coffee, Syd's Bagelry doubles as the town's morning meeting point.
