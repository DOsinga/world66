The small log museum sits at the lake outlet right beside the Fanny Bridge. It covers the early days of Tahoe — the loggers, dam keepers, and tourist resorts of the late 19th and early 20th centuries — and houses the outstanding Marion Steinbach Indian Basket Museum, with hundreds of Washoe, Maidu, and Pomo baskets.

The Washoe people summered around Lake Tahoe for thousands of years before being pushed out by 19th-century settlers. The collection is one of the best of its kind in the West and worth a deliberate visit, not just a passing look.
