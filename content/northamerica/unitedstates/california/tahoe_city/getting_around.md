---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Tahoe City itself is walkable — the main street, the beach, the Fanny Bridge, and the marinas are all within a few blocks. A car is useful for getting to the ski resorts and to the more distant lakefront state parks.

**TART** (Tahoe Area Regional Transit) buses run year-round around the north shore: Tahoe City to Truckee, to Kings Beach, and seasonally to the ski resorts. The free Night Rider runs evenings in summer. Schedules thin out in shoulder season.

**Cycling** is the best way to see the immediate area in summer — the Truckee River Trail and the West Shore bike path are paved, scenic, and avoid the highway traffic. Several rental shops in town will deliver bikes to your accommodation.

**Driving** is straightforward but parking in town fills up on summer weekends and holidays. Use the free public lots near the Y intersection and walk in.
