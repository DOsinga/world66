Torrance is a city in the South Bay region of Los Angeles County, sitting between the beach cities of Redondo Beach and Hermosa Beach. It is primarily a residential and industrial city rather than a tourist destination, but it has a pleasant [downtown area](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/torrance/old_torrance) and is home to the largest Honda America headquarters outside Japan, reflecting the large Japanese-American community in the South Bay.

Torrance has good ramen and Japanese food — the [Mitsuwa Marketplace](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/torrance/mitsuwa_marketplace) on Artesia Boulevard is one of the best Japanese supermarkets and food courts in Southern California, worth a stop for lunch or grocery shopping. The beach at [Torrance Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/torrance/torrance_beach) is accessible and less crowded than the beaches further north. For most visitors, Torrance functions as an affordable base within reach of the South Bay beaches and Los Angeles attractions.
