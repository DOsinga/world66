---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Torrance sits at the south end of the LA basin, accessible from the 405 (San Diego) freeway, the 110 (Harbor) freeway, and the Pacific Coast Highway. **LAX airport** is 15–25 minutes north depending on traffic, **Long Beach Airport** is a similar drive east. The city has no rail or subway service of its own.

The Torrance Transit bus system operates local routes within the city; the LA Metro 232 bus runs the length of Hawthorne Boulevard between the LAX area and Long Beach. Realistically, almost everyone visits Torrance by car.
