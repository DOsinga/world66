The Mare Island San Pablo Bay Trail follows the quiet western edge of Mare Island through wetlands and open bay views. It is a flat, easy walk, with birds, marsh plants, and a very different mood from the naval shipyard history on the island's eastern side.

Bring water and sun protection, because the route is exposed and services are limited. The reward is space: broad views across San Pablo Bay and a peaceful break from Vallejo's busier waterfront.
