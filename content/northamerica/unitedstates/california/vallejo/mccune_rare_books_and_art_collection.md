The McCune Rare Books and Art Collection is a specialist stop for visitors interested in printing, illustration, and book arts. The collection includes hundreds of rare books, among them early printed volumes, a leaf from the Gutenberg Bible, the Kelmscott Chaucer, and botanical portfolios by Henry Evans.

It is not a broad city museum, but the material is strong enough to justify a short visit if you are already in Vallejo.
