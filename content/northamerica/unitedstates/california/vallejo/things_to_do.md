title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Vallejo's main attractions are spread between the waterfront and Mare Island. [Six Flags Discovery Kingdom](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/vallejo/six_flags) draws the biggest crowds, but the real discovery is [Mare Island Naval Shipyard](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/vallejo/mare_island) — 142 years of Navy history, with officer's mansions, dry docks, and a chapel full of Tiffany glass. The downtown arts district around the [Empress Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/vallejo/empress_theatre) gives a sense of the city's ongoing reinvention.
