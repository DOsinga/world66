---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

West Hollywood has two distinct bar scenes that rarely overlap. Santa Monica Boulevard between La Cienega and Doheny is the heart of LA's gay nightlife, anchored by The Abbey and a cluster of bars running west toward Robertson. The Sunset Strip is different — louder, heavier, historically tied to rock music — with Barney's Beanery as the long-running dive at the eastern end and the Rainbow Bar and Grill still drawing musicians at the western. The comedy clubs (The Comedy Store, the Laugh Factory) are technically on Sunset but have their own culture entirely.
