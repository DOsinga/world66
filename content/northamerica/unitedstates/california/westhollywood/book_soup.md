Book Soup at 8818 Sunset Boulevard has been the best independent bookshop in greater Los Angeles since 1975. It specialises in film, art, photography, music, and literature — the books that the entertainment industry actually reads — and stocks an unusually wide selection of small-press and international titles alongside the standard backlist.

The shop is narrow and dense, with shelves running floor to ceiling and a basement section for remainders. Signings and readings happen regularly; the event calendar draws authors who wouldn't typically stop in Los Angeles on a book tour. Open late most nights.
