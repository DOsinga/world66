---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

West Hollywood is one of the few parts of greater Los Angeles where you can walk comfortably between destinations. The city is only 1.9 square miles, the streets are flat and laid out on a grid, and the main strips — Sunset, Santa Monica, Melrose — are each a manageable ten to fifteen minutes' walk apart. Most visitors based in WeHo can reach any bar, restaurant or venue on foot.

For getting into the rest of LA, the Metro's B Line (Red Line) at Hollywood/Highland or Hollywood/Vine is the most practical connection — those stations are a short ride-share east on Sunset, and the B Line reaches downtown, Universal Studios, and North Hollywood. The D Line (Purple Line) at Wilshire/La Cienega on the southern border gives access to the Miracle Mile museums.

Driving and parking in WeHo is frustrating. Most streets have metered parking that fills by evening; paid lots are scattered near Santa Monica Boulevard. Ride-shares are very heavily used — most locals default to them for any trip outside walking distance.

Several bike-share docks (Metro Bike Share) are dotted through the city and work well for rides along the flat east–west corridors.
