Plummer Park is the largest park in West Hollywood, on the eastern edge of the city near Gardner Street. It has a community garden, a small pond, picnic areas, and two historic buildings — Rooms 1 and 2 — that date from the 1930s WPA era and are used for community events. The park is a gathering place for the city's large Russian-speaking population, and on weekdays you'll often find older residents playing chess and backgammon in the shade.

More central and walkable than any other green space in WeHo; worth visiting if you're in the neighbourhood or want somewhere quiet to sit outside.
