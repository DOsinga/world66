---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Melrose Avenue and the blocks around Beverly Boulevard make up the West Hollywood Design District, where you'll find mid-century furniture showrooms, art galleries, and boutiques alongside the kind of streetwear shops that have always orbited the Sunset Strip. Book Soup on Sunset is the best independent bookshop in greater LA — the kind of shop that stocks obscure film histories, LA-set crime novels, and photography monographs you won't find anywhere else. The Pacific Design Center's three buildings are the landmark of the district, with showrooms and galleries inside open to the public.
