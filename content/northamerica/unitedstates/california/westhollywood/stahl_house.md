The Stahl House is one of Los Angeles's defining modernist houses: a glass-and-steel Case Study House set high above the city. Even if the name is unfamiliar, its silhouette and night views over the basin have appeared in countless photographs, films, and design books.

Tours are limited and need advance booking, but architecture fans should make the effort. The house is as much about the view and the hillside setting as the building itself.
