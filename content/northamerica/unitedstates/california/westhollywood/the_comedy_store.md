The Comedy Store at 8433 Sunset has been the central institution of American stand-up comedy since Mitzi Shore took it over in 1972. Richard Pryor refined his material here. Robin Williams, David Letterman, and Jim Carrey all cut their teeth on its stage. The building's exterior — black facade, the name in white letters — is one of the most recognisable on the Strip.

There are three rooms: the Main Room for headliners, the Belly Room for newer acts, and the Original Room for open-mic nights. Shows run most nights of the week. The bar downstairs is open late and has an industry crowd after shows. Book tickets online; walk-ins are possible for smaller rooms but never guaranteed.
