---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

West Hollywood's compact 1.9 square miles pack in an extraordinary range of things to see and do. The Sunset Strip is the anchor — a stretch of Sunset Boulevard lined with legendary music venues, a few still-going from the rock-and-roll 1960s and 1970s. Elsewhere, the Pacific Design Center anchors a design district, the Schindler House is one of the most important pieces of modernist architecture in California, and Plummer Park offers a rare patch of green on the eastern edge of the city.
