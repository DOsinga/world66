---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

West Hollywood has a Mediterranean climate — warm, dry summers and mild winters — and the outdoor-facing bar and restaurant scene means there's no bad time of year. Two events are worth timing a trip around.

**LA Pride** takes place on Santa Monica Boulevard in June. The parade runs east from Doheny on a Sunday morning, and the festival fills the street and adjacent blocks for the entire weekend. It is one of the largest Pride events in the US and has particular resonance in a city that was built partly around its LGBT community.

**Halloween Carnaval** on 31 October is West Hollywood's biggest single event — a free street party on Santa Monica Boulevard that draws around 500,000 people. Costumes range from elaborate handmade constructions to almost nothing at all. The city takes it seriously: barricades go up by mid-afternoon, and the street closes to traffic from Doheny to La Cienega. Arrive by early evening for the best of it.

For the Sunset Strip music venues, there is no particular season — shows happen year-round and tickets are cheap. Summer weekends can be very busy.
