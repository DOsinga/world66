One or two local bars in the downtown area are frequented by agricultural workers and long-time residents. No-frills, local, and friendly — the kind of places where strangers are noticed but welcomed. Don't come expecting cocktails or craft beer; do come if you want a cold drink and a glimpse of small-town Central Valley life.
