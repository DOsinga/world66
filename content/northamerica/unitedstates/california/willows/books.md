---

**Cadillac Desert** by Marc Reisner is the definitive account of water and agriculture in the American West — essential reading for understanding the landscape you're driving through, including the irrigation systems and water politics that made the Sacramento Valley into rice country.

**The Mountains of California** by John Muir is classic California nature writing. Muir spent much of his life in this state and his observations on the natural world remain vivid and relevant.

A **Pacific Flyway waterfowl guide** or California birding field guide is invaluable if you're visiting the Sacramento National Wildlife Refuge. The refuge hosts dozens of species and a good guide will help you identify the geese, ducks, and waders in the fields and wetlands.

A **Sacramento Valley history** or California agriculture book will put the surrounding flatlands in context — this region was transformed by water projects and rice cultivation in the 20th century, and the story of that transformation is surprisingly rich.
