Classic California highway diners and Mexican restaurants line the I-5 frontage road and spread into downtown. Nothing remarkable, but portions are generous and prices are honest — solid fuel for the road. The Mexican restaurants in particular tend to be better than you'd expect, serving the agricultural workforce rather than passing tourists.
