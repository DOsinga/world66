type: poi

The small historic downtown has a few original storefronts from the early 20th century that hint at the town's earlier ambitions as a Glenn County centre. The Glenn County Courthouse (1918) is an attractive building worth a look. It's a quiet stop for those with an interest in rural California history, though expectations should be modest — this is a working agricultural town, not a preserved heritage district.
