---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Aurora's pleasures are dispersed — this is a suburb of more than 390,000 people spread across some 400 square kilometres — so plan your visits geographically rather than trying to walk between them. The Stanley Marketplace and the Anschutz medical campus cluster on the city's north-western edge; Cherry Creek State Park and the Aurora Reservoir take up the south. The big draws on Colfax and Havana are eating, not sightseeing.
