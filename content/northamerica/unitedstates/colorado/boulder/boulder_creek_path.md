Boulder Creek Path is the city's most useful linear park, following the creek through the middle of Boulder and tying together the university, downtown, parks, and the canyon mouth. It works as both transport and pleasure: locals commute on it, runners use it daily, and visitors can use it to understand the city without sitting in traffic.

The path is best in the morning or late afternoon, when the creek shade and mountain backdrop do their work. Rent a bike or walk a central stretch, then detour into Pearl Street, the CU campus, or the canyon depending on how energetic you feel.
