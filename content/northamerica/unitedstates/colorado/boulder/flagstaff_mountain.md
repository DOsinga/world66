Flagstaff Mountain rises immediately west of Boulder, close enough to feel like part of the city but high enough to change the perspective completely. Trails, overlooks, and picnic areas look back over the university, downtown, and the plains beyond.

It is one of the easiest ways to sample Boulder's mountain edge without committing to a long hike. Drivers can reach several viewpoints, while walkers can link paths from Chautauqua and the lower foothills, making it flexible for sunset views, short rambles, or a first look at the Front Range.
