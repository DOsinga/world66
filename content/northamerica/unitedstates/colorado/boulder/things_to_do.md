Boulder's outdoor offerings are its greatest draw. [Chautauqua Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/boulder/chautauqua_park) at the foot of the Flatirons gives access to the most photographed hiking terrain in Colorado; the Flatirons themselves draw rock climbers from across the country. The surrounding Open Space and Mountain Parks system adds hundreds of kilometres of trails.

In town, [Pearl Street Mall](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/boulder/pearl_street_mall) is the four-block pedestrian heart of the city — street performers, good cafes and restaurants, and an architecture that mixes Victorian commercial buildings with the mountains visible at the end of every street. The [Dushanbe Teahouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/boulder/dushanbe_teahouse) is an extraordinary piece of Tajik craftsmanship, gifted to Boulder by its sister city. The [University of Colorado Museum of Natural History](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/boulder/cu_museum_natural_history) on campus has a good collection of paleontology and anthropology. The [Fox Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/boulder/fox_theatre) is the city's main live music venue.
