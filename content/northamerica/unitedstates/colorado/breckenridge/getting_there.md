---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Breckenridge is about 80 miles west of Denver via I-70 and Colorado 9. Winter traffic from Denver can be brutal on Fridays, Sundays, and powder mornings, so build in slack. Shuttles run from Denver International Airport, and Summit County's free bus system links Breckenridge with Frisco, Dillon, Keystone, and Silverthorne.
