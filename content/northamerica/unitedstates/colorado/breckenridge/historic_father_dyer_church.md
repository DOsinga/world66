Historic Father Dyer Church dates from 1880 and is one of Breckenridge's small but meaningful historic buildings. It is named for Father John Lewis Dyer, the Methodist circuit rider remembered as the "snowshoe itinerant" for crossing the Rockies to serve mining camps.

The church is still active, but visitors can usually step in quietly outside services. Look for the stained-glass window showing Father Dyer skiing, a local detail that connects the church to Breckenridge's mountain identity.
