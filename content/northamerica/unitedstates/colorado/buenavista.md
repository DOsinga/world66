Buena Vista sits in the heart of Colorado's Collegiate Peaks, surrounded by some of the state's highest fourteeners and the San Isabel National Forest. Originally a brawling mining supply town — it had 68 bars before its first church was built in 1879 — it has settled into a picturesque base for outdoor pursuits. The [Arkansas River](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/buenavista/arkansas_river_rafting) running through the valley offers some of Colorado's best whitewater rafting.

The historic hot springs, sacred to the Ute people for centuries, remain a draw — [Mount Princeton Hot Springs](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/buenavista/mount_princeton_hot_springs) is the best known. Hiking and mountain biking trails radiate into the surrounding mountains, and the town itself has the [Buena Vista Heritage Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/buenavista/buena_vista_heritage_museum) in the old courthouse. The [Collegiate Peaks Scenic Byway](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/buenavista/collegiate_peaks_scenic_byway) frames the valley's lineup of fourteeners, and [Brown's Canyon National Monument](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/buenavista/browns_canyon_national_monument) south of town protects some of the finest canyon scenery in the Arkansas River Valley. [Leadville](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/leadville), another old mining town, is a short drive north.
