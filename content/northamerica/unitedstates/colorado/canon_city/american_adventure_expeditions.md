American Adventure Expeditions is one of the Canon City outfitters using the Arkansas River, with trips that can include the Royal Gorge or the gentler Bighorn Sheep Canyon depending on season, water level, and ability. Rafting is one of the strongest reasons to base yourself here rather than simply drive through.

Choose the trip honestly: the Royal Gorge can be serious whitewater, while family runs are designed for a different day entirely. Book with licensed guides, listen to safety instructions, and expect spring snowmelt to change the river's personality.
