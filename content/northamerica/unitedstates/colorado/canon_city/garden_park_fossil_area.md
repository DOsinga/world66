The Garden Park Fossil Area, twenty kilometres north of Cañon City along Field Avenue, is one of the most productive dinosaur fossil sites in North America. The famous Marsh-Cope "bone wars" of the 1870s tore through this hillside, sending crate after crate of complete skeletons east to Washington and New York.

Today the area is managed by the Bureau of Land Management. A self-guided interpretive trail leads to several active and historic quarry sites; the Cleveland-Lloyd Quarry is sometimes worked by visiting palaeontologists in summer. The Royal Gorge Regional Museum in town holds a small collection of finds from the area.
