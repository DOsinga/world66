The Abbey of the Holy Cross was a Benedictine monastery founded in 1886, and for over a century it ran a boys' school and one of the country's oldest small wineries. The monastic community dissolved in 2006, but the dramatic red-brick Gothic Revival abbey church and the school buildings remain on the east edge of town.

The Winery at Holy Cross Abbey continues independently as a working winery on the abbey grounds, with a tasting room open most afternoons and a small museum on the abbey's history. The grounds are open to walk, and the church can sometimes be entered when there are events on. Worth ten minutes if you're heading out of town on the 50.
