The Royal Gorge Route Railroad runs scenic excursion trains along the Arkansas River through the bottom of the Royal Gorge — directly below the suspension bridge, deep within the canyon walls. The two-hour round trip from Cañon City covers around 38 kilometres on standard-gauge track originally laid in 1880.

Several classes of service are offered, from open-air coaches to dome cars and a more elaborate dinner train. The track squeezes through the famous Hanging Bridge at the canyon's narrowest point — only nine metres wide — where the rail bed had to be suspended from the cliff above. The light in the gorge is best in the morning.
