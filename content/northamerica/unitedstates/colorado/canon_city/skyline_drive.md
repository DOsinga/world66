Skyline Drive is a three-mile, one-way road that runs along the spine of a thin limestone ridge above western Cañon City. The ridge is barely wider than the car: a near-vertical drop on each side gives the impression of driving along a knife edge with the town and the Arkansas valley spread out below.

The drive takes about fifteen minutes. There are no guardrails, no pullouts, and the road is closed in winter. About halfway along, fossilised Cretaceous dinosaur footprints can be seen embedded in the limestone at the road edge — three-toed Acrocanthosaurus tracks among them. The viewpoint at the south end overlooks the entire Royal Gorge area.
