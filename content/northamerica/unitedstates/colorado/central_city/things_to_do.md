---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Central City is small enough to walk in an hour. Beyond the gambling halls, the real draws are the Victorian streetscape, the surprisingly serious summer opera season at the historic Opera House, the surrounding ghost towns, and several working gold-mine tours within a short drive. The town's most interesting attractions sit on the National Register-listed Main Street and along the road up to the abandoned mining camp of Nevadaville.
