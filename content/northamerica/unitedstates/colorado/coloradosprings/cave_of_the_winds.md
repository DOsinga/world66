Cave of the Winds sits in the foothills west of Colorado Springs, close enough to combine with Manitou Springs or Garden of the Gods. Guided tours take you through narrow limestone passages, decorated chambers, and old show-cave routes that have been drawing visitors since the nineteenth century.

The modern site is also an adventure park, with cliff-edge rides, climbing features, and lookout points over Williams Canyon. Choose the basic cave tour if you want geology and history; the wilder lantern-style and crawling routes are better if you are comfortable in tight spaces.
