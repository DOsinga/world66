Helen Hunt Falls is an easily reached waterfall in North Cheyenne Cañon Park, where water drops through a narrow rock setting just off the road. Short trails and viewpoints make it one of Colorado Springs' most convenient mountain-nature stops.

It works well as part of a canyon drive or hike rather than as a long destination by itself. Go early or outside peak periods if you want the sound of the falls without a crowded roadside scene.
