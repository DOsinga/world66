The Manitou Springs Penny Arcade is a nostalgic sidestep from the bigger Colorado Springs sights: rows of old pinball machines, skee-ball lanes, mechanical games, and newer cabinets scattered through a cluster of arcade rooms. It feels informal and slightly chaotic in the right way.

It is best treated as part of a Manitou Springs wander, not as a stand-alone destination. Stop in after Garden of the Gods or the mineral springs, bring small bills or cards depending on current machines, and enjoy the rare attraction where the point is still to play for a while.
