Old Colorado City is a historic district along West Colorado Avenue on the western edge of the city, originally incorporated as a separate town in 1859 and briefly serving as the first capital of Colorado Territory before being absorbed by Colorado Springs in 1917. The strip of Victorian commercial buildings now houses an eclectic mix of art galleries, independent restaurants, antique shops, and outdoor gear stores.

It is the most characterful street in Colorado Springs for an evening out or an afternoon browse. The area sits at the foot of the foothills, with Garden of the Gods a ten-minute walk north and Manitou Springs another mile or so west along the same road. A good base of operations for exploring the mountain parks without driving back downtown.
