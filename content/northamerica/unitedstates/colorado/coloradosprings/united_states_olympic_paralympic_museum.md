The United States Olympic & Paralympic Museum is one of Colorado Springs' signature modern attractions, fitting the city's role as a hub for U.S. Olympic training and administration. Interactive exhibits cover athletes, ceremonies, equipment, design, and the history of Olympic and Paralympic competition.

The museum works for sports fans, families, and design-minded visitors because it is more immersive than a trophy hall. Its downtown location makes it easy to combine with the rest of Colorado Springs before heading toward the mountain sights.
