The U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Training Center is one of Colorado Springs' most distinctive sports-related stops. Tours introduce the campus where elite athletes train, with exhibits and stories that put Olympic and Paralympic preparation into a more tangible setting than television ever can.

It is especially good for sports fans and families, but even casual visitors get a clearer sense of why Colorado Springs has such a strong Olympic identity. Check tour availability in advance, since access is structured rather than self-guided roaming.
