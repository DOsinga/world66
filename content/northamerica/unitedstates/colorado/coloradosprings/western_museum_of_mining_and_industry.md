The Western Museum of Mining and Industry is a strong fit for Colorado Springs because it explains the extractive economy that shaped much of the region. Its collection includes mining machines, steam engines, ore-processing equipment, and demonstrations that make the technology physical rather than abstract.

The museum sits well north of downtown, so it works best if you have a car or are already heading toward the Air Force Academy. For families and industrial-history fans it is worth the detour, especially because many exhibits are designed to move, rattle, crush, and show how hard-rock mining actually worked.
