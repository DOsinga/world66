The Cortez Cultural Center is a free museum and community space in the centre of town, focused on the archaeology and cultures of the Four Corners region. The exhibits cover Ancestral Puebloan pottery, basketry, and stone tools from sites across the Colorado Plateau, with clear explanations of the culture, timeline, and eventual dispersal of the cliff-dwelling communities.

The museum is modest in scale but well done — a useful primer before heading to Mesa Verde or the Anasazi Heritage Center. In summer, it hosts free Native American dance performances on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday evenings between June and September. The gift shop carries authentic Native American art and jewellery at fair prices. Admission is free; donations are welcomed.
