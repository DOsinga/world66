---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late spring and early autumn are the best times. May and September offer comfortable temperatures (15–25°C), clear skies, and manageable crowds at Mesa Verde. Summer (June–August) is peak season — cliff dwelling tours at Mesa Verde require advance booking and the park can be congested — but the weather is reliably sunny and the days are long.

The monsoon season runs from mid-July to mid-September, bringing afternoon thunderstorms that build rapidly and clear quickly. These are rarely dangerous but can make canyon hiking unpredictable; start early and be off exposed ridges by noon.

Winter is quiet and cold. Mesa Verde stays open year-round but the Cliff Palace and Balcony House tours only run from late spring to early autumn. The area receives occasional snow, which makes the landscape beautiful but the roads icy. For archaeology without crowds, a weekday visit in October or early November is hard to beat.
