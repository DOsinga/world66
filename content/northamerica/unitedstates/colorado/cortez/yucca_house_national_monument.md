Yucca House National Monument protects the remains of an Ancestral Puebloan community southwest of Cortez. The site was occupied roughly between 1150 and 1300 and is largely undeveloped, with no visitor center or reconstructed buildings.

That low-key character is part of the appeal: it is a quiet archaeological stop for travelers already exploring Cortez, Mesa Verde, and the wider Four Corners region.
