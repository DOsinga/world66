Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument protects one of Colorado's stranger landscapes: ancient redwood stumps and delicate plant and insect fossils preserved by volcanic ash. The visitor centre explains the geology well, but the short trails are the real draw, taking you past huge petrified trunks with Pikes Peak country on the horizon.
