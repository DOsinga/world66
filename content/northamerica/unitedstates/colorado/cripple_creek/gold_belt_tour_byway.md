The Gold Belt Tour is a National Scenic Byway that loops through the entire Cripple Creek gold district, connecting the town with Cañon City and Florence via three routes — two paved and one unpaved. The byway passes through landscape defined by the mining era: abandoned headframes, leached mine dumps, and the ruins of ore processing mills dot the hillsides alongside the ranches and small communities that outlasted the rush.

The unpaved section — the High Park Road — traverses remote terrain above 9,000 feet and is suitable for high-clearance vehicles in dry conditions. The paved routes are accessible to all vehicles. The full loop is around 130 miles; most visitors drive a portion of it, stopping at viewpoints and interpretive signs along the way. The byway connects naturally with a visit to the Royal Gorge and Cañon City, making a full-day excursion possible.
