story: >
  Mollie Kathleen Gortner discovered gold here in 1891 while her husband and son went ahead to stake claims elsewhere. She filed the claim herself, becoming one of the first women to do so in the Cripple Creek district. The mine she named after herself operated continuously until 1961 and is now one of the best-preserved vertical-shaft mines open to the public in Colorado.
---

The Mollie Kathleen is the most accessible underground mine experience in the Cripple Creek district. Visitors descend a thousand feet in a vertical cage — not a cable car, but the same ore hoist used by miners — to explore tunnels at three levels, where guides demonstrate original drilling equipment and explain how the ore was extracted and processed. The narrow tunnels, low ceilings, and 47-degree temperature underground are all authentic; the mine was never modified to make it more comfortable for tourists.

Above ground, the site preserves original headframe and hoist machinery. Tours run hourly in season and take about an hour. The mine is located just north of town on Highway 67; it's easy to combine with the narrow gauge railroad, whose depot is a short drive away.
