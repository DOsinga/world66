Tenderfoot Pass, at 10,200 feet, is a mountain pass northeast of Cripple Creek that offers sweeping views over the entire gold district and, on clear days, toward Pikes Peak to the east. The pass is accessible by a scenic road and makes a worthwhile detour for those with time to spare, particularly in the morning when the light falls across the old mine workings below.

The pass sits above the tree line and the terrain around it is open tundra — wildflowers in July and August, frost-burned grasses by September. The road to the pass is paved and accessible in summer; check conditions before heading up in spring or after early autumn snowfalls.
