The 32-foot-tall fibreglass mustang sculpture at the entrance to Denver International Airport — officially Blue Mustang, locally Blucifer — is one of the strangest welcomes any major American airport offers. Rearing, electric blue, with glowing red eyes that switch on at night, it has been the subject of half-serious removal petitions for two decades; just as often, fans appear in cosplay to defend it.

You'll see it on the drive in or out from the airport on Peña Boulevard; there's a small lay-by where you can pull over for a photo.
