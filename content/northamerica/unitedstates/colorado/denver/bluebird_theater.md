The Bluebird Theater is a Colfax Avenue landmark, smaller and rougher-edged than Denver's grand performing arts venues but beloved for live music. Its neon sign and former movie-house bones fit the street perfectly: part old Denver, part late-night concert corridor.

It is worth checking the calendar if you want to see how Denver goes out after dark. The room is intimate enough that even mid-level touring acts feel close, and the surrounding stretch of Colfax has plenty of bars and food before or after the show.
