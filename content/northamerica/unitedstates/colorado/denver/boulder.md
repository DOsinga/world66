Half an hour northwest of Denver up the Boulder Turnpike, Boulder is the Front Range college town with a particular Coloradan brand of outdoorsy progressivism — the kind of place where the trailhead and the espresso bar share a building. The Pearl Street Mall, a four-block pedestrian zone, is full of buskers, restaurants, and the Boulder Bookstore.

A standard day trip: hike or scramble in the Flatirons (Chautauqua trailhead is the easy start), wander Pearl Street, get a beer at Avery or Mountain Sun, and back to Denver by 6. Public buses (RTD Flatiron Flyer) make the trip easy without a car.
