The 1892 Brown Palace is Denver's grand hotel — a triangular Italian Renaissance landmark of Arizona sandstone wrapped around a soaring atrium lobby with iron filigree balconies. Every US President since Teddy Roosevelt except Calvin Coolidge has stayed at least once. The afternoon tea, served daily in the atrium under live harp, is a Denver institution; book a couple of days ahead.

The Ship Tavern off the lobby is one of the better hotel bars in the city, with maritime paneling that feels engagingly out of place a thousand miles from any ocean.
