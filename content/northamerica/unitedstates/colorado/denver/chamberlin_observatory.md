Chamberlin Observatory was built in 1890 and remains one of Denver's more unusual historic landmarks. The Romanesque-style observatory belongs to the University of Denver and is still used for astronomy programs.

The best reason to go is one of the public star parties or special events, when visitors can see the old observatory doing the work it was built for. At other times, it is mainly a handsome architectural stop on the south side of the city.
