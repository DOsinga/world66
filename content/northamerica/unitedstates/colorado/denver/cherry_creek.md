Cherry Creek, two miles southeast of downtown, is Denver's luxury retail district — the Cherry Creek Shopping Center on the south side and the smaller Cherry Creek North on the north, full of boutiques, salons, galleries, and the city's densest cluster of high-end restaurants. The neighborhood feels distinctly suburban-Californian inside what is otherwise a brick-warehouse city.

It's most useful as a destination for shopping, a long lunch (Matsuhisa, Fruition, North Italia), or a wander through commercial galleries. The Cherry Creek Trail along the creek connects all the way back to Confluence Park.
