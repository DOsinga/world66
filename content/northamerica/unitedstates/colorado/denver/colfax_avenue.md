Colfax (US Route 40) runs straight through the heart of Denver, east to west, and has been the city's main commercial spine for a century. The most interesting stretch is between the Capitol and Colorado Boulevard — Vietnamese pho joints, vintage stores, the Ogden and Bluebird theatres, dive bars, and a thick density of mid-century neon.

It's a long walk; the 15 bus runs end to end if you want to sample. Highlights along the way: the Fillmore Auditorium for major concerts, Sie Film Center for independent cinema, the Pete's chain of Greek diners, and a long string of bars that nobody is going to call gentrified.
