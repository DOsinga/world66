The Colorado Railroad Museum in Golden is the Denver area's best stop for railway history, with locomotives, cabooses, model layouts, and exhibits on the railroads that pushed mining towns, freight, and tourists into the Rockies. It is especially strong on narrow-gauge equipment, the kind that made Colorado railroading so distinctive.

Families come for the train ride days and special events; rail fans come for the rolling stock. Pair it with Golden or the Coors Brewery tour rather than treating it as an inner-Denver sight.
