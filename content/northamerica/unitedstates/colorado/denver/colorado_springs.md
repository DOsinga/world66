An hour and twenty minutes south of Denver on I-25, Colorado Springs is a worthwhile long day or overnight trip. The Garden of the Gods (free city park, dramatic red sandstone formations against the snowy Pikes Peak) is the standout natural sight; the Air Force Academy chapel is among the most striking modern church buildings in the US; the Olympic and Paralympic Museum opened in 2020 to widespread praise.

Pikes Peak itself rises to 14,115 feet just west of town. A cog railway runs to the summit in summer; you can also drive the toll road or hike the 13-mile Barr Trail. The view from the top inspired the lyrics to "America the Beautiful".
