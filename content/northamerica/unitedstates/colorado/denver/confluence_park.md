Confluence Park is the spot where the South Platte and Cherry Creek meet — the literal site where the gold prospectors of 1858 founded the city, and now a small green amphitheatre stepping down to the water. In summer it fills with people tubing or kayaking the gentle whitewater feature, and the REI flagship store (in the restored Tramway Power Building right above) makes it the natural launching point for the city's bike paths.

The Platte River Trail north and the Cherry Creek Trail south are both Denver's best bike commutes; you can cycle uninterrupted from here to Confluence, Cheesman Park, or out to the Highlands without crossing a major road.
