Coors Brewery dominates Golden, both physically and by smell: on many days the air carries grain, hops, and warm malt from the vast plant. It is one of the world's largest single-site breweries, and the public tour is the reason most travelers come.

The visit is most interesting as industrial heritage rather than craft-beer romance. You see how beer is made at a huge scale, then end with samples if you are old enough to drink.
