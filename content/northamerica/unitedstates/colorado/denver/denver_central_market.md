A 12-stall food hall in a 1929 plumbing warehouse on Larimer in RiNo, Denver Central Market gathers some of the city's best independent food vendors under one roof: Tammen's Fish Market, Curio Bar, Vero pizza, Crema coffee, the legendary High Point Creamery for ice cream. It's the easy answer to "where can a group eat in RiNo if they all want different things?"

Open all day from coffee onwards; the cocktail bar (Curio) is a fine post-mural-walk pre-dinner stop.
