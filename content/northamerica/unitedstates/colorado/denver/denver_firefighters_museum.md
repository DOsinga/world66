Denver Firefighters Museum tells the story of the city's fire service through old equipment, hands-on exhibits, and local rescue history. The building itself, a former fire station, helps the displays feel grounded rather than abstract.

It is a modest museum, but a useful downtown stop if you are travelling with children or want a bit of Denver civic history between the larger museums.
