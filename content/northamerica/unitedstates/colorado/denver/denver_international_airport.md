DEN is the second-busiest airport in the world by traffic since 2021 — and a building unlike any other. Its distinctive white peaked roof, designed by Curt Fentress in 1995, was meant to evoke the Rocky Mountains and the tepees of the Plains tribes. Inside, it has accumulated a peculiar reputation for hidden symbolism: enormous murals about apocalypse and reconciliation, a Masonic-engraved time capsule, gargoyles in the baggage claim — all of which feed an active conspiracy-theory cottage industry that the airport itself now leans into with cheeky marketing.

It's 25 miles northeast of downtown — much further than visitors expect. The A-line commuter rail to Union Station takes 37 minutes and runs every 15.
