The Denver branch of the US Mint, opened in 1906, produces several billion coins a year — every American penny, nickel, dime and quarter with a D mintmark was struck here. Free public tours (book online well in advance) take you through the production floor with views of giant coin presses in action.

The neoclassical building itself is worth a slow look from the outside if tour slots are booked out. It's a five-minute walk from the State Capitol.
