Michael Graves's 1995 Central Library is a postmodern landmark on Civic Center — a stack of differently shaped, differently coloured volumes that looks like a cubist parcel-sorting depot. Inside, the Western History/Genealogy department holds one of the country's deepest collections on the American West: rare books, maps, photographs, oral histories, plus rotating exhibitions that are free and frequently surprising.

The fifth-floor reading room, with its long maple tables and view of the mountains, is one of Denver's best public spaces. Even if you're not a researcher, walk through.
