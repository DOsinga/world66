The opera house tucked inside the DCPA complex — home to Opera Colorado and the Colorado Ballet, but also major touring opera and dance. The 2005 renovation kept the original 1908 horseshoe but completely rebuilt the acoustics; the room now compares favourably with any regional opera house in the country.

Catch a major performance — full season runs September to May. The under-30 ticket programs offered by both Opera Colorado and the Ballet are unusually generous.
