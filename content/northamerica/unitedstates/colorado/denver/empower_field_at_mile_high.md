Home of the Denver Broncos since 2001 (replacing the original Mile High Stadium, on the same site). The current building seats 76,000 and has an architectural nod to its predecessor: the white horse "Bucky" on top of the south scoreboard, a tradition since 1975. A game here is the Sunday social event of the year for a swathe of Colorado.

In the off-season the stadium runs behind-the-scenes tours of the locker rooms, press box, and field. The view of the downtown skyline backed by the mountains from the upper concourse is among the better stadium vistas in the country.
