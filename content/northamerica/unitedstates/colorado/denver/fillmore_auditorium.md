A grand 1907 ballroom on Colfax restored into one of Denver's main mid-sized concert venues — 3,600 capacity, intimate enough that the sound and sightlines are excellent. The chandeliered foyer is properly historic, the main hall has a horseshoe balcony that's worth lining up for.

Check the program in advance: most weeks see two or three significant national tours.
