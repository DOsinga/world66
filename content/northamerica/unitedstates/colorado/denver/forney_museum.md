The Forney is a slightly oddball collection in a warehouse north of downtown — Big Boy Locomotive 4005 (one of only eight surviving Union Pacific Big Boys, then the largest steam locomotives ever built), Amelia Earhart's 1923 Kissel "Gold Bug" Speedster, and dozens of vintage cars, carriages, and motorcycles. The exhibits are dense, occasionally chaotic, and never the same twice.

It's a hidden gem for transport history nerds and a comfortable hour-plus visit for families. The neighborhood is industrial; don't try to walk from downtown.
