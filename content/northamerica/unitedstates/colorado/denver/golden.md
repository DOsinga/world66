Twenty minutes west of Denver, Golden is a small foothills town wedged in the gap where Clear Creek leaves the mountains. The Coors Brewery — the largest single brewing site on earth — runs a free tour ending at a generous tasting room. The Colorado School of Mines campus and its excellent geology museum are also here.

Combine with a hike on North Table Mountain (mesa above town) or a short kayak down Clear Creek. The historic downtown along Washington Avenue is one block of red brick that survives basically intact from the 1880s.
