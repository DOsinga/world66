This converted historic church on South Logan Street is exactly what it sounds like: a place of worship for the loosely organised religious movement that holds cannabis as a sacrament that aids self-discovery. The nave is now a kaleidoscopic painted environment by Spanish street artist Okuda — geometric, multicoloured, and disorientingly beautiful.

Non-members can attend laser shows and concerts; full services are members-only. The visitor experience is more secular art-installation than religious — a 30-minute light show inside one of the country's strangest interiors. Photography is encouraged.
