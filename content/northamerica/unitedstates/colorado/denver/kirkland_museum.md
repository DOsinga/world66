The Kirkland is built around three things: the work of Colorado modernist Vance Kirkland, an international design collection (over 30,000 pieces of mid-century decorative art), and other Colorado artists of the early twentieth century. The displays are deliberately salon-style — densely hung, mixing eras and media — so the experience is more like wandering a serious collector's house than a sterile museum.

The 2018 move from Kirkland's original studio in Capitol Hill to a striking gold-clad building on the Golden Triangle gave the museum room to show much more of the design hoard. Saarinen Womb Chairs, Eames lounges, Italian glass and Bauhaus ceramics all sit alongside paintings. An hour does it; combine with the DAM and Clyfford Still, all within a block.
