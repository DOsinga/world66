Lakeside Amusement Park is a Denver throwback: neon signs, vintage rides, a lakeside setting, and a mood that feels far removed from the city’s newer polished districts. It is not sleek, and that is part of its charm.

Go in season and go for atmosphere as much as rides. Families come for the classics, photographers come for the signage, and travellers interested in old amusement parks will find more character here than at many larger, cleaner, less personal parks.
