The first proper drive into the high country from Denver — 11,990 feet on US 6, just shy of the Eisenhower Tunnel. The pass is the easiest place to step out of a car directly into the alpine zone (parking on top, tundra walks within steps). Backcountry skiers in winter hitchhike up and ski down the bowls beside the road.

Loveland Pass marks the Continental Divide; rain falling on the west side flows to the Pacific, on the east to the Atlantic. About an hour and a quarter from downtown.
