Meow Wolf is genuinely unlike anything else in Denver — a four-story walk-through art installation by the Santa Fe collective, opened in 2021 in a striking purple-and-orange building near Interstate 25. Visitors enter what looks like a 1970s transit hub, are issued a "QPass" card, and then wander a labyrinth of richly imagined parallel-world environments: an alien convenience store, a fungal forest, a memory-trading bureau.

There's a story you can chase if you collect clues from cards and computer terminals, but you can equally just wander and let the spaces hit you. Allow at least two and a half hours; book tickets a week or two in advance because timed-entry slots regularly sell out, especially on weekends.
