The 1889 sandstone house on Pennsylvania Street belonged to Margaret "Molly" Brown — Titanic survivor, suffragist, and Denver philanthropist who was rather quickly written out of polite society for being too forthright. The interiors are restored to their 1910 appearance: heavy Victorian furniture, polar bear rugs from the Browns' trips abroad, and the Tiffany-style stained glass in the dining room that they kept long after fashion moved on.

Tours are guided and run roughly hourly. The neighborhood, Capitol Hill, is one of Denver's nicest for an after-museum walk — quiet, leafy, and full of similar but less famous Victorian mansions, plus the original Cheesman Park.
