Denver's oldest continuously operating bar, opened in 1873 — and a documented Beat Generation hangout. The room is unfussy: dark wood booths, a long bar, no TVs, no signage outside until recently. The food is straightforward American (the JCB jalapeño cream cheese burger is the move), the beer list is small but well chosen, and the back patio in summer is one of the city's better hidden corners.

Walk over from Confluence Park or LoDo — five minutes across the 15th Street bridge.
