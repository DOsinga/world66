The National Ballpark Museum is a small family-run museum near Coors Field with a deep private collection of baseball and ballpark artifacts. Exhibits include old signs, bricks, seats, turnstiles, and other pieces rescued from classic American stadiums.

It works best before or after a Rockies game, but baseball fans may find it worth a separate stop for the ballpark history alone.
