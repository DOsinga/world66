The Oriental Theater is an old movie palace in northwest Denver that now works as a lively neighbourhood venue. Its programme ranges from concerts and comedy to film nights, with enough period detail left in the building to make the room part of the appeal.

It is most useful for visitors staying around Berkeley, Highlands, or northwest Denver who want a local night out away from the downtown venues.
