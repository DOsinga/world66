The Denver REI is the chain's second flagship and occupies the magnificently restored 1901 Denver Tramway Power Building above Confluence Park — soaring brick interiors, climbing pinnacle, kayak demo pool. Even if you're not buying anything, it's worth a wander as a building.

The location, right beside Confluence Park where the Cherry Creek and Platte River trails meet, makes it the natural launchpad for a city bike ride or a tubing trip down the Platte.
