A 1927-vintage downtown diner — neon "Coney Island" sign out front, vinyl booths inside, an exhaustive menu of green-chile breakfast burritos, Greek diner classics, pork-belly hash, milkshakes. Appearing on Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives raised its profile, but it was a Denver institution long before that.

The green chile is the giveaway: rich, properly hot, a foundational Denver dish. Open from breakfast through late at night.
