The Wash Park location of Kevin Morrison's mini-empire is the easiest to drop into — small, lively, walls of agave, queue forming at 5. The tacos are Cal-Mex with serious cooking behind them: duck confit with mango habanero, fried Baja fish, slow-cooked carnitas. The tequila/mezcal/whiskey list is genuinely long.

Cash-only and no reservations at most branches. Go early or accept the wait at the bar.
