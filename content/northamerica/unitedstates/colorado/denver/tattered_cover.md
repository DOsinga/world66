Founded in 1971, Tattered Cover is the city's beloved independent bookshop and one of the largest in the United States. The original Cherry Creek branch was the heart of literary Denver for decades; today the McGregor Square location across from Coors Field is the main downtown branch, with reading nooks, a café and a strong events program.

If you only have time for one stop, the McGregor Square branch is the easiest from downtown. The bookshop made national news in the 2000s for refusing to release customer records to federal investigators — a quiet act of principle that bookshop staff are still pleased to discuss.
