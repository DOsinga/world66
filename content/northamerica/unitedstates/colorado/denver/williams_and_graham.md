Hidden behind a bookshop facade on Tennyson in the Highlands, Williams & Graham was one of the first wave of Prohibition-themed speakeasies to take cocktails seriously in Denver. The room is small and convincingly old (dim lighting, pressed-tin ceiling, leather banquettes), the menu is long and changes regularly, and the bartenders consistently rank among the country's best.

Reservations strongly recommended at weekends. The bookshop hostess station is real, with real books.
