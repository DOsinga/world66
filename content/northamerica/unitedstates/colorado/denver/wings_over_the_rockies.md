Wings Over the Rockies fills a huge former Lowry Air Force Base hangar with aircraft, cockpit displays, space exhibits, and the kind of Cold War hardware that looks more impressive indoors than it ever does in photographs. It is one of Denver's better specialty museums, especially if you are travelling with someone who wants machines rather than paintings.

The location in Lowry is a short drive east of the central museums, so it pairs best with other east-side stops or a deliberately aviation-focused morning. Give yourself time to look up as well as around; the scale of the hangar is part of the pleasure.
