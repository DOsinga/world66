The local history museum is housed in the 1904 Animas City schoolhouse, a red-brick two-storey building that is itself the main artefact. Exhibits cover the area's early settlement, railroad history, mining boom, and the merging of Animas City into Durango.

The museum is small and easily seen in an hour; a restored 19th-century classroom on the upper floor is especially charming and well worth the stop on the way north out of town.
