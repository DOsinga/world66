The two day trips from Durango that everyone makes are **Mesa Verde National Park**, about 35 miles west, with the largest concentration of Ancestral Puebloan cliff dwellings in the United States; and **Silverton**, an old mining town up the Animas River reachable by car (50 miles) or on the heritage railroad. Either fills a day.

Purgatory Mountain ski area is 25 miles north on Highway 550 and worth a chairlift ride in summer for the views. The Weminuche Wilderness east of town has some of the longest, most remote trails in Colorado for those with backcountry experience.
