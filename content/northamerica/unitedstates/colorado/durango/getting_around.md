---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Downtown Durango is compact and walkable — Main Avenue between 5th and 13th streets is the whole core. **The Trolley** is a free city bus that runs the length of Main throughout the day; the same agency runs a paid local bus network reaching Fort Lewis College and the surrounding neighbourhoods.

A car is useful but not essential for staying in town. For day trips — Mesa Verde, Silverton via the road, the Animas wilderness — you'll want a car or a guided tour.
