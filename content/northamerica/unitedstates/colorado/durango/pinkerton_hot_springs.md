About 15 miles north of Durango on Highway 550, a brightly coloured travertine mound bubbles hot mineral water out onto the roadside. Iron and copper in the water have stained the surrounding rock orange, green and rust-red.

You cannot bathe — the spring is small and dribbles into a culvert under the road — but it is a good five-minute stop on the way north toward Silverton.
