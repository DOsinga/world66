A hands-on science museum aimed at children, set in a 1893 brick power-generating station beside the river. The original turbines and steam pipes are still in place — the museum has built its exhibits around them, which gives the space more character than the usual children's discovery centre.

There are exhibits on the Animas watershed, energy, and the Ancestral Puebloan astronomy of the Four Corners. A good rainy-afternoon stop with kids.
