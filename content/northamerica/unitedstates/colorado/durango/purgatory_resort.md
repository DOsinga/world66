Purgatory Resort is Durango's main lift-served mountain playground, set high in the San
Juan Mountains north of town. In winter it is a ski area; in summer it shifts toward
mountain biking, chairlift rides, hiking access, and family activities in cool alpine
air.

It is a resort, but the travel value is the mountain setting rather than the
accommodation. Go if you want a more active day outside Durango or if snow conditions
make a ski day possible. The drive itself is scenic, climbing from town into a
noticeably different landscape.
