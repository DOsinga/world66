---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Summer (June–September)** is the high season, with the steam train running daily, rivers in good shape for rafting, and the mountains accessible for hiking. Days are warm, nights cool — Durango is at 6,500 feet — and afternoon thunderstorms are common in July and August.

**Autumn** is arguably the best season — golden aspens light up the surrounding mountains from mid-September to mid-October, and the crowds have thinned. **Winter** brings deep snow to Purgatory Mountain, 25 minutes north, and the town slows down but keeps its restaurants and breweries running. **Spring** is mud season; not the best time to visit unless you are skiing the late snow.
