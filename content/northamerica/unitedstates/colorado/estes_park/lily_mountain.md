Lily Mountain is a short but rewarding hike near Estes Park, just outside Rocky Mountain National Park. The trail climbs to a rocky summit with broad views toward the Front Range, Longs Peak, and the surrounding foothills.

The round trip is about 3 miles, with enough ascent to feel like a real hike without taking a full day. It is a good option when higher park trails are snowbound or when you want a quick viewpoint close to town.
