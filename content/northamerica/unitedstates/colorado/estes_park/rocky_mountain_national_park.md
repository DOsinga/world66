Rocky Mountain National Park is why Estes Park exists as a travel destination. From the east side of town, roads lead quickly into glacier-carved valleys, alpine lakes, tundra, and high passes where the weather can change in minutes.

For a first visit, focus on Bear Lake Road, Moraine Park, and one good hike rather than trying to cover the whole park from a car. Timed entry rules and parking pressure change by season, so check the park site before committing your day.
