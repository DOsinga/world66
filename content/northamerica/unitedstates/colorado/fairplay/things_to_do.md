Fairplay is a small ranching and mining town at 2,989 metres altitude in South Park — the high-altitude mountain valley that inspired the animated TV series of the same name. The main draws are [South Park City](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/fairplay/south_park_city), one of Colorado's best open-air history museums, and the dramatic mountain scenery of Park County.
