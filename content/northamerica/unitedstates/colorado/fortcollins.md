Fort Collins sits where the plains meet the foothills of the Rocky Mountains, a college town with a disproportionate number of breweries. [Colorado State University](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/fortcollins/colorado_state_university) sets the pace, but it is the craft beer scene that put Fort Collins on the map — [New Belgium](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/fortcollins/new_belgium), [Odell](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/fortcollins/odell_brewing), and Horse & Dragon are all here, and you can spend a full day hopping between taprooms along the river.

[Old Town](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/fortcollins/old_town), the restored downtown district, is walkable and lively, with independent shops, restaurants, and a stretch of buildings that reportedly inspired Disneyland's Main Street USA. The [Cache la Poudre River](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/fortcollins/cache_la_poudre) runs through town and up into the canyon to the west, offering rafting, fishing, and hiking within minutes of downtown.

The vibe is outdoorsy and relaxed. [Horsetooth Reservoir](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/fortcollins/horsetooth_reservoir), just west of town, is where locals swim and paddleboard in summer. The foothills trails are busy with runners and mountain bikers year-round. Fort Collins is not a destination most international travelers plan around, but anyone passing through northern Colorado should stop — the beer alone is worth it.
