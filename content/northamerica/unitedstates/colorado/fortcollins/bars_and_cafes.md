---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Fort Collins has been called the craft beer capital of Colorado, and while Denver might dispute that claim, the density of quality breweries here is genuinely remarkable for a city of 165,000 people. There are over 20 craft breweries within city limits, and most have taprooms worth visiting.

Beyond New Belgium and Odell, look for **Horse & Dragon Brewing** (excellent IPAs and a large outdoor area), **Snowbank Brewing** (Colorado-focused beers in a relaxed taproom), and **Equinox Brewing** (Old Town location, reliable year-round range). The brewery scene is tight-knit and collaborative — staff at one taproom will happily recommend the next.

For coffee, **Happy Lucky's Teahouse** is a local institution for tea drinkers; **Mugs Coffee Lounge** is the student café of choice near CSU. Old Town has enough café options that you will not lack for a good espresso between brewery visits.
