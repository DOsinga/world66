Colorado State University is the institution that defines Fort Collins — a major research university with around 35,000 students, whose presence keeps the city young, progressive, and supplied with concerts, sporting events, and cultural programming. The campus is attractive, with mature trees and a mix of 19th-century brick buildings and modern research facilities.

The university is well-known for its veterinary and agricultural programmes, its environmental science research, and its engineering school. Visitors can wander the campus freely; the Lory Student Center hosts cafés and regular events, and the campus botanical garden is a quiet spot worth finding. CSU's football team (the Rams) plays at the modern Canvas Stadium on the edge of campus, and games are worth catching if you are in town during the season.

The giant Campbell's Soup can — a piece of pop art on campus created in collaboration with Andy Warhol — is the campus's most unusual sight. Seek it out near the art buildings.
