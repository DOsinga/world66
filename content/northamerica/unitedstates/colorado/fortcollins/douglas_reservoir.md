Douglas Reservoir is a small foothill reservoir north of Fort Collins, used for fishing, birding, and quiet walks with open views toward the Front Range. It is a modest stop, but useful if you want a less busy water-and-sky break than Horsetooth Reservoir.

The area is best in mild weather, when shorebirds and waterfowl are around and the plains light is clear. Bring shade and water; facilities are limited.
