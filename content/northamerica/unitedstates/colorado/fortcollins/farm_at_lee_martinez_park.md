The Farm at Lee Martinez Park is a small family attraction where children can meet farm animals and get a simple introduction to rural life. It fits Fort Collins well: outdoorsy, practical, and easygoing.

It is not aimed at travellers seeking a major sight, but it is valuable if you are visiting with younger children. Combine it with a walk along nearby trails or an Old Town stop to make an easy half-day.
