The Fort Collins Municipal Railway preserves a piece of the city's streetcar past, with a restored trolley running seasonally from a historic depot area. It is a small attraction, but it has the charm of real machinery operating on real track rather than a static display.

Check operating dates before planning around it, as the trolley is seasonal. When running, it pairs well with Old Town and gives a tangible sense of how Fort Collins moved before the automobile took over.
