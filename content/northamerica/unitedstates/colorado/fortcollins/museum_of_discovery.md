The Fort Collins Museum of Discovery is an all-ages science and cultural museum in Old Town, well-suited for families but interesting enough to hold the attention of adults. The exhibits cover natural history, local history, and interactive science — including a music and sound lab that is consistently popular with kids of all ages.

The local history section has good coverage of the Fort Collins area from its origins as a military post in the 1860s through its development as a college and agricultural town. Rotating exhibits bring in travelling shows on scientific and cultural topics. The museum is compact enough to cover in two hours and is an easy walk from the main Old Town restaurant and bar strip.
