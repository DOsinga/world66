The Swetsville Zoo is one of the great roadside oddities of northern Colorado — a private folk art installation on a farm east of Fort Collins, where retired farmer Bill Swets has spent decades creating an ever-growing menagerie of welded metal sculptures from old farm equipment and machine parts.

The creatures range from giant dinosaurs to small birds, insects, horses, and fantastical beings with no precedent in nature. They are scattered across the farmland in a loosely organized way, some weathered and moss-covered, others still gleaming. Entry is free, and the whole place has the quality of a dream slightly gone wrong. Worth the short drive east of town if you appreciate the genuinely eccentric.
