type: section
tags: []
---

Glenwood Springs has a lively downtown bar scene, fuelled by outdoor adventurers who have spent the day on the river, trail, or slopes. Several craft breweries have opened in recent years, reflecting the wider Colorado brewing boom. The coffee scene is solid for a town this size, with a few independent cafes catering to the morning crowd heading out to Hanging Lake or the ski resorts.
