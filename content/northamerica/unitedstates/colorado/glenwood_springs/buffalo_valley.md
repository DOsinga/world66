A large, casual bar and restaurant that feels like the social hub of Glenwood Springs on weekend nights. The main draw is the outdoor patio, a sprawling space that fills with locals and visitors alike in summer. There is live music most weekends — local and regional acts playing country, rock, and bluegrass. The bar pours a wide selection of Colorado craft beers and the kitchen produces solid comfort food. Gets genuinely loud and busy on Friday and Saturday nights.
