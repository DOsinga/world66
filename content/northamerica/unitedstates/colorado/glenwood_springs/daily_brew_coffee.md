A reliable independent coffee shop in the heart of downtown, a cut above the chain options. The espresso drinks are well made and the space has enough seating for remote workers and hikers plotting their routes over a laptop. Opens early, which matters if you are catching a shuttle to Hanging Lake or the morning Amtrak east. The baked goods — muffins, breakfast burritos — are worth arriving for on their own.
