Glenwood Canyon is the dramatic Colorado River gorge east of Glenwood Springs, with cliffs rising above the highway, rail line, river, and bike path. It is one of the most scenic stretches of western Colorado and the access point for hikes, rafting, fishing, and the route to Hanging Lake.

Even if you do not hike, the canyon is worth slowing down for. Cyclists can use the paved path through the gorge, while drivers should leave time for a pullout or two instead of treating it as just another section of Interstate 70.
