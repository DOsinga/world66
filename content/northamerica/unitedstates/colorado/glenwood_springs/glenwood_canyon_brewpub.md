The most established craft brewery in town, brewing a rotating lineup of ales and lagers that skew toward the approachable rather than the experimental. The Canyon Kolsch and the Hanging Lake Honey Ale are perennial favourites. The pub occupies a big, warehouse-style space downtown with a lively atmosphere, especially after ski season and on summer weekends. Food is brewpub standard — burgers, nachos, pretzels — but reliable. A good choice for a post-rafting or post-hot-springs pint.
