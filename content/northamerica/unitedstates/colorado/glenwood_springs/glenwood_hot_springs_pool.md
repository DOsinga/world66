The Glenwood Hot Springs Pool is one of the most recognisable landmarks in Colorado — a vast outdoor pool stretching more than 100 metres fed by the Yampah Spring, which pushes out around four million litres of mineral water every day at a natural temperature of about 51°C. The water is cooled before entering the main pool, so you swim in comfortable warmth year-round. There is also a smaller therapy pool kept hotter for soaking aching muscles after a day on the slopes or trail.

The pool complex includes waterslides, a fitness centre, and a lodge. It is crowded on weekends in summer and after ski season; go early on a weekday if you want peace. The surrounding views — canyon walls rising on both sides, steam rising off the water on cold mornings — are genuinely spectacular whatever the season.
