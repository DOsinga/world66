The restaurant inside the Hotel Colorado has long been the most handsome dining room in town — high ceilings, arched windows, and the building's Italianate bones intact. The menu leans toward American standards done carefully: steaks, pasta, and a good weekend brunch that draws both hotel guests and locals. The bar area is a fine spot for a drink even if you are not eating, with the same ornate surroundings at lower cost.
