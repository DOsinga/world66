Rivers sits right on the bank of the Colorado River, with a terrace that overlooks the water and the canyon walls beyond — one of the better dining views in Glenwood Springs. The menu is American with a contemporary tilt: grilled fish, Colorado lamb, and seasonal salads sourced from nearby farms. It is a step up from typical mountain-town cooking, and popular for sunset dinners when the canyon turns golden. Reservations are a good idea in summer.
