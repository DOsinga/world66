A well-regarded downtown Mexican spot that goes beyond the standard Tex-Mex playbook. The green chili — a Colorado staple made with roasted Hatch or Pueblo chiles — is the thing to order, either as a bowl or smothered over a burrito. Portions are large, prices are reasonable, and the room fills quickly at lunch with people who have just emerged from the hot springs across the street. The carnitas and carne adovada are also worth seeking out.
