Sunlight Mountain Resort is the local ski hill for Glenwood Springs, tucked into White River National Forest rather than built out as a self-contained resort village. The terrain is broad enough for a full day, with long intermediate runs, beginner slopes, and steeper sections that look toward Mount Sopris and the Elk Mountains.

Its appeal is the contrast with Colorado's bigger-name mountains. You can soak in Glenwood's hot springs at night and ski the next morning without committing to the expense or logistics of Aspen or Vail. In summer the mountain is quieter, but the road into the area still gives a good sense of the wooded country south of town.
