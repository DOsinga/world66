Cross Orchards Historic Farm preserves a piece of Grand Junction before the city became a gateway to desert trails and wine country. The site focuses on fruit growing, packing sheds, farm equipment, and the irrigation culture that made the Grand Valley productive.

It is a practical stop for travellers who want local history rather than another overlook. Visit it with the botanical gardens or a Palisade wine detour to understand how agriculture shaped this corner of western Colorado.
