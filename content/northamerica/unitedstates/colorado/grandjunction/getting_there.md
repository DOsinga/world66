---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air:** Grand Junction Regional Airport (GJT) has daily flights to Denver and seasonal or limited service to other western cities. Rental cars are available at the airport.

**By train:** The Amtrak California Zephyr passes through Grand Junction once daily in each direction, connecting Denver (5 hours) with Salt Lake City (6 hours), Reno, and San Francisco. The station is centrally located.

**By car:** Grand Junction is on I-70, the main east–west highway through Colorado. Denver is 4 hours east; Moab (Utah) is 1.5 hours south-west via US-191; Salt Lake City is about 4 hours via I-70 and I-15.

**By bus:** Greyhound stops in Grand Junction on routes connecting Denver with Salt Lake City.
