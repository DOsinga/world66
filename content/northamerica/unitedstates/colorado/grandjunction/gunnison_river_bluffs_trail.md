Gunnison River Bluffs Trail gives Grand Junction a quieter desert outing than the better-known Colorado National Monument routes. The trail follows broken country above the Gunnison River, with open views, spring flowers in good years, and the dry smell of sage and dust.

Hikers, runners, and mountain bikers share the network, so keep your head up on bends and narrow sections. There is little shade and the surface can be rough, which makes morning or late afternoon the sensible choice in warm weather. It is close to town but still feels satisfyingly open.
