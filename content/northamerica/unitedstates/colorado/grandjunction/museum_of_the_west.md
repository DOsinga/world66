The Museum of the West is the main history museum in downtown Grand Junction and part of the Museums of Western Colorado. Its exhibits cover Indigenous history, Spanish trails, mining, ranching, firearms, and the settlement of the Colorado Plateau region.

It is a useful first stop before driving into Colorado National Monument or the surrounding canyon country, because it gives the landscape a human timeline. The museum also helps explain Grand Junction's role as a service town between mountains, desert, rail routes, and agricultural valleys.
