Powderhorn is Grand Junction's mountain escape: a ski area on the edge of Grand Mesa, high above the desert and vineyards of the Grand Valley. In winter it gives western Colorado a local alternative to the bigger resort corridors farther east, with broad views and a less frantic feel.

It also works outside ski season, when the lifts and trails shift toward mountain biking and summer recreation. The drive is long enough that you should treat it as a deliberate outing rather than a casual city stop, but it adds an alpine option to a destination better known for red rock, orchards, and canyon country.
