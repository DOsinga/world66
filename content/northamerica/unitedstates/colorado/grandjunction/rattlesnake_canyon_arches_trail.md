Rattlesnake Canyon Arches Trail is a serious desert outing west of Grand Junction, inside the McInnis Canyons country managed by the BLM. The reward is a line of sandstone arches and windows tucked into a rugged canyon, often described as one of the richest arch concentrations outside Utah's Arches National Park.

Access is the catch. The shorter approach depends on seasonal, rough high-clearance roads, while the longer Pollock Bench route turns the day into a strenuous backcountry hike. Carry more water than feels reasonable, avoid the hottest part of summer, and check BLM road and fire restrictions before setting out.
