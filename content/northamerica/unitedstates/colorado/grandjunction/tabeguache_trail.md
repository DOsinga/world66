The Tabeguache Trail is a big western Colorado route rather than a single afternoon stroll. It runs for roughly 142 miles between Grand Junction and Montrose, crossing public land, canyons, mesas, and the Uncompahgre Plateau. Most visitors touch it from the Grand Junction end, especially around the Lunch Loops trail system.

For travellers, its value is the taste it gives of the backcountry just beyond town. Mountain bikers know the name well, but hikers can also sample short sections for red-earth views and a quick sense of the plateau. Conditions change with heat, mud, and exposure, so carry water and check local trail advice before setting out.
