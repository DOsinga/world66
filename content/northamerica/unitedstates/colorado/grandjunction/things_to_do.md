---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Grand Junction's main draw is the landscape immediately outside the city. **Colorado National Monument** is just minutes from downtown — a canyon country of red sandstone monoliths, rim drives, and hiking trails that rivals Utah but draws a fraction of the crowds. The **Palisade** wine region, ten miles east, has about two dozen wineries and some of the best peaches in the state. Mountain bikers come for the **Lunch Loops** and the Kokopelli Trail system. The rivers — the Colorado and the Gunnison, which meet here — offer rafting from spring through late summer.
