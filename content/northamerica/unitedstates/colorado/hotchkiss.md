Hotchkiss sits in Colorado's North Fork Valley, a working farm and orchard town between Paonia and Crawford. It is not polished resort Colorado. Its appeal is more useful than glossy: fruit stands, high-country wineries, dry hills, local artists, and a downtown that still feels tied to ranch and orchard life.

The town shares the valley's wine identity with [Paonia](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/paonia), but it has its own stops. [The Storm Cellar](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/hotchkiss/the_storm_cellar) is one of the valley's strongest wineries, and [Creamery Arts Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/hotchkiss/creamery_arts_center) gives downtown a real creative anchor. The [Hotchkiss-Crawford Historical Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/hotchkiss/hotchkiss_crawford_historical_museum) fills in the older story of settlement, farms, and small-town Delta County.

Come with a car and a loose plan. Hotchkiss is best as part of a North Fork day of tastings, gallery stops, farm markets, and back roads rather than as a stand-alone monument town.
