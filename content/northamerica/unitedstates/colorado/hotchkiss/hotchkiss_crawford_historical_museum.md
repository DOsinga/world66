Hotchkiss-Crawford Historical Museum is a small local museum with photographs, clothing, tools, business equipment, and other objects from the surrounding Delta County communities. It is not flashy, but it gives useful background on the farms, ranches, coal, and small towns that shaped the North Fork Valley.

Drop in if it is open while you are downtown. The museum pairs well with the Creamery Arts Center because together they show the valley before and after its creative, wine, and farm-to-table reinvention.
