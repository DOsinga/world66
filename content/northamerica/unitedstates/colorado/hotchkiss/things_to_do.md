Hotchkiss is a slow North Fork Valley stop. Pair [The Storm Cellar](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/hotchkiss/the_storm_cellar) with a look at [Creamery Arts Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/hotchkiss/creamery_arts_center), then use the [Hotchkiss-Crawford Historical Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/hotchkiss/hotchkiss_crawford_historical_museum) to ground the valley's farm and ranch history.
