---
title: "Argo Gold Mine and Mill"
type: poi
---

The Argo Gold Mine and Mill is Idaho Springs' most substantial historic attraction — a working remnant of the town's mining past that offers genuine underground tours rather than a sanitized theme-park experience. The mine operated from 1893, and the Double Eagle tunnel was one of the most productive in Clear Creek County. The large mill building above ground was completed in 1913 and processed ore from multiple mines in the area. At its peak, the Argo tunnel stretched nearly five miles into the mountains, connecting dozens of individual mine workings.

Tours take visitors through the original tunnel and explain the whole arc of the gold mining process: how ore was blasted and hauled out, how the mill crushed and processed the rock, and how gold was finally separated and smelted. The equipment in the mill is largely intact and in good condition, which makes the operation unusually legible compared to sites where only the ruins remain. Guides are generally knowledgeable about both the history and the geology of the region. There is also a working gold panning operation on the grounds where visitors can try their hand.

The Argo is one of the better mine tour experiences in Colorado, and it holds up well for visitors who have already been to similar attractions elsewhere. Allow at least two hours. The site sits just east of downtown Idaho Springs, visible from the road.
