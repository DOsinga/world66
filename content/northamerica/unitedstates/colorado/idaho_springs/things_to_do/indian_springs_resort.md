---
title: "Indian Springs Resort"
type: poi
---

Indian Springs Resort has been operating as a hot springs destination since the 1860s, when the geothermal waters were marketed to visitors as a curative treatment for various ailments. The springs are fed by underground sources heated by the residual geothermal activity associated with the Rocky Mountain volcanic past, and the water emerges at a consistent temperature that is then routed into the various pools on the property. The resort sits right in town, a short walk from the main street.

The facilities include an indoor pool complex fed directly by the hot spring water, private cave pools carved into the rock beneath the main building, and an outdoor geothermal pool. The cave pools are the most unusual feature — small private soaking rooms accessed through a tunnel beneath the resort, warm and dimly lit, where the hot spring atmosphere is most evident. The indoor pools are warmer than the outdoor pool, and the whole complex feels pleasantly old-fashioned rather than spa-slick.

The resort also has a hotel with rooms ranging from simple motel-style to more characterful historic accommodation, a restaurant, and a day-use area for visitors who are not staying overnight. Weekend afternoons can be busy with day-trippers from Denver, and advance booking is recommended for the cave pools.
