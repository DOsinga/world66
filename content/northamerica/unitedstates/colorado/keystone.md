Keystone is a ski resort town in Summit County, Colorado, about 90 miles west of Denver along Interstate 70. [Keystone Resort](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/keystone_resort) spreads across three interconnected mountains — Dercum, North Peak, and the Outpost — covering over 3,000 acres, and it stands out among Colorado ski areas for its extensive night skiing: nearly 1,700 lit acres skiable until 8 pm. The season runs from mid-November through mid-April, one of the longer runs in the state.

Summit County has the highest concentration of ski resorts in the US, and Keystone sits at the middle of it. [Arapahoe Basin](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/arapahoe_basin) is six kilometres up the road — smaller, steeper, and high enough to stay open into June. [Breckenridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/breckenridge) is fifteen minutes over the ridge, and Copper Mountain is not much further. All are included on the Epic Pass.

In summer the resort converts to mountain biking and hiking, with the gondola running to serve the [Keystone Bike Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/summer_activities) and trails into the White River National Forest. The real draw in summer is [Lake Dillon](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/lake_dillon) — a 3,300-acre reservoir just minutes away with sailing, kayaking, paddleboarding, and a 27-mile circumference trail. The nearby towns of Dillon and Silverthorne offer more affordable dining and lodging than the resort villages. The two base areas — [River Run Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/river_run_village) and [Keystone Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/keystone_village) — provide the restaurants, rental shops, and apres-ski activity that hold resort guests in place between runs.
