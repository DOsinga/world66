---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Keystone's activities split cleanly between winter and summer. In winter, the three interconnected mountains offer over 3,000 acres of skiing and snowboarding, with [Keystone Resort](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/keystone_resort) unique among Colorado ski areas for its extensive night skiing. In summer, the same lifts that serve skiers carry mountain bikers, the trails convert to hiking routes, and [Lake Dillon](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/lake_dillon) becomes the focus for water sports. The two base areas — [River Run Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/river_run_village) and [Keystone Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/keystone_village) — serve as the social hubs year-round.

[Arapahoe Basin](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/keystone/arapahoe_basin), just a few kilometres up the road, is a worthy companion to Keystone: smaller, steeper, and one of the last Colorado resorts to close each spring.
