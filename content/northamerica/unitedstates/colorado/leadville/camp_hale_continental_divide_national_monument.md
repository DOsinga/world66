Camp Hale preserves the high mountain valley where the U.S. Army trained the 10th Mountain Division for winter warfare during World War II. The old camp remains are spread across a broad, austere landscape north of Leadville, with interpretive stops that connect the military history to Colorado's ski culture after the war.
