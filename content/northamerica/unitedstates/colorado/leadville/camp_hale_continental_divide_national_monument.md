Camp Hale-Continental Divide National Monument protects the high mountain valley where the U.S. Army trained the 10th Mountain Division during World War II. Remains of the camp, interpretive signs, and the stark alpine setting make it more than a roadside history marker.

The site is north of Leadville on the way toward Vail. It works best as a half-day stop for travelers interested in military history, skiing history, or the human stories behind Colorado's mountain landscape.
