Healy House and the adjacent Dexter Cabin are operated as a Colorado State Historic Site, preserving two contrasting windows into Leadville's silver boom. Healy House, built in 1878, is an elegantly furnished Victorian home with original wallpaper, furniture, and domestic artefacts. Guided tours run through the house and provide a detailed account of middle-class life in a Colorado mining town at its peak.

Dexter Cabin, built in 1879, is the more surprising of the two: from the outside it looks like a simple log cabin, but James Dexter, a wealthy mining investor, used it as a private club room. The interior features ornate woodwork, a poker room, and furnishings that belie the rustic exterior. Together the two buildings offer an hour's worth of genuinely interesting history.
