North Pole Home of Santa's Workshop is a Christmas-themed amusement park in Cascade, just northwest of Manitou Springs. It is old-fashioned rather than slick: small rides, Santa photo rituals, and mountain scenery doing a lot of the work. Families with younger children will get the most out of it.
