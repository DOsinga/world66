Badger House Trail is one of Mesa Verde's most useful self-guided archaeology walks, especially if you want context beyond the famous cliff dwellings. The route links several mesa-top sites that show changes in Ancestral Pueblo life over roughly six centuries.

Because the trail is relatively short and partly paved or graveled, it lets you slow down and read the landscape without committing to a major hike. Bring sun protection and water; Wetherill Mesa can feel exposed even when the distance looks easy on the map.
