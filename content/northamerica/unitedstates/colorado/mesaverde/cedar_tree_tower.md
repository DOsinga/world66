Cedar Tree Tower is a compact Ancestral Puebloan site on the mesa top, far less dramatic than the cliff dwellings but useful for understanding that Mesa Verde was not only a place of alcove villages. The tower stands beside a kiva, one of several tower-kiva complexes in the park, and its purpose is still debated.

It is a quick stop along the park road, best treated as a pause between the larger overlooks and house sites. That shortness is part of its value: in a few minutes you can see how ceremonial, domestic and landscape features were tied together across the mesa before descending again toward the more famous cliff dwellings.
