Knife Edge Trail follows part of Mesa Verde's old entrance road from the Morefield Campground area. The route is short, but it gives a good view over Montezuma Valley and the broken country around the park.

It is a useful early or late day walk when you want something less involved than the main archaeological loops. The trail is exposed, so bring water and avoid the hottest part of the afternoon.
