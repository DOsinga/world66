Nordenskiold Site No. 16 Trail is a Wetherill Mesa hike in Mesa Verde National Park, leading to a viewpoint over an unexcavated cliff dwelling. It is named for Gustaf Nordenskiold, whose early documentation helped shape outside understanding of the park's archaeological sites.

The trail is not as famous as Cliff Palace or Balcony House, which is exactly why it can be satisfying. Check seasonal access to Wetherill Mesa, carry water, and treat the viewpoint with the restraint all ancestral Puebloan sites deserve.
