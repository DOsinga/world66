Point Lookout Trail is one of Mesa Verde's good leg-stretchers, climbing from the campground area to a blunt summit with a wide view over the surrounding valleys. It shifts the park experience from cliff dwellings to mesa country, which helps you understand why this landscape mattered.

The route is short but not effortless, with switchbacks and exposed sections that feel hot in summer. Start early, carry water, and save it for a day when smoke or storm clouds are not hiding the long view.
