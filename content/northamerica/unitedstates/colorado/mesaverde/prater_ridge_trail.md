Prater Ridge Trail is one of Mesa Verde's better hikes for travellers who want landscape as well as archaeology. The loop climbs from the Morefield area onto a wooded ridge, with views across the park's mesas, canyons, and distant mountains. It gives you a sense of the country the Ancestral Puebloans lived in, not just the cliff dwellings they built.

The trail is moderately demanding by park standards, partly because of elevation and exposure. Start early in warm weather, carry water, and check current park conditions before setting out. It is a strong choice if you have already seen the major ruins and want a quieter half-day on foot.
