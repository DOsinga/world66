Spruce Canyon Trail is one of Mesa Verde's useful escapes from the cliff-dwelling crowds. The loop drops from Chapin Mesa into a side canyon, trading overlooks and guided-house logistics for pinyon, juniper, sandstone, and the slower rhythm of walking below the rim.

Check current National Park Service conditions before setting out, because closures and heat warnings matter here. Carry water even for a moderate hike; the canyon can feel much hotter and more exposed than the map distance suggests.
