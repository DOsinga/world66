Ouray sits at 2,400 meters in a box canyon carved by the Uncompahgre River in the San Juan Mountains of southwestern Colorado, and it is one of the most dramatically situated towns in the American West. The mountains rise precipitously on all four sides — you can be hiking above treeline within 30 minutes of leaving town — and the Victorian-era mining architecture survives largely intact, making Ouray feel both visually spectacular and genuinely historic in a way that many Colorado mountain towns don't.

The town earned its nickname "Switzerland of America" honestly. The [Ouray Ice Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/ouray_ice_park) in the gorge below town — a man-made ice climbing venue using piped-in water — has made Ouray one of the world's premier destinations for ice climbing in winter, drawing elite climbers and beginners to several kilometers of frozen waterfalls. In summer, the hot springs pools in the center of town are fed by naturally heated water from the mountains, and hiking and 4WD roads into the surrounding peaks attract outdoor enthusiasts of every kind.

The Black Bear Road and the [Alpine Loop Scenic Byway](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/alpine_loop) connect Ouray to [Telluride](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/telluride) and the ghost towns of the San Juan mining district — some of the finest high-altitude driving and cycling in Colorado. The [Bachelor Syracuse Mine Tour](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/bachelor_syracuse_mine) above town gives a genuine understanding of what silver and gold mining in these mountains actually involved. The Victorian commercial district on Main Street — anchored by the [Beaumont Hotel](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/beaumont_hotel) and [Wright's Opera House](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/wrights_opera_house) — is remarkably intact and makes for good architectural wandering.

Ouray has a permanent population of about 1,000, which doubles or triples in summer and during ice climbing season in January and February. It is small enough that the main sights are all within a 20-minute walk of anywhere in town.
