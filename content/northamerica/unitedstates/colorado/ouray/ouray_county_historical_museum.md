Ouray County Historical Museum fills the former St. Joseph's Miners' Hospital, built in 1886, with the story of the town and the surrounding San Juan mining country. The rooms mix mineral specimens, Ute artefacts, mine exhibits, photographs, Victorian domestic displays, and medical spaces left from the building's hospital days.

It is a good first stop before walking Ouray's restored 19th-century streets. The setting helps: this was a working mountain hospital, not a neutral gallery, so the exhibits feel tied to the same cliffs, mines, and hot springs you see around town.
