title: Things to Do
type: section
---

[Box Canyon Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/box_canyon) is the most accessible natural sight — a short walk from Main Street into a spectacular slot canyon. [Cascade Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/cascade_falls) above the Amphitheater campground requires a 45-minute climb but rewards with views back over the entire canyon setting.

The [Ouray Ice Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/ouray_ice_park) in the Uncompahgre Gorge is the reason serious climbers come in winter, and the rim walk is dramatic for spectators. The [Bachelor Syracuse Mine Tour](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/bachelor_syracuse_mine) above town is the best historical experience — a real mine with former miners as guides.

On Main Street, the [Beaumont Hotel](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/beaumont_hotel) and [Wright's Opera House](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/ouray/wrights_opera_house) anchor the Victorian commercial district. The Ouray Hot Springs Pool, in the center of town, is fed by natural hot springs and open to the public — a good way to end a day of hiking or cycling.

The unpaved 4WD roads into the surrounding mountains — the Alpine Loop to Silverton and Lake City, the Imogene Pass road to Telluride — are among the best of their kind in Colorado. A high-clearance 4WD vehicle opens up a remarkable network of ghost towns, mine ruins, and high-alpine passes.
