Treasure Falls is a scenic waterfall near Wolf Creek Pass, east of Pagosa Springs. It is visible from the road, but the short, steep trail from the parking area gets close enough to feel the spray and makes a good break on the mountain drive.

The stop is simple and weather-dependent, but the access is easy and the view is strong enough to justify pulling over when crossing the pass.
