Wolf Creek Ski Area sits over the pass east of town and is famous for deep snow, a simpler atmosphere, and fewer resort-town frills. In winter it is the major outdoor reason to base yourself in Pagosa Springs; outside ski season, the drive over the pass is still a fine mountain outing.
