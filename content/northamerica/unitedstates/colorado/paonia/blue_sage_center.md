Blue Sage Center for the Arts is Paonia's community arts hub, occupying a storefront on Grand Avenue with a gallery, performance space, classes and concerts. For a town this small, the programming is unusually active, ranging from local exhibitions to chamber music and touring performers.

It is worth checking the calendar before you arrive, since the best reason to go is not the building itself but the event happening inside. Even without a show, the gallery gives a quick read on Paonia's creative side.
