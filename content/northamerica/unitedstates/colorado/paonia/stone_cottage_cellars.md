Stone Cottage Cellars is a family-run winery on Reds Road above Paonia, built around fieldstone structures and a small vineyard with big views toward the West Elks. It is one of the better places to understand why the North Fork Valley has become serious Colorado wine country rather than just pretty orchard land.

The tasting room usually opens seasonally from late spring into autumn. Gewurztraminer is the signature grape to look for, grown here in a cool, high-elevation setting that gives the wines a brighter edge than many visitors expect from Colorado.
