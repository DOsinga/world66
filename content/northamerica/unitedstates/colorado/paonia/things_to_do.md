type: section
---

Paonia is best approached as a valley day rather than a single downtown sight. Taste wine at [Stone Cottage Cellars](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/paonia/stone_cottage_cellars) or [Azura Cellars](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/paonia/azura_cellars), check what is on at [Blue Sage Center for the Arts](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/paonia/blue_sage_center), and leave time for the North Fork riverbank at [Paonia River Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/paonia/paonia_river_park).

If you have a car, the drive up to [Paonia State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/paonia/paonia_state_park) adds the mountain setting: reservoir water, wildflowers, summer camping and views into the Ragged Mountains.
