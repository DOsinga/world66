Pueblo is southern Colorado's old steel city, set where Fountain Creek meets the Arkansas River. It does not have Denver's gloss or Colorado Springs' mountain drama, but it has a stronger local flavour than either: working-class history, Mexican and immigrant food traditions, public art, and a stubborn pride in the Pueblo chile.

The best introduction is the [Historic Arkansas Riverwalk](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/pueblo/historic_arkansas_riverwalk), a revived downtown waterway that turned flood-control history into a walkable civic centre. Nearby are the [El Pueblo History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/pueblo/el_pueblo_history_museum), the glowing signs of [Neon Alley](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/pueblo/neon_alley), and the galleries and children's museum at the [Sangre de Cristo Arts Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/pueblo/sangre_de_cristo_arts_center).

Pueblo also works outdoors. [Lake Pueblo State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/pueblo/lake_pueblo_state_park) has big-water boating, fishing, and dry-country trails just west of town. Give Pueblo a day and it stops looking like a highway exit between better-known Colorado places.
