Pueblo Weisbrod Aircraft Museum has a large collection of aircraft and military memorabilia, with exhibits reaching from the world wars through later American military history and the Space Race. The aircraft are the main draw, but the smaller displays give useful context to Pueblo's aviation links.

It sits by the airport, away from downtown, so plan it as a separate stop rather than something to fold into the riverwalk.
