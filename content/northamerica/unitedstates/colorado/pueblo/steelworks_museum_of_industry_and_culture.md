The Steelworks Museum of Industry and Culture tells the story of Colorado Fuel and Iron, the company that shaped Pueblo's steel industry from the 1870s into the late 20th century. The museum occupies the National Register-listed CF&I Medical Dispensary, part of the old office complex.

Exhibits cover steelmaking, mining-camp life, company history, minerals and gems, a working nail machine, and a mine rescue car. It is one of Pueblo's clearest links to its industrial past.
