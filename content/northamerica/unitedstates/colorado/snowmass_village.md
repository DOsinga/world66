Snowmass Village is a mountain resort community in Colorado's Elk Mountains, sitting about 12 km from [Aspen](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/aspen) and sharing the same dramatic scenery. In winter it operates as a major ski resort — the [Snowmass ski area](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/snowmass_village/snowmass_ski_area) has over 90 runs and is one of the largest in Colorado — less glamorous than Aspen, but with more varied terrain and generally shorter lift lines. In summer the ski trails become hiking and mountain biking paths, and the village hosts an outdoor music festival season.

The [Base Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/snowmass_village/base_village) at the foot of the mountain has been substantially rebuilt in recent years and now has a concentration of restaurants, shops, and lodging options clustered around a lively plaza. The village is explicitly oriented toward outdoor activity — ski-in/ski-out is the norm in winter, and the summer trail network connects to the [Maroon Bells](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/snowmass_village/maroon_bells) wilderness area above.

Beyond the slopes, the village holds two unexpected institutions: the [Anderson Ranch Arts Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/snowmass_village/anderson_ranch_arts_center), a serious working campus for ceramics and visual arts, and the site of the 2010 [Snowmastodon dig](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/snowmass_village/snowmastodon_site) — one of the most important Ice Age fossil finds in North America. The summer [Jazz Aspen Snowmass](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/snowmass_village/jazz_aspen_snowmass) festival fills Town Park over Labor Day weekend.

Snowmass makes a practical and slightly less expensive alternative to staying in Aspen itself, with frequent shuttle connections between the two.
