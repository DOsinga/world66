[Aspen](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/aspen) is the obvious day trip — twenty minutes by the free RFTA bus, with a real downtown, the Aspen Art Museum, several galleries, and the cluster of ski mountains (Buttermilk, Aspen Highlands, Aspen Mountain) shared on the Aspen Snowmass pass.

[Glenwood Springs](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/glenwood_springs), about an hour down the Roaring Fork valley, is the place to go for an outdoor hot-spring pool — the original Hotel Colorado spring is one of the largest in the world. The drive there through Glenwood Canyon is itself an attraction.
