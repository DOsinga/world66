---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Restaurants in Snowmass cluster in the Base Village plaza and along the Snowmass Mall, the older walking street higher up. Prices are mountain-resort serious, but generally a notch below equivalent places in [Aspen](/northamerica/unitedstates/colorado/aspen) — a small saving that adds up over a week.

Recommended for a mountain dinner: **Eight K** in the Viceroy Hotel does a tight modern American menu with good wine; **Venga Venga**, on the plaza, serves Mexican that holds up after several margaritas; **Sake** is a surprisingly capable sushi place for a town this small. For something lower-key, the **Stew Pot** on the Snowmass Mall has been around for half a century and still does excellent house-made stews and soups at lunch.

For breakfast the bakery at **The Daily Bread** on the Mall is the place for pastries and serious coffee. On-mountain dining at **Lynn Britt Cabin** (a ski-up, snowcat-down sit-down lunch) and the bowl-side **Up 4 Pizza** are both worth working into a ski day.
