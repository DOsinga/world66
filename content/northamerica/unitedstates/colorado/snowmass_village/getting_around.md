---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

A car is not necessary inside the village — and is mostly inconvenient, since parking is limited and expensive. The **Village Shuttle**, free, runs continuously between the Base Village, the Snowmass Mall, and the major lodges, and drops at the main lift bases.

The **RFTA** (Roaring Fork Transportation Authority) free bus runs every 15–30 minutes to Aspen, with extra runs in winter. The ride takes about 20 minutes. If you are skiing the four-mountain pass and want to try Buttermilk, Aspen Highlands, or Aspen Mountain, RFTA is the way to get there.

Bikes can be hired in summer and there is a network of paved bike paths down the valley toward Aspen.
