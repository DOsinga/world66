At 99 metres, Bridal Veil Falls is the tallest free-falling waterfall in Colorado, dropping in a single white curtain from the headwall at the east end of the box canyon down into the valley below. It is visible from almost anywhere in town, a permanent backdrop that makes Telluride's setting even more theatrical than it would otherwise be.

A trail from the south end of town (the far end of Pine Street) climbs steeply through forest to reach the base of the falls, and continues more steeply still to the top and the historic powerhouse. The round trip to the powerhouse is about 6km and gains significant elevation. The falls are most dramatic in late spring and early summer when snowmelt is at its peak.
