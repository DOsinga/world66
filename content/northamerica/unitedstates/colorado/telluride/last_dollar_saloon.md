At the west end of Colorado Avenue, the Last Dollar is the classic dive bar that every ski town needs and most resort towns eventually lose to gentrification. Pool tables, cheap beer, locals playing darts alongside visitors who have figured out where to actually drink. The decor has not been updated recently, which is entirely to its credit.

It is the antidote to the more polished options elsewhere on the street, and it closes later than most. A reliable last stop on any night out in Telluride.
