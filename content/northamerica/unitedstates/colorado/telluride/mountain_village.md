Mountain Village is the ski resort village on the far side of the ridge from historic Telluride, built in the 1990s as a purpose-built resort community at the base of the lifts. It is orderly, expensive, and has none of the Victorian character of the town below — but it has the ski resort infrastructure: rental shops, ski school, a large number of restaurants, and direct access to the lifts without having to ride the gondola first.

Whether you prefer to stay in Telluride or Mountain Village comes down to what you value. Mountain Village is convenient and modern; historic Telluride has more character but requires the gondola to reach the ski area. Most visitors end up spending time in both, which is exactly what the free gondola makes easy.
