The free gondola connecting Telluride to Mountain Village takes about 13 minutes and rises over 600 metres above the valley floor, with views down the box canyon and out across the San Juan Mountains that would cost good money at most ski resorts. It runs year-round from early morning to late night.

Even if you have no particular business in Mountain Village, the gondola is worth riding for the views alone. The mid-station at San Sophia Ridge offers a stopping point with a particularly good panorama: Telluride laid out in the canyon below, the waterfall at the far end, and the peaks forming the tight ring that defines the town's dramatic geography.
