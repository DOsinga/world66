Mountain Standard on Bridge Street in Vail Village is one of the most consistently good restaurants in town — focused on wood-fired cooking, high-quality Colorado ingredients, and a menu that changes with the seasons. The wood-burning grill dominates the open kitchen and gives the place an honest, informal feel despite the upscale surroundings.

The menu typically centres on steaks, lamb, and fish prepared simply over the fire, with vegetables and sides that reflect what the Colorado high country offers in each season. In winter, rich braises and root vegetables sit alongside the grilled cuts. The bar is well-stocked and the wine list is serious. Reservations are recommended in ski season.
