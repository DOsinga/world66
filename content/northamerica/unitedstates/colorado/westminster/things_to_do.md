Westminster's main draws are practical rather than spectacular. The Butterfly Pavilion is the standout attraction for families and anyone with an interest in invertebrates. The city's open space system offers accessible trail running and cycling along Big Dry Creek. For shopping and eating, the Promenade is the hub. Most visitors come through Westminster on their way somewhere else — but the following are worth stopping for.
