The Shed is a local favourite on the main drag in Winter Park, a casual Mexican restaurant that does reliably good burritos, tacos, and margaritas at prices that don't require a ski resort budget. It fills up quickly after the lifts close and the crowd is solidly local — a good sign anywhere.

The menu is straightforward: choose your protein, build your burrito or tacos, wash it down with something cold. After a day on the mountain, the portions feel appropriately generous. The atmosphere is lively and informal, which fits a ski town well. Lines can be long on weekends at dinner; showing up at 5pm beats the 7pm rush.
