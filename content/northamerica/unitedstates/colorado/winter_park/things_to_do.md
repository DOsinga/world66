---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Winter Park's activities divide neatly between winter and summer. In winter, it's skiing and snowboarding at Winter Park Resort, with over 3,000 acres across seven territories. In summer, the resort converts to a mountain bike park and the surrounding Arapaho National Forest opens for hiking, fishing, and camping. Year-round, the Fraser Valley and Berthoud Pass provide a dramatic mountain setting that rewards slow exploration.
