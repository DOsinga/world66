Elmer's is Danbury's classic diner: gleaming stainless steel outside, vinyl booths and a long counter inside, and a menu that runs from all-day breakfast through burgers, melts, and Greek-American standards. It sits on Padanaram Road north of downtown and draws the full Danbury cross-section — retirees at the counter, families after church, night workers coming off shift.

Nothing here will surprise you, which is the point. The pancakes are large, the coffee is bottomless, and the prices are honest. If you want to understand everyday Danbury rather than just its museums, an hour in a booth at Elmer's is a reasonable research method.
