A block west of Main Street, the Octagon House is exactly what its name promises: a two-storey, eight-sided house built in 1852, wrapped in a veranda and topped with a cupola. It is one of the survivors of the strange mid-19th-century octagon craze, and its unusual geometry still stops walkers on Spring Street mid-stride.

The house is privately owned and not open for tours, so this is a look-from-the-sidewalk sight — best combined with a walk through the surrounding Main Street Historic District, whose 19th-century commercial buildings mark the prosperous heart of the old Hat City. Five minutes is enough, but for anyone who collects architectural oddities it is a satisfying five minutes.
