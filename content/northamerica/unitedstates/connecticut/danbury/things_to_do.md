---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Danbury offers a mix of local history, outdoor spaces, and some genuinely quirky sights. The [Danbury Museum and Historical Society](/northamerica/unitedstates/connecticut/danbury/danbury_museum) explores the city's remarkable hat-making past. The [Danbury Railway Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/connecticut/danbury/danbury_railway_museum) occupies a beautifully restored Union Station. [Tarrywile Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/connecticut/danbury/tarrywile_park) provides woodland trails close to downtown, and [Candlewood Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/connecticut/danbury/candlewood_lake) offers boating and swimming just to the west of the city.
