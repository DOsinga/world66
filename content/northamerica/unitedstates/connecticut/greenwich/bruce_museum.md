The Bruce Museum is Greenwich's main cultural anchor, a compact but serious museum that mixes art, science, and natural history. Its location near downtown makes it easy to pair with Greenwich Avenue and the waterfront edge below I-95.

Check the exhibition calendar before you go. The museum's strength is variety, so the visit changes a lot depending on what is on view.
