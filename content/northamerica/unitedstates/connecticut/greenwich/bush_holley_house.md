Bush-Holley House is the historic core of the Greenwich Historical Society campus in Cos Cob. The house connects two different Greenwich stories: colonial life along the coast and the later Cos Cob art colony, where American Impressionist painters gathered around the Holley family's boarding house.

Take a guided tour if one is available. Without context it can look like another old New England house; with context it becomes one of the town's better windows into art, money, and place.
