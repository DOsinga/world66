The Connecticut Historical Society Museum and Library is one of Hartford's strongest history stops. Founded as one of the oldest historical societies in the United States, it holds a major collection of New England manuscripts, photographs, objects and printed material.

The museum side gives visitors a focused way into Connecticut history, while the library and archives make it especially useful for researchers. It pairs well with the Mark Twain House and Harriet Beecher Stowe House in Hartford's West End.
