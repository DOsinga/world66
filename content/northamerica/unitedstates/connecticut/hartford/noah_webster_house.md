The Noah Webster House preserves the birthplace of the dictionary maker whose spelling books and dictionaries helped shape American English. The museum also covers West Hartford history, including later local textiles and 19th-century pottery, making it a good companion to Hartford's Mark Twain and Harriet Beecher Stowe houses.
