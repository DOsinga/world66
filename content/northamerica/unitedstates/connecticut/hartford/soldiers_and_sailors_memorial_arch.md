The Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Arch marks the northern entrance to Bushnell Park and is one of Hartford's strongest downtown landmarks. Dedicated in 1886, it honours Connecticut soldiers who died in the American Civil War, with carved figures and reliefs that also acknowledge emancipation and the struggle of enslaved people.

It is an easy stop while walking between the State Capitol, Bushnell Park, and downtown Hartford. The arch is especially good from the park side, where its Gothic details stand clear against the open green.
