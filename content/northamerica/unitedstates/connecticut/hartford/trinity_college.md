Trinity College gives Hartford one of its most attractive campus walks, with Collegiate Gothic buildings arranged around a large green. It is a calm contrast to the downtown museums, state buildings, and Mark Twain-era literary sights.

The campus is most worthwhile for architecture and atmosphere rather than a specific museum collection. Walk respectfully, check access rules for buildings, and treat it as a short detour if you are already exploring south of downtown.
