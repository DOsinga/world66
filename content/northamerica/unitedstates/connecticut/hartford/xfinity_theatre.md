Xfinity Theatre is Hartford’s major large-scale concert venue, a place built for touring acts rather than intimate local performance. Its amphitheatre setup makes it especially active in summer, when the lawn and pavilion fill for rock, country, pop, and nostalgia tours.

It is not a sightseeing stop in the traditional sense, but it matters if your trip overlaps with a show. Check transport and parking before you go; the venue experience is much smoother when you treat it as an evening plan rather than an add-on after downtown museums.
