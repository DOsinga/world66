---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

New London has a good range of restaurants for a small city, concentrated in the downtown and on Bank Street near the waterfront. Seafood is the obvious choice this close to Long Island Sound.

**Recovery Room** on Huntington Street is a reliable downtown spot for burgers, sandwiches, and pub food with a friendly neighbourhood feel. **Captain Scott's Lobster Dock** on Hamilton Street is the classic New London seafood shack — lobster rolls, clam chowder, and fried seafood plates in a no-frills setting right on the water. For something more substantial, **Muddy Waters Cafe** on Bank Street serves good coffee, sandwiches, and light meals. **Frank Pepe's Pizzeria** has a New London location — the white clam pizza is a Connecticut tradition worth trying if you haven't had it.
