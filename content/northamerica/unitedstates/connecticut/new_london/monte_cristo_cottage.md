Monte Cristo Cottage is the New London summer home associated with playwright Eugene O'Neill and his family, later echoed in the setting of Long Day's Journey into Night. The house gives literary history a specific domestic setting, with rooms and views that help explain why this place mattered to O'Neill's imagination.

It is one of New London's most distinctive cultural stops, especially for theatre and literature travellers. Visits are usually tour-based or seasonal, so confirm access before going out of your way.
