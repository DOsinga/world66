Ocean Beach Park is New London's family beach park, with a sandy beach, boardwalk, pool, splash pool, waterslide, arcade, playground, rides, mini-golf, nature walk, and observation deck.

It is more developed than a simple public beach, so it works best for families who want a full day of activities by the water. Check the seasonal schedule and fees before going.
