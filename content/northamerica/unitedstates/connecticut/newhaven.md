New Haven is a city defined by Yale University, but it has a character that goes well beyond the campus gates. The university gives the city world-class museums, a lively arts scene, and some genuinely extraordinary architecture, while the surrounding neighbourhoods contribute some of the best pizza in the United States and a gritty urban energy that keeps things real.

Start at the Green, the original town common laid out in 1638 and still the heart of the city. Three historic churches line its north side — Centre Church on the Green, Trinity Episcopal, and United Church on the Green — and from here it is a short walk into the Yale campus. The Yale University Art Gallery and the Yale Center for British Art face each other across Chapel Street and are both free, making for one of the most rewarding afternoons a visitor can spend without spending a dollar. A block away, the Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library announces itself with walls of translucent Vermont marble that glow amber in sunlight.

The campus itself rewards slow walking. Harkness Tower, a 216-foot Gothic Revival campanile completed in 1921, is the university's most recognisable landmark. The Memorial Quadrangle behind it encloses Branford and Saybrook residential colleges in a grand courtyard of honey-coloured stone. Rudolph Hall, the brutalist Art and Architecture building designed by Paul Rudolph in 1963, offers a sharp counterpoint — loved and hated in equal measure, it remains one of the most discussed buildings in American architectural history.

Beyond the campus, New Haven has good green space and a serious theatre culture. Lighthouse Point Park on the harbour is a recreation area and nature preserve with a restored 1916 carousel. West Rock Ridge State Park offers hiking trails and views across Long Island Sound. The Yale Repertory Theatre and the Long Wharf Theatre both stage ambitious productions that frequently transfer to Broadway. And on Wooster Street, the coal-fired pizza parlours that have operated since the 1920s remain a pilgrimage destination for food lovers from across the country.
