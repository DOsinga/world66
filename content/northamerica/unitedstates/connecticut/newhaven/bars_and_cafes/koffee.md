A neighbourhood cafe on Audubon Street, Koffee is the anti-Starbucks option favoured by graduate students and faculty. The space is relaxed, the coffee is good, and the hours are long. It is a dependable place to work for an afternoon or meet for a casual coffee. The back patio is pleasant in warmer months. Located in the arts district between downtown and Science Hill.
