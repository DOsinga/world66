Blessed Michael McGivney Pilgrimage Center, formerly the Knights of Columbus Museum, focuses on the New Haven priest who founded the Knights of Columbus and on the wider story of Catholic service, charity, and community life. Exhibits combine religious history with rotating cultural displays.

It is a specialized stop, but a meaningful one for Catholic pilgrims, religious-history travelers, or visitors exploring New Haven beyond Yale. Its central location makes it easy to add if the subject interests you.
