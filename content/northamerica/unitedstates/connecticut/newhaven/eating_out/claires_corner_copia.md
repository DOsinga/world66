A New Haven institution since 1975, Claire's Corner Copia is a vegetarian and vegan restaurant on Chapel Street near the corner of College — hence the name. The menu spans Lithuanian coffee cake, Central American dishes, sandwiches, soups, and daily specials. It is unpretentious, inexpensive, and open long hours, making it reliable at almost any time of day. The restaurant has hosted a remarkable number of celebrity visitors over the decades and the walls are covered with photographs to prove it.
