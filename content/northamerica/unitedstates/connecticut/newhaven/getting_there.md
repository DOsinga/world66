---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train** is the most practical way to reach New Haven from New York or Boston. Metro-North's New Haven Line runs from Grand Central Terminal in New York to New Haven Union Station, with express trains taking about 1 hour 40 minutes; local trains take longer. Amtrak also serves New Haven on its Northeast Corridor route, with trains from New York Penn Station taking about 1 hour 45 minutes and from Boston South Station around 2 hours 30 minutes. Union Station on Union Avenue is a handsome Beaux-Arts building about a 15-minute walk from the Green, or a short cab ride.

**By car**, New Haven sits at the junction of I-91 (north to Hartford, south via I-95 toward New York) and I-95 along the coast. Parking garages are available downtown. Traffic on I-95 between New Haven and New York can be severe on weekends.

**By air**, the nearest major airports are Bradley International Airport (BDL) in Windsor Locks, about 45 minutes north, and John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) in New York, about 2 hours by car or train. Tweed New Haven Airport (HVN) handles limited regional service.
