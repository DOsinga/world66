Marsh Botanical Garden is Yale's living plant collection, used for teaching, research, and public appreciation. Greenhouses and garden areas highlight plant diversity in a quieter corner of New Haven's university landscape.

It is most rewarding for garden lovers, botany-minded travelers, or visitors who have already seen the main Yale museums and want something calmer. Access and hours can vary, so it is a place to check before building a visit around it.
