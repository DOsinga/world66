Pardee Morris House is a colonial New Haven house built in 1750 and later rebuilt after being burned during a British raid on New Haven Harbor in 1779. It now operates as a seasonal historic site, with tours, classes, and events that give a quieter look at the city's early history beyond the Yale campus.
