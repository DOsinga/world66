A dive bar on College Street that has served generations of Yale students and New Haven locals since the 1950s. The Anchor is unpretentious, affordable, and reliably busy — cheap beer, no fuss, and a jukebox that skews toward classic rock. It is the kind of bar where a professor and a first-year student can end up having the same conversation without either finding it strange. Open until 1am most nights.
