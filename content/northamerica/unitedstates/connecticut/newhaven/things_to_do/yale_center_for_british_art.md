The largest collection of British art outside the United Kingdom, the Yale Center for British Art holds paintings, drawings, prints, rare books, and sculpture spanning the 16th century to the present. Constable, Turner, Hogarth, Gainsborough, and Reynolds are all strongly represented. The building, designed by Louis Kahn and completed in 1977 (his last major project), is a masterwork of natural light: the concrete and oak interior is lit by skylights that filter daylight through fabric panels, creating a calm, contemplative atmosphere. Admission is free and it sits directly across Chapel Street from the Art Gallery.
