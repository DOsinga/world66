Storrs is the village face of the University of Connecticut, set in the rural town of Mansfield. It is not a classic New England sightseeing town, and it does not pretend to be one. Its reason to visit is the UConn cluster: museums, performance spaces, student energy, and a surprisingly strong puppetry tradition.

The best anchor is the [Ballard Institute and Museum of Puppetry](/northamerica/unitedstates/connecticut/storrs/ballard_institute_and_museum_of_puppetry), a small but distinctive museum tied to one of the country's serious university puppetry programs. Nearby, the [William Benton Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/connecticut/storrs/william_benton_museum_of_art), [Jorgensen Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/connecticut/storrs/jorgensen_center_for_the_performing_arts), and [Connecticut Repertory Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/connecticut/storrs/connecticut_repertory_theatre) give the campus more cultural weight than most college towns of this size.

Outside the campus core, [Mansfield Hollow State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/connecticut/storrs/mansfield_hollow_state_park) adds the spare-day argument: trails, water, and quiet woods a short drive from downtown Storrs. Combine a museum, a campus walk, and the park, or time the trip around a concert or basketball game.

Storrs is a selective stop. It makes sense if you are already in eastern Connecticut, visiting UConn, or looking for a low-key day with one unusual museum and good performing arts. It is too thin to pull most travelers far out of the way.
