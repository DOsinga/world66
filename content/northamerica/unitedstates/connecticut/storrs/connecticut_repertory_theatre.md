Connecticut Repertory Theatre is UConn's producing theatre, with student, faculty, and guest artist work across several campus stages. The programming changes by season, but it gives Storrs another reason to stay into the evening rather than treating the campus as a quick daytime stop.

Check schedules before you go. When there is a strong production running, it pairs well with the Ballard Institute and makes the village's theatre culture feel more coherent.
