Jorgensen Center for the Performing Arts is UConn's main touring performance venue, bringing concerts, dance, comedy, speakers, and stage productions to Storrs. It is the place to check first if you want to turn a campus visit into an evening out.

For travelers, Jorgensen matters because it gives Storrs a calendar. On a good night the village feels less like a campus service centre and more like a small arts town with a university behind it.
