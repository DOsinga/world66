The William Benton Museum of Art is UConn's campus art museum, with changing exhibitions and a permanent collection that ranges across European and American work, prints, drawings, and modern pieces. It is not large, but it gives Storrs a proper museum stop beyond campus nostalgia.

The museum sits near the centre of campus, so it fits naturally into a walk between the older university buildings, the student core, and Downtown Storrs. Admission is usually free.
