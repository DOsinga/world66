The Palace Theater opened in 1922, when Waterbury had the money and confidence to build a proper downtown showplace. Its renovated lobbies, domed ceilings, and ornate auditorium are part of the pleasure, even before the performance starts.

Check the calendar before you go. A tour or evening show makes the building feel alive in a way a quick look from East Main Street cannot.
