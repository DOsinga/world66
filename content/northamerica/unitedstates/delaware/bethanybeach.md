Bethany Beach is a small, family-oriented beach town on the Delaware coast, often called one of the "Quiet Resorts" along with Fenwick Island and South Bethany. It lacks the boardwalk amusements and nightlife of [Rehoboth Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach) a few miles north, and that is exactly the point — people come here for the beach itself and not much else.

The beach is wide and clean, with a [short boardwalk](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/bethanybeach/bethany_beach_boardwalk) and a bandstand that hosts free concerts on summer evenings. The town centre has a handful of restaurants and ice cream shops along Garfield Parkway, and the [Bethany Beach Nature Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/bethanybeach/bethany_beach_nature_center) has marshland trails and exhibits a short walk away. [Delaware Seashore State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/bethanybeach/delaware_seashore_state_park) is just north of town, preserving six miles of ocean and bay shoreline with surf fishing at the Indian River Inlet jetties and a campground. The [Freeman Arts Pavilion](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/bethanybeach/freeman_arts_pavilion) near Selbyville runs an outdoor performing arts season through the summer.

Bethany is a popular weekend and summer rental destination for families from the Washington, D.C. and Baltimore area. September is arguably the best month to visit: the water is at its warmest after the summer's heat, the beaches thin out, and the prices drop.
