Dewey Beach is a small resort community on the Delaware coast, wedged between the Atlantic Ocean and Rehoboth Bay. It is one of the liveliest beach towns in the Mid-Atlantic states, drawing a youthful summer crowd with its laid-back atmosphere, watersports, and a stretch of bars and restaurants that keep things animated well into the night.

The beach itself is wide and well maintained, and [Rehoboth Bay](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/dewey_beach/rehoboth_bay_watersports) on the western side is ideal for windsurfing, kiteboarding, and kayaking. The town is tiny — a few blocks wide — but the concentration of restaurants and live music venues makes it feel far larger in summer. It sits just south of [Rehoboth Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach), which is larger and more family-oriented, making Dewey the destination for those who want more of a party atmosphere.

The main bar strip centres on Coastal Highway, with [The Starboard](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/dewey_beach/starboard) serving as the social hub since the 1970s. [Delaware Seashore State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/dewey_beach/delaware_seashore_state_park) stretches south from town along the barrier spit, offering surf fishing, nature trails, and the excellent fishing at Indian River Inlet.
