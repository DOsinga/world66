---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Dewey Beach packs a lot into a very small space. The ocean beach runs the length of the town and is wide, clean, and well maintained. On the bay side, Rehoboth Bay is ideal for watersports — the calmer water and reliable breezes make it one of the better spots on the Mid-Atlantic coast for windsurfing and kiteboarding. Delaware Seashore State Park, just to the south, extends the natural setting considerably and offers surf fishing, nature trails, and access to Indian River Inlet.
