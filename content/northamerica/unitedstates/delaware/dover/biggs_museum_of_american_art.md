Biggs Museum of American Art holds a collection of American fine and decorative art from the 18th century to the present. The galleries include paintings, furniture, silver, glass, ceramics, and textiles, with a strong focus on the Mid-Atlantic.

Its location beside The Green makes it easy to pair with Dover's historic state buildings. It is one of the better cultural stops in the city and gives useful context to Delaware's material and artistic history.
