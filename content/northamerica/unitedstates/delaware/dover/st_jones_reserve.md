St. Jones Reserve protects a stretch of tidal marsh and upland habitat just east of Dover, where Delaware's inland capital begins to give way to the bay. Trails and boardwalk sections introduce salt-marsh plants, birdlife, and the slow water systems that shape the coast.

It is not a big-ticket attraction, but it is a good low-key stop for birders, walkers, and anyone trying to understand the landscape beyond the statehouse and racetrack. Go in decent weather, bring insect repellent in warm months, and let the place be quiet.
