Middletown is a small but rapidly growing town in New Castle County, southern Delaware — about 50 km south of Wilmington on US Route 301, and one of the fastest-growing municipalities in the state. Historically a 19th-century farming community (the [Middletown Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/middletown/middletown_historic_district) along Main Street is the surviving reminder), it has boomed in recent decades into a commuter suburb for the Wilmington and Philadelphia job markets.

There is not much to pull in travellers specifically, but Middletown is a convenient stop for anyone heading down the Delmarva Peninsula toward the Delaware beaches, and the surrounding countryside still has working farms and a scattering of pre-Revolutionary houses. Combine a visit with a side trip to [Historic Odessa](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/middletown/historic_odessa) — a tiny, beautifully preserved 18th-century port village that is one of the best small historic sites in the state and absolutely worth the 10 km detour.
