Appoquinimink Friends Meetinghouse is a 1785 brick Quaker meeting house near Odessa. It is tied to Delaware's Underground Railroad history, associated with abolitionists John Hunn and Thomas Garrett.

The building's hiding places, including spaces under the eaves and access from the cellar, make it more than a quiet old church. It is a compact but meaningful stop for travellers following early American and abolitionist history.
