Middletown's main historic interest lies in the [Middletown Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/middletown/middletown_historic_district), where a concentration of 19th-century commercial and residential buildings along Main Street gives a flavour of what a prosperous Delaware market town once looked like. The nearby village of [Historic Odessa](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/middletown/historic_odessa) is the compelling nearby excursion — one of the best-preserved 18th-century villages on the Delmarva Peninsula.
