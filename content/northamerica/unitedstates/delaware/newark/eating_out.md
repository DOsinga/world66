---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Newark has a strong college-town food scene, with Main Street carrying most of the better options. The range is wide: pizza, subs, casual American, and a few sit-down places worth seeking out. Caffe Gelato is the standout for Italian food and has been a local institution for years. The scene is busiest during the school year; expect a noticeable slowdown in summer when many students leave.
