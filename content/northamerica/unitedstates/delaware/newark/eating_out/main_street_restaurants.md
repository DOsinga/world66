The main stretch of restaurants in Newark runs along Main Street and covers most bases: pizza, subs, casual American diners, and a handful of more interesting independents. Caffe Gelato is the standout — a long-established Italian restaurant with a loyal following among students and locals alike, known for its pasta and gelato. The street is particularly lively on weekend evenings during term time, when the mix of students and locals gives it real energy.
