Around the campus itself, the eating options lean toward the practical: chains, fast-casual spots, and small independents catering to students on a budget. The value is generally good and there is no shortage of choice at lunch and dinner. It is not where you come for a special meal, but for a quick and cheap feed near the university it does the job well.
