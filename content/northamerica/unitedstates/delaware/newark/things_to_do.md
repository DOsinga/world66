---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Newark's main draws are its university campus, the green trails of White Clay Creek State Park, and a handful of historic and cultural stops. The University of Delaware dominates the town's character, and its handsome grounds are open to anyone who wants to explore. Just north of town, White Clay Creek offers a proper escape into woodland. History buffs will find the Iron Hill Museum and the legendary Deer Park Tavern worth a look.
