A small but worthwhile museum housed in a former schoolhouse, the Iron Hill Museum of Natural History focuses on the local geology and archaeology of the region. Displays cover fossils found in the area, the distinctive iron-ore geology that gives Iron Hill its name, and Native American history in the Delaware Valley. It is a modest operation but well put together, and gives good context to the landscape you are walking through in the surrounding parks.
