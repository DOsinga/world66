The University of Delaware campus is one of the most attractive in the Mid-Atlantic, with a long green mall flanked by Georgian brick buildings that give it a classic collegiate feel. The grounds are open to the public and make for a pleasant walk even if you have no academic business here. The main green stretches south from the library and is particularly lively during term time. The Delaware Art Museum and a Center for the Arts are nearby, adding cultural weight to a campus visit.
