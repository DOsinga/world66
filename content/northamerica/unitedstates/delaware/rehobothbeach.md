Rehoboth Beach is a small, walkable beach town on Delaware's Atlantic coast that bills itself as "The Nation's Summer Capital" — a reference to the waves of Washington DC families, politicians, and lobbyists who have been coming here since the late 19th century. The mile-long [boardwalk](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach/boardwalk) is the heart of it: [Thrasher's fries](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach/thrashers_fries), Grotto Pizza, Dolle's saltwater taffy, and a bandstand with free concerts on summer evenings. [Funland](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach/funland), a family-owned amusement park on the boards since 1962, remains one of the better value entertainment options on the East Coast.

Beyond the boardwalk, the town has a good restaurant scene that punches above its weight for a place this size. [Rehoboth Avenue](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach/rehoboth_avenue), the main drag, runs straight from the beach to a cluster of shops and restaurants. The town is notably welcoming and has a large LGBTQ community, particularly visible during the summer months.

Cape Henlopen State Park, at the north end of town, has wide beaches, a fishing pier, and dunes. The [Junction and Breakwater Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach/junction_trail) — a six-mile rail trail — connects Rehoboth to [Lewes](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/lewes) by bike. Delaware has no sales tax, which makes the [Tanger Outlets](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach/tanger_outlets) on Route 1 a draw for shoppers from the neighbouring states. [Dewey Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach/dewey_beach), immediately to the south, operates as the party beach of the Delaware coast. [Silver Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/delaware/rehobothbeach/silver_lake) provides a quiet freshwater alternative for wildlife watching.
