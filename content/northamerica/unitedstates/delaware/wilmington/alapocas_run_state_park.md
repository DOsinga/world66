Alapocas Run State Park gives Wilmington a surprisingly rugged green space close to the city. Trails drop through wooded ravines toward Brandywine Creek, and the park’s exposed rock faces are used for managed climbing, which is unusual for such an urban-accessible park.

It works well as an active break between museums and estate visits. Walkers can link it with nearby Brandywine Park, while families often head for the Can-Do Playground. The park is compact, but its terrain makes it feel more varied than its size suggests.
