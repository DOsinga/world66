Nemours Mansion & Gardens is one of Wilmington's strongest estate visits, built by Alfred I. du Pont with the confidence and scale of an American industrial fortune. The mansion's French-inspired formality, long garden axes, fountains, and collections make it feel closer to a European palace fantasy than a simple country house.

Allow time for both house and grounds. The visit pairs well with other Brandywine Valley estates, but Nemours has its own personality: polished, symmetrical, and slightly theatrical in the way Gilded Age wealth often is.
