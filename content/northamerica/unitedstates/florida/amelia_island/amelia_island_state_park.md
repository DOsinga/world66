Amelia Island State Park protects the island's southern end, where beach, salt marsh, tidal creeks, and maritime vegetation meet around Nassau Sound. It feels wilder than the resort and historic-district parts of Amelia Island.

The park is best for beach walking, birding, fishing, and quiet coastal scenery. Conditions can be exposed, so bring sun protection and check access or activity rules before planning a long visit.
