Leffis Key Preserve protects a compact but varied patch of mangrove, tidal water, and restored coastal habitat on Anna Maria Island. Boardwalks and sandy paths make it an easy nature stop without leaving the island for a larger state park.

Climb the small hill for open views over Sarasota Bay, then loop through the mangroves for birds and shade. Go early or late in the day for cooler temperatures and better wildlife activity.
