The Boca Express Train Museum is housed in a restored 1930 Florida East Coast Railway station, a reminder that Boca's growth followed the rails before it followed the interstate. Historic railcars on site help make the story tangible rather than just a set of old photographs.

It is a niche stop, best for railway buffs, families, or anyone interested in how South Florida towns were stitched together in the early 20th century. Pair it with the nearby historical society museum if you want a compact history morning.
