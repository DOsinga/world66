Sugar Sand Park is Boca's best family park, with walking paths, sports facilities, a science-focused children's centre, and one of the area's better playgrounds. It is not a must-see for every traveller, but it is genuinely useful if you are breaking up beach days with children.

The park also works for a quiet walk away from traffic and shopping plazas. In a city that can feel highly polished, Sugar Sand is a practical public space where local life shows through.
