Town Center at Boca Raton is the city's major enclosed mall, and it is unapologetically upscale. Department stores, designer labels, and polished interiors make it a better fit for serious shopping than casual browsing.

It is most useful in bad weather or peak summer heat, when an air-conditioned afternoon suddenly becomes attractive. Travellers who prefer street life should stick with Mizner Park; shoppers looking for brands will do better here.
