---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Boynton Beach sits on Interstate 95 and US Route 1, about 50 miles north of Miami and 15 miles south of West Palm Beach. The nearest major airports are **Palm Beach International** (PBI), roughly 12 miles north, and **Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International** (FLL), about 30 miles south — both well-served by domestic carriers. **Miami International** (MIA) is around 60 miles south and offers more international options.

By train, **Tri-Rail** stops at Boynton Beach station, on the line between Miami and West Palm Beach, with connections to most communities on the southeast coast. By bus, Palm Tran operates local routes throughout Palm Beach County.

If you are driving from the north along I-95, take exit 57 for Boynton Beach Boulevard east toward the ocean. US-1 is slower but runs right through downtown and along the coastal strip.
