Brooksville's 1885 train depot still stands on Russell Street, just south of downtown. The old rail bed has become the Good Neighbor Trail, which makes the museum feel connected to the route that once brought freight, passengers, and growth into town.

Inside are railroad and local-history exhibits, including depot rooms, freight displays, model trains, and a restored utility box car. It is a compact stop, best paired with May-Stringer House.
