Bald Point State Park sits east of Carrabelle where beaches, tidal marsh, and pine flatwoods meet. It is a good place for a slow coastal walk, birding, kayaking, or a swim when the weather is settled.

The park is a short drive rather than a town sight, but it is one of the strongest natural anchors in the Carrabelle area.
