Camp Gordon Johnston Museum tells the story of the huge military training camp that operated around Carrabelle during the Second World War. Soldiers trained here for amphibious landings before being sent to battlefields overseas.

It is a small, specific museum, but it gives the quiet coast a sharper historical edge.
