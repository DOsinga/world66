Carrabelle Beach is the easy beach stop west of town on US 98. It has white sand, picnic shelters, restrooms, and simple access to the Gulf without the resort feel of busier Florida beaches.

Come for a swim, a picnic, or a quiet sunset. It is not flashy, which is exactly the point.
