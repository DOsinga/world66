Carrabelle History Museum is a simple local-history stop a block off US 98. Its exhibits cover the town's fishing life, early tourism, the old railroad, and the way this stretch of the Forgotten Coast developed.

It pairs well with the nearby police-station oddity and a walk along the riverfront.
