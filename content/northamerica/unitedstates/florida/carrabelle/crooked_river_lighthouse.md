Crooked River Lighthouse is a restored lattice tower west of Carrabelle, built to guide vessels through the shifting waters around St. George Sound. The grounds include a small museum and park.

If climbs are running, go up for the coastal view. Even from the ground it is one of the clearest landmarks along this quiet stretch of US 98.
