Tate's Hell State Forest covers a huge area behind Carrabelle, with pine flatwoods, swamp, blackwater creeks, and a grid of former logging roads. It is best for travelers who like quiet nature more than groomed attractions.

Check conditions before driving deep into the forest. After rain, some roads can be rough, and distances feel longer than they look on a map.
