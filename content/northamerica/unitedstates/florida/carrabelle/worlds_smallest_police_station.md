The World's Smallest Police Station is Carrabelle's classic roadside oddity: a police phone housed in what looks like a tiny booth beside US 98. It will not hold you long, but it is memorable and very much in the spirit of the town.

Stop for the photo, then keep walking to the riverfront or local history museum.
