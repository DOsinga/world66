---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Clearwater Beach has a lively bar scene anchored by tiki bars, rooftop decks, and beachfront patios — the kind of places where flip-flops are the dress code and rum drinks come in oversized glasses. The vibe peaks at sunset and stays up late on weekends. Downtown Clearwater is quieter but has a few spots worth seeking out, especially along Cleveland Street near the Capitol Theatre.
