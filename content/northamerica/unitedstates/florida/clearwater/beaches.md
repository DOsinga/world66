---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Clearwater's beaches are the main event. The barrier island on the Gulf side has miles of fine white quartz sand, calm turquoise water, and reliable sunshine — the combination that has made Clearwater Beach one of the most celebrated stretches of coastline in the United States. The main beach is lively and activity-packed, while Sand Key to the south offers a quieter alternative. Beyond the city limits, Honeymoon Island and Caladesi Island state parks preserve some of the last undeveloped barrier island beaches on the Gulf Coast, both within easy reach for a day trip.

No alcohol is allowed on any of the public beaches. Lifeguards are on duty until 4:30 p.m. at the main beaches. Parking is metered and fills early on weekends — arrive before 10 a.m. or take the Jolley Trolley. The best swimming conditions are April through September, with warm water temperatures and generally gentle surf.
