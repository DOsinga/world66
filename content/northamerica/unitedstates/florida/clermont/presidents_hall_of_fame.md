The Presidents Hall of Fame is the kind of eccentric roadside museum that used to be common in Florida. Its best-known exhibit is a large, detailed miniature White House, backed by presidential wax figures, campaign material, and patriotic odds and ends.

The museum sits beside the Citrus Tower, so the two make an easy pair. Go with a taste for handmade Americana and you will have more fun than if you expect a polished national museum.
