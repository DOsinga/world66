Crackers is the most reliable sit-down seafood restaurant in Crystal River, occupying a waterfront spot with dockside seating and views across Kings Bay. The menu centres on local Gulf catches — grouper prepared several ways, scallop dishes in season, fresh oysters, and crab. The shrimp and grits is a dependable choice year-round. Service is straightforward, the room is large, and it handles tour group volumes without collapsing — useful to know during the busy manatee season from December through February.
