---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Crystal River's restaurant scene is modest but reliably focused on what the town does best: fresh Gulf seafood. Stone crab claws (in season October–May), grouper, and scallops are the things to order. Most restaurants are casual waterfront spots where you can arrive in board shorts without raising an eyebrow. The town is small enough that you'll exhaust the good options quickly, but quality is generally solid within that limited range.
