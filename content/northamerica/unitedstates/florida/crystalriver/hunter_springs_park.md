Hunter Springs Park gives public access to Crystal River's spring-fed water, with a small beach, kayak launch, boardwalk, and picnic areas. In the right season, manatees may be seen in the wider spring and river system.

It is a practical, low-key stop rather than a wilderness experience. Come for a swim, paddle launch, or relaxed waterfront break, and respect manatee protection rules when wildlife is present.
