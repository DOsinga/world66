The tiki bar at the Plantation Adventure Center sits on a private marina at the edge of Kings Bay, surrounded by water and the palms and mangroves of the nature coast. It's primarily used by guests of the adjacent resort and divers and kayakers who have just come in from the springs, but day visitors are welcome. Cold beer, simple bar food, and a location that puts you as close to the bay as you can get without getting in the water. Best in the late afternoon when the light drops across the bay.
