Daytona Lagoon is a family entertainment park near the beachside core, with a water park, miniature golf, go-karts, laser tag, and arcade-style activities. It is one of the easier all-ages options when the beach weather turns or a group wants a structured break from the sand.

The water park is the main draw in warm weather, while the indoor and dry attractions make it useful for families with children of different ages.
