The Southeast Museum of Photography is a serious photography museum on the Daytona State College campus, with changing exhibitions that range from documentary work to contemporary fine-art photography. It gives Daytona Beach a quieter cultural stop away from the speedway and beach strip.

The museum is compact, so it suits visitors who want a focused hour indoors rather than a large museum day. Pair it with the Museum of Arts and Sciences if you are building a broader arts-and-culture itinerary on the mainland side of Daytona.
