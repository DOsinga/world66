Tiger Bay State Forest protects a broad sweep of pine flatwoods, swamps, lakes, and wet prairie west of Daytona Beach. It is not a polished beachside attraction, but it gives the area a genuine backcountry counterpoint to the speedway, boardwalk, and oceanfront.

Visitors come for hiking, cycling, horseback riding, fishing, and wildlife watching on forest roads and marked trails. Conditions can be wet and buggy, so it is best treated as a nature outing that rewards preparation rather than a casual stroll.
