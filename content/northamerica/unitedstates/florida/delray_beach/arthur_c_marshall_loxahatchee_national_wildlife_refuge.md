Arthur C. Marshall Loxahatchee National Wildlife Refuge is the Delray Beach area's strongest gateway to the northern Everglades. Sawgrass, canals, cypress swamp, alligators, wading birds, and big skies make it feel far removed from the coast despite the manageable drive.

The refuge works for several styles of visit: a short boardwalk, a birding stop, a bike ride along levees, or a longer paddle if conditions are right. Check weather, insects, and seasonal water levels, because the Everglades experience changes dramatically through the year.
