Most things worth seeing in southeast Florida are within an hour. Just south is [Boca Raton](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/bocaraton) with the Boca Raton Museum of Art and Mizner Park; just north, the **Wakodahatchee Wetlands** is a free boardwalk through reclaimed water-treatment wetlands packed with herons, ibises, alligators and turtles — one of the best birding spots in South Florida. **West Palm Beach** and the Norton Museum of Art are 25 minutes north; **Fort Lauderdale** is 30 minutes south. Miami is an hour-plus depending on I-95.
