The historic centre of Delray Beach, anchored by three former school buildings — the 1913 Delray Elementary, the 1925 Delray High, and a 1926 gymnasium — restored in the 1980s after the city nearly demolished them, and now collectively a National Register district. The buildings house the **Cornell Art Museum** (rotating contemporary exhibitions, mostly local and regional), the **Crest Theatre** (small live music and theatre venue), and the **Fieldhouse** event space.

Outside, the brick courtyards and the **Pavilion** open-air stage host a year-round programme of free concerts, food truck nights, and farmers' markets. It is the social and visual focal point of central Delray. The grounds are open day and night; museum hours vary.
