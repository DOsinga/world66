A small natural history centre for kids and curious adults, housed in the 1936 J.B. Evans House right on A1A across from the beach. The exhibits are modest — touch tanks with horseshoe crabs and starfish, a Florida shell collection, a small reef aquarium, a saltwater eel — but the daily shark feedings at 10:30am draw a crowd, and the grounds make a pleasant break from the beach.

Closed Mondays and Sunday afternoons. Admission about $8 for adults, less for children. Allow an hour, more if you have small kids.
