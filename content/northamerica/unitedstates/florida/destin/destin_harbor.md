Destin Harbor is the town's old fishing core and its modern evening stage. Charter boats line up here for deep-sea, bay, dolphin, and snorkeling trips, while HarborWalk Village adds restaurants, bars, and a steady flow of visitors. It is touristy, but it is also where Destin's fishing identity is easiest to see.
