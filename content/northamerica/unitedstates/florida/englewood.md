Englewood is a quieter Gulf Coast beach town between Venice and Port Charlotte. It does not have the polish of Sarasota or the high-rise resort feel of bigger Florida beaches, which is part of the point. The town sits behind Manasota Key, with Lemon Bay on one side and the Gulf of Mexico on the other.

The best reasons to stop are outdoors: Stump Pass Beach State Park at the south end of Manasota Key, the quieter Blind Pass Beach, and small water access points around Lemon Bay. The beaches are broad, shell-strewn and good for the kind of unhurried day Florida still does well when it is not trying too hard.

Englewood's centre is modest but pleasant, especially around Dearborn Street, where galleries, restaurants and the Lemon Bay Playhouse give the town more life than a pure resort strip. Come for a beach-and-bay day, not for nightlife or grand sights.
