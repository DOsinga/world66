Indian Mounds Park is a small Lemon Bay stop with trails, a boat ramp and a reminder that this coast had a long human story before modern beach towns arrived. It is not a major archaeological park, but it adds depth to an Englewood day.

Use it as a quiet bay-side break between beach stops, especially if you want to look across the water rather than sit on the Gulf sand all afternoon.
