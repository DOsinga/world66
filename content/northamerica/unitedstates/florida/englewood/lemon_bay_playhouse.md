Lemon Bay Playhouse is the clearest sign that Englewood is more than beach access and seafood plates. The small theatre stages plays and concerts on Dearborn Street, close to the town's galleries and restaurants.

Check the schedule rather than assuming there is something on. When the timing works, it makes an easy evening after a day on Manasota Key.
