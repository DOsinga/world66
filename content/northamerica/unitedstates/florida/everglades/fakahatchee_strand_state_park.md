Fakahatchee Strand State Park protects a long swamp forest on the western edge of the Everglades. It is known for rare tropical plants, orchids, bald cypress, royal palms, and the possibility of seeing wildlife such as alligators, deer, black bears, and Florida panthers.

This is a wilder stop than the developed Gulf Coast beaches nearby. Bring insect protection, expect wet conditions after rain, and check current access before setting out.
