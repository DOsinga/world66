The Gulf Coast Visitor Center is the western gateway to Everglades National Park and the main starting point for the Ten Thousand Islands. The surrounding mangrove islands and waterways are best explored by boat, kayak, or canoe.

Use the center for orientation, permits, and current conditions before heading into the backcountry. It is less about exhibits than access: this is where the Everglades turns from road trip into water maze.
