Dolphin Research Center is a marine education centre in the Middle Keys where visitors can learn about dolphins in a managed lagoon setting. Regular visits cover the animals, their care, and the centre's research and rescue work, while interactive programs require advance booking.

It is one of the more family-friendly stops in the Florida Keys and pairs well with other Marathon-area sights such as the Turtle Hospital and Seven Mile Bridge.
