The Florida Keys Memorial in Islamorada commemorates the victims of the 1935 Labor Day hurricane, one of the most devastating storms in United States history. The monument is solemn and compact, with a crypt containing ashes of victims who could not be individually identified.

It is not a long visit, but it changes how you read the Keys. The islands can feel carefree in good weather; this memorial is a blunt reminder of how exposed they are when the ocean turns.
