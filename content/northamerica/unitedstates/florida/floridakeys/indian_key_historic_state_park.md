Indian Key is a small island off Islamorada with the ruins of a 19th-century settlement, reached by kayak or boat across clear shallow water. Trails lead through the old townsite, where foundations and interpretive signs hint at the era of wrecking ships and salvaging cargo from the reef.

It is a better outing than it first appears on the map. The paddle is short in good conditions, the history is unusual, and the island feels far removed from the traffic just across the channel on US-1.
