The A. E. Backus Gallery and Museum focuses on the work of Fort Pierce painter A. E. "Beanie" Backus and the Florida Highwaymen, the group of Black landscape painters he encouraged. It is one of the better cultural stops in town if you want a sense of Florida's coastal landscape through local art.
