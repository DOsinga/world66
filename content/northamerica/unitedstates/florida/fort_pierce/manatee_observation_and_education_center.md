The Manatee Observation and Education Center sits by the Indian River Lagoon in downtown Fort Pierce. It focuses on manatees, seagrass, estuaries, and the fragile coastal ecosystems that make this stretch of Florida so productive.

Manatee sightings are seasonal and never guaranteed, but the centre is still a useful stop, especially with children.
