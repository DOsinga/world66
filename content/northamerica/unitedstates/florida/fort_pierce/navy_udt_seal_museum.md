The Navy UDT-SEAL Museum stands near the beaches where underwater demolition teams trained during World War II, making Fort Pierce one of the birthplaces of modern naval special warfare. Exhibits cover frogmen, SEAL teams, equipment, vehicles, and missions.

It is a specialist museum, but a strong one, and more grounded in local history than the subject might suggest.
