Savannas Preserve State Park protects a broad strip of freshwater marsh between Fort Pierce, Port St. Lucie, and Jensen Beach. Trails, paddling routes, and quiet observation points make it one of the better places on the Treasure Coast to see scrub, wet prairie, wading birds, and the open landscape that once covered much more of southeast Florida.
