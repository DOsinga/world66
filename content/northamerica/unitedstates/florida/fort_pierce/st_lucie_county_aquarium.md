The St. Lucie County Aquarium is small, but it does a good job of explaining Florida's linked marine habitats. Exhibits model seagrass beds, mangroves, estuaries, reefs, and deeper-water environments, with living displays rather than big-show spectacle.

It pairs well with the Manatee Center and a walk along the Fort Pierce waterfront.
