Sunrise Theatre is a restored Mediterranean Revival theatre in downtown Fort Pierce, opened in 1923 and still used for concerts, comedy, jazz, and touring shows. Its stucco facade, marquee, and intimate auditorium make it one of the city's better historic buildings.

Even if you are not catching a performance, it is worth a look while walking the old downtown blocks near the waterfront.
