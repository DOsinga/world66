Heritage Park and Cultural Center is the best reason to leave the beach for downtown Fort Walton Beach. Its centrepiece is the Fort Walton Temple Mound, a large earthen ceremonial mound built by the Pensacola culture more than 1,000 years ago. The mound is fenced and protected, but it still gives the low-rise downtown a sudden sense of age.

The Indian Temple Mound Museum beside it displays pottery, shell tools, stone points, and other artifacts from northwest Florida's Native cultures. The same city-run site also includes the Camp Walton Schoolhouse, Garnier Post Office Museum, and a Civil War exhibit building, so a short visit turns into a compact local-history circuit.
