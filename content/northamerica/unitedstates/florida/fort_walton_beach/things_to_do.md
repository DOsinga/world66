---
title: "Things to Do in Fort Walton Beach"
type: section
---

Fort Walton Beach works best when you split your time between the Gulf and the compact set of sights just off the sand. Okaloosa Island gives you the beach, pier, boardwalk, and Gulfarium in one easy strip. Downtown adds the Indian Temple Mound and the Heritage Park museums, while Eglin Air Force Base keeps one of the Panhandle's better free museums just north of town.
