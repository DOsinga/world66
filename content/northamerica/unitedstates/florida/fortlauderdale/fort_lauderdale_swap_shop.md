The Fort Lauderdale Swap Shop is part flea market, part drive-in movie complex, and part local curiosity. The market is big, busy, and uneven, but it is a useful stop if you enjoy rummaging through stalls rather than polished shopping streets.

The drive-in side is the unusual draw: the complex has long been known for its many outdoor screens. It is not Fort Lauderdale's prettiest sight, but it is a distinct piece of local culture away from the beach and Las Olas.
