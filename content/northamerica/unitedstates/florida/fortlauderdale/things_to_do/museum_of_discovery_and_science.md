The Museum of Discovery and Science anchors the south end of Riverwalk at SW 2nd Street and makes an excellent rainy-day stop, particularly with children in tow. The building is easy to spot: a 52-foot Great Gravity Clock on the exterior face counts down the seconds with kinetic sculpture. Inside, the exhibits cover Florida ecology, paleontology, aviation, and space exploration, with enough hands-on stations to keep kids moving for several hours.

The Florida EcoScapes section is the strongest part of the permanent collection, with live animals and detailed habitat re-creations including the coral reef tank, an Everglades exhibit with alligators, and a bat cave. The IMAX theater attached to the museum screens both educational films and Hollywood features on a five-story screen. The museum sits on SW 2nd Street at the western end of Riverwalk, convenient to parking and the New River water taxi stop.
