Gainesville is a college town in north-central Florida, home to the [University of Florida](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/gainesville/university_of_florida) and shaped almost entirely by it. When school is in session the city buzzes with energy; when students leave, it goes noticeably quiet. For visitors, Gainesville offers a pleasant surprise — more culture and nature than you expect from inland Florida.

The [Florida Museum of Natural History](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/gainesville/florida_museum_of_natural_history) is excellent, and the adjacent Butterfly Rainforest is one of the best of its kind in the country. The [Devil's Millhopper](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/gainesville/devils_millhopper) — a 120-foot sinkhole with its own cool microclimate — is one of the more genuinely unusual natural sights in the state. The surrounding area is rich in natural springs; Ichetucknee Springs and Ginnie Springs are both within an easy drive and offer crystal-clear water for swimming and tubing. The town itself has a decent live music scene, good affordable restaurants near the university, and the kind of independent bookshop culture that college towns do well. The [nightly bat emergence](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/gainesville/university_of_florida_bat_houses) at the campus bat houses is a free spectacle worth timing your evening around.
