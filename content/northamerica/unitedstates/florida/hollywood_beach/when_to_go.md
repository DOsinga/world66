---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Hollywood Beach is from November through April — warm and dry, with temperatures in the mid-20s Celsius (mid-70s Fahrenheit), low humidity, and clear skies. This is high season and the beach and Broadwalk are at their liveliest. December through February attracts snowbirds from the northeast and Canada, which keeps the place busy even in what would be off-season elsewhere.

Summer (June to September) is hot and humid with frequent afternoon thunderstorms. Temperatures regularly reach 32–35°C (90–95°F) with high humidity. The beach is less crowded than in winter because the locals know better. Hurricane season runs from June through November, peaking in August and September, though direct hits are rare. The shoulder months of October and May offer good value and good weather.
