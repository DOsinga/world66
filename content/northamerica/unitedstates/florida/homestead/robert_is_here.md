Robert Is Here is a tropical-fruit market, milkshake stand, petting zoo, and accidental Florida institution on the road from Homestead to Everglades National Park. The shakes — mamey, key lime, sapodilla, soursop, guanábana — are the main draw. They are made with whatever fruit is ripe that morning, blended thick with ice cream and milk.

The market sells dozens of tropical fruits you cannot find elsewhere, plus honey, hot sauces, and tropical jam. Out back there is a small petting zoo for kids and a covered seating area shaded by mango trees. A required stop, both on the way in and the way out.
