Schnebly Redland's Winery is an unexpected find — a tropical winery producing wines from lychee, mango, avocado, passion fruit, guava, and other South Florida crops. The tasting room operates in a lush tropical garden with a waterfall, and the wines are idiosyncratic: some are genuinely good, some are novelty items, and all are unlike anything produced in conventional wine regions. Worth a visit as a curiosity whether or not wine is your main interest.
