The two national parks are the main reason to stop in Homestead. [Everglades National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/homestead/everglades_national_park) — the largest subtropical wilderness in the US — is 15 minutes west, its main road threading south through sawgrass prairie to Florida Bay at Flamingo; alligators and (more rarely) crocodiles are both visible from the boardwalks. [Biscayne National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/homestead/biscayne_national_park) is mostly water — a marine park east of town with glass-bottom boat tours and snorkeling on some of Florida's best-preserved offshore reefs.

In town, [Coral Castle](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/homestead/coral_castle) is a genuinely strange monument: 1,100 tons of coral rock carved and assembled by one small Latvian immigrant working alone over 28 years, the mechanics of which remain unexplained. The [Fruit & Spice Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/homestead/fruit_and_spice_park) is a 37-acre tropical garden where fallen fruit can be tasted freely, and [Schnebly Redland's Winery](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/homestead/schnebly_winery) makes wines from lychee, mango, and other local tropical crops.
