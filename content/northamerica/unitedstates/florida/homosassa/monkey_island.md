A small artificial island in the middle of the Homosassa River, just outside the Riverside Resort, populated by a colony of spider monkeys who have lived there for sixty years. They have a small lighthouse, a faux castle, palm trees and not a lot to do — but they are easy to see, easy to photograph, and a useful argument with children that the world is sometimes very strange.

You cannot land on the island, but boats stop at the dock of MacRae's nearby for views from the river. Sunset is a good time — the monkeys are active, and the resort bar serves drinks on the deck.
