---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Manatees gather in the warm springs in greatest numbers from mid-November through March, when Gulf temperatures drop. December and January are peak season — and peak crowds at the springs.

Summer is hot, humid and prone to afternoon thunderstorms, but kayakers love the empty rivers and the early-morning light. May and October are the sweet spots: warm, mostly dry, and quiet at the parks.
