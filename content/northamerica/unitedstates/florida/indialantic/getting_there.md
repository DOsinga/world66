---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Indialantic is on the Atlantic coast of Brevard County, about 60 miles east of Orlando. The nearest airport is Melbourne Orlando International Airport (MLB), about 10 miles away, with limited connections — most visitors fly into Orlando International (MCO) and drive east on US-192 or SR-528 (the Bee Line Expressway), a drive of about an hour.

By car, Indialantic is reached via the Melbourne Causeway (SR-192) or the Eau Gallie Causeway (SR-518). There is no practical public transport. Having a car is essential.
