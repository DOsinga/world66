Cypress Creek Natural Area protects blackwater streams feeding the Loxahatchee River, just northwest of Riverbend Park. Trails and waterways run through pine flatwoods, wetlands, and historic Seminole War ground.

It is a good choice if you want a quieter natural area near Jupiter, with room for hiking, horseback riding, canoeing, and kayaking.
