The Key West Art and Historical Society preserves several of the island's most important cultural sites, with exhibitions that connect art, architecture, maritime history, and the odd politics of the Florida Keys. Its museum spaces are a useful corrective to the party-strip version of Key West.

Visit when you want context for the island's houses, harbour, and creative reputation. Check which society site and exhibition you are aiming for, since the organisation manages more than one historic property.
