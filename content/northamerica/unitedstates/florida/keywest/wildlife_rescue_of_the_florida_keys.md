Wildlife Rescue of the Florida Keys, often visited through the Key West Wildlife Center, cares for injured birds and other local wildlife in Indigenous Park. It is a modest place, but it gives visitors a close look at the fragile animal life that shares the island with traffic, fishing lines and tourism.

Treat it as a respectful short stop rather than a zoo. The most useful visit is one that supports the rehabilitation work and leaves you more alert to pelicans, herons and other birds around the Keys.
