KICCO Wildlife Management Area protects a broad stretch of oak hammock, scrub, and Kissimmee River country in central Florida. The Florida Trail crosses the area for about 13 miles, and shorter tracks make it useful for hiking, biking, birdwatching, and a quiet look at the river landscape away from the resort belt.

Access is more controlled than in a normal park. Vehicle entry may require a permit or quota authorization, and hunting seasons can change the mood of the place, so check the current rules before setting out.
