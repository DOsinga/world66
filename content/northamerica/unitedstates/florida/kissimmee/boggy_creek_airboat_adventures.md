Boggy Creek Airboat Adventures gives Kissimmee visitors an easy way to get out onto the marshes and
open water at the northern headwaters of the Everglades. The rides skim through sawgrass and cypress
edges where wading birds, turtles, and alligators are the main draw, making the experience feel very
different from the theme-park corridor nearby.

It is a commercial operation, but the setting is the reason it clears the travel threshold: wide
Florida sky, shallow lake country, and a landscape that helps explain why central Florida was once
more cattle range and wetland than resort strip. Early morning tours are usually the most
atmospheric and the best chance for wildlife activity.
