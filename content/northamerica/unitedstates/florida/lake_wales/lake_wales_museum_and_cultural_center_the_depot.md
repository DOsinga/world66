The Lake Wales Museum & Cultural Center occupies the town's restored Atlantic Coast Line railway depot, an appropriate home for local history in a city shaped by rail, citrus and Florida land-boom dreams. Exhibits cover the area's growth, industries and community life.

It is a modest but worthwhile stop before or after Bok Tower Gardens. The depot setting helps connect Lake Wales to the broader story of inland Florida towns built along tracks and groves rather than beaches.
