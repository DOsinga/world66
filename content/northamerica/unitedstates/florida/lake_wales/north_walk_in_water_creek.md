North Walk-in-Water Creek is a low-key Polk County nature preserve east of Lake Wales, protecting creek corridor, wetlands, and pine flatwoods. It is the kind of place where the reward is space, birds, and a sense of central Florida away from traffic.

This is not a polished attraction like Bok Tower Gardens, and that is part of its appeal. Wear shoes that can handle sandy or damp ground, carry water, and come for a quiet walk rather than a list of facilities.
