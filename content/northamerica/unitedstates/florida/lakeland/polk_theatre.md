The Polk Theatre is Lakeland's grand old movie palace, a 1928 Spanish Baroque revival building with an elaborately decorated interior — arched entryways, ornate plasterwork, a coffered ceiling, and the original Wurlitzer organ that was used for silent film accompaniment. The restoration in the 1990s brought it back to something close to its original splendour.

Today the theatre hosts live performances, film screenings, and community events. It remains the most architecturally distinguished building in downtown Lakeland. The interior can only be seen during performances or scheduled tours, but the facade is impressive from the street. Check the current events calendar if you want to catch a show.
