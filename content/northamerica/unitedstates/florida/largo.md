Largo is a city in Pinellas County on Florida's Gulf Coast, situated on the peninsula between Tampa Bay and the Gulf of Mexico. It is primarily a residential community rather than a tourist destination, but it sits just minutes from Clearwater Beach and the string of barrier island beaches that make this part of Florida so popular.

At the south end of the city, Pinewood Cultural Park holds two of the best free attractions in Pinellas County: the [Florida Botanical Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/largo/florida_botanical_gardens), 182 acres of themed gardens open every day of the year, and [Heritage Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/largo/heritage_village), an outdoor museum of relocated historic buildings spanning a century of local settlement. Both are genuinely worth a few hours.

[Largo Central Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/largo/largo_central_park) in the city centre hosts the Largo Cultural Center and an amphitheatre used for outdoor concerts. [Eagle Lake Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/largo/eagle_lake_park) in the north of the city is one of the better birding spots on the Pinellas peninsula. The [Pinellas Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/largo/getting_around) — a 38-mile paved cycling and walking path — passes through the city, making it easy to reach the beaches of Clearwater to the north or St. Petersburg to the south without a car.
