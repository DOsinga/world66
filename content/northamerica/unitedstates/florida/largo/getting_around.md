---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Largo is a car-dependent city. Most attractions are spread across a wide grid of residential streets that make walking between them impractical. The Pinellas Suncoast Transit Authority (PSTA) runs buses throughout the county, including routes to St. Petersburg and Clearwater, but service is infrequent outside peak hours.

The Pinellas Trail, a 38-mile paved multi-use path running the length of the county, passes through Largo and connects the city to Clearwater to the north and St. Petersburg to the south. It is a good option for cycling between the main parks and the botanical gardens.
