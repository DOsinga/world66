Cayo Costa State Park protects a barrier island of beaches, pine woods, and mangroves off the Lee County coast. You get there by private boat, charter, or ferry, which keeps it far quieter than the mainland beaches.

The draw is simple and strong: long shell-strewn beaches, birdlife, dolphins and manatees offshore, and trails through the island interior. Facilities are basic, with a small nature centre, picnic areas, boat slips, and primitive cabins, so bring what you need and treat the trip as a proper day out.
