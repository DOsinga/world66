Fort Myers Beach occupies Estero Island, a long barrier island connected to the mainland by a bridge south of Fort Myers proper. The northern end, around Times Square and the pier, is the most developed stretch — outdoor bars, seafood restaurants, souvenir shops, and the full vocabulary of a busy Florida beach town. It is loud, cheerful, and unambiguous about what it is. The beach itself is wide and white, with gentle surf and warm water for most of the year.

Further south along Estero Island the development thins and the beach becomes quieter. Lovers Key State Park marks the island's southern end, where a long stretch of undeveloped beach is accessible by tram or a short walk through mangroves — far fewer people than the resort end, good shelling, and a good chance of seeing dolphins in the channel. Hurricane Ian in 2022 caused major damage to Fort Myers Beach; rebuilding was still ongoing in 2025, and some businesses that closed have not returned.
