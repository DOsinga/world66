Four Mile Cove Ecological Preserve protects mangroves and river-edge habitat along the Caloosahatchee River in Cape Coral. A raised boardwalk makes the wetland easy to explore, with good chances for birds and, in season, manatees.

The preserve is best as a slow walk rather than a quick photo stop. Go early for cooler weather and quieter wildlife viewing.
