Koreshan State Historic Site preserves the strange remains of the utopian community Cyrus Teed founded as his "New Jerusalem." The Koreshans believed, among other things, that humanity lived inside a hollow earth, which gives the preserved settlement a wonderfully odd backstory.

Several century-old buildings survive among the grounds, making it part historic village and part state park. It is one of Lee County's more distinctive stops, especially if you like Florida history with a few sharp edges.
