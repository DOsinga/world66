Manatee Park is a winter wildlife-viewing spot on the warm-water outflow from a nearby power plant. When Gulf water temperatures drop, manatees often gather here, making the park one of the easiest places around Fort Myers to see them from shore.

The best chance is usually from December to March, especially after colder nights. Boardwalks and viewing areas make it simple for families, though sightings still depend on weather and water temperature.
