Matlacha Pass Aquatic Preserve protects a mangrove-lined channel and islands between Cape Coral and Pine Island. It is most rewarding from the water, where kayakers can explore sheltered passages and watch for wading birds.

The preserve is a quiet nature stop rather than a built attraction. Visitors come for paddling, birdwatching, and fishing, with the mangrove habitat giving a good look at the coastal ecology of Lee County.
