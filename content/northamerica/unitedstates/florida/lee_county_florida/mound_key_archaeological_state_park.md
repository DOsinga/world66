Mound Key Archaeological State Park protects a shell mound island in Estero Bay that was once the centre of Calusa power in southwest Florida. The height of the island is not natural land in the usual sense, but centuries of shells, bones, tools, and settlement debris built into a commanding place above the water.

There are no roads here, which is part of the appeal. You arrive by boat or kayak, then walk short trails through tropical vegetation and archaeological remains. It is one of the more evocative places in Lee County if you want Florida history before resorts, highways, and Spanish maps.
