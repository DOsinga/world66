Six Mile Cypress Slough Preserve protects a strip of cypress wetland on the edge of Fort Myers. A raised boardwalk takes you through the slough, where the water, trees, and shade make it feel much farther from the city than it is.

Birds are the easiest wildlife to spot, but alligators, turtles, otters, and wading birds also use the preserve. Go slowly and quietly; this is a place where patience pays off.
