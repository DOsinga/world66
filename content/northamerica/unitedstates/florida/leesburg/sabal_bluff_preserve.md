Sabal Bluff Preserve protects a slice of inland Florida habitat near Leesburg, with sandy uplands, wetland margins, and quiet trails rather than manicured park scenery. It is the sort of place where the details matter: palmettos, birds, changing water levels, and the sudden openness of scrubby ground.

The preserve suits travelers who want a simple nature walk away from the busier lakefronts and highways. Go early or late in the day for better light and wildlife activity, and expect a low-key preserve rather than a staffed attraction.
