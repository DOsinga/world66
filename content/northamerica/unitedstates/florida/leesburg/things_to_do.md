title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Leesburg's best assets are its lakes and its annual Bikefest. Most of the sights worth seeing are either on or near the water, or clustered in the compact historic downtown.

[Venetian Gardens](#venetian_gardens) is the centrepiece of the waterfront — 45 acres of landscaped parkland tucked into the northwest corner of Lake Harris, with walking bridges, boat ramps, a splash pad, and open lawns that make it the best free afternoon in town. [Lake Griffin State Park](#lake_griffin_state_park) offers a quieter alternative on the east side of the city: a boardwalk through ancient cypress hammock, alligators basking on the banks, and one of the better birdwatching sites in Lake County.

The [Downtown Historic District](#downtown_leesburg) runs along Main Street with brick-paved streets and turn-of-the-century storefronts that have survived in decent condition — a few galleries, antique shops, and restaurants make it worth a slow walk.
