Collier-Seminole State Park protects mangrove swamp, cypress swamp, salt marsh, river estuary, and pine flatwoods on the edge of the Everglades. Visitors come for wildlife viewing, hiking, canoeing, and the Bay City Walking Dredge, a National Historic Mechanical Engineering Landmark used in building the Tamiami Trail.
