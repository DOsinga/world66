Ten Thousand Islands National Wildlife Refuge protects a maze of mangrove islets, marsh, and shallow coastal water south of Marco Island. It is one of the largest mangrove estuaries in North America and a good place to look for birds, dolphins, and manatees.

Most visitors experience the refuge by boat or kayak. The appeal is the scale and quiet of the mangrove coast, not a single viewpoint or developed attraction.
