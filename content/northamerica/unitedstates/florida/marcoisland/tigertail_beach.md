Tigertail Beach sits on the northern side of Marco Island, where a lagoon separates the developed shore from the broader Gulf beach. The area is good for watching shorebirds and other coastal wildlife, and paths lead out toward the main beach.

It is one of Marco Island's better places to combine a beach day with a bit of nature, especially if you are happy to walk beyond the easiest access points.
