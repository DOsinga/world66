Black Point Wildlife Drive is a six-mile self-guided route through Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge. The loop passes pine flatwoods, marsh impoundments, and open water, with good chances of seeing wading birds, raptors, alligators, and seasonal migratory wildlife.

Go slowly, bring binoculars, and allow time to stop at pullouts. The drive is especially rewarding early in the morning or late in the day, when birds are most active and the light over the marshes is better.
