Cuban Memorial Boulevard is a median in Little Havana lined with monuments tied to Cuban exile history. Look for the eternal flame for the Bay of Pigs Invasion, a relief map of Cuba with a José Martí quotation, and memorials to figures from the exile community.

It is a short walk from the louder parts of Calle Ocho, but it gives useful context for why Little Havana matters to Miami beyond food, music, and street life.
