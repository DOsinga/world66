Frost Art Museum is the art museum of Florida International University. Its collections include American photography from the 1960s and 1970s, pre-Columbian objects, African and Asian bronzes, and Caribbean and Latin American art.

It is most useful for visitors already exploring western Miami or the FIU area, but the range of the collection makes it more than a campus gallery.
