Matheson Hammock Park is a bayside park south of Coral Gables, known for its man-made atoll pool, mangroves, picnic areas, and easy shoreline walks. The protected water makes it feel calmer than Miami's ocean beaches.

It is a good choice for a slow outdoor break: walk by Biscayne Bay, bring a picnic, or use it as a gentler family beach stop. The park is open daily from early morning to sunset.
