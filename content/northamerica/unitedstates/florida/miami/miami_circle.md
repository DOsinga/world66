The Miami Circle is a rare archaeological site at the mouth of the Miami River, where a ring of postholes and other marks in the limestone has been linked to the Tequesta people. The site is modest to look at, but its age and downtown location make it an unusual counterpoint to the surrounding Brickell towers.
