Rosa Sky sits on the roof of the AC Hotel on Brickell Avenue and aims for a more relaxed, party-leaning crowd than the loftier hotel bars. The signature drinks are whimsical and photogenic, there is a dedicated rosé list, and the kitchen turns out globally inspired tapas to share. A small pool deck and DJ sets give it a daytime-into-night rhythm.

The skyline views look north over the Brickell towers, best in the golden hour before the after-work crowd arrives. Weekends can get clubby, so it is a livelier choice than Sugar's polished calm.
