The University of Miami campus gives Coral Gables a leafy college-town edge, with broad lawns, modern academic buildings, and cultural stops such as the Lowe Art Museum and Gusman Concert Hall. It is worth a look if you are already exploring Coral Gables and want a quieter break from the beaches.
