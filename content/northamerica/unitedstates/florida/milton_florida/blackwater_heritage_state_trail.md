The Blackwater Heritage State Trail is a paved rail-trail that starts near downtown Milton and runs north through old neighbourhoods, pine woods, and rural country. It is flat, well kept, and easy for cyclists, walkers, runners, and wheelchair users.

Bring water, especially in warm weather. Once the trail leaves town, services thin out quickly.
