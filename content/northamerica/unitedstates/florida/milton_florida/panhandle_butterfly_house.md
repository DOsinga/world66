Panhandle Butterfly House is a seasonal butterfly house in Milton, with native plants, butterflies, and volunteers who explain the life cycle and habitat needs of the insects. It is small, but easygoing and especially good with children.

Opening is usually limited to the warmer months, so check before making a special trip. Admission has traditionally been free, with donations encouraged.
