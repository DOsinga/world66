---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Naples is not a late-night city, but it has a civilised drinking culture centred around the happy hour ritual and the sunset. The Gulf-view bars fill up from around 5pm as locals and visitors converge for the light show. Third Street South and Fifth Avenue South have the densest concentration of places; the Naples Beach Hotel is the classic spot for a drink with the water in front of you.
