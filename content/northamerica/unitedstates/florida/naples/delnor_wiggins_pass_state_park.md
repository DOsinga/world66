Delnor-Wiggins Pass State Park protects one of the better natural beaches around Naples, with pale sand, clear Gulf water, and mangrove edges near Wiggins Pass. It is more about swimming, shelling, and watching shorebirds than resort polish.

Arrive early in season because parking fills quickly. The park is a good counterweight to Naples' manicured downtown: still comfortable, but with enough wildness to remind you that this coast is an ecosystem, not just real estate.
