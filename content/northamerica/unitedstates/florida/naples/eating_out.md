---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Naples eats well for a city its size. The concentration of wealth means the restaurant scene punches well above its weight, particularly along Third Street South and Fifth Avenue South, where Italian, seafood, and farm-to-table kitchens sit cheek by jowl. Fresh Gulf seafood — grouper, snapper, stone crab in season — is the obvious focus, but the range runs from casual fish tacos to serious tasting menus.
