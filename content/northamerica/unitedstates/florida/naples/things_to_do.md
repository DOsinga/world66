---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Naples rewards visitors who slow down. The Gulf-front pier is the obvious starting point, but the real pleasures are spread across the city: a world-class botanical garden, a remarkable automotive museum, and a nature centre that rehabilitates injured wildlife on the edge of the Everglades. The historic Old Naples neighbourhood, centred on Third Street South and Fifth Avenue South, adds galleries, boutiques, and some of the best sunset-watching on the Gulf Coast.
