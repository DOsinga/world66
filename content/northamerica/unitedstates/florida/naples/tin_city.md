Tin City is a casual waterfront shopping and dining complex on Naples Bay, set in buildings that nod to the area's working waterfront past. It is touristy, but it has a more informal feel than the polished avenues and resort zones elsewhere in Naples.

Come for a low-key meal, souvenir browsing, or to connect with boat trips from the waterfront. It is not a cultural heavyweight, but it is a recognizable Naples stop and useful when you want something easy near downtown.
