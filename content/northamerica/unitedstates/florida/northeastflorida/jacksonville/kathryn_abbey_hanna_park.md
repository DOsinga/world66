Kathryn Abbey Hanna Park is one of Jacksonville's strongest outdoor escapes, combining Atlantic beach, dune habitat, bike trails, camping, and a freshwater lake. It feels wilder than the more developed beach towns nearby.

The park is large enough to fill a half-day: swim or walk the shore, ride the trails, then retreat under the pines. It belongs naturally in Jacksonville because the city sprawls all the way to the coast, and this is one of its best pieces of public shoreline.
