Machaba Balu Preserve protects salt marsh and coastal habitat within the wider Timucuan area north of Jacksonville. The landscape gives a sense of how this part of northeast Florida looked before modern development, with wetlands, open water, and wildlife habitat.

It is a low-key nature stop, best for travellers already exploring the Timucuan preserves and historic sites. Expect scenery and birdlife rather than major visitor facilities.
