The Museum of Contemporary Art Jacksonville is one of downtown's strongest cultural anchors. Its galleries focus on modern and contemporary work, with rotating exhibitions alongside pieces from the permanent collection, and the building sits right by Hemming Park and the central library.

MOCA works well as part of a downtown walk rather than a standalone pilgrimage. It gives Jacksonville's urban core a dose of current art and design, and it is especially useful on hot or rainy afternoons when the riverfront needs an indoor counterweight.
