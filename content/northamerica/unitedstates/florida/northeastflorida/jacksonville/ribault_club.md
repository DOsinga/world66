Ribault Club is a 1928 Spanish Revival mansion on Fort George Island, near the waterways and historic sites of the Timucuan Preserve. It is a short architectural stop rather than a full museum, but the building and setting make it worth pairing with Kingsley Plantation or the Talbot Islands.
