The Ritz Theatre and Museum anchors LaVilla, the historically Black neighbourhood that once made Jacksonville an important stop on the Chitlin' Circuit. The museum covers African-American life in northeast Florida, while the theatre keeps the building active with concerts, comedy, film, and community events.

It is one of Jacksonville's better cultural stops because the place and the story line up. Visit for the museum during the day, or check the calendar and come back at night when the theatre side of the Ritz gives LaVilla some of its old performance energy.
