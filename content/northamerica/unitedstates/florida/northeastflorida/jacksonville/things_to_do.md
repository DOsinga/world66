---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Jacksonville's size and sprawl mean you need to pick your neighbourhood and explore from there. Downtown and the Southbank Riverwalk are the logical starting point; Riverside-Avondale is where the architecture and independent restaurants are; the beach towns are a half-hour east. Give the city at least two full days — one for the river and neighbourhoods, one for the beaches and Fort Caroline.
