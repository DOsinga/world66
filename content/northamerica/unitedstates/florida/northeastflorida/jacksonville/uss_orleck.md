  The Orleck served in three wars and in the navies of two countries. Built in 1945, she saw action in the Korean War and Vietnam before being decommissioned by the US Navy in 1982. She was then sold to Turkey, which operated her as the TCG Yücetepe until 1993. She returned to the United States and is now moored at the Northbank Riverwalk as a museum ship — one of the few surviving Gearing-class destroyers and the only American warship to have served in both the Korean and Vietnam conflicts.

The USS Orleck is a Gearing-class destroyer moored at the Northbank Riverwalk and open to visitors as a naval museum. Guided tours take you through the ship's interior — the engine room, crew quarters, combat information centre, and deck guns — with commentary from veteran volunteers who served on similar vessels.

The Orleck's unusual history (three wars, two navies) makes it more interesting than a standard museum ship, and the guided tour format means the experience is shaped around the stories of the people who served rather than just the hardware. Tours run on weekends and by appointment.
