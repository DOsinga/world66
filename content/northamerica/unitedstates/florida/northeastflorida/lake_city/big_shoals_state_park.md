Big Shoals State Park protects one of the most surprising stretches of the Suwannee River: limestone bluffs, forest trails, and the largest whitewater rapids in Florida when the river is high enough. It feels very different from the flat, spring-fed Florida that many travellers expect.

The park is best for hikers, paddlers, and anyone who wants a wilder outing from Lake City. Conditions matter: the rapids depend on water level, and paddling them is not for beginners. Even without a boat, the trails to the bluffs make a worthwhile half-day in north Florida.
