---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Lake City's position at the I-10/I-75 junction makes it an easy launching pad for north Florida's natural and historical sites. White Springs, home to the Stephen Foster Folk Culture Center State Park, is about 15 miles northeast — a quiet riverside town on the Suwannee River with a state museum dedicated to Florida's official song. Jacksonville is an hour east for anyone wanting a city fix, beaches, or a larger airport.
