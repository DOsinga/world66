The Ichetucknee River is one of north Florida's finest natural places. Nine major springs feed a 6-mile run of crystal-clear water through a hardwood forest, maintaining a constant temperature of 68°F year-round. Tubing the upper section on a summer afternoon — drifting through the shade, watching turtles bask and manatees hover in the deeper pools — is as good as Florida gets.

The park operates a tram to the upper put-in during peak season (June through Labor Day), limiting daily tubers to protect the ecosystem. Outside summer, the park is far quieter and still beautiful — good for snorkeling, swimming at the head spring, and hiking the short trails through the floodplain forest.

Canoe and kayak rentals are available from outfitters outside the park on US-27. Reserve your spot early on summer weekends; the park fills its daily limit before noon.
