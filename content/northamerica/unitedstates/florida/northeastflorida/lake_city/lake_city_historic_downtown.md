The Lake City Historic Commercial District along Marion Avenue and US-90 preserves a stretch of late-19th and early-20th century commercial architecture. The Hotel Blanche, built in 1925, is the visual anchor — a brick building with ornate detailing that served as the social center of town during the railroad era. A handful of historic houses survive in the residential streets nearby, including the Horace Duncan House and the T.G. Henderson House, both on the National Register of Historic Places.

The downtown is modest but intact enough to give a sense of what a north Florida crossroads town looked like before the interstate bypassed the old highway. A short walk covers the main historic blocks.
