Fifteen miles northeast of Lake City on the banks of the Suwannee River, this state park in White Springs commemorates the composer Stephen Foster and Florida's official state song. Foster never actually visited the Suwannee — he picked the name from a map because it scanned well in the lyric — but the park leans into the irony gracefully.

The park houses a museum of Florida folk culture and craft, with demonstrations of traditional crafts such as quilting, weaving, and blacksmithing. The carillon tower plays Foster tunes on the hour. The real draw for many visitors is the setting itself: the Suwannee River here is wide and dark and slow, ringed by cypress trees, and the grounds are peaceful. The Florida Folk Festival, held each Memorial Day weekend, is one of the oldest and most beloved folk music events in the Southeast.
