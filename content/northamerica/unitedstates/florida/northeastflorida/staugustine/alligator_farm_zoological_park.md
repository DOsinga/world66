The Alligator Farm Zoological Park is one of St. Augustine's classic family attractions, with a serious collection of crocodilians behind the showier name. Alligators, crocodiles, reptiles and a busy rookery of wild wading birds make it more varied than a simple roadside animal stop.

It pairs naturally with the lighthouse and Anastasia Island sights. The bird rookery is especially good in nesting season, when photographers come for herons, egrets and spoonbills at surprisingly close range.
