Faver-Dykes State Park is the quieter, wilder counterpoint to St. Augustine's historic streets and busy beaches. The park protects pine flatwoods, scrub, marsh, and the winding edge of Pellicer Creek, a dark tidal waterway that leads toward the Matanzas River. It is a good choice when you want a half-day outside the city without turning the outing into a long expedition.

The appeal here is low-key nature rather than a grand landmark. Canoes and kayaks work well on the creek when the tide is right, and the trails give you a close look at northeast Florida's sandy uplands and bird life. Bring insect repellent in warm months, and expect a slower pace than Anastasia or Fort Matanzas: that is the point.
