Mission Nombre de Dios marks one of St Augustine’s founding landscapes, just north of the old city core on San Marco Avenue. The grounds are associated with the Spanish landing of 1565 and the first parish mission in what became the United States, with a chapel, shrine, museum, and large cross spread across a quiet waterfront site.

It is a religious place as well as a historic one, so it feels different from the fort, Flagler buildings, and commercial old-town streets. Visit for the founding story, but stay long enough to walk the grounds; the open space helps you imagine the city before the souvenir shops and carriage traffic arrived.
