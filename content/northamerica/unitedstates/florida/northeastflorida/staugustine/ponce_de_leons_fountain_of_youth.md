Ponce de Leon's Fountain of Youth Archaeological Park is one of St. Augustine's classic old Florida attractions. The legend is theatrical, but the site also sits on ground tied to early Spanish settlement and Indigenous Timucua history, which makes it more substantial than the name alone suggests.

Go in with the right expectations: part roadside tradition, part open-air history park, part archaeology stop. The grounds are pleasant, the demonstrations are family-friendly, and the location just north of the old city makes it easy to combine with the Castillo and the quieter streets beyond the main tourist drag.
