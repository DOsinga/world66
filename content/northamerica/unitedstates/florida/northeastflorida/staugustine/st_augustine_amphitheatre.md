The St. Augustine Amphitheatre is more than a place to catch a touring band. It sits on Anastasia Island near old coquina quarry land, and its modern life grew out of the city's 400th-anniversary commemorations in the 1960s. After years of decline, it was rebuilt into one of northern Florida's strongest outdoor venues.

Check the calendar before you come. On a good night the amphitheatre gives St. Augustine a different rhythm from the colonial streets across the water: warm air, pine edges, food stalls, and a crowd that is usually more local than the historic district. Even outside a show, the Saturday market can make the grounds worth a look.
