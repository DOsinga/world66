Washington Oaks Gardens State Park combines formal gardens with a stretch of northeast Florida barrier-island landscape. The gardens are the gentle side of the visit, while the shore gives you coquina rock, wind, and Atlantic surf.

It is a good change of pace from St. Augustine's historic core if you have a car and want a coastal walk with more texture than a standard beach stop.
