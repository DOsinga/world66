---
title: "Things to Do in Ocala"
type: section
---

Ocala's best sights split between water, horses, and frontier history. Silver Springs is the essential stop, especially if you have never seen a Florida spring from a glass-bottom boat or kayak. Fort King gives the city a specific historical weight, and the Appleton and World Equestrian Center explain why horses are so central to Ocala's identity.
