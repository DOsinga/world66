Aerophile is the tethered balloon ride over Disney Springs, lifting riders high enough for a broad look across Walt Disney World. It is a short, weather-dependent experience, but the view is the point: lakes, hotels, roads, and theme-park landmarks spread out in every direction.

Do it for the panorama rather than for thrills. Wind can stop flights, so check before queuing and keep it as a flexible add-on to a Disney Springs visit.
