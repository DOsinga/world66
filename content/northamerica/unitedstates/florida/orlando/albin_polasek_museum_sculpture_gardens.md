The Albin Polasek Museum & Sculpture Gardens preserves the Winter Park home and studio of Czech-American sculptor Albin Polasek. The house galleries show his work in a domestic setting, while the garden places sculptures among lawns, palms, and lake views.

It is one of the calmer cultural stops in the Orlando area. Pair it with nearby Winter Park museums or a walk around the lakes if you want a break from the theme-park side of central Florida.
