title: A World of Orchids
type: poi

A tropical greenhouse in Kissimmee, about twenty minutes south of International Drive, that for years displayed thousands of flowering orchids alongside exotic birds, fish, and small animals. It was the kind of low-key, non-corporate attraction that gave the Orlando area its older, pre-theme-park character; check ahead before making the trip, as it has gone in and out of operation.
