---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The best drinking in Orlando is away from the tourist corridor. Downtown, [The Social](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/the_social) on Orange Avenue books the most consistent live music in the city — indie rock, electronic, and underground touring acts in an intimate room. [Pharmacy Bar](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/pharmacy_bar) on North Orange has a good craft beer list and a beer garden. Nearby, [Maxine's on Shine](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/maxines_on_shine) in Thornton Park is a no-nonsense neighbourhood bar with a covered porch and Wednesday trivia.

East of downtown, the Audubon Park neighbourhood has [Redlight Redlight](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/redlight_redlight) — a beer parlour with 300-plus bottles and 40 taps focused on Florida craft and Belgian production. CityWalk at Universal and Disney Springs both offer polished, tourist-facing bar experiences; Wall Street Plaza downtown is the main late-night cluster if you want something louder and more concentrated.
