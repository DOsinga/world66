The Basilica of Mary, Queen of the Universe is a large Catholic church built to serve Orlando's constant stream of visitors as much as its resident community. Its scale, stained glass, and shrine-like gardens make it more interesting than a routine parish stop.

It is most relevant for travellers staying near Lake Buena Vista who want a quiet hour away from theme parks and malls. The basilica is active, so visit respectfully and check service times if you want to see the interior without interrupting worship.
