The Morse Museum in Winter Park holds the world's largest collection of work by Louis Comfort Tiffany, including glass, lamps, jewelry, pottery, paintings, and the chapel interior Tiffany designed for the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition. It is one of the Orlando area's strongest art stops, especially for decorative arts.
