Discovery Cove is one of Orlando's more controlled theme-park experiences, built around snorkeling lagoons, rays, tropical fish, and scheduled marine-life encounters rather than rides. The limited-capacity format makes it feel different from the city's large gate-and-queue parks.

It is expensive and should be planned as a full-day booking, but it is travel-worthy for visitors who want a water-based park day without leaving the Orlando resort corridor. Check the current package rules carefully, since inclusions and animal encounters change over time.
