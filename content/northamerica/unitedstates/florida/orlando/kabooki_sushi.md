One of Orlando's most serious sushi restaurants, run by chef Henry Moso on the east end of Colonial Drive near the Mills 50 district. The omakase experience at the bar is the way to go — a chef-directed progression of nigiri and small dishes that runs well beyond what the menu suggests is possible in this city. The à la carte rolls are creative without tipping into gimmicky. Reservations recommended, especially for the omakase seats.
