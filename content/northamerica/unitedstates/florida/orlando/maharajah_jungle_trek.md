Maharajah Jungle Trek is the Asian wildlife walking trail inside Disney's Animal Kingdom. The route is staged as a weathered palace and ruin complex, with viewing areas for tigers, fruit bats, birds, and other animals along the way.

It is worth doing slowly rather than treating it as a shortcut between rides. The aviary and tiger viewing areas are the highlights, and the shaded path is a useful break from the more crowded parts of the park.
