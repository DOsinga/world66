A neighbourhood bar in Thornton Park housed in a 1930s building, with a covered front porch perfect for warm evenings and a no-nonsense interior that hasn't changed much in years. The beer selection is adequate rather than ambitious, but Maxine's earns its reputation through atmosphere and consistency — a proper local bar in a part of town that leans increasingly toward the polished and expensive. Wednesday trivia nights draw a loyal crowd.
