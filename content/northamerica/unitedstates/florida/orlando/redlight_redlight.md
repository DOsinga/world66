A serious beer bar in the Audubon Park neighbourhood east of downtown, with around 300 bottles from Florida craft breweries, Belgian Trappists, and American wild ales alongside 40-plus taps. The list changes constantly and the staff know what they are pouring. The room is small, relaxed, and entirely without pretense. No TVs, no loud music — this is a place to drink attentively. The covered outdoor area works year-round.
