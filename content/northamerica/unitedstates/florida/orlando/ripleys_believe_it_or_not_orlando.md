Ripley's Believe It or Not! Orlando is a classic International Drive odditorium: part curiosity museum, part optical-illusion playground, part air-conditioned escape from the theme-park machine. It is commercial and kitschy, but that is exactly the point.

Go with children, teenagers, or a high tolerance for the strange and silly. It should not outrank Orlando's major parks or real museums, but it can fill a hot afternoon or a low-stakes evening on I-Drive.
