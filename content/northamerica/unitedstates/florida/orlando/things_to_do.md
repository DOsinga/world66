title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Orlando is one of the world's top theme park destinations. [Walt Disney World](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/disney_world) is the centrepiece — a small city of parks including [Magic Kingdom](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/magic_kingdom), [Epcot](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/epcot), [Hollywood Studios](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/hollywood_studios), and [Animal Kingdom](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/animal_kingdom), plus two water parks and a shopping district. [Universal Orlando](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/universal_orlando) runs a close second, with Islands of Adventure, Universal Studios Florida, and the 2025-opened Epic Universe together making it a multi-day destination with the Wizarding World of Harry Potter split across two halves.

[International Drive](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/international_drive) is the tourist corridor connecting the parks — a strip of hotels, outlet malls, and secondary attractions that functions as a self-contained ecosystem. Beyond the theme park cluster, [SeaWorld](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/seaworld) offers a mix of marine exhibits and serious roller coasters, and [Gatorland](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/gatorland) is the most Orlando-original thing you can do — alligators, zip lines, and cypress swamp for a fraction of the big-park price. The [Kennedy Space Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/kennedy_space_center) is an hour east on the Atlantic coast and earns a full day.

Downtown Orlando has its own separate character: [Lake Eola Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/lake_eola_park) with its Sunday farmers' market, the [Orange County Regional History Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/orange_county_regional_history_center) in the restored courthouse, and the [Dr. Phillips Center for the Performing Arts](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/orlando/dr_phillips_center) for Broadway touring productions and concerts. Park tickets are expensive and the parks are vast — pick your days carefully to avoid spending most of your time in queues.
