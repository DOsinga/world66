Bulow Plantation Ruins Historic State Park protects the remains of a large antebellum sugar plantation, including coquina ruins of the sugar mill. The park also has trails, a boat ramp, and access to Bulow Creek, a state canoe trail.

It is a strong stop for visitors interested in Florida history, quiet hiking, paddling, and wildlife such as birds and manatees.
