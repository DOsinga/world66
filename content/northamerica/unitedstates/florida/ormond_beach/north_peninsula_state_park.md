North Peninsula State Park protects a quieter stretch of barrier island north of Ormond Beach, where dunes, coastal scrub, and Atlantic beach take over from development. It is the kind of park that rewards travelers who want a simple walk, birding, or a less crowded shoreline rather than facilities and spectacle.

Pair it with nearby Tomoka State Park for a fuller nature day. North Peninsula is the ocean-facing half of that equation: wind, sand, low vegetation, and the chance to see what this coast looks like when buildings step back.
