Ormond Beach's appeal rests on two complementary pillars: its history as the original Birthplace of Speed, where early automobiles set land speed records on the packed Atlantic sand, and its natural areas, particularly Tomoka State Park at the northern end. The Casements, John D. Rockefeller's winter cottage, anchors the historic district along the Halifax River.
