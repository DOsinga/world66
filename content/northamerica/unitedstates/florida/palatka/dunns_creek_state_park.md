Dunns Creek State Park is a low-key natural outing near Palatka, protecting pine flatwoods, sandhills, wetlands, and creek corridors tied to the St. Johns River system. It is not as landscaped as Ravine Gardens, but it gives a better sense of the wilder country around town.

The park is best for hiking, birding, and a quiet half-day outdoors. Travellers expecting dramatic scenery may find it understated, but those using Palatka as a base for north Florida rivers and springs will appreciate the unpolished habitat.
