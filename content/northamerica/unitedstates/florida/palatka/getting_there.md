---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Palatka is in north-central Florida, about 60 miles south of Jacksonville and 30 miles east of Gainesville on the St. Johns River. The nearest major airport is **Jacksonville International** (JAX), roughly an hour's drive north on US-17. **Gainesville Regional** (GNV) is about 45 minutes west and serves a handful of domestic routes.

Amtrak's Silver Star and Silver Meteor trains stop at **Palatka station** on the line between New York and Miami — one of the more useful and underused train options in north Florida. The trip from Jacksonville takes about an hour; from Orlando about 2.5 hours.

By car, US-17 runs north-south through the heart of Palatka. From I-95, take exit 311 at Bunnell and drive west on SR 100; from I-75, take exit 384 at Gainesville and drive east on SR 20 or SR 26. The town is compact and easy to navigate.
