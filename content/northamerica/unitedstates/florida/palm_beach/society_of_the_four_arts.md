The Society of the Four Arts is a cultural institution on the Lake Worth Lagoon, founded in 1936 to bring art, music, drama, and literature to Palm Beach in the season (roughly November through April, when the town is populated). It operates a gallery, a library, a film series, lecture programmes, and botanical gardens, all in a pleasant lakefront setting.

The Philip Hulitar Sculpture Garden attached to the Society is open to the public and free to enter. It is a formal garden of hedged enclosures containing rotating sculpture exhibitions, with views across the lagoon. The main gallery spaces host temporary exhibitions during the season, with an emphasis on American art and photography. The library — officially the Esther B. O'Keeffe Library — is a good public library with an art-history focus, also free to use. Even outside the season, the gardens and library building make for a pleasant stop, especially compared to the crowded Worth Avenue sidewalks a few minutes away.
