Palm Harbor sits north of [Clearwater](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/clearwater) on the quieter side of Pinellas County, close to [Tarpon Springs](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/tarpon_springs), Dunedin, and the Gulf barrier islands. It is suburban and spread out, but not without character: old village pockets, live oaks, bayou water, golf courses, and easy access to some of the better parks on the county's north coast.

This is not the place for a classic beach-strip holiday. Stay here if you want a calmer base within reach of [Honeymoon Island](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/clearwater/honeymoon_island), Clearwater Beach, and Tarpon Springs without sleeping in the thick of the crowds. [Wall Springs Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/palmharbor/wall_springs_park) is the local nature highlight, while [Lake Tarpon](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/palmharbor/lake_tarpon) gives the area freshwater fishing and paddling that the beach towns lack.

Palm Harbor's old waterfront communities, [Ozona](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/palmharbor/ozona) and [Crystal Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/palmharbor/crystal_beach), are small but worth a slow look if you like Gulf Coast backroads. In winter and spring the area fills with snowbirds and golfers, especially around [Innisbrook](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/palmharbor/innisbrook_resort_and_golf_club), so book early if you are coming for tournament week or peak beach season.
