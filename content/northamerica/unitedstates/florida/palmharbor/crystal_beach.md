Crystal Beach is a quiet Gulf-side community within Palm Harbor, laid out along narrow streets that end near St. Joseph Sound. It has old cottages, palms, small docks, and a local pier atmosphere that feels far removed from Clearwater Beach's crowds.

There is no big beach here, despite the name. Come for a short stroll, sunset light over the sound, or as part of a slow backroad route between Dunedin, Ozona, and Wall Springs Park.
