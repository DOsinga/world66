Innisbrook is Palm Harbor's best-known address, a large golf resort whose Copperhead Course hosts the PGA Tour's Valspar Championship. Even if you are not staying there, tournament week brings a very different energy to an otherwise quiet suburb.

Golfers come for the courses; non-golfers should treat it mainly as context unless they are attending an event. If you are visiting during the championship, expect higher room rates and heavier traffic in the north Pinellas area.
