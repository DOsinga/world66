Ozona is a small old waterfront community tucked between Palm Harbor and Dunedin, with marinas, cottages, seafood spots, and a slower village feel than the commercial strips along US-19. It is not a formal attraction, but it is one of the area's better places for an unhurried wander.

The best way to experience it is to combine a short drive or bike ride with lunch near the water. Keep expectations modest: Ozona's charm is in the scale, not in a checklist of sights.
