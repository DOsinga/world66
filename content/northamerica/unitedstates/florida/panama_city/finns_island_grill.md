Finn's is a small, perpetually busy Caribbean-influenced spot a few blocks south of the courthouse, with a short menu of fish tacos, grilled mahi, jerk chicken, and a rotating list of cocktails based around dark rum. The space is funky — corrugated tin, surf-shop posters, picnic-table seating — and it works.

The pulled-pork sandwich with mango barbecue sauce is the local favourite; the blackened grouper sandwich is also reliably good. Service is friendly and slow, and you'll wait if you go at peak times. No reservations; arrive early or be prepared to put your name down and wander Harrison Avenue for half an hour.
