Frank Brown Park is Panama City Beach's big municipal recreation area, set back from the sand near Pier Park. Travellers use it for walking paths, playgrounds, sports facilities, and a breather from the resort strip.

It is most useful for families staying nearby or anyone in town for a tournament or event. The park will not replace the beach, but it gives the city a practical green space when you want shade, exercise, or a less commercial afternoon.
