The Museum of Man in the Sea is a small, specialist museum devoted to diving, submarines, and the history of underwater exploration. Panama City Beach is usually sold as sand and seafood, so the museum gives the coast a more technical and maritime angle.

The exhibits are a little old-fashioned in places, but that suits the subject. Expect diving helmets, submersible equipment, decompression gear, and stories from the experimental side of going below the surface. It is best for curious adults and older children rather than anyone expecting a polished aquarium-style attraction.
