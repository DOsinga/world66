St. Andrews State Park occupies the eastern tip of the barrier peninsula, a few miles from the condo strip, and it is a completely different experience. The beach here is the same white quartz sand as everywhere else on this stretch of the Panhandle, but without the development, the crowds thin out fast.

The park protects a mix of habitats: pine flatwoods, tidal marshes, dunes, and a stretch of Gulf beach that sees a fraction of the visitors of the main tourist corridor. A jetty extends into the mouth of the channel between the Gulf and St. Andrews Bay, and it's a fine spot to fish or watch dolphins work the pass. Kayak and paddleboard rentals are available, and a short boat service runs out to Shell Island, an undeveloped barrier island across the inlet, where the snorkeling in the clear shallow water is excellent.
