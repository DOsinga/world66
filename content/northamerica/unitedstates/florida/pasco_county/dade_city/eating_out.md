---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The downtown food scene is small but characterful.

**Lunch on Limoges** is the local favourite — a lunch-only spot on 7th Street that serves a single daily-changing menu on actual Limoges china. Mini-muffin appetisers are the signature. Reservations recommended.

**Kafe Kokopelli** occupies a converted auto-mechanic's garage. Tables made from the original Ford dealership's garage doors and a southwestern-inspired menu. Good for an unhurried dinner.

**A Matter of Taste** is the third pillar of downtown lunches: salads, sandwiches, daily specials, and a small selection of pies. Good and dependable.
