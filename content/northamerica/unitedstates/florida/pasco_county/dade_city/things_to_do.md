Dade City rewards a slow morning. Park near the [Pasco County Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pasco_county/dade_city/pasco_county_courthouse), wander the brick streets of the [Church Street Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pasco_county/dade_city/church_street_historic_district), drop into the antique shops, and have lunch downtown.

Out of town there are two good detours. [Pioneer Florida Museum and Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pasco_county/dade_city/pioneer_florida_museum) is a serious open-air collection of nineteenth-century buildings. [Kumquat Growers](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pasco_county/dade_city/kumquat_growers) is the largest kumquat farm in the country and the source of the strange and excellent kumquat pies the town is locally famous for.

For something more active, the paved [Hardy Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pasco_county/dade_city/hardy_trail) runs through downtown and links to longer rail-to-trail networks heading north.
