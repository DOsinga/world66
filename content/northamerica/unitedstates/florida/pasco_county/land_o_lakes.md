Land O Lakes is a spread-out community in central Pasco County, north of [Tampa](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/tampabayarea/tampa). It has no old downtown or easy pedestrian centre; most of the place is suburban Florida, strung along US-41 and State Road 54.

That makes it more useful as a local base than a destination in itself. The better reason to stop is the green edge around it: lakes, flatwoods, and preserves that give a glimpse of the quieter Pasco County landscape before the Tampa suburbs took over.

You will want a car here. Shops, restaurants, and trailheads are scattered, and public transport is slow outside the main roads.
