---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Pensacola's food scene leans heavily on Gulf seafood and Southern cooking, with a growing number of good restaurants in the revitalised downtown area. The [Gulf seafood restaurants](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola/gulf_seafood_restaurants) along the waterfront serve fresh-caught grouper, oysters, and shrimp with little ceremony. Palafox Street downtown has the widest range of options, from casual lunch spots to more ambitious dinner restaurants.
