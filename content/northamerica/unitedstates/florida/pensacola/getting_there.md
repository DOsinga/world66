---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Pensacola International Airport (PNS) is a small regional airport with direct flights to Atlanta, Charlotte, Dallas, Houston, and other hub cities, and connections to most US destinations. American, Delta, Southwest, and United all serve Pensacola. The airport is about 8 km northeast of downtown.

By road, Interstate 10 runs through Pensacola connecting it to Jacksonville (650 km east) and New Orleans (450 km west). US-98 runs along the Gulf Coast connecting to Destin and Fort Walton Beach to the east. There is no passenger rail service to Pensacola.
