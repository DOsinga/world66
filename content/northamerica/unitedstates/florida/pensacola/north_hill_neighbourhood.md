North Hill, immediately north of the Palafox downtown area, is a neighbourhood of fine Victorian and Edwardian houses built during Pensacola's prosperous late 19th and early 20th century. The preservation district contains over 500 historic structures on tree-lined streets, and is one of the most intact Victorian residential areas in Florida. Walking the streets from the Historic Pensacola Village northward into North Hill gives a sense of the city's pre-air-conditioning prosperity — covered porches, high ceilings, and generous gardens designed for a different relationship between indoors and outdoors.
