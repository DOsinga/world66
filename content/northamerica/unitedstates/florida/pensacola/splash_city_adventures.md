Splash City Adventures is Pensacola's local amusement and water park, with slides, pools, rides, and arcade-style attractions aimed mostly at families. It is not the reason to come to the Gulf Coast, but it can be a useful break from beach weather, long drives, or museum-heavy days.

The park sits north of the historic centre, so it works best with a car. Check opening days before going, as seasonal hours matter more here than at the city's bigger sights.
