---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Beyond the beach, Pensacola rewards exploration. The [Seville Square Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola/seville_square_historic_district) and [Palafox Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola/palafox_street) make for a good half-day on foot. The [National Naval Aviation Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola/national_naval_aviation_museum) is free and genuinely impressive — one of the largest aviation museums in the world, with over 150 aircraft spanning a century of naval flight. The Civil War forts along the barrier islands — [Fort Pickens](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola/fort_pickens) and [Fort Barrancas](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola/fort_barrancas) — are undervisited and historically significant. Divers should look into the [USS Oriskany](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola/uss_oriskany), an aircraft carrier sunk as an artificial reef and one of the best wreck dives in the Gulf of Mexico.
