The USS Oriskany, a decommissioned Essex-class aircraft carrier, was sunk 38 km south of Pensacola in May 2006 to create an artificial reef. The "Great Carrier Reef" sits upright in about 65 metres of water (210 feet), with the flight deck accessible to advanced recreational divers at around 37 metres. Technical divers can explore deeper sections. The wreck is encrusted with marine life after nearly 20 years underwater and attracts significant fish populations.

Several Pensacola-based dive operators run day trips to the Oriskany. The dive requires advanced certification at minimum; the deeper sections are technical territory. Visibility in the Gulf of Mexico varies by season but is often excellent. The wreck sits alongside older wrecks from World War II — Pensacola Bay and the surrounding waters are one of Florida's major diving destinations.
