Casino Beach is the main public beach at the heart of Pensacola Beach, set beside the pier and the most active cluster of Gulf-front restaurants and shops. The name comes from an older beach casino rather than gambling, but the location still feels like the resort's front door.

Come here when you want easy access, lifeguarded sand in season, and a straightforward beach day without hunting for a quieter pull-off. It is busier than the national seashore stretches nearby, but that convenience is exactly why many visitors start here.
