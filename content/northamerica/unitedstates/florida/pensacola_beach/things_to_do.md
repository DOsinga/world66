---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pensacola Beach is primarily a beach destination, and the beach is genuinely excellent — white quartz sand that is fine and cool even in mid-summer, clear green Gulf water, and about eight miles of shoreline to spread out on. The [Gulf Islands National Seashore](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola_beach/gulf_islands_national_seashore) protects the quieter eastern end of the island. [Pensacola Pass](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola_beach/pensacola_pass) at the western tip is good for fishing and watching boat traffic.

Watersports are the main activity: parasailing, jet skiing, paddleboarding, and dolphin-watching boat tours are all available from the boardwalk area. The [Navarre Beach Marine Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/pensacola_beach/navarre_beach_marine_park) to the east has one of Florida's longest fishing piers and good snorkelling around the artificial reef.
