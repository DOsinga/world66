The Sarasota Mangrove Tunnels are narrow paddling routes through the mangroves near Lido Key. Kayaks and paddleboards can move through shaded passages where roots, birds, fish, and shallow water make the route feel more intimate than an open bay paddle.

Go for an active nature stop close to St. Armands and the Sarasota waterfront.
