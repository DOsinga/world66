Van Wezel Performing Arts Hall is one of Sarasota's most recognizable modern landmarks, set on the bayfront and known for its bold purple exterior. It anchors the city's performing-arts scene with touring music, theatre, comedy, dance, and community events.

The hall is most rewarding when you attend a show, but its architecture and waterfront position also make it a useful landmark while exploring central Sarasota. For culture-focused travellers, it is a practical way to connect the city with an evening plan.
