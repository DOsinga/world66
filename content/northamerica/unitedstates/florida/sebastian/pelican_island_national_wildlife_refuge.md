Pelican Island is small, but its importance is large. In 1903 Theodore Roosevelt protected the rookery here, making it the first national wildlife refuge in the United States and setting a pattern for protected bird habitat across the country.

The refuge is best as a slow stop: boardwalks, lagoon views, mangroves, and birds rather than big-ticket spectacle. Bring binoculars if you have them.
