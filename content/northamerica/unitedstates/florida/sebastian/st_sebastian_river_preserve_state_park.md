St. Sebastian River Preserve State Park is the quieter inland counterpoint to the inlet. The preserve protects pine flatwoods, scrub, and river habitat, with long trails for walking, cycling, and horseback riding.

It is a good choice when the beach is busy or windy. The appeal is space and Florida habitat rather than facilities.
