Sebastian's strongest sights are outdoors. [Sebastian Inlet State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/sebastian/sebastian_inlet_state_park) is the main draw for surfing, fishing, beaches, and two compact local museums. [Pelican Island National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/sebastian/pelican_island_national_wildlife_refuge) adds a quieter conservation story, while [St. Sebastian River Preserve State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/sebastian/st_sebastian_river_preserve_state_park) turns inland toward pine flatwoods, scrub, and long sandy trails.
