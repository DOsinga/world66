Sebring International Raceway is one of the oldest continuously operating road-racing circuits in the United States. Its signature event is the 12 Hours of Sebring, a long-running endurance race that gives this small central Florida city an outsized name in motor sport.

Outside race periods it is more specialist interest than casual sightseeing, but for racing fans it is the reason Sebring is on the map.
