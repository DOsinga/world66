Apollo Beach is the northern district's wild Atlantic beach, reached from New Smyrna Beach rather than Titusville. It has fewer Space Coast rocket views than Playalinda but easier access to Turtle Mound, Eldora, and the lagoon-side stops.

Facilities are minimal and the beach can feel remote even when the parking lots are busy. It is good for long walks, surf fishing, and watching shorebirds, but bring shade and water because there is little commercial backup inside the park.
