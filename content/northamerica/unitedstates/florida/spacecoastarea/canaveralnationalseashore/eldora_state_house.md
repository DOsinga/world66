The Eldora State House, also known as the Moulton-Wells House, is a surviving piece of the small lagoon-side settlement of Eldora. The house sits near Mosquito Lagoon and helps explain the human history of a coast that now feels almost empty.

It is a quiet stop, best appreciated if you like old Florida stories: fishing, citrus, boat access, and communities that rose and faded before the park protected the land. Check whether the interior is open before counting on more than the exterior and setting.
