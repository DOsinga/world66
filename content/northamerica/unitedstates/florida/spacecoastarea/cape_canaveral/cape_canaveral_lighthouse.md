Cape Canaveral Lighthouse predates the space age and still stands inside the active launch range. That setting makes it more interesting than a standard coastal lighthouse: you are looking at old maritime Florida in the middle of rocket country.

Access is by organized tour, not casual drop-in, so book ahead and expect identification rules.
