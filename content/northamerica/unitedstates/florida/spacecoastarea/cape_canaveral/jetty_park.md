Jetty Park is Cape Canaveral's most useful public outdoor space. It has beach access, a long pier, port views, and one of the better off-site angles for watching rockets when the trajectory and weather cooperate.

On ordinary days, it works just as well for fishing, ship-spotting, or an easy beach afternoon before or after a cruise.
