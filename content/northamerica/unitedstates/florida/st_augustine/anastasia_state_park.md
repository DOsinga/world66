Anastasia State Park lies on the barrier island across the Matanzas River from downtown St. Augustine, and it is easily the best beach in the area. Four miles of undeveloped Atlantic beach fronted by tall dunes make it an unusually unspoiled stretch of coast for this part of Florida. The water is warm, the waves are gentle, and the crowds are more manageable than on the commercial beaches further south.

The park has more to it than just the beach. A tidal lagoon system on the inland side supports osprey, great blue herons, and roseate spoonbills year-round; the maritime hammock trail passes through dense oak and cabbage palm forest. Canoe and kayak rentals are available at the park for exploring the salt marsh. The park gets busy on weekends from spring through autumn — arrive early or come during the week.
