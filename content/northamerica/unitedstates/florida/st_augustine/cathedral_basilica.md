The Cathedral Basilica of St. Augustine anchors the south side of the Plaza de la Constitución and has occupied this spot in one form or another since the city's founding in 1565. The current building is a handsome Spanish Colonial structure with a distinctive bell tower; the interior is cool and dignified, with good tilework and painted wooden ceiling beams.

The basilica is an active parish church and visitors are welcome during non-service hours. The plaza in front of it — the Plaza de la Constitución — is one of the oldest public squares in the United States, laid out according to the 1573 Laws of the Indies that governed Spanish colonial town planning. The market building on the east side of the plaza dates from 1824.
