The Colonial Quarter is a living-history museum on St. George Street that reconstructs life in 18th-century Spanish St. Augustine. Costumed interpreters demonstrate period skills — musket firing, blacksmithing, leatherworking — in recreated colonial structures representing the various phases of the city's history. The site is hands-on and family-friendly.

It is a step up from the typical living history experience because it integrates the actual archaeology of the site. The reconstructed buildings stand on their original foundations, and the interpretive program draws on genuine documentary and material evidence. The musket demonstrations on the upper deck, with views over the old town toward the Castillo, are a highlight. Allow an hour to get the most out of the interpreter talks.
