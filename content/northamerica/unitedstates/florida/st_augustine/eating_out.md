---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

St. Augustine's restaurant scene has improved dramatically in recent years. The tourist-focused places on St. George Street are mostly avoidable, but a short walk off the main drag turns up some genuinely good cooking. The city has a strong tradition of fresh seafood — particularly Minorcan clam chowder, a local specialty with a tomato base and datil pepper heat quite different from the creamy New England style.
