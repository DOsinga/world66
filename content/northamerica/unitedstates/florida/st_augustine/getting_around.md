---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The historic district is compact and best explored on foot. Most of St. Augustine's sights lie within a mile-radius of the Plaza de la Constitución, and the car-free stretch of St. George Street makes walking the most practical option. Comfortable shoes are essential — the old town's uneven brick and cobblestone streets are charming but hard on feet.

A **hop-on hop-off trolley** (operated by Old Town Trolley Tours) loops around the major sights and is popular with first-time visitors. It is a reasonable way to get your bearings and reach outlying sites like the Lightner Museum and Memorial Presbyterian Church without backtracking on foot. The **Old Town Trolley** runs continuously from roughly 8:30am to 5pm.

For Anastasia State Park and the St. Augustine Lighthouse, you need a car or bicycle — both are on Anastasia Island across the Bridge of Lions, about 15 minutes from downtown. Fort Matanzas is a further 14 miles south on A1A and is only reachable by car.

**Parking** in the historic district is limited and fills quickly on weekends. The Visitor Information Center on Castillo Drive has large lots and is a reliable starting point. Street parking on the periphery of the old town is free but highly competitive in season.
