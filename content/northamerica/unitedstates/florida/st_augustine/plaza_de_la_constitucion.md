The Plaza de la Constitución is the geographic and historical center of St. Augustine, laid out in 1573 as part of the original colonial town plan. It is flanked on its west side by the Cathedral Basilica, on its north by Government House, and on its east by an 1824 market building whose open arcade still hosts occasional markets. The obelisk in the center commemorates the Spanish constitution of 1812.

The plaza is a pleasant place to sit and orient yourself before exploring the historic district. The market building's covered walkway is lined with small stalls selling local goods. From the north end of the plaza, St. George Street runs into the heart of the old town; from the south, Avilés Street leads toward the Oldest House and quieter colonial lanes. The Castillo de San Marcos is a ten-minute walk north along the bay front.
