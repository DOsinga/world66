Ponce de Leon's Fountain of Youth Archaeological Park is part history site, part old Florida attraction. It leans into the legend of the fountain, but the grounds also interpret early Spanish St. Augustine, Timucua history and archaeological remains from the first colonial settlement area.

Come with the right expectations and it is enjoyable: peacocks, demonstrations, exhibits and a theatrical dose of mythmaking. It is more playful than the Castillo or the old streets, but still tied to the city's origin story.
