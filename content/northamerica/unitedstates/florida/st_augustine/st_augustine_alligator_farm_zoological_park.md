St. Augustine Alligator Farm Zoological Park is one of Florida's oldest animal attractions, with a strong focus on alligators, crocodiles, and other reptiles. Its long history and specialized collection make it more distinctive than a generic roadside zoo.

The park is also known for its bird rookery season, when nesting wading birds can be seen at close range. It is especially good for families, photographers, and visitors who want a break from the colonial historic district.
