The St. Augustine Lighthouse is a 165-foot black-and-white spiral-striped tower on Anastasia Island, a 15-minute drive from downtown. It is the best-preserved 19th-century lighthouse in Florida and still operates as an active aid to navigation. The 219-step climb to the top is well worth the effort: the view takes in the Matanzas River, the old town, and the Atlantic horizon in all directions.

The lighthouse museum, housed in the restored Victorian keeper's cottage, covers the history of the lighthouse and its keepers with well-researched exhibits. The Maritime Hammock Preserve adjacent to the site has a short nature trail. Combined with a visit to Anastasia State Park next door, this makes a good half-day excursion from downtown.
