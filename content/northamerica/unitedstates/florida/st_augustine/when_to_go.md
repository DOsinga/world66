---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

St. Augustine's best months are **October through April**. Spring and autumn bring mild temperatures (low 70s°F / low 20s°C), comfortable humidity, and manageable crowds. Winter is warm enough to be pleasant — January averages around 55–65°F (13–18°C) — and the city is notably quieter on weekdays.

Summer (June–September) is hot, humid, and prone to afternoon thunderstorms. The city is also at its most crowded during this period. Hurricane season runs June through November; the area is not especially vulnerable compared to South Florida, but tropical storms can bring flooding and closed roads.

The **Nights of Lights** festival from mid-November through January transforms the old town with millions of small white lights — the historic streets look genuinely beautiful and it draws visitors from across the state. Book accommodation well in advance if you want to visit then.
