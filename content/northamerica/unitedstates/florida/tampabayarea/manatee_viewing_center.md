Tampa Electric's Manatee Viewing Center is one of the easier places in the Tampa Bay area to see manatees in winter. The animals gather in the warm discharge water from the power station, and the viewing decks make it simple to watch them without joining a boat trip.

The setting is industrial, not wild, but that contrast is part of the interest. There are boardwalks, an education centre, and usually rays and coastal birds around the shallows, so it works well as a short nature stop south of Tampa.
