Bayshore Boulevard is Tampa's classic waterfront walk, a long curving promenade along Hillsborough Bay with views across the water and back toward the city. It is used constantly by runners, cyclists, dog walkers and anyone who wants breeze without leaving town.

The boulevard is not a single attraction so much as a civic habit. Go early or near sunset, when the heat eases and the bay catches the light. It combines well with Hyde Park, the Tampa Riverwalk or a drive past the grand houses that line parts of the route.
