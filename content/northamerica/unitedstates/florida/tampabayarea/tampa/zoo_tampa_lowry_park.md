ZooTampa at Lowry Park is Tampa's main zoo, with Florida wildlife, Asian and African animal areas, an aviary, children's zones, and a manatee care centre. It is a full half-day family stop rather than a quick look-in, especially in warm weather when the water play areas are part of the draw.
