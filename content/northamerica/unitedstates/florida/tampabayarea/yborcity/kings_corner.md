A combined cigar lounge, café, and bar on 7th Avenue that splits the difference between the neighbourhood's tobacco past and its current bar scene. The walk-in humidor stocks house-rolled and imported cigars; the café serves Cuban coffee, sandwiches, and beer. The shaded sidewalk patio is a good place to spend an afternoon watching 7th Avenue pass.
