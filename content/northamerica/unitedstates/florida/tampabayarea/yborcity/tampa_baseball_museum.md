A small museum housed in the boyhood home of Cuban-American baseball legend Al López, the first Hispanic manager in Major League Baseball and a Hall of Famer. The exhibits cover 135 years of Tampa baseball, from amateur Cuban-immigrant leagues through the spring-training era to the Tampa Bay Rays. The two-storey wooden house is itself a good example of an Ybor worker cottage. Allow forty-five minutes.
