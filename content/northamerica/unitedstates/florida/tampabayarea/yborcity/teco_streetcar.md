The TECO heritage streetcar runs from Ybor along a 4.3 km route through the Channel District to downtown Tampa, with stops near the Florida Aquarium and Amalie Arena. The cars are replicas of the 1923 Birney safety cars that served the city in the streetcar era. Rides are free, and the line is the easiest way to combine an Ybor visit with downtown sights.
