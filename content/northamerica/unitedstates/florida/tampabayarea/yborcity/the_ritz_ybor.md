A 1917 vaudeville and movie theatre on 7th Avenue, converted in the 2000s into one of Tampa's main live-music venues. The interior has kept its tiled lobby and painted ceiling and now hosts touring rock, hip-hop, and DJ acts in a 1,500-capacity main room. On nights without a show the venue often runs themed club nights.
