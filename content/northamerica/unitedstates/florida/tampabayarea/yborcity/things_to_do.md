---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Ybor's main attractions cluster along 7th Avenue (La Sétima) and the side streets a block or two off it. The neighbourhood is compact and walkable — almost everything below is within a fifteen-minute walk of the Centro Ybor streetcar stop. Start at the museum, walk the historic streets, look in on a cigar shop, and end up at the Columbia for dinner.
