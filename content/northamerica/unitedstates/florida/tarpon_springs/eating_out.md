---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The food in Tarpon Springs is the main reason to visit beyond the Sponge Docks — fresh seafood and Greek cooking at prices that have not yet caught up with the town's popularity.

**Hellas Restaurant and Bakery** on Dodecanese Boulevard is the most famous Greek restaurant in town and has been here for decades. The lamb dishes are reliable, the baklava is excellent, and the in-house bakery turns out fresh pastries all day. It is a tourist institution at this point, but the food is still genuinely good.

**Mykonos** is a smaller, less flashy alternative on Dodecanese with excellent grilled octopus and the freshest fish on the docks. The owners are Greek, the menu is simple, and the prices are fair.

**Rusty Bellies** on the Sponge Docks focuses on local seafood — grouper, snapper, and shrimp from the Gulf, simply prepared. The waterfront deck is one of the better spots in town for a sunset dinner.

**Costa's Restaurant** is a local favourite a block off the tourist strip — the lunch specials (stuffed grape leaves, spanakopita, lamb stew) are cheap and excellent. No waterfront view, but more of an honest local restaurant than a tourist experience.

For pastries and coffee, the bakeries along Dodecanese open early and serve excellent Greek coffee alongside honey-soaked pastries. Start the morning there before the crowds arrive.
