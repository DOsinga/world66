---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Tequesta itself has a small selection of restaurants — it is a residential village, not a dining destination. Most of the better eating is in adjacent Jupiter, a short drive south. What is here tends toward the casual: seafood shacks, waterfront grills, and the kind of Florida diner that has been in the same spot for decades. Fresh fish is the obvious choice; the grouper, snapper, and mahi-mahi coming through Jupiter Inlet are excellent.
