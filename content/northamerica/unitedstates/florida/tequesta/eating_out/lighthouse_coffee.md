A small, unpretentious breakfast and lunch spot in Tequesta that locals favour for its relaxed pace and solid basics. The breakfast menu covers the Florida diner standards — eggs, pancakes, biscuits and gravy — done reliably and served quickly. Good for an early start before a day on the river or at the park. Cash-friendly and comfortable, the kind of place that fills with regulars most mornings.
