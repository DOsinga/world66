Fort Gadsden Historic Site sits in the Apalachicola National Forest, on a remote bend of the river north of town. Low earthworks and an interpretive kiosk mark a place tied to the early 19th-century border wars, when Native American communities, Black Seminoles, escaped slaves, Spanish Florida, and the expanding United States all collided here.

It is not a polished fort visit with walls and cannons. Come for the setting and the history, and combine it with a slow drive through the river country behind Apalachicola.
