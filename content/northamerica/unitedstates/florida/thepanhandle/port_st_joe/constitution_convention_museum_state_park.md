This small Florida state park tells the story of old St. Joseph, the boomtown that hosted the 1838 convention where delegates drafted Florida's first constitution. The museum is modest, but the story is not: for a few years this was one of the most ambitious ports on the Gulf Coast.

Come before the beach, not instead of it. An hour is enough for most visitors, and it gives useful context to a town that otherwise looks like a quiet bayfront fishing base.
