T.H. Stone Memorial St. Joseph Peninsula State Park is the beach reason to come this far down the Forgotten Coast. The park protects a long barrier peninsula, with Gulf surf on one side and the calmer waters of St. Joseph Bay on the other.

The simplest visit is a swim and beach walk, but the park is better if you slow down. Bring binoculars for shorebirds, check which areas are open after storm damage, and consider the bay side for kayaking when the Gulf is rough.
