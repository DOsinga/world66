Lake Jackson Mounds Archaeological State Park protects one of the most important pre-Columbian sites in the Florida Panhandle. The preserved temple mounds were part of a Mississippian-period ceremonial centre, giving Tallahassee a deep history that predates the Capitol and plantation-era narratives.

The park is compact and easy to visit, with trails, interpretive signs, and views of the mounds themselves. It is a strong stop for travellers interested in archaeology, Indigenous history, or quiet outdoor time close to the city.
