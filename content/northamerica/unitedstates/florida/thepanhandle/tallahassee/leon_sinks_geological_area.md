Leon Sinks Geological Area is a good short escape south of Tallahassee, with boardwalks and trails linking a chain of limestone sinkholes. Some are deep, steep-sided pools; others are dry basins filled with magnolias and forest.

The area is fragile, and the rules are strict: no swimming, diving, bikes, horses, or dogs near the sinks. Come for a quiet walk, read the interpretive signs, and treat the edges with real caution.
