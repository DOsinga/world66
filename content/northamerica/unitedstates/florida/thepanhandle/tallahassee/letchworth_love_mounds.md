Letchworth-Love Mounds Archaeological State Park protects one of the most impressive Indigenous earthworks in Florida: a ceremonial mound rising from quiet woodland near Lake Miccosukee. The scale is hard to appreciate until you stand near it, where the mound reads less like a hill and more like a deliberately shaped piece of civic architecture.

The park is simple, with trails, picnic areas, and interpretive material rather than a large visitor centre. That restraint suits the place. It makes a good half-day outing from Tallahassee for anyone interested in archaeology, Native history, or the older human geography of north Florida beyond plantations and state politics.
