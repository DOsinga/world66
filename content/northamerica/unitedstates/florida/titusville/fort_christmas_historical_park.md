Fort Christmas Historical Park preserves a reconstructed Seminole War fort built on Christmas Day in 1837, along with a cluster of old rural buildings from the surrounding community. The fort itself is a reconstruction, but the site gives a clear, hands-on sense of frontier Florida away from the coast and space industry.

It is a good stop if you are driving between Titusville, Orlando, and the St. Johns River country. The displays are modest, but the setting and preserved town buildings make it more than a roadside marker.
