The high-arched bridge carrying SR 406 east from Titusville across the Indian River Lagoon to Merritt Island. The current four-lane bridge opened in 2011, replacing an older drawbridge, and includes a wide pedestrian and bike lane on the north side with built-in viewing benches.

The bridge is a favourite of launch watchers and birders — you can see straight up the lagoon toward Kennedy Space Center, and the railing height puts you above the mangrove canopy. There is a small fishing area at the east abutment that is open day and night. The bridge is also the only road access to the [Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/merritt_island_national_wildlife_refuge) from the Titusville side.
