A small Carpenter Gothic Episcopal church built in 1888, with the original pine pews and stained-glass windows. The church sits in a quiet residential block south of the commercial district and remains an active parish.

It is generally unlocked during weekday daylight hours; visitors are welcome to sit and look. The interior is plain — no elaborate ornament — but the proportions and the original woodwork are excellent. A good ten-minute stop on a downtown walk.
