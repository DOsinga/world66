The Valiant Air Command Warbird Museum is a strong Space Coast stop for aviation enthusiasts, with restored military aircraft, engines, uniforms, and memorabilia housed near Space Coast Regional Airport. It adds a military aviation angle to a region better known for NASA and rocket launches.

The museum is hands-on in spirit, with volunteers and restoration work giving the aircraft a lived technical context. It pairs well with the American Space Museum in downtown Titusville if you want a fuller day of flight history rather than focusing only on Kennedy Space Center.
