**West Palm Beach** is the largest city on Florida's Treasure Coast, sitting across the Intracoastal Waterway from its more famous neighbour, Palm Beach. It has developed its own identity in recent years — the [Clematis Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/clematis_street) waterfront district and [Rosemary Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/cityplace) (formerly CityPlace) have made the downtown lively and walkable.

The [Norton Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/norton_museum) is one of the best in South Florida, and the [Ann Norton Sculpture Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/ann_norton_sculpture_gardens) are a quiet escape. The waterfront along [Flagler Drive](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/flagler_drive) is pleasant for walking, with views across to Palm Beach. The [Kravis Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/kravis_center) is South Florida's main performing-arts venue, and in May the [SunFest](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/sunfest) music festival takes over the waterfront for five days.

West Palm Beach is also a convenient base for nature outings: [Manatee Lagoon](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/manatee_lagoon) just to the north fills with West Indian manatees in winter, [Mounts Botanical Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/mounts_botanical_garden) is fifteen minutes west, and [Lion Country Safari](/northamerica/unitedstates/florida/westpalmbeach/lion_country_safari) — the first drive-through safari park in the United States — is half an hour out toward the Everglades. The beaches of Singer Island and Jupiter sit ten to twenty minutes north.
