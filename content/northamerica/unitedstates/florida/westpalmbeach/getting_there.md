---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Palm Beach International Airport (PBI) sits five minutes west of downtown, with daily nonstops to most US hub cities and seasonal service to several European and Caribbean destinations. The airport is small, the security lines short, and ground transport into town is straightforward — a taxi or Uber runs about $15, or take the PalmTran bus 1A.

By train the new Brightline high-speed service from Miami stops in central downtown (the West Palm Beach station, with onward continuation north to Orlando opened in 2023); the trip from Miami is about 75 minutes and from Orlando around 3 hours. Amtrak's Silver service also stops in West Palm Beach, with a daily connection to New York. Driving in from Miami via I-95 takes about an hour outside of peak.
