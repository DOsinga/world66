John D. MacArthur Beach State Park protects one of Palm Beach County's wilder coastal stretches. A long boardwalk crosses a lagoon to reach dunes and an undeveloped Atlantic beach, while the park's nature center explains the coastal and mangrove habitats. Kayak rentals are available for exploring the lagoon and mangrove hammocks, making this one of the area's better nature-focused beach stops.
