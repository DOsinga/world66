McCarthy's Wildlife Sanctuary is a nonprofit wildlife rehabilitation centre west of West Palm Beach. Guided visits focus on rescued and rehabilitated animals, including big cats, reptiles, and birds.

It is a more structured visit than a casual zoo stop, and advance booking is usually the sensible approach. The appeal is the close look at animals and the rehabilitation work behind the sanctuary.
