Peanut Island Park is a small island park reached by boat or ferry from the Port of Palm Beach area. Visitors come for beaches, snorkeling in clear water near the inlet, picnic areas, and short walks around the shore, making it one of the more memorable outdoor stops near West Palm Beach.
