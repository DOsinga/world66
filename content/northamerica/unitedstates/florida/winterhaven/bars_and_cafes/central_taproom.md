A craft beer bar on Central Avenue with a rotating selection of Florida-brewed beers on tap. The space is unpretentious — a renovated shopfront with communal tables and no-frills service — and the beer selection is genuinely good, with a focus on local Polk County and Tampa Bay breweries. Live music on some weekends. Opens from mid-afternoon.
