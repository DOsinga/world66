A specialty coffee shop in the downtown historic district, one of the better independent cafes in Polk County. Beans are sourced and roasted with care, the espresso is well-made, and the space is comfortable enough to sit in for an hour. Good selection of pastries. Fills up with locals on weekend mornings. A useful stop before a day at LEGOLAND or Bok Tower.
