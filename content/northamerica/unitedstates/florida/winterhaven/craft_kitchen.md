A farm-to-table restaurant in the downtown historic district with a short menu that changes with what is available locally. Good lunch option — sandwiches, salads, and seasonal specials prepared with noticeably more care than the surrounding chain restaurants. The building is a renovated 1920s storefront and the interior has been kept simple. Popular with the downtown office crowd at lunch; quieter at dinner.
