A long-running Italian restaurant in the downtown area, reliable rather than exceptional. The pasta dishes and pizza are solid, portions are generous, and prices are fair by Florida standards. Good for a family meal after a day at LEGOLAND when everyone just wants something filling and straightforward. Closed Mondays.
