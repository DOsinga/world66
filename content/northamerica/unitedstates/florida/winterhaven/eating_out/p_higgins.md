A lakefront restaurant on Lake Silver with outdoor seating overlooking the water. The menu runs to American comfort food and seafood — nothing that will surprise anyone, but the setting does a lot of the work. A good option for a relaxed dinner at sunset, when the lake light is at its best. Reservations recommended on weekends.
