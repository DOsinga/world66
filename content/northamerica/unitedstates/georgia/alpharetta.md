Alpharetta is an affluent suburb of Atlanta in north Fulton County, about 26 miles north of downtown. It's a significant tech hub — dozens of major technology companies have offices here — and has developed a pleasant, walkable town center around [Alpharetta City Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/alpharetta/alpharetta_city_center) in recent years.

For visitors, Alpharetta is primarily a base for exploring north Atlanta or a stop on the way to the north Georgia mountains. The town center has good restaurants and a farmers market on Saturdays, and the larger [Avalon](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/alpharetta/avalon) development is the high-end shopping and dining destination on the south side of town. The [Ameris Bank Amphitheatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/alpharetta/ameris_bank_amphitheatre) is a major outdoor concert venue that draws big-name acts throughout the summer.
