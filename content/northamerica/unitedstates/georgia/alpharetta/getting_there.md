---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Alpharetta is about 26 miles north of downtown Atlanta along Georgia 400. The drive from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport takes around an hour outside rush hour, longer at peak times. There is no rail service to Alpharetta; the nearest MARTA stations are several miles south in Sandy Springs or Dunwoody.

Most visitors arrive by car. Free parking is plentiful at City Center, Avalon, and the Ameris Bank Amphitheatre.
