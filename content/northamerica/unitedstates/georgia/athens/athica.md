ATHICA is a small independent contemporary art space in Athens, Georgia, with exhibitions that tend to be more experimental than decorative. It broadens the city's cultural map beyond the university campus, music history, and downtown bars.

The gallery is worth checking when an exhibition or event is running, but it is not a drop-in attraction every traveller needs. Look up hours first, then combine it with nearby studios, cafes, or a wider wander through Athens' creative side.
