---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Athens has a bar scene out of proportion to its size, driven by the University of Georgia and a long tradition of indie music. The density of venues on and around West Washington Street and Clayton Street downtown means you can walk from place to place without needing a car.

[Creature Comforts Brewing](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/athens/creature_comforts_brewing) produces some of the best craft beer in the South — their Tropicalia IPA is a Georgia institution. The taproom on Broad Street is a good starting point. The [40 Watt Club](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/athens/forty_watt_club) on West Washington Street is the famous music venue, but it functions as a bar on quiet nights too. Trappeze Pub on Broad Street has one of the better beer selections in town if you want to sit and drink without a show.
