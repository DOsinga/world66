The R.E.M. Murmur Railroad Trestle is a modest piece of Athens infrastructure turned music landmark by the cover of the band's 1983 debut album. For fans, the draw is the collision of ordinary Southern rail scenery and one of college rock's defining images.

It is a quick pilgrimage stop rather than a developed attraction, so be mindful of access, safety, and private property. Pair it with downtown venues, record shops, and other Athens music-history stops for the visit to make sense.
