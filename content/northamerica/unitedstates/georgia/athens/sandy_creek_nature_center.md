Sandy Creek Nature Center gives Athens an easy outdoor counterweight to music venues, campus landmarks, and downtown bars. Trails run through woods and wetlands, with exhibits that make it approachable for families and first-time visitors.

It is close enough to fold into a relaxed day in town, especially if you have a car. Go for a walk rather than just the visitor centre; the trails are the reason it clears the threshold as a travel stop.
