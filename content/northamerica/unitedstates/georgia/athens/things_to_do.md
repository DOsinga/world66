---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Athens punches well above its size for a city of 120,000. The University of Georgia drives much of the cultural life, but the music scene, independent restaurants, and walkable downtown hold up on their own. The [40 Watt Club](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/athens/forty_watt_club) is the spiritual center of the famous Athens sound that produced R.E.M. and the B-52s, and it still hosts live music most nights.

On campus, the [University of Georgia](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/athens/university_of_georgia) North Campus is worth a wander for its antebellum architecture. The [State Botanical Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/athens/state_botanical_garden) on the south end of campus is free and covers 300 acres of gardens and river forest. The [Georgia Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/athens/georgia_museum_of_art) is also free and has a solid American collection.

Downtown, the [Double-Barreled Cannon](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/athens/double_barreled_cannon) outside City Hall is Athens's most photographed curiosity — a Civil War-era engineering failure that is now a civic landmark. The [Morton Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/athens/morton_theatre), a block away, tells a less-told chapter of Athens history as one of the first theatres in the South built and owned by an African American businessman. The [Tree That Owns Itself](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/athens/tree_that_owns_itself) is a short walk from downtown into a residential neighborhood — silly, but charming.
