A puppet museum and theatre next to Federal Reserve Plaza in Midtown, with the world's largest collection of Jim Henson material. Galleries cover global puppet traditions — Bunraku, shadow puppets, Mr. Rogers — alongside the Hensonalia, which is the obvious main draw.

The theatre programmes daily children's performances and occasional grown-up shows in the evenings. Allow two to three hours including a show. One of Atlanta's surprises.
