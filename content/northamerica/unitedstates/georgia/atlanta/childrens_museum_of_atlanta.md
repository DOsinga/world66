Children's Museum of Atlanta is a hands-on museum aimed at young children, with play-based exhibits built around imagination, learning, and shared activities with adults.

Its downtown location makes it easy to combine with nearby Centennial Olympic Park, the National Center for Civil and Human Rights, World of Coca-Cola, or Georgia Aquarium when travelling with children.
