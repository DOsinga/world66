The Delta Air Lines Museum, beside Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, traces the airline's history through restored aircraft, uniforms, route maps, and aviation artifacts. Highlights include historic planes in the hangars and a flight simulator experience that makes it more than a static aircraft display.
