Atlanta's Flatiron Building is an 11-story wedge-shaped landmark in the city centre and one of the city's oldest surviving skyscrapers. It is mainly an exterior architecture stop, easy to fold into a downtown walk between the historic business district, Five Points, and nearby civic landmarks.
