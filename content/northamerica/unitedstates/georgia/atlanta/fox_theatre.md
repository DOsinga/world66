---

A 4,665-seat Moorish-Egyptian movie palace on Peachtree Street, one of the most extravagant surviving 1920s atmospheric theatres in the country. The auditorium ceiling is painted with stars and moving clouds; the side walls mimic the courtyard of a fortress in Granada.

The Fox survived a near-demolition campaign in the 1970s and is now a working venue for touring Broadway shows, ballet, concerts, and the Atlanta Symphony Orchestra. Behind-the-scenes architectural tours run a few times a month. Catch a performance if you can.
