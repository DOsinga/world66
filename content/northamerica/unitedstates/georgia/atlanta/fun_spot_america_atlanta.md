Fun Spot America Atlanta is a compact amusement park south of the city, with go-kart tracks, rides, miniature golf, laser tag, arcade games, and a roller coaster. It is smaller and less polished than the big-name theme parks, but the lighter crowds can make it easier with children.

Treat it as a half-day family outing from Atlanta rather than a core city sight.
