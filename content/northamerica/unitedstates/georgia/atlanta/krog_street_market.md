The smaller, more food-focused sibling to Ponce City Market, in a 1920s warehouse on the edge of Inman Park. The food hall has ten or so vendors — Hop's Chicken, Fred's Meat & Bread, Yalla Mediterranean, Suzy Siu's Baos — and a handful of bars including the well-regarded Ticonderoga Club.

It is right on the Eastside BeltLine and the obvious lunch stop for a walk along it. Smaller crowds than Ponce City Market and a denser cluster of well-rated kitchens.
