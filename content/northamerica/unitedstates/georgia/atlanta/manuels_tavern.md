A wood-panelled, low-ceilinged neighbourhood tavern on North Highland Avenue, owned by the same family since 1956 and the unofficial political clubhouse of Atlanta Democrats for decades. Photographs of presidents, governors, and city councillors cover the walls.

The food is bar food — burgers, wings, fish and chips — but the place is the point. Open until late and crowded after Falcons or Braves games. A working bar, not a tourist stop, which is exactly why it is worth visiting.
