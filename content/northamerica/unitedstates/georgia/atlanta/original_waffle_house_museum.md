The Original Waffle House Museum preserves a re-creation of the first Waffle House, which opened in Avondale Estates in 1955. Exhibits include a 13-stool diner counter, vintage equipment, old uniforms, memorabilia, and a jukebox.

It is a small, appointment-oriented museum, but a fun stop for travellers interested in Southern roadside food culture and Atlanta-area business history.
