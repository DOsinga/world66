The Rialto Center for the Arts is an 833-seat Georgia State University venue in downtown Atlanta. Its calendar includes jazz, dance, film, and university music performances, and it has also been associated with the Atlanta Film Festival.

It is worth checking when planning an evening downtown, especially for visitors looking for live performance rather than another museum stop.
