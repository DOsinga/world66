A long, dimly lit dive bar in Old Fourth Ward whose full name is Sister Louisa's Church of the Living Room and Ping Pong Emporium. The interior is hung with thrift-store religious paintings the owner Grant Henry has 'corrected' with cheerful obscene captions; the back room has a ping pong table and a piano singalong on Friday nights.

The drinks are cheap and the crowd is mixed — gay, straight, on dates, on solo nights. One of the more characterful bars in the city. Late opening, cash and cards.
