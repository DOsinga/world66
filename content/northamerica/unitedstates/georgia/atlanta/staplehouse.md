A serious, slightly understated modern American tasting-menu restaurant in Old Fourth Ward, run as a non-profit — the kitchen's profits fund the Giving Kitchen, a charity that helps restaurant workers in crisis. The food is precisely composed and seasonal; the dining room is small and quiet.

The à la carte menu is offered at the bar; the tasting menu in the back dining room. Reservations essential and often weeks out. Worth the planning.
