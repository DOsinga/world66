Your DeKalb Farmers Market is closer to a food hall, produce market, and international grocery warehouse than a conventional farmers market. The scale is the attraction: aisles of fruit, vegetables, spices, seafood, breads, and packaged foods from around the world, staffed by people whose name tags often include their home countries.

It is not a polished tourist sight, but it is a memorable stop if you like food markets or want to see Atlanta's international side away from the usual downtown attractions. Go with an appetite and enough time to wander.
