Butt Memorial Bridge is a small but distinctive Augusta landmark, built to honour Archibald Butt, a local-born military aide who died on the Titanic. Its sculptural details make it more interesting than a normal road crossing.

It is best folded into a broader walk or drive around downtown Augusta rather than treated as a destination by itself. Stop for the story, the stonework, and the oddity of finding a Titanic memorial in inland Georgia.
