The James Brown statue gives Augusta a public nod to the performer who remains its loudest cultural export. It stands near Augusta Common, close to the downtown streets where visitors are likely to walk between the riverfront, museums, restaurants, and Broad Street.

This is a short stop, but a good one if music matters to you. The statue works less as a monument of grandeur than as a local marker: Augusta claiming the Godfather of Soul in the middle of its everyday downtown rather than hiding him in a museum case.
