The Lamar Building is Augusta's tallest historic landmark, a 1910s downtown tower later topped with a sharply modern penthouse. It is mainly an exterior stop, but the contrast between the original masonry high-rise and the 1970s addition makes it one of the city's stranger architectural silhouettes.
