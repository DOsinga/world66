Meadow Garden is a small but meaningful historic house in Augusta, best known as the home of George Walton, one of Georgia’s signers of the Declaration of Independence. The house gives the city a tangible link to Revolutionary-era Georgia.

The visit is intimate rather than grand: period rooms, local stories, and a sense of how early American political memory is preserved away from the big coastal cities. It works well for travellers already interested in Augusta’s older layers beyond golf and the riverfront.
