The Morris Museum of Art is Augusta's strongest art stop, focused on painting and works on paper connected to the American South. That regional focus gives it a clearer identity than a general small-city art museum.

The museum is easy to combine with the Riverwalk and downtown Augusta. It is especially worthwhile if you want cultural context for the region rather than another antebellum house or canal-related site.
