Old Government House is one of Augusta's better preserved civic landmarks, a handsome early 19th-century building with the balanced proportions and restrained detail typical of Federal architecture. It served as a government and public-use building before later becoming a house museum and event space.

For travelers, the appeal is less about a single dramatic exhibit and more about seeing a piece of Augusta's older urban fabric up close. It pairs well with a walk through the historic center, where the city's river-town history is easier to read in brick, porches, and shaded streets than from a car window.
