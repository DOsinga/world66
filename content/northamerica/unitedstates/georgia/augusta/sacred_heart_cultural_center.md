Sacred Heart Cultural Center is a restored former Catholic church and one of Augusta's most impressive architectural interiors. Its brick exterior, twin towers, stained glass, and vaulted space give the building a level of craft that stands out among the city's historic sites.

Today it functions as a cultural venue and event space, but visitors can still appreciate the building as a landmark. It is worth including on a downtown architecture walk, especially with the canal, riverfront, and older civic buildings nearby.
