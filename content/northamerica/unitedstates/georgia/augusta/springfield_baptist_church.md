Springfield Baptist Church is one of Augusta's most important African American heritage sites. Its congregation traces back to the late 18th century, and the church has long been tied to Black religious life, education, and civic organizing in the city.

The site is close to downtown and works well as part of a broader walk through Augusta's historic core. It is most meaningful when viewed not just as a church building, but as an institution that helped sustain community life through slavery, Reconstruction, segregation, and the civil-rights era.
