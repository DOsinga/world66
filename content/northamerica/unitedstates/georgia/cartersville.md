Cartersville is a small city about 45 miles northwest of [Atlanta](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/atlanta) in Bartow County, a convenient stop between Atlanta and Chattanooga on I-75.

The main draw for visitors is [Etowah Indian Mounds](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/cartersville/etowah_indian_mounds), one of the best-preserved pre-Columbian Native American sites in the eastern United States. The complex includes large platform mounds dating from 1000 to 1550 AD, a defensive ditch, and a museum displaying carved stone figures and shell gorgets found on site. Downtown, the [Booth Western Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/cartersville/booth_western_art_museum) has an impressive collection of Western American painting and sculpture — unexpectedly strong for a small Georgia city — along with a Civil War gallery and a presidential gallery with letters from every US president. South of the city, the [Tellus Science Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/cartersville/tellus_science_museum) covers minerals, dinosaurs, and aviation. The corner of West Main has the [world's oldest surviving outdoor Coca-Cola sign](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/cartersville/young_brothers_pharmacy), painted in 1894.
