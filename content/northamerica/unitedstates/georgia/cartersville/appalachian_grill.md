A long-running mid-range restaurant on East Main, with a Southern menu pulled towards Appalachian and Cajun flavours — blackened catfish, jambalaya, fried green tomatoes, plus serious steaks. White-tablecloth setting in a restored downtown building, popular with the courthouse crowd at lunch and a steady dinner draw at weekends.
