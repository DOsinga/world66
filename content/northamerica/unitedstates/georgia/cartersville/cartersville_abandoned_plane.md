A long-abandoned Grumman G-159 twin-turboprop sits in tall grass at the southern edge of Cartersville Airport, covered in elaborate spray-paint graffiti. The plane has been there for at least fifteen years; its provenance is unclear and its owner unknown. It is on private land but plainly visible from Airport Drive, and visitors regularly walk up for photographs. An odd local curiosity rather than a sight in any conventional sense.
