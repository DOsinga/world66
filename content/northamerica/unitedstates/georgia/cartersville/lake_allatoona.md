Lake Allatoona is one of north Georgia's main escape valves from Atlanta: a large Corps of Engineers reservoir with marinas, campgrounds, boat ramps, and wooded shoreline. Around Cartersville, it pairs naturally with Red Top Mountain State Park for hiking, swimming, and lake views.

It gets busy in warm weather, but the easy access is exactly why people come.
