Crawfordville's one genuine destination is [A.H. Stephens State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/crawfordville_georgia/ah_stephens_state_park), which preserves [Liberty Hall](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/crawfordville_georgia/liberty_hall), the home of the Confederate Vice President, along with a Confederate museum, fishing lake, and wooded trails. The [Taliaferro County Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/crawfordville_georgia/taliaferro_county_courthouse) square is worth a look for the intact, time-stopped character of the town centre.

This is not a place to fill a full day unless you are interested in Civil War and Reconstruction-era history specifically. It works well as a 2-3 hour stop on a drive through the Georgia Piedmont between Augusta and Atlanta, or as a base for exploring the rural backroads of this part of the state.
