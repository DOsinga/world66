Gordon Lee Mansion is a historic house tied to the early settlement and plantation history of Chickamauga. The house belonged to Gordon Lee, a mill owner and local figure whose family name is still attached to several sites in the area.

It is a modest stop, but it pairs well with the nearby battlefield and Gordon Lee Mill if you are tracing the older history of the Chickamauga area.
