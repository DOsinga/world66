The Walker County Regional Heritage Museum covers local history in the Chickamauga and Fort Oglethorpe area, with Civil War material forming a large part of the story. It is a small museum, but useful if you are already visiting the Chickamauga battlefield and want more local context.

Expect a regional collection rather than a polished national museum. The value is in tying the battlefield, nearby towns, and northwest Georgia's older settlement history together in one stop.
