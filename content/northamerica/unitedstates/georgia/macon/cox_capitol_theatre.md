Cox Capitol Theatre is part of Macon's downtown music-and-performance circuit, occupying a restored historic theatre on Second Street. Its programming ranges from concerts and films to comedy and community events, giving visitors an evening option beyond bars and restaurants.

The venue matters because Macon is a music city, and live performance is one of the better ways to experience that identity. Check the calendar first; the building is most rewarding when you can actually catch a show.
