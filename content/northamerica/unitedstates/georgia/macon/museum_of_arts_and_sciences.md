The Museum of Arts and Sciences is Macon's broadest museum, mixing fine art, natural science, changing exhibitions, and a planetarium under one roof. That range makes it especially useful for families or travelers looking for an indoor half-day.

It sits away from the downtown music landmarks, but it helps round out Macon as more than a heritage and concert stop. Check current exhibits and planetarium times, since the best reason to go can change with the programme.
