Marietta is the seat of Cobb County, about 20 miles northwest of Atlanta. The [Marietta Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/marietta/marietta_square) — antebellum buildings, boutique shops, restaurants, and Glover Park — is one of the best-preserved historic town centres in Georgia and a destination in its own right. The [Marietta Museum of History](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/marietta/marietta_museum_of_history) and the [Confederate Cemetery](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/marietta/confederate_cemetery) are a short walk from the square.

[Kennesaw Mountain National Battlefield Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/marietta/kennesaw_mountain), just north of Marietta, preserves the site of a pivotal June 1864 battle from the Atlanta Campaign. More than 17 miles of trails wind through original earthworks and artillery positions; the summit of Big Kennesaw offers the best panoramic view of the Atlanta skyline in the metro area.

The [Gone with the Wind Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/georgia/marietta/gone_with_the_wind_museum) at Brumby Hall holds an impressive collection of memorabilia from the 1939 film, including original costumes and signed first editions. The novel's setting — the plantation country of northwest Georgia — is the landscape you're standing in.
