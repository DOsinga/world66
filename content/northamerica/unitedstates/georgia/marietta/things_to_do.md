title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Marietta packs a surprising amount of history into a compact area. The downtown square is anchored by antebellum architecture and a lively local scene, while Kennesaw Mountain National Battlefield Park just north of town preserves some of the most dramatic landscape of the 1864 Atlanta Campaign. The Gone with the Wind Museum appeals to film history enthusiasts; the Marietta Museum of History to anyone curious about how a Southern county seat actually worked.
