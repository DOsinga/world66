One of the most visited of Savannah's 22 historic squares, Chippewa Square is dominated by a bronze statue of James Oglethorpe, Georgia's founder, looking south toward the Spanish settlements he spent his career fighting back. The square is well-proportioned and surrounded by handsome Federal-era buildings.

Most visitors know it as the filming location for the park bench scenes in *Forrest Gump*, though the actual bench used in the film is now in the Savannah History Museum a few blocks away. The filming connection aside, Chippewa is worth visiting on its own terms: the scale is right, the shade is generous, and the surrounding streetscape is among the better-preserved in the district.
