Georgia State Railroad Museum occupies part of the old Central of Georgia Railway complex, with locomotives, rolling stock, workshops, and industrial buildings that explain Savannah's rail era. It is one of the city's strongest family-friendly history stops.

The site pairs naturally with the Savannah History Museum and nearby Battlefield Memorial Park. Check demonstration and train-ride times if those are important to your visit.
