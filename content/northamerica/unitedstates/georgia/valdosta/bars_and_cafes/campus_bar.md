The strip of bars within walking distance of Valdosta State University's campus follows a predictable formula — cheap pitchers, pool tables, game nights, and a crowd that empties out during semester breaks. They're not destination bars, but they're lively enough when school is in session, and the drink prices reflect the student budget. If you're staying overnight and want somewhere low-key to wind down, these are your best option in Valdosta. Don't expect much on a Sunday or during any school holiday.
