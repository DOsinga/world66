Lowndes County Historical Museum tells the local story of Valdosta and the surrounding county. It is a modest museum, but a useful stop if you want more context after walking the historic downtown and courthouse square.

Exhibits cover local families, civic life, and the industries that shaped this part of south Georgia. It is maintained by the county historical society and is typically a low-key, quick visit.
