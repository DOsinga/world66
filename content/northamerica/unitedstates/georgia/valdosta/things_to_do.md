---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Valdosta's appeal as a stopover is real but limited. The clear standout is Wild Adventures Theme Park, which draws families from across south Georgia and north Florida and is worth planning a half-day around if you're traveling with children. Beyond that, a short walk through the historic downtown reveals some well-preserved antebellum architecture around the courthouse square — enough for a stretch-your-legs stroll. The campus of Valdosta State University brings a modest cultural pulse to the city. For nature, Grand Bay Wildlife Management Area sits just outside of town and rewards birders and hikers willing to leave the interstate corridor behind.
