A tiny counter-service poke spot in Honolulu that has built a devoted following for the quality of its fish. Poke (pronounced poh-keh) — cubes of raw tuna, salmon, or other seafood seasoned with soy sauce, sesame oil, and various toppings — is Hawaii's great contribution to world food culture, and this is one of the best places to eat it. Build your own bowl with a base of rice or greens, choose your fish, and load on toppings. The spicy tuna is the classic order.

**Address:** 1540 S King Street, Honolulu. Closed weekends.
