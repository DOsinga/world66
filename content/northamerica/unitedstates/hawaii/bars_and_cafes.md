---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Hawaii's bar scene reflects the islands' casual character — outdoor bars with ocean views, tiki-influenced cocktails, and live Hawaiian music. The mai tai is the signature cocktail, and the best versions bear little resemblance to the sweet neon-coloured drinks sold at tourist spots. Look for bars that use fresh-squeezed lime juice and quality aged rum. Craft beer has taken hold on all the major islands; Kona Brewing Company's ales are ubiquitous and reliable. Coffee culture is strong on all the islands, fuelled by the excellent local beans from Kona on the Big Island and Ka'u in the south.
