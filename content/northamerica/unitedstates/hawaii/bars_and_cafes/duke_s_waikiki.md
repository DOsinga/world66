Named for Duke Kahanamoku — Olympic swimming champion, surfing ambassador, and the most beloved figure in Hawaiian sporting history — this open-air bar and restaurant on the beach at the Outrigger Waikiki is the classic Waikiki watering hole. The food is unremarkable but the mai tais are strong, the live Hawaiian music runs all afternoon, and the view over the water to Diamond Head is exactly what most visitors came to Hawaii for. It gets loud and packed on weekends. Drinks are expensive. Go anyway, at least once, and watch the surfers in the break out front while someone plays slack-key guitar.
