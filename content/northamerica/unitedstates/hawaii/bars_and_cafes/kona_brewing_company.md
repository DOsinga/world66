The original Kona Brewing brewpub in Kailua-Kona on the Big Island is a relaxed outdoor space where the flagship ales are served alongside pizzas and the full tap list. Longboard Island Lager and Big Wave Golden Ale are the session beers; the Pipeline Porter is flavoured with real Kona coffee. The brews now sold across the US mainland are brewed under license elsewhere, but the Kailua-Kona taproom serves fresh beer brewed on-site. There is a second location on Oahu in Hawaii Kai. Tours of the Kailua-Kona brewery run several times daily.
