The historic waterfront street in Lahaina, Maui's old whaling port, was lined with open-air restaurants, bars, and galleries. The enormous banyan tree at the town square — planted in 1873 and now spreading across nearly an acre — was the heart of the town's social life. In August 2023, a catastrophic wildfire destroyed most of Lahaina, including much of Front Street. The town is closed to visitors as of 2025 while rebuilding proceeds and the community determines its future. When it does reopen, it will be a fundamentally different place. The banyan tree survived, though badly scorched.
