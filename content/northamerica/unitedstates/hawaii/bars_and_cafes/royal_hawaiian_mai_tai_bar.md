On the beach at the Royal Hawaiian — the famous pink palace of Waikiki, opened in 1927 — this open-air bar serves arguably the best mai tais in Waikiki. The Royal Hawaiian mai tai recipe uses a float of aged Meyers dark rum over a blend of fresh citrus and Cointreau. You sit on the sand or at the bar with your feet essentially in the ocean, watching the surf break at the edge of one of the great hotel properties in the Pacific. It is expensive, as Waikiki goes, but this is a genuinely storied place.
