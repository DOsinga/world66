The definitive epic of the islands, published in 1959 — the year Hawaii became a state. Michener begins with the geological formation of the islands and traces human arrival from Polynesia, the coming of missionaries, the plantation era, and the immigrant waves from China, Japan, and Portugal that shaped modern Hawaii. It is long, sometimes slow, and occasionally dated, but there is nothing else that gives you the full sweep of Hawaiian history in narrative form. Michener spent years in the islands researching this book, and the depth shows.
