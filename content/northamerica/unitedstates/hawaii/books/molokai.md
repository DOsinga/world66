A novel set largely in the Kalaupapa leprosy settlement on Molokai, following a young girl who is diagnosed with leprosy and sent there at the age of seven in 1891, never to leave. Brennert researched the colony deeply and writes with empathy about the community that grew up inside it — how ordinary life continued within its boundaries, how love and art and faith persisted. It is the best popular account of one of the darkest chapters in Hawaiian history, and it will stay with you. A sequel, Honolulu, follows a Korean picture bride to the city in the early 20th century.
