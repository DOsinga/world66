title: "The Descendants"
type: poi
author: "Kaui Hart Hemmings"
isbn: "978-0812980448"
---

A contemporary Honolulu novel about a man from an old missionary family who discovers, while his wife lies comatose after a boating accident, that she was having an affair. What follows is part dark comedy, part meditation on what it means to belong to Hawaii — to be a haole (white person) with deep roots in a place where you will always be an outsider. Hemmings writes with dry wit and real affection for Oahu's social landscape. The 2011 film with George Clooney is good, but the novel is sharper.
