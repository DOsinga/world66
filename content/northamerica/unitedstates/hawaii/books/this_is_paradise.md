A debut collection of six stories set across the islands, from Waikiki nightclubs to ranch country on Maui to the North Shore of Oahu. Kahakauwila is a Native Hawaiian writer who understands both the beauty and the weight of the islands — the tourism economy that changes what home means, the locals who can no longer afford to live where they were born, the old Hawaiian culture that persists in fragments. Short, precise, and unsentimental.
