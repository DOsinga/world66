The most recognizable landmark on Oahu and arguably in all of Hawaii. The 232-metre (761 ft) tuff cone looms over Waikiki's eastern end, and the 2.6 km (1.6 mile) round-trip trail to the summit is one of the best short hikes in the state. The ascent takes you through a World War II-era military tunnel and up a series of steep concrete steps to a lighthouse and fire control station at the top, with sweeping views over Waikiki, Honolulu, and the Pacific.

Go early — the trail opens at 6am and the parking lot fills by 8am. Wear good shoes; the final stretch involves ladders and steep stairs. Bring water, sunscreen, and a hat. The trail is doable for most fit visitors in about an hour and a half round trip. Admission is $5 per person ($10 per car). The Diamond Head State Monument is reached from inside the crater via Diamond Head Road.
