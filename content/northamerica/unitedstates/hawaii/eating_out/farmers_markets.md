The best introduction to Hawaiian produce happens at the island farmers markets. Hawaii grows a remarkable variety of tropical fruit — rambutan, longan, starfruit, lychee, papaya, breadfruit — alongside macadamia nuts, lilikoi (passion fruit), taro, and several varieties of banana not exported from the islands. On Maui, the Upcountry Farmers Market in Pukalani (Saturday mornings) draws the best Upcountry growers. On the Big Island, the Hilo Farmers Market (Wednesday and Saturday) is the largest in the state, with local farmers alongside prepared food vendors serving laulau, saimin, and fresh fish. On Kauai, the Kauai Community Market in Lihue (Saturday mornings) is well worth the visit.
