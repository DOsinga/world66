The original shrimp truck on Oahu's North Shore, parked near Kahuku since 1993. The white truck is covered in signatures from visitors over three decades, and the menu is short: garlic shrimp, lemon butter shrimp, or hot and spicy shrimp, served over rice with a wedge of lemon. The shrimp come from the nearby aquaculture farms along the Kahuku coast — there is genuine provenance here. You eat at picnic tables in a small roadside lot. It is one of the most satisfying and honest meals in Hawaii. There are now several competitors nearby, but Giovanni's is the one worth queuing for.
