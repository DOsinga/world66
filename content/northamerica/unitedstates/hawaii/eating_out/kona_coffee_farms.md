The Kona district on the western slope of the Big Island's Hualalai volcano is the only place in the United States that produces coffee commercially, and genuine 100% Kona coffee is one of the world's finest. Dozens of small farms are open for tours and tastings along the Kona Coffee Living History Farm road south of Kailua-Kona. Be wary of blends labelled "Kona style" or "Kona blend" — these can contain as little as 10% actual Kona beans. The Greenwell Farms tour is free and informative. The harvest season runs October through February, and freshly processed coffee from a farm-gate sale during harvest is a significantly different experience from the exported bags sold everywhere else.
