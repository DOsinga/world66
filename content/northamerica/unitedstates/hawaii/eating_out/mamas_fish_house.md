The most famous restaurant on Maui and one of the most famous in Hawaii, set in a vintage plantation house on a rocky cove just east of Paia. The menu changes daily based on what the fishing boats bring in, and each fish dish is listed with the name of the fisherman who caught it and where in the Pacific. The food is excellent — fresh ono, mahimahi, and ahi prepared with care — but part of what you are paying for is the setting and the romance of the idea. Reservations need to be made weeks in advance. The parking lot fills with expensive cars; dress accordingly.
