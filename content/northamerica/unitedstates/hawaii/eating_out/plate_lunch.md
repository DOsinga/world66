The quintessential Hawaiian meal: two scoops of white rice, a side of macaroni salad, and a protein — kalbi short ribs, chicken katsu, lau lau, or teriyaki beef. The format originated with the plantation-era bento boxes brought by Japanese and other Asian workers and evolved into a uniquely Hawaiian institution. Every island has its local plate lunch spots, many of them roadside windows or food trucks. On Oahu, Rainbow Drive-In in Honolulu has served the same reliable plates since 1961. On Maui, look for the Maui plate lunch trucks in Kahului. On Kauai, the small takeaway windows along the highway are often the best options.
