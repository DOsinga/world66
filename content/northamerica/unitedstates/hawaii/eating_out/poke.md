Poke — cubed raw fish marinated in soy sauce, sesame oil, and various seasonings — is as Hawaiian as it gets. It predates tourism by centuries, evolving from simple seasoned raw fish preparations native to the islands. The best poke is found not in restaurants but in fish markets and grocery stores: Tamura's Fine Wine and Liquors on Maui has one of the best selections on the islands, with dozens of varieties daily. Don Quijote and Foodland supermarkets across Oahu offer impressive poke counters. The Ahi and Vegetable or shoyu ahi are reliable starting points; the spicy ahi is ubiquitous but good. Eat it fresh, the same day it is made.
