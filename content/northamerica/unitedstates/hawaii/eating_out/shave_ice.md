Hawaiian shave ice is not the same as a snow cone. The ice is shaved to a fine powder — almost the texture of snow — and flavours from tropical fruit syrups soak all the way through rather than pooling at the bottom. The best versions are served with azuki beans or a scoop of vanilla ice cream at the base and sweet condensed milk drizzled over the top. Matsumoto Shave Ice in Haleiwa on Oahu's North Shore is the most famous stand in the state, operating since 1951. On Maui, Ululani's Hawaiian Shave Ice has several locations and is considered by many to be the finest. The wait at both places can be long. It is worth it on a hot day.
