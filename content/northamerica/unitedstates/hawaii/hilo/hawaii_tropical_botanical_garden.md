Hawaii Tropical Botanical Garden is a lush rainforest garden north of Hilo, set in a steep valley that drops toward Onomea Bay. Trails pass palms, orchids, heliconias, waterfalls, and ocean viewpoints, making it one of the easiest ways to experience the wet side's plant life in a curated setting.

It is a paid garden rather than a wild hike, but the density of tropical planting and the dramatic coastal setting justify the stop. It fits naturally into a Hamakua Coast drive from Hilo, especially with scenic pullouts and nearby small bays.
