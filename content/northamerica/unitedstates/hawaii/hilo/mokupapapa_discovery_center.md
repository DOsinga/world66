Mokupapapa Discovery Center introduces Papahanaumokuakea Marine National Monument, the vast protected area of the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands. Exhibits cover coral reefs, seabirds, Hawaiian cultural connections, and the science behind one of the world's largest marine conservation areas.

It is a good Hilo stop for families and anyone trying to understand Hawaii beyond the main islands. The historic downtown building makes it easy to pair with the Pacific Tsunami Museum and Hilo Farmers Market.
