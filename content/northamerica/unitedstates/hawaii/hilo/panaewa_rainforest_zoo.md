Pana'ewa Rainforest Zoo is a modest, local-feeling zoo set in a lush pocket south of Hilo, where the plants are almost as important as the animals. The grounds mix palms, orchids, bamboo, and tropical trees with animal enclosures, making it a relaxed family stop rather than a polished big-city zoo.

It works best when you treat it as a gentle hour or two outdoors, especially if you are travelling with children or need a low-key break between waterfalls, markets, and volcano-country drives. The rainforest setting gives it a distinctly Hilo character, and admission has traditionally been easy on the budget.
