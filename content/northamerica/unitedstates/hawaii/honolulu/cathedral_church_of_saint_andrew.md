The Cathedral Church of Saint Andrew is the seat of Hawaii's Episcopal diocese and one of downtown Honolulu's most important historic churches. King Kamehameha IV and Queen Emma commissioned it, and the first phase was completed under Kamehameha V in 1867.

The stone Gothic Revival building is a calm stop near the State Capitol and Iolani Palace, with stained glass and royal history that tie it closely to the Hawaiian Kingdom.
