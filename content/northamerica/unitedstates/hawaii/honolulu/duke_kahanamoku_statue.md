The bronze statue of Duke Paoa Kahanamoku stands on Kuhio Beach in Waikiki, his arms outstretched and hung with leis. Duke was a five-time Olympic medalist in swimming and is credited with popularising surfing internationally. He is also Waikiki's patron saint — born here in 1890 — and his likeness is the centrepiece of the tourist district he helped create.

The statue is the standard gathering point on the beach and the backdrop for countless photographs. Outrigger canoe operators work from the beach immediately in front. His birthday, August 24th, is celebrated as Duke Kahanamoku Day, and leis are piled on the statue.
