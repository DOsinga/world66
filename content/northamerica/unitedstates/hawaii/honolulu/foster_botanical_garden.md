Foster Botanical Garden is a downtown Honolulu garden with rare tropical plants from around the world. Its mature trees and shaded paths make it a calm break from the surrounding city streets.

Docent-led tours are usually the best way to understand the collection, especially the older trees and unusual palms. It pairs easily with Chinatown and the civic sights nearby.
