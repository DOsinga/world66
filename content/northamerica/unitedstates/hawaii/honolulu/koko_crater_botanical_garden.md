Koko Crater Botanical Garden fills the inside of a dry volcanic crater with plants suited to arid climates. The loop path passes palms, plumeria, cacti, succulents, and native Hawaiian dryland species, making it a quieter counterpoint to the nearby ridge trail and beaches.
