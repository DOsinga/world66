Liliuokalani Botanical Garden is a small public garden on land once associated with Queen Liliuokalani. It focuses on native Hawaiian plants, making it a quieter and more local-feeling counterpart to Honolulu's larger botanical gardens. Come for a short walk, shade, and a look at native species rather than a long formal garden visit.
