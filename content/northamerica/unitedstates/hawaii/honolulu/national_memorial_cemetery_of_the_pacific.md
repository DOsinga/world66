The National Memorial Cemetery of the Pacific fills Punchbowl, an extinct volcanic crater above central Honolulu. It is a solemn military cemetery and memorial, but the crater rim also gives broad views over the city, making it one of the few places where Honolulu's wartime history and geography meet so clearly.
