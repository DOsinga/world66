The Nu'uanu Pali Lookout sits at 350 metres in the Ko'olau Mountains above Honolulu, on the edge of a sheer cliff that drops into the windward side of Oahu. The view is one of the best on the island — the Koolau Valley, the coastal lowlands of Kaneohe, and the windward coast stretching north and south. The wind at the lookout is permanent and frequently violent enough to lean into; visitors regularly hold onto railings.

The site is historically significant: it was here that Kamehameha I completed his conquest of Oahu in 1795. The drive from Honolulu takes 20 minutes through the Nu'uanu Valley. Often combined with a stop at the Byodo-In Temple on the windward side.
