Queen Emma Summer Palace is a restored 1847 retreat in Nuuanu Valley, once used by Queen Emma and King Kamehameha IV. Self-guided and docent-led tours show royal furnishings and personal objects, making it one of Honolulu's quieter places to understand the Hawaiian monarchy.
