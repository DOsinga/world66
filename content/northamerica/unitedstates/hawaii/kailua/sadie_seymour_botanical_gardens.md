Sadie Seymour Botanical Gardens is a small, community-minded garden above Kailua-Kona, organised around Hawaiian, Polynesian, and tropical plantings. It is not a grand estate garden; it feels more like a careful local project with good shade and useful labels.

The garden works best as a gentle break from beaches, traffic, and resort corridors. If you are curious about the plants behind Hawaiian gardens and lei traditions, it offers a compact introduction without needing a full-day excursion.
