Kauai Museum is the island's key indoor cultural stop, especially useful when weather interrupts beach or trail plans. Its exhibits focus on Kauai and Niihau, with material on Native Hawaiian culture, the monarchy period, plantation history, navigation, and local art.

The museum is in Lihue, so it fits naturally after arriving, before flying out, or while crossing between the north, east, and south shores. It gives helpful context for places visitors often experience only as scenery: valleys, coastal settlements, taro fields, and former plantation towns.
