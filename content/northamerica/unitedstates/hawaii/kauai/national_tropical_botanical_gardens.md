The National Tropical Botanical Garden on Kauai is one of Hawaii's great plant experiences, with conservation collections, native species and lush valley landscapes near Poipu. The gardens show the islands as living botanical refuges, not just beaches and waterfalls.

Tours vary by garden and season, but the best visits combine cultivated beauty with the serious work of protecting rare Pacific plants. Book ahead if you want a guided tour, and allow time for the south-shore setting rather than treating it as a quick roadside stop.
