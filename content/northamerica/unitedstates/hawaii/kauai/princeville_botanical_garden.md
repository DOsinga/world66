Princeville Botanical Garden is a guided garden experience on Kauai’s north shore, with tropical ornamentals, useful plants, fruit trees, and cacao woven into a private landscape. It is more structured than simply wandering a public garden, but that structure helps visitors understand what they are seeing.

The setting gives a lush counterpoint to beaches and cliff viewpoints. Book ahead, wear shoes that can handle damp paths, and expect the tour to be as much about tasting and interpretation as about flowers.
