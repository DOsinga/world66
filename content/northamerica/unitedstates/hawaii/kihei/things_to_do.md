---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Kihei's main activity is the beach — and the water. The string of Kamaole parks gives you easy access to swimming, snorkelling, and whale watching, while boat operators along South Kihei Road offer Molokini trips and dive charters. For those who want something beyond the beach, the ancient Hawaiian fishpond and the drive to La Pérouse Bay at the south end provide a different perspective on this part of Maui.
