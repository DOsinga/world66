---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Koloa town has a cluster of small restaurants and takeaway spots along its main street, catering to a mix of locals and visitors. The better options lean into local ingredients — fresh fish, plate lunches, tropical fruit — rather than replicating mainland styles. Poipū resort strip has more dining options but at resort prices; for better value and more character, eat in Koloa itself.
