The last authentic tiki bar in Honolulu, and one of the last in America. Founded in 1955 by Annette Nahinu, the place hasn't changed much since: bamboo walls, fishing floats, Japanese glass fishing balls, a grand piano, and a staff that has been there for decades. The mai tais are strong and the fish is fresh from the adjacent boatyard. It has the feel of a place that outlasted its era without noticing. Go for a late-afternoon drink and watch the boats come in.

**Address:** 50 Sand Island Access Road, Honolulu.
