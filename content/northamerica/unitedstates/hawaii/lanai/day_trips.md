Lanai's island neighbours are close enough for a day trip, and the ferry connection to Maui makes it genuinely easy. From Maui, boats also run to Molokini Crater, one of Hawaii's premier snorkeling destinations — a crescent-shaped volcanic remnant partially submerged offshore of Maui's southwest coast, with visibility that can exceed 100 feet on a good day.

[Maui](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/maui) is the obvious base for a Lanai visit, and the relationship works in reverse: travellers staying on Lanai often take the morning ferry to Maui for a day of driving the Hana Highway, visiting Haleakala, or simply shopping in Lahaina before the last ferry home.

[Molokai](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/molokai) is Lanai's near neighbour to the north, visible across the channel from Shipwreck Beach. It is the least-visited of the main Hawaiian islands and fiercely proud of it — a place of small farms, sea cliffs, and the remote Kalaupapa peninsula where a leprosy colony operated well into the 20th century. Getting there from Lanai requires routing through Maui or Honolulu; there is no direct ferry.
