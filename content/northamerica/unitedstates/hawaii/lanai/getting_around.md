---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Getting around Lanai requires either your own two feet (in Lanai City) or a four-wheel-drive vehicle (everywhere else). There is no public transportation anywhere on the island.

**Lanai City** is small enough to walk entirely. The town covers only a few blocks and the main attractions — Dole Park, the restaurants, the shops, Coffee Works — are all within easy walking distance of each other. If you're spending a day in town, leave the car parked.

**4WD rental** is essential for reaching the best beaches and sites. Shipwreck Beach, Polihua Beach, and the Garden of the Gods all require driving on unpaved dirt roads that a standard car will either refuse to complete or damage attempting. Dollar Rent A Car and the Four Seasons both offer 4WD vehicles on island — availability is limited, especially in peak season, so book well in advance. Rates are high by mainland standards; factor that into the trip budget.

The Four Seasons operates a shuttle between the resort and Lanai City for guests; day-trippers arriving by ferry are met by resort vehicles if they have reservations, but independent travellers are on their own.

A handful of taxis operate on the island, but they are not reliably available and should not be counted on for exploring beyond town. If you're arriving by ferry without a vehicle reservation, confirm your transportation before you leave Maui.
