Consistently rated one of the best beaches in America. Powder-white sand, calm protected water (a reef keeps the swells down), and two small offshore islands — the Mokulua — that you can kayak to. The beach is in a residential neighbourhood on the windward side of Oahu, with no hotels and limited parking. It is narrow, short, and intimate — about half a kilometre — which keeps the crowds manageable. Go early on weekdays for the best experience. The sunrise here is spectacular, with the Mokulua islands catching the first light.
