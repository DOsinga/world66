Kahana Pond Bird Sanctuary protects a wetland near Kahului that supports native Hawaiian waterbirds, including the Hawaiian coot and Hawaiian stilt. It is a short, low-key nature stop rather than a long hike.

The sanctuary is best for visitors with an interest in birds or Maui's remaining coastal wetlands.
