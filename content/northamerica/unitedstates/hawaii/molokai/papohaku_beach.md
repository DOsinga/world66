Papohaku Beach is one of Molokai's great open spaces: a long sweep of white sand that can feel almost empty even when the weather is good. Its size is the attraction, with three miles of beach backed by a simple beach park with restrooms, showers, and fresh water.

The ocean deserves respect here. Rip currents and shore break can be dangerous, so swimming only makes sense when the sea is flat and calm. Even then, this is as much a walking and sunset beach as a swimming beach.
