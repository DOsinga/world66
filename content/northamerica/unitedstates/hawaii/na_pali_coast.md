The northwest coast of [Kauai](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/kauai) is among the most spectacular coastal scenery on earth. Fluted green cliffs rise more than 1,200 metres (4,000 ft) directly from the ocean, carved by millions of years of rain and surf into knife-edged ridges and deep sea caves. No road reaches it — you access Na Pali by trail, by boat, or by air.

The **Kalalau Trail** (18 km one-way) is the classic hike, beginning at Ke'e Beach and passing through five valleys before reaching Kalalau Beach. The first 3.5 km to Hanakapi'ai Beach is accessible as a day hike and gives a strong taste of the terrain. The full trail requires an overnight permit, which must be reserved months in advance. Sea tours depart from Port Allen and Hanalei Bay from May through September (winter swells make the passage dangerous). Helicopter tours offer a bird's-eye view of the entire coast and are worth it if the budget allows.
