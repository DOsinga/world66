Dole Plantation grew from a roadside pineapple stand into one of central Oahu's busiest family attractions. The grounds include a pineapple garden, a miniature train through the fields, and a large garden maze.

It is a polished, commercial stop, but a useful one for families or anyone driving between Honolulu and the North Shore who wants an easy break with a bit of pineapple history.
