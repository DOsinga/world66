Kahana Bay Beach Park is a quiet cove on Oahu's windward coast, beside Ahupua'a O Kahana State Park and below the Kualoa mountains. The beach is shaded by trees and usually feels calmer than the better-known North Shore stops.

It is a good place for a slower coastal break, with mountain views, soft light, and a more local feel than the island's headline beaches.
