Kualoa Ranch is a private ranch on Oahu's Windward Coast, set in a dramatic green valley backed by steep volcanic ridges. It is best known for guided tours through landscapes used as filming locations for movies and television, including Jurassic Park and Lost.

The ranch is commercial, but the scenery is the reason it earns its place. Book ahead if you want a specific tour, and treat it as a way to see one of the island's most recognisable landscapes rather than as a quick roadside stop.
