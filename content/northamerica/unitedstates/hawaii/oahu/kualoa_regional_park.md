Kualoa Regional Park sits on Oahu's windward coast, with a broad lawn, a quiet beach, and one of the island's best views of Mokoliʻi, the small offshore island often called Chinaman's Hat. It is usually calmer than the busier beaches near Honolulu.

Come for a picnic, an easy beach stop, or a photo of the green Koʻolau cliffs meeting the sea. The water can be shallow and reefy, so it is better for scenery and wading than serious swimming.
