The most visited site in Hawaii. The USS Arizona Memorial sits above the sunken battleship, whose rusted hull is still visible through the water six metres below. Oil continues to seep from the wreck — a thin rainbow slick on the surface called the "tears of the Arizona" by locals.

Admission to the memorial itself is free, but boat tickets (included) must be reserved online well in advance, especially in peak season. Arrive early — the visitor centre opens at 7am and tickets often go quickly. Allow at least three hours for the main sites: the Arizona Memorial, the battleship Missouri (where Japan signed the surrender documents in 1945), the submarine Bowfin, and the Pacific Aviation Museum. The full complex is on the western side of Oahu, about 13 km northwest of Honolulu.
