The most accessible of the Big Island's black sand beaches, formed from lava that cooled rapidly when it hit the ocean and shattered into fine black particles. The sand is coarse and hot in the sun. What makes Punalu'u remarkable is the regularity with which Hawaiian green sea turtles (honu) come ashore to bask — you will almost certainly see several on the beach or in the adjacent pond. Do not touch or approach the turtles: they are protected under federal law. The beach is about 50 km south of Hawaii Volcanoes National Park on the southern tip of the Big Island.
