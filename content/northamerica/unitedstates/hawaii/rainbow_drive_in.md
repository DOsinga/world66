A Honolulu institution since 1961, famous for its plate lunches — the working-class meal that defines Hawaiian food culture. A plate lunch comes with two scoops of white rice, a scoop of macaroni salad, and a protein (teriyaki beef, chicken katsu, mahimahi, or mixed plate). The loco moco here — rice topped with a hamburger patty, fried egg, and brown gravy — is one of the best in the city. Outdoor seating, cash only, very cheap, often a queue out the door.

**Address:** 3308 Kanaina Avenue, Honolulu. Open daily 7am–9pm.
