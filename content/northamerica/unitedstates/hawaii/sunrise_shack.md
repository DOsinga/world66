A surf shack turned cult cafe on the North Shore of Oahu, opened by a former professional surfer and drawing the surf community and tourists alike. The specialty is cold-brew coffee blended with coconut milk — a combination that makes perfect sense at 7am before a dawn surf session. The acai bowls are fresh and filling. There are now multiple locations across Oahu, but the original Haleiwa location has the most character, right across from the famous surf breaks.

**Address:** 66-250 Kamehameha Highway, Haleiwa (North Shore, Oahu).
