The premier natural and cultural history museum in Hawaii, founded in 1889 by Charles Reed Bishop in memory of his wife, Princess Bernice Pauahi Bishop, the last descendant of the royal Kamehameha family. The Hawaiian Hall — three floors of Victorian gallery space — holds the most comprehensive collection of Hawaiian artifacts in the world: feathered capes, royal kahili standards, traditional weapons, canoes, and cultural objects from across the Pacific. The planetarium runs regular shows covering Polynesian celestial navigation, which is one of the museum's genuine strengths. Budget at least two hours.
