The tuff cone at the eastern end of Waikiki is one of Oahu's most recognisable landmarks and offers one of its best easy hikes. A 1.6-mile round-trip trail climbs through World War II-era bunkers and tunnels to the 761-foot summit, with panoramic views over Honolulu, Waikiki, and the coastline in both directions. The hike is moderately steep but manageable for most people in about an hour round-trip. Book tickets online in advance — they sell out on busy days. The name comes from British sailors in the 19th century who mistook calcite crystals in the rocks for diamonds.
