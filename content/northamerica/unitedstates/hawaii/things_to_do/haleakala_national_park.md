The summit of Haleakala on [Maui](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/maui) stands at 10,023 feet — high enough that you are almost always above the clouds. The crater below is vast and otherworldly, its cinder cones and lava formations coloured in shades of red, black, and grey. Watching sunrise from the summit is a signature Hawaiian experience: you need to arrive before dawn, the temperature will be near freezing, and the view when the sun breaks the horizon is genuinely worth the early alarm. Reservations for the sunrise are required and sell out weeks in advance. The park also has excellent hiking inside the crater itself, including the 12-mile Sliding Sands Trail that descends into the caldera.
