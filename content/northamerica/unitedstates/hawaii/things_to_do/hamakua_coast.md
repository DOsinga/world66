The northeastern coast of the Big Island between Hilo and Waipio Valley is one of the most dramatic drives in Hawaii. The old sugar cane highway winds through lush jungle, across ravines on old iron bridges, past waterfalls tumbling directly into the sea. Akaka Falls State Park has an easy 0.4-mile loop trail past two waterfalls, the larger of which drops 442 feet into a gorge. At the northern end, Waipio Valley is a steep-walled valley with a black sand beach that was once the political and spiritual capital of the Hawaiian kingdom — a four-wheel-drive road descends to the valley floor.
