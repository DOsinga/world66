A collapsed volcanic crater on Oahu's southeastern coast, now one of the best snorkelling sites in the state. The bay is a marine life conservation district — fishing is prohibited — and the result is an extraordinary density of fish in shallow, calm water. Green sea turtles, parrotfish, and hundreds of other species are visible without going far from shore. Hanauma Bay is closed on Tuesdays and requires advance reservations; it gets crowded quickly and the daily visitor count is capped. Arrive early. A mandatory educational video plays before you enter the water, covering the bay's ecology and what not to do.
