A privately-owned botanical garden on the Hamakua Coast of the Big Island, set in a lush valley that drops to a black sand beach. The garden holds more than 2,000 species of tropical plants, including palms, heliconias, gingers, orchids, and bromeliads from across the tropics. Trails wind through the valley past waterfalls and ponds to the ocean. It is one of the most beautiful gardens in Hawaii and receives far fewer visitors than attractions near Hilo, which is about 8 miles south.
