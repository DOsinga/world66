The only official royal palace in the United States, built in 1882 for King Kalakaua and finished for his successor Queen Liliuokalani. The palace had electricity and telephone service before the White House did. In 1893 a group of American businessmen and US Marines overthrew the Hawaiian monarchy here; Liliuokalani was imprisoned in her own palace for eight months after a failed 1895 counter-revolution. She spent her captivity in an upstairs room composing music, including the song "Aloha Oe." The palace serves as a museum today and guided tours are excellent, placing the events of 1893 — still bitterly contested in Hawaiian memory — in their full context.

story: >
  During her imprisonment, Queen Liliuokalani composed music and completed a translation of the Hawaiian creation chant, the Kumulipo. Her bedroom — where she was confined under guard — is part of the palace tour today.
