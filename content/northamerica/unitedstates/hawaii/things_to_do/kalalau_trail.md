Eleven miles of trail along the Na Pali Coast of [Kauai](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/kauai), considered one of the most spectacular hikes in America. The trail begins at Kee Beach at the end of the paved road and traverses five valleys before ending at Kalalau Beach, a remote crescent at the base of 2,000-foot cliffs accessible no other way. The first two miles to Hanakapi'ai Beach are the most popular — a manageable day hike with big views. Going further requires permits (available online from the state) and commitment: sections of the trail are narrow, exposed, and can be treacherous when wet. The beach campsites at Kalalau are among the most spectacular in the Pacific.
