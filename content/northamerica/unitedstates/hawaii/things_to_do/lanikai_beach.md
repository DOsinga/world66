On the windward side of Oahu, Lanikai Beach is regularly cited among the finest beaches in the world. The sand is powder-fine and white, the water turquoise and calm — protected by the Mokulua Islands offshore — and the sunrise here, with the twin islets silhouetted against a glowing sky, is one of the most photographed scenes in Hawaii. The beach is residential and lacks facilities; park on the street and access it through the public right-of-way paths between the houses. It is best early in the morning before it fills up. From the beach you can kayak to the Mokulua Islands, about a mile offshore.
