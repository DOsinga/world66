A traditional Hawaiian feast with cultural performance, the luau is one of the most accessible introductions to Hawaiian culture available to visitors. The format is similar across the islands: an outdoor setting, a buffet centred on kalua pig (slow-roasted in an underground imu oven), Hawaiian dishes like poi and lomi salmon, and a hula and fire-dance performance after sunset. Quality varies considerably. On Maui, the Old Lahaina Luau is consistently rated among the best in the islands for its authenticity and storytelling. On Oahu, the Polynesian Cultural Center's evening show is more theatrical. Book well in advance — good luaus sell out weeks ahead in peak season.
