The best aquarium in Hawaii, focused entirely on Hawaiian and Pacific marine life. The highlight is a 54-foot walk-through underwater tunnel that passes through a 750,000-gallon open ocean tank filled with hammerhead sharks, stingrays, green sea turtles, and large pelagic fish. The Humpbacks of Hawaii exhibit includes full-scale models and underwater recordings of whale song. The centre is well-designed and uncrowded compared to most Hawaii attractions. It is located in Maalaea, conveniently close to the ferry dock for Molokini crater boat trips.
