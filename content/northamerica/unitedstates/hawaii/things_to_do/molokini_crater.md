A crescent-shaped volcanic crater rising from the ocean channel between [Maui](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/maui) and Kahoolawe, now one of the best snorkelling and diving sites in Hawaii. The crater's rim creates a sheltered lagoon with exceptional clarity — visibility of 100 feet or more is common — and the walls drop sharply on the seaward side to over 300 feet. Hundreds of endemic fish species live in the crater, including large schools of damselfishes, butterfly fish, and the occasional eagle ray. Boat tours depart from Maalaea Harbor in the early morning; book well in advance in peak season.
