Seventeen miles of towering green cliffs on the northwestern coast of [Kauai](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/kauai), accessible only by sea, air, or the Kalalau Trail. The cliffs rise up to 4,000 feet from the ocean, their faces deeply fluted by centuries of erosion, streaked with waterfalls and hung with tropical vegetation. The Kalalau Trail — 11 miles each way from Kee Beach — is one of the finest coastal hikes in the world, and genuinely strenuous. Permits are required for the overnight campsites at Kalalau Beach. Boat tours along the coast are excellent and more accessible for most visitors; summer months offer the calmest seas.
