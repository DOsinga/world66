A lush valley on the North Shore of Oahu with one of the best-preserved ancient Hawaiian settlements on the island. A paved walking path leads 0.75 miles through botanical gardens containing more than 5,000 tropical plant specimens to Waihi Falls, where swimming is permitted in the pool below the waterfall. The valley once held more than 600 ancient Hawaiian sites including heiau (temples), terraced taro fields, and fishponds. The cultural centre runs demonstrations of traditional Hawaiian activities including hula, lauhala weaving, and canoe paddling.
