In winter months (November through February), the North Shore of Oahu hosts the biggest rideable waves on the planet. Pipeline, Sunset Beach, and Waimea Bay all come to life when the season's first swells arrive from the north Pacific, drawing the world's best surfers and thousands of spectators. Even if you do not surf, watching professionals charge 20- to 30-foot faces at Banzai Pipeline from the beach is one of the most thrilling spectator sports available. In summer the same beaches are calm and swimmable. The small town of [Haleiwa](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/haleiwa) serves as the North Shore's hub, with excellent shrimp trucks, coffee, and shave ice.
