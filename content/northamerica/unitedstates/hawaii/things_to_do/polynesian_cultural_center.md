A large living museum on Oahu's North Shore, run by Brigham Young University Hawaii, where students from across Polynesia perform and demonstrate the traditional arts of their home islands. Six village areas represent Hawaii, Samoa, Tonga, Fiji, Aotearoa (New Zealand), and Tahiti. Activities include weaving, fire-making, outrigger canoe paddling, and spear-throwing. The evening show — a full-scale theatrical production involving traditional dance and music from across the Pacific — is genuinely impressive. This is one of Oahu's most popular attractions and fills up; book well ahead for the combination packages that include dinner and the evening show.
