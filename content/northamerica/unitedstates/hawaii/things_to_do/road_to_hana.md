A 52-mile drive on [Maui](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/maui) that crosses 59 bridges and curves through more than 600 bends, threading past waterfalls, bamboo forests, taro fields, and black sand beaches. The road itself is the experience — not Hana town at the end, which is pleasant but unremarkable. Stop at Twin Falls near the beginning (easy walk to two waterfalls), Waikamoi Ridge Trail for a quick hike through bamboo, Garden of Eden botanical garden, and Wailua Falls near the end. The drive takes three to four hours each way if you stop regularly, which you should. The road is narrow and requires full attention; turn back before dark.
