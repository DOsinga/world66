The most famous beach in Hawaii and one of the most famous in the world — a mile-long crescent of golden sand backed by a wall of hotels and Diamond Head at its eastern end. The waves are gentle enough for beginners (surf lessons are available from beach boys just as they were in Duke Kahanamoku's era a century ago), the water is warm year-round, and the scene is reliably lively. It is not a secluded tropical beach — it is an urban beach of the highest order, and on its own terms it delivers. The sunrise over Diamond Head from Waikiki is worth getting up for.
