Mark Twain reportedly called it the "Grand Canyon of the Pacific," and while that might be overselling it, Waimea Canyon on [Kauai](/northamerica/unitedstates/hawaii/kauai) is genuinely spectacular. The canyon is 14 miles long, a mile wide, and over 3,600 feet deep, its layered lava walls stained in bands of red, brown, and green. The road to the top passes several overlooks, the best at Waimea Canyon Lookout (3,400 feet) and Kalalau Valley Lookout at Kokee State Park, which peers into the Na Pali Coast valleys on the north shore. On clear days the views from both lookouts are extraordinary; the canyon is often shrouded in cloud, so check conditions before making the drive.
