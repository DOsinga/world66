North Pacific humpback whales migrate to Hawaiian waters between December and May to breed and give birth, with peak numbers in February and March. The channel between Maui, Molokai, and Lanai — the Auau Channel — is the most important humpback breeding ground in the North Pacific. Boat tours from Maalaea and Lahaina on Maui offer the best experience, and the Hawaii Whale Research Foundation runs some of these trips with scientist naturalists aboard. From the shore, whales are often visible from Papawai Point on Maui's western coast, which has a dedicated scenic overlook. On a good day you can watch mothers and calves from the cliff edge without leaving your car.
