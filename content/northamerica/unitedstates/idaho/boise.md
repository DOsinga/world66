Idaho's capital sits where the high desert meets the foothills of the Rocky Mountains, a mid-sized western city that punches above its weight in livability and outdoor access. The [Boise River Greenbelt](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/boise_river_greenbelt) runs 25 miles through the heart of town, and you can be hiking in the [Boise Foothills](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/boise_foothills) within minutes of downtown.

The city's core is compact and walkable. 8th Street is the main drag for restaurants and bars, while the [Basque Block](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/basque_block) — one of the largest Basque communities outside Europe — gives the city an unexpected cultural dimension, complete with the [Basque Museum and Cultural Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/basque_museum) and [Bar Gernika](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/bar_gernika). The [Idaho State Capitol](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/idaho_state_capitol) building anchors the downtown grid.

[Freak Alley Gallery](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/freak_alley_gallery) is one of the larger outdoor mural galleries in the Pacific Northwest — worth finding even if art is not your usual priority. The [Old Idaho Penitentiary](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/old_idaho_penitentiary) to the east of downtown is a well-preserved Victorian-era prison with a genuinely atmospheric tour. [Table Rock](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/table_rock), the sandstone mesa above the city, offers the best panoramic view over Boise and the Snake River Plain.

Boise makes a natural base for exploring central Idaho. The Sawtooth Mountains are a few hours north, the Snake River canyon lies to the south, and the city's own backyard — [Bogus Basin Mountain Recreation Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/boise/bogus_basin) — is just 16 miles from downtown.
