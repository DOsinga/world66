Albertsons Stadium on the Boise State University campus is home to one of the most recognizable football fields in college sports: the Smurf Turf, a vivid blue artificial surface that has been Boise State's visual signature since 1986. The blue turf was the first non-green playing field in college football and caused immediate controversy, both aesthetically and competitively.

The stadium holds about 36,000 and is consistently one of the louder venues in the Mountain West conference. Boise State football has been a genuine national success story — the 2007 Fiesta Bowl upset of Oklahoma is the most famous game in school history. Even if you are not a football fan, a game day in Boise is an experience worth catching, with tailgating that spreads across campus and the Greenbelt.
