Boise Art Museum, usually shortened to BAM, is the city's main visual-arts museum and one of the anchors of Julia Davis Park. Its exhibitions mix regional work, contemporary art, craft, photography, and touring shows, so the visit changes noticeably from season to season.

The museum is compact enough for a focused hour but serious enough to reward more time when the exhibitions are strong. It pairs naturally with the Idaho State Museum, the zoo, and the riverfront paths nearby, making Julia Davis Park one of Boise's easiest half-day cultural clusters.
