Boise Contemporary Theater gives Idaho's capital a serious small-stage venue, with programming focused on contemporary plays and new work. It is a good reminder that Boise's cultural life is not limited to outdoor access and state institutions.

The theatre is worthwhile when a production matches your dates. Its intimate scale makes performances feel immediate, and an evening here pairs well with downtown Boise's restaurants, bars, and walkable core.
