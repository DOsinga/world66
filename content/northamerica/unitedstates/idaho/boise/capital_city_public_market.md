Capital City Public Market is Boise's downtown Saturday market, with Idaho produce, baked goods, prepared food, flowers, crafts, and the easy social energy that makes a city feel lived in. It is less about a single famous stall than the pleasure of browsing a compact, walkable downtown while locals do the same.

The market is seasonal and weather-dependent, so check dates before planning around it. If it is running, go hungry and use it as a morning anchor before the Basque Block, Freak Alley, or the Boise River Greenbelt.
