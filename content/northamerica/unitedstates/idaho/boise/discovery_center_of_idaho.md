The Discovery Center of Idaho is Boise's hands-on science museum, built around experiments, demonstrations, and rotating exhibitions rather than glass-case displays. It is especially useful for families, but curious adults can still enjoy the better interactive installations.

Its downtown location makes it easy to combine with the Boise River Greenbelt and nearby parks. Check the current exhibition before going, since the quality of a visit depends heavily on what temporary show is installed.
