The Idaho State Museum is Boise’s main place to understand the state beyond its fast-growing capital. Exhibits cover Indigenous history, mining, migration, agriculture, landscapes, and the political story of Idaho in a polished, accessible format.

It pairs naturally with the State Capitol and Julia Davis Park area. The museum is especially useful for travellers using Boise as a launch point into a state whose geography and culture change dramatically from desert to mountains.
