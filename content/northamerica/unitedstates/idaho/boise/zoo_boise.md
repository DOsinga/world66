Zoo Boise is one of southern Idaho's main family attractions, with animals from dozens of species and conservation-focused exhibits. A Gorongosa National Park-themed expansion added more space and an Africa conservation focus.

The zoo sits close to central Boise and works well for families looking for a manageable half-day stop. Check the schedule for feeding times, special programs, and discount admission days.
