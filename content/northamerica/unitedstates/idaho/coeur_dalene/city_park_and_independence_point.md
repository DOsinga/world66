City Park and Independence Point make the lake feel public, not just scenic. The park has a swim beach, lawns, picnic space, volleyball courts, and shade, while Independence Point connects it to downtown and the marina activity around the resort.

Come here for an easy swim, a picnic, or the evening light over Lake Coeur d'Alene. It is not wilderness, but it is the place where the city, the beach, and the boat traffic all meet.
