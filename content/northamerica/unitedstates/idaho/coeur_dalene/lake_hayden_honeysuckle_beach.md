Lake Hayden is a popular summer lake north of Coeur d'Alene, with Honeysuckle Beach serving as one of its easiest public access points. The beach has lifeguards in summer and draws families for swimming, picnics, and lake views.

It is best treated as a warm-weather outing rather than a quick roadside stop. The lake is deep, the shoreline is developed in places, and parking can be busy on good weekends.
