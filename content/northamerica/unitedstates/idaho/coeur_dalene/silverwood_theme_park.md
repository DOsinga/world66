Silverwood Theme Park is the major family attraction north of Coeur d'Alene, with roller coasters, rides for younger children, and the adjacent Boulder Beach water park. It is large enough to fill a full day, and families often split time between the dry rides and water attractions.

It is outside town, so plan it as a dedicated outing rather than a quick stop between lake activities.
