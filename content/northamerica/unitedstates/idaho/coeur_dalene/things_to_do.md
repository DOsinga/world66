type: section
---

Coeur d'Alene is an easy place to spend a day outside. Start with the trails around [Tubbs Hill](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/coeur_dalene/tubbs_hill), then drop to [City Park and Independence Point](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/coeur_dalene/city_park_and_independence_point) for the beach, marina views, and a walk along the lakefront.

If you want the lake without renting a boat, [Lake Coeur d'Alene Cruises](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/coeur_dalene/lake_coeur_dalene_cruises) runs scenic trips from downtown. Leave time for the [Museum of North Idaho](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/coeur_dalene/museum_of_north_idaho), which explains the region behind the resort gloss: Indigenous history, Fort Sherman, railroads, steamboats, timber, and mining.
