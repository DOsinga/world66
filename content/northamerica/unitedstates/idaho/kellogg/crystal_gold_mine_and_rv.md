Crystal Gold Mine gives visitors a direct way into the Silver Valley's mining story. Rather than just seeing headframes and scars from the road, you can take a guided underground tour and get a clearer sense of the narrow, physical work behind the region's boom years.

It is a small commercial attraction, but it fits Kellogg well because mining is the reason the town is here. Pair it with the Bunker Hill story or Silver Mountain and the valley begins to read as a landscape shaped by extraction, recreation, and reinvention.
