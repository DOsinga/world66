The Mining and Smelting Museum is a focused Silver Valley stop, preserving the industrial story that made Kellogg and nearby mining towns. Exhibits cover hard-rock mining, smelting, labor, community life, and the environmental legacy of one of Idaho's defining resource districts.

For visitors driving I-90 or exploring the old mining corridor, the museum gives context that scenic viewpoints alone cannot provide. It pairs naturally with the Bunker Hill complex, historic downtown Kellogg, or the Route of the Hiawatha region.
