title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kellogg's main draw is [Silver Mountain Resort](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/kellogg/silver_mountain_resort), accessible by the longest single-stage gondola in the world — a 3.1-mile ride that climbs nearly 3,400 feet from downtown. In winter this means skiing and snowboarding on over 80 runs; in summer it means hiking and mountain biking with the lift doing the work uphill. The gondola alone is worth taking for the views across the Silver Valley and the Coeur d'Alene National Forest.

The [Bunker Hill Mine and Smelting Complex](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/kellogg/bunker_hill_mine) tells the other half of the town's story — one of the worst environmental contamination episodes in US history, now one of the country's largest Superfund cleanup projects. The iconic [Miner's Hat](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/kellogg/miners_hat), a 1950s root-beer-stand shaped like a giant helmet, is on the National Register of Historic Places and worth a stop just for the absurdity.

The [Coeur d'Alene National Forest](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/kellogg/coeur_dalene_national_forest) surrounding the town has fishing, camping, and wildlife — elk and moose in the valleys, mountain goats higher up.
