Fort Hall Replica recreates the 19th-century trading post that once stood on the Snake River plain. The site is compact, with log walls, period rooms, and displays about trappers, traders, emigrants, and Native communities who moved through the region.

It works best paired with the Bannock County Historical Museum next door. Neither is slick, but together they give Pocatello a historical anchor that is easy to miss if you only see the town from the interstate.
