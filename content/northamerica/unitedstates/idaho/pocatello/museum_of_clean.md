The Museum of Clean is exactly what it sounds like, and stranger than that sounds. Its collection ranges from antique vacuums and washing machines to household tools, advertising, and displays about hygiene and domestic work.

The charm is in the commitment. It is not a must-see for everyone, but on a long road trip it offers the rare pleasure of a museum you will not confuse with anywhere else.
