St. Joseph's Chapel was built in 1897 and is one of Pocatello's oldest surviving churches. Its stone construction makes it a rare local example of nineteenth-century church architecture.

The chapel is a short architecture and history stop near Old Town, best visited as part of a wider walk through Pocatello's older streets.
