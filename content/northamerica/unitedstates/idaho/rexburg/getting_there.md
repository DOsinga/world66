---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Rexburg has its own small airport — Rexburg-Madison County Airport — but scheduled commercial service is limited. Most visitors fly into **Idaho Falls Regional Airport** (IDA), about 30 miles to the south, which has connections to Salt Lake City, Denver, and Seattle. From Idaho Falls, Rexburg is a straightforward 30-minute drive north on US-20.

By road, Rexburg sits on US-20, the main corridor between Idaho Falls and West Yellowstone, Montana. If you are driving from Salt Lake City, the journey is around four hours north via I-15 and US-20. From Boise, it's roughly four and a half hours east across southern Idaho. There is no passenger rail service. Greyhound and regional bus connections exist but are infrequent — a car is the practical choice for this part of Idaho, especially if you intend to continue to Yellowstone or Grand Teton.
