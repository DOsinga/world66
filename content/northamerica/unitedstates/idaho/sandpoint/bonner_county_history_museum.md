The Bonner County History Museum is a modest but useful stop in Lakeview Park, marked by the yellow caboose on its lawn. Its collections cover the wider Sandpoint region, from settlement and railroads to timber, lake travel, and local communities.

It pairs well with a walk by the lake. Sandpoint's natural setting is obvious at a glance; the museum helps explain how people made a town at this particular bend of water and rail.
