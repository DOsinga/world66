type: section
---

Sandpoint's best day mixes lake, mountain, and downtown. Spend the warm part of the day at [Sandpoint City Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/sandpoint/sandpoint_city_beach) or head up to [Schweitzer](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/sandpoint/schweitzer) for skiing, biking, hiking, or a scenic lift ride, depending on the season.

Back in town, the [Panida Theater](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/sandpoint/panida_theater) is the cultural anchor and the [Bonner County History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/sandpoint/bonner_county_history_museum) is the local history stop that helps the lake-and-railroad setting make sense.
