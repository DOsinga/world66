The novel of the Spanish Civil War that Hemingway wrote in Suite 206 of the Sun Valley Lodge in the autumn of 1939, with Martha Gellhorn typing alongside him. The wide Wood River Valley views from his window are nothing like the Sierra de Guadarrama where the story is set, but the long stretches of waiting and weather and inner monologue might have been easier to write from the bottom of a snowed-in Idaho hotel than from anywhere closer to the war.

Not the place to start with Hemingway, but the place to read him if you are already here.
