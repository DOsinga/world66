The Sun Valley Museum of History uses historic 1933 buildings to tell the story of the Wood River Valley. Exhibits cover the area's Indigenous history, mining and ranching years, ski culture, artists, and athletes.

It is a useful stop before diving into Sun Valley's resort history, especially if you want the place to feel like more than slopes and lodges.
