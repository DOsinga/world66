Driggs is the practical centre of [Teton Valley](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/teton_valley), a small Idaho town with a large mountain horizon. It has groceries, outfitters, cafes, and enough local services to make it more than a bedroom community for Jackson Hole, though the Wyoming side of the Tetons is never far from the conversation.

The biggest draw is access. [Grand Targhee Resort](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/teton_valley/driggs/grand_targhee_resort) sits up the road on the western slope of the Tetons, with deep winter snow and summer mountain biking. In town, the [Teton Geotourism Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/teton_valley/driggs/teton_geotourism_center) and a compact Main Street help orient you before you head for trailheads, scenic drives, or the valley trail toward [Victor](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/teton_valley/victor).

Driggs is quieter and cheaper than Jackson, but it is no longer undiscovered. Book ahead in ski season and summer, and remember that Teton Pass can turn a short-looking drive into a serious winter crossing.
