Driggs-Reed Memorial Airport is not a standard tourist sight, but aviation people may want to detour past it. Small aircraft, mountain flying, and the Teton skyline give the place a character that fits the valley.

Treat it as a quick look rather than a destination unless an event or museum exhibit is open. The airport also explains why Driggs attracts pilots and aircraft owners as well as skiers.
