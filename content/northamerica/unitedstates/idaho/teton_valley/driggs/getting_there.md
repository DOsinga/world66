---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Driggs is on Idaho Highway 33 in Teton Valley, about 35 miles from Jackson by way of Teton Pass and about 75 miles from Idaho Falls. The nearest major airport choices are Jackson Hole Airport and Idaho Falls Regional Airport.

In winter, check conditions before relying on Teton Pass. The road is steep, snowy, and sometimes closed for avalanche work; the Idaho Falls route is longer but often simpler.
