Grand Teton Distillery is a good reminder that eastern Idaho is potato country as well as mountain country. The distillery produces vodka and other spirits, with tours or tastings depending on schedule and season.

It is not a reason to come to Driggs by itself, but it makes an enjoyable low-key stop after skiing or hiking. Check opening times before driving out; small-town tasting rooms can keep irregular hours.
