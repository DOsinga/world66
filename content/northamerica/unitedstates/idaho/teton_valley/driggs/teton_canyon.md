Teton Canyon is one of the best ways to feel the mountains from the Idaho side. The road east from Driggs climbs toward trailheads, campgrounds, and views into the western folds of the Teton Range.

Conditions change quickly with snowmelt and storms, so check local reports before committing to a long hike. For many visitors, even a short walk from the canyon area is enough to understand why Teton Valley has become such a prized base.
