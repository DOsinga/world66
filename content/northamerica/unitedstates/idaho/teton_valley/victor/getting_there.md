---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Victor is 13 kilometres south of [Driggs](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/teton_valley/driggs) on US-33, and about 48 kilometres from Jackson, Wyoming, over Teton Pass. The drive from Jackson takes about 45 minutes in good conditions; allow longer in winter when the pass can be slow or temporarily closed.

The nearest commercial airport is Jackson Hole Airport in Wyoming (JAC), which has direct flights to major US hubs. Idaho Falls Regional Airport (IDA), about 90 minutes west, offers more affordable options with connections through Salt Lake City.

There is no public transit to Victor. A car is essential.
