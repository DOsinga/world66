The Teton Valley Trail is a paved path running through the heart of Teton Valley, connecting Victor in the south with [Driggs](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/teton_valley/driggs) and extending toward [Tetonia](/northamerica/unitedstates/idaho/teton_valley/tetonia) to the north. The trail runs along the valley floor with almost no elevation change, making it accessible to cyclists and walkers of any fitness level.

What makes it exceptional is the view: the trail runs with the Teton Range in full sight to the east throughout most of its length. On a clear day, the Grand Teton dominates the skyline in a way that feels almost theatrical — you can see why early visitors thought they were looking at a painting. The valley floor is flat and wide, and the trail gives unobstructed views that are impossible to get from a car on the adjacent highway.
