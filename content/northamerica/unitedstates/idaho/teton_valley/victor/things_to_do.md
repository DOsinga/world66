Victor is a small town, and the main draws are either in or near it: the railroad depot, the views of the Teton Range from the valley floor, and the backcountry skiing and biking access from Teton Pass. The town itself has a few galleries and outdoor outfitters along Main Street, and the recent growth has brought several good restaurants that serve the outdoor crowd.
