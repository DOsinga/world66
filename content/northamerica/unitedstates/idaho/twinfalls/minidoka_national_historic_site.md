Minidoka National Historic Site preserves part of the World War II camp where Japanese Americans were incarcerated after being forcibly removed from the West Coast. The site includes remains of the entrance area, reconstructed features, interpretive trails, and a visitor center with exhibits and a film. It is a sobering, important stop in the Magic Valley region.
