The Anchor is a reliable downtown spot that handles the transition from lunch spot to evening restaurant well. The menu runs to burgers, sandwiches, and more substantial plates, with local ingredients woven through. The bar side is lively on weekends and carries a decent selection of regional craft beers, including offerings from local breweries in the Magic Valley.

It's the kind of place that fills a gap Twin Falls needs — comfortable, not trying too hard, and consistent. The patio is good in warm weather. Prices are moderate and portions are generous.
