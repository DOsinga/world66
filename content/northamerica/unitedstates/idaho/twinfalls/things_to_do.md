Twin Falls packs surprising variety into a small agricultural city. The Snake River Canyon dominates the experience — it runs right along the north edge of town and you can walk to the rim in minutes. Shoshone Falls, the Perrine Bridge, and the canyon itself are the headline attractions, but the city has more substance than most drive-through visitors realise.
