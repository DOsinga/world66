Beall Mansion is a turn-of-the-century house in Alton's Middletown Historic District, built as a wedding gift and preserved with period furnishings. Guided tours focus on the building's architecture, interiors, and local history.

It is a compact stop rather than a full museum visit, but it adds another piece to Alton's cluster of historic houses, river-town landmarks, and Civil War-era sites.
