The Lewis & Clark Trail Site and Museum stands near the confluence country where the Corps of Discovery prepared to head west. Its reconstructed Camp River Dubois setting helps turn the expedition from a schoolbook route into a specific winter of logistics, training, and uncertainty.

For Alton travelers, it fits naturally with the Meeting of the Great Rivers scenic drive. The museum is not huge, but the river geography gives it meaning: this was the edge between known routes and the long interior journey. It is best visited with a little time to continue along the river road.
