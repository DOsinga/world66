Near the southern end of Alton's College Avenue stands a life-size bronze statue of Robert Wadlow, the tallest person in recorded history. Born in Alton in 1918, Wadlow grew to 2.72 metres (8 feet 11.1 inches) before his death at age 22 — a height never equalled before or since in documented medical records.

The statue is accurate to his actual dimensions, which means it is startlingly tall when you stand next to it. Most visitors have a photo taken beside it for scale. The nearby Alton Museum of History and Art has exhibits on Wadlow's life, including original clothing, shoes, and photographs documenting his childhood and young adulthood in Alton.
