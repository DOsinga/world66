---
title: "Things to Do in Alton"
type: section
---

Alton packs a remarkable amount of American history into a small river town. The Lincoln-Douglas debate site, the Elijah Lovejoy monument, Robert Wadlow's statue, and the Alton Museum of History and Art can all be covered in a day. The river views from the limestone bluffs are among the best on the Upper Mississippi, especially in autumn when the trees turn.
