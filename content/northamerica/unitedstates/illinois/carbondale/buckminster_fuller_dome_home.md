The R. Buckminster Fuller and Anne Hewlett Dome Home at 407 S. Forest Avenue is one of the most architecturally significant small buildings in Illinois. Fuller — inventor of the geodesic dome, the word "Spaceship Earth," and dozens of other design innovations — lived and worked here from 1960 to 1971 while teaching at Southern Illinois University Carbondale.

The dome is a modest structure by any measure — it looks more like a large igloo than a grand monument — but its cultural and historical significance is considerable. Fuller used the house as a kind of living laboratory for his ideas about efficient, lightweight shelter. It has been preserved largely intact and is now managed as a museum by the R. Buckminster Fuller & Anne Hewlett Dome Home Trust. Tours can be arranged in advance.
