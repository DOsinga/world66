Jeremy "Boo" Rochman Memorial Park, often called Boo Castle Park, is a fantasy playground just outside Carbondale. Its castle-like play structure, carved figures, dragons, and storybook details make it more memorable than a routine neighborhood park, especially for families with younger children.
