Anderson Mansion is an 1883 house museum in Carlinville, with local history exhibits, period material, and memorabilia inside a large late-19th-century residence. Visits are by tour, so check ahead before making it the centre of a stop.

It pairs naturally with Carlinville's courthouse, jail, and Sears homes for a compact local-history circuit.
