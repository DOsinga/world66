311 South Wacker Drive is a postmodern skyscraper just west of the Loop, easy to spot by its illuminated crown. For a time it was the tallest building in the world without an official name, though its shape and bright top made it anything but anonymous.

If you are nearby, step into the lobby for the large indoor garden and fountain. The building is best treated as a quick architecture stop near Willis Tower.
