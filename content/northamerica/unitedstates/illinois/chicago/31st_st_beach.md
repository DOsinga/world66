31st St Beach is a quieter South Side lakefront beach with open views back toward the Chicago skyline. It is smaller and less hectic than the better-known North Side beaches, which is the point.

Come for a swim, a picnic, or a walk along the lakefront path. The beach is family-friendly and works especially well when you want the Lake Michigan setting without the downtown crowds.
