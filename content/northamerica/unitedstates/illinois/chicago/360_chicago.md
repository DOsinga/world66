360 Chicago is the observation deck on the 94th floor of 875 North Michigan Avenue, the former John Hancock Center. It is not as high as the Willis Tower Skydeck, but the lakefront position gives excellent views over Lake Michigan, the Gold Coast, and the downtown skyline.
