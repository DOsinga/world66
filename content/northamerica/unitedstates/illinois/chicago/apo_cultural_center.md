The A.P.O. Cultural Center is one of Pilsen's smaller arts stops, set inside a heavy stone building that feels more institutional than polished. Exhibitions are usually free, with occasional concerts and community events, so it works best as part of a wider walk through the neighbourhood's murals and galleries.
