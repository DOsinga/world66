Auditorium Theatre is one of Chicago's great architectural interiors, designed by Dankmar Adler and Louis Sullivan and admired by Frank Lloyd Wright, who worked on the project as an apprentice. The room was built for music and opera, and it still feels grand without losing its warmth.

If there is a performance you want to see, that is the best way in. Otherwise, check tour times: the building is worth seeing for its acoustics, ornament, and place in Chicago's architectural story.
