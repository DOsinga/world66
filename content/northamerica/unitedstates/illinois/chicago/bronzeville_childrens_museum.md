Bronzeville Children's Museum is a small South Side museum focused on African American history and culture for younger children. Despite the name, it is farther south than Bronzeville, but the exhibits use the neighborhood's history as a starting point for hands-on learning.

This is mainly a family stop, best for travelers with children who want something more local and less crowded than the major lakefront museums.
