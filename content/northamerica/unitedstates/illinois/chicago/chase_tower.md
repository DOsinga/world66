Chase Tower is one of the Loop's more recognisable office towers, with a tall, slightly curved facade rising above the centre of Chicago's transit grid. The building itself is businesslike, but the plaza is worth a quick look for Marc Chagall's large mosaic, *Four Seasons*, set among the surrounding canyon of downtown towers.
