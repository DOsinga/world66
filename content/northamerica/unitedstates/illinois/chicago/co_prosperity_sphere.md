Co-Prosperity Sphere is an experimental art space in Bridgeport, used for exhibitions, performances, community events, and projects that sit outside Chicago's more formal gallery circuit. It is the sort of place where the programme matters more than the building.

Check what is on before crossing town. When an exhibition or event is running, it gives a good look at Bridgeport's independent arts scene.
