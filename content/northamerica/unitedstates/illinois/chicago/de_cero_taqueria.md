One of the neighbourhood's most reliable taquerias — a counter-service spot on West 26th Street where the tortillas are made fresh throughout the day and the fillings (carnitas, barbacoa, al pastor, lengua) are prepared from whole cuts rather than pre-packaged. The salsas come in varying intensities of heat and are all worth trying.

Pilsen has arguably the best Mexican food in Chicago — the neighbourhood's taquerias and bakeries serve a resident Mexican-American population rather than tourists, which keeps standards high and prices reasonable. La Paloma is a particularly good starting point for exploring the neighbourhood's food on a budget. Pink Line, 18th station.
