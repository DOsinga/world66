The DuSable Museum is Chicago's main museum of African American history, named for Jean Baptiste Point du Sable, the Haitian-born trader usually remembered as the city's first non-Indigenous settler. Its galleries cover Black history in Chicago and beyond, with temporary shows that often give the museum its strongest punch.

It sits in Washington Park, close to the University of Chicago and the rest of Hyde Park's museums and architecture. Pair it with a walk through the Midway or the nearby Robie House if you are spending a day on the South Side.
