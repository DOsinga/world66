The DuSable Museum is Chicago's main museum of African-American history, named for Jean Baptiste Point du Sable, the Haitian trader widely regarded as the city's first non-Indigenous permanent settler. Its galleries cover Black history, art, politics, and civic life, with temporary exhibitions that often give the museum its sharpest edge.

It sits in Washington Park, just west of the University of Chicago and Hyde Park's other museums. Pair it with the Smart Museum, Robie House, or a walk through the surrounding South Side neighborhoods for a fuller sense of Chicago beyond the Loop.
