The Elks National Veterans Memorial is a grand neoclassical memorial near Lincoln Park, built by the Benevolent and Protective Order of Elks to honor American veterans. The exterior is imposing, but the interior is the real draw, with marble, bronze sculpture, stained glass, and large murals.

It is worth a short detour for architecture and memorial history, especially if you are already exploring Lincoln Park or the lakefront nearby.
