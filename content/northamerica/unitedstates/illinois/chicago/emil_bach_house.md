The Emil Bach House is a compact late Prairie School house designed by Frank Lloyd Wright in Rogers Park. It is privately owned, so most visitors see it from the street, but the restored exterior is still a worthwhile stop for architecture fans tracing Wright's work on Chicago's North Side.
