Chicago's Federal Center is a sharp modernist plaza framed by Mies van der Rohe's Dirksen and Kluczynski federal buildings. The main reason to stop is the contrast between the severe black towers and Alexander Calder's bright red Flamingo sculpture in the open plaza.
