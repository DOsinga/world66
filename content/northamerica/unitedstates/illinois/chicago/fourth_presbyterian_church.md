Fourth Presbyterian Church brings a dose of Gothic Revival calm to one of Chicago's busiest shopping streets. The 1912 church sits beside the Magnificent Mile, with stone tracery, a quiet courtyard, and a sanctuary that feels far removed from the traffic outside.

It is an easy stop while walking North Michigan Avenue, especially if you want a short architectural pause between the shops, galleries, and museums of the Near North Side.
