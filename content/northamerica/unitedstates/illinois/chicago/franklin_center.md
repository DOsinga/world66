Franklin Center is one of the more distinctive towers in the Loop, a granite-clad 1989 skyscraper with sharp roof pinnacles and a mix of Gothic and Japanese-inspired details. It is mainly an exterior stop, best appreciated while walking between the Loop's older landmarks and the Willis Tower.
