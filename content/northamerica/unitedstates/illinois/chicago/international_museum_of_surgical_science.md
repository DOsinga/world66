The International Museum of Surgical Science fills a historic lakefront mansion with four floors on the history of medicine and surgery. The collection ranges from ancient surgical tools to prosthetics, medical art, and instruments that make the subject feel physical and sometimes unsettling.

It is a strong niche museum, especially for travellers interested in science, anatomy, or medical history. The setting near North Avenue Beach and Lincoln Park makes it easy to combine with a wider walk on the lakefront.
