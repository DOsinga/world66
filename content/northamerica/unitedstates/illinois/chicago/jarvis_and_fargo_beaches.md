Jarvis and Fargo beaches are small Edgewater lakefront spots that feel more local than Chicago's better-known swimming beaches. Jarvis is the livelier of the two in summer, while Fargo is better for a quiet walk, a patch of sand, and a look at the far north side's everyday relationship with Lake Michigan.
