King Drive Gateway is a stretch of Martin Luther King Jr. Drive in Bronzeville lined with monuments, plaques, and public art tied to the neighborhood's African-American history. Look for the Monument to the Great Northern Migration, the Victory Monument, and sidewalk plaques honoring notable Bronzeville residents.

The whole route is more than a mile long, so it works best as a short self-guided walk around the main monuments rather than a single formal attraction.
