The broad residential neighbourhood north of Lincoln Park, most famous internationally for Wrigleyville — the tight grid of streets around **Wrigley Field** where the Chicago Cubs play. On game days the neighbourhood is transformed: every bar on **Clark Street** fills up, the surrounding streets clog with traffic, and the rooftop bleachers on Waveland and Sheffield avenues — positioned to look directly into the outfield — host their own parallel parties.

Beyond baseball, Lakeview is home to a dense concentration of bars, theatres, and restaurants along **Broadway** and **Belmont Avenue**. The **Boystown** section around Halsted and Belmont is Chicago's main LGBT neighbourhood. **Schubas Tavern** on Southport is one of the best small music venues in the city; the Southport strip more broadly is one of the quieter, more pleasant shopping streets on the North Side.
