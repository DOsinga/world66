Diversey Harbor, accessible from the lakefront path at Diversey Parkway, is a full-service marina at the east end of the Lakeview neighbourhood. The harbour offers kayak and paddleboard rentals, a boathouse café, and the lakefront path access that connects the neighbourhood to the parks to the north and south.

The Diversey driving range (miniature golf and driving range) operates on the lakefront here, providing an unusual amenity in a major urban park. The harbour is considerably quieter than the beaches further north and south, making it a good base for a morning on the water. The park around the harbour includes the Sydney Marovitz Golf Course, one of the few public golf courses accessible from a major urban L stop.
