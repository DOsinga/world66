The neighbourhood stretching north from Old Town along the lakefront, named for the vast park it borders. Lincoln Park is prosperous, green, and well-served with restaurants and bars — the kind of neighbourhood where young professionals settle and stay for decades. The park itself — Chicago's largest, at over 1,200 acres — runs the full length of the neighbourhood along Lake Michigan.

The **Lincoln Park Zoo** is free, which is both unusual and excellent — one of the last major urban zoos in the country without an admission charge. **North Avenue Beach** draws enormous crowds in summer. **Depaul University** occupies a stretch of the southern section. **Armitage Avenue** and **Lincoln Avenue** are the main restaurant and shopping strips; **Halsted Street** has the most concentrated nightlife. The neighbourhood has some of Chicago's most handsome Victorian greystones.
