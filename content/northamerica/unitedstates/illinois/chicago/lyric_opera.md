Lyric Opera performs in the Civic Opera House, a grand Art Deco building on the Chicago River. The company is one of the major opera institutions in the United States, and the auditorium is worth noticing even if you are mainly interested in the architecture.

The best reason to plan around it is a performance, but the riverfront setting also makes it easy to combine with a walk through the western Loop and nearby early skyscraper landmarks.
