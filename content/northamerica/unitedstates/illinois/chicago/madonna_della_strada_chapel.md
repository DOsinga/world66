Madonna della Strada Chapel is an Art Deco chapel on Loyola University's lakefront campus. Built in 1938, it combines a strong modern profile with traditional Catholic decoration and has one of Chicago's largest pipe organs.

It is worth a short stop if you are exploring Rogers Park or the Loyola area, especially for the lakeside setting and the restored interior.
