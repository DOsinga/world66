Maggie Daley Park is the active eastern half of the Millennium Park area, with a skating ribbon in winter, climbing walls, mini golf, and a large play garden. It is especially useful for families who need space to move after museums or downtown sightseeing.

The park also works as part of a longer walk from Michigan Avenue toward the lakefront, linking Millennium Park, the Art Institute area, and the paths along Lake Michigan.
