Masjid Al-Faatir is one of Chicago's notable mosques, founded in 1987 by Muhammad Ali and his manager Jabir Herbert Muhammad. That connection gives it a place in the city's African-American, Muslim, and sporting history.

It is not a conventional museum or landmark with long visitor interpretation, so treat it respectfully as an active religious site. For travellers interested in Ali's life beyond the boxing ring, it is a meaningful Hyde Park-area stop.
