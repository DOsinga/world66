The McCormick Tribune Campus Center at Illinois Institute of Technology is a Rem Koolhaas building wrapped around the elevated Green Line tracks. Its stainless-steel tube over the tracks is the feature most visitors notice first.

It is a campus building, but architecture-minded travellers may want to detour here while exploring Bronzeville or the IIT campus, especially with Mies van der Rohe buildings nearby.
