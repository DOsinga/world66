Montrose Beach is one of the main North Side beaches on Lake Michigan, with a wider, less central feel than the beaches closer to downtown. It is a good stop for a lakefront walk, summer swimming, or a break from the dense streets around Uptown and Lakeview.

The beach is also known for its dog-friendly area, and Foster Beach is a short distance north along the lakefront.
