The Museum of Contemporary Photography is a Columbia College Chicago museum devoted to contemporary image-making. Its exhibitions cover photography, digital processes, and experimental work that pushes beyond the traditional camera print.

It is a compact but serious museum, easy to pair with the Art Institute, Grant Park, or Printer's Row when you want a focused cultural stop in the South Loop.
