The National Italian American Sports Hall of Fame is a small museum devoted to Italian-American athletes and sports figures. Its collection highlights inductees from baseball, boxing, football, and other professional sports.

It is a niche stop, but worthwhile for sports fans already exploring the Near West Side or nearby Greektown.
