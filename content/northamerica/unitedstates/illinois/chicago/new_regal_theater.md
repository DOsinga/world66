The New Regal Theater is a grand 1927 movie palace on Chicago's South Side, built in ornate Moorish Revival style. Its stage later drew major blues, jazz, soul, and R&B performers, giving the building a long place in the city's music history.

Access depends on events and current use, but the facade and side mural still make it a worthwhile architecture stop if you are exploring this part of Chicago.
