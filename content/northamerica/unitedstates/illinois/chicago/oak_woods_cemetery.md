Oak Woods Cemetery is a large, landscaped South Side cemetery with ponds, mature trees, and graves tied to Chicago and American history. Notable burials include Jesse Owens, Enrico Fermi, Ida B. Wells, Harold Washington, and Confederate prisoners from Camp Douglas.
