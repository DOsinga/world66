A scruffy, loveable dive bar on North Wells Street that has been operating since 1958 and shows no signs of self-improvement. The walls are covered with portraits of regulars, politicians, and celebrities painted by the owner over several decades — including a notorious series of caricatures that made national news. The crowd is a genuine neighbourhood mix: old regulars, performers from Second City next door, and late-night crowds after the comedy shows.

Cash only. No food. Open until 4 am on weekends. The bar's charm is entirely in its refusal to become anything other than what it is. One of the few authentic old-school Chicago bars left on the North Side. Red or Brown Line, Sedgwick station.
