Old Town occupies a small area just north of downtown, bounded by Division Street to the south and Armitage to the north. It has been continuously popular since the 1960s when the Second City comedy club established its current address here, and has retained more of its 19th-century residential architecture than most comparable Chicago neighbourhoods.

The best approach is to walk North Wells Street from Division to North Avenue — the commercial spine, with restaurants, bars, boutiques, and the Old Town School of Folk Music at the north end. The residential streets east and west of Wells retain much of their 1880s-era two- and three-flat buildings, making this one of the better neighbourhoods for understanding what Chicago looked like before the 20th century.
