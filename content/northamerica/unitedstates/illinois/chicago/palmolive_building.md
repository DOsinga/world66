The Palmolive Building is a 1929 Art Deco tower on North Michigan Avenue, first built for Colgate-Palmolive and later known for the Playboy offices. Its beacon still makes it a recognizable part of the Chicago skyline, though the beam is now limited by nearby taller buildings.
