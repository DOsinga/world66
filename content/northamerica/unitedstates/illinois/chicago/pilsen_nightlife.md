Pilsen's bar scene is centered on 18th Street and has developed a character distinct from the north-side neighbourhood bars: cheaper, louder, and more willing to mix live music with an everyday neighbourhood tavern function. Skylark and HopLeaf are the most established destinations; Thalia Hall (a 1892 Czech immigrant community hall converted to a music venue and bar complex) is the neighbourhood's most notable concert venue.

The bars here fill on Friday and Saturday nights with a mixed crowd of Pilsen residents, artists from the local studios, and visitors from other neighbourhoods. The neighbourhood's BYOB restaurant culture means eating out is cheaper here than almost anywhere else accessible on the L. Pilsen's nightlife is unpretentious and worth seeking out if the polished cocktail bars of River North or Wicker Park feel over-managed.
