The Polish Museum of America documents Polish culture in Chicago and Poland through art, archives, folk objects, and historic memorabilia. Its best-known holdings include material connected to pianist and statesman Ignacy Jan Paderewski.

It is a focused stop rather than a blockbuster museum, but it adds useful context to Chicago's long Polish story and the old immigrant neighbourhoods west of downtown.
