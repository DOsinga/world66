Prospectus Art Gallery is a small Pilsen stop for traditional and contemporary Latin American art. Its exhibitions change, and it can close for stretches between shows, so check before making a special trip. When it is open, it pairs well with the Pilsen mural district and the neighbourhood's wider gallery scene.
