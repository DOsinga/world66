A boutique hotel in a 1929 art deco bank building at the six-corners intersection, notable primarily for its rooftop bar — one of the better outdoor drinking venues on the North Side, with views across the flat Chicago grid and, on clear days, the downtown skyline. The bar is open to non-guests.

The ground-floor café and cocktail bar (called Up Room on the roof; the café Below for coffee and light meals) are both worth visiting. The hotel's design is thoughtful and the programming of its public spaces makes it a genuine neighbourhood amenity rather than a sealed tourist hotel. Reservations recommended for the rooftop in summer. Metro Blue Line, Damen station.
