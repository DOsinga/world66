Saint Barbara Church is a 1914 Catholic church in Bridgeport, built in the Polish Cathedral style to serve the growing parish around Saint Mary of Perpetual Help. Its scale and towers make it one of the landmarks of the Bridgeport skyline.

It is most rewarding for visitors interested in Chicago's immigrant neighbourhoods and church architecture, especially when paired with nearby Pilsen and Bridgeport heritage stops.
