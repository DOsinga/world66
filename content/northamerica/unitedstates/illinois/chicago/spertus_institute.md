Spertus Institute is a Jewish learning and culture centre on Michigan Avenue, housed in a striking glass-fronted building in the South Loop. Its public-facing exhibitions and events focus on Jewish art, culture, history, and social issues, with programming that changes through the year.

Check what is on before visiting, since it is more of an exhibition and events venue than a conventional museum with a large permanent display. It is close to Grant Park, the Museum Campus, and the South Loop's architecture corridor.
