St. Mary of the Angels is one of Chicago's great Polish Cathedral-style churches, with a broad dome, twin towers, and an ornate interior that reflects the scale and confidence of the city's Polish Catholic community.

It is worth a deliberate detour for church architecture, especially if you are exploring Bucktown, Wicker Park, or the Polish heritage sites on the Northwest Side.
