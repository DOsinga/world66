The Stephen A. Douglas Tomb and Memorial marks the grave of the Illinois senator best remembered for his debates with Abraham Lincoln and his 1860 presidential run. The 46-foot column stands in the Douglas neighborhood, a short historical stop that ties the South Side to one of the country's defining political arguments over slavery and union.
