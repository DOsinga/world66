A magnificently restored 1892 Czech community hall on West 18th Street, now operating as a music venue, two bars (Dusek's Board & Beer downstairs, Punch House cocktail bar below), and a restaurant. The main hall has a horseshoe balcony and excellent acoustics; it programmes indie rock, jazz, and international music.

The Punch House in the basement is one of the best cocktail bars in Chicago — a narrow room focused on punch bowls and classic cocktails, with a very serious but completely unpretentious approach. Come for a show and stay for cocktails, or just come for cocktails. Pink Line, 18th station.
