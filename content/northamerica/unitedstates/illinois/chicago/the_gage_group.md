The Gage Group is a row of late-19th-century commercial buildings on South Michigan Avenue, with the northern building designed by Louis Sullivan and the others by Holabird & Roche. Look up at the facade details: Sullivan's ornament and the cleaner neighbouring fronts make the group a useful stop for anyone tracing Chicago architecture.
