Tribune Tower is a Gothic Revival landmark on Michigan Avenue, completed in 1925 for the Chicago Tribune. Its exterior includes stones collected from famous sites around the world, including the Taj Mahal, the Parthenon, the Great Pyramid, Notre-Dame, and the Berlin Wall.
