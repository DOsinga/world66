The main commercial strip of Old Town — a stretch of North Wells Street between Division Street and North Avenue lined with independent restaurants, bars, vintage shops, and the occasional long-established institution. The **Spoon Thai** restaurant on North Wells has been feeding the neighbourhood since 1995; **Kamehachi** is a reliable sushi option; **Café Ba-Ba-Reeba!** on Halsted does creditable Spanish tapas.

The street has gentrified considerably since the 1970s bohemian days but maintains more independent character than comparable North Side strips. On summer evenings, the pavement fills out and the whole stretch becomes a pleasant place to walk and choose somewhere for dinner. Red Line, North/Clybourn station.
