A neighbourhood American bistro on West Chicago Avenue that has been serving the Ukrainian Village and West Town community since 2003. The menu is classic comfort food executed with care: braised short rib, roast chicken, mac and cheese, and seasonal specials that change with what's available at the Green City Market. The bar programme features a serious whiskey selection and well-made cocktails.

The dining room is warm and unfussy — exposed brick, low lighting, the kind of neighbourhood restaurant where regulars eat weekly. Reservations recommended on weekends; the bar takes walk-ins. Blue Line, Chicago station.
