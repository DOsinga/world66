Wicker Park is defined by the six-corners intersection where Milwaukee, North, and Damen avenues meet — one of the most energetic street-level environments in Chicago. The neighbourhood built its reputation through the 1990s as the city's alternative arts and music hub, a character it has mostly held despite significant gentrification since.

**Reckless Records** on Milwaukee Avenue is one of the last great independent record stores in the Midwest, carrying new and used vinyl across every genre. A few blocks away, **Myopic Books** on Milwaukee stocks an enormous collection of used books floor to ceiling. **Quimby's Bookstore** on North Avenue specialises in small press, zines, comics, and counterculture — it has operated continuously since 1991.

The **Empty Bottle** on Western Avenue is the neighbourhood's anchor music venue, known for booking noise, experimental, jazz, and indie acts in an unpretentious bar setting. The now-closed **Double Door** on Milwaukee was the larger venue for touring acts through the 1990s and 2000s; the building still stands at the six-corners. The **Flat Iron Arts Building** on Milwaukee houses artist studios and remains a working creative hub.

The surrounding blocks are lined with Queen Anne and Italianate houses from the 1880s and 1890s, built when the neighbourhood was home to prosperous German and Scandinavian merchant families. The **Damen** Blue Line stop puts you at the centre of everything; the **Division** stop on the Red Line is a short walk east. The adjacent **Bucktown** neighbourhood to the north shares a similar character with slightly more polish.
