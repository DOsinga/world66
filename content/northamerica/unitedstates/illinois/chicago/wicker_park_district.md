title: Wicker Park (the Park)
type: poi
---

The small triangular park that gives the neighbourhood its name, situated at the intersection of Damen, North, and Milwaukee Avenues — the commercial crossroads of one of Chicago's most dynamic neighbourhoods. The park itself is minor (a few benches, some grass, a fountain) but the surrounding intersection is the heart of a district that has been cycling through creative reinvention since the 1980s.

The six-corners of Damen/North/Milwaukee concentrates the neighbourhood's independent restaurants, boutiques, record stores, and bars. **Quimby's Bookstore** on North Avenue is the city's essential independent comic and zine shop. The **Flat Iron Building** at the intersection is a landmark building now filled with artist studios. Metro Blue Line, Damen station.
