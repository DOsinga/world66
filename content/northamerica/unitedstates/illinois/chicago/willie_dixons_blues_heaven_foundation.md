Willie Dixon's Blues Heaven Foundation occupies the old Chess Records building, where many of Chicago blues' best-known recordings were made. The site is tied to Muddy Waters, Howlin' Wolf, Etta James, Chuck Berry, and other major names.

For music fans, it is a focused stop that connects Chicago's South Side recording history with the sound that shaped blues, rock, and soul.
