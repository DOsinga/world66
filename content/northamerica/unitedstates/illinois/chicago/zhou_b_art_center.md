Zhou B Art Center is a large contemporary art space in Bridgeport, founded around the work and network of the Zhou Brothers. The building hosts galleries, studios, and changing exhibitions, with a stronger local art-scene feel than Chicago's major downtown museums.

The best time to visit is during public openings, especially the monthly third-Friday events when more of the building is active. Check the schedule before going, since individual galleries and studios may keep different hours.
