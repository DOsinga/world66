A small independent coffee shop on Collinsville's historic Main Street, open early on weekdays and doing brisk trade in espresso drinks and pastries. The space is comfortable without being fussy — exposed brick, a few leather chairs, decent Wi-Fi. It closes earlier than a city café would, typically by mid-afternoon, so plan accordingly. A good breakfast stop before heading to Cahokia Mounds.
