A Route 66-themed neighborhood bar near the Catsup Bottle that leans into the roadside Americana aesthetic without being gimmicky about it. The walls carry vintage road signs and old highway maps; the taps cover a mix of domestic and regional craft beers. Pool table, dartboards, and a decent jukebox. Locals outnumber tourists most nights, which keeps the atmosphere honest. Happy hour runs weekday afternoons.
