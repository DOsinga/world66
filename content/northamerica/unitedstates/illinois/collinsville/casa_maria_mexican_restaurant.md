A longtime local favorite on Collinsville's main commercial strip, Casa Maria serves straightforward Tex-Mex — enchiladas, fajitas, tamales — in generous portions at modest prices. The margaritas are strong and the booths fill up on weekends with families and convention center overflow. Nothing here will surprise you, but the food is consistently good and the service friendly. Cash and cards both accepted.
