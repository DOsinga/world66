Collinsville's position in Metro East puts several worthwhile destinations within an easy drive.

**St. Louis, Missouri** (20 minutes west) is the obvious anchor. The Gateway Arch National Park is free to enter the grounds and cheap to ride the tram to the top — arrive early to beat the lines. The St. Louis Art Museum in Forest Park is free and genuinely excellent, with a strong collection of European masters and pre-Columbian antiquities. The City Museum, a sprawling industrial sculpture playground, is unlike anything else.

**Grafton, Illinois** (45 minutes north) sits at the confluence of the Mississippi and Illinois rivers and has the feel of a small river town that discovered wine tourism. The bluffs above town offer dramatic views, especially in autumn. Pere Marquette State Park nearby has good hiking trails.

**Alton, Illinois** (30 minutes north) is a historic Mississippi River town with antique shops, a notable Civil War history as the site of the last Lincoln-Douglas debate, and a riverfront worth a walk.
