World Wide Technology Raceway, long known to many fans as Gateway, is the main motorsports venue for the St. Louis area and sits near Collinsville in Madison, Illinois. Its oval, drag strip, and road-course facilities host a mix of NASCAR, IndyCar, drag racing, and local events.

It is not a casual sightseeing stop; it matters when engines are running. Check the calendar before making the trip, and expect the experience to be about race-day noise, tailgating, and Midwestern motorsport culture.
