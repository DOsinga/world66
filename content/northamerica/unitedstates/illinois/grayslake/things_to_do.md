title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Grayslake's main outdoor asset is the network of forest preserves and restored natural areas in Lake County. Rollins Savanna is the standout — one of the largest restored grassland and wetland areas in northeastern Illinois, with good trails and excellent birding. The lake at the centre of town, Grays Lake, is a small kettle lake created by glacial meltwater that gives the village its name.
