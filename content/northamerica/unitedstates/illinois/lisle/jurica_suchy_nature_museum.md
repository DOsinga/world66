Jurica-Suchy Nature Museum is a compact natural history museum on the Benedictine University campus. Its collection includes fossils, shells, insects, birds, mammals, and teaching specimens gathered for students but useful for curious visitors too.

This is not a large public museum, and visits may require advance registration. It is best paired with other Lisle stops rather than treated as a standalone reason to travel.
