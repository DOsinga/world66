The Museums at Lisle Station Park are a small local-history complex near downtown, focused on 19th-century life in Lisle and DuPage County. Historic buildings and exhibits give the village a little depth beyond its commuter-suburb surface.

It is free and easy to combine with a walk around the train station area. Check opening days before going, since small local museums often keep limited hours.
