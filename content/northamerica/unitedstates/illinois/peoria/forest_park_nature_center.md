Forest Park Nature Center protects a wooded ravine landscape in the Illinois River bluffs, with trails, wildlife, and a visitor centre. It is one of Peoria's easiest ways to get into real greenery without leaving the city area.

Come for a short hike, birding, or a family nature break. The paths can feel surprisingly wild for an urban park, so choose a route that matches your time and footwear.
