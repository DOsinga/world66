One World Coffee and Goods operates out of a converted warehouse space in Peoria, and the building suits the business well: high ceilings, industrial details, plenty of natural light, and the slightly rough-edged character that comes from a space repurposed rather than built from scratch. It is the kind of coffee shop that a city of Peoria's size needs — independent, comfortable, with good espresso and a genuine atmosphere rather than a franchise formula.

The coffee is sourced thoughtfully and prepared well, and the space doubles as a gathering point for Peoria's creative and professional community. It is a reliable stop for breakfast or a mid-afternoon break, and the combination of good drinks and interesting surroundings makes it worth seeking out even if you are only passing through.

One World Coffee and Goods is a well-regarded independent coffee shop in a converted warehouse space north of downtown, serving specialty coffee alongside a small food menu in an industrial-chic setting with exposed beams and brick. It has become the preferred working and meeting spot for Peoria's creative and tech community, and the quality of the coffee — sourced from specialty roasters and prepared carefully — is considerably above what you will find at the chain cafés that dominate the suburbs.

The space is large and informal, with plenty of seating and reliable wifi. It opens early and is a good option for a morning before heading to the Caterpillar Visitors Center or the Riverfront Museum. One World also carries a selection of locally made goods, gifts, and provisions that reflect a genuine commitment to local producers rather than the usual generic gift shop merchandise.
