Walking or driving from one planet to the next takes a surprisingly long time, which is precisely the point.

The Peoria Scale Model of the Solar System is one of the largest such models in the world, spreading the planets across the city at a scale of one to ten billion. The Sun is represented by a large sphere at the Peoria Riverfront Museum on the riverfront, and each planet is located at its proportionally correct distance across the city — which means that the inner planets are relatively close together while the outer planets require a real journey by car or bicycle to visit.

It is a genuinely educational experience, and one that leaves a different impression than any diagram or animation. The model forces you to reckon physically with the scale of interplanetary distances; by the time you reach the outer planets you understand in your body, not just your head, how vast the gaps are. A map of the model's locations is available at the Riverfront Museum.
