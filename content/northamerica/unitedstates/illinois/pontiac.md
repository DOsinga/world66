Pontiac is a small Central Illinois city that has made itself useful to travelers by leaning hard into Route 66. That could have become kitsch, but here it mostly works: downtown is walkable, the museums are close together, and the murals give the streets a clear story.

The [Illinois Route 66 Hall of Fame and Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/illinois/pontiac/illinois_route_66_museum) is the main reason to stop, especially if you are following the old road from Chicago toward St. Louis. The [Murals on Main Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/illinois/pontiac/murals_on_main_street) turn the rest of downtown into a loose outdoor museum, while the [Pontiac-Oakland Automobile Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/illinois/pontiac/pontiac_oakland_automobile_museum) gives car people something more specific to chew on.

Pontiac is not a grand destination, but it passes the spare-day test if you are already nearby or driving Route 66. It has enough local pride, odd museums, and river walks to beat another generic interstate stop.
