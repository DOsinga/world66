---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Prairie du Rocher is a small village, but it sits in one of the most historically significant corners of Illinois — the old French colonial corridor along the Mississippi. The main draw is Fort de Chartres, one of the best-preserved French colonial sites in North America. The surrounding bottoms offer wildlife watching and quiet river scenery. If you are exploring the Great River Road, this is a worthwhile stop.
