Lincoln's Tomb in Oak Ridge Cemetery is the final resting place of Abraham and Mary Todd Lincoln, along with three of their four sons. The monument, a 117-foot obelisk topped with a bronze statue of Lincoln, is one of the most visited sites in Illinois. The interior contains a burial chamber and several memorial rooms with bronze reproductions of Lincoln's most famous addresses and sculptures depicting scenes from his life.

Oak Ridge Cemetery itself is one of the largest and oldest cemeteries in the United States, with notable monuments to Civil War veterans and Illinois leaders throughout. The walk from the parking area to the tomb passes through a landscape of mature trees and Victorian-era funerary sculpture. The site is managed by the Illinois Historic Preservation Agency and is open daily except Tuesdays; the grounds remain accessible outside of building hours.
