The Springfield and Central Illinois African-American History Museum adds an essential local perspective to a city often framed almost entirely through Abraham Lincoln. Exhibits focus on Black communities in Springfield and the wider region, including migration, education, military service, civic life, and the long struggle for civil rights.

It pairs well with the Lincoln sites because it complicates the usual presidential-history itinerary with community memory and lived experience. Allow enough time for the smaller displays, where photographs, documents, and local stories carry much of the weight.
