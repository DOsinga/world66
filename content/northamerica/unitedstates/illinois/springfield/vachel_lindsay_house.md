The Vachel Lindsay House adds a literary stop to a city dominated by Lincoln history. Lindsay was born here in 1879 and became known for performance-like poetry rooted in American rhythm, politics, and the Midwest's own mythmaking.

The house is modest, which is part of its appeal. It gives Springfield a more varied cultural texture, and it is close enough to the Lincoln Home area to visit without much planning. Check opening times before you go, as small state historic sites can keep limited hours.
