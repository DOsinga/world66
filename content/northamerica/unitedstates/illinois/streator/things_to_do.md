title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Streator's interest is in its history as a Gilded Age glass-and-coal town turned community-event capital of the prairie. Walk the short historic downtown around **Main Street**, stop at the **Streator Public Library** — a small Carnegie-funded gem from 1903 — and circle the Queen Anne mansions of the **Silas Williams** and **Ruffin Drew Fletcher** houses just east of the centre. The restored **Majestic Theatre** anchors the cultural calendar.

For a green hour, **City Park** along the Vermilion is the heart of community life and the venue for the September Arts on the Prairie fair. The town's astronomical claim to fame — Pluto's discoverer **Clyde Tombaugh** was born here — is commemorated with a small marker that's worth seeking out.
