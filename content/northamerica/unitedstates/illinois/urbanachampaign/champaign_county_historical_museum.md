Champaign County Historical Museum is a small local-history museum with quilts, photographs, documents, and everyday artefacts from Champaign County. It is a modest stop, but a useful one if you want context for Urbana-Champaign beyond the university.

The museum works best as a short visit while downtown, especially for travellers interested in local settlement, civic history, and the surrounding prairie towns.
