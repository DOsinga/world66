Japan House is a calm corner of the University of Illinois campus, built around Japanese tea rooms and landscaped gardens. The rooms were constructed by Japanese artisans, and the surrounding plantings give the place a slower rhythm than the busy campus streets nearby.

Tea ceremonies, tours, and classes are the best reason to plan ahead, but the gardens alone make it worth a stop in season. It pairs naturally with the Krannert Art Museum and the other campus sights.
