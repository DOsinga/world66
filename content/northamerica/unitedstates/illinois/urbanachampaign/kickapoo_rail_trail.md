The Kickapoo Rail Trail is a flat cycling and walking route running east from Urbana toward St. Joseph. The converted rail corridor is an easy way to get into open countryside without leaving the Urbana-Champaign area for long.

It is best treated as a half-day ride, with a meal or drink in St. Joseph before returning. Bring water and check the current trail length and surface conditions before setting out.
