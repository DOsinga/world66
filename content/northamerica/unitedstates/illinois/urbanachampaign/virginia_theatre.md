The Virginia Theatre is a restored 1921 movie palace in downtown Champaign, still used for films, concerts, and live theatre. It is best known as the home of Roger Ebert's Film Festival, but its regular programming makes it worth checking even outside festival week.

Look for the original Wurlitzer pipe organ, which is still played before some screenings and performances.
