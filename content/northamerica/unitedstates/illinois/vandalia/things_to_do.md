Vandalia is a small town with a specific historical importance — its two decades as Illinois state capital and its position on the original National Road. The main sights are all within easy walking distance of the town square, and the whole historic core can be covered in two to three hours. It is a worthwhile stop on a cross-Illinois drive rather than a destination in itself.
