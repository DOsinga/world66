---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Wheeling is about 30 miles northwest of central [Chicago](/northamerica/unitedstates/illinois/chicago).

**By car.** From the Loop, take I-90/94 west, then I-294 (Tri-State Tollway) north to the Willow Road or Lake-Cook Road exits, then east to Milwaukee Avenue. Allow 45 minutes to an hour in normal traffic, longer at rush hour.

**By Metra.** The Metra North Central Service runs from Chicago Union Station to Wheeling — the station is just east of Milwaukee Avenue on Dundee Road. Trains are infrequent, especially on weekends; check the schedule.

**By air.** O'Hare International Airport (ORD) is about 15 minutes south of Wheeling by car — a useful detail if you fly in and want to skip downtown traffic. Chicago Executive Airport (PWK) within Wheeling itself handles general aviation only.
