The Shri Swaminarayan Mandir sits just off Milwaukee Avenue and is one of the most distinctive buildings in Wheeling — a white temple with traditional Indian architectural detail in a setting otherwise dominated by strip malls and motels. The temple is open to visitors outside service times.

Visit modestly dressed and remove your shoes at the entrance. There is usually someone happy to explain the meaning of the carvings and rituals.
