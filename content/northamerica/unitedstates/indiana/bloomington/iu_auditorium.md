IU Auditorium is Bloomington's main stage for touring Broadway shows, concerts, lectures, dance, and large university events. The 3,200-seat hall sits at the heart of the Indiana University campus, close enough to combine with the art museum, Lilly Library, and a walk through the limestone academic core.

Check the calendar before you go. On the right night it is one of the best ways to see Bloomington behaving like a much larger city.
