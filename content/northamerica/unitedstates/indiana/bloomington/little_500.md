The Little 500 is an annual 200-lap bicycle race held at IU's Bill Armstrong Stadium each April, modelled loosely on the Indianapolis 500. Teams of four riders take turns on their bikes — a single-gear bike with no brakes, racing on a quarter-mile cinder track — over the course of several hours. The race was made famous nationally by the 1979 film *Breaking Away*, which used the actual race footage and Bloomington locations.

The race has been held since 1951, originally as a fundraiser for student scholarships. It has grown into the biggest event in Bloomington's annual calendar, with the week leading up to it involving parties, qualifying races, and a kind of organised mayhem that only a college town can produce. Teams train for months; the competition for spots in the race is genuine. There is also a women's race, started in 1988.

Tickets sell out in advance, but the atmosphere extends well beyond the stadium. Race week in Bloomington is an event worth timing a visit around if you can.
