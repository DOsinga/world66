The Mathers Museum of World Cultures is an Indiana University museum built around ethnographic collections from across the world. Its objects, photographs, and rotating exhibitions are most interesting when they connect everyday materials to the people and rituals behind them.

For visitors, it is a good campus stop if you want something quieter and more reflective than Bloomington's bars and music venues. Check the current exhibition schedule before making a special trip.
