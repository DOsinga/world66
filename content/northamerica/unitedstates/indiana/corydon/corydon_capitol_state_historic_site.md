The Corydon Capitol is the reason the town matters to Indiana history. The small limestone building served as the state capitol from 1816 to 1825, before the government moved north to Indianapolis.

It is a modest site, but the scale is part of the charm: early state politics happened in a building that feels closer to a village courthouse than a grand monument.
