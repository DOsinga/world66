Zimmerman Art Glass keeps Corydon from feeling like a town preserved only in sepia. The family glass business has deep local roots and usually offers the most engaging stop on the square after the capitol.

The best reason to go is the demonstration: molten glass, practiced handwork, and a craft tradition that is easier to understand when you see it made.
