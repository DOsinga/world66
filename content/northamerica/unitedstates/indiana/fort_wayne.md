Fort Wayne is Indiana's second-largest city, set where the St. Marys, St. Joseph, and Maumee rivers meet in the state's northeast corner. It is a real working Midwestern city, but it has enough good stops to justify a day rather than just a coffee break.

Families come first for the [Fort Wayne Children's Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/fort_wayne/fort_wayne_childrens_zoo), one of the city's strongest attractions. Downtown adds the [Foellinger-Freimann Botanical Conservatory](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/fort_wayne/foellinger_freimann_botanical_conservatory), the [Fort Wayne Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/fort_wayne/fort_wayne_museum_of_art), and the restored [Embassy Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/fort_wayne/embassy_theatre).

The city also has a quieter research draw: the Allen County Public Library's genealogy center, one of the largest public genealogy collections in the United States. Add riverfront paths and a baseball park downtown, and Fort Wayne has a full, varied travel-guide day.
