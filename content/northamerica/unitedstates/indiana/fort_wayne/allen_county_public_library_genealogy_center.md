The Allen County Public Library's Genealogy Center is a serious travel reason for people tracing family history. It holds one of the largest public genealogy collections in the United States, with staff and resources aimed at researchers rather than casual browsers.

If genealogy matters to you, budget more time than you think. If it does not, the library is still a useful downtown landmark, but this stop is mainly for people with a name, family line, or question to chase.
