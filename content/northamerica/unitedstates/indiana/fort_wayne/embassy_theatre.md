The Embassy Theatre is a restored 1928 movie palace, the kind of ornate downtown theatre many Midwestern cities lost. Its balcony, stage, and old glamour make it worth noticing even if you are only passing by.

For the best experience, check the calendar before you come. Concerts, touring shows, symphony events, and classic-film screenings turn the building from a landmark into a night out.
