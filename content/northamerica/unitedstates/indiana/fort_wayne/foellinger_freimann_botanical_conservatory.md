Foellinger-Freimann Botanical Conservatory gives downtown Fort Wayne a warm, green pause, especially useful in winter or bad weather. The indoor gardens include tropical and desert spaces, with seasonal displays changing through the year.

It sits close to the convention centre and Embassy Theatre, so it is easy to combine with other downtown stops.
