The Fort Wayne Children's Zoo is the city's strongest family attraction. It covers 40 acres and is set up as a full outing rather than a quick animal loop, with themed areas, rides, landscaped paths, and close-up experiences.

It is seasonal, so check opening dates before planning a trip around it. When it is open, it is reason enough for families to put Fort Wayne on an Indiana route.
