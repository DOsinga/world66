The Fort Wayne Museum of Art is the city's main art museum, with a focus on American work, regional artists, and changing exhibitions. It is compact enough for a relaxed visit but serious enough to be more than a rainy-day fallback.

It sits near other downtown cultural stops, so it works well as part of a walking route through the centre.
