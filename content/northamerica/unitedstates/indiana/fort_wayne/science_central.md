Science Central is Fort Wayne's hands-on science museum, a few minutes north of downtown. The exhibits lean practical and interactive, with optical, mechanical, and industrial displays that work well for children.

The building's old industrial shell adds character, including a more-or-less working grain elevator inside. It is a strong family stop and a useful rainy-day choice.
