Brickyard Crossing is mainly for golfers, but it has a setting that makes it more than a routine course. Several holes sit inside the Indianapolis Motor Speedway, so a round here comes with the odd thrill of playing beside one of America's most famous sporting landmarks.

If you are not playing, the Speedway Museum and track tours will make more sense. If you do play, this is a rare Indianapolis experience: part country-club afternoon, part motorsport pilgrimage, and very hard to confuse with any other golf course.
