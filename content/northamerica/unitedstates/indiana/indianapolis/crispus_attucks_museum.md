Crispus Attucks Museum preserves the history of Crispus Attucks High School and African American life in Indianapolis. The school was an important Black educational institution, and the museum connects local stories with wider themes of segregation, culture and achievement.

It is a meaningful specialist museum rather than a large general collection. Confirm access before visiting, since school-based museums may have limited public hours, but do not overlook it if you are interested in Indianapolis beyond downtown monuments.
