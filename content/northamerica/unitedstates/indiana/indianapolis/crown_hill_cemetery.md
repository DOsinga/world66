The fourth-largest cemetery in the United States, set on a 555-acre wooded hilltop a couple of miles north of downtown. President Benjamin Harrison, poet James Whitcomb Riley, and three vice-presidents are buried here, along with the outlaw John Dillinger — the city's most-visited grave. The Riley monument crowns the highest point in Indianapolis with a long view back across the city.

The cemetery offers guided weekend tours (Crime, Civil War, architecture, ghost), and the public is welcome to walk the grounds daily during opening hours. Pick up a map at the gatehouse on Boulevard Place; Dillinger is in section 44, lot 94.
