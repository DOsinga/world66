A veteran-owned craft distillery in Fletcher Place producing whiskey, gin, vodka, and rum on site. The tasting room and small bar serve cocktails built on the house spirits along with light bites. The original location's name ("Hotel Tango" is the NATO phonetic for "HT," the founder's initials) was the country's first combat-veteran-owned distillery.

Distillery tours by appointment. The patio is a good summer stop on a Fletcher Place evening crawl.
