The Indiana State Capitol, completed in 1888, is the working centre of state government, housing the governor's office, the legislature, and the state supreme court. Its broad dome and classical stone facade give downtown Indianapolis one of its strongest civic landmarks.

The building is worth seeing for its architecture and for the way it ties the nearby monuments, offices, and formal public spaces together.
