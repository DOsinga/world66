The Indianapolis Art Center is a Broad Ripple arts complex with changing exhibitions, classes, a gallery shop, and ARTSPARK, an outdoor sculpture garden along the White River. It works well as a relaxed stop paired with the Monon Trail or Broad Ripple village.
