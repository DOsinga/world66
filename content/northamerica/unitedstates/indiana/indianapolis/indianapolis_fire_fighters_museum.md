The Indianapolis Fire Fighters Museum covers the city's firefighting history through hand pumpers, horse-drawn fire carts, steam pumpers, uniforms, photographs, and other equipment. The collection is strongest as a look at how urban fire departments worked before modern engines and communications.

It is a modest museum, but a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in local history, emergency services, or historic vehicles. The Indianapolis Firefighters Memorial is nearby.
