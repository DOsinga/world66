Indy's Teeny Statue of Liberty Museum is a tiny, oddball collection devoted to Statue of Liberty memorabilia. It is not a conventional museum, and part of the charm is that visitors ask at the adjacent hardware store for access.

The stop is quick, but it suits travelers who like small local collections and roadside-style curiosities. It is best treated as a detour rather than a headline Indianapolis attraction.
