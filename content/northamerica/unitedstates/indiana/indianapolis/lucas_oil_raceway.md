Lucas Oil Raceway gives Indianapolis another motorsport option beyond the famous speedway. The venue is known especially for drag racing, with oval and road-course facilities adding to the calendar of race events.

It is not a casual drop-in sight, so its value depends on whether an event is scheduled during your visit. For motorsport fans, though, it can be a worthwhile addition to an Indianapolis trip, particularly when paired with the Indianapolis Motor Speedway and racing museums.
