The retractable-roof home of the NFL Indianapolis Colts, opened in 2008. The stadium has hosted a Super Bowl (XLVI), multiple NCAA Final Fours, and during the 2021 NCAA tournament served as the central hub for the entire pandemic-bubble March Madness.

If you cannot catch a game, the stadium runs daily public tours when there is no event — locker rooms, field, press box. The brick-and-limestone exterior is a deliberate nod to the Hoosier vernacular architecture rather than the more usual concrete and glass.
