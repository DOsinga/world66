In the courtyard of the Indiana State Forestry building on West Washington Street stands an unremarkable-looking American sycamore. Its sycamore seed was carried to the moon and back on the Apollo 14 mission, brought home, germinated by the US Forest Service, and planted here in 1976.

A small plaque identifies the tree. The site sees almost no visitors despite being two blocks from the canal and four from Monument Circle.
