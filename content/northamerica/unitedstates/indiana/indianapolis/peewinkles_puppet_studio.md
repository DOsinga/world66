Peewinkle's Puppet Studio is a small downtown puppet theatre with family shows in a 50-seat room. The puppets are made in-house, and some performances pair the show with a hands-on workshop.

It is a modest attraction, but useful for families looking for something local and low-key between the larger museums and parks.
