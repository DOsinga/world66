Radio Radio is a Fountain Square music venue with an independent streak, known for rock, punk, roots, and other small-stage touring acts. It is the sort of place to check when you want Indianapolis after dark without defaulting to a big arena show.

The appeal is the room and the booking calendar, so look up the current lineup before making the trip across town.
