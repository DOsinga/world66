It is the oldest continuously operating bar in Indiana."
---

The oldest bar in Indiana and the most famous live blues venue in the state. Two stages — front and back — host bands seven nights a week. The kitchen does decent pub food (the Cajun catfish is the standout). The crowd skews older blues fan in the front room, younger and rowdier in the back.

No cover most weeknights; modest cover on weekends. Free parking in the back lot is rare in downtown Indy.
