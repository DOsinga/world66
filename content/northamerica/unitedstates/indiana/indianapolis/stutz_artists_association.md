The Stutz Artists Association occupies part of the former Stutz automobile factory, built in 1918. The building now holds artists' studios, small businesses, meeting spaces, and displays connected with the Stutz car story.

It is most interesting during open studio events or when the showroom is accessible, combining local art with Indianapolis industrial history.
