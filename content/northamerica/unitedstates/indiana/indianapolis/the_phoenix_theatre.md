The Phoenix Theatre is an Indianapolis stage known for contemporary, sometimes provocative productions rather than safe touring fare. Its past seasons have mixed classics, new work, musicals, and shows that draw a lively local arts crowd.

It is worth checking the calendar if you want an evening beyond sports bars and big downtown venues. Ticket prices are usually modest, and the theatre is close enough to central Indianapolis to combine with dinner before the show.
