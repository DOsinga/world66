Lafayette and its twin city West Lafayette sit on the Wabash River in central Indiana. West Lafayette is home to [Purdue University](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/lafayette/purdue_university), which dominates the local economy and gives the area a college-town energy — good restaurants, decent nightlife during the school year, and a young population funnelled through one of America's top engineering schools.

The most historically significant site is the [Tippecanoe Battlefield](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/lafayette/tippecanoe_battlefield) north of town, where William Henry Harrison defeated Tecumseh's confederacy in 1811 — a battle that launched Harrison toward the presidency and broke organised Native American resistance in the Ohio Valley. [Columbian Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/lafayette/columbian_park) along the Wabash River is the main green space, and the [Moses Fowler House](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/lafayette/moses_fowler_house) on 10th Street — now the Greater Lafayette Museum of Art — is the finest Victorian mansion in town.
