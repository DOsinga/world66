---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Lebanon is about 25 miles northwest of [Indianapolis](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/indianapolis) on Interstate 65. The drive takes around 30 minutes in normal traffic, making it an easy day trip from the state capital. There is no intercity rail or regular bus service — a car is essential.

Indianapolis International Airport is the nearest major airport, about 40 miles away. Several smaller hotels and chain motels operate in Lebanon and along the I-65 corridor for those stopping overnight.
