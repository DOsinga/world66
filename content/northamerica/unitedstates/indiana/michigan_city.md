Michigan City sits on Lake Michigan's southern shore, just east of the Indiana Dunes. It is a working northern Indiana city rather than a polished beach resort, but the lakefront gives it a clear reason to stop.

[Washington Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/michigan_city/washington_park_and_beach) is the centre of the visit: a broad beach, marina, zoo, and the long pier out to the Michigan City East Light. Inland, [Barker Mansion](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/michigan_city/barker_mansion) shows the money made here in the railroad and freight-car years, while the [Old Lighthouse Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/michigan_city/old_lighthouse_museum) keeps the lake history close to the water.

Come in warm weather if you want the beach to carry the day. The city also works as a quieter Lake Michigan stop before or after [Indiana Dunes National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/porter/indiana_dunes_national_park), especially if you want a real town with a pier walk instead of only dunes and parking lots.
