The Atheneum is where New Harmony should begin. Richard Meier's white, angular visitor center is a museum, orientation point, and architectural statement at the edge of town.

Inside, exhibits and films set up the Harmonist and Owenite stories before you walk the streets. The building itself is part of the experience, with ramps and sharp views that turn arrival into a small procession.
