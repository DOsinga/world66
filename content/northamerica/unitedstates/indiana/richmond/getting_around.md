---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Richmond is a car-dependent small city. The main sights are spread out enough that walking between all of them is impractical, though downtown and the Whitewater Gorge trail are walkable from each other. The Levi Coffin House is eight miles north in Fountain City and requires a car. The Cardinal Greenway rail trail is good for cycling if you have a bike.
