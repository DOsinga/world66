Thistlethwaite Falls is a compact natural stop in Richmond, set within Springwood Park rather than deep countryside. The falls are modest, but the limestone ledges, creek bed, and fossil-hunting associations give the place more character than its size suggests.

It is worth a short detour if you are already in town and want fresh air between historic houses or antique shops. Wear shoes that can handle uneven ground, especially after rain.
