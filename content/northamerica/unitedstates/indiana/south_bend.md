South Bend sits on the St. Joseph River in northern Indiana, and it punches well above its size. The [University of Notre Dame](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/south_bend/notre_dame_campus) is the obvious draw — the golden dome, Touchdown Jesus, and the electricity of a home football weekend make it worth a visit even if you have no strong feelings about college sports. The broader campus is beautiful and open to visitors year-round.

Beyond Notre Dame, the city has more to offer than most people expect. The [Studebaker National Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/south_bend/studebaker_national_museum) tells the story of the South Bend automaker that built everything from covered wagons to automobiles before closing in 1963 — it's a genuinely good industrial history museum. Downtown has seen real investment over the past decade, with independent restaurants and bars filling spaces that were empty not long ago. The [East Race Waterway](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/south_bend/east_race_waterway), an artificial whitewater channel carved from the St. Joseph River, hosts kayaking and rafting in summer. The [Morris Performing Arts Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/south_bend/morris_performing_arts_center) brings in national touring acts throughout the year. For a fine dinner in a remarkable Victorian mansion, [Tippecanoe Place](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/south_bend/tippecanoe_place) — once the Studebaker family home — is the city's most memorable restaurant.
