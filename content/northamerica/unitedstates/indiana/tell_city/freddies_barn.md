A long-running family steakhouse on the north side of town, popular with locals for the prime rib and the salad bar. The menu is American — steaks, fried chicken, catfish — but the restaurant has been here long enough that it counts as a Tell City institution. Big portions, very fair prices, and a Friday-night seafood buffet that fills the place.
