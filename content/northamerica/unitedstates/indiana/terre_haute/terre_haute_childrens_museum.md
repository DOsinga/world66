The Terre Haute Children's Museum is a hands-on museum on Wabash Avenue, with interactive exhibits aimed at younger visitors. It is not a reason to cross the state by itself, but it can rescue a family travel day on I-70.

Its central location makes it easy to combine with downtown food stops or the Swope Art Museum if the group splits between adults and children.
