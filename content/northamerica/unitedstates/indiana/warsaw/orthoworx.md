Warsaw's claim to fame beyond its lakes is a peculiar one: it is the orthopedic manufacturing capital of the world. Nearly a third of all artificial hips, knees, and other orthopedic implants used globally are designed and manufactured in and around Warsaw. Companies including Zimmer Biomet, DePuy Synthes, Biomet, and dozens of smaller manufacturers are headquartered here.

The OrthoWorx initiative promotes the industry's history and works to keep Warsaw at the centre of the global orthopaedic trade. The on-site exhibit space tells the story of how a splint-maker's decision in 1895 snowballed into a multi-billion dollar cluster in northern Indiana. For anyone interested in industrial history, manufacturing, or the history of medicine, it is a genuinely surprising and informative visit. The Warsaw Courthouse Square Historic District nearby has the conventional county-seat architecture typical of 19th-century Indiana.
