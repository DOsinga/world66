The Amana Meat Shop and Smokehouse is a practical food stop with a bit of local character: smoked meats, sausages, cheeses, and the kind of picnic supplies that fit a slow drive between the villages.

It is not fancy, but it keeps the German-Iowan food thread visible outside the sit-down restaurants.
