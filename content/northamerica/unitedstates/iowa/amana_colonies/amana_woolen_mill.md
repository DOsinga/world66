The Amana Woolen Mill is one of the clearest links between the old communal economy and the modern villages. Wool work was a core Amana industry, and the mill complex grew from the 1850s into a long-running local business.

Even if you only browse the shop, it gives the Colonies more substance than a row of gift stores. The craft history here is real.
