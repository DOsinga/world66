The communal kitchens are the detail that makes Amana different from many pretty historic villages. Families did not cook privately in the usual way; kitchen houses fed groups of residents under a tight daily rhythm.

The Communal Kitchen and Cooper Shop makes that system tangible, with the work spaces and tools that kept the villages fed, repaired, and supplied.
