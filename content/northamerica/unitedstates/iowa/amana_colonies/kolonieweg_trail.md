The Kolonieweg Trail is a simple way to see the Colonies as a landscape rather than a shopping stop. The route links Main Amana and Middle Amana, passing Lily Lake, fields, and the millrace.

It is only about 3 miles, mostly paved with some gravel, so it works as an easy walk or bike ride when the weather is kind.
