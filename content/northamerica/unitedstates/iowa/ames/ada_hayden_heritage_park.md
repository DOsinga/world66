Ada Hayden Heritage Park is Ames' best easy nature escape: a pair of former gravel pits restored as lakes, prairie, and wetland habitat. The paved loop trail makes it accessible without turning the outing into a serious hike.

Birders get the most from it, especially around migration periods, but it is just as useful for a quiet walk, a run, or sunset over the water. In a college town with a lot of campus energy, the park gives Ames a calmer northern edge.
