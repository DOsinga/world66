The Christian Peterson Art Museum occupies one of Iowa State University's older campus buildings and focuses on changing contemporary art exhibitions. Behind it, a small sculpture garden adds a quieter outdoor stop to the visit. It is a modest museum, but useful if you are already exploring the campus art collection around Ames.
