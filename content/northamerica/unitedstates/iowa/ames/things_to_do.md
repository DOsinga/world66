title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Most of what's worth seeing in Ames is on or beside the Iowa State University campus — [Reiman Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/ames/reiman_gardens) with its butterfly wing, the prairie-style [Brunnier Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/ames/brunnier_art_museum), and the [Memorial Union](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/ames/memorial_union) at the heart of student life. Downtown, the [Main Street Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/ames/main_street_historic_district) is a compact stretch of late-19th-century commercial buildings, and the Octagon Center for the Arts holds the local visual-arts scene. For something completely different, head out to see [Elwood](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/ames/elwood_concrete_gnome), reputedly the world's tallest concrete gnome.
