title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cedar Falls is strongest when you mix town, campus, and trails. Start downtown in the [Main Street District](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/cedarfalls/main_street_district), visit the [Ice House Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/cedarfalls/ice_house_museum), check the [Hearst Center for the Arts](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/cedarfalls/hearst_center_for_the_arts), and leave time for the [Cedar Valley Trails](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/cedarfalls/cedar_valley_trails).
