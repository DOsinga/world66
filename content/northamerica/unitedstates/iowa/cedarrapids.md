Cedar Rapids is Iowa's second-largest city, a manufacturing and processing centre on the Cedar River in east-central Iowa. It is best known as a production hub for grain processing — the smell of Quaker Oats cereals has historically been synonymous with parts of the city — and for its large Czech and Slovak immigrant community, which shaped the culture of the surrounding area in the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

The [Czech Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/cedarrapids/czech_village) neighbourhood along 16th Avenue SW preserves some of this heritage, with Czech bakeries, restaurants, and the [National Czech & Slovak Museum and Library](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/cedarrapids/national_czech_slovak_museum), which is genuinely worth a visit for anyone interested in immigration history. The [Cedar Rapids Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/cedarrapids/cedar_rapids_museum_of_art) holds a significant collection of works by Grant Wood, the regionalist painter who depicted Iowa rural life and is best known for American Gothic. Cedar Rapids is not a typical tourist destination, but it has more substance than many midwestern cities of similar size — the [Peoples Savings Bank](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/cedarrapids/peoples_savings_bank), a Louis Sullivan 'jewel box' building, and the restored [Paramount Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/cedarrapids/paramount_theatre) are both worth a detour.
