The Cedar Rapids Museum of Art holds one of the most significant collections of Grant Wood's work anywhere in the world. Wood, who grew up on an Iowa farm and studied art in Paris before returning to paint the rural Midwest with a distinctive ironic warmth, is Cedar Rapids' most famous cultural figure — and the museum makes a convincing case that he deserves far more attention than his reputation as the painter of one famous image.

The collection includes early experimental work, the mature regionalist paintings of the 1930s, and the murals Wood created for various Iowa buildings. The museum also has a strong collection of Marvin Cone, Wood's fellow Cedar Rapids artist and lifelong friend. Temporary exhibitions bring in national touring shows. Allow two hours.
