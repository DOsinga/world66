Cedar Rapids has a solid dining scene for a Midwestern city of its size. Czech and Slovak cooking — roast pork, dumplings, koláče pastries — appears at Czech Village spots, but the broader restaurant landscape is more diverse than you might expect. NewBo City Market, a converted industrial building on the east side, houses a mix of food vendors and is the most interesting place to graze.
