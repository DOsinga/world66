Davenport is the largest of the Quad Cities and the main Iowa anchor on this bend of the Mississippi. It is a working river city rather than a polished weekend escape, with bridges, bluffs, old industrial edges, and enough museums to reward a stop.

The riverfront is the obvious place to start. [Figge Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/davenport/figge_art_museum) gives downtown a serious cultural landmark, the [Davenport Skybridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/davenport/davenport_skybridge) offers a quick river view, and [Modern Woodmen Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/davenport/modern_woodmen_park) makes baseball feel very close to the water. Inland, [Vander Veer Botanical Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/davenport/vander_veer_botanical_park) and the [Putnam Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/davenport/putnam_museum) are the strongest non-river stops.

Davenport works best when you treat it as part of a cross-river cluster. [Bettendorf](/northamerica/unitedstates/iowa/bettendorf), Moline, and Rock Island are minutes away, and the Mississippi River Trail ties the area together better than the municipal boundaries suggest.
