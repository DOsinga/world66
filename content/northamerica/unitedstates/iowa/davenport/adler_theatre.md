The Adler Theatre is downtown Davenport's restored Art Deco performance hall, opened in 1931 as the RKO Orpheum. Its polished interior and old movie-palace scale make it worth noticing even if you are not catching a show.

It is home to the Quad City Symphony Orchestra and hosts touring Broadway productions, concerts, ballet, and other events. Check the programme if you are spending an evening in Davenport.
