The Davenport Skybridge is a short pedestrian bridge linking downtown with the riverfront. Its main value is the view: the Mississippi, the bridges, and the downtown edge all line up neatly from inside the glassy walkway.

It is not worth a special trip across town, but it is an easy add-on before or after the Figge. At night, the lighting gives it a little more drama.
