The German American Heritage Center occupies a former hotel near the Centennial Bridge, a fitting location for a museum about immigration and settlement. Exhibits focus on German-American communities in Davenport and the wider Mississippi valley.

It is a small museum, but the building and subject matter give it more local specificity than many heritage centres. Visit if you are already downtown or interested in the ethnic history that shaped the Quad Cities.
