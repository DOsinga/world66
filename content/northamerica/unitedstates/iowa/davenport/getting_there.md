---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Davenport is on Interstate 80 and Interstate 74, with easy road access from Chicago, Des Moines, Iowa City, and Peoria. The closest airport is Quad Cities International Airport in Moline, Illinois, across the river.

There is no Amtrak station in Davenport itself. Most visitors arrive by car, which also makes it easier to move among the Quad Cities.
