The International Fire Museum occupies a former Hose Station Number 4 building and displays firefighting memorabilia from Davenport and beyond. Its collection includes historic equipment, uniforms, and a 1951 Mack pumper once used by nearby Riverdale.

It is a small, specialist museum, but it gives the Village of East Davenport area a worthwhile local-history stop.
