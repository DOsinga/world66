Modern Woodmen Park is a fine minor-league ballpark in a memorable spot, pressed between downtown Davenport and the Mississippi River. On game nights, the setting does a lot of the work.

Even if you do not care deeply about baseball, a summer evening here is one of the easier ways to enjoy Davenport like a local. Check the schedule for the Quad Cities River Bandits.
