Quad City Botanical Center has indoor and outdoor gardens close to the Davenport riverfront. The year-round tropical garden is the main draw, with seasonal outdoor areas including prairie plantings, water features, and themed garden spaces.

It is a calm stop for plant lovers or families looking for a slower break between downtown sights.
