Rock Island Arsenal occupies Arsenal Island in the Mississippi River, between Davenport and Rock Island. It is still an active military manufacturing site, but its history reaches back to Fort Armstrong and a Civil War prison camp.

For travellers, the interest is the island's layered military story and river setting. Check access rules before going, as security requirements can change and this is not an ordinary city park.
