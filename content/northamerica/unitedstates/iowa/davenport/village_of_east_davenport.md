The Village of East Davenport is a compact historic district east of downtown, with brick storefronts, restaurants, bars, and a looser pace than the central riverfront. It feels like a small town folded into the city.

Come for an unhurried meal or a wander after seeing the main museums. The district is not large, but it gives Davenport a neighbourhood face that visitors often miss from the highway.
