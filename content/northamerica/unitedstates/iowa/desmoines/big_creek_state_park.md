Big Creek State Park is a practical outdoor escape north of Des Moines, centred on a
reservoir with beaches, boat ramps, picnic areas, and trails. It is not wild Iowa, but
it gives the capital region an easy place for water, sky, and a slower afternoon.

The park is best in warm weather, when swimming, paddling, and cycling make the short
drive worthwhile. In cooler months it still works for a walk or a quiet loop around the
lake, especially if you want to see the city give way to open country.
