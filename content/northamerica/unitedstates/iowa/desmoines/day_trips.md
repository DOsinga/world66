Des Moines sits in the middle of the state and gives easy reach to half of Iowa within an hour or two. The covered bridges of Madison County (40 minutes southwest) are the best-known move; Ames and Iowa State University make a college-town counterpart 30 minutes north; Iowa City and the university there are two hours east; and the Loess Hills on the western edge of the state make a three-hour run if you want big landscape.
