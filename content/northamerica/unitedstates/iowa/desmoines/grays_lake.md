Gray's Lake is the outdoor reset button near central Des Moines: a city lake wrapped by a popular loop trail and connected to the broader trail system. It is close enough to downtown for a quick walk, run, or paddle, but open enough to feel like a real break from traffic and office blocks.

The lake works best in fair weather, especially around sunset when the skyline and pedestrian bridge give the park a more urban character than a standard neighborhood green space. Beach conditions vary with water quality monitoring, so check local advisories before planning a swim.
