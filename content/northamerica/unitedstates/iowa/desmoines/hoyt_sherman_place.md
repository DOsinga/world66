Hoyt Sherman Place began as the home of a 19th-century Des Moines civic figure and has grown into one of the city’s most useful cultural venues. The complex combines a restored mansion, an art gallery, and an intimate theatre, so it feels more personal than the larger downtown performance halls.

It is a good choice when you want an evening that belongs to Des Moines rather than a generic touring stop. Check the calendar before you go: concerts, comedy, lectures, and local events can all land here, and the historic setting gives even a straightforward show a little extra character.
