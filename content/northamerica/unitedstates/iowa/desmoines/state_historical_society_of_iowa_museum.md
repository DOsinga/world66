The State Historical Society of Iowa Museum is the best place in Des Moines to get a quick grip on the state's story. Exhibits range from Ice Age landscapes and prairie settlement to politics, farming, and everyday life, with enough hands-on material to keep children from fading out.

It sits near the Capitol, so it fits neatly into an East Village and government district walk. Give it at least an hour if you want more than a skim.
