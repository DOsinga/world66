---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Late spring through early autumn (May–October)** is the most pleasant time to visit Dubuque. The Mississippi River landscape is at its best in June and July when the bluffs and river islands are fully green. Autumn — particularly late September and October — brings spectacular fall foliage along the river bluffs and the Great River Road, which is the best single reason to time a visit if you have flexibility.

Summer (June–August) is warm and humid by Iowa standards (high 80s°F/30°C) but manageable. Winters are cold and snowy, with January temperatures regularly below freezing. The indoor attractions at the National Mississippi River Museum remain open year-round, but the outdoor experience is limited.

The **Galena Country Eagle Watch** in January brings bald eagle watchers to the river bluffs on the Illinois side, and Dubuque is a natural base for it.
