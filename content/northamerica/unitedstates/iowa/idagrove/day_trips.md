---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Denison** (25 miles south) is the largest nearby town and the seat of Crawford County. It is the birthplace of Donna Reed and has a small museum dedicated to the actress. The town itself has more dining options than Ida Grove and a pleasant main street.

**Cherokee** (30 miles northeast) is another small county seat worth a quick stop. The Sanford Museum and Planetarium there has a surprisingly strong collection of regional archaeology and natural history, along with one of Iowa's few public planetariums.

**Sioux City** (60 miles southwest) is the major regional city and offers the full range of urban amenities: shopping, a larger restaurant scene, the Sergeant Floyd Monument, and the Sioux City Art Center. It makes for a comfortable half-day or full-day excursion.
