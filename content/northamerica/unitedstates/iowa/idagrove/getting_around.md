---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Ida Grove is a small town and most of its sights are easily covered on foot or by a short drive. The castle buildings are scattered across different parts of town, so driving between them is the most practical approach. There is no local public transit. Parking is plentiful throughout town. A bicycle works well for exploring the flat streets and the path along the Maple River on a fine day.
