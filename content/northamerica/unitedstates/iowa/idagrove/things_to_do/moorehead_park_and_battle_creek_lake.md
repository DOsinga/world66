---
title: "Moorehead Park and Battle Creek Lake"
type: poi
---

Battle Creek Lake, a few miles east of Ida Grove, is the main outdoor recreation draw for the area. The lake is modest in size but well-suited to fishing, with populations of largemouth bass, bluegill, and catfish. A boat ramp and small picnic area serve anglers and day visitors. The surrounding land is managed as a county conservation area, and the quiet shoreline makes for an easy afternoon if the novelty of castle-spotting has worn off.

Back in town, Moorehead Park along the Maple River offers a pleasant green space for a walk or a picnic. The park takes its name from an early Ida County settler family and includes the Moorehead House, a Victorian-era home that stands as one of the more architecturally distinguished buildings in a town otherwise better known for its synthetic medieval architecture. The riverbank path is flat and accessible.

Together, the lake and the park give Ida Grove a reasonable claim to being a legitimate stop rather than just a roadside curiosity — worth an hour or two on a fine day.
