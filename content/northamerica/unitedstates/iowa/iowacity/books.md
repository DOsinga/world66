Iowa City has produced an enormous amount of literature; pick a few to read before visiting.

- **Marilynne Robinson, *Gilead* (2004)** — set just up the road in the fictional town of Gilead but written in Iowa City, this Pulitzer-winning novel captures the slow, religious, prairie-Iowa interior that surrounds the university bubble.
- **John Irving, *The Water-Method Man* (1972)** — Irving wrote this at the Writers' Workshop and the early chapters are recognisably Iowa City: the brick streets, the dive bars, the Iowa River.
- **Flannery O'Connor, *Wise Blood* (1952)** — completed while she was a graduate student here. Not set in Iowa but unmistakably the work of an outsider who used her two cold Iowa winters to find her voice.
- **Eleanor Catton, *The Rehearsal* (2008)** — written by a New Zealander who came through the Workshop; useful for showing how the program funnels international voices through this small Midwestern town.
- **Ethan Canin, *A Doubter's Almanac* (2016)** — set partly in Iowa City and at the university by a longtime Workshop faculty member.
