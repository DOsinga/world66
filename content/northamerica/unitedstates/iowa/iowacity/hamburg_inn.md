The Hamburg has been on Linn Street since 1948 and is the diner of record in Iowa City — neon-and-vinyl, hash browns and ham, and a mandatory pie-shake (a slice of pie blended into a milkshake). Politicians on the Iowa-caucus campaign trail stop in nearly compulsorily.

Open from breakfast through dinner. Cash and card both fine; lines on weekend mornings but tables turn fast.
