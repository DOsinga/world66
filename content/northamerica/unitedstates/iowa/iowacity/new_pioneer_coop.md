New Pi is the city's member-owned grocery cooperative, founded in 1971 and now operating three stores around the corridor. The Iowa City branch on Van Buren Street is the original: a busy ground-floor full of organic produce, regional dairy, baked-on-premises bread, and a hot-food bar that draws lunch crowds out of the surrounding offices.

A good stop for picnic supplies before heading to the river or out to Hickory Hill Park.
