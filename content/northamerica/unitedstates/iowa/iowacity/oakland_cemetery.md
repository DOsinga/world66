The Black Angel of Oakland Cemetery is one of those small obsessions a college town accumulates over the decades. Eight and a half feet of grim, weathered bronze, the angel marks the Feldevert family plot and has been a stop on every dorm-night ghost tour since at least the 1920s.

Cemetery aside, Oakland is a pleasant short walk from downtown, especially in autumn when the maples turn. Brown Street, the route in, is one of the prettiest residential streets in the city.
