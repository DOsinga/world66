Prairie Lights is the kind of independent bookstore that gives a town its identity. Founded in 1978 and now operating out of a three-storey building on Dubuque Street, it stocks the wide and deep selection a UNESCO City of Literature deserves, plus a busy reading series that brings two or three authors a week — many of them Iowa Writers' Workshop alumni passing back through town.

The upstairs café is one of the better workspots in central Iowa City: large windows over the Ped Mall, decent coffee, and the kind of background quiet only a bookstore can produce.
