---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The university and downtown bleed into each other, which means most of what's worth seeing sits within a fifteen-minute walk of the Pedestrian Mall: the Old Capitol and Pentacrest, the Stanley Museum of Art and the natural history museum, Prairie Lights, the Englert Theatre, and the brick streets that hold them together. Add Oakland Cemetery and Hickory Hill Park if you have a full day; add Kinnick Stadium if you're here on a fall Saturday.
