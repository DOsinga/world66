Cedar Bridge has had the bad luck of being the most picturesque of the six. It is the bridge on the cover of *The Bridges of Madison County* and the one Francesca and Robert visit at night in the novel. The original 1883 structure was burned by arsonists in 2002 and again in 2017; what stands today is a careful 2019 reconstruction over Cedar Creek north of Winterset.

Still beautiful in its rebuilt form, and worth visiting for the story alone.
