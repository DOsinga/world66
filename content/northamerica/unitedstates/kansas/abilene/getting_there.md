---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Abilene is on Interstate 70 about 90 miles west of [Topeka](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/topeka) and 160 miles west of Kansas City — an easy two-and-a-half-hour drive from KCI airport. Most travellers reach the town by car as part of a wider cross-country trip or a Kansas loop with [Salina](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/salina) and the Flint Hills. There is no scheduled passenger air service or Amtrak stop in Abilene; the closest train station is in Topeka.
