---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Abilene's sights cluster in two areas: the Eisenhower Presidential Center on the south side of town, where you can spend a half day walking through Ike's boyhood home, the museum, the chapel where he is buried and the visitor centre; and the downtown historic district, where you can ride a steam train, climb aboard a 1901 Parker carousel, and wander a recreated cattle-drive boardwalk.
