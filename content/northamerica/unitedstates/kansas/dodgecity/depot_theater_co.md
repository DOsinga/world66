Depot Theater Co. stages dinner-theatre productions inside Dodge City's historic Santa Fe Depot. It is a good evening option if you are staying in town after the Boot Hill museums close, especially because the building itself carries some of the rail-town atmosphere that shaped Dodge City's early growth.
