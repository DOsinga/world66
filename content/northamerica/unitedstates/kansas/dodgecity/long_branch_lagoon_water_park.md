Long Branch Lagoon Water Park is Dodge City's summer water park, with slides, a lazy river, pools, and shaded places to cool off. It is aimed squarely at families and is most useful on a hot afternoon after the museums and historic sights.

Hours are seasonal and usually tied to the school calendar, with daily opening in high summer and weekend hours toward the end of the season. Check before crossing town for it.
