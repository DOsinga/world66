Kansas City — "KC" — is an American metropolis with a state line running down the middle of it. Kansas City, Missouri is the larger and better-known half; Kansas City, Kansas (affectionately "KCK") sits just across the border. Dozens of suburbs in both states fill out the metropolitan area.

The city is famous for its barbecue — slow-smoked, heavily sauced, and the subject of fierce local rivalries between joints like [Arthur Bryant's](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/arthurs_bbq) and [Joe's Kansas City](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/joes_kansas_city_bbq) — and for its jazz heritage, which dates back to the 1920s and 30s when the [18th and Vine district](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/18th_and_vine) was one of the centres of American jazz. Charlie Parker grew up and learned his craft here; Count Basie built his first great band here. The [American Jazz Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/american_jazz_museum) and [Negro Leagues Baseball Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/negro_leagues_baseball_museum) together form one of the most significant cultural complexes dedicated to African American history in the country.

The [Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/nelson_atkins_museum) is world-class, free, and anchored by one of the best Asian art collections in North America. [Union Station](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/union_station), restored to its 1914 Beaux-Arts grandeur, is worth stepping into even if you're not catching a train. The [Arabia Steamboat Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/steamboat_arabia_museum) holds 200 tons of perfectly preserved 1856 frontier goods recovered from the Missouri River. The [National WWI Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/national_wwi_museum) is by consensus the finest WWI museum in the English-speaking world. [Country Club Plaza](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/country_club_plaza) — Spanish-influenced, tile-roofed, architecturally improbable — was the first automobile-oriented shopping district in America.

Kauffman Stadium, home of the Kansas City Royals, and Arrowhead Stadium next door give the city one of the best sports complexes in the country. The [Crossroads Arts District](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/kansascity/crossroads_arts_district) has emerged as the city's creative hub, with galleries, restaurants, and First Friday events that draw the whole city out. Kansas City surprises people — it's bigger and richer than its reputation suggests, and it rewards a few days of genuine exploration.
