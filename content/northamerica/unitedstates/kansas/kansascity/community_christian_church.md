Community Christian Church is a notable Kansas City work by Frank Lloyd Wright, designed with a low, angular form that feels very different from the city's older stone churches. The building is especially known for the modernist spire of light concept associated with the congregation's later installations.

Architecture travellers should include it with the Country Club Plaza and nearby museums. It remains an active church, so visits need to be respectful and timed around services or events.
