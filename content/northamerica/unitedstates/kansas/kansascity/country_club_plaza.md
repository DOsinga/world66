The Country Club Plaza is a Spanish-influenced shopping and entertainment district in the Midtown neighbourhood, about four miles south of downtown. Developed in the 1920s by J.C. Nichols, it is often cited as the first automobile-oriented shopping centre in America. The architecture is extravagant — tiled towers, ornamental fountains, wrought iron, and terracotta inspired by the buildings of Seville — and remarkably well-maintained.

Today the Plaza is a mix of upscale retail, restaurants, and bars. It's particularly pleasant for a walk in the evening or during the holiday season when the buildings are outlined in Christmas lights. Worth an hour even if you're not shopping.
