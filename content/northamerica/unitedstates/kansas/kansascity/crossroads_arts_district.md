The Crossroads Arts District occupies the blocks south of downtown between the highway and the Country Club Plaza, filling former warehouse and industrial space with galleries, studios, restaurants, and bars. It's Kansas City's most creative neighbourhood: dense with independent businesses, lively at First Fridays (when galleries open simultaneously on the first Friday of each month), and rapidly expanding as the city's restaurant scene has grown.

The architecture is a mix of restored brick warehouses and new infill construction. The area around 20th and Wyandotte is the densest concentration of galleries. The Kansas City Repertory Theatre is here. First Fridays run from 6–10pm and draw large crowds — if you're in town, it's worth timing your visit around one.
