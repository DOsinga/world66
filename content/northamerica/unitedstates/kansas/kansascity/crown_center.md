Crown Center is a useful stop when you are already around Union Station and the National WWI Museum. The complex mixes shops, restaurants, fountains, and seasonal events in a set of connected buildings that were developed by Hallmark, whose headquarters are still part of the area.

It is not the most atmospheric neighbourhood in Kansas City, but it works well for families and bad-weather days. In winter the ice terrace gives the district a bit of sparkle; the rest of the year it is a convenient indoor-outdoor pause between bigger sights.
