---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Kansas City is fundamentally a car city. Most of the interesting sights are spread across a large metropolitan area that's difficult to cover without driving. That said, the streetcar running along Main Street from Union Station through the Power and Light District and up to the River Market is free to ride and genuinely useful for the downtown and Midtown corridor.

The Crossroads Arts District and 18th and Vine are walkable from downtown. The Country Club Plaza is about four miles south — the streetcar doesn't reach that far, so a taxi or ride-share makes sense. The Nelson-Atkins Museum is a short walk from the Plaza. Parking is generally easy and cheap by the standards of major American cities. Cycling infrastructure has expanded in recent years, but the city remains car-oriented.
