---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Kansas City International Airport (MCI) is about 20 miles northwest of downtown. A new terminal opened in 2023, replacing the city's famously dated single-level terminals. Direct flights connect to most major US hubs and some international destinations. Taxis, ride-shares, and a dedicated bus route (the Airport Express) connect the airport to downtown.

By train, Amtrak's Missouri River Runner runs twice daily between Kansas City and St. Louis (five hours). The California Zephyr and Southwest Chief both pass through Union Station — the Zephyr heads to Chicago and San Francisco, the Chief to Chicago and Los Angeles. These long-distance routes are scenic and worth considering if you have flexibility. Union Station is in downtown, just south of the interstate. By road, Kansas City sits on I-70 (east-west) and I-35 (north-south), well-positioned for driving from any direction.
