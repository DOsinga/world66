Historic City Market is one of the most lived-in parts of Kansas City, Kansas's cross-river urban landscape, even though the market district is closely tied to the wider Kansas City metro. The draw is the mix: produce vendors, small food shops, restaurants, and the bustle of a market that still functions rather than just posing for visitors.

Go on a weekend morning if you want the fullest version, with more stalls and people-watching. It is also a useful place to eat casually, especially if you are tired of choosing between barbecue institutions and downtown sit-down restaurants.
