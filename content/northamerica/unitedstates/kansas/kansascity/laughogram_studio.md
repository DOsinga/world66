The Laugh-O-Gram Studio is where Walt Disney started out. In 1921, the young animator rented space at 1712 Holmes Street and set up his first studio, producing a series of modernised fairy-tale shorts. The company folded in 1923, and Disney moved to California. The building later became a warehouse, then a rooming house, and was near demolition when a preservation group stepped in.

The restored building is now open for tours and tells the story of Disney's Kansas City years — often overlooked in the official Disney mythology. The neighbourhood is modest and the building is small, but for anyone interested in American animation history, this is the source.
