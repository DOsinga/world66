The National World War I Museum and Memorial is, by consensus, the best WWI museum in the English-speaking world. The collection is vast — uniforms, weapons, personal effects, letters, and propaganda — but the design of the space is what makes it exceptional. Visitors enter across a glass floor over a field of 9,000 red poppies representing soldiers killed in the war. Each poppy represents 1,000 deaths.

The adjacent Liberty Memorial tower can be climbed for views over the city. The museum handles the subject with intelligence and genuine emotional weight, covering the war from multiple national perspectives rather than just the American angle. Allow three hours minimum.
