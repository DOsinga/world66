The Arabia Steamboat Museum is one of the most unusual museums you'll encounter in any American city. In 1856, the steamboat Arabia sank in the Missouri River with a full cargo of frontier goods bound for general stores along the western frontier. For 130 years the wreck lay buried under a Kansas farm field as the river changed course. When it was excavated in 1988, the contents were astonishingly intact.

The museum displays the recovered cargo in extraordinary detail: thousands of glass bottles, bolts of fabric, tools, china, shoes, and clothing, all preserved in remarkable condition. The scale of the find — two hundred tons of goods — gives a vivid picture of daily life on the American frontier. Worth every minute.
