The National Museum of Toys and Miniatures occupies a grand 1911 mansion near the Country Club Plaza and holds two distinct collections: one of fine-scale miniature rooms and objects made by master craftspeople, and one of historic antique toys spanning over a century. Together they form the world's largest collection of their kind.

The miniature rooms are the more striking of the two — complete furnished interiors built to 1:12 scale, some with working lights and plumbing, all made with extraordinary precision. The toy collection is vivid for the nostalgia it provokes, with dolls, trains, and mechanical toys that trace the history of childhood play from the 1870s onward. Underrated and genuinely wonderful.
