Kansas City's Union Station is one of the great Beaux-Arts train stations of America, completed in 1914 and restored to its original grandeur after a years-long renovation completed in 1999. The main hall, with its 95-foot barrel-vaulted ceiling and three giant chandeliers, is genuinely spectacular. It's worth stepping inside even if you're not catching a train.

Today the station houses Science City, a hands-on science museum aimed at families, as well as restaurants, a cinema, and the Harvey House restaurant that echoes the famous Harvey Houses that once fed railroad passengers across the country. Amtrak still runs trains from here — the California Zephyr departs for San Francisco, the Southwest Chief for Los Angeles.
