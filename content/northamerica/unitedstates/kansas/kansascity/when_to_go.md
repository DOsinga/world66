---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Kansas City has four genuine seasons. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are the most pleasant — warm but not brutal, with clear skies and the trees in good form. The Royals season runs through these months, and outdoor dining is at its best.

Summer is hot and humid, with temperatures regularly hitting the 90s Fahrenheit in July and August. The barbecue and festival scene is in full swing, including the American Royal Barbecue Contest in autumn, one of the largest BBQ competitions in the world. Winter is cold with occasional snow — not brutal by Upper Midwest standards, but enough to affect travel. The Power and Light District is lively year-round, and the Country Club Plaza puts up spectacular Christmas lights in late November.
