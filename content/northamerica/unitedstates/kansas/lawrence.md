Lawrence is a university town in eastern [Kansas](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas), home to the University of Kansas and one of the most charming small cities in the state. The National Trust for Historic Preservation once named it among America's "Dozen Most Distinctive Destinations," and the praise is deserved.

[Massachusetts Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/lawrence/massachusetts_street) — "Mass Street" to locals — is the heart of it: a well-preserved downtown strip lined with antiques shops, independent bookstores, cafes, and live music venues. The [Eldridge Hotel](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/lawrence/eldridge_hotel) at its northern end has been rebuilt three times after being burned or attacked — a reminder that Lawrence was founded in 1854 as an abolitionist stronghold, and paid for it. The [Granada Theater](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/lawrence/granada_theater), a 1927 movie palace converted to a live music venue, anchors the city's cultural reputation.

The [University of Kansas campus](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/lawrence/university_of_kansas_campus) on Mount Oread is worth a visit for the two free museums alone: the [Spencer Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/lawrence/spencer_museum_of_art) has a strong Asian collection and good American painting, and the [Natural History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/lawrence/ku_natural_history_museum) holds the preserved body of Comanche, the horse who survived the Battle of Little Bighorn. The city's history of Civil War violence is covered at the [Watkins Museum of History](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/lawrence/watkins_museum) and commemorated at the [Lawrence Massacre site](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/lawrence/quantrill_raid).
