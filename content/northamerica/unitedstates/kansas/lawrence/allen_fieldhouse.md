Allen Fieldhouse is one of the most storied college basketball arenas in the United States, home to the Kansas Jayhawks and a major piece of Lawrence identity. Even from the outside, it signals how deeply the university shapes the city.

Game days are the real experience, but basketball fans may also appreciate the attached displays and campus setting when access is available. Plan around university schedules and parking, which can change dramatically during events.
