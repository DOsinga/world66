Liberal is a southwest Kansas town on the open plains near the Oklahoma border. It sits at the meeting of U.S. Highways 54 and 83, with grain elevators, wind, long horizons, and the practical feel of a service town for a wide farming region.

The surprise is how specific its attractions are. Liberal has one of the country's largest aircraft museums, a whole complex built around *The Wizard of Oz*, and an annual Pancake Day race against Olney in England. None of this is grand, but it gives the town more character than most quick highway stops.

Use Liberal as a break on a plains road trip rather than a base for a long stay. The distances are large, the landscape is exposed, and the best parts are small, odd, and very local.
