Dorothy's House and Land of Oz is Liberal at its most unabashed. The complex includes a restored farmhouse dressed as Dorothy's Kansas home, a walk-through Oz attraction, and the Coronado Museum's local history displays. It is kitsch, but sincere kitsch, and worth a stop if you have any affection for the story.
