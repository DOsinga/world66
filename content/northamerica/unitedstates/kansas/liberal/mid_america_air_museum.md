The Mid-America Air Museum is Liberal's strongest attraction, with more than 100 aircraft covering general aviation, helicopters, and military planes from the Second World War through Vietnam. It is the kind of museum that rewards slow looking: less polished spectacle, more hangar full of machinery with real local pride behind it.
