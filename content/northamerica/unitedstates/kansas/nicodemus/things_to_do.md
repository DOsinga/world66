---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Nicodemus is a National Historic Site, so almost everything worth doing here is tied to that story. The National Park Service maintains a visitor center in the Township Hall and has preserved five historic buildings. The entire site is walkable — park once and see everything on foot. Allow at least two to three hours to do it justice, and budget time to watch the orientation film and talk with the rangers, who know this history intimately.
