Paola is the county seat of Miami County, about 40 miles south of Kansas City. It's a small, historically preserved town built around [Park Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/paola/park_square), a leafy two-block green ringed by late 19th-century brick storefronts. The 1898 [Miami County Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/paola/miami_county_courthouse), a striking Romanesque Revival building with a tall clock tower, anchors the south side of the square.

The town takes its name from Baptiste Peoria, the indigenous-French trader who set up [Paola's Post](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/paola/paolas_post) here in the 1840s; the Algonquian-speaking Peoria people had been forcibly relocated to Kansas from Illinois only a few years before. The [Miami County Historical Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/paola/miami_county_historical_museum) tells the full story.

The town is most lively during its festivals: the Miami County Fair in July, the Roots Festival in August, and the Harvest Festival in October. [Lake Miola](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/paola/lake_miola), just outside town, offers camping and water recreation.
