---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Paola has a small but pleasant eating scene clustered on and around Park Square, with a mix of cafes, diners, and a couple of more ambitious places. **Beethoven's #9**, a corner restaurant on the square, has been the go-to upscale spot for decades — steaks, German-influenced classics, a good wine list. **Smokin' Joes BBQ** is the local barbecue institution, with brisket and ribs in the Kansas City style. The independent **Wendell's Pizza Pub** does respectable hand-tossed pizza and pours a decent selection of local craft beer. For breakfast, the **Park Square Cafe** is the usual diner, big plates and bottomless coffee, busy on weekends.
