Salina is a central Kansas crossroads, the kind of town many travellers first meet from Interstate 70. Slow down and it becomes more interesting: a revived Santa Fe Avenue, a good local museum, public art, and enough food and performance venues to make it more than a fuel stop.

The city's best compact stretch is downtown. [Smoky Hill Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/salina/smoky_hill_museum) explains the prairie and town history without charging admission, while the [Stiefel Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/salina/stiefel_theatre) gives the old commercial strip a handsome performing-arts anchor. Families should make time for [Rolling Hills Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/salina/rolling_hills_zoo), west of town, which is larger and better kept than you may expect.

Salina also makes a useful base for nearby Kansas detours. [Abilene](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/abilene) is just east on I-70, and the Smoky Hills and small state parks around the city give you a taste of central Kansas without a long back-road expedition.
