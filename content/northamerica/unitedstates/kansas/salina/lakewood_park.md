Lakewood Park is a simple, useful green space on Salina's east side. Trails, water, trees, and picnic areas make it a good place to stretch your legs after a long drive.

It is not a destination park, but it gives the city a softer edge. Combine it with Indian Rock Park if you want a short outdoor loop without leaving town.
