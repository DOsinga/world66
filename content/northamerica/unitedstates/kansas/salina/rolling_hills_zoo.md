Rolling Hills Zoo sits west of town and is more substantial than a passing traveller might expect. The grounds are spacious, with animal exhibits, walking paths, and a wildlife museum included with admission.

It is especially good for families who need a real break from the interstate. Allow more time than you think; the zoo is not just a roadside petting stop.
