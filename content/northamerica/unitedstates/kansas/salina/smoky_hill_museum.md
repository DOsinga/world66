Smoky Hill Museum is the best first stop in Salina. It covers the settlement, work, weather, and community life of central Kansas, with enough local specificity to keep it from becoming a generic prairie-history display.

Admission is free, and the downtown location makes it easy to combine with a walk along Santa Fe Avenue. Give it an hour if you want context before heading back onto the highway.
