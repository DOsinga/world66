The Stiefel Theatre for the Performing Arts is Salina's handsome downtown stage, a restored historic venue on South Santa Fe Avenue. Its calendar brings touring musicians, comedians, and community events into the centre of town.

Even if you do not catch a show, the building helps explain why downtown Salina feels more alive than many central Kansas main streets. If the timing works, it is the best evening plan in town.
