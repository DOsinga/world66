Spring Hill Ranch is the preserve's historic anchor, a stone ranch house and limestone barn set into the rolling grass just above the visitor center. It gives human scale to the place: cattle, fences, wealth, work, and the long argument between ranching and wild prairie.

The buildings are worth lingering over before you hike. Their thick stone walls and careful placement make more sense once you feel the wind and heat outside.
