Lake Shawnee is Topeka's outdoor release valve, a large county park wrapped around water on the city's southeast side. Locals come for boating, fishing, walking, gardens, playgrounds, and summer picnics, so it feels less like a tourist site than a piece of everyday Topeka.

That local quality is the point. After the Capitol, museums, and civil-rights history downtown, the lake gives you space to slow down, walk by the water, and see where families spend a warm afternoon.
