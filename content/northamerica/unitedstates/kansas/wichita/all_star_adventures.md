All Star Adventures is a straightforward family amusement park in Wichita, built for children and groups who want rides, games, and a few hours of easy entertainment. It is more local fun park than destination theme park.

That limitation is important: adults without children will usually have better options in Wichita. For families on the road, though, mini golf, go-karts, and seasonal rides can be exactly the reset the day needs.
