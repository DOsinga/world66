The Great Plains Transportation Museum preserves locomotives, rail cars and transport artifacts tied to Wichita and the wider plains. Its outdoor equipment gives the museum a hands-on scale that suits railroad enthusiasts and families.

The museum is compact and close to downtown, so it works as a short stop rather than a full museum day. It pairs naturally with other Old Town and river-area sights if you are exploring Wichita without a car-heavy itinerary.
