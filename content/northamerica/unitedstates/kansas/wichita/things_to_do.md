title: "Things to Do"

---

The [Keeper of the Plains](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/wichita/keeper_of_the_plains) stands at the confluence of the Arkansas and Little Arkansas rivers and is Wichita's most powerful sight — a 44-foot steel sculpture lit by fire columns at dusk. The [Wichita Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/wichita/wichita_art_museum) and [Frank Lloyd Wright's Allen House](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/wichita/allen_house) are the other major draws: the museum has a remarkable collection of American art, and the house is among Wright's finest Prairie-style designs.

For aviation history, the [Kansas Aviation Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/wichita/kansas_aviation_museum) tells the story of how Wichita became the Air Capital of the World, with an impressive collection of historic aircraft in a restored 1935 art deco terminal building. The [Museum of World Treasures](/northamerica/unitedstates/kansas/wichita/museum_of_world_treasures) is a genuinely eclectic collection spanning dinosaurs, Egyptian mummies, and presidential artifacts. Old Town, the revived warehouse district east of downtown, is the liveliest neighbourhood for walking, restaurants, and bars.
