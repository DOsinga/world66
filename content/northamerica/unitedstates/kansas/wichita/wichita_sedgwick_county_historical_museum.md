The Wichita-Sedgwick County Historical Museum occupies the former City Hall, a landmark
building that already tells part of the story before you reach the exhibits. Inside, the
collections trace Wichita's growth from frontier settlement through aviation, commerce,
domestic life, and civic ambition.

It is one of the best stops for understanding Wichita as a city rather than just a place
to pass through. The museum pairs well with the nearby downtown core and other river-
area sights.
