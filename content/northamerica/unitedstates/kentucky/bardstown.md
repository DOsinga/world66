*North America > United States > Kentucky > Bardstown*

Bardstown is the kind of Kentucky town that makes bourbon feel less like a brand and more like a landscape. It sits southeast of Louisville in the rolling Bluegrass, with a walkable courthouse square, old brick buildings, distillery warehouses nearby, and enough 19th-century architecture to make a slow afternoon worthwhile.

The town calls itself the Bourbon Capital of the World, and for once the slogan has weight. [Heaven Hill Bourbon Experience](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/bardstown/heaven_hill_bourbon_experience) is on the edge of town, Willett and Barton are close by, and the [Oscar Getz Museum of Whiskey History](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/bardstown/oscar_getz_museum) gives the industry some historical backbone beyond tastings and gift shops.

Bardstown is not only bourbon. [My Old Kentucky Home](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/bardstown/my_old_kentucky_home) ties the town to Kentucky's state song and old plantation mythology, while the [Civil War Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/bardstown/civil_war_museum) and the historic district fill out the day. It is a strong stop between Louisville, Lexington, and Mammoth Cave, especially if you want a small town with a clear sense of itself.
