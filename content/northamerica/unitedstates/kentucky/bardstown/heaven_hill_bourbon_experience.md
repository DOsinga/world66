Heaven Hill Bourbon Experience is the polished visitor face of one of Kentucky's major whiskey families. The centre has exhibits, guided tastings, and tours that explain mash bills, barrel aging, rickhouses, and the business of bourbon without pretending the process is simple rustic magic.

It is a good first stop if Bardstown is your introduction to the Bourbon Trail. The experience is more curated than dusty, but the scale of Heaven Hill's operation and the depth of its brands make it feel firmly tied to the town.
