Lux Row Distillers is a modern Bardstown bourbon stop producing labels such as Ezra Brooks, Rebel, Blood Oath, and David Nicholson. Tours cover the stillhouse, barrel warehouses, and tasting room, with the occasional deeper VIP tour for visitors who want more distilling detail.

It is a useful contrast to Bardstown's older bourbon institutions: less historic, but polished, accessible, and very much part of the current Kentucky whiskey boom.
