Springhill Winery is a small Bardstown-area wine stop with cellar tours, tastings, and displays on Kentucky's wine history. Bourbon dominates this part of the state, so the appeal here is the change of pace: a rural winery visit in a landscape better known for distilleries.

It is most useful if you are already driving between Bardstown sights and want something quieter than the big whiskey visitor centres. Check opening hours before making the detour.
