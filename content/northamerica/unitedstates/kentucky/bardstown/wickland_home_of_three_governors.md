Wickland is a Federal-style house museum built around 1825-1828 and known as the Home of Three Governors. The self-guided tour focuses on the house's architecture and the families linked to Kentucky politics, the Civil War, and Black history.

It is a smaller stop than Bardstown's main museum row, but worthwhile for visitors interested in early Kentucky houses and the town's political history.
