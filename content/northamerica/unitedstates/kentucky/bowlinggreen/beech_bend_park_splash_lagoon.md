Beech Bend Park & Splash Lagoon is Bowling Green's big family amusement park, with more than 40 rides, go-karts, miniature golf, games, a large pool, water slides, and children's water-play areas. It is a seasonal stop, generally running from around Memorial Day to Labor Day, with some weekend openings on the edges of the season.
