Bowling Green's main performing arts venue occupies a beautifully restored 1939 movie palace on Fountain Square in the heart of downtown. The Capitol Arts Center presents a year-round programme of concerts, theatre, dance, and comedy acts, ranging from touring Broadway productions to regional folk music. The building itself — with its original marquee, painted ceiling, and ornate plasterwork — is worth a look even when nothing is playing.

The venue books an eclectic mix of acts: recent seasons have included bluegrass festivals, touring comedy shows, symphony concerts, and film series. It's the clearest sign that Bowling Green has a cultural life beyond Corvettes, and it draws audiences from across the region.
