---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car**, Bowling Green sits on I-65, about 100 miles (1.5 hours) south of Louisville and 65 miles (1 hour) north of Nashville. It's the most practical way to arrive. **By air**, the nearest major airport is **Nashville International (BNA)**, about an hour south via I-65; Louisville Muhammad Ali International (SDF) is about 1.5 hours north. Bowling Green's own Warren County Regional Airport handles charter and general aviation only. **By bus**, Greyhound stops in Bowling Green on its Louisville–Nashville route, with connections to major cities across the region.
