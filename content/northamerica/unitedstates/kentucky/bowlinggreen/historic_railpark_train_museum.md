The Historic RailPark & Train Museum occupies Bowling Green's old Louisville and Nashville depot. Inside are railway exhibits, while guided tours take you through restored rail cars parked beside the station.

The collection includes one of the last surviving L&N railway post office cars, which helps explain how mail once moved across the United States by train. It is an easy downtown stop and a good counterpoint to the city's better-known Corvette sights.
