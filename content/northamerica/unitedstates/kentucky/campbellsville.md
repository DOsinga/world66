Campbellsville is a small central Kentucky town whose travel case rests on water, Civil War memory, and a handful of old houses. It is not a broad city break, but it does make sense if you are already moving between Bardstown, Hodgenville, Mammoth Cave country, or Lake Cumberland and want a day that is more than a lunch stop.

[Green River Lake State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/campbellsville/green_river_lake_state_park) is the main draw: boating, fishing, trails, and wooded coves around a large reservoir. South of town, [Tebbs Bend Battlefield](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/campbellsville/tebbs_bend_battlefield) and the [Atkinson-Griffin Log House](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/campbellsville/atkinson_griffin_log_house) tell the local chapter of John Hunt Morgan's 1863 raid.

The town itself is modest. [Hiestand House](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/campbellsville/hiestand_house) adds a rare German stone-house stop, and Campbellsville University keeps some life in the centre, but the reason to come is the loop of lake, battlefield, and small historic sites rather than downtown wandering.
