The Atkinson-Griffin Log House is a restored early Kentucky building near Green River Lake. Its strongest story is Civil War-era: after the Battle of Tebbs Bend, it was used as a Confederate hospital.

Inside, the displays cover the battle and local folk architecture. It is a small stop, but paired with Tebbs Bend it gives Campbellsville's history a clear shape.
