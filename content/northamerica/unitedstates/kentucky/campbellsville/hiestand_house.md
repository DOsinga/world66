Hiestand House is an 1823 German stone house, unusual in Kentucky and more interesting than its roadside setting suggests. The masonry and period rooms make it a useful stop for travelers who like vernacular buildings.

Opening hours are limited, so check before building a day around it. Even a short visit helps round out Campbellsville beyond the lake and battlefield.
