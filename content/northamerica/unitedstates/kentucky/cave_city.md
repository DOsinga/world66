Cave City is a small south-central Kentucky town whose name is the honest part of the sales pitch. It sits just outside Mammoth Cave National Park and has grown around cave tours, family attractions, rock shops, and the steady traffic between Louisville and Nashville.

The town itself is not polished, and some of the roadside attractions are proudly old-fashioned. The reason it passes the travel-guide test is the cave country around it: [Mammoth Cave National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/cave_city/mammoth_cave_national_park), [Crystal Onyx Cave](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/cave_city/crystal_onyx_cave), [Diamond Caverns](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/cave_city/diamond_caverns), and smaller oddities that make a spare day easy to fill.

Use Cave City as a practical base, not as the destination in itself. Book the big cave tour first, then add one shorter show cave or a family stop if you still have energy.
