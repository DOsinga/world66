Crystal Onyx Cave is a smaller guided cave tour near Cave City. The route is lit and compact, with formations and archaeological material that give it more substance than a simple roadside stop.

It makes sense if you want a second cave after Mammoth Cave, or if the national park tours you wanted are full. Expect stairs and uneven underground walking.
