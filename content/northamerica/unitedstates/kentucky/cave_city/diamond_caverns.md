Diamond Caverns is another of the area's classic show caves, with guided tours through rooms of stalactites, stalagmites, and flowstone. It sits close to Mammoth Cave, so it can be paired with the national park if you are making a cave-focused day.

It is less essential than Mammoth Cave, but it gives visitors a denser, more decorative cave experience.
