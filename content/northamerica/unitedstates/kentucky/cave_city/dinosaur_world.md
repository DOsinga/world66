Dinosaur World is the clearest family stop in Cave City: outdoor dinosaur models, simple exhibits, and enough space for children to burn off energy between cave tours.

It is not a reason to drive across Kentucky by itself, but it fits the town's roadside cave-country mood and can rescue an afternoon with kids.
