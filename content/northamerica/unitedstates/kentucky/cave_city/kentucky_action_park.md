Kentucky Action Park is a family-oriented stop near Cave City, best known to travellers for its guided cave tour. The route is short, easy, and filled with stalactites and stalagmites, with local outlaw stories folded into the patter.

It is not Mammoth Cave, and it should not be treated as a substitute for the national park. Think of it as a light, low-effort add-on if you have children, limited mobility, or an extra hour between bigger cave visits.
