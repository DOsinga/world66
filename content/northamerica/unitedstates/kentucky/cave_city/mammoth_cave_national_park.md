Mammoth Cave National Park is the reason Cave City matters. The cave system is the longest known in the world, with hundreds of miles of mapped passages beneath wooded hills and the Green River valley.

Tours vary from easy walks through large chambers to longer routes with more stairs and tighter passages. Reserve ahead when you can, especially in summer and on holiday weekends.
