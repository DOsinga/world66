Danville is a small Bluegrass city with a bigger historical footprint than its size suggests. Kentucky's early statehood story is centered here, and the compact downtown makes it easy to combine old political history, a medical landmark, and a surprisingly memorable museum in one day.

Start at [Constitution Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/danville/constitution_square), where Kentucky's first constitution was signed. A block away, the [McDowell House Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/danville/mcdowell_house_museum) tells a stranger and more specific story: the first successful removal of an ovarian tumor, performed in 1809 without modern anesthesia or antisepsis.

Danville also has enough character beyond plaques and cabins. [Centre College](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/danville/centre_college) keeps the town lively, while the [Great American Dollhouse Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/danville/great_american_dollhouse_museum) is much better than its name may suggest: miniature neighborhoods, houses, shops, and social worlds arranged with obsessive care.
