Jacobs Hall at the Kentucky School for the Deaf houses a small museum on the school's history and the wider history of deaf education in Kentucky. Its 18 exhibit rooms make it more substantial than a quick campus marker, and it is close enough to Danville's other historic sights to fit into a short walking route.
