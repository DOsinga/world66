Leslie Morris Park at Fort Hill preserves the ridge where Union troops built earthworks to defend Frankfort during the Civil War. The remains are modest rather than dramatic, but the site gives useful context to Kentucky's divided wartime position and the strategic value of the capital's river valley.

The park works best as a history walk with views, not as a polished museum visit. Trails climb through woods to interpretive signs, reconstructed defensive lines, and overlooks that help explain why this hill mattered. It pairs well with Frankfort's downtown historic houses.
