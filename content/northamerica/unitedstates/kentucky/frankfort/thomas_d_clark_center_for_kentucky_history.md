The Thomas D. Clark Center for Kentucky History is the main museum of the Kentucky Historical Society. Its exhibits cover thousands of years of state history, from early settlement and frontier politics to military history, culture, and the modern Commonwealth.

The centre works well with the nearby Old State Capitol and Old State Arsenal, making an easy history walk through central Frankfort. It is also home to major archives, so opening hours and exhibition access are worth checking before a focused visit.
