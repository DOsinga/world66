The African American Heritage Center in Franklin is a small museum with a specific job: preserving local Black history in Simpson County and south-central Kentucky. Its value is in names, stories, photographs, and community memory that rarely appear in broad state histories.

This is a local-history stop, not a large interpretive museum. Pair it with Franklin's courthouse square and other downtown sights, and give it the attention you would give any place built from community preservation rather than big budgets.
