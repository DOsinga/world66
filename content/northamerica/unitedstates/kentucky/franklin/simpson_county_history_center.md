The Simpson County Historical Society and History Center is Franklin's local-history museum, with exhibits on the town, the county, and the old jail and jailer's quarters across the street. It is a useful stop if you want more context before walking the courthouse square.

Expect a modest community museum rather than a large institution. Its value is in local records, photographs, and small details about south-central Kentucky.
