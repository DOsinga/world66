---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Franklin punches above its weight for a small Southern town. **Octagon Hall** north of town is a Civil War-era oddity — one of the only eight-sided brick houses ever built in Kentucky and now a museum. **Kentucky Downs**, just south on the Tennessee line, is the only all-turf flat thoroughbred track in the United States. The compact **Downtown Commercial District** around the **Simpson County Courthouse** is on the National Register, an unusually intact block of late-19th-century brickwork. Outside town, Kenny Perry's **Country Creek Golf Club** draws golfers who appreciate the work of a PGA Tour pro on his home soil.
