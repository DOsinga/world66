Shaker Village of Pleasant Hill is the strongest reason to come to Harrodsburg. The restored village preserves plain 19th-century Shaker buildings in rolling farmland, with exhibits, craft demonstrations, gardens, and trails that explain the community without turning it into a theme park.

Give it time. The buildings are simple by design, so the visit works best when you slow down enough to notice the workmanship, symmetry, and disciplined beauty of the place.
