Jeffers Bend Recreation Area is a local green space on the edge of Hopkinsville, built around walking paths, water, gardens, and environmental education. It is modest, but it gives visitors a calm outdoor stop when they need a break from museums, downtown streets, or highway driving.

The area is especially useful for families and for travellers who like low-key nature walks. Do not expect a major park; come for an easy hour outside and a glimpse of the community's softer, greener side.
