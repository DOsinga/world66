A small but ambitious aviation museum on the edge of Blue Grass Airport with a collection of restored World War II and Cold War aircraft. The interior hangar holds a P-51 Mustang in flying condition, a TBM Avenger, and a number of vintage trainers; outside there is a US Navy F-14 Tomcat. The museum runs a Young Eagles programme and the resident planes occasionally fly. A useful diversion if you have an hour at the airport.
