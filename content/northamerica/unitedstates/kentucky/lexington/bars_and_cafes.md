---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Bourbon is the obvious drink here, and several downtown bars have serious back walls — sometimes 100+ bottles deep. The **Distillery District** has the most concentrated bourbon scene, anchored by the bottling rooms of the old James E. Pepper distillery. The **Chevy Chase** neighbourhood on the south side has a more student-oriented bar crawl. Bourbon trail enthusiasts can use Lexington as a base for tours of Woodford Reserve, Buffalo Trace, Four Roses, and the Castle & Key distillery, all within 30–45 minutes of downtown.
