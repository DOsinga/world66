Bluegrass Distillers is a Lexington bourbon stop with tours and tastings, fitting neatly into the city's wider distillery and horse-country itinerary. It is smaller in scale than the region's marquee bourbon names, but that can make it a more manageable visit.

The draw is strongest for travelers already interested in Kentucky whiskey who want another in-city tasting room rather than a long drive into the countryside. Check tour times and age restrictions before building a day around it.
