Boone Station is a modest historic site east of Lexington associated with Daniel Boone and Kentucky's early frontier settlement. The site is understated, with open land and interpretation rather than reconstructed spectacle.

It is most worthwhile for travellers following Boone, frontier, or Bluegrass history. Combine it with other Lexington-area historic sites, because Boone Station alone is a short and quiet visit.
