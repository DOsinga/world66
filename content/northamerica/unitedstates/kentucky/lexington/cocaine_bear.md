story: "In September 1985 a 175-pound black bear was found dead in the north Georgia woods next to 40 plastic-wrapped packages of cocaine that had been dumped from an airplane by a smuggler who later died parachuting from the same plane. The bear was taxidermied and embarked on a long, strange property odyssey before being installed at Kentucky for Kentucky in 2015. A 2023 Elizabeth Banks film loosely based on the incident pushed the attraction onto international tourist lists."
---

The taxidermied body of the actual bear that ate 75 pounds of cocaine in 1985, on permanent display at the **Kentucky for Kentucky Fun Mall** on Bryan Avenue near downtown. The Fun Mall is the merchandise store of a Lexington-based design collective; their bear sits in a custom case with mood lighting at the back of the shop. Free to view. There is a guestbook, fan letters, flowers occasionally left by visitors. The most unlikely tourist attraction in Kentucky.
