Four Roses Distillery is one of the major bourbon names within easy reach of Lexington, and its Spanish Mission-style distillery building gives it a look unlike the brick warehouses and industrial campuses elsewhere on the Bourbon Trail. Tours usually focus on the brand's unusual production approach, which combines multiple yeast strains and mash bills to create different bourbon recipes.

The distillery is near Lawrenceburg, so it works best as part of a planned bourbon day rather than a quick downtown stop. Book ahead during busy seasons, bring a designated driver, and leave time for the countryside between distilleries. Even for non-specialists, Four Roses helps explain why central Kentucky treats bourbon as landscape, industry, and culture at once.
