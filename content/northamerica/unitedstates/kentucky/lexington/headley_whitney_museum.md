The most unusual museum in the Bluegrass country. Jewellery designer George Headley built it on his farm in the 1960s to house his collection of decorative arts and his **bibelots** — miniature objects of precious stone and metal. The grounds also hold a **Jewel Room** lined with shells and semi-precious stones and a private library shaped like a country chapel. Strange, opulent, and entirely worth the 20-minute drive west of downtown. Open afternoons Thursday through Sunday.
