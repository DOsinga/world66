In Midway, fifteen minutes west of Lexington, Ouita Michel's farmhouse restaurant has been the standard-bearer of Kentucky farm-to-table cooking for two decades. The fixed-price tasting menu uses produce, meat, and dairy from named farms across central Kentucky. Reservations essential; expect to spend around $80 per person without wine. A pleasant short drive into horse country if you want a special-occasion meal.
