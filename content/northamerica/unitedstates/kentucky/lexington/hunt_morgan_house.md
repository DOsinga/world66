A 1814 Federal-style townhouse in the **Gratz Park** historic district, built for John Wesley Hunt, the first millionaire west of the Allegheny Mountains. Hunt's grandson Thomas Hunt Morgan, who lived briefly in the house as a child, went on to win the 1933 Nobel Prize in Medicine for his work on chromosomes — making this an unusually high-achievement family home. Gratz Park itself, with several other antebellum houses and a fountain, is worth a stroll.
