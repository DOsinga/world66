Keeneland is one of the most beautiful racetracks in the world — a low limestone grandstand, white-fenced paddocks, dogwoods in spring, sugar maples in autumn. Live racing runs over three weeks in April and three weeks in October; the rest of the year the track is open for **morning workouts** (free, with coffee and pastries served at the Track Kitchen) and four major **horse sales** that account for a huge share of the global thoroughbred trade. National Historic Landmark since 1986. Six miles west of downtown — 12 minutes by car.
