A 1,200-acre working horse park 15 minutes north of downtown, conceived in the 1970s as both a tourist attraction and a venue for equestrian events. There are presentations of every major breed in the daily **Parade of Breeds**, a horse-drawn trolley tour of the grounds, and two strong museums: the **International Museum of the Horse** (Smithsonian affiliated) and the **American Saddlebred Museum**. The grave of Man o' War, the most famous thoroughbred of the early 20th century, is in the centre of the grounds. Open year-round but most rewarding in spring and autumn.
