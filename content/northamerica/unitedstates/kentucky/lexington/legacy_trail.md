The Legacy Trail is one of Lexington’s best low-key ways to feel the city’s shift from downtown streets to bluegrass horse country. The paved route runs north from town through parks, university land, and open countryside, making it useful for cycling, running, or a long walk.

It is not a single monument, but it earns its place because Lexington’s landscape is part of the attraction. Rent a bike if you can, start early in warm weather, and use the trail as a slower counterpoint to bourbon bars, historic houses, and racecourse visits.
