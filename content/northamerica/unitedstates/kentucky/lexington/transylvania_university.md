A compact liberal arts college in the centre of Lexington, with **Old Morrison** — a grand 1833 Greek Revival temple — as its most photographed building. Henry Clay laid the cornerstone in 1832. The campus is small enough to walk through in 20 minutes and the **Monroe Moosnick Medical and Science Museum** (open by appointment) holds a remarkable 19th-century medical teaching collection — early anatomical models, a life-size papier-mâché figure cast from cadavers, instruments and apparatus from the medical school that closed in 1859.
