---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The two big windows are **April** for Keeneland's spring meet and the start of foaling season on the farms, and **October** for the autumn meet and the bluegrass turning gold. Both are also pleasant weather windows; July and August can be hot and humid. The first Saturday in May is technically the Kentucky Derby — held in Louisville, but Lexington fills up too. December and early January are quiet, but Keeneland is closed and many farms restrict access.
