Wild Turkey Distillery is one of the better-known Kentucky Bourbon Trail stops within day-trip range of Lexington. The modern visitor centre and tour programme give travellers a structured look at bourbon production, while the Kentucky River valley setting adds more scenery than many distillery visits.

Book ahead if you want a tour or tasting, especially on weekends. The visit pairs well with other Bluegrass bourbon or horse-country stops, but it has enough name recognition and setting to stand on its own for travellers choosing just one distillery near Lexington.
