---

The most beautiful bourbon distillery to visit, in a stone-walled creek valley about 18 miles west of Lexington. Tours run hourly during the day; the standard tour is 90 minutes and includes the pot stills, the fermenters, and a tasting in the post-Prohibition stone visitor centre. The longer **Corn-to-Cork** experience is the deepest dive for serious drinkers. Book ahead — tours fill weeks in advance from April through October.
