The American Printing House for the Blind is both a working producer of educational materials for blind and visually impaired students and a museum about that work. Exhibits explain the history of education for blind people and show how braille, audio books, tactile graphics, and other tools are made and used.

Weekday tours may include the factory floor, where visitors can see braille and audio materials in production. It is one of Louisville's more unusual museums and worth seeking out if you are interested in design, education, or accessibility.
