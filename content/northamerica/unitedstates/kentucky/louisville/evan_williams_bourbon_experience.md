The Evan Williams Bourbon Experience brings a working distillery, a compact bourbon museum, and guided tastings into downtown Louisville's Whiskey Row. The tour explains the city's whiskey trade, follows the production process, and ends with a tasting for visitors old enough to drink.

It is an easy add-on to the Louisville Slugger Museum or the riverfront, and a good choice if you want bourbon history without leaving the city.
