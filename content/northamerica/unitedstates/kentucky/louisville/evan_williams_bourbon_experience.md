Evan Williams Bourbon Experience is a downtown Louisville distillery attraction with exhibits on bourbon history, a working artisanal distillery, guided tours, tastings for adults, and a retail shop.

It fits naturally into a walk along Whiskey Row and is a good choice for visitors who want bourbon context without leaving the city center.
