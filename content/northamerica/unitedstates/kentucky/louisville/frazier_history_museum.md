The Frazier History Museum presents Kentucky history through a broad mix of exhibitions, from Native American history and historical miniatures to bourbon culture and regional identity. It also houses the official starting point of the Kentucky Bourbon Trail, which makes it a natural first stop for visitors planning distillery trips.

The museum sits on Louisville's Museum Row, close to the Louisville Slugger Museum and the Muhammad Ali Center. It is most rewarding if you want context for Kentucky beyond a single famous industry or figure.
