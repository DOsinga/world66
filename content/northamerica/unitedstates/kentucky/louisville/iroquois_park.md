Iroquois Park is one of Louisville's major Olmsted-designed parks, a broad landscape of wooded slopes, trails, playing fields, and scenic overlooks on the city's south side. The high ground gives it a different mood from the flatter riverfront and downtown sights.

Come for a hike, a drive to the overlook, or an outdoor concert at the amphitheater when events are running. It is especially worthwhile for travellers interested in how Louisville's park system shapes everyday life beyond the bourbon-and-downtown circuit.
