Jefferson Memorial Forest is Louisville's big green escape: thousands of acres of knobs, hardwood forest, creeks, and hiking trails on the south side of the county. It feels much farther from the city than it is, especially once you are on the longer loops and the traffic noise drops away.

Come prepared as you would for a real walk in the woods, not a landscaped downtown park. Trails can be steep and muddy, water is not always close at hand, and summer heat is no joke; in return you get ridgelines, birdsong, and a useful reminder that Louisville sits at the edge of hill country.
