Kentucky Derby Museum sits beside Churchill Downs and explains both the Derby and the broader history of American thoroughbred racing. Exhibits cover famous horses, race-day traditions, jockeys, owners and the long social ritual around the first Saturday in May.

It is the best way to understand Churchill Downs outside Derby season, and it pairs naturally with a track tour when those are running.
