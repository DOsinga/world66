Kentucky Peerless Distilling Co. is a small downtown distillery making rye whiskey and bourbon in a restored industrial building west of the main Whiskey Row cluster. Tours focus on the family story, grain-to-bottle production, and the return of a historic Kentucky whiskey name.

It is best for travellers who want one more focused tasting stop after the bigger bourbon sights. Book a tour rather than just turning up, especially on weekends.
