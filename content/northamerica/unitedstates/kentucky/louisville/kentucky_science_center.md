Kentucky Science Center is a hands-on science museum in downtown Louisville, with interactive exhibits aimed especially at families and children. It also has a theater that usually shows documentaries.

Its location near the riverfront museum cluster makes it easy to combine with the Louisville Slugger Museum, Muhammad Ali Center, and other downtown stops.
