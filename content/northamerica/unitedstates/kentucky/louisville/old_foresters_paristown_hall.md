Old Forester's Paristown Hall is a modern live-event venue in Louisville's Paristown district, used for concerts, comedy, and community events. It matters less as a building than as part of the city's growing cluster of mid-sized performance spaces outside the downtown arena circuit.

Check the calendar before treating it as a POI. With the right show, it makes a good evening plan and pairs easily with the food, drink, and public-space energy around Paristown.
