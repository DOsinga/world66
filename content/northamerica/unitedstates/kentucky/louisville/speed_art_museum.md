The Speed Art Museum is Louisville's leading art museum, with collections that range from European and American painting to contemporary work, decorative arts, and changing exhibitions. Its modern expansion gives the city a serious cultural anchor beyond bourbon and racing.

Allow at least a couple of hours if the special exhibitions are strong. The museum sits by the University of Louisville, so it combines easily with Old Louisville or a broader south-of-downtown itinerary.
