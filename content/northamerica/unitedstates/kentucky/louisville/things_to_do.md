---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Louisville punches above its weight for a mid-sized American city. The Kentucky Derby brings the whole world to Churchill Downs for two weeks every May, but the rest of the year has plenty to offer: the Muhammad Ali Center, a serious museum about a genuinely great man; Whiskey Row and its bourbon distilleries; the Belle of Louisville steamboat; and the haunted sanatorium on the hill that attracts a very different kind of visitor.
