---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Madisonville's sights are modest but the surrounding region has more to offer than passing visitors realise. The [Hopkins County History Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/madisonville/history_center) in the old city hall covers the coal-mining past that shaped the area. The [Pennyrile Forest State Resort Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/madisonville/pennyrile_forest) about 20 km south offers lake, hiking, and camping in pleasant Kentucky woodland. The [Trover Clinic Sculpture Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/kentucky/madisonville/sculpture_garden) is a small but genuine collection of outdoor sculpture in an unexpected setting.
