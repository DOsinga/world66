The Kentucky Gateway Museum Center is Maysville's main local-history museum. Its collections cover early European settlement, Ohio River commerce, African American history, and the Mason County families and industries that shaped the town.

It is the place to go when you want context rather than a single historic house. The museum works especially well before wandering the downtown blocks, because it explains why this small river city once mattered so much to northern Kentucky.
