Old Pogue Distillery keeps Maysville connected to Kentucky's bourbon story. The Pogue family was making whiskey in the area in the 19th century, and the modern distillery offers a small-scale counterpoint to the larger bourbon trail operations farther south.

Tours and tastings are limited, so check before showing up. For travellers following northern Kentucky's river towns, it adds a useful local flavour to the history-heavy downtown stops.
