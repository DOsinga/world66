The Simon Kenton Memorial Bridge is the classic Maysville river view. Built in 1931, the suspension bridge links downtown Maysville with Aberdeen, Ohio, and gives the riverfront its strongest visual landmark.

You do not need long here. Walk the riverfront, look back at the old town, and use the bridge to understand Maysville as a crossing point as much as a town.
