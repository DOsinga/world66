Shelbyville's horse farms are the town's strongest travel identity. Tours are usually arranged through individual farms or the local visitor office, and they give you a close look at American Saddlebreds rather than the more famous Thoroughbred world around Lexington.

Do not treat this as a walk-up attraction. Call ahead, be flexible about timing, and expect the best visits to feel personal rather than polished.
