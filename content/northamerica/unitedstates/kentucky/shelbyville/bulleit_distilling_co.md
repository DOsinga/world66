Bulleit Distilling Co. is the big, modern bourbon stop east of Shelbyville. The visitor experience is built around guided tastings and the brand's frontier-style story, but the setting matters too: a large distillery campus in open Shelby County farmland.

It works best if you are already tracing the Bourbon Trail between Louisville, Frankfort, and Lexington. Book ahead on busy weekends.
