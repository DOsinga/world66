Jeptha Creed is the smaller and more personal of Shelbyville's bourbon stops. The family-run distillery grows Bloody Butcher corn on its own farm and builds tours around that farm-to-glass story.

It is a good counterpoint to Bulleit: less corporate, more local, and close enough to town that it fits easily into a Shelbyville day.
