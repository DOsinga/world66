Wakefield-Scearce Galleries fills the former Science Hill School with English antiques, silver, furniture, and a lot of old-South atmosphere. Even if you are not shopping, the building makes it worth a look: Science Hill was a well-known girls' school, and the galleries still feel more like rooms in a historic institution than a standard antiques store.

Opening days are limited, so check before making it your downtown anchor.
