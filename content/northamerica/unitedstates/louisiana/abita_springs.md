*North America > United States > Louisiana > Abita Springs*

Abita Springs is a small town on the Lake Pontchartrain Northshore, close enough to New Orleans for an easy day trip but different in mood. It grew around mineral springs, picked up a Victorian pavilion from the 1884 World's Fair, and now mixes quiet piney-woods streets with one of Louisiana's better-known breweries.

The town is not big, and that is part of the appeal. Start at [Abita Springs Tourist Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/abita_springs/abita_springs_tourist_park), where the old pavilion, river, playgrounds, and picnic lawns give the centre a gentle shape. The [Abita Springs Trailhead Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/abita_springs/abita_springs_trailhead_museum) adds local history in a former rail corridor setting.

Abita also has a sense of humour. [UCM Museum / Mystery House](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/abita_springs/ucm_museum_mystery_house) is the town's oddball stop: folk art, homemade contraptions, and roadside-Americana strangeness arranged with real affection. Add a brewery visit, or drive out to [Insta-Gator Ranch & Hatchery](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/abita_springs/insta_gator_ranch_hatchery), and Abita Springs becomes a compact, worthwhile Northshore detour rather than just a name on a beer label.
