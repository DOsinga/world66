UCM Museum / Mystery House is Abita Springs at its strangest: folk art, odd machines, curious old objects, and lovingly arranged junk. It is the kind of roadside place that is easy to underestimate from the outside and hard to mistake for anything else once you are in.

Come for humour rather than polish. The museum's charm is in the homemade display, not in museum-grade presentation.
