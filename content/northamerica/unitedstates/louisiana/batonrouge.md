Baton Rouge is Louisiana's state capital and second-largest city, sitting on the east bank of the Mississippi River about 80 miles northwest of New Orleans. It's a working city — the River Road chemical plants and oil refineries that line the Mississippi here are the industrial backbone of Louisiana's economy — but it has a distinct character, especially around the LSU campus and the old downtown.

The city has two capitol buildings. The [New State Capitol](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/new_state_capitol), completed in 1932 under Governor Huey Long, is a 34-storey Art Deco tower — the tallest state capitol building in the US — with an observation deck and a hall of outsized governor portraits inside. Huey Long was assassinated in the building in 1935; the bullet holes are still visible in the marble. The [Old State Capitol](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/old_state_capitol), a neo-Gothic castle on the bluff above the river, was once called a "mortified carbuncle" by Mark Twain; now restored, it houses a museum of Louisiana political history. Nearby, the [Old Louisiana Governor's Mansion](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/old_governors_mansion) — deliberately built to resemble the White House — is open for tours, and the [Pentagon Barracks](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/pentagon_barracks) predate the more famous building in Virginia by over a century.

Louisiana State University (LSU) is one of the South's major universities, with a sprawling campus and a football culture that dominates autumn weekends in the city. [Tiger Stadium](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/lsu_tiger_stadium) — known as Death Valley — holds over 102,000 people on game nights. The [LSU Campus Mounds](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/lsu_campus_mounds) are 11,000-year-old earthworks sitting quietly between the campus lakes, among the oldest human constructions in the Americas. South of campus, [Magnolia Mound Plantation](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/magnolia_mound) and the [LSU Rural Life Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/lsu_rural_life_museum) give a grounded picture of Louisiana's plantation-era past.

On the riverfront, the destroyer [USS Kidd](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/uss_kidd) is moored as a museum ship, and the [Capitol Park Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/capitol_park_museum) covers Louisiana history through a replica flatboat, Mardi Gras floats, and an offshore oil exhibit. The [Shaw Center for the Arts](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/shaw_center) anchors the downtown arts district. The surrounding streets have the best concentration of bars and restaurants in Baton Rouge, particularly in the [Spanish Town](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/spanish_town) and [Beauregard Town](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/batonrouge/beauregard_town) neighbourhoods.
