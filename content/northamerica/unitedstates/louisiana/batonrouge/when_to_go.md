---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

**Spring (March–May)** is the most comfortable time to visit. Temperatures range from the mid-teens to mid-twenties Celsius, the azaleas and magnolias are in bloom, and Mardi Gras falls in February or early March — the Spanish Town parade is one of the city's most distinctive events. The weather is warm but not brutal.

**Autumn (September–November)** is the other good window, particularly if you want to experience LSU football culture. Night games at Tiger Stadium are a defining Baton Rouge experience, but the city fills up completely on game weekends and hotel prices spike sharply.

**Summer (June–August)** is hot and humid, with temperatures regularly above 35°C and high humidity making it genuinely uncomfortable. Hurricane season runs June through November; August and September are the peak risk months. Most indoor attractions are heavily air-conditioned, which helps, but outdoor sightseeing is draining.

**Winter (December–February)** is mild — temperatures rarely drop below 5°C — but the city is at its quietest. The exception is Mardi Gras season, which builds from mid-January and peaks in February or early March depending on the year.
