Bossier City sits across the [Red River](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/bossiercity/red_river) from [Shreveport](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/shreveport), and the two cities function as a single metropolitan area. Bossier's main draw is its cluster of riverboat casinos along the river — [Horseshoe](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/bossiercity/horseshoe_bossier_city), [Margaritaville](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/bossiercity/margaritaville_resort_casino), and [Boomtown](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/bossiercity/boomtown_bossier_city) — where most of the area's gaming action is concentrated.

The [Louisiana Boardwalk](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/bossiercity/louisiana_boardwalk), a shopping and entertainment district along the river, gives the waterfront some life. [Barksdale Air Force Base](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/bossiercity/barksdale_air_force_base), home to the 2nd Bomb Wing and the [Eighth Air Force Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/bossiercity/eighth_air_force_museum), is a major presence in the city. The small [East Bank District](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/bossiercity/east_bank_district) is the closest thing to a walkable neighbourhood. Beyond the casinos and the base, Bossier City is a practical stop for anyone passing through on Interstate 20 between Dallas and Jackson.
