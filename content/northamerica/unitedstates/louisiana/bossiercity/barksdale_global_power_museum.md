Barksdale Global Power Museum interprets the aircraft, people, and strategy associated with one of the United States' major Air Force bases. Outdoor aircraft displays and indoor exhibits make the story more tangible than a standard military-history panel display.

Access requirements can matter because of the base setting, so visitors should check current arrangements before going. For aviation and Cold War history interests, it is one of the strongest Bossier City-area sights.
