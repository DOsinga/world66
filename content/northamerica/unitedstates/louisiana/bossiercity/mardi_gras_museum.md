The Mardi Gras Museum in Bossier City focuses on the carnival traditions of Shreveport-Bossier, with floats, costumes, krewe memorabilia, and the theatrical craft that goes into parade season. It is a useful reminder that Mardi Gras is not only a New Orleans story.

The museum is especially worthwhile if you are visiting outside parade season and still want to understand the local version of the celebration. It is colourful, accessible, and specific to the region, making it a stronger cultural stop than another generic entertainment venue.
