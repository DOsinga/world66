Margaritaville is the Jimmy Buffett-themed casino on the river, occupying the former Isle of Capri site after a major rebrand and renovation. The property leans hard into the parrothead aesthetic: tropical décor, frozen drinks, a LandShark Bar & Grill, and live music most nights.

It is connected directly to the Louisiana Boardwalk by a pedestrian bridge, so it functions as both a casino and an extension of the boardwalk's nightlife. The gaming floor is mid-sized by Bossier standards, but the rooftop pool and the upper-floor river views make it the most photogenic of the four riverside casinos.
