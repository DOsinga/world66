title: Lafitte's Landing at Bittersweet Plantation
type: poi

Lafitte's Landing is the flagship restaurant of Chef John Folse, one of Louisiana's most celebrated and academically minded chefs. Folse has spent decades documenting and reviving Cajun and Creole culinary traditions — his encyclopaedia of the cuisine runs to nearly a thousand pages — and the restaurant reflects that seriousness. The setting is a restored plantation house; the food is upscale Southern Louisiana cooking — turtle soup, duck and andouille gumbo, Louisiana seafood prepared with classical technique. It is a destination meal for the region, not a quick lunch stop. Book ahead and expect to spend accordingly.
