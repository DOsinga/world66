Laura Plantation is a Creole plantation site on the River Road, known for tours that focus on the families, women, and enslaved people who shaped the estate. The interpretation is the reason to visit: it gives more context than a simple house tour.

The site pairs naturally with other River Road plantations, but it stands on its own if you want one focused, story-led stop. Book ahead when possible, since the guided tour is central to the visit.
