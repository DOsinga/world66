The River Road African American Museum tells the history of African American life along Louisiana's plantation corridor, from slavery and sugar work to churches, schools, music, and local families. It is small, but the subject matter is central to understanding the River Road.

The museum is a useful counterweight to plantation-house tours, which can still lean too heavily toward architecture and wealth. Staff are known for giving generous context, so allow time for conversation rather than treating it as a quick display room.
