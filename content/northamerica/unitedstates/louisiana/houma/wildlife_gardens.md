Wildlife Gardens preserves a patch of swamp country near Houma, with shaded walking paths, peacocks, alligators, and other animals on display. It is a small, family-oriented stop rather than a wilderness experience.

The appeal is the easy access to bayou scenery without committing to a boat tour. Treat it as a relaxed add-on if you are already exploring the wetlands south of Houma.
