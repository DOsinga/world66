The Atchafalaya Basin — about 30 minutes east of Lafayette — is the largest river swamp in the United States, a million-acre wetland of bald cypress, water tupelo, and slow black water. The basin is home to alligators, black bears, hundreds of thousands of crawfish, and a still-active community of swamp Cajuns who fish, trap, and guide tours through it.

The best access is from Henderson, where several operators run flat-bottomed boats into the swamp. McGee's Landing and Champagne's Cajun Swamp Tours are both long-running. A two-hour tour gives the basics; longer trips reach the wilder backwater channels.
