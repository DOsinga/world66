Cameron Prairie National Wildlife Refuge protects coastal prairie and wetland south of Lake Charles, with alligators, wading birds, and seasonal migrants often visible from the wildlife drive. Stop at the visitor center first, then take the boardwalks and levee paths when the weather is kind.
