The Imperial Calcasieu Museum covers the history, art, and culture of the five-parish region around Lake Charles. It is a useful first stop if Southwest Louisiana feels like a blank spot between Lafayette and Texas.

The museum grounds also hold the Sallier Oak, a huge live oak estimated at several centuries old. It gives the visit a strong sense of place before you even step inside.
