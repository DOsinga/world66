type: poi

Lake Charles has one of Louisiana's biggest Mardi Gras traditions outside New Orleans, and this museum turns that seasonal culture into something you can see year-round. The displays focus on elaborate costumes, krewes, parade traditions, and the local family-friendly side of carnival.

The museum reopened in a new facility on Enterprise Boulevard in 2026 after years of storm-related disruption. Check current hours before going, especially outside festival season.
