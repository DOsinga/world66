North Beach is the city's easy lakefront stop, with sand, volleyball nets, picnic space, and views across Lake Charles. It is close to Interstate 10, so it is not a remote escape, but it is still a pleasant place to break a drive.

The beach is most useful in warm weather or around sunset. Pair it with the lakefront parks rather than treating it as a full beach holiday.
