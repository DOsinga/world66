Mansfield is a small northwest Louisiana town whose travel interest is tightly focused on history. It is not a broad destination, but Civil War travellers and anyone tracing the Red River Campaign will find one of Louisiana's more important battlefield sites just outside town.

The [Mansfield State Historic Site](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/mansfield/mansfield_state_historic_site) interprets the 1864 Battle of Mansfield with a museum, trails, and living-history events. In town, the [Mansfield Female College Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/mansfield/mansfield_female_college_museum) adds a different story: women's education in antebellum Louisiana, with the college buildings later used as Civil War hospitals.

For most travellers, Mansfield is a purposeful stop rather than a place to linger. Give it a half-day if the history matters to you, then continue toward Shreveport or the backroads of western Louisiana.
