---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

This is where Metairie genuinely earns its keep as a destination. Local food writers have long maintained that some of the best restaurants in the greater New Orleans area are here — lower rents, free parking, and a clientele that cares about food without necessarily caring about atmosphere. The cuisine is the full range of New Orleans Creole and Cajun cooking, plus Vietnamese (Metairie has a substantial Vietnamese community along Airline Drive).
