The Biedenharn Museum on Riverside Drive tells two stories in one building. Joseph Biedenharn opened his candy store in Monroe in 1890, and in 1894 he became the first person to bottle Coca-Cola commercially — a decision that changed how the drink was sold and consumed worldwide. The museum preserves his original candy store and soda fountain, along with exhibits on the bottling innovation that made him a footnote in American commercial history.

Biedenharn's daughter Emy-Lou donated the family home and its surrounding gardens to the city after her death, and added her own remarkable collection: one of the finest private assemblages of Bibles in the United States, including early editions and illuminated manuscripts. The gardens along the Ouachita River are manicured and pleasant, and the overall complex is larger and more interesting than most people expect from a small Louisiana city museum.
