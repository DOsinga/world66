Monroe holds a claim that few cities can match: it is the birthplace of Delta Air Lines, now one of the largest airlines in the world. The company began as a crop-dusting operation, Huff Daland Dusters, before C.E. Wooldridge renamed it Delta Air Service and launched scheduled passenger flights from Monroe on June 17, 1929.

The Monroe Regional Airport has a small display marking the city's aviation heritage, and the local historical society has documented the story extensively. The airport itself — one of the company's earliest hubs — still carries Delta flights, making it one of the few original Delta destinations still served by the airline. For aviation enthusiasts, Monroe is a meaningful stop on any road trip through Louisiana.
