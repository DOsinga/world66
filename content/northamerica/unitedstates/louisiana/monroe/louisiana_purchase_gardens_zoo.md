Louisiana Purchase Gardens & Zoo is Monroe's main family wildlife attraction, set in a garden-like park with animal habitats, paths, and seasonal programming. It gives visitors a simple, accessible outing away from the city's museums and riverfront.

The zoo is not a reason to travel to Monroe by itself, but it clears the bar as a solid local things-to-do stop. It is best for families, casual walkers, or anyone looking for an easy outdoor break.
