The Northeast Louisiana Delta African American Heritage Museum gives Monroe a cultural stop with real regional weight. Its focus is African American history and art from the Delta, including work connected to local artists and the wider story of Black life in northeast Louisiana.

It is not a huge institution, so check hours before you go, but it is exactly the kind of local museum that can make a road trip more grounded. Pair it with Monroe's aviation and river history for a fuller picture of the city beyond the interstate.
