Restoration Park in West Monroe is a reclaimed natural area with boardwalks and trails through wetland, cypress, and bayou-edge habitat. Its appeal is quiet and ecological: a place to walk slowly and notice water, trees, birds, and the softer side of northeast Louisiana.

The park is a good low-key counterweight to Monroe's museums and riverfront stops. Go in cooler parts of the day, bring insect repellent in warm months, and expect a nature walk rather than a formal garden.
