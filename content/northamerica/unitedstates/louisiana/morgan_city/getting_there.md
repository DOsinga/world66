---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Morgan City is on US Route 90 in southern Louisiana, roughly halfway between New Orleans and Lafayette.

**By Car from New Orleans**: Take I-310 west to US-90 west. The drive is about 80 miles and takes just under two hours.

**By Car from Lafayette**: US-90 east. About 60 miles, roughly one hour.

**By Train**: Amtrak's Sunset Limited (Los Angeles to New Orleans) stops at nearby Schriever, about 30 miles northeast. This is a limited service (three times weekly) and practical only if you are already travelling that route.

There is no regular bus service to Morgan City from major cities. A car is the practical way to visit.
