The Rig Museum Mr. Charlie turns a retired offshore drilling rig into Morgan City's most
distinctive museum. Rather than explaining the Gulf oil industry from a distance, it
puts visitors on the structure itself, with machinery, crew spaces, and industrial scale
underfoot.

It is a strong fit for anyone interested in Louisiana beyond plantations, bayous, and
food culture. Tours make the difference, so check schedules and be ready for stairs,
metal decks, and a working-industrial feel.
