title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Morgan City's appeal is honest and workmanlike. It is not a tourist destination, but the combination of its offshore oil history, the Atchafalaya Basin at its doorstep, and its Cajun cultural identity make it an interesting stop along US 90.

The [Southwest Reef Lighthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/morgan_city/southwest_reef_light) display is a highlight — one of the distinctive screw-pile lighthouses that once marked Gulf Coast approaches, now restored and open to the public. The annual Louisiana Shrimp and Petroleum Festival in September is the town's biggest event, a celebration of its two defining industries that is more charming than its name suggests.

Swamp tours into the Atchafalaya Basin are the main outdoor activity. The basin — North America's largest river swamp — surrounds Morgan City on several sides, and a boat ride through the cypress-tupelo forest gives a strong sense of how this part of Louisiana has defined itself around water for centuries.
