Natchitoches National Fish Hatchery raises species used to restock wildlife refuges, including striped bass, pallid sturgeon, and paddlefish. It is a working conservation site rather than a polished museum, but visitors can stop in and group tours can be arranged.

It makes most sense if you are interested in the rivers and wetlands behind this part of Louisiana. The hatchery adds a practical wildlife angle to a town better known for colonial and Creole history.
