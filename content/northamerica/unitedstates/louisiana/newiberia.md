New Iberia sits in the heart of Cajun Country, along Bayou Teche in southern Louisiana. It is a small, unhurried city where the French-speaking Acadian heritage still runs deep -- in the food, the music, and the easygoing pace of life along the bayou.

The main draw is [Avery Island](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/newiberia/avery_island), just south of town, home to the Tabasco factory and the lush Jungle Gardens. [Shadows-on-the-Teche](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/newiberia/shadows_on_the_teche), a restored antebellum plantation house on Main Street, offers a window into the area's sugar-planting past. The streets of downtown New Iberia are walkable and pleasant — [Bouligny Plaza](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/newiberia/bouligny_plaza) and the [Bayou Teche walking trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/newiberia/bayou_teche) both anchor good explorations of the historic district — with a handful of good restaurants specializing in the Cajun and Creole cooking that this part of Louisiana does better than anywhere else.

New Iberia also serves as a good base for exploring the wider Acadiana region, including [Lafayette](/northamerica/unitedstates/louisiana/lafayette) to the north and the swamps and marshes of the Atchafalaya Basin to the east.
