---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

New Iberia's main attractions cluster around Bayou Teche and the surrounding countryside. Avery Island — home to the Tabasco factory and a remarkable wildlife sanctuary — is the headline draw. Shadows-on-the-Teche, the antebellum plantation house on Main Street, provides the historical counterpoint. The Conrad Rice Mill and the downtown historic district fill out a full day of exploration, and the surrounding Acadiana countryside has swamp tours, plantation houses, and the broader Cajun cultural landscape to explore.
