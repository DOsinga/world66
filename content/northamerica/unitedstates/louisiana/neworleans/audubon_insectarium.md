The Audubon Insectarium is a polished, family-friendly museum devoted to insects and their place in the natural world. Exhibits lean into close-up encounters, live displays, and the sheer oddness of bugs, which makes it more engaging than the dry subject might sound.

Its central location makes it easy to combine with the riverfront or the French Quarter. Expect security screening at the entrance, since the museum is housed in the historic U.S. Custom House.
