Jackson Barracks Military Museum is on a military post that has been part of New Orleans since the 1830s. The museum covers Louisiana National Guard history, with artifacts, weapons, and memorabilia from major American wars.

It is an unusual Lower 9th Ward stop, but worthwhile for visitors with a strong interest in military history.
