The French Quarter visitor center for Jean Lafitte National Historical Park is a useful first stop for understanding the park's New Orleans sites and the history of the Quarter. Inside are small exhibits, maps, and National Park Service staff who can point visitors toward walks, talks, and nearby historic stops.

Arrive early if you want a place on one of the free ranger-led French Quarter walking tours, which are often the main reason to come.
