New Orleans Jazz National Historical Park is a National Park Service site focused on the city's jazz heritage. Its French Quarter presence is around Dutch Alley and the French Market, where ranger programmes and live music are often scheduled.

It is a good low-key stop if you want context for the music heard around the city. Check the current programme before going, since concerts and talks vary by day.
