The Old U.S. Mint is a historic mint building at the edge of the French Quarter, now used as a museum. Its exhibits connect two parts of New Orleans history: coin production downstairs and the city's jazz heritage upstairs.

It is an easy add-on to a French Quarter or Frenchmen Street walk. The building itself is part of the appeal, especially for visitors interested in the city's older civic architecture.
