A long, narrow downtown bar with exposed brick and live music most nights — blues, folk, jazz, and singer-songwriters. The crowd is a mix of theatre-goers from the Strand around the corner, downtown workers, and out-of-towners who walked over from the casinos.

The kitchen does decent burgers and salads until late. Cash bar; tip the musicians.
