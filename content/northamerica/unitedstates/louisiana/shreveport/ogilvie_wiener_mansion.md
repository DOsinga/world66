A turreted Queen Anne house on Crockett Street, dating from the late nineteenth century and now used as an event venue. The exterior is the main attraction — the wraparound porch, the spire, and the shingle work are all original. Not generally open to the public, but easy to see from the street if you are walking around the south side of downtown.
