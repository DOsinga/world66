The serious coffee roaster in Shreveport, with a flagship cafe on Line Avenue and a smaller downtown branch. Espresso drinks, pour-overs, and a small pastry case. Reliable wifi, a steady freelancer crowd, and beans you can take home.

The Line Avenue location has a small patio shaded by a magnolia.
