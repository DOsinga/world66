Wonder Oasis Waterpark, formerly Splash Kingdom Family Waterpark, is a small seasonal water park on the west side of Shreveport. It has slides and rides aimed at a range of ages, making it most useful for families looking for a low-key break from museums and city sightseeing.

Check opening dates before going, as water parks in this part of Louisiana are strongly seasonal.
