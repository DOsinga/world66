Bass Harbor Head Light is one of Acadia's classic coastal views: an 1858 lighthouse set above a rocky Atlantic cliff on the southwest side of Mount Desert Island. The tower itself is not open to visitors, but the grounds and viewpoints give you the postcard angle.

For the best view, take the short trail and stairs from the parking area down toward the shoreline. The return climb is steep but brief, and sunset is the busiest time.
