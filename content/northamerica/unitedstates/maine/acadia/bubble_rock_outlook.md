Bubble Rock is the famous glacial boulder perched near the summit of South Bubble Mountain. The rock was carried here by ice and left in a position that looks improbable enough to draw nearly everyone with a camera.

The outlook also gives wide views over Jordan Pond and the surrounding Acadia hills. It is best visited as part of the Bubble Rock trail rather than as a quick roadside stop.
