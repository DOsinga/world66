Carroll Homestead is one of the few surviving houses from Mount Desert Island's settlement days, preserved inside Acadia National Park. It gives a quieter, more human view of the island than the carriage roads and cliff overlooks: farm life, family history, and the older communities that predate the park.

Access is limited and tours run only on selected days, so check with park headquarters before making a special trip.
