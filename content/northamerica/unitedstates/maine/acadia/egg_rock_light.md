Egg Rock Light is an 1875 lighthouse on a small barren ledge in Frenchman Bay, east of Mount Desert Island. The lighthouse is not a walk-up attraction, but it is a distinctive sight from the water and from parts of Acadia's coast.

Look for it from viewpoints along the Acadia National Park Loop Road or on boat trips through Frenchman Bay.
