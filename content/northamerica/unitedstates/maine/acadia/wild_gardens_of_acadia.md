Wild Gardens of Acadia is a compact garden at Sieur de Monts that brings together plant communities from across Mount Desert Island. Small labelled areas represent habitats such as bogs, shorelines, meadows, and forest.

It is a useful stop before heading deeper into Acadia, especially if you want a slower look at the park's ecology rather than only its views and carriage roads.
