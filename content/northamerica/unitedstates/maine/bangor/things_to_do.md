Bangor punches well above its weight as a destination for literary and history enthusiasts. The city's Victorian architecture and its association with Stephen King give it a distinctive character, while the waterfront and surrounding landscape remind you that this is also the gateway to Maine's vast northern wilderness. For Stephen King fans this is essentially a pilgrimage city — the real-life locations that inspired the fictional town of Derry are scattered throughout.
