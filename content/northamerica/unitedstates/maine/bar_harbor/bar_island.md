A small wooded island just off downtown Bar Harbor, reachable on foot across a tidal gravel bar at low tide. The crossing itself is the attraction: bare ocean floor underfoot, mussel shells crunching, a low fog drifting in. On the island, a short trail climbs to an open ledge with the best view back at the town and the surrounding peaks of [Acadia](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/acadia).

Check the tide chart before you go. The bar is walkable for roughly three hours total — about ninety minutes either side of low tide. The trailhead is at the north end of Bridge Street.
