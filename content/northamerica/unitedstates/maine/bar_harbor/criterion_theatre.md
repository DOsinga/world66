A 1932 Art Deco theatre on Cottage Street, recently restored, still operating with films, live music, and stage shows. The interior is the draw — geometric plaster, the original chandelier, and a cantilevered balcony that hangs over the orchestra without a single post in the way.

Worth checking the calendar even if you have no plans for a show; the building survives largely because tickets keep selling. 35 Cottage Street.
