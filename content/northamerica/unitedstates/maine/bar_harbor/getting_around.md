---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Downtown Bar Harbor is best walked — it is only about ten blocks square. For everything else, use the **Island Explorer**, the free propane-powered bus network that runs from late June to mid-October and connects the town to all the main destinations in [Acadia National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/acadia), as well as Bass Harbor and Northeast Harbor on the other side of Mount Desert Island.

Parking in town is metered, scarce, and policed. If you have a car, the simplest plan is to leave it at your accommodation and use the bus. Bike rentals are easy to find on Cottage Street; the carriage roads inside Acadia are flat gravel and built for them.
