The Jordan Pond House sits inside [Acadia National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/acadia), about eight miles from town, with the pond and the twin domes of the Bubbles framed perfectly behind the lawn. It is the one Acadia institution that almost everyone makes time for: a pot of tea and a tray of warm popovers with strawberry jam and butter, eaten on the grass.

Reservations are essential in season and can be made online a month ahead. The full menu is mediocre by Bar Harbor standards but the setting and the popovers are the point. Drive or take the Island Explorer Route 5.
