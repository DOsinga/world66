---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

For such a small place Belgrade Lakes Village has a respectable lineup of restaurants. The **Village Inn** is the oldest and best known, famous regionally for its roast Long Island duckling — half a duck served with the chef's signature orange-and-applesauce — and for its waterside dining room overlooking the channel between Great Pond and Long Pond. **Day's Store** lunch counter does breakfast sandwiches and lobster rolls; the **Sunset Grille** at the marina serves casual dinners on the dock; and the **golf club restaurant** has lunch and dinner with the best view in the area.

For groceries, beer and bait, Day's Store is the year-round option. Most of the village restaurants close from late September to mid-May.
