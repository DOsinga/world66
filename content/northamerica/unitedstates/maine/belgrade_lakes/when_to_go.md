---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Belgrade Lakes are a strongly seasonal destination. The summer season runs from **late June to Labor Day** (early September), when the lake camps are open, restaurants and shops are operating, and the water is warm enough to swim — usually 70–75°F by late July. **September** is the best month for visitors who want fewer people: warm days, cool nights, the start of fall colour by month's end, and the lakes almost empty.

**October** brings full foliage and the closure of most summer businesses. From **November through April** the lakes freeze over and the area becomes a quiet residential community with some ice fishing and a small cross-country ski trail network. **May and June** are mud season and black-fly season; July is peak.
