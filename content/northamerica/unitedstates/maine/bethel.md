Bethel is a western Maine mountain village on the Androscoggin River, close to Sunday River and the New Hampshire line. It has the bones of a classic New England town: a compact centre, old houses, a college-prep campus, and forested hills rising quickly outside town.

The outdoor draw is strong. [Sunday River](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bethel/sunday_river) is one of the major ski areas in the Northeast and keeps busy in summer with lift rides, hiking, biking, and events. North of town, [Grafton Notch State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bethel/grafton_notch_state_park) brings the Appalachian Trail into a rugged landscape of waterfalls, notches, and mountain views.

Bethel also has a better rainy-day hand than many mountain towns. The [Maine Mineral and Gem Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bethel/maine_mineral_and_gem_museum) is serious and surprising, with meteorites and Maine tourmaline, while the [Museums of the Bethel Historical Society](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bethel/bethel_historical_society) keep the village's 19th-century story visible.
