Boothbay Harbor is a classic Maine coastal village on a protected inlet about an hour north of Portland. The harbor is the center of activity — [whale watching and puffin cruises](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/boothbay_harbor/whale_watching_cruises) depart from the waterfront, and the shops and restaurants along the narrow streets cater to summer visitors without feeling entirely overrun.

The [Coastal Maine Botanical Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/boothbay_harbor/coastal_maine_botanical_gardens), just outside town, are worth the trip on their own — 300 acres of garden rooms and native woodland. The [Burnt Island Light](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/boothbay_harbor/burnt_island_light), accessible by guided boat tour, is one of the oldest lighthouses in Maine and runs an entertaining living history program. In late June the town hosts [Windjammer Days](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/boothbay_harbor/windjammer_days), when tall ships fill the harbor for a week.
