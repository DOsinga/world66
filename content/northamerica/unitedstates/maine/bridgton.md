Bridgton sits in the heart of western Maine's lakes and mountains region, roughly equidistant from [Portland](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portland), Freeport, and North Conway, New Hampshire. It is a genuine four-season town: swimming and canoeing on [Long Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bridgton/long_lake) and [Highland Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bridgton/highland_lake) in summer, spectacular foliage drives in fall, and skiing at [Pleasant Mountain](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bridgton/pleasant_mountain) and snowmobiling on a vast trail network in winter. The surrounding lakes and clean mountain air have drawn families here for generations, and the town still has the feel of old-fashioned New England summer vacations.

Downtown Bridgton is small but has a good cluster of shops and restaurants along Main Street. The [Bridgton Twin Drive-In](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bridgton/bridgton_twin_drive_in) is one of the few remaining drive-in theaters in New England and is worth a visit on a warm summer night. The [Magic Lantern Cinema](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bridgton/magic_lantern), a four-screen movie house with old-fashioned balconies and a pub inside, is a pleasure in any weather. The town also has a farmers market that runs through the warmer months.

The [Rufus Porter Museum of Art and Ingenuity](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bridgton/rufus_porter_museum) is the best surprise in town — a small museum dedicated to a western Maine native who founded *Scientific American*, painted murals across New England, invented the revolving rifle (and sold the patent to Colt for $100), and lived to 92. It alone is worth the detour for anyone interested in American folk art or the history of invention.

If you are based here, [Long Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bridgton/long_lake) stretches north from town and is good for kayaking, fishing, and swimming. [Pleasant Mountain](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/bridgton/pleasant_mountain) rises to the west and offers hiking trails with views across the lakes region all the way to Mount Washington on a clear day. Bridgton is also well positioned as a base for day trips to the coast or the White Mountains — both are about an hour's drive.
