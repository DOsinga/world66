**The Mist** by Stephen King (1980) — King's novella is set on Long Lake, and anyone who has seen the morning mist roll off the water in early summer will recognize the atmosphere immediately. It was adapted into a film in 2007. King had a long association with the Bridgton area.

**The Story of a New Name** and related books — E.B. White, who owned a vacation home on Long Lake, wrote about New England lake life with an affection that resonates here. His essays in *One Man's Meat* capture the quality of Maine summers better than most travel writing.

**Yankee Magazine's New England** — not a work of literature, but the magazine's decades of coverage of western Maine's lakes region offers good historical texture for understanding why Bridgton and its neighbors developed the way they did.
