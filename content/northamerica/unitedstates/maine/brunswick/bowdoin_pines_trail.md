Bowdoin Pines Trail is a short wooded escape beside the Bowdoin College campus. The 33-acre old-growth pine stand has easy paths for walking, jogging, bird-watching, picnics, and winter cross-country skiing.

It is not a long hike, but it is a pleasant pause if you are already exploring the college area.
