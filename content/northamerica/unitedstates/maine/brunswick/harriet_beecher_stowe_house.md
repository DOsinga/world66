Harriet Beecher Stowe lived in this Federal Street house while her husband taught at Bowdoin, and it was here that she wrote much of *Uncle Tom's Cabin*. The building is not a large conventional house museum, so check access before planning a visit.

Even from the outside, it is one of Brunswick's most important addresses. Pair it with nearby First Parish Church, where Stowe worshipped.
