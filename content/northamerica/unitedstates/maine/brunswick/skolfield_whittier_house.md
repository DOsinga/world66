The Skolfield-Whittier House is a preserved Victorian duplex often described as a time capsule. Its Italianate rooms, furnishings, and family collections show how a well-off Brunswick household lived in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, making it a useful companion to the town's Bowdoin College museums and historic houses.
