A handful of small pubs and taverns in town serve the local population and passing travellers. These are friendly, unpretentious places — not destination drinking, but solid and welcoming after a day on Borestone Mountain or the river. You are likely to end up talking to someone who knows the area well, which is often the most useful thing you can get from a bar in a town like this.
