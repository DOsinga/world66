Downtown has a handful of classic Maine diners and lunch counters serving the kind of food you want after a morning outdoors: chowder, lobster rolls in season, and breakfast plates that actually fill you up. These are the places where locals gather over coffee, and the conversation tends to be about weather, fishing, and what the roads are like further north. Exactly what a small Maine town diner should be.
