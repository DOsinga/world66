type: poi

The general stores around town serve a practical function for anyone heading into the backcountry: sandwiches, hot food from the deli counter, snacks, and provisions for the trail. If you are stocking up before Borestone Mountain, Moosehead Lake, or points north, these are your best options. The deli sandwiches tend to be generous and the staff will usually know conditions on the roads and trails.
