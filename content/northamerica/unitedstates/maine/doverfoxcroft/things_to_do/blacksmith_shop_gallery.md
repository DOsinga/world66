A local art gallery housed in a converted blacksmith shop, showing work by Maine artists. The setting fits the town — practical space turned to creative use. Exhibits rotate and cover painting, photography, and craft work from the region. Worth a look if you have the time, and a good reminder that the North Woods has always attracted artists as well as outdoorspeople.
