The Center Theatre is a restored 1940s movie house in the middle of downtown that has been brought back to life as a genuine community venue. It shows films and hosts live performances — music, theater, and community events — and its survival in a town this size says something about the place. If something is on when you are passing through, it is worth stopping for. Check ahead for the schedule.
