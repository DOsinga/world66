title: "Piscataquis River"
type: poi
latitude: 45.182
longitude: -69.226
---

The Piscataquis River runs right through town and offers solid fishing for brook trout and smallmouth bass through town and along its broader reaches. Canoe access points near the bridge make it easy to put in for a paddle — the river is calm enough through this stretch for recreational paddling and slow-moving exploration. For serious fishing, ask locally about the best reaches upstream; the trout fishing in particular can be excellent in the right season.
