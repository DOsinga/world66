Eastport packs a surprising range of experiences into a small island city. The drama begins with the tides themselves — 18 to 28 feet of rise and fall twice a day, among the largest tidal swings on the American coastline. The working waterfront, the Tides Institute, and a handful of genuinely distinctive attractions make this one of the most rewarding small towns in New England.
