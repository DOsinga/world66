Raye's Mustard Mill is the last remaining stone-ground, cold-process mustard mill in North America. J.W. Raye founded the operation in 1900 to supply mustard to the sardine canneries that once lined the Eastport waterfront; those canneries are long gone but the mill keeps grinding. Visitors can tour the old stone-grinding machinery, watch mustard being made, and buy from the full range of flavours in the mill store. The building itself — a working artifact of the town's canning-industry heyday — is as interesting as the product.
