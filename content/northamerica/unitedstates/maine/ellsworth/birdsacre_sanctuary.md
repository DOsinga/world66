Birdsacre Sanctuary preserves the former homestead of ornithologist Cordelia Stanwood and a pocket of woodland on the edge of Ellsworth. The trails are gentle, the bird rehabilitation work gives the place a living purpose, and the old homestead adds a human story to the natural setting.

This is a quiet stop, best for travellers who like nature centers, short walks, and small museums with a local soul. It pairs well with Ellsworth's role as a gateway to Acadia, especially when you want something calmer than the national park crowds.
