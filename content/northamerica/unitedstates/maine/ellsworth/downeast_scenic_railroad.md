The Downeast Scenic Railroad runs heritage train excursions from Ellsworth, giving visitors a slow ride through the woods, marshes, and back-country edges of coastal Maine. It is a modest operation, but it offers a nice change of pace from driving between Acadia, Route 1, and the small towns around Mount Desert Island.

The ride is most appealing for families, railway enthusiasts, and anyone who likes seeing a landscape at a slower speed. Check the seasonal timetable before going; when trains are not running, there is little reason to treat the station as a standalone sight.
