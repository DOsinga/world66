story: >
  Lovewell Pond is one of those Maine lakes that has not been developed beyond recognition. The shoreline is mostly wooded, the water is clear and cold, and it is primarily used by local families rather than tourist crowds. It takes its name from John Lovewell, whose 1725 battle with the Pigwacket Abenaki took place near its shores.
---

The pond offers good swimming from its public access areas, and it is popular with local anglers for bass and pickerel. Kayaks and canoes handle the calm water easily. There is no formal boat launch infrastructure, so a cartop boat or a kayak dragged to the water's edge is the practical approach.

The combination of the pond's quiet character and its proximity to the battle monument makes for an interesting afternoon -- history and landscape in a compact package. The pond is at its best on weekdays in summer, when it sees a fraction of the traffic that the better-known lakes in the region attract.
