---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Greenville Junction is genuinely off the main road. **By car** it is about two hours north of Bangor on Route 15, or three from Portland. The last 40 miles north of Dover-Foxcroft are scenic forest road with very little traffic.

There is no scheduled passenger train despite the depot, and **no scheduled bus service** — the Concord Coach lines end at Bangor. The nearest commercial airport is **Bangor International** (two hours' drive). **Greenville Municipal Airport**, two miles east, takes private aircraft and seaplanes operate directly off Moosehead Lake.
