Moosehead Marine Museum is a small seasonal museum beside the mooring for the historic steamboat Katahdin. Exhibits cover Moosehead Lake's boating history and the role the lake played in local transport, work, and tourism.

It pairs naturally with the Katahdin steamboat and the Greenville waterfront, making it a useful stop before or after time on the lake.
