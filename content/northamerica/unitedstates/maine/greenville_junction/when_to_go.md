---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The classic season is **June to early October**. June is good for moose-watching (cows with calves come down to the logging roads), July and August give reliable lake weather and the warmest swimming, and **late September into early October** brings the fall colour — the hardwoods turn brilliant red and yellow against the dark spruce.

**Winter** is the other distinct season: snowmobiling is huge in this corner of Maine, with a major trail network radiating from Greenville. Cross-country skiing, ice fishing, and ice climbing all run from late December through March. Days are short and very cold (regularly minus 20°F at night).

**Black fly season** runs from mid-May to mid-June; bring DEET or stay south. The lake is mostly frozen from December to April — no boat rides.
