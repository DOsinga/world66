Baxter State Park is Maine at its wildest: a huge protected landscape of forest, lakes, ridges, and the Katahdin massif. It is deliberately low on services, so come prepared for carry-in, carry-out travel, reserved camping, and changeable mountain weather.

For hikers, this is the classic gateway to Mount Katahdin and the northern end of the Appalachian Trail. Even if you are not climbing the mountain, the park is worth a deliberate stop for quiet ponds, wildlife, and some of the best backcountry scenery in the state.
