Kennebunkport is a fishing port and tourist town on Maine's southern coast. The old section is worth a slow walk — shops, galleries, and restaurants line the streets near the harbour around [Dock Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/things_to_do/dock_square), and several beaches are within easy reach. The town is best known outside Maine as the location of [Walker's Point](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/things_to_do/walkers_point), the Bush family summer estate visible from the Ocean Avenue drive.

[Cape Porpoise](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/things_to_do/cape_porpoise), a fishing village a few kilometres east, is the least touristy part of the area and the best place to eat lobster. The [Seashore Trolley Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/things_to_do/seashore_trolley_museum) outside town is a surprise — the world's largest collection of historic streetcars, with rides through the Maine woods. The area is popular with weekenders from Boston, so summer weekends can be crowded; September is the best time to visit.
