---
title: "Bars and Cafes in Kennebunkport"
type: section
---

Kennebunkport's bar scene is modest and largely tied to its restaurants. The town is relatively quiet after dinner, particularly in the shoulder season, and the drinking culture is relaxed rather than rowdy. Most bars are attached to inns or restaurants around Dock Square.

[Nonantum Resort's Bonfire Bar](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/nonantum_bonfire_bar) on Ocean Avenue has a good outdoor deck with river views, popular in summer. [Alisson's Restaurant](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/alissons) on Dock Square is the most central spot for a drink, with a long bar and reliable pub food. For coffee, [Port Bakery and Cafe](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/port_bakery) near Dock Square is the best morning option, with good pastries and a small seating area.
