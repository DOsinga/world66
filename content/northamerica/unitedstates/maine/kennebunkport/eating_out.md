---
title: "Eating Out in Kennebunkport"
type: section
---

Kennebunkport's food scene is dominated by seafood — specifically, lobster, in every possible preparation. The region is among the best places in the world to eat fresh Maine lobster, and even the tourist-facing restaurants around Dock Square manage to keep quality high when the raw material is this good.

**The Clam Shack** (2 Western Avenue, at the Kennebunk River bridge) is the most famous address: a tiny window operation that has been serving fried clams and lobster rolls since the 1960s. The lobster rolls are split-top, cold with mayo, and widely considered among the best in Maine. Expect a line in summer.

**Nunan's Lobster Hut** in Cape Porpoise is the local institution for whole boiled lobster — a no-frills boathouse on the harbour that has been doing the same thing since 1953. Cash only; BYOB; no reservations. Arrive early.

**The White Barn Inn** restaurant is at the other end of the spectrum — a converted barn with a formal tasting menu, national press attention, and prices to match. It's one of the better fine-dining rooms in New England.
