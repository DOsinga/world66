---
title: "Eating Out in Kennebunkport"
type: section
---

Kennebunkport's food scene is dominated by seafood — specifically lobster, in every possible preparation. The region is among the best places in the world to eat fresh Maine lobster, and even the tourist-facing restaurants around Dock Square keep quality high when the raw material is this good.

[The Clam Shack](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/eating_out/the_clam_shack) (2 Western Avenue, at the Kennebunk River bridge) is the most famous address: a tiny walk-up window that has been serving fried clams and lobster rolls since the 1960s. The lobster rolls are split-top, cold with mayo, and widely considered among the best in Maine. Expect a line in summer.

[Nunan's Lobster Hut](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/eating_out/nunans_lobster_hut) in Cape Porpoise is the local institution for whole boiled lobster — a no-frills boathouse on the harbour that has been doing the same thing since 1953. Cash only; BYOB; no reservations. Arrive early.

[The White Barn Inn](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/kennebunkport/eating_out/white_barn_inn) restaurant is at the other end of the spectrum — a converted barn with a formal tasting menu, national press attention, and prices to match. It's one of the better fine-dining rooms in New England.
