---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Kennebunkport's core — Dock Square, Ocean Avenue, and the beaches immediately south — is walkable from the centre of town. Cape Porpoise and Goose Rocks Beach require a car or bicycle: both are about 5 km from Dock Square, on roads with variable shoulders.

**On foot**, the walk from Dock Square south along Ocean Avenue to Walker's Point and back takes about an hour and passes the best of the town's scenery — the harbour, the rocky shore, the Ocean Avenue mansions.

**By bicycle**, the terrain is gentle and the roads are manageable outside peak summer hours. Several shops in and around Dock Square rent bikes by the half-day or full day. Cape Porpoise Road (Route 9) is the most direct bike route east to the fishing village, with a separated shoulder for much of its length.

**By car**, parking is the constraint. In July and August, the Dock Square lot fills before 9am on weekends. Peripheral lots on Route 9 west of the bridge have overflow parking; the town runs a shuttle in peak season. Driving between Dock Square and Cape Porpoise takes about 10 minutes.

The **Intown Trolley** runs a sightseeing loop in summer (late May through October), hitting the main sights including Walker's Point, Goose Rocks Beach, and Cape Porpoise. It's a reasonable way to orient on arrival without navigating the one-way streets and parking.
