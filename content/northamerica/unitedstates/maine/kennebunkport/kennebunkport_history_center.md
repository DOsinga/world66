The Kennebunkport History Center is the practical home base for understanding the town beyond Dock Square shops and summer-house scenery. Run by the Kennebunkport Historical Society, it anchors archives, exhibits, and several historic properties that tell the story of a coastal village shaped by shipbuilding, trade, and seasonal visitors.

It is most useful if you are already curious about the old houses and riverfront streets around the village. Hours and open buildings vary, so check ahead rather than assuming every property is open. Even a brief visit helps put Kennebunkport's polished present back into its working maritime context.
