---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Kittery sits at the Maine-New Hampshire border, making it easy to reach by car from either direction. From Boston, take I-95 north across the Piscataqua Bridge — Kittery is the first exit in Maine, about 60 miles from the city. From Portland, head south on I-95 for about 50 miles. The drive from either direction is straightforward, though I-95 approaching the bridge can back up considerably on summer weekends.

There is no train or bus service that stops in Kittery directly. The nearest Amtrak station is in Durham, New Hampshire, and the COAST bus system connects Kittery to Portsmouth and other Seacoast communities. For the outlets and most attractions, a car is essentially required.

From Portsmouth, New Hampshire, Kittery is a five-minute drive over the Memorial Bridge on Route 1, or slightly longer via I-95. The bridges across the Piscataqua provide constant connection between the two towns — they function as a single urban area in practice, despite the state line.
