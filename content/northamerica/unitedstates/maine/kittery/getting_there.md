---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---
Kittery is the first town you reach in Maine after crossing the Piscataqua River from New Hampshire on I-95. From Boston the drive takes about an hour without traffic. Exit 2 off I-95 puts you directly onto Route 1 and the outlet strip. There is no passenger rail service to Kittery; the nearest Amtrak station is in Portsmouth, New Hampshire, a few miles across the river, from which a taxi or rideshare is straightforward.
