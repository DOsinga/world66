The Kittery outlet strip runs for roughly two miles along Route 1, making it the largest concentration of factory outlet stores in northern New England. The stores occupy a mix of purpose-built outlet plazas and older commercial buildings strung together without much planning. Brands range from outdoor and apparel staples — L.L.Bean, Eddie Bauer, Patagonia, Levi's — to kitchen goods, luggage, and home furnishings. Prices are genuinely discounted off retail in most cases, which is why the Massachusetts license plates reliably outnumber Maine plates in the parking lots.

The strip works best if you have a specific store in mind and go on a weekday. Weekend traffic, especially in fall foliage season and around holidays, turns Route 1 into a slow crawl from the bridge to Kittery's town center. The outlets are pedestrian-unfriendly by design — each plaza has its own lot and getting between them on foot means crossing multiple driveways. Treat it as a series of parking stops rather than a walkable shopping district, and you will manage it fine.
