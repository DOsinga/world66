More than 120 outlet stores line Route 1 through Kittery, making this one of the densest concentrations of retail in New England. The stores range from mainstream clothing brands and sporting goods to kitchen equipment, books, and luggage — most offering significant discounts on standard retail prices.

The strip is spread over about two miles and is best navigated by car, though it is walkable if you don't mind the roadside setting. It gets extremely busy on weekends and during holiday periods; weekday mornings are noticeably quieter. There is no central mall — the stores sit in a series of low-rise complexes along the highway, some with food courts and cafes attached.
