Robert's Maine Grille sits in the middle of the outlet corridor, which might make you skeptical, but the kitchen takes its seafood seriously. The menu focuses on Maine standards — lobster rolls, chowder, fried clams, broiled fish — prepared without unnecessary complication. The lobster stew, in particular, has a reputation that extends beyond the outlet crowd. It is one of the few places in the district where you will see locals eating alongside the shoppers.

The building has a traditional New England interior, nothing fussy, with booth seating and a full bar. Portions are substantial and the seafood is sourced locally when possible. This is not a destination restaurant in the way that Portsmouth's waterfront places are, but it is honest food at reasonable prices for this part of the coast. Waits can be long on weekend afternoons — the outlet foot traffic ensures that — so a reservation or an early lunch is worth planning ahead.
