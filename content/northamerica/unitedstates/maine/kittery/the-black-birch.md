The Black Birch opened as a bottle shop and tap room and settled into a role as one of the few genuinely local gathering places in Kittery's commercial center. The focus is on regional craft beer and cider, with a rotating selection of New England breweries and a handful of well-chosen imports. The food menu is simple — charcuterie, cheese, small plates — which suits the format. It is a place to drink well and talk, not to have a full dinner.

The interior is comfortable without being precious about it, and the clientele skews toward people who actually live in the area rather than outlet day-trippers. On weekday evenings it can feel like a neighborhood bar, which is harder to find in this part of Kittery than you might expect. If you are spending a day shopping the strip and want somewhere to decompress before the drive home, this is the most sensible option in the immediate vicinity.
