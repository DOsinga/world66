Kittery is best known as Maine's outlet shopping capital, but there is more to the town than discount stores along Route 1. This is one of the oldest settlements in Maine, with a naval history stretching back to the 17th century. The working Portsmouth Naval Shipyard sits on an island in the Piscataqua River, historic Fort McClary guards the harbour entrance at Kittery Point, and the rocky coastline offers fine views back towards New Hampshire and the open Atlantic.
