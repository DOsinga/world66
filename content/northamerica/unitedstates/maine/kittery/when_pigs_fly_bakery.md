When Pigs Fly is a well-regarded artisan bakery that started in Kittery and has since expanded across New England, but the original Route 1 location remains the flagship. The bakery is known for its unusual flavour combinations in sourdough and other breads — think garlic Parmesan, cranberry walnut, or roasted garlic and rosemary — baked in a wood-fired oven.

The Kittery shop also operates as a full-service restaurant serving breakfast and lunch, with sandwiches made on their own bread, soups, and seasonal specials. It is a genuine local institution and a good reason to take a break from outlet shopping. Arrive early for the widest bread selection, as popular loaves sell out by mid-afternoon on weekends.
