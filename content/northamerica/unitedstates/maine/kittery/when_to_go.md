---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Kittery's outlet stores are open year-round, so there is no bad season for the shopping that drives most visits. For the historic sites and waterfront, late May through October gives the best weather. Summer weekends bring heavy traffic on Route 1 — the parking lots fill by mid-morning and the road backs up badly in both directions on Saturdays. If you are coming specifically to shop, a weekday visit avoids the worst of it.

Fort McClary and Kittery Point are at their best in the shoulder seasons: spring and fall light is good, the crowds are thin, and the views along the Piscataqua are clear. Winter is quiet and the waterfront feels genuinely remote — the summer tourist overlay completely disappears and you get a sense of the working coast. The Piscataqua Bridge is open year-round, and the drive along Route 1A through Kittery Point is beautiful in any season if the weather holds.
