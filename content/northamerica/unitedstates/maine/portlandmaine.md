Portland is Maine's largest city and its cultural capital. The [Old Port](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portlandmaine/old_port) — a compact grid of brick buildings along the waterfront — is packed with restaurants, bars, and independent shops. The food scene here punches well above its weight for a city this size, with a focus on local seafood and farm-to-table cooking.

Beyond the Old Port, the Arts District downtown is home to the [Portland Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portlandmaine/portland_museum_of_art), the [Wadsworth-Longfellow House](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portlandmaine/longfellow_house), and the lavish [Victoria Mansion](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portlandmaine/victoria_mansion). The working waterfront is still active with fishing boats and the [Casco Bay ferries](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portlandmaine/casco_bay_ferries) to the islands; the [Eastern Promenade](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portlandmaine/eastern_promenade) and the [Portland Observatory](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portlandmaine/portland_observatory) on [Munjoy Hill](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portlandmaine/munjoy_hill) give the best view of the bay. South of the city, the postcard-perfect [Portland Head Light](/northamerica/unitedstates/maine/portlandmaine/portland_head_light) stands on the cliffs of Cape Elizabeth.
