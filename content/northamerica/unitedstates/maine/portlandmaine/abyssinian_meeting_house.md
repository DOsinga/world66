The Abyssinian Meeting House is Maine's oldest African-American church, built in 1831 by Portland's free Black community. It was closely tied to the Underground Railroad through Reverend Amos Noe Freeman, who hosted abolitionist speakers and helped organise testimony from people escaping slavery.

The building is modest, but its history is unusually strong for New England. Restoration work has aimed to turn it into a museum on Maine's African-American community, so check ahead for open days, tours, and events.
