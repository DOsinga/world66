The Children's Museum & Theatre of Maine is Portland's main hands-on stop for younger travellers. Exhibits are built around play, with pretend town spaces, nature areas, and practical activities that give children something to do rather than just look at.

It is best treated as a family visit, especially on rainy days or when the Old Port and art museums need balancing with kid-friendly time.
