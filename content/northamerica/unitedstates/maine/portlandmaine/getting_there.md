---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By air.** Portland International Jetport (PWM), three miles southwest of downtown, has direct flights from major East Coast cities (New York, Washington, Philadelphia) and seasonal service further afield. Public bus 5 connects the airport to the Old Port in about twenty minutes for under three dollars.

**By bus.** Concord Coach Lines and Greyhound run multiple daily services from Boston (around two hours) and points south. The terminal is on Thompson's Point, a few minutes from downtown by taxi or bus 8.

**By train.** The Amtrak Downeaster connects Portland with Boston (just over two hours) and continues north to Brunswick. The Portland Transportation Center on Thompson's Point handles both train and bus arrivals; a free shuttle runs into the Old Port.

**By car.** I-95 (Maine Turnpike) is the main highway approach. From Boston, expect two hours. Downtown street parking is metered and tight; large garages on Spring and Fore Streets are easier in summer.
