Two Lights State Park is a Cape Elizabeth headland with picnic tables, short shoreline trails, and open Atlantic views. The name comes from the nearby pair of lighthouses, though the park itself is more about sea cliffs, passing ships, and a quiet break from Portland.

Come for a simple coastal walk rather than a full day out. It pairs naturally with Portland Head Light and the other lighthouse stops south of the city.
