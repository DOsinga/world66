Western Promenade is a quiet historic area on Portland's west side, with large 19th-century houses built by merchants and sea captains. The appeal is a slow walk rather than a single monument: look for the old mansions, the broad streets, and the views out toward the Fore River. It pairs well with Portland's better-known downtown sights when you want a less crowded hour outside.
