---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Rockland's appeal is divided between the waterfront and the museums. The Farnsworth Art Museum is the town's standout cultural institution — one of the best small art museums in New England. The harbour has a fleet of windjammer schooners that operate multi-day cruises in summer. The mile-long granite breakwater walk is free, accessible, and genuinely rewarding.
