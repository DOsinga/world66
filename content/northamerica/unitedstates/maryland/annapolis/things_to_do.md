---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Annapolis packs an extraordinary amount of history into a few walkable blocks. The state house, the Naval Academy, three preserved 18th-century mansions, and a working harbour all sit within a fifteen-minute stroll of each other. Start at the dome of the Maryland State House and let the radial street plan — laid out in 1695 by Governor Francis Nicholson — guide you down to the water.
