The Baltimore Museum of Industry focuses on the work that built the city, with recreated cannery, textile, printing, pharmacy, and factory exhibits. It is hands-on and family-friendly, but still gives adults a clear picture of Baltimore's waterfront, manufacturing, and labor history.
