---
title: "Baltimore Blues"
type: poi
author: "Laura Lippman"
---

Laura Lippman's debut novel introduced private detective Tess Monaghan and launched a series set entirely in Baltimore's neighbourhoods, bars, and newsrooms. Lippman grew up in Baltimore and worked at the Sun alongside David Simon; her fiction captures the city's geography, its class tensions, and its specific flavour of mid-Atlantic urban life with the accuracy of someone who walks those streets. Any of the Monaghan novels work as Baltimore companions.
