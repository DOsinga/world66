title: "Shock Value: A Tasteful Book About Bad Taste"
type: poi
author: "John Waters"
---

John Waters's memoir covers his early life making underground films in Baltimore in the 1960s and 1970s, and is essentially a love letter to the city's strangeness. Waters grew up in Baltimore's suburbs, chose to stay when he could easily have left, and has spent decades documenting and celebrating the city's peculiar working-class aesthetic. Reading Waters explains Hampden, explains the city's relationship with kitsch, and explains why Baltimore has never been absorbed into the generic American urban character.
