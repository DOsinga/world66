---
title: "Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass, an American Slave"
type: poi
author: "Frederick Douglass"
---

Douglass's 1845 autobiography is one of the most important American books of the 19th century, and a substantial part of it is set in Baltimore. His descriptions of working in the Fells Point shipyards, of teaching himself to read, and of the city's complex position as a slave-holding urban centre with a large free Black population give Baltimore a literary presence it is rarely given credit for. Essential before visiting the city.
