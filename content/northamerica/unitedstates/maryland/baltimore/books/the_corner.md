---
title: "The Corner: A Year in the Life of an Inner-City Neighbourhood"
type: poi
author: "David Simon and Ed Burns"
---

David Simon's follow-up to his earlier journalism about the Baltimore police department is a full-year immersion in a single corner of the West Baltimore drug trade. Simon and Burns lived in and reported on the neighbourhood for a year, and the result is one of the most unsparing accounts of American urban poverty and the failure of the war on drugs ever written. It later became the basis for the TV series *The Corner* and informed *The Wire*. Reading it before or after visiting Baltimore's west side is transformative.
