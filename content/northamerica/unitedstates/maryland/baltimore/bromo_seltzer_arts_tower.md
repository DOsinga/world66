Bromo Seltzer Arts Tower is a 1911 clock tower built for the factory that made Bromo-Seltzer headache remedy. The factory is gone, but the landmark tower remains.

The city converted the building into artist studios, and visitors can usually see the interior on Saturday open days. It is a distinctive stop between downtown Baltimore and the stadium district.
