Carroll Mansion tells the story of Charles Carroll, the last surviving signer of the Declaration of Independence, and the many later lives of his house as a saloon, tenement, school, and recreation centre. Nearby, the Phoenix Shot Tower is a brick landmark from 1828, built for dropping molten lead into shot and once among the tallest structures in the United States.
