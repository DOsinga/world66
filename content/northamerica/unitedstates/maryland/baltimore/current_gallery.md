Current Gallery is a small downtown Baltimore art space with a stronger local pulse than many formal galleries. By day it is a place to see work by Baltimore artists; by night it has been known for independent shows, DIY music, and other low-key events.

Check what is on before going, since the appeal depends on the current exhibition or listing. When the timing works, it is a good way to catch Baltimore's scrappier creative side close to the centre.
