The Horse You Came In On is the quintessential Baltimore bar — dark, historic, unpretentious, and loud. Located on Thames Street in the heart of Fells Point, it has been serving drinks since 1775, making it one of the oldest continuously operating bars in the United States. The interior has the accumulated patina of 250 years of use, and the crowd on any given night is a genuinely democratic mix of locals, visitors, and people who simply materialised there.

Live music most nights, cheap drinks always, and the kind of atmosphere that cannot be manufactured. The Poe connection is more legend than history, but everything else about this bar is real.
