The Liberty Ship SS John Brown is an operational World War II cargo and troop ship preserved in Baltimore. Tours explain the role of Liberty ships in the war effort and let visitors see the machinery, crew spaces, and working structure of a wartime merchant vessel.

The ship is especially worthwhile for naval history and engineering interests. It sits at Pier 13, away from the Inner Harbor cluster, so check tour times before making the trip.
