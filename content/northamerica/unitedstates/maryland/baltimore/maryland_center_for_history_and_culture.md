The Maryland Center for History and Culture is a major state-history museum in Mount Vernon. Its collections cover Maryland art, documents, decorative arts, and everyday objects, with the manuscript of "The Star-Spangled Banner" among the headline pieces.

It is a strong companion to the nearby Walters Art Museum and Washington Monument if you want to understand Baltimore and Maryland beyond the waterfront.
