Mencken House preserves the home of H. L. Mencken, the Baltimore journalist, critic, and scholar best known for *The American Language*. He lived here from childhood until his death in 1956.

The house is a small public museum and National Historic Landmark. It is most rewarding for visitors interested in Baltimore writers, journalism, and the city's rowhouse neighborhoods west of downtown.
