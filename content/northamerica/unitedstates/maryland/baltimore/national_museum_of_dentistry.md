The National Museum of Dentistry is one of Baltimore's more unusual museums. Its collection covers dental tools, medical history, and odd artifacts, including space-age toothbrushes and examples of early dental work.

It is a niche stop, but a memorable one for visitors who like specialist museums, medical history, or small collections that are stranger than they first sound.
