Power Plant Live is an entertainment complex a few blocks from the Inner Harbor, housed in a renovated historic building and its adjacent constructed plaza. It contains a cluster of bars, restaurants, and live music venues that together make it the city's most concentrated nightlife zone. The bars range from sports-focused to music-oriented, and the outdoor plaza area is used for concerts and events in warmer months.

This is where the Inner Harbor tourist crowd and the downtown after-work crowd intersect, which means it is rarely intimate but frequently lively. The music-focused venues in the complex — particularly Rams Head Live, a mid-size concert venue — book better touring acts than the neighbourhood setting might suggest.
