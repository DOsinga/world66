Dinosaur Park protects a Cretaceous fossil bed about 110 million years old, one of the most important dinosaur sites east of the Mississippi. It is a modest outdoor stop rather than a big museum, but the story is strong: bones, teeth, and plant fossils from this patch of Maryland have helped fill in a rare chapter of eastern North America's dinosaur record.
