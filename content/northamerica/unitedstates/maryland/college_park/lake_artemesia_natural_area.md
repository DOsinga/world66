Lake Artemesia Natural Area is one of College Park’s easiest outdoor escapes. The lake, paths, bridges, and wetland edges create a pleasant loop for walking or cycling, with enough birdlife to make it more than just a neighbourhood park.

It is especially useful if you are in town for the University of Maryland and want a low-effort break from campus. The setting is modest, but the access is good, and the trails connect into the wider Anacostia tributary trail network.
