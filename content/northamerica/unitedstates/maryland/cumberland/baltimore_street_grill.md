The Baltimore Street Grill is the most reliable mid-range dining option on Cumberland's pedestrian street. Solid steaks, a serious burger, Maryland crab cakes (yes, this far inland), and a strong local beer list. The dining room is in a renovated late-19th-century storefront with the original tin ceiling and exposed brick.

Mains run $18-35, and the bar is a popular after-work spot for downtown professionals. Open from lunch into the evening, closed Sundays.
