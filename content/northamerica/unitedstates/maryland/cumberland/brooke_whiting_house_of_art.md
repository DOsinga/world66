The Brooke Whiting House of Art is a small museum built around the home and collection of a Cumberland traveller and collector. Its rooms hold decorative arts from East Asia, Europe, and America, giving the place the feel of a personal cabinet of curiosities rather than a formal gallery.

It is best for visitors who like house museums and local collections. Combine it with the Allegany Museum and the historic centre of Cumberland for a fuller picture of the town.
