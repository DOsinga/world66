---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The countryside around Cumberland is the real draw — half a dozen state parks, the Allegheny National Forest, and a series of small mountain towns within an hour's drive.

**Frostburg**, 15 miles west and 1,400 feet higher, is the terminus of the scenic railroad and a pleasant small college town with a strolling main street. **Berlin, Pennsylvania**, just over the state line, is one of the GAP stops. **Deep Creek Lake** is the regional summer destination, an hour south. **Antietam National Battlefield**, 90 minutes east, is one of the most affecting Civil War sites in the country.
