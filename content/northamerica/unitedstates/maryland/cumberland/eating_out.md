---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Cumberland's dining scene is small but solid, concentrated along Baltimore Street and Centre Street. Expect Appalachian-meets-Mid-Atlantic — meat-and-three diners, a couple of decent pubs, a coal-region pizza tradition, and a growing handful of more ambitious restaurants riding the GAP-cyclist tourism wave.

Look for **western Maryland kielbasa** (the local Polish-immigrant version is its own thing), **mountain trout**, and crab cakes — Cumberland is far inland, but Maryland is Maryland.
