---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car.** Cumberland sits on I-68, the main east-west route across western Maryland. It is roughly 2 hours from Baltimore (140 miles), 2.5 from Washington DC, and 2.5 from Pittsburgh.

**By train.** Amtrak's *Capitol Limited* stops at Cumberland once a day in each direction, running between Washington DC (3 hours) and Chicago (15 hours) via Pittsburgh. The station is in the centre of town.

**By bus.** Greyhound and Megabus serve Cumberland with daily connections to Baltimore, DC, and Pittsburgh.

**By bicycle.** Cumberland is the eastern terminus of the Great Allegheny Passage and the western terminus of the C&O Canal Towpath. The town is one of the most cyclist-friendly small towns in the country.

**By air.** Greater Cumberland Regional Airport (CBE) has limited general-aviation service. Pittsburgh (PIT) and Baltimore-Washington (BWI) are the nearest major airports.
