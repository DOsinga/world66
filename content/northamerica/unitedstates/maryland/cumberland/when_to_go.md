---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May through October** is when Cumberland makes sense as a destination — the steam railroad runs, the towpath and GAP are open and dry, the state parks are in full swing.

**October** is the standout month. The fall colours in the Allegheny Mountains around the Narrows are exceptional, the cycling weather is perfect, and the steam railroad runs special leaf-peeping excursions on weekends. Hotels fill up — book ahead.

**June through August** is reliably warm and humid. The Lake Habeeb beach at Rocky Gap is busy on weekends, and the GAP/C&O cyclist traffic is at its peak.

**Winter** is quiet and cold. Snow on the mountains is possible from November through March. The railroad runs a small holiday series in December but most other things close down.
