Font Hill Wetlands Park is a small 26-acre refuge of ponds, streamside woods, and marshy habitat in Ellicott City. It is best for an easy nature walk: ducks, geese, frogs, turtles, fish, and plenty of insects make it a lively little stop, especially with children.
