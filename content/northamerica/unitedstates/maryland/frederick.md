Frederick is one of Maryland's most rewarding inland towns, close enough to Washington, DC, and Baltimore for an easy day trip but with a character of its own. Its old center has brick storefronts, church spires, antique shops, galleries, and a creek walk that makes the city feel more like a real place than a preserved historic district.

History is the main reason to come. The [National Museum of Civil War Medicine](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/frederick/national_museum_of_civil_war_medicine) is unusually specific and better than its niche sounds, while [Monocacy National Battlefield](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/frederick/monocacy_national_battlefield) sits just south of town on open fields where an 1864 battle delayed a Confederate move toward Washington.

Frederick also works as a slow wandering stop. Walk [Carroll Creek Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/frederick/carroll_creek_park), browse Market Street, and leave time for the German colonial stonework at [Schifferstadt](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/frederick/schifferstadt_architectural_museum). The city is not grand, but it has enough good anchors and enough street life to justify a spare day.
