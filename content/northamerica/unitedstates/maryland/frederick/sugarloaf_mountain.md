Sugarloaf Mountain is one of Maryland's best-known monadnocks, rising above the Piedmont with hiking trails and broad views toward the Appalachian foothills. The summit area is popular, but the trail network gives several options for short walks or longer loops.

It is an easy nature escape from Frederick and a good choice when you want a half-day hike rather than a full mountain trip.
