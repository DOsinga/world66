Linthicum, officially Linthicum Heights, is a quiet commuter suburb in Anne Arundel County, Maryland, sitting just north of Baltimore-Washington International Thurgood Marshall Airport (BWI). For most travellers it is a place to sleep before an early flight rather than a destination in its own right — but the village has a long history and a couple of genuine corners worth a look.

The community grew up around a stop on the old Baltimore and Annapolis Railroad, and the streets behind the modern strip malls still hold a cluster of clapboard farmhouses from that era. The [Linthicum Heights Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/linthicum/linthicum_heights_historic_district) preserves much of this original village fabric. The [Benson–Hammond House](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/linthicum/benson_hammond_house), an 18th-century farmhouse on Poplar Avenue, is open as a small museum run by the Ann Arrundell County Historical Society.

Linthicum is divided into two communities — North Linthicum and Linthicum-Shipley — each on a stop of the Baltimore Light Rail line, which makes getting into downtown Baltimore or out to BWI very straightforward without a car. The town is also home to the [William P. Didusch Center for Urologic History](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/linthicum/didusch_center), which is exactly what it sounds like, and to the Friendship Annex, an NSA facility you cannot visit but can drive past.
