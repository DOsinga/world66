A small museum on the grounds of the American Urological Association's headquarters, dedicated to the history of urology — the surgical instruments, the textbooks, the diagnostic devices, the early X-ray plates. It is genuinely interesting if you have any patience for the history of medicine, and it is free.

Hours are weekday business hours by appointment. Call ahead. You will not see many other visitors.
