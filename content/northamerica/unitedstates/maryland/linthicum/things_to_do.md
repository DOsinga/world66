Linthicum is small. The two historical sights are the [Benson–Hammond House](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/linthicum/benson_hammond_house) farmhouse-museum and the [Linthicum Heights Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/linthicum/linthicum_heights_historic_district). For more specialised tastes, the [William P. Didusch Center for Urologic History](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/linthicum/didusch_center) is one of the more unusual museums in the Baltimore area.
