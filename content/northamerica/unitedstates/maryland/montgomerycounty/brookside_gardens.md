Brookside Gardens is a large public garden in Wheaton Regional Park, with formal gardens, ponds, wooded paths, and seasonal displays. In summer the butterfly pavilion is the main draw, especially for children.

It is a good slow stop in Montgomery County: come for a walk, bring a picnic, and leave time to wander rather than treating it like a quick photo stop.
