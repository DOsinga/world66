Homestead Farm is a seasonal family farm in northern Montgomery County, best known for pick-your-own fruit, pumpkins, Christmas trees, and a small farm market. It is the sort of low-key rural stop that works best with children or if you are already driving through the county.

The farm animal area is the main extra attraction for younger visitors. Check the crop calendar before going, since the appeal depends heavily on what is in season.
