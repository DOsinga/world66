Rockville is the county seat of Montgomery County, Maryland, just north of Washington, D.C. It is primarily a suburban city, but the historic town centre has some character — the area around the courthouse and along East Montgomery Avenue has been revitalised with restaurants and shops centred on [Rockville Town Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/montgomerycounty/rockville/rockville_town_square).

The most notable landmark is [St. Mary's Catholic Church](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/montgomerycounty/rockville/st_marys_church), where F. Scott Fitzgerald and his wife Zelda are buried. The small churchyard is easy to find and worth a quiet visit if you are a fan of the writer. The [Beall–Dawson House](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/montgomerycounty/rockville/beall_dawson_house), a Federal-style mansion from 1815, houses the county historical society and an unusual 19th-century doctor's office museum next door.

Rockville is well-served by the Metro (Red Line) and makes a practical base for visiting Washington if you prefer staying outside the city. The Korean restaurant scene along Veirs Mill Road is one of the better in the Washington suburbs.
