The Carl Henn Millennium Trail is a paved loop that ties together Rockville neighborhoods, parks, and civic areas. It is more local infrastructure than wilderness, but it gives active visitors a useful way to see the city beyond Town Square.

It works for cycling, running, or a long walk broken into sections. Pay attention at road crossings and choose a segment if you do not want to commit to the full loop.
