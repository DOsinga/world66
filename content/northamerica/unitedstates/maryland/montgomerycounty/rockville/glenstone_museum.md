Glenstone Museum combines contemporary art, large-scale sculpture, architecture, and carefully designed grounds. The museum is as much about moving through the landscape as it is about individual works, with galleries and outdoor pieces spread across a quiet campus.

Reservations are usually needed, and visitors should plan ahead rather than treating it as a quick drop-in stop.
