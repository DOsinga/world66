Go Ape in Rockville is a commercial adventure course, but its setting in regional parkland makes it a legitimate active outing rather than just another suburban business. The draw is straightforward: rope crossings, platforms, and zip lines through the trees, with enough structure to suit families, groups, and nervous first-timers.

It is best for travellers who want to do something physical between Washington-area museums and historic sites. Book ahead in busy seasons, wear clothes that can handle harnesses and bark dust, and treat it as a half-day activity.
