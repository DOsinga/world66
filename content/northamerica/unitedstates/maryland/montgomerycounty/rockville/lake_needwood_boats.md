Lake Needwood Boats is the seasonal boating operation on Lake Needwood, part of Rock Creek Regional Park north of Rockville. Pedal boats, kayaks, and rowboats make the lake an easy family-friendly outing rather than a demanding outdoor activity.

The boats are best treated as one part of a park visit. Combine them with lakeside paths, picnic areas, or nearby nature trails, and check seasonal opening times before making the trip.
