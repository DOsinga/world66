The LDS Mormon Temple Visitor Center is the public-facing part of the Washington D.C. Temple complex near Kensington. The white temple is one of the most visible landmarks in the Maryland suburbs, rising above the Beltway with six sharp spires.

The temple itself is not open to casual visitors, but the visitor center and grounds are. It is most worthwhile for the architecture, the landscaped setting, and the chance to see a building many travellers only glimpse from the highway.
