Meadowside Nature Center is a family-friendly gateway to Rock Creek Regional Park, north of central Rockville. Exhibits, short trails, and naturalist programming make it an easy place to understand the local woods, streams, and wildlife before going farther into the park.

It is strongest for families with children or travelers who want a gentle outdoor break rather than a strenuous hike. Pair it with nearby park trails, and check program schedules if you are hoping for raptor talks or guided activities.
