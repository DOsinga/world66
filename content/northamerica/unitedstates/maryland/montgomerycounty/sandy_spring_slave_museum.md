Sandy Spring Slave Museum is a small museum focused on African American history, from West African heritage and the Middle Passage to the Underground Railroad, civil rights, and Black achievement in the United States.

It is a modest, community-rooted museum rather than a large institution, but that is part of its value. Check opening hours before making the trip, since small museums in the county can keep limited schedules.
