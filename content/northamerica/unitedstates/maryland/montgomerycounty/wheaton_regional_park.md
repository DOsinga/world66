Wheaton Regional Park is a large Montgomery County park with enough variety to fill a relaxed family outing: trails, picnic areas, a lake, a nature centre, gardens, a carousel, and a miniature train. It is most useful if you want green space without leaving the Washington suburbs for a full countryside trip.
