Trimper's Rides near the inlet is a remnant of the old-fashioned American amusement park, family-run since the late 19th century and still occupying its original location at the south end of the boardwalk. The outdoor section has the expected rides — spinning teacups, miniature roller coasters, a Ferris wheel. Inside the main building, the 1902 carousel is the real attraction, a hand-carved antique with a Wurlitzer organ, officially a National Historic Landmark.

Trimper's survives because it's genuinely beloved — local families who rode the carousel as children bring their own kids. It's not flashy, but that's the point. Open in season (roughly Memorial Day through Labor Day); the carousel operates year-round on weekends.
