The Bay Wolf is a small, dark, wood-panelled bar on Main Street with a long wooden bar, a few tables, and a respectable beer list. It is the sort of place where you can have a quiet drink with someone, listen to the regulars argue about the oyster harvest, and eat a perfectly good crab cake sandwich.

The food menu is short but reliable. It is open most nights. Quiet midweek; livelier on summer weekends when boaters tie up nearby.
