*North America > United States > Maryland > Solomons*

Solomons sits at the mouth of the Patuxent River, where Southern Maryland leans into the Chesapeake Bay. It is small, waterside, and busy with boats, but it is more than a restaurant strip: the town has one of Maryland's best regional marine museums and enough public waterfront to make a spare day feel well used.

The [Calvert Marine Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/solomons/calvert_marine_museum) is the anchor, with Chesapeake workboats, fossils from the Calvert Cliffs, aquariums, and the relocated [Drum Point Lighthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/solomons/drum_point_lighthouse). On the island side, the [Solomons boardwalk](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/solomons/solomons_boardwalk) and fishing pier give you the slow version of the place: river views, gulls, bridge traffic, and boats working in and out of Back Creek.

Add [Annmarie Sculpture Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/solomons/annmarie_sculpture_garden) in nearby Dowell if you want art and shade, or use Solomons as a base for Calvert Cliffs and St. Mary's County. It is strongest as a day trip from Washington, Baltimore, or Annapolis when you want Chesapeake history without a long Eastern Shore drive.
