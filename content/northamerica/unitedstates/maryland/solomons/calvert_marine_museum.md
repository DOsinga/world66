Calvert Marine Museum is the reason Solomons clears the day-trip test. It brings together three Southern Maryland stories: Calvert Cliffs fossils, the Patuxent and Chesapeake estuary, and the workboat culture that shaped the region.

The exhibits are practical and family-friendly without feeling shallow. Leave time for the outdoor pieces too, especially the boat basin and the Drum Point Lighthouse on the museum grounds.
