Patuxent River Naval Air Museum sits outside the Navy's main aircraft test base and is open to visitors without entering the base. The collection focuses on naval aviation and test aircraft, with jet-age Navy planes, experimental aircraft, and exhibits on the role of Pax River in aircraft development.
