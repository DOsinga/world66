Trinity Episcopal Church is a quiet historic stop near the museum area. The present church dates from the 19th century, but it reused brick from earlier colonial buildings in St. Mary's City.

It is worth a short look if you are walking the historic area. The church connects the museum landscape with a living parish, which keeps the place from feeling like a closed archaeological park.
