---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

St. Michaels is about 75 miles from Washington, D.C. and [Baltimore](/northamerica/unitedstates/maryland/baltimore), and there is no direct public transport. A car is essentially required. From Baltimore, take US-50 east across the Bay Bridge and follow Route 33 west onto the peninsula.

The nearest airport is BWI (Baltimore/Washington International), about 90 miles away. Annapolis is the closest city, about 45 miles away.
