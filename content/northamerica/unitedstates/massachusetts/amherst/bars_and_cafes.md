---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Amherst's bar and café scene is firmly student-flavoured but rewards a sober afternoon as much as a Friday night. **Amherst Coffee** on Main Street is the serious-coffee spot — and after dark turns into a respectable cocktail bar with a quiet back room. **The Black Sheep**, around the corner, is the long-running independent café with good sandwiches and a community noticeboard that tells you what is on this week.

For pints, **High Horse** and the **Moan and Dove** in South Amherst are both solid: the Moan and Dove in particular has an unusually deep beer list for a place this size. **Stackers** is more of a sports bar but does decent wings. The Amherst Brewing Company has moved a few times but its current incarnation is a reliable taproom for valley-brewed ales.
