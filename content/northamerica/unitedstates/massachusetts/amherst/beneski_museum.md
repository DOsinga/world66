A surprisingly grand three-storey natural history museum, free and open to the public on the Amherst College campus. The atrium is dominated by a complete mounted mammoth and a Cretaceous mosasaur skeleton, with a balcony of mounted skeletons running around above. The Hitchcock Ichnological Cabinet on the lower floor — Jurassic dinosaur footprints set into stone slabs — is the world-class collection.

The Rausch Mineral Gallery and the comparative anatomy displays are worth an hour each. Closed Mondays during the academic year; admission is free.
