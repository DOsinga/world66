The classic short day trip from Amherst is **Northampton**, eight miles west — the more bohemian of the Pioneer Valley college towns, with a denser restaurant scene and Smith College's Museum of Art. Boston is two hours east by car or bus and works as a long day trip; allow more time if you want to see anything beyond the Freedom Trail.
