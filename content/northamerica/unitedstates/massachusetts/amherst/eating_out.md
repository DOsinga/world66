---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Amherst eats like a college town — which in the Pioneer Valley means well, cheaply, and with a real commitment to local sourcing. The downtown blocks around Main and Pleasant Streets hold most of the better restaurants and almost all of the bars and coffee shops.

For a sit-down dinner, **Johnny's Tavern** does upmarket New England comfort food (the burgers and the seasonal fish dishes are reliably good); **Bistro 63** has a more modern menu. For lunch, **Antonio's Pizza** is the local institution — slices by the inch with a famously inventive rotation of toppings, often involving chicken parm or buffalo cauliflower. **High Horse** is a brewpub with solid food and a good local beer list.

In nearby Hadley, the **Esselon Café** is the best coffee in the valley, and the farm stands along Route 9 (Atkins Farms in particular) sell apple cider doughnuts that are worth a detour in autumn.
