---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Late September through mid-October is the best time to visit Amherst. The Pioneer Valley's autumn foliage is among the finest in New England — the surrounding hills turn orange, gold, and red in succession over about three weeks — and the temperatures are still mild enough to walk the rail trail and the campuses comfortably.

May and early June are the next best time: lilacs in bloom on Main Street, Dickinson's garden at its most photogenic, and the students still in residence. The **Emily Dickinson Festival** runs in August and brings readings, scholarly talks, and themed walks.

Winter is genuinely cold — snow on the ground from late December through March is normal — but the campuses are pretty under snow and the museums are uncrowded. Summer is hot, humid, and quiet, since the students are away; aim for early or late summer if you come then.
