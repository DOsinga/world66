North Shore Music Theatre is Beverly's big regional stage, a theatre-in-the-round set on a wooded campus north of the centre. It is best known for large-scale musicals, concerts, and family shows, with a production quality that draws audiences from across the North Shore.

Check the calendar before making the trip, since the theatre is only worth a stop when something is on. It pairs well with Beverly's smaller historic theatres if you are planning an evening around live performance.
