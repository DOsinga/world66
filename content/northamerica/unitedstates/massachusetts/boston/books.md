Boston has produced more than its share of American writing. Henry James, Henry Adams, William Dean Howells, Robert Lowell, and Sylvia Plath all wrote about the city's particular weight of class, history, and self-regard. More recently, Dennis Lehane has done for working-class Dorchester and South Boston what Hammett did for San Francisco — given it a sharp, durable literary voice. The novels below are the ones to read before you arrive.
