The Boston Athenaeum is one of the oldest independent libraries in the United States, founded in 1807. Its Beacon Hill building preserves a long tradition of private libraries as cultural institutions.

Visitors come for the architecture, exhibitions, and the sense of Boston's literary history. It is a quieter alternative to the city's larger museums and historic sites.
