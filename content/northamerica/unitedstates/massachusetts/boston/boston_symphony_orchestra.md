The Boston Symphony Orchestra performs in Symphony Hall, a 1900 concert hall famous for its acoustics and formal setting. Classical concerts are the main draw, while the Boston Pops offers a lighter program for visitors who want the hall and orchestra experience in a more relaxed form.
