Charlestown Navy Yard is a major stop on Boston's Freedom Trail and part of Boston National Historical Park. The shipyard was established more than 200 years ago, and today its docks, museum exhibits, visitor center, USS Constitution, and USS Cassin Young make it one of the strongest historic waterfront walks in the city.
