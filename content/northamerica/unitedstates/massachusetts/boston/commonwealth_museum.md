The Commonwealth Museum is the public museum of the Massachusetts Archives on Columbia Point. Its exhibits turn state records into a compact story of Massachusetts history, including colonial charters, Revolutionary-era documents, and material connected with figures such as Paul Revere and John Hancock.

It is a small, often overlooked stop, but it makes sense if you are already visiting the nearby John F. Kennedy Presidential Library and Museum and want a deeper look at the state's documentary history.
