John F. Kennedy National Historic Site preserves the Brookline birthplace and early childhood home of the 35th president of the United States. The house presents the Kennedy family's life in 1917, with rooms arranged to evoke the period.

It is a focused historic-house visit, most rewarding for travellers interested in presidential history, the Kennedy family, or early 20th-century domestic life around Boston.
