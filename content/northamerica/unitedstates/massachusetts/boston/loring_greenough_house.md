Loring-Greenough House is a mid-Georgian mansion built in 1760 as a country residence and farmstead for a British naval officer. The house preserves colonial-era rooms and historical collections, while the grounds have lawns, old trees, and flower beds that often host markets and community events in warmer months. Call ahead if you want to tour the interior.
