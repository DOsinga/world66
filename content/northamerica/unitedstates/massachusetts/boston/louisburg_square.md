Louisburg Square is a private Beacon Hill square lined with some of Boston's best-preserved Federal-style townhouses. The fenced garden is not open to the public, but the surrounding streets show the neighbourhood at its most polished.

It is a short architectural stop, best combined with Acorn Street, Charles Street, Boston Common, and the Black Heritage Trail.
