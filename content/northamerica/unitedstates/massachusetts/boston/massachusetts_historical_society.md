The Massachusetts Historical Society is the oldest historical society in the United States, with exhibitions drawn from a deep archive of early American papers, maps, portraits, and printed material. Its best-known holdings include Thomas Jefferson's handwritten copy of the Declaration of Independence.

This is a selective stop rather than a general Boston must-see, but it rewards visitors who like primary sources and quiet museums. Check the current exhibition schedule before making a special trip.
