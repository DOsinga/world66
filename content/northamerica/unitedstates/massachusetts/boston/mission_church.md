The Mission Church, formally the Basilica of Our Lady of Perpetual Help, is a Romanesque church built from Roxbury puddingstone in the late 19th century. Its stained glass, pilgrimage shrine, and role in Boston Catholic history make it worth a deliberate stop for visitors interested in architecture or local history.
