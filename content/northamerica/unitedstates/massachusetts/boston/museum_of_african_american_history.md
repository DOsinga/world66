Boston's Museum of African American History is the best starting point for the Black Heritage Trail on Beacon Hill. Its sites and exhibits focus on free Black communities, abolitionism, education, and church life in colonial and 19th-century Boston.

This is not a large museum, but it adds essential context to a city often told only through the Revolution and the Freedom Trail. Pair it with a walk through the north slope of Beacon Hill, where several trail stops are close together.
