New England Conservatory is one of the oldest independent music schools in the United States, and its Jordan Hall is the reason travellers should pay attention. The hall is a National Historic Landmark, loved for its warm acoustics and handsome old-world interior.

Check the calendar before you go. Student and faculty concerts can be excellent, and they give you a chance to hear the room doing what it was built to do.
