Boston's Old City Hall is a handsome Second Empire building from 1865, and a sharp contrast to the brutalist city hall that replaced it. It served as the seat of city government until 1969 and now holds offices and restaurants. Even if you only pass by, the facade is worth a stop on a walk between King's Chapel, the Old State House, and the Common.
