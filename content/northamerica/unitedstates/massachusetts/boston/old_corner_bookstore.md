The Old Corner Bookstore is a small but important Freedom Trail landmark. Built in the early 18th century, it later became a literary centre where major 19th-century American writers worked with the publisher Ticknor and Fields.

The interior no longer matches that history, but the preserved building is still a worthwhile pause between the Old South Meeting House and other central Boston sites.
