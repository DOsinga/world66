Old South Church is one of Back Bay's major High Victorian Gothic landmarks. Its patterned stonework, stained glass, and richly detailed interior make it a strong architectural stop near Copley Square.

The congregation's history reaches back to colonial Boston, though this building belongs to the 19th-century Back Bay expansion. It pairs naturally with nearby Trinity Church and the Boston Public Library.
