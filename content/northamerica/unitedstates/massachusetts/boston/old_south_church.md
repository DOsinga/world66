Old South Church is one of Back Bay's great Gothic Revival landmarks, with patterned stonework, stained glass, carved wood, and a richly restored interior. The congregation is older than the building and has links to figures such as Samuel Adams, Benjamin Franklin, and William Dawes.

It pairs naturally with nearby Trinity Church and the Boston Public Library, making Copley Square one of Boston's best short architecture walks.
