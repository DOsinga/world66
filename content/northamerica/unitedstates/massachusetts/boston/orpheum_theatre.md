Boston's Orpheum Theatre dates back to 1852, making it one of the oldest theatre buildings in the United States. It was once home to the Boston Symphony Orchestra and remains a working performance venue, so the best way to see it is to catch a show and look past the later rebuilds to the building's long musical history.
