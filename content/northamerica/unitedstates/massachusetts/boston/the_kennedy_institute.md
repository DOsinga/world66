The Kennedy Institute is an interactive political museum focused on Senator Edward M. Kennedy and the United States Senate. Exhibits use the Senate chamber, digital displays, and lawmaking simulations to explain how American legislation works.

It sits near the John F. Kennedy Presidential Library and Museum, so the two can be paired as a longer Columbia Point visit.
