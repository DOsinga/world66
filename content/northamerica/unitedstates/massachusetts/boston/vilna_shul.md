The Vilna Shul is a historic synagogue building on Beacon Hill, now used as a Jewish cultural center. It preserves part of Boston's immigrant Jewish history in a neighbourhood better known for Federal houses and state institutions.

Check the current schedule before going, as visits are tied to programs, tours, services, and events rather than regular museum hours.
