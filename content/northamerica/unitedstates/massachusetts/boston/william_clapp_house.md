William Clapp House is an 1806 Dorchester house run by the Dorchester Historical Society. It tells a local story of ship captains, orchards, and the Clapp family, whose pear-growing helped make the neighborhood prosperous.

Public opening times are limited, often tied to monthly tours, so check ahead. If it is open, it gives a quieter neighborhood counterpoint to Boston's central historic sites.
