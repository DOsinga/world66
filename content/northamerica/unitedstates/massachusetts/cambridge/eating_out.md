---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Cambridge has excellent, varied food — better than Boston's in many people's opinion. Central Square and Inman Square have the highest concentration of good restaurants, covering Ethiopian, South Asian, Middle Eastern, Japanese, and a strong Boston-area Italian tradition. Harvard Square has more tourist-oriented options but also some long-established standbys.

**Oleana** on Hampshire Street in Inman Square is one of the best restaurants in greater Boston — chef Ana Sortun's interpretation of eastern Mediterranean cooking is creative and deeply good. The lamb dishes and the mezze are the place to start. Book ahead.

**Mamaleh's** in Kendall Square is a Jewish deli done properly — pastrami on rye, matzoh ball soup, egg creams, and rugelach. It fills up at lunch and is good at any time of day.

**Life Alive Organic Café** on Massachusetts Avenue has been a Central Square staple for years — grain bowls, fresh juices, and vegetarian-focused food that is actually flavourful rather than virtuous.

**Harvest** on Brattle Street in Harvard Square is the oldest upscale restaurant in Cambridge, with a New England seasonal menu and a reliable kitchen. The outdoor courtyard is one of the better al fresco dining spots in the city.
