The Harvard Museum of the Ancient Near East holds more than 40,000 objects from Egypt, Mesopotamia, the Levant, and nearby ancient cultures. The collection is strong on archaeology, writing, daily life, and religion rather than blockbuster art.

It pairs well with the Harvard Art Museums and the Harvard Museum of Natural History, both close by. For visitors interested in the ancient world, it is one of Cambridge's most rewarding smaller museums.
