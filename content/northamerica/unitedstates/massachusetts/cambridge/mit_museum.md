The MIT Museum mixes science, engineering, art, and student culture in a way that feels very Cambridge. Exhibits cover holography, kinetic sculpture, robotics, and the famous MIT "hacks" that have put police cars, fire trucks, and other unlikely objects in unlikely places.

It is a strong stop for families because many displays are hands-on, but adults who like design, invention, and clever practical jokes will also find plenty to enjoy.
