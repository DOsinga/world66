Temple Beth Shalom, often called the Tremont Street Shul, is a small but significant Jewish landmark in Cambridge. It reflects the immigrant and neighbourhood history that sits behind the city's better-known university face.

This is not a big-ticket sight, and visitors should be respectful of services and community use. It is most worthwhile as part of a walk through Cambridgeport and Central Square, where religious buildings, old workers' housing, and new development sit close together.
