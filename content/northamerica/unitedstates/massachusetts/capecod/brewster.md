Brewster is one of the calmer Route 6A towns on [Cape Cod](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod). It does not have Provincetown's theatre or Chatham's polish, but it has a good Cape mix: bay-side light, old houses, galleries, kettle ponds, and bike paths through pine woods.

The strongest reason to stop is the landscape around [Nickerson State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod/brewster/nickerson_state_park) and the [Cape Cod Museum of Natural History](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod/brewster/cape_cod_museum_of_natural_history). Add the [Stony Brook Grist Mill](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod/brewster/stony_brook_grist_mill) in herring season and Brewster becomes a good low-key day, especially by bike.

Brewster is not a place for a checklist. It is for travellers who like the quieter Cape: ponds, marshes, old windmills, and enough local history to slow the drive between bigger towns.
