The Cape Cod Rail Trail is one of Brewster's practical pleasures. The paved route follows an old railroad bed through several Cape towns and links directly with Nickerson State Park.

For visitors, it turns Brewster from a car stop into a much better day out. Rent a bike, ride through the pine woods, and use the trail to connect the town's scattered sights.
