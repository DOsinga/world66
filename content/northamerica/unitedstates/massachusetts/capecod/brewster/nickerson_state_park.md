Nickerson State Park protects a wide stretch of pine woods and kettle ponds on the Brewster side of the Cape. Trails, park roads, and swimming ponds make it one of the town's best outdoor anchors.

The park also connects to the Cape Cod Rail Trail, so it works well by bike. Come for a ride, a pond swim, or a quieter day away from the busier ocean beaches.
