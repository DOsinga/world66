Windmill Village is a small historical cluster beside Drummer Boy Park, with the Higgins Farm Windmill and Harris Black House. The setting is simple: open meadow, gazebo, playground, and the windmill at one end.

It works best as part of a slow Brewster loop with Stony Brook Grist Mill rather than as a major destination. That is part of its charm.
