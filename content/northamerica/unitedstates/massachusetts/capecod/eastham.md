Eastham is an Outer Cape town where [Cape Cod](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod) narrows to a strip between the Atlantic and Cape Cod Bay. It is quieter than Provincetown and less polished than Chatham, but it has some of the Cape's best access to dunes, marshes, bike trails, and big ocean beaches.

The Atlantic side is protected by Cape Cod National Seashore. [Coast Guard Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod/eastham/coast_guard_beach) and [Nauset Light](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod/eastham/nauset_light) are the obvious anchors, with surf, seals offshore, shifting dunes, and the red-and-white lighthouse that has become a Cape icon. Inland, the [Salt Pond Visitor Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod/eastham/salt_pond_visitor_center) is the best starting point for understanding the seashore.

Eastham also has a softer bay-side story. [First Encounter Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod/eastham/first_encounter_beach) faces sunset and remembers the 1620 meeting between the Pilgrims and the Nauset people. Around [Fort Hill](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod/eastham/fort_hill), trails look over salt marsh, old fields, and the curve of Nauset Marsh.
