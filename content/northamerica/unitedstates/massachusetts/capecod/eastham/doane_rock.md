Doane Rock is the largest exposed boulder on Cape Cod, left behind by glaciers around 15,000 years ago. It is an easy short stop near the Cape Cod National Seashore trails and beaches, especially for anyone interested in the glacial landscape beneath the cape's dunes and ponds.
