Fort Hill is one of Eastham's best short walks. The trail crosses old fields and dips toward marshland, with broad views over Nauset Marsh, barrier beach, and the low Outer Cape landscape.

It is a good change of pace from the surf beaches, especially in the morning or near sunset. In summer, insects can be fierce in the marshy sections, so bring repellent and avoid treating this as a breezy beach stroll.
