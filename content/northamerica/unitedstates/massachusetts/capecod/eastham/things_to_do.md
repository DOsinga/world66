---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Eastham's strongest sights belong to Cape Cod National Seashore, but they cluster closely enough around town to make a real Outer Cape day. Start at the [Salt Pond Visitor Center](salt_pond_visitor_center), then continue to [Coast Guard Beach](coast_guard_beach) or [Nauset Light](nauset_light) depending on weather and parking.

For a slower visit, walk [Fort Hill](fort_hill) for marsh views and old Cape farm scenery, then cross to [First Encounter Beach](first_encounter_beach) for sunset over Cape Cod Bay. The Cape Cod Rail Trail runs through town, so Eastham also works well by bicycle once you are on the Outer Cape.
