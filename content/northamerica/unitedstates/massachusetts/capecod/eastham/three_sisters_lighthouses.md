The Three Sisters Lighthouses are a group of restored wooden light towers in Eastham. The first brick towers were built in 1836 and lost to erosion; later wooden replacements were designed to be moved as the shoreline changed.

They are close to Nauset Light and the Cape Cod National Seashore sights, making them an easy add-on for anyone following the outer Cape's lighthouse history.
