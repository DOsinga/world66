Brewster's Woods Wildlife Sanctuary protects a quiet patch of Concord landscape associated with ornithologist William Brewster. Woods, river edges, and meadow-like openings make it a gentle place to walk, watch birds, and step away from the town's more literary historic houses.

Its appeal is subtle rather than monumental. Bring binoculars or simply go for the change in pace; the sanctuary works best for travellers who appreciate nature woven into cultural history.
