Codman Estate lies just outside Concord in Lincoln, but it belongs to the same world of old New England houses, reformers, and country landscapes. The property preserves a furnished family home, carriage house, gardens, and grounds shaped by generations of the Codman family.

It is quieter than Concord's literary heavy hitters, which is part of the appeal. Come if you enjoy historic interiors and designed landscapes, or if you want to balance Walden, the Old North Bridge, and the authors' houses with a less crowded estate visit.
