---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Concord is 20 miles west of Boston, easily reached by commuter rail on the MBTA Fitchburg Line from North Station (about 45 minutes). The station is in the centre of town, within walking distance of most attractions. By car, take Route 2 west from Cambridge; the drive takes 45 minutes to an hour depending on traffic. Parking in the town centre is generally available.
