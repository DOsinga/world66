Great Meadows National Wildlife Refuge protects marshes and river floodplain along the Concord and Sudbury Rivers. For Concord visitors, it offers a nature break that still feels connected to the town's literary and Revolutionary landscape.

Bring binoculars if you have them: birds, turtles, and seasonal wetland life are the main draw. Trails can be muddy or buggy, so dress for marsh conditions rather than a manicured town walk.
