Hapgood Wright Town Forest is a local conservation area north of Concord center, with wooded trails, ponds, wetlands, and glacial features. It offers a quieter outdoor alternative to the more famous literary and Revolutionary sites around town.

The forest is best for a simple walk, birding, or a reset between house museums. It is not a destination of national importance, but it rounds out Concord well for visitors who want to see the town's everyday New England landscape.
