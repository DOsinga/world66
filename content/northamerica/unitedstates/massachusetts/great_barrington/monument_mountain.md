Monument Mountain is one of the classic short hikes in the southern Berkshires, with forest trails leading to rocky viewpoints over the Housatonic Valley. Its literary associations and accessible trail network make it more than just a workout.

Choose footwear for rocks and roots, and avoid exposed sections during storms or icy weather. The hike pairs well with Great Barrington's downtown, making it an easy nature-and-town day.
