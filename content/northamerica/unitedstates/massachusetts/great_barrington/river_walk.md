Great Barrington's River Walk is a short but thoughtful path along the Housatonic River, restored by
local volunteers from a neglected industrial edge into a place to walk, sit, and notice the water.
It is not long, but it gives the town a quieter dimension away from shops and restaurants.

Use it as a gentle pause while exploring downtown, especially in good weather. Interpretive signs
and river views make it more interesting than a simple footpath, and it connects well with Great
Barrington's small-town Berkshire rhythm.
