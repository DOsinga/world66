Ski Butternut is Great Barrington's practical winter playground, a compact Berkshire ski area with downhill runs, terrain parks, tubing, rentals, and lessons close enough to town for an easy half-day. It is not a high-mountain destination, but that is part of the appeal: the slopes are approachable, family-friendly, and less intimidating than larger New England resorts.

In winter it gives Great Barrington a clear cold-weather anchor; outside ski season the surrounding woods and hills still shape the town's outdoorsy feel. Check conditions before going, since natural snow can be irregular in the southern Berkshires and snowmaking schedules matter.
