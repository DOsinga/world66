---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Lowell is about 30 miles northwest of [Boston](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/boston) and is easily reached by **commuter rail** on the MBTA Lowell Line from North Station. Trains run roughly every hour and the journey takes about 50 minutes; this is the most practical option from Boston. Lowell station is a five-minute walk from the national historical park visitor centre.

**By car**, Lowell is on Route 3 and I-495. Parking is available near the national park and in several downtown garages; the city is not difficult to drive to or navigate. From Boston, the drive takes 45–60 minutes depending on traffic.

There is no direct **airport service** to Lowell. The nearest major airport is Logan International in Boston (about 35 miles via I-93 and I-495); Manchester-Boston Regional Airport in New Hampshire is about 30 miles north and occasionally offers cheaper fares.
