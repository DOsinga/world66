Old Burial Hill, dating from 1638, is one of the oldest cemeteries in New England and has some of the best-preserved colonial headstones in Massachusetts. The carved imagery — death's heads, hourglasses, weeping willows — follows the standard Puritan iconography, but several stones are outstanding in their quality. The Susanna Jayne headstone from 1776, near the centre of the cemetery, is particularly fine.

The hill itself offers another good view point over Marblehead Harbor, and the arrangement of tilted old stones against the sky has a stark beauty. Some graves commemorate men lost at sea; the stones rarely say how. At the top of the hill is the site of the town's original meetinghouse. Walk up from the centre via Redd's Pond.
