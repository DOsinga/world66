The dramatic cliffs at Aquinnah — until the 1990s known as Gay Head — are layered orange, ochre, grey, and rust-coloured clay that drop straight to the sea at the island's western tip. The brick **Gay Head Light** above them dates from 1856 and was moved 40 metres back from the edge in 2015 as coastal erosion threatened to take it down. It is the most photogenic spot on Martha's Vineyard.

There is a viewing platform at the top with the best overlook; a steep stairway leads down to Moshup Beach below. Climbing on the cliffs is forbidden and bad for the cliffs anyway. Combine the visit with the small souvenir shops and clam shacks at the Aquinnah Circle, which are mostly Wampanoag-run.
