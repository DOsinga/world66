Cape Poge Lighthouse stands at the remote northern end of Chappaquiddick, overlooking Cape Poge Bay and the outer beach. The setting is the point: dunes, marsh, and open water make it feel far from Edgartown despite being on the same island.

Access is usually by four-wheel-drive beach route or guided tour, so check current conditions before setting out.
