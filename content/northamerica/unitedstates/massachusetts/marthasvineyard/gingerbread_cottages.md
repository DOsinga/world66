A few minutes' walk from the centre of Oak Bluffs, the Methodist campground of Wesleyan Grove is one of the loveliest pieces of nineteenth-century townscape in America. Around 300 small wooden cottages in pastel colours — pink, mint, butter yellow, lavender — surround a central iron and wood **Tabernacle** built in 1879.

The cottages are private homes still owned by Methodist families, many for generations, but the streets are public and walkers are welcome. The **Illumination** in mid-August, when the cottages are lit with paper lanterns and the residents open their porches to the public, is a Vineyard institution.
