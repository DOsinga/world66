Cisco Brewers operates from a compound on Bartlett Farm Road in the south of the island that functions as brewery, distillery, winery, and outdoor concert venue all at once. The beer garden fills up on summer afternoons with a crowd that crosses all demographic lines — families, surfers, day-trippers, and longtime island regulars. Live music plays on summer weekends.

The brewery produces year-round and seasonal beers, a range of spirits under the Triple Eight Distillery label, and wine from the island's sandy soil. The Whale's Tale Pale Ale is their most popular beer and widely sold. Tours of the brewing and distilling operations run daily in summer. Getting there from town means either a taxi, a bike ride (the bike path ends about a mile away), or renting a car.
