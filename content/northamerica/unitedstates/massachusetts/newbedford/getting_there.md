---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

New Bedford is about 60 miles south of [Boston](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/boston) and 20 miles east of Providence, Rhode Island. The MBTA Commuter Rail runs from South Station in Boston to New Bedford on the Middleborough/Lakeville line, with a journey time of about 75 minutes. By car, take I-93 south to Route 24 south to Route 140 south into downtown New Bedford; the drive from Boston takes an hour to 90 minutes depending on traffic. Parking in the historic district is metered; free parking is available at various lots a short walk from the waterfront.

Peter Pan Bus Lines runs services to New Bedford from Boston's South Station and Providence. The city has a regional bus system but no rail connection to [Cape Cod](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/capecod) or the islands; most visitors use a car for exploring the surrounding area.
