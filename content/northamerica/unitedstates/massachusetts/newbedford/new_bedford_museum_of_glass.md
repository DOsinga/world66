The New Bedford Museum of Glass focuses on antique and art glass, with material ranging from ancient Mediterranean pieces to Victorian New England glass and contemporary studio work. It is a specialist stop, but a good one for visitors interested in the city's Pairpoint and Mount Washington glassmaking history.
