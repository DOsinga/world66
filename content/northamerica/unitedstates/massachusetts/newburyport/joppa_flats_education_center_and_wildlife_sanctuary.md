Joppa Flats Education Center sits on the approach to Plum Island, exactly where Newburyport’s maritime townscape gives way to salt marsh, tidal flats, and big coastal sky. It is one of the best local starting points for understanding the birds and habitats of the Parker River area.

The center is especially useful for birders, with programs, sightings information, and access to the wider refuge landscape. Even casual visitors benefit from stopping here before heading to Plum Island, because the exhibits and staff help turn a pretty marsh view into a living system of tides, migration, and shorebird habitat.
