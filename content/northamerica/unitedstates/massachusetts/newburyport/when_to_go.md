---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The peak season is **May through October**, when the boardwalk is busy, the river cruises and whale-watch boats are running out of the harbour, and the Plum Island beach is open. **September and early October** are the best weeks: warm days, cool nights, autumn colour in the trees lining High Street, and the September hawk migration over Parker River.

Winter is genuinely cold — the river ices up against the docks in January — but the town stays open, the restaurants thin out pleasantly, and the snowy owls arrive on Plum Island. The **Yankee Homecoming** festival in early August is the city's big week; expect crowds and book restaurants ahead.
