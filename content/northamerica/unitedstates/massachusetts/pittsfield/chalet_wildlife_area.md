Chalet Wildlife Area protects nearly 8,000 acres of forest across the hills north of Pittsfield. It is open for hiking, wildlife watching, fishing, and seasonal hunting, with hiking-only areas where hunting is not allowed.

The appeal is simple: a large, quiet stretch of Berkshire woodland rather than a built attraction. Check current rules and seasons before heading deep into the area.
