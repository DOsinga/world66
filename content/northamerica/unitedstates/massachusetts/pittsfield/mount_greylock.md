Mount Greylock is the highest summit in Massachusetts, rising above the northern Berkshires with long views from the top. You can drive the seasonal auto roads or hike up on trails including the Appalachian Trail; the summit has the Veterans War Memorial Tower and the rustic Bascom Lodge.
