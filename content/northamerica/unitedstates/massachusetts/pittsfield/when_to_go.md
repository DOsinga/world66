---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Summer is festival season in the Berkshires, with theatre, music, and museum programming in full swing. Autumn is the showiest season, when the hills around Pittsfield turn red, orange, and gold and every country road suddenly looks expensive.

Winter is quieter but not dead: nearby ski areas and indoor cultural events keep the region moving. Spring can be muddy in the woods but pleasant downtown.
