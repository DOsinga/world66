Brewster Gardens follows Town Brook through a landscaped pocket of central Plymouth, close to the waterfront and the town’s heavy concentration of Pilgrim history. The brook mattered to the early English settlement, and the park gives that practical geography a pleasant modern setting.

It is a short walk, not a major attraction, but it links Plymouth’s monuments to the water and land that sustained the colony. Use it as a quiet route between the waterfront, Burial Hill, and the older streets rather than as a destination by itself.
