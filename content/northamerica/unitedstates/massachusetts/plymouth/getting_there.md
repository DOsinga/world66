---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

Plymouth is about 40 miles south of [Boston](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/boston), roughly an hour by car or commuter rail.

By train, the MBTA Plymouth/Kingston Commuter Rail line runs from South Station in Boston to Plymouth station, with trains running several times daily. The journey takes around 75 minutes and costs a few dollars on the commuter fare. Plymouth station is about a mile from the waterfront — a short taxi or rideshare ride.

By car, take Route 3 south from Boston (or I-93 south to Route 3). The drive is straightforward outside of rush hour. Parking in the downtown area is paid; the large lots near the waterfront fill early in summer.

There is no direct service from Logan Airport — take the Silver Line or the Blue Line to South Station and board the commuter rail from there.
