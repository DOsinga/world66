Holy Ghost Festa is North Plymouth's traditional Portuguese summer festival, built around a procession before and after morning Mass and an afternoon gathering at Holy Ghost Field. Food, auctions, folk dancing, and music make it more than a church event; it is a strong expression of the area's Portuguese-American community.

It is worth seeking out if your visit lines up with the date. Plymouth's headline history is English colonial, but this festival shows a very different local layer.
