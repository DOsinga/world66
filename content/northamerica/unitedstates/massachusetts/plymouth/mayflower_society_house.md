The Mayflower Society House is an 18th-century house museum near Plymouth's waterfront, run by the General Society of Mayflower Descendants. Tours focus on the house, Plymouth's early colonial history, and the society's large collection of Pilgrim genealogy material.

It is a good companion to Mayflower II, Plymouth Rock, and Burial Hill, especially for visitors tracing family history or looking for a quieter historic house stop.
