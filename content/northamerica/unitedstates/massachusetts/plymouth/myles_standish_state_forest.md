Myles Standish State Forest protects a broad stretch of southeastern Massachusetts pine barrens, a landscape of sandy soil, scrub oak, kettle ponds, and quiet forest roads. It is one of the best nature escapes near Plymouth, especially for travellers with a car who want more than the town's colonial history.

The forest is useful for hiking, cycling, paddling, fishing, and camping, with several ponds providing easy goals for a relaxed outing. It is spread out rather than concentrated, so pick a trailhead or pond before you go instead of expecting a single visitor hub.
