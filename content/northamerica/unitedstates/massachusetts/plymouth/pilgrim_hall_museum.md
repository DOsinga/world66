Pilgrim Hall Museum is the serious history stop in Plymouth, with early colonial objects, portraits, documents, and belongings connected to the Mayflower passengers and their descendants. It gives useful context before or after the waterfront sights.

The collection is not large, but it is unusually direct: these are the kinds of objects that make Plymouth's familiar story feel less abstract.
