White Horse Beach is a broad, residential Atlantic beach south of central Plymouth, popular with local families and summer renters. It has a more neighbourhood feel than the historic waterfront, with sand, surf and cottage streets replacing monuments and harbour cruises.

Access and parking can be the practical challenge, so check local rules before setting out. If you are staying nearby, it is one of the easier ways to turn a Plymouth trip into a beach day.
