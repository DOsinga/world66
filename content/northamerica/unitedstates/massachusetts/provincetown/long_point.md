Long Point is the far sandy hook curling around Provincetown Harbor, a place that feels much farther away than it looks on a map. The beaches, dunes, and lighthouse views give you the satisfying sense of standing at the loose end of Cape Cod.

Getting there takes effort: a long walk across sand, a seasonal shuttle, or a boat option depending on conditions and services. Bring water, sun protection, and a plan for the return. The reward is space, wind, and a quieter view back toward Provincetown's busy waterfront.
