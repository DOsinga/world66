Old Harbor Life-Saving Station is a restored 1897 coastal rescue station at Race Point. It was moved from Chatham to its current site in 1977 and furnished as it would have looked during its working life.

The station adds human history to a Cape Cod National Seashore visit, especially if you are already heading to Race Point Beach or Race Point Lighthouse. Seasonal opening times can vary.
