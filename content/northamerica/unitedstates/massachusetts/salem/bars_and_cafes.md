---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Salem has a lively bar scene, particularly in October when the city fills with visitors, but the year-round options are respectable for a city of its size. The **Derby Pub** on Derby Street is the straightforward local option — good draft selection, unpretentious. **Turner's** doubles as a bar in the evenings. **Bit Bar** on Washington Street is worth knowing about: it's an arcade bar with classic games that works surprisingly well as a late-evening option.

For coffee, **Jaho Coffee** on Derby Street is Salem's best independent roaster, with a comfortable room and good espresso. **Ledger Bar** already mentioned under eating is also a reliable cocktail stop. During Haunted Happenings in October, the scene migrates partly outdoors, and the area around the Pickering Wharf becomes very animated after dark.
