---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Salem is 25 miles north of Boston and extremely well-connected by public transport, which is by far the best way to arrive — driving into Salem in October is a serious ordeal. The **MBTA Newburyport/Rockport Line** runs commuter rail from Boston's North Station directly to Salem Depot, with trains roughly every 30–60 minutes; the journey takes about 30 minutes and is straightforward. The station is a 10-minute walk from the main tourist area.

By car, take Route 128 North to Route 114 East into downtown. Parking in October is expensive and difficult; the city operates a large garage off Riley Plaza, but it fills early on weekends. **Salem Ferry** from Rowes Wharf in Boston runs seasonally (May to October) and deposits passengers directly at Pickering Wharf — one of the more pleasant approaches if the weather cooperates.

**Logan Airport** is the nearest major hub, about 45 minutes to an hour from Salem depending on traffic. Boston's South Station does not serve Salem; you must use North Station.
