Punto Urban Art Museum is an outdoor mural district in Salem's Point neighborhood, created to spotlight Dominican, New England, and international artists. More than 75 large-scale murals cover walls around the neighborhood, turning an ordinary walk into a compact street-art route.

It is best treated as a self-guided neighborhood walk rather than a single building. Groups can book educational tours that connect the murals with Salem's immigrant history, public art, and local culture.
