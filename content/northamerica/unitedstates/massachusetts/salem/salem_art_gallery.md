Salem Art Gallery is connected with The Satanic Temple and presents exhibitions, installations and events in a city already shaped by witch-trial memory and alternative religious tourism. Its best-known image is the Baphomet sculpture, but the gallery also functions as a contemporary cultural space.

This is a niche stop and not for every Salem itinerary, yet it is clearly part of the city's modern occult-and-counterculture landscape. Visit if that context interests you, and check current access because hours and exhibitions can change.
