The Salem Witch Museum is one of the main visitor stops tied to the 1692 witch trials. Its presentation covers the trials themselves and the wider history of witchcraft imagery and belief.

It is more theatrical than scholarly, but it gives useful context before walking to the Witch Trials Memorial, the Old Burying Point, and the other compact sights of central Salem.
