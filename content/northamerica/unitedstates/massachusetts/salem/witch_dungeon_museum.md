The Witch Dungeon Museum presents Salem witch trial history through short reenactments and exhibits. It is smaller and more theatrical than Salem's major historic houses, but it can be a useful stop if you want more context around the 1692 trials.

Combination tickets are sometimes offered with nearby witch-history attractions, so check before buying separate admissions.
