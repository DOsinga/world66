Minot Beach is a small residential beach with enough rock features and tidal pools to keep it interesting. On clear days you can look out toward Minot Light and across Massachusetts Bay.

Like Scituate's other town beaches, parking is the catch in summer. If you are visiting without a local beach sticker, plan to walk, bike, or come outside the peak season.
