The Pioneer Valley north of Springfield is one of New England's more rewarding corners. [Northampton](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/northhampton) is a lively small city with good independent restaurants and an active arts scene, about 20 minutes north. [Amherst](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/amherst), home to Amherst College and UMass Amherst, has bookshops and cafes and is worth combining with Northampton in a single day trip. The Five Colleges area between the two towns — also including Hampshire, Mount Holyoke, and Smith — gives the whole valley a student energy that keeps it interesting year-round.
