title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Springfield has more to offer than its industrial reputation suggests. The Basketball Hall of Fame is the obvious draw for sports fans, but the city's museums are genuinely strong — the Springfield Armory tells a distinctive chapter of American history, and the Museum Quadrangle clusters four museums on State Street that easily fill half a day. The Dr. Seuss Sculpture Garden is unexpectedly moving.
