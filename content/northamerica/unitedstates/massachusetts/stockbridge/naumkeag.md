Naumkeag is the former summer estate of New York lawyer Joseph Hodges Choate, set on a hillside overlooking the Stockbridge Bowl and the Berkshire hills beyond. The house — a Queen Anne-style shingle cottage designed by Stanford White in 1886 — is handsome but it is the garden that people come to see.

Designed across several decades by landscape architect Fletcher Steele, the Naumkeag garden is one of the most inventive garden designs in the United States: part Arts and Crafts formalism, part playful modernism, part pure garden theatre. The famous Blue Steps cascade down through a birch grove; other sections include a Chinese garden, a rose garden, and a series of outdoor rooms that open unexpectedly onto each other. Now operated by the Trustees of Reservations, the house and garden are open from late spring through October.
