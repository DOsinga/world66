Leadmine Mountain Trails give Sturbridge a practical outdoor option beyond its historic village. The paths run through typical central Massachusetts woods, with rocky rises, wetlands, and traces of the old mining landscape that gave the area its name.

It is a good choice when you want a low-key walk before or after museum time, especially in fall. Trails can be muddy after rain, so wear shoes that can take a little abuse and bring a map or downloaded route.
