Wellfleet sits on the Outer Cape between ocean, harbor, salt marsh, and pine woods. It is quieter than [Provincetown](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/provincetown), but it has a strong enough identity of its own: oysters, galleries, kettle ponds, surf beaches, and a working harbor that still feels connected to the tides.

The town's best days are split between the Cape Cod National Seashore and the bay side. [Marconi Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/wellfleet/marconi_beach) gives you Atlantic dunes and surf, [Great Island Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/wellfleet/great_island_trail) gives you a long, sandy walk into marsh and bay country, and [Wellfleet Bay Wildlife Sanctuary](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/wellfleet/wellfleet_bay_wildlife_sanctuary) is one of the Cape's better nature stops.

Food matters here too. Wellfleet oysters are famous for good reason, and they give the town a taste memory as clear as its landscape. Come outside the most crowded summer weekends if you can; the Outer Cape is better when it has room to breathe.
