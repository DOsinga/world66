Atwood-Higgins preserves a quiet piece of old Cape Cod inside the national seashore: an 18th-century house, later outbuildings, and the open landscape around them. It is a modest stop rather than a headline sight, but it gives Wellfleet's dunes and beaches a bit of human history.
