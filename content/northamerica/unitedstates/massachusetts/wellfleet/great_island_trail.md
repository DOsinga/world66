Great Island Trail is one of Wellfleet's best walks, a sandy route through pitch pine, dunes, salt marsh, and bay shoreline. It is long enough to feel like a small expedition by Cape standards, especially in heat or soft sand.

Check tides, bring water, and do not underestimate the return walk. The reward is a wilder Wellfleet than you see from the harbor or beach parking lots.
