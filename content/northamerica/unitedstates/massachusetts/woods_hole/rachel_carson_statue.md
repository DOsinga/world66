A bronze statue of Rachel Carson stands near the WHOI campus, honouring the marine biologist and author who spent time in Woods Hole during the formative years of her career. Carson is best known for Silent Spring (1962), the book that launched the modern environmental movement, but her earlier work — The Sea Around Us and The Edge of the Sea — grew directly from her research in and around these waters.

The statue, by the sculptor Wendy Ross, depicts Carson seated with a notebook, looking out toward the harbour. It was installed in 2013. The surrounding area offers good views of the WHOI dock and the tidal passage between Great Harbor and Eel Pond.
