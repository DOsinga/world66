---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

Worcester is 45 miles west of [Boston](/northamerica/unitedstates/massachusetts/boston) and easy to reach by car, train, or bus.

**By car**, the city sits at the junction of the Massachusetts Turnpike (I-90), I-290, and I-190 — a one-hour drive from Boston, two and a half hours from New York, two hours from Hartford.

**By train**, the MBTA Commuter Rail runs from Boston's South Station to Worcester's Union Station, taking about 90 minutes; Amtrak's Lake Shore Limited stops here on its New York–Chicago route.

**By bus**, Peter Pan and Greyhound both run frequent buses to and from Boston and New York from Union Station.

**By air**, Worcester Regional Airport has a small handful of commercial flights, but most travellers fly into Boston Logan and continue by train or car.
