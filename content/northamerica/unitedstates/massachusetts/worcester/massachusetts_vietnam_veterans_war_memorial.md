The Massachusetts Vietnam Veterans' War Memorial is a contemplative landscaped memorial in Worcester, built around names, water, stone, and a sequence of spaces for reflection. It honors service members from across the state rather than only the city.

It is a sober stop, not a sightseeing spectacle. Pair it with Green Hill Park or other Worcester landmarks if you want to understand the citys quieter civic side.
