Salisbury Mansion is a compact but worthwhile house museum that preserves the domestic world of Worcester’s late-18th-century merchant class. Built for Stephen Salisbury, it has been moved, altered, and restored over time, which is part of the story: Worcester grew around houses like this, then nearly swallowed them.

The appeal is in the rooms and local detail rather than grand architecture. Go if you like early American interiors, city history, or small museums where a guide can explain how trade, family money, and civic power worked in one New England town. It combines well with Worcester Art Museum nearby.
