Tuckerman Hall is a refined early-20th-century concert hall and one of Worcester's better pieces of
civic architecture. Designed by Josephine Wright Chapman, it has a graceful exterior and an intimate
auditorium used for music, lectures, and private events.

It is most rewarding when you can attend a performance, but architecture-minded visitors can still
appreciate it as part of a walk around Worcester's museum district. Pair it with Mechanics Hall or
the Worcester Art Museum for a fuller sense of the city's cultural ambitions.
