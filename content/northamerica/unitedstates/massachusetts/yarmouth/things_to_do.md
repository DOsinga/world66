---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Yarmouth's highlights are split between its three villages. Yarmouthport on Route 6A is where you find the old Cape Cod — sea captains' houses, antique shops, the Edward Gorey House, and the Bass Hole Boardwalk. The south shore has the beaches and the Whydah Pirate Museum. The conservation trails through the wooded interior are worth seeking out for a quieter experience.
