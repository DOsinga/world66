The Argus Museum gives Ann Arbor a useful counterpoint to its university museums. It occupies part of the old Argus camera complex, where one of America's best-known mid-century camera brands made boxy, affordable cameras that travelled far beyond Michigan.

The collection is compact, but it has a clear point of view: cameras, company history, advertising, and factory context all sit together. It is best treated as a short stop for photographers, design-minded travellers, or anyone interested in the city's manufacturing past before the university became its dominant story.
