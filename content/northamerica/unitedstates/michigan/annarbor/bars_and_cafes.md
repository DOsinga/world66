---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Ann Arbor has the cafe and bar density of a city several times its size, thanks to the university population and a community that genuinely values its third places. The downtown coffee scene is strong — multiple serious roasters compete within walking distance. For drinking, the options range from divey college bars to craft cocktail spots, with a healthy live music scene running through it all. South Main Street and the blocks around South First Street are the main bar corridors.
