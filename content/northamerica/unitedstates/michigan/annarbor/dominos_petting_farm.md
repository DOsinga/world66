Domino's Petting Farm is a 15-acre family attraction on a former 1920s working farm near Ann Arbor. The barns and paddocks hold domestic animals from around the world, including donkeys, goats, cows, sheep, ponies, rabbits, and more unusual breeds.

It is aimed mostly at children, but the old farm setting gives it more character than a simple petting zoo. Tours, hay rides, and educational programmes are often part of the visit.
