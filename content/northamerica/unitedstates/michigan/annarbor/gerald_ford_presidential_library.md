The Gerald R. Ford Presidential Library preserves the papers and records from Ford's presidency, including millions of pages of documents, photographs, recordings, and film. Most archival material is for researchers by appointment, but visitors can see free lobby exhibits on Gerald and Betty Ford.

The library also screens a short introductory film and hosts public talks and evening programs, making it a worthwhile stop for anyone interested in modern American politics.
