Leslie Science and Nature Center is a practical nature stop on Ann Arbor's north side, especially for families. The grounds include short trails, outdoor learning areas, and resident birds of prey that make the visit more focused than a simple neighbourhood park walk.

It is best treated as a low-key complement to the University of Michigan museums and downtown. Check the program calendar if you are travelling with children, as guided events and seasonal activities can make the centre much more rewarding.
