Matthaei Botanical Gardens is the University of Michigan's big plant-and-landscape escape on the edge of Ann Arbor. The conservatory brings together tropical, temperate, and arid collections, while the outdoor gardens and trails move into native plantings, wetlands, and woods.

It is one of the best choices in Ann Arbor when you want something quieter than campus or downtown. Garden lovers can linger over collections and seasonal bloom, while casual visitors can treat it as a walk with better labels and more variety than a normal park.
