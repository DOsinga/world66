The Sindecuse Museum of Dentistry is one of Ann Arbor's more unusual small museums, tucked into the
University of Michigan's dental school. Displays of early tools, dental chairs, advertisements, and
inventions trace how dentistry moved from ordeal to profession.

It is niche, but that is part of the appeal. If you enjoy specialist university collections or
slightly odd museums, it makes a memorable short detour while walking around the central campus.
