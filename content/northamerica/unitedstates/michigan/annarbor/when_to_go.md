---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Ann Arbor has a continental climate with four distinct seasons. Summers are warm and pleasant, with highs in the low 80s F (27-30 C) and long days perfect for outdoor dining and walking. This is when the city is most relaxed — fewer students, more space, and the farmers' market at its peak.

Autumn is arguably the best time to visit. September through mid-November brings crisp weather, spectacular fall color in the Arb and around campus, and the energy of football season. Home game weekends transform the city — expect crowds, noise, and a charged atmosphere.

Winters are cold and gray, with temperatures regularly dropping below freezing from December through February. Snowfall is moderate but persistent. The upside is that indoor attractions — museums, bookshops, restaurants, music venues — are at their most appealing when it is miserable outside.

Spring arrives slowly, usually by late April. The **Ann Arbor Film Festival** in March and the **Ann Arbor Summer Festival** in June are the two major cultural events worth timing a visit around.
