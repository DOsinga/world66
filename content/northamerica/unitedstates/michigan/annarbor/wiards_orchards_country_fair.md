Wiard's Orchards and Country Fair is a seasonal apple orchard and cider mill southeast of Ann Arbor. In autumn it becomes a full family outing, with a corn maze, hay rides, pony rides, miniature golf, a petting farm, and a haunted barn.

It is more local tradition than essential sightseeing, but it works well if you are in the Ann Arbor area with children or want a cider-and-orchard afternoon outside town.
