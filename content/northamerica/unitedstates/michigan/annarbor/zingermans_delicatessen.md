The most famous food destination in Ann Arbor and arguably in all of Michigan. Zingerman's is a traditional Jewish deli that has grown into a nationally recognized food institution, known for towering sandwiches made with excellent bread and carefully sourced ingredients. The corned beef, pastrami, and Reuben are the signatures, but the menu is long and the quality is consistent across it.

Expect a line, especially at lunch on weekends. Prices are higher than a typical deli — a sandwich runs $18-25 — but the portions are large and the ingredients justify the cost. The shop also sells a curated selection of cheeses, oils, vinegars, and baked goods that make good gifts or picnic supplies. Located in the Kerrytown neighborhood at the corner of Detroit Street and Kingsley Street.
