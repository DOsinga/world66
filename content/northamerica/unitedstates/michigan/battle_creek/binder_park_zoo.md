Binder Park Zoo is Battle Creek's strongest family attraction, spread across wooded grounds south of town. The headline is the African savanna area, reached by tram, where the giraffe feeding deck is the visit's most memorable moment.

The zoo is large enough to take several hours but relaxed enough not to feel like a theme park. It is the easiest Battle Creek sight to recommend to travellers with children.
