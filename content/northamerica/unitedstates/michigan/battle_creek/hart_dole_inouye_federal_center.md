The Hart-Dole-Inouye Federal Center occupies the former Battle Creek Sanitarium, the health institution made famous by John Harvey Kellogg. Its twin towers and broad institutional mass still stand out above the city.

Security rules mean this is mostly an exterior stop, but the building matters. It ties Battle Creek's cereal industry to the 19th- and early 20th-century health reform movement that made the city nationally known.
