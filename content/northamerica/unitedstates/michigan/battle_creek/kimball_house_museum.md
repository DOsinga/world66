Kimball House Museum is an 1886 Queen Anne house that now tells Battle Creek's local history. Its rooms are arranged around Victorian domestic life, with exhibits on the Kimball family, the Battle Creek Sanitarium, and Sojourner Truth.

It is a selective stop rather than an essential one, but it gives useful texture to the city beyond cereal branding.
