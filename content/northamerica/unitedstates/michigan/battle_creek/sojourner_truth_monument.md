The Sojourner Truth Monument marks one of Battle Creek's most important stories. Truth, the abolitionist and women's rights advocate, spent her later years in the area and is buried in the city.

The downtown statue is a straightforward stop, but the plaques around it help place Battle Creek in the wider history of abolition, reform, and civil rights in Michigan.
