Home of [Andrews University](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/berrien_springs/andrews_university) and [Pioneer Memorial Church](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/berrien_springs/pioneer_memorial_church), Berrien Springs is a small village just off US-31 in southwestern Michigan. The village itself is not much more than a few stores, gas stations, and restaurants, but it carries more historical weight than its size suggests, especially in relation to the early history of Seventh-day Adventism and to nineteenth-century county government.

The old [Berrien Springs Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/berrien_springs/berrien_springs_courthouse) on the original [Courthouse Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/berrien_springs/courthouse_square) — said to be the oldest town square in the Midwest — is the centrepiece of the village. The [James White Library](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/berrien_springs/james_white_library) on the Andrews campus houses the largest collection of original writings by Ellen G. White, a founding figure of the SDA church. For shopping, beaches, and more substantial restaurants, head north to Benton Harbor and St. Joseph.
