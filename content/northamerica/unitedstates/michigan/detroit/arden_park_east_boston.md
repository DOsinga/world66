Arden Park-East Boston is a small historic district laid out in 1892 for Detroit's wealthy residents. Its broad lots and large houses show off the city's early-20th-century confidence, with Tudor, Colonial Revival, Italian Renaissance, and bungalow details sitting close together.

This is a neighbourhood for a slow architecture walk rather than a checklist sight. It pairs well with nearby New Center landmarks such as the Fisher Building if you want to see Detroit beyond the downtown core.
