The Argonaut Building is an Albert Kahn-designed Art Deco landmark in New Center, built for General Motors and now used by the College for Creative Studies. Its brick and limestone exterior makes a good short stop if you are already walking the area around the Fisher Building and Detroit's old auto-industry architecture.
