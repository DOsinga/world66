Boston-Edison is one of Detroit's grand residential historic districts, with more than 900 homes laid out on broad lots from the late 19th century onward. Walk or drive the streets for Tudor Revival, Italian Renaissance, Colonial Revival, Prairie, and other styles; Henry Ford was among the early residents.
