Bricktown is a compact historic district in downtown Detroit, close to Greektown and the riverfront. It includes St Peter and Paul's Catholic Church, the oldest standing church in Detroit, and the Italian Renaissance-style Wayne County Building. The People Mover stops nearby, making it an easy short walk while exploring downtown.
