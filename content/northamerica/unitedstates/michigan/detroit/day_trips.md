Detroit sits within easy striking distance of excellent day trips in both Michigan and Ohio.

[Ann Arbor](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/annarbor) is the quickest escape — 45 minutes west on I-94, a lively university town with a great art museum, good bookshops, and one of the most impressive college football stadiums in the country. [Frankenmuth](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/frankenmuth), about 90 minutes north on I-75, is Michigan's Little Bavaria: half-timbered streets, legendary all-you-can-eat chicken dinners at Zehnder's and the Bavarian Inn, and Bronner's Christmas Wonderland. [Cedar Point](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/cedar_point) in Sandusky, Ohio is two hours south via the Ohio Turnpike and holds more record-breaking roller coasters than any other park in the world.
