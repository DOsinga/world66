First Living Museum is an Underground Railroad museum at Detroit's First Congregational Church. Its main experience is the Flight to Freedom Tour, a guided reenactment that follows the story of people escaping slavery.

The visit is more immersive than a standard exhibit, so it is best for travellers who want context on Detroit's role in the Underground Railroad and do not mind a staged, participatory format.
