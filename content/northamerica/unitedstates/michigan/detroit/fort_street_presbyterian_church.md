Fort Street Presbyterian Church is a richly detailed Gothic Revival landmark in downtown Detroit, first built in the 1850s and rebuilt after a major fire in the 1870s. Its limestone exterior, tall spire, and stained glass make it one of the more rewarding church buildings to notice while walking west of the core.
