The Maccabees Building is a limestone-clad Neo-Gothic tower in Midtown Detroit, designed by Albert Kahn and listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Its pointed arches, vertical lines and carved details make it one of the stronger historic high-rise facades near the Detroit Institute of Arts and Wayne State University.
