The Museum of Contemporary Art Detroit is a non-collecting contemporary art museum in Midtown, housed in a former car dealership with a deliberately raw, unfinished interior. The building's rough edges are part of the experience, matching the museum's focus on experimental and current work.

Exhibitions change often, so it is best treated as a flexible stop while visiting Midtown's larger museum and cultural district.
