Old Mariners' Church is an independent Anglican-style church completed in 1849 to serve sailors on the Great Lakes. It is best known to many visitors from Gordon Lightfoot's "The Wreck of the Edmund Fitzgerald", and also has links to Detroit's Underground Railroad history.
