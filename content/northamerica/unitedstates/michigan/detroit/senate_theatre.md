The Senate Theatre is a 1926 neighborhood movie palace in southwest Detroit, best known today as the home of the Detroit Theater Organ Society. Come for classic film screenings, organ concerts, and the rare chance to hear a restored theatre organ in the kind of room it was built to fill.
