St. Florian Church is a 1928 Gothic Revival landmark in Hamtramck, designed by Ralph Adams Cram and listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Its tall profile and elaborate brickwork make it one of the strongest architectural stops in the Polish-American heart of metro Detroit.
