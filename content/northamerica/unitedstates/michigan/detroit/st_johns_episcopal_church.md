St. John's Episcopal Church is a Gothic Revival church from 1860 near Detroit's theatre and stadium district. It is listed on the National Register of Historic Places and stands out as one of the older surviving religious buildings downtown.

The church is a short architectural stop rather than a long visit. It pairs naturally with the Fox Theatre, Comerica Park, and other Woodward Avenue landmarks.
