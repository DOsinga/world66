A downtown pub in a restored 19th-century brick building, this is one of the longest-running food-and-drink rooms downtown and a reliable stop for a burger and a craft beer after a museum visit. The menu is gastropub standard — burgers, wings, fish and chips — but the kitchen is consistent and the rotating Michigan beer list takes the place seriously.

The wide front patio fills up on warm evenings and is a good vantage point for a slow look at downtown.
