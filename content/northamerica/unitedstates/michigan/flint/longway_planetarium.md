Michigan's largest planetarium, with a 60-foot dome and a high-definition full-dome projection system installed during a major 2016 renovation. The programme rotates between traditional star shows, music-and-laser concerts, and full-dome films that range from natural-science documentaries to children's entertainment.

It sits alongside the Sloan Museum and the Flint Institute of Arts in the Cultural Center campus, making the trio an easy three-stop afternoon if the weather is poor.
