One of the two great fried-chicken institutions of Frankenmuth, in a sprawling half-timbered building on Main Street. The all-you-can-eat chicken dinner is the headline (around $25 in 2024) — three pieces of fried chicken with a parade of sides, refilled until you surrender.

The complex includes a glockenspiel tower, gift shops, and a basement of older Bavarian curio rooms. Reserve ahead on summer weekends or expect a long wait.
