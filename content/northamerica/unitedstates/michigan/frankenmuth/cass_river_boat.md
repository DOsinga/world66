A small Mississippi-style stern-wheeler that runs scenic cruises along the Cass River, with a narrated history of the town and the original 1845 Lutheran settlement. Trips take around 50 minutes and run on the hour from May through October.

The river is shallow and slow, which suits the leisurely pace. The lower deck is enclosed and air-conditioned; the upper deck is open and the better choice when the weather is good.
