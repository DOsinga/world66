story: "The original Frankenmuth Brewery was the oldest in Michigan, founded by Bavarian immigrants in 1862. A tornado destroyed most of it in 1996 and the brewery limped on under successive owners until it closed in 2018. The current operation reopened in 2019 in part of the rebuilt brewery building and revived several historic Frankenmuth Bavarian recipes."
---

A working microbrewery on the bank of the Cass River, in part of the original 1862 Frankenmuth Brewery building. The restored brewery serves its own beers (the Pilsener and the Twisted Helles are the staples) plus a full pub menu — German-American comfort food, better than you might expect.

The deck along the river is a pleasant place for a beer on warm afternoons. Brewery tours run on weekends.
