The larger and slightly more old-fashioned of the two great Frankenmuth chicken houses, Zehnder's has been in business in some form since 1856. The colonial-style building seats 1,500 diners across multiple rooms; service is fast even when the place is full to capacity, which is most weekends.

The chicken dinner runs the same as at the Bavarian Inn — three pieces, sides refilled, around $25. The two restaurants together feed more than a million people a year. Zehnder's also runs a year-round bakery and gift shop.
