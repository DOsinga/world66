Frederik Meijer Gardens and Sculpture Park is one of Grand Rapids' signature attractions, combining botanical gardens, conservatories, and a serious outdoor sculpture collection. It is polished, spacious, and worth planning around rather than treating as a filler stop.

The best visit mixes the indoor tropical and seasonal gardens with the outdoor sculpture landscape. Allow enough time to wander; the scale is closer to a campus than a small city garden.
