Holland is a small west Michigan city with a clear identity: Dutch immigrant history, tulips in spring, a walkable downtown, and Lake Michigan beaches just beyond town. It is not the Netherlands transplanted whole, but the best pieces are specific enough to make a good day if you are already on the lakeshore.

[Windmill Island Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/holland/windmill_island_gardens) is the signature stop, with De Zwaan, a working Dutch windmill brought from the Netherlands. [Veldheer Tulip Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/holland/veldheer_tulip_gardens) adds the big spring color, while the [Holland Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/holland/holland_museum) gives the heritage a bit more depth than wooden shoes and souvenirs.

The lake is the other half of the trip. [Holland State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/holland/holland_state_park) has broad sand, sunset views, and the postcard view of Big Red across the channel. Come in May for Tulip Time if you want the town at full volume, or outside festival weekends if you want the same setting with fewer crowds.
