The Holland Museum fills in the story behind the tulips and windmill. Its collections cover Dutch cultural objects, local history, and the settlement period that shaped the city.

The museum also operates related historic properties, including the Cappon House and Settlers House. It is the right stop if you want Holland to feel like a real town, not just a festival theme.
