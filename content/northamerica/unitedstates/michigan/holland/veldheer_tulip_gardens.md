Veldheer Tulip Gardens is the big floral stop north of town. In season, the fields carry millions of tulips along with daffodils, hyacinths, and other spring flowers.

It is a simple pleasure rather than a deep one, but that is fine. If you came to Holland for tulips, this is where the scale makes sense.
