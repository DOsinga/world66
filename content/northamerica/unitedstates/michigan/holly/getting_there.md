---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

**By car.** Holly sits right on I-75 at exit 101 (Grange Hall Road) and exit 102 (Holly Road). Detroit is 50 miles to the south, Flint 20 miles north, and Pontiac about 25 miles south-east. Most visitors arrive by car.

**By air.** Detroit Metropolitan (DTW), an hour to the south, is the nearest major airport. Flint Bishop (FNT) is closer, with limited domestic service.

**By train.** Amtrak's Blue Water service between Chicago and Port Huron stops in Flint and Pontiac, both within a half-hour drive of Holly. There is no passenger station in Holly itself any more, though the historic Holly Union Depot still stands downtown.
