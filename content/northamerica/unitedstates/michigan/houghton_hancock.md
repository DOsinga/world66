Houghton and Hancock face each other across the Portage Lake Ship Canal in Michigan's Upper Peninsula. They read as one town split by water: Houghton has Michigan Tech and the larger downtown; Hancock climbs the hill on the north side and points toward the old copper country.

The pair is worth stopping for because the Keweenaw's story is unusually strong. The [Quincy Mine](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/houghton_hancock/quincy_mine) is one of the best places in the region to understand deep copper mining, while the [A. E. Seaman Mineral Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/houghton_hancock/ae_seaman_mineral_museum) explains why this peninsula mattered geologically. The [Portage Lake Lift Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/houghton_hancock/portage_lake_lift_bridge) gives the towns their shared landmark.

This is also a northern college town with serious winters. Come for mines, minerals, canal views, snow culture, and as a base for the Keweenaw Peninsula rather than for a polished city break.
