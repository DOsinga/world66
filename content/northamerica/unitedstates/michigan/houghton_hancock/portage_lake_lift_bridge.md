The Portage Lake Lift Bridge is the shared landmark of Houghton and Hancock. It carries traffic across the canal between the two downtowns and marks the gateway toward the Keweenaw Peninsula.

It is a quick stop, but a useful one. Walk or view it from the waterfront path to get the shape of the place: steep streets, water between the towns, and a bridge that makes the twin-city setup work.
