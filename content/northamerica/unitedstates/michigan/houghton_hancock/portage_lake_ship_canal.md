The Portage Lake Ship Canal is more than background scenery. It cuts between Houghton and Hancock, carries the waterfront trails, and gives the towns their fjord-like feel, with steep slopes rising from both sides.

Walk a stretch of the path if the weather is fair. It is the easiest way to see how college town, mining town, and Lake Superior waterway fit together here.
