Houghton Lake is a small resort community wrapped around Michigan's largest inland lake. It is not polished, and that is part of the point: this is pontoon boats, fishing docks, snowmobiles, family cabins, and a winter carnival built around ice fishing.

The lake is the reason to come. In summer, people rent boats, fish, swim, and drift into the [Muskegon River](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/houghton_lake/muskegon_river) at the north end. In winter, the rhythm moves onto the ice, especially during [Tip-Up Town USA](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/houghton_lake/tip_up_town_usa), a long-running January festival of snowmobiles, fishing contests, and cold-weather local pride.

Houghton Lake will not beat Traverse City for restaurants or Petoskey for charm. But if you are already in central northern Michigan with a spare day, the lake, the river, the [Historic Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/houghton_lake/houghton_lake_historic_village), and a few low-key outdoor stops make it more rewarding than another day on the highway.
