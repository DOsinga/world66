---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Jackson sits on **I-94** halfway between [Detroit](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/detroit) (75 miles east) and [Battle Creek](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan), and the city is the railroad hub it has been since the 1840s. The **Jackson Amtrak station** — itself a National Historic Landmark — is a stop on the Wolverine line between Chicago and Pontiac, with three trains daily in each direction.

The nearest commercial airports are **Detroit Metropolitan** (DTW), about 90 minutes east, and **Lansing's Capital Region** (LAN), 45 minutes north.
