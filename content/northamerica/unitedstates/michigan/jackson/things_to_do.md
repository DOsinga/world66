Jackson is a half-day or one-day stop rather than a weekend destination. The reliable draws are the [Cascade Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/jackson/cascade_falls) — a coloured concrete waterfall that ranks among the larger man-made cascades anywhere — the rural history at the [Ella Sharp Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/jackson/ella_sharp_museum), and the **Michigan International Speedway** half an hour south.
