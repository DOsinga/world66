---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer is the obvious window. The **Cascade Falls** lights run nightly from Memorial Day through Labor Day; the **Hot Air Jubilee** balloon festival fills the sky on the third weekend in July; **NASCAR** races at the Michigan International Speedway are in June and August.

Autumn is genuinely good for the surrounding lakes country — the maples around the Jackson chain of lakes turn early October. Winters are grey and cold; January and February see lake-effect snow blowing in from the west.
