Kalamazoo is one of those Midwestern cities whose name is famous before the place is. The reality is better than the joke: a college town and county seat in south-west Michigan, with Western Michigan University, Kalamazoo College, a strong arts scene, and a downtown that once created the first outdoor pedestrian mall in the United States.

The city has enough for a full day without pretending to be Chicago or Detroit. [Air Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/kalamazoo/air_zoo) is the big family draw, the [Kalamazoo Institute of Arts](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/kalamazoo/kalamazoo_institute_of_arts) and [Kalamazoo Valley Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/kalamazoo/kalamazoo_valley_museum) cover art and local history, and [Bell's Eccentric Cafe](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/kalamazoo/bells_eccentric_cafe) gives the city a real place in American craft beer.

Kalamazoo works especially well as a low-key stop between Chicago, Detroit, and Grand Rapids. It is not flashy, but it is walkable, useful, and more interesting than its highway exits suggest.
