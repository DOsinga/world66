Bell's Eccentric Cafe is the public face of the brewery that put Kalamazoo on the craft-beer map. The beer garden, taproom, and music calendar make it more than a place to sample Two Hearted close to the source.

It is still a bar, not a museum, but for beer travellers it is one of the city's real anchors. Check the event schedule if you want the livelier version of the place.
