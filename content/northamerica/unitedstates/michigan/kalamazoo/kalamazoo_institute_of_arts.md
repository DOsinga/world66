The Kalamazoo Institute of Arts is the city's serious art stop, with a collection strongest in American painting, works on paper, photography, and contemporary art. The museum is compact enough for a casual visit but curated well enough to reward more time.

It also runs an active art school, which gives the place a civic role beyond the galleries. Pair it with a downtown walk rather than rushing straight back to the highway.
