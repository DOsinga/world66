The Kalamazoo Nature Center protects woods, wetlands, prairie, and rolling glacial terrain north of the city. It is the best nearby place to trade downtown streets for trails, birds, and a clearer sense of south-west Michigan's natural landscape.

Trail conditions vary with weather, so bring shoes that can handle mud in spring and after rain.
