Kalamazoo State Theatre is a restored 1927 movie palace that still works hard, hosting touring bands, comedy, films, and local events. The marquee gives downtown Kalamazoo some old show-business glow, and the interior keeps the ornate feel of a prewar theatre.

Check the schedule if you are in town overnight. It is one of the city's better evening options.
