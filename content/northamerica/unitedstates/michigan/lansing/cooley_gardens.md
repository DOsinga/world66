Cooley Gardens is a small but worthwhile green stop just south of downtown Lansing, remembered for the formal plantings and terraces associated with the former Cooley estate. It works best as a quiet add-on to a walk through the river corridor rather than as a full afternoon destination.

Come for a short stroll, seasonal flowers, and a change of pace from the Capitol and museum circuit. The garden is most rewarding in late spring and summer, and its compact scale makes it easy to combine with nearby parks or Old Town Lansing.
