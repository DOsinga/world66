Turner-Dodge House is a useful historic counterpoint to Lansing's state-government identity. The large nineteenth-century house belonged to families tied to the city's early development, and today it works as a heritage center rather than just a preserved private home.

Check opening hours before making the trip, because historic houses often run limited tours and events. Even from outside, the house and grounds add a calmer, older layer to a city that many travellers otherwise experience only through the capitol area and Michigan State University.
