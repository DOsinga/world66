---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Downtown Marquette punches well above its weight for restaurants and brewpubs.
Lagniappe is the long-running Cajun-Creole favourite; Iron Bay Restaurant &
Drinkery does upscale comfort food in a former warehouse; the Vierling
Restaurant has the best dining-room view of the lake and ore dock.

For beer, Blackrocks Brewery is the local institution — a small craft brewer
in a converted house north of downtown, with a friendly tap room and live music
several nights a week. Ore Dock Brewing Company also has good beer and a
larger space. Donckers, a downtown candy shop and cafe in business since 1896,
is the standard breakfast stop.

Pasties — folded meat-and-vegetable pies brought by Cornish miners in the 19th
century — are the iconic U.P. food. Jean Kay's Pasties on Third Street is the
local choice.
