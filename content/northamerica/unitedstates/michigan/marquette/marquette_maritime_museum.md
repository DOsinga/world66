The Marquette Maritime Museum, in a small brick building right on the harbour,
tells the story of Lake Superior shipping, the iron-ore freighters that built
the city, and the many shipwrecks scattered along this stretch of coast. The
exhibits are modest but well-curated, with an Edmund Fitzgerald display that is
particularly moving.

Admission includes a guided tour of the Marquette Harbor Lighthouse, the red
brick beacon that has stood watch over the harbour entrance since 1866 and
still functions today. The walk out to the light gives one of the better
viewpoints back over the downtown waterfront.
