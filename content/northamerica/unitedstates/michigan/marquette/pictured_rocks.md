Pictured Rocks, an hour east of Marquette around the lakeshore, is one of the
most spectacular stretches of coast on any of the Great Lakes — forty miles of
multicoloured sandstone cliffs, sea arches, beaches, and waterfalls. The most
famous formations (Miners Castle, Chapel Rock, Battleship Row) are best seen
from the water on one of the boat tours that leave from Munising.

For walkers, the North Country Trail runs the full length of the lakeshore and
offers some of the finest day hikes in the Midwest. The Chapel Rock loop is
the classic — about 10 miles, with beaches, waterfalls, and the cliffs all in
one walk. Bring layers: Lake Superior keeps even summer days cool.
