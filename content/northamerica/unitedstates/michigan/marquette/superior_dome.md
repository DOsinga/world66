The Superior Dome on the campus of Northern Michigan University is, depending
on how you measure, either the largest or the second-largest wooden dome in the
world. The 14-storey structure encloses about 5.1 acres and serves as the home
field for the NMU Wildcats football team along with a wide range of other
sports and events.

Self-guided tours are possible when the dome is not in use; staff at the
adjacent athletic offices will usually point you toward the public viewing
gallery. It's a brief stop but visually impressive — the curved fir ceiling
seen from inside is genuinely striking.
