---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

[Presque Isle Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/marquette/presque_isle_park)
is the obvious starting point — a forested peninsula at the edge of town with
swimming holes, a black rock cliff that locals jump off, and the best sunsets
in the Upper Peninsula. The historic
[Lower Harbor Ore Dock](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/marquette/lower_harbor_ore_dock)
gives a sense of Marquette's iron-shipping past.

For a longer adventure, head out to
[Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/marquette/pictured_rocks)
for the cliffs and beaches, or to the
[Sugarloaf Mountain](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/marquette/sugarloaf_mountain)
overlook for the city's best panoramic view. Quirky stops include
[Lakenenland](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/marquette/lakenenland) and the
genuinely impressive
[Superior Dome](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/marquette/superior_dome).
