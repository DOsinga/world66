Lakeshore Museum Center is Muskegon’s main local-history and science museum network, anchored by the Muskegon Museum of History and Science on West Clay Avenue. It is strongest when it connects the city to the wider Lake Michigan shore: lumber, industry, natural history, and the families who built the town through boom years and hard ones.

The centre also links to historic sites and specialist collections nearby, so check which buildings are open before planning your day. For families it is an easy indoor choice; for adults it helps make sense of Muskegon beyond the beach and the ferry dock.
