In the 1870s the water was promoted as a health cure for various ailments — a marketing claim that was entirely unsubstantiated but very effective. Mineral Well Park is a small lakeside park where a natural mineral spring flows into a stone trough — visitors still come to taste the water, which has a slight iron tang from the local geology. The park is on the waterfront between the Gaslight District and Magnus Park, in a pleasant setting with benches, picnic tables, and a view across Little Traverse Bay.

The park is the starting point for the Little Traverse Wheelway, a paved non-motorized trail that runs along the bay south to Charlevoix (about 26 miles total). Even a short section — to Petoskey State Park north of town, or south along the bay — makes a good afternoon walk or bike ride. Bike rental is available from shops in the Gaslight District.
