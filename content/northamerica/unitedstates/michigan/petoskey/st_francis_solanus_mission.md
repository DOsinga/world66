St. Francis Solanus Mission is a modest building with a layered northern Michigan story. The mission is associated with the Odawa community at Little Traverse Bay and with Bishop Frederic Baraga, the 19th-century missionary whose work left traces across the Upper Great Lakes.

It is not a long visit, but it adds useful context to Petoskey beyond the waterfront and resort-town surface. Stop if you are interested in Native, Catholic, and settler histories meeting in one small place, and treat the site with the quiet expected at an active religious landmark.
