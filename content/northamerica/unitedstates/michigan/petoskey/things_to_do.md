title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Petoskey's appeal is split between the land and the water. The Gaslight District in the walkable downtown is the shopping and eating centre; the waterfront along Little Traverse Bay is where you go for the lake, the beaches, and the famous Petoskey stones. Beyond town, the state park, the ski hills, and the Hemingway country draw people into the forests and lakeshores.
