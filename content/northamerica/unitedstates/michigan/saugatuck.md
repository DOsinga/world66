Saugatuck is one of the better small towns on Michigan's Lake Michigan shore, and it earns that reputation by being more than a strip of sand. The Kalamazoo River, wooded dunes, galleries, summer theatre, and a compact downtown give it a real shape. It is polished and busy in season, but not empty behind the postcard.

[Oval Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/saugatuck/oval_beach) is the headline, a broad freshwater beach backed by dunes. The most charming way to reach it is to cross the river on the [Saugatuck Chain Ferry](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/saugatuck/saugatuck_chain_ferry), climb [Mount Baldhead](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/saugatuck/mount_baldhead), and walk down through the sand toward the lake. North of town, [Saugatuck Dunes State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/saugatuck/saugatuck_dunes_state_park) gives you a quieter version of the same coast, with trails through forested dunes to Lake Michigan.

The arts scene matters too. The [Saugatuck Center for the Arts](/northamerica/unitedstates/michigan/saugatuck/saugatuck_center_for_the_arts) anchors performances and exhibitions, and the downtown galleries are part of the town's identity rather than a decorative afterthought. Saugatuck is still a resort town, but it has enough texture to justify a spare day if you are already on the west Michigan coast.
