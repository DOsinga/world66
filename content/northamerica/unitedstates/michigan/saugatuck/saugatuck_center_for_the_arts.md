The Saugatuck Center for the Arts gives the town's art reputation a real venue. Its programme changes through the year, but it typically mixes theatre, concerts, exhibitions, classes, films, and community events.

Check the calendar before you go. On the right night it can turn Saugatuck from a beach day into a proper evening stop.
