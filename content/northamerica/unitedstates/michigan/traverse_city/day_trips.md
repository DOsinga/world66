---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

The country around Traverse City is the main reason to come. Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore sits 25 miles to the west and is one of the great landscapes in the Midwest. The two peninsulas — Old Mission to the north, Leelanau to the northwest — each reward a half-day of unhurried driving, with wineries, orchards, and bay views at regular intervals. The charming small towns of the region, including Suttons Bay and Leland, make good lunch stops.
