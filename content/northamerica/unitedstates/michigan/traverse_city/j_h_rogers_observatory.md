J. H. Rogers Observatory is a modest but worthwhile astronomy stop on the edge of Traverse City. Run with an educational mission, it gives visitors a chance to look beyond the resort-town version of northern Michigan and spend an evening with telescopes, planets, and the dark sky above Grand Traverse Bay.

This is not a polished museum; it is a small public observatory where the experience depends on clear weather and scheduled events. That is also what makes it appealing. If the sky cooperates, it is an intimate way to add a little science and stillness to a Traverse City trip.
