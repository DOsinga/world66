Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore stretches for 35 miles along the Lake Michigan shore, about 25 miles west of Traverse City, and is one of the most dramatic landscapes in the Midwest. The dunes themselves are perched atop glacial bluffs, giving the sand formations a height and scale that makes them genuinely impressive — at the dune climb near Empire, you can see all the way out to the Manitou Islands. The park also protects two of those islands, accessible by ferry from Leland.

The Pierce Stocking Scenic Drive, a 7-mile loop through the dunes with several overlooks, is the easiest way to see the park. The overlook at stop 9 offers the best view of the dunes tumbling down to Lake Michigan. The swimming beaches at Glen Haven and Platte River are among the best freshwater beaches in the country. Allow at least half a day; a full day lets you do the dune climb, the scenic drive, and a swim.
