The drive out Old Mission Peninsula is one of northern Michigan's finest: a 20-mile finger of land with the bay visible on both sides for most of the route, flanked by cherry orchards and vineyards that turn spectacular in blossom season (late May) and again at harvest (September). The road terminates at Mission Point Lighthouse, where you can picnic on the rocky shore and look back down the length of the peninsula.

Chateau Grand Traverse, Bowers Harbor Vineyards, and Brys Estate are among the most established wineries, each with tasting rooms and views over the vineyards. The Rieslings — both dry and semi-dry — are the regional specialty and worth seeking out. A half-day tour stopping at two or three wineries before ending at the lighthouse is the classic Old Mission itinerary.
