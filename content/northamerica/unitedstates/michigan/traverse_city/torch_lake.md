Torch Lake, about 30 miles east of Traverse City, is a long, narrow inland lake in Antrim County whose turquoise color in summer sun looks more Caribbean than Midwestern. The clarity of the water — visibility down 30 feet in places — and the sandbar shallows on the western shore create the color, which photographs well but is more striking in person.

The lake is primarily a local recreation area: boating, swimming, and sandbar socializing in summer. The Chain of Lakes that connects Torch Lake to six other lakes and eventually to Lake Michigan provides an extended waterway for kayakers and canoeists. The small town of Bellaire at the south end of the lake has a few good restaurants and the Short's Brewing Company, one of Michigan's most creative craft breweries. Allow a half-day for the drive, a swim or boat ride, and lunch.
