---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Bloomington is well-placed for reaching Minneapolis (20 minutes by car or light rail) and Saint Paul (30 minutes). Fort Snelling State Park, at the confluence of the Minnesota and Mississippi rivers just north of Bloomington, combines historic fort buildings with good riverside hiking. The Mall of America has a direct light rail connection to downtown Minneapolis, making it easy to spend the morning at the mall and the afternoon in the city. Mall of America station is on the Blue Line.
