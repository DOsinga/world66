[Minneapolis](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis) is twenty minutes away by car or Blue Line light rail — the Mall of America station connects directly to downtown Minneapolis, so you can spend the morning at the mall and the afternoon in the city. [St. Paul](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/stpaul), Minnesota's capital and the quieter half of the Twin Cities, is about thirty minutes by car and well worth a half-day for Summit Avenue's Victorian mansion row and the magnificent Cass Gilbert state capitol. Both cities share an extensive light rail and bus network that makes combining them straightforward.

[Historic Fort Snelling](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/bloomington/fort_snelling) sits at the confluence of the Minnesota and Mississippi rivers, just north of Bloomington. The Minnesota Historical Society operates the restored 19th-century fort as a living history site, and the surrounding state park offers good riverside hiking and birdwatching — a full half-day from Bloomington.
