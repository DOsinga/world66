---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The restaurant scene in Bloomington is dominated by chain dining around the Mall of America, but there are some worthwhile options. The mall itself has a full range of fast food and sit-down chains across its food court and restaurant levels. For something more local, the area along Old Shakopee Road has Korean and East African restaurants reflecting the Twin Cities' large immigrant communities. The hotels near the airport tend to have solid bar menus for travellers arriving late.
