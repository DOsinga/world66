The Works Museum is a hands-on engineering museum in Bloomington, aimed especially at children who like building, testing, and taking things apart. Exhibits focus on design, simple machines, problem-solving, and the practical side of science rather than passive displays.

It is not a marquee Twin Cities museum, but it is a useful family stop when you are based near Bloomington or the Mall of America and want something more constructive than another shopping or amusement-park session. Check hours and programming, as workshops and school-group periods can shape the visit.
