Leif Erickson Park sits along Duluth's Lake Superior shore, close enough to downtown to fold easily into a waterfront walk. Its lawns, paths, and formal rose garden make it one of the gentler stops on a shoreline otherwise defined by ore docks, lift bridges, and cold inland-sea weather.

The park is best in season, when the rose garden is blooming and the lake breeze feels like a relief rather than a warning. It pairs naturally with the Lakewalk, Canal Park, and a slow look at the ships moving in and out of the harbour.
