---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The most famous food stop in town is [World's Best Donuts](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/grand_marais/worlds_best_donuts) on Wisconsin Street — a tiny seasonal shop open since 1969 with summer queues out onto the pavement. The Angry Trout Café on the harbour does sustainable lake fish, including trout sandwiches and whitefish chowder. The Crooked Spoon Café on Wisconsin is the town's serious dinner option, with an ambitious menu rooted in regional ingredients. South Of the Border Café (mornings only, breakfast standards) and Hughie's Taco House round out a small but unusually good eating scene for a town this size.
