---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The first stop is the [Grand Marais Light](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/grand_marais/grand_marais_light) at the end of the harbour breakwater — a short, exhilarating walk over Lake Superior. The [Cook County History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/grand_marais/cook_county_history_museum) is in the old lightkeeper's house just above the harbour and ties together the fishing, voyageur, and Anishinaabe history of the North Shore. Out toward the Gunflint Trail, the [Devil's Kettle](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/grand_marais/devils_kettle) waterfall in Judge C.R. Magney State Park is the canonical day hike — half the river vanishes mysteriously into a pothole and reappears no one knows where. [Eagle Mountain](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/grand_marais/eagle_mountain) is Minnesota's highest point, a moderate hike with summit views. [Naniboujou Lodge](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/grand_marais/naniboujou_lodge), 15 miles up the shore, is an art-deco supper club designed in the 1920s for a private celebrity members' club. The [Pincushion Mountain](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/grand_marais/pincushion_mountain) overlook just inland of town is the easiest way to see the whole harbour from above. In town, the [North House Folk School](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/grand_marais/north_house_folk_school) runs short courses in traditional crafts — boatbuilding, weaving, baking, blacksmithing — and is open to wander.
