---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Grand Portage is the last community on Minnesota Highway 61, 36 miles northeast of Grand Marais and about 150 miles from Duluth. The Pigeon River border crossing into Ontario is 6 miles further on, with onward connections to Thunder Bay (45 miles) and the Trans-Canada Highway.

There is no public transport into Grand Portage; you need a car. The closest airports are Thunder Bay (CYQT) in Ontario and Duluth International (DLH) in Minnesota.

The Voyageur Marina at the end of Stevens Road is the departure point for the **Isle Royale Queen IV** and the smaller **Voyageur II**, the only passenger ferries to Isle Royale National Park. Crossings run mid-May through late September; book months ahead in season. Grand Portage is also a base for floatplane service to the island through Isle Royale Seaplanes.
