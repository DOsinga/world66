---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Grand Portage is small and the attractions are spread along Highway 61 and the lakeshore. Most visitors come for the National Monument, but the surrounding country — the High Falls of the Pigeon River, the cliffs of Hat Point, the long views from Mount Josephine — rewards anyone willing to linger a day or two. The Voyageur Marina at the end of Stevens Road is the departure point for ferries and seaplanes to Isle Royale National Park.
