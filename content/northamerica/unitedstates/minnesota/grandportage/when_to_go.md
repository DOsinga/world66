---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The summer visitor season is short — late May to mid-October — and tightly tied to the National Monument's interpretive program, the Isle Royale ferry schedule, and the weather. **August** is the warmest and busiest month and the time of the annual **Rendezvous Days and Powwow** (second weekend of August), when reenactors, traders, and Ojibwe dancers fill the depot grounds.

**September** brings clearing weather, fewer bugs, and the start of fall colour along the Highway 61 corridor; this is the connoisseur's month. **Winter** is long and serious: the Monument's grounds are open year-round but most buildings close, the ferries don't run, and temperatures regularly drop below -20°F (-29°C). Snowshoeing and cross-country skiing are possible on the Grand Portage Trail with proper gear.
