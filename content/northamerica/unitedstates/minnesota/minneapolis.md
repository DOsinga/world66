Minneapolis is a city that punches well above its weight in culture, food, and green space. It sits on both banks of the Mississippi River, connected by a network of parks and lakes that would make most American cities jealous. The Chain of Lakes — Cedar, Isles, [Bde Maka Ska](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/bde_maka_ska), and [Lake Harriet](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/lake_harriet) — threads through the western neighbourhoods, drawing runners, cyclists, and swimmers year-round. In winter, the lakes freeze and the city doesn't slow down.

The neighbourhoods give the city its character. **[Uptown](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/uptown)** is the centre of the twenty-something scene, with independent shops on Hennepin Avenue and Lake Street, good restaurants, and the [Uptown Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/uptown_theater). A few blocks east, Lyn-Lake is a creative district where [Bryant-Lake Bowl](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/bryantlakebowl) — part bowling alley, part restaurant, part theatre — captures the city's knack for unexpected combinations. The **[Warehouse District](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/warehouse_district)** downtown has the [Guthrie Theater](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/guthrie_theater) on the riverbank and [First Avenue](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/first_avenue) — the venue where Prince built his legend — on Hennepin. The converted loft buildings of the **[North Loop](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/north_loop)** hold the city's densest concentration of serious restaurants. **[Northeast](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/northeast)** (Nordeast) is the arts and brewery district, its Eastern European heritage preserved in places like [Kramarczuk's](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/kramarczuks) alongside two dozen craft taprooms. **[Dinkytown](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/dinkytown)**, near the University of Minnesota, is where Bob Dylan lived when he arrived in Minneapolis in 1959.

Minneapolis has world-class cultural institutions — the [Walker Art Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/walker_art_center) with its [Spoonbridge and Cherry sculpture garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/spoonbridge_and_cherry), the [Minneapolis Institute of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/minneapolis_institute_of_art), and the [Weisman Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/weisman_art_museum) (Frank Gehry's stainless steel building above the river) — alongside a music scene that shaped Prince and the Replacements. On the riverfront, [Saint Anthony Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/saint_anthony_falls) and the [Stone Arch Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/stone_arch_bridge) anchor the [Mill City Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/mill_city_museum) and [Owamni](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/owamni), the James Beard Award-winning Indigenous restaurant. In south Minneapolis, [Minnehaha Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/minnehaha_falls) drops into the river gorge in a park accessible by light rail. The food is better than outsiders expect — [Eat Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/eat_street), [Midtown Global Market](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/midtown_global_market), and the [Somali restaurants of Cedar-Riverside](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/minneapolis/somali_restaurant_row) reflect immigrant communities that have made this one of the most genuinely diverse dining cities in the Midwest.
