The Cedar Cultural Center is an intimate nonprofit music venue in Cedar-Riverside, with a calendar that leans toward jazz, folk, blues, indie, and global music. It is a good choice when you want a smaller Minneapolis show with serious listening-room energy rather than a big club night.
