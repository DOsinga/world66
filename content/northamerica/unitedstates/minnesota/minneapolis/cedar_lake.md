Cedar Lake is the quietest of Minneapolis's Chain of Lakes, with public swimming beaches and links by lagoon to Lake of the Isles and Bde Maka Ska. It works best as a low-key outdoor stop for swimming, paddling, or a walk along the west side of the city.
