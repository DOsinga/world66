The Como-Harriet Streetcar Line is a short heritage ride between Lake Harriet and Bde Maka Ska, using restored streetcars from the Twin City Rapid Transit era. It is a small survivor of a much larger streetcar network that once linked Minneapolis and Saint Paul.

Rides start at Linden Hills Station on the west side of Lake Harriet. It is an easy add-on to a lake walk, and a good stop for families or anyone interested in the city's old transit history.
