Upton Block, built in 1855, is the oldest surviving masonry building in Minneapolis. Over time it has held shops, offices, and residences, making it a small but concrete link to the city's earliest urban growth.

It is a quick architecture stop, best folded into a walk around the riverfront and Northeast rather than treated as a stand-alone destination.
