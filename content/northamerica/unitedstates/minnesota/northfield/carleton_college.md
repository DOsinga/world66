Carleton College sits just east of downtown and is worth a wander even if you have no campus business. The grounds are attractive, and the college's Cowling Arboretum gives Northfield a large stretch of public walking and running trails close to town.

For travellers, Carleton is part of what makes Northfield feel unusually lively for a small Minnesota city.
