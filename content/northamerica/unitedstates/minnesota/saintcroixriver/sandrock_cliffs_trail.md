Sandrock Cliffs Trail is a five-mile network of foot trails along the St. Croix River north of Highway 70. The loops pass sandstone cliffs and wooded river scenery, with a three-mile southern loop and connecting trails adding more distance.

Trailheads are at the Highway 70 Landing parking area and near Tennessee Road. The trail is open year-round for walking only; bicycles and motorized vehicles are not allowed.
