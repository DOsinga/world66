Sauk Centre is a small central [Minnesota](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota) town with a literary chip on its shoulder. Sinclair Lewis was born here, used the town as the raw material for Gopher Prairie in *Main Street*, and left behind enough friction that the place is more interesting than a standard freeway stop.

The best reason to come is the [Sinclair Lewis Boyhood Home](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/sauk_centre/sinclair_lewis_boyhood_home), a preserved 1889 house where the first American Nobel laureate in literature grew up. A few blocks away, the [Palmer House Hotel](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/sauk_centre/palmer_house_hotel) anchors the Original Main Street Historic District and still carries the atmosphere of an old railroad-era hotel.

Sauk Centre also sits on the [Lake Wobegon Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/minnesota/sauk_centre/lake_wobegon_trail), so it works as a pleasant biking stop between the dairy towns and lakes of central Minnesota. It is not a full weekend destination for most travelers, but if Lewis, small-town America, and easy trail riding appeal to you, it is worth more than a quick exit from I-94.
