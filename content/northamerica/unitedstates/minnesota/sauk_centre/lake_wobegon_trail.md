The Lake Wobegon Trail gives Sauk Centre an easy outdoor reason to linger. The local trailhead connects riders to a long paved route through central Minnesota farm country, with lakes, small towns, and old rail corridors along the way. It is best as a half-day ride or as one stop on a longer cycling trip.
