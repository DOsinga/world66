The Sinclair Lewis Boyhood Home is the town's main reason to stop. The house is modest, but that is part of the point: this was the small-town world Lewis later dissected in *Main Street*. Tours usually run in the summer season, and the rooms are kept close to the period when Lewis was growing up here.
