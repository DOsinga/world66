F. Scott Fitzgerald spent his early years and several significant periods of his adult life in St. Paul. The house at 599 Summit Avenue, where he lived while writing "This Side of Paradise," is a private residence but marked with a plaque. His birthplace at 481 Laurel Avenue, a brownstone a few blocks away, is also private but worth walking past.

The Great Gatsby author left a substantial mark on the city's sense of itself. The Minnesota Historical Society maintains some records and artefacts related to his St. Paul years, and Summit Avenue walking tours regularly include the Fitzgerald stops. For a deeper dive, the Fitzgerald Theatre on Exchange Street — a beautifully restored 1910 theatre named after the author — hosts regular performances including the live broadcasts of A Prairie Home Companion.
