Minnesota Children's Museum is one of downtown St. Paul's most useful family stops, with hands-on galleries built for younger children rather than passive looking. Exhibits lean into climbing, building, water play, pretend towns and other ways for children to burn energy while still learning something.

For adults without kids it is an easy skip, but families with children under about ten should put it high on the St. Paul list. It is close enough to the Landmark Center and the State Capitol area to combine with a broader downtown walk.
