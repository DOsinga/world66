Biloxi's appeal is spread across its history, its beaches, and its casino strip — three things that sit in somewhat uncomfortable proximity along Highway 90. The [Biloxi Lighthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/biloxi/biloxi_lighthouse) in the middle of the road is the most photographed spot, while [Beauvoir](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/biloxi/beauvoir) — Jefferson Davis's postwar home — sits on a bluff overlooking the sound. The [Ohr-O'Keefe Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/biloxi/ohr_okeefe_museum) is the most architecturally interesting building in the city, designed by Frank Gehry to house the work of Biloxi's most eccentric son.

The [Gulf Coast beaches](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/biloxi/gulf_coast_beach) stretch for miles along the coast road and are genuinely pleasant outside the casino zone. The [Mississippi Aquarium](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/biloxi/mississippi_aquarium), which opened in 2020, has become one of the city's better attractions. For the casino scene, [Beau Rivage](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/biloxi/beau_rivage) is the flagship — a large MGM-operated resort that also hosts concerts and boxing events.
