---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Live music is the reason to stay into the evening. Check current schedules before you build a night around one venue, then aim for at least one proper juke-joint set rather than treating the blues as something that ended in a museum case.

**Ground Zero Blues Club** is the easiest first stop, with regular shows and a downtown location. **Red's Lounge** is smaller, rougher, and closer to the Delta juke-joint idea many visitors came looking for.
