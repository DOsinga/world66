Harrell Prairie Botanical Area is a 160-acre remnant tallgrass prairie preserved within Bienville National Forest, about five miles south of Forest. Prairies are rare in Mississippi, and this one supports a rich variety of native grasses and wildflowers, including several state-rare plant species. The best time to visit is late summer and early autumn, when the grasses reach full height and the wildflowers are at peak bloom.

The prairie sits on a hilltop with open views in multiple directions — unusual in a state so densely forested. Walking across the open grassland feels genuinely different from anything else in the region. The site requires a short drive on forest roads from Highway 35; check with the Bienville National Forest ranger district for current road conditions.
