The Moore Lookout Tower is a historic fire lookout tower standing in Bienville National Forest north of Forest, on Scott County Road 503. It was built by the US Forest Service in the early 20th century as part of the regional fire detection network and is on the National Register of Historic Places.

The tower offers elevated views over the forest canopy — a rare perspective in this flat, heavily wooded terrain. It is accessible by car via forest roads, though the access road may be rough. Contact the Bienville National Forest office in Forest to confirm current access conditions before visiting.
