Forest's main appeal for visitors is the Bienville National Forest that surrounds it. The [Bienville Pines Scenic Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/forest/bienville_pines_scenic_area) is a 223-acre old-growth pine preserve with easy walking trails. The [Harrell Prairie Botanical Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/forest/harrell_prairie_botanical_area) protects a rare stretch of tallgrass prairie. The [Shockaloe Horse Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/forest/shockaloe_horse_trail) offers 23 miles of riding and hiking through deep forest. The [Moore Lookout Tower](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/forest/moore_lookout_tower) provides elevated views over the forest canopy.

In September, the Annual Wing Dang Doodle Festival brings blues music and a chicken-wing cooking contest to town — the livest Forest gets all year.
