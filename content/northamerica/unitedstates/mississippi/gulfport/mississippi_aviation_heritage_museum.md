The Mississippi Aviation Heritage Museum gives Gulfport a specialised but worthwhile aviation stop, with a strong emphasis on John C. Robinson, the Gulf Coast-born pilot known as the Brown Condor. Exhibits connect local flying history to broader stories of training, engineering, and Black aviation achievement.

It is not a large national air museum, but it adds depth to a coast better known for beaches, casinos, and seafood. Aviation enthusiasts should make time for it, and families may find the hands-on pieces more engaging than another waterfront stroll.
