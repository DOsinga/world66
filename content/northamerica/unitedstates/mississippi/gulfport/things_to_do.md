---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Gulfport's best asset is Ship Island, a barrier island accessible by ferry that offers some of the finest undeveloped beach on the Gulf Coast along with the Civil War ruins of Fort Massachusetts. In town, the Mississippi Aquarium opened in 2020 on the anniversary of Hurricane Katrina, replacing the Marine Life Oceanarium that was destroyed in the storm. The beachfront along Highway 90 and Jones Park are pleasant for walking, and the small-boat harbour is a quieter alternative to the casino strip concentrated further east toward Biloxi.
