TrainTastic is a hands-on railway museum in Gulfport, built around model trains, interactive exhibits, and the enduring appeal of watching miniature worlds move on schedule. It is especially useful for families, but there is enough railway detail to keep adult train enthusiasts engaged too.

This is not Mississippi's deepest history stop, and it does not pretend to be. It works because it is specific, tactile, and easy to combine with the aquarium or a rainy Gulf Coast afternoon.
