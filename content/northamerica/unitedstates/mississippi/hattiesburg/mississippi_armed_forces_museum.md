The Mississippi Armed Forces Museum at Camp Shelby is one of the strongest military-history museums in the region. Its exhibits cover Mississippi service members, uniforms, vehicles, weapons, and conflicts from the 19th century through modern deployments.

It is south of Hattiesburg and requires base-access awareness, so check current visitor procedures before going. For anyone interested in military history, the scale and depth make it a worthwhile trip from town.
