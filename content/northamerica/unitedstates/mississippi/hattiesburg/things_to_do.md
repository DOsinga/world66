---

Hattiesburg's attractions are low-key and genuinely local. The [Hattiesburg Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/hattiesburg/hattiesburg_zoo) in Kamper Park is free to enter — a rarity in American zoos. The [Tiger Drive-In](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/hattiesburg/tiger_drive_in) on Highway 49 is the only operating drive-in theater in Mississippi, running double features on Friday and Saturday nights.

The Civil Rights history is substantial and honestly presented. The [African American Military History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/hattiesburg/african_american_military_museum) on Mobile Street occupies the former segregated USO club, and [Smith Drug Co.](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/hattiesburg/smith_drug_co) documents the local voter registration campaigns of the 1960s. The [World Trade Center Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/hattiesburg/world_trade_center_memorial) on Main Street is easy to miss but worth pausing at.

The University of Southern Mississippi runs a lively [arts and performance calendar](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/hattiesburg/usm_arts) through the academic year, and the downtown creative district has the [Hattiesburg Pocket Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/mississippi/hattiesburg/hattiesburg_pocket_museum) — a rotating exhibition in a street-level window, always free.
