The Tiger Drive-In on Highway 49 is the only operating drive-in theater left in Mississippi, and one of fewer than 300 still operating in the entire United States. It shows double features on Friday and Saturday nights, and sometimes Sunday and during school holidays. Tune your car radio to the broadcast frequency for the audio; bring your own food or use the snack stand.

The experience is genuinely enjoyable — families in lawn chairs, teenagers in truck beds, the movie projected on a screen you can see from 100 metres. The films are usually recent releases rather than classics. The drive-in is south of downtown Hattiesburg on Highway 49; you'll see the screen from the road. Gates open about an hour before showtime. Arrive early on summer weekends to get a good spot.
