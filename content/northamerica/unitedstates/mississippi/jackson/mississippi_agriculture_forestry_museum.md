The Mississippi Agriculture & Forestry Museum combines a living-history farm, agricultural exhibits, gardens, a blacksmith shop, and displays on cotton, catfish, and rural town life. It is one of Jackson's better family stops, especially if children will enjoy the farm animals, train, and carousel.
