Mynelle Gardens is a small, shaded garden in west Jackson, built around ponds, footbridges, azaleas, camellias, and old-fashioned Southern planting. It is not a grand botanical garden, but it gives the city a gentle, green pause.

Spring bloom is the best time, though the paths and water make it pleasant whenever the weather is not too hot. Come for a quiet hour rather than a major outing, especially if you are balancing Jackson’s heavier civil-rights and history stops with something softer.
