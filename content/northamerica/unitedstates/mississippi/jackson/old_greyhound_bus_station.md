---

A perfectly preserved 1938 Streamline Moderne bus terminal on Lamar Street, now adapted for office use but with its curved glass blocks and chrome trim intact. The building is a small monument to one of the pivotal moments of the civil rights movement.

There is no museum here; this is a building to look at from the outside and read a plaque. The story is told properly across the street at the Civil Rights Museum.
