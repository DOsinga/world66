The Greek Revival Old State Capitol on State Street served as the seat of Mississippi government from 1839 to 1903. It is now a museum about the political and legislative history of the state — the secession debates, Reconstruction, the long resistance to civil rights.

The building itself is the most architecturally distinguished on the Jackson skyline. Free admission, and the exhibits are good.
