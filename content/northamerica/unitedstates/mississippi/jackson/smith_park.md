Jackson's oldest park, set aside in 1822 when the city was laid out, sits surrounded by the National Register-listed Smith Park Architectural District. The buildings around the square include the Federal Reserve, the Heidelberg Hotel, and a clutch of early-twentieth-century commercial blocks.

Bring a coffee and a book; the park itself is a quiet square of live oaks and benches in the middle of downtown.
