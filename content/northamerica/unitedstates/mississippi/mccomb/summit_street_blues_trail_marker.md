Summit Street was once McComb's Black business and entertainment district, with clubs and cafes that drew touring blues, jazz, rhythm and blues, and gospel acts. The Mississippi Blues Trail marker gives you the names and the setting: a once-busy strip on the circuit between Jackson and New Orleans.

Read it together with the Bo Diddley marker. Between them, McComb stops looking like just another interstate town and starts to show its place in the cultural map of southern Mississippi.
