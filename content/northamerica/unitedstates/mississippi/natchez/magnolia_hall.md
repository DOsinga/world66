Magnolia Hall is one of the grandest of the Greek Revival mansions clustered in the historic district, a five-bay Italianate building on Pearl Street with elaborate plasterwork cornices and original interior fittings largely intact. It was built by Thomas Henderson, a wealthy merchant, in 1858 and survived the Civil War largely because Union officers found it suitable for their own use.

The Natchez Garden Club operates it as a house museum, with period furnishings and a collection of historic costumes from the Natchez Pilgrimage. Tours are offered year-round; spring and fall coincide with the Pilgrimage events when the house is at its most atmospheric. The garden is worth a walk-through.
