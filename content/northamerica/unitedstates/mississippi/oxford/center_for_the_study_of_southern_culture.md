This antebellum building on the University of Mississippi campus was intended to house a huge telescope, but the Civil War and money trouble changed that plan. It now houses the Center for the Study of Southern Culture and hosts small exhibits. The story of the never-installed telescope gives the stop more character than its plain academic name suggests.
