---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Oxford is about an hour and a quarter southeast of Memphis on I-22 and about three and a half hours north of Jackson on I-55. There is no scheduled commercial passenger air service to Oxford itself — the small **University-Oxford Airport** handles only private aviation.

**By air.** The nearest commercial airports are **Memphis International** (MEM, ~1.5 hours by car) and **Jackson** (JAN, ~3.5 hours). Memphis is far better connected for international and domestic flights. Several daily shuttle and rental car services run between the Memphis airport and Oxford.

**By car.** From Memphis, I-22 east to Highway 7 south. From Jackson, I-55 north to Highway 6 east. From Nashville, around four and a half hours via I-40 and I-22. The drive is straightforward and the last stretch through the Holly Springs National Forest is wooded and pleasant.

**By bus.** Greyhound and the regional carrier **Megabus** stop in nearby Tupelo and Batesville. There is no Greyhound service direct to Oxford. The **Oxford-University Transit** runs limited shuttles to neighbouring towns.

**By train.** The nearest Amtrak station is in **Memphis** (the City of New Orleans line). There is no passenger rail service to Oxford.
