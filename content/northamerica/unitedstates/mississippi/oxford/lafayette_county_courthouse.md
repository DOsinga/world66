The Lafayette County Courthouse anchors the Square at the centre of Oxford and is the building Faulkner used over and over as the courthouse in his fictional Yoknapatawpha. The current structure — a square brick Federal building with a tall white cupola — dates from 1872, replacing the original 1840 courthouse burned by Union troops during the Civil War.

The lawn around the building is a small park and a regular meeting point. The Confederate monument that stood for over a century at the southern entrance was relocated to a Civil War cemetery north of campus in 2020. Inside, the building is still a working courthouse — visitors may walk the main hall during opening hours but should stay out of operating courtrooms. The interior staircase and original woodwork are worth a look.
