Square Books is the bookshop at the centre of one of America's most literary small towns. Opened in 1979 by Richard and Lisa Howorth in a small upstairs room on the Square, the shop has grown into three connected buildings: the original Square Books, **Off Square Books** across the street (used and remaindered), and **Square Books Jr** for children.

The main store carries a deeply curated Southern fiction and non-fiction section and is regularly cited as one of the best independent bookstores in the United States. The upstairs **café and balcony** overlooks the Square and is one of the better spots in town to read with a cup of coffee. The shop hosts dozens of author events a year — the calendar is worth checking before a visit.
