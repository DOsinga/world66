---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Oxford is small, walkable, and surprisingly dense with things worth seeing. The natural starting point is the **Courthouse Square** — the literal centre of town and the model for the square in Faulkner's Jefferson — with the antebellum **Lafayette County Courthouse** at its middle and ringed with independent bookshops, restaurants, and bars. **Square Books** is one of the most celebrated independent bookstores in the South and could easily eat half a morning.

A short walk southwest brings you to **Rowan Oak**, William Faulkner's home for thirty-two years, kept much as he left it. North of town the **University of Mississippi** campus has its own slow walking circuit through **The Grove**, the **Lyceum**, and the **University Museum**. Outside town, the lakes and woods of the **Sardis Reservoir** and the **Holly Springs National Forest** make for good half-day escapes.
