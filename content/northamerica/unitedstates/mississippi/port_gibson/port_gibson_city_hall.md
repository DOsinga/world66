Port Gibson City Hall is a restored Greek Revival building from around 1840, first built for the Port Gibson Female College. It later became the town hall, but the building is still interesting as part of Port Gibson's unusually rich collection of antebellum architecture.

Inside, the Allen Collection preserves early 20th-century photographs of Port Gibson and Claiborne County. It is a short stop, best combined with the town's churches and historic houses.
