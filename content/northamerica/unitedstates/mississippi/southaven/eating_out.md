---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Southaven's restaurant scene is dominated by national chains along Goodman Road, but a handful of locally owned places are worth seeking out — and the great Memphis barbecue houses are a short drive across the state line.

For genuinely local cooking, try **Cafe Olé** for Tex-Mex, **Bonne Terre Café** at the country inn of the same name for upscale Southern, and **Genghis Grill** for build-your-own Mongolian stir-fry. The local **Memphis Barbecue Co.** on Goodman Road, started by competition pitmasters John and Melissa Cookston, does some of the best pulled pork on the Mississippi side of the line. For breakfast, **Bryant's Breakfast** in Memphis is twelve minutes north and worth the drive.
