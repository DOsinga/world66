---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Southaven sits at the intersection of Interstates 55 and 69 just south of Memphis. Most visitors fly into **Memphis International Airport (MEM)**, which is fifteen minutes north of Southaven and one of FedEx's main global hubs — flights from across the United States are easy to find.

The car is essential. Public transit is minimal and ride-share covers Southaven but is more expensive than in Memphis proper. From Nashville the drive south on I-40 and then I-269/I-55 takes about three and a half hours.
