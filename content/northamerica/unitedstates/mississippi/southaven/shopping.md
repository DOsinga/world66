title: Shopping
type: section
---

Southaven is one of the Mid-South's biggest retail destinations. Shoppers from rural Mississippi, west Tennessee, and east Arkansas converge here for the sales-tax differential and the sheer choice on offer along Goodman Road and Airways Boulevard.

The **Tanger Outlets Southaven** opened in 2015 and is the centrepiece — an open-air outlet centre with around 75 stores including Nike, Coach, Polo Ralph Lauren, and Levi's. The **Southaven Towne Center** is the adjacent lifestyle centre with department stores, restaurants, and a multiplex. Between the two you can fill a serious afternoon and a serious car boot.
