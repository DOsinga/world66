The Birthplace of Elvis Presley is the reason many travellers know Tupelo exists. The small two-room house where Elvis was born in 1935 still stands on the site, surrounded by a museum complex, chapel, statues, and interpretive material that traces the early poverty and church music that shaped him before Memphis and fame changed everything.

The place is modest in scale, which is part of its force. It asks you to begin the Elvis story not with Graceland excess but with a poor Mississippi family, a borrowed few dollars for a house, and a child absorbing gospel music in a small Southern town. Even if you are only a casual fan, it is Tupelo's essential stop.
