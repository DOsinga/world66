Brice's Cross Roads National Battlefield Site marks a sharp, compact Civil War battlefield northeast of Tupelo. The 1864 fight is remembered for Confederate cavalry commander Nathan Bedford Forrest's defeat of a larger Union force, and the preserved crossroads gives a clearer sense of the tactical terrain than a large monument park would.

The site is modest, so it works best for travelers who already care about Civil War history or who are pairing it with Tupelo National Battlefield. Stop at the interpretive panels, read the sequence of the battle, and use the quiet rural setting to understand how road junctions and wooded ground shaped the fighting.
