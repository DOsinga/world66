---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tupelo has a small regional airport, Tupelo Regional Airport (TUP), with flights to Atlanta and Charlotte. Most visitors drive — the city sits at the intersection of US-78 (now I-22) and US-45, making it reasonably accessible from Memphis (about 1.5 hours west) and Birmingham (about 2.5 hours southeast). There is no passenger rail service.

Greyhound buses connect Tupelo to Memphis and other regional cities, but the schedule is limited and the station is not centrally located. A car is effectively required to reach most of the sites of interest.
