---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Tupelo's main draw is Elvis, but the city rewards a half-day of proper exploration beyond the birthplace. The [Natchez Trace Parkway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natchez_Trace_Parkway) begins almost at the city's doorstep and is one of the most beautiful drives in the South. The Tupelo National Battlefield is a compact, well-interpreted Civil War site that most visitors can cover in an hour.
