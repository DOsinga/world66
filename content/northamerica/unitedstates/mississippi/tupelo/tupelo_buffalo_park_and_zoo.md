Tupelo Buffalo Park and Zoo is a family-oriented wildlife stop outside the city, known for its bison herd, drive-through safari area, and smaller zoo exhibits. It broadens a Tupelo visit beyond Elvis sites and Civil War history, especially for travellers with children.

The experience is casual and weather-dependent, so it works best as a flexible half-day outing rather than a tightly scheduled museum visit. Check current animal encounters and opening times before driving out.
