---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Tupelo has a humid subtropical climate. Summers are hot and humid, with temperatures regularly above 35°C from June through August. Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons — March through May and September through November offer pleasant temperatures and the chance to see the Natchez Trace in bloom or in colour.

The annual Tupelo Elvis Festival takes place each summer, usually in late August, drawing Elvis fans from around the world to a weekend of tribute acts, memorabilia vendors, and general celebration of the King. If this is the reason for your visit, book accommodation well ahead — the town fills up. If you would prefer the birthplace without the crowds, go at any other time of year.
