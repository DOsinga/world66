The Biedenharn Museum of Coca-Cola Memorabilia is housed in the restored 1890 building where Coca-Cola was first bottled in 1894. That origin story gives the small museum more weight than a standard collection of advertising signs and soda bottles.

Inside are reproduction bottling works, Coca-Cola memorabilia, and an old soda fountain that help explain how a fountain drink became a bottled national brand. It is a compact downtown stop and pairs easily with Vicksburg's riverfront museums.
