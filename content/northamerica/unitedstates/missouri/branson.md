Branson is Missouri's loud, busy, unapologetic Ozarks resort town: live shows, family attractions, outlet malls, lake weekends, and enough neon along Highway 76 to make subtlety feel like a foreign language. It is easy to mock and easier to underestimate.

The best Branson trip balances the spectacle with the hills and water around it. See a show, ride the [Branson Scenic Railway](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/branson/branson_scenic_railway), wander [Branson Landing](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/branson/branson_landing), then get out to [Table Rock Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/branson/table_rock_lake), [Lake Taneycomo](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/branson/lake_taneycomo), or [Ruth and Paul Henning Conservation Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/branson/henning_conservation_area).

Families come for [Silver Dollar City](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/branson/silver_dollar_city), the [Titanic Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/branson/titanic_museum), [White Water](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/branson/white_water), and the strip's endless mini-golf-and-music energy. If that sounds exhausting, it can be. Choose a few things, leave breathing room, and let the Ozark landscape do some of the work.
