---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Most visitors drive to Branson. US Route 65 connects the city with Springfield to the north and Arkansas to the south, while Missouri Route 76 is the famous attraction strip once you are in town.

Springfield-Branson National Airport is the main practical airport for most travelers, about an hour away by car. Branson also has smaller aviation facilities nearby, but you should not plan a trip around them unless you have checked current service carefully.
