The Hollywood Wax Museum is pure Branson strip entertainment: celebrity figures, photo setups, and a building designed to stop traffic. Go in with the right expectations and it can be silly fun.

This is not a museum in the sober sense. It is a pop-culture attraction for families, groups, and anyone who wants an hour indoors between bigger plans.
