Silver Dollar City is Branson's signature theme park, built around an old-time Ozark village idea with rides, crafts, music, food, and seasonal festivals. It is polished and commercial, but it has more regional character than many theme parks.

Families can easily spend a full day here. Go early, pace yourself, and do not ignore the craft demonstrations if you want something more memorable than coaster queues.
