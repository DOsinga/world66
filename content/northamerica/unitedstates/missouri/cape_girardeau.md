Cape Girardeau sits on a limestone bluff above a bend in the Mississippi River in the southeastern corner of Missouri. Known locally as "the Cape," it's the largest city in the region and home to Southeast Missouri State University, which gives it more life than most towns this size. The historic downtown along Broadway runs close to the river with a [floodwall painted in murals](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/cape_girardeau/mississippi_river_tales_mural) depicting local and regional history.

The area carries real historical weight. The Trail of Tears passed through here in the 1830s — [Trail of Tears State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/cape_girardeau/trail_of_tears_state_park) north of town marks one of the Cherokee crossing points on the Mississippi — and the region saw significant Civil War activity, including the earthwork fortifications of [Fort A](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/cape_girardeau/fort_a_site) on the bluffs above town. The [1854 Court of Common Pleas](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/cape_girardeau/cape_girardeau_court_of_common_pleas) and the [Warehouse Row Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/cape_girardeau/warehouse_row_historic_district) give the downtown genuine architectural distinction.
