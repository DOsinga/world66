title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cape Girardeau's appeal is concentrated in its historic downtown and its relationship with the Mississippi River. The floodwall murals, the bluffs, and a compact collection of museums and historic buildings form a half-day circuit. The Trail of Tears National Historic Trail passes through town, and the Cape Girardeau Court of Common Pleas building — one of the oldest courthouses on the Mississippi — anchors a downtown with genuine historic depth.
