The MKT Trail (Missouri-Kansas-Texas) runs southwest from downtown Columbia for about 5 km before joining the Katy Trail, which follows the Missouri River valley for 240 miles across the state. Together they represent one of the best rail-trail systems in the United States — flat, scenic, and well-maintained.

The Columbia section of the MKT is an easy ride or walk, passing through woodland and creek bottomland before reaching the Katy Trail junction. From there, cyclists can follow the river east through the pretty river town of Hartsburg or west toward Jefferson City. The trail surface is compacted gravel, suitable for hybrid or gravel bikes. Bikes can be rented in Columbia from several shops near the campus, or at outfitters along the Katy Trail.
