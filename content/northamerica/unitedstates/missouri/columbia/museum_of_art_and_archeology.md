The Museum of Art and Archaeology is the University of Missouri's broad teaching museum, with collections that range from ancient Mediterranean material to European, American and Asian art. Its strength is variety: the galleries are built for looking closely rather than rushing through a blockbuster show.

For visitors to Columbia, it is a thoughtful indoor stop that reflects the city's university character. Check current location and hours before going, as campus museums can shift galleries or close between exhibitions.
