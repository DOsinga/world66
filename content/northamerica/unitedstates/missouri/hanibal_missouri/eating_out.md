---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Hannibal's eating-out scene is small and centred on Main Street. **Mark Twain Dinette** — a 1940s diner across from the boyhood home — serves the kind of American breakfast and lunch food the town runs on: pancakes, root beer, pork tenderloin sandwiches. **Lula Belle's** in the old red-light district building on North Main does upmarket American with a river view.

For something less obvious, **Rustic Oak Cabin** and **Big Muddy BBQ** serve good slow-cooked meat at lower prices, and the food trucks at Riverfront Park during summer festivals are reliable. Hannibal is a wine-making region; the small **Cave Hollow West Winery** at Mark Twain Cave is worth a stop for a tasting.
