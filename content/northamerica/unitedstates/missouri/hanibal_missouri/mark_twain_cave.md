A short drive south of town, Mark Twain Cave is the oldest commercial show cave in Missouri, open for tours since 1886. Guides take you through about half a mile of cool limestone passages and explain the cave's geology, its history, and the connection to Tom Sawyer.

The cave stays at about 12 C year-round — a relief in summer, when central Missouri can get into the high thirties. There is a small winery on the grounds and a gift shop, but the cave is the reason to go.
