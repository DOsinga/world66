Riverview Park gives Hannibal its broadest Mississippi River views, with roads and paths winding through wooded blufftop ground. It is a quieter counterweight to the Mark Twain sights downtown.

The park is best for a drive, picnic, or short walk to viewpoints. Come in clear weather, when the river bends and Illinois shore make the town's geography obvious.
