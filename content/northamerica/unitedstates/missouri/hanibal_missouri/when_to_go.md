---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Hannibal has the typical Midwestern climate: long cold winters, hot humid summers, pleasant springs and autumns. **May, June, September, and October** are the most comfortable months and the best for walking the historic district and climbing Cardiff Hill.

The biggest event of the year is **National Tom Sawyer Days**, held over the Fourth of July weekend, with parades, a frog-jumping contest, and a fence-painting championship. Late September brings the **Big River Steampunk Festival** along Main Street. Avoid January and February unless you genuinely want to see an iced-up Mississippi — many attractions reduce their hours or close.
