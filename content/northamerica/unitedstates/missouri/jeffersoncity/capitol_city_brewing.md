The Capitol City Brewing Company is Jefferson City's home brewpub, a reliable stop for a beer and a meal after a stretch on the Katy Trail. They brew a rotating selection of ales and lagers alongside the expected pub menu — burgers, sandwiches, salads. The location near the downtown core makes it a natural end point to a day of sightseeing, and the patio is pleasant in warm weather. Nothing extraordinary, but exactly what a state capital town needs.
