Runge Nature Center is a Missouri conservation center on the west side of Jefferson City, with wildlife exhibits, an indoor viewing area, a small nature library, and more than two miles of walking trails. The Naturescape Trail is accessible, and the short loop trails make it an easy outdoor stop without leaving town for long.
