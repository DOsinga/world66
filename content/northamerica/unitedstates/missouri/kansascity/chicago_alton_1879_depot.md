The Chicago & Alton 1879 Depot is a restored railway depot in Independence. The first floor preserves the waiting room, stationmaster's room, and baggage room, while the upstairs rooms show the stationmaster's living quarters.

Period furnishings, old railway advertising, stoves, and benches make it a small but focused stop for visitors interested in rail history and Independence's transport past.
