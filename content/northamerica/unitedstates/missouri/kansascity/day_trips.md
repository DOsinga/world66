---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

**Independence, Missouri** (15 miles east) was Harry Truman's hometown and home to the excellent Truman Presidential Library and Museum, which underwent a major renovation in 2020. The city is also the official starting point of the Santa Fe, Oregon, and California Trails — a visitor center marks the point where the wagon trains gathered.

**Weston, Missouri** (45 miles north) is a well-preserved 19th-century river town with brick storefronts, antique shops, and several wineries and distilleries. McCormick Distilling Company, one of the oldest in Missouri, offers tours. The town is small and walkable.

**Lawrence, Kansas** (45 miles west) is a university town with a lively main street, strong indie bookshop and record store scene, and a history as a flashpoint of the pre-Civil War "Bleeding Kansas" conflict. The Quantrill's Raid massacre of 1863 is well interpreted at the local museum.

**Excelsior Springs, Missouri** (40 miles northeast) has a collection of 1920s mineral springs spa architecture — the Hall of Waters is one of the finest Art Deco buildings in the region and has been restored as an event venue and spa.
