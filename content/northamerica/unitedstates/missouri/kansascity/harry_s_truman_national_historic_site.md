The Harry S. Truman National Historic Site preserves the Independence home where the 33rd president lived before and after the White House. The rooms remain much as Harry and Bess Truman left them, making the guided tour feel unusually domestic for a presidential site.

Pair it with the Truman Presidential Library and Museum if you want the full story: the house gives you the private life, while the library handles the politics and the presidency.
