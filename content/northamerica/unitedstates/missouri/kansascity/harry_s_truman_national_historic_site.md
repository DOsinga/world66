Harry S. Truman National Historic Site preserves the Independence home of the 33rd president of the United States. The house is furnished much as it was when Truman lived there, and visits are by guided National Park Service tour.

It is a strong companion to the Truman Presidential Library and Museum, giving a more domestic view of Truman's life before and after the presidency.
