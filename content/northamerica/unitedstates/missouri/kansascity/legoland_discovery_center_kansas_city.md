Legoland Discovery Center Kansas City is an indoor Lego attraction in Crown Center aimed mainly at younger children. Expect model displays, hands-on building areas, small rides, and play zones rather than a full outdoor theme park.

It works best as a family stop paired with nearby Union Station or Crown Center. Adults visiting without children should check entry rules before going, as the attraction is designed around child-focused visits.
