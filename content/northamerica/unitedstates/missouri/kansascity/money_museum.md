The Money Museum at the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City turns currency, banking, and monetary policy into public exhibits. Displays on cash handling, security, and the financial system make abstract economics more concrete.

It is a good short stop for families, students, and visitors interested in civic institutions. Security procedures may apply, so check current visitor requirements before going.
