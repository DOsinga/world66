Sea Life Kansas City Aquarium is an indoor aquarium in Crown Center, with close-up displays of sharks, rays, seahorses, starfish, and other marine life. It is compact and family-oriented rather than a full-day aquarium.

It works best as part of a Crown Center or Union Station visit, especially for families with children or for a weatherproof stop near downtown Kansas City.
