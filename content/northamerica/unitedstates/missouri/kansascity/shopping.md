The [Country Club Plaza](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/kansascity/country_club_plaza) is Kansas City's main upscale shopping district, with a mix of national brands and a few independent shops in Spanish Colonial Revival buildings. It is pleasant to walk even without buying anything.

For independent retail, the [Crossroads Arts District](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/kansascity/crossroads_arts_district) has galleries, vintage shops, and design-oriented stores. [Westport](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/kansascity/westport) has vintage clothing stores, record shops, and independent booksellers. [Brookside](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/kansascity/brookside) has a compact strip of specialty food and gift shops.

The [River Market](/northamerica/unitedstates/missouri/kansascity/river_market) international grocery stores — particularly the Hong Kong Supermarket and the African and Caribbean grocers in the City Market area — are excellent for food shopping, with produce and ingredients unavailable elsewhere in the city.
