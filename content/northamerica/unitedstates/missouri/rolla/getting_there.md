---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Rolla sits directly on Interstate 44, making it an easy stop between St. Louis (90 miles east) and Springfield (100 miles west). The nearest commercial airport is St. Louis Lambert International Airport (STL), about 100 miles away. Greyhound buses stop in Rolla on the I-44 corridor route. Rolla Downtown Airport, southwest of the city, handles small private aircraft.
