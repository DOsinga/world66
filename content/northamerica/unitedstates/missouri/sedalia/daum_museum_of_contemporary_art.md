Daum Museum of Contemporary Art is the strongest year-round cultural stop in Sedalia, with a collection that reaches well beyond what you might expect from a small Missouri city. Exhibits include modern and contemporary painting, sculpture, works on paper, and rotating shows in a clean campus setting.

The museum is especially useful outside state fair season, when Sedalia's visitor energy can feel quieter. It gives art-minded travelers a reason to linger and balances the city's fairground and railroad associations.
