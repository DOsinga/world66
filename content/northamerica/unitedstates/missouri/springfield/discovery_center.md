Discovery Center is Springfield's hands-on science museum, built around interactive exhibits rather than quiet cases. It is especially useful for families, with enough buttons, experiments, and practical demonstrations to keep children engaged on a hot, cold, or rainy day.

Adults travelling without kids may prefer the city's larger museums and outdoor stops, but this still fills an important niche. Put it on the list when you need an indoor activity that feels active rather than like a waiting room.
