The Route 66 Car Museum leans into Springfield's place on the old Mother Road. Its collection includes classic American cars, European models, movie-style oddities, and motoring memorabilia, all arranged for easy browsing rather than academic study.

It is a good fit if your Springfield visit is already about Route 66, diners, neon, and roadside Americana. Non-car people may find it quick, but enthusiasts can happily linger over the details of chrome, fins, badges, and dashboards.
