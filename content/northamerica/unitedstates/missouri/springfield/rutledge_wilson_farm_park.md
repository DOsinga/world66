Rutledge-Wilson Farm Park gives Springfield a hands-on rural attraction on the west side of town. Children can see farm animals, gardens, and seasonal displays without leaving the city.

It is most useful for families or anyone curious about the agricultural landscape around the Ozarks. Pair it with one of Springfield's larger parks if you want an easy outdoor day.
