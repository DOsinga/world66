Springfield Conservation Nature Center is an easy way to sample Ozarks nature without leaving the city. Trails loop through forest, glades, and creekside habitat, with a visitor centre that explains local wildlife and conservation work.

It is a good morning walk, especially with children or anyone who wants fresh air between museums, restaurants, and Route 66 stops. Keep an eye out for deer and birds, and choose your trail length honestly; the paths are well kept, but Missouri heat can make even a modest loop feel longer.
