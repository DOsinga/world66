The original Bass Pro Shops flagship store is one of the most visited retail destinations in the United States, and calling it a store sells it short. The sprawling building — styled to look like a massive Ozark lodge — contains a four-story indoor waterfall, multiple large aquariums stocked with native fish species, a museum's worth of taxidermied wildlife, antique boats and hunting memorabilia, and enough outdoor gear to outfit an expedition to any corner of the world. Bass Pro Shops founder Johnny Morris opened the original shop here in 1972, and the Springfield location has since grown into something closer to a theme park than a sporting goods store.

Plan to spend at least an hour wandering through the departments: the archery range, the indoor shooting gallery, the massive fishing section, the wildlife gallery with its bronze sculptures and mounted record catches. Even visitors with no interest in hunting or fishing tend to be impressed by the sheer scale and presentation. The store is free to enter, located in the heart of Springfield, and open daily. It anchors a commercial district that includes an aquarium, bowling alley, and restaurants.
