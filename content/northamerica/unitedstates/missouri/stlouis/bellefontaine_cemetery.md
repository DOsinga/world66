Bellefontaine Cemetery, opened in 1849 north of downtown, was the first rural cemetery west of the Mississippi — laid out as a landscaped park as well as a burial ground, with winding roads and specimen trees. It is now one of the great open-air museums of St Louis civic history.

The roll call of the buried is remarkable: explorer William Clark of Lewis and Clark; brewer Adolphus Busch in a mausoleum modelled on a Bavarian chapel; poet Sara Teasdale; novelist William S Burroughs; Pulitzer Prize founders. The cemetery offers free maps for self-guided tours and runs occasional themed walks. The neighbouring Calvary Cemetery, where Tennessee Williams is buried, can be visited on the same trip.
