The Cardinals Hall of Fame and Museum is housed in Ballpark Village next to Busch Stadium. It holds the second-largest team-specific collection of baseball memorabilia in the country, behind only the National Hall of Fame in Cooperstown — over 16,000 items including the bat Mark McGwire used to hit his 70th home run in 1998, and Stan Musial's first major-league contract.

The exhibits trace the team from its founding in 1882 through its 11 World Series titles. There's a stadium tour combo ticket worth considering if you have time. Cardinals fandom is essentially a regional religion in this part of the country; the museum is the cathedral.
