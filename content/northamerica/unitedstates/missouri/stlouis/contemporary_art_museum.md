CAM, in the Grand Center arts district near St Louis University, is a non-collecting museum dedicated to contemporary art. Without a permanent collection, the program turns over fast — typically four or five exhibitions a year of recent or commissioned work by artists working internationally and locally.

The building, designed by Brad Cloepfil's Allied Works Architecture and opened in 2003, is itself worth seeing — a stripped-back concrete and steel shed that frames the work without competing with it. Free admission, with a strong public events programme of talks and screenings. Pair with the Pulitzer Arts Foundation next door for a half-day of contemporary art.
