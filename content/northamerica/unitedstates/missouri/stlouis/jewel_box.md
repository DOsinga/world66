The Jewel Box is a stepped-pyramid greenhouse in the southeast corner of Forest Park, built in 1936 in a striking Art Deco style by the Works Progress Administration. The cantilevered glass walls — designed to withstand the hailstones that had shattered earlier conservatories — were technically innovative for the time and still look futuristic.

Inside, the central display rotates with the seasons: chrysanthemums in autumn, poinsettias at Christmas, lilies at Easter, then a long summer of tropicals. There is a small admission fee on Wednesday and Saturday mornings; free the rest of the time. The Jewel Box is a popular wedding venue and is sometimes closed to private events on weekends — check ahead.
