The Lemp Mansion was the home of the family that ran the second-largest brewery in St Louis in the 19th century. Built in the 1860s as a private mansion just south of the brewery, it is now a restaurant, bed-and-breakfast, and the site of nightly ghost tours that play heavily on the family's tragic 20th-century history.

Even setting aside the ghost stories, the building is impressive: a 33-room Italianate mansion with stained-glass windows imported from Italy, hand-painted ceilings, and a private tunnel that once connected to the brewery. Dinner in the original dining room is a more relaxed introduction than the late-night tours. Pair with the Lemp Brewery ruins next door, which can be glimpsed from the surrounding streets.
