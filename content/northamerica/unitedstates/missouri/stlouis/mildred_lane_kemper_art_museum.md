Mildred Lane Kemper Art Museum is Washington University's art museum, with a collection that reaches from 19th-century European work to modern and contemporary art. It is one of the older art museums west of the Mississippi, and the Fumihiko Maki-designed building makes it a worthwhile stop for art-minded visitors already heading toward Forest Park or the university area.
