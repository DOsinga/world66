Soulard Market is one of St. Louis's oldest public institutions, a covered market where produce stalls, butchers, spices, flowers, snacks, and weekend crowds keep the neighbourhood's old market role alive. It is liveliest on Saturday, when the place feels less like a preserved landmark and more like a working city habit.

Come hungry and unhurried. The market pairs naturally with a walk through Soulard's brick streets or a visit to the nearby brewery, and it gives a more everyday counterpoint to the city's museums, arches, and big-ticket attractions.
