The Delmar Loop is a walkable entertainment district along Delmar Boulevard near the St. Louis and University City line. It is known for independent shops, restaurants, music venues, record stores, and busy street life.

The district has a strong local identity and is tied to St. Louis music history, including Chuck Berry's long-running appearances at Blueberry Hill. It is a good area for an evening walk, dinner, or live music.
