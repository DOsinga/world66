The World Chess Hall of Fame moved to St Louis in 2011, settling in the Central West End across the street from the Saint Louis Chess Club — itself one of the leading chess centres in the world. St Louis has emerged unexpectedly as the capital of American chess, largely through the patronage of billionaire Rex Sinquefield.

The museum's exhibitions rotate every few months and combine chess history with broader cultural connections — the role of chess during the Cold War, chess in art and pop music, the rise of online play. Free admission. The gift shop is unexpectedly good, and the giant chess piece on the sidewalk outside is one of the city's stranger photo opportunities.
