Big Sky Resort is the main reason most visitors know the town. Its terrain spreads across Lone Mountain and connected ski areas, with a reputation for long runs, serious steeps, and fewer crowds than the better-known Colorado resorts.

Winter is the point, but the mountain is not idle in summer. Hiking, biking, scenic lift rides, and events keep the resort useful once the snow has gone.
