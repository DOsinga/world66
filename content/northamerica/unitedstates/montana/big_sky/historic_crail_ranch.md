Historic Crail Ranch is a useful corrective to Big Sky's modern resort polish. The preserved homestead buildings date from the early 1900s, when this was ranch country rather than a ski destination.

The museum is small and seasonal, but it gives the meadow a past. Stop here when you want to understand what Big Sky was before lifts, condos, and second homes changed the valley.
