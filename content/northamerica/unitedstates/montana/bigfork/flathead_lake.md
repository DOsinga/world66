Bigfork sits at the northeastern corner of Flathead Lake, the largest natural freshwater lake west of the Mississippi River. The lake stretches 30 miles to the south and is so clear and cold that it remains excellent for swimming, kayaking, sailing, and fishing throughout the summer.

From Bigfork, the lake is immediately accessible — you can walk to the water in minutes from the village centre. Kayak and paddleboard rentals are available in town and at Wayfarers State Park, just south of the village. The lake supports a good population of lake trout and bull trout, and fishing guides operate out of Bigfork Marina.

Sailing is a genuine local tradition — Flathead Lake has reliable afternoon breezes and enough open water to make it one of the better sailing lakes in the inland Northwest. The Flathead Lake Sailing Association runs races throughout summer.
