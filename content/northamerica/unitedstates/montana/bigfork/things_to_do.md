---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bigfork's pleasures are mostly outdoors and on the water. The village itself is compact enough to walk in an hour, but Flathead Lake, the Swan River, and the mountains behind the town offer far more than a day's worth of activity. The Bigfork Summer Playhouse gives the town a cultural centre-of-gravity that's unusual for a place this small.
