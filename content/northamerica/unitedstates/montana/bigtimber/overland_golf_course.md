Overland Golf Course is a minor but pleasant Big Timber activity, especially if you want a low-pressure round with a mountain backdrop. The Crazy Mountains make the setting stronger than the course's modest profile would suggest.

Non-golfers can skip it. Golfers looking for an easy local activity after a drive, museum stop, or river morning will find it fits the town's unpretentious rhythm.
