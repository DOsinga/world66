The Yellowstone River Trout Hatchery is a practical stop for travelers curious about Montana's fishing culture. It raises and manages fish in a state where trout streams are both an ecological concern and a serious part of local recreation.

Call ahead if you want a proper tour, as access can depend on staffing and operations. Even a short visit pairs well with time along the Yellowstone or Boulder rivers, where the hatchery's work connects directly to what anglers come here for.
