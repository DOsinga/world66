DanWalt Gardens is a small, carefully tended garden in Billings, the kind of place that works best when you want a gentle pause rather than a major sight. Seasonal flowers, mature trees, and shaded corners make it feel more personal than municipal.

Visit expectations matter here. This is not a vast botanical garden, but it is a pleasant local stop and a good change of pace from sandstone rims, breweries, and downtown streets.
