The original craft brewery in Billings, operating since 1994 in a converted historic building on North Broadway. The beers are solid regional craft ales and lagers — the Whitetail Wheat and Big Sky Stout are reliable choices — and the kitchen serves burgers, wings, and pub standards that pair well with them. A good central option if you want a decent beer in a non-corporate setting. Open daily from 11am.
