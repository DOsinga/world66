Oasis Waterpark is a summer family attraction in Billings, with slides, pools, and splash features aimed at hot-weather relief rather than sightseeing. It fills a practical niche in a city where many stronger attractions are museums, trails, or regional drives.

Check dates and hours before going, because the appeal depends entirely on weather and season. Travellers without children may skip it, but families can treat it as an easy afternoon break.
