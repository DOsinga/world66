One of Billings' better farm-to-table restaurants, with a menu that changes with the seasons and a commitment to sourcing from Montana ranches and farms. Expect well-prepared Montana beef alongside lamb, elk, and seasonal vegetables — the cooking is more careful than the cowboy-town setting might suggest. Dinner only; reservations recommended on weekends. On 1st Avenue North downtown.
