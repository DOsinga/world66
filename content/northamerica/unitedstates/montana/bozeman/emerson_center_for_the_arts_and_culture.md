The Emerson Center for the Arts and Culture fills a former 1918 school building listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Inside are galleries, studios, classes, concerts, and community arts events.

It is worth checking the current programme before you go, since the experience depends on what is showing. The Jessie Wilber Gallery is the main reason to make a short visit.
