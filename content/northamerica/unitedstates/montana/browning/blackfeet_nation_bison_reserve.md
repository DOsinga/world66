The Blackfeet Nation Bison Reserve sits along US 2 between Browning and East Glacier. It is not a zoo or a staged attraction; the point is seeing the tribal herd in open prairie country, with the Rocky Mountain Front close by.

Sightings depend on where the animals are, so keep expectations loose. Even a short look from the road adds context to the reservation and to the Blackfeet story beyond the museum cases.
