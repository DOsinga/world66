Lodgepole Gallery sits a few miles west of Browning on open prairie near the approach to Glacier. The gallery focuses on contemporary and traditional Native art, with Blackfeet cultural programming tied to the wider camp.

The accommodation side is not the reason to list it here. The value for a day visitor is the art and the chance to meet the cultural landscape outside the museum setting.
