Butte Irish Festival reflects one of the city's strongest cultural identities: the Irish mining
families who helped shape Uptown Butte, its unions, parishes, saloons, and political life. The event
brings that history into the streets through music, dance, food, and community gatherings.

It is schedule-dependent, but it clears the threshold because Butte's Irish heritage is not
incidental decoration; it is central to the city's story. If your visit overlaps the festival, it is
a better cultural lens than many standard attractions.
