A small open-air memorial on the hillside above Uptown, commemorating the 1917 Granite Mountain - Speculator fire. Interpretation panels lay out the events of that night and the labour struggles that followed; the memorial is a few hundred metres from the surviving headframe of the original mine.

The view from up here, looking down on the city and across at the Berkeley Pit, is one of the best in town. The memorial is open all year and free to visit.
