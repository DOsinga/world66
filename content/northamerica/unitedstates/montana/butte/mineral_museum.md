The Mineral Museum at Montana Tech displays minerals from Montana and around the world, including crystals, ores, and specimens tied to Butte's mining history. It is a good companion to the World Museum of Mining, with more focus on geology than on the town's labor and industrial story.

The museum also gives a look at earthquake monitoring in Montana, with seismology displays connected to the Montana Bureau of Mines and Geology.
