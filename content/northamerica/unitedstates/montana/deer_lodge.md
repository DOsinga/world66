**Deer Lodge** sits in a wide valley between the Flint Creek and Highland mountain ranges, a small Montana town with a disproportionately interesting past. It is the county seat of Powell County and home to around 3,000 people.

The main draw is the [Old Montana State Prison](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/deer_lodge/old_montana_prison), right at the south end of Main Street. The original prison operated from 1871 to 1979, and the old cellblock is now a museum complex that includes an automobile museum, a frontier Montana museum, and a theatre. It is genuinely fascinating — the crumbling cell tiers and guard towers make for an atmospheric visit. At the north end of town, [Grant-Kohrs Ranch National Historic Site](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/deer_lodge/grant_kohrs_ranch) preserves a complete 19th-century cattle operation run by the National Park Service.

Deer Lodge was also once a division headquarters for the Milwaukee Road railroad before its abandonment in 1980, and railroad history still marks the town. Main Street is now a [historic district](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/deer_lodge/deer_lodge_historic_district) of well-preserved late-Victorian commercial buildings. The surrounding valley offers good fishing on the [Clark Fork River](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/deer_lodge/clark_fork_river) and access to the mountains for hiking and hunting.
