Yesterday's Playthings is a small museum of toys, dolls, and childhood objects in Deer Lodge. It is not the town's headline attraction, but it adds a softer, more personal stop beside the prison museum and Grant-Kohrs Ranch.

Families and nostalgia-minded travellers will get the most from it. Check opening hours before counting on it, since small-town museums can be seasonal or volunteer-run.
