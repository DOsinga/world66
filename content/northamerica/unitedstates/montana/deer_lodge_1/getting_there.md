---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Deer Lodge is on Interstate 90, about 75 km northwest of Butte and 100 km east of Missoula. Both cities have airports with connections to major hubs; flying into either and renting a car is the practical option. Greyhound buses stop in Deer Lodge on the I-90 corridor, but service is limited and a car is essential for exploring the surrounding valley and mountains.
