The Old Montana Prison operated as a state penitentiary from 1871 to 1979, when overcrowding and a series of violent incidents led to the construction of a modern facility outside town. The old cellblock, guard towers, and perimeter walls have been preserved as a museum complex at the south end of Main Street.

Visitors can walk through the original cell tiers — still lined with narrow iron cells — through the exercise yard, and into the guard towers. The site also includes the Powell County Museum of Montana history, an automobile museum with over a hundred vintage vehicles, and a theatre used for summer performances. The combination of crumbling cell blocks and Western artefacts makes for a genuinely unexpected visit. Open daily; admission charged.
