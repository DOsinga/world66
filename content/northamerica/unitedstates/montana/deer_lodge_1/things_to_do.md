---
title: "Things to Do in Deer Lodge"
type: section
---

Deer Lodge's main attraction is the Old Montana Prison museum complex at the south end of Main Street — one of the more atmospheric historical sites in the state. Beyond the prison, the town sits in the Deer Lodge Valley at the foot of the Flint Creek Range, with the Clark Fork River running through and good access to fishing and backcountry. It is an honest small Montana city with more historical depth than its size suggests.
