A no-frills working bar on Main Street that fills up with fishing guides and locals after the boats come off the water around six. Wood floors, neon beer signs, decent pub food, and the kind of half-friendly half-distracted bartending that comes with people who know almost everyone in the room. It is not a craft cocktail place; it is where you go after a long day in waders.
