Camas Road is a quieter west-side route through Glacier National Park, running roughly parallel to Inside North Fork Road. Unlike that rougher track, Camas Road is paved for most of its length, making it a useful scenic drive toward Polebridge and the park's less crowded northwest corner.

It is not as dramatic as Going-to-the-Sun Road, but that is partly the point. Use it for forested views, access to the Huckleberry Mountain Nature Trail, and a slower approach to the North Fork country.
