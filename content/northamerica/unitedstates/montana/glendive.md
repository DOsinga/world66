*North America > United States > Montana > Glendive*

Glendive is an eastern Montana town with one very good reason to stop: the badlands of [Makoshika State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/glendive/makoshika_state_park) rise almost from the edge of town. The eroded hills are part of the Hell Creek Formation, a fossil-rich landscape tied to Triceratops, Tyrannosaurus rex, and other late Cretaceous finds.

That dinosaur identity gives Glendive more pull than its size suggests. The state park visitor centre, the [Frontier Gateway Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/glendive/frontier_gateway_museum), and the private [Glendive Dinosaur and Fossil Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/glendive/glendive_dinosaur_and_fossil_museum) make it one of the clearer fossil stops on the long drive across eastern Montana.

The town is still a small highway and railroad place, so expectations matter. Come for badlands light, short trails, and fossil context, not for urban charm.
