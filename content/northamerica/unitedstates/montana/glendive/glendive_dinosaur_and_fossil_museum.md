Glendive Dinosaur and Fossil Museum is a large private fossil museum near the interstate. It has dinosaur skeleton casts, fossil displays, and a clear focus on prehistoric life from the surrounding region.

Travellers should know the framing before they go: the museum presents its exhibits through a Biblical creationist interpretation. If that is not what you want from a science museum, Makoshika and Frontier Gateway will be the better stops.
