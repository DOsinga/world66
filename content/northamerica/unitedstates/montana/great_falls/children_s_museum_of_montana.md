The Children's Museum of Montana is a practical family stop in Great Falls, built around play-based exhibits rather than formal displays. It is most useful for younger children who need room to touch, build, pretend, and reset during a road trip.

Adults travelling without children can safely skip it, but families may find it one of the easier indoor breaks in town. Check current hours before relying on it, as smaller children's museums often adjust schedules around staffing and school calendars.
