Gibson Park is Great Falls' classic city park, close to downtown and easy to use between museum visits or river stops. Ponds, lawns, flower gardens, and walking paths make it a relaxed place rather than a wilderness outing.

It is especially useful for families, with playgrounds and open space, and it often hosts concerts or community events in warm weather. Pair it with the Missouri River trail system for a gentler side of Great Falls.
