The H. Earl Clack Museum is a small, useful context stop for Havre. Its collections cover local history, prehistoric finds, railway life, and the wider Hill County story, with enough variety to justify a short visit before or after the bigger sights.

It also helps organize visits to Wahkpa Chu'gn, so check with the museum if you are planning around tour times.
