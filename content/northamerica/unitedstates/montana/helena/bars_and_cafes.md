---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Montana's craft beer scene is well-represented in Helena, with a handful of taprooms that serve as the city's primary social spaces after work hours. The two main options — Lewis & Clark Brewing and Blackfoot River Brewing — are genuine neighborhood places with loyal local followings, not tourist traps dressed in reclaimed wood. Both are within easy walking distance of downtown.

Wine bars are sparse; this is beer and whiskey country by inclination. For coffee, the independent options along Last Chance Gulch hold up well — Helena doesn't feel underserved on espresso. The overall after-dark scene is quiet by most urban standards, which suits the city's character: Helena is a working capital, not a party town. Friday evenings around the taprooms have energy; the rest of the week is considerably more settled.
