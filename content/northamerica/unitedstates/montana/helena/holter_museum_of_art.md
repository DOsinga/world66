Holter Museum of Art is Helena’s main contemporary art stop, showing regional, national, and rotating exhibitions in a compact downtown setting. It gives the Montana capital a cultural counterweight to the cathedral, capitol, and mining-era streets.

The visit is manageable rather than monumental, but that suits Helena. Stop in while walking downtown, especially if the current exhibition leans into Montana artists or the landscape and social questions of the wider West.
