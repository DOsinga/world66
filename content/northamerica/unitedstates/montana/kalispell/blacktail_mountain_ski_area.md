Blacktail Mountain Ski Area is a regional winter destination above the Flathead Valley, close enough to Kalispell to serve as a practical day on snow. Its appeal is a quieter, local atmosphere with views toward Flathead Lake and the surrounding mountains.

It is seasonal and weather-dependent, so check operations before building a trip around it. For visitors staying in Kalispell, Blacktail offers a simpler alternative to driving farther for larger resorts, especially for families and intermediate skiers.
