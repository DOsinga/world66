---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Glacier Park International Airport (FCA) is 7 miles northeast of Kalispell and serves the region year-round with direct flights from major western hubs including Seattle, Salt Lake City, Denver, and Phoenix, with additional summer service from more cities. Car rental is available at the airport.

By road, Kalispell sits at the junction of US-2 and US-93. From Missoula (115 miles south on US-93), the drive takes about two hours and passes through mountain scenery most of the way. From the Canadian border at Roosville, it's about 60 miles south on US-93.

There is no Amtrak service to Kalispell, though the Empire Builder stops at Whitefish, 15 miles north, with a shuttle connection into town.
