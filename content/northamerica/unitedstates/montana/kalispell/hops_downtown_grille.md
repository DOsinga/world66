A reliable local favourite on Main Street, Hop's does straightforward American food — burgers, steaks, sandwiches, and a good selection of Montana craft beers on tap. The setting is a converted downtown storefront, busy at dinner time and a good place to sit at the bar if you're on your own. Nothing fancy, but consistent and popular with locals as well as visitors.
