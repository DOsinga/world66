Lewistown sits close to the geographic centre of Montana, in a wide basin wrapped by the Judith, Snowy, Moccasin, Belt, and Highwood ranges. It is a small town, but the setting gives it more weight than its population suggests.

This is not the Montana of big-name national parks. Lewistown is quieter: [Big Spring Creek](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/lewistown/big_spring_creek) runs right through town, the [Central Montana Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/lewistown/central_montana_museum) is stronger than expected, and the [Limekiln Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/lewistown/limekiln_trail) climbs quickly into the Judith Mountains.

The town works best for travelers who like the in-between parts of the West: wheat country, old brick storefronts, fishing access, and long views. If the seasonal [Charlie Russell Chew Choo](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/lewistown/charlie_russell_chew_choo) is running, it turns Lewistown from a pleasant stop into a full evening.
