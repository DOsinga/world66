Big Spring Creek runs through Lewistown and gives the town its easiest outdoor pleasure. You can walk sections of it, look for trout in the clear water, or use it as a low-effort break between the museum and downtown.

For anglers, the creek is more than scenery. It is one of central Montana's better-known trout streams, with public access points around town and a quieter feel than the famous rivers farther west.
