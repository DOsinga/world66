The Central Montana Museum is the best indoor anchor in Lewistown, with exhibits on homesteading, ranch life, Native history, fossils, saddles, firearms, and the odd details that build a local museum into something more than a room of labels.

It is worth visiting early in your stay because it gives context to the basin around town. The museum helps explain why Lewistown feels like a centre for a large rural region rather than just another highway stop.
