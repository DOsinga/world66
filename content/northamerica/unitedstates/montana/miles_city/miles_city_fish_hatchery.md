Miles City Fish Hatchery is a small but worthwhile stop if you are curious about the practical side of keeping Montana's rivers, lakes, and ponds stocked. Visitors can see species such as pallid sturgeon, walleye, tiger muskie, and trout, and get a plain look at how fish management works in eastern Montana.
