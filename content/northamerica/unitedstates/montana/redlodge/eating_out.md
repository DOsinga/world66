---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

For a town of 2,300, Red Lodge eats unusually well. Broadway has a string of solid restaurants — local steaks, decent Mexican, a couple of cafes that bake their own bread — and the standard is higher than in most of small-town Montana, helped by the summer Yellowstone traffic. Lunch is the easy meal; dinner books up in July and August.
