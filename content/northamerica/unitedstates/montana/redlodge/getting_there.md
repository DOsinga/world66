---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car.** Red Lodge sits 60 miles southwest of Billings on US 212, the only paved road in. From Interstate 90 take exit 434 at Laurel and follow US 212 south for an hour; the more scenic route is exit 408 at Columbus, then MT-78 south through Absarokee and Roscoe, which adds 20 minutes but is one of the prettier valleys in this part of Montana.

**From Yellowstone.** US 212 is the Beartooth Highway, the dramatic alpine road from Cooke City over the 10,947-foot pass. It is open mid-May to mid-October only, depending on snow. In winter you must go around via Livingston and I-90 — a 4-hour drive instead of 90 minutes.

**By air.** Billings Logan International Airport (BIL) is the nearest commercial airport, with direct flights from Denver, Minneapolis, Salt Lake, Seattle, and Phoenix. Rental cars at the terminal; the drive to Red Lodge is one hour. There is no bus or shuttle service from the airport.

**By bus.** None. You need your own car or a rental.
