The best breakfast on Broadway, a small storefront with a counter and a few tables that fills up quickly on summer weekends. The thing to order is the green chile breakfast burrito; the huevos rancheros are also good, and the pastries come from a tiny in-house bakery that runs out by mid-morning. Coffee is properly pulled from a real espresso machine.

Cash and card both fine; service is friendly Montana style, meaning chatty but quick. Expect a 15-minute wait for a table at 09:00 on a July Saturday. Closed for two weeks in October.
