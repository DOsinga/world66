The classic Montana bar at the south end of Broadway: long oak bar, pressed-tin ceiling, taxidermy on the walls, a pool table at the back, and a clientele evenly split between locals and skiers from Red Lodge Mountain. Drafts are cold, the whiskey list is reasonable, and the burger is the best in town for under twelve dollars.

Live music most Friday and Saturday nights in summer, usually bluegrass or country. Card or cash. The bar gets loud after 22:00 and is one of the few places in town that stays open until 02:00 year-round.
