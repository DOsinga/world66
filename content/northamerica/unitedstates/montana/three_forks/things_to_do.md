Three Forks is built around one of the most historically significant river confluences in North America. The [Missouri Headwaters State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/three_forks/missouri_headwaters_state_park) sits just north of town where the Jefferson, Madison, and Gallatin rivers join — the spot Lewis and Clark reached in July 1805 after months of upstream travel. The [Headwaters Heritage Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/three_forks/headwaters_heritage_museum) on Main Street tells that story alongside the town's railroad and homesteading history.

For most visitors today, the draw is the fishing. Three blue-ribbon trout rivers converge here, and the [Madison, Gallatin, and Jefferson](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/three_forks/fly_fishing) offer very different experiences within a short drive. The [Sacagawea Hotel](/northamerica/unitedstates/montana/three_forks/sacagawea_hotel), a restored 1910 landmark on Main Street, is the most comfortable base in town.
