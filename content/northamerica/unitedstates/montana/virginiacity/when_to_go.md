---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Virginia City is essentially a summer destination. The opera house, the Short Line steam train, and most of the museums operate from late May through early September. Outside those months, the town is very quiet — some businesses close entirely, and the few year-round residents have the place to themselves. A winter visit is possible but requires accepting that most of what makes Virginia City distinctive will be shut.

July and August are the busiest months, with the fullest opera house schedule and the most visitor activity. Midweek is noticeably quieter than weekends, and arriving in the evening after the day-trip crowds have left reveals a different town — quieter, more genuinely atmospheric. If you are planning to see an opera house show, book tickets in advance for July and August.

For Madison River fishing, late May through June and September through October are the prime windows. Summer heat can push water temperatures up in some stretches, reducing fish activity. The drive from Ennis along the river is scenic in any season, and fall colour in the Madison Valley runs from late September into October.

Spring in the Madison Valley arrives slowly. Snow is possible through May at elevation. The roads are paved and generally clear by mid-May, but mountain passes can close without warning in early spring.
