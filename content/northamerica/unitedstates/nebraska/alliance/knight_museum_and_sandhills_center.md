The Knight Museum is the town's main history stop. Its exhibits move through Native American life, ranching, pioneer settlement, the railroad, and the characters who shaped Alliance and the Nebraska Sandhills.

It gives useful context before or after the roadside oddity of Carhenge. For a traveller crossing western Nebraska, it is the place that explains why this small town grew where it did.
