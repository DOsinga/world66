The Sallows Military Museum focuses on local military service, including material connected to the Alliance Army Air Field during World War II. Displays include uniforms, weapons, photographs, documents, and memorabilia from several conflicts.

It is a modest stop, but it fills in a piece of Alliance history that would otherwise be easy to miss.
