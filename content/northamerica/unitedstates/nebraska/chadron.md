Chadron sits in the Nebraska Panhandle, where the flat Great Plains begin to wrinkle into the Pine Ridge. It is a small college town and service centre, but it has more travel weight than that suggests: fur-trade history, High Plains literature, fossils, and Nebraska's first state park all cluster close enough for a good spare day.

The strongest stop is the [Museum of the Fur Trade](/northamerica/unitedstates/nebraska/chadron/museum_of_the_fur_trade), just east of town on the site of the old Bordeaux Trading Post. In town, the [Mari Sandoz High Plains Heritage Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/nebraska/chadron/mari_sandoz_high_plains_heritage_center) gives the region a literary voice, while the college geology museum adds fossils and minerals to the mix.

Chadron is also a useful base for Pine Ridge country. [Chadron State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/nebraska/chadron/chadron_state_park) is close enough for an easy hike, and Crawford's Fort Robinson is a short drive west. If you are crossing western Nebraska on US-20 or US-385, Chadron is worth more than a fuel stop.
