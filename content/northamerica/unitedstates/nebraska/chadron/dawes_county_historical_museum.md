Dawes County Historical Museum is a local history stop just south of Chadron, with county records, historical newspapers, and exhibits that help place the town in the wider High Plains story.

The grounds include a log cabin and a one-room schoolhouse, making it most worthwhile for visitors interested in regional history, genealogy, or the small settlements around northwest Nebraska.
