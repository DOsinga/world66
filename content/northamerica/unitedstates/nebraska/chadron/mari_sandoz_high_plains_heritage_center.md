The Mari Sandoz High Plains Heritage Center introduces one of western Nebraska's most important writers. Sandoz wrote about the Plains, Native history, ranching, and settlement with a sharp eye, and the centre keeps that regional voice alive through exhibits, manuscripts, and personal material.

It is also a good companion to the fur-trade museum. Together they turn Chadron from a highway town into a place with a real story: trade, cattle, Native nations, and writers trying to make sense of the High Plains.
