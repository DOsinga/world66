*North America > United States > Nebraska > Crawford*

Crawford is a small town in the far northwestern corner of [Nebraska](/northamerica/unitedstates/nebraska), where the plains begin to rise toward the Pine Ridge and the Black Hills. It has barely a thousand people, a few blocks of old main street, and a position that is far more interesting than its size suggests.

The town grew beside Fort Robinson, the old cavalry post three miles west. Today [Fort Robinson State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/nebraska/crawford/fort_robinson_state_park) is the main reason to come: part military history site, part fossil and prairie landscape, part family outdoor base. Crawford itself works as the small-town service stop beside it, with a local museum and a quiet downtown.

This is open-country travel. You need a car, and distances are long, but Crawford makes a useful base for Fort Robinson, Toadstool Geologic Park, Agate Fossil Beds, and the lonely roads of the Nebraska Panhandle.
