---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Lincoln's dining scene benefits enormously from the university presence — a steady flow of students and faculty has sustained more ambitious restaurants than you might expect in a city this size. The Haymarket District concentrates many of the best options, with a good mix of independent spots from farm-to-table Midwestern cooking to Vietnamese and Ethiopian. O Street, the city's main artery, has plenty of everyday options.
