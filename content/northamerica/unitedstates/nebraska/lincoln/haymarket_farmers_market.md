The Haymarket Farmers Market is Lincoln's best weekly gathering, running every Saturday morning from May through October in the streets of the Haymarket District. It draws over a hundred vendors selling local produce, Nebraska meats and cheeses, baked goods, flowers, plants, and prepared food — a genuine working market rather than a curated boutique experience.

The market runs from 8 a.m. to noon and gets busy by 9. The surrounding restaurants and cafes open early to catch the foot traffic, making a Saturday morning at the market a pleasant way to spend a few hours. Come with a bag if you are staying somewhere with a kitchen; come without one if you just want to eat breakfast and watch the city at its most relaxed.
