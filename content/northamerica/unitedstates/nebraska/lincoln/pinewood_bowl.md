Pinewood Bowl is Lincoln's open-air concert venue inside Pioneers Park, a summer amphitheatre where the setting matters almost as much as the act. Trees, evening air and a relaxed park approach make it a different experience from the downtown arenas.

It only matters when something is on, so check the schedule before building plans around it. With the right concert, it is one of the city's better warm-weather nights out.
