---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lincoln punches above its weight for a plains capital. The Nebraska State Capitol alone justifies a stop — it is one of the genuinely great pieces of American civic architecture, and the observation deck gives a panoramic view of the flat land stretching in every direction. The Haymarket District offers pleasant wandering, and the University of Nebraska campus brings world-class collections in art and natural history that most visitors entirely overlook.
