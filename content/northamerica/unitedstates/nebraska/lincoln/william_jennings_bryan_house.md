The William Jennings Bryan House, also known as Fairview, was built in 1902 for the three-time presidential candidate and populist orator. Its mix of Classical Revival and Queen Anne details is handsome, but the main reason to visit is the link to one of Nebraska's most nationally visible political figures. Tours usually need to be arranged in advance.
