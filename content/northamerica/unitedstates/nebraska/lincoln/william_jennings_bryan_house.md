The William Jennings Bryan House, also known as Fairview, was built in 1902 for the three-time presidential candidate and influential orator. Its mix of Classical Revival and Queen Anne detail makes it one of Lincoln's more interesting political homes; guided visits generally need to be arranged ahead.
