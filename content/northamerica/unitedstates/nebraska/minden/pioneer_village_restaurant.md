The restaurant on the Pioneer Village grounds is the most practical lunch option if you're spending the day at the museum. It serves straightforward American diner fare — sandwiches, burgers, hot meals — at modest prices, and is geared toward the museum's family visitors. Hours align with the museum's operating times.

For a town Minden's size, this is a reasonable choice rather than a destination in its own right. If you want something different, Kearney, 15 miles north on State Route 10, has a much wider range of restaurants.
