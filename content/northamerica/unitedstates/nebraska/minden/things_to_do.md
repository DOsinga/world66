title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Minden's singular attraction is [Harold Warp's Pioneer Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/nebraska/minden/pioneer_village) — one of the most remarkable private collections of Americana in the United States, and a legitimate reason to exit the interstate. The village takes the better part of a full day to explore properly, and it is the reason virtually all visitors come to Minden.

The town itself has a modest historic downtown with the [Kearney County Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/nebraska/minden/kearney_county_courthouse) and the historic [W.T. Thorne Building](/northamerica/unitedstates/nebraska/minden/wt_thorne_building) on the main square, worth a brief walk. The nearby Germantown Colony site is of interest to those curious about the history of religious utopian communities on the Great Plains.
