---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Pioneer Village is open daily year-round except Christmas, so Minden can be visited at any time. Summer (June through August) is the most popular season for road-trippers on the I-80 corridor, with warm weather and long days that suit a full day at the museum.

The most distinctive time to visit is the holiday season. From Thanksgiving through New Year's Day, Minden earns its regional nickname — the "Christmas City" of the Great Plains — with elaborate light displays covering the courthouse square and surrounding blocks. The display draws visitors from across south-central Nebraska and beyond, and the town has a festive atmosphere quite unlike its sleepy summer character.

South-central Nebraska winters can be cold and occasionally severe, with blizzard risk from December through February. Spring and autumn are pleasant and less crowded.
