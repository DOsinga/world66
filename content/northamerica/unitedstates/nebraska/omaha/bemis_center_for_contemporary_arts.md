The Bemis Center for Contemporary Arts is a contemporary art space and nationally known artist residency in Omaha's Old Market area. Its warehouse-style galleries host changing exhibitions, often connected to artists working on site.

It is a good stop for visitors looking beyond Omaha's historic museums and riverfront sights, especially when current exhibitions line up with your interests.
