The Gerald R. Ford Birthsite and Gardens mark the Omaha lot where the future president was born, though the original house is gone. What remains is a modest memorial landscape with plaques, roses, and enough context to make a quick stop worthwhile for presidential-history completists.

It is not one of Omaha's headline sights, but it fits easily into a route through the city's older neighbourhoods. Treat it as a short reflective pause rather than a museum visit.
