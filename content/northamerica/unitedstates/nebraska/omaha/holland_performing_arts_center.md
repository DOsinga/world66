The Holland Performing Arts Center is Omaha's polished modern concert venue, home to symphony performances, touring musicians, and larger cultural events. Its glassy form and warm interior are a clear break from the brick warehouses of the nearby Old Market.

It is worth checking the programme if you are staying downtown. Even if you do not go inside, the building helps show how Omaha's riverfront and central districts have been remade around culture, restaurants, and evening traffic rather than just offices.
