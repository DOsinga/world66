Love's Jazz and Art Center preserves and presents North Omaha's African-American music and arts
heritage, with its name honoring saxophonist and bandleader Preston Love. The centre connects local
jazz history to a wider story of migration, neighbourhood culture, and Black creativity in Omaha.

For travellers, it is most meaningful as part of a North Omaha itinerary rather than a quick
downtown add-on. Combined with historic neighbourhood sites and local music history, it shows a side
of the city that is easy to miss from the Old Market or riverfront.
