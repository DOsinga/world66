The Malcolm X Birthsite marks the North Omaha place where Malcolm Little was born in 1925. The original house is gone, so this is a reflective memorial landscape rather than a furnished historic home, but it adds an important layer to Omaha beyond the Old Market and zoo.

Visit with some background reading and combine it with other North Omaha history if you can. The site is quiet and understated, which makes it easy to miss if you arrive expecting a conventional museum.
