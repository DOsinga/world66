Omaha Children’s Museum is a practical family stop in downtown Omaha, with hands-on exhibits built for younger children rather than quiet looking. Expect water play, science demonstrations, art spaces, and rotating installations that reward energy more than patience.

For adults travelling without children it is easy to skip, but for families it can save a hot, cold, or rainy afternoon. Its value is less about Omaha history than about giving kids a place where touching things is the point.
