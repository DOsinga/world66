Omaha Community Playhouse is one of the city's durable cultural institutions, known for a busy season of musicals, plays, and community theatre productions. It is not a tourist landmark in the way the zoo or Old Market are, but it gives visitors a strong local night-out option.

Check the schedule before adding it to your plans. When a production lines up with your dates, the playhouse is a good reminder that Omaha's cultural life is not limited to museums and summer baseball.
