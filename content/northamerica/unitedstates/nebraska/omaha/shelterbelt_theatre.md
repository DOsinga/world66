Shelterbelt Theatre gives Omaha a small, local counterpoint to its larger performing arts venues. The company is known for intimate productions, often with contemporary scripts and regional voices that suit a compact theatre space. It is the sort of place where the audience feels close to the work, for better and more interesting.

This is a niche stop for visitors, but a good one if you want an evening that feels rooted in Omaha rather than imported wholesale. Check the current season before planning around it, as show dates matter more than the venue itself. Pair it with dinner in a nearby neighborhood.
