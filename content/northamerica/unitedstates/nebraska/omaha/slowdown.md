Slowdown is one of Omaha's key live-music rooms, closely associated with the city's indie-rock reputation and the Saddle Creek orbit. It sits near the downtown/NoDo entertainment area, making it easy to pair with the Old Market or a drink before a show.

Check the calendar rather than just showing up; the value is in the booking. When the right band is on, Slowdown gives travellers a much more current Omaha night than another passive landmark.
