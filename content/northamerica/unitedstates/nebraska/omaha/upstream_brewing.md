A brewpub in the Old Market district, set inside a converted Victorian firehouse. The house beers are reliably good — the Dundee Pale Ale and the seasonal wheat beer are the most popular — and the food is better than average for a brewpub. The downstairs bar gets crowded on weekend evenings; upstairs is quieter and better for conversation.

One of the longer-running craft breweries in Omaha, established before the city's current beer scene took off, and still among the best options in the Old Market area.
