Vala's Pumpkin Patch and Apple Orchard is a seasonal farm attraction west of Omaha, open around the autumn harvest. Families come for the pumpkins, apple orchard, play areas, and fairground-style extras, so it works best as a half-day outing rather than a quick stop.
