---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ponca is a small town with two reasons to stop. **Ponca State Park**, four miles north of town on bluffs above the Missouri, is the main draw — hardwood trails, river overlooks, and one of the few stretches where the Missouri still moves the way it did before the Corps of Engineers locked the lower river behind dams. In town, the 1884 **Dixon County Courthouse** and the preserved **Cook Blacksmith Shop** museum hold what little built history there is.

Outdoor activities centre on the river. The **Missouri National Recreational River** runs along the Nebraska–South Dakota border here, and a short drive upstream gets you to put-ins for canoes, fishing boats, and birding skiffs.
