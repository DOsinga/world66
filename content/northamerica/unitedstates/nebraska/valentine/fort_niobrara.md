Established on the grounds of a former frontier cavalry post, Fort Niobrara National Wildlife Refuge protects a remnant herd of bison along with elk, longhorn cattle, and prairie dogs on roughly 19,000 acres of mixed-grass prairie above the Niobrara valley. A self-guided auto tour road loops through the preserve, and an observation deck overlooks the bison range. The refuge also has hiking trails that descend into the river canyon and canoe access at Fort Falls, one of the prettiest put-in points on the scenic river corridor.
