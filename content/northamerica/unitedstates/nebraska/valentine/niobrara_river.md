The stretch of the Niobrara River west of Valentine is one of the most popular paddling rivers on the Great Plains, with six distinct ecosystems converging in a single valley. The designated scenic corridor runs for 76 miles; most floaters put in at Cornell Dam and take out at Rocky Ford, a four- to six-hour trip past waterfalls, spring-fed side canyons, and canyon walls covered in ferns. Canoe and tube outfitters line the highway west of town and rent equipment by the day.
