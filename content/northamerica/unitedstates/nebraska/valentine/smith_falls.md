Smith Falls State Park protects Nebraska's tallest waterfall, a narrow cascade that drops from a sandstone ledge into a fern-lined grotto before joining the Niobrara. The park is accessible by footbridge from the north bank of the river — canoeists often pull out here mid-float — or via a short trail from the park's campground. The falls flow year-round, fed by a Sand Hills aquifer that maintains a steady temperature even in the height of summer.
