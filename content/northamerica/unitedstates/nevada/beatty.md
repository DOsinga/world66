Beatty is a small desert town on US-95, close to the Nevada edge of [Death Valley](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/deserts/deathvalley). It is often treated as a fuel stop or motel base, but the country just west of town gives it a stronger claim: the ruins of [Rhyolite](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/beatty/rhyolite_historic_area), the strange sculptures of [Goldwell Open Air Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/beatty/goldwell_open_air_museum), and a stark road over Daylight Pass into the national park.

The town itself is plain, dusty, and practical. That is part of the appeal. Beatty has enough services to make a desert road trip easier, but it still feels close to the old mining frontier, where boomtowns appeared almost overnight and then emptied just as quickly.

Give Beatty a half-day if you are driving between Las Vegas and Death Valley, or a full day if you like ghost towns, roadside museums, and empty basin-and-range scenery. Rhyolite and Goldwell are the real draw; Beatty is the place that makes them easy to see.
