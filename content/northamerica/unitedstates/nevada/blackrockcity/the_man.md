The Man is the central landmark of Black Rock City, the temporary desert city built each year for Burning Man. Its design changes from year to year, but its role remains constant: a visual anchor, meeting point, and ritual focus in a place where almost everything is temporary.

Seeing it is inseparable from the event itself. By day it helps orient you across the playa; by night it becomes part of the illuminated cityscape, before the burn turns it into the festival's most visible shared moment.
