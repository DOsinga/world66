The Temple is Burning Man's quieter counterpart to the Man: a temporary structure built anew each year as a place for grief, remembrance, reflection, and personal messages. Its design changes with the chosen artist and theme, but its emotional role in Black Rock City is remarkably consistent.

The Temple burn is usually more subdued than the Saturday night burn, and many participants treat it with genuine reverence. Because it is temporary, this POI is about the recurring ritual rather than a permanent object; check the current year's map and respect the mood around it.
