The Coffee Cup is a diner on Nevada Way with a Guy Fieri seal of approval (it appeared on *Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives* in 2008 and the certificate is framed inside the door). The food matches the type — heavy breakfasts, biscuits and gravy, chorizo fajita omelettes — and the queue starts forming by eight in the morning on weekends.

It is open for breakfast and lunch only. The eggs benedict and the homemade salsa are the local favourites. Cash and card both accepted. Mains 12-18 dollars.
