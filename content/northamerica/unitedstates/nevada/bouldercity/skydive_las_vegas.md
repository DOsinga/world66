Skydive Las Vegas is a high-adrenaline option based near Boulder City, using the open desert and big sky south of Las Vegas as its backdrop. The appeal is obvious: a tandem jump with views that can include Lake Mead, desert ridges, and the wider Las Vegas basin.

This is an activity rather than a sight, and it depends on weather, nerves, and budget. Use licensed operators, read restrictions carefully, and leave plenty of schedule slack in case wind or aircraft timing changes the plan.
