The Nevada Governor's Mansion is a handsome early-20th-century residence north of the state capitol, built when Nevada wanted its executive home to look suitably permanent. Its white columns, broad porch, and formal symmetry give Carson City a touch of old capital dignity without the scale or crowds of bigger statehouses.

You will usually see it from the street rather than tour the interior, so fold it into a walk through the historic district. Together with the capitol, Orion Clemens House, and older residential streets, it helps Carson City feel like more than a service stop on the way to Tahoe or Virginia City.
