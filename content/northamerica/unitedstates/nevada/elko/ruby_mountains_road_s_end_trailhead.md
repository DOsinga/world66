Road's End Trailhead is the practical gateway to some of the Ruby Mountains' best hiking, reached by following Lamoille Canyon Road into alpine-looking country that surprises many travelers crossing Nevada. From here, routes lead toward lakes, passes, and the Ruby Crest backcountry.

For Elko visitors, the trailhead is valuable because it turns the mountains from a distant backdrop into a real outing. Check snow and road conditions, start early in summer, and treat even short hikes seriously because elevation and weather can change the day quickly.
