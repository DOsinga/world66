Dooby Lane is a folk art installation a couple of miles north of Gerlach, where an assortment of found objects, signs, and sculptures have accumulated along a short stretch of dirt road over several decades. The installation has no single creator — locals, passing travellers, and Burning Man participants have all contributed pieces. It is a small but characterful stop on the way out to the playa, a kind of informal monument to the eccentric spirit of the Black Rock Desert. The name is unofficial; locals simply know where it is.
