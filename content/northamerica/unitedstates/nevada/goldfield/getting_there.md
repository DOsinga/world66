---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Goldfield sits on US-95, roughly 185 miles north of [Las Vegas](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/lasvegas) (about three hours) and 245 miles south of [Reno](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/reno) (about four). There is no public transport, no Greyhound stop, and the nearest commercial airports are McCarran in Las Vegas and Reno-Tahoe. A car is essential.

The drive itself is a good part of the visit — long, empty desert valleys flanked by basin-and-range mountains, with only a few small towns (Beatty, Tonopah) along the way. Fill the tank in Tonopah, 26 miles north, or in Beatty, 65 miles south — there are gas stations in Goldfield but they are not always open and prices are noticeably higher.
