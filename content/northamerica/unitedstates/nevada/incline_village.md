Incline Village sits on the northeast shore of [Lake Tahoe](/northamerica/unitedstates/california/laketahoe), on the Nevada side. It is one of the more upscale communities on the lake — no chain restaurants, no strip malls, just pine forest, mountain views, and easy access to the water.

In winter, [Diamond Peak](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/incline_village/diamond_peak) ski resort is right above town, offering a smaller and less crowded alternative to the bigger resorts on the California side. In summer, the beaches here — particularly [Sand Harbor](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/incline_village/sand_harbor), just south of town in Lake Tahoe Nevada State Park — are among the best on the lake, with clear turquoise water and granite boulders. The Shakespeare Festival at Sand Harbor amphitheatre runs through July and August. The [Flume Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/incline_village/flume_trail) above town is one of the best mountain-bike routes in the Sierra Nevada.
