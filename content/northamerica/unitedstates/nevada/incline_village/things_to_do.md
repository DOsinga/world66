title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Incline Village divides its activities cleanly between seasons: winter brings skiing at [Diamond Peak](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/incline_village/diamond_peak) just above town, while summer fills [Sand Harbor](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/incline_village/sand_harbor) with swimmers, kayakers, and picnickers. The [Flume Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/incline_village/flume_trail) is one of the best mountain-bike routes in the Sierra Nevada and is accessible year-round in good conditions. The town's nine private beaches are for residents and registered guests, but Sand Harbor State Park is open to all.
