Absinthe is a compact, adult-only Las Vegas show mixing acrobatics, comedy, and deliberately rude carnival energy. It is staged close to the audience, which makes the stunts feel more immediate and the jokes harder to ignore.

This is not the safe, glossy version of a Strip production. Go if you want something sharp-edged and physical; skip it if blue comedy is not your thing.
