The 65,000-seat home of the NFL's Las Vegas Raiders opened in 2020 just west of the Strip across I-15. The exterior is sleek black glass shaped like a death-star wedge, lit at night with team red. It also hosts UNLV football, the annual Las Vegas Bowl, and major concert tours that need a stadium-sized venue.

Public tours run several times a week when there is no event in the building — the loop covers the Raiders locker room, the field level, a luxury suite, and the Al Davis memorial torch (which is the tallest 3D-printed structure on Earth). Buy game tickets early; Raiders home games are some of the harder NFL tickets to find at face value.
