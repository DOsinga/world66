The Bunkhouse is a converted bungalow on 11th Street that functions as the Arts District's principal live music venue. The indoor stage is intimate — capacity is a few hundred at most — and the booking leans toward local indie acts, touring singer-songwriters, and the occasional bigger name playing a small room. The outdoor stage and patio extend the capacity in decent weather.

Beer prices are low by Las Vegas standards, the sound is good, and the crowd is reliably local rather than tourist. Cover charges are modest when they exist at all. A few blocks from the main Charleston strip but walkable from most Arts District spots. Check the schedule before you go — the best nights sell out fast.
