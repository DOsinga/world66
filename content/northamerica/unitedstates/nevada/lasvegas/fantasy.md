Fantasy is one of the long-running adult revue shows on the Strip, staged at the Luxor with dance numbers, vocals, comedy, and celebrity impersonations. It is very much a Las Vegas production: glossy, loud, and built for a late-night crowd rather than a quiet theatre evening.
