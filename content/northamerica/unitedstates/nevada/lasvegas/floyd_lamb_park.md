Floyd Lamb Park is a green oasis on the north side of Las Vegas, with ponds, cottonwoods, and the historic Tule Springs Ranch. The setting also connects to the area's Pleistocene fossil story, giving the park more substance than a simple picnic ground.

It is a useful break from casinos and desert heat, especially for families or anyone looking for shade, birds, and a quieter outdoor stop. The park sits well away from the Strip, so it works best with a car or as part of a North Las Vegas outing.
