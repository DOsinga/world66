The Golden Tiki takes the tiki bar format and pushes it to the edge of total immersion. The interior is dense with hand-carved tikis, vintage treasures, a ceiling hung with skeleton-and-greenery installations, and the kind of detail that rewards multiple visits and extended examination. Unlike many tiki bars, the cocktail programme is genuinely serious — drinks are built with house-made syrups, fresh juice, and rum selections that go well beyond the basics.

The space is large enough that it rarely feels crowded, which is unusual for a bar this popular. Located on Spring Mountain Road in the Chinatown corridor, it draws both neighbourhood locals and people who have specifically come for the experience. The Thursday-through-Sunday live entertainment ranges from acoustic sets to full swing bands.
