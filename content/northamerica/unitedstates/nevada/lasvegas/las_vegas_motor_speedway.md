Las Vegas Motor Speedway sits well north of the Strip, out where the city thins toward the desert and Nellis Air Force Base. It is the main place to see big-ticket racing in Las Vegas, including NASCAR weekends, drag racing, driving experiences, and other motorsport events that feel very different from casino entertainment.

It is not a casual walk-up sight; the speedway is worth the trip when something is on, or if you have booked a driving experience. Check the calendar and transport before committing, because distances are larger than they look on a map and event traffic can be its own part of the day.
