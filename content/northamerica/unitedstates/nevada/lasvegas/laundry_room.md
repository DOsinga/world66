The Laundry Room is a reservation-only cocktail bar tucked inside the Commonwealth building on Fremont Street, accessible by text message to a number that circulates through Las Vegas bar culture. Seats are limited to twelve; the experience is built around a rotating menu of original cocktails made using premium spirits and house-made ingredients, served by bartenders who explain each drink.

Reservations open each week via text and go quickly. The secrecy has faded as the bar has become famous but the quality has not — the small format keeps standards high in a way that is genuinely unusual for Las Vegas. Booking weeks ahead for weekend slots is standard. Come for the drinks; the experience of being inside it is secondary.
