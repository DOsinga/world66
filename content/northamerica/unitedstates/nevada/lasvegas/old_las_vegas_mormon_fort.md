Old Las Vegas Mormon Fort preserves the site of the first permanent structure built by European settlers in the Las Vegas Valley. It is a small but useful counterpoint to the city's casino image, with a visitor center explaining the fort, the spring-fed settlement, and early travel through the region.

The stop works well with the Neon Museum and other downtown sights. It will not take long, but it gives Las Vegas a deeper timeline than most visitors expect.
