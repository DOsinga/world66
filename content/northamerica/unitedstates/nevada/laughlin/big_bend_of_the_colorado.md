Big Bend of the Colorado is the quieter river stop south of Laughlin. The state recreation area protects a long west-bank stretch of the Colorado, with beaches, picnic areas, boat access, short trails, and enough riparian habitat to make birdwatching worthwhile.

It is not wild country, but it gives you a better feel for the river than the resort promenade does. Come for a swim, a picnic, or a slow hour by the water, especially if you are driving between Laughlin, Bullhead City, and Needles.
