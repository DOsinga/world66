The Colorado River Heritage Greenway is Laughlin's best low-effort escape from the resort corridor. The trail system links paved river paths, desert tracks, fishing spots, picnic areas, and viewpoints north of town, with Davis Dam as the backdrop.

Use it for an early walk, a bike ride, or a sunset look at the river after the heat drops. The terrain is not hard, but shade is limited away from the water and the Mojave sun is unforgiving.
