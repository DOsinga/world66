Lovelock is a small Interstate 80 town in northern Nevada, and most travellers first meet it as fuel, food, and a break from the long desert drive. It clears that low expectation by having a real story: prehistoric Lovelock Cave, a local museum in an 1874 house, and a courthouse-square love-lock ritual that leans into the town's name without taking itself too seriously.

The best reason to stop is [Lovelock Cave](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/lovelock/lovelock_cave), southwest of town on a backcountry byway. It is one of the major archaeological sites of the Great Basin, known for tule duck decoys and other material preserved in the cave deposits. Back in town, [Marzen House Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/lovelock/marzen_house_museum) gives the cave and county history some context.

Lovelock is still a highway town, and it is not trying to be Reno or Virginia City. But if you have a spare day on the Cowboy Corridor, it offers a better mix of desert archaeology, odd civic pride, and reservoir-country side trips than the map suggests.
