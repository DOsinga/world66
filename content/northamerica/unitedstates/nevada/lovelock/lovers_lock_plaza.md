Lovers Lock Plaza is a bit of roadside theatre, but it works because the town name does half the joke. Couples attach padlocks to chains in the park near the Pershing County Courthouse, turning a quick highway pause into a small ritual.

It is free, quick, and best combined with a walk past the courthouse and the old commercial blocks nearby.
