Court Rock is a small, odd Panaca landmark: a natural rock formation that local tradition says was used as a jail until the early 1870s. It is not a major sight, but it gives the town a bit of frontier texture and is easy to pair with the ward chapel.

Stop if you are already in town. The appeal is the story more than the scale.
