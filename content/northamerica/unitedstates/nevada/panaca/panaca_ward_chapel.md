Panaca Ward Chapel dates from the first years of the Mormon settlement and is one of the oldest buildings in Lincoln County. Its plain shape suits the town: practical, religious, and far from the mining-camp bravado of nearby Pioche.

It is a short stop rather than a deep visit, but it helps explain why Panaca feels different from most small Nevada towns.
