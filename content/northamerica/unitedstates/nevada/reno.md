Reno calls itself 'The Biggest Little City in the World,' and the famous [arch](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/reno/reno_arch) over Virginia Street backs up that claim. This is a gambling town in northern Nevada, smaller and scrappier than Las Vegas, with a growing arts scene and easy access to the Sierra Nevada mountains.

Reno is a good starting point for [Black Rock City](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/blackrockcity) and the annual Burning Man festival. The rest of the year, the casinos downtown — anchored by the three-property [Eldorado/Silver Legacy/Circus Circus Row](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/reno/eldorado_silver_legacy) — are the main draw, but skiing at nearby Lake Tahoe, the [Truckee River whitewater park](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/reno/truckee_river_walk) running through downtown, the [National Automobile Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/reno/national_automobile_museum), and the [Nevada Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/reno/nevada_museum_of_art) give non-gamblers plenty of reasons to visit.
