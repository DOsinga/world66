The Blue Lamp is a [Reno](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/reno) nightlife fixture, a live music venue and bar that positions itself as an alternative to the casino entertainment scene. The venue hosts local and touring bands across a range of genres and is one of the better spots in town for catching live music outside the casino circuit. Cover charges can be steep, particularly on weeknights when the lineup does not always justify the price.
