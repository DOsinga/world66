The Fog Lifter Caffe at 246 W 1st St was an independent coffee shop in downtown [Reno](/northamerica/unitedstates/nevada/reno), a few blocks from the Truckee River and within easy walking distance of the Virginia Street casino strip. It served espresso drinks and light fare in a relaxed, low-key setting at a time when downtown Reno was still developing the cafe culture that has grown since.

The café occupied a spot in the West 1st Street corridor, an area that attracted a mix of locals, university students, and visitors looking for somewhere quieter than the casino floors. It was a useful stop before heading out to explore the city or the Sierra Nevada mountains an hour to the west.
