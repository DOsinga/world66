---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Reno is the gateway to northwest Nevada and the Lake Tahoe region, and the easiest international airport for travellers heading to Burning Man on the Black Rock Desert.

**By air** — **Reno-Tahoe International Airport (RNO)** is just south of downtown, with frequent domestic flights from the major western hubs (Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle, Denver, Phoenix, Salt Lake City) and a handful of seasonal services. A taxi or rideshare to downtown takes ten minutes; the RTC Ride bus also runs to the city centre.

**By car** — Reno is on **Interstate 80**, the main east–west route across the northern US. San Francisco is about 220 miles (3.5 hours) west; Salt Lake City is 520 miles east. **US 395** runs north–south through the city, linking Susanville and the Lassen volcanic country to the north with the eastern Sierra and Mammoth Lakes to the south.

**By train** — Amtrak's **California Zephyr** stops at downtown Reno's station once daily in each direction, running between Chicago and Emeryville (San Francisco Bay). It is a beautiful but slow journey; check current schedules.

**By bus** — Greyhound and several smaller operators run intercity buses; the **bus to South Lake Tahoe** is a useful option for skiers without a car.
