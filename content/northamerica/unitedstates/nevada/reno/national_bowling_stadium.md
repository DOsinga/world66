The National Bowling Stadium is one of those Reno oddities that turns out to be exactly what the name says. Opened in 1995 and four storeys tall, the building contains **78 championship tournament lanes** along a single 440-foot row — the largest bowling-only facility in the world — plus a giant suspended ball-shaped IMAX-style theatre over the entrance.

It is primarily a tournament venue and is not always open for casual play; check the schedule. But even passing by, it is worth a look from the outside: the polished steel ball entrance and the giant pin-shaped sculpture have made it one of downtown Reno's stranger landmarks. Tours are sometimes available; ask at the lobby.
