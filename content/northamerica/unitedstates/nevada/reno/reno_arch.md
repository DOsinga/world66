The Reno Arch is the city's emblem — a steel and neon span across Virginia Street at Commercial Row carrying the famous slogan **"The Biggest Little City in the World"**. The first arch went up in 1926 to celebrate the completion of the Lincoln and Victory highways; the current version is the third major rebuild and dates from 1987.

The block under the arch is the symbolic centre of downtown Reno and the natural place for a photograph after dark, when the neon and the surrounding casino marquees blaze in every colour. The **Old Reno Arch** (1934 version) was preserved when it was retired and now stands a few blocks west on Lake Street, where it makes a quieter and equally photogenic stop.
