---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Skiing, snowboarding, gambling, kayaking through downtown in the Truckee River whitewater park, watching the sunset turn the Sierra Nevada pink — there is plenty to do in Reno.

The big casino blocks downtown — **The Row** (Eldorado, Silver Legacy, Circus Circus), Harrah's, and the Whitney Peak — are within easy walking distance of each other on Virginia Street and Second Street. When you want a break from the tables, the **National Automobile Museum** is one of the great car museums anywhere, the **Nevada Museum of Art** is a serious surprise in this city, and the **Truckee River** runs straight through downtown with a whitewater park you can kayak or tube. North of town, the **Wilbur D. May Museum** is a wonderfully eccentric one-man curiosities collection. For a half-day in the Sierra, the **Hunter Creek Falls** trail starts in suburban west Reno.
