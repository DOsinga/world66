Seven Magic Mountains stands in flat open desert about 16 km south of Sloan Canyon, just off Interstate 15 — seven stacks of huge cubic boulders painted in saturated fluorescent colours, each tower more than ten metres tall. The installation by Swiss artist Ugo Rondinone is a deliberate visual rupture: vivid Day-Glo against the long beige horizon of the Mojave, with the mountains of Sloan Canyon and the McCulloughs as backdrop.

It is the most-photographed art piece in southern Nevada and gets crowded at sunrise and sunset, when the colour is at its best. Parking is free, the walk in is short, and the site is open at all times. Combine it easily with a morning at the Sloan Canyon petroglyph trail.
