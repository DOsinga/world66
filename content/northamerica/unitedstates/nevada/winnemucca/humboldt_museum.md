A small free regional museum in a former church building, the Humboldt Museum holds the best collection on Humboldt County history — Paiute and Shoshone material, ranching and mining tools, 19th-century photographs, a fine carriage room, and the famous Columbian mammoth bones from a local quarry. Volunteer docents are knowledgeable and chatty.

Donations welcome but not required. Allow an hour. The mammoth display and the basketry are the highlights.
