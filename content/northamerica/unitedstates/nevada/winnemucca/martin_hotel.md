The Martin Hotel is one of the great American regional restaurants, a working Basque boarding-house dining room that has been serving family-style meals since 1898. You sit at a communal long table; the courses arrive in sequence — soup, salad, beans, bread, the entrée of your choice (lamb shanks, steak, sweetbreads, picon ribs), dessert. Pitchers of red wine come with the meal.

Reservations recommended on weekends but you can usually just walk in. Cash and card both work. The dining room is plain, the food is plain, and the experience is one of the most authentic regional eating traditions still alive in the American West.
