A modest but handsome 1936 federal post office on 4th Street, built in the WPA-era Mediterranean Revival style that was used for small-town civic buildings across the West. Listed on the National Register of Historic Places, it is still the working post office.

A two-minute stop while walking the main street — the kind of small-town public architecture that survives in northern Nevada precisely because the town has not grown enough to replace it.
