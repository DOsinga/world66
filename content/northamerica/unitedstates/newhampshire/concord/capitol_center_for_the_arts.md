The Capitol Center for the Arts occupies a 1927 vaudeville and movie palace that was rescued from decline in the 1990s and rebuilt as Concord's main performing arts venue. The Chubb Theatre seats 1,300 and is the prettiest interior in the city — gilded plasterwork, a coffered ceiling, and tiered balconies that wrap around the stage. A second, smaller venue, the Bank of New Hampshire Stage, is around the corner on South Main Street.

Programming runs the full range: touring Broadway shows, comedians, folk and rock acts, classical concerts, and the home seasons of the New Hampshire Theatre Project and the Symphony NH. Even if you cannot catch a show, it is worth poking your head into the lobby during opening hours.
