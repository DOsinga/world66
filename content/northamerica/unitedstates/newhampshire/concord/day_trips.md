Concord is well placed for day trips into the rest of New Hampshire. [Manchester](/northamerica/unitedstates/newhampshire/manchester), the state's largest city, is half an hour south and has the strong Currier Museum of Art and the well-preserved 19th-century Amoskeag mill district along the Merrimack River. Lake Winnipesaukee and the central lakes region are 45 minutes north on I-93 — Wolfeboro, Meredith, and Weirs Beach all make easy outings. The White Mountains are within two hours: Franconia Notch State Park, the Kancamagus Highway, and Mount Washington are all reachable for a long day from Concord.

To the south and west, Lake Sunapee and the Mount Sunapee state park are 45 minutes via I-89, with hiking in summer and skiing in winter. The seacoast — Portsmouth, the historic shipyards, and the short stretch of Atlantic that is New Hampshire's coast — is an hour east on Route 4.
