---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Concord eats simply but well. Main Street and the side streets around the State House hold most of the options, from old-school diners and craft-beer pubs to a small but growing crop of independent restaurants doing more ambitious work. Local farm-to-table is taken seriously in this part of New Hampshire — the Saturday farmers market on Capitol Street keeps menus seasonal. Lunch service caters to the state government crowd; dinner tends to be quieter and you can almost always walk in.
