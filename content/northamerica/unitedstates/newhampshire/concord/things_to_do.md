Concord's sights cluster tightly along North Main Street, where the [State House](/northamerica/unitedstates/newhampshire/concord/new_hampshire_state_house) and the [Museum of New Hampshire History](/northamerica/unitedstates/newhampshire/concord/museum_of_new_hampshire_history) sit a short walk apart in the compact downtown. The [McAuliffe-Shepard Discovery Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/newhampshire/concord/mcauliffe_shepard_discovery_center), north of the centre, is the city's other essential stop — a planetarium and space museum named for two New Hampshire-born astronauts. A short drive out of town, [Canterbury Shaker Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/newhampshire/concord/canterbury_shaker_village) is one of the most complete and atmospheric Shaker sites in the country.
