A Durham institution since 1954, Young's is a family diner and ice cream stand on Main Street that has outlasted generations of students. Breakfast and lunch are the core — pancakes, omelettes, burgers, sandwiches — at prices that haven't entirely followed inflation. The ice cream counter draws long lines on warm evenings. It's unpretentious, quick, and reliably good: the right spot for breakfast before exploring campus or ice cream after.
