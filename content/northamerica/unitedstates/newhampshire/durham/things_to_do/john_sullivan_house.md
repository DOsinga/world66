The home of John Sullivan, one of New Hampshire's most prominent Revolutionary War figures, stands on Newmarket Road at the edge of the historic district. Sullivan was a major general in the Continental Army, a delegate to the Continental Congress, and three-time governor of New Hampshire. The house is a National Historic Landmark, a plain 18th-century farmhouse that has survived in remarkably good condition. It's viewable from the road, though not open for regular tours.
