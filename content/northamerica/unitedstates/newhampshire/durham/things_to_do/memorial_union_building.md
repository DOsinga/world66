The Memorial Union Building is the social hub of the UNH campus and also serves as New Hampshire's official state war memorial for soldiers killed in action since World War I. The building contains a food court, meeting rooms, and the student-run MUB Cinema which screens current and classic films at reduced prices. For visitors, it's the easiest place on campus to get a meal, use restrooms, or pick up information about university events. The memorial plaques inside are worth pausing over.
