The University of New Hampshire's Museum of Art holds a collection of around 10,000 works, including paintings, prints, photographs, and decorative objects. The permanent collection has genuine strengths in 19th- and 20th-century American art and Japanese woodblock prints. Admission is free, and the temporary exhibitions tend to be ambitious for a university museum of this scale. Located in Paul Creative Arts Center on the edge of campus.
