Moose Alley is the informal name for the stretch of US-3 north of Pittsburg toward the Canadian border. Drivers come here for one main reason: the chance of seeing moose along the road and nearby wetlands. Go slowly, especially around dawn and dusk, and treat the wildlife as a reason to pull over carefully rather than stop in the lane.
