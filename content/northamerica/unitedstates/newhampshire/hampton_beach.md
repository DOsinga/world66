Hampton Beach is New Hampshire's liveliest stretch of coastline — a compact, old-school beach resort about 15 miles south of [Portsmouth](/northamerica/unitedstates/newhampshire/portsmouth) on the Atlantic. It draws crowds all summer with a wide sandy beach, a boardwalk lined with fried dough stands and souvenir shops, and the kind of seaside carnival atmosphere that bigger resort towns have long since polished away.

[Ocean Boulevard](/northamerica/unitedstates/newhampshire/hampton_beach/ocean_boulevard) runs the length of the beach, packed with arcades, ice cream parlours, and seafood shacks. The [Hampton Beach Casino Ballroom](/northamerica/unitedstates/newhampshire/hampton_beach/hampton_beach_casino_ballroom) — not a casino at all, but a storied concert venue — hosts national acts throughout the summer. On Wednesday nights the beach puts on a free fireworks show that is one of the best on the New England coast. [Hampton Beach State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/newhampshire/hampton_beach/hampton_beach_state_park) at the northern end of the strip offers a quieter alternative to the main boardwalk.

Outside peak season the village is quiet, the beach nearly empty, and the sunrises over the Atlantic are worth the drive alone.
