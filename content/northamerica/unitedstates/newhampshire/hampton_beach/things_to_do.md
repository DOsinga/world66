---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hampton Beach is a summer resort, and its attractions are the classic American beach town variety: the beach itself, the boardwalk, the Casino Ballroom, the Wednesday fireworks, and the sand sculpture competition that has been running since the 1930s. None of it is surprising, but all of it is genuinely enjoyable on the right summer day. The New Hampshire Marine Memorial and Hampton Beach State Park add a bit more substance on either end of the strip.
