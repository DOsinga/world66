A beloved independent coffee shop on Main Street, open since 1993. Serious espresso drinks, good drip coffee, and a small selection of pastries. The exposed brick interior and mismatched furniture make it a natural gathering place for students, faculty, and visiting hikers. One of the better independent cafes in the Upper Valley.
