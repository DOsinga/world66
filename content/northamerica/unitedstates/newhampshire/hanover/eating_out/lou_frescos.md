A Hanover institution since 1947, Lou's serves breakfast and lunch on Main Street in a diner-style space that fills up fast on weekend mornings. The pancakes are thick and good, the baked goods come from their own bakery, and the service moves quickly. Arrive early or expect a wait. Cash and card accepted. Closes in the afternoon.
