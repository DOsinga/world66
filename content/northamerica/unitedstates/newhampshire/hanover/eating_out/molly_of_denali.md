A reliable Main Street standby with a broad menu covering burgers, sandwiches, salads, and comfort food. Good for a casual dinner without fuss or expense. The bar side is lively on weekends when Dartmouth is in session. Reasonable prices by Upper Valley standards. Open for lunch and dinner daily.
