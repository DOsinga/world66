---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hanover's attractions are concentrated on and around the Dartmouth campus. The Hood Museum of Art and the Orozco murals in Baker-Berry Library are the highlights for most visitors, but the campus as a whole is worth wandering — the architecture is handsome and the green is one of the nicest open spaces in New England. The Appalachian Trail runs right through town if you want to walk a stretch of it.
