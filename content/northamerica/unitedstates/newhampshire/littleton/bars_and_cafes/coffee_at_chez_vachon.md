Littleton's independent bookshop doubles as a coffee spot, the kind of combination that makes small New England towns disproportionately pleasant to spend time in. Browse a well-curated selection of regional titles, hiking guides, and general fiction, then settle in with a coffee. The section on White Mountains hiking and natural history is particularly good — useful for planning walks in the surrounding area. Staff are genuinely knowledgeable about both the books and the region.
