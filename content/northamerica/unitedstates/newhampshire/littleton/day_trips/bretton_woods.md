About 30 miles east of Littleton via US-302, the Mount Washington Hotel is one of the grandest of the surviving White Mountains grand hotels — a vast white-and-red Spanish Renaissance pile set against the Presidentials with Mount Washington itself visible from the front porch. It opened in 1902, and in July 1944 hosted the Bretton Woods Conference that established the post-war international monetary system and created the IMF and World Bank. The Gold Room where the delegates signed the agreement is still there.

The hotel operates year-round and allows non-guests to walk the grounds and visit the lobby. A ride on the Cog Railway, which chugs up Mount Washington from a base station nearby, can be combined with a Bretton Woods visit — book ahead in summer, as seats sell out. The railway has been running since 1869, making it the second-oldest mountain-climbing cog railway in the world.
