A tiny hilltop town about 10 miles southwest of Littleton, Sugar Hill is worth a detour in June when its roadsides and meadows fill with lupine — tall spires of purple, pink, and white blooms that put on one of the most vivid floral displays in New England. The Lupine Festival, held for two weeks in mid-June, draws visitors from across the region.

Harman's Cheese and Country Store on Route 117 has been selling aged cheddar from the same recipe since 1955. The sharp cheddar, aged two years, is exceptional and ships nationwide — but it's worth buying a wedge directly at the store. The views from the hill on a clear day extend across the Franconia Range to the north and Vermont's Green Mountains to the west.

Polly's Pancake Parlor, a Sugar Hill institution since 1938, serves pancakes made from stone-ground flours — the buckwheat and cornmeal versions are the ones to order.
