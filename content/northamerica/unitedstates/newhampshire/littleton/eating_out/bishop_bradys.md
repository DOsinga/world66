A Main Street pub with a menu built around local sourcing — burgers from nearby farms, seasonal sides, a good selection of New Hampshire craft beers on tap. The interior is warm wood and exposed brick, the kind of place that fills up on a cold October evening with hikers who've come in from the mountains. Portions are substantial. Worth noting that the kitchen closes earlier than you might expect for a pub, so arrive by eight if you want a full meal.
