McIntyre Ski Area is a compact local ski hill inside Manchester, useful less for epic terrain than for easy winter fun close to the city. Lessons, tubing, and short runs make it especially practical for families or beginners.

It only clears the travel threshold in season and with the right expectations. Check snowmaking, hours, and tubing sessions before going; experienced skiers will want the larger New Hampshire mountains instead.
