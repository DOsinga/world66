The Loon Center is the public face of New Hampshire's loon conservation work. Exhibits explain the bird, the threats it faces, and the monitoring done across the state.

The setting matters as much as the displays. The center sits on the Markus Wildlife Sanctuary near the north shore of Lake Winnipesaukee, with trails that give you a quieter look at the lake environment behind the summer cottages and boat traffic.
