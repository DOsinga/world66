The Old Country Store and Museum is a working general store with deep roots in the village. Its owners describe it as a store since 1781, and the state tourism listing calls it one of the oldest general stores in the United States.

The shelves are part practical, part souvenir, and part New England time capsule. The small museum upstairs gives the stop enough local history to make it more than a quick shopping break.
