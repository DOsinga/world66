The Music Hall is Portsmouth's primary performing arts venue, housed in a beautifully restored 1878 Victorian theatre on Chestnut Street. The auditorium seats 895 and hosts a year-round program of concerts, film series, author events, stand-up comedy, and theatrical productions. The acoustics are good and the sightlines from all parts of the house are excellent.

The venue also operates a second, smaller space called The Loft for more intimate performances and events. Checking the Music Hall program before a visit to Portsmouth often turns up something worth building an evening around. Tickets can be bought online or at the box office.
