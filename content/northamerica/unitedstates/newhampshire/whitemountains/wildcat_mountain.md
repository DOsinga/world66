Wildcat Mountain rises across Pinkham Notch from Mount Washington and is known for some of the best mountain views in the White Mountains. In clear weather, the summit looks straight into the Presidential Range.

In winter it is a ski area with steep, scenic terrain. Outside ski season, the mountain still works as a viewpoint and hiking destination, especially for travellers based around Jackson or Gorham.
