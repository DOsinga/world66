The Belmar boardwalk was rebuilt after Superstorm Sandy destroyed it in 2012 and runs for about a mile along the oceanfront, connecting south toward the Spring Lake border. It is a simple, well-maintained wooden walkway — no arcades, no rides — used mostly by joggers in the morning and walkers in the evening. Cyclists are allowed in the early hours.

At the southern end it connects to the Spring Lake boardwalk, which continues through the quieter, more upscale town to the south. Running the combined stretch is a solid five-kilometre route with unbroken ocean views. In the evening, the boardwalk fills with people drifting between the beach and the Main Street bars — a leisurely Shore ritual that needs no improvement.
