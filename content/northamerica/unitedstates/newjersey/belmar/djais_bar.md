D'Jais (pronounced "dee-jays") has been running on Belmar's Ocean Avenue since 1963 and is the best-known bar on the central Jersey Shore. The formula is uncomplicated: a large outdoor deck facing the ocean, live music and DJs from afternoon through the night, and a crowd drawn from across North Jersey and New York. On summer Saturday afternoons the place is already packed by 2 pm.

The bar serves the full Shore menu — frozen drinks, buckets of beer, basic food — without any pretension about it. This is not a craft cocktail spot. It is a place to be loud and happy with a large number of strangers on a hot summer night, which is a specific and irreplaceable experience. Go early on weekends if you want any hope of space at the bar.
