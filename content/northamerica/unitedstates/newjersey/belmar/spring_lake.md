---

Spring Lake sits immediately south of Belmar — you can walk between them along the connected boardwalk — but the atmosphere shifts almost immediately. The town is one of the wealthiest communities on the Jersey Shore, and it shows: wide porched Victorian houses, carefully tended gardens, and a boardwalk free of arcades and concession stands. The beach is good and the lake in the centre of town is a quiet counterpoint to the ocean.

The boardwalk connecting Belmar to Spring Lake makes for an excellent run or walk of around five kilometres with unbroken ocean views. Coming from the noise of Belmar, the transition to Spring Lake's quieter streets is refreshing. There are several good cafes and lunch spots on Spring Lake's main street. The two towns together make a natural half-day combination.
