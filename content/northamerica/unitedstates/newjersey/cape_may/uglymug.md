A neighbourhood bar on Washington Street Mall that has been there since 1926 and never bothered to be anything fancier. Burgers, beer, and Wednesday-night Mug Club. The atmosphere is loud, friendly, and totally unpretentious — a useful counterweight to the porch-rocker gentility of the rest of town.
