---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Hoboken is one of the easiest places to reach in greater New York. The PATH train runs directly from Manhattan (Christopher Street, 9th Street, 14th Street, 23rd Street, 33rd Street) every few minutes and takes under ten minutes. NJ Transit commuter trains stop at the historic Terminal from Newark, Newark Liberty Airport, and points further out in New Jersey. NY Waterway ferries cross the Hudson from Midtown West and Wall Street.

By car, the Lincoln Tunnel feeds straight into Hoboken via Weehawken; parking on the street is metered and tightly enforced. Newark Liberty (EWR) is about 30 minutes by NJ Transit train; LaGuardia (LGA) and JFK are easier reached by ride-share via the Lincoln Tunnel.
