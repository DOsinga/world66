title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Lambertville rewards slow exploration on foot. The compact grid of streets near the Delaware River is small enough to cover thoroughly in a morning, and that is precisely how it should be experienced — strolling in and out of antique shops, pausing on the pedestrian bridge over to [New Hope](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/newhope), doubling back for a coffee.

The [People's Store Antiques Center](#peoples_store) is the largest single antique destination, with over 50 dealers spread across four floors of a historic building. [The Delaware and Raritan Canal towpath](#delaware_raritan_canal) runs through the centre of town and offers a flat, car-free route for walking or cycling north and south along the water. The [New Hope-Lambertville Bridge](#new_hope_lambertville_bridge) connects the two towns via a pedestrian walkway — cross it at least once, ideally in both directions as the light changes over the Delaware.

For those interested in local history, the [Lambertville Historical Society](#lambertville_historical_society) occupies the Marshall House, a Federal-style building on the National Register of Historic Places.
