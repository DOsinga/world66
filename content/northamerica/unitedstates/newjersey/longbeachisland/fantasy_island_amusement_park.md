Fantasy Island Amusement Park is Long Beach Island's old-fashioned evening fun zone, with rides, arcade games and bright boardwalk energy in Beach Haven. It is aimed squarely at families, and the appeal is simple: a few hours of lights, noise and summer ritual after a beach day.

It is not a destination theme park, but it is one of the island's most dependable family stops. Check seasonal hours, since the whole experience is tied to shore-town summer rhythms.
