The Holgate Unit of Edwin B. Forsythe National Wildlife Refuge protects the wild
southern tip of Long Beach Island, where beach, dunes, marsh, and ocean edge are managed
first for birds and coastal habitat. It is the island at its least built-up and most
exposed.

Access can be seasonal or restricted to protect nesting wildlife, so check refuge rules
before setting out. When open, it is a superb walk for birders, beachcombers, and anyone
who wants to see LBI beyond houses and summer crowds.
