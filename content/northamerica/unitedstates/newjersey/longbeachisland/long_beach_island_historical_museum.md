The Long Beach Island Historical Museum is a small local museum in Beach Haven, focused on the island's resort, maritime, and community past. It gives useful depth to a place many visitors experience only as beach houses, dunes, and summer traffic.

The collection is best enjoyed as a short stop on a cloudy day or between beach sessions. Old photographs, objects, and local stories help make the barrier island feel like a lived-in place with memory, not just a vacation strip.
