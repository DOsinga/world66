One of the oldest African American churches in New Jersey, Bethel A.M.E. was founded in 1843 by free Black residents of Morristown. The Carpenter Gothic church building from 1849 survives on Spring Street, a striking and rare example of pre-Civil War Black religious architecture in the region.

The congregation has a long history of activism and community support, and the church remains active today. The building is modest but historically significant; visitors are welcome during services and by arrangement at other times. It's a short walk north of the Green.
