Aljira is a contemporary art space in downtown Newark known for socially and politically engaged exhibitions. It is smaller and more experimental than the Newark Museum of Art, which makes it a useful stop for visitors interested in the city's current art scene.

Check the exhibition schedule before making a special trip, as the experience depends heavily on the current show.
