Newark Symphony Hall is a major historic performance venue south of downtown, with a legacy that spans classical music, jazz, gospel, comedy, and civic events. It is one of the city's anchor cultural buildings, even when a visit depends on the current calendar.

For travelers, the hall is most relevant when there is a performance, but the building itself also belongs in Newark's cultural map alongside NJPAC and the Newark Museum of Art. Check listings before planning around it.
