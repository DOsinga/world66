The World Cup Corner Mural is a public-art landmark in Newark's Ironbound, a neighbourhood where Portuguese, Brazilian, and other immigrant communities have made football part of the street culture. The mural is less a formal monument than a local expression of identity, fandom, and neighbourhood pride.

It is worth a quick stop if you are already exploring Ferry Street and the Ironbound's restaurants, bakeries, and shops. Do not build a whole itinerary around it, but let it point you toward the neighbourhood's character: multilingual, football-mad, and far more interesting than Newark stereotypes suggest.
