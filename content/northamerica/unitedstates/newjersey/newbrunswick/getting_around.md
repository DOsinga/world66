---
title: 'Getting Around'
type: section
---

Downtown New Brunswick is walkable — most sights, theatres, restaurants, and the train station are within a 15-minute walk. To reach the further Rutgers campuses (Busch, Livingston, Douglass, Cook) take the free Rutgers bus system, which loops between them throughout the day. For Johnson Park and Rutgers Gardens to the south, you will want a car or a rideshare.
