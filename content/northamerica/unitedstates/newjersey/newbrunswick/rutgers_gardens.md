Rutgers Gardens is New Brunswick's easiest green escape: a public garden connected with Rutgers University but open in spirit to anyone who wants a slower hour outside the downtown grid. The grounds mix formal plantings, woodland paths, shade gardens, and seasonal collections, so it changes character through the year.

It is most worthwhile in spring, summer, and autumn, when the plantings and trails give you something more than a simple campus stroll. Bring it into a New Brunswick visit if you have a car or are already exploring Rutgers beyond the historic core. The garden is quiet rather than spectacular, but that is part of its appeal.
