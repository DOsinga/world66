---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Princeton's coffee scene runs heavy on independents. **Small World Coffee** has two cafes (Witherspoon and Nassau) and roasts its own beans; the Witherspoon branch is the one for working alone on a laptop. **Sakrid Coffee Roasters** on Witherspoon is the newer challenger, slightly more serious about the pour-over.

For evening drinks, the **Nassau Inn**'s Yankee Doodle Taproom is the unavoidable institution — a long wooden bar, panelled walls, and a vast painted mural by Norman Rockwell of a young Yankee Doodle himself. **Triumph Brewing Company** brews on the premises and is the obvious choice for craft beer. **Witherspoon Grill** has the best after-work bar scene for the older crowd; **Alchemist & Barrister** is the dependable pub for students.
