Princeton sits in central New Jersey within easy reach of several worthwhile destinations. **Lambertville**, twenty minutes northwest on the Delaware, is the prettiest small town in the state — a strip of antique shops, restaurants, and a footbridge over the river to New Hope, Pennsylvania, on the other side. **Washington Crossing State Park** between them marks the spot where Washington crossed the icy Delaware on Christmas night 1776 — combine with the battlefield for the full opening week of the Trenton-Princeton campaign.

**Trenton**, ten minutes south, is the state capital and has the New Jersey State Museum, the Old Barracks Museum (the only surviving Revolutionary War barracks), and the State House. **New Hope** and **Doylestown** in Pennsylvania add a half-day each: galleries, the Mercer Museum, and the Moravian Tile Works.

For longer day trips, **New York** is about 90 minutes by train and **Philadelphia** is about 70 minutes by car. Both are perfectly doable as one-day trips from Princeton if you start early.
