---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By train.** From New York Penn Station, take an NJ Transit train on the Northeast Corridor line to **Princeton Junction** (about 50 minutes), then change to the **Dinky** — a tiny two-car shuttle that runs the three-minute hop from Princeton Junction into the campus station behind the Nassau Inn. From Philadelphia 30th Street, the SEPTA-then-NJ Transit route via Trenton is the usual approach (90 minutes).

**By Amtrak.** Some Amtrak trains stop at Princeton Junction, which is faster than NJ Transit but costs significantly more. For most people NJ Transit is the sensible choice.

**By car.** Princeton is about an hour from New York by Lincoln Tunnel and the New Jersey Turnpike, and an hour from Philadelphia by I-95. Parking inside the centre is limited and not free; the surface lots around the train station are the best option for day-trippers.

**By air.** Newark Liberty (EWR) is the closest major airport, about 50 minutes by car or about 90 minutes by airport bus and train. Trenton-Mercer (TTN) is closer but has very limited service.
