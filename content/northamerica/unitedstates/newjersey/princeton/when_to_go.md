---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May, September, and October** are the best months for Princeton. The campus is at its most photogenic in late spring (flowering dogwoods and tulips in the quadrangles) and in mid-autumn (the maples around Nassau Hall and the chapel turn fierce red). The weather is dependable, students are around, and the town has its proper rhythm.

**Reunions** at the end of May — when 25,000 alumni descend on town for a long weekend — is a unique experience if you happen to be in the area, but a difficult time to find a hotel. **Commencement** the week after is similar. **August** is hot, humid, and the campus is empty; **January and February** are cold but the campus in snow is genuinely beautiful and rooms are easy to come by.

The town empties dramatically during the winter and summer breaks; if you want the place at its liveliest, time a visit to the academic year between mid-September and early May, ideally on a weekday.
