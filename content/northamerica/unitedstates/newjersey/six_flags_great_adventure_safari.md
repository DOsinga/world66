Six Flags Great Adventure & Safari is New Jersey's major theme park, with big roller coasters, animal-drive safari areas, and the Hurricane Harbor water park next door. It is a full-day stop rather than a quick diversion, especially for families or coaster fans crossing the state.

The park changes rides and seasonal events often, so check the current lineup before planning around a specific attraction. Summer weekends are busy; arriving early makes a real difference.
