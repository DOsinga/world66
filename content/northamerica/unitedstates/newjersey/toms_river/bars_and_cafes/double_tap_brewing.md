One of the better examples of New Jersey's craft beer expansion reaching into the Shore suburbs, Double Tap Brewing operates a taproom in Toms River with a rotating selection of house-brewed beers. The space is comfortable and relaxed, with food trucks or pub snacks depending on the day. A welcome option for anyone who finds the standard bar scene uninspiring. Check the website for current tap lists and food availability.
