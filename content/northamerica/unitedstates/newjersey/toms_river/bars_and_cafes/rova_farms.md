A remarkable piece of New Jersey history, Rova Farms in the Pine Barrens just west of Toms River was established in 1934 by Russian-Orthodox immigrants as a community resort and retreat. The sprawling property still operates as a social club and event venue, with a bar and restaurant serving the membership and occasional visitors. Borscht features on the menu alongside standard American offerings. It's an unusual and genuinely fascinating place — a slice of immigrant community life preserved in the Pine Barrens. Call ahead before visiting.
