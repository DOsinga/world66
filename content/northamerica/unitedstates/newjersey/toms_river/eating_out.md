---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The dining scene in Toms River runs heavily to Italian-American and seafood, with a scattering of good diners. The barrier island towns are where the seafood gets serious — Ortley Beach, Seaside Park, and the towns along the bay strip serve fried seafood platters, steamers, and chowder through the summer season. In town, the options are more year-round and less tourist-oriented.
