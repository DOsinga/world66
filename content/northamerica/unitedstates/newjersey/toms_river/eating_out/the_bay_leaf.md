A well-regarded Italian-American restaurant in the heart of the Toms River downtown, The Bay Leaf draws a mix of locals and visitors looking for something more substantial than boardwalk food. The menu runs through the familiar Shore-area canon — pasta, veal, chicken, and fish — but the kitchen executes it well. The dining room is comfortable rather than fancy, and the service is practiced. Reservations recommended on summer weekends.
