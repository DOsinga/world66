A reliable waterfront bar in the Toms River downtown area, Offshore is the kind of place where locals go after work and boaters tie up in season. The outdoor deck over the water is the main draw when the weather is good — cold drinks, river views, and a bar menu heavy on fried appetisers and sandwiches. The atmosphere is unpretentious and genuinely local. Gets busy on summer weekends but is rarely unpleasant about it.
