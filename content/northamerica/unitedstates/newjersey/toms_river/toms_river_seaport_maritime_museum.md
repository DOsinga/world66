The Toms River Seaport Society & Maritime Museum is a small local museum focused on boats and maritime life around the Jersey Shore. The collection includes historic craft, local boating material, a library, and a small gift shop.

It is a good short stop if you are interested in Barnegat Bay's working waterfront and small-boat history.
