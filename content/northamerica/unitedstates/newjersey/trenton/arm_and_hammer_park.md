Arm & Hammer Park is Trenton’s riverfront baseball ground, best known as the home of the Trenton Thunder. It gives the city a relaxed, inexpensive evening option when games are on, with views and breezes from the Delaware River side of town.

The ballpark is more about atmosphere than architecture. Go for minor-league pace, families, between-innings rituals, and the easy pleasure of a summer game rather than a polished big-city stadium experience.
