Grounds for Sculpture is the Trenton area's standout art excursion, a broad landscaped park filled with contemporary sculpture, gardens, ponds, and paths that encourage slow wandering. It turns art into a walk rather than a wall label, which makes it approachable even for travellers who do not usually plan museum days.

The park is large enough to deserve several hours, especially in good weather. Some works are playful, others formal or strange, and the changing planting gives repeat visits a different feel. Book ahead when required and treat it as a major stop, not a quick detour from downtown Trenton.
