The Trenton Transit Center on Angle Street handles NJ Transit regional rail, SEPTA regional rail, Amtrak, and NJ Transit bus routes. It is a functional if unremarkable station about ten minutes' walk from the State House. The River Line light rail departs from the surface platform here for Camden and intermediate stops.

Lockers, a waiting room, and a small cafe are available inside. Taxis and rideshares meet trains on the Angle Street side.
