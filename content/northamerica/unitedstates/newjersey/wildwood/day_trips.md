[Cape May](/northamerica/unitedstates/newjersey/cape_may), with its Victorian houses and quieter beaches, is fifteen minutes south. [Atlantic City](/northamerica/unitedstates/newjersey/atlanticcity), about an hour up the coast, makes a contrasting day out — casinos, a longer boardwalk, and a much harder edge. The Cape May–Lewes Ferry, departing from North Cape May, lets you cross to Lewes in Delaware in 85 minutes if you want a third state in a day.
