---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Wildwood food is boardwalk food: pizza by the slice, funnel cake, fried Oreos, fudge from Laura's, and salt water taffy. For something with table service, the back streets between the boardwalk and Pacific Avenue hold the better bets — Italian-American family restaurants, Cuban sandwich counters, and a few diners that open before dawn for the fishing crowd.
