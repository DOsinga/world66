Morey's Piers is the heart of Wildwood's amusement scene — three piers (Mariner's, Surfside, and Adventure) plus two beachfront waterparks running along the boardwalk between Spencer and 25th avenues. There are more than a hundred rides in total, from the **Great White** wooden roller coaster (which actually dips beneath the boardwalk) to the **Great Nor'Easter** suspended looping coaster and a tall Ferris wheel that gives a long view down the coast on a clear evening.

A day-pass wristband covers all three piers and one of the waterparks. Lines build through the afternoon; the smart play is to arrive at opening, ride the big coasters first, then drift to the waterparks for the heat of the day. The piers stay open late on summer weekends — the lights and music after dark are part of the appeal.
