Tularosa Basin Historical Museum is a local history museum near Alameda Park Zoo, with photographs, documents, and everyday objects from Alamogordo and the surrounding basin. It is a useful stop for context on the railroad, ranching, military, and space-history strands that shaped this corner of southern New Mexico.
