The ABQ BioPark Aquarium is part of Albuquerque's riverside BioPark complex, presenting ocean and river habitats with a strong family focus. Its exhibits move from Rio Grande ecology toward larger marine tanks, giving desert-city visitors a watery counterpoint to the bosque and mountains outside.

The aquarium is most rewarding when combined with the Botanic Garden, zoo, or a walk near Tingley Beach. It is manageable in size, which makes it useful with children or on hot afternoons. Do not expect a giant coastal aquarium; expect a well-placed local institution that broadens Albuquerque's natural-history story.
