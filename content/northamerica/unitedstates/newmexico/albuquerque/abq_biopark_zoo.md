ABQ BioPark Zoo is one of Albuquerque's main family attractions and part of the wider BioPark system that also includes the botanic garden and aquarium. The zoo's animal areas, shade, and central location make it easy to fit into a city itinerary.

It is especially useful for families, but it also rounds out the BioPark as a full-day option. If you are choosing only one nature-oriented stop, compare it with the botanic garden and Bosque trails depending on the weather and your interests.
