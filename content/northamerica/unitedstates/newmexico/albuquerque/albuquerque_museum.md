Albuquerque Museum is the main city museum for art and history, a short walk from Old Town. The permanent exhibits cover Albuquerque's development and the wider Rio Grande valley, while the art collection focuses on New Mexico and the Southwest.

It is also a practical starting point for guided Old Town walks. Allow time for the sculpture garden and rotating exhibitions if you want more than a quick historical overview.
