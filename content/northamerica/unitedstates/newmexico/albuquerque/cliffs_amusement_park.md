Cliff's Amusement Park is a compact Albuquerque amusement park with thrill rides, family rides, kiddie rides, and a small water play area. Its scale is modest, but it is one of the clearest family-focused attractions in the city.

Check the operating calendar before going, since hours and ride availability can vary by season.
