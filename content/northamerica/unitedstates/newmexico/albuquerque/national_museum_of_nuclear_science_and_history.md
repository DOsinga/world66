The National Museum of Nuclear Science and History is one of Albuquerque's strongest museums, and not just for science specialists. It traces nuclear research from the Manhattan Project and Cold War weapons programmes through medicine, energy, and space technology, using aircraft, missiles, reactor models, and clear historical displays.

The outdoor heritage park gives the visit its physical punch, with large military and research hardware that makes the abstract history tangible. It is a serious museum with difficult material, but it explains New Mexico's atomic-era role better than almost anywhere else in the city.
