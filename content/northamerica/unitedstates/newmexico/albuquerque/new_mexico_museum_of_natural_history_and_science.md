The New Mexico Museum of Natural History and Science is one of Albuquerque’s strongest family-friendly museums, with dinosaurs, fossils, volcanoes, space science, and geology tied to the landscapes of the Southwest. It is polished without losing its regional focus.

The museum works for children, but adults get plenty from the deep-time story of New Mexico’s deserts, rift valleys, and ancient life. It pairs well with Old Town, since both sit close together but tell completely different versions of the city’s setting.
