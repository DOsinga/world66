The Box Performance Space is a small downtown venue for sketch comedy, improv, children's theatre, and independent stage work. It is home to local groups including Cardboard Playhouse Productions and Blackout Theatre Company.

Check the schedule rather than dropping by at random; the appeal here is catching a local show in a compact room.
