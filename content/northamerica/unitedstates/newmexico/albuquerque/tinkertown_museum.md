Tinkertown Museum is a folk-art museum in the Sandia Mountains, packed with hand-carved miniature Western towns, carnival scenes, animated figures, old arcade machines, dolls, tools, and memorabilia. It is eccentric, detailed, and best treated as a short side trip rather than a conventional museum visit.
