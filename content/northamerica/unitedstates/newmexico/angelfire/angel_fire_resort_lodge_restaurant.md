The main sit-down restaurant at the resort lodge serves predictable but well-executed mountain fare: New Mexican green chile dishes, steaks, burgers, and pasta. It is open for dinner most nights during ski season and lunch on weekends. In summer the schedule is reduced; call ahead. The room is comfortable and the portions are generous — this is a good default option if you are staying on the mountain and do not want to drive into town.
