The Legend Bar, at the base of the resort, is the natural gathering point after a day on the mountain. Cold beers, a pool table, and a relaxed crowd of skiers and snowboarders; in summer it catches the mountain bike crowd coming off the trails. It opens for happy hour in the afternoon on ski days. The vibe is unpretentious and the drinks are reasonably priced for a resort.
