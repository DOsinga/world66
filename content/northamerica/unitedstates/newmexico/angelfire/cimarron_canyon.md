Cimarron Canyon State Park lies about 30 miles southeast of Angel Fire on US-64, where the road descends from the high valley into a dramatic narrow canyon cut through red and grey rock by the Cimarron River. The canyon is one of the best trout fishing spots in New Mexico, and the hiking trails climb the canyon walls into ponderosa pine forest. Further east, the town of Cimarron was a stop on the Santa Fe Trail and has a well-preserved historic hotel, the St. James, where bullet holes in the ceiling recall its rougher days.
