---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Angel Fire's position on the Enchanted Circle makes it an excellent base for day trips. Taos, with its historic pueblo, world-class art galleries, and demanding ski valley, is 25 miles west over the pass. Red River, a small mountain town with its own ski area, sits about 35 miles north via the Enchanted Circle. Cimarron and the Philmont Scout Ranch are a scenic drive east through Cimarron Canyon. Santa Fe is about two hours south — a long day trip but worth it for the museums, the Palace of the Governors, and the canyon road galleries.
