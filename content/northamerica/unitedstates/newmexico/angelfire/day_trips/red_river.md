Red River is a former gold-mining town at the north end of the Enchanted Circle, about 35 miles from Angel Fire via NM-38. The town sits in a steep canyon at around 8,700 feet and retains an old-west wooden-storefront character that feels less manufactured than many ski towns. Red River Ski Area is small and affordable, popular with families from Texas and Oklahoma. In summer the canyon road is excellent for cycling and the surrounding national forest trails are lightly used.
