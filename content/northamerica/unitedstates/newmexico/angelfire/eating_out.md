---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Dining in Angel Fire is limited but satisfying. Most restaurants cluster near the resort base area and along NM-434; expect casual mountain cooking rather than destination cuisine. The best meals tend to be comfort food done well — green chile, burgers, and hearty breakfasts. A few places step it up in ski season; in summer options thin out, so check that a place is open before making a special trip.
