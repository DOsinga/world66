The café attached to the Abominable Snowmansion hostel in nearby Arroyo Seco (a short drive from Angel Fire) draws a loyal following for its breakfasts and simple lunches. Pancakes, breakfast burritos, and strong coffee make it a reliable start to a ski or hiking day. The hostel atmosphere is welcoming to non-guests; the kitchen uses local ingredients where it can.
