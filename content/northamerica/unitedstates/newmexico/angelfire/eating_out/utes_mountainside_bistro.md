A small, locally owned bistro that draws regulars for its green chile cheeseburgers and New Mexican combination plates. The menu leans on local flavours — red and green chile sauces made in-house — and the setting is informal. Lunch and early dinner are the best times to visit; it gets busy on weekend evenings in peak ski season.
