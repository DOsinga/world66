A small coffee shop on the main village road serving espresso drinks and drip coffee alongside pastries and light sandwiches. It opens early enough to catch skiers heading to the mountain for first lift; in summer it caters to hikers and cyclists. Nothing fancy, but a welcome anchor in a village that otherwise runs on fast food and resort kitchens.
