Taos sits 25 miles west of Angel Fire over the Palo Flechado Pass on US-64, a drive that takes about 40 minutes in good conditions. The town has enough to fill a full day: the Taos Pueblo, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the oldest continuously inhabited communities in North America; the Rio Grande Gorge Bridge, a dramatic span 650 feet above the river; and a compact historic plaza ringed with galleries and restaurants. Taos Ski Valley, a further 20 minutes into the mountains, is one of New Mexico's most challenging ski resorts.
