---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The railroad season, May through October, is the main factor shaping a visit to Chama. **June through October** is the sweet spot: the train is running, the high country is accessible for hiking, and the weather is reliable if afternoon thunderstorms are common. **September and October** are exceptional — the aspen groves turn gold, the crowds thin after Labor Day, and the weather is cooler and steadier. The fall colour along the rail route is extraordinary.

**Summer** (July–August) is warm and busy, with the train fully booked on weekends. Winter brings snow and cold; Chama is ski country in a modest way, and the cross-country skiing in the mountains is good when conditions are right. The town is very quiet from November to April, and many services operate reduced hours or close entirely.
