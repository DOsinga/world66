---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The reason to be in this corner of New Mexico is the country around it. **White Sands National Park**, an hour and a half north on US-54, is the largest gypsum dune field on earth and the standout sight of southern New Mexico. **El Paso** and across the border into **Ciudad Juárez** sit twenty minutes south; the Franklin Mountains State Park, which fills the city's centre, has some of the best urban hiking in the United States. **Las Cruces** and the **Organ Mountains** are 30 minutes northwest, and the Mesilla historic district just south of Las Cruces has the only nineteenth-century plaza in the area that still feels like the old Southwest.
