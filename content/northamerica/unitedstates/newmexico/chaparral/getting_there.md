---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Chaparral straddles US-54, the main highway between El Paso and Alamogordo. From **El Paso International Airport** it is a twenty-minute drive north; from **Las Cruces** about twenty-five minutes south on NM-404. There is no public transport. The nearest Greyhound stop is in El Paso. Most people who pass through Chaparral are doing so by car, often on the way to **White Sands** or **Carlsbad Caverns**.
