---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Chaparral itself has no real sights — it is a stretch of gas stations, mobile homes and fast-food strips along US-54. What makes the area worth pulling over for is the desert outside town: the open Tularosa Basin to the north, the Franklin Mountains rising above El Paso to the south, and the empty sky in between. Bring water, fill the tank and keep going.
