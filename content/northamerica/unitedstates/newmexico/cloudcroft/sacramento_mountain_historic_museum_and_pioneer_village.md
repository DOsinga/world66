The Sacramento Mountain Historic Museum and Pioneer Village gives Cloudcroft a useful historical anchor. Its buildings and exhibits explain the mountain settlement, logging, rail links, and high-country life that made this village more than a cool summer retreat.

It is a small, local museum, but that scale suits Cloudcroft. Visit before or after hiking in Lincoln National Forest and the old resort town starts to make more sense.
