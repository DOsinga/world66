---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Clovis is at the junction of **US-60**, **US-70** and **US-84**, about 100 miles west of Amarillo, Texas and 220 miles east of Albuquerque. By road it is a long, level drive across the High Plains either way.

The town has a **municipal airport** (CVN) but no regular commercial passenger service; the nearest major airport is **Amarillo (AMA)**, about an hour and forty minutes' drive east.

Amtrak's **Southwest Chief** stops at Clovis once a day in each direction on its run between Chicago and Los Angeles — one of the few remaining long-distance passenger trains in the southwest. The depot is on 1st Street downtown and the platform sits next to the original AT&SF Mission Revival station building.
