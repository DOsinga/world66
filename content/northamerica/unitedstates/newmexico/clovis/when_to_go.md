---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Clovis sits at about 1,300 metres on the southern High Plains, with a semi-arid climate of hot dry summers and cold dry winters. **Spring (March–May)** can be windy with occasional dust storms but is otherwise pleasant. **Summer (June–August)** brings highs of 32–36°C and most of the year's thunderstorms, often dramatic late-afternoon affairs. **Autumn (September–November)** is the most reliable travel season — long sunny days, cool nights, and the best light for photography.

**Winters** are cold but not severe; daytime highs run 8–14°C, with the occasional Arctic front pulling temperatures briefly below freezing. The **Clovis Music Festival** in early September celebrates the town's Buddy Holly heritage and is the busiest weekend of the year — book lodging well ahead.
