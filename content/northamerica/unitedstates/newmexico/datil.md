A tiny ranching village on US-60 in west central New Mexico. The BLM campground at [Datil Well](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/datil/datil_well_campground) is a pleasant overnight stop -- it marks the spot where cattle were watered on their way to [Magdalena](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/magdalena) and the railroad. If you are driving the lonely stretch between Socorro and [Pie Town](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/pie_town), this is your midpoint.

Datil's main draw, though, is what stretches out to the south: the [Plains of San Agustin](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/datil/plains_of_san_agustin), an enormous high-desert basin that hosts the [Very Large Array](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/datil/very_large_array) — twenty-seven radio telescope dishes arranged across miles of dry lake bed, one of the most striking man-made sights in the American Southwest. Allow half a day for the side trip; the visitor centre is open every day until sunset.
