Deming sits in the open desert of southwest New Mexico, close to the Florida Mountains and the old Mimbres country. The town itself is plain and practical, but it makes a useful base for a day of rockhounding, desert geology, and regional history.

The best reason to stop is the landscape around town. [Rockhound State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/deming/rockhound_state_park) lets visitors search for agate, jasper, and other small specimens on the slopes of the Little Florida Mountains, while [City of Rocks State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/deming/city_of_rocks_state_park) is a strange field of volcanic boulders north of town. In town, the [Deming Luna Mimbres Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/deming/deming_luna_mimbres_museum) gives the stop some human context, with Mimbres pottery, ranching history, and borderland material in an old armory.

Deming is not a polished resort town, and that is part of the deal. It works best for travelers already crossing southern New Mexico who would rather spend a spare day in desert parks and local museums than hurry between Las Cruces and Silver City.
