Rockhound State Park protects the rugged lower slopes of the Little Florida Mountains southeast of Deming. The unusual draw is that visitors may collect small amounts of jasper, agate, quartz, and other minerals for personal use, which turns an ordinary desert walk into a slow treasure hunt.
