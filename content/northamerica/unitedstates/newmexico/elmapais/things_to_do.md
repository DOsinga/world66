title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The best easy visit at El Malpais starts at the [Sandstone Bluffs Overlook](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/elmapais/sandstone_bluffs_overlook) for the panorama, then drops down to the [La Ventana Natural Arch](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/elmapais/la_ventana_arch) for a short hike to one of the largest sandstone arches in the state. With more time, swing around to the west side for the [Ice Cave and Bandera Volcano](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/elmapais/ice_cave_bandera_volcano) and the cinder-cone hike at [El Calderon](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/elmapais/el_calderon).

For the more committed: the [Zuni-Acoma Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/elmapais/zuni_acoma_trail) is a 7.5-mile traverse of the ancient lava flows along a pre-Columbian trade route — hard but unforgettable. Equally serious cavers can pick up a permit at the visitor center for the [Big Tubes lava cave system](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/elmapais/lava_tubes). The youngest lava flow, the [McCarty's Flow](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/elmapais/mccartys_flow), is just over 3,000 years old and looks like it cooled yesterday.
