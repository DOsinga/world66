Plaza de Espanola is a small arts and culture complex centered on the Convento, a replica church inspired by historic missions in northern New Mexico. The complex includes gallery space and a gift shop, with local cultural events at times.

It is a useful short stop for visitors passing through Espanola who want a quick look at the area's Hispanic and Pueblo-influenced heritage.
