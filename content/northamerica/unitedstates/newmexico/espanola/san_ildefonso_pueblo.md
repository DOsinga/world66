San Ildefonso Pueblo is best known for black-on-black pottery, a style made famous by Maria Martinez and later adopted by many Pueblo artists. The community sits near Black Mesa on the Rio Grande, and visits should be planned with respect for Pueblo rules on access, photography, and feast-day etiquette.
