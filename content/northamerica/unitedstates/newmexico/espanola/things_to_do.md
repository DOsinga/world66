title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Española punches well above its size when it comes to history and culture. The valley around town has been settled for centuries, and the surrounding pueblos, colonial-era churches, and unexpected local art scene give you more to do than a quick drive-through would suggest. The low rider culture here is genuine and pervasive — this is the city that legitimately claims the title of Low Rider Capital of the World, and the cruising culture is woven into everyday life.
