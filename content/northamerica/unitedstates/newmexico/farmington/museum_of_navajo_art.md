Located in historic downtown Farmington, this museum holds a significant collection of Navajo rugs spanning the early 1900s to the present day. The collection illustrates the evolution of Navajo weaving traditions and the distinct regional styles — from the geometric patterns of the Two Grey Hills area to the vivid colours of Wide Ruins and Crystal. The accompanying cultural exhibits provide context on Navajo history and the importance of weaving within the culture.

The museum is a good complement to buying rugs at the trading posts scattered around Farmington and the surrounding Navajo Nation. Understanding the provenance and style of what you're looking at makes the experience considerably richer. Staff are knowledgeable and often willing to discuss individual pieces in detail.
