The Inter-Tribal Indian Ceremonial, held each August in Gallup since 1922, is one of the most significant Native American cultural gatherings in the United States. Dozens of tribes from across the country send dancers, artists, and athletes to compete and perform over several days. The events include traditional dance competitions, a rodeo, a parade through downtown Gallup, and one of the largest Native American art markets in the region.

The Ceremonial is not a tourist performance — it is a genuine competition and celebration with tribal prizes at stake, and the participants take it seriously. For visitors, it offers an unparalleled chance to see the range of Native American cultural traditions in one place, from the dramatically different dance styles of different nations to the breadth of artistic production. Gallup's trading posts fill up for the week, and hotel rooms book out months in advance. If you're anywhere near the Four Corners area in mid-August, this is worth building a trip around.
