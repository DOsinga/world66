Red Rock Park is Gallup's signature outdoor landscape, where red sandstone cliffs rise around trails, rodeo grounds, and event spaces. It is close enough to town for a short visit but dramatic enough to feel like a real New Mexico landscape stop.

Go for the views, short hikes, and photography, especially near sunrise or late afternoon. The park also hosts major events, so check the calendar if you want either the liveliest atmosphere or a quieter trail day.
