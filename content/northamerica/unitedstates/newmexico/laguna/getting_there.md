---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Laguna Pueblo is located directly off Interstate 40 at Exit 114, about 45 miles west of Albuquerque. By car from Albuquerque it is a straightforward 40-minute drive west on I-40. There is no regular public transit to the pueblo itself, though Greyhound buses stop in Grants, about 30 miles further west. The Dancing Eagle Casino and Route 66 Casino — both operated by the Laguna tribe — are visible from the highway and have fuel and convenience facilities.
