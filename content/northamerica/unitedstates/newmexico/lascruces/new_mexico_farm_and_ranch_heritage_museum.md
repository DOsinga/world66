The New Mexico Farm and Ranch Heritage Museum is one of Las Cruces' strongest museums, covering thousands of years of farming, ranching, irrigation, and rural life in the region. Indoor galleries, livestock areas, and demonstrations make the subject more vivid than a standard history display.

Give it enough time: the site is large, and the best visit includes both exhibits and the outdoor areas. It is especially useful before driving through the Mesilla Valley, where the museum's themes are still visible in the landscape.
