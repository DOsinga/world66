A high desert semi-ghost town and genuine wild west icon, Magdalena sits in the shadow of the Magdalena Mountains about 27 miles west of Socorro. For decades it was the end of the "hoof highway" from Arizona — a busy railroad shipping point where hundreds of thousands of cattle were sold and loaded onto trains. The 1915 [Santa Fe Depot](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/magdalena/santa_fe_depot) still stands at the heart of the village.

Today it's a sleepy little place with [historic buildings](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/magdalena/main_street_historic_district), a small museum, and an offbeat appeal for travellers who want something well off the beaten track. The [Very Large Array](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/magdalena/very_large_array) radio telescope facility is a short drive to the west on US-60, which makes a compelling reason to come this way, and the ghost-mining-town ruins of [Kelly](/northamerica/unitedstates/newmexico/magdalena/kelly_ghost_town) sit three miles south in the mountains.
