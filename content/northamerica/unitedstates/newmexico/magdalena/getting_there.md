---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Magdalena is on US Highway 60, about 27 miles west of **Socorro** on I-25. From Albuquerque it's a 90-mile drive south on the interstate, then west — figure two hours.

There is no public transport. Continuing west on US-60, the highway crosses the Plains of San Agustin past the Very Large Array and on toward the Arizona state line — one of the most desolate and scenic stretches of road in the lower 48.
