Magdalena Ridge Observatory is a high-elevation astronomy facility in the mountains above town, giving Magdalena a science-oriented point of interest beyond its historic mining and railroad associations. The location itself also hints at why clear, dark New Mexico skies matter to research.

Access is not the same as a normal museum, so travellers should check public program or tour details before making the drive. When open to visitors, it is a distinctive stop for anyone interested in astronomy, mountain landscapes, or New Mexico Tech's research presence.
