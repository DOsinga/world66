---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Magdalena sits at 6,500 feet, so summers are warm rather than scorching and winters bring cold nights and the occasional snow. **April–May** and **September–October** are the most reliable months — clear skies, mild days, and the high desert in flower.

In **July and August** the monsoon brings dramatic afternoon thunderstorms that build over the mountains — spectacular to watch from the VLA if you keep one eye on the weather. The annual **Old Timer's Reunion** in early July is the village's big festival, with a parade, rodeo, and dance.
