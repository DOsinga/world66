Peñasco Blanco is the most distant of the Chacoan great houses on the loop, an oval pueblo on a mesa top at the far west end of the canyon. The 11 km round-trip trail follows the wash, passing pictograph panels including the famous Supernova Pictograph on the cliff face about 5 km in.

Few visitors make the full walk, and on a quiet day you can have the ruin to yourself. Allow four to five hours, and start at dawn in summer — there is no shade.
