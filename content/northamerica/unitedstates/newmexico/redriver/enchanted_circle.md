The Enchanted Circle is the road that makes Red River easy to understand. The loop circles Wheeler Peak, New Mexico's highest mountain, and links Red River with Questa, Taos, Angel Fire, and Eagle Nest. From town, the drive east over Bobcat Pass is the quickest way to feel the scale of the Sangre de Cristo range.

Treat the circle as a day drive with stops, not just a transfer route. Taos gives you art and pueblo history, Eagle Nest has lake views, and the high passes show why this corner of New Mexico feels more alpine than desert.
