Mallette Park is not a headline sight, but it is useful in the way resort towns need useful places. It gives you grass, trees, picnic space, and an easy walk near the river without committing to a mountain trail.

Use it between bigger activities: after the ski area, before dinner, or when children need room to run around. In a town built around outdoor recreation, a simple park in the right place earns its keep.
