---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Roswell eats New Mexican: enchiladas, sopaipillas, chile rellenos, and the local insistence on choosing red or green chile (or "Christmas" — both). Outside the chain-strip on the highway, the better places are downtown.

A typical meal in a casual restaurant runs $12-18. Most places close earlier than you'd expect — this is a small city, not a 24-hour town.
