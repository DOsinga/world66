A much more substantial museum than first-time visitors expect. Founded in 1937 as a WPA project, the collection covers Taos Society of Artists work, Henriette Wyeth and Peter Hurd (who lived nearby), and a rotating contemporary collection.

The real surprise is the Robert H. Goddard wing — a complete reconstruction of the rocketry pioneer's Roswell workshop, with original tools, blueprints, and a launch tower model. There is also a small planetarium operating Friday and Saturday evenings. Free admission. Closed Mondays.
