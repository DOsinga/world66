---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November)** are best — daytime highs of 18–27°C, cool nights, very little rain. Summer is hot (32–37°C is normal and 40°C is possible) and winter mornings can be sharply cold despite the desert sun.

The big event is the **Roswell UFO Festival**, held every year over the Independence Day weekend (early July). Tens of thousands of visitors arrive for the parade, costume contests, alien-themed art shows, lectures by UFOlogists, and a generally festive embrace of the town's reputation. Book accommodation months in advance and accept that it will be hot — but it's the obvious time to visit if the UFO angle is what brings you.

October to February is sandhill crane season at Bitter Lake — a serious draw for birders.
