Spencer Theater sits in the hills north of Ruidoso, a 514-seat performance hall that feels more ambitious than you might expect from a mountain resort town. The building opened in 1997 and was designed with sharp limestone forms that echo the Sacramento and Capitan Mountains around it; inside, the draw is the small scale, good acoustics, and a programme that ranges from touring musicians to theatre and dance.

It is best treated as an evening plan rather than a casual drop-in. Check the calendar before you drive out, and book through the box office when possible. Public guided tours are sometimes offered, which make sense if you are interested in the architecture or the theatre's glass art as much as the performances.
