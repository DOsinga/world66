---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Downtown Santa Fe is walkable. The Plaza, the cathedrals, Canyon Road, and the Railyard are all within 15 minutes of each other on foot. Wear comfortable shoes — many sidewalks are uneven and the altitude makes a casual walk more tiring than you expect.

For Museum Hill, the opera, Meow Wolf, or trips outside the city, you need wheels. The free **Santa Fe Pickup** city shuttle runs three routes including a useful loop between downtown, Museum Hill, and the Railyard. **Santa Fe Trails** city buses cover the wider city.

**Uber and Lyft** work well in town. **Renting a car** is the simplest option for any trip beyond walking distance — and essential for day trips to Ojo Caliente, the pueblos, Bandelier, or Taos.
