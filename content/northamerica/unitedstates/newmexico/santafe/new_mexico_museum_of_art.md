The New Mexico Museum of Art sits just off the Plaza in a Pueblo Revival building that is part of the experience. Its collection is strongest on artists connected to New Mexico and the Southwest, with enough variety to make it more than a regional footnote. The St. Francis Auditorium also hosts concerts and public events.
