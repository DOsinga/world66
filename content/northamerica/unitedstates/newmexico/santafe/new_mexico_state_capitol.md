The New Mexico State Capitol, known locally as the Roundhouse, is one of the most distinctive state capitols in the United States. Its circular plan and low profile set it apart from the domed capitol buildings found in many other states.

Visitors can usually enter during working hours. The building is worth a look for its architecture and for the public art displayed through the capitol complex.
