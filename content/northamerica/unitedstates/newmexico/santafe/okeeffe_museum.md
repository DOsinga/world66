A modest single-storey museum two blocks west of the Plaza, dedicated entirely to Georgia O'Keeffe — the painter most associated with the New Mexican landscape. The rotating exhibitions draw from a collection of around 3,000 pieces and pair her paintings with photographs, sketches, and personal materials.

The museum is small enough to absorb in an hour and a half. For a deeper engagement, book one of the day trips to her Abiquiú home (about an hour north), which the museum runs in summer. Closed some Wednesdays.
