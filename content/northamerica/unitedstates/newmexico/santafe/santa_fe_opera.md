A 2,128-seat open-air theatre in the hills 11 km north of the city, hosting one of the world's great summer opera festivals — early July to late August, five operas in rotation, sunset performances under desert skies. The architecture (rebuilt in 1998 by James Stewart Polshek) is unforgettable: a flared concrete roof, open sides, and a backdrop of the Jemez Mountains.

Pre-performance tailgating in the parking lot — proper picnics with china and candelabra — is half the experience. Bring layers; the sun sets at curtain-up and the desert cools fast. Tickets sell months ahead for the marquee productions.
