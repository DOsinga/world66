Santuario de Guadalupe is a Spanish Colonial church west of Santa Fe's historic centre. It is valued both for its architecture and for its collection of religious art.

The sanctuary is a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in Santa Fe's Catholic heritage beyond the plaza and cathedral area.
