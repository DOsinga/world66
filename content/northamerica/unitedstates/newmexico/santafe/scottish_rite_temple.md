Santa Fe's Scottish Rite Temple is a vivid pink Moorish Revival building modeled loosely on the Alhambra in Granada. Its color, arches, and towers stand out sharply from the adobe and Pueblo Revival architecture that defines much of central Santa Fe.

It is mainly an exterior architecture stop, best seen while walking between the Plaza, Cross of the Martyrs, and the museums around downtown.
