A non-collecting contemporary art space that has anchored the cutting edge of Santa Fe's art scene since 1995. SITE runs major exhibitions, an art biennial (now triennial), and a programme of artist talks and performances. The 2017 expansion by Shop Architects added a striking metal-clad façade that broadcasts the building's contemporary purpose against the railyard backdrop.

Shows rotate every few months. Closed Tuesdays. Admission free on Fridays.
