The smartest restaurant in Santa Fe, set in a 19th-century hacienda on Canyon Road. The dining rooms are spare, white-walled, and softly lit — a deliberate retreat from the Southwestern visual overload of the city outside. Chef Mark Kiffin (a James Beard winner) cooks an American menu with Mediterranean and Southwestern accents.

Reserve weeks ahead in summer and opera season. Dress code is smart-casual; jackets welcome but not required. Tasting menus available. Expect to spend serious money.
