Little Toad Creek is the obvious downtown stop when you want something local in the glass. It makes beer and spirits in New Mexico and keeps the mood casual, with pub food and regular live music.

It is more useful than fancy: a good place to compare notes after the Gila road or settle in when the evening cools down.
