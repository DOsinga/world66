Trinity Site is one of the most sobering places in New Mexico, though it is not a casual stop. The first atomic bomb was detonated here in July 1945, deep in the desert of the Jornada del Muerto, and public access is usually limited to occasional open-house days.

If your timing works, the visit is stark rather than scenic: a fenced ground zero, desert silence, and a history that still feels uncomfortably close. Check official access rules before planning around it.
