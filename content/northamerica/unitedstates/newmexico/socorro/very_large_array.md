The Very Large Array is a long drive west of Socorro, but it is worth the effort if the idea of radio astronomy in a wide, empty plain appeals at all. The white dishes stand far apart on rails, listening to the universe from a landscape that already feels half extraterrestrial.

Visitor access changes with operations, so check current hours before driving out. Even a simple self-guided visit gives you a rare chance to see big science at landscape scale.
