---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Taos has a surprisingly good restaurant scene for its size. New Mexican cuisine — green and red chile, posole, sopapillas, blue corn enchiladas — is the backbone, but you will also find creative contemporary kitchens and good breakfast spots. The standard question when ordering is "red or green?" (meaning chile sauce), to which the correct answer is "Christmas" if you want both.
