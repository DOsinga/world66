---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Plattsburgh International Airport** (PBG), a former Air Force base converted to civilian use in 2007, has direct flights from Florida and a handful of east-coast cities. It is the cheapest airport for travellers from Montreal looking to fly south — the "Montreal South" branding is deliberate.

**Amtrak's Adirondack** train runs once daily from New York City to Montreal via Plattsburgh — a slow but spectacular 10-hour journey up the Hudson Valley and along Lake Champlain. The station is in central Plattsburgh.

By car, Plattsburgh sits on **Interstate 87** (the "Northway"), 100 km south of Montreal and 480 km north of New York City. The **Burlington–Plattsburgh ferry** runs across Lake Champlain to Vermont year-round; the crossing takes around an hour and is one of the more pleasant ways to enter the city.
