Ticonderoga stands at one of the great old pinch points of North American travel: the short portage between Lake George and Lake Champlain. Armies, traders, and travellers all cared about this gap, which is why a small Adirondack town carries a name far larger than its size.

The obvious reason to come is **Fort Ticonderoga**, one of the continent's best-known 18th-century military sites. The fort, the lake narrows, Mount Defiance, and Mount Independence across the water make sense as one landscape; from the heights you can see why control of this place mattered.

The town itself is modest, but not empty. Downtown has the odd and excellent **Star Trek Original Series Set Tour**, while Bicentennial Park and the Heritage Museum keep the industrial side of Ticonderoga in view. It is worth a day if you care about early American history, military geography, or lovingly precise television fandom.
