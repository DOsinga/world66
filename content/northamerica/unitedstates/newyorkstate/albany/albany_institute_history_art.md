The Albany Institute of History and Art is one of the oldest museums in the United States, with
collections rooted in Albany and the upper Hudson Valley. Its galleries move between regional
painting, decorative arts, political history, and objects that explain the capital city's Dutch,
industrial, and civic past.

It is one of Albany's strongest indoor sights and a good companion to the State Capitol. The museum
gives the city more depth than a quick government-district visit, especially for travelers
interested in how upstate New York became wealthy and politically important.
