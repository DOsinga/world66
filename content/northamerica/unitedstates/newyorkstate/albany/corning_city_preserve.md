Corning City Preserve gives Albany its most direct connection to the Hudson River. Paths, lawns, boat access and event spaces line the water just below downtown, with views across the river and north toward the Livingston Avenue Bridge.

It is a good place to walk or cycle when the weather is kind, and it helps correct the impression that Albany is only government buildings and hill streets. Summer concerts and festivals make the preserve feel more central than its position beyond the highway might suggest.
