The Corning Tower Observation Deck gives Albany its best high-level view. From the 42nd floor you can look over the Empire State Plaza, the Hudson Valley, and on clear days toward the Adirondacks, Catskills, and Berkshires.

Access is through the concourse security desk below the tower, and photo ID is required. It is a practical add-on to the New York State Museum, Empire State Plaza, and the Capitol, all within the same civic district.
