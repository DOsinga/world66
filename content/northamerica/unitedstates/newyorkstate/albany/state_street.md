State Street runs down toward the Albany waterfront with a mix of civic scale, old houses, and rougher edges. The lower blocks have lost some of their polish, but the street still gives a clear sense of Albany as an old capital city rather than just a government office town.

Walk it for the architecture and the changing mood between downtown, Pearl Street, and the river. It works best as part of a broader wander through central Albany.
