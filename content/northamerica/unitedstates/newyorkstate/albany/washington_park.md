Washington Park is Albany's classic urban park, laid out with curving paths, lawns, water, and formal plantings in the 19th-century landscape tradition. It gives the capital a softer centre, a short walk from the denser streets and monumental government buildings.

The park is good in any season, but spring tulips and autumn colour are the obvious highlights. It also helps orient Albany as a lived-in city rather than just a state-government machine, with residential streets and historic architecture wrapping around the green space.
