Ashford is especially scenic in the autumn as the fall foliage colours the hillsides. The main attraction is [Griffis Sculpture Park](#griffis_sculpture), one of the largest outdoor sculpture parks in the United States — 250-plus large-scale works spread across 425 acres of fields and woodland, most of them designed to be climbed on or walked through. It is an extraordinary thing to encounter in rural western New York, and a genuine reason to plan a stop rather than drive through.

[Cattaraugus Creek](#cattaraugus_creek) runs through the valleys below town, offering kayaking in spring and steelhead trout fishing in autumn. Snowmobile trails connect the area to Erie County to the north in winter. The surrounding hardwood forests are good for hiking in summer, and the old farm roads around Ashford Hollow make for quiet cycling when the weather allows.
