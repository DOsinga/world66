The Cayuga Museum combines regional history and art with one very specific technological story: the Case Research Lab, where Theodore Case worked on early sound-on-film systems. That connection gives the museum more weight than a typical county-history collection.

For travellers in Auburn, it complements the Seward and Harriet Tubman sites by showing another side of the city's past. The house, changing exhibits, and laboratory together make a compact but varied stop, especially if early cinema or local invention interests you.
