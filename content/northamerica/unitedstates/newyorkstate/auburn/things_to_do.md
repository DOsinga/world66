---
title: "Things to Do in Auburn"
type: section
---

Auburn's most significant attraction is the Harriet Tubman Home, a National Historical Park preserving the place where Tubman lived and worked in freedom. The city also has the Seward House Museum, one of the most intact 19th-century political homes in the country, and the Willard Memorial Chapel — the only fully intact Tiffany-designed interior anywhere in the world.

The combination of abolitionist history, Tiffany glass, and Finger Lakes scenery makes Auburn an unusually rich stop for a small city.
