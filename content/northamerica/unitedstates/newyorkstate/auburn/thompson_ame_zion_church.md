Thompson AME Zion Church is a late-19th-century African Methodist Episcopal Zion church closely tied to Harriet Tubman. Tubman attended services here, and she later entrusted the church with managing the Home for the Aged after her death.

The church is a meaningful stop in Auburn's Tubman and Underground Railroad history, especially when paired with the Harriet Tubman Home and Fort Hill Cemetery.
