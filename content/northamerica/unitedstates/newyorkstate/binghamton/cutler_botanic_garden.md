Cutler Botanic Garden is a small teaching garden maintained by Cornell Cooperative Extension. Its 3.5 acres are laid out for practical plant learning rather than spectacle, with demonstration beds, seasonal flowers, and plantings suited to the local climate.

It is best for gardeners or anyone who wants a quiet green stop in Binghamton. Do not expect a grand botanical garden; the pleasure is in the detail.
