Kopernik Observatory and Science Center is a public observatory and science centre outside Binghamton, with astronomy programs, telescopes, and hands-on science activities. Clear-night observing sessions are the main draw, especially in warmer months.

Check the event schedule before driving out, since the best visits depend on public programs and weather.
