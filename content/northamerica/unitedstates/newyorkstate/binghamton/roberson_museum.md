Roberson Museum and Science Center is Binghamton's most useful all-purpose museum, combining regional history, art exhibitions, science displays, and a planetarium in and around the Roberson mansion. It is the right first stop if you want the city to make sense before walking the historic districts.

The permanent material on local industry and flight is especially relevant in a city shaped by manufacturing, early computing, and the broader Southern Tier economy.
