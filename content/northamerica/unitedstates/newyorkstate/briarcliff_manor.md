Briarcliff Manor is a leafy Westchester village about 50 km north of [New York City](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/newyork). It is close enough to Manhattan to be commuter country, but the mood is very different: wooded roads, old estates, church towers, village parks, and glimpses west towards the Hudson.

The village grew around Walter W. Law's late-19th-century estate development, and that history still shows. Churches by named architects, Tudor Revival civic buildings, and private mansions give Briarcliff more architectural interest than you might expect from a small suburban place. It is not a museum town, though. Most of the rhythm is local: school events, parks, trails, and errands along Pleasantville Road.

For travellers, Briarcliff Manor works as a quiet Hudson Valley stop, especially if you are already exploring Sleepy Hollow, Ossining, or the lower river towns. Bring walking shoes for the trails, and check for ticks after any brushy park walk.
