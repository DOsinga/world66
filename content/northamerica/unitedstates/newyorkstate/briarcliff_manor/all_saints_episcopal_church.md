All Saints' Episcopal Church is one of Briarcliff Manor's strongest pieces of architecture. Richard Upjohn designed the English Gothic church in 1854, and later additions include a Stick Style rectory and an Arts and Crafts parish hall. It is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, but it remains a working church, so be respectful if you visit.
