The Old Croton Aqueduct trail is one of the best reasons to walk in Briarcliff Manor. It follows the route of the 19th-century aqueduct that carried fresh water towards New York City, with old stone structures and wooded stretches along the way. The surface is unpaved, and in winter it can be used for cross-country skiing when there is enough snow.
