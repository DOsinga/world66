The 500 Block of Main, also called the Main-Genesee Local Historic District, preserves a concentrated slice of downtown Buffalo's commercial architecture. The block is useful for a short architecture walk, especially when combined with nearby landmarks such as Shea's Performing Arts Center and the Guaranty Building.
