Alleyway Theatre is a small downtown Buffalo stage with a steady programme of plays, musicals, and new writing. The room is intimate, which suits the theatre's mix of local productions, seasonal shows, and one-act festivals.

It is a good pick when you want an evening that feels more local than a touring arena show. Check the current schedule before going, as the best reason to visit is the production on that week rather than the building itself.
