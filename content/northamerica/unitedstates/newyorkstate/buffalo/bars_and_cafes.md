---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Buffalo has two main bar zones: the **Chippewa Street** entertainment strip near downtown, which gets loud and crowded with nightclub crowds on weekends, and the more interesting neighbourhood bars along **Elmwood Avenue** and **Allen Street** to the north. The Elmwood stretch has better variety — craft beer bars, music venues, and coffee shops with genuine character.

Buffalo has a solid craft brewing scene; Flying Bison and Community Beer Works are the most established local names, with several taprooms around the city.
