Bethel AME Church is an important African American religious landmark in Buffalo, rooted in the history of the city's Black community and the wider AME tradition. It adds a necessary layer to Buffalo's story beyond grain elevators, mansions, and downtown architecture.

The church is an active congregation, so visitors should be respectful and check access before going inside. Even from outside, it is worth noting as part of a broader East Side history walk or a deeper look at Buffalo's civil-rights and community institutions.
