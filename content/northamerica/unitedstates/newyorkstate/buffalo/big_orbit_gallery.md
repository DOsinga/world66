Big Orbit Gallery is a West Side arts space known for experimental exhibitions, installations and work by local artists. Its warehouse setting gives the shows room to breathe, and the programme has tended to be more adventurous than a standard commercial gallery.

It is a selective stop, best for travellers already interested in Buffalo's contemporary art scene or wandering Elmwood Village and the West Side. Check the current exhibition schedule before making a special trip.
