Birds Excited Into Flight is a 1981 steel sculpture by Buffalo artist Larry Griffis, set just outside Delaware Park. Seven human figures reach upward as their hands turn into a burst of birds, making it a worthwhile quick stop while walking the Elmwood and museum district.
