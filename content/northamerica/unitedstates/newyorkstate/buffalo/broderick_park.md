Broderick Park sits on the Niagara River at the foot of Ferry Street, where boats once crossed between Buffalo and Fort Erie. For people escaping slavery, that short stretch of water could mean reaching Canada and a different legal world.

The park has memorials and plaques explaining its Underground Railroad history, along with river views toward the Peace Bridge. It is a quiet stop, but an important one if you are following Buffalo's abolitionist history.
