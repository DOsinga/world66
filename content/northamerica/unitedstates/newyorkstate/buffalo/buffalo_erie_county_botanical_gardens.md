Buffalo and Erie County Botanical Gardens is one of the city's loveliest pieces of inherited grandeur: a late-19th-century glasshouse set in South Park, designed in the era when civic parks and plant collections were treated as public treasures. The domes and conservatory rooms make it a good stop in any season, but especially in winter.

Inside, the mood shifts from Buffalo weather to palms, orchids, desert plants, and seasonal displays. It is a gentle attraction, best combined with nearby South Park or as a warm, green counterweight to Buffalo's heavier architecture and industrial history.
