Buffalo and Erie County Naval and Military Park anchors the waterfront beside Canalside with historic warships open for self-guided tours in season. The collection includes the cruiser USS Little Rock, the destroyer USS The Sullivans, and the submarine USS Croaker, with a small museum of military memorabilia on shore.
