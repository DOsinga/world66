Buffalo Museum of Science fills a handsome 1929 building in Martin Luther King, Jr. Park with natural and physical science exhibits. Its collections cover anthropology, botany, zoology, paleontology, and more, with hands-on studios and family-friendly displays that make it one of Buffalo's stronger indoor attractions.
