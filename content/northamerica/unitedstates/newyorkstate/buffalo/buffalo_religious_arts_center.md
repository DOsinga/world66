Buffalo Religious Arts Center preserves stained glass, statues, vestments, and other church art from closed or changed religious buildings around the city. The collection gives visitors a close look at the craftsmanship behind Buffalo's old parish churches.

It is a focused museum, best for people interested in sacred art, local history, or architecture. Check access before going, as small museums and heritage centers may not keep daily hours.
