Burchfield Penney Art Center focuses on the art and culture of Buffalo and Western New York. Its core collection includes the world's largest public holdings of work by watercolorist Charles E. Burchfield, along with exhibitions by regional artists.

The museum sits across from the Buffalo AKG Art Museum, so the two can be paired into a strong art-focused visit on Elmwood Avenue.
