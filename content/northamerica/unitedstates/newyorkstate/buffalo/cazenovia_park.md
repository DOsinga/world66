Cazenovia Park spreads around Cazenovia Creek in South Buffalo, with wooded paths, open lawns, and one of the city's public golf courses. It is part of Buffalo's wider park tradition, but it feels more neighbourhood than showpiece.

The park is best for a relaxed walk, a picnic, or a break if you are exploring South Buffalo. The creek and mature trees give it more texture than a plain recreation ground, especially away from the busiest sports areas.
