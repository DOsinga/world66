CEPA Gallery is a downtown Buffalo exhibition space focused on photography and photo-related contemporary art. It sits in the historic Market Arcade, which makes it easy to combine with the Theater District and nearby architecture.

The programme changes regularly, with a strong emphasis on local photographers and artists from underrepresented communities. It is a compact stop, but a worthwhile one if you like smaller galleries with a clear point of view.
