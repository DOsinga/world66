The Colored Musicians' Club Museum tells the story of Buffalo's historic Black musicians' union and the jazz club that grew around it. The upstairs club became a major stop for local players and touring musicians, with names such as Count Basie, Duke Ellington, Ella Fitzgerald, Dizzy Gillespie, and Billie Holiday tied to its history.

The museum is the reason to come by day, with exhibits on the club, segregation, and Buffalo's jazz scene. If you can, pair it with a live music night upstairs, when the building feels less like a preserved landmark and more like the living institution it still is.
