Delaware Park is the central piece of Buffalo's Olmsted park system, with broad lawns, wooded paths, Hoyt Lake, and long views that show Frederick Law Olmsted's landscape ideas at city scale. It is also the setting for the Buffalo Zoo.

Come for a walk, a picnic, or a break between the nearby Darwin D. Martin House and Buffalo AKG Art Museum. The park is large enough to feel like a destination rather than just a neighborhood green.
