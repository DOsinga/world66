Eleven Twenty Projects occupies a former REO Motor Car Company showroom from Buffalo's old Auto Row. The 1926 Art Deco building gives the gallery a big, spare interior, more like an industrial hall than a white-box art room.

Exhibitions lean contemporary and local, but the building is part of the draw. Even if the current show is not your thing, it adds a useful stop on Main Street between Buffalo's theatre district, medical campus, and Allentown.
