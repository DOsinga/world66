Explore & More is Buffalo's children's museum at Canalside, with four floors of hands-on exhibits aimed at younger visitors. The move from East Aurora to the waterfront put it in the middle of the city's most useful family cluster, close to the canal walks, naval park, and summer events.

This is not a must for adults travelling without children, but for families it is one of the easiest ways to turn the downtown waterfront into a half-day stop rather than a quick look around.
