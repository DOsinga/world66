First Presbyterian Church is one of Buffalo's notable historic churches, with heavy stonework and a strong Richardsonian Romanesque presence. In a city famous for civic and domestic architecture, it adds another layer to the story of late-19th-century wealth, faith, and urban design.

It is best seen as part of an architecture walk rather than as a standalone destination. Check access before planning to go inside, and be respectful of services and parish activity.
