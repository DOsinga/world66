Forest Lawn Cemetery is one of Buffalo's most rewarding historic landscapes. Founded in 1849 as a rural-style cemetery, it combines rolling grounds, funerary sculpture, mausoleums, and the graves of many people tied to the city's civic and industrial history.

It works well as a slow walk near Delaware Park and the Elmwood area. Give yourself time to wander rather than treating it as a quick monument stop.
