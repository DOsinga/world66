The Genesee Gateway Local Historic District is a compact row of late-19th-century brick commercial buildings near the east end of downtown Buffalo. The restored storefronts give a clear look at the scale and style of the city's older business district.

It is a quick architectural stop rather than a long attraction, best folded into a downtown walk between the theatre district, Michigan Street, and City Hall.
