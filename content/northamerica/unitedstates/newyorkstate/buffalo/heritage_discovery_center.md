Buffalo's Heritage Discovery Center occupies part of a former industrial site in South Buffalo. It brings together community history groups and small museums focused on the city's industrial, railway, and working-class past.

It is best for visitors with a specific interest in Buffalo's local history rather than a first stop in the city.
