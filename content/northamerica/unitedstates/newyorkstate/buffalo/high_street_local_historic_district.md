High Street Local Historic District is a small Fruit Belt architecture stop, covering a few surviving 19th-century buildings on High Street between Maple and Mulberry Streets. The former High Street Baptist Church, the old Henry Schirmer meat market building, and the Meidenbauer-Morgan House show what this part of Buffalo looked like before the surrounding medical corridor began to change the neighbourhood. It is best for visitors already following Buffalo's architecture trail.
