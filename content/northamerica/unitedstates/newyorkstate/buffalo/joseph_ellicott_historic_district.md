Joseph Ellicott Local Historic District covers the blocks around Niagara Square and part of downtown Buffalo's original street plan. It is best explored as an architecture walk, with buildings ranging from early Greek Revival and Gothic Revival landmarks to Art Deco civic offices and modern courthouses.
