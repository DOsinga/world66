Karpeles Manuscript Library's North Hall branch occupies a former First Church of Christ, Scientist building in Allentown. Rotating exhibits draw from a large private collection of historic documents and manuscripts, so the displays change over time.

It is most worthwhile for visitors already exploring Allentown's architecture and museum corridor, especially if the current exhibit matches an interest in history, maps, literature, or rare documents.
