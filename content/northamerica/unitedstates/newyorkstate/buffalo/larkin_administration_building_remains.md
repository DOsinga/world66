The surviving wall of Frank Lloyd Wright's Larkin Administration Building is a small but meaningful stop for architecture-minded visitors. The original 1906 building was one of Buffalo's great industrial-era landmarks and an early model for modern office design.

Only a 20-foot brick and sandstone section remains, so it is not a long visit. It works best as part of a broader Buffalo architecture route.
