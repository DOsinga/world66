The Lower Lakes Marine Historical Society Museum tells the story of Buffalo's Great Lakes shipping trade. It is a small, specialist museum, but the subject matters here: lake freighters, grain elevators, canals, and harbour work all helped shape the city's economy.

Expect photographs, models, documents, and local maritime artifacts rather than a polished blockbuster museum. It is best for travellers who already have an interest in Buffalo's waterfront or Great Lakes history.
