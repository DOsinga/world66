Market Square Historic District covers a small stretch of Amherst Street in Lower Black Rock, where the old village centre still shows through the later city around it. The grassy esplanade was once a public market, and the surrounding blocks hold some of Buffalo's older surviving houses and churches.

Look for the Federal-style Jacob Schmidt House and Tavern, the Stephen W. Howell House and Store, St. John's United Evangelical Church and the large St. Francis Xavier Roman Catholic Church. It is a good short walk if you are tracing Buffalo's neighbourhood architecture beyond downtown.
