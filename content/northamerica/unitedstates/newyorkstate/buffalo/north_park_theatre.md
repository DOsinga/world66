North Park Theatre is a restored 1920 movie palace in North Buffalo, known for its ornate Baroque interior and old-fashioned cinema atmosphere. It remains an active theatre rather than a static monument.

Even visitors who are not planning a film may want to notice the building while exploring Hertel Avenue. For film lovers, catching a screening is the best way to see the interior properly.
