Our Lady of Seneca Street Shrine is a small roadside Catholic shrine in Larkinville, built around 1950 by local barber Joseph Battaglia after he reported a vision of the Virgin Mary. It is a quick, unusual stop for visitors already exploring the area's restored warehouses and industrial streets.
