Our Lady of Victory Basilica in Lackawanna is one of the grandest churches in the Buffalo area. Completed in 1926, it was the life work of Father Nelson Baker, whose orphanages and charitable institutions helped give Lackawanna its "City of Charity" nickname.

The basilica is built in an ornate Baroque Revival style, with white Georgia marble, a large copper-topped dome, and a richly decorated interior. It pairs well with the nearby botanical gardens and is worth a deliberate stop for architecture.
