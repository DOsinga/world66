Prospect Hill Historic District is a small West Side heritage area near the Peace Bridge, with surviving 19th- and early-20th-century houses from one of Buffalo's older residential districts. It is not the city's grandest architecture walk, but it adds useful texture if you are exploring the waterfront, Front Park, or Buffalo's Italian-American West Side history.
