Seneca Bluffs Natural Habitat Park protects a stretch of floodplain along the Buffalo River, with meadows, wetlands, and woods replacing former truck-farm land. It is a modest place, but a good one for a quiet walk, birdlife, and a look at the river away from downtown traffic.

Trails cross the greenery, and anglers come for walleye in this part of the river. Pair it with Tifft Nature Preserve or the Outer Harbor if you are exploring Buffalo's reclaimed waterfront landscapes.
