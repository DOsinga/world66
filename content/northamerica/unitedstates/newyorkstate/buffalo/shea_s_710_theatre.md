Shea's 710 Theatre gives Buffalo a more intimate performance space alongside the grander Shea's Performing Arts Center. The venue hosts plays, touring productions, and local arts programming in a downtown setting.

For travellers, it is worth checking the calendar rather than visiting as architecture alone. If a show fits your dates, the theatre can make a good evening anchor before or after dinner downtown.
