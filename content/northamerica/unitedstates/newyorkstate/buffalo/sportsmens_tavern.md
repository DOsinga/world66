Sportsmens Tavern is a low-key Buffalo music room known for blues, Americana, country, and roots acts rather than arena-scale shows. It is the kind of place where the city feels local and lived-in, especially if you want an evening away from downtown's bigger venues.

Check the calendar before going, because the draw depends entirely on the night's booking. When the lineup is right, it is one of Buffalo's better small-stage experiences.
