Temple Beth Zion is one of Buffalo's most striking modern religious buildings, a bold synagogue on Delaware Avenue whose sanctuary rises like a great tent. Its glass, concrete, and angular form make a sharp contrast with the city's better-known Richardsonian, Art Deco, and early-20th-century landmarks.

The building rewards architecture-minded travellers who are already exploring Delaware Avenue, the museum district, or nearby Elmwood. Access depends on services and events, but even from outside it adds an important modern chapter to Buffalo's unusually rich architectural story.
