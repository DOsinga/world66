Times Beach Nature Preserve is a reclaimed waterfront habitat on Buffalo's outer harbour, where trails and viewing areas look over marsh, scrub and Lake Erie birdlife. Its location near grain elevators and port infrastructure makes the nature feel distinctly urban.

It is a good stop for birders or anyone exploring the waterfront beyond Canalside. Expect a quiet, windswept place rather than a manicured park, and bring binoculars if migration is underway.
