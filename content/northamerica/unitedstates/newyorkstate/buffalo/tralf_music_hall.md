Tralf Music Hall is a long-running downtown Buffalo venue that grew from a jazz club into a broader room for roots, rock, soul, R&B, world music, and strong local bills. It sits in the Theatre District, so it is easy to combine with dinner or a drink before a show.

Check the calendar rather than dropping by cold. On the right night, it is one of the better small-stage music experiences in the city centre.
