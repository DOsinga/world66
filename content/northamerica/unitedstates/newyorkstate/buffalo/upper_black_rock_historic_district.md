Upper Black Rock preserves an unusually intact industrial streetscape along Niagara Street, where late-19th- and early-20th-century factories, warehouses, houses, and storefronts show how this part of Buffalo grew around rail and canal traffic. Look for the former Sterling Engine Company buildings and the old Union Meeting House on Breckenridge Street.
