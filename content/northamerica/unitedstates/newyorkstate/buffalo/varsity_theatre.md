The Varsity Theatre is a restored 1920s neighbourhood movie palace on Buffalo's East Side, revived as a live music and performance venue. Its comeback gives the building more interest than a routine entertainment listing.

It is worth checking the schedule if you want to see a show outside Buffalo's better-known downtown theatre district. Even from the street, the restoration tells a useful story about the neighbourhood's older commercial corridors.
