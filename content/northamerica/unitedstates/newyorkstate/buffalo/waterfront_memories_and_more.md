Waterfront Memories & More is a small neighborhood museum devoted to Buffalo's riverfront, harbor, and old industrial belt. The appeal is local and archival: photographs, family histories, church and school records, and other material that fills in the human side of the Erie Canal and grain-elevator waterfront.
