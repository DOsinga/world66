Western New York Book Arts Center is a hands-on arts space devoted to printing, bookbinding, papermaking, screen printing, and letterpress. The permanent displays explain the craft, while changing exhibitions bring in local writers, illustrators, and printmakers.

It is a good downtown stop if you like working studios more than formal galleries. Workshops and open studio days make it especially interesting when your timing lines up.
