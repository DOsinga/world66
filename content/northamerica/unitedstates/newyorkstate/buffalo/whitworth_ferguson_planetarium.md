Whitworth Ferguson Planetarium is Buffalo State's astronomy theatre, with public shows that make it a useful indoor stop near the museum district. The original mid-century planetarium was replaced by a newer facility, but the point is the same: a compact, accessible way to add some science to an Elmwood Village or Delaware Park day.
