Canandaigua sits at the northern end of Canandaigua Lake, one of the broad, clear Finger Lakes south of Rochester. It is more workaday than storybook [Skaneateles](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/skaneateles), but that is part of its appeal: a real small city with a long main street, boat traffic at the pier, and lake hills rising just beyond town.

The best reason to stop is the mix of water and old Finger Lakes prosperity. [Canandaigua City Pier](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/canandaigua/canandaigua_city_pier) still feels tied to the lake's steamboat past, [Sonnenberg Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/canandaigua/sonnenberg_gardens) gives the city a grand Victorian estate, and [Granger Homestead](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/canandaigua/granger_homestead) keeps a quieter Federal-era story alive.

Canandaigua works well as a relaxed Finger Lakes base. You can spend a morning in the gardens, walk the pier at sunset, catch a summer concert at [CMAC](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/canandaigua/cmac), and still be within easy reach of wineries, Bristol Mountain, and the smaller lake towns to the east.
