title: Things to Do

---

Catskill punches above its weight for a village of its size. The main draw is the [Thomas Cole National Historic Site](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/catskill/thomas_cole_national_historic_site), where the founder of the Hudson River School lived and painted — it is one of the most significant art-history sites in the country. Beyond Cole, the village has a compact main street with working galleries, antique shops, and a handful of good restaurants. The [Catskill Creek](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/catskill/catskill_creek) runs through town and meets the Hudson at the southern edge of the village, offering easy waterside walks. The [Rip Van Winkle Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/catskill/rip_van_winkle_bridge) carries Route 23 across the Hudson and gives excellent views up and down the river. For a full day excursion, [Olana](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/catskill/olana_state_historic_site) — the hilltop estate of Hudson River School painter Frederic Church — is just across the river in Hudson.
