Cazenovia is a handsome village east of Syracuse, set by a long lake and surrounded by rolling farm country. It is small, but it has more depth than a quick glance suggests: early American houses, a preserved estate, an outdoor art park, and one of central New York's better waterfall walks close by.

The village itself is pleasant rather than dramatic. The reason to spend a spare day here is the triangle around it: [Lorenzo State Historic Site](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cazenovia/lorenzo_state_historic_site) for Federal-era architecture and estate life, [Stone Quarry Hill Art Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cazenovia/stone_quarry_hill_art_park) for sculpture in open fields, and [Chittenango Falls State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cazenovia/chittenango_falls_state_park) for a short gorge walk to a 67-foot cascade.

Cazenovia works best as a slow day from [Syracuse](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/syracuse) or as a pretty stop along US Route 20. Come in good weather, when the lake and the art park can do their share of the work.
