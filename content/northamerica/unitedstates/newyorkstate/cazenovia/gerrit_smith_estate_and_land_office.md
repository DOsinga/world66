The Gerrit Smith Estate and Land Office preserves the home and working estate of the abolitionist, philanthropist, and Underground Railroad supporter. Exhibits cover freedom seekers, Smith's reform work, his family, and the land office that helped fund his causes.

It is a rural stop outside Cazenovia, best paired with other historic sites or outdoor stops in the area.
