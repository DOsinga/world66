Stone Quarry Hill Art Park is the best reason to leave the village centre. Sculptures and installations sit across 104 acres of fields, woodland, and hilltop paths, with long views over the countryside east of Cazenovia.

It is open-air and informal, so it feels more like a walk with surprises than a museum visit. Wear shoes for grass and uneven ground, and give it enough time to wander beyond the first cluster of works.
