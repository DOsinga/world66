The Hall of Philosophy is an open-sided Greek Revival colonnade at the centre of the Institution grounds, where the morning interfaith lectures and religious debates take place each week of the summer season. The format is serious: speakers engage with theological and philosophical questions before an audience of a thousand or more who then participate in structured discussion. The range is genuinely ecumenical — Protestant, Catholic, Jewish, Muslim, Buddhist, and secular humanist traditions are all represented across the season.

The hall was designed from the start as a space for public thinking rather than worship, which gives it a different character from a church or a lecture hall. It is open to all gate ticket holders and no additional admission is charged. The surrounding gardens are pleasant for walking in the mornings before the lectures begin.
