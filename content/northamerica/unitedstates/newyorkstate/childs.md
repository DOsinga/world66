Childs is a small hamlet on the old Ridge Road in Orleans County, west of [Rochester](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/rochester). It is not a place of big-city distractions. The reason to stop is the [Cobblestone Museum Complex](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/childs/cobblestone_museum_complex), a tight group of 19th-century buildings that show off the cobblestone masonry once common along this stretch of western New York.

The hamlet is compact enough to take in on foot. The old church, schoolhouse, Ward House, and trade buildings sit within a few minutes of each other, with the feel of a preserved roadside settlement rather than a formal outdoor museum. If you are crossing the state on secondary roads, Childs makes a pleasant, specific break from highway travel.
