The Cobblestone Museum Complex is the point of coming to Childs. Its core is the Cobblestone Historic District, including the 1834 First Universalist Church, the Ward House, and a one-room schoolhouse, all built or faced with the rounded lake stones that gave this part of western New York a distinctive architectural style.

The museum also keeps a blacksmith shop, print shop, harness shop, farmer's hall, and research centre. A standard guided visit gives you a close look at how a small Ridge Road community worked in the 1800s, and the buildings are close enough together that the whole place still feels like a hamlet rather than a collection.
