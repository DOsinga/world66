Clayton sits on the St Lawrence River in New York's Thousand Islands, small enough to feel like a village but serious about boats. Its waterfront has the easy rhythm of marinas, tour docks, summer cottages, and freighters moving along the Seaway.

The [Antique Boat Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/clayton/antique_boat_museum) is the main reason to stop. It gives the river's wooden launches, skiffs, houseboats, and resort-era wealth a proper stage, and it is much more interesting than the words "boat museum" may suggest.

Clayton works best as a slow spare day on the river. Add the [Thousand Islands Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/clayton/thousand_islands_museum), the [Thousand Islands Arts Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/clayton/thousand_islands_arts_center), and a beach or picnic at [Cedar Point State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/clayton/cedar_point_state_park), and you get a compact version of the American side of the Thousand Islands without the heavier resort bustle of Alexandria Bay.
