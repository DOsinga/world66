title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The [National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cooperstown/baseball_hall_of_fame) is the reason most people come, and it justifies the trip even if you're only a casual fan — it's a well-conceived museum about American cultural history as much as a sport. [Doubleday Field](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cooperstown/doubleday_field), a block away, is worth a look if a game is on.

The cultural attractions north of the village are underrated. The [Fenimore Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cooperstown/fenimore_art_museum) has a first-rate collection of American Indian art, and the adjacent [Farmers' Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cooperstown/farmers_museum) — including the [Cardiff Giant](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cooperstown/cardiff_giant) — is one of the best living history complexes in the Northeast.

[Otsego Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cooperstown/otsego_lake) is beautiful and swimmable in summer. [Hyde Hall](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cooperstown/hyde_hall), at the northern end of the lake, is a remarkable Federal-period country house worth the short drive. The [Fly Creek Cider Mill](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/cooperstown/fly_creek_cider_mill) is a good destination in fall foliage season.
