Van Cortlandt Manor is the village's main historic anchor, a stone and brick house tied to Dutch colonial settlement, Revolutionary-era politics, and the long history of labour on Hudson Valley estates.

The site has been undergoing restoration, so check access before building a day around an interior visit. Even when programming is limited, it helps explain why this quiet river junction mattered.
