Ellenville has long been known in the Northeast hang-gliding community, and the flight park keeps that identity visible. The launch sits on the Shawangunk ridge above a broad landing field, giving pilots the lift and open space that made the area famous.

Travellers can watch from the ground when conditions are right, or book instruction and tandem-style experiences through local operators when available. This is weather-dependent by nature, so do not treat it like a fixed-hour museum visit.
