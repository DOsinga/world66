The Hunt Memorial Building is the most distinctive piece of architecture in Ellenville's centre, a late 19th-century civic landmark in heavy stone. It will not detain you long, but it gives the village a sharper historic profile than the highway approach suggests.

Pair it with a short walk around Canal Street and Shadowland Stages rather than making a special trip just for the building.
