Shadowland Stages gives Ellenville a proper evening option after a day on the ridge. The company produces professional theatre in a restored downtown venue, with a season that usually mixes contemporary plays, musicals, and smaller performances.

It is worth checking the schedule before you come. A performance here can make Ellenville feel like a destination rather than just the village below Sam's Point.
