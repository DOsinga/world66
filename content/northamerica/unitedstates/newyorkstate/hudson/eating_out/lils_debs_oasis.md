Lil' Deb's Oasis is probably Hudson's most talked-about restaurant — a Caribbean-inspired, queer-owned spot on Columbia Street that blends tropical flavours, communal warmth, and a pleasingly eccentric aesthetic. The menu changes frequently and draws on plantains, coconut, smoked fish, and vivid salsas; the room is small and loud and fills quickly on weekends. Widely reviewed in national food press and beloved locally. Book ahead, especially Friday and Saturday nights.

Address: 747 Columbia St, Hudson, NY 12534.
