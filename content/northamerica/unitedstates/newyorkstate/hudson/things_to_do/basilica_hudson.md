A former 19th-century iron and steel foundry complex near the train station, Basilica Hudson has been repurposed as one of the Hudson Valley's main contemporary arts and culture venues. The vast industrial interiors — exposed brick, iron columns, sawtooth skylights — host music festivals, theatre, art installations, film screenings, and community events. The annual Basilica Farm & Flea market runs here in summer, and the site's scale allows for events that would be impossible in Hudson's more intimate downtown venues.

Performances and events run mainly from spring through fall; check the programme before visiting as the space is event-driven rather than a permanent exhibition venue.
