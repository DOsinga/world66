---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ithaca punches above its weight for food. The Ithaca Commons downtown has the densest cluster of restaurants and cafes, while Collegetown near Cornell is more casual and student-oriented. Local and organic food is taken seriously here — several restaurants source directly from Finger Lakes farms. Vegetarian and vegan options are everywhere, partly thanks to the long shadow of Moosewood.
