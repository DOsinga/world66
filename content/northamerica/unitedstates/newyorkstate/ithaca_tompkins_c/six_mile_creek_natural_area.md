Six Mile Creek Natural Area is one of Ithaca's easiest ways to step straight from town into gorge country. Trails follow wooded slopes, creek edges, small waterfalls, and reservoir viewpoints, giving a compact version of the landscape that makes the Finger Lakes region so good for walking.

The area is less formal than the state parks, so pay attention to trail conditions and posted rules, especially near water. For travellers without time for a full excursion to Robert Treman or Buttermilk Falls, it offers a strong local hike within easy reach of downtown.
