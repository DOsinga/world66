Jamestown is a small city in the southwestern corner of New York State, on the outlet of [Chautauqua Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/jamestown_and_surr/chautauqua_lake). It is best known as the birthplace of Lucille Ball — the [National Comedy Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/jamestown_and_surr/national_comedy_center), opened in 2018, is the main draw and a genuinely engaging museum dedicated to the art and history of comedy. Just up the street, the smaller [Lucy-Desi Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/jamestown_and_surr/lucy_desi_museum) tells the personal story of Lucy and Desi Arnaz.

A short drive north, the [Chautauqua Institution](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/chautauqua) on the lake's western shore has been running summer programmes in arts, education, and religion since 1874. The grounds are peaceful and the architecture is distinctive — a gated Victorian campus on the lakeshore.

Downtown Jamestown itself has been quietly recovering: the [Downtown Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/jamestown_and_surr/jamestown_downtown_historic_district) preserves a tight cluster of late-19th-century commercial buildings, the [Reg Lenna Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/jamestown_and_surr/reg_lenna_center) hosts touring music and film, and the [Robert H. Jackson Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/jamestown_and_surr/robert_h_jackson_center) honours the local lawyer who became the chief American prosecutor at Nuremberg. The [Fenton History Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/jamestown_and_surr/fenton_history_center) and the [Roger Tory Peterson Institute](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/jamestown_and_surr/roger_tory_peterson_institute) cover regional history and natural history respectively.

The surrounding Chautauqua County has rolling farmland, wineries along the Lake Erie grape belt, and good access to Allegany State Park for hiking.
