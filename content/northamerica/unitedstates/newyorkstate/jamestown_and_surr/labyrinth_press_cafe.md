Labyrinth Press Café occupies a tall-windowed storefront on East Third Street and is downtown Jamestown's go-to for proper coffee, espresso drinks, and a small but dependable list of breakfast bowls, paninis, and salads. The space is welcoming, with local art on the walls and big communal tables that fill with the laptop crowd in the morning.

It is the natural meeting point before walking down to the National Comedy Center, and the only reliable source of a decent cappuccino in the immediate downtown. Closes early most evenings; not open Sundays in the off season.
