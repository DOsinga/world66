Lucille Ball Memorial Park sits on Chautauqua Lake in Celoron, just outside Jamestown, and celebrates the comedian whose hometown identity still shapes local tourism. The park is simple: lake views, lawns, and Lucy-themed sculpture rather than a formal museum experience.

It pairs naturally with Jamestown’s National Comedy Center and Lucy Desi Museum. Stop for the setting and the pop-culture connection, especially if you want a quick lakeside pause between the area’s more structured attractions.
