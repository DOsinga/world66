The Lucy-Desi Museum on West Third Street is the smaller, older sister to the National Comedy Center a few blocks west. It tells the story of Lucille Ball and Desi Arnaz: their meeting on the RKO lot in 1940, their twenty-year working partnership, and the way *I Love Lucy* invented the situation comedy and the syndication business that still funds Hollywood today.

Highlights include the Pontiac convertible from the *Long, Long Trailer* film, Lucy and Desi's awards, recreated sets from the show, and personal artefacts donated by the family. A combined ticket with the National Comedy Center makes the most sense — most visitors do them on the same day. Lucy's childhood home is a short drive away on West 8th Street; it's a private residence but visible from the street.
