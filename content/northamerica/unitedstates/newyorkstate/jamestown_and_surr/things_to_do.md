---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Jamestown's main draw is the National Comedy Center, a slick contemporary museum that turned the town's most famous daughter — Lucille Ball — into the anchor for a broader celebration of American comedy. The historic downtown around Third Street has been steadily restored, with several buildings on the National Register, and the surrounding Chautauqua County offers the lake, vineyards, and the Roger Tory Peterson Institute for birders and nature lovers.
