Fort William Henry Museum is a reconstructed 18th-century British fort at the south end of Lake George. The original fort was destroyed during the French and Indian War; the modern reconstruction follows the old plans and footprint.

Guided tours, exhibits, and reenactments explain the military history of the lake corridor. It is an easy stop from Lake George village and works well with a walk along the waterfront.
