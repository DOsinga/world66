---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Lake Placid has a good range of restaurants for a small mountain village. Main Street has a concentration of options from casual to moderately upscale.

**Generations** on Main Street has been the reliable choice for dinner for many years — a broad menu of American and Italian dishes in a comfortable setting. The Lake Placid Pub and Brewery is the local beer destination, with a good selection of house-made ales and pub food; in winter a pint after skiing is earned. **Mirror Lake Inn Resort and Spa** has a formal dining room (Taste) that represents the upmarket option in the village, with good wine and views over the lake.

For casual eating, **Simply Gourmet** on Main Street does breakfasts and sandwiches. In summer, ice cream from **Donnelly's Soft Ice Cream** on Saranac Avenue is the standard after-swim treat.
