---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Fire Island is best treated as a chain of very different stops rather than a single beach. Start at the Fire Island Lighthouse for the classic view over the barrier island and Great South Bay. Sailors Haven is the place for the Sunken Forest boardwalk, where old holly trees grow in a sheltered hollow behind the dunes. Further east, Watch Hill and the Otis Pike wilderness give the island its quiet side: beach walking, marsh birds, and a sense of distance from the city that feels improbable this close to Manhattan.
