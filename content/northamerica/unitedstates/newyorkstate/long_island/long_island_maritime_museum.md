Long Island Maritime Museum tells the island's working-water history through boats, historic buildings, and exhibits on fishing, oystering, and coastal life. The site includes the Rudolph Oyster House and the historic vessels Priscilla and Modesty, all tied to the old South Shore maritime economy.

It is a stronger stop for history-minded travellers than for anyone looking for a quick beach diversion. Give it time if you want to understand Long Island beyond the usual seaside image.
