The Science Museum of Long Island sits in a historic house at Leeds Pond Preserve in Plandome Manor. Its strength is hands-on science for children, with exhibits and programs built around nature, physics, and the local environment.

It is most useful for families already on Long Island's North Shore. Combine it with a walk around the preserve rather than treating it as a full-day museum.
