Stony Brook is a north-shore Long Island hamlet with two personalities. The university sits inland by the railway station, while the older village centre stays closer to the harbour, with a grist mill, pond, old-fashioned shops, and a surprising amount of open green space.

For travellers, the best day is compact but varied. Start around the harbour and [Stony Brook Grist Mill](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/long_island/stony_brook/stony_brook_grist_mill), walk the trails at [Avalon Nature Preserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/long_island/stony_brook/avalon_nature_preserve), then spend time with carriages and American art at the [Long Island Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/long_island/stony_brook/long_island_museum).

Stony Brook is not a beach resort and it is not trying to be one. Its charm is more particular: a well-kept village edge, marsh and sound views at [West Meadow Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/long_island/stony_brook/west_meadow_beach), and enough history to make a one-day stop feel like more than another Long Island suburb.
