Avalon Nature Preserve gives Stony Brook its best walk. The preserve links woods, meadow, pond edges, and harbour-side views in a landscape that feels carefully kept without becoming stiff.

It is free and easy to combine with the grist mill and village centre. Go for an hour if you only want a quiet loop, or linger longer if you enjoy small designed landscapes, birds, and the change from salt marsh to upland trees.
