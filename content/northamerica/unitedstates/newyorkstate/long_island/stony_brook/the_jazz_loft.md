The Jazz Loft is a compact museum and performance venue near Stony Brook Village, with memorabilia, education programs, and regular concerts. It gives the old village centre a cultural stop after the daytime sights.

Check the calendar before building a visit around it. The space is most rewarding when there is live music, but the museum side still adds a different note to a Stony Brook walk.
