Target Rock National Wildlife Refuge protects a pocket of Long Island shoreline, woodland, and salt-marsh habitat near Huntington. Short trails lead down toward the beach and the large boulder that gives the refuge its name, with good chances for birdwatching and, in season, horseshoe crabs along the water.

It is a small refuge, but a rewarding one if you want a quieter north-shore stop away from village centres and beach traffic.
