title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
tags: [bars_and_cafes]
---

New Paltz has a compact but characterful bar scene anchored by the village's Main Street. Students, climbers back from the Gunks, and weekend visitors from the city share the same barstools. Several spots double as live music venues, and the town's progressive culture means you'll find places that are genuinely welcoming to whoever shows up.
