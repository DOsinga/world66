New Paltz has a lively, unpretentious food scene driven by the college crowd, the climbing community, and day-trippers from New York City. Farm-to-table cooking is not a marketing term here — the farms are visible from the restaurant windows. Main Street and the blocks around it hold a concentrated mix of cafes, brewpubs, and ethnic kitchens.
