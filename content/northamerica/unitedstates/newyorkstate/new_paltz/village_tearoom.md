The Village Tea Room is a long-standing New Paltz institution occupying a Victorian house on Wall Street, a block off the main drag. The focus is on local, seasonal food: salads from nearby farms, hearty soups, and sandwiches made with real care. The dining room is small and cozy, with mismatched furniture and a warmth that feels genuinely domestic rather than contrived.

Brunch on weekends is particularly good and draws a loyal crowd, so arrive early or expect a wait. The afternoon tea service is a civilized option on a slow afternoon — proper loose-leaf tea, scones, and small sandwiches in a setting that makes a real effort to be charming without tipping into kitsch. This is not the place for a quick meal, but it is the right place for a leisurely one.
