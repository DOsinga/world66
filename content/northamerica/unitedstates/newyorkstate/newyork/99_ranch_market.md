99 Ranch Market opened its first New York City location here in 2025, in the space vacated by the long-running Hong Kong Supermarket. The 22,000-square-foot ground floor includes a live seafood section and curated goods from across Asia, making it one of the most significant recent additions to Flushing's food retail scene.
