The African Burial Ground Memorial marks part of a 17th- and 18th-century burial ground where thousands of Africans, both free and enslaved, were interred. Rediscovered during federal construction work, the site is now a sober stop near City Hall and an important counterpoint to Lower Manhattan's colonial history.
