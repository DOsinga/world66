A.I.R. Gallery was founded in 1972 as the first nonprofit, artist-run gallery dedicated to women artists in the United States, and it remains a rare survivor of DUMBO's original loft-gallery era. Rotating exhibitions continue to showcase women artists' work, open Wednesday through Sunday.
