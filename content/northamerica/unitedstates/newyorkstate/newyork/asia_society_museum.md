Asia Society Museum is a focused Upper East Side museum with exhibitions and collections centered on Asian art and culture. It is smaller than the major Museum Mile institutions, but its program often gives useful context on art, history, and current cultural ties across Asia and the United States.

Use it as a quieter museum stop near Central Park, especially if the current exhibition fits your interests.
