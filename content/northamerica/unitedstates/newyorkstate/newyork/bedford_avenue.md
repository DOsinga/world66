Bedford Avenue around the Williamsburg subway stop is one of north Brooklyn's liveliest walking areas. The draw is not a single monument but the street life: small shops, cafes, bars, music venues, and a steady mix of locals and visitors.

It is a good starting point for exploring Williamsburg on foot, especially if you want to combine browsing, food, and a walk toward the East River waterfront.
