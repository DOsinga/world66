Named after Barcelona's famous market, Boqueria does a solid run of Spanish small plates — patatas bravas, jamon croquetas, gambas al ajillo, tortilla espanola. The long communal table fills with a post-work crowd most evenings; the sangria is better than it needs to be for a tapas bar.
