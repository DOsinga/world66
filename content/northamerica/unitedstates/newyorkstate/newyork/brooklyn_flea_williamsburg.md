The Brooklyn Flea was founded in 2008 and is credited with establishing the format of the artisan-and-vintage weekend market that has since spread to cities around the world. The Williamsburg location (check website for current address, which has moved over the years) brings together vintage clothing dealers, antiques sellers, food vendors and jewellery makers. The sister Smorgasburg food market grew out of it.
