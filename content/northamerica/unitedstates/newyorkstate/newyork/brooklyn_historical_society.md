The Center for Brooklyn History (formerly Brooklyn Historical Society) occupies an 1881 Queen Anne-style building on Pierrepont Street that is itself a landmark. The library and archive hold the most complete collection of Brooklyn materials in existence; rotating exhibitions interpret Brooklyn history from the Lenape period to the present. The reading room, with its carved woodwork and stained glass, is worth seeing regardless of the current programme.
