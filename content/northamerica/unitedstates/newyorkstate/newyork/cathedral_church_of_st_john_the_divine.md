The Cathedral Church of St. John the Divine is one of New York's most imposing religious buildings, a vast neo-Gothic cathedral that has remained famously unfinished for more than a century. Its scale is the main shock: the nave, chapels, stonework, and changing light make it feel closer to a European cathedral than a neighborhood church.

The grounds are worth a slow look too, especially if the resident peacocks are out. It is an easy stop on a Morningside Heights walk or before heading north into Harlem.
