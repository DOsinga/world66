Built inside the old National Biscuit Company factory (Nabisco made the Oreo here in 1912), Chelsea Market is a covered food hall and retail complex in the Meatpacking District. The vendors are good: The Lobster Place for seafood, Dickson's Farmstand for meat, Los Tacos No. 1 for the queue-inducing tacos. The High Line runs directly above. Between 9th and 10th Avenues on West 15th Street.
