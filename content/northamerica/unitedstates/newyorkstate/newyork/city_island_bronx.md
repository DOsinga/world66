City Island is a small island off the Bronx with a fishing-village feel that is unusual inside New York City. Marinas, seafood restaurants, low houses, and views over Long Island Sound make it feel separate from the rest of the borough.

It is best as a half-day detour for travellers who want a quieter waterfront corner of the city rather than another major museum or landmark.
