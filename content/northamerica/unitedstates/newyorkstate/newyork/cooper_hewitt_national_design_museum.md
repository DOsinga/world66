Cooper Hewitt is the Smithsonian's design museum, housed in Andrew Carnegie's former mansion on Museum Mile. The changing exhibitions are the draw, ranging across product design, graphics, textiles and interiors, with enough hands-on material to make design feel practical rather than precious.
