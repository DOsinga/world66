New York is surrounded by escapable distances in every direction. New Jersey is the most accessible: cross the Hudson or take NJ Transit from Penn Station and you're out of the city in under an hour.

**New Jersey shore**

[Atlantic City](/northamerica/unitedstates/newjersey/atlanticcity) is about two and a half hours south by car or NJ Transit bus from Port Authority — a strange, entertaining place to spend a day, with a free beach, the historic Boardwalk, and the surreal déjà vu of walking past Monopoly street names in real life. [Cape May](/northamerica/unitedstates/newjersey/cape_may) sits at the very tip of New Jersey, an hour further south: a Victorian seaside town with landmark gingerbread cottages, wide Atlantic beaches, and one of the best birding spots on the East Coast. Both are doable as long day trips; Cape May rewards an overnight if you can swing it.

**Within the boroughs**

You don't have to leave the city for a change of scene. Head up to the Bronx for a day combining the New York Botanical Garden and Bronx Zoo, finishing with dinner on Arthur Avenue — the real Little Italy.
