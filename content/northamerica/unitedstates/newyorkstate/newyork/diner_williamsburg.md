Diner opened in 1999 in a genuine 1927 Kullman dining car and is credited with helping establish Williamsburg as a restaurant destination. The menu changes daily based on what the kitchen finds at market, and the waiter writes it on the paper tablecloth as they recite it. The burger is legendary. The proximity to the Williamsburg Bridge gives the car an appealing industrial frame.
