Domino Park opened in 2018 on the former Domino Sugar refinery site on the Williamsburg waterfront. The park preserved the old refinery's syrup tanks, filter house and gantry cranes as sculptural elements along the esplanade. There are sports courts, a waterfront pathway and a seasonal food market. The views north to the Williamsburg Bridge and Manhattan are excellent.
