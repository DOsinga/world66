Dutch Kills is a cocktail bar in Jackson Heights that draws serious drinkers from across the city for its pre-Prohibition cocktail programme, hand-cut ice, and genuinely knowledgeable bar staff. The space, a converted garage with exposed brick and low lighting, has the atmosphere of a speakeasy. It is one of the few destinations in Queens that competes with Manhattan cocktail bars on equal terms.
