Dyker Heights Christmas Lights is a seasonal neighborhood display centered around 84th Street and 12th Avenue in Brooklyn. In December, several blocks of homes are covered with elaborate holiday decorations, from giant figures to animated displays.

It is best treated as an evening walk rather than a single address. Friday and Saturday nights are the busiest, but also when the displays feel most lively.
