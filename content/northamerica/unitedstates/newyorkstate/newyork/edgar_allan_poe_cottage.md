Edgar Allan Poe Cottage is a small wooden farmhouse in the Bronx where Poe lived from 1846 to 1849, the last three years of his life. He wrote some of his best-known late works here, including "Annabel Lee" and "The Bells."

The cottage has been restored with period furnishings and is run by the Bronx County Historical Society. Visits usually include a short film or guided interpretation, making it one of New York's better literary house museums.
