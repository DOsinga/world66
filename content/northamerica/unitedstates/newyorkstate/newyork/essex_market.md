The Essex Market moved to its current home in the Essex Crossing development in 2019, carrying on the tradition of the original covered market that opened on Essex Street in 1940. The market brings together prepared food vendors, fresh produce sellers, a butcher, a fishmonger and specialty food stalls. Several long-standing LES vendors relocated here from the original market, including Shopsin's diner.
