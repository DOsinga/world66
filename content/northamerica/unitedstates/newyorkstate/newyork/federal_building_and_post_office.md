The Federal Building and Post Office is a grand Romanesque landmark in Downtown Brooklyn, all turrets, arches, and heavy stonework. It still has a working post office, so it is not just a preserved facade; step by for the architecture while walking between Brooklyn Heights, Borough Hall, and the approach to the bridge.
