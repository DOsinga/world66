Fontainhas is an all-day cafe, wine bar, and small market built around Goan-Portuguese cooking — a cuisine rarely found in New York, let alone in DUMBO's otherwise generic coffee-shop landscape. Rose-cinnamon chai, Goan snacks, and a compact wine list draw a mostly local crowd rather than tourists passing through for the bridge photo.
