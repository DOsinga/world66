The Four Horsemen is a natural wine bar on Grand Street in Williamsburg co-owned by James Murphy of LCD Soundsystem. The wine list is one of the most carefully curated in Brooklyn, focused on small producers working without additives. The food — simple, precise, ingredient-led — matches the approach. It is consistently packed and reservations are essential.
