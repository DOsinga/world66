General Grant National Memorial is the imposing mausoleum of Ulysses S. Grant and his wife Julia Dent Grant. The building is the largest tomb in North America, with a monumental facade that is worth seeing even when the interior is closed.

Inside, visitors can see the tomb chamber, murals, and exhibits on Grant's life and legacy. Riverside Drive also gives good Hudson River views nearby.
