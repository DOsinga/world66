Greenacre Park is one of Midtown Manhattan's best pocket parks: a small, shaded courtyard with a waterfall, plenty of seats, and a calmness that feels almost implausible this close to the office towers. It is not a destination to cross town for, but it is a lovely place to pause if you are walking between the East Side and Fifth Avenue.
