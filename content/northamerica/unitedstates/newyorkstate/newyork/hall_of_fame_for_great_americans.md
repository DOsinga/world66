The Hall of Fame for Great Americans is an open-air colonnade of bronze busts on the Bronx Community College campus. Founded in 1900, it was an early American hall of fame and honors writers, scientists, political leaders, inventors, and other national figures.

The setting, designed as part of the former New York University campus, is as interesting as the busts themselves. Check campus access before going, and combine the visit with other Bronx sights if you are already in the area.
