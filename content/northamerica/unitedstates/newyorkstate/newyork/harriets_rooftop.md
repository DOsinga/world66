Harriet's Rooftop, on the eleventh floor of the 1 Hotel Brooklyn Bridge, gives sweeping, unobstructed views of the Manhattan skyline and Brooklyn Bridge alongside Asian-inspired small plates. It's the destination for sunset cocktails away from the more crowded Empire Stores rooftop scene.
