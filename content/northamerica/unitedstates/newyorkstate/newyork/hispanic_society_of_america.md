The Hispanic Society of America is a museum and research library devoted to the art and culture of Spain, Portugal, Latin America, and the Philippines. Its collections range from paintings and sculpture to rare books, manuscripts, textiles, and decorative arts.

It sits well uptown, away from New York's main museum circuit, which is part of the point. Come for a focused collection with real depth rather than a quick greatest-hits gallery.
