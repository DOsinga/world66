The Hotel St. George occupied an entire city block in Brooklyn Heights and was, in the 1920s and 1930s, the largest hotel in the United States. The complex has since been converted to apartments and student housing, but the original 1929 lobby building with its elaborate terracotta facade remains. The building is a useful marker for the architectural ambition of Brooklyn in its pre-Depression peak.
