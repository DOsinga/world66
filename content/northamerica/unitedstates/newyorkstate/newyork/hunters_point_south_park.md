Hunters Point South Park is a recently built waterfront park on the Queens shore of the East River, with an unobstructed panoramic view of the Midtown Manhattan skyline across the water. The park includes a kayak launch, a children's playground, a wetland area and a birdwatching platform. At sunset the view of the Chrysler and Empire State Buildings is one of the best in the city.
