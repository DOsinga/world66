The Intrepid Sea, Air & Space Museum is built around the aircraft carrier USS Intrepid, docked on Manhattan's West Side. Its collection includes military aircraft, the prototype Space Shuttle Enterprise, a guided-missile submarine, and a Concorde, making it one of New York's strongest museums for aviation and naval history.
