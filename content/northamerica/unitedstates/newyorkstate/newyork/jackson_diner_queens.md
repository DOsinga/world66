Jackson Heights in Queens is consistently rated among the most ethnically diverse neighbourhoods on earth. The corridor along 74th Street is the heart of the South Asian commercial district, with Indian, Bangladeshi, Pakistani and Nepali restaurants, sari shops and sweet sellers. Further along Roosevelt Avenue the Latin American presence — Colombian, Ecuadorian, Mexican — dominates. The food scene is extraordinary and very inexpensive by New York standards.
