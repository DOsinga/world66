Jane's Carousel is a meticulously restored 1922 wooden carousel housed in a glass pavilion designed by Jean Nouvel, on the Brooklyn Bridge Park waterfront. Rides cost $2 and the carousel is free to view, framed by the Manhattan Bridge behind it — it has become something close to an unofficial symbol of DUMBO.
