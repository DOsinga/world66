Jean-Georges Vongerichten's flagship restaurant has held three Michelin stars for over two decades. The cooking integrates Asian spicing into classic French technique with a lightness that was influential when the restaurant opened in 1997. The dining room looks out over Central Park. The lunch prix fixe is one of the better value tasting menus in the city at its price point.
