The Jewish Museum sits on Museum Mile and holds one of the world's major collections of Jewish art, ritual objects, photography, archaeology, and modern visual culture. It is smaller than the Met or Guggenheim, but it rewards time: the best visits move between sacred objects, family histories, and contemporary art without treating Jewish culture as a single fixed story.
