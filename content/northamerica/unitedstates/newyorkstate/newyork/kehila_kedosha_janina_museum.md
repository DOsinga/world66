Kehila Kedosha Janina Museum is inside the only Romaniote, or Greek-speaking Jewish, synagogue in the Western Hemisphere. The small Lower East Side museum tells the story of Greek Jews in New York and preserves a community history that is easy to miss among the neighborhood's larger immigrant landmarks.
