Kingsland Homestead is an 18th-century house in Flushing, built by Charles Doughty around 1774 and now run as a local history museum. Exhibits cover Victorian domestic life, slavery in Queens and the borough's experience of the Second World War.

It pairs well with nearby John Bowne House and Flushing Town Hall for a compact local history walk.
