Le Bernardin has held three Michelin stars for decades and ranks among the finest seafood restaurants in the world. Chef Eric Ripert's menu is organised by cooking method — barely touched, lightly cooked, loosely interpreted — applied to impeccably sourced fish. The room is formal without stuffiness, the service is precise, and the tasting menu is one of the great luxury experiences the city offers.
