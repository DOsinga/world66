The Leslie-Lohman Museum of Gay and Lesbian Art is a focused SoHo museum devoted to queer art and artists. It is strongest if you want New York's art story beyond the big institutions, with exhibitions that connect political history, identity, and the city's long role as a queer cultural centre.
