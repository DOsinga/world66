Built in 1906 as the Strand Hotel for poultry wholesalers and renamed in 1969, the Liberty Inn is an odd flatiron-shaped survivor of the Meatpacking District's pre-gentrification, red-light-adjacent era, still operating as a rent-by-the-hour hotel a block from the High Line.
