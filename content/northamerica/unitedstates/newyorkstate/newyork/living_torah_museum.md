The Living Torah Museum is a small Orthodox Jewish museum in Brooklyn, focused on Biblical and Talmudic archaeology, Judaica, and objects used to explain traditional Jewish texts. It is more specialised than the city's large museums, but that is the point: the collection is intimate and closely tied to religious study.

Displays have included antique books, ritual objects, archaeological pieces, and material connected to animals mentioned in the Bible and Talmud. Check opening details before going, as this is not a drop-in museum on the scale of the Brooklyn Museum or the Museum at Eldridge Street.
