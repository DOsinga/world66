Madame Tussauds New York is the Times Square branch of the wax museum chain, filled with posed figures from entertainment, politics, sports, and pop culture. It is touristy and priced accordingly, but it suits visitors who want a light, indoor Midtown stop between Broadway, shops, and the lights of 42nd Street.
