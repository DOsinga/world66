Marcus Garvey Park is one of Manhattan's older neighborhood parks, set around a rocky rise in central Harlem. Its high point, known as the Acropolis, gives views toward the Empire State Building, the George Washington Bridge, and the surrounding brownstone streets.

Come for a short walk, the overlook, and a feel for Harlem away from the busiest avenues.
