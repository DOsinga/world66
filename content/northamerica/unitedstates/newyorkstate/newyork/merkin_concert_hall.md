Merkin Concert Hall is a compact classical and contemporary music venue near Lincoln Center, known for chamber music and adventurous programming. It belongs to Kaufman Music Center rather than Lincoln Center itself, and works best for visitors who want a smaller, more focused performance than the big-name halls nearby.
