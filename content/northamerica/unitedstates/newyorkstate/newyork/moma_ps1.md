MoMA PS1 occupies a converted 1890s public school building in Long Island City, Queens, affiliated with MoMA since 2000. It is one of the leading venues for contemporary and experimental art in the United States, with a programme that emphasizes younger and international artists. The building's original classrooms and corridors are used as exhibition spaces. The summer Warm Up series brings DJs and live music to the courtyard every Saturday from July through September.
