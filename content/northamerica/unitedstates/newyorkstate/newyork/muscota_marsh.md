Muscota Marsh is a small salt-marsh restoration at the northern tip of Manhattan, where an elevated walkway and jetty look across the Harlem River toward the Bronx cliffs. It is one of the rare places in New York where the island's tidal edge feels visible and alive rather than buried under highway or seawall.

It is a quiet stop, best folded into a walk through Inwood or along the northern waterfront. Birdlife, marsh plants, and the open river view are the point.
