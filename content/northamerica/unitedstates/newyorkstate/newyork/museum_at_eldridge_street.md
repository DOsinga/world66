The Museum at Eldridge Street occupies the restored 1887 Eldridge Street Synagogue, a landmark of the Jewish immigrant Lower East Side. The building is the main attraction: Moorish Revival arches, stained glass, and a quiet sanctuary that tells a compact story of worship, migration, and neighbourhood change.
