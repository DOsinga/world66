The Museum of the Moving Image in Astoria is devoted to screen culture in the broadest sense: film, television, animation, video games, and the technology that made moving images possible. Exhibits range from early optical toys to contemporary digital media.

It is one of Queens' strongest museums and a good deliberate stop for film fans, families, and anyone interested in how movies and television are made. The screening programme is also worth checking before visiting.
