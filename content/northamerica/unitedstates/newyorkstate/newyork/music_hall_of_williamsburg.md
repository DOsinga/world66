The Music Hall of Williamsburg is a 550-capacity venue on North 6th Street that opened in 2007 and has been a key node in the Brooklyn indie music ecosystem since. It is operated by the same group as Terminal 5 and is a sister venue to Bowery Ballroom in Manhattan. The sound is good, the sightlines are better than average, and the neighbourhood location makes it a natural anchor for a night in Williamsburg.
