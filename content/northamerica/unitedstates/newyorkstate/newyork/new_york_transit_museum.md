The New York Transit Museum fills the abandoned Court Street subway station in Brooklyn, which is exactly the right setting for a museum about how New York moves. Vintage subway cars line the platforms, while exhibits explain tunnels, tokens, maps, and the engineering that keeps the city stitched together.

It is especially good with children, train obsessives, or anyone who wants to understand why the subway is more than just transport here.
