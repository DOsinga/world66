The Nicholas Roerich Museum is dedicated to the Russian painter, writer, and mystic Nicholas Roerich. Set near Riverside Drive on the Upper West Side, it is a focused art stop for visitors interested in Roerich's Himalayan landscapes, spiritual symbolism, and Russian cultural history.
