Nougatine is the more casual front room of the Jean-Georges complex, facing Columbus Circle. It shares the same kitchen and some of the same dishes as the main restaurant at lower prices and without the formality. Open from breakfast through dinner, it is one of the better options around Columbus Circle for well-executed, ingredient-led New American cooking.
