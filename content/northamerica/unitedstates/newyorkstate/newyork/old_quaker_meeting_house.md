The Old Quaker Meeting House in Flushing was built in 1694 and is one of New York City's oldest surviving houses of worship. Its plain timber-frame meeting room is still used by the Religious Society of Friends, and the site gives a quiet, early-colonial counterpoint to the busy streets around Main Street.
