At 541 metres, One World Trade Center is the tallest building in the Western Hemisphere. The observatory spans floors 100-102 with 360-degree views and an impressive "See Forever" theatre experience in the elevator. The location at the base of Manhattan means views across New York Harbour, to Brooklyn and New Jersey, and straight up the island. Book online; the combination ticket includes the 9/11 Memorial Museum nearby.
