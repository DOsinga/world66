One World Trade Center rises above Lower Manhattan as the tallest building in the United States. Its observation deck gives broad views over the harbor, the Hudson, Brooklyn, and the rest of the skyline.

The tower is best paired with the 9/11 Memorial & Museum and a walk through the Financial District. Book ahead if the observatory is the main reason for visiting.
