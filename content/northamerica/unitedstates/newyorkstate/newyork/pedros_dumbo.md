Pedro's has poured strong margaritas from the same Jay Street spot since long before DUMBO's loft conversions and tech offices arrived, and it still draws a loyal local crowd rather than the neighbourhood's newer arrivals. The food is straightforward Mexican-American bar fare, but the appeal is the unpretentious, decades-old atmosphere.
