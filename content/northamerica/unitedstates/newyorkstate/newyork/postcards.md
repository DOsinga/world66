Postcards is Staten Island's memorial to the borough residents killed in the September 11 attacks. The two white wing-like forms frame a view across the harbor toward Lower Manhattan, giving the memorial a quiet force that is strongest at the waterfront.

It is a short walk from the Staten Island Ferry terminal, so it is easy to combine with the ferry ride, the St. George waterfront, and nearby cultural stops.
