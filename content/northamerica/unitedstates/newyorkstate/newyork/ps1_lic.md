MoMA PS1 in Long Island City is one of the oldest and largest non-profit contemporary art organisations in the United States. The programme emphasizes emerging and international artists in a building that still reads as a school — the former classrooms, rooftop and courtyard all used as exhibition spaces. The summer Warm Up series on Saturday afternoons brings DJs, live music and crowds to the courtyard.
