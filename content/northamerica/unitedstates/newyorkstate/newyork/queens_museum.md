The Queens Museum is located in Flushing Meadows-Corona Park in a building that served as the New York City Pavilion at both the 1939 and 1964 World's Fairs. The museum holds the Panorama of the City of New York — a 9,335-square-foot scale model of every building in all five boroughs, updated regularly since Robert Moses commissioned it for the 1964 Fair. The Tiffany glass collection is also significant.
