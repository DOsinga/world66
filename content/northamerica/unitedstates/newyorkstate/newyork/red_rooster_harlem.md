Red Rooster is chef Marcus Samuelsson's tribute to the culinary traditions of Harlem — African American cooking alongside Scandinavian, Ethiopian and Caribbean influences that reflect the neighbourhood's successive immigrant communities. The fried yard bird (chicken) and the cornbread are benchmarks. Live music most evenings.
