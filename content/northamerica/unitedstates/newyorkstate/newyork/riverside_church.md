Riverside Church is a towering neo-Gothic church near Columbia University and Riverside Park, built with Rockefeller money and long associated with progressive politics, civil rights, and anti-war activism. Its size alone makes it hard to miss, but the history gives the building more weight than another handsome church facade.

The interior is worth seeing when open, especially if you enjoy Gothic Revival architecture on an American scale. Combine it with Grant's Tomb, Riverside Park, and a walk through Morningside Heights.
