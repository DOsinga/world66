Russian & Turkish Baths is an old-school East Village bathhouse, with steam rooms, saunas, cold plunges, and a no-frills New York feel. It is more of a local institution than a polished spa, which is the point.

Plan it as a relaxed break from walking downtown, with time afterward for food around the East Village or Lower East Side.
