Skovorodka is the most popular sit-down restaurant currently operating on Brighton Beach Ave — a giant bear sculpture marks the entrance, and weekend evenings bring live accordion music. The menu is Ukrainian-Russian comfort food at neighbourhood prices: borscht, varenyky (Ukrainian dumplings), stuffed cabbage rolls, and grilled meats. The dining room is large enough to handle the crowds it draws without feeling rushed. A more accessible introduction to the Brighton Beach restaurant scene than the full dinner-show format at Tatiana.
