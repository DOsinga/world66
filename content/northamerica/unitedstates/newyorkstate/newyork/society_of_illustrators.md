The Society of Illustrators is a small Upper East Side museum devoted to illustration, comics, editorial art, and graphic storytelling. Its galleries draw from a collection that reaches back to the society's 1901 founding, with rotating exhibitions that often highlight both classic commercial art and contemporary illustrators.
