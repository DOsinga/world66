The Soldiers and Sailors Monument stands above Riverside Park as New York's grand memorial to Union soldiers and sailors of the Civil War. Its temple-like form dates from 1902, long after the war itself, and gives this stretch of the Upper West Side a formal landmark above the Hudson.

It is not worth crossing the city for on its own, but it is a good stop on a Riverside Park walk. In summer, performances are sometimes staged on the monument steps.
