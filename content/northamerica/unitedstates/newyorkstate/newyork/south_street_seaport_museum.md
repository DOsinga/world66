South Street Seaport Museum tells the story of New York's old port district, with exhibits on waterfront trade, maritime work, and the ships that tied the city to the world.

The museum's biggest draw is its historic fleet on the East River. Several vessels are open for tours, making this a good stop for visitors who want Lower Manhattan history beyond Wall Street and the harbor skyline.
