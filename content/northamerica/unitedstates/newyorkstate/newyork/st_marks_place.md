St. Marks Place is the East Village stretch of 8th Street east of Third Avenue, long associated with counterculture, cheap eats, bars, shops, and people-watching. It is best treated as a short walk rather than a single sight: go for the street life, storefronts, and the sense of the neighborhood's layered history.
