St. Patrick's Cathedral is a grand neo-Gothic Catholic cathedral on Fifth Avenue, directly across from Rockefeller Center. Its twin spires, marble interior, stained glass, and busy Midtown setting make it one of New York's most visited churches.

Step inside for a short break from the street noise, or pause outside to see how the cathedral's 19th-century Gothic facade contrasts with the surrounding office towers.
