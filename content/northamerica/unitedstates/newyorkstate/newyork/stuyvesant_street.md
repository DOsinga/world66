Stuyvesant Street is a short historic East Village street known for running due east by the compass, a rarity in Manhattan's street grid. Its blocks include several 18th- and early 19th-century buildings, with St. Mark's Church in-the-Bowery and its old graveyard anchoring the eastern end.
