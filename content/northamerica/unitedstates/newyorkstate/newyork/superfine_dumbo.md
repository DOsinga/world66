Superfine has served organic, farm-to-table food from the same Front Street space for over two decades, making it a holdout from DUMBO's pre-gentrification artist-loft era. A free pool table and informal live-music nights keep the bar side feeling more like a neighbourhood clubhouse than a restaurant.
