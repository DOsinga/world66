Sushi Yasuda is austere in decor and disciplined in approach — vinegared rice at precise temperatures, fish sourced daily, nigiri pressed and handed across a blond hinoki wood counter. The no-tipping policy (service is included) and the quiet atmosphere make it feel unlike most Midtown restaurants. Reserve well in advance for counter seats.
