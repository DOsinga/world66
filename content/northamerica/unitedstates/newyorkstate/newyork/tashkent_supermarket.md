Tashkent Supermarket captures the evolution of Little Odessa better than any restaurant: a halal Uzbek-run grocery open nearly 24 hours, stocking Russian pickled vegetables, Turkish sweets, Chinese noodles, and Central Asian spices side by side. The deli counter sells smoked fish, prepared salads, and hot food. Since the closure of M&I International Foods — the legendary Soviet-Jewish anchor deli that defined the neighbourhood for nearly 50 years — Tashkent and a handful of similar stores have become the main shopping stop on the Ave.
