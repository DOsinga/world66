The Cloisters is the Metropolitan Museum of Art's branch for medieval European art, set above the Hudson River in Fort Tryon Park. Its galleries are built around architectural elements from medieval French cloisters and monasteries, with highlights including the Unicorn Tapestries and a setting that feels far removed from midtown Manhattan.
