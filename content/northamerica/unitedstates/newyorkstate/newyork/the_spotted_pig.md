Opened in 2004, The Spotted Pig is credited with establishing the gastropub format in New York. The chargrilled burger with Roquefort and shoestring fries remains one of the most replicated dishes in the city. The room is two floors of dark wood and hanging hams; expect a wait at peak hours.
