The Theodore Roosevelt Birthplace National Historic Site preserves the place where the future president lived until he was 14. The original house was demolished, but the reconstruction includes family furnishings and exhibits on Roosevelt's early life, making it a focused stop for visitors interested in New York history or presidential sites.
