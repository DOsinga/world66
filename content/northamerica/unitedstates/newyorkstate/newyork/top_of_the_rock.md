The observation deck on the 70th floor of 30 Rockefeller Plaza. The advantage over the Empire State Building is directional: you get to photograph the Empire State Building rather than stand on top of it, and the view south across Midtown is arguably the definitive New York skyline shot. Book timed entry online to avoid the queue. Sunset is the obvious time, but the night view — when the grid of streets becomes a river of light — is just as good.
