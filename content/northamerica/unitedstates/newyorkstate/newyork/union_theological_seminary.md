Union Theological Seminary is worth a look for its Gothic Revival campus on Broadway, especially the castle-like Brown Memorial Tower. It sits beside Columbia and belongs to the same Morningside Heights academic landscape, with gray brick, stone trim, and a quiet institutional heft.
