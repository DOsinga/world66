The Unisphere is a 140-foot stainless steel representation of the Earth, built for the 1964 New York World's Fair at Flushing Meadows-Corona Park. It is the largest representation of the Earth ever constructed, weighing 900,000 pounds, and stands on a foundation designed by Gilmore Clarke. The globe was a gift from US Steel and remains the symbol of Queens.
