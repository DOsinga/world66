Van Cortlandt Park is one of New York City's largest parks, filling a broad green corner of the Bronx with woods, playing fields, a freshwater lake, and long walking paths. It is also home to the Van Cortlandt House, the oldest surviving building in the Bronx.

The park is a good stop if you want a break from dense Manhattan sightseeing or are following Broadway north. It feels more local than famous, but its scale and history make it more than just another neighbourhood park.
