Vessel is the honeycomb-like stair tower at Hudson Yards, built as a public lookout and photo magnet at the northern end of the High Line. The appeal is the geometry as much as the view: bronze-toned flights of stairs fold around each other, with the Hudson River and the towers of the West Side opening up between them.

Access rules have changed over time, so check the current ticketing before making a special trip. Even from the plaza, the structure is a useful landmark for the newer, glossier edge of Manhattan.
