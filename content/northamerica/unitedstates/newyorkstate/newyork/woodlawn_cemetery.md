Woodlawn Cemetery is a 400-acre historic cemetery in the Bronx, opened in 1863. Its landscape, mausoleums, and monuments make it one of New York City's major cemetery walks, with notable burials including Joseph Pulitzer, Herman Melville, David Farragut, and Duke Ellington.
