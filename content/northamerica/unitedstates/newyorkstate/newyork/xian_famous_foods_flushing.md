Xi'an Famous Foods started as a basement stall in Flushing's old Golden Mall before growing into a citywide chain, and this Main Street location is the original. The hand-pulled noodles and spicy cumin lamb burgers showcase Shaanxi cuisine that was barely known in New York before this shop opened.
