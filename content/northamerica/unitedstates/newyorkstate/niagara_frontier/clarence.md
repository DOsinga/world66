Clarence is a well-off, spread-out town east of Buffalo, but the reason to come is more specific than suburbia. Clarence Hollow, along Main Street, is one of the older settlements in Erie County and has become the area's best-known antique-shopping strip.

The town still has pieces of its rural past: old churches, farm roads, small museums, and trails following former rail lines. It is not a place for big-ticket sightseeing, but it makes a pleasant half day if you like antiques, local history, and low-key walking or cycling.

You need a car to use Clarence well. Transit Road is pure suburban commerce, while the Hollow and the greener eastern parts of town hold most of the character.
