The Clarence Historical Society is a small but worthwhile stop in a former church in the Hollow. Its exhibits cover early settlement, local families, and the rural town that existed before Buffalo's suburbs pushed east.

The standout is the old log cabin behind the museum. It gives the place a practical sense of scale that display cases alone cannot manage.
