Clarence Hollow is the part of town visitors actually remember. Main Street here holds antique shops, small restaurants, old houses, and the modest historic core of a town that once covered a much larger piece of western New York.

It is best explored as a browse rather than a checklist. Park once, wander, and let the antiques decide how long you stay.
