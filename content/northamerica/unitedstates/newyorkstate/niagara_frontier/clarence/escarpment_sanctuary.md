Escarpment Sanctuary protects a small slice of the Onondaga Escarpment, the low rocky ledge that cuts across southern Clarence. Trails pass ponds, wetland, woods, and a viewing platform.

It is not wilderness, but it is a good reminder that this town was shaped by geology and farm country before it was shaped by subdivisions.
