Tillman Road Wildlife Management Area is the town's best nature stop if you want more than a village stroll. The preserve mixes cattail marsh, open water, and hardwood forest, with boardwalks and short trails.

It is especially good for birdwatching and a quiet leg-stretch before heading back toward Buffalo.
