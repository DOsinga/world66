The Maid of the Mist boat tour is the essential Niagara Falls experience. You board a flat-bottomed tour boat at the base of the gorge, are handed a blue poncho, and cruise directly into the spray at the base of the American Falls and then into the boiling mist at the foot of the Horseshoe Falls. At close range, the scale of the falls becomes physical rather than visual — the noise, the force of the air, the soaking spray, the sense of the sheer volume of water. The poncho is not sufficient.

The boats run from late April through early November. Lines can be long at peak times; arriving early or late in the afternoon helps. Tickets are purchased at the dock, which is reached by elevator from Prospect Point in the state park. The tour takes about 20 minutes.
