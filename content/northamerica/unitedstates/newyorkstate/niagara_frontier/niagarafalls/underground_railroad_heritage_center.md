The Niagara Falls Underground Railroad Heritage Center gives the American side of the falls a serious historical anchor beyond the water itself. It focuses on the people who crossed, helped, or resisted slavery along this border, where Canada was visible but not easily reached.

The museum is compact, direct, and worth making time for before or after the state park. It changes the way you read the river: not just as scenery, but as a line of danger, hope, law, and human courage.
