The Adirondack Railroad runs scenic trips from Thendara station, a short drive from Old Forge. It is a deliberately slow way to see the woods, rivers, and old rail corridor that helped open this part of the Adirondacks to travellers.

Schedules vary by season, so check before building a day around it. The ride works especially well for families, rail fans, or anyone who wants a break from driving Route 28.
