The Goodsell Museum is a small local history museum run by the Town of Webb Historical Association. It is not a reason to travel to Old Forge by itself, but it gives useful background on the Fulton Chain, logging, rail travel, tourism, and the families who built the resort economy.

Drop in if the weather turns or if you want a quieter counterpoint to the water park and Main Street.
