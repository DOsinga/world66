The Aviation Mall on Aviation Road is the main covered shopping centre in the southern Adirondack region, opened in 1975 and steadily renovated since. It has the usual mix of mid-tier American mall tenants — Target, Dick's Sporting Goods, JCPenney, Bath & Body Works, a multiplex cinema — and a small food court.

It is convenient as a rainy-day option if you are travelling with family, or for stocking up on outdoor gear before heading deeper into the Adirondacks. Open daily until 9 pm in summer; 8 pm in winter.
