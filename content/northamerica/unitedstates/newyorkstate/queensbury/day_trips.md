Queensbury makes a useful base for the southern Adirondacks. **Lake George** village, with its boat cruises, the rebuilt fort and the lake itself, is five minutes north. The eastern shore of Lake George leads into the **Adirondack Park**, the largest protected area in the lower 48 — with several million acres of forest, lakes and mountains. Half-day hikes within easy reach include **Prospect Mountain** (a 25-minute climb with a long view over the lake) and **Buck Mountain** (a more serious morning up the east shore).

Going north along the I-87 corridor, Lake Placid and Saranac Lake are about two hours away and worth at least one overnight. Going south, Saratoga Springs is forty minutes; its racecourse and the natural mineral baths in the state park are the standouts. The Hudson cuts through Glens Falls a few miles south of Queensbury, with a walking path along the river and the Hyde Collection art museum nearby.
