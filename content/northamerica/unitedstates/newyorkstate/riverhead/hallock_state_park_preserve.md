Hallock State Park Preserve protects a quiet stretch of North Fork shorefront east of Riverhead. Trails cross woods and fields before reaching bluffs and beach along Long Island Sound.

It is a good antidote to the traffic and retail strips around town, especially for birding, a slow walk, or a picnic with a sea breeze.
