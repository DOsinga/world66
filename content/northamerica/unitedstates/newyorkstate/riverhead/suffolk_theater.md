Suffolk Theater is one of downtown Riverhead's better pieces of old architecture, a restored 1930s theatre now used for concerts, comedy, cabaret seating, and private events.

Check the calendar before you go. The building is worth noticing from the street, but it works best when you can actually see a performance inside.
