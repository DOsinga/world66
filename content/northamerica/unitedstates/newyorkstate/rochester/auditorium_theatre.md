Rochester's Auditorium Theatre is a historic performance venue used for touring Broadway shows, concerts, comedy, and community events. It is a practical evening anchor if you want the trip to include more than museums and daytime sightseeing.

The building matters most when there is a good show on, so check the calendar before adding it to an itinerary. The surrounding area is urban and event-oriented; plan transport and parking ahead.
