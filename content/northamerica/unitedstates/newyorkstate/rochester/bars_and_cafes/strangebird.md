A craft brewery and taproom in the High Falls district, Strangebird occupies a converted industrial space with the kind of high ceilings and exposed ductwork that brewery architects dream about. The beers lean experimental — farmhouse ales, sours, mixed-fermentation projects — and the kitchen turns out wood-fired pizza and small plates that hold up to serious drinking. The outdoor patio has views of the gorge. This is where you go if you've had your Genny and want to see what Rochester's newer brewing generation is doing.

**62 Marshall Street.**
