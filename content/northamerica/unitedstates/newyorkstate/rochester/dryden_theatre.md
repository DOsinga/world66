Dryden Theatre is the George Eastman Museum's cinema, a handsome repertory theatre devoted to film as an art form. Its programme ranges from classics and restorations to international cinema and special series.

For film lovers, it can be one of Rochester's best evenings. Check the schedule before your visit; the theatre matters most when you can pair it with the museum's photography and cinema collections.
