Kodak Center is a performing arts venue inside the former Kodak Park industrial complex, a useful reminder that Rochester's cultural life still overlaps with its photographic past. The main theatre seats nearly 2,000 and hosts touring concerts, comedy, film events, and community performances.

It is worth checking the programme if you are staying in the city for more than a night. The location is north of downtown, so plan transport rather than assuming you can easily wander over after dinner.
