The Rochester Medical Museum and Archives brings together collections on the public-health history of Rochester and Monroe County. Exhibits and archival material cover local medical institutions, health work, and the people who shaped care in the region.

It is a specialist museum, but a useful stop for visitors interested in medical history or Rochester's civic institutions.
