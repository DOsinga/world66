Seneca Park follows the Genesee River north of central Rochester, giving the city a long green corridor of woods, gorge edges, and recreation space. It is also home to Seneca Park Zoo, which makes the area useful for families.

The park is best approached as part of Rochester's wider river story, from High Falls down toward Lake Ontario. Walk a section of trail if you want something quieter than the museum circuit.
