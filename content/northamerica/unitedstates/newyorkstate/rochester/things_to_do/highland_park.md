Frederick Law Olmsted designed Highland Park in the 1890s as part of Rochester's park system, and the city planted it with one of the finest lilac collections in the world — over 1,200 plants representing more than 500 varieties. In mid-May, when the lilacs bloom, the park draws up to 100,000 visitors for the **Lilac Festival**, the largest free lilac festival in North America. The rest of the year it's a quietly beautiful arboretum, with panoramic views over the city from the Warner Castle observation area, a pansy bed that blooms through summer, and an extensive collection of rhododendrons, magnolias, and horse chestnuts. Serious arborists and casual walkers are equally at home here.

**Highland Avenue and South Avenue. Open daily, free.**
