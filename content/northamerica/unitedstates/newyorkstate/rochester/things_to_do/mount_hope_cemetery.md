Established in 1838, Mount Hope is one of the oldest municipal Victorian cemeteries in the United States and one of the most beautiful. The grounds are designed in the romantic landscape tradition — winding paths, wooded hillsides, Gothic Revival gatehouse — and the list of people buried here is extraordinary: **Frederick Douglass** and **Susan B. Anthony** both rest here, a coincidence that makes this cemetery a place of genuine historical gravity. The Victorian monuments and mausoleums are architecturally impressive, and the trees are magnificent in autumn. The Friends of Mount Hope Cemetery run excellent guided tours.

**791 Mount Hope Avenue. Open daily, dawn to dusk. Free.**
