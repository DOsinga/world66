An enormous, exuberant celebration of play in all its forms — toys, games, puzzles, dolls, video games, pinball machines, and interactive exhibits spread across 285,000 square feet. The museum houses the **National Toy Hall of Fame** (inducting a handful of classic toys each year — LEGO, Barbie, the cardboard box), the **World Video Game Hall of Fame**, and the **International Center for the History of Electronic Games**, which holds one of the most significant collections of gaming hardware and software in the world. There's a full-sized carousel inside, a butterfly garden, and a recreated 1950s street scene. Adults without children sometimes feel self-conscious coming here; they shouldn't.

**1 Manhattan Square Drive. Open daily.**
