Pendragon Theatre is one of Saranac Lake's small-town cultural strengths. It stages plays, readings, workshops, and community productions, giving the village an evening option that does not depend on weather, paddling conditions, or the ski calendar.

Check the schedule before building plans around it, but keep it in mind if you are staying overnight. Saranac Lake has always had a creative streak, and Pendragon is one of the places where that energy shows up on a regular stage.
