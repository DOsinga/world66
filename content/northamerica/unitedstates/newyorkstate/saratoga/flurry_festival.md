Flurry Festival brings several thousand people to Saratoga Springs for a winter weekend of folk music and dance. Contra dance is the best-known draw, but the programme usually spreads across concerts, workshops, and participatory sessions.

It is worth planning around if you like traditional music or social dance, and it gives Saratoga a very different feel from the summer racing season.
