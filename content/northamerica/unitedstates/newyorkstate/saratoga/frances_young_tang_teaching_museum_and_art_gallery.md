The Tang, on the Skidmore College campus, is Saratoga Springs at its most intellectually playful. It is a teaching museum, but that should not put you off: the exhibitions are polished, often surprising, and willing to mix contemporary art with science, history, performance, and social questions.

It makes a good change of pace from the race course, mineral springs, and Broadway storefronts. Check opening hours and current shows before crossing town; the permanent draw is the museum's restless programming rather than a single famous object.
