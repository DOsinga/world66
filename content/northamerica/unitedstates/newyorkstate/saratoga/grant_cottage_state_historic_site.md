Grant Cottage State Historic Site preserves the mountaintop house where Ulysses S. Grant spent his final weeks finishing his memoirs before his death in 1885. The rooms remain closely tied to that last chapter, making the site more intimate than many presidential or military memorials.

It is outside Saratoga Springs, but worthwhile for Civil War and presidential-history travelers. The hilltop setting also gives the visit a reflective quality, with views and quiet grounds balancing the dense biographical story inside.
