The New York State Military Museum occupies a former armory in Saratoga Springs and tells the state's military story through uniforms, weapons, battle flags, photographs, and archival material. It is especially strong for visitors interested in the Civil War and the long civic memory of New York regiments.

This is a focused museum rather than a broad Saratoga highlight, but it rewards an hour if you have already done the springs, racing, and downtown. The armory setting helps: the building feels connected to the subject rather than merely housing it.
