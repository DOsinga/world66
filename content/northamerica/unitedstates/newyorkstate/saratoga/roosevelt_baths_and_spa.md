Roosevelt Baths and Spa is the classic mineral-bath experience in Saratoga Spa State Park. The bathhouse was built in 1935 as part of the park's development and still uses mineral water drawn from the springs.

The setting is more historic than glossy, with an old institutional feel that suits the place. It is the most direct way to connect Saratoga Springs' resort history with a present-day visit.
