  The Saratoga Race Course opened in 1863 — in the middle of the Civil War — making it the oldest major thoroughbred racing track in the United States. It has hosted some of the sport's most famous upsets, including the defeats of Triple Crown winners Man o' War in 1919 and Secretariat's stablemates in 1973. The track earned the nickname "The Graveyard of Champions."
---

One of the great sporting venues in America, the Saratoga Race Course operates for just six weeks each August and draws crowds of up to 50,000 on weekends. The atmosphere is unlike any other racetrack — Victorian grandstands, elm trees, the smell of fresh turf, and a genuine social mix from dedicated handicappers to families there for the spectacle.

General admission is inexpensive and places you in the paddock and the apron areas close to the track. The Backstretch Program offers early morning tours to watch horses train, which is arguably better than the races themselves. The track is a mile and an eighth from the Saratoga Spa State Park entrance.
