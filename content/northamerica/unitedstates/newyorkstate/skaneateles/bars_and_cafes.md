---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Skaneateles is not a late-night town, but there are enough options for a pre-dinner drink or a morning coffee. The Sherwood Inn tavern is the classic choice for a drink in an old-inn atmosphere; the wine-country location means Finger Lakes rieslings and cabernet francs show up on most lists alongside the usual beer taps.
