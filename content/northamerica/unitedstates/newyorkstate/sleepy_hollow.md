Sleepy Hollow sits on the east bank of the Hudson River, just north of Tarrytown and close enough to [New York City](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/newyork) for an easy day trip. Its name is doing a lot of work: Washington Irving set **The Legend of Sleepy Hollow** in this low valley of old Dutch farms, churchyards, and wooded roads.

The village is not just a Halloween stage set. The **Old Dutch Church** and the adjacent cemetery are real 17th-century survivals, and the headstones and maples still give the place its best atmosphere. A few minutes away, **Philipsburg Manor** tells a harder Hudson Valley story of Dutch trade, milling, and enslaved labour.

North and east of the village, the Rockefeller presence is everywhere: **Kykuit**, the hilltop family estate, the stained glass at Union Church, Stone Barns, and the carriage roads of **Rockefeller State Park Preserve**. Come in October if you want the full Headless Horseman circus, but the quieter seasons are better if you want to see the place itself.
