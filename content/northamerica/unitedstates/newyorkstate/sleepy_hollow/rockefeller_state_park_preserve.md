Rockefeller State Park Preserve protects the old estate carriage roads, Pocantico River woods, fields, and Swan Lake. It is the place to walk off the cemetery crowds: broad gravel paths, stone bridges, and glimpses of the landscape that made this part of Westchester so attractive to the Rockefellers.
