Sleepy Hollow Cemetery spreads north of the Old Dutch burying ground with steep lanes, wooded slopes, and elaborate mausoleums. Washington Irving is buried here, along with Andrew Carnegie, Walter Chrysler, William Rockefeller, and many less famous names that make the cemetery feel like a walk through Hudson Valley money, industry, and myth.
