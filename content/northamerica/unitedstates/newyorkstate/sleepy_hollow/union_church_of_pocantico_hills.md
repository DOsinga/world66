Union Church of Pocantico Hills is a small stone church made remarkable by its stained glass. Windows by Henri Matisse and Marc Chagall were commissioned by the Rockefeller family, including Matisse's final completed work.

It pairs naturally with Kykuit and the Rockefeller State Park Preserve, but check tour hours before going; access is more limited than at an ordinary parish church.
