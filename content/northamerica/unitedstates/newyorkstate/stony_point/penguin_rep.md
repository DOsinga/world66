Penguin Rep is a small professional theatre in a converted 19th-century hay barn. It gives Stony Point a cultural anchor beyond the battlefield and makes the town more than a quick historic-site pull-off.

Check the schedule before building plans around it. When a performance lines up with a Hudson Valley day trip, it is a pleasant way to turn a short stop into an evening.
