Stony Point Battlefield preserves the rocky Hudson River headland where American forces captured a British position during the Revolutionary War. The site is small enough to understand on foot, with earthworks, markers, open lawns, and wide river views.

It is the one essential stop in town. The landscape explains the military logic immediately: a high point projecting into the Hudson, controlling movement along a river that both armies cared about deeply.
