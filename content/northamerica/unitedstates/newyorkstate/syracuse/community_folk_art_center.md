The Community Folk Art Center is a small arts space focused on artists of the African Diaspora. Run through Syracuse University, it hosts exhibitions, performances, talks, and community programs rather than a large permanent collection.

It is close to Syracuse Stage and works well as a cultural add-on in the university-adjacent part of downtown. Check the current exhibition calendar before going, since the value of a visit depends on what is on.
