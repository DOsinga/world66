The Landmark Theatre on South Salina Street is a 1928 movie palace that ranks among the finest surviving examples of the form in the northeastern United States. The interior is an extravagant fantasy of Moorish, Indian, and Byzantine ornament — gilded columns, painted ceilings, peacock motifs, and elaborate plasterwork covering every surface. It seats 2,800 people and remains in regular use for concerts, dance performances, Broadway touring shows, and film screenings.

The building was saved from demolition in 1977 by a community campaign and has been lovingly maintained since. If you are in Syracuse and anything is playing here, go — the experience of sitting in the room is worth it regardless of what's on stage. Check the schedule in advance; the box office is on South Salina Street.
