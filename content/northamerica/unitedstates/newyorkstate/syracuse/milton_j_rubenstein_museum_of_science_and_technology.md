The Milton J. Rubenstein Museum of Science and Technology, usually shortened to the MOST, is Syracuse's hands-on science museum. It is especially good with children, with interactive exhibits, a climbing wall, a geologic cave, flight-simulator displays, and a large indoor play area.

The museum also has a domed theatre and planetarium, which makes it useful on bad-weather days or when you need a break from more traditional sightseeing downtown.
