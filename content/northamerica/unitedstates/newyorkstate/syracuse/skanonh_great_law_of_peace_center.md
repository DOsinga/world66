The Skä-noñh Great Law of Peace Center is one of the most important history stops around Syracuse because it tells the region's story from an Onondaga and Haudenosaunee perspective. Exhibits focus on the Great Law of Peace, the formation of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy, forced change after European settlement, and the continuing presence of Indigenous nations in central New York.

It sits near Onondaga Lake, a place with deep cultural and environmental significance. Visit before walking the lakefront or after seeing downtown Syracuse's civic sights; it changes the way the surrounding landscape reads. The centre is compact, but the subject matter is large and deserves unhurried attention.
