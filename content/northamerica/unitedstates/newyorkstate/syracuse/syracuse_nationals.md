Syracuse Nationals is a major classic-car event at the New York State Fairgrounds, drawing hot rods, customs, restored cars, vendors, and a crowd that treats the weekend as a rolling reunion. If you like American car culture, it is one of Syracuse's livelier date-specific events.

It is not relevant outside event dates, but during the show it can reshape the west side of the city with traffic, hotel demand, and polished chrome. Book ahead and bring sun protection for the fairgrounds.
