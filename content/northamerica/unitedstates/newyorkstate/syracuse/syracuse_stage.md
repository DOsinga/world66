Syracuse Stage is the city's main professional theatre company, based near Syracuse University and sharing the cultural orbit of the campus. Its seasons usually mix contemporary drama, classics, and crowd-pleasing productions.

For travellers, it is a planned evening rather than a daytime sight. Check the current season before your trip; when the timing works, it adds a strong local arts option beyond the Landmark Theatre and museums.
