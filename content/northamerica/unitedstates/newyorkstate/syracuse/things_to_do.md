Syracuse's most visited indoor attraction is the [Erie Canal Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/syracuse/erie_canal_museum), housed in the 1850 Weighlock Building — the only surviving example of a canal boat weigh station in the country. The [Everson Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/syracuse/everson_museum) is a significant regional art museum with strong collections of American ceramics and studio pottery. The [Landmark Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/syracuse/landmark_theatre) is a magnificently restored 1928 movie palace that still hosts concerts, comedy, and touring shows.

Outside the city, [Green Lakes State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/syracuse/green_lakes) has two meromictic glacial lakes with striking turquoise water. For the peculiar, the [Upside-Down Traffic Light](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/syracuse/upside_down_traffic_light) in Tipperary Hill is a local institution with a story. The [24-Second Shot Clock Monument](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/syracuse/shot_clock_monument) celebrates one of basketball's most important rule changes, invented here in 1954.

Sports fans will want to know about the JMA Wireless Dome on the Syracuse University campus — home to Orange football and basketball with a capacity of over 49,000.
