The Westcott Theater is a live-music venue in Syracuse's Westcott neighborhood, hosting touring
bands, DJs, comedy, and community events in a converted theatre space. It gives visitors a more
local night out than the downtown arena or university event calendar.

Its value depends on the schedule, so check listings before planning around it. When the bill is
right, the venue is a useful window into Syracuse's student-and-neighbourhood music scene, with
restaurants and bars nearby for an easy evening.
