Corning punches above its weight for a small city, anchored by two world-class museums and a walkable historic downtown. The [Corning Museum of Glass](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/the_finger_lakes/corning/corning_museum_of_glass) and the [Rockwell Museum of Western Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/the_finger_lakes/corning/rockwell_museum) are the main draws; the [Market Street Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/the_finger_lakes/corning/market_street) connects them with galleries, restaurants, and shops. The [Benjamin Patterson Inn Museum Complex](/northamerica/unitedstates/newyorkstate/the_finger_lakes/corning/benjamin_patterson_inn) gives a sense of the town before the glass industry arrived.
