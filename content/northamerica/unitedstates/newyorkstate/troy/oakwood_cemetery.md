Oakwood Cemetery is one of Troy's richest historic landscapes, spread over hills north of the city with winding roads, old trees, elaborate monuments, and long views toward the Hudson Valley. It is a cemetery, but also an outdoor museum of nineteenth-century taste and local ambition.

Come for a quiet walk and give it time. The graves and memorials connect to Troy's industrial, political, and military history, while the terrain makes the place feel more like a park than a formal burial ground. It is one of the city's strongest stops after the downtown historic district.
