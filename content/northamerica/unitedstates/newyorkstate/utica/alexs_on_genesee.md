A reliable Bosnian restaurant on the Bleecker Street corridor, serving cevapi, pljeskavica, and grilled meats that draw both the Bosnian community and Utica locals who've learned to appreciate the food. The portions are generous, the prices modest, and the atmosphere is more neighbourhood diner than anything resembling fine dining. A good introduction to the immigrant food scene that defines this part of Utica.
