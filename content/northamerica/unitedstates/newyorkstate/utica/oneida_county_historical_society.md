The Oneida County Historical Society has a local history museum, a historical and genealogical library, and a small shop. Founded in 1876, it is one of Utica's better stops for understanding the Mohawk Valley's settlement, industry, and civic history.

It is most useful for visitors with an interest in regional history or family research.
