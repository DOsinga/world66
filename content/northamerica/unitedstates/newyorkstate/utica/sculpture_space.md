Sculpture Space is an artist residency and working studio that gives Utica a contemporary art angle beyond its larger museums and theatres. The interest is in process: artists developing large sculptural work, exhibitions, talks, and occasional open-studio access.

It is worth checking when public events are scheduled. Without one, you may find limited visitor access, so treat it as a programme-based POI and pair it with Munson or downtown Utica if timing lines up.
