---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Utica's cultural core is compact and manageable on foot. The Munson-Williams-Proctor complex — combining a Philip Johnson-designed museum with a genuine Victorian mansion — is the anchor. The Stanley Theater is a 1928 movie palace worth seeing for its interior alone. Beyond the downtown core, Bleecker Street's immigrant food scene is Utica's most distinctive and surprising cultural offering.
