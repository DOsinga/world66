The Black River is Watertown's outdoor surprise. Below downtown, the river cuts through a gorge with ledges, play waves, and summer rafting runs that are much livelier than the city streets suggest.

Guided rafting trips usually handle the logistics and safety gear. If you are not rafting, the river still gives Watertown a rougher, more interesting edge than many upstate county seats.
