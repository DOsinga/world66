Thompson Park is Watertown's main green space, set on high ground east of downtown. The park has paths, picnic space, a pool, playgrounds, and broad views back over the city.

Zoo New York sits inside the park and focuses on animals and habitats from New York State. It is modest, but the local focus gives it more character than a generic small zoo, especially for families or anyone curious about the region's wildlife.
