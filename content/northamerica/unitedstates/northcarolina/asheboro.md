Asheboro is a small Piedmont city with one exceptional reason to visit: the [North Carolina Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/asheboro/north_carolina_zoo). The zoo is not a token local attraction. It spreads across thousands of wooded acres south of town and is one of the largest natural-habitat zoos in the world, with long walking routes through Africa and North America habitats.

That alone can justify a day, especially for families. Asheboro also has a useful second identity as the service town for Randolph County's pottery country. [Seagrove pottery country](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/asheboro/seagrove_pottery_country), a short drive south, has dozens of studios and the North Carolina Pottery Center, making the area more than a one-sight zoo stop.

The town itself is modest but pleasant. Downtown has a few cafes, old storefronts and the [Sunset Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/asheboro/sunset_theatre), while the [North Carolina Aviation Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/asheboro/north_carolina_aviation_museum) and [Pisgah Covered Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/asheboro/pisgah_covered_bridge) add enough local interest to round out a weekend.
