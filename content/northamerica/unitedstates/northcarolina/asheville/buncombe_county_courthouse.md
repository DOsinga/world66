Buncombe County Courthouse is one of the big civic buildings around Pack Square, completed in 1928 with setbacks, grouped windows, and neoclassical ornament. Its granite exterior has the stern confidence of a courthouse that wanted to look permanent from day one.

The lobby is the surprise, with a sweeping marble staircase, bronze and glass screens, a coffered ceiling, and mosaic tile floor. It is an easy architecture stop while walking through downtown Asheville.
