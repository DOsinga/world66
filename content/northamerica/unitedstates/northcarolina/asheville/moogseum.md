The Moogseum is a small interactive museum dedicated to Bob Moog, the engineer who built the first widely-used analogue synthesiser. It opened in 2019 just off Pack Square, very close to where the Moog factory still operates. The exhibits are unusually hands-on: visitors can build patches on a giant modular synth, mess with theremins, and trace the history of electronic music from early oscillators to modern modular setups.

It is small — an hour is enough — but worth a stop even for visitors who think they have no interest in synthesisers. The shop sells a good selection of records and Moog merchandise.
