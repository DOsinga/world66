The Grey Eagle is one of Asheville's most dependable live-music rooms, a relaxed venue in the River Arts District with a calendar that leans into roots, indie rock, folk, bluegrass, and touring Americana. It is the kind of place where the city's music scene feels lived-in rather than packaged for visitors.

Check the calendar before you go; the room can be quiet one night and packed the next. When the booking lines up with your taste, it makes a good low-key evening after galleries, breweries, or a day on the Blue Ridge Parkway, and it gives Asheville a different rhythm from the downtown busker-and-brewery circuit.
