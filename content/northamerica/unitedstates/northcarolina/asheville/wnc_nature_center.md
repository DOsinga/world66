The WNC Nature Center is a family-friendly wildlife attraction focused on the animals and habitats of the Southern Appalachians. Rather than presenting itself as a general zoo, it emphasizes regional species and conservation education.

It is a good Asheville stop for travelers with children or anyone who wants a low-key nature visit without committing to a longer Blue Ridge hike. The center also helps connect the city to the mountain ecosystems that surround it.
