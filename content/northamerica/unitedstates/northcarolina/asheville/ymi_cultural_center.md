The YMI Cultural Center occupies one of Asheville's most important Black landmarks, a late nineteenth-century building commissioned during the Vanderbilt era and later restored as a cultural anchor. Its galleries and programmes focus on African-American art, local history, and the wider Black experience in Buncombe County.

It is not a large museum, and visitors should check the current exhibition and event schedule before making a special trip. But as a stop in downtown Asheville it adds a necessary layer to the city's story, especially if your visit has otherwise revolved around architecture, breweries, and mountain scenery.
