Appalachian State University is the defining institution of Boone, with around 20,000 students who give the town an energy that would otherwise be absent from a mountain community of its size. The campus is handsome — a mix of brick buildings and modern additions set against the surrounding peaks — and merges naturally into the downtown without a hard edge.

The campus is worth walking for its own sake. The Turchin Center for the Visual Arts hosts strong rotating exhibitions and is free to enter. The Schaefer Center for the Performing Arts brings a steady programme of music, theatre, and dance to town. Even on a casual visit, the university's presence makes Boone feel connected to broader currents in art and ideas.
