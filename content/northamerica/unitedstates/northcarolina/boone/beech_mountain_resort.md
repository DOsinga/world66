Beech Mountain Resort is one of the main winter-sports draws in the High Country, sitting high enough to make skiing and snowboarding plausible in a state better known for blue ridges than alpine conditions. In winter, it is a straightforward day trip from Boone for snow tubing, lessons, and lift-served runs.

The resort has pushed into warm-weather use as well, with mountain biking, scenic lift rides, and events. It is still best judged seasonally: check conditions before building a trip around it, especially in the shoulder months when mountain weather can change the plan quickly.
