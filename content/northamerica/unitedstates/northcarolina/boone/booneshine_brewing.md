Booneshine Brewing is one of the better breweries in the High Country, with a taproom on King Street that stays busy most evenings. The beer leans toward well-made approachable styles — IPAs, lagers, a solid stout in winter — with occasional limited releases that reward a second visit. The taproom has a mountain-lodge feel without veering into kitsch, and the outdoor patio is the place to be on warm evenings.

The food menu is simple but competent: flatbreads, a few shareable plates. It works as a late-afternoon stop or a pre-dinner drink. The staff are knowledgeable about the beer and unstuffy about recommendations.
