---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Boone has no commercial airport. The nearest options are Charlotte Douglas International (about 100 miles south, roughly two hours by car on US 321 and I-40), Piedmont Triad International in Greensboro (about 90 miles southeast), and Tri-Cities Airport in Blountville, Tennessee (about 75 miles west). Renting a car is effectively required — there is no train service and bus connections are very limited.

By car from Charlotte, take I-85/I-40 west to Hickory, then US 321 north through Lenoir to Boone. The approach from the south gives you an increasingly dramatic succession of ridgelines as you climb into the High Country. US 421 from Winston-Salem is another good option.
