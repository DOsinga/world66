---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Boone has no commercial airport and no passenger rail. Most visitors drive.

The town sits where US-321 and US-421 cross, well into the mountains and reached only by winding two-lane road from any direction. The nearest interstate exits are at Hickory (about 70 miles south on US-321) and Wilkesboro (about 35 miles east on US-421). Allow extra time in winter — snow and ice can close roads.

**Nearest airports:**
- **Charlotte Douglas (CLT)** — about 2 hours by car, the best mainline option.
- **Tri-Cities Regional (TRI)** in Tennessee — about 90 minutes, with limited service.
- **Asheville Regional (AVL)** — about 2 hours via the southern Blue Ridge Parkway when the road is open.

Once in town, downtown Boone is walkable, but everywhere else — the Parkway, Grandfather Mountain, the ski areas — needs a car. There is no taxi standing fleet; rideshare coverage is patchy.
