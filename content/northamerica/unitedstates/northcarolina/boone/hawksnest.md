Hawksnest is a Blue Ridge activity resort best known for snow tubing in winter and ziplines in warmer months. It gives the Boone area another family-friendly outdoor option beyond hiking, skiing, and scenic drives on the Blue Ridge Parkway.

It is most useful when you want structured fun rather than a wild mountain experience. Book ahead in peak winter tubing season, and check weather carefully; mountain conditions can change a day quickly.
