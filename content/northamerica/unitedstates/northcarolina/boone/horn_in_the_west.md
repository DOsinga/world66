Horn in the West is an outdoor historical drama performed every summer in the Daniel Boone Amphitheater. Written by playwright Kermit Hunter, it dramatises the conflicts and alliances between Cherokee people, colonial settlers, and frontier figures including Daniel Boone during the Revolutionary War period. The setting — a natural hillside amphitheater with Blue Ridge peaks visible above the stage — is perfectly suited to the subject.

Performances run June through August. Adjacent to the amphitheater is the Hickory Ridge History Museum, which reconstructs an 18th-century Appalachian homestead with demonstrations of period crafts and tools. The combination of living history and drama makes for a full afternoon and evening.
