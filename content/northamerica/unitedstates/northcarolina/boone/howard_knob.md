story: >
  Howard Knob was the site of a proposed wind farm in the late 2000s that would have placed turbines directly on the ridgeline. Local opposition was fierce — the knob is visible from the Blue Ridge Parkway and from much of downtown Boone. The project was ultimately abandoned after years of legal battles, and the knob remains undeveloped, its 360-degree views intact.
---

Howard Knob rises to nearly 4,400 feet just north of downtown Boone and offers one of the best accessible viewpoints in the area. A short but steep trail leads to the summit, where the views extend across the town below and out to multiple layers of Blue Ridge ridgelines in every direction. On a clear day you can see Grandfather Mountain to the southeast.

The park is free and the trailhead is easily reached by car. The hike to the summit takes about 30 minutes and the trail is rocky but manageable. It is a popular spot at sunrise and sunset, and the views reward the effort at any time of day.
