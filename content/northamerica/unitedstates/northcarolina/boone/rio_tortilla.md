A Boone institution on King Street, Rio Tortilla Factory has been feeding students and locals for years with straightforward Mexican food and generous portions at low prices. The tortillas are made in-house, which makes a noticeable difference. The burritos are the thing to order. Cash is preferred, the line can be long at lunch, and the seating is minimal — it is essentially a counter operation in a small space.

Do not expect much in the way of atmosphere. What you get instead is honest food at a fair price in a town that takes its Mexican hole-in-the-wall seriously. A Boone rite of passage.
