---
title: 'Bars and Cafes'
type: section
---

Franklin Street stays lively most nights, and on game days the energy is hard to match anywhere in the South. The mix of long-established bars and newer places gives the strip a character that holds up even when UNC is on break.

The **Carolina Coffee Shop** has been serving coffee and meals on Franklin Street since 1922 — the oldest restaurant in Chapel Hill and a genuine piece of town history. **Top of the Hill** is both a brewpub and a rooftop terrace overlooking Franklin Street, with its own craft beers and good food; the views from the upper deck on a warm evening are excellent.

Carrboro's bar scene is more relaxed and music-focused than Chapel Hill's. **Cat's Cradle** is the area's premier live music venue, drawing national touring acts and beloved local bands to a room that holds around 800 people.
