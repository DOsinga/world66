Top of the Hill sits at the corner of Franklin Street and Columbia Street, right at the heart of Chapel Hill's commercial strip. The rooftop terrace on the third floor looks out over Franklin Street in one direction and the UNC campus in the other — hard to beat on a warm afternoon. The house beers are well made, with a rotation of seasonals alongside the year-round lineup. The food is pub-quality and a step above most brewpubs.
