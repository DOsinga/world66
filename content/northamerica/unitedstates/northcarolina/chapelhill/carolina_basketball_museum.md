The Carolina Basketball Museum is Chapel Hill distilled into hardwood, banners, and blue-and-white memory. It tells the story of UNC basketball through trophies, jerseys, video, and tributes to the coaches and players who made the programme part of the town's identity.

Non-fans may find it niche, but college basketball is not a side note here; it is local culture. Pair it with a walk around campus or a game at the Dean Smith Center if you can get tickets.
