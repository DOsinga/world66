The Carolina Coffee Shop opened in 1922 and is the oldest operating restaurant in Chapel Hill. It is a genuine piece of local history: generations of students, professors, and town regulars have sat at these same tables. The food — breakfasts and lunch staples — is straightforward and honest. Come for the atmosphere as much as the menu. The booths and the worn counter have a patina that no amount of renovation could replicate.
