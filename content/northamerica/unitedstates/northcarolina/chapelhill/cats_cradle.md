Cat's Cradle is the classic live-music club for the Chapel Hill and Carrboro music scene. Its stage has long been associated with indie rock, alternative music, and touring acts that fit the area's college-town energy.

The venue sits in Carrboro, just west of Chapel Hill proper, and is best planned around a specific show. If the calendar lines up, it is one of the most authentic ways to spend an evening in the area.
