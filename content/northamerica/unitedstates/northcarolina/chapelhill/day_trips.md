Chapel Hill sits at the western point of the Research Triangle, making Durham and Raleigh easy day trips — both are less than 30 minutes away by car. Durham has a vibrant food scene and the Duke University campus; Raleigh is the state capital with several good museums. The three cities together form a metropolitan area defined by universities, research institutions, and tech companies, each with a distinct character that rewards a day of exploration.
