Mediterranean Deli is a Chapel Hill institution — enormous portions of Lebanese and Middle Eastern food at prices that work for students and visitors alike. The falafel, shawarma, and mezze plates are reliably good, and the place is open late, which matters in a college town. Lines form at peak times but move quickly. On Franklin Street in Carrboro, just west of the UNC campus.
