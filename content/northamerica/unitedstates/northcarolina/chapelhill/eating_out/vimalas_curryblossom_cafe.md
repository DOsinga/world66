Vimala's is a beloved Carrboro institution serving home-style South Indian cooking in a warm, community-minded space. Owner Vimala Rajendran is something of a local hero — she has fed people regardless of ability to pay for years and is deeply embedded in the town's progressive culture. The food is excellent: fragrant curries, dosas, and rice dishes cooked with real care. A Sunday brunch draws long lines.
