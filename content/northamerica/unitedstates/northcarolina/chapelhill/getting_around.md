---
title: 'Getting Around'
type: section
---

Chapel Hill is a walkable town. The university campus, Franklin Street, and most of what visitors want to see are all within a comfortable 15–20 minute walk of each other. Carrboro is immediately adjacent — the boundary between the two towns is invisible on foot, and the walk along Franklin Street from the UNC campus into Carrboro takes about 10 minutes.

**Chapel Hill Transit** runs buses throughout the town and is free to all riders — no fare required. Routes connect the campus, downtown, the South Square area, and the outer neighborhoods. Buses run frequently during the day and into the evening.

**Rideshare** (Uber, Lyft) is widely available. Useful for reaching the Dean Smith Center or the Botanical Garden if you don't want to walk, and for late-night returns after the buses stop.

**Parking:** Free street parking exists on some residential blocks adjacent to downtown. The Wallace Parking Deck on West Rosemary Street is the most convenient paid garage for Franklin Street visits.
