Gimghoul Castle is one of Chapel Hill's stranger landmarks: a small stone castle linked to university folklore, secret-society stories, and the legend of Peter Dromgoole. It sits near campus but feels deliberately tucked away, more curiosity than formal attraction.

The castle is private property, so the point is a respectful look from public approaches, not poking around the grounds. It is worth knowing about because it gives Chapel Hill a Gothic footnote that contrasts nicely with the polished university landmarks nearby.
