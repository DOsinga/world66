PlayMakers is the University of North Carolina's resident theatre company, staging classics, new work, and contemporary drama on campus. It is one of the better cultural nights out in Chapel Hill, especially when the student-heavy town quiets down between basketball games and bar nights. Check the season before you plan around it.
