The Billy Graham Library is a museum and public archive about the life of evangelist Billy Graham. Exhibits trace his North Carolina roots, international preaching career, and role in 20th-century American religious life.

The complex is set near Graham's childhood home and is most rewarding for visitors interested in religion, modern American history, or Charlotte's connections beyond banking and sport.
