Charlotte Motor Speedway is technically in Concord, northeast of the city, but it belongs to the Charlotte travel picture because stock-car racing is one of the region's defining obsessions. The huge oval hosts major NASCAR weekends and sits among the race shops, team facilities, and motorsport businesses that make the area feel like the sport's home ground.

It is most rewarding when there is an event on, from headline race weekends to smaller track programmes. Even if you are not a racing devotee, the scale is impressive: grandstands, infield, lights, traffic plans, and the sheer amount of infrastructure required to turn speed into a regional ritual.
