The Charlotte Museum of History covers the city's past on the grounds of the Hezekiah Alexander House, an 18th-century homestead. The site includes the original stone house, related historic buildings, and a modern museum with focused exhibits.

Guided tours are the main reason to visit, especially if you want a deeper look at colonial-era Charlotte and local history away from the city center.
