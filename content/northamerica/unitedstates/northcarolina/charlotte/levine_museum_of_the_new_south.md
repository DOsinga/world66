Levine Museum of the New South is Charlotte's main museum for the history of the South after the Civil War. Its exhibits focus on the region's social, political, and economic changes, with material on race, migration, urban growth, and daily life.

The museum is most useful early in a Charlotte visit, because it gives context for the city's banking boom, neighbourhood history, and wider regional identity.
