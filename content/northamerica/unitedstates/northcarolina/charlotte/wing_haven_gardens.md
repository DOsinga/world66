Wing Haven Gardens and Bird Sanctuary is a small but carefully designed garden in Myers Park, with winding paths, pools, formal plantings, and bird habitat around a 1927 house. It is strongest in spring, when the garden makes a quiet contrast to Charlotte's busier uptown attractions.
