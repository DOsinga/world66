American Tobacco Campus is the obvious starting point for any walking tour of central Durham. The red-brick warehouses and tall water tower of the old cigarette factory still dominate the southern edge of downtown, and the boardwalk that runs through the complex — flanked by restaurants, ice-cream shops, and the lawn outside the Durham Performing Arts Center — is one of the city's most agreeable hangouts on a warm evening.

The campus links directly south to the Durham Bulls Athletic Park and east to the city's tech offices in the old Lucky Strike printing buildings. The renovated American Underground complex within the campus has been one of the most successful tech start-up hubs in the South.
