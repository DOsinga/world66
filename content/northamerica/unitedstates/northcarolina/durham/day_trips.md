title: Day trips
type: section
---

The other two points of the Research Triangle make the obvious day trips. **[Chapel Hill](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/chapelhill)**, fifteen minutes west, offers the University of North Carolina campus, a small lively college-town main street, and the excellent Ackland Art Museum. **[Raleigh](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/raleigh)**, half an hour east, is the state capital — the cluster of free state museums on Bicentennial Plaza is one of the great free attractions of the South-East.

Further afield, the small mountain town of **Saxapahaw** on the Haw River (forty minutes south-west) is a quietly thriving cultural and food destination, with the Saxapahaw General Store an unlikely culinary landmark. **Hillsborough**, twenty minutes north-west, is a Revolutionary War town with an intact eighteenth-century main street and the Eno River running through it.

For a longer weekend, the **Outer Banks** beaches are around three and a half hours east, and the **Blue Ridge Mountains** around Asheville are four hours west.
