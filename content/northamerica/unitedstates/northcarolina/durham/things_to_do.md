title: Things to do
type: section
---

Durham splits cleanly into three main zones for visitors: the redeveloped downtown and warehouse districts around the [American Tobacco Campus](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/durham/american_tobacco_campus); the leafy Duke University campus a short drive west, with the [Duke Chapel](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/durham/duke_chapel) and [Sarah P. Duke Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/durham/duke_gardens) at its heart; and the historic [Black Wall Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/durham/black_wall_street) on Parrish Street, an essential stop for understanding the city's twentieth-century history. A weekend covers the highlights; a long weekend gives time to wander.
