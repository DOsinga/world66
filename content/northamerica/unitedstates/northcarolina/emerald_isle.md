Emerald Isle is a low-key beach town at the western end of Bogue Banks, part of North Carolina's Crystal Coast. It is not a town of grand monuments or dense old streets. Its appeal is simpler: long Atlantic beach, Bogue Sound marshes, a fishing pier, and enough room to spend the day outside.

This is still a resort strip, so the bar should be honest. If you want museums, architecture, or nightlife, go elsewhere on the coast. If you want to walk to [The Point](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/emerald_isle/the_point), fish from [Bogue Inlet Pier](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/emerald_isle/bogue_inlet_pier), bike the island path, or slip into [Emerald Isle Woods Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/emerald_isle/emerald_isle_woods_park) for a soundside change of scene, it earns a spare day.

The town is best in the shoulder seasons, when the beach is still usable but the rental-house traffic has thinned. Summer works if you are here for family beach time and do not expect solitude.
