Emerald Isle's bike path is not dramatic, but it changes how the town works. Instead of treating the island as a line of beach accesses, you can move at an easy pace between rental houses, shops, parks, and the pier.

The route is flat and practical rather than scenic all the way through. That makes it most useful for families and shoulder-season visitors who want to leave the car parked for a while.
