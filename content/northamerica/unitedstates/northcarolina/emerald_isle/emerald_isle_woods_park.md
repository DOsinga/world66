Emerald Isle Woods Park is the easiest way to trade ocean glare for maritime forest and soundside marsh. The trails are short, but they add real variety to a beach day, with birding, disc golf, picnic spots, and views across Bogue Sound.

It is especially useful with children or anyone who needs a break from sun and surf. Go in the morning if you want the quietest walk.
