---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Emerald Isle's attractions are mostly open-air and informal. The beach is the spine of the visit, but the western point, the pier, and the soundside woods give it more shape than a generic row of rental houses.

Bring a bike if you can. The island is flat, and moving slowly between ocean, pier, and sound is a better fit than driving from parking lot to parking lot.
