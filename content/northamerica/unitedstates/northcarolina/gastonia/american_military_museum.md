The American Military Museum on West Second Avenue is a volunteer-run collection focused on the military history of Gaston County and North Carolina, with artifacts spanning from the Civil War through the conflicts of the 20th century. The collection emphasizes the personal experiences of soldiers rather than grand strategy — uniforms, letters, photographs, equipment, and medals from local men and women who served in every major American conflict.

It is a modest operation by national standards, but the personal scale and the depth of the local focus make it surprisingly affecting. The volunteers are knowledgeable and enthusiastic, and the museum functions as much as a community memorial as a historical exhibition.
