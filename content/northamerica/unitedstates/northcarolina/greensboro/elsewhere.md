A genuinely odd and wonderful place. Elsewhere is housed in a former thrift store on South Elm Street stuffed to the rafters with the lifelong hoard of its founder's grandmother — fabrics, toys, books, dolls, beads, jars of buttons. Visiting artists are given residencies in the building on the strict rule that nothing leaves and nothing is added; they can only reuse what is there. The result is a constantly-changing installation that has been called a "living museum."

Open for tours and events, generally Friday through Sunday. Check the website for current hours — it varies with the residency cycle. Found via Atlas Obscura.
