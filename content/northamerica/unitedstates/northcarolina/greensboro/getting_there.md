---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Piedmont Triad International Airport (GSO) sits about 15 km west of downtown Greensboro and serves the wider Triad — Greensboro, Winston-Salem, and High Point. Direct flights connect to the major US hubs (Atlanta, Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas, New York). International travellers will usually connect through Atlanta or Charlotte.

Amtrak's Crescent line stops at the J. Douglas Galyon Depot downtown, with services to New York, Washington, Charlotte, and New Orleans. The Piedmont and Carolinian services run several times daily to Charlotte and Raleigh.

By road, Greensboro is at the junction of Interstates 40, 85, and 73, making it a natural pause on any North Carolina road trip.
