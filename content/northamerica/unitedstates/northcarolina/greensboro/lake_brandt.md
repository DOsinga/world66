Lake Brandt is one of Greensboro's main outdoor recreation areas, with wooded shoreline, trails, and access for paddling on the reservoir. It gives visitors a practical way to get outside without leaving the city region for a full day trip.

The lake is best for travellers who want walking, kayaking, cycling, or a quiet break from downtown and museum stops. It pairs naturally with nearby greenways and the other north Greensboro lakes.
