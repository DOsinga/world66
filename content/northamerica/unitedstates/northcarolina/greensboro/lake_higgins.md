Lake Higgins is one of Greensboro's northern reservoir parks, a relaxed outdoor stop for paddling, fishing, and short walks. It feels more local than iconic, but the water, trees, and easy access make it useful when you want a low-key break from downtown and campus sights.

Boat rentals and fishing access are the main draws, with trails around the park adding enough for a half-day in good weather. Check city park hours and rental availability before going, especially outside the warmer months.
