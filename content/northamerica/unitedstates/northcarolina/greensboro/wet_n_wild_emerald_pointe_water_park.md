Wet'n Wild Emerald Pointe is a seasonal water park south of Greensboro, built for hot Piedmont summer days. Slides, wave pools, and family areas make it a straightforward entertainment stop rather than a cultural sight.

It earns a place because Greensboro can be very hot and humid, and the park is a real regional draw for families. Check operating dates, ticket rules, and weather before going. Outside summer, this is not relevant to most travelers; in July, it may be exactly what children want.
