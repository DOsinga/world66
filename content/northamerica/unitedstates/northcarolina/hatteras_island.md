Hatteras Island is the destination for vacationers in search of surfing, fishing, camping, birdwatching, beachcombing, or simply watching the sunset with family and friends. Part of the [Outer Banks](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/outerbanks), this long, narrow island is wilder and less developed than the northern beaches, with small villages separated by stretches of open dune and marsh. Almost the entire island lies within the [Cape Hatteras National Seashore](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/hatteras_island/cape_hatteras_national_seashore), which has protected it from large-scale development since 1937.

The [Cape Hatteras Lighthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/hatteras_island/cape_hatteras_lighthouse), at 198 feet, is the tallest brick lighthouse in the United States. You can climb to the top in season for views that stretch across the island from ocean to sound. The fishing — both surf casting and offshore charter trips — is some of the best on the East Coast, and the consistent wind and waves make Hatteras one of the premier kiteboarding and windsurfing spots in North America.

The island's history is entwined with shipwrecks and the sea. The waters off Cape Hatteras, where the Gulf Stream collides with the cold Labrador Current over the shallow Diamond Shoals, have claimed over 600 vessels since the 16th century. The [Graveyard of the Atlantic Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/hatteras_island/graveyard_of_the_atlantic_museum) in Hatteras Village tells this story for free. At the northern end of the island, the [Chicamacomico Life-Saving Station](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/hatteras_island/chicamacomico_life_saving_station) in Rodanthe preserves the stations from which brave crews rowed out in surfboats to rescue shipwrecked sailors. The [Pea Island National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/hatteras_island/pea_island_national_wildlife_refuge), at the island's northern end, is one of the best birdwatching sites on the entire Atlantic coast.
