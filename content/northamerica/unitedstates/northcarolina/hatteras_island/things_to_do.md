title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Hatteras Island packs a remarkable range of experiences into a narrow strip of sand between the Atlantic and Pamlico Sound. The Cape Hatteras National Seashore shapes almost everything: miles of undeveloped beach, wild dunes, historic lighthouses, and a maritime heritage tied to centuries of shipwrecks. Water sports dominate in summer, birdwatching rules in spring and autumn, and fishing is good year-round.
