Flat Rock Playhouse grew from the Vagabond Players and has been staging theatre in Flat Rock since the early 1950s. Its programme changes through the season, usually mixing musicals, comedies, concerts, and regional productions.

Check the calendar before building a day around it. With a good show, it gives Hendersonville and Flat Rock a cultural anchor that most small mountain towns lack.
