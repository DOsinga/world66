The Mineral and Lapidary Museum is a compact Main Street stop devoted to minerals, gems, fossils, and local geology. It is the sort of small museum that works best as part of a downtown wander rather than as the reason for the trip.

The appeal is practical and tactile: visitors can look closely at regional specimens and, when available, crack open a geode. It is especially good if weather cuts short a mountain overlook or orchard visit.
