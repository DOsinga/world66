Hickory Furniture Mart is not subtle, but it explains a lot about the city. Hickory built much of its reputation on furniture manufacturing, and this huge marketplace keeps that identity visible to travellers.

Come only if you care about furniture or design shopping. As a travel sight, its value is in how directly it connects the modern city to the industry that shaped it.
