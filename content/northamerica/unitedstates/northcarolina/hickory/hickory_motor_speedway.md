Hickory Motor Speedway is the city's loudest piece of heritage. The short track opened in 1951 and is closely tied to the early stock-car scene that fed NASCAR.

It matters most when there is racing on. Check the schedule, bring ear protection if you are sensitive to noise, and expect a local night out rather than a polished arena event.
