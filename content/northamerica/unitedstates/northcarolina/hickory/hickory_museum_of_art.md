Hickory Museum of Art is the city's best cultural anchor. Founded in 1944, it focuses on American art and has a strong collection of Southern contemporary folk art, including work by self-taught artists from the region.

It is part of Hickory's SALT Block, where science, art, and performance share a campus. Check current visiting details before going, as renovation work can affect gallery locations.
