Kinston sits roughly halfway between [Raleigh](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/raleigh) and the coast on Highway 70. This small eastern North Carolina town has gained a reputation that far exceeds its size, largely thanks to [Chef and the Farmer](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/kinston/chef_and_the_farmer), a nationally acclaimed restaurant on Queen Street that put Kinston on the food map. The restaurant and the surrounding downtown revival were the subject of the PBS series *A Chef's Life*.

Beyond the food, Kinston has a growing arts scene, a respected craft brewery in [Mother Earth Brewing](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/kinston/mother_earth_brewing), and the [CSS Neuse Civil War Interpretive Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/kinston/css_neuse), which houses the remains of a Confederate ironclad gunboat recovered from the Neuse River. A full-scale [replica of the ironclad](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/kinston/css_neuse_ii) sits a few blocks away — the only such full-scale walk-aboard replica anywhere. The city also has a notable African American music heritage, with members of Parliament-Funkadelic and Maceo Parker among the local sons.
