Chef and the Farmer is the restaurant that put Kinston on the national food map. Vivian Howard and Ben Knight built it around the produce and meat of eastern North Carolina farms — pole beans, butterbeans, collards, fresh sausage, slow-cooked pork — treated with the technique of a fine-dining kitchen. The PBS series A Chef's Life turned it into a destination.

The dining room is in a converted warehouse on Queen Street. Reservations are essential; the menu rotates with the seasons and what the farmers brought in that week. There have been periodic closures and reopenings over the years — call ahead. Even if Chef and the Farmer is not serving when you visit, the surrounding stretch of downtown owes much of its revival to the restaurant.
