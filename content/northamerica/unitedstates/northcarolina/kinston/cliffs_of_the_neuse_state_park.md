Cliffs of the Neuse State Park protects one of eastern North Carolina's more dramatic river landscapes: high sandy bluffs dropping toward a bend in the Neuse River. Short trails lead through pine woods and hardwood forest to overlooks, with enough variety for an easy half-day outdoors.

The park also has a visitor centre, swimming lake, picnic areas, and camping. It is a better nature stop than the flat farmland around Kinston might lead you to expect, especially in spring or autumn.
