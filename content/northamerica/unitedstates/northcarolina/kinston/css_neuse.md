The CSS Neuse Civil War Interpretive Center is built around the raised hull of a Confederate ironclad gunboat, scuttled in 1865 and recovered from the Neuse River in 1963. The charred remains of the 158-foot vessel sit inside a custom climate-controlled building — one of very few Civil War ironclads anywhere that survive in this kind of detail.

Exhibits walk through the ship's improbable construction, her short operational life, and the post-war recovery effort. A separate site nearby, the CSS Neuse II, hosts a full-scale replica that you can walk on the deck of — built by a local volunteer over two decades. Both are managed under North Carolina Historic Sites; admission is modest.
