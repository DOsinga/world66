---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eastern North Carolina barbecue — whole-hog, vinegar-sauced, served with hush puppies and slaw — is the regional staple, and Kinston has several options. Chef and the Farmer is the famous one; for a more everyday meal, try King's Restaurant on US-70 for the local barbecue tradition, or one of the smaller spots along Queen Street.
