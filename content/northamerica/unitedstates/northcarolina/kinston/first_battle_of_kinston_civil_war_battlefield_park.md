First Battle of Kinston Civil War Battlefield Park preserves ground associated with the December 1862 fighting around Kinston. The site is modest, but it helps explain why this small eastern North Carolina city appears in Civil War itineraries.

It works best for travellers already interested in military history or pairing it with the CSS Neuse sites in town. Expect interpretation and landscape more than a large museum experience.
