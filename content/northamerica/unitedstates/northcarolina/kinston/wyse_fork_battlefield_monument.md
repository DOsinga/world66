The Wyse Fork Battlefield Monument marks an important Civil War battlefield near Kinston, where fighting in March 1865 formed part of the Carolinas Campaign. It is a modest site, but meaningful for travelers following Civil War history in eastern North Carolina.

The monument works best when paired with Kinston's CSS Neuse Civil War Interpretive Center. Together they give a clearer picture of the area's wartime role than either stop alone.
