The North Carolina Aquarium at Fort Fisher is the dependable rainy-day and too-much-sun stop on this part of the coast. Its exhibits focus on local marine life, freshwater habitats, reptiles, rays, sea turtles, and the wider ecology of the Cape Fear region.

Check current opening details before building a day around it, since the aquarium has been going through a large renovation and expansion. When open, it pairs naturally with the Fort Fisher historic site and recreation area.
