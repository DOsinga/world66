A two-storey log house built in 1793 by John Carson, one of the first white settlers in this part of the North Carolina mountains. The original logs are visible from inside the additions tacked on over the next century. Now run as a museum of McDowell County history, with rooms furnished to different periods and a small barn outside displaying farm implements.

Open weekends and by appointment. About five miles west of central Marion on the old Pleasant Gardens road.
