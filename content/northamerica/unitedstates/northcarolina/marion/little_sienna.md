A well-regarded Italian restaurant on US 70 in Pleasant Gardens, halfway between Marion and Old Fort. The menu runs to American-Italian standards — baked pastas, parmigianas, calzones — done well, with a daily lunch buffet that draws a regular crowd from both towns.

North Carolina's brown-bag rule applies, so customers bring their own wine; no corkage charge. Cash and cards both accepted.
