Ocean Isle Beach is a quiet, unspoiled family beach on the southern North Carolina coast, just north of [Myrtle Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/myrtlebeach). The island has managed to avoid the heavy commercial development that defines its neighbours, making it a good choice for families looking for a peaceful week by the water.

The beach itself is wide and uncrowded, with gentle surf suitable for younger swimmers. Most visitors rent a house for the week and settle into the rhythm of the island — mornings on the sand, afternoons fishing off the [Ocean Isle Pier](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/ocean_isle_pier), evenings on the porch. There are a handful of restaurants and shops along the causeway and the main beach road, but this is not a place you come to for nightlife.

The waters around the island offer good fishing, kayaking, and paddleboarding. [Bird Island Reserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/bird_island_reserve), at the western tip of the beach, is a protected sanctuary accessible only on foot — one of the last undeveloped barrier islands in North Carolina and worth the walk.
