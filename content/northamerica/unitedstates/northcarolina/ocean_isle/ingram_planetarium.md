Ingram Planetarium is a small coastal planetarium near Sunset Beach, useful on a rainy afternoon or when children need something beyond sand and shells. Shows cover astronomy and space science, with a modest science hall attached.

It is not a big-city science museum, but it fills a real niche on this stretch of the North Carolina coast. Check the daily show schedule before driving over, since programming changes by season.
