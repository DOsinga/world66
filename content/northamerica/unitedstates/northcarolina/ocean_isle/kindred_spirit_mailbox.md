The Kindred Spirit Mailbox is a wooden mailbox mounted on a post near the western end of Bird Island, reachable only after a 1.5-mile walk along the beach from the Ocean Isle Beach access. Inside are notebooks, pens, and decades of entries from visitors — poems, confessions, love letters, grief, and gratitude written by strangers who made the walk.

The tradition started in 1983 and has continued without any formal organisation. There is no website, no social media presence, and no monument. The mailbox simply stands at the edge of a protected island where no cars can go, and people who find it leave something of themselves behind. It is one of those genuinely unusual places that resists being turned into a tourist attraction, partly because getting there requires effort.
