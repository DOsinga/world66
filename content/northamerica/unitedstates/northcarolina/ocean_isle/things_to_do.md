---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ocean Isle Beach is best enjoyed simply — sun, water, and the particular pleasure of a beach that hasn't been overdeveloped. The [Ocean Isle Pier](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/ocean_isle_pier) is the social hub of the island, a good spot for fishing or just watching pelicans work the surf. At the west end of the island, [Bird Island Reserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/bird_island_reserve) — accessible only on foot — offers an experience of the North Carolina coast as it used to look everywhere. The [Kindred Spirit Mailbox](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/kindred_spirit_mailbox) on Bird Island is a well-known local tradition. Kayak and paddleboard rentals are available from several outfitters on the island, and the calm waters of the Intracoastal Waterway on the back side of the island are ideal for beginners.
