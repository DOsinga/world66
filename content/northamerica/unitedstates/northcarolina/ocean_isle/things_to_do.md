---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ocean Isle Beach is best enjoyed simply — sun, water, and the particular pleasure of a beach that hasn't been overdeveloped. The [Ocean Isle Pier](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/ocean_isle_pier) is the social hub of the island, a good spot for fishing or just watching pelicans work the surf. At the west end of the island, [Bird Island Reserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/bird_island_reserve) — accessible only on foot — offers an experience of the North Carolina coast as it used to look everywhere. The [Kindred Spirit Mailbox](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/kindred_spirit_mailbox) on Bird Island is a well-known local tradition.

The [Intracoastal Waterway](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/intracoastal_waterway) on the back side of the island is ideal for kayaking and paddleboarding — calm water, good wildlife, and regular dolphin sightings. The [Museum of Coastal Carolina](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/museum_of_coastal_carolina) is worth an hour, especially for families with young children. The neighbouring island of [Sunset Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/ocean_isle/sunset_beach) to the west is a pleasant half-day excursion — even quieter, with tidal flats and the western edge of the same undeveloped barrier island chain.
