The bar at Howard's Pub is the social centre of the island after dark. It stocks a decent selection of local and regional beers and keeps serving well past the time everything else has closed. The outdoor deck is ideal on a clear evening — cooler than inside and with enough breeze to keep the insects manageable. During storms and off-season weekends, this is where the island's year-round residents end up.
