Ocracoke Coffee Co. is the village's morning gathering point — a small cafe in the heart of the village where cyclists stop before heading up the island and ferry passengers wait out the gap between arrivals. The coffee is good and the atmosphere is easy, with indoor and outdoor seating and a friendly counter operation. They also serve pastries and light breakfast items. It fills up quickly on summer mornings, but turnover is fast.
