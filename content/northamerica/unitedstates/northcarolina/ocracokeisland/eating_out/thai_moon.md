Thai Moon has been a fixture on the island for years and regularly surprises first-time visitors who aren't expecting to find genuine Thai cooking on an isolated barrier island. The menu covers the familiar range — curries, noodles, stir-fries — and does them well, with real heat available on request. The dining room is small and the service friendly. It is consistently one of the most popular tables on the island, so book ahead in season or arrive early.
