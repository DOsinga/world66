Springer's Point is a nature preserve at the southern tip of Ocracoke village, owned by the North Carolina Coastal Land Trust. A short trail winds through ancient live oaks and cedar forest to the water's edge — the same inlet, Teach's Hole Channel, where Blackbeard was killed in 1718. The preserve is quiet and easy to miss, but the setting has barely changed in three centuries. The trail takes about 20 minutes to walk. In the village itself, plaques and markers at several spots tell the broader story of Blackbeard's career and his connection to the island.
