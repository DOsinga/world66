Old Fort is a small mountain town in [McDowell County](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/mcdowell_county), sitting at the eastern gateway to the Blue Ridge. It is a modest place — a few hundred people, a main street, a train depot — but it has an easy, unpretentious character and more to offer than its size suggests.

[Andrew's Geyser](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/old_fort/andrews_geyser), a few minutes from the center of town, is a curiously delightful sight: a fountain created in the 1880s to honor the railroad engineer who conquered the Swannanoa Gap. The [Old Fort Depot and Railroad Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/old_fort/old_fort_depot) houses exhibits on the heroic tunnel-building that finally broke through the Blue Ridge, and the [Mountain Gateway Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/old_fort/mountain_gateway_museum) down the street digs into Appalachian history with genuine care. For local craft, **Talis Gallery** in the Appalachian Artisan Society building sells eclectic art, beads, and photography, and **Turtle Creek Pottery** is a short drive up Bat Cave Road.

[Davidson's Fort Historic Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/old_fort/davidsons_fort), a reconstruction of a pioneer-era stockade, hosts an annual living history event in early summer. On Friday evenings, [Old Fort Mountain Music](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/old_fort/old_fort_mountain_music) fills a local venue with free country and old-time music — you can jam, dance, or just sit back and listen.
