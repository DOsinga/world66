Bodie Island Lighthouse is the black-and-white striped lighthouse south of Nags Head, standing between the Atlantic and Roanoke Sound. The current tower was built in 1872, after two earlier lighthouses in the area were lost.

The setting is part of the appeal. Boardwalks lead through marsh and open water views, and when climbs are operating the tower gives a wide look across the Outer Banks.
