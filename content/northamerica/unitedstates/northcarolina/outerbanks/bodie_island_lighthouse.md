Bodie Island Lighthouse is the black-and-white striped 1872 lighthouse on the Roanoke Sound side of the Outer Banks. The tower is a classic Cape Hatteras National Seashore landmark, and when open for climbing it gives broad views over marsh, sound, and barrier island.
