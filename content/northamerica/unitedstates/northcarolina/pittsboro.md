*North America > United States > North Carolina > Pittsboro*

Pittsboro is a small Chatham County town west of Chapel Hill, with a courthouse square, old brick storefronts, and enough odd local anchors to justify more than a fuel stop. It is close to the Research Triangle, but it feels slower and more rural, with the Haw River cutting through woods just north of town.

The standout is [Carolina Tiger Rescue](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/pittsboro/carolina_tiger_rescue), where guided visits focus on rescued big cats rather than spectacle. Downtown, the [Chatham County Historic Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/pittsboro/chatham_county_historic_courthouse) gives the town its centre, and nearby Bynum adds street art, a pedestrian bridge, and access to the [Lower Haw River Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/pittsboro/lower_haw_river_trail).

Pittsboro is not a major destination, and that is part of the appeal. If you are already in Chapel Hill, Carrboro, or Raleigh and want a low-key day with walking, history, pottery, and a proper small-town lunch, it clears the bar.
