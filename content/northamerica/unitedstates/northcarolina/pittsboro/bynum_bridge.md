Bynum Bridge is an old road bridge turned pedestrian crossing over the Haw River. Its railings and deck have become an informal gallery of graffiti, local messages, and changing art.

The bridge pairs well with a walk along the Lower Haw River Trail. Bynum itself is tiny, but the bridge gives it a clear personality.
