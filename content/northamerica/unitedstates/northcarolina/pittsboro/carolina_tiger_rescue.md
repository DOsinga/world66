Carolina Tiger Rescue is a serious sanctuary for big cats rescued from private ownership, roadside zoos, and poor conditions. Visits are by guided tour, with an emphasis on the animals' stories and the problems created by captive exotic pet ownership.

Book ahead rather than assuming you can wander in. This is the strongest single reason to make Pittsboro a day trip.
