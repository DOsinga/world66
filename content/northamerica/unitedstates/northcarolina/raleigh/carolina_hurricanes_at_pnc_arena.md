A Carolina Hurricanes game is Raleigh's biggest professional sports night out, with PNC Arena pulling fans from across the Triangle. The hockey itself is the draw, but the tailgating, red jerseys, and loud indoor atmosphere are what make it feel locally specific.

This is obviously seasonal and ticket-dependent, so plan around the NHL schedule. If you are in town during a home stand, it is a lively contrast to Raleigh's museums, parks, and government buildings.
