The City of Raleigh Museum is a local history museum in downtown Raleigh. Its exhibits introduce the city's growth, civic life, and neighbourhood stories, making it a useful first stop before visiting the larger state museums nearby.

It is small enough to fit into a downtown walk, especially if you want context for the City of Oaks rather than another broad North Carolina overview.
