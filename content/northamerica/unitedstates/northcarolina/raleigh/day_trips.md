---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Durham and Chapel Hill are the closest day trips — both part of the Research Triangle and each worth a half-day. Durham's American Tobacco Campus and the Nasher Museum of Art at Duke University are good targets. [Chapel Hill](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/chapelhill) has the University of North Carolina's campus and a walkable downtown.

Further afield, the [Outer Banks](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/outerbanks) are about three hours east and worth a dedicated trip for the barrier islands, lighthouses, and the Wright Brothers National Memorial at Kitty Hawk. The [Smoky Mountains](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/smokymountains) are four hours west.
