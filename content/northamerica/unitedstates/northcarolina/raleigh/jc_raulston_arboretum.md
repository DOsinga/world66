JC Raulston Arboretum is an eight-acre botanical garden run by North Carolina State University. Its compact grounds hold thousands of plant varieties, with display beds, trial gardens, and shaded paths that are easy to explore in under an hour. It is a good Raleigh stop for gardeners, plant people, or anyone pairing it with the nearby North Carolina Museum of Art.
