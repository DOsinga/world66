Lake Crabtree County Park is a wooded lake park on Raleigh's western edge, with short hiking loops, mountain-bike trails, and a longer route around the water. It is a good choice when you want a practical outdoor break near the airport and Research Triangle Park rather than a full day in the countryside.

The park is especially useful for walking, cycling, and birding, with enough trail variety to make repeat visits worthwhile.
