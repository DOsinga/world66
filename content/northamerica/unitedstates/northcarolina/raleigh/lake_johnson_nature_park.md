Lake Johnson Nature Park is one of Raleigh's easiest outdoor escapes, with wooded trails looping around the lake and places to get onto the water in season. The eastern loop is the classic walk, while the quieter dirt paths on the far side feel more like a local nature break.

It works well for a run, a short hike, or a paddle when you want something green without leaving the city.
