The Martin Marietta Center for the Performing Arts is Raleigh's main downtown stage complex, hosting Broadway tours, symphony concerts, ballet, theatre and large civic performances. Its several venues make it more of an arts campus than a single theatre.

It is most useful to travellers when something is on the calendar, so check listings before you go. A performance here also gives an easy reason to spend an evening downtown, with restaurants and bars within walking distance.
