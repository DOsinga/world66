The North Carolina Museum of Art, a short drive west of downtown, houses an impressive permanent collection spanning 5,000 years of art history across Egyptian antiquities, European Old Masters, American paintings, and contemporary works. Admission to the permanent collection is free; special exhibitions charge separately. The 164-acre museum park surrounding the building is as much an attraction as the galleries — five miles of trails connect monumental outdoor sculptures in a parkland setting that attracts as many walkers and cyclists as art visitors.

The museum shop is unusually good, and the museum café and restaurant serve food worth eating. Plan an easy half-day here: an hour in the galleries, a walk through the sculpture park, lunch.
