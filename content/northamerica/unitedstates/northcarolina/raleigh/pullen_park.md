Pullen Park is one of Raleigh's classic family stops and among the oldest public parks in North Carolina. Its carousel, miniature train, pedal boats, playgrounds, and lawns make it feel like a civic pleasure ground rather than just another neighbourhood park.

It sits close to downtown and North Carolina State University, so it is easy to fold into a city day. Families will get the most from it, but anyone can appreciate the shade, space, and old-fashioned park atmosphere.
