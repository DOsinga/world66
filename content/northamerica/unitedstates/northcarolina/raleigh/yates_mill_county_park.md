Yates Mill County Park protects a restored water-powered grist mill, a pond, and walking trails a short drive south of downtown Raleigh. The mill is the last of its kind in Wake County and is the reason to choose this park over a generic green space.

Weekend guided tours usually give the best look at the mill and its machinery. The surrounding trails and visitor center make it a useful family stop, with enough history and outdoor time to justify a deliberate visit.
