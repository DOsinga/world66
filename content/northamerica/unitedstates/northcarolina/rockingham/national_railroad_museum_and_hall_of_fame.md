The National Railroad Museum and Hall of Fame covers the rail history of the Rockingham and Hamlet area, with photographs, model trains, and Seaboard Air Line memorabilia. It is small, but the subject matters here: the railroad shaped this part of North Carolina.

It is most worthwhile for rail enthusiasts or travellers already exploring Rockingham's downtown and nearby historic sites.
