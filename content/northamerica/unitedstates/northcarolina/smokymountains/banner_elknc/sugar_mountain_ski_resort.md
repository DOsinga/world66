Sugar Mountain is the largest ski resort in the Southeast, with 125 acres of skiable terrain and a 1,200-foot vertical drop. It sits at an elevation of 5,300 feet on the flanks of Sugar Mountain, which keeps it reliably cold enough for snowmaking when natural snow is thin — a common situation in the southern Appalachians. The resort has 20 slopes and trails, a terrain park, and a tubing park, plus ice skating on a separate rink.

In summer, Sugar Mountain shifts into a different gear. Mountain biking trails open up, and the gondola runs for sightseers who want panoramic views across the high country without the effort of hiking. The village at the base has a cluster of restaurants and shops that stay busy year-round.
