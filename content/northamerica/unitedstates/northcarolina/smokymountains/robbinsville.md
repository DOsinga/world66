Robbinsville is the county seat of Graham County, tucked into one of the most remote and scenic corners of the Smoky Mountains. This is genuine mountain country — untouched forests, quiet lakes, and deep valleys at prices that are surprisingly affordable compared to the more developed mountain towns.

[Lake Santeetlah](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/smokymountains/robbinsville/lake_santeetlah) and [Fontana Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/smokymountains/robbinsville/fontana_lake) offer boating, fishing, and kayaking in settings that feel far from civilisation. The [Cherohala Skyway](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/smokymountains/robbinsville/cherohala_skyway), a scenic highway running from Robbinsville into Tennessee, rivals the Blue Ridge Parkway for mountain views but sees a fraction of the traffic.

The [Joyce Kilmer Memorial Forest](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/smokymountains/robbinsville/joyce_kilmer_memorial_forest) preserves one of the last old-growth temperate forests in the eastern United States — trees up to 400 years old, never logged. The [Cheoah Dam](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/smokymountains/robbinsville/cheoah_dam), made briefly famous by the film The Fugitive, stands at the head of a whitewater gorge that kayakers consider one of the finest runs in the southeast.
