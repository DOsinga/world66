The Swansboro Area Heritage Center is a small local-history museum run by the town's historical association. It is useful before or after a walk through the historic district, especially if you want names and stories for the old port, fishing village, and riverfront.

Hours are limited, so check before making a special trip. If it is closed, the historic district and waterfront still carry most of the town's appeal.
