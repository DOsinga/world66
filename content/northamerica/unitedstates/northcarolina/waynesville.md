Waynesville is a mountain town just west of [Asheville](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/asheville), sitting at 2,700 feet in the Southern Appalachians where the Blue Ridge meets the Great Smokies. The [downtown Main Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/waynesville/main_street_downtown) is lined with independent shops, galleries, and restaurants that reward a slow afternoon of browsing — one of the more intact small-town commercial streets in the region.

The town's biggest event is [Folkmoot USA](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/waynesville/folkmoot), an international folk festival held each July that brings dance and music troupes from a dozen countries to perform in the mountain communities of Haywood County. It is one of the most remarkable cultural celebrations in the region, and worth timing a visit around if you can.

The [Frog Level Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/waynesville/frog_level) along Richland Creek has been reborn as a brewery and restaurant quarter with outdoor seating and a greenway walk. The [Museum of North Carolina Handicrafts](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/waynesville/museum_of_north_carolina_handicrafts) in the Shelton House documents the Appalachian craft traditions that still define the region. Waynesville makes a good base for exploring the [Blue Ridge Parkway](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/waynesville/blue_ridge_parkway) and [Great Smoky Mountains](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/smokymountains) National Park, both accessible within 30 minutes.
