Cameron Art Museum is Wilmington’s strongest art stop, set a little outside the historic riverfront on grounds that mix galleries, sculpture, and local memory. The programme balances regional artists, travelling exhibitions, and community events, so it feels tied to coastal North Carolina rather than dropped in from somewhere else.

The museum also sits on land connected to the Battle of Forks Road, and its outdoor interpretation adds a Civil War and United States Colored Troops layer to the visit. Come when the exhibitions appeal, then leave time for the grounds and cafe; it is a better half-day than its modest size suggests.
