The Cape Fear Serpentarium is a private reptile facility housing one of the most diverse collections of venomous snakes in the United States. In a downtown Wilmington storefront, Gaboon vipers, black mambas, king cobras, and a remarkable array of other species live in custom-built enclosures that allow close viewing. The collection is unusual enough to draw herpetologists from across the country.

This is emphatically not a sanitised zoo experience. The animals are kept in a naturalistic, somewhat menacing setting that makes the visit feel more like entering a serious collector's private world than a mainstream attraction. Children who are fascinated by reptiles will find it extraordinary; those easily frightened might not. The owner's deep expertise comes through in every enclosure, and for anyone interested in venomous snakes, it is simply one of the best places in the country to see them.
