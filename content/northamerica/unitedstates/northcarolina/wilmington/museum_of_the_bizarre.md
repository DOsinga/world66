Museum of the Bizarre is a small oddities museum in Wilmington, built around curios, sideshow-style objects, movie-prop energy, and the city's taste for ghost stories. It is not a conventional museum, and that is the point.

Go when you want a quick, strange stop between the riverfront and historic district, not when you need a polished cultural institution. Its value depends heavily on your appetite for the weird and theatrical.
