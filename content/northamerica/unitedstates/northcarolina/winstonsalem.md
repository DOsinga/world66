Winston-Salem is the fifth-largest city in North Carolina and one of the most historically interesting. Moravians settled here in the 1700s, establishing what became Salem — they founded the first women's school in the United States ([Salem Academy](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/salem_college)) and later Salem College, both still in operation. The tobacco industry made the city wealthy through R.J. Reynolds, and the textile tradition lives on through Hanes and the hosiery business.

[Old Salem](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/old_salem) is the essential stop — a restored Moravian settlement where costumed interpreters demonstrate eighteenth-century crafts and daily life. Do not miss the [Winkler Bakery](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/winkler_bakery) for sugar cake and lovefeast buns, recipes that have not changed in centuries. The attached [MESDA](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/mesda) holds the country's only major collection of early southern decorative arts, and the Moravian cemetery [God's Acre](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/gods_acre) is unlike any other graveyard in America.

Downtown is dominated by the Art Deco [Reynolds Building](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/reynolds_building), the prototype for the Empire State Building, with the [Stevens Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/stevens_center) hosting the city's professional-level performances. The [Innovation Quarter](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/innovation_quarter) — built into the former Reynolds tobacco factories — is where the new restaurants, bars and breweries cluster. West of the centre, the [Reynolda House Museum of American Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/reynolda_house) houses an outstanding collection in the original R.J. Reynolds country estate, and the contemporary [SECCA](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/secca) and the Norman-revival [Graylyn Estate](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/graylyn_estate) are both worth half a day each.

[Tanglewood Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/tanglewood_park) nearby is a pleasant day out, with a lake, golf courses, an equestrian center, and bike trails spread across the former estate of the Reynolds family. For roadside Americana, the [last Shell clamshell station](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/last_shell_station) and the [original Krispy Kreme site](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/krispy_kreme_original) are quick stops on the way out of town.
