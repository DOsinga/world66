---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The craft-beer scene is genuinely strong here. **Foothills Brewing** on West Fourth Street is the local institution — their Hoppyum IPA put Winston-Salem on the southern beer map. **Wise Man Brewing**, in a converted Innovation Quarter warehouse, runs the most interesting taproom downtown. **Incendiary Brewing**, also in the Innovation Quarter, leans into Belgian and farmhouse styles.

For coffee, **Camino Bakery** and **Krankies Coffee** are the two reliable choices; Krankies doubles as a live-music venue in the evening. For cocktails, **The Honey Pot** on Trade Street does the most ambitious mixology in the city.
