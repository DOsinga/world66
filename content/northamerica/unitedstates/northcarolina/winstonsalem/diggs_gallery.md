Diggs Gallery is an art gallery at Winston-Salem State University with a strong focus on Africa and the African diaspora. It is named for James Thackeray "T" Diggs Jr., a painter and long-serving art professor at the university.

The gallery is a useful stop if you want a smaller cultural visit beyond Old Salem and the city's larger museums. Exhibitions are joined by occasional performances, workshops, film screenings, and talks.
