Winston-Salem's Downtown Arts District is a compact cluster of galleries, studios, small shops, and restaurants in the city center. It is more of a walkable creative area than a single attraction, but it gives visitors a good reason to explore downtown on foot.

Come when galleries are open or during district events, when the working studios and street life make the area feel livelier. It sits close to other downtown sights, so it is easy to combine with a broader Winston-Salem walk.
