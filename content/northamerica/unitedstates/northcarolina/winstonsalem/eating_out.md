---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Winston-Salem's food scene has grown alongside the Innovation Quarter and the downtown arts crowd. **The Tavern in Old Salem** is the traditional choice — slow-cooked pot roast and Moravian chicken pie served in an 18th-century stagecoach inn. Downtown, **Mozelle's** does inventive southern cooking with a strong vegetable side game, and **Sweet Potatoes** in the Arts District is the city's most-cited soul-food restaurant.

For barbecue, **Little Richard's** off Country Club Road serves textbook North Carolina pulled pork with a vinegar-based sauce. The downtown brunch crowd splits between **Mary's Gourmet Diner** (eccentric and beloved) and **Camino Bakery** (excellent pastries and the city's best espresso). Don't leave without a sugar cake from [Winkler Bakery](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/winkler_bakery) and a hot original from the Stratford Road [Krispy Kreme](/northamerica/unitedstates/northcarolina/winstonsalem/krispy_kreme_original).
