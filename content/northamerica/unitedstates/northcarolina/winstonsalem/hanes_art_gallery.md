The Charlotte and Philip Hanes Art Gallery is Wake Forest University's main exhibition gallery, with rotating shows in the Scales Fine Arts Center. It is worth checking when you are already visiting Reynolda or the Wake Forest area.

The draw is the changing exhibition program rather than a permanent collection, so check current hours and shows before making a special trip.
