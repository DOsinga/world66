The 22-storey Reynolds Building was the tallest skyscraper in the South when it opened in 1929 and is one of the great Art Deco towers in the United States. The lobby — bronze elevators, marble floors, a coffered ceiling — is open during business hours and worth the detour. The exterior, with its limestone setbacks and the stylised tobacco-leaf motif at the entrance, is the single best piece of architecture in downtown Winston-Salem.

The building has been converted to a hotel (the Kimpton Cardinal) so you can also have a drink in the basement bar, which preserves the original recreation room with bowling lanes and an indoor pool used by Reynolds Tobacco employees in the 1930s.
