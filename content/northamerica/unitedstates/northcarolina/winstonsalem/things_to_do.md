---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Winston-Salem is two cities folded into one: the carefully preserved 18th-century Moravian settlement of Old Salem, and the early 20th-century tobacco-and-textiles boomtown that built the downtown skyline. A full day in Old Salem plus an afternoon at Reynolda House covers most visitors' needs; arts-minded travellers will want to add the SECCA and the Stevens Center calendar.
