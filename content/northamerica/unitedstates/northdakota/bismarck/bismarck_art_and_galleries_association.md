The Bismarck Art and Galleries Association gives the North Dakota capital a compact, approachable art stop. Its exhibitions rotate through regional artists, juried shows, and community programs, which makes it a useful place to see what local painters, photographers, and craftspeople are working on now.

It is not a large museum, but that is part of the charm. Drop in between bigger Bismarck sights for a quieter look at the city's creative side, especially if the weather is pushing you indoors or you want something beyond state history.
