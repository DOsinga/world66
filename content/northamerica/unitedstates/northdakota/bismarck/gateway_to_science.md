Gateway to Science is Bismarck's hands-on science center, aimed especially at children and families. Exhibits are built around touching, testing, building, and asking questions rather than standing quietly in front of cases.

It is a practical stop on a family trip through North Dakota, particularly in bad weather or between longer drives. The museum also rounds out Bismarck's visitor mix, adding play and experimentation to the city's stronger state-history offerings.
