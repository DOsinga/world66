Grahams Island State Park sits on the lake west of town and is the easiest place to turn Devils Lake from scenery into a day outside. There are wooded campsites, hiking trails, a boat ramp, picnic areas and direct access to the fishing that makes the lake famous.

The park stays useful in winter too, with groomed cross-country ski trails and lake access for ice fishing.
