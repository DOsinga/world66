Fisherman's Dream fills the roadside with metal fish, a boat, and underwater figures, all wildly out of proportion to the dry prairie around them. The mismatch is the charm: you are in farm country, looking at a giant imaginary lake.

Stop long enough to walk around the pullout. The details are easier to enjoy outside the car than through a windshield.
