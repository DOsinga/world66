Geese in Flight is the northern gateway to the Enchanted Highway, standing just off Interstate 94 at the Gladstone exit. The sculpture is enormous: a metal sunburst and flock of geese rising above the prairie, made from oilfield scrap and visible before you have fully left the freeway.

It is the best single stop on the route and the easiest one to see if you only have a few minutes.
