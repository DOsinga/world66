Giant Grasshoppers is where the route starts to feel properly surreal. The metal insects seem to leap out of the fields, turning a quiet farm road into a cartoon-scale scene.

It is also a good reminder that the highway is less about polished public art than about local persistence, welding, and a stubborn refusal to let the plains be boring.
