Fargo Air Museum is one of Fargo’s better specialist museums, with a collection of historic, military, and sometimes flyable aircraft near the airport. The hangar setting keeps the focus on machines at full scale rather than display cases.

It works for aviation fans, families, and anyone looking for an indoor stop that is not another downtown storefront. The museum also gives Fargo a link to the wide-open regional geography where aviation has long mattered.
