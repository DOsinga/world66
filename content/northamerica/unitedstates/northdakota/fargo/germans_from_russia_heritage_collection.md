The Germans from Russia Heritage Collection is a small exhibit and research collection inside North Dakota State University's library. Photographs, documents, and artifacts trace the communities of German-speaking immigrants who came to the northern plains from Russia.

It is a specialist stop, but a useful one if you are trying to understand Fargo's wider regional history. Check access before going, since it follows campus library hours.
