Thunder Road Family Fun Park is a seasonal family attraction in Fargo with go-karts, bumper cars, laser tag, batting cages, mini golf, and arcade games. Most outdoor activities run from late spring through summer, with some indoor attractions opening earlier.

It is most useful for families looking for an easy break from museums and downtown sightseeing.
