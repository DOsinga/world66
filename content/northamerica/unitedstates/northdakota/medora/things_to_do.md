---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Medora's draws are split between the town itself — full of Marquis de Mores history and a summer-evening musical — and Theodore Roosevelt National Park, which begins at the southern edge of the streets. Allow a full day for the park's scenic loop and at least half a day to walk the town. The Burning Hills Amphitheatre's nightly Medora Musical is the town's social centrepiece in summer.
