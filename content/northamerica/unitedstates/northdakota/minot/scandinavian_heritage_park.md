Scandinavian Heritage Park is Minot's clearest expression of itself. The 14-acre park honours Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland and Iceland, with monuments, a visitor centre, a Danish windmill, a large Dala horse and a full-scale replica of a Norwegian stave church.

It could easily have become kitsch, but it works because Scandinavian settlement is central to northern North Dakota's identity. Give it an hour, more if you have family roots in the region or catch one of the cultural events.
