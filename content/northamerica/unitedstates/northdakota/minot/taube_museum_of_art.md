The Lillian and Coleman Taube Museum of Art is a small downtown gallery with rotating exhibitions, a permanent collection, and a useful focus on artists from North Dakota and the northern plains. It is not a huge museum, but it gives Minot a real art stop beyond the larger aviation and Scandinavian heritage sights.

The museum is easy to fold into a walk through central Minot. Check the current exhibitions before going, since the appeal depends heavily on what is on the walls.
