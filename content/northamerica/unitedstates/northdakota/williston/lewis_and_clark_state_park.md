Lewis and Clark State Park sits on Lake Sakakawea east of Williston, where prairie,
reservoir water, and big North Dakota sky meet. It is a straightforward outdoor stop,
good for boating, camping, swimming in season, and watching the light change over the
water.

The park is not a museum-heavy Lewis and Clark site despite the name; its value is
recreation and landscape. It makes sense for road-trippers who want a quiet break from
oil-patch towns and long highway distances.
