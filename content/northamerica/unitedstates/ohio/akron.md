Akron sits in the foothills of the Cuyahoga Valley about 40 miles south of [Cleveland](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/cleveland). Once the rubber capital of the world — Goodyear, Firestone, and B.F. Goodrich were all headquartered here — the city has reinvented itself around its university, a revitalised downtown, and a surprisingly good food scene. [Cuyahoga Valley National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/akron/cuyahoga_valley_national_park), which stretches north toward Cleveland, puts hiking trails, waterfalls, and the scenic Cuyahoga Valley Scenic Railroad within easy reach.

The downtown core along Main and Market streets has the [Akron Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/akron/akron_art_museum) (free admission, housed in a striking modern expansion), the restored [Akron Civic Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/akron/akron_civic_theatre), and the canal-side [Lock 3 Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/akron/lock_3_park). The rubber-era legacy is everywhere: [Stan Hywet Hall](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/akron/stan_hywet_hall), Goodyear founder F.A. Seiberling's Tudor Revival country house, is the city's grandest single sight; the [Goodyear Airdock](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/akron/goodyear_airdock) on the south side is one of the largest single-room buildings in the world and reportedly generates its own indoor weather. The [Highland Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/akron/highland_square) neighbourhood to the west has a more eclectic, independent feel.
