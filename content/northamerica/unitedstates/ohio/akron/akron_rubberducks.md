The Akron RubberDucks play minor-league baseball at Canal Park in downtown Akron, giving the city a relaxed, affordable summer evening ritual. The appeal is classic minor-league sport: close seats, between-innings silliness, local food, and a pace that welcomes families and casual fans.

Go when the team is at home and the weather is kind. Canal Park's downtown location makes it easy to combine with dinner or a drink before the game. It is not an architectural or historical sight, but it is one of the best ways to see Akron enjoying itself.
