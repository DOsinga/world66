The All-American Soap Box Derby is one of Akron's oddest and most likable claims to fame: a youth gravity-racing championship that has been tied to the city since the 1930s. The purpose-built Derby Downs track sits beside the Akron airport and still hosts races, rallies, and the championship week.

It is worth seeking out if you like Americana, amateur sport, or travelling with children who will understand the appeal immediately. Outside event days the track is a quick stop, so plan around the calendar if you want the place to feel alive.
