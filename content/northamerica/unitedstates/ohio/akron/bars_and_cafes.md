---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Akron's bar scene is unpretentious and concentrated in three areas: downtown (the new microbreweries and the bourbon bars), Highland Square (the indie / dive end), and the campus periphery on Exchange Street.

**Thirsty Dog Tap House** and **Aqueduct Brewing** anchor the downtown end — both make their own beer on site, both serve solid pub food, and both have outdoor seating in summer. **R. Shea Brewing** in Merriman Valley is worth the short drive for the canal-side patio.

**The Lockview** on South Main does grilled cheese sandwiches and a serious local-craft draft list — exactly the right place after an evening event at Lock 3 across the street. **The Office Tavern** in Highland Square is the after-hours bar for everyone leaving the closing restaurants. **Annabell's** on Market in Highland Square has live music most nights, ranging from local punk to jazz.

For coffee, **Akron Coffee Roasters** (downtown on Exchange and at Lock 3) and **Compass Coffee** (Northwest Akron) are the two reliably good local roasters with sit-down cafés.
