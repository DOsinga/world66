Cascade Valley Metro Park is one of Akron's best close-in green spaces, spread along wooded ravines and the Cuyahoga River corridor. It gives visitors a quick sense of the landscape that sits behind the city's industrial history: steep slopes, second-growth forest, river views, and trails that feel wilder than their urban location suggests.

The park works well when you do not have time for a full Cuyahoga Valley National Park outing. Choose a trail section, bring decent shoes after rain, and use it as a reminder that Akron's geography is more interesting than its freeway approaches imply.
