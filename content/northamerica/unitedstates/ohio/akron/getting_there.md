---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car**, Akron is at the junction of I-76, I-77, and the Ohio Turnpike (I-80), making it one of the better-connected cities in northeast Ohio. Cleveland is 40 minutes north on I-77, Columbus two hours south on I-71, Pittsburgh two hours east on I-76.

**By air**, the closest major airport is Cleveland Hopkins (CLE), about 45 minutes north — most travellers fly into Cleveland and rent a car or take the short shuttle. Akron-Canton Regional Airport (CAK), 30 minutes south, has a thinner schedule (mostly Delta, Spirit, and seasonal service) and is sometimes cheaper.

**By bus**, Greyhound and Megabus stop downtown at the South Main Street terminal, with services to Cleveland, Columbus, Pittsburgh, and Erie.

**By train**, there is no Amtrak service to Akron itself; the nearest stations are Cleveland and Alliance, both about 45 minutes away.
