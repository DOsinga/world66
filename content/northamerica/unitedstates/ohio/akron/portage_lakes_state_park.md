Portage Lakes State Park is Akron's easy escape to water, woods, and low-key outdoor recreation. The park spreads around a chain of lakes with boat launches, fishing spots, a beach, and short hiking trails through the surrounding green space.

It is more of a local day outdoors than a wilderness trip, but that is part of the appeal. Come for paddling, a swim in warm weather, or a simple lakeside picnic without driving far from the city.
