---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

**May to October** is the comfortable stretch. Daytime temperatures sit in the 20s Celsius (70s and 80s Fahrenheit), the Cuyahoga Valley trails are open, Lock 3 has its summer concert and food-festival schedule running, and the patios are out. June and September are arguably the best months — the heat is reliable but not yet oppressive, and the humidity has not quite arrived.

**Autumn (mid-September to late October)** is the photographer's season. The Cuyahoga Valley turns spectacularly: sugar maples, beeches, oaks, and hickories on every hillside, with the river canyon as the backdrop. The first weekend of October is generally peak colour.

**Winter** is grey, snowy, and cold. Lake-effect snow off Lake Erie pushes south to Akron occasionally; January averages -3°C (about 26°F). The upside: Brandywine Falls partly freezes into ice walls, downhill skiing is twenty minutes north at Boston Mills, and the city's holiday lights at Stan Hywet and Lock 3 are genuinely good.

**Spring** is wet and slow to warm. May, when the dogwoods bloom, is when the gardens reopen and the place comes alive again. Avoid April if you can — it tends to be the rainiest month.
