Ashtabula sits where the Ashtabula River reaches Lake Erie, closer in spirit to the old Great Lakes harbour towns than to Cleveland's suburbs. The city has a working-waterfront past, a small beach-and-park edge, and one of Ohio's better harbour districts.

The [lift bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/ashtabula/ashtabula_lift_bridge) is the visual anchor, rising over the river near Bridge Street. Nearby, the [Hubbard House](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/ashtabula/hubbard_house) tells Ashtabula's Underground Railroad story, while the [harbour light](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/ashtabula/ashtabula_harbor_light) marks the breakwater out on Lake Erie.

Ashtabula is not a polished resort town, and that is part of the point. It works best for travellers already along the Lake Erie shore who want a harbour walk, a bit of maritime history, and a less packaged stop than the summer strips farther west.
