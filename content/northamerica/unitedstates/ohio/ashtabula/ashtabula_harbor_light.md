Ashtabula Harbor Light stands at the end of the breakwater, a white and red lighthouse visible from the harbour and from the water. The present light dates from the early 20th century and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

It is best viewed from shore or by boat; the breakwater is exposed and not a casual walking route. Pair it with the lift bridge and maritime museum area for the harbour's full story.
