The Western Reserve Greenway is a paved rail-trail that starts in Ashtabula and runs south through farms, woods, and small towns. It is useful for cyclists who want a quiet ride away from the lake traffic.

For most visitors, a short out-and-back is enough. Longer-distance riders can make it part of a broader northeast Ohio trail day.
