Barnesville is a small village in southeast Ohio, west of Wheeling and close to Interstate 70. Most of the year it is quiet, but it has enough odd pieces to reward a detour: a restored Victorian mansion, a railroad depot, prehistoric petroglyphs, and a working cattle ranch that leans hard into horned breeds.

The [Belmont County Victorian Mansion Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/barnesville/belmont_county_victorian_mansion_museum) is the most polished sight, with carved woodwork, decorative hardware, and period clothing inside a large Romanesque house. The [Barnesville B&O Railroad Depot](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/barnesville/barnesville_bo_railroad_depot) adds a smaller industrial-history stop, while the [Barnesville Petroglyph](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/barnesville/barnesville_petroglyph) pulls the story much further back.

If you time it for late September, the [Barnesville Pumpkin Festival](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/barnesville/barnesville_pumpkin_festival) changes the scale of the place completely, drawing enormous crowds for giant pumpkins, parades, old farm machinery, craft displays, and small-town contests. Outside festival weekend, Barnesville works best as a half-day stop between the Ohio River towns and the back roads of eastern Ohio.
