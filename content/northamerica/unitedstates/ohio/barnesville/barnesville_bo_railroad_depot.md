The old Baltimore and Ohio depot preserves a small piece of Barnesville's rail history, with the depot building, a caboose, and the nearby railroad tunnel along a walking trail.

It is a short stop rather than a destination by itself, but it pairs well with the Victorian mansion and a walk through the centre of town.
