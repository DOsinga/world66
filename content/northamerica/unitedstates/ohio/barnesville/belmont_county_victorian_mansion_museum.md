This restored 26-room Romanesque mansion is Barnesville's best indoor stop. The interior has carved woodwork, mouldings, decorated mantels, ornate hinges and locks, and a large collection of 1890s clothing.

It is open seasonally, with a Christmas tour between Thanksgiving and Christmas. If you only have time for one sight in town outside festival season, make it this one.
