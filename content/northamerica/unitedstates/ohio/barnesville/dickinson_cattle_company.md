North of Barnesville, Dickinson Cattle Company runs tours on a large reclaimed strip-mine ranch stocked with Texas longhorn, African watusi, and other horned cattle breeds. The tour mixes cattle, local history, and the odd sight of western-style ranching in the Ohio hills.

It is a specialist stop, but a memorable one if you are travelling with children or have any interest in working farms. Check opening times before driving out.
