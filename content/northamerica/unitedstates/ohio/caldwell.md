Caldwell is a small county-seat village in southeast [Ohio](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio), just off I-77 in Noble County. It is not a polished destination, and you should not expect a full slate of city attractions. Its appeal is narrower: Appalachian back roads, old oil history, local glass collecting, and easy access to quiet state-park land.

The strongest stop is just east of town at [Thorla-McKee Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/caldwell/thorla_mckee_park), where a small site marks what is often described as North America's first oil well. In town, the [Baker Family Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/caldwell/baker_family_museum) is an eccentric private collection heavy on regional glass. North of the village, [Wolf Run State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/caldwell/wolf_run_state_park) gives Caldwell a real outdoor anchor.

Caldwell is best for travelers already crossing southeast Ohio or following the region's industrial and rural history. It is worth a deliberate half-day if the oil-well story or Wolf Run appeals to you; otherwise it is a useful, low-key base between Zanesville and Marietta.
