Wolf Run State Park is Caldwell's best outdoor outing. The park covers wooded hills around Wolf Run Lake, with hiking, swimming, boating, fishing, and camping. It is quieter than Ohio's better-known parks, which makes it a good fit for travelers who want a low-key Appalachian stop rather than a big attraction.
