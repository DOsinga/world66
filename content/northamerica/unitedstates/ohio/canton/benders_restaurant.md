Bender's Tavern is one of Canton's oldest restaurants, operating since 1902 in a building that has changed remarkably little. The long mahogany bar, dark wood panelling, pressed tin ceiling, and stained glass windows are all original, giving the place the atmosphere of a pre-Prohibition saloon still in working order. It is a genuine piece of downtown Canton's history.

The menu is solidly American — steaks, chops, lake perch — with prices that reflect the quality of the ingredients and the seriousness of the kitchen. It is not cheap but it is not pretentious either, and it draws a loyal local crowd as well as visitors who seek it out. Reservations are advisable for dinner, especially on weekends when the after-theatre crowd from the Palace Theatre fills the place.
