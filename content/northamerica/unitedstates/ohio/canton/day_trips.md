The countryside around Canton makes for easy day trips. The Cuyahoga Valley National Park is about 30 miles north — a protected river valley of waterfalls, towpaths, and forest that feels surprisingly wild given its proximity to Cleveland and Akron. Holmes County, about 40 miles southwest, is the heart of Ohio's Amish Country, with rolling farmland, farm stands, cheese producers, and furniture workshops that make for a genuinely interesting afternoon regardless of your views on handmade quilts.
