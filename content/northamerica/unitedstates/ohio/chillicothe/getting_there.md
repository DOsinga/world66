---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Chillicothe sits about 45 miles south of Columbus on US-23, an easy hour-long drive. From Cincinnati, the city is roughly two hours east on US-50. There is no passenger rail or scheduled commercial flight service; the nearest large airports are John Glenn Columbus International (CMH) and Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky International (CVG).

Greyhound operates a limited bus service from Columbus.
