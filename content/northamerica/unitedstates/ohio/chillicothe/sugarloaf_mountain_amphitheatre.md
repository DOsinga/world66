Sugarloaf Mountain Amphitheatre sits in the hills south of Chillicothe and is best known as the home of *Tecumseh!*, the outdoor historical drama staged here in summer. The setting does much of the work: wooded slopes, open air, and a large stage that can handle horses, battle scenes, and the kind of broad theatrical gesture that would look absurd indoors.

This is a seasonal evening outing rather than a casual daytime sight, so check the current programme before driving out. If the show is running, it gives Chillicothe visitors a direct, old-fashioned way into the frontier history that shaped this part of Ohio.
