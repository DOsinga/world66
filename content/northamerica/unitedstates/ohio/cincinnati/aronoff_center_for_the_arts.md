The Aronoff Center for the Arts is Cincinnati's main downtown stage for touring Broadway shows, ballet, comedy, and large performing-arts events. It is a practical evening anchor if you want something more polished than a bar or club, and its central location makes it easy to pair with dinner nearby.

Check the programme before you go; the draw here is the performance rather than the building alone.
