Carew Tower is Cincinnati's great Art Deco skyscraper, a downtown landmark whose design helped inspire the Empire State Building. The common areas are worth a look for their period detail, and the observation deck gives one of the best high views over the Ohio River, downtown Cincinnati, and northern Kentucky.
