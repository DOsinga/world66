The Cincinnati Museum Center is housed inside Union Terminal, a 1933 Art Deco train station widely regarded as one of the most beautiful in the United States. The soaring half-dome facade faces a grand forecourt, and the interior rotunda features dramatic murals and tilework that survived decades of neglect before a major restoration completed in 2018.

Inside the complex are three separate museums: the Cincinnati History Museum, the Museum of Natural History and Science (with a replica limestone cave you can walk through), and the Cincinnati Children's Museum. The building itself is the main event — see it before you decide which museum interests you. The Omnimax Theater, housed in the former train shed, shows science and nature films on a dome screen.
