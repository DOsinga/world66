Cincinnati Nature Center is the easy escape east of the city: 1,500 acres of woods, lakes, and walking trails where the traffic noise drops away fast. It is good for a proper hike, a gentle family walk, birdwatching, or just feeding fish and turtles during posted times.

Start at the visitor center near the parking area if you want trail advice. The preserve is large enough to feel like a day outdoors rather than a city park with a path through it.
