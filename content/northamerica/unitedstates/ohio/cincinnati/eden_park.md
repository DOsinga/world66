Eden Park is a hilltop Cincinnati park near Mount Adams, with paths, lawns, city views, and several major cultural stops nearby. The Cincinnati Art Museum, Krohn Conservatory, and Playhouse in the Park all sit in or around the park.

It is a good place to break up museum visits with a walk, especially if you want views over the Ohio River and the eastern side of downtown.
