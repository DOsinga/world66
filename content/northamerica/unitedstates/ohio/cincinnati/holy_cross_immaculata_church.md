Holy Cross-Immaculata Catholic Church is the "Church on the Hill" in Mt. Adams, one of Cincinnati's more distinctive Catholic landmarks. Its local fame is tied especially to Good Friday, when many Cincinnatians climb the steps of Mt. Adams while praying on each step.

The church is worth adding to a Mt. Adams walk if you are interested in local religious traditions or want a stop above the river and downtown.
