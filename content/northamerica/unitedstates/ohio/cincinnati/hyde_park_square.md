Hyde Park Square is the green centre of one of Cincinnati's older, wealthier east-side neighbourhoods. The small park is ringed by shops, cafes, and tidy streets, making it a pleasant place to slow down after the city's bigger museums and riverfront sights.

Come on a warm afternoon if you want a quieter look at residential Cincinnati, or fold it into a visit to nearby Cincinnati Observatory.
