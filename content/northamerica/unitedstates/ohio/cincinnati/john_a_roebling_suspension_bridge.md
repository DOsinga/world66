The John A. Roebling Suspension Bridge links downtown Cincinnati with Covington across the Ohio River. When pedestrians first crossed in 1866 it was the longest suspension bridge in the world, and its design helped shape Roebling's later Brooklyn Bridge.

Walk across for river views, the blue steelwork, and an easy connection to Covington's historic riverside streets.
