Mt. Echo is a west-side Cincinnati overlook with one of the city's better skyline views. From the park edge, downtown rises beyond the Ohio River valley, making it a useful stop at sunset or when you want a wide view without leaving the city.

The park is simple, but the view is the point. It is also known locally as the setting for the cover photo of Over-the-Rhine's album "Ohio."
