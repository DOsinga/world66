Pendleton Art Center fills a large former warehouse with artist studios and galleries. It is best known for Final Friday open-studio evenings, when visitors can browse several floors of local work and meet the artists behind it.

Come during an open-studio event if you can. Outside those times, access and atmosphere vary by studio, so it is worth checking the current schedule before making a special trip.
