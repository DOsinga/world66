---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Cincinnati's best shopping is concentrated in Over the Rhine, where independent boutiques line the streets around Vine, Main, and 12th. The Findlay Market (1801 Race Street) is the oldest continuously operating public market in Ohio, running on Saturdays year-round and Sundays in summer — local produce, artisan foods, and a genuine neighbourhood scene. The Gateway Quarter around 12th and Vine has a cluster of independent clothing, home goods, and record shops that opened as the neighbourhood revived.
