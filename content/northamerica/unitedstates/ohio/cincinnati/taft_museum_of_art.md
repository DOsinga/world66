The Taft Museum of Art is one of Cincinnati's best small museums, set inside a National Historic Landmark house from around 1820. The collection includes European and American paintings, Chinese porcelains, and decorative arts, so it feels more like visiting a cultivated private home than a large civic museum.

Its scale is part of the appeal. You can see the highlights without losing half a day, then continue exploring downtown or the riverfront.
