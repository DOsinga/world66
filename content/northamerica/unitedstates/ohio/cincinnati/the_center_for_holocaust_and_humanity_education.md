The Center for Holocaust and Humanity Education presents Holocaust history through local Cincinnati connections. Its Mapping Our Tears exhibit follows the stories of survivors, resistance figures and Cincinnatians involved in the aftermath of Nazi rule.

It is a focused, serious museum stop rather than a large general history museum, and works best for visitors interested in personal testimony and human-rights education.
