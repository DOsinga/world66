78th Street Studios is a large art complex in a former motor factory in Cleveland's Detroit-Shoreway area. More than 30 galleries and creative spaces are based here, making it one of the city's better stops for contemporary local art.

The best time to visit is during the monthly open house on the third Friday, when more studios and galleries are open at once.
