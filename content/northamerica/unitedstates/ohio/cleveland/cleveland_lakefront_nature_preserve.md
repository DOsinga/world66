Cleveland Lakefront Nature Preserve is an 88-acre peninsula on Lake Erie, reclaimed as a wild-feeling patch of shoreline with walking trails, scrub, meadows, and open water views. It is one of the better places in the city for birdwatching, especially during migration.

The preserve is simple rather than manicured: come for a walk, lake air, and a break from traffic. Bicycles are not allowed on the trails, which keeps the paths quiet for walkers.
