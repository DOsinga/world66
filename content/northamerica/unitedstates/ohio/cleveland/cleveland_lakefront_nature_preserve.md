Cleveland Lakefront Nature Preserve is an 88-acre peninsula on Lake Erie with several miles of walking trails and wide shoreline views. The preserve is one of Cleveland's better close-in places for birdwatching, especially during migration, and it gives a quieter look at the lake than the city's busier waterfront stops.
