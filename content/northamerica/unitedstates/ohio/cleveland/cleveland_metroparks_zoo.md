Cleveland Metroparks Zoo is the city's major wildlife attraction, with large themed areas covering African, Australian, rainforest, primate, cat, aquatic, and wilderness exhibits. It is a substantial visit, especially with children, and sits southwest of downtown near the Old Brooklyn area.

Some special exhibits can carry an extra charge, so check current details before planning the day around them.
