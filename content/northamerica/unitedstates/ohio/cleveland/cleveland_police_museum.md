The Cleveland Police Museum is a small downtown museum with uniforms, equipment, old call boxes, crime-scene material, and stories from the city's policing history. Some displays are grim, including material connected to the Kingsbury Run murders.

It is a specialist stop rather than a first-rank Cleveland sight, but worthwhile if you are interested in crime history or public-safety memorabilia. It pairs easily with other downtown landmarks.
