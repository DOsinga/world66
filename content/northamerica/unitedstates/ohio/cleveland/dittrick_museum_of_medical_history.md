The Dittrick Museum of Medical History uses rare books, instruments, images, and archive material to explain the history of medicine. It is a compact specialist museum in Cleveland's University Circle area.

It is best for visitors with a real interest in science, medicine, or unusual museum collections, and pairs easily with the larger University Circle museums nearby.
