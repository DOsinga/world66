The Federal Reserve Bank and Money Museum explains money, banking, and economic history inside Cleveland's historic Federal Reserve Bank. Exhibits include interactive displays, tours, and the museum's oversized money tree.

It is a useful downtown stop if you like civic buildings, financial history, or museums that make abstract systems a little more tangible.
