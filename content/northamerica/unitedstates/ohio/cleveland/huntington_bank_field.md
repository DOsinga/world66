The lakefront stadium known successively as Cleveland Browns Stadium, FirstEnergy Stadium, and (since 2024) Huntington Bank Field has been the home of the Cleveland Browns NFL franchise since 1999, on the site of the demolished Municipal Stadium that the team called home for half a century before that. The setting — a wide-open horseshoe pointed at Lake Erie — is unusually attractive for an American football stadium, and tailgating in the West 3rd parking lot before a Sunday game is a Cleveland institution.

Browns season runs September through January. Tickets are easier to get than for the Cavaliers; the city has been talking about a new stadium in Brook Park for years, so the current site may not last forever.
