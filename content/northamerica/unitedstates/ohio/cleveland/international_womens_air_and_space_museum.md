The International Women's Air & Space Museum preserves the stories of women in aviation and spaceflight. It is a small, specialist museum, but its subject is more unusual than Cleveland's bigger lakefront institutions.

The exhibits are a useful add-on for aviation-minded visitors, especially if you are already heading to the lakefront or Burke Lakefront Airport area. Expect a focused collection rather than a full-scale science museum.
