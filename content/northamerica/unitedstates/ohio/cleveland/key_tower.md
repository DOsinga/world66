Key Tower is the tall, pale granite skyscraper that anchors Public Square and the downtown Cleveland skyline. Designed by Cesar Pelli, it is the tallest building in Ohio and one of the most recognisable pieces of late-20th-century architecture in the city.

This is mainly an exterior architecture stop rather than a full visit. It fits naturally into a walk around Public Square, the Old Stone Church, Terminal Tower, and the civic buildings at the heart of downtown.
