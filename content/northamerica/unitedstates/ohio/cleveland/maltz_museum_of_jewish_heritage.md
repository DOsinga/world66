The Maltz Museum of Jewish Heritage tells the story of Cleveland's Jewish community and its wider American context. Permanent exhibits cover immigration, local history, religious life, and civic contributions, with objects and stories that connect family memory to the city's growth.

The museum also includes pieces from the Temple-Tifereth Israel Judaica collection and hosts temporary exhibitions. It is in Beachwood, east of central Cleveland, so it is easiest to visit by car.
