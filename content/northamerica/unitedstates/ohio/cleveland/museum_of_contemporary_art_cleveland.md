The Museum of Contemporary Art Cleveland, usually shortened to moCa, adds a sharper contemporary edge to University Circle's museum cluster. It has no permanent collection, so the visit depends on the current exhibition, with a strong tilt toward new work and artists from northeastern Ohio. Check the programme first, then combine it with the Cleveland Museum of Art or the natural history museum nearby.
