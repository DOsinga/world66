Columbus Commons is a downtown green space and outdoor event ground on the site of the old City Center mall. On concert days and summer evenings it becomes one of central Columbus's easiest gathering places.

When nothing is scheduled, it works as a simple open lawn with paths and places to sit. It is not a destination to cross town for by itself, but it is a useful pause while walking downtown.
