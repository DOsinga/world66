Columbus Zoo and Aquarium is one of central Ohio's major family attractions, with thousands of animals arranged in themed geographic areas. It sits north of Columbus near Dublin and is best treated as a half-day or full-day outing, especially if combining it with the adjacent Zoombezi Bay water park.
