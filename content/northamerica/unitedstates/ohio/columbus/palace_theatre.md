Palace Theatre is a restored downtown performance hall at the base of the LeVeque Tower on Broad Street. The 1920s theatre is part of Columbus's historic entertainment core and is worth checking for concerts, touring shows, and special events.

Even without a ticket, its location makes it easy to pair with a look at the LeVeque Tower lobby and the nearby Ohio Statehouse.
