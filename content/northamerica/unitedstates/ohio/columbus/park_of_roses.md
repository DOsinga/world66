The Park of Roses is one of the largest public rose gardens in the United States, with thousands of rose bushes planted in formal beds. It is at its best during the main bloom, but the garden remains a calm walk in good weather.

Come for the flowers rather than a long hike; this is a focused garden stop, not a full park day.
