type: section
---

Columbus rewards visitors who dig a little. The [Short North](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus/short_north) arts district north of downtown is the best neighbourhood to walk — galleries, independent shops, bars, and the [North Market](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus/north_market) all within a mile of High Street. [German Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus/german_village) to the south of downtown is a well-preserved Victorian brick neighbourhood with the labyrinthine [Book Loft](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus/book_loft) and the institution that is [Schmidt's Sausage Haus](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus/schmidts_sausage_h).

Downtown, the [Ohio Statehouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus/ohio_statehouse) is free and worth an hour, and [COSI](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus/cosi) is one of the best science museums in the Midwest. The [LeVeque Tower](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus/leveque_tower) is the city's most elegant building, and the [Scioto Mile](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus/scioto_mile) riverfront park connects it all with a pleasant walking path.
