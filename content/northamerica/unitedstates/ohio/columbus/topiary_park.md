Topiary Park is a small downtown Columbus park best known for its living recreation of Georges Seurat's painting A Sunday Afternoon on the Island of La Grande Jatte. Shrubs are clipped into human figures, boats, trees, and animals arranged around the pond.

It is a quick stop, but a distinctive one, especially if you are already visiting the Columbus Museum of Art or walking through the Discovery District.
