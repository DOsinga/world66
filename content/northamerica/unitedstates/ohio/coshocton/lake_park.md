Lake Park is Coshocton's main outdoor add-on to Historic Roscoe Village. The park has trails, picnic space, disc golf, camping, and open green space, but its most distinctive feature is the canal-boat landing.

When the Monticello III is running, the horse-drawn canal boat makes the Ohio and Erie Canal history around Roscoe feel practical rather than decorative. Even without a ride, the park is a good breather between the village, the museum, and the town centre.
