title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Almost everything worth seeing in Coshocton is concentrated in [Roscoe Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/coshocton/roscoe_village), the restored canal-era town on the western edge of town. Allow half a day for the village itself, including the [Johnson-Humrickhouse Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/coshocton/johnson_humrickhouse_museum), which is good enough to hold its own anywhere.
