Cathedral of Tomorrow is a large modern church in Cuyahoga Falls, known for its huge indoor cross and unfinished tower. It is an unusual religious landmark rather than a conventional historic church.

The building is mainly of interest to visitors looking for distinctive roadside architecture or local religious landmarks around Akron and Cuyahoga Falls.
