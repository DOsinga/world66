title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cuyahoga Falls packs a surprising amount into a small footprint. The Cuyahoga River gorge is the heart of it: the rapids and cascades run through the middle of town, and Waterworks Park puts you right on the water. Blossom Music Center brings in major performers throughout the summer. For outdoor exploration, Cuyahoga Valley National Park borders the city to the south — one of the most-visited national parks in the country, easily accessible from town.
