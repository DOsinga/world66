The Cuyahoga Valley Scenic Railroad runs vintage coaches through the national park, linking river towns, towpath trailheads, and historic stops in the valley. It is one of the easiest ways to see the park without driving from trailhead to trailhead, and is especially useful for combining a one-way bike ride or walk with a relaxed train return.
