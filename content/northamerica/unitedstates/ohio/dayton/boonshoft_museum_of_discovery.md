The Boonshoft Museum of Discovery is Dayton's best family museum, mixing natural history, science exhibits, live animals, and a planetarium. It is designed for touching and experimenting rather than quiet glass-case viewing.

For travellers with children, it is one of the easiest ways to break up the city's aviation-heavy sightseeing. Adults will still find enough local natural history and astronomy to make the stop worthwhile.
