Day Air Ballpark is the home of the Dayton Dragons, the Single-A affiliate of the Cincinnati Reds, and one of the most popular minor league baseball venues in the country. The Dragons set a professional sports record for consecutive sellouts — over 800 games — a run that lasted from 2000 until COVID stopped play in 2020.

The ballpark sits in the heart of downtown on the banks of the Great Miami River, with the stadium's open design giving views of the water from certain sections. A Dragons game on a warm summer evening is a quintessentially Midwestern experience: relaxed, affordable, and genuinely entertaining regardless of your attachment to baseball. Games typically run from April through September.
