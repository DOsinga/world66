This National Historical Park ties together the multiple Dayton sites connected to Orville and Wilbur Wright and poet Paul Laurence Dunbar. The core unit is the Wright Brothers' bicycle shop on West Third Street — the actual workspace where they developed their aviation ideas, with period tools and equipment on display. A short walk away is the Paul Laurence Dunbar House, home of the pioneering African American poet who was a schoolmate and friend of the Wright Brothers.

The park also administers Huffman Prairie Flying Field east of the city, where the brothers refined and demonstrated controlled flight between 1904 and 1905 after their initial Kitty Hawk success. A free trolley connects some of the sites on summer weekends.
