---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By air:** Dayton International Airport (DAY) sits about 15 km north of downtown and receives direct flights from major US hubs. It is a smaller, easy-to-navigate airport with no real congestion.

**By road:** Dayton sits at the intersection of I-70 (east–west) and I-75 (north–south), making it easy to reach by car. [Columbus](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/columbus) is about 75 km to the east and [Cincinnati](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/cincinnati) about 85 km to the south.

**By bus:** Greyhound serves Dayton with connections to Columbus, Cincinnati, Indianapolis, and Chicago. The bus station is downtown.

**By train:** Amtrak does not currently serve Dayton. The closest Amtrak stop is Cincinnati.
