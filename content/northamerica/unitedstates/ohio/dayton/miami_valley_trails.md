The Miami Valley Trails give Dayton one of the Midwest's best urban cycling networks, with paved paths spreading out from the Great Miami River into the surrounding counties. You do not need to ride the whole system; even a short downtown stretch is a good way to see the riverfront and understand why local cyclists value the city.
