The Wright "B" Flyer Museum keeps a flying replica of the Wright brothers' second production aircraft, the Model B. It is a small, focused aviation stop, but in Dayton that focus matters: the city is built around the Wright story.

Visitors can see the replica up close and, when flights are operating, may be able to ride in it. Pair it with Dayton Aviation Heritage National Historical Park or Carillon Park for a fuller picture of early flight.
