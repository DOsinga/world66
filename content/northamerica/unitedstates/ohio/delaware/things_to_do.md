---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Delaware punches above its weight for a city of its size. The compact downtown has genuine nineteenth-century architecture worth looking at slowly, Ohio Wesleyan University brings intellectual and cultural life to the centre of town, and the Delaware County Fairgrounds draw harness racing fans from across the country every September. There is enough here for a half-day visit from Columbus, or a full day if you take your time.
