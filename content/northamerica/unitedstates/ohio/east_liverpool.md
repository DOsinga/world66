East Liverpool sits on the Ohio River where Ohio, Pennsylvania, and West Virginia almost touch. It is not a polished weekend city, and that is part of the truth of it. This was once one of America's great pottery towns, producing ceramic ware at a scale that shaped the streets, fortunes, and civic buildings around downtown.

The reason to come is that pottery story. [The Museum of Ceramics](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/east_liverpool/the_museum_of_ceramics) is the anchor, with Lotus Ware and the industrial history of the city once called the Pottery Capital of the Nation. Around it, the [Diamond Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/east_liverpool/diamond_historic_district), [C.C. Thompson House](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/east_liverpool/cc_thompson_house), and riverfront markers give a short but coherent day of Ohio Valley history.

East Liverpool clears the bar for travellers who like industrial towns, ceramics, or forgotten river cities. If you need a broad menu of restaurants and nightlife, stay in [Pittsburgh](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/pittsburgh). If you want a specific American craft story in the place that made it, East Liverpool is worth the detour.
