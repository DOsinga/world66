The Beginning Point of the U.S. Public Land Survey marks the place from which the federal government began surveying the Northwest Territory. It is a small marker, but the idea behind it is large: the grid that later shaped much of the American Midwest starts here.

This is for map people, history people, and anyone who likes quiet landmarks with outsized consequences. Pair it with downtown rather than making it the whole outing.
