C.C. Thompson House is a Victorian home built for one of East Liverpool's pottery magnates. Its tower and Italianate detail are a reminder that the ceramic industry did not just fill factories; it also paid for ambitious houses and civic buildings.

Check current access before planning around the interior. Even from outside, it helps connect the museum's factory story to the people who profited from it.
