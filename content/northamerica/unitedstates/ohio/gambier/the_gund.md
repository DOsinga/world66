The Gund is Kenyon's modern and contemporary art gallery, set beside Chalmers Library in the middle of campus. Admission is free, and exhibitions change through the year, so it is worth checking what is on before you go.

The gallery is small enough for an easy visit and good enough to anchor a campus walk. Even if you only have half an hour, it adds a sharper edge to Gambier's otherwise pastoral college-town mood.
