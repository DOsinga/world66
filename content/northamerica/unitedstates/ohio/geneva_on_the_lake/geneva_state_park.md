Geneva State Park gives the village its real Lake Erie breathing room. The park has a beach, marina, fishing areas, walking and biking paths, and a quieter shoreline mood than the Strip a short distance east.

This is the place to reset if the resort strip starts to feel too loud. Bring a picnic, watch the boats, and stay for sunset if the weather is clear.
