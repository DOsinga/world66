The Jennie Munger Gregory Museum is a preserved early-19th-century farmhouse and a useful reminder that Geneva-on-the-Lake was not always only a summer strip. The building connects the village's resort story to the older farms and lake communities of the Western Reserve.

It is a small stop, best treated as a half-hour history break rather than a major museum. Pair it with the Strip so the village's older and louder sides sit next to each other.
