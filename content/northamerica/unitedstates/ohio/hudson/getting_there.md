---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Hudson sits on Route 91, about 25 km southeast of downtown Cleveland and 15 km north of Akron. By car it is straightforward: take I-480 or I-271 to the Route 91/SR 14 exit. The drive from Cleveland takes about 30-40 minutes depending on traffic; from Akron, 20 minutes.

There is no direct public transit to Hudson from Cleveland or Akron. The nearest Amtrak station is Cleveland, and the nearest Greyhound connections are in Akron. A rental car or rideshare from either city is the practical option for those without their own vehicle.
