Hudson Springs Park is the easiest place near Hudson to trade the historic green for woods and water. A loop trail circles the lake, with picnic areas, fishing spots, and seasonal paddling that make it feel more substantial than a neighborhood park.

It is not a reason to travel across Ohio, but it is a solid addition for visitors already in Hudson who want fresh air after the preserved town centre and Western Reserve architecture.
