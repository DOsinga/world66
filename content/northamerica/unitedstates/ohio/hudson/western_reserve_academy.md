Western Reserve Academy occupies the original campus of Western Reserve College, founded in 1826, and is one of the older boarding schools in the United States. The campus is a handsome collection of 19th-century brick buildings laid out around a central green, architecturally consistent and well preserved. Walking through it feels like stepping into the early republic.

The school is private but the grounds are generally accessible to visitors. The campus anchors the north end of Hudson's historic district and is visually striking — particularly the original college buildings clustered around Seymour Hall, built in the 1830s. Combined with the Loomis Observatory nearby, the campus gives a strong sense of what American higher education looked like two centuries ago.
