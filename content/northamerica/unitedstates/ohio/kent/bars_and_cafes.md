---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Kent has a lively bar scene when the university is in session — driven almost entirely by the student population, concentrated on East Main Street, and quieting sharply in summer when most students leave. The coffee shops are better than you might expect for a town of this size, with several independent options that draw a studious crowd. If you're visiting during the academic year, the evenings have genuine energy; if you're here in July, manage your expectations.
