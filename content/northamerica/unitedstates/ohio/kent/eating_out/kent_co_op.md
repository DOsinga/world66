A natural foods cooperative with a deli counter and prepared foods — the kind of community institution that a college town tends to sustain better than most places. Good for a healthy lunch, local produce, and prepared items that are a cut above standard fast food. The co-op has been part of the Kent community long enough that it feels embedded rather than trendy.

Worth a stop if you want something other than the burgers-and-pizza mainstream of East Main, or if you're putting together provisions for a day in the Cuyahoga Valley.
