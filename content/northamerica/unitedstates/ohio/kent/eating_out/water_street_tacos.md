A popular local taco spot near the downtown core that has built a consistent following among students and longtime residents alike. The approach is Mexican-American street food — fresh ingredients, straightforward preparations, nothing overwrought. Prices are reasonable even by Kent standards.

This is a reliable lunch stop when you're in the area, particularly after a visit to the downtown strip or the farmers' market.
