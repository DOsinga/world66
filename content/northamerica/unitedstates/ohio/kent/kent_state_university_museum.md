The Kent State University Museum is a fashion and decorative-arts museum in Rockwell Hall on the Kent State campus. Its exhibitions draw on a collection of historic dress, textiles, and contemporary fashion, so it is more focused than a general campus gallery.

It is worth considering if you are already visiting Kent State for the May 4 site or if fashion history is a specific interest.
