Kidron is barely a village, but in Ohio Amish Country it has a clear job. It is a trading place: Amish and Mennonite families come in for livestock sales, hardware, groceries, thrift goods, and the practical business of the week.

That makes it more interesting than a normal roadside stop. [Lehman's](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/kidron/lehmans) has turned non-electric tools and homesteading supplies into a destination store, while the [Kidron Auction](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/kidron/kidron_auction) still gives the town its working rhythm. On the edge of town, [Sonnenberg Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/kidron/sonnenberg_village) preserves the Swiss Mennonite settlement story that explains why Kidron exists here in the first place.

Kidron is not a full-day city break. It works best as a focused half-day inside Amish Country, especially on auction day, when the small town feels like itself rather than a themed version of itself.
