Lehman's is the stop most visitors know before they arrive. It began by serving Amish customers who needed non-electric tools and appliances, then became a sprawling store for oil lamps, wood stoves, hand tools, kitchen gear, and other low-tech goods.

It is commercial, but it is not just another gift shop. The shelves explain a lot about the practical side of Amish Country.
