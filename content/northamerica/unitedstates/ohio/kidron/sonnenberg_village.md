Sonnenberg Village is a modest heritage campus rather than a polished museum complex. Its preserved buildings and historical marker point back to the Swiss Mennonite settlement founded here in the early 19th century.

It is most useful if you want context for Kidron's Amish and Mennonite present. A short self-guided walk is enough.
