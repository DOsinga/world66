---

Kidron's worthwhile stops are tied to local life rather than formal sightseeing. [Lehman's](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/kidron/lehmans) is the famous one, but [Kidron Auction](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/kidron/kidron_auction) and [Sonnenberg Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/kidron/sonnenberg_village) make the place feel less like a shopping stop and more like a working Amish and Mennonite village.
