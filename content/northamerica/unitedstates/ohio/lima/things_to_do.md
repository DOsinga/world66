title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lima's best sights cluster around its industrial and civic heritage. The [Allen County Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/lima/allen_county_museum) is the centrepiece — one of the better county history museums in Ohio, with a strong railway collection and the [MacDonell House](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/lima/macdonell_house) on the same campus. [Lincoln Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/lima/lincoln_park) adds outdoor space along the Ottawa River with a preserved steam locomotive. For something more unusual, the [Lima Army Tank Plant](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/lima/lima_tank_plant) is the sole producer of the M1 Abrams tank and has occasional public events.
