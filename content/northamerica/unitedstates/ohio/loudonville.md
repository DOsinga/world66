Loudonville is a small town in north-central Ohio, just north of the wooded gorge country of Mohican State Park. Its downtown is modest, but the surrounding Mohican area gives it a clear travel identity: canoe liveries, forest trails, a restored grist mill, and one of Ohio's more interesting literary farms all sit within an easy drive.

This is not a city to cross the country for, but it is a good one-day target if you are already in central or northern Ohio. Start with the gorge trails in [Mohican State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/loudonville/mohican_state_park), stop at [Wolf Creek Grist Mill](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/loudonville/wolf_creek_grist_mill), then detour to [Malabar Farm State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/loudonville/malabar_farm_state_park) for Louis Bromfield's Big House and the pastoral hills around it.
