The Cleo Redd Fisher Museum is Loudonville's local history museum, run by the Mohican Historical Society in the centre of town. It covers the Mohican area's settlement, material culture, genealogy, and regional stories through small exhibitions and regular history programmes.

It is a useful stop if rain interrupts a hiking day or if you want the town itself to be more than a lunch break. Check hours before going, as small-town museum schedules can be limited.
