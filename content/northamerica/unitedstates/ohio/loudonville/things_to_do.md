title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Loudonville's appeal is the Mohican countryside more than the town grid. The best stops are [Mohican State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/loudonville/mohican_state_park), [Wolf Creek Grist Mill](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/loudonville/wolf_creek_grist_mill), [Malabar Farm State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/loudonville/malabar_farm_state_park), and the small [Cleo Redd Fisher Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/loudonville/cleo_redd_fisher_museum) downtown.
