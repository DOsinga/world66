Mansfield is a north-central Ohio city with one attraction strong enough to pull travellers off the interstate: the [Ohio State Reformatory](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/mansfield/ohio_state_reformatory), the huge former prison made famous by *The Shawshank Redemption*. That landmark gives the city a sharper identity than many towns of similar size.

There is enough around it for a full day. Pair the reformatory with [Kingwood Center Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/mansfield/kingwood_center_gardens), a walk through the compact downtown carousel district, and, if you have a car, [Malabar Farm State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/mansfield/malabar_farm_state_park) in the rolling countryside southeast of town.

Mansfield is not glamorous, and that is part of the appeal. Its best stops are practical, odd, and very Ohio: a castle-like prison, a hand-carved carousel, a formal garden, and a literary farm landscape within easy reach.
