Malabar Farm State Park preserves the country estate of writer and conservation-minded farmer Louis Bromfield, southeast of Mansfield near Lucas. The farm mixes fields, woods, old buildings, and gentle trails in a landscape that feels more rural than park-like.

It is worth the short drive if you want a break from town. The Big House, farm setting, and surrounding hills add a literary and agricultural side to a Mansfield visit.
