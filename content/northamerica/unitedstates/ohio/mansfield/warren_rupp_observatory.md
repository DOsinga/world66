Warren Rupp Observatory sits in a rural setting south of Mansfield, where the dark skies make it a strong stop for anyone interested in astronomy. The observatory is run by the Richland Astronomical Society and is known for its large amateur-operated telescope.

Check the schedule before going, since the best visits are public observing nights or special events. On a clear evening, it offers the kind of sky view that is hard to find close to larger Ohio cities.
