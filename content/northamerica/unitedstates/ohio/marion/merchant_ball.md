The Merchant Ball in Marion Cemetery is one of Ohio's more peculiar tourist attractions — a large, perfectly polished granite sphere atop a grave marker that has, according to local lore and cemetery records, been slowly rolling over the years. It sits in a curved stone cup that allows it to rotate, and cemetery staff have documented its movement across multiple decades.

Whether the movement is genuine rotation, thermal expansion, or the accumulation of small shifts during the ball's occasional cleanings is a matter of gentle local debate. Either way, the ball is an impressive piece of Victorian funerary craftsmanship — a two-ton granite sphere with a near-perfect mirror polish — and the cemetery itself, with its mature trees and Victorian and Edwardian monuments, is a pleasant place to walk. The Harding Memorial is about a 15-minute walk away.
