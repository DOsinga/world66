Millersburg is the county seat of Holmes County and one of the practical bases for Ohio Amish Country. It has enough of its own town life to be more than a signpost: a brick downtown, a serious Victorian house museum, the Holmes County Trail, and farm-country roads that quickly leave the grid behind.

The town's strongest sight is the [Victorian House Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/millersburg/victorian_house_museum), a 28-room Queen Anne mansion that also tells local Holmes County history. For the Amish Country side of the trip, [Yoder's Amish Home](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/millersburg/yoders_amish_home) and [Hershberger's Farm and Bakery](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/millersburg/hershbergers_farm_and_bakery) are both easy drives through rolling farmland.

Millersburg is best used slowly. Walk the downtown, ride or stroll part of the [Holmes County Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/millersburg/holmes_county_trail), then take the back roads toward Walnut Creek, Berlin, or Sugarcreek rather than rushing from shop to shop.
