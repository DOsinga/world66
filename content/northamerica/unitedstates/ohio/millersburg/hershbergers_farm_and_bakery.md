Hershberger's Farm and Bakery is one of the busiest farm stops in Holmes County, with baked goods, produce, a farm store, food stands, buggy rides, and a large animal area. It is not quiet, but it is useful if you want a single stop that gathers several Amish Country habits in one place.

Go for fry pies, bread, seasonal produce, and the simple pleasure of watching children lose their minds over farm animals. It is closed on Sundays, like many Amish Country businesses.
