title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Millersburg's sights split neatly between town history and Amish Country drives. See the [Victorian House Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/millersburg/victorian_house_museum), spend time on the [Holmes County Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/millersburg/holmes_county_trail), then head east toward [Yoder's Amish Home](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/millersburg/yoders_amish_home) or [Hershberger's Farm and Bakery](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/millersburg/hershbergers_farm_and_bakery).
