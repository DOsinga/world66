Victorian House Museum is Millersburg's best in-town sight, a 28-room Queen Anne mansion run by the Holmes County Historical Society. The house is listed on the National Register of Historic Places and works both as a Victorian interiors museum and a local history collection.

It is the stop that gives Millersburg a town identity separate from Amish Country shopping. The rooms are rich with period furniture and county artifacts, and seasonal events make the old mansion feel less like a static house tour.
