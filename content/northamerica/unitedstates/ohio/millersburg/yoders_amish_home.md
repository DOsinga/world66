Yoder's Amish Home is a guided introduction to Amish domestic and farm life east of Millersburg. Tours cover the house, barn, one-room schoolhouse, buggy rides, and the everyday customs that visitors often misunderstand from the roadside.

It is a tourist attraction, but a useful one if you are new to Holmes County. The best visits are curious and respectful: ask questions, listen closely, and remember that the surrounding farms are working homes, not scenery built for visitors.
