Nelsonville is a small Appalachian Ohio town with more texture than its size suggests. Coal, clay, brickmaking, and railroads shaped it; the old Public Square and depot still give the centre a compact, lived-in feel.

The best reason to come is the mix of old industry and arts. [Hocking Valley Scenic Railway](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/nelsonville/hocking_valley_scenic_railway) runs heritage trains from the depot, [Stuart's Opera House](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/nelsonville/stuarts_opera_house) keeps the square culturally alive, and the [Historic Square Arts District](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/nelsonville/historic_square_arts_district) is good for a short wander.

Nelsonville is not a substitute for Hocking Hills, but it is a good spare-day stop if you are already in Athens or the Hocking Valley. Add [Brick Kiln Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/nelsonville/brick_kiln_park) or the [Hockhocking Adena Bikeway](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/nelsonville/hockhocking_adena_bikeway), and the town earns its place.
