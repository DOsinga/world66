Nelsonville's Historic Square Arts District is small, but it concentrates the town's personality. Old commercial buildings, galleries, restaurants, and Stuart's Opera House frame a square that still reads as a real centre.

Come without expecting a big-city gallery scene. The appeal is the mix of Appalachian brick town, student and artist energy from nearby Athens, and the visible work of keeping old buildings useful.
