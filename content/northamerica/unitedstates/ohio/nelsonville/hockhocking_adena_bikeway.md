The Hockhocking Adena Bikeway gives Nelsonville an easy outdoor connection to Athens and the Hocking River valley. It follows old transport corridors, which makes it a natural companion to the scenic railway.

For travellers, the value is simple: you can turn Nelsonville from a look-around stop into a half-day ride. Bring your own bike or check rental options in Athens before building plans around it.
