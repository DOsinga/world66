Hocking Valley Scenic Railway is Nelsonville's clearest visitor anchor. Trains leave from the old depot and roll through the wooded Hocking Valley, with seasonal rides adding extra reason to plan ahead.

This is not polished theme-park nostalgia, which is a good thing. It feels connected to the valley's coal, clay, and brick history, and it gives the town a reason to be more than a quick highway stop.
