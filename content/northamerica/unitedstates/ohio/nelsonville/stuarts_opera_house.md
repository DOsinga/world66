Stuart's Opera House is the cultural heart of Nelsonville. The building anchors Public Square and hosts concerts, theatre, films, and community events in a room that still carries the scale of a prosperous nineteenth-century coal town.

Check the calendar before you go. The exterior is worth a look, but the building makes most sense when the lights are on and the square has people moving around it.
