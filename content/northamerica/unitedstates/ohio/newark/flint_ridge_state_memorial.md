Flint Ridge State Memorial protects one of the most important ancient quarry landscapes in Ohio. The colourful flint found here was worked and traded by Indigenous peoples over long distances, making the ridge part of a much larger archaeological story than its quiet woods first suggest.

For travellers already visiting the Newark Earthworks, Flint Ridge adds useful context. Trails and interpretive material help connect the raw material in the ground with the tools, ceremonial objects, and exchange networks that shaped the region long before modern Newark existed.
