The Works is Newark's hands-on museum for local history, art, science, and technology. It is broader than a single-subject museum, with exhibits and activities that make it a practical stop for families as well as visitors interested in the city's industrial and creative past.

Its strongest role is as an in-town complement to Newark's major outdoor heritage sites. Pair it with the earthworks or downtown historic stops when weather or timing favors an indoor visit.
