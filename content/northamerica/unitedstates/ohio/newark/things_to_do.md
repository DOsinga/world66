---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Newark's main claim on a traveller's attention is the Newark Earthworks — the largest collection of geometric earthworks in the world, built by the Hopewell culture around 2,000 years ago and now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Beyond the earthworks, the Heisey Glass Museum, the Longaberger Basket Building, and the Dawes Arboretum round out a day of genuinely unusual attractions.
