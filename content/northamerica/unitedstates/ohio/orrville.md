Orrville is a small city in Wayne County, northeast Ohio, best known as the home of the J. M. Smucker Company. The fruit-spread business started here in the late 19th century and still gives the town its most recognizable name.

The town is modest and easy to pass by, but it has a bit more texture than a highway stop. Railroad history, local museums, and the Smucker campus give visitors a few reasons to pause, especially if they are already exploring the Wooster area or Ohio's Amish Country.

Come for a short stop, not a full itinerary. Orrville works best as part of a wider Wayne County loop.
