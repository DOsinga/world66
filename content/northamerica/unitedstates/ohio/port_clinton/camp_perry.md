Camp Perry is a National Guard base on the Lake Erie shore just west of Port Clinton, and once a year it hosts the National Matches — the largest shooting competition in the United States and one of the oldest. For two to three weeks in July and August, competitive rifle and pistol shooters from across the country gather here to compete in events that have been running continuously since 1907.

The National Matches are open to civilians (many events use military and civilian mixed brackets) and the atmosphere during match week is genuinely unusual — a temporary city of thousands of shooters, campers, and equipment vendors on a working military base beside the lake. Outside match season, the base is less accessible to visitors, but the site's connection to the history of American marksmanship makes it notable.
