---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Port Clinton is in northwest Ohio, about 90 miles west of [Cleveland](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/cleveland) and 60 miles east of Toledo. It sits on US Route 2, the main east-west corridor along the Lake Erie shore. **By car** is the practical way to arrive — the drive from Cleveland takes about 90 minutes, from Columbus about two hours.

There is no commercial airline service to Port Clinton itself. The nearest major airports are **Cleveland Hopkins International** (about 70 miles east) and **Toledo Express** (about 60 miles west). Neither is particularly convenient; this is firmly car country.

**Amtrak** does not serve Port Clinton. The nearest Amtrak stop is Sandusky (home of Cedar Point), about 20 miles to the east. From Sandusky, you would need to arrange a taxi or rental car for the remaining distance.
