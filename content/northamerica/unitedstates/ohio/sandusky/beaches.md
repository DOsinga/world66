---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Lake Erie is the warmest of the Great Lakes and Sandusky has several swimmable beaches within a short drive of downtown.

**[Cedar Point Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/sandusky/cedar_point_beach)** — a mile of sand on the south side of the peninsula, included with park admission.

**Cedar Point Shores** — Cedar Point's separate water park, with wave pools and slides, open from Memorial Day to Labor Day.

**East Harbor State Park** — 20 minutes east on the Marblehead Peninsula, with a long natural sand beach, picnic areas, and a small campground.

**Sandusky Bay** itself is shallow and muddy and not suitable for swimming; head out toward the lake proper for usable beach.
