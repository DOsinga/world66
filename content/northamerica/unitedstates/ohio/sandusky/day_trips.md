The [Lake Erie Islands](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/sandusky/put_in_bay_ferry) — Put-in-Bay on South Bass Island and the quieter [Kelleys Island](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/sandusky/kelleys_island) — are the obvious day trips, both reachable by ferry from the downtown waterfront or from nearby Marblehead.

**Marblehead Peninsula**, 20 minutes east, has the photogenic Marblehead Lighthouse (Ohio's oldest, in continuous operation since 1822) and a string of small lake towns.

**[Cleveland](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/cleveland)**, 60 minutes east, makes a longer day trip — the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, the Cleveland Museum of Art (free, world-class), and the West Side Market are all worth the drive.

**Milan**, 15 minutes south, is the birthplace of Thomas Edison and has a small Edison Birthplace Museum on the original site.
