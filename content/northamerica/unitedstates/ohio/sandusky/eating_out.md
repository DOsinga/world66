---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Sandusky's food scene splits between the Cedar Point/Hotel Breakers crowd (most options inside the resort) and the downtown waterfront, where a small but real restaurant scene has grown up around the redeveloped Jackson Street and Water Street area. Lake Erie perch, walleye, and yellow lake perch are the regional specialty — most decent restaurants carry one or both, usually as a sandwich for lunch and a plated dinner.
