Follett House Museum is Sandusky Library's local history museum, spread across four floors in a historic house near downtown. Exhibits cover Johnson's Island Civil War Prison Camp, Cedar Point, and the people and industries that shaped Sandusky.

It is a good companion to the city's maritime and carousel museums, especially on a rainy day or if you want more context before heading out to the lakefront.
