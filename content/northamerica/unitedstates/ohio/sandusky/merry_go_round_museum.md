The Merry-Go-Round Museum lives in a handsome 1927 federal-style building that was originally a US Post Office — a domed circular space that turned out to be ideal for displaying a working carousel. Exhibits trace the history of carousels from the European originals through the American golden age (1880–1930) and the recent restoration revival, with original carved horses, menagerie animals, and band-organ instruments.

The working carousel in the centre — restored from a 1939 original — is the most fun element; admission includes unlimited rides. A small but well-judged museum, easily combined with the Sandusky downtown waterfront on a non-Cedar Point day. Open daily in summer, weekends in winter.
