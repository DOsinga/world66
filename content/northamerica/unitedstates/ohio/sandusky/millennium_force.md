The headline coaster at Cedar Point — a 310-foot Intamin giga-coaster that drops at 80 degrees and reaches 93 mph. The cable-lift to the top is fast (no slow chain rattle) and the layout sends riders across the back of the peninsula with a long over-water section that is unlike anything else in the park.

The line is the longest in the park on any busy day; ride first thing in the morning or use the Fast Lane Plus pass. Multiple times rated the best steel coaster in the world by Amusement Today's Golden Ticket Awards.
