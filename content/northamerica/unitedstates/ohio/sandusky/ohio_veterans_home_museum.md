The Ohio Veterans Home Museum & Hall of Fame covers American military history through artifacts from the Civil War, World War I, World War II, Korea, Vietnam, and later conflicts. Its local angle is Johnson's Island, the nearby Civil War prisoner-of-war site, which gives the collection a Sandusky connection beyond a general veterans display.
