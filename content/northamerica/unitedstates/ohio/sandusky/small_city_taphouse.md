A craft-beer-focused taphouse in a converted downtown building on Columbus Avenue, with 30+ rotating taps weighted heavily toward Ohio breweries. The food menu is short but solid — soft pretzels, sausages, sandwiches — and designed to drink with rather than compete.

Bright wood interior, friendly bar staff. A good break from the resort bars near the park.
