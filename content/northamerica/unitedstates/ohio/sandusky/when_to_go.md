---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Sandusky is essentially a summer destination — Cedar Point opens in early May and closes in early November, and the Lake Erie Islands run a similar season.

**Mid-June to mid-August** is the peak, with all attractions open, ferries running their fullest schedule, lake water warm enough for swimming, and the longest park hours. Expect long queues at Cedar Point on weekends.

**Late August to mid-September** is the sweet spot — the kids are back in school, lines shrink dramatically, the weather is still warm, and the park's Halloweekends programme begins in mid-September with serious haunt productions.

**October** is the last good window — cool but tolerable, Halloweekends in full swing on weekends, brilliant fall colour around the lake.

**November to April** is the off-season; Cedar Point is closed, the islands are quiet, and most lakefront restaurants run shortened schedules. Sandusky does have some winter charm and is uncrowded — but the headline attractions are not in operation.
