The Age of Steam Roundhouse is the standout attraction in Sugarcreek. Its 18-stall brick roundhouse, turntable, locomotives, passenger cars, tools, and restoration work make it much more substantial than a roadside railroad display.

Tours are scheduled rather than casual walk-ins, so check the current calendar before you drive out. For anyone interested in rail history, this is the sight that lifts Sugarcreek above a simple photo stop.
