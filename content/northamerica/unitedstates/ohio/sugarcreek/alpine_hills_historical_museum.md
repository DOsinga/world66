Alpine Hills Historical Museum is the small museum that explains Sugarcreek's costume. Its exhibits cover Swiss and Amish settlers, local crafts, village life, and the everyday material culture behind the town's Little Switzerland identity.

It is a good first stop if the clock feels too cartoonish without context. The museum is downtown, easy to pair with the clock, and modest enough not to overstay its subject.
