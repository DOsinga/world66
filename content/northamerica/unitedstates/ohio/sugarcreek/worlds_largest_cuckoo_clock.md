Sugarcreek's giant cuckoo clock is big, silly, and completely central to the village's identity. The clock stands at Main Street and Broadway, where carved figures come out with music and dancing on the half hour.

It is not a deep sight, but it is exactly the sort of thing worth pulling over for in Amish Country. See it as part of a walk through the Swiss-style downtown rather than as a destination on its own.
