Collingwood Arts Center occupies a former convent in Toledo's Old West End, a neighbourhood already known for its grand old houses. The building now hosts performances, arts events, workshops, and occasional ghost-themed visits.

It is worth checking the calendar rather than just turning up. The appeal is as much the odd reuse of the building as the events themselves.
