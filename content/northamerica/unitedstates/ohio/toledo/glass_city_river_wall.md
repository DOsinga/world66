The Glass City River Wall turns a line of Maumee River grain silos into one of Toledo's most visible public artworks. The mural plays on the city's "Glass City" identity while giving an industrial riverfront structure a new civic role.

It is best seen as part of a drive, bike ride, or walk that takes in the river and nearby downtown sights. The scale is the point: this is not a gallery piece but a huge outdoor marker, and it helps make Toledo's working waterfront feel more legible to visitors.
