Queen of the Most Holy Rosary Cathedral is one of Toledo's architectural standouts, built in an ornate Spanish Plateresque style that feels unusual in northwest Ohio. Its carved stonework and detailed interior reward a slow look.

It is an active Catholic cathedral, so visit respectfully and check service times. Architecture-minded travellers should pair it with the city's museums and riverfront for a broader sense of Toledo's civic ambitions.
