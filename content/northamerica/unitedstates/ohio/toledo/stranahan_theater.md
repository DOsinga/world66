Stranahan Theater is one of northwest Ohio's main performing arts venues, hosting touring musicals, plays, concerts, and other stage events. It is not a casual sightseeing stop, but it is useful for visitors looking for an evening performance in Toledo.

Check the calendar before planning around it. For the right show, the theater can turn a practical overnight stop into a fuller city visit.
