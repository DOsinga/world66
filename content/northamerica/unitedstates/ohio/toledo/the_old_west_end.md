The Old West End is Toledo's best neighbourhood walk for architecture, a district of large late-nineteenth and early-twentieth-century houses built when the city was flush with industrial confidence. Turrets, porches, brickwork, stained glass, and deep lawns make it feel very different from the downtown grid.

It pairs naturally with the Toledo Museum of Art, which sits nearby and anchors the area for visitors. Go in daylight, walk slowly, and remember that these are lived-in streets rather than a museum village; the reward is seeing Toledo's old ambition house by house.
