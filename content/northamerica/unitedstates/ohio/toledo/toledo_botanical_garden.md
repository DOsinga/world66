Toledo Botanical Garden gives the city a calm, green counterweight to its museums and riverfront. The grounds include themed gardens, sculpture, seasonal plantings, and studio spaces tied to the local arts community.

It is strongest in the growing season, but the layout makes it pleasant for a walk whenever the weather cooperates. Pair it with the Toledo Museum of Art if you want a day that mixes outdoor space with serious culture.
