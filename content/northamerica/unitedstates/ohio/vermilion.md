Vermilion is a small harbour town on Lake Erie, west of [Cleveland](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/cleveland) and east of [Sandusky](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/sandusky). It sits where the Vermilion River reaches the lake, which gives the town its best feature: a compact waterfront of marinas, boat traffic, old captain's houses and summer foot traffic.

This is not a major Ohio destination, but it is a pleasant stop along the Lake Erie shore. The centre is easy to walk, with small shops and restaurants close to the harbour, and the river gives the town more character than the highway strips inland.

Vermilion works best in warm weather, when the municipal beach, docks and river cruises make sense. Pair it with the islands, Sandusky or Cleveland's west side if you are following the lake coast.
