Worthington Presbyterian Church anchors the north end of the Village Green and has stood at the civic heart of the town since its congregation was established in 1805. The current structure, built in 1837 in the Greek Revival style, is a clean and well-preserved example of the form: a broad pediment, a columned entry, and a restrained interior that reflects the Calvinist taste of its founders.

The church is one of the oldest continuously active congregations in central Ohio and a contributing structure in the Worthington Historic District. It is primarily a working church, not a museum, but the exterior is the most prominent historic building on the Green and worth a look as you pass through. Services are held on Sunday mornings; visitors are usually welcome at other times by arrangement.
