Yellow Springs is a small southwest Ohio village with more personality than its size suggests. Antioch College, independent shops, a long activist streak, and a walkable downtown give it a clear identity.

The strongest reason to come is the mix of village life and nearby nature. [Glen Helen Nature Preserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/yellow_springs/glen_helen_nature_preserve) starts close to town, while [Clifton Gorge](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/yellow_springs/clifton_gorge) and [John Bryan State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/yellow_springs/john_bryan_state_park) make the area one of the better walking and hiking pockets in western Ohio.

Yellow Springs works best as a relaxed day from Dayton or Columbus: a morning on the trails, lunch or coffee downtown, then a slow browse through shops or a film at the [Little Art Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/ohio/yellow_springs/little_art_theatre). Warm weekends can be crowded, but weekdays keep the place easier to enjoy.
