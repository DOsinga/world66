Clifton Gorge is a narrow, rocky nature preserve east of Yellow Springs. Trails follow the Little Miami River through limestone cuts and wooded slopes, giving the area a sharper landscape than much of southwest Ohio.

It pairs naturally with John Bryan State Park next door. Together they make the strongest outdoor reason to spend a day around Yellow Springs.
