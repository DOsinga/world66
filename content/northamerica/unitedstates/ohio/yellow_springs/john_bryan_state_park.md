John Bryan State Park spreads around the Little Miami River just outside Yellow Springs. It is useful for longer walks after Clifton Gorge, with trails, river scenery, camping, biking, and climbing areas.

The park is not dramatic in a western-mountain sense, but for this part of Ohio it gives the day real outdoor weight.
