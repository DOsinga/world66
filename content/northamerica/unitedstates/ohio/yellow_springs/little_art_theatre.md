The Little Art Theatre is a small downtown cinema that has been part of Yellow Springs since 1929. It shows a mix of independent and mainstream films, which suits the village better than a standard multiplex would.

It is not the reason to plan a whole trip, but it is exactly the kind of local anchor that makes Yellow Springs feel lived-in.
