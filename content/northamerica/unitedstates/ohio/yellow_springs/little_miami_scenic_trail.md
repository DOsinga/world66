The Little Miami Scenic Trail runs through Yellow Springs and makes the village easy to fold into a bike day. The paved rail trail links north and south through the Miami Valley, with Xenia a manageable ride away.

Even if you are not cycling far, the trail helps explain the town's easy weekend rhythm. People arrive by bike, stop downtown, and drift back onto the path.
