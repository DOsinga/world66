Stambaugh Auditorium is one of Youngstown's great civic buildings, a limestone concert hall opened in 1926 on a rise north of downtown. Its neoclassical facade, broad steps, and formal interior belong to the era when industrial cities built public culture on a grand scale, and the auditorium still hosts concerts, lectures, weddings, and community events.

It is worth pairing with nearby Wick Park or the Butler Institute if you are exploring Youngstown's North Side. The building rewards anyone interested in architecture, but it is best experienced with a performance, when the main hall feels less like a preserved monument and more like the civic room it was meant to be.
