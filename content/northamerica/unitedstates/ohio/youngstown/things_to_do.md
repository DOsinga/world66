---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Youngstown's most rewarding cultural attraction is the Butler Institute of American Art, a world-class collection housed in a building designed by McKim, Mead and White. The Youngstown Historical Center of Industry and Labor — locally known as the "steel museum" — tells the story of the city's rise and catastrophic fall with impressive candor. Mill Creek MetroParks offers genuine outdoor recreation in the middle of the metro area, with gorge trails, a rose garden, and ponds.
