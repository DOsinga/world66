Youngstown Playhouse is a long-running community theatre that has been part of the city's cultural life for generations. Its productions, workshops, and local performers give Youngstown a performing-arts anchor beyond the university and downtown event spaces.

It is worth considering if a show is running during your visit. The appeal is local and human-scale: an evening here tells you something about the city's persistence and creative life that industrial-history stops cannot show alone.
