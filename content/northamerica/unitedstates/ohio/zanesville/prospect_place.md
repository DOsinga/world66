Prospect Place is a large nineteenth-century mansion near Trinway, north of Zanesville, associated with abolitionist George Willison Adams and local Underground Railroad history. Its scale, hilltop setting, and restoration story make it a more substantial stop than a simple historic house marker.

The mansion is not a walk-up attraction every day, so check tour availability before driving out. It suits travellers interested in Ohio history, preservation projects, or regional stories that sit outside the better-known riverfront sights in Zanesville itself.
