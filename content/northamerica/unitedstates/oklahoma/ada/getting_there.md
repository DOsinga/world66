---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Ada has no commercial air service. The nearest airports with regular flights are **Oklahoma City's Will Rogers World Airport** (about 140 km / 87 miles north-west) and **Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport** (about 225 km / 140 miles south). Most visitors arrive by car.

**By car**, Ada sits at the junction of US Highway 177 and State Highway 3. From Oklahoma City, take I-35 south to Pauls Valley, then US-177 east — about 90 minutes. From Dallas, take US-75 north to Atoka and then State Highway 3 west — around two hours depending on traffic through the northern Dallas suburbs.

**By bus**, Greyhound and regional carriers provide limited service to Ada, with connections through Oklahoma City and Dallas. The schedule is infrequent and journey times are long; a car is strongly recommended for this part of Oklahoma.

There is no passenger rail service to Ada.
