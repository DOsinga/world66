---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Ada's sights are modest but genuine. The downtown area contains a cluster of well-preserved 1920s and 1930s buildings, and Wintersmith Park offers one of the better public green spaces in this part of Oklahoma. For anyone with an interest in Native American history and culture, the proximity to both the Chickasaw Nation tribal complex and the Chickasaw Cultural Center in Sulphur makes Ada a useful base.
