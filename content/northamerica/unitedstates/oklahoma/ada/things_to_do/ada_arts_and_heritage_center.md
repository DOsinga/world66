Housed in a Colonial Revival building at 400 South Rennie Street, the Ada Arts and Heritage Center serves as the city's main cultural venue for local history exhibits and rotating art shows. The building itself — a handsome Colonial Revival structure on the National Register of Historic Places — is as much a draw as the contents. Exhibits cover Pontotoc County history, Chickasaw heritage, and regional art, giving visitors a grounded sense of what shaped this corner of Oklahoma.
