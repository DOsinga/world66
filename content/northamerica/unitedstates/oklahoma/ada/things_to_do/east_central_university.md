East Central University is a public university founded in 1909 that gives Ada much of its civic identity. The campus sits on the north-east side of downtown, and its buildings and green spaces are open to visitors. ECU has a particular strength in education and nursing programs and draws students from across south-central Oklahoma and north Texas. The campus is pleasant to walk through and occasionally hosts public events, lectures, and athletic competitions worth checking in on.
