The Pontotoc County Courthouse anchors the downtown square and is the most prominent civic building in Ada. Listed on the National Register of Historic Places, it represents the kind of confident public architecture that county seats across Oklahoma erected in the early twentieth century. The building serves as a useful landmark for orienting yourself in the downtown area, and the surrounding square retains some of the commercial streetscape that once characterised the town centre.
