The Sugg Clinic is one of the finest examples of Streamline Moderne architecture in Oklahoma, a style that swept through American commercial design in the 1930s with its aerodynamic curves and horizontal emphasis. Listed on the National Register of Historic Places, the building stands as a reminder of the confident modernism that arrived even in small Oklahoma cities during the Depression era. It sits close to the downtown core and is easy to take in on a walking loop of the historic district.
