The real attractions in this corner of Oklahoma sit outside town. Two natural sights in particular justify a visit to Alva.

The [Salt Plains National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/salt_plains_national_wildlife_refuge), about 40 miles southeast, is unlike anywhere else in the state. You can dig for hourglass selenite crystals on the salt flats (April through mid-October), watch sandhill and whooping cranes on migration, and drive through a landscape that looks more like a salt desert than the landlocked plains you might expect. Give it a half day at minimum.

[Alabaster Caverns State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/alabaster_caverns_state_park), about 30 miles south near Freedom, contains one of the largest natural gypsum caves open to the public in the world. Guided tours run hourly and last about 45 minutes; the cave stays at 50°F year-round. A bonus in summer is the bat emergence at dusk.
