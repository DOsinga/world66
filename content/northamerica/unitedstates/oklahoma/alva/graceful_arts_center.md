The Graceful Arts Center occupies a restored historic building just off the courthouse square. It runs rotating exhibitions of work by Oklahoma artists, holds classes and workshops, and hosts occasional readings and concerts. For a town of this size it punches well above its weight.

Check the schedule before you visit — programming is busiest during the university semester.
