---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ardmore's dining scene reflects its role as a regional hub for Carter County. You'll find a reliable mix of locally owned diners, barbecue joints, and Tex-Mex spots alongside the usual chain restaurants that line US-77 and I-35. The downtown area has seen modest renewal, with a handful of cafes and casual restaurants within walking distance of the historic courthouse square.

For a taste of local color, look for the small family-run Mexican restaurants that serve the working population of south-central Oklahoma. Barbecue is well represented — Oklahoma-style brisket and ribs are available at several smokehouses around town. If you are driving through on I-35, the exit at US-77 (Exit 29) has the densest cluster of options.
