---
title: "Hardy Murphy Coliseum"
type: poi
---

Hardy Murphy Coliseum is a large indoor arena in Ardmore that serves as the main events venue for Carter County and the surrounding region. Built in the 1950s and named after a prominent local rancher and civic figure, it has a capacity of several thousand and hosts a varied calendar of rodeos, livestock shows, concerts, and sporting events throughout the year.

The coliseum is especially busy during the annual Carter County Free Fair and the Ardmore Championship Rodeo, both of which draw participants and spectators from across south-central Oklahoma and north Texas. If you are visiting in late summer or fall, checking the coliseum schedule is worthwhile — a small-town rodeo or livestock show offers an authentic window into the agricultural culture that still underpins life in this part of Oklahoma. The facility is located on the north side of Ardmore and is easy to reach by car.
