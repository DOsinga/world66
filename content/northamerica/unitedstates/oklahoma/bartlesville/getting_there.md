---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By road:** Bartlesville is 47 miles north of Tulsa on US-75. From Tulsa International Airport, the drive takes about 45 minutes. Tulsa is the nearest major airport with good connections.

**By air:** Bartlesville Municipal Airport (BVO) handles private and charter aircraft but has no scheduled commercial service.

A car is essential for getting around — Bartlesville is a compact city but Woolaroc is 14 miles out, and there is no public transport worth using.
