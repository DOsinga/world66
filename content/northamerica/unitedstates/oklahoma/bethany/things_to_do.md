---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Bethany's draw is the Route 66 corridor through town, the historic Southern Nazarene University campus, and the Oklahoma Museum of Flying at Wiley Post Airport. Add Eldon Lyon Park for a riverside picnic and a couple of mid-century roadside survivors along the old alignment, and you have a half-day's poking around for a Route 66 traveller.
