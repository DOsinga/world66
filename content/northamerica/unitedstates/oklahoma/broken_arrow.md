Broken Arrow is the largest suburb of [Tulsa](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa), sitting just southeast of the city in eastern Tulsa County. The city is named for a Creek Native American community that relocated here from Alabama on the Trail of Tears — the name derives from the Muscogee word "Rekackv." With a population pushing past 115,000, it has grown into a proper city in its own right, though most visitors will use it as a base for exploring the Tulsa area.

The [Rose District](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/broken_arrow/rose_district) downtown has a handful of restaurants, boutiques, and a farmers' market worth wandering, anchored by the [Broken Arrow Performing Arts Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/broken_arrow/performing_arts_center) — whose main stage is named for Kristin Chenoweth, the Tony Award-winning actress who was born here. The [Ray Harral Nature Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/broken_arrow/ray_harral_nature_center) on the western edge of the city provides a quiet green retreat. Antique hunters will find the Persimmon Hollow Antique Village on South 115th East Avenue a serious operation with dozens of dealers.
