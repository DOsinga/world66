Beavers Bend Wildlife Museum is a modest natural-history stop near the outdoor recreation area that brings most visitors to Broken Bow. Displays focus on regional animals, habitat, and mounted specimens, giving names and context to what you may see in the surrounding woods and water.

It is best used as a supplement to Beavers Bend State Park, not as the main event. On a rainy spell or with children, it can add a useful hour to a trip otherwise built around cabins, trails, fishing, and the lake.
