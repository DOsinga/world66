---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Broken Bow has no commercial flights or train service. The town sits at the junction of Highway 70 and Highway 259, roughly three hours northeast of Dallas, three hours southeast of Oklahoma City, and three hours south of Tulsa. Most visitors drive.

The closest commercial airports are Texarkana (about 90 minutes south), Shreveport (two and a half hours), and the major Dallas/Fort Worth and Oklahoma City hubs. There is no public transport once you arrive — a car is essential for moving between the town, Hochatown, the park, and your cabin.
