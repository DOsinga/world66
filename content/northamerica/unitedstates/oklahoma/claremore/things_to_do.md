Claremore's two main draws are both on Will Rogers Boulevard, which follows the original Route 66 alignment through town. The [Will Rogers Memorial Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/claremore/will_rogers_memorial) on the hill northwest of downtown is the more significant of the two, a genuinely good small museum that covers a genuinely interesting man. The [J. M. Davis Arms and Historical Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/claremore/jm_davis_arms_museum) is one of the largest privately assembled gun collections in the world — a niche draw but a substantial one.

For Route 66 atmosphere, the [Claremore Auto Dealership](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/claremore/claremore_auto_dealership) on Will Rogers Boulevard is a preserved 1930s Streamline Moderne building that is both a historic landmark and still in use as a car dealership. The old downtown on Will Rogers Boulevard has several period-appropriate buildings and the low-key buzz of a county seat town that knows its history without overdoing the presentation.

Will Rogers himself was born not in Claremore but at the Dog Iron Ranch near Oologah, a few miles north — the birthplace is preserved and visitable for anyone wanting to complete the Will Rogers trail.
