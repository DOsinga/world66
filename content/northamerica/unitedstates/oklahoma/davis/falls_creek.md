Falls Creek is best known as a large Baptist conference center tucked into the wooded hills southeast of Davis. It is not a casual tourist attraction in the same way Turner Falls is, but the scale of the place tells you something about Oklahoma's church-camp culture.

Visitors usually encounter it from the roads around the Arbuckles rather than as a destination in itself. If you are interested in regional history, it is worth noting as one of the largest and longest-running religious camp complexes in the state.
