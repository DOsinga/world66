---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Davis sits just east of I-35 in Murray County, roughly halfway between Oklahoma City and the Texas line. Driving is the sensible option; exits off the interstate put you within minutes of town and Turner Falls.

There is no useful rail or air service for visitors. If you are flying in, rent a car in Oklahoma City, Dallas-Fort Worth, or Wichita Falls and treat Davis as part of a road trip through the Arbuckles.
