Treasure Valley Casino sits just west of Davis near I-35, making it one of the more visible modern landmarks around town. It mainly serves road traffic and regional visitors rather than travelers crossing Oklahoma for scenery.

If you like small casinos, it can fill an evening after the parks close. If not, treat it as a landmark on the drive and spend your daylight in the Arbuckles.
