Turner Falls is the reason most travelers know Davis. The 77-foot waterfall drops into a broad swimming area framed by limestone cliffs, with walking paths, caves, and picnic spots spreading through the canyon below.

It is beautiful, but it is not a secret. On hot weekends the park can feel more like a water park than a wilderness retreat, and entry rules change seasonally. Go early, bring water shoes, and check current access before building a whole day around it.
