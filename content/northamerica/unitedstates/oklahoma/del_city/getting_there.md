---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Del City is immediately east of Oklahoma City, about 15 minutes from downtown by car. It sits at the intersection of I-40 and SE 15th Street, and is easily accessed from the I-40/I-35 junction — one of the main crossroads for long-distance driving through central Oklahoma.

The nearest airport is Will Rogers World Airport, about 25 minutes west. There is no commercial rail service to Del City. Oklahoma City's public bus network (EMBARK) serves parts of the metro area, but a car is the practical way to get around Del City itself.
