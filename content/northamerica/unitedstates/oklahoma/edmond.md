Edmond sits just north of Oklahoma City, close enough to the capital to share its commuting rhythm but old enough to have its own downtown and university life. It began as a stop on the Santa Fe Railway and later became one of the better-known towns along Oklahoma's stretch of Route 66.

The city is tidy, spread out, and comfortable rather than dramatic. Its strongest travel case is a mix of [University of Central Oklahoma](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/edmond/university_of_central_oklahoma), small historic buildings, public art, and quick access to Lake Arcadia. It works best for travelers tracing Route 66, visiting family or campus, or looking for a calmer base on the north side of Oklahoma City.

Downtown Edmond has enough restaurants and shops for a pleasant walk, but you will want a car. Distances are suburban, and the most useful sights sit in separate pockets rather than along one compact tourist route.
