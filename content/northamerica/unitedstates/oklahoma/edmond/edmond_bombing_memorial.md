The Edmond Bombing Memorial is tied to the 1995 Oklahoma City bombing and the victims from Edmond. It is a quiet, local place of remembrance rather than a major museum.

Visit only with the right tone. For travelers, it helps connect Edmond to the larger story of Oklahoma City and the way the bombing affected communities across the metro area.
