Old North Tower is the historic face of the University of Central Oklahoma. The building's surviving tower and restored fabric are a useful reminder that Edmond's campus history reaches back to territorial Oklahoma.

It is not a long stop, but it gives a walk around campus a focal point. Pair it with the nearby downtown streets rather than treating it as a standalone attraction.
