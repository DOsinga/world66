  El Reno once had its own electric street railway, which ran from 1911 to 1947. The current Heritage Express trolley is a fully working reproduction, hand-built locally on the original gauge, and uses a short restored section of track from the railroad depot west into downtown. Volunteer drivers ring the bell and narrate, in a Route 66 way.
---

The Heritage Express is a working replica trolley that runs weekend afternoons through the warmer months between the railroad museum and central downtown. The route is short — a few blocks — but the experience is closer to a small-town parade than to a transit ride.

Tickets are sold from the museum's depot ticket office. The trolley does not run in winter and is weather-dependent in the shoulder seasons; check the museum's schedule before you turn up.
