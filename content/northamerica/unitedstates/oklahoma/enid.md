Enid sits in the wheat plains of northwestern Oklahoma, the seat of Garfield County and the state's ninth-largest city. It grew up around grain — the massive elevators along the railroad tracks make that clear at a glance — and it remains one of the largest grain storage centres in the United States. The downtown square, anchored by the [Garfield County Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/enid/garfield_county_courthouse), has a quiet, unhurried feel that is typical of small-city Oklahoma.

There is more here than grain. The [Cherokee Strip Regional Heritage Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/enid/cherokee_strip_heritage_center) is one of the better regional history museums in Oklahoma, covering the 1893 land run that founded the city in a single day. The [Railroad Museum of Oklahoma](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/enid/railroad_museum_oklahoma) occupies the restored Santa Fe freight depot. And the [Enid Symphony Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/enid/enid_symphony_center), housed in a 1924 Masonic temple, hosts the state's second-oldest orchestra. Astronaut Owen K. Garriott — who flew on both Skylab and the Space Shuttle — grew up here.
