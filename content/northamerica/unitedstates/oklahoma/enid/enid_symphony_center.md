The Enid Symphony Center — housed in the historic Enid Masonic Temple / Cole Building on the main downtown square — is the home of the Enid Symphony Orchestra, one of the longest-established orchestras in Oklahoma. The building dates from 1924 and is an imposing Classical Revival structure that dominates its block.

The symphony season runs from September through May, with a mix of classical and popular programmes. The venue also hosts theatrical productions and community events. Check the schedule if you are in town on a weekend — the quality of performance is consistently above what you might expect in a city this size.
