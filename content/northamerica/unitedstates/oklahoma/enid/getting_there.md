---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Enid is on US Highway 412 about 100 kilometres (60 miles) north of Oklahoma City, roughly an hour's drive north on US-81 or US-60. There is no commercial airline service at Woodring Airport (WDG), which serves only general aviation. The nearest commercial airports are Oklahoma City Will Rogers World Airport (OKC, about 110 km south) and Tulsa International (TUL, about 160 km east).

No passenger rail serves Enid. Bus service is limited; most visitors arrive by car.
