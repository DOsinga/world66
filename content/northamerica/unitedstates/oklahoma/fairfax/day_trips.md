Almost everything that draws visitors to Fairfax is actually within a short drive: the larger Osage town of [Pawhuska](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/pawhuska) (25 miles east, with the Osage Nation Museum, the Pioneer Woman Mercantile and the gateway to the **Tallgrass Prairie Preserve**), and [Ponca City](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/ponca_city) (45 miles north-west, with the Pioneer Woman Museum and the **Marland Mansion**).
