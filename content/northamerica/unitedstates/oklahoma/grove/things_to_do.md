---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Grove's draw is the lake and a handful of unusual local sights — chief among them Har-Ber Village, an enormous open-air collection of antiques and reconstructed pioneer buildings, and Lendonwood Gardens, a quiet botanical garden built around one of the largest rhododendron collections in the central United States. Beyond that, Grand Lake itself is what brings most visitors: boating, fishing, and lakeside parks within a few minutes' drive.
