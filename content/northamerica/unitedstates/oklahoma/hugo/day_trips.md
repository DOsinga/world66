---
title: Day Trips from Hugo
type: section
---

Southeastern Oklahoma is lake and hill country with several destinations worth a day's drive from Hugo.

**Beavers Bend State Park** is about 130 kilometres east, near Broken Bow. The Mountain Fork River runs cold and clear through dense pine forest — dramatically different from the surrounding Oklahoma landscape. The park has canoeing, trout fishing, hiking trails, and a nature centre. One of the most scenically impressive state parks in Oklahoma and worth the drive.

**Tishomingo National Wildlife Refuge** lies about 80 kilometres northwest, at the confluence of the Washita and Red rivers. The refuge protects wetlands important to migrating waterfowl and is a good stop for birdwatchers, particularly in autumn during waterfowl migration.

**Durant and the Choctaw Nation** — Durant, about 90 kilometres west on US 70, is the headquarters of the Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma and has a tribal museum and cultural centre worth visiting for context on the deep Choctaw history of this part of the state. The region around Hugo is within the historic Choctaw homeland.

**Sardis Lake** is about 60 kilometres north and slightly west, near Wilburton. A large Corps of Engineers reservoir surrounded by wooded hills, good for fishing and boating, and notably less visited than more famous Oklahoma lakes.
