---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Most dining in Jenks clusters along the Riverwalk Crossing development, where a string of casual restaurants faces the Arkansas River. The setting — outdoor seating, river views, proximity to the aquarium — is what drives traffic here more than any single standout kitchen. For a more local experience, the older commercial strip along Main Street has a handful of diners and barbecue joints that predate the Riverwalk development by decades.
