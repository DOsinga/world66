---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Jenks is about six miles south of downtown [Tulsa](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa) on US-75. The drive takes fifteen minutes in normal traffic. There is no practical public transit connection from Tulsa. Almost everyone arrives by car, and the town is easy to navigate — the Oklahoma Aquarium and Riverwalk Crossing are signed from the highway, and the antique strip along Main Street is a short drive west of the river.

Tulsa International Airport is about twelve miles north. Rental cars are the sensible choice if you are combining Jenks with a broader [Oklahoma](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma) road trip.
