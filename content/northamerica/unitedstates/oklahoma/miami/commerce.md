The next town up Route 66 from Miami is Commerce, the boyhood home of New York Yankees great Mickey Mantle. A nine-foot bronze statue of "The Commerce Comet" in mid-swing stands outside the high-school baseball field at North Mickey Mantle Boulevard.

Mantle's modest childhood house at 319 South Quincy Street has been restored and bears a marker. Bring a baseball; people often leave them at the base of the statue.
