---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Miami has a small but solid eating scene. **Waylan's Ku-Ku Burger**, a 1965 burger shack on Main Street topped with a vintage "Ku-Ku" clock-shaped sign, is the obvious Route 66 stop — order a double cheeseburger, a side of fries and a chocolate malt. Good diner food is also served at the **Pork Belly Eatery** on Main Street and at **Miami Coal House**, a barbecue place a few blocks south.

For a sit-down dinner, **Brentwood Wine Bistro** in the historic district has the most ambitious kitchen in town, with steaks, salads and a respectable Oklahoma wine list. Most places close early; do not expect to eat after about 9 pm.
