---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable times in northeastern Oklahoma — warm days, cool nights and dramatic skies. Summer is hot, humid and prone to violent thunderstorms; this is the western edge of Tornado Alley and severe-weather watches are common between April and June.

If you can time your visit, the **Miami Heritage Festival** at the Coleman Theatre and the **NEO Rodeo** in the autumn are the most distinctive local events. Winter is usually mild but ice storms can close roads for a day or two at a time.
