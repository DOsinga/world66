---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Moore's restaurant landscape is dominated by chain restaurants along I-35 and 19th Street — the usual suburban roster of Texas Roadhouse, Cracker Barrel, Chick-fil-A, and the like. Look harder and you can find a handful of locally owned places worth the effort. For real Oklahoma City dining variety, the short drive north to the Plaza District, Western Avenue, or downtown OKC is hard to beat.
