Orr Family Farm is a farm-themed family attraction on the south side of the Oklahoma City metro area. It has play areas, seasonal activities, animal encounters, rides, and paid extras such as pony rides, with a stronger focus on children than on agricultural history.

It is most useful for families based in Moore or south Oklahoma City who want an easy half-day outing. Check the seasonal calendar before going, since the best experience depends on what activities are running.
