A small but serious museum dedicated to the **Cherokee, Chickasaw, Choctaw, Muscogee (Creek), and Seminole** nations — the five tribes the US government coerced into adopting European-style governments and farming, and then forcibly relocated to what is now eastern Oklahoma. The collection is strong on early-20th-century tribal art (paintings, beadwork, textiles) and rotating exhibitions feature contemporary Native artists. The handsome 1875 limestone building is itself an artefact. Allow 90 minutes.
