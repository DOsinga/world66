Downtown Muskogee's most distinctive feature is a tight cluster of early-1910s commercial buildings the National Register collectively designates the **Pre-Depression Muskogee Skyscrapers**. None taller than ten stories but all handsomely detailed, they include the **Manhattan Building** (1911), the **Surety Building** (1910), the **Severs Hotel** (1910), the **Baltimore Hotel** (1912), and the **Railway Exchange Building** (1912). A walk of about fifteen minutes covers all five. Some are restored, others vacant; the Severs in particular is in need of a tenant.
