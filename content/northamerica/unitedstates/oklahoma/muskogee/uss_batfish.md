A US Navy Balao-class submarine sitting on dry land north of Muskogee, three blocks from the Arkansas River. Visitors clamber down the conning tower hatch and walk the length of the boat through the torpedo rooms, the control room, the galley, and the cramped crew quarters. The grounds outside hold a memorial wall, a Vietnam-era patrol boat, and a small museum of submarine and naval history. An unexpected, slightly surreal experience for a small Oklahoma town and a worthwhile 90 minutes.
