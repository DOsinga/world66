---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Most travellers reach Norman via Will Rogers World Airport (OKC) in Oklahoma City, about 35 minutes north by car. Major US carriers serve OKC with direct flights from most hub cities. From the airport, rideshare to Norman runs about $40; rental cars and several shuttle services are available.

Amtrak's Heartland Flyer stops at Norman station downtown on its daily Fort Worth–Oklahoma City run — a useful option for visitors coming up from Texas. By car, Norman sits directly on Interstate 35, two hours north of the Texas border and four hours south of Wichita, Kansas.
