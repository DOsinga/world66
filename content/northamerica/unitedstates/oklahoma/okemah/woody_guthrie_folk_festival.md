Held over four days around Guthrie's birthday in mid-July, WoodyFest is a free, volunteer-run festival that takes over much of downtown Okemah. Main concerts happen at the Crystal Theatre and the Pastures of Plenty outdoor stage; impromptu jams break out on every porch and bar patio in town from morning to night.

The lineup leans toward folk, alt-country, and roots — singer-songwriters who take Guthrie's tradition seriously, with the occasional surprise from a bigger name. The crowd is a mix of Oklahoma locals, ageing folkies, and a younger generation of Americana fans. Book accommodation months in advance; Okemah has limited rooms and they fill up fast.
