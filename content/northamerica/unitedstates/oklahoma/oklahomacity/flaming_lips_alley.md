A short, deliberately weird pedestrian alley between East Sheridan and the Bricktown Canal, dedicated to Oklahoma City's longest-serving rock band the Flaming Lips. The walls are painted with murals referencing the band's work — the giant smiley face from their album covers, dancing alien suits, a stylised mountain of speakers — and the alley feeds visitors out toward the canal and the rest of Bricktown.

A quick stop on the way to a meal in Bricktown. The alley sometimes hosts impromptu music events; check city listings if you're interested.
