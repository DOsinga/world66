A small Western-themed amusement park on the north edge of the city, with around forty rides spread out across a faux frontier town. Nothing about it competes with the big Six Flags parks elsewhere in the country, but the wooden Wildcat roller coaster is a perfectly respectable old-school ride and the water-park area is welcome relief from Oklahoma's brutal summer heat.

Open weekends from spring and daily through summer. Aimed firmly at families with kids under twelve, and best enjoyed in that spirit; older teens may find it limited.
