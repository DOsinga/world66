The neighbourhood immediately north of downtown along North Walker and 10th Street, anchored by the restored Plaza Court building and a cluster of small streets full of brick warehouses converted into restaurants, breweries, and small offices. Midtown was abandoned in the postwar decades and has been steadily revived since the early 2000s.

The result is one of the city's better dinner-and-drinks neighbourhoods — denser than Bricktown, less obvious, and slightly more sophisticated. Several of the best independent restaurants in Oklahoma are within a five-minute walk of each other here.
