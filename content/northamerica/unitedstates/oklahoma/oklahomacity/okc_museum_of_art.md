A surprisingly strong art museum for a city this size. The permanent collection runs from European Old Masters through American post-war painting, and there is a particularly good gallery of contemporary photography on the third floor. The Chihuly collection — twenty-two works by the glass artist, including the enormous tower in the rotunda — is the standout, and worth the visit on its own.

The museum is small enough to do thoroughly in two hours. The ground-floor cinema is one of Oklahoma's main venues for independent and foreign films, and the rooftop terrace offers good views over the downtown skyline. Closed Mondays.
