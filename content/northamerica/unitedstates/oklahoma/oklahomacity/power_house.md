A craft cocktail bar inside a small brick electrical substation in the heart of Bricktown — one of the original 1908 power plant buildings preserved through the canal redevelopment. The interior is dark and intimate, the cocktail list runs to about forty drinks built around local Oklahoma spirits, and the small kitchen turns out elevated bar food.

A good escape from the louder Bricktown bars on the canal. Open evenings only, closed Mondays.
