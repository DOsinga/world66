---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Oklahoma City punches above its weight for a Plains city. The unmissable sights are the **Oklahoma City National Memorial** (allow at least two hours, ideally with the museum) and the **National Cowboy & Western Heritage Museum** — easily the best Western museum in the country. Add the spectacular new **First Americans Museum**, devoted to the thirty-nine tribes headquartered in Oklahoma, and a contender for one of the most important new American museums of the last decade.

For an evening, head to **Bricktown** for the canal, the Land Run Monument, and a brewpub dinner; or to the **Plaza District** for something less obvious. The **Stockyards** livestock auction (Monday and Tuesday mornings) is a genuine piece of American West history still in operation, and Cattlemen's Steakhouse next door is the city's signature meal.

For something different, the city has a remarkable density of small specialist museums — banjos, pigeons, women pilots, osteology, firefighting, Western art — most of which are free or cheap and worth a side visit if the topic catches you.
