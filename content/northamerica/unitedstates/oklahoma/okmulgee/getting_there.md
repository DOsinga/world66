---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Okmulgee is on US-75 about 40 miles south of [Tulsa](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa) and 90 miles east of Oklahoma City. From Tulsa the drive is 45 minutes through farmland and small oil-patch towns; from Oklahoma City take I-40 east to Henryetta and then US-75 north, about 90 minutes.

The nearest commercial airport is Tulsa International, with a wide range of domestic flights. Okmulgee Regional Airport handles only general aviation. There is no scheduled rail or intercity bus service to the town, so most visitors arrive by car.
