---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Oologah is about 50 kilometres northeast of Tulsa on US-169. From Tulsa International Airport, take US-75 North and then head east on OK-88 — the drive takes around 45 minutes. There is no public transport to speak of; a car is essential.

The Will Rogers Memorial Museum in [Claremore](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/claremore), the more comprehensive of the two Rogers sites, is about 20 kilometres south and makes a natural companion stop.
