Oologah is Will Rogers country, and most of what draws visitors here connects to the man who grew up on this land. The birthplace ranch west of town is the main attraction, though the Historical Museum in town and the lake itself are both worth your time. This is a small, quiet Oklahoma town — do not expect a full day's itinerary, but what is here is genuinely good.
