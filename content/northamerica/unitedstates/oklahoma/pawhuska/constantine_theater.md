The Constantine Theatre is one of Pawhuska's best-preserved early 20th-century buildings, a reminder of the brief period when Osage oil wealth flowed into downtown construction and civic improvement. The building stands on Agency Hill Road in the old town centre and is a tangible link to the prosperity that made the Osage a target.

The theatre now functions as a community venue and is occasionally used for screenings and events. Even if nothing is programmed during your visit, the exterior is worth looking at as part of a walk through the historic downtown.
