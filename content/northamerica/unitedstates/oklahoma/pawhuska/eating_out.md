---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The Pioneer Woman Mercantile is the dominant draw for food in Pawhuska, but the town has a small collection of local diners and cafes that serve straightforward American and Oklahoma cooking. After a morning at the Tallgrass Prairie Preserve, the Mercantile's pot pie or a burger at a local spot makes a satisfying midday stop before the drive back.
