The Osage County Historical Society museum gathers local material from Pawhuska and the surrounding county: ranching, oil wealth, early settlement, Osage history and the civic life of a small Oklahoma county seat. It is modest, but it fills in context that bigger attractions can skip.

Visit it alongside the Osage Nation Museum for a more rounded sense of place. Pawhuska's story is complicated and layered; even a small local-history museum helps connect the town's storefronts and courthouse streets to the wider county.
