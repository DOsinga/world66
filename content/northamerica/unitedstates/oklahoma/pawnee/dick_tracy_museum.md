The Chester Gould-Dick Tracy Museum in downtown Pawnee celebrates the birthplace of one of American comics' most influential characters. Chester Gould, who created Dick Tracy in 1931 and drew the strip for 46 years, was born here in 1900. The museum covers Gould's life, the history of the strip, the villains he invented (many of whom are still cultural touchstones), and the various film and television adaptations.

Small but well-organized, with original artwork and strip archives on display. The building is in the historic courthouse square area, making it an easy combination with a look at the Pawnee County Courthouse. Budget an hour.
