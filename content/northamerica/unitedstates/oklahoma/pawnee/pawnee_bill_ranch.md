Pawnee Bill Ranch is an Oklahoma state historic site preserving the home and grounds of Gordon W. Lillie, the showman who built one of the great Wild West shows of the late 19th century. The 280-acre property includes the original 1910 ranch house (an imposing Mission Revival building that Lillie built at the height of his wealth), the Blue Hawk Peak mansion, and working herds of bison, longhorns, and American draft horses.

The museum in the ranch house tells the story of Lillie's life and the Wild West show era with a good collection of original posters, costumes, and photographs. Free-ranging bison on the property make it feel more alive than a typical historic house museum. On summer weekends the ranch hosts a free Wild West show with trick riding, roping, and mounted shooting — check the schedule before visiting. Admission to the site is free; donations appreciated.
