The Ponca City City Hall on East Grand Avenue is one of the more elegant Public Works Administration projects from the New Deal era — a 1936 Spanish-revival building of buff brick, with arched window arcades and a tiled tower. The interior includes an auditorium with original WPA murals.

The building is still in use as the city's main administrative office and is open during business hours; the lobby and auditorium are accessible to visitors.
