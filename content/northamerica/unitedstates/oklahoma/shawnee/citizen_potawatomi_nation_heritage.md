The Citizen Potawatomi Nation is headquartered in Shawnee, and its Cultural Heritage Center presents the tribe's history, traditions, and contemporary life with genuine depth. The exhibits cover the Potawatomi's origins in the Great Lakes region, the devastating forced removal of 1838 (called the Potawatomi Trail of Death), and the tribe's reconstruction in Oklahoma. Contemporary exhibits address the tribe's economic and cultural revival in the twentieth and twenty-first centuries.

The center occupies a modern building on the tribal complex south of the city. It is free to visit. The tribal complex also includes Fire Lake Grand Casino and resort facilities, but the heritage center is a distinctly separate and more substantive draw — worth allocating an hour or more.
