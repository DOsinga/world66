Bennett Memorial Chapel is a small, graceful campus chapel at Oklahoma State University. It is most useful as a quiet architectural pause during a walk through the busier parts of campus.

There is no need to plan a trip around it. But if you are already near Old Central or the library, it adds a calmer note to the OSU circuit.
