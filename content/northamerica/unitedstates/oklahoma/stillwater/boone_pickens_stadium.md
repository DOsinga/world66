Boone Pickens Stadium is Stillwater's loudest landmark on autumn Saturdays. Oklahoma State football turns the campus orange and fills the streets with tailgates, alumni, and the kind of small-city traffic jam that tells you a game matters.

Even outside football season, the stadium is worth a look if you are walking campus. Its scale explains how strongly OSU athletics shape Stillwater's calendar and economy.
