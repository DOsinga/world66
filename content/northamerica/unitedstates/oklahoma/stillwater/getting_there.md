---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Stillwater is in north-central Oklahoma, roughly 65 miles northeast of Oklahoma City and 70 miles west of Tulsa. Most visitors arrive by car via US-177, OK-51, or nearby turnpike connections.

The city has a regional airport, but commercial options are limited and can change. For most trips, fly into Oklahoma City or Tulsa and rent a car.
