Hideaway Pizza began in Stillwater and still feels like a college-town institution rather than just another regional chain. The original location near campus is the one alumni remember, especially on weekends when orange shirts fill the room.

Order pizza, keep it simple, and accept that nostalgia is part of the seasoning. It is a better stop for atmosphere than for culinary revelation, which is exactly the point.
