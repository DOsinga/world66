The Oklahoma State University Museum of Art occupies Stillwater's former post office and shows rotating exhibitions from the university collection and visiting artists. The building gives the museum a civic setting rather than a campus-only feel.

It is an easy downtown stop and works well with a walk through nearby campus landmarks such as Old Central.
