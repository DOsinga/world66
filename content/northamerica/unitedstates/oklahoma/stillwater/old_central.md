Old Central is the symbolic heart of Oklahoma State University. Its red-brick Victorian form is small beside the modern campus, but it carries the weight of the university's earliest years.

Stop here on any campus walk. It gives Stillwater's college-town identity a physical starting point and pairs naturally with the library lawn and nearby academic buildings.
