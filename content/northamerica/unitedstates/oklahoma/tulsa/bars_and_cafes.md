---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Tulsa's bar scene is concentrated in a few walkable areas: the **Blue Dome District** downtown, **Cherry Street** in midtown, and the **Brady Arts District** north of downtown.

The **Blue Dome District** (around E. 2nd Street) is the liveliest on weekend evenings. Numerous bars cluster within a few blocks, ranging from craft beer-focused to cocktail bars to dive bars with live music.

**Cain's Ballroom** on Main Street is the historic anchor of the Brady Arts District — a legendary venue where Bob Wills played western swing in the 1930s and where touring acts from Americana to punk still play today.

**The Church Studio**, nearby on S. Trenton, was Leon Russell's recording space and has been restored as a music venue and bar. The space retains the feel of a working studio.

On Cherry Street (E. 15th Street), coffee shops, wine bars, and cocktail lounges mix with restaurants in the city's most pleasant outdoor-dining neighbourhood.
