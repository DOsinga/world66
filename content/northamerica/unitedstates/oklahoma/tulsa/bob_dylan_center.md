The Bob Dylan Center gives Tulsa a second major reason to visit its Arts District for American music history. Built around Dylan's archive, it uses manuscripts, recordings, photographs, film, and listening stations to trace a career that kept changing shape just when people thought they understood it.

It pairs naturally with the Woody Guthrie Center next door. Guthrie is the Oklahoma root; Dylan is the restless branch that grew outward into folk, rock, literature, and argument. Give yourself time for both if lyrics, protest music, or 20th-century American culture matter to you.
