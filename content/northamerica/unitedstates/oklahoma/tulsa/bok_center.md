BOK Center is Tulsa's major downtown arena, a sweeping silver building designed by Cesar Pelli and built to pull big concerts and sports events into the city centre. Its curved exterior makes it one of the more visible pieces of modern architecture in Tulsa.

The reason to go is the schedule: a concert, hockey game, rodeo, or touring show. Even without a ticket, the building helps explain downtown Tulsa's recent revival, linking the arts district, Deco-era core, and event economy into one evening circuit.
