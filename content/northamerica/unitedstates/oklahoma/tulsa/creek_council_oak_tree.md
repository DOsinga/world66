The Creek Council Oak Tree is a small but meaningful historic site near downtown Tulsa. The tree is traditionally associated with the Lochapoka Creek people, who gathered here after removal from Alabama in the nineteenth century. It marks a Native history that predates Tulsa's oil boom skyline and gives the city a deeper origin story than its Art Deco facades alone suggest.

The site is simple: a protected tree, a marker, and a patch of green space. That simplicity is the point. It is best visited as part of a wider walk through central Tulsa, especially if you are trying to understand how Indigenous, settler, and oil-era histories overlap in the city.
