---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**Tulsa International Airport** (TUL) is six miles northeast of downtown, with direct flights to most major US hubs. The airport is compact and easy to navigate. No light rail connects the airport to downtown; taxis, rideshares, and rental cars are the options.

By car, Tulsa sits at the junction of I-44 (the Turner Turnpike connects it to Oklahoma City, 100 miles southwest) and the Cimarron Turnpike. US-412 runs east to west through the city. The Will Rogers Turnpike (I-44 continued) runs northeast toward Joplin, Missouri.

Amtrak does not currently serve Tulsa. **Greyhound** runs buses to Oklahoma City, Kansas City, Dallas, and other regional destinations from the downtown bus terminal at 317 S. Detroit Avenue.
