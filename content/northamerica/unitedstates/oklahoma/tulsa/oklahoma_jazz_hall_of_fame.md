The Oklahoma Jazz Hall of Fame occupies the restored 1931 Union Depot, a Spanish Colonial Revival building that once served Tulsa's railway passengers. The depot's grand waiting hall, with its painted wood-beamed ceiling and terracotta tile, is a handsome home for a museum dedicated to the remarkable number of jazz musicians Oklahoma has produced.

The permanent collection honours figures from Don Cherry and Barney Kessel to Charlie Christian, the Tulsa-born guitarist who invented the modern jazz electric guitar style. The museum also hosts live performances in the restored ballroom on weekends. If you are in town on a Friday or Saturday evening, check the schedule — it is one of the better ways to spend a night in Tulsa.
