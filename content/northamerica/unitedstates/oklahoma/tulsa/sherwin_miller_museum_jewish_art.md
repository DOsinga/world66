The Sherwin Miller Museum of Jewish Art adds an important cultural layer to Tulsa’s museum scene. Its collections include Jewish ritual objects, fine art, and historical material, with interpretation that connects religious practice, migration, community life, and memory.

The Holocaust exhibition gives the museum a more serious register than many small art collections. It is worth visiting if you are interested in Tulsa beyond Art Deco and music history, and it pairs well with the city’s broader story of communities building institutions on the plains.
