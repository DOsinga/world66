The Spotlight Theater is a small Tulsa performance venue best known for its long-running Saturday-night melodrama tradition. It sits in a distinctive Bruce Goff-designed building, so the attraction has two layers: local theatre history and a piece of Oklahoma architectural eccentricity.

It is not a broadway-style night out, and that is the point. Go for an old-fashioned, participatory local performance, with the understanding that schedules and productions matter more than the building alone.
