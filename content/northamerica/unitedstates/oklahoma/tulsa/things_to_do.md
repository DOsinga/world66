title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tulsa's oil-boom wealth built museums and an art-deco downtown that together make it one of the most surprising cities in the central United States. The [Philbrook Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa/philbrook_museum), set in a 1920s Italianate villa with formal gardens, is the standout; the [Gilcrease Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa/gilcrease_museum) holds one of the country's largest collections of Western American art. The [Boston Avenue Methodist Church](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa/boston_avenue_methodist_church) is one of the finest Art Deco buildings in the United States. Downtown has more art-deco buildings per block than almost anywhere else in the US — the [Philtower Building](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa/philtower_building) and Philcade Buildings on S. Boston Avenue are a good starting point.

The [Greenwood District](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa/greenwood_district) is essential history — this is where Black Wall Street stood before the 1921 Race Massacre, and the Greenwood Rising History Center opened on the centenary is the most complete account of what happened. [Gathering Place](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa/gathering_place) on the Arkansas River is one of the best free urban parks in the country. The [Center of the Universe](/northamerica/unitedstates/oklahoma/tulsa/center_of_the_universe) is a five-minute detour worth making for the acoustic mystery alone.
