Tulsa Zoo is the main family attraction in Mohawk Park, with animal habitats, themed
exhibits, and enough space to make it a half-day outing. It adds a green, north-side
counterweight to Tulsa's downtown architecture and arts districts.

The zoo is strongest for families, but it also works for anyone looking for an easy
outdoor activity with clear logistics. Check seasonal hours and keeper talks if you want
more than a simple walk-through.
