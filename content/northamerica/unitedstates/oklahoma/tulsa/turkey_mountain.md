Turkey Mountain is Tulsa's main urban wilderness area, with hiking and mountain-bike trails through wooded hills above the Arkansas River. The trail network has side paths to explore and occasional views toward downtown Tulsa and Oral Roberts University. It is a good stop when you want a rougher, less manicured outdoor break than a city park.
