The Grady County Fairgrounds serve as the community's main event space and the venue for much of Tuttle's public life, including the Tuttle Roundup. Agricultural fairs, livestock shows, and local events cycle through here across the year.

For a town of Tuttle's size, the fairgrounds give it a sense of civic life that purely residential suburbs often lack. If you are passing through and something is on, it is worth stopping — county fairs in this part of Oklahoma tend to be unpretentious, affordable, and genuinely local.
