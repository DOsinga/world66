The Will Rogers Route 66 Monument in downtown Vinita commemorates the connection between the celebrated Oklahoma humorist, philosopher, and entertainer and the Mother Road. Rogers, who grew up in the region, frequently referenced the road and the towns along it, and Route 66 enthusiasts have placed monuments to him at various points along the highway.

The monument provides a good excuse to look at the historic downtown, where the Hotel Vinita and several other old commercial buildings survive from the early 20th century. Vinita was founded in 1871 as one of the first towns in Indian Territory, and the downtown still carries some of that history in its bones.
