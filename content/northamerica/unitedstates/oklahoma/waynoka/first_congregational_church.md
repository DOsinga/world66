A modest brick church at 1887 E. Cecil Street, dating from the early 20th century when Waynoka was a booming railroad town. It's listed on the National Register of Historic Places and is one of the few intact buildings from that era still in town. Not a destination in itself, but worth a five-minute stop if you are walking the historic downtown.
