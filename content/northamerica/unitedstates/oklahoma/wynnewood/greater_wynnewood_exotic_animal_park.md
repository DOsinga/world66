The Greater Wynnewood Exotic Animal Park — known variously as the G.W. Zoo, Tiger King Park, and other names depending on its current ownership — sits about 5 miles west of Wynnewood on a rural property. It became internationally famous as the setting for the Netflix documentary *Tiger King* (2020), which chronicled the rivalry between its flamboyant founder and big-cat conservationists.

The park's status changes with ownership and legal circumstances, so check current conditions before visiting. At various times it has been open to visitors as a private zoo displaying big cats, bears, and other exotic animals. Whether it's operating as a tourist attraction, a rescue facility, or something in between, the property remains Wynnewood's most widely known landmark — for better or worse.
