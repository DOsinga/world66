Albany Historical Carousel and Museum combines a working historic-style carousel with displays about carousel carving and restoration. Visitors can see carved animals, carousel memorabilia, and sometimes craftspeople at work in the carving studio.

It is one of Albany's more family-friendly indoor stops and fits naturally with a walk through the town's historic districts.
