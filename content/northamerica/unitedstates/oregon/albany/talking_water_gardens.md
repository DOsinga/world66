Talking Water Gardens is an unusual Albany stop: a working wetland built to cool and further clean treated wastewater before it returns to the Willamette River. The result is more appealing than that sounds, with ponds, channels, small falls, cattails, and easy paths through a landscape that has become a real birding site.

It is not a manicured botanical garden, and that is part of its charm. Come for a quiet walk, herons and ducks, and a practical example of environmental engineering that has slipped into everyday civic life. It pairs well with Albany's riverfront and historic districts when you want a low-key outdoor break.
