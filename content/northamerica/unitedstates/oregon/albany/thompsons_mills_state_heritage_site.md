Thompson's Mills State Heritage Site preserves a working piece of Oregon’s agricultural history: a water-powered grain mill that operated for well over a century. The building, millrace, and machinery make the site more concrete than a plaque or roadside marker.

It is a rural detour rather than an urban Albany stop, but that is part of the point. The surrounding fields help explain why the mill mattered, and tours can make the mechanics clear even if you have no special interest in milling. It pairs well with Albany’s historic districts and covered-bridge country.
