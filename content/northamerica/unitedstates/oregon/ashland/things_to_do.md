Ashland punches well above its size. The [Oregon Shakespeare Festival](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/ashland/oregon_shakespeare_festival) dominates the cultural calendar from February through October, but there is plenty beyond the theatre: [Lithia Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/ashland/lithia_park) is one of the finest urban parks in the Pacific Northwest, and the surrounding hills offer serious outdoor recreation.
