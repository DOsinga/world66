Garden of Surging Waves is a small downtown Astoria garden and plaza honouring the Chinese community's role in the city and the Lower Columbia River Basin. Its sculptures and landscape details make it more than a simple pocket park.

It is an easy short stop while walking between Astoria's riverfront, museums, and historic commercial streets.
