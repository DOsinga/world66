---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Astoria's attractions cluster around two themes: history and the outdoors. The Columbia River Maritime Museum, the Astoria Column, and Fort Clatsop cover the history with unusual depth. The Victorian houses on the hillside, the riverside trolley, and the bridges and waterfront give the town its particular atmospheric character. It is a small city that rewards a day or two of slow exploration.
