Baker City is a small eastern Oregon town with more substance than its size suggests. It grew rich on mining, ranching, and the traffic of the Oregon Trail, and its downtown still has a run of handsome brick buildings from the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

The strongest reason to stop is the [National Historic Oregon Trail Interpretive Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/baker_city/national_historic_oregon_trail_interpretive_center), where wagon ruts still cut across the dry hills above town. Back in Baker City, the [Baker Heritage Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/baker_city/baker_heritage_museum) fills in the local story, while the [Historic Downtown](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/baker_city/historic_downtown_baker_city) gives the town its best walk.

Baker City also works as a base for the Elkhorn Mountains and old mining country. The [Sumpter Valley Gold Dredge](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/baker_city/sumpter_valley_gold_dredge) is a hulking reminder of industrial-scale gold extraction, and [Anthony Lakes](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/baker_city/anthony_lakes) gives the area a high-mountain escape. It is not a big-city destination, but it easily clears a spare day if you are crossing eastern Oregon.
