Anthony Lakes is the high-country escape northwest of Baker City, set among the Elkhorn Mountains. In winter it is a small ski area known for dry snow; in summer it gives access to lakes, trails, and cooler air above the ranch country.

It is not a polished resort, which is part of its appeal. Check road and season conditions before driving up, especially outside summer.
