Baker Heritage Museum occupies the old Baker Municipal Natatorium, a handsome 1920s building that gives the museum half its charm before you even reach the exhibits. Inside, the collections cover mining, ranching, timber, local families, and the natural history of northeast Oregon.

It is a good second stop after the Oregon Trail center because it brings the story back into town. The museum is strongest when it shows how Baker City's boom years shaped the streets you walk outside.
