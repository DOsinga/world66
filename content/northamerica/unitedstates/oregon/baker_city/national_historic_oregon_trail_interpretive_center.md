The National Historic Oregon Trail Interpretive Center sits on open hills east of Baker City, where wagon ruts from the emigrant trail are still visible in the ground. That physical trace is what makes the place work: the story is not just inside the museum.

The exhibits cover the long overland journey, the choices families made, and the cost of pushing west. Walk the short outdoor trails after the galleries; the dry hills and big sky make the history feel less abstract.
