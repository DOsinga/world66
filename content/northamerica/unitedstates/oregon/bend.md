Bend is central Oregon's outdoor capital, a small city on the Deschutes River with the Cascade Range rising immediately to the west. In winter people ski and snowboard at [Mount Bachelor](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/mount_bachelor); in summer they mountain bike, hike, paddle, and fish. The town itself has grown from a logging settlement into a craft-beer-and-adventure hub with a lively downtown, good restaurants, and an easy, outdoorsy energy.

The [Old Mill District](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/old_mill_district) along the river has shops and restaurants in converted industrial buildings. Drake Park and Mirror Pond — reached on foot via the [Deschutes River Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/deschutes_river_trail) — are the heart of downtown. The [High Desert Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/high_desert_museum) south of town is one of Oregon's best small museums, part natural history, part living exhibit, with raptors, porcupines, and a walk-through desert canyon.

Day trips are excellent: [Crater Lake National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/crater_lake) is two hours south, [Smith Rock State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/smith_rock) (world-class rock climbing) is half an hour north, and the [Newberry National Volcanic Monument](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/newberry_volcanic) — with obsidian flows, a caldera lake, and [lava tubes](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/lava_river_cave) — is just outside town. The Cascade Lakes Scenic Byway loops past a string of alpine lakes ideal for kayaking and swimming in summer.

The craft beer scene is a genuine draw. [Deschutes Brewery](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/deschutes_brewery), founded here in 1988, helped put Bend on the map; [Bend Brewing Company](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/bend_brewing), on Mirror Pond, has the best outdoor patio. The [Tower Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/tower_theatre) on Wall Street anchors the cultural life of downtown. For orientation and history, the [Deschutes Historical Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/deschutes_historical_museum) covers everything from the Paiute people through the timber era. And [Pilot Butte](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/bend/pilot_butte), a volcanic cinder cone at the edge of town, gives a 360-degree view of the entire Cascade Range in about 30 minutes on foot.
