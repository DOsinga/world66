type: section
---

Bend has more craft breweries per capita than almost any city in the United States. The downtown core is thick with taprooms — Deschutes Brewery, Boneyard Beer, 10 Barrel Brewing, and Crux Fermentation Project all have tasting rooms within a short walk of each other. After a day on the mountain or the trails, a cold local beer is practically built into the itinerary.
