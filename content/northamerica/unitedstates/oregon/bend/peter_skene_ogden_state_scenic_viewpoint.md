Peter Skene Ogden State Scenic Viewpoint overlooks the Crooked River Gorge, where basalt cliffs drop sharply to the river below. The old highway bridge gives the most dramatic angle on the canyon, with modern bridges and the high desert beyond it.

It is a short stop rather than a full outing, but it pairs well with Smith Rock or a drive between Bend, Redmond, and Madras. Come for the view, stretch your legs, and keep a firm grip on hats and phones near the railings.
