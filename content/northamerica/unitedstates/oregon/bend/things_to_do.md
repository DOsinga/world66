---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bend is built around outdoor life, and the range is remarkable for a city of its size. Skiing at Mount Bachelor in winter, mountain biking and paddling in summer, and world-class rock climbing at Smith Rock year-round give the city a reputation as one of the best outdoor destinations in the Pacific Northwest. The High Desert Museum adds depth for those interested in the natural and cultural history of the region.
