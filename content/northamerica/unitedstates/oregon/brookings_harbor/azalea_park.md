Azalea Park is Brookings' town park, but it earns its place when the azaleas and rhododendrons are in flower. Paths, open lawns, a bandshell, and a disc golf course make it useful even outside the spring bloom.

It is not the wild coast, but it adds a gentle inland stop between beaches and headlands.
