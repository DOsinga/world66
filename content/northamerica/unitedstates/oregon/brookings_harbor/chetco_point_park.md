Chetco Point Park is a quick, satisfying headland walk near the mouth of the Chetco River. Short paths lead to ocean overlooks, pocket beaches, tide pools, and views back toward the harbor.

It is one of the easiest places in town to get above the surf without committing to a long hike. Wind is part of the experience.
