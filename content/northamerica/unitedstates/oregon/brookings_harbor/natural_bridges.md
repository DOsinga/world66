Natural Bridges is one of the classic stops on the Samuel H. Boardman State Scenic Corridor north of Brookings-Harbor. The viewpoint looks over forested headlands, sea arches, and narrow coves where the Pacific does the sculpting.

Stay on the signed viewpoints unless you know the trail conditions. The informal paths can be steep, loose, and less forgiving than they look.
