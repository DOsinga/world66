Harney County Historical Museum is the town stop that makes the surrounding landscape easier to read. Its collections cover ranching, local families, frontier settlement, and the county's long relationship with the high desert.

Visit before driving south if you want context for the Round Barn, cattle country, and the way Burns became the service centre for such a large, thinly settled area.
