Corvallis is the home of Oregon State University and the county seat of Benton County — a compact, walkable city of around 50,000 in the heart of the Willamette Valley. The [OSU campus](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/corvallis/oregon_state_university) gives it an active café culture and a younger energy than most towns of its size in Oregon.

The historic downtown around Second Street has an unusually high concentration of well-preserved late-nineteenth-century buildings, including the [Benton County Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/corvallis/benton_county_courthouse) and the [Whiteside Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/corvallis/whiteside_theatre), a 1922 movie palace still showing films. The [Saturday farmers market](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/corvallis/farmers_market) at the Willamette waterfront runs from spring through autumn and showcases the best of the valley's agricultural produce. The [sidewalk contractor stamps](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/corvallis/corvallis_sidewalk_stamps) pressed into the pavements since 1908 are a small and genuinely odd local detail worth knowing about.

It makes an excellent base for a Willamette Valley trip: the Pacific coast is less than an hour west, the Cascade mountains are within reach to the east, and Portland is 90 minutes north. Wine country surrounds it — the Eola-Amity Hills and Chehalem Mountains appellations are both close by.
