Eugene is Oregon's second city, with around 175,000 people, sitting in the Willamette Valley where the McKenzie River joins the Willamette. It is a university town — the [University of Oregon](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/university_of_oregon) is its heart — and the culture reflects that: progressive, outdoors-oriented, with good live music and a strong running tradition. The legendary long-distance runner Steve Prefontaine trained here under Bill Bowerman, who went on to co-found Nike, and [Hayward Field](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/hayward_field) remains one of the finest track venues in the world.

The downtown has [Kesey Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/kesey_square), the [Hult Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/hult_center) for performing arts, [5th Street Public Market](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/fifth_street_market), and a [Saturday Market](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/saturday_market) that has run weekly since 1970. The [Whiteaker](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/whiteaker) neighbourhood, just northwest of downtown, is Eugene at its most countercultural — breweries, taquerias, and warehouse murals.

The Willamette River provides paths for running, cycling, and kayaking; [Alton Baker Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/alton_baker_park) on the north bank has Pre's Trail, the bark-chip loop Prefontaine designed. [Spencer Butte](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/spencer_butte) to the south of downtown is a forested park with trails and sweeping views of the valley, while [Skinner Butte](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/skinner_butte) sits at the bend of the river with the fairy-tale Victorian [Shelton McMurphey Johnson House](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/shelton_mcmurphey_johnson_house) halfway up its slope.

Eugene is a natural base for exploring the McKenzie River valley to the east, with hot springs, waterfalls, and lava fields, and it is about an hour from the Oregon coast.
