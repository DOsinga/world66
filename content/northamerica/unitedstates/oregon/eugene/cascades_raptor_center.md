Cascades Raptor Center is a nature center and wildlife hospital on Eugene's south side, focused on birds of prey. Visitors can see resident owls, hawks, falcons, and eagles that cannot be released back into the wild.

It is a good family stop and pairs naturally with the nearby Ridgeline Trail or Spencer Butte. The setting is wooded and quiet, so allow time to walk the outdoor aviary paths rather than treating it as a quick indoor exhibit.
