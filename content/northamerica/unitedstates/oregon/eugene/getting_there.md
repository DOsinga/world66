---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** **Eugene Airport (EUG)**, 15 minutes northwest of downtown, has direct flights from a dozen West Coast and Mountain West cities — convenient enough that PDX (Portland) is rarely necessary. Rideshares run about $25 to downtown; the LTD bus 99 is a cheap option.

**By car.** Eugene is on I-5, two hours south of Portland and four hours north of the Bay Area. Highway 126 east leads up the McKenzie River valley toward the Cascades; Highway 126 west crosses the Coast Range to the coast at Florence.

**By rail.** Amtrak's **Coast Starlight** (LA–Seattle) and **Cascades** (Eugene–Vancouver BC) both stop at the downtown station at 4th and Willamette — a five-minute walk from Saturday Market. The trip from Portland is around 2½ hours.

**By bus.** **FlixBus** and **Greyhound** run frequent service to Portland, Seattle, and points south. Local LTD buses run from the downtown Eugene Station.
