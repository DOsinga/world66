A small downtown plaza at the corner of Broadway and Willamette, dominated by Pete Helzer's 2003 bronze of **Ken Kesey** — Eugene's most famous literary son — seated on a stack of books and reading to three children. The sculpture is officially called *The Storyteller*, but everyone calls it Kesey Square.

It is the default downtown meeting point and the venue for Saturday-Market overflow, street performers, the occasional protest, and as much skateboarding as the city tolerates. Five minutes here orients you in the city centre; Broadway runs west from here through what was once the pedestrianised Eugene Mall.
