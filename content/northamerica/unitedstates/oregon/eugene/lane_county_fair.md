The Lane County Fair is Eugene's summer county-fair blowout, with rides, livestock barns, fair food, concerts, and the democratic chaos that makes American fairs worth seeing. It is not subtle, but it is very local.

Because it is date-specific, check the annual schedule before adding it to an itinerary. If your visit lines up, it gives a lively contrast to Eugene's markets, university corners, and outdoor trails.
