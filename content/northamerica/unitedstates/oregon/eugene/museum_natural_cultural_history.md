The University of Oregon's natural history museum sits on the east side of campus and packs a lot into a small building: dinosaur skulls, Pacific Northwest archaeology, ethnographic collections of the Indigenous peoples of the region, and case after case of basketry. The standout artefact is the case of **Fort Rock sandals** — woven sagebrush-bark sandals, over 9,000 years old, the oldest dated shoes ever found.

Allow 60–90 minutes. Modest admission; free on the first Friday of the month. A useful complement to the Schnitzer for a rainy afternoon on campus.
