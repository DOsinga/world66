The Mount Pisgah Mushroom Festival is one of Eugene's more distinctive seasonal events, built around the fungi of the southern Willamette Valley. Displays, guided learning, food vendors, and family activities make it more than a simple market day.

Because it is annual, timing matters: do not plan around it unless your visit overlaps the festival date. If it does, the arboretum setting gives the event a strong sense of place and a useful link to Eugene's outdoorsy culture.
