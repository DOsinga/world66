Oregon Air and Space Museum is a compact aviation museum on the edge of Eugene, close to the airport. Its aircraft, models, uniforms, and space-related displays give aviation enthusiasts a focused stop away from the university-centered parts of town.

The museum is best for travelers with a specific interest in flight history or families looking for a short indoor activity. It is not central, so combine it with airport-area plans or a wider drive rather than making a special cross-town trip without checking hours.
