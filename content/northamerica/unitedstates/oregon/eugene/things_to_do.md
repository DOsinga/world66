---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Eugene is small enough to cover on foot and by bike, with three useful clusters: the **University of Oregon campus** to the southeast (Hayward Field, the Schnitzer Museum, the Museum of Natural and Cultural History); **downtown** around Kesey Square, the Hult Center, and Saturday Market; and **the Whiteaker** to the northwest, the older industrial neighbourhood that has become Eugene's hippiest pocket. Add Spencer Butte and Skinner Butte for the views, and Pre's Rock for the running pilgrimage.
