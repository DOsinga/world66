The University of Oregon is Eugene's largest single thing — a 295-acre campus of brick buildings, oaks, and rhododendrons immediately southeast of downtown. It is best wandered on foot or bike: cross the Willamette via the **Autzen Footbridge**, pick up the river path, and loop back through the leafy academic quad.

The buildings worth a closer look are **Johnson Hall** (the columned 1915 administration block at the head of the main quad), the **Knight Library**, and the **Pioneer Cemetery** tucked into the campus's northwest corner. Two excellent museums — the [Jordan Schnitzer Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/jordan_schnitzer_museum) and the [Museum of Natural and Cultural History](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/eugene/museum_natural_cultural_history) — sit on campus. Free public tours of the campus run most weekdays.
