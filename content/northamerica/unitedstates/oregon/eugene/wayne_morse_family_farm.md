Wayne Morse Family Farm preserves the former home landscape of U.S. Senator Wayne Morse as a public park. The grounds mix open pasture, trees, historic structures, and community use, giving Eugene a political-history site that still feels like a neighbourhood green space.

It is a gentle stop rather than a major museum. Visit if you are interested in Oregon civic history, want a quiet walk, or are exploring the south side of Eugene beyond the university district.
