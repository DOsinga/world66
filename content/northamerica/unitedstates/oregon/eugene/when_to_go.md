---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Eugene's climate is classic western Oregon: wet, cool winters and dry, warm summers. Rain falls between November and April — not torrential but persistent, and the valley fog can sit for days. **July, August, and September** are the dry months, with daytime highs in the 25–30°C (75–85°F) range and very little rain.

**Late May to early October** is the comfortable window. The **Oregon Country Fair** in nearby Veneta, second weekend of July, is a regional institution. The **Prefontaine Classic** at Hayward Field, late May or early June, is the running event of the year. **Football Saturdays** in autumn turn the city green and yellow and book up the hotels — avoid or commit accordingly.
