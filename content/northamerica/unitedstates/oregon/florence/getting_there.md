---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Florence sits on US Highway 101, the main Oregon coast road, about 290 kilometres south of Portland. The drive from Portland takes roughly three and a half hours via Highway 126 through Eugene — a scenic route that follows the Siuslaw River through the coast range.

**By car**: Most visitors drive. From Eugene (90 kilometres east), Highway 126 connects directly, taking about 75 minutes. From Portland, the quickest route goes south on I-5 to Eugene, then west on 126.

**By bus**: Coastal Express buses run along the coast between Coos Bay and Newport, stopping in Florence, with connections to Eugene at several points. It is infrequent — check Oregon Department of Transportation schedules before relying on it.

**Florence Municipal Airport** (FMU) handles small private and charter aircraft but has no scheduled commercial service. The nearest commercial airports are Eugene (100 kilometres) and Portland (290 kilometres).
