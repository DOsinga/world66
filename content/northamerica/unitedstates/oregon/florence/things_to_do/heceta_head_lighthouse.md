Perched on a basalt headland 21 kilometres north of Florence, Heceta Head Lighthouse has been guiding ships since 1894. The white tower and keeper's cottage sit in a cove backed by dark forest, framed by the cape to the south — the composition is why this is the most photographed lighthouse on the Oregon coast. The light itself is still active, one of the most powerful on the coast. A short trail leads up from the car park; the lighthouse is open for tours in summer. The adjacent 1893 keeper's quarters operate as a bed and breakfast.
