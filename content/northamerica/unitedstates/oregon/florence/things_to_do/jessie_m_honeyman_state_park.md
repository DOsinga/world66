Five kilometres south of town, this state park sits where the dunes meet the forest and two freshwater lakes — Cleawox and Woahink — make swimming and kayaking easy without fighting surf. Cleawox has a sandy beach and a swim area; Woahink is larger and less busy. Dunes rise immediately behind the beach on Cleawox, and you can climb them to look out over the treetops toward the ocean. The park has full camping facilities and fills up in summer — booking ahead is necessary from June through August.
