The Muffler Man Paul Bunyan is a classic piece of roadside Americana: oversized, slightly absurd, and very much tied to the era when highway businesses used giant figures to pull drivers off the road. In Grants Pass, it fits a town already rich in neon signs, old motels, and Rogue River road-trip energy.

This is a quick photo stop, not a deep cultural monument. Its value is as texture: a small reminder of the pre-interstate travel landscape that still gives parts of southern Oregon their distinctive character.
