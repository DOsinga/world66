The Rogue is one of the great whitewater rivers of the American West. From Grants Pass it cuts west into the Klamath Mountains and runs eighty-four miles through a roadless canyon to the Pacific at Gold Beach. The most famous stretch is a thirty-four-mile Wild and Scenic section that can only be accessed by raft or on foot via the Rogue River Trail.

Commercial outfitters in Grants Pass run trips from a few hours to four days. The water is reliably runnable from May to October, with the bigger rapids — Rainie Falls, Mule Creek Canyon, Blossom Bar — sitting in the wilderness section downstream. The river also has a celebrated summer steelhead run, and fly fishing for half-pounders in late summer is a regional ritual.
