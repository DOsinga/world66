Iwetemlaykin State Heritage Site protects open meadow and moraine land just south of Joseph, part of the ancestral homeland of the Nez Perce. Its trails are short and quiet, with views toward the Wallowas and interpretive signs that ask you to slow down rather than consume the place as scenery.

It pairs naturally with Old Chief Joseph Gravesite nearby. Together they give the valley's beauty a sharper historical edge.
