The Favell Museum is the cultural stop that makes Klamath Falls feel less like a pass-through town. Its collection focuses on Native American artifacts and western art, with thousands of arrowheads, beadwork, baskets, stone tools, and works by contemporary western artists.

The museum is small enough to handle in a morning but serious enough to linger over. It is especially good if your Oregon trip has been mostly coast, forest, or mountain scenery and you want a stronger sense of the basin's human history.
