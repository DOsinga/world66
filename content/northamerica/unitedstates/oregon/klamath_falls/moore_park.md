Moore Park sits on the southern side of Upper Klamath Lake and is the easiest place to trade town streets for lake air. Trails climb into dry woodland and connect with longer routes, while lower areas give you picnic spots and water views.

Birding is part of the point. Watch for raptors, waterbirds, and woodland species, especially if you are already walking the Link River area and want to keep going toward the lake.
