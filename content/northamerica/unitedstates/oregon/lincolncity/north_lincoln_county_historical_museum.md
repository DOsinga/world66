North Lincoln County Historical Museum is a compact but worthwhile stop for understanding Lincoln City beyond its beaches and highway strip. Exhibits cover coastal settlements, fishing, local families, glass floats, and the separate towns that eventually merged into modern Lincoln City.

It is especially useful on a foggy or rainy coast day, when a short museum visit can give context to the coves, roads, and neighborhoods outside. The scale is modest, but the local focus makes it more rewarding than a generic seaside display.
