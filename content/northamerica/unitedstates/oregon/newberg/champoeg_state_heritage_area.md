Champoeg State Heritage Area sits south of Newberg on a quiet bend of the Willamette River. In 1843, settlers met here and voted to form Oregon's provisional government, a small political moment that helped shape the future state.

The old town was later wiped out by flood, which gives the park its open, slightly ghostly feel. Today you come for walking and cycling paths, river views, oak and wetland habitat, and enough history to make the picnic grounds feel less ordinary.
