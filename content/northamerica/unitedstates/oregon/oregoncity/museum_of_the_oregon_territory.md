The Museum of the Oregon Territory sits above Willamette Falls, which gives it one of the better museum settings in the Portland area. Inside, the focus is Oregon City and Clackamas County: Native history, overland migration, industry, domestic life, and the complicated legacy of the Oregon Trail.

It pairs naturally with the End of the Oregon Trail Interpretive Center and the McLoughlin House, but feels more local and less packaged. Give it time if you want Oregon City to read as more than a historic label on the south edge of Portland.
