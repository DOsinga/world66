Just south of [Cannon Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/oregoncoastnorth/cannonbeach), Manzanita is the quiet, uncommercial alternative to its busier northern neighbour. The town is little more than a few blocks of beach houses, a handful of restaurants, and a long stretch of sand backed by Neahkahnie Mountain — and that simplicity is the whole point.

Manzanita attracts people who want to walk the beach without dodging tour groups, read a book in a decent café, and eat well without gallery-and-gift-shop bustle. The four-mile beach is wide, dark-sanded, and dependably uncrowded; the hiking trail up Neahkahnie Mountain rewards the effort with sweeping Pacific views and the lingering shadow of a Spanish treasure legend. Oswald West State Park, just to the north, holds Short Sand Beach and the Cape Falcon trail — among the finest coastal hiking in Oregon.

The Nehalem Bay to the south adds another dimension: flat water for kayaking, mudflats rich with shorebirds, and the quiet town of Wheeler where crab boats tie up and the pace makes Manzanita itself seem rushed. Tillamook, thirty-five miles south, rounds out a stay with its famous creamery and the dramatic Three Capes Scenic Route.

Come here when you want the Oregon coast at its most elemental — dramatic weather, empty sand, dark forest, and very little noise.
