Italian-inflected casual dining that has been a Manzanita staple for years. Wood-fired pizzas, handmade pasta, a decent wine list, and a lively room that fills quickly on summer evenings. The patio is pleasant when the fog stays away. Walk-ins welcome early in the week; reservations advised Friday through Sunday.
