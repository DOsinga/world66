A small, seasonal restaurant in the centre of town that has built a loyal following on the strength of its wood-fired cooking and locally sourced ingredients. The menu changes with what's available — Dungeness crab in season, razor clams, halibut, Nehalem Valley beef — and the room is warm and unhurried. Reservations essential on summer weekends.
