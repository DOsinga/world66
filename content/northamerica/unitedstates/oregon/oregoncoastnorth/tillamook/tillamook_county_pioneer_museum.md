This small downtown museum is the place to put Tillamook's cheese-and-coast image into context. Its collections cover local Indigenous and settler history, logging, dairy farming, maritime life, and the everyday objects that made a wet, isolated county work.

It is not a grand museum, but it is useful before driving the capes or the bay roads. The town feels less like a roadside stop once you understand how much of the county's history came from timber, cows, rivers, and the sea.
