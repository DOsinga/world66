Heritage Station Museum fills in the everyday history around Pendleton: railroads, farming, schools, wagons, and the county stories that sit behind the better-known rodeo image. The setting in the former railway station helps, as the railroad is one reason the town grew into a regional centre.

It is not the first stop if you only have an hour, but it is worth adding if you are already downtown for the underground tour.
