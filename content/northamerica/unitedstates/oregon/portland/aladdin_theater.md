The Aladdin Theater is an old movie house turned live venue on Portland's east side. Its calendar leans toward singer-songwriters, indie acts, comedy, and touring musicians who suit a seated room better than an arena.

Food and drinks are available, and the theatre is small enough that most seats feel close to the stage. It is a good pick when you want Portland's music scene in a neighbourhood setting rather than a downtown hall.
