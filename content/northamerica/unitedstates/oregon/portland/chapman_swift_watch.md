Chapman Swift Watch is Portland's September evening ritual at Chapman Elementary School, where crowds gather to watch thousands of Vaux's swifts spiral into the school's chimney to roost during migration.

It is seasonal and informal, but memorable: bring something to sit on, arrive before dusk and expect parking in the immediate neighbourhood to be tight.
