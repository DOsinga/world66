Crystal Springs Rhododendron Garden sits on the edge of the Reed College campus in the Eastmoreland neighbourhood, surrounding a spring-fed lake that attracts year-round waterfowl including wood ducks, herons, and Canada geese. The garden is well designed at any time of year, but the April–May rhododendron bloom is the peak experience — the lake's reflections and the density of the plantings make it one of Portland's most photographed spots during spring.

Admission is free from September through February; a small fee applies during the peak bloom season (roughly mid-March through Labor Day in September). The garden is managed by the American Rhododendron Society in partnership with Portland Parks. Access is from SE 28th Avenue; the nearest MAX station is Woodstock (Green Line), about a 20-minute walk.
