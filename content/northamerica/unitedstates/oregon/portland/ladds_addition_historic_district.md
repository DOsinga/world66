Ladd's Addition is one of Portland's most distinctive residential districts, laid out from 1905 with diagonal streets cutting through the normal city grid. Small rose gardens and parks sit at the centre and corners of the plan, surrounded by early 20th-century houses.

The appeal is the walk: quiet streets, mature trees, old homes, and a street pattern that feels almost formal compared with the rest of Southeast Portland. It pairs well with the nearby Hawthorne and Division areas.
