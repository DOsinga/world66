The Oregon Maritime Center and Museum is housed aboard the Portland, a steam sternwheeler tug moored on the Willamette River. Inside are ship models, maritime artifacts, and memorabilia from the river and harbour history that shaped Portland.

It is a compact museum, but the setting gives it character. Check tour times if you want to see more of the vessel itself.
