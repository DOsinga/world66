Oregon Rail Heritage Center is a working railway museum on Portland's east side, close to OMSI and the riverfront. Its main draw is the collection of restored steam locomotives, including engines that still run on special excursions.

This is a good stop for families, railway fans, and anyone who likes museums where restoration work is part of the visit. Volunteers are often working around the locomotives, which gives the place more life than a static display hall.
