  Portland Saturday Market has operated continuously under the Burnside Bridge since 1974, making it the longest-running, continuously operating outdoor arts and crafts market in the United States. All vendors must make what they sell — no resale goods, no imports. During the height of the season, more than 350 artisans and food vendors set up in the Saturday and Sunday market under and around the bridge.
---

Under and around the Burnside Bridge in Old Town, Portland Saturday Market (which also runs on Sundays) is one of the best places in the city to eat cheaply and find genuinely handmade goods. The food section covers an impressive range — wood-fired crepes, Ethiopian injera, fresh tamales, Vietnamese banh mi, and local smoked meats. All prepared on site.

The craft stalls sell clothing, ceramics, jewellery, woodwork, paintings, and various peculiarities — the quality varies but the better stalls are genuinely excellent. The market runs every weekend from March through Christmas Eve, and on the Sunday before Christmas. The location, beside the waterfront park and near the Skidmore Fountain, makes it a natural stop on a walk along the Willamette.
