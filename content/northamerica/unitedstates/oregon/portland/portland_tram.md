The Portland Aerial Tram connects the South Waterfront district on the Willamette River to the Oregon Health and Science University campus on Marquam Hill. The gondola ride takes about 4 minutes, covers 500 feet of vertical elevation, and gives excellent views over the river and the city. It runs on a continuous loop during operating hours.

The tram is primarily a commuter service for OHSU employees and patients, but it's open to the public for a small fare in each direction. The lower terminal is at the end of SW Moody Avenue in the South Waterfront — reachable by the Portland Streetcar (NS Line). From the upper terminal at OHSU, it's a short walk to a viewpoint over the city and the Cascades. The tram does not run on Sundays (except for OHSU special event days).
