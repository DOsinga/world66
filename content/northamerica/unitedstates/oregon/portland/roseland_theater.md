Roseland Theater is a central Portland concert venue known for rock, hip hop, and touring acts that want a room bigger than a club but still close to the crowd. Most shows are standing-room downstairs, with balcony seating above.

It is not a sightseeing stop on its own, but it is a useful name to know if you want a live show downtown. Check the calendar, as the venue's appeal depends entirely on who is playing.
