The Springwater Corridor is a long multi-use trail running from southeast Portland toward Boring. It is used by walkers, cyclists, wheelchair users, and riders, and connects several green spaces on the east side.

Visitors will usually sample a section rather than cover the whole route. It is most useful as a low-stress bike ride or a way to link parks without dealing with traffic.
