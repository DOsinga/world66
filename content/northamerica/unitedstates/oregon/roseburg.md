Roseburg sits in the Umpqua River Valley of southern Oregon, where Interstate 5, old timber country, wineries, and waterfall roads all cross paths. The city itself is practical rather than polished, but it has enough local history and river-valley character to make it more than a fuel stop between Eugene and Grants Pass.

The best reason to come is the countryside around it. The [Umpqua Valley wineries](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/roseburg/umpqua_valley_wineries) are among Oregon's older wine areas, [Wildlife Safari](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/roseburg/wildlife_safari) sits just south in Winston, and the North Umpqua Highway leads east toward [Watson Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/roseburg/watson_falls), river trails, and forest.

In town, the [Douglas County Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/oregon/roseburg/douglas_county_museum) gives useful context for the land of the Umpqua, from natural history to pioneer and timber stories. Roseburg is not the prettiest city in Oregon, but for a traveler with a day in southern Oregon, it has a strong mix of wine, water, and working-town history.
