The Floed-Lane House is a small historic house museum near downtown Roseburg, run by the Douglas County Historical Society. It is the kind of place to visit if you enjoy local history at human scale: rooms, furnishings, and stories rather than a big interpretive production.

Opening hours are limited, so check before building a day around it. If it is closed, the exterior still fits into a short walk through Roseburg's older core.
