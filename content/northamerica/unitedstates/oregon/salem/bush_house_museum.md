Bush House Museum sits within Bush's Pasture Park and preserves the home of Asahel Bush, an influential Oregon newspaper publisher and businessman. The house adds a domestic and political layer to Salem's state-capital story.

It is a compact historic-house visit, best paired with a walk through the park or nearby gardens. Check tour availability before going, since access to preserved interiors can be limited.
