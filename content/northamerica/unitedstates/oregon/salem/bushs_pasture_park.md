Bush's Pasture Park is 90 acres of wooded paths, open meadows, and formal gardens tucked into a residential neighbourhood south of downtown. The rose gardens in the north section bloom from May through October and are free to wander.

The Bush House, an 1877 Italianate mansion on the north edge of the park, is one of the best-preserved Victorian homes in Oregon. Tours run through the original furnished interior, including a rare intact parlour with the family's original marble fireplaces and wallpaper. The adjacent Greenhouse Conservatory, built in 1882, is one of the oldest still-operating greenhouses in the western United States.

The Bush Barn Art Center on the south side of the house holds rotating contemporary art exhibitions and is operated by the Salem Art Association. Admission to the art centre is free.
