Powerland Heritage Park is a sprawling volunteer-run collection of machinery museums just north of
Salem. The appeal is very hands-on: steam engines, antique tractors, logging equipment, fire
engines, trucks, miniature railways, and other pieces of Oregon's industrial and farm past are
spread across the grounds.

It is best for travelers who like practical history rather than polished display cases. Check
opening days before going, since several museums keep seasonal or event-based hours, and the park is
at its liveliest during steam-up and vintage machinery weekends.
