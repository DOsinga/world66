Salem Riverfront Park is the city's main downtown green space on the Willamette River. It has lawns, paths, public art, event space, and access toward the pedestrian bridge and riverfront trails, making it a natural break between the civic core and the water.

The park is most useful as part of a Salem walk: Oregon State Capitol, downtown cafes, the Willamette Heritage Center, and then the river. It also hosts festivals and seasonal events, so the feel can shift from quiet lunch stop to busy civic gathering place depending on the day.
