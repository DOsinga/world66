The Lewis and Clark Salt Works is a tiny site, but it tells a practical story from the expedition's winter on the Pacific. Members of the Corps of Discovery came to this stretch of shore to boil seawater for salt, a dull but essential job after months of travel.

Do not expect a major museum. The value is in standing near the beach and imagining the expedition reduced to fire, kettles, rain, and survival logistics. It pairs well with Seaside's promenade and with a wider Lewis and Clark route along the north Oregon coast.
