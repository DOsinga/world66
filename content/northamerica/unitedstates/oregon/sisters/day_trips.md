---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Sisters' position between the Cascades and the high desert makes it an excellent base for day trips in almost every direction.

**Smith Rock State Park**, 40 minutes east, is the standout — internationally famous for sport climbing, but the hiking is the equal of the climbing and the high-desert landscape is dramatic. Easily a full day out.

**McKenzie Pass and the Dee Wright Observatory**, west on Highway 242, opens in summer only and is one of Oregon's great scenic drives — through the Belknap and Yapoah lava flows up to a panoramic viewpoint of the Cascade peaks. Combine with a visit to the Sahalie and Koosah waterfalls further down the McKenzie River.

**Bend**, 30 minutes south, is the regional centre — bigger restaurants, breweries, the High Desert Museum south of town. **Mount Bachelor** beyond Bend has the largest skiing in the area.

**The Three Sisters Wilderness** trailheads are technically not a day trip but a starting point — Pole Creek, Park Meadow, Tam McArthur Rim, Green Lakes all open out from short drives outside town.

For something further afield, **Crater Lake National Park** is around three hours south and is genuinely the highlight of southern Oregon — an exceptional but long day.
