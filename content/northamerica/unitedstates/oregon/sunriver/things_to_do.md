---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Sunriver's outdoor appeal is built on a few genuine strengths: a car-free bike path network that weaves across the entire community, easy access to the Deschutes River, and one of the darker skies in the Oregon Cascades. In winter, Mount Bachelor is close enough for a day trip from your rental. The Nature Center and Observatory give the place something most resort communities lack — a reason to come that isn't just a tee time.
