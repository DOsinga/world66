title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Yachats earns its reputation on the strength of its coastline and the natural features within a few miles in either direction. Cape Perpetua to the south is the headline draw, with trails, sea caves, and the best coastal views in this part of Oregon. The tidepools along the Yachats State Recreation Area are among the richest on the Oregon coast. Beachcombing, agate hunting, and whale watching round out the outdoor options.
