The Allentown Art Museum is the city's strongest cultural stop, with collections that range from European works to American art and textiles. It gives downtown Allentown a reason to linger beyond a meal or arena event.

The museum is manageable rather than exhausting, which is a virtue on a day trip. Pair it with nearby historic buildings and a walk along Hamilton Street.
