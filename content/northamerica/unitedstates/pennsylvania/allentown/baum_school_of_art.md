The Baum School of Art is a community art school near the Allentown Art Museum. Its exhibitions and classes give the downtown arts district a living, local side rather than leaving culture entirely to formal institutions.

Check what is on before making a special trip. When galleries are open, it is an easy add-on to the art museum next door.
