The George Taylor House is an 18th-century Georgian house associated with George Taylor, one of the signers of the Declaration of Independence. The restored house is open as a small historic house museum, with tours focused on Taylor's life and the Revolutionary-era Lehigh Valley.

It is a compact stop rather than a large museum, but it adds useful context if you are following early American history sites around Allentown and the Lehigh Valley.
