---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Downtown Allentown is walkable around Hamilton Street, the arts district, and the arena. For Dorney Park, the Rose Garden, airport hotels, and neighboring Bethlehem or Easton, drive.

Local buses exist, but they are built for residents rather than quick sightseeing. If you are visiting without a car, choose a downtown base and use rideshares for scattered stops.
