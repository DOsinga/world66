The Liberty Bell Museum tells Allentown's best Revolutionary War story: the hiding of Philadelphia's bells here during the British advance. It is a small museum, but the local connection is strong and genuinely worth knowing.

Do not expect the scale of a national museum. Go for the specific episode, the church setting, and the reminder that famous objects often survive because smaller towns did the unglamorous work.
