The Mack Experience Center tells the story of Mack Trucks, one of Allentown's best-known industrial names. Its historical museum holds company records, photographs, and artifacts tied to more than a century of American truck building.

It is most rewarding for visitors interested in transport, machinery, or local industry. Check ahead for tour and museum access, as visits can be more structured than a walk-in city museum.
