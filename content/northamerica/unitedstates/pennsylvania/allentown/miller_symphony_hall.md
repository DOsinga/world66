Miller Symphony Hall is the main performing-arts landmark in downtown Allentown. Its historic facade and concert calendar give Hamilton Street a touch of old-city elegance.

It is most rewarding if you can catch a performance. Otherwise, include it as part of a downtown architecture walk between the art museum, old churches, and the arena district.
