The Museum of Indian Culture preserves and interprets the history of the Lenape and other Native peoples connected to the Lehigh Valley. Its exhibits focus on the region before and beyond European settlement.

It is a smaller museum, but it fills an important gap in an Allentown visit, especially when paired with the city's colonial and industrial history sites.
