The National Museum of Industrial History is a Smithsonian-affiliated museum in Bethlehem, close to the former Bethlehem Steel plant. Exhibits cover steelmaking, machinery, manufacturing, and the workers and inventions that shaped American industry.

It pairs well with a visit to the old steelworks area, where the scale of the furnaces gives the museum's machines and stories their setting.
