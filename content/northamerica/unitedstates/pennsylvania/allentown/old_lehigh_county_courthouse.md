The Old Lehigh County Courthouse is one of downtown Allentown's stronger historic buildings. It helps anchor the older civic district near the art museum and gives the city a more layered feel than its modern highway approaches suggest.

It is a quick stop, but a worthwhile one if you enjoy county-seat architecture. Combine it with the museum and nearby churches for a compact walk.
