PPL Center is the modern arena that helped pull more evening life back into downtown Allentown. It hosts hockey, concerts, and touring events, and on event nights the surrounding blocks feel livelier than they once did.

For travelers, it is worth checking the schedule rather than visiting as a sight in itself. A game or concert can make Allentown feel much more animated.
