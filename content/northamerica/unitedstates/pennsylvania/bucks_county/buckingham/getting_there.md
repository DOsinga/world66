---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Buckingham sits at the intersection of Routes 202 and 263, roughly half-way between Doylestown and New Hope, about 35 miles north of Philadelphia. The drive from Center City Philadelphia takes around an hour via I-95 or Route 611. From New York, the trip is about 90 minutes via I-78 or the New Jersey Turnpike.

There is no rail service to Buckingham. SEPTA's Lansdale–Doylestown regional line ends at Doylestown, eight miles southwest, and Trans-Bridge Lines runs buses from New York to Doylestown several times a day. From either, you will need a car or taxi to reach Buckingham.

The nearest airports are Philadelphia (PHL) about 90 minutes south, and Trenton-Mercer (TTN) about 45 minutes east in New Jersey.
