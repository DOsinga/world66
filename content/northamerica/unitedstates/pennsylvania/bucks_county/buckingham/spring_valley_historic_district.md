Spring Valley, also known historically as Mechanic's Valley, is a tiny cluster of stone houses and former mill buildings along the Cuttaloosa Creek south of Lahaska. The district is on the National Register of Historic Places, recognised for the unusually well-preserved layout of a 19th-century rural mill village — grist mill, blacksmith, miller's house, workers' cottages, all clustered around the stream that powered them.

The mills closed long ago and the buildings are now private homes, but the lane through the valley still gives a clear sense of pre-industrial Pennsylvania. Drive slowly: the road is one car wide in places.
