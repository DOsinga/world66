The Doylestown Historic District is the town's walkable core, with older houses, civic buildings, shops, and side streets that still feel like a county seat rather than a suburb. It is the part of town that makes visitors linger after the museums close.

Wander without over-planning. The best route is simply to connect the train station, the museums, and the central streets, stopping when a storefront or cafe looks promising.
