Fonthill Castle is Henry Mercer's former home, a poured-concrete fantasy filled with tiles, books, and rooms that refuse to behave like ordinary rooms. It is one of the most personal house museums in Pennsylvania.

Tours are the way to see it properly. The building rewards anyone interested in craft, architecture, or the productive weirdness of people who had both money and very strong ideas.
