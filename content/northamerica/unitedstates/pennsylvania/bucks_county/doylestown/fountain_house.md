The Fountain House is an old Doylestown landmark at the center of town. Buildings like this give the borough its settled, lived-in feeling: not frozen colonial pageantry, but layers of local commerce and civic life.

It is a quick architectural stop rather than a museum-scale attraction. Notice it while walking the center, especially if you are piecing together Doylestown beyond the Mercer sights.
