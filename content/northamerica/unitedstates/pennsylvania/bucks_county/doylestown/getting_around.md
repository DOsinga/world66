---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Central Doylestown is walkable, including the train station, restaurants, shops, and the Michener and Mercer museums. Fonthill Castle and the Tile Works are close but sit just outside the tightest downtown grid.

If you are relying on the train, plan your route before wandering too far. With a car, Doylestown becomes an easy hub for the rest of central Bucks County.
