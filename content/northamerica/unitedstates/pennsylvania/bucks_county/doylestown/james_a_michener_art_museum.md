The James A. Michener Art Museum gives Doylestown a second strong museum identity beyond Henry Mercer. It focuses on regional art, especially the Pennsylvania Impressionists, and occupies a site that once formed part of the county prison.

It is polished and calm, a good counterweight to the cluttered drama of the Mercer Museum. Visit both if you can; they show two very different sides of Bucks County culture.
