The Mercer Museum is Doylestown's essential stop. Its towering concrete interior is packed with tools, vehicles, trade signs, and everyday objects that Henry Mercer collected to document American life before industrial mass production.

It is eccentric, dense, and memorable. Give it more time than you think, because the pleasure is in looking up, around, and into odd corners rather than marching through a simple sequence of rooms.
