The Moravian Pottery and Tile Works preserves the tile-making side of Henry Mercer's world. The building itself is part of the pleasure: another concrete structure, more workshop than castle, where decorative tiles connect Bucks County craft to Arts and Crafts ideals.

It pairs naturally with Fonthill, which used Mercer tiles lavishly. If you like handmade things, this may be the stop that makes Doylestown feel less like a museum town and more like a working craft story.
