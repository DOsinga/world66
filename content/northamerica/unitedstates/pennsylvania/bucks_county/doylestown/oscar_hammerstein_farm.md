Oscar Hammerstein II lived and worked at Highland Farm outside Doylestown, tying the town quietly to the golden age of American musical theatre. The rural setting suited a writer whose work is often remembered for big emotions and deceptively simple songs.

Access and programming can vary, so check before making a special trip. Even from the outside, it is a useful reminder that Bucks County drew artists and writers as well as antique hunters.
