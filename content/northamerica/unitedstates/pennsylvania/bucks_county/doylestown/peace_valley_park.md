Peace Valley Park spreads around Lake Galena just outside Doylestown, with trails, picnic areas, boating, fishing, and a nature centre. It is a useful place to slow down between Bucks County's historic houses and museums, especially if you want an easy walk by the water rather than another indoor stop.
