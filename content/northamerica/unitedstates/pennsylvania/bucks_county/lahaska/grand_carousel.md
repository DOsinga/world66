The Grand Carousel is the most historically significant attraction at Peddler's Village, and one of the finest surviving examples of American carousel craftsmanship. Built around 1922 by the William H. Dentzel Company of Philadelphia — one of the great American carousel makers — it features 56 hand-carved wooden animals in a mix of horses, pigs, cats, rabbits, and a giraffe, all rendered with the individual character and flowing manes that made Dentzel carousels prized.

The carousel operates at Peddler's Village seasonally, usually from spring through the holiday season. A single ride costs a few dollars; the slow spin through the music and the sight of the carved animals in motion is worth it regardless of age. The carousel is listed in the National Register of Historic Places.
