A family-run Mexican restaurant in the borough centre, Al Mexica serves straightforward and satisfying plates — tacos, enchiladas, chile rellenos — made with care and without concessions to a blander American palate. Portions are generous, prices are low, and the lunch crowds from local businesses give it a lively midday atmosphere. A reliable and inexpensive choice after a morning at the historic district.
