---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Carlisle's bar and cafe scene runs on two overlapping currents: the college crowd from Dickinson and the military community from the Barracks. The historic district holds most of the independent spots, clustered along High Street and the streets radiating from the central square. Expect a mix of traditional American taverns, a few craft beer-focused bars, and a handful of coffee shops that double as study spaces during the academic year.

The town is lively on weekend evenings when Dickinson is in session; quieter in summer when students are away.
