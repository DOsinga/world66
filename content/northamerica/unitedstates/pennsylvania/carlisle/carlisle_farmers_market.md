The Saturday farmers' market runs in the central square of the historic district through the warmer months. Vendors bring Cumberland Valley produce, Pennsylvania Dutch baked goods, locally raised meats, artisan cheeses, honey, and preserves. It is the best single place to sample the agricultural character of the region — this is rich limestone farmland, and the quality of the vegetables and dairy reflects it. The market draws a lively morning crowd and is usually done by midday.
