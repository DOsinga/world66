A well-regarded independent coffeehouse near the Dickinson College campus, Cornerstone serves quality espresso drinks and drip coffee in a relaxed space with plenty of seating. It is the natural choice for a mid-morning break after walking the historic district or the campus. The atmosphere is collegiate but unhurried — regulars include students writing papers, faculty between classes, and locals who have been coming here long enough to have a usual order. Pastries and light snacks are available.
