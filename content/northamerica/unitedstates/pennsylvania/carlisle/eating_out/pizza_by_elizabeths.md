A popular local pizza shop in the historic district, Pizza By Elizabeth's has built a following for its hand-tossed pies with quality toppings and a light, crisp crust. The menu is straightforward — classic combinations done well — and the slices are a reliable and inexpensive lunch option when you are walking the town centre. Takeout is the main mode; there is limited seating.
