A long-standing bar and gathering place in the Carlisle historic district, The Grog draws a mixed crowd of locals, Dickinson students, and military personnel from the Barracks. The atmosphere is unpretentious, the beer selection leans toward Pennsylvania craft options, and the bar food is solid. It has the feel of a place that has been here for decades and expects to be here for decades more — which, in Carlisle's bar landscape, is something worth valuing.
