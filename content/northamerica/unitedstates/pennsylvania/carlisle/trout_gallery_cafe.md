A lively bar in the historic district, The Depot draws a broad mix of townspeople, college students, and visitors to Carlisle for the car auctions or the military sites. The building has bones — exposed brick, high ceilings — and the drinks menu includes a decent range of Pennsylvania craft beers alongside the standards. It gets busy on Friday and Saturday evenings when Dickinson is in session. A reliable choice for an evening drink in the town centre.
