---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

**Two Rivers Brewing** is the obvious anchor — a brewpub in a restored hotel building one block off Centre Square, with a long list of house beers and a cellar tasting room. **Weyerbacher Brewing** does not have its own tasting room in town any more but its beers are stocked everywhere.

For coffee, **Terra Café** on 3rd Street is the standard, and **Easton Coffee Co.** does single-origin pour-overs. **Riverside Barre & Tavern** sits literally on the river, with summer outdoor seating overlooking the Lehigh.

The cocktail bar scene is small but real: **Stoke** does seasonal cocktails with a Pennsylvania-rye lean, and **3rd & Ferry Fish Market** has good oysters at the bar.
