Bethlehem (15 minutes west) is the obvious add-on — for the **SteelStacks** music and arts complex on the rusted hulks of the old Bethlehem Steel mill, and for the **Moravian Bookshop** in the historic district. **Allentown** further west has the **Da Vinci Science Center** and Dorney Park.

For a more rural day, head north into the **Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area** — 30 minutes up I-80 — with kayaking, hiking the Mt Tammany trail, and the Bushkill Falls. South towards [Bucks County](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/bucks_county) puts you in the riverine villages of New Hope and Lambertville. [Philadelphia](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/philadelphia) is about 90 minutes south on the turnpike for a full day in the city.
