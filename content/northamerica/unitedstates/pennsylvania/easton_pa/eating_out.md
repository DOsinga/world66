---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Easton's restaurant scene has come a long way since the early 2000s. The cluster around Centre Square is the obvious place to look: **Mister Lee's Noodles** for late-night ramen, **Two Rivers Brewing** for hearty bar food and decent house IPAs, and **The Bayou** for Cajun. **Easton Wine Project** does small plates in a tucked-away wine bar.

The genuine highlight for many visitors is the **Easton Public Market** on Northampton Street — a permanent indoor food hall with stalls for empanadas (Don Juan), Bolognese (Modern Crumb), wood-fired pizza, gelato, and one of the better fishmongers in the Lehigh Valley. Open Wednesday through Sunday.

A short drive across the bridge in Phillipsburg, **Pizzeria Marcellos** is the kind of neighbourhood Italian-American spot where the menu hasn't changed in thirty years and shouldn't.
