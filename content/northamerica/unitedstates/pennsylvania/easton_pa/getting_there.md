---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Easton is just off **Interstate 78**, exit 75, 70 miles west of New York City (about 1h 20m by car), 60 miles north of Philadelphia, and 5 miles east of Allentown / Bethlehem. The cross-river bridge from Phillipsburg, New Jersey, lands you within walking distance of Centre Square.

There is no passenger rail. **Lehigh Valley International Airport (ABE)** is around 20 minutes west and has limited service — mainly to Florida, Detroit, and the New York hubs. Most fly-in visitors use **Newark Liberty (EWR)** instead, roughly 70 minutes away by car or via the connecting **Trans-Bridge Lines** coach, which runs from Easton's intermodal terminal direct to the Port Authority and to Newark Airport several times daily.
