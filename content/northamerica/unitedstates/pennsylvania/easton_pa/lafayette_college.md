A small liberal-arts college spread over a wooded hill at the north end of Easton, with about 2700 students. The campus is one of the prettier in the northeast — red-brick Colonial Revival buildings, big oaks, and panoramic views down to the river junction.

Visitors are welcome on the College Hill grounds. The **Williams Visual Arts Building** at the foot of the hill hosts changing exhibitions, and the **Skillman Library** has a small museum of college artefacts. The Saturday afternoon football games at **Fisher Stadium** are an event in their own right; the Lafayette–Lehigh rivalry, "the most played rivalry in college football," dates to 1884.
