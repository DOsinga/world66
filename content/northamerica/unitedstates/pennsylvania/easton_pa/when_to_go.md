---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Easton is at its best in **late spring and early fall**. May to early June has clear days, the canal boat is back in operation, and the riverside is at peak green. **October** brings the foliage along the Delaware and Lehigh valleys — the towpath walks at Hugh Moore Park and the views from College Hill at Lafayette are at their best.

Summer is humid but pleasant; the **Heritage Day** celebrations on the Sunday closest to 8 July recreate the 1776 reading of the Declaration on Centre Square. **Easton Out Loud** (third Friday of each month) draws crowds for late-night gallery openings and music.

December is a clear local high point: the **Peace Candle** goes up over Centre Square for the holiday season, and the surrounding shops stay open late through the **Easton Heritage Holiday Parade**.
