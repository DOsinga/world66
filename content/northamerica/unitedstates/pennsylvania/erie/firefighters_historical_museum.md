Firefighter's Historical Museum is a small, specialist museum, but it adds a useful local-history layer to Erie. The collection focuses on firefighting equipment, firehouse culture, and the dangerous work that shaped city life long before modern emergency services.

It is best for travellers who enjoy civic history, vintage vehicles, or compact museums with a strong volunteer spirit. Pair it with downtown Erie sights rather than making it the centre of a day, and check opening hours carefully before turning up.
