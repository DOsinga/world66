The Bicentennial Tower rises from Dobbins Landing at Erie's waterfront, giving one of the simplest and best views over Presque Isle Bay, the downtown shoreline, and the peninsula beyond. Its purpose is straightforward: go up, look out, and understand the geography that defines the city.

It pairs naturally with the maritime museum, bayfront restaurants, and a drive or bike ride toward Presque Isle. Clear weather matters, and sunset can be especially good when the bay, boats, and low city skyline all catch the light.
