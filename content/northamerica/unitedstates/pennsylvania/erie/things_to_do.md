---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

[Presque Isle State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/presque_isle_state_park) is the city's signature attraction — a sandy peninsula in Lake Erie with swimming beaches, bike paths, kayaking, and superb sunsets. The [Tom Ridge Environmental Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/tom_ridge_environmental_center) at the park entrance explains the geology and ecology before you set out. The [Erie Land Lighthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/erie_land_lighthouse) on the harbour bluff offers panoramic views and is one of the oldest lighthouse sites on the Great Lakes.

Downtown is anchored by [Perry Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/perry_square), a wooded park at the centre of the historic grid, and surrounded by notable buildings including the [Warner Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/warner_theatre), [Erie Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/erie_art_museum), and the [Old Customshouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/old_customshouse). The [Erie Maritime Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/erie_maritime_museum) and the tall ship Niagara are docked on the waterfront. For architecture, the [Cashier's House](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/cashiers_house) and [Charles Manning Reed Mansion](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/reed_mansion) are among the finest Greek Revival buildings in western Pennsylvania. [Frontier Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/frontier_park) on the west side offers a creek trail and woodland walks away from the crowds, while the [Erie Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/erie/erie_zoo) is a solid half-day family option in the south of the city.
