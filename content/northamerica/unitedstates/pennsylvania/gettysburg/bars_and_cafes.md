---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Nightlife in Gettysburg revolves around the pubs in the centre of town and the bars of the historic taverns south of the square. Term-time at Gettysburg College adds a small but lively student bar scene around Chambersburg Street. For a quiet drink, the bars at the Federal Pointe Inn and the Lord Nelson's Gallery downtown are pleasant. Ghost tours of the battlefield, leaving from various points in town, are surprisingly competitive among each other for evening business.
