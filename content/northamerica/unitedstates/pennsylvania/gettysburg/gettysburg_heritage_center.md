The Gettysburg Heritage Center introduces the Civil War era and the Battle of Gettysburg through exhibits, recreated scenes, and a self-guided museum route. It is designed as an accessible overview before or after exploring the battlefield.

It is a useful stop for visitors who want context on the causes, course, and memory of the battle without committing to a longer museum visit.
