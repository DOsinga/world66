The cemetery where President Lincoln delivered the Gettysburg Address on 19 November 1863. More than 3,500 Union soldiers killed in the battle are interred here in concentric semicircular plots arranged by state. A small marker on the slope above the cemetery — the David Wills "Soldiers' National Monument" — sits roughly where Lincoln stood.

Walking the cemetery is a quiet, sobering hour. The visitor centre across the road can supply context. Open dawn to dusk.
