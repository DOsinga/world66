Harrisburg Midtown Arts Center is a reused industrial building that gives Midtown a lively arts stop beyond the museums and state-government landmarks. The complex mixes live music, gallery space, film offices, a cafe, and other small creative businesses.

It is best as an evening or bad-weather stop, especially if a show is on at Stage on Herr. During the day it is more of a quick look while walking between Broad Street Market and the river.
