The Pennsylvania National Fire Museum occupies the 1899 Reily Hose Company No. 10 firehouse, a Victorian station north of central Harrisburg. Its collection follows firefighting from hand-drawn equipment to modern tools, with engines, uniforms, alarms, and smaller artifacts that make the old firehouse setting part of the visit.
