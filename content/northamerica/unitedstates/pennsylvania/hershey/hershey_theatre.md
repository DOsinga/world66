Hershey Theatre opened in 1933 and remains the town's main historic performance venue, hosting touring Broadway shows, concerts, and other stage events. Its appeal is not only the programme but the ornate early-20th-century theatre interior, built during the same era as many of Hershey's civic landmarks.

Check the schedule if you are staying overnight in Hershey; it is most rewarding when you can see the building during a performance.
