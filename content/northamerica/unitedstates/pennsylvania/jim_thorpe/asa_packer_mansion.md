The Asa Packer Mansion is the town's essential house museum, built for the founder of the Lehigh Valley Railroad and Lehigh University. It sits above town with the kind of confidence only railroad money could buy.

Inside, the draw is the preserved Victorian detail rather than a reconstructed display. Pair it with a walk down Broadway to see how closely the mansion, railroad, and old town fit together.
