Blue Mountain Ski Area is a winter sports stop within reach of Jim Thorpe, with downhill runs and snow tubing on the southern edge of the Poconos. It is a practical add-on if you are already in the area in winter and want a day outside rather than another museum or mansion tour.

Conditions matter here more than charm, so check the snow report before going. In season, it gives Jim Thorpe visitors a straightforward way to turn a heritage-town trip into a more active weekend.
