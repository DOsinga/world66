The Mauch Chunk Museum is the compact local-history stop for understanding Jim Thorpe before it became Jim Thorpe. Exhibits cover the old town, coal, canals, railroads, and the strange rise of a small gorge town into a 19th-century tourist destination.

Visit early if you like context before wandering. The town's name changes, mansions, rail line, and steep streets make more sense afterward.
