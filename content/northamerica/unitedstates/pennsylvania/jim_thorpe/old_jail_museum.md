The Old Jail Museum is a stern stone reminder that the coal region's history was not all scenic trains and pretty houses. The building operated as a county jail into the late 20th century and is closely tied to stories of labor conflict and punishment in the anthracite era.

Tours are the point here. Go when you are ready for a darker piece of local history, not just another preserved building.
