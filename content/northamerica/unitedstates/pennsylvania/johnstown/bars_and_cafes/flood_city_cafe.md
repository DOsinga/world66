Flood City Cafe is one of the more welcoming spots in downtown Johnstown — a coffee shop with good espresso drinks, light food, and a relaxed atmosphere. It draws students, remote workers, and locals who want somewhere comfortable to sit. The name is an unsentimental acknowledgement of what the city is famous for. Worth knowing if you need a good coffee before or after visiting the nearby flood museum.
