Coney Island Lunch on Clinton Street is a Johnstown institution that has been dishing out its signature hot dogs for over a century. The Johnstown coney — a steamed bun, hot dog, and a distinctive thin chili meat sauce — is one of those hyper-regional foods that locals are fiercely loyal to and visitors find surprisingly good. The restaurant is basic: counter seating, fast service, low prices. Order two or three. Cash is preferred.
