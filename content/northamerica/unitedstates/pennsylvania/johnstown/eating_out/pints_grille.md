Pint's Grille is a reliable downtown spot for straightforward pub food — burgers, sandwiches, wings, and daily specials. The atmosphere is casual and the portions are generous. It draws a mix of office workers at lunch and locals in the evening, and is one of the more reliably busy spots in a downtown that can feel quiet outside of events.
