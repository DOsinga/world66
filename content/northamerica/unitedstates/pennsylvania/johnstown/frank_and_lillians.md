Frank and Lillian's is a neighbourhood tavern in Cambria City, the old immigrant workers' district along the Conemaugh River. The bar has the feel of a place that hasn't changed much in decades, which is part of its appeal. Cold beer, low prices, and locals who have been coming in for years. It's the kind of bar that Cambria City was full of during the steel era and there aren't many left. Worth a stop if you're exploring the neighbourhood after visiting the Heritage Discovery Center.
