This small specialist museum is a change of pace from the quilt and craft shops. Its collection focuses on American military edged weapons, including knives, swords, bayonets, and related artifacts from several conflicts.

It is niche, but worthwhile if military material culture interests you or if you need a more focused stop along Old Philadelphia Pike.
