Kitchen Kettle Village is the main visitor cluster in Intercourse: small shops, food stands, crafts, quilts, and plenty of jars of jam and relish. It is commercial, but it is also convenient if you want a quick taste of Lancaster County products in one stop.

Weekends are busy. Go early or late if you prefer to browse without the bus-tour crush.
