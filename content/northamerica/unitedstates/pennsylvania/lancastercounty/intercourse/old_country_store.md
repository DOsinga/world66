The Old Country Store is one of the better stops if quilts and textiles are what brought you to Lancaster County. It sells fabrics and locally made work, and the building has long been tied to quilt displays in the village.

It is a shop rather than a museum in the strict sense, but the best pieces show the skill and patience behind Amish and Mennonite textile traditions.
