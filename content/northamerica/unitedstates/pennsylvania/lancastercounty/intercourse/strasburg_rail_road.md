Strasburg Rail Road is a heritage steam railway in Lancaster County, using restored passenger cars and coal-fired locomotives. The ride crosses Amish farmland and is one of the area's most popular family-friendly historic attractions.

Come for the train ride itself, but leave time for the nearby rail museums and village stops if you are making a full day of the area.
