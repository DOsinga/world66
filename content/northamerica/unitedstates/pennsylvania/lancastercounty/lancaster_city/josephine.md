Josephine's is a longstanding upscale-casual spot near Penn Square, with a small dining room and a menu that runs from raw oysters to substantial steaks. The dishes pull from French, Italian, and American traditions without being faithful to any of them; the result is reliably good rather than thrilling.

Worth knowing for a more polished evening out in the city centre. The bar serves a short list of classic cocktails. Reservations recommended.
