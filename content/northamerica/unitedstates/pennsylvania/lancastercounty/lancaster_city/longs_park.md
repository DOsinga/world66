Long's Park is a large green stop on Lancaster's west side, best known for its summer concert series and annual arts and crafts festival. The lake, lawns, and paths make it an easy break from downtown sightseeing.

It is most worth planning around when an event is on, but it also works as a low-key family stop before or after visiting nearby museums and historic sites.
