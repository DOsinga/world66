The seven-acre park stretches behind the main church complex, with the spring-fed Lititz Run threading its length. Founded by the Moravian congregation and now publicly owned, it is the town's gathering space — concerts in the bandshell on summer Sunday evenings, the Fourth of July candle ceremony, and a steady stream of families and walkers the rest of the time.

Pleasant year-round, with mature trees, picnic tables, a small bridge across the creek, and a children's play area. Free.
