---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Lititz is a town built for slow exploration. Most of the historic core is concentrated within a few blocks of Main Street and Broad Street, and the Moravian heritage shows in the simple early-American architecture, the small museums, and the quirky food landmarks that built the town's modern fame. Two food-makers — the Julius Sturgis Pretzel Bakery and Wilbur Chocolate — are the most visited, but the older Moravian church and the surrounding historic district are the heart of the place.
