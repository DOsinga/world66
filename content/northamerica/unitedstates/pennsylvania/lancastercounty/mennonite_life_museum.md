Mennonite Life Museum is a visitor centre and museum introducing Mennonite history, faith, and everyday life in Lancaster County. It is a useful first stop if you want context before exploring the county's Amish and Mennonite communities, especially because it helps separate real traditions from tourist shorthand.

The museum works best for travellers who want a quieter cultural stop rather than a photo-op version of Pennsylvania Dutch country.
