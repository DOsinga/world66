The Amish Village is a small open-air museum built around an 1840s farmhouse furnished to show everyday Old Order Amish life in Lancaster County. The grounds include a barn with farm animals, a smokehouse, a blacksmith shop, and a water wheel, so it works well if you want a simple introduction before exploring the surrounding countryside.

It is a deliberately tourist-facing version of Amish culture, but the house tour and farm buildings are useful for first-time visitors. Allow an hour or so, longer if you are travelling with children.
