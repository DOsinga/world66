Fallingwater is Frank Lloyd Wright's famous house built over a waterfall in the Laurel Highlands. Its terraces, stonework, and woodland setting make it one of the best-known works of modern architecture in the United States.

Visits are by timed tour, and tickets should be booked ahead. The grounds are also worth time before or after the house tour, especially for the classic view from below the falls.
