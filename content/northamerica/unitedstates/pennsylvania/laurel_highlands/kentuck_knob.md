Kentuck Knob is one of Frank Lloyd Wright's late Usonian houses, built from sandstone and red cypress on a wooded ridge near Ohiopyle. It is smaller and more intimate than nearby Fallingwater, but the low horizontal lines, hexagonal plan, and careful siting make it a strong companion visit.

Tours require tickets, and advance booking is wise in peak season. The grounds include a sculpture meadow and views over the Laurel Highlands.
