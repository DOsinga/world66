Laurel Ridge State Park follows the long spine of Laurel Ridge and is best known for the 70-mile Laurel Highlands Hiking Trail. The park protects forested highland terrain with shelters, winter trails, and long views across the ridge country.

It is a worthwhile stop for travellers in the Laurel Highlands who want a serious hike or a quieter stretch of mountain scenery.
