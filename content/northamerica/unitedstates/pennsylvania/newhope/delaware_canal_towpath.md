The Delaware Canal towpath gives New Hope its quiet side. A few steps from the galleries and restaurants, the path follows the old canal north and south, shaded in places and flat enough for almost anyone to walk or cycle.

Use it when Main Street gets crowded. Even a short stretch changes the mood of the visit, replacing traffic and shop windows with water, sycamores, old locks, and glimpses of the river corridor that made the town possible.
