George Nakashima Woodworkers is one of the most important design sites in the Delaware Valley. The house, studio, and workshop complex south of New Hope preserves the world of the Japanese-American furniture maker whose live-edge tables became icons of 20th-century craft.

Visits require planning, and it is not a casual drop-in attraction. For travellers interested in architecture, woodworking, or modern design, though, it is more memorable than almost anything on Main Street.
