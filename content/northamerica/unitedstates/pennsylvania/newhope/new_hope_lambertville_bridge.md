The New Hope-Lambertville Bridge is more than a way across the Delaware. It is the easiest, cheapest, and best view in town, with New Hope's riverfront on one side and Lambertville's brick storefronts on the other.

Walk it in both directions if you can. Morning light favours the Pennsylvania side; late afternoon warms the Lambertville facades and turns the river into a broad strip of reflected sky.
