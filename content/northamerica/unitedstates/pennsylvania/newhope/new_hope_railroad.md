The New Hope Railroad runs heritage excursions from the old New Hope station, a short walk from the centre of town. The ride is not transportation in the practical sense; it is a nostalgic loop through Bucks County woods and fields, with vintage coaches doing most of the work.

It is especially good with children or anyone who likes old rail infrastructure. Book ahead on popular autumn and holiday weekends, when foliage trains and themed rides can sell out.
