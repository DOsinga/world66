---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

New Hope's main pleasure is wandering: river, bridge, canal, theatre, galleries, and the old railroad are all within a short walk of one another. The strongest sights sit along the Delaware or just outside town in the wooded hills of Solebury Township.

Make time for the [Bucks County Playhouse](#bucks_county_playhouse), the [New Hope-Lambertville Bridge](#new_hope_lambertville_bridge), and the [Delaware Canal towpath](#delaware_canal_towpath) before chasing the smaller historic houses. If you have a car, [Bowman's Hill Tower](#bowmans_hill_tower) and the [George Nakashima Woodworkers complex](#george_nakashima_woodworkers) are the best short excursions.
