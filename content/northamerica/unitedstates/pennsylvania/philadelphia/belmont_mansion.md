Belmont Mansion is an 18th-century house on the edge of Fairmount Park, built in 1745 and later used as a stop on the Underground Railroad. The restored rooms now interpret the site's colonial history and its links to the fight against slavery, with views from Belmont Plateau toward the Philadelphia skyline.
