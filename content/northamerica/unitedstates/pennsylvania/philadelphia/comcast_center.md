The Comcast Center is a 975-foot Center City skyscraper and one of Philadelphia's most visible modern towers. Visitors can stop at the public plaza and the tall winter garden, where the huge lobby video wall turns a corporate headquarters into a brief but worthwhile architecture and media-art detour.
