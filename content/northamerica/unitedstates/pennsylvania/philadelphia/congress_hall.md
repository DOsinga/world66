Congress Hall stands beside Independence Hall and served as the meeting place of the United States Congress from 1790 to 1800, when Philadelphia was the national capital. The Bill of Rights was ratified here, and George Washington's second inauguration took place inside.

The interior tour is worth making time for because much of the furniture and decoration has been restored to the period. It gives Independence National Historical Park a more intimate political setting than the famous hall next door.
