Fireman's Hall Museum fills a restored 1902 firehouse in Old City with firefighting equipment, photographs, uniforms, and fire marks from the 18th century onward. It is a small museum, but Philadelphia's firefighting history gives it more depth than you might expect.

It works well as a short stop while walking between the Betsy Ross House, Elfreth's Alley, and Christ Church.
