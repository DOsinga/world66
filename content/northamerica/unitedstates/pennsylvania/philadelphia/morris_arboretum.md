Morris Arboretum is a 92-acre public garden in Northwest Philadelphia, with thousands of plants from North America, Asia, and Europe. The collection is especially strong on Asian flora, set in an English park-style landscape with Japanese influences.

It is a good change of pace from central Philadelphia's museums and historic sites. Give it time if you like trees, formal gardens, or quiet walks with room to breathe.
