The National Museum of American Jewish History stands near Independence Hall and the Liberty Bell, making its story of Jewish life in the United States part of the wider Old City history circuit. Exhibits trace immigration, religion, culture, civic life, and the changing place of Jewish communities in American public life.

It is a strong museum stop for visitors who want more than the standard Revolutionary Philadelphia route, and it pairs naturally with the National Constitution Center and the Museum of the American Revolution.
