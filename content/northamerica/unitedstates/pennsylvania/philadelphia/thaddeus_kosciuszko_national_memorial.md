The Thaddeus Kosciuszko National Memorial preserves the Philadelphia house associated with the Polish engineer and Revolutionary War officer. Exhibits introduce his work designing American fortifications, his role in Poland's struggles, and his links to the broader Atlantic fight for independence.

The memorial is small, but it adds a useful international angle to the Independence Hall area. It is best combined with nearby Old City and Society Hill sights rather than treated as a long stand-alone visit.
