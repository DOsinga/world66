Thaddeus Kosciuszko National Memorial is a small National Park Service site in the house where the Polish military engineer stayed in 1797. Kosciuszko designed important Revolutionary War fortifications, argued for liberty on both sides of the Atlantic, and remains a hero in Poland and the United States.

The visit is brief but worthwhile if you are already walking the historic district. Exhibits and a short film explain his career, and the preserved bedroom gives the place a human scale that the larger Independence Mall museums can miss.
