The Byham Theater is a restored historic theater in Pittsburgh's Cultural District, with the kind of gilded interior that makes even a small performance feel like an occasion. It hosts concerts, dance, theater, film events, and touring productions.

It is most useful to visitors as an evening stop rather than a daytime sight. Check the calendar before you go; the building is at its best when you are actually sitting inside for a show.
