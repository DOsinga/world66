The Cathedral of Learning is the Gothic Revival tower at the heart of the University of Pittsburgh. The exterior is impressive from across Oakland, but the main reason to go inside is the Commons Room and the Nationality Rooms, a set of decorated classrooms representing many of Pittsburgh's immigrant communities.
