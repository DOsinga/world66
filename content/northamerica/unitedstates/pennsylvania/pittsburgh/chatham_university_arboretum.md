Chatham University Arboretum covers the former Mellon estate in Shadyside, with mature flowering trees and landscaped grounds shaped in part by the Olmsted Brothers. It is a quiet walk rather than a headline attraction, but it rewards visitors already exploring Pittsburgh's East End.
