The Children's Museum of Pittsburgh is a hands-on museum on the North Side, aimed at families with younger children. Exhibits include optical illusions, water play, making spaces, performance areas, and playful nods to Mister Rogers' Neighborhood.

It is one of the most useful Pittsburgh stops for families, especially in bad weather, and combines well with the National Aviary or the Andy Warhol Museum nearby.
