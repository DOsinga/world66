The Frick Building is a 1902 downtown tower built for industrialist Henry Clay Frick. It is mainly an exterior and lobby stop, but the marble, metalwork, and stained glass make it worthwhile for visitors interested in Pittsburgh's early 20th-century wealth and architecture.
