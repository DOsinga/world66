The Frick Fine Arts Building brings an Italian Renaissance villa mood to the University of Pittsburgh campus. Inside, the cloister displays Nicholas Lochoff's careful reproductions of Italian Renaissance paintings, made with the seriousness of a scholar rather than a souvenir painter.

It is a worthwhile campus stop if you are already visiting the Carnegie museums, Phipps Conservatory, or the Cathedral of Learning nearby.
