Gulf Tower is one of downtown Pittsburgh's sharpest Art Deco skyscrapers, completed in 1932 for the Gulf Oil company. Look up after dark: the stepped top is lit in changing colors, a local habit that once doubled as a coded weather signal and now often marks holidays and sports wins.
