Highland Park is one of Pittsburgh's big green escapes, with wooded paths, formal gardens, reservoir views, and enough space to feel removed from the city without leaving it. The park was shaped around the old municipal water system, so its lakes and reservoirs give the landscape a different feel from a simple neighbourhood park.

Come for a walk, a picnic, or the views from the higher ground. It also makes a natural pairing with the nearby Pittsburgh Zoo and Aquarium.
