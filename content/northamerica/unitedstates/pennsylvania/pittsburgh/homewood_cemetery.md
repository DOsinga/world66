Homewood Cemetery is a large 19th-century cemetery in Pittsburgh's East End. It is quieter than the famous old cemeteries of cities such as New Orleans or Paris, but its mausoleums, statues, and memorial buildings make it worthwhile for visitors interested in funerary art and local history.
