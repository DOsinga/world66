Kennywood is Pittsburgh's classic amusement park, operating since 1898 in West Mifflin. It mixes modern rides with historic wooden roller coasters, including the Jack Rabbit, the Racer, and the Thunderbolt, giving it more character than a standard regional theme park. Families should plan a full outing rather than a quick stop.
