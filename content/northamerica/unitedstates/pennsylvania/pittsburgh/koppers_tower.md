Koppers Tower is one of downtown Pittsburgh's strongest Art Deco skyscrapers, faced in Indiana limestone with a polished granite base and a dark copper roof. The building is best appreciated from the surrounding streets, where its vertical lines and setbacks stand out among the Golden Triangle's office towers.
