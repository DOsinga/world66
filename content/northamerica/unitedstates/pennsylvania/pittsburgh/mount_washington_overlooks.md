The overlooks along Grandview Avenue give Pittsburgh its classic postcard view: downtown packed into the wedge between the Allegheny and Monongahela, with bridges and river traffic below. Take one of the inclines up to Mount Washington and walk the ridge for shifting angles on the skyline, Point State Park, and the river valleys.
