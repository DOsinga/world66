North Shore Riverfront Park follows the Ohio and Allegheny rivers with lawns, public art, boat tie-ups, and open views back to downtown Pittsburgh. The trail makes it useful as more than a photo stop: you can walk the river edge from the stadiums toward the science centre and keep the skyline in sight.

In warm weather, the Water Steps near PNC Park are a lively little pause, especially for families with children.
