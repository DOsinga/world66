Pittsburgh Zoo and PPG Aquarium is a large family attraction with animal habitats arranged around a long loop through themed areas. The zoo covers regions such as Asia, Africa, and rainforest environments, while the aquarium adds marine exhibits to the visit. Weekends can be crowded, so arrive early if you want a calmer walk through the grounds.
