PPG Place is Pittsburgh's glassy neo-Gothic landmark, a cluster of mirrored office towers built for Pittsburgh Plate Glass. The pinnacled main tower is one of the skyline's most recognisable shapes, and the plaza below turns into an outdoor ice rink in winter.
