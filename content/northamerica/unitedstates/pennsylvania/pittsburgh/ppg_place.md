PPG Place is one of downtown Pittsburgh's most distinctive architectural complexes, a cluster of glass towers built for Pittsburgh Plate Glass. The mirrored walls and spired roofline make it stand out from the surrounding office blocks, and the plaza is an easy stop while walking between Market Square, Point State Park, and the riverfront.
