Riverview Park is a wooded, hilly park on Pittsburgh's North Side, with walking trails, a playground, a swimming pool, and broad views back toward the city. It feels more rugged than many urban parks, especially once you leave the road and follow the paths into the trees.

It is a good choice when you want a break from museums and riverfronts without leaving Pittsburgh. Wear shoes that can handle slopes and muddy patches after rain.
