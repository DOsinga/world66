Rodef Shalom Biblical Botanical Garden is a small seasonal garden on the grounds of Pittsburgh's historic Rodef Shalom Congregation. It grows and interprets plants connected with the Bible, with displays evoking landscapes such as the River Jordan, the Dead Sea, and desert regions.
