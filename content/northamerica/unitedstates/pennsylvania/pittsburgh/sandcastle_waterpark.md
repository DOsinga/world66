Sandcastle Waterpark is a summer water park on the Monongahela River southeast of central Pittsburgh. It has pools, body slides, tube slides, and water-play areas aimed mainly at families.

It is a seasonal outing rather than a city landmark, but it is a useful option for visitors looking for a full warm-weather family activity near Pittsburgh.
