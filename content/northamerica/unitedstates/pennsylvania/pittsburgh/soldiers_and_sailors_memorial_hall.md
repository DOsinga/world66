Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Hall and Museum is a Beaux-Arts memorial building in Oakland, inspired by the Mausoleum at Halicarnassus. Its exhibits cover military equipment, technology, and the social effects of war, with a strong focus on remembering the people who served.
