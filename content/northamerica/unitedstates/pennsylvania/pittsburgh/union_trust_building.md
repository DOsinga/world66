The Union Trust Building is one of Downtown Pittsburgh's most dramatic early-20th-century office towers. Built by Henry Clay Frick in 1916, it mixes Flemish Gothic details with a steep mansard roof, terra-cotta dormers, and twin chapel-like towers.
