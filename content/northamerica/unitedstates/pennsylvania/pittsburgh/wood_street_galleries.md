Wood Street Galleries is a downtown contemporary art space with a focus on new media and installation work. It is smaller than Pittsburgh's major museums, but its exhibitions can be sharper and more experimental.

Use it as a short downtown stop, especially if you are already near the Cultural District or moving between the Andy Warhol Museum and Point State Park. Check the current exhibition schedule before making a special trip.
