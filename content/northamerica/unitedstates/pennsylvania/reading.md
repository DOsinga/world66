Reading is a mid-sized city in the Schuylkill Valley of eastern Pennsylvania, roughly halfway between Philadelphia and Harrisburg. It's a working-class city with a large Latino population — one of the largest proportionally in the US — and an economy that has seen better days since its industrial manufacturing base declined. But it has a genuine character and a few things worth knowing about.

The [Pagoda](reading/reading_pagoda), a red-painted Japanese-style tower perched on the ridge of Mt Penn above the city, is Reading's signature landmark — visible for miles at night when lit. It was built in 1908 as a hotel but never opened; now it's a public viewpoint with panoramic views over the city and the Berks County hills. Free to visit. Downtown, the [GoggleWorks Center for the Arts](reading/goggleworks) occupies a converted factory building and has galleries, studios, and a cinema. The [Historical Society of Berks County](reading/historical_society_berks) covers the region's Pennsylvania German heritage. The [Santander Arena and Performing Arts Center](reading/santander_arena) anchor the downtown entertainment district.

Reading was for decades a major outlet shopping destination — the old factory buildings downtown were converted into discount malls, and the area earned the nickname "The Outlet Capital of the World." The [Vanity Fair Outlets](reading/vanity_fair_outlets) in West Reading remain the largest concentration. The Reading Regional Airport houses the [Mid-Atlantic Air Museum](reading/mid_atlantic_air_museum), focused on WWII aviation, with a major annual air show each June.
