Reading Public Museum is the city's broadest cultural stop: part art museum, part natural-history collection, part science museum. The permanent galleries range from ancient artefacts and paintings to minerals, animals, and changing exhibitions, so it works well for mixed-interest groups.

The museum's grounds are part of the appeal. The arboretum, creekside paths, and nearby planetarium turn the visit into more than a quick indoor stop, especially if you are travelling with children or need a quieter break from downtown Reading.
