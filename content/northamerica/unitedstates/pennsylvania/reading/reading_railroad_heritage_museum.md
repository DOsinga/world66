The Reading Railroad Heritage Museum is the place to put the Monopoly-board name back into its real Pennsylvania setting. Its collection in Hamburg includes locomotives, passenger cars, cabooses, signs, photographs, and operating material from the Reading Company, which helped shape the industrial towns north and west of Philadelphia.

It sits outside Reading proper, so it works best for travellers with a car or a specific interest in rail history. For them, it is much more than a rainy-day filler: the museum gives a local, mechanical counterpoint to Reading’s architecture, mills, and wider coal-region story.
