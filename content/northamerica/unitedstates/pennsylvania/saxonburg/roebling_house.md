This is the modest two-storey clapboard house where John A. Roebling lived from the early 1830s while he developed his wire rope manufacturing in Saxonburg. It is privately owned and closed to the public, but worth a brief stop to read the historical marker and appreciate the unlikely origin of one of the great American engineering dynasties.

The Saxonburg Memorial Presbyterian Church next door, founded by the same German settlers, is the other significant landmark. The Roebling Park at the southern edge of town has interpretive panels that fill in more of the story.
