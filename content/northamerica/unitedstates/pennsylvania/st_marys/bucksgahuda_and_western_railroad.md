The Bucksgahuda and Western Railroad preserves narrow-gauge industrial railroad equipment from western Pennsylvania. It is a niche stop, but a good one for anyone interested in logging, mining, and the small railways that once did hard work away from the main lines.

Public access can be limited and rides are often by appointment or during events, so check before going. When it is open, it adds a different layer to St. Marys' heritage.
