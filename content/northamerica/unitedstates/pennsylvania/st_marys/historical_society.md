The Historical Society of St. Marys and Benzinger Township is the place to understand the town's German Catholic beginnings and the families who built it. Displays cover local artifacts and community history, and the society also keeps genealogy files for visitors tracing local roots.

It is a small museum, but useful if you want St. Marys to read as more than a brewery stop. The town makes more sense after you have seen its settlement story.
