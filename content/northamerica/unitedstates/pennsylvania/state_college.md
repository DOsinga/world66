State College is a university town in the geographic center of Pennsylvania, home to Penn State University and its Nittany Lions. On football weekends in autumn, the population swells as fans descend on [Beaver Stadium](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/state_college/beaver_stadium), one of the largest stadiums in the world.

Beyond football, the town has a lively downtown along College Avenue with restaurants, bars, and bookshops catering to the student crowd. The [Palmer Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/state_college/palmer_museum_of_art) on campus has a respectable collection, and the [Penn State Creamery](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/state_college/penn_state_creamery) sells some of the best ice cream in the state — the lines can be long, but it is worth the wait. Tucked inside the mathematics building, the [Octacube](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/state_college/octacube) sculpture is the kind of unexpected delight that a good university town produces.

The surrounding Nittany Valley is beautiful rolling farmland ringed by forested ridges, with good hiking at Rothrock State Forest and excellent fly fishing on [Spring Creek](/northamerica/unitedstates/pennsylvania/state_college/spring_creek).
