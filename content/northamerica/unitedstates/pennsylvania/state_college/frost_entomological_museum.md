Penn State's Frost Entomological Museum is a small but distinctive science stop in State
College, built around insects, biodiversity, and the research collections behind them.
It gives a close look at specimens that most travellers would never notice in the woods
and fields around central Pennsylvania.

The museum is not a blockbuster attraction, but it is a good campus-side visit for
curious families, naturalists, and anyone who likes specialist collections. Pair it with
other Penn State museums or arboretum time for a fuller day.
