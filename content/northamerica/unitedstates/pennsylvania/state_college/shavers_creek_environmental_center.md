Shaver's Creek Environmental Center is Penn State's nature center in the forested ridges south of State College. It offers trails, educational exhibits, and wildlife programming, including raptor-focused interpretation that makes it more than a generic park stop.

The center is worth the drive for travelers who want a nature break from campus and downtown. It also works well for families, especially when programs or seasonal events are running.
