Colton Point State Park faces Leonard Harrison from the west rim of Pine Creek Gorge. It is less polished, with rustic picnic areas and viewpoints that feel a little farther from the road-trip circuit.

The park is best if you want a second angle on the canyon or a quieter walk after the main overlook. Trails can be steep and muddy, and the drive around from Wellsboro takes longer than the distance suggests.
