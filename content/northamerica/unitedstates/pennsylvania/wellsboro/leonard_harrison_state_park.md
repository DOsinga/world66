Leonard Harrison State Park is the easy, first-stop view of the Pennsylvania Grand Canyon. The main overlook looks straight into Pine Creek Gorge, with forested walls falling toward the creek and the rail trail far below.

The park has a visitor centre, picnic areas, and trail access, so it suits travelers who want a strong view without committing to a long hike. Arrive early on summer weekends and in October, when the fall colour brings crowds.
