The William Goodridge House and Museum tells the story of William C. Goodridge, a formerly enslaved man who became a prominent York businessman and is believed to have helped people escaping slavery through the Underground Railroad.

The house is both a local history museum and a concrete stop for understanding Pennsylvania's role in the Underground Railroad network.
