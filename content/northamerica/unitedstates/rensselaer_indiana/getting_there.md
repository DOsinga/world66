---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Rensselaer sits on Indiana 114 immediately west of I-65, the main north–south highway between [Chicago](/northamerica/unitedstates/illinois/chicago) and [Indianapolis](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/indianapolis). Driving times are about 90 minutes from Chicago, two hours from Indianapolis, and an hour north from [Lafayette](/northamerica/unitedstates/indiana/lafayette).

Amtrak's *Cardinal* (Chicago–Cincinnati–New York City) stops at the unmanned Rensselaer station three times a week in each direction — one of the few small Indiana towns with a passenger rail service. Greyhound buses also stop in town on the Chicago–Indianapolis route.
