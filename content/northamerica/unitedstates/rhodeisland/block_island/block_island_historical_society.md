The Block Island Historical Society keeps exhibits on the island's past, from settlement and seafaring to tourism and daily life. It is a compact stop, but useful for putting the beaches, ferries, and old houses into context.

Visit early in a stay on Block Island if you want a better sense of the place before exploring by bike or on foot.
