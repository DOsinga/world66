North Light stands at Sandy Point on the northern tip of Block Island. The present lighthouse dates from 1867, the fourth built here after shifting sands and rough weather defeated earlier lights.

The lighthouse sits within a wildlife refuge, so the walk is part of the appeal: dunes, surf, birds, and a wide sense of the island's exposed Atlantic edge.
