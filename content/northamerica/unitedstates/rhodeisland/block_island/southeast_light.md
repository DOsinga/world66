Southeast Light stands above the Mohegan Bluffs, where the island drops sharply toward the Atlantic. The brick lighthouse is one of Block Island's classic stops, both for the building itself and for the wide view over the water.

Pair it with the bluff stairs nearby. Even a short visit gives you the dramatic southern edge of the island without needing a long hike.
