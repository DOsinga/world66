Paine House is a small historic house museum in Coventry, preserving an 18th-century domestic setting and local-history collections. It helps make sense of Rhode Island beyond the Newport mansions and Providence institutions, showing a more rural side of the state.

Opening hours can be limited, so check before making a special trip. If it is open, the house is a worthwhile short stop for travellers interested in early American domestic life and local preservation.
