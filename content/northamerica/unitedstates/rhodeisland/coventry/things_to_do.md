Coventry is a small New England town — most visitors come for a specific reason rather than to explore broadly. The [Nathanael Greene Homestead](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/coventry/nathanael_greene_homestead) is the principal historical attraction, a well-preserved 18th-century house with knowledgeable guides and a genuine connection to the Revolutionary War. Nearby in Whaley's Hollow, the [Waterman Tavern](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/coventry/waterman_tavern) is a 1744 colonial roadhouse that was a militia muster point during the war.

For outdoor time, the [Trestle Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/coventry/trestle_trail) follows a converted rail line through the forests of western Coventry into Connecticut — good for cycling and walking. The [George B. Parker Woodland Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/coventry/george_parker_woodland_wildlife_refuge) is a large Audubon property with quiet trails through stone-walled forest. In the east of town, [Flat River Reservoir](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/coventry/flat_river_reservoir) — known locally as Johnson's Pond — is the largest lake in town and a popular spot for fishing, kayaking, and summer swimming.
