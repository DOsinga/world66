Coventry is a small New England town — most visitors come for a specific reason rather than to explore broadly. The [Nathanael Greene Homestead](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/coventry/nathanael_greene_homestead) is the principal historical attraction, a well-preserved 18th-century house with knowledgeable guides and a genuine connection to the Revolutionary War.

For outdoor time, the [Trestle Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/coventry/trestle_trail) follows a converted rail line through the forests of western Coventry into Connecticut — good for cycling and walking. Lake Tiogue and Heyburn Lake in the western part of town are popular with fishing, kayaking, and (in summer) swimming.
