Narragansett is a coastal town on the south shore of [Rhode Island](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland), looking out across the Atlantic and Narragansett Bay. It has been a beach resort since the 19th century — the ornate stone [Towers](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/narragansett/the_towers), the last remnant of a grand Victorian casino that burned down in 1900, still straddle the road leading to the beach.

[Narragansett Town Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/narragansett/narragansett_town_beach) is the main draw, a wide sandy stretch popular with surfers and families. The [Point Judith](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/narragansett/point_judith_lighthouse) area at the southern tip has a lighthouse, a fishing harbour at [Galilee](/northamerica/unitedstates/rhodeisland/narragansett/galilee), and the ferry to Block Island. In summer the town is busy but never overwhelming — it has a relaxed, unpretentious feel compared to the Newport scene across the bay.
