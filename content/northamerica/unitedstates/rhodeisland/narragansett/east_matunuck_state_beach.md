East Matunuck State Beach is a quieter South County beach near the mouth of Point Judith Pond, with enough surf to interest local board riders and enough space for families who want a simpler beach day. The sand sits a short walk from the parking area, backed by dunes and marshy edges rather than heavy development.

It is less central than Narragansett Town Beach and less sheltered than Roger Wheeler, which is part of the appeal. Come for room to spread out, Atlantic water, and an easy pairing with Galilee or the seafood shacks around Point Judith.
