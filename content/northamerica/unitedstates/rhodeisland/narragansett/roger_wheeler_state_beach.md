Roger Wheeler State Beach is the practical, easygoing beach choice at the Point Judith end of Narragansett. Its breakwater-protected water is usually gentler than the town's surf-facing beaches, which makes it popular with families, cautious swimmers, and anyone who wants a simple sand-and-swim afternoon.

It is not the most dramatic beach in South County, but it is one of the most usable. Pair it with Galilee, Point Judith Lighthouse, or a seafood stop nearby, and check Rhode Island state beach updates in summer for parking and facility conditions.
