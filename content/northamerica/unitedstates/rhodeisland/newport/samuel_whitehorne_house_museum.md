The Samuel Whitehorne House Museum offers a quieter Newport than the Gilded Age mansions. Built in the 18th century, it focuses on Newport furniture, craftsmanship, and the prosperous mercantile world that existed long before Bellevue Avenue became famous.

That earlier layer is important. The museum helps explain Newport as a colonial port city, not just a summer playground for industrial fortunes. Its scale is domestic and detailed, making it a good stop for travelers who like houses where joinery, rooms, and objects tell the story.
