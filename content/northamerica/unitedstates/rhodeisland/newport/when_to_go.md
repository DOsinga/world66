---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Newport is primarily a summer destination, and **July and August** are when everything is open, the harbor is busy, and the city is at its most festive — also when it is most expensive and most crowded. The **Newport Folk Festival** (late July) and **Newport Jazz Festival** (early August) at Fort Adams are among the most storied music events in America and require advance booking well ahead; the rest of the city is packed during those weekends.

**Late May, June, and September** are the best times to visit — the mansions are open, the weather is pleasant, the sea is swimmable (by September, at least), and the crowds are thinner. Hotel prices drop substantially after Labor Day. **October** is beautiful in the harbor and useful for off-peak mansion visits, though some amenities reduce hours.

**Winter** closes many Bellevue Avenue properties and most of the waterfront restaurants, but the core Preservation Society mansions operate on a reduced schedule year-round. A winter day in Newport — the Cliff Walk deserted, the mansions ghostly quiet, the harbor empty — has a melancholy quality that is entirely its own.
