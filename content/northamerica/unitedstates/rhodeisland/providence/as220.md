AS220 is a downtown Providence arts centre with galleries, performance space, artist studios, and a bar-cafe under one roof. The programme changes constantly: local exhibitions, live music, readings, talks, and small shows are all part of the mix.

It is a good place to see Providence's creative side without making a formal night of it. Check the schedule before you go; on the right evening it can be one of the liveliest stops downtown.
