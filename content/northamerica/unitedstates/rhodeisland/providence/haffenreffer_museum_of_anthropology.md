The Haffenreffer Museum of Anthropology is Brown University's anthropology museum, with ethnographic and archaeological collections from around the world. Its holdings are especially strong in Indigenous arts of the Americas, Africa, and Southeast Asia.

The museum is a focused College Hill stop, best paired with the RISD Museum, the Providence Athenaeum, and a walk through Brown's historic campus.
