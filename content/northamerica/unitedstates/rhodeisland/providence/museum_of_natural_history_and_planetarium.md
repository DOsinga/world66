Providence's Museum of Natural History and Planetarium is a modest but useful stop in Roger Williams Park. The collections cover Rhode Island birds, marine life, insects, minerals, fossils, and Native American cultural material.

It is especially good with children, partly because the scale is manageable and partly because the Cormack Planetarium adds short sky shows to the visit. Check the show schedule before going, since planetarium times are more limited than museum hours.
