Aldrich Mansion is a large late-19th-century estate on Warwick Neck, more restrained than Newport's famous cottages but cut from a similar world of water views, formal grounds, and old wealth. Today it is best known as an event venue.

Public access depends on events and policies, so check before planning around it. Even from outside, it helps explain why this stretch of Warwick Neck became one of the state's desirable bayfront addresses.
