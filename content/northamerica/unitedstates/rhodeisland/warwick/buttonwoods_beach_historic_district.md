Buttonwoods Beach Historic District preserves a quieter shoreline community on Greenwich Bay. Its value is not one grand building, but the pattern of summer cottages, narrow streets, and bay-facing domestic architecture that once defined a lot of Rhode Island resort life.

Walk or drive respectfully; this is a lived-in neighbourhood, not an open-air museum. It works best for travellers who like vernacular architecture and small coastal settlements.
