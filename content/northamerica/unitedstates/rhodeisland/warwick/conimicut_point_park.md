Conimicut Point Park is a small bayfront park with views over Narragansett Bay and toward Conimicut Lighthouse. At low tide, a sandbar reaches out in the direction of the light, though currents make it a place to admire rather than casually explore. It works best as part of a Warwick shoreline loop with Oakland Beach, Rocky Point, and Warwick Neck.
