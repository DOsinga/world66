The Historical Warwick Museum of Art is a local arts centre with exhibitions, performances, and community events. It has been part of Warwick's cultural scene since the 1970s and is the city's main non-profit arts organization.

Check the current programme before going, since the value of a visit depends on what is on. When exhibitions or events line up with your interests, it is a useful stop in Warwick.
