Oakland Beach is Warwick's classic local waterfront, set on Greenwich Bay with a small beach, a seawall, and seafood restaurants nearby. It is not wild or remote; it is the sort of place where you get chowder, watch families walk the promenade, and remember that Rhode Island's coast is often urban as well as pretty.

The beach is best in warm weather, but the bayfront walk is pleasant outside peak season too. Keep expectations modest and it delivers: water, gulls, boats, and a neighbourhood feel that is very different from Newport's mansion coast.
