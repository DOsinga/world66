Warwick's sights are spread out, so a car helps. The coast is the reason to come: [Rocky Point State Park](#rocky_point_state_park), [Oakland Beach](#oakland_beach), [Warwick Neck](#warwick_neck), and [Conimicut Light](#conimicut_light) show different sides of the same bay landscape.

For history and village texture, add [Apponaug](#apponaug), [Buttonwoods Beach Historic District](#buttonwoods_beach_historic_district), or the [Aldrich Mansion](#aldrich_mansion) grounds when access and events allow.
