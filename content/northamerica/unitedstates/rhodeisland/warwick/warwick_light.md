Warwick Light marks the southern end of Warwick Neck, where the bay opens toward the islands and shipping channels. The lighthouse itself is historic, but the bigger pleasure is the sense of being at the end of the peninsula, with water on several sides.

Access can be limited by surrounding private property, so treat it as a scenic drive and photo stop rather than a full attraction. It pairs well with Rocky Point and Oakland Beach on a slow coastal loop.
