Warwick Neck is a long residential peninsula reaching into Narragansett Bay. Much of it is private and quiet, but the drive gives a good sense of Warwick's older coastal geography: coves, lawns, yacht clubs, stone walls, and glimpses of open water.

Do not expect a boardwalk or a dense visitor district. The point is the landscape itself, especially when combined with nearby Rocky Point or a detour toward Warwick Light.
