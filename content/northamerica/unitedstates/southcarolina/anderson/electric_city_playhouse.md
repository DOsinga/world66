Electric City Playhouse is Anderson's small downtown community theatre, the sort of place that matters more to the town than to a checklist tourist itinerary. Productions range from comedies and musicals to seasonal shows, with local actors and a friendly audience.

It is worth checking the calendar if you are overnighting in Anderson or spending time around Lake Hartwell. The playhouse gives the compact downtown a useful evening option beyond dinner and a walk past the historic storefronts.
