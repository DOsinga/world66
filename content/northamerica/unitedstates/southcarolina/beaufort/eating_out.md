---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Beaufort eats Lowcountry: shrimp and grits, she-crab soup, fried oysters, and any iteration of the local prawn that the kitchen feels like cooking. The waterfront has a cluster of restaurants ranging from the dressed-up to the rough-and-friendly, and most do a respectable job. Bay Street and the side streets a block back are where to look.
