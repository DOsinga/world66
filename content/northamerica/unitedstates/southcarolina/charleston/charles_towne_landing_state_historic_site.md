Charles Towne Landing State Historic Site marks the place where English settlers established the colony that later became Charleston. The site combines walking trails, archaeological interpretation, a replica trading vessel, gardens, and exhibits on the early colonial period.

It is a strong choice if you want Charleston history beyond the polished streets of the old city. The grounds are spread out, so allow time to walk rather than treating it as a quick museum stop.
