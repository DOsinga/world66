City Gallery is Charleston's municipal contemporary art space, housed in a modern building at Joe Riley Waterfront Park. Its exhibitions change, often highlighting regional artists, civic themes, and work that broadens the city beyond carriage tours and preserved houses.

Because it sits beside one of Charleston's best waterfront walks, the gallery is easy to sample without overplanning. Check the current exhibition first; the experience depends entirely on what is on show. When the program is strong, it adds a useful contemporary voice to a city famous for its past.
