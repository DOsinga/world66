The Gibbes Museum of Art has occupied its Beaux Arts building since 1905. Its collection focuses on American art, with particular strength in works connected to Charleston and the South.

It is one of the better indoor stops in the historic district, especially if you want context for the city's visual culture beyond house museums and church architecture.
