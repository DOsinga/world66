The Halsey Institute of Contemporary Art is a compact but serious exhibition space attached to the College of Charleston. It focuses on contemporary artists, often with shows that bring in social, regional or experimental themes rather than simply decorating a historic city itinerary.

It is a good counterweight to Charleston's house museums, churches and antebellum streets. Admission policies and exhibition schedules can change, so check what is on before making a special trip.
