Kahal Kadosh Beth Elohim is one of Charleston's most important religious landmarks. The congregation dates to 1749, and the Greek Revival synagogue from 1840 is closely tied to the early history of Reform Judaism in the United States.

The building is a strong stop for visitors interested in Charleston's layered religious and civic history. Check current tour or visiting hours before going, as access can vary around services and events.
