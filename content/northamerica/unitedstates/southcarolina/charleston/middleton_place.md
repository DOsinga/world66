Middleton Place is one of the major plantation sites near Charleston, known for its long formal gardens along the Ashley River and for a layered history that includes wealth, rice culture, war damage, and slavery. The grounds are beautiful, but the visit is strongest when you give equal attention to the people whose labour made that beauty possible.

It is a day-trip style sight rather than a quick downtown stop, so allow time for the gardens, house museum, stableyards, and interpretation. Pairing it with other Ashley River sites can help put Charleston's plantation landscape in context.
