The South Carolina Aquarium sits on Charleston Harbor and works because it is rooted in local ecosystems rather than generic spectacle. Exhibits move from mountain streams and coastal plain habitats to salt marshes and the Atlantic.

Families are the obvious audience, but adults will get a good introduction to the state's environmental range. It is also an easy waterfront stop, pairing well with a harbour walk or a break from Charleston's dense historic streets.
