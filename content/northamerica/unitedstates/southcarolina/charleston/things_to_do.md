Charleston is best seen on foot: the [South of Broad](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/charleston/south_of_broad) neighbourhood is small, flat, and packed with 18th-century houses, churches and the [Battery](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/charleston/battery) waterfront. The slavery history — [Old Slave Mart](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/charleston/old_slave_mart), [International African American Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/charleston/iaam), [Fort Sumter](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/charleston/fort_sumter) — is the essential counterweight to the beautiful facades. [Rainbow Row](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/charleston/rainbow_row) and the [Charleston City Market](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/charleston/city_market) anchor the tourist circuit; the [H.L. Hunley](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/charleston/hl_hunley) submarine in North Charleston is one of the most extraordinary archaeological sites open to the public in the country.
