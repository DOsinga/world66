Clemson is a small Upcountry South Carolina town whose travel identity is almost entirely tied to the university. That can be a strength. The campus gives the town a walkable centre, a real sense of ritual, and a set of public sights that are more interesting than the average college stop.

The [South Carolina Botanical Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/clemson/south_carolina_botanical_garden) is the best all-purpose anchor, with trails, display gardens and the [Bob Campbell Geology Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/clemson/bob_campbell_geology_museum) nearby. [Fort Hill](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/clemson/fort_hill) adds the complicated plantation and university-founder history at the heart of campus.

On football weekends Clemson becomes louder, orange and far less subtle around [Memorial Stadium](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/clemson/memorial_stadium). Outside those weekends, the town is easier to enjoy slowly: campus lawns, downtown bars and cafes, and Lake Hartwell on the edge of town.
