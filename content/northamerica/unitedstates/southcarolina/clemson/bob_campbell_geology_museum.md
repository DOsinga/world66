The Bob Campbell Geology Museum sits by the botanical garden and gives Clemson a small but solid science stop. Its collections include minerals, meteorites, fossils and cut stones, with enough variety to work for families as well as geology-minded adults.

It is not a full-day museum, but it pairs neatly with the garden. Together they make the strongest non-sports reason to detour into Clemson.
