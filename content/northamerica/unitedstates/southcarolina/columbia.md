Columbia is the capital of South Carolina, sitting in the centre of the state at the confluence of the Broad and Saluda rivers. It is a university city — the University of South Carolina campus anchors the downtown — with a growing food and arts scene and a Civil War history written in cannon-ball scars on the [State House](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/south_carolina_state_house) walls.

Much of Columbia was burned by Sherman's troops in February 1865. The [South Carolina State Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/south_carolina_state_museum), housed in what was the world's first totally electric textile mill, covers this history with unusual depth — the Civil War galleries are among the strongest in the South. The [Columbia Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/columbia_museum_of_art) downtown holds a European collection of surprising quality. The [Mann-Simons Site](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/mann_simons_cottage) on Richland Street documents the life of Celia Mann, a formerly enslaved woman who walked from Charleston to Columbia in the 1840s after purchasing her freedom — one of the more remarkable stories in the city.

[Congaree National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/congaree_national_park), about 20 minutes southeast of the city, is the real reason to linger. It protects the largest old-growth bottomland hardwood forest remaining in North America — tall cypress and tupelo trees over a swampy floodplain that floods several times a year and resets the ecosystem each time. The elevated boardwalk is free, uncrowded, and genuinely spectacular. [Riverbanks Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/riverbanks_zoo) on the Saluda River is one of the better regional zoos in the country.

The [Vista district](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/vista_district) along Gervais Street has the best restaurants and the main cultural institutions. [Five Points](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/five_points) is the university neighbourhood, livelier at night.
