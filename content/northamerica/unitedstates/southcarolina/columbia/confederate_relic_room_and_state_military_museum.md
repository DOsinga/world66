The Confederate Relic Room and State Military Museum is one of Columbia’s more complicated history stops. Its collections began with Confederate material, but the museum now covers South Carolina military history more broadly, including later wars and home-front stories.

Go with an eye for interpretation as much as objects. Flags, uniforms, weapons, and personal items can be powerful, but the framing matters in a state where Civil War memory remains contested. The museum pairs naturally with the State Museum and State House for a fuller look at South Carolina’s public history.
