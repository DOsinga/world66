---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Columbia's restaurant scene has grown considerably, with a good range of options concentrated in the [Vista district](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/vista_district) along Gervais Street and in [Five Points](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/five_points) near the university. Southern comfort food, barbecue, and a growing number of international restaurants are all well represented.

[Motor Supply Co. Bistro](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/motor_supply_co) in the Vista is the standout for serious cooking. For South Carolina barbecue — whole-hog, mustard-sauce, the real thing — [True BBQ](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/true_bbq) is the benchmark. The university area has cheaper options including a good Vietnamese and Korean presence around Rosewood Drive southeast of campus.
