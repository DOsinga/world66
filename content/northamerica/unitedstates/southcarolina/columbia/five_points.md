Five Points is the student neighbourhood adjacent to the University of South Carolina campus, a dense cluster of bars, restaurants, coffee shops, and small businesses built around a five-street intersection. It is the most energetic part of Columbia on weekend nights and noticeably quieter during summer when students leave.

During the day, Five Points is good for a walk — there are independent bookshops, a number of well-regarded casual restaurants, and several coffee shops that make it easy to spend an afternoon. The neighbourhood hosts the St. Patrick's Day festival each March, one of the largest in the state.
