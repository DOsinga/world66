Riverbanks Zoo and Garden, a mile west of downtown on the Saluda River, is consistently ranked among the best regional zoos in the United States. The 170-acre complex spans both banks of the river — the zoo occupying one side, a 70-acre botanical garden the other — connected by a pedestrian bridge over a dramatic river gorge.

The zoo is well-designed with naturalistic habitats, and the botanical garden is worth exploring even without the animals. The South Africa section, with lions, giraffes, and rhinos in a large open enclosure, is a highlight. Good for families; allow a full day.
