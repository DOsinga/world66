---

Columbia offers a mix of Civil War history, strong museums, and easy access to remarkable nature. The [South Carolina State House](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/south_carolina_state_house) and its cannon-ball-marked walls are the symbolic centre. The [South Carolina State Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/south_carolina_state_museum) in the old Columbia Mills building is one of the better state history museums in the South. [Congaree National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/congaree_national_park), 20 minutes southeast, is the real standout — old-growth floodplain forest that feels genuinely wild.

The [Vista district](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/vista_district) and [Five Points](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/five_points) are the main social areas. For deeper history, the [Mann-Simons Site](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/mann_simons_cottage) and the [Woodrow Wilson Family Home](/northamerica/unitedstates/southcarolina/columbia/woodrow_wilson_boyhood_home) fill out the picture of who Columbia has been across different eras.
