---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Darlington's main draw is its raceway — NASCAR's oldest surviving superspeedway and one of the most storied tracks in the sport. On non-race days the track offers tours and the Hall of Champions museum provides serious depth for anyone interested in stock car history. The Public Square downtown gives the town its Southern county-seat character, and the two pieces of Darlington — the racetrack to the east and the quiet historic centre — sit only a couple of miles apart.
