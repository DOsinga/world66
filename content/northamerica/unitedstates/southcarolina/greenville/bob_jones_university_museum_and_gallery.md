The Bob Jones University Museum and Gallery is known for its collection of European Old Master paintings, with strengths in Italian Baroque and religious art. The collection includes works by artists such as Rubens, van Dyck, Reni, Tintoretto, Cranach, Ribera, and Murillo, displayed with period furniture and decorative arts.
