A notable fine-dining restaurant in the heart of downtown Greenville, celebrated for its creative American cooking with Southern roots. The menu changes with the seasons and the kitchen sources locally where possible. Scoundrel has earned a reputation as one of the best tables in upstate South Carolina — reservations are advisable, especially on weekends.
