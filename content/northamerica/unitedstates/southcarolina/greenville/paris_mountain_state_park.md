Paris Mountain State Park gives Greenville a real outdoor escape within a short drive of downtown. The park's wooded trails, lake, and ridges make it useful for hiking, mountain biking, paddling, or simply cooling off under trees in summer.

It is not wilderness, and that is the point. You can spend the morning in the park and still be back in town for lunch, making it one of the easiest ways to add a bit of Blue Ridge foothill landscape to a Greenville visit.
