Greenville's main performing arts venue, a complex of concert hall, theatre, and outdoor amphitheatre set along the Reedy River adjacent to Falls Park. The Peace Center hosts over 300 events a year — Broadway touring productions, symphony concerts, pop and country acts, and family shows. The riverside setting makes it one of the more appealing mid-sized performing arts facilities in the region. Check the schedule before you arrive; there is usually something on.
