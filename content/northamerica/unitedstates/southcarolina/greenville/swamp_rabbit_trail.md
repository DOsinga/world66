The Swamp Rabbit Trail is one of Greenville's best pieces of visitor infrastructure: a paved greenway that turns the city and its suburbs into an easy cycling or walking route. It links downtown, the Reedy River corridor, parks, cafes, and Travelers Rest.

Rent a bike if you want to make a half day of it, or sample the central stretch around Falls Park if time is short. The trail is practical as well as scenic, which is why it has become part of the city's identity.
