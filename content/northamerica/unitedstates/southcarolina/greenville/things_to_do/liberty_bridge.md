A 355-foot pedestrian suspension bridge that curves in a gentle S-shape above the Reedy River Falls inside Falls Park. Opened in 2004, it was designed to give an unobstructed view of the falls below — there are no vertical support cables on the downstream side, so the waterfall is visible from the full length of the bridge. The design won several architecture awards and has become Greenville's most photographed landmark.
