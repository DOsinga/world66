Mount Atlanticus is a large, deliberately odd miniature-golf attraction in central Myrtle Beach. Its two courses climb through a fantasy mountain with waterfalls, streams, murals, and themed structures.

It is a good family stop for travellers who want classic Myrtle Beach amusement rather than another beach or shopping complex.
