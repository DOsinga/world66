The Carolina Opry is one of Myrtle Beach's signature live-entertainment venues, built around polished variety shows, music, comedy, and seasonal productions. It reflects the resort's family-friendly performance culture as much as its beaches and golf courses do.

It is a night-out POI rather than a daytime attraction. If you want Myrtle Beach at its most choreographed and crowd-pleasing, check the current show and book ahead during busy holiday periods.
