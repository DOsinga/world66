The closest thing Aberdeen has to a third-wave coffee shop, the Red Rooster sits on South Main Street and pulls a decent espresso. Bagels, scones, and a tight breakfast menu; free wifi and a steady crowd of NSU students and downtown professionals.

Open from early morning through mid-afternoon. The default place to start the day if you are downtown.
