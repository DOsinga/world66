---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Aberdeen has a continental climate with extreme seasons. Summer (June–August) is warm and humid with daytime highs in the 80s F (around 27–32 C) and pleasant long evenings. This is the best time to visit — Storybook Land and Wylie Park are fully open, and the surrounding wheat country is at its most photogenic.

Autumn (September–October) is dry, crisp, and beautiful, with the prairie turning gold and the air sharply clear. Winter is genuinely brutal: temperatures regularly drop below 0°F (-18°C), and ground blizzards can close roads for days. Avoid December through February unless you have business here.

Spring is short and unpredictable; April can still produce snowstorms. The Brown County Fair in mid-August is the social event of the year and a good time to visit if you want to see the region at full energy.
