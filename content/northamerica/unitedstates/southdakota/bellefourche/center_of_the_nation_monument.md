The Center of the Nation Monument is Belle Fourche's signature roadside stop. A large compass-rose marker, flags, and visitor centre make the abstract idea of the country's geographic centre feel like a proper waypoint.

It is quick, free, and exactly the kind of slightly odd Great Plains stop that improves a long drive. Read the explanation, take the picture, and then give the nearby museum a look if you have time.
