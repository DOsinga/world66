title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Belle Fourche is a small stop, but it has enough for a short walk and a leg-stretching break. Start with the [Center of the Nation Monument](#center_of_the_nation_monument), then look around the [Commercial Historic District](#commercial_historic_district) and the museum/courthouse area east of downtown.

If you are interested in local history, [Minnesela](#minnesela) adds a ghost-town footnote to the valley's settlement story.
