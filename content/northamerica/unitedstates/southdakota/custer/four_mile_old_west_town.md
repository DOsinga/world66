Four Mile Old West Town is a modest, hands-on roadside attraction about four miles west of Custer. It is not a polished museum; the appeal is wandering through old-style buildings, boardwalks, wagons, and bits of frontier stage-road atmosphere.

This is best treated as a short, relaxed stop rather than a major sight. Families tend to get the most from it, but anyone interested in how the tourist West remembers the mining and stagecoach era can spare an hour here.
