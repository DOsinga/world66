Deadwood is a historic gold-rush town tucked into a gulch in the Black Hills of South Dakota. The gold rush began in 1876 and the town quickly became notorious — Wild Bill Hickok was shot dead at a card table here that same year, and Calamity Jane made it her base. Both are buried in [Mount Moriah Cemetery](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/deadwood/mount_moriah_cemetery), which overlooks the town.

The entire downtown is a National Historic Landmark. Today Deadwood runs largely on casinos — gambling was legalized in 1989 to fund historic preservation — but the old wooden storefronts, saloons along [Main Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/deadwood/main_street), and the general frontier atmosphere are genuine. The [Adams Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/deadwood/adams_museum) is the best place to make sense of the gold rush; the giant pit of the [Homestake Mine](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/deadwood/homestake_mine) up the road in Lead shows where the actual wealth came from. Mount Rushmore is about 50 miles to the south, making Deadwood a natural stop on a Black Hills road trip.
