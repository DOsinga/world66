Saloon No. 10 on Main Street is the closest thing Deadwood has to a single landmark bar. It claims, with some creative licence, to be "the only museum in the world with a bar," and its walls are crowded with mounted heads, antique guns, sepia photographs and the chair that Hickok was supposedly sitting in when he was shot — the original Nuttal & Mann's, where the killing actually happened, was a few doors away and burned long ago.

The shooting is re-enacted several times a day in summer, which is touristy but well-staged, and the upstairs steakhouse (Deadwood Social Club) is a step up from the casino fare elsewhere on Main. Drinks are reasonably priced and the place is full of locals in the evening.
