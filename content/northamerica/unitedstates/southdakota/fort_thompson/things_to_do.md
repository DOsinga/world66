title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Fort Thompson's principal draw is its landscape: the Missouri River backed up behind Big Bend Dam to form Lake Sharpe, the hills above the water, and the flat reservation land stretching out in every direction. The [Fort Thompson Mounds](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/fort_thompson/fort_thompson_mounds) are the most historically significant site — a complex of ancient burial mounds that survived the flooding of the original Fort Thompson townsite. [Big Bend Dam](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/fort_thompson/big_bend_dam) itself is worth a stop to understand what was lost when the Missouri was tamed, and [Lake Sharpe](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/fort_thompson/lake_sharpe) draws anglers and boaters from across the region.
