Madison is a small city of about 7,000 people in eastern South Dakota, roughly 40 miles northwest of [Sioux Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/siouxfalls). It sits on the shore of Lake Herman and is the home of [Dakota State University](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/madison/dakota_state_university), which gives it a bit more life than most towns this size on the prairie.

[Lake Herman State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/madison/lake_herman_state_park), just west of town, is a pleasant spot for swimming, fishing, and camping in summer. The park includes a historic homestead cabin from 1871. [Prairie Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/madison/prairie_village) on the north shore of the lake preserves over 40 historic buildings from the Dakota Territory era, and hosts an annual steam-threshing jamboree in July that is one of the region's more unusual events.
