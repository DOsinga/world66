Lake County Museum is a modest local museum in Madison, focused on the early settlement and community history of eastern South Dakota. Its collections are the kind that reward travelers who like ordinary objects, family stories, and the practical details of prairie life.

It is not a destination museum, but Madison is not overloaded with indoor sights, and this one gives the town some historical depth. Pair it with a walk around Dakota State University or nearby Lake Herman State Park.
