Pierre is one of America's smallest state capitals, and that is part of its charm. It sits on the Missouri River in the middle of [South Dakota](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota), far from interstate traffic and big-city self-importance, with government buildings, river bluffs, fishing boats, and prairie weather all sharing the same stage.

The [South Dakota State Capitol](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/pierre/south_dakota_state_capitol) is the obvious first stop, especially for its marble interior and lakeside grounds. Just north of it, the [South Dakota Cultural Heritage Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/pierre/cultural_heritage_center) is the best introduction to the state before you head west to the Badlands or Black Hills.

Pierre's other strength is its river setting. Cross to [Fort Pierre](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/pierre/fort_pierre) for early exploration history and Missouri River views, or stay downtown for old commercial blocks and the Trail of Governors statues. The city is not flashy, but it is a good place to understand the scale and solitude of central South Dakota.
