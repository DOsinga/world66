The Hughes County Courthouse is a useful downtown landmark, close enough to the capitol to make an easy architectural walk. Its presence reminds you that Pierre is both a state capital and a small county seat.

The building is a quick stop, not a major attraction. Pair it with the nearby historic commercial blocks and the Trail of Governors rather than making a special trip across town.
