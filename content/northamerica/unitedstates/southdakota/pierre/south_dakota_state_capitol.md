The South Dakota State Capitol is Pierre's essential sight, a handsome early-20th-century building with a copper dome, marble stairways, murals, and formal grounds. It feels grand without being overwhelming, which suits the city perfectly.

Give yourself time to walk the interior and the lakeside grounds. The building is especially rewarding if you have been crossing the open plains for hours and want a reminder that state government here still has a civic stage.
