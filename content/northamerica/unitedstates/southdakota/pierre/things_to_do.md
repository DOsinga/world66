title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Pierre's sights cluster around the capitol, downtown, and the river crossing to Fort Pierre. Start with the [South Dakota State Capitol](#south_dakota_state_capitol) and [Cultural Heritage Center](#cultural_heritage_center), then walk or drive the downtown blocks to spot governors' statues and older commercial buildings.

Across the Missouri, [Fort Pierre](#fort_pierre) and the [Verendrye Site](#verendrye_site) add the older fur-trade and exploration story that predates the capital.
