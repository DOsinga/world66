Art Alley is a downtown lane covered in murals, tags, stencils, wheatpaste, and whatever else local artists have recently added. It changes often enough that repeat visits do not feel redundant.

It is rougher and more spontaneous than a formal public-art walk, which is the appeal. Pair it with Main Street Square and the presidents statues for an easy downtown loop.
