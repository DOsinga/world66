Bear Country USA is a drive-through wildlife park south of Rapid City, best known for black bears but also home to other North American animals. It is a controlled attraction rather than wilderness, so set your expectations accordingly.

Families tend to love it, and it fits naturally on the road toward Mount Rushmore. Travellers looking for wild bison and open landscapes should prioritise Custer State Park instead.
