Chapel in the Hills is a Norwegian-style stave church tucked into the wooded west side of Rapid City. It feels surprisingly calm after downtown traffic and family attractions, with carved wood, steep roofs, and a small Scandinavian heritage setting.

It is a short visit, but a memorable one. Go for the craftsmanship and the contrast: a piece of Nordic church architecture sitting at the edge of the Black Hills.
