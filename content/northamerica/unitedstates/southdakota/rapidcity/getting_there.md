---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Rapid City Regional Airport is the main air gateway for the Black Hills and western South Dakota. Most visitors rent a car, which is the right choice unless you plan to stay entirely downtown.

I-90 passes just north of the city, linking Rapid City with [Sturgis](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/sturgis), [Deadwood](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/deadwood), the Badlands, and the long drive east across the plains. U.S. 16 is the classic route south toward Reptile Gardens, Keystone, Mount Rushmore, and Custer State Park.
