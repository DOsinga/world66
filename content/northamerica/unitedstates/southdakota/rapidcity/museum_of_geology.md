The Museum of Geology at South Dakota Mines is one of Rapid City's best small museums, with fossils, minerals, skeletons, and enough regional geology to make the surrounding hills and badlands feel less abstract.

It is particularly good for families and anyone heading toward Badlands National Park. The museum is compact, so you can do it properly without losing half a day.
