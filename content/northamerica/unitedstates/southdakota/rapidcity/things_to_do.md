title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Rapid City mixes downtown wandering with family attractions and Black Hills day trips. Start downtown at [Main Street Square](#main_street_square), [Art Alley](#art_alley), the [City of Presidents](#city_of_presidents), and the [Hotel Alex Johnson](#hotel_alex_johnson), then head up to [Dinosaur Park](#dinosaur_park) for the view.

For museums, the [Journey Museum](#journey_museum) and [Museum of Geology](#museum_of_geology) are the best bets. Families usually add [Storybook Island](#storybook_island), [Reptile Gardens](#reptile_gardens), or [Bear Country USA](#bear_country_usa) on the way toward the hills.
