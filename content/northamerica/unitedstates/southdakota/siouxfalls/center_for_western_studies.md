The Center for Western Studies at Augustana University is a focused stop for travellers interested in the Northern Plains rather than a general museum. Its galleries, collections, and programs look at regional history, Native cultures, immigration, and the wider American West.

Because it sits on a university campus, the visit is quieter and more deliberate than a mainstream attraction. Check current exhibitions before going; when the subject lines up with your interests, it adds useful depth to Sioux Falls beyond the falls, sculpture walk, and downtown restaurants.
