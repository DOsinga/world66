Spearfish sits at the northern edge of the [Black Hills](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/blackhills), where the open prairie suddenly gives way to pine ridges, limestone cliffs, and cold trout water. It is quieter than [Deadwood](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/deadwood) and less single-purpose than [Sturgis](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/sturgis), which makes it one of the easier Black Hills towns to actually settle into for a few days.

The best reason to come is [Spearfish Canyon](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/spearfish/spearfish_canyon), a narrow scenic drive south of town with waterfalls, trailheads, and rock walls that glow gold in late afternoon. [Bridal Veil Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/spearfish/bridal_veil_falls) is the quick roadside stop; [Roughlock Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/spearfish/roughlock_falls) and [Spearfish Falls](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/spearfish/spearfish_falls) reward a little more time.

In town, the [D.C. Booth Historic National Fish Hatchery](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/spearfish/dc_booth_historic_national_fish_hatchery) is better than its name suggests: ponds full of trout, old hatchery buildings, and a surprisingly good archive of early fish-culture work in the West. Black Hills State University keeps Spearfish from feeling like a pure tourist stop, and the compact downtown has enough coffee, beer, and local shops to make it a useful base.

Spearfish works especially well as the northern Black Hills overnight if you want to link [Devils Tower](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/devilstower), Deadwood, Sturgis, and the canyon without sleeping in the middle of the rally traffic. Winters can be sharp and windy, but the canyon is beautiful in snow; autumn is the safest bet for clear air, yellow aspens, and cool hiking weather.
