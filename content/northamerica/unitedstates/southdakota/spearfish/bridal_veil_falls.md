Bridal Veil Falls is the easiest waterfall stop in Spearfish Canyon, visible from a roadside viewing platform with almost no effort required. It drops in a thin white ribbon down the canyon wall, especially pretty after rain or spring snowmelt.

Because it is so accessible, it can feel crowded by Black Hills standards. Stop early or late if you want the sound of the falls without a line of cars behind you.
