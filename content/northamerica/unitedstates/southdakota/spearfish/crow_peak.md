Crow Peak rises west of Spearfish and gives hikers a real Black Hills workout without a long drive. The trail climbs through pine forest to open views across the northern hills and the prairie beyond.

It is a better choice for active travelers than for casual sightseers. Bring water, check conditions after storms, and expect the final stretch to feel steeper than it looks from town.
