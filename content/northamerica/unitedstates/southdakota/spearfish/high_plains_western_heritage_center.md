The High Plains Western Heritage Center sits north of Spearfish and tells the ranching, rodeo, mining, and Native history of the northern plains and Black Hills. It is a useful counterweight to the canyon: less dramatic, but good at explaining the country around it.

Expect saddles, wagons, regional artifacts, and a strong emphasis on working Western life. It is best for travelers who like local museums and want more context than a scenic drive can provide.
