The Sturgis Motorcycle Museum and Hall of Fame occupies a building on Main Street and earns its keep even for visitors who came for the scenery rather than the rally. The collection covers the full arc of American motorcycling — from early belt-driven machines to landmark race bikes to custom show pieces — with a particular emphasis on the history of the Sturgis rally itself.

The Hall of Fame honours inductees who shaped motorcycle culture, from engineers to racers to stunt riders. The exhibits are well-organised and the staff are genuinely knowledgeable. It is open year-round, which makes it one of the few places in Sturgis with something to offer during the quiet months. Admission is modest.
