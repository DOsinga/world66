Vermillion is a small college town above the Missouri River in southeastern South Dakota. The University of South Dakota gives it a steadier cultural pulse than many towns its size, but the real surprise is how quickly the town opens onto prairie, river bluffs, and Lewis and Clark country.

The [National Music Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/vermillion/national_music_museum) is the anchor that lifts Vermillion above an ordinary university stop. Add [Spirit Mound](/northamerica/unitedstates/southdakota/vermillion/spirit_mound_historic_prairie), a short but memorable prairie walk north of town, and the Missouri National Recreational River nearby, and Vermillion has enough for a spare day if you are already in the southeast corner of the state.

Do not expect the drama of the Black Hills. Vermillion is quieter: campus brick, broad streets, bars and cafes around Main Street, and open country just beyond town.
