The National Music Museum is Vermillion's exceptional anchor. Its collection runs to thousands of instruments from many cultures and periods, including rare early European, American, and non-Western pieces that make the museum far more serious than its small-town setting suggests.

Even travellers who are not musicians can enjoy the craftsmanship and odd variety of the instruments. For anyone interested in music history, it is reason enough to detour to Vermillion.
