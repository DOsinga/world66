title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Vermillion's best stops split neatly between culture and open country. Start with the [National Music Museum](#national_music_museum), then head north to [Spirit Mound Historic Prairie](#spirit_mound_historic_prairie) for the landscape view. If you have more time, the [W. H. Over Museum](#w_h_over_museum) adds local natural and cultural history on the USD campus.
