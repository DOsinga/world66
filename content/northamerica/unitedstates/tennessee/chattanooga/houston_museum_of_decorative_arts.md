The Houston Museum of Decorative Arts occupies a Victorian house in Chattanooga's Bluff View Art District. Its collection centres on antique glass and ceramics, with furniture, music boxes, coverlets, and other pieces gathered by Anna Safley Houston.

The museum is small, but it pairs well with the Hunter Museum and the bluff-top walkways nearby. It is best for visitors who enjoy domestic interiors, decorative craft, and the odd pleasure of a collection built around thousands of pitchers.
