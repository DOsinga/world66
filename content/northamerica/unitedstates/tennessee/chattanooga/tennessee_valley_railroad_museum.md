The Tennessee Valley Railroad Museum runs vintage train excursions from its preserved station east of central Chattanooga. The short Missionary Ridge Local includes a look at the restoration shop, while longer seasonal rides head into north Georgia. It is both a working heritage railway and a hands-on museum for visitors who want more than static displays.
