---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Clarksville is 40 miles northwest of Nashville on Interstate 24, about a 45-minute drive. Nashville International Airport (BNA) is the nearest major airport. No Amtrak service runs to Clarksville.

By bus, Greyhound connects Clarksville to Nashville, Louisville (Kentucky), and Memphis, with the bus station on Madison Street downtown. The Ride bus system provides limited local service within the city.
