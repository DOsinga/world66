Historic Collinsville is a rural open-air museum south of Clarksville, built around preserved log houses, outbuildings, and farm structures from the 19th century. The setting is part of the appeal: it feels like a small settlement rather than a display case.

The site is strongest for travelers interested in frontier and everyday history. Instead of a single grand house, you get a picture of work, worship, schooling, and domestic life in Middle Tennessee, with enough space around the buildings for the countryside to do some of the explaining.
