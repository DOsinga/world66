Cleveland's natural day trips are eastward into the **Cherokee National Forest**, the **Ocoee** and **Hiwassee** rivers, and across the state line into the small Cherokee historic sites at New Echota in Georgia. [Chattanooga](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/chattanooga), an hour to the south, is the obvious urban day trip — Lookout Mountain, the Tennessee Aquarium, and the Bluff View arts district are easy targets. **Dayton**, west of Cleveland, is the site of the 1925 Scopes Monkey Trial; the courthouse and small museum on the square are open to visitors.
