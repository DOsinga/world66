---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Cleveland's food scene has improved noticeably in the last decade as Lee University's student population has grown. The classic stop is **Jenkins' Deli** on Inman Street, a long-standing sub-and-soup shop in a converted gas station. **Bald Headed Bistro** on Frontage Road does fine-dining standards (steak, seafood) in a slick contemporary room.

For barbecue, **Curly's Backyard** north of town is a reliable stop with smoked brisket, ribs and excellent baked beans; **Stuart's Grill** does roadhouse-style breakfasts and meat-and-three lunches. Around the courthouse square, **Village Bakery and Café** is the place for pastries, sandwiches, and coffee.

Downtown has half a dozen small craft breweries and a couple of decent wine bars, mostly clustered around Ocoee Street.
