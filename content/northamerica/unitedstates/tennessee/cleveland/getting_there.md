---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By car.** Cleveland sits at the junction of **Interstate 75** and the east–west **US Highway 64 (Ocoee Scenic Byway)**, which makes it easy to reach by road. Chattanooga is about 40 minutes south on I-75, Knoxville about 90 minutes north, Atlanta about two hours south.

**By air.** The nearest commercial airport is **Chattanooga Metropolitan (CHA)**, about 50 km south, with several daily flights to Atlanta, Charlotte, Houston, and Washington. **McGhee Tyson Airport (TYS)** in Knoxville is the larger alternative.

**By bus.** Greyhound stops at a small terminal on Inman Street with daily connections to Chattanooga and Knoxville, but the schedules are limited. There is no passenger rail.

**Local transit** is provided by the **Cleveland Urban Area Transit System (CUATS)** with daytime fixed-route buses; most travellers will need a car to explore the surrounding national forest.
