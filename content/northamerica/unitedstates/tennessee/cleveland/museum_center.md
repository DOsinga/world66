The **Museum Center at 5ive Points** occupies a 1924 brick Carnegie library at the Five Points intersection. It is a modest community museum but well done, with rotating exhibits on Cherokee heritage, the Trail of Tears, the railroads and bobbin mills that built Cleveland in the late nineteenth century, and the Bradley County contribution to the Civil War. A small art gallery on the upper floor shows local artists.

Allow an hour. Free admission; donations welcomed.
