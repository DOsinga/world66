About 50 km east of Cleveland, the **Ocoee Whitewater Center** sits on the banks of the Middle Ocoee River, where a stretch of natural rapids was reshaped to host the **1996 Olympic whitewater slalom**. It is the only Olympic whitewater venue ever built on a natural river, and it is still used regularly by the US team for training.

For visitors, the venue is the easiest place in the southeastern United States to ride genuinely powerful whitewater. Several commercial rafting outfitters run trips on the Middle Ocoee (a thrilling Class III–IV run with named rapids — Hell Hole, Diamond Splitter, Tablesaw) on the days when the dam upstream releases water. There is also a respectable visitor centre, a network of mountain-biking and hiking trails, and a viewing platform above the main slalom drop.

Trips can be booked through several outfitters along Highway 64; allow most of a day for the round-trip from Cleveland.
