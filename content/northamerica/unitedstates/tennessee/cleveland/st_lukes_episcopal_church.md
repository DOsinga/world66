St. Luke's Episcopal Church is a small historic stop in Cleveland, best known for the Craigmiles Mausoleum behind it. The mausoleum was built for a seven-year-old girl killed in a carriage accident in 1871, and local stories still point to the reddish stains that appear on the stone.

Come for the churchyard and the piece of local lore rather than a long visit. It pairs naturally with a walk through Cleveland's downtown historic district.
