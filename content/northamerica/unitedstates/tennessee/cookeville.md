Cookeville is a small city in north-central [Tennessee](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee), set on the Cumberland Plateau between [Nashville](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville) and [Knoxville](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/knoxville). It is home to Tennessee Tech University and is the main urban base for the Upper Cumberland region.

The city itself is practical rather than grand: a courthouse square, student energy, local restaurants, and easy access from Interstate 40. The reason to linger is the country around it. Within a short drive are some of Tennessee's best waterfall parks, including Cummins Falls and Burgess Falls, plus lake and trail space at Cane Creek Park.

Cookeville makes sense as a stop if you are crossing Tennessee slowly or using the plateau as an outdoor base. It is less crowded than the Smokies and less flashy than Nashville, with a good mix of college-town services and close-by nature.
