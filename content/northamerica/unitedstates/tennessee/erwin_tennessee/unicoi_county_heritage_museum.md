The Unicoi County Heritage Museum on Federal Hatchery Road is the best place in Erwin to make sense of the town's layered history — the railroad era, the elephant hanging, the Southern Potteries operation, and the broader story of Appalachian settlement in the Nolichucky valley.

Southern Potteries deserves particular attention. The company operated in Erwin from 1916 to 1957, producing hand-painted ceramic dishware under the "Blue Ridge" brand. At its peak, the pottery employed hundreds of local women who painted individual designs on every piece — birds, flowers, fruit — making no two items identical. Blue Ridge pottery is now a collector's item, and the museum has examples alongside the railroad and community history. The elephant story is covered here too, handled with more nuance than the tabloid coverage that made Erwin famous for the wrong reasons.
