Hollywood Star Cars Museum is pure Gatlinburg Parkway entertainment: shiny, nostalgic, and unapologetically built around the pleasure of recognising a famous vehicle. The collection mixes screen-used cars and replicas associated with films, television, music, and celebrity culture, so the appeal depends less on automotive engineering than on memory and spectacle.

It is not a Smokies experience, and that is partly the point. After a wet hike, a family dinner, or a walk through the neon side of town, the museum fits Gatlinburg’s oddball strip culture better than a more serious attraction would.
