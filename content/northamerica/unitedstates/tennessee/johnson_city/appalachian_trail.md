The Appalachian Trail passes through the mountains about an hour from Johnson City, giving the city easy access to one of America's great long-distance hiking routes. The most popular local access points are at **Roan Mountain State Park** and the **Carvers Gap** trailhead on the Tennessee–North Carolina border, where the trail crosses some of the most open and dramatic high-country terrain in the southern Appalachians.

The **Roan Mountain balds** — treeless summit grasslands with panoramic views — are extraordinary at any time of year, but the June rhododendron bloom is the peak draw. Day hikers can access miles of trail from Carvers Gap without needing overnight gear; the hike along the ridge to **Round Bald** and **Jane Bald** offers some of the finest views in the eastern mountains.

Roan Mountain State Park itself has a campground, cabin rentals, and a swimming pool — it makes a fine base for a weekend in the mountains, about 45 minutes from Johnson City on US-19E.
