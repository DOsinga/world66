---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Johnson City is in the northeastern corner of Tennessee, roughly equidistant from Asheville, North Carolina (about 80 miles) and Knoxville, Tennessee (about 100 miles). The nearest commercial airport is **Tri-Cities Regional Airport** (TRI) in Blountville, about 15 miles northwest of downtown, with flights to Charlotte, Atlanta, and other hubs.

By car, the main routes into Johnson City are Interstate 26 from Asheville and US-11E/US-19E from Kingsport. There is no Amtrak service to the Tri-Cities area, and intercity bus service is limited. A car is essentially required for exploring the surrounding mountains.
