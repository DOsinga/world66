Rocky Mount is a living history site near Johnson City with a story bigger than its rural setting suggests. In the 1790s it served as the territorial capital of the Southwest Territory, an important step on Tennessee's road to statehood.

Interpreters and historic buildings make the place work best as an immersive visit rather than a quick photo stop. It is especially worthwhile if you want Appalachian and Tennessee history told through households, work, and frontier politics instead of battlefield monuments.
