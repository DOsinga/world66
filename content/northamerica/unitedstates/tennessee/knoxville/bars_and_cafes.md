---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The Old City is Knoxville's nightlife quarter — warehouse buildings converted into bars and live music venues, busy from Thursday through Saturday. Market Square has calmer options: outdoor seating, craft cocktails, and local bands on summer evenings. Tennessee whiskey and craft beer are the local drinks of choice. The bar scene skews young owing to the university, but there are plenty of places that don't feel like a college bar.
