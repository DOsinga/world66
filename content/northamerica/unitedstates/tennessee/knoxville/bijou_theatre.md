The Bijou is Knoxville's oldest continuously operating theatre — the building dates to 1817, when it served as a hotel, and the current theatre has occupied part of the structure since 1909. It sits on Gay Street a few blocks from Market Square and is the city's main venue for indie rock, alternative, and roots music.

The interior is intimate by concert standards, with capacity around 700 and reasonable sightlines from most positions. The programming is consistently interesting — touring indie acts, local favourites, and occasional theatrical productions. It occupies a peculiar building that has been a hotel, a Confederate hospital, and an opera house at different points in its history, and the combination of old bones and live music gives it a character that purpose-built venues rarely achieve.
