---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Knoxville's food scene has grown substantially since the downtown revival took hold. Market Square is the epicentre — restaurants ring the square and spread through the adjacent streets. The city has a genuine Southern food culture: barbecue, country ham, biscuits, and cornbread done well. A growing number of chef-driven spots have added more variety, and the Old City neighbourhood has strong bar food and late-night options. The Tennessean appetite for craft beer has also encouraged decent gastropub kitchens.
