The Knoxville Museum of Art faces World's Fair Park in a building designed by Edward Larrabee Barnes — all Tennessee pink marble and clean modernist lines. The collection focuses primarily on American art and has a particular strength in works by artists with Tennessee or Appalachian connections.

The permanent collection includes a significant room of works by Richard Jolley, whose large-scale glass sculptures fill one of the galleries with refracted colour. There is also a strong programme of temporary exhibitions that rotates several times a year. The museum is free to visit on Fridays, which makes it a natural stop alongside a walk through World's Fair Park. The scale is manageable — two or three hours is enough to see everything without fatigue.
