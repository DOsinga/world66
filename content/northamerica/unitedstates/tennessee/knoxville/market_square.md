Market Square is the living centre of downtown Knoxville — a pedestrianised plaza surrounded by restaurants, bars, and two-storey brick buildings, many of them restored after decades of neglect. The revival of this square is the clearest evidence of what happened to Knoxville's centre in the 2000s and 2010s: a city that had written off its downtown found it again.

On weekend mornings a farmers' market sets up in the square with local produce, honey, baked goods, and crafts. On warm evenings, outdoor tables fill and bands play from the raised stage at the square's east end. The surrounding restaurants cover a reasonable range — good for a casual lunch, a sit-down dinner, or just a beer outside. The square is genuinely used by locals for daily life, not just positioned for tourists, which makes it worth more than a quick look.
