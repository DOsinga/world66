Oliver Royale occupies one of the finest rooms on Market Square — high ceilings, exposed brick, and tall windows looking out over the square itself. The menu is American with European leanings: good steaks, well-made pasta, and cocktails that match the surroundings. It is not inexpensive, but it is the kind of place that justifies a long dinner.

The bar programme is taken seriously, and the cocktail list changes seasonally. If you want one place for a proper evening meal on Market Square, this is the most reliable choice.
