Stock and Barrel sits on Market Square and does one thing very well: quality burgers and Tennessee whiskey. The burgers are made from locally sourced beef and come with a short list of creative toppings. The whiskey selection runs to well over a hundred bottles, with a strong focus on Tennessee and Kentucky producers.

It is a casual spot, good for lunch or an early dinner before heading to a show at the Tennessee Theatre or the Bijou. The outdoor seating on Market Square is among the best in the city on a warm evening.
