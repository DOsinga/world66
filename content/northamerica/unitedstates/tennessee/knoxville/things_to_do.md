---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Knoxville rewards slow walking. The compact downtown clusters museums, historic theatres, and the remnants of the 1982 World's Fair within easy reach of Market Square. Beyond the centre, the University of Tennessee campus opens onto the river, and a string of parks follows the Tennessee's banks. The city leans toward outdoor culture — hiking trails, greenways, and river access — and the Smoky Mountains loom on the horizon, close enough for a day trip.
