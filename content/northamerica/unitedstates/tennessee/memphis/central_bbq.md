Central BBQ is what happens when a serious barbecue operation decides to scale up without losing quality. The local chain has several locations across Memphis, and the debate about which is best is a reliable conversation starter — the Central location on Central Avenue and the downtown location on Second Street are both considered prime. The ribs are dry-rubbed and smoked low and slow, coming out with a deep bark and tender pull; the pulled pork is equally good. The nachos topped with pulled pork are a crowd-pleasing variation that regulars either embrace or dismiss on principle.

Memphis barbecue is a distinct tradition — wet or dry ribs, pork as the default protein, sauce served on the side rather than slathered on during cooking. Central BBQ represents the tradition well while also being clean, well-run, and consistent, which explains why it has expanded while remaining credible. It is a reliable choice for visitors who don't know where else to start with Memphis barbecue.
