Lichterman Nature Center is an urban nature preserve in East Memphis, with trails, lake edges and exhibits focused on local habitats. It offers a quieter, greener Memphis stop far from Beale Street and the riverfront museums.

The center is especially useful for families or travelers who want a low-key walk without leaving the city. It is not central, so it fits best into an East Memphis day rather than a downtown itinerary.
