Memphis Rock 'n' Soul Museum tells the story of the city's music through film, exhibits, audio commentary, and plenty of songs. The strongest parts connect blues, gospel, country, soul, and rock to the social history of Memphis and the Mississippi Delta.

It sits right by Beale Street, so it is easy to pair with live music later in the day. Use the audio guide rather than rushing; the museum makes most sense when you let the songs carry the story.
