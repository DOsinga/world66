Minglewood Hall is a useful Midtown music venue for travellers who want Memphis nightlife beyond Beale Street. Its calendar runs through touring bands, dance nights, and local events, so the experience depends heavily on who is playing.

Check listings before going rather than treating the building as a sight. On the right night, it gives you a more contemporary Memphis music stop, less polished for visitors and more tied to the city's regular gig circuit.
