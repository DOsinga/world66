T.O. Fuller State Park covers wooded land and Mississippi bluff scenery in south Memphis. It has hiking trails, a nature center, and a swimming pool, and its history is tied to its original role as Shelby County Negro State Park.

The park is named for Thomas Oscar Fuller, an African American educator, minister, and politician.
