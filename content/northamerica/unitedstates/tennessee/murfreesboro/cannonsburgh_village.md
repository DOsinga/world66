Cannonsburgh Village is an open-air heritage village covering about a century of early Tennessee life, from the 1830s to the 1930s. The site includes a gristmill, schoolhouse, general store, blacksmith shop, caboose, chapel, and other restored or reconstructed buildings. Self-guided visits are free, with guided tours sometimes available for a fee.
