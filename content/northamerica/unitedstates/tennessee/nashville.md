Nashville is the capital of country music and one of the most visited cities in the American South. The [Broadway](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/broadway) strip — blocks of honky-tonks, neon signs, and live music spilling out of every door — is the face the city shows to the world. But Nashville is also a college town ([Vanderbilt](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/vanderbilt_university), Belmont, [Fisk](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/fisk_university)), a major healthcare hub, and a city that has been reinventing itself for twenty years. [East Nashville](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/east_nashville), once overlooked, is now dense with restaurants and independent businesses. [The Gulch](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/the_gulch) has become a polished, walkable neighbourhood of new hotels and good eating. The food scene, long rooted in Southern comfort food and the meat-and-three tradition, has broadened considerably.

The music is genuine. The [Country Music Hall of Fame](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/country_music_hall_of_fame) and the [Ryman Auditorium](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/ryman_auditorium) are world-class institutions. [Music Row](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/musicrow) still functions as the industry's nerve centre — the bungalows and publishing houses along 16th and 17th Avenues South are where careers are built and deals are made; [RCA Studio B](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/rca_studio_b), where Elvis and Dolly Parton recorded, is open to the public via Hall of Fame tours. The [Bluebird Cafe](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/bluebird_cafe), a 90-seat listening room in Green Hills, is where Garth Brooks was discovered and where the songwriter-in-the-round format — performers taking turns, acoustic — is practiced most seriously. The [Grand Ole Opry House](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/grand_ole_opry) ten miles north-east of downtown remains the institutional home of the show that built Nashville's reputation.

One surprise: [Centennial Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/centennial_park) contains a full-scale, full-colour [replica of the Parthenon](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/parthenon), complete with a [42-foot gold Athena](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/the_parthenon_athena) inside. It was built for Tennessee's centennial exposition in 1897. Nashville's nickname "Athens of the South" predates the replica, but the building makes the point visually.

Hot chicken — bone-in fried chicken coated in a paste of cayenne and spices — is the food Nashville is most proud of. [Prince's Hot Chicken Shack](/northamerica/unitedstates/tennessee/nashville/princes_hot_chicken) is the originator; the heat levels run from mild to Extra Hot, which is not a bluff.
