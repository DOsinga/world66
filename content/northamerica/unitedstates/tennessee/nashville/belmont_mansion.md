Belmont Mansion is one of Nashville's best house museums, a 19th-century Italianate villa on the Belmont University campus. The guided tour takes you through richly furnished rooms, while the grounds add marble statues, cast-iron garden pieces, and a glimpse of the estate scale Nashville had before Music Row took over the neighbourhood.
