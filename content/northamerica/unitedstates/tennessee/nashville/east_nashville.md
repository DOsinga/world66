East Nashville sits across the Cumberland from downtown — close enough to walk, far enough to feel like a different city. Once down on its luck and largely overlooked, the neighbourhood has been the focus of the city's last twenty years of cultural and culinary regeneration: it is now the centre of Nashville's indie music scene, the bulk of its best independent restaurants, and the most concentrated stretch of locally-owned bars.

The action concentrates along **Eastland**, **Gallatin**, and **Main** between 5th and Riverside Village. Notable food: the original **Prince's Hot Chicken** location, **Lockeland Table**, **Mas Tacos**, **Two Ten Jack** (ramen and izakaya), **Rosepepper Cantina**. For drinks and music: **The 5 Spot** (long-running indie venue), **The Family Wash**, **Drifters**. Walkable, leafy, and at its best on a Sunday morning when the neighbourhood farmers' markets are open.
