Fisk University is the cultural heart of historically Black Nashville: founded in 1866 to educate the recently emancipated, the university gave the world the **Fisk Jubilee Singers**, whose 1870s concerts of spirituals introduced the form to Europe and the United States. **Jubilee Hall**, the 1876 Victorian Gothic landmark, was funded by their tours and is the oldest permanent structure built for the education of Black Americans in the country.

The **Carl Van Vechten Gallery** in the historic Fisk Library holds half of the famous **Stieglitz Collection** — Picasso, Renoir, Cézanne, O'Keeffe — and is open to the public on weekdays. Check ahead for opening hours. The campus is small but rewarding; visit on the same trip as the **Ryman**, which is less than two miles south-east.
