Hillsboro Village is a short two-block stretch of independent shops, cafes, and restaurants between Vanderbilt University and the Green Hills area — a slightly older-feeling, slightly less Instagram-driven retail strip than 12 South. The presence of the university gives it a steady undergraduate-and-faculty mix that the more polished districts don't quite have.

**Pancake Pantry** on the corner of 21st and Belcourt is the local breakfast institution, with regular morning queues out the door. **Fido**, the cafe across the street, has been a Nashville fixture since the 1990s — strong coffee, all-day kitchen, a lot of writers on laptops. The **Belcourt Theatre**, a 1925 cinema turned non-profit arthouse, programmes one of the best film schedules in the South.
