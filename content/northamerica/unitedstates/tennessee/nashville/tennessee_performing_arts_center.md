The Tennessee Performing Arts Center is Nashville's main downtown stage complex for touring Broadway shows, ballet, opera, theatre, and concerts. It is a good choice when you want a night out that is not built around honky-tonks, and its location near the State Capitol makes it easy to fold into a central Nashville evening.
