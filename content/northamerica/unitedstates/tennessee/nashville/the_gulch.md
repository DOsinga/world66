The Gulch is the densest pocket of new-build in Nashville — a polished, mid-rise neighbourhood of glass-and-brick apartment towers, hotels, cocktail bars, boutique retail, and upmarket restaurants on what was, until the 1990s, a rail yard and warehouse district south-west of downtown. It is also the first LEED-certified neighbourhood in the South.

The pedestrian focus is the section of 11th and 12th Avenues South near the Frist Art Museum. **Whiskey Kitchen**, **The Pub**, **Adele's**, and **Husk Nashville** are local fixtures. The **Yazoo Brewing** taproom is a useful lunch stop. The well-known **"What Lifts You"** wings mural on the side of 302 11th Avenue South is the city's most-photographed Instagram backdrop, and the queue to pose under it is no joke.
