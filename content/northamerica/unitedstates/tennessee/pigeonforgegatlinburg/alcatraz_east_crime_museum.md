Alcatraz East looks at American crime, punishment, and forensic work through a broad collection of artifacts and hands-on exhibits. The displays range from old jail-cell replicas and moonshine stills to vehicles and objects tied to notorious criminal cases.

It is very much a Pigeon Forge attraction, but a better one than the name might suggest. Go for the forensic labs and the strange mix of true-crime history, law enforcement equipment, and pop-culture fascination with famous cases.
