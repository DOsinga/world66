The Titanic Museum is hard to miss: it sits inside a half-scale model of the ship, built along the busy Pigeon Forge strip. Inside are galleries on the voyage, the passengers, and the wreck, with hundreds of artifacts and a ticketing setup that assigns visitors a real passenger's identity.

It is polished and theatrical, but the scale of the collection makes it one of the stronger family museum stops in town.
