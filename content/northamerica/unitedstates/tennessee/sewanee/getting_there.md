---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Sewanee sits on the Cumberland Plateau at the junction of US-41A and Tennessee Route 56, about 45 minutes northwest of Chattanooga and just over an hour south of Nashville.

**By car** is the only practical option for most visitors. From Chattanooga, take I-24 west to Exit 134 at Monteagle, then US-41A south about 5 miles to Sewanee. From Nashville, take I-24 southeast all the way to the same Monteagle exit. There is no public transport to Sewanee.

**Nearest airports:** Chattanooga Metropolitan Airport (CHA) is the closest at around 50 miles. Nashville International (BNA) is about 90 miles northwest. Both have rental car counters.

**By train or bus:** There is no practical public transit option. The nearest Greyhound stops are in Winchester and Chattanooga.

Parking on campus is generally straightforward — visitor lots are signposted near the main entrance on University Avenue. During graduation weekend in May, the small town fills up; book accommodation well in advance if visiting then.
