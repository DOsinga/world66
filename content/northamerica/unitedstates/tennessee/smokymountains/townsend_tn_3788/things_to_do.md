---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Townsend sits at the quietest entrance to Great Smoky Mountains National Park, and most of what there is to do here involves the park itself. Cades Cove, about 10 miles east, is the non-negotiable stop: a broad valley with preserved 19th-century farms, mills, and churches set against the surrounding mountains. The 11-mile one-way loop road through the cove is the most visited spot in the most visited national park in the United States, so go early or in the off-season.

In town, Tuckaleechee Caverns is one of the better cave systems open to the public in the eastern United States — larger than most people expect, with significant formations. The Little River itself invites tubing and swimming in summer; locals know the best spots along its banks.
