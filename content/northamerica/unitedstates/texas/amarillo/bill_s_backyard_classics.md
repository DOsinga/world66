Bill's Backyard Classics is a classic-car collection with the informal feel of a private passion project rather than a polished national museum. For travellers following Route 66 themes through Amarillo, it adds another layer to the city's roadside Americana.

The museum is best for visitors who already like vintage cars, chrome, signs, and mid-century design. It pairs naturally with Cadillac Ranch, the RV Museum, and other Amarillo stops that turn vehicle culture into part of the trip.
