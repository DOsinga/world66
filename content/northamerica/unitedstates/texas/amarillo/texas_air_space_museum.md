Texas Air & Space Museum gives Amarillo an aviation stop that fits the wide-open geography of the Panhandle. Displays include aircraft, flight artifacts, and regional aviation stories, with the airport setting reinforcing the subject matter.

The museum is a good choice for aviation enthusiasts, families, or travelers passing through Amarillo with time beyond Route 66 and Cadillac Ranch. Its distance from the city centre means it works best by car.
