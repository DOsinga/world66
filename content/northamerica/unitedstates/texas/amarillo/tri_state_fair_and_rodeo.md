The Tri-State Fair and Rodeo is one of Amarillo's major annual events, drawing on the Panhandle's ranching culture as much as its appetite for rides and fair food. Rodeo events, livestock shows, concerts, and midway attractions make it a broad regional gathering rather than a small town carnival.

It is seasonal, but if your dates match, it is a strong way to see Amarillo acting like itself. Book rooms early around busy fair nights and expect traffic near the fairgrounds.
