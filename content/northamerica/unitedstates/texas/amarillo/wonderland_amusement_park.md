Wonderland Amusement Park is Amarillo's long-running seasonal amusement park, set in Thompson Park with roller coasters, water rides, family rides, and midway-style attractions. It is a straightforward change of pace from Route 66 nostalgia, canyon scenery, and museums.

The park is most useful for families or anyone spending more than a quick overnight in Amarillo. Because hours are seasonal and weather-sensitive, check the calendar before planning an evening around it.
