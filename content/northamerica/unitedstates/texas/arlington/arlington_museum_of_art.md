The Arlington Museum of Art gives the city a cultural stop that is not tied to stadiums or theme parks. Its exhibitions change through the year, ranging from regional artists to travelling shows, so it is worth checking the current programme before building a visit around it.

For most travellers this is a downtown add-on rather than an essential North Texas museum. Still, it clears the threshold in Arlington because it offers a compact, independent reason to spend time in the centre and pairs easily with a meal nearby.
