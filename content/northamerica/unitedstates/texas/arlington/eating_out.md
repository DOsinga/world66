---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The entertainment district has the largest concentration of restaurants — the Texas Live complex near Globe Life Field has dozens of options at varying price points, from barbecue to upscale Tex-Mex. The quality is generally decent and the portions are Texan in scale. Beyond the stadium zone, Arlington has a sprawling dining landscape spread across strip malls and suburban thoroughfares, with strong Vietnamese and Indian options reflecting the city's immigrant communities.
