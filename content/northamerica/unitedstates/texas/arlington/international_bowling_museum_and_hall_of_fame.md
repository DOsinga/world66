The International Bowling Museum and Hall of Fame is more entertaining than its niche might suggest. Exhibits trace bowling from ancient games to American league culture, professional competition, equipment design, and the personalities inducted into the hall of fame.

Its location near Arlington's stadium district makes it easy to add before or after a sports outing. For travelers interested in Americana, games, or sports history, it is a distinctive stop rather than filler.
