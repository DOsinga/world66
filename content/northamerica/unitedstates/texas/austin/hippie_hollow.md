Hippie Hollow is a clothing-optional public park on the rocky north shore of Lake Travis. It is often described as the only legal nude beach in Texas, though the shoreline is steep and stony rather than sandy.

The park is adult-only and better for sunning and lake views than for an easy swim. Bring water, sun protection, and shoes that can handle the limestone shore.
