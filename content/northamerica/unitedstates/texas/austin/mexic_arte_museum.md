Mexic-Arte Museum collects, preserves, and interprets Mexican, Latino, and Latin American art and culture. Its downtown location makes it an easy addition to a walk along Congress Avenue or through Austin's central museum and theatre district.

Expect focused exhibitions rather than a huge collection. The museum is most useful for visitors who want Austin's cultural story to include its Mexican and Latin American roots.
