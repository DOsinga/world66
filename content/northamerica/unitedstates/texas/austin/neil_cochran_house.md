The Neil-Cochran House is an 1855 Greek Revival estate house and one of Austin's important surviving historic residences. Guided visits cover the house, its period rooms, and the wider story of early Austin.

It sits near the University of Texas area, making it easy to combine with the Harry Ransom Center, Blanton Museum of Art, or a walk along the Drag.
