The Texas State Cemetery on the east side of Austin is the state's equivalent of Arlington: the official burial ground for Texas heroes, governors, and notable citizens. Established in 1851, the cemetery covers about 18 acres of neatly maintained grounds in a historic neighbourhood east of Interstate 35.

The graves range from Republic of Texas veterans to Vietnam War soldiers, with stops at nearly every chapter of Texas history in between. The memorial to the veterans of the Texas Revolution is the oldest section; the more recent burials include Ann Richards, the memorable governor who told Texans things they didn't always want to hear. The cemetery is free to visit and open every day; a self-guided tour map is available at the visitor centre.
