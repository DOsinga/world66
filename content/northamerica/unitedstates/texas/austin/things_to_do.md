type: section
---

Austin's identity rests on three things: live music, outdoor life, and a determined refusal to be like the rest of Texas. The [Congress Avenue Bats](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/austin/congress_avenue_bats) and [Barton Springs Pool](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/austin/barton_springs_pool) are the iconic experiences; the [Texas State Capitol](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/austin/texas_state_capitol) and [Bob Bullock Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/austin/bob_bullock_museum) cover the history. For the weirder, more characterful Austin, head to [6th Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/austin/sixth_street), [South Congress](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/austin/south_congress_avenue), and [East Austin](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/austin/east_austin).
