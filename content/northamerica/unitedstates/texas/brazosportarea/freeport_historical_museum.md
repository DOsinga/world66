Freeport Historical Museum covers the local history of Freeport and the lower Brazos area, with exhibits on maritime life, military history, and the town's development. Displays range from cannons to a shark exhibit, giving the small museum more variety than its size suggests.

It is a useful stop for context before visiting the harbour, river, and nearby historic sites.
