Gulf Coast Bird Observatory is a small nature stop near Lake Jackson with short trails, birding stations, and education programs focused on the coastal migration routes of Texas.

It is best for patient visitors who like wetlands, native plants, and quiet wildlife watching. The trails are modest rather than dramatic, but the site adds a useful inland birding stop to the Brazosport area's larger refuges and beach parks.
