title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Brazosport area's main appeal is a combination of Gulf Coast beach access and exceptional birdwatching. [Quintana Beach County Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/quintana_beach_county_park) is the most rewarding beach destination — well-maintained, uncrowded, and good for fishing and families. [Surfside Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/surfside_beach) is four miles of open Gulf shoreline close to Houston, popular for swimming, surf fishing, and casual beach days.

[Brazoria National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/brazoria_national_wildlife_refuge) and the adjacent [San Bernard National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/san_bernard_wildlife_refuge) together form one of the most important winter waterbird refuges on the Texas coast. [Sea Center Texas](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/sea_center_texas) in Lake Jackson is a free state aquarium and the world's largest redfish hatchery. The [Brazosport Center for the Arts and Sciences](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/brazosport_center_for_arts_sciences) in Clute has a planetarium and natural history museum. For fishing, the [Freeport harbor](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/freeport_harbor) is where the working shrimping fleet docks and where you can buy fresh catch direct from the boats; [Brazos River fishing and kayaking](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/brazos_river_fishing) offers inshore action in the tidal river channels.

History is not the area's main draw, but the [Battle of Velasco site](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/battle_of_velasco) at the river mouth marks the first armed clash of the Texas Revolution. [Lake Jackson's City Hall](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/brazosportarea/lake_jackson_city_hall), designed by Alden B. Dow, is the most architecturally significant building in the area, a reminder that this company town was built with modernist ambitions.
