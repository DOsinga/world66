The Brownsville Museum of Fine Arts focuses on work by local and Latin American artists. It is close to Gladys Porter Zoo, making it easy to pair with other central Brownsville stops.

The museum is a useful counterpoint to the city's battlefield and border-history sights, showing a more contemporary and creative side of the Rio Grande Valley.
