The Commemorative Air Force Museum at Brownsville South Padre Island International Airport displays World War II aircraft and aviation memorabilia.

It is a specialist stop, but worthwhile for aviation and military-history travellers. The museum is also tied to AirFiesta, an annual air show usually held in mid-February.
