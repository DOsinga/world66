Laguna Atascosa National Wildlife Refuge protects a large stretch of South Texas habitat near the lower Laguna Madre. Birders come for the refuge's coastal and brushland species, while patient visitors may also learn about efforts to protect the endangered ocelot.

It is a strong nature stop from Brownsville or South Padre Island, with the best visits planned around current road access, trails, and wildlife-viewing conditions.
