Resaca de la Palma National Battlefield preserves the site of an 1846 battle from the opening phase of the Mexican-American War. Trails lead through the resaca landscape where the fighting took place, with interpretation that explains the battle and the wider border history.

The site is quieter than Palo Alto Battlefield, but it is a meaningful stop for anyone tracing Brownsville's military history.
