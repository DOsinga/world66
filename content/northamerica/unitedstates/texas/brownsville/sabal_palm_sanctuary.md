Sabal Palm Sanctuary protects a rare pocket of native palm forest near the Rio Grande. The trails are short, flat, and quiet, which is exactly the point: this is a place for green jays, chachalacas, butterflies, and slow looking.

It is one of the best low-effort nature stops around Brownsville. Bring water, insect repellent, and binoculars if you have them.
