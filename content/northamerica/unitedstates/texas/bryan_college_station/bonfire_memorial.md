The Bonfire Memorial marks the 1999 collapse of the Aggie Bonfire stack, which killed twelve Texas A&M students and injured many others. It is a sober stop, and one that explains an important piece of modern Aggie memory.

The memorial is most meaningful if you know a little of the tradition before you arrive. It is not a spectacle; it is a place to slow down.
