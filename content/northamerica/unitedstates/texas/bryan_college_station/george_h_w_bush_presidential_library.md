The George H. W. Bush Presidential Library is the strongest museum stop in Bryan-College Station. It covers the life and presidency of the 41st U.S. president, with the kind of archives, exhibits, and civic ceremony that presidential libraries do well.

The Bush family burial site is beside the library grounds. Give the place enough time; it is more substantial than a quick campus exhibit.
