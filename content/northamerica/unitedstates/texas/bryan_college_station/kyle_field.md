Kyle Field is Texas A&M's football home and one of the largest college stadiums in the United States. Outside game day, it is still worth seeing for its sheer scale and for what it says about local priorities.

On fall Saturdays, the stadium changes the whole city. Traffic, parking, restaurants, and hotel prices all bend around Aggie football, so plan ahead if you are visiting then.
