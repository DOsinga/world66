The Texas A&M campus is the heart of College Station and the reason the city exists. It has been here since 1876, and even a short walk gives you a sense of how much local life is built around the university.

Look for the formal entrances, the student crowds around academic buildings, and the way the campus changes on football weekends. It is not just a school visit; it is the main cultural landscape of the city.
