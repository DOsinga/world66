A tiny oasis of about 500 people in West Texas, Buffalo Gap sits in a pass through the Callahan Divide and is home to some of the oldest [live oak trees](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/buffalo_gap/old_oak_trees) in the state, several over 800 years old. Three hours from [Dallas](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/dallas) and [Austin](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/austin), it makes a rewarding day trip or weekend getaway.

The [Buffalo Gap Historic Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/buffalo_gap/historic_village) is a collection of frontier buildings showing how West Texas was settled. [Perini Ranch Steakhouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/buffalo_gap/perini_ranch_steakhouse) is famous for its mesquite-grilled tenderloin, and [Abilene State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/buffalo_gap/abilene_state_park) just west of town has an outdoor pool built by CCC troops in the 1930s.
