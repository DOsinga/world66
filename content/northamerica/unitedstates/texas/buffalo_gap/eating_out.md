---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

[Perini Ranch Steakhouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/buffalo_gap/perini_ranch_steakhouse) is the reason most people drive to Buffalo Gap. There are a couple of other places — a barbecue spot or two, a small cafe near the Historic Village — but none worth a special trip. Most visitors do lunch at Perini's, walk the village, and head back to Abilene for dinner.
