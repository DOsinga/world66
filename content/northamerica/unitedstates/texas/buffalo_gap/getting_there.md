---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Buffalo Gap is fifteen miles south of [Abilene](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/abilene), reached along Texas Farm-to-Market Road 89 — a pleasant rural drive across the Callahan Divide. There is no public transport. The nearest commercial airport is Abilene Regional, with limited connections via Dallas.

For a day trip from Dallas or Fort Worth, plan on about three hours each way; from Austin, about four. The roads are flat and easy and there is essentially no traffic.
