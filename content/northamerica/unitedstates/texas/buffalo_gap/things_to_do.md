title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Buffalo Gap is small enough that an afternoon will cover its main sights. The [Buffalo Gap Historic Village](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/buffalo_gap/historic_village) is the obvious anchor — twenty preserved frontier buildings on a single grass site — and a meal at the [Perini Ranch Steakhouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/buffalo_gap/perini_ranch_steakhouse) is the other reason a steady trickle of West Texans drives out here.

The surrounding Callahan Divide country is pleasantly empty rolling-hill terrain dotted with old live oaks, a few over eight hundred years old. [Abilene State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/buffalo_gap/abilene_state_park) just west of town has an outdoor pool built by the Civilian Conservation Corps in the 1930s and an easy network of short hiking trails.
