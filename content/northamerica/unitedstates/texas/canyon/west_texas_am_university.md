West Texas A&M University gives Canyon its college-town energy. The campus sits close to the town centre, with enough students, sports, and events to keep Canyon from feeling like merely a service stop for Palo Duro.

It is not a destination campus in the architectural sense, but it matters to the feel of the place. Check calendars for football weekends, concerts, and local events if you are staying overnight.
