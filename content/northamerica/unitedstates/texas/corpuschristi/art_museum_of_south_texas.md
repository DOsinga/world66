The Art Museum of South Texas stands right on the bayfront in a striking white building, with galleries that focus on regional, Texas, and Latin American connections. It is a compact museum, but the setting gives it more presence than its size suggests.

Pair it with the science and history museum next door if you want a downtown cultural afternoon. The views across the water are part of the visit, especially when the light is sharp and the bay is choppy.
