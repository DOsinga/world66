Corpus Christi Cathedral is the city's main Catholic cathedral, set just inland from the bayfront. Its twin towers and pale stone exterior make it one of downtown's clearer architectural landmarks.

Visit briefly if you are already walking nearby or interested in local religious architecture. It is most atmospheric during services, though travelers should be unobtrusive if they step inside.
