The Corpus Christi Museum of Science and History covers the Coastal Bend through shipwrecks, early settlement, natural history, and hands-on science exhibits. It is especially useful for families or for travelers who want context before driving out to the barrier islands.

The museum sits near the bayfront and the Art Museum of South Texas, so it can anchor a downtown museum circuit. It is also a good bad-weather option when beach plans collapse.
