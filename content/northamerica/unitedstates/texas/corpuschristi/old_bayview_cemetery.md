Old Bayview Cemetery is a small but historically loaded burial ground near downtown Corpus Christi. It is often described as one of the oldest federal military cemeteries west of the Mississippi, with graves tied to the Mexican-American War era and early South Texas settlement.

This is a quiet, reflective stop rather than a major attraction. It pairs well with a walk through the older part of downtown if you like local history in compact, physical form.
