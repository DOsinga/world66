The Chapel of Thanksgiving is the quiet centre of Thanks-Giving Square, a small downtown Dallas complex devoted to gratitude across faith traditions. From outside, the chapel rises as a white spiral; inside, the focus is the Glory Window, a swirl of stained glass that tightens toward a bright centre overhead.

It is a compact visit, but a good one if you are walking between the Arts District, Main Street, and the more corporate parts of downtown. The contrast is the point: a contemplative room tucked into one of the most hard-edged business districts in Texas.
