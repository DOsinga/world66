Dallas World Aquarium is more than a standard aquarium. The visit starts in a recreated rainforest canopy and winds down past birds, mammals, reptiles, and aquatic exhibits before reaching the tanks below.

It is a strong family stop in downtown Dallas and pairs well with nearby museums and Dealey Plaza.
