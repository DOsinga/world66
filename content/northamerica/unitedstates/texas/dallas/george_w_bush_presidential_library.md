The George W. Bush Presidential Library is part of the Bush Center at Southern Methodist University. Its exhibits cover George W. Bush's presidency, including domestic policy, foreign affairs, and major events from the early 2000s.

The library is a focused stop for visitors interested in recent United States history and presidential archives. Allow time for the permanent exhibits and any rotating displays at the center.
