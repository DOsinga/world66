Dallas's Koreatown is the main concentration of Korean restaurants, shops, and community businesses in the city. The surrounding Korean-American community is spread across North Dallas and the northern suburbs, but this commercial district is the easiest place for visitors to experience that part of Dallas through food, bakeries, markets, and nightlife.
