The Old Red Museum tells the story of Dallas County inside the restored 1892 courthouse, a red sandstone landmark beside Dealey Plaza. The exhibits move from early settlement through the growth of modern Dallas, with more context than you might expect from a county history museum.

It pairs naturally with the Sixth Floor Museum nearby. Go here when you want the wider Dallas story, not only the single moment for which Dealey Plaza is famous.
