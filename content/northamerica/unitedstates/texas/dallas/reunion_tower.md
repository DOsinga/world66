Reunion Tower is one of the most recognizable shapes in the Dallas skyline: a tall concrete shaft topped by a glowing geodesic ball. The observation deck gives a clear view over downtown, the Trinity River corridor, and the freeway web that defines the city.

It is most useful at the beginning of a Dallas visit, when the view helps make sense of the scale and layout of the city. The tower is beside Union Station and close to Dealey Plaza.
