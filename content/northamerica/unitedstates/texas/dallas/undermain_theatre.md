Undermain Theatre is a small performance space tucked below Main Street in Deep Ellum. Its programme leans toward new plays, international work, and productions with more bite than a standard commercial stage.

For travellers, the appeal is partly the venue itself: intimate, underground, and tied to Deep Ellum's long habit of making room for independent culture. Check the calendar before going, since it works best as an evening plan rather than a walk-by sight.
