Bayless-Selby House Museum is a small restored Victorian house a few blocks from the courthouse square. It is not a blockbuster museum, but it gives useful texture to Denton's older residential streets and the town's late-19th-century prosperity.

Visit it as part of a slow walk around the square rather than as a destination by itself.
