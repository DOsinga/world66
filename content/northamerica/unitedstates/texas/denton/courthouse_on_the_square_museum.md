The Courthouse-on-the-Square is Denton's visual centre and its best single sightseeing stop. The 1896 courthouse gives the square its shape, and the museum inside covers local history through objects from Denton County life.

The building matters as much as the collection. Its stone arches, clock tower, and lawn make the square feel like a real town centre rather than another North Texas strip of traffic and parking lots.
