The Denton Arts and Jazz Festival is the city's biggest annual expression of its music-school identity. It fills Civic Center Park with jazz, arts stalls, food, and outdoor stages, usually over a spring weekend.

It is worth planning around if you are already in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. Outside the festival dates, the park is just a park, so check the calendar before building a day around it.
