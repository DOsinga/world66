The One O'Clock Lab Band is the best shorthand for Denton's music reputation. Based at the University of North Texas, the ensemble has long been one of the most respected university jazz bands in the United States.

If there is a performance during your visit, it is a stronger Denton experience than another generic night out. It also explains why the city punches above its weight for live music.
