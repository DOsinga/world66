Thin Line is a film and music festival that adds another layer to Denton's arts calendar. The programme changes each year, but the appeal is the same: documentary screenings, concerts, and a reason for downtown to feel busy outside the usual bar circuit.

It is a timing-dependent reason to visit, but a good one if your North Texas trip overlaps with the festival.
