Kachina Prairie Park protects a small remnant of Blackland Prairie, a once widespread North Texas ecosystem that has mostly disappeared under farms and towns. The draw is the open grassland itself: seasonal wildflowers, prairie plants, and a quiet look at what the Ennis area looked like before most of the land was plowed.
