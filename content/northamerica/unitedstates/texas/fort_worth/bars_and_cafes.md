---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Fort Worth's nightlife is split across three districts. The Stockyards is honky-tonks and country bars, with Billy Bob's at the centre and a strip of smaller saloons along Exchange Avenue. West 7th, between downtown and the Cultural District, is more polished — cocktail bars and beer halls aimed at a younger urban crowd. Magnolia Avenue on the Near Southside has the best of the independent coffee and craft-beer scene.
