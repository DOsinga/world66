The home of Fort Worth Symphony Orchestra, Texas Ballet Theater, and the Van Cliburn International Piano Competition, opened in 1998 to a David Schwarz design loosely modelled on European opera houses. Two 48-foot limestone angels with golden trumpets flank the main entrance — a sentimental but unmistakable Fort Worth landmark.

Worth a look from outside even if you cannot get to a performance. Public tours are offered most weekdays; check the box office for times.
