A museum in the Cultural District devoted to the women of the American West — ranchers, rodeo riders, artists, writers, and entertainers. Galleries trace pioneer life, the development of women's rodeo (still going strong), and the long tradition of women breaking horses and running ranches when the men were away or dead. The Hall of Fame inducts a new class each year; current inductees include Sandra Day O'Connor, Annie Oakley, and Patsy Cline.

A reasonable hour. Sits a short walk from the Kimbell and Amon Carter.
