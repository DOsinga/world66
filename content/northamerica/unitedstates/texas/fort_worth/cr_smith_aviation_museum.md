C.R. Smith Aviation Museum tells the story of American Airlines and the wider growth of commercial aviation. The exhibits are hands-on enough for families, with aircraft, airline history, and displays that explain how flying changed from a specialist pursuit into mass transport.

It is near the airline's Fort Worth campus, east of the city's main sights, so it works best for visitors with a car or a specific interest in aviation.
