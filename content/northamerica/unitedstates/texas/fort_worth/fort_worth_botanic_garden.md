Fort Worth Botanic Garden is the city's main garden complex, with broad lawns, formal flower beds, conservatory planting, and the much-loved Japanese Garden inside the grounds. It is large enough for an unhurried walk rather than a quick photo stop.

The garden sits in Fort Worth's cultural district, so it combines easily with the nearby museums. Go in the morning if you want cooler paths and softer light.
