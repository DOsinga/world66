The Fort Worth Museum of Science and History is the hands-on anchor of the Cultural District, a short walk from the Kimbell, the Modern, and the Cowgirl Museum. Its exhibits lean practical and tactile: dinosaurs, energy, space, regional history, and a children’s museum built for younger visitors who need to touch things rather than stare at cases.

For adults it works best as part of a Cultural District day rather than a stand-alone pilgrimage. Families can easily spend half a day here, especially if the planetarium or IMAX schedule lines up with your visit. It adds a useful counterweight to Fort Worth’s art-heavy museum cluster.
