Fort Worth Nature Center & Refuge is the city's big wild-space escape: thousands of acres of prairie, bottomland forest, wetlands and lake edge northwest of downtown. It is a very different Fort Worth from the Stockyards and museum district, with trails, birding, paddling possibilities and the chance to see bison in a managed herd.

Come prepared for heat and distance, because this is not a pocket park. The reward is space: long horizons, quieter trails and a reminder that North Texas landscape still has teeth when it is given room.
