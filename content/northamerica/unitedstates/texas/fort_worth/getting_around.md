---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Fort Worth is spread out and most visitors find driving the most practical option. Each district — Stockyards, downtown, Cultural District, Magnolia Avenue — is walkable internally; getting between them is a five-to-fifteen minute drive.

The Trinity Metro bus network covers the city and is cheap, but routes between visitor districts are infrequent. Rideshare is widely available and cost-effective for short hops. The TEXRail commuter line connects DFW Airport with the Stockyards and downtown Fort Worth — useful as a car-free option, but limited to that single corridor.
