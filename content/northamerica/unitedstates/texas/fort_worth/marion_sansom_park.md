Marion Sansom Park is a rugged patch of north Fort Worth, with rocky trails dropping through woodland toward views over Lake Worth. It feels much wilder than its city address suggests, especially on the steeper paths where the limestone, cedar, and switchbacks take over.

Hikers and mountain bikers share the trail network, so stay alert on blind corners and choose routes that match your footing. It is a good counterweight to the Stockyards and museums: less polished, more local, and best for travellers who want a few hours outdoors without leaving Fort Worth.
