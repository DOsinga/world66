The Oscar E. Monnig Meteorite Gallery at TCU is a small science museum devoted to rocks
that arrived from space. Its specimens, impact stories, and hands-on framing make
meteorites feel less abstract and more connected to Earth history.

It is a focused Fort Worth stop, best for curious families, geology fans, or anyone
already visiting the TCU area. Pair it with nearby cultural-district museums if you want
a broader museum day.
