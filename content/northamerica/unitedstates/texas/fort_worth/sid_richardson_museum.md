A small free museum in Sundance Square devoted entirely to the Western art of Frederic Remington and Charles Russell, drawn from the personal collection of the oilman Sid Richardson. The galleries take less than an hour to see, but include some of the most reproduced images in American Western art. The location in restored downtown makes it an easy add to a half-day in Sundance Square.
