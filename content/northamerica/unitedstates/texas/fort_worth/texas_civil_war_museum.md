This west Fort Worth museum concentrates on the American Civil War through uniforms, weapons, flags, letters, and domestic material from both Union and Confederate contexts. The collection is unusually broad for a regional museum and includes a separate gallery of Victorian-era dress and household objects.

It is not in the central museum district, so it suits visitors with a specific interest in nineteenth-century history or those already heading toward the west side. The interpretive value is strongest when treated as an artifact collection rather than a battlefield narrative.
