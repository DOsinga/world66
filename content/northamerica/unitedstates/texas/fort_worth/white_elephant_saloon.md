A classic Stockyards saloon — long wooden bar, tin ceiling, Texas longhorn skulls, country bands every night, peanut shells underfoot. The atmosphere is genuine rather than themed, and a Friday or Saturday night here is one of the easiest ways to see why Fort Worth still calls itself Cowtown.

Two-step lessons are sometimes offered in the early evening. The dance floor fills up by ten.
