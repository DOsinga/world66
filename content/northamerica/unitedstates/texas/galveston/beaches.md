---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Galveston has 32 miles of Gulf Coast beaches, but visitors should understand the character of Gulf water before arriving expecting Caribbean clarity. The water is warm but murky — brown-green from Mississippi River sediment carried along the coast — and the waves are small. None of this stops Houstonians from filling the island every summer weekend, and Galveston's beaches are perfectly functional for swimming, sunbathing, and building sandcastles.

**East Beach** is the largest and most energetic, with facilities, parking, and a reputation for a livelier atmosphere including permitted alcohol on the sand. **Stewart Beach** near the eastern end of the Seawall is the family-friendly option with a water park, restrooms, and lifeguards. The **free county beaches** along the Seawall from 25th Street west are the most accessible and least commercial. **West Beach**, farther along the island toward Jamaica Beach, is less developed and popular with windsurfers and kitesurfers. The best time for a quieter beach day is a weekday in May, September, or October, when the water is warm but the Houston crowd is at work.
