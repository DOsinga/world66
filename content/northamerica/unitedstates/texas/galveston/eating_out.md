---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Galveston's restaurant scene reflects its two main constituencies: tourists working through seafood on the Strand and Seawall, and a local population that has developed genuinely good options over the past two decades. The Strand area has the greatest concentration, though quality varies considerably.

**Gaido's** on Seawall Boulevard has been serving Gulf seafood since 1911 — redfish, flounder, shrimp, and crab in a no-nonsense room that gets very busy in summer. **Rudy and Paco's** on 21st Street is one of the better restaurants in the city, with a menu mixing Galveston seafood with Latin American influences. The **Mosquito Cafe** on 14th Street is a good choice for a less formal meal, with a seasonal menu and reliably good breakfasts. For something quick on the Strand, **Mod Coffeehouse** does sandwiches and espresso in a good old building. Note that Galveston has a surprisingly strong Mardi Gras tradition — the seafood options around Mardi Gras season are worth timing a visit around.
