Fort Travis Seashore Park preserves the remains of a coastal defense site on Bolivar Peninsula, looking back toward Galveston and across the ship channel. Bunkers, open lawns, picnic areas, and water views make it part history stop, part breezy waterfront park.

It is best for travelers already taking the ferry or exploring beyond Galveston Island. The military history is modest but tangible, and the setting makes the detour more rewarding than the structures alone would suggest.
