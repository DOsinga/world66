Ocean Star is a retired offshore drilling rig turned museum at Galveston's harbor. The exhibits explain how offshore oil drilling works, using the rig itself as the main artifact rather than hiding the subject behind display cases.

It is a good fit for Galveston, where port history, shipping, and Gulf industry are part of the landscape. Even if you are not an engineering enthusiast, walking through a real rig gives the visit some weight.
