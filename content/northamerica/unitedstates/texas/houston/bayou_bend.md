Bayou Bend is the Museum of Fine Arts, Houston's house museum for American decorative arts and paintings. The collection sits in a historic mansion surrounded by 14 acres of landscaped gardens near Buffalo Bayou.

It is a quieter counterpoint to the main Museum District, with period rooms, garden walks, and a strong focus on American material culture.
