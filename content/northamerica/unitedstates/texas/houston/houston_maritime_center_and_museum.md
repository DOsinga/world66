Houston Maritime Center and Museum interprets the city's long connection with shipping, ports, offshore work, and the Gulf Coast. Its collection includes maritime artifacts, ship models, navigation material, and exhibits that make Houston's industrial waterfront easier to understand.

It is a focused museum rather than a large all-day attraction, but it fills a useful gap for visitors interested in how the Houston Ship Channel shaped the city.
