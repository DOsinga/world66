Miller Outdoor Theater is a useful Houston choice when you want culture without a ticket price. From late spring into autumn it hosts free professional music, dance, theatre, and other performances in Hermann Park.

Bring a picnic or arrive early for popular shows. The setting is informal, but the programme is serious enough to make it more than a casual park stage.
