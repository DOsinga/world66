Sam Houston Park is a downtown heritage park that preserves a cluster of historic Houston buildings from the 19th and early 20th centuries. The old houses make a sharp contrast with the surrounding office towers.

The Heritage Society Museum interprets the buildings and the city's growth from frontier settlement to Gulf Coast metropolis. It is the most useful green stop in downtown Houston if you want history rather than just a lunch-break lawn.
