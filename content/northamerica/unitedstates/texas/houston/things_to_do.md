title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Houston's scale is its most disorienting quality — the city covers over 600 square miles and most of it looks the same from a highway. What makes it work for visitors is that the best experiences are concentrated in a handful of distinct neighbourhoods and destinations. Plan around them rather than trying to cover the city comprehensively.

The [Museum District](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/houston/museumdistrict) alone is worth several days, with the Museum of Fine Arts, the Menil Collection, the Rothko Chapel, and the Houston Museum of Natural Science all within walking distance. [Space Center Houston](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/houston/space_center_houston), NASA's official visitor complex in the Clear Lake area 40 km southeast, is unlike anything else in the country. The [Hermann Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/houston/hermann_park) adjacent to the Museum District has a Japanese garden, a free outdoor theatre, and the Houston Zoo.

[Montrose](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/houston/montrose) is Houston's most interesting neighbourhood for wandering — a grid of bungalows, galleries, and independent restaurants with no equivalent in Texas. [Downtown](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/houston/downtown) has the Theater District, Market Square Park, and Sam Houston Park.
