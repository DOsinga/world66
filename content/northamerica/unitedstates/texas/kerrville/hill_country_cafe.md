The Hill Country Cafe is a classic small-town Texas diner — counter seating, booths, a menu that does not change much, and breakfast served all day. The biscuits are made from scratch, the coffee is strong and refilled without asking, and the chicken-fried steak is the thing to order if you are only going once. It is the kind of place that has been feeding the same regulars for decades.

Lunch is equally straightforward: burgers, sandwiches, daily specials built around whatever the kitchen feels like cooking. Prices are what you would expect from a Main Street diner that has no interest in being trendy. It opens early and closes mid-afternoon.
