Kilgore is an East Texas town in the pine woods south of Longview, best known for the East Texas Oil Field. The city still wears that history openly: downtown derricks, oil-boom stories, and the excellent [East Texas Oil Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/kilgore/east_texas_oil_museum) make the 1930s feel close.

This is not a big-ticket destination, but it has a clear identity. The [World's Richest Acre Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/kilgore/worlds_richest_acre_park) marks the dense cluster of wells that once produced an astonishing amount of oil from a tiny patch of downtown land. Kilgore College adds another local point of pride through the Rangerettes, the first precision dance-drill team.

Kilgore works best as a short stop on an East Texas drive, especially if you are interested in oil history, small-town downtowns, or the piney stretch between Tyler, Longview, and Shreveport.
