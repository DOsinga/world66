World's Richest Acre Park marks the downtown site where oil wells once stood unusually close together, producing millions of barrels from a tiny patch of land. One original derrick remains, joined by steel reproductions that give Kilgore's skyline its odd, memorable shape.

The park is small, but it is the quickest way to understand why Kilgore calls itself the Oil Capital of East Texas.
