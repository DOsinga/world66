The Bayer Museum of Agriculture preserves the farming history of the South Plains. Its collection ranges from household objects to farm equipment and implements, and it is also the permanent home of the Lubbock County Historical Collection. It pairs naturally with Lubbock's other specialist museums east of downtown.
