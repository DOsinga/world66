Llano Estacado Winery is the name most visitors hear first when Lubbock's wine country comes up. The winery sits south of the city and helped prove that the dry, high, windy plains could produce serious Texas wine.

Come for a tasting rather than scenery. This is working agricultural country, not a romantic hill-country vineyard, and that is part of the point.
