The National Ranching Heritage Center is one of Lubbock's best reasons to stop. Its outdoor park gathers ranch houses, barns, bunkhouses, windmills, and other buildings from across the West, arranged so you can walk through the changing architecture of ranch life.

It is more grounded than a cowboy theme park. The buildings are real, the scale is manageable, and the story fits the country around Lubbock: cattle, weather, distance, and people making practical things last.
