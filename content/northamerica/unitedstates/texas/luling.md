Luling is a small Central Texas town with more personality than its size suggests. Oil made it rowdy in the 1920s, watermelons gave it a festival, and the old pumpjacks around town have been painted into cartoon characters, which tells you a lot about the local mood.

This is not a polished Hill Country destination. It is a half-day to full-day stop for people who like odd Texas towns: [Zedler Mill](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/luling/zedler_mill), the [Luling Oil Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/luling/luling_oil_museum), the [pumpjack trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/luling/pumpjack_trail), and barbecue smoke drifting through the centre.

If you want nature, [Palmetto State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/luling/palmetto_state_park) is close enough to fold into the same day, with dwarf palmettos, swampy trails, and a very different feel from the dry ranch country nearby.
