The Luling Oil Museum tells the story of the oil field that turned a farming town into a boomtown. Expect tools, photographs, documents, and local stories rather than a slick museum experience.

It pairs well with the painted pumpjacks around town, because the machinery outside suddenly has a history attached to it.
