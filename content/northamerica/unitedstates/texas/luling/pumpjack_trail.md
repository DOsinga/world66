Luling's pumpjacks are still tied to oil, but many have been painted into roadside characters: cows, birds, athletes, and other small-town jokes. It is a simple idea that gives the town an odd visual signature.

Pick up a local map if one is available, or just wander the central streets and roads out from downtown.
