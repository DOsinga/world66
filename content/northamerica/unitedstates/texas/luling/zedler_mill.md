Zedler Mill is Luling's prettiest stop, a restored mill complex beside the San Marcos River. The old industrial buildings, dam, and water channel explain the town before oil, when river power mattered to the farms around it.

The grounds are now a park, so it works as both a historical stop and a place to slow down by the water.
