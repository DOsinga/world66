Ballroom Marfa gives the town a more changeable art pulse than the Judd institutions. The nonprofit space occupies a former dance hall and hosts contemporary exhibitions, films, concerts, talks, and community programs.

It is worth checking the schedule before you arrive. Some visits are a quick look through a current show; others catch Marfa at its best, when a small desert town suddenly feels plugged into a much wider art conversation.
