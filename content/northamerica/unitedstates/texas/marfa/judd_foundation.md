The Judd Foundation opens the artist's downtown Marfa spaces by guided visit, including La Mansana de Chinati, known as The Block, and a group of studios and work spaces around town. These are not neutral galleries. They show Judd's furniture, books, early work, architecture, and domestic spaces as one carefully held environment.

Reserve ahead and pay attention to the timing. Visits are guided and controlled to protect the spaces, which makes them feel more like entering a working mind than touring a museum.
