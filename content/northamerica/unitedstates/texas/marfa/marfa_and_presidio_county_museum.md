This small museum in an 1880s house is the useful counterweight to Marfa's art fame. Its rooms cover the town's start as a railroad water stop, its ranching and military years, Presidio County history, and the movies that have used the desert around town.

Come here when you want Marfa to feel less like an art-world idea and more like a real West Texas place with layers under the surface.
