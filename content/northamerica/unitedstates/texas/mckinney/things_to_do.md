title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

McKinney's draw is its historic downtown square and its parks. The [Old Collin County Courthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/mckinney/old_collin_county_courthouse) anchors a square ringed by Victorian storefronts now full of independent shops and restaurants. A few blocks east, the [Heard-Craig House](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/mckinney/heard_craig_house) preserves the look of late-19th-century life. South of town the [Heard Natural Science Museum and Wildlife Sanctuary](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/mckinney/heard_natural_science_museum) is the area's main outdoor draw.
