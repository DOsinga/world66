The Fredda Durham Turner Children's Museum is the family wing of the Museum of the Southwest, with hands-on exhibits designed for younger visitors. The galleries are bright and practical, built around interactive learning rather than display cases.

It is most useful for families spending time in Midland, especially when paired with the wider Museum of the Southwest campus nearby.
