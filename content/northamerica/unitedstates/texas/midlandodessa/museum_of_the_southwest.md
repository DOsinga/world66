A cultural complex on West Missouri Avenue anchored by the 1934 Turner Mansion, the Museum of the Southwest houses an art museum, children's museum, planetarium, and sculpture garden on a single campus. The art collection focuses on American Southwest and Taos school paintings, with some strong examples of the regional tradition.

The planetarium runs public shows on a regular schedule and is genuinely good — it was designed with serious astronomical equipment, not just as a tourist attraction. The children's museum section makes the complex a practical option for families passing through. The sculpture garden is pleasant on a clear day, which in West Texas is most days.

1705 W Missouri Avenue, Midland, TX 79701. Tuesday–Saturday 10am–5pm, Sunday 2–5pm.
