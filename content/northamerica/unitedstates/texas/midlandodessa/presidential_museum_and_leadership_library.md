The Presidential Museum and Leadership Library in Odessa collects memorabilia and exhibits connected with the U.S. presidency. Its scope is broader than the nearby George W. Bush Childhood Home, with displays that look across multiple administrations rather than one local story.

It is a niche museum, but a useful stop for travellers interested in American political history or anyone linking Odessa and Midland's presidential sites into one short circuit.
