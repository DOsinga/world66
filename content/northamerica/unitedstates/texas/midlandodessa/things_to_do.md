title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Midland-Odessa is not a sightseeing destination in any conventional sense, but the oil industry has left behind some genuinely interesting museums, and the flat West Texas landscape has its own strange appeal. The [Permian Basin Petroleum Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/midlandodessa/permian_basin_petroleum_museum) in Midland is one of the better industry museums in the country. Odessa has the [meteor crater](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/midlandodessa/odessa_meteor_crater) and its own eccentric Stonehenge replica.

For most visitors passing through on the way to Big Bend, Midland-Odessa is a practical stop for fuel, food, and supplies. But if you have a few hours, the museums are worth the time.
