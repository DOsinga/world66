White-Pool House is a turn-of-the-century Odessa home preserved as a small local history museum. Its rotating exhibits focus on West Texas life, so it works best as a quiet stop for travellers interested in the Permian Basin beyond oil, highways, and roadside distances.

The house is modest, but that is part of the appeal. It gives Odessa a human scale and a domestic counterpoint to the larger museums around Midland-Odessa.
