Oak Grove Cemetery is a quiet, shaded place to connect Nacogdoches with the wider Texas story. Four signers of the Texas Declaration of Independence are buried here, along with other early figures from the town's political and commercial life.

The cemetery is not large, but it rewards slow reading. Come after the museums, when the names on the stones have started to mean something.
