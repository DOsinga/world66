Freedom Museum USA is a compact military-history museum with uniforms, vehicles, weapons, photographs, and memorabilia connected to American service members. Its most useful local angle is the Pampa Army Air Base, where pilots and mechanics trained during World War II.

The collection is earnest rather than slick. Give it a short visit before or after the White Deer Land Museum, especially if you are interested in how wartime aviation reached deep into the High Plains.
