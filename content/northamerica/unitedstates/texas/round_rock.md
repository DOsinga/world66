Round Rock sits just north of [Austin](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/austin) and has grown from a small limestone-quarry town into one of Texas's fastest-expanding suburbs. Dell Technologies has its headquarters here, and the tech economy has reshaped the city over the past two decades.

The town takes its name from a [large round rock](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/round_rock/the_round_rock) in the middle of Brushy Creek, which once marked a low-water crossing on the Chisholm Trail. You can still see it at [Chisholm Trail Crossing Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/round_rock/chisholm_trail_crossing_park). The [old downtown](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/round_rock/round_rock_commercial_historic_district) around Mays Street has antique shops and restaurants worth a wander, and the [Dell Diamond](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/round_rock/dell_diamond) is home to the Round Rock Express, the local minor-league baseball team. [Round Rock Donuts](/northamerica/unitedstates/texas/round_rock/round_rock_donuts), operating since 1926, has become famous far beyond the city for its outsized orange-glazed rounds.

Round Rock is not a destination in itself, but it makes a convenient base if Austin hotels are full, and the outlet malls on I-35 draw shoppers from across central Texas.
