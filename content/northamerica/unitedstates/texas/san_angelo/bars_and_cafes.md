---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Downtown San Angelo has a walkable strip of bars and restaurants along Concho Street and Chadbourne Street, busiest on weekend evenings. The area around the historic downtown is unpretentious — cold beer, live country music, and a friendly crowd. The Deadpool Bar and Eola's are among the regular stops. Angelo State University brings a student population that keeps a few spots lively mid-week. The city is dry in some rural outskirts, so stick to the downtown core for evening options.
