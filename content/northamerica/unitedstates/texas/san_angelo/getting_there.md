---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

San Angelo Regional Airport (SJT) has daily connecting flights to Dallas/Fort Worth via American Eagle. By road, the city sits at the junction of US 67, US 87, and US 277 — expect long stretches of open highway from every direction. From San Antonio it is about three hours; from Dallas about four. There is no rail service. Greyhound buses connect San Angelo to larger Texas cities, though services are infrequent.
