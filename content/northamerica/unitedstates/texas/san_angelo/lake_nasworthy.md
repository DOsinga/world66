Lake Nasworthy is San Angelo's practical outdoor escape, especially when West Texas heat makes the city feel landlocked. The lake is used for boating, fishing, waterskiing, and lazy afternoons in the public parks along the shore.

It is not wilderness, and that is not the point. Come for an easy picnic, a sunset over the water, or a place to cool off between the city's museums, missions, and river walks.
