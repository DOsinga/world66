The Meadows Center protects Spring Lake, where the San Marcos Springs feed the river. The glass-bottom boat tours are the draw: you glide over clear water, spring vents, fish, plants, and the remains of a place that has attracted people for thousands of years.

It is an easy, calm contrast to the louder tubing scene downstream. Go early if you want the quieter version, then follow the river into town.
