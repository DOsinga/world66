Wonder World Cave is a show cave formed by movement along the Balcones Fault rather than by the usual slow work of water dissolving limestone. That makes it a good small geological stop, especially if you are already in town for the river.

The park around it is family-oriented and a little odd, with an anti-gravity house and other roadside-attraction touches. The cave tour is the part that matters.
