Eisenhower Park gives San Antonio visitors a quick taste of Hill Country terrain without a long drive. Its trails climb through limestone, oak, and cedar scrub to viewpoints that feel very different from the River Walk and mission corridor.

The park is best in the morning or late afternoon, especially in hot months. Take water seriously, choose a trail length that matches the weather, and use it as an outdoor counterbalance to the city's history-heavy itinerary.
