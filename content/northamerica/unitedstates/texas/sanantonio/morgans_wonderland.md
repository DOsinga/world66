Morgan's Wonderland is one of San Antonio's more unusual attractions: a theme park designed from the start around accessibility and inclusion. The rides, playgrounds, sensory spaces, and water-play areas are built so guests with disabilities can participate alongside family and friends, rather than watch from the edge. That mission gives the park a clearer identity than most local amusement parks.

It is especially useful for families who need a low-stress day away from the downtown museum-and-mission circuit. The park sits northeast of the centre, so plan transport rather than treating it as a casual River Walk add-on. Check seasonal schedules and ticket policies before going, as the water park and special events can change the rhythm of a visit.
