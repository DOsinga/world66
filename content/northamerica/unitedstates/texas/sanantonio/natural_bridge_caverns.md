Natural Bridge Caverns is the big underground excursion north of San Antonio, reached near the Hill Country edge rather than in the downtown core. The main cave route descends through broad limestone chambers hung with flowstone, stalactites, and heavy curtains of calcite, with enough scale to feel like a proper half-day trip rather than a quick roadside stop.

Guided tours are the reason to come. The standard route works for most visitors, while more adventurous options add crawling passages and a rougher sense of the cave system. It pairs naturally with the nearby wildlife ranch, but the caverns are the stronger attraction if you only have time for one stop outside the city.
