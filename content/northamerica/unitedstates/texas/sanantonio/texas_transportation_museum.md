The Texas Transportation Museum collects locomotives, railway equipment, vintage vehicles and transport memorabilia on San Antonio's north side. Its appeal is hands-on and family-friendly, with train rides or demonstrations often doing more to sell the place than static displays alone.

It is outside the central tourist circuit, so it suits visitors with children, rail enthusiasts or anyone looking beyond the Alamo-and-River-Walk core. Check operating days before going, because the best parts depend on volunteer schedules and running equipment.
