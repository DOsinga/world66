The SpaceX South Texas Launch Site at Boca Chica is the launch and test area for Starship. Visitors cannot tour the facility, but the launch tower, tanks, and large rocket hardware are visible from public roads when access is open.

It is worth the detour for spaceflight enthusiasts, with the usual warning that road closures and launch operations can change access with little notice.
