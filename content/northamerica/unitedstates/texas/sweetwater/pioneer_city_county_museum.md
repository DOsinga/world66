The Pioneer City-County Museum fills out Sweetwater's local story after the bigger WASP museum. It occupies the 1906 Ragland House and covers Nolan County settlement history through rooms of photographs, ranching material, household objects, and local records.

It is a modest stop, best for travelers who like small-town museums and the details they preserve. Pair it with a walk around downtown rather than treating it as a destination on its own.
