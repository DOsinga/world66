The Ace of Clubs House at 420 Pine Street is one of the most unusual houses in Texas: a Victorian mansion built in 1885 with a floor plan shaped like a playing card. The three-story structure with its distinctive cupola is now operated as a museum by the Texarkana Museum System, with period furnishings and a guided tour that explains the legend of the poker-winnings origin story.

The architecture is eclectic Victorian — bay windows, ornate trim, a wraparound porch — but the octagonal layout gives it an internal logic that standard Victorian houses lack. The museum presents the domestic life of a wealthy Texarkana family in the late nineteenth century, when the city was a prosperous railroad junction. The guided tours are essential since the layout of the house is difficult to understand without explanation.
