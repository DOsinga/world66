The Museum of Regional History is Texarkana's best stop for understanding why this border city grew where it did. Exhibits cover Caddo heritage, railroads, the state-line identity, local industry, and the people who shaped northeast Texas and southwest Arkansas.

It is a traditional local-history museum, but the subject is unusually useful because Texarkana can otherwise feel like a place defined by a courthouse photo and a state-line joke. Visit before walking the downtown district and the city starts to make more sense.
