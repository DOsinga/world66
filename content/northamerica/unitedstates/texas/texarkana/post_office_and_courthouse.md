The Federal Building at the center of downtown Texarkana is the city's most distinctive landmark and its primary tourist attraction: a neoclassical federal building positioned precisely on the state line, with half in Texas and half in Arkansas. The date of construction is 1933, and the building is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. The building houses both a post office and federal court facilities, and the postal operation for both Texarkanas runs from here.

The most photographed spot in the city is the steps and entrance, where visitors take pictures with one foot in each state. It is a simple pleasure but a genuine one — there is something inherently funny about standing with one hand touching the Texas side of the door frame and the other hand on the Arkansas side. The architecture is dignified limestone neoclassical, typical of federal buildings of its period, and the interior (what is accessible) is worth a glance.
