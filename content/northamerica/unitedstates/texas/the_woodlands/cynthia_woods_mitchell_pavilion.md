The premier outdoor concert venue in the Houston area, located in the southern part of The Woodlands off Six Pines Drive. It hosts the Houston Symphony's summer season alongside major touring rock, country, and pop acts throughout the spring and fall. The design is a classic amphitheater with a roofed main section and a large open lawn behind.

Tickets for lawn spots are always cheaper than reserved seats and perfectly enjoyable on clear nights. The venue is easy to reach from I-45 and has ample parking. Check the schedule before you visit — when a major act is in town, the Waterway area restaurants fill up fast.
