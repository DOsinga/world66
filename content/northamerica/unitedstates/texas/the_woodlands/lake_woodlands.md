A 200-acre artificial lake at the heart of the Town Center, surrounded by a continuous trail used by joggers, cyclists, and dog walkers. The 8-mile loop around the lake is the most popular outdoor exercise route in The Woodlands. Sections near the Waterway and Northshore Park are especially scenic.

The lake is stocked with bass, catfish, and crappie — fishing from the banks is common and permitted. Several parks and access points ring the shoreline. Water taxi service connects the Waterway to the lake area on weekends.
