A waterfront park on Lake Woodlands, with a large pavilion, boat launch, picnic areas, and walking paths along the shoreline. The park hosts many of The Woodlands Township's outdoor events — concerts, festivals, and seasonal markets. The lake views are pleasant and the pavilion area has good shade.

Kayak and paddleboard rentals are available at the park on weekends in warmer months. The trail along the lake connects to the broader Township trail system. Free parking in the adjacent lot.
