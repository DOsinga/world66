A craft beer bar on the Waterway with a rotating tap list focused on Texas breweries. The space is comfortable without being noisy, with indoor and outdoor seating. A solid food menu covers bar food elevated above the usual — the pretzels and the smoked wings are worth ordering.

Good happy hour deal on weekdays from 3 to 7pm. No reservations; first-come for outdoor seating on nice evenings.
