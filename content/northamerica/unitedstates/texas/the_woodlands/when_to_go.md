---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit is March through April and October through November, when temperatures are in the 20s Celsius and the humidity is manageable. Houston summers are brutal — humidity above 90% and temperatures regularly above 35°C from June through August — and The Woodlands offers little escape from it. The concert season at the Pavilion runs spring through fall, with the heaviest schedule in September and October.
