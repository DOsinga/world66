A small children's museum in Market Street with interactive exhibits aimed at children up to age 10. The exhibits change seasonally and cover themes like science, art, and cultural exploration. Not a blockbuster museum, but well maintained and genuinely engaging for young children.

Admission is modest. Open Tuesday through Sunday; closed Mondays. Best visited on weekday mornings when it's less crowded.
