Armstrong Browning Library stands on the Baylor University campus and holds a major collection related to Robert and Elizabeth Barrett Browning. The building is as much a draw as the collection, with 62 stained-glass windows inspired by the poets' work.

It is best for literature and architecture-minded visitors, but the stained glass alone makes it more than a routine campus library.
