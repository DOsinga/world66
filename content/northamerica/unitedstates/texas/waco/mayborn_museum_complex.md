The Mayborn Museum Complex is Baylor University's big family-friendly museum, mixing natural history, hands-on discovery rooms, and Central Texas history. The exhibits move from limestone caves and forest dioramas to transport, media, pioneers, and the Waco mammoth story.

The grounds include the Governor Bill and Vara Daniel Historic Village, a reconstructed 1890s village with a church, livery, general store, and plantation home moved from Liberty, Texas.
