The Red Men Museum and Library is a niche history museum devoted to the Improved Order of Red Men, a fraternal organisation that traces its origins to the 18th century. Exhibits include documents, regalia, and historical objects connected to the order and early American civic life.

It is not Waco's obvious first stop, but it is the kind of odd specialist museum that rewards travellers who like local history with a few strange corners.
