The Texas Sports Hall of Fame is a broad sports museum near Baylor University, with exhibits on Texas athletes in football, baseball, basketball, golf, tennis, horse racing, and other sports. It also holds the Southwest Conference's 75-year collection of memorabilia, which gives the museum a strong college-sports angle.

It is an easy add-on to the Texas Ranger Hall of Fame, the Dr Pepper Museum, or a Baylor visit, especially for travellers who follow American sports.
