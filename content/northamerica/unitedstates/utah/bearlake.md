Bear Lake is Utah's turquoise surprise, a high mountain lake straddling the Idaho border and glowing a strange, almost Caribbean blue against the sagebrush hills. Locals call it the "Caribbean of the Rockies," which sounds like chamber-of-commerce nonsense until you see the water on a clear afternoon.

The Utah side is the easier side for most travellers, with [Garden City](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/bearlake/garden_city) as the main base and [Bear Lake State Park Marina](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/bearlake/bear_lake_state_park_marina) handling the practical business of boats, fuel, and lakeside picnics. The south shore at [Rendezvous Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/bearlake/rendezvous_beach) is better for families and swimmers; the east side at [Cisco Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/bearlake/cisco_beach) feels quieter and more open.

Bear Lake is very seasonal. Summer brings boating, raspberry shakes, and full campgrounds; winter is silent except for ice anglers and the occasional snowmobile. The loveliest approach is from Logan through Logan Canyon, especially if you stop at the [Bear Lake Overlook](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/bearlake/bear_lake_overlook), where the whole basin suddenly opens below the road.
