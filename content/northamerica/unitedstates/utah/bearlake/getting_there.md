---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Most visitors reach Bear Lake from [Logan](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/logan) on US-89 through Logan Canyon. The road is scenic and winding, and it tops out at a viewpoint where the lake appears suddenly below you.

From Salt Lake City, allow about two and a half hours by car in good weather. Public transport is not useful here; you need a car for the beaches, marina areas, and trailheads around the lake.
