Brigham City Museum is a small but worthwhile art and history museum covering Brigham City and southern Box Elder County. Its permanent displays explain the local story, while temporary exhibitions give the museum more variety than many small-town history rooms.

It works well as a short stop before heading out to the Bear River Migratory Bird Refuge or the historic buildings around town. Families will appreciate the hands-on area for children.
