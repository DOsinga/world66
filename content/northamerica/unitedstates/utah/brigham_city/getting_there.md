---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Brigham City is on I-15, about 60 miles north of Salt Lake City and 25 miles south of the Idaho border. It is an easy stop on the drive between Salt Lake and Yellowstone. UTA's FrontRunner commuter rail runs from Salt Lake to Ogden, but does not extend to Brigham City — from Ogden it is another 25 miles north by car. Salt Lake City International Airport is the nearest major airport.
