Bryce Point is one of Bryce Canyon's strongest overlooks, with a wide view across the amphitheater of hoodoos and ridges. The angle is especially good at sunrise, when the first light catches the orange and pink limestone formations.

The viewpoint can be reached by road, and hikers can also connect it with the rim trail. It is one of the park stops worth making even on a short visit.
