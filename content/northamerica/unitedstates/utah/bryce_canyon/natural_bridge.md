Natural Bridge is one of the quick roadside viewpoints on Bryce Canyon's scenic drive, where erosion has cut a clean arch through the red Claron limestone. It is smaller than Utah's famous natural arches, but the contrast of orange rock, forest, and open sky makes it a worthwhile stop on the way to the southern overlooks.
