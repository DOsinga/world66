The Navajo Loop is one of Bryce Canyon's signature short hikes, dropping from the rim into the amphitheatre among hoodoos, switchbacks, and narrow passages. The classic route is about 1.3 miles round trip and passes formations such as Thor's Hammer.

It is short but steep, so the climb back to the rim feels harder than the distance suggests, especially at Bryce's altitude. Many walkers combine it with the Queens Garden Trail for a longer and more varied loop through the heart of the amphitheatre.
