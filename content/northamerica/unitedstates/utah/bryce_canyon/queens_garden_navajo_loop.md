The Queen's Garden/Navajo Loop Combination is Bryce Canyon's classic short hike, linking Sunrise Point and Sunset Point through the heart of the amphitheatre. The route is about 2.9 miles round trip and drops among hoodoos, narrow fins, and close-up views that are impossible to get from the rim alone.

It is popular for good reason. Start early for cooler temperatures and fewer people, and expect a steady climb back out of the canyon at the end.
