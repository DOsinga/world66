Cave Spring Trail is a short loop in Canyonlands that combines desert walking with history. The route passes a former cowboy line camp and prehistoric pictographs, giving a quick sense of how people have used this dry country across very different periods.

The loop is about 0.6 miles, but it includes two wooden ladders, so it is not a flat stroll.
