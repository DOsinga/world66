The Chocolate Drops are a cluster of sandstone buttes in Canyonlands National Park, rising sharply from the surrounding desert. Their dark, isolated shapes make them a useful landmark in the Maze district and a striking sight for visitors exploring the park's more remote terrain.

Reaching this area takes planning, high-clearance travel, and desert backcountry caution.
