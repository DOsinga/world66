Confluence Overlook is a demanding day hike in the Needles district of Canyonlands, ending at a cliff above the meeting of the Green and Colorado rivers. The round trip is about 11 miles, crossing dry, open country shaped by the faults that cut through this part of the park.

There is no water on the route, and shade is limited. Start early, carry more water than you think you need, and treat the overlook as a serious desert hike rather than a casual viewpoint.
