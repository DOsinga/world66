Confluence Overlook looks toward the meeting of the Green and Colorado rivers in Canyonlands National Park. The view is remote and hard-earned, with the two great river canyons cutting through the desert plateau below.

Reaching the overlook takes commitment: access is by hiking route or high-clearance four-wheel-drive road, depending on conditions and route choice. That effort is part of the appeal for travellers who want Canyonlands at its wilder scale.
