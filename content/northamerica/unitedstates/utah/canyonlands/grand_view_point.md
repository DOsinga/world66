Grand View Point is one of the classic overlooks in the Island in the Sky district of Canyonlands National Park. The viewpoint looks across a maze of canyons, cliffs, and distant mesas, with especially strong light near sunset.

It is reachable by road, so it works even on a short park visit. If you have more time, walk part of the rim trail from the overlook for quieter views away from the parking area.
