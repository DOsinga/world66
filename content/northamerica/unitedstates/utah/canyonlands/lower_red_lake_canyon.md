Lower Red Lake Canyon is a remote canyon route in the Needles area of Canyonlands, with desert scenery, slickrock, and a much quieter feel than the park's easy roadside overlooks. It is for experienced hikers rather than casual sightseers.

Treat it as backcountry travel: carry enough water, know the route, and check current conditions with the park before setting out. Visitors looking for a short, simple stop should choose Mesa Arch or the main Needles viewpoints instead.
