Salt Creek Canyon is a long backcountry route in the Needles area of Canyonlands, following a drainage past cottonwood groves, arches, and archaeological sites. It is a serious desert hike rather than a quick viewpoint: expect brushy sections, route-finding, and limited water. Check current conditions and permits before setting out.
