Frontier Homestead State Park Museum tells Cedar City's early story through wagons, historic buildings, Native heritage exhibits, and the failed-but-revealing attempt to build an iron industry in the Mormon frontier.

It is hands-on and local rather than grand, which suits the subject. The museum makes the town's Main Street setting feel less anonymous before you head out toward the plateau roads.
