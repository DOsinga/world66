---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Cedar City works because its sights are not all the same thing. You can see a play at the Utah Shakespeare Festival, spend an hour with pioneer wagons and iron-country history at Frontier Homestead, visit SUMA for regional art, then drive up to Cedar Breaks for high-elevation cliffs and bristlecone pines. Winter adds Brian Head's ski slopes just up the road.
