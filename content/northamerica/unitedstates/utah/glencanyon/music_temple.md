Music Temple is a natural grotto in Glen Canyon, formed by high vaulted walls above a deep pool of water. Its scale and acoustics made it one of the canyon's named geological features.

Access depends on Lake Powell and Colorado River conditions, so it is mainly relevant to visitors exploring Glen Canyon by boat or on a specialist river trip.
