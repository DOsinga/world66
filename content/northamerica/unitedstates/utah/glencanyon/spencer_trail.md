Spencer Trail climbs from the Lees Ferry area up steep switchbacks to wide views over the Colorado River, Marble Canyon, and the cliffs around Page. It is a short but exposed hike, roughly 4 miles round trip, and the elevation gain feels serious in hot weather.

Start early, carry more water than you think you need, and avoid the climb in the middle of a summer day. The payoff is one of the better land-based views in the lower Glen Canyon area.
