---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Green River sits at the intersection of two great American landscapes: the San Rafael Swell to the west and the Canyonlands country to the south. Almost anything you reach in 90 minutes is worth the drive.

- **Goblin Valley State Park** (1 hr southwest) — hoodoo basin you can wander freely.
- **Buckhorn Wash** (45 min north) — pictograph panel in the San Rafael Swell.
- **Sego Canyon** (30 min east) — three styles of rock art near Thompson Springs.
- **Crystal Geyser** (10 min south) — a cold-water carbon dioxide geyser on the riverbank that erupts erratically.
- **[Moab](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/moab)** (50 min southeast) — base for Arches and Canyonlands.
- **[Canyonlands National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/canyonlands)** — the Island in the Sky district is 90 minutes south.
