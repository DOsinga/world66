Hurricane sits in the middle of some of the most dramatic red-rock scenery in the American West. The town itself is mostly a service strip, but within a short drive you have a national park, two state parks, a geological fault worth its own detour, and a canal that took nine years to carve by hand. The emphasis here is overwhelmingly outdoors — swimming in warm reservoirs, hiking slot canyons, and watching sandstone cliffs turn red at dusk.
