Little Hollywood Movie Museum leans into Kanab's long role as a filming base for westerns and desert adventure productions. The site preserves old movie sets, props, photographs, and memorabilia from the period when crews used the red-rock country around town as a stand-in for the mythic American West.

It is informal and a little nostalgic, but that suits Kanab. The museum gives context to a town that many visitors now use mainly for national-park access, reminding you that scenery here has been packaged, performed, and projected on screen for generations.
