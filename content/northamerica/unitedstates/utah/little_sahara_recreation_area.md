Little Sahara Recreation Area is a broad sweep of dunes in Utah's west desert, used for off-highway riding, camping, hiking, biking, horseback riding, and wildlife watching. It is a big, exposed place rather than a quick roadside stop, so bring more water and fuel than you think you need and leave room in the day for the drive back.
