Logan is a university town tucked into the Cache Valley in northern Utah, surrounded by mountains on three sides. [Utah State University](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/logan/utah_state_university) gives it a youthful energy, and the setting — green farmland ringed by the Bear River and Wellsville ranges — is striking.

The town itself is small and quiet, but [Logan Canyon](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/logan/logan_canyon) heading east into the Wasatch Range is excellent — a scenic drive year-round giving access to hiking, fishing, and skiing at Beaver Mountain, with [Tony Grove Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/logan/tony_grove_lake) the best high-alpine destination. In town, the [Logan Utah Temple](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/logan/logan_utah_temple)'s twin Gothic towers dominate the skyline, and the [Ellen Eccles Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/logan/ellen_eccles_theatre) hosts a surprisingly ambitious summer opera season. The Cache Valley is also known for its dairy farms, and the local cheese-making tradition is a genuine point of pride.
