The Ellen Eccles Theatre is Logan's main performing arts venue — a 1923 Italian Renaissance movie palace restored in 1993 to its original grandeur. The ornate interior, with its plasterwork, painted ceiling, and gilded details, makes it one of the finest historic theatre spaces in Utah.

The theatre is home to the **Utah Festival Opera and Musical Theatre**, which runs a summer season of opera and Broadway musicals with professional casts — a cultural offering well above what you'd expect in a city Logan's size. Check the calendar; if a performance coincides with your visit, it's worth attending. The building is worth seeing even on a daytime walkthrough if the lobby is open.
