---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Logan is about 80 miles north of [Salt Lake City](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity) via US-89 or I-15 — roughly an hour and a half by car. There is no commercial air service to Logan; the nearest airports are Salt Lake City International and Ogden-Hinckley Airport.

Public transport options are limited. Utah Transit Authority runs a commuter bus route between Logan and Salt Lake City, but most visitors drive. Logan is also accessible from [Bear Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/bearlake) via US-89 over the Logan Canyon pass.
