The Logan Tabernacle is a large LDS meeting hall in downtown Logan, built between 1865 and 1891 in a Romanesque Revival style with a distinctive octagonal central tower. The building served as the main community gathering place for the Cache Valley's Mormon settlers and continues to host concerts, community events, and church meetings.

The tabernacle's interior is notable for its excellent acoustics, which have made it a popular concert venue. Free organ recitals and other musical performances are occasionally held here — check the LDS visitors centre next door for the current schedule. The building is a good complement to the temple visit and sits just a few blocks away in the downtown core.
