---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Logan's best seasons depend on what you're after. **Summer** (June–August) is ideal for hiking in Logan Canyon and surrounding wilderness, with long days and warm temperatures. The Cache Valley agricultural festivals and the Utah Festival Opera season run through summer.

**Winter** brings skiing at Beaver Mountain (mid-December through March), one of Utah's lesser-known but reliably good ski resorts. Spring wildflower season in the canyon and valley (April–May) is beautiful. The valley gets cold in winter — temperatures below freezing are common from November through February.
