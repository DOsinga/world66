Manti Historic City Hall is a small but handsome civic building on the town’s main street, built from local limestone in the same Utah tradition that gives Manti much of its character. It now serves as a visitor information and local-history stop rather than a grand museum.

The building is worth a short visit if you are already in town for the temple, tabernacle, or Sanpete Valley scenery. It helps ground Manti as a working settlement with its own civic history, not only a backdrop to the famous hilltop temple.
