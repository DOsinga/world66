The Manti-La Sal National Forest sweeps across the Wasatch Plateau east of Sanpete Valley, with access roads climbing from Manti into subalpine terrain of aspen groves, conifer forests, and alpine meadows. The Skyline Drive, a high-clearance dirt road running along the crest of the Wasatch Plateau, offers extraordinary panoramic views over the valley and is accessible in summer and early autumn.

The forest is lightly visited compared to Utah's famous national parks, which means you can find genuine solitude on the trails. Fishing is the main draw for many visitors — the streams hold brook trout and the reservoirs have cutthroat — but hiking and autumn foliage drives in September and October are equally worthwhile. The summit area above 10,000 feet (3,000 m) typically has snow from October through May.
