The Manti Pageant is an outdoor theatrical production staged on the hillside beside the Manti Utah Temple each June. Originally created in 1967 as the Mormon Miracle Pageant to dramatise the early history of the LDS Church — Joseph Smith's visions, the westward migration, the settlement of Utah — it drew audiences of up to 60,000 each year to this small town of 3,000 people.

After the LDS Church discontinued its formal pageant programme in 2020, local community members and faithful revived the production independently. The scale remains impressive: a cast of hundreds, elaborate costumes, a large outdoor stage built into the temple hillside, and performances running across two weeks in June. If you are passing through central Utah in June and have any interest in the history of the American West or the Mormon faith, it is a singular experience.
