The Manti Tabernacle was built between 1878 and 1903, constructed in phases by the local Pioneer-era congregation and finished in the same pale limestone as the temple. It served as the main LDS meeting house for Sanpete County for most of a century. The building was closed in 2013 for seismic retrofitting and structural repairs, and its current status fluctuates — check locally whether it is open for tours before making a specific visit.

The tabernacle sits on the main street and its exterior gives a good sense of the ambitious civic and religious building programme that the early Mormon settlers undertook in these small valley towns. Alongside the temple, the courthouse, and a handful of well-preserved Victorian houses, it completes a streetscape that makes Manti feel surprisingly substantial for a town of 3,000.
