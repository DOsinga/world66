---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Manti's appeal is focused but genuine. The **Manti Utah Temple** dominates the skyline and is the reason most people make the detour. The historic main street preserves a concentration of 19th-century Pioneer-era buildings rarely seen outside national parks. Every June the town transforms for the outdoor **Manti Pageant**, drawing tens of thousands to an amphitheatre carved into the hillside beside the temple.

Beyond the temple and pageant, the Manti-La Sal National Forest begins just east of town, offering access to hiking trails and fishing streams in the Sanpete Valley foothills.
