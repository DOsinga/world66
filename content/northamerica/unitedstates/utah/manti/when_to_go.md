---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

June is the one time of year Manti gets crowded — the Manti Pageant fills every available bed in the valley for two weeks. Book ahead if you are coming for that reason, or plan around it if you want a quiet visit.

The rest of the year the town is very quiet. Spring (April–May) brings green fields across the valley. Autumn (September–October) is the best season for scenery: the aspens in the Manti-La Sal National Forest turn gold and the crowds are nil. Winters are cold and snowfall is common, but the roads are maintained and the setting can be beautiful.
