Mexican Hat offers deep-canyon river country, ancient rock formations, and access to some of the most remote scenery in the American Southwest. There is no better way to experience it than by water — on a raft, kayak, or dory — or by driving the lonely paved and dirt roads that connect the great geological set-pieces of the San Juan.
