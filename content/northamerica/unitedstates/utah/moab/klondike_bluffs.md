Klondike Bluffs sits in the quieter northwest corner of Arches National Park, reached by dirt road or on foot. Short trails lead to Tower Arch and the Marching Men formations, with far fewer visitors than the park's famous central sights. It is a good choice when you want the sandstone without the procession.
