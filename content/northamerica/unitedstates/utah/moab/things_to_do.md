---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Moab is an outdoor adventure town, and the list of things to do is essentially the list of ways to move through red rock desert: hiking, mountain biking, river rafting, four-wheel driving, and canyoneering. The two national parks alone could absorb a week. Add the state parks, BLM land, and river trips, and the limitation is time, not options.
