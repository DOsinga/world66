The Windows section of Arches National Park gathers North Window, South Window, and Turret Arch along an easy loop trail. It is one of the park's best short walks: quick to reach, simple underfoot, and packed with the kind of red-rock openings that make Arches feel almost theatrical.

Come early or late if you can. The trail is exposed and popular, but the reward-to-effort ratio is excellent, especially when low sun brings out the shape of the arches and the La Sal Mountains sit on the horizon.
