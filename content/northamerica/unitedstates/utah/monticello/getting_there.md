---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Monticello sits at the junction of US-191 and US-491, about 50 miles south of Moab and 50 miles north of Blanding. There is no commercial air service — the nearest airports are in Moab (a small regional airport with limited service) or Grand Junction, Colorado, about 140 miles northeast.

The town is an easy drive from [Moab](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/moab) and makes a reasonable base or stopping point when traveling between Moab and the Four Corners area.
