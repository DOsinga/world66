One of Monticello's older surviving structures, the Hyland Hotel is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Built in 1916–1918, it started as a private residence before serving as the town's first proper hotel. The building reflects the modest but sturdy vernacular architecture of early 20th-century Utah frontier towns.

The building is not currently operating as a hotel and is not always accessible for interior visits, but it is worth noting as part of Monticello's small historic core. It sits a short walk from the center of town.
