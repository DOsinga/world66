Ogden sits where the Wasatch Mountains meet the Great Basin, a railroad town that grew up rough and never quite smoothed out all its edges. For decades it was the junction where transcontinental trains changed crews, and that history of movement and commerce still shapes the place. [Historic 25th Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/historic_25th_street), once notorious for its saloons and gambling dens, is now lined with restaurants, brewpubs, and shops — but the old brick buildings still have character the suburbs cannot match.

The surrounding mountains make Ogden a serious base for outdoor pursuits. [Snowbasin](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/snowbasin_resort) and [Powder Mountain](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/powder_mountain) are within easy reach for winter skiing, and in summer the trails above Ogden Valley offer hiking, mountain biking, and reservoir swimming. [Ogden Canyon](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/ogden_canyon) is the dramatic gateway, and the [Ogden River Parkway](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/ogden_river_parkway) runs right through town for an easy walk or ride.

For history, the city punches above its weight. [Union Station](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/union_station) is a grand reminder of the railroad era, and the [Hill Aerospace Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/hill_aerospace_museum) south of town has one of the best military aircraft collections in the country. The [golden spike](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/golden_spike_national_historic_site) that joined the transcontinental railroad was driven at Promontory Summit, a short drive northwest. [Peery's Egyptian Theater](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/peery_egyptian_theater) is one of the finest surviving movie palaces in the West, and the [Miles Goodyear Cabin](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/ogden/miles_goodyear_cabin) is the oldest non-Native American structure in Utah.
