About 90 minutes northwest of Ogden, Promontory Summit is where the Union Pacific and Central Pacific railroads met on 10 May 1869, completing the first transcontinental railroad and transforming a continent. The national historic site preserves the location with a visitor centre, a reconstructed section of both competing railroads, and two working replica steam locomotives that run on summer weekends.

The landscape is stark, high desert — sagebrush and sky in every direction, with the Great Salt Lake visible in the distance. The site feels remote in a way that actually enhances the experience: it is hard to believe that people laid track across this terrain at all. Living History Days in spring and autumn bring costumed interpreters and a ceremonial spike-driving recreation that is surprisingly moving.
