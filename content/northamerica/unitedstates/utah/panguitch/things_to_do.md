---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Panguitch sits at the center of some of the most dramatic canyon country in the American Southwest. The main draw is [Bryce Canyon National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/panguitch/bryce_canyon), 25 miles east on UT-12, but the immediate surroundings offer plenty of their own rewards. [Red Canyon](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/panguitch/red_canyon) is free and striking, [Cedar Breaks](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/panguitch/cedar_breaks) to the west rivals Bryce without the crowds, and the [historic downtown](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/panguitch/downtown_historic_district) is worth a slow walk for its well-preserved 1880s brick architecture.
