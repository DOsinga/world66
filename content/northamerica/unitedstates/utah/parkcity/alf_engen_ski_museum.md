The Alf Engen Ski Museum sits inside the Joe Quinney Winter Sports Center at Utah Olympic Park and tells the story of skiing in the Wasatch Mountains. Exhibits cover Norwegian-American ski jumper Alf Engen, early ski culture, resort development, avalanche science, and the 2002 Winter Olympics.

It is a natural add-on to a Utah Olympic Park visit, especially if you want more context than the zip lines and bobsled rides provide. Admission is free, and the museum helps explain why Park City became one of North America's defining ski towns.
