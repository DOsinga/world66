---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Park City has an unusually good restaurant scene for its size, shaped by ski money, Sundance culture, and proximity to Salt Lake City's increasingly cosmopolitan food world. Main Street has the highest concentration of restaurants; the food ranges from upscale American to sushi to Peruvian, with prices that reflect the resort-town economy. Reservations are essential during ski season and Sundance.
