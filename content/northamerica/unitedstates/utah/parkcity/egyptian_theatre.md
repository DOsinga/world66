The Egyptian Theatre is Park City's old Main Street showpiece, a 1926 theatre whose Egyptian Revival facade fits the town's mining-era street far better than it has any right to. It is one of the Sundance Film Festival's best-known venues, but it also works year-round as a live theatre and concert hall.

Check the programme before you go. A performance here gives you a good excuse to spend an evening on Main Street after the ski lifts close.
