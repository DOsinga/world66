---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Park City is 30 miles east of Salt Lake City and served by **Salt Lake City International Airport** (SLC), one of the main Delta hubs and well connected to cities across the US. The drive from the airport takes 35–45 minutes on Interstate 80. Several shuttle companies run direct transfers from the airport to Park City; book in advance during ski season.

There is no direct public transit from Salt Lake City to Park City, though the Utah Transit Authority operates a limited park-and-ride service from the Salt Lake Valley during ski season. Most visitors arrive by car or shuttle.
