Kimball Art Center is Park City's main year-round art space, a useful stop when the town's ski-resort surface starts to feel too polished. The exhibitions are usually contemporary, accessible, and free, with a programme that leans toward regional artists, travelling shows, and community-minded work rather than blockbuster museum spectacle.

The center has been part of Park City's cultural life since the 1970s and is also behind the long-running Park City Kimball Arts Festival. It sits at The Yard on Kearns Boulevard, a short drive from Main Street, so it works best as an hour-long detour between outdoor plans or on a stormy afternoon when the mountains are not cooperating.
