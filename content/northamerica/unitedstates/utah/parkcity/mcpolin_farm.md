McPolin Farm is the white barn and farmstead that marks one of Park City's most recognizable approaches. The site preserves a piece of the valley's agricultural history, which can otherwise be easy to miss amid ski resorts, trailheads, and Main Street.

Most visitors experience it as a short scenic and historic stop, often from the roadside or nearby trails. It is not a large museum, but it is a strong local landmark and a useful reminder that Park City's story is not only mining and skiing.
