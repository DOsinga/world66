Park City Mountain Resort is the largest ski resort in the United States by acreage, covering over 7,300 acres of terrain on the Wasatch Range. The mountain receives more than 350 inches of snow annually — the famous "Greatest Snow on Earth" — and the combination of terrain variety, reliable snow, and proximity to Salt Lake City (35 minutes by car, 45 minutes by shuttle) makes it one of the most visited ski resorts in North America.

The resort has terrain for every ability level, from gentle beginner runs near the base to challenging black diamonds and gladed tree skiing in the upper mountain. The **Empire Express** lift accesses some of the mountain's most open and rewarding intermediate terrain. The ski-in, ski-out access from Main Street, via a gondola from the top of the historic mining district, is a practical luxury.

In summer, the resort switches to lift-accessed mountain biking and hiking, with the gondola carrying bikers and their equipment to the top of the mountain. The trail network is one of the best in Utah.
