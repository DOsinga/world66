---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Park City has three distinct high seasons. **January** is peak Sundance time — the film festival runs for ten days usually starting the third Thursday of the month. Accommodation is extremely tight and expensive; book six months ahead if you want to attend. **December through March** is the ski season, with Christmas–New Year and Presidents' Week being the busiest and most expensive periods. **Summer** (June–September) is pleasant for hiking and biking with much lower prices and crowds.

The shoulder months of April–May and October–November are the quietest: lifts are closed, the festival crowd is gone, and the town is genuinely peaceful. Snow can fall at any time from October through May at this elevation (7,000 feet).
