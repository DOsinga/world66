The BYU Monte L. Bean Life Science Museum is a natural history museum with a large collection of taxidermied animals displayed in habitat settings. The collection ranges from familiar North American wildlife to unusual specimens, and the museum often runs live-animal shows and children's programmes.

It is on the Brigham Young University campus, but it is a public-facing museum and a good family stop in Provo.
