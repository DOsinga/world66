The BYU Museum of Paleontology is a small, well-kept fossil museum on the Brigham Young University campus. Its strength is western North American dinosaur material, including Jurassic fossils that once sat in storage beneath the football stadium.

It will not take long, but it is a worthwhile stop if you are already in Provo or travelling with someone who lights up at sauropods and ancient bones.
