The BYU Museum of Peoples and Cultures is a small cultural anthropology museum with exhibits on Native American cultures and other world traditions. It is more modest than the university's art museum, but the displays are thoughtful and often draw on BYU's archaeological and ethnographic collections.

It works best as a focused stop for visitors already exploring the BYU area or anyone interested in the human history of the region.
