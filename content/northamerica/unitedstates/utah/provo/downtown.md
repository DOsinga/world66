Downtown Provo is the city's most walkable historic district, with older commercial buildings, local restaurants, shops, and civic landmarks clustered around Center Street and University Avenue.

The area is worth a deliberate stroll for its architecture and street life, especially when combined with the Provo City Center Temple and the small historic sites nearby.
