Kyhv Peak, formerly known as Squaw Peak, is reached by a mountain road above Provo and has broad views over Utah Valley. Two overlooks make the drive worthwhile: the main Kyhv Peak Overlook and the Little Rock Canyon Overlook farther along the road.

The road is a popular sunset drive in good weather, with Provo, Utah Lake, and the Wasatch foothills spread out below. Winter conditions can limit access, so check the road before heading up outside the warmer months.
