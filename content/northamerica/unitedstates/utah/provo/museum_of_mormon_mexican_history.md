The Museum of Mormon Mexican History in Provo focuses on Latter-day Saint communities and migration histories connected with Mexico and the Americas. It is a specialist museum, but one that fits Provo's broader religious and family-history landscape.

The museum is best for travelers with a specific interest in Latter-day Saint history or Mexican Mormon communities. Confirm hours before visiting, as small museums often operate by appointment or limited schedule.
