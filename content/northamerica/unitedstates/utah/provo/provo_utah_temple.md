The Provo Utah Temple is one of the city's most recognisable landmarks, set against the Wasatch foothills with a circular modernist form that stands apart from older Latter-day Saint temple architecture. The building has long shaped Provo's skyline and religious geography.

Temple interiors are reserved for church members in good standing, so most visitors experience it from the grounds and exterior. Even so, it is a useful stop for understanding Provo, where the LDS Church, Brigham Young University, and mountain setting are tightly interwoven.
