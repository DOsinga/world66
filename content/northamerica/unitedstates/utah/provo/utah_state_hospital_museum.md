The Utah State Hospital Museum is a small museum about the history of the state mental hospital, which has operated in Provo since the 1880s. Displays cover the institution's changing treatment methods and the lives of people connected with the hospital.

The hospital campus also has notable older buildings, including a castle-like Works Progress Administration structure from the 1930s. Access to some campus buildings may require permission, so treat the museum as the main visitor stop.
