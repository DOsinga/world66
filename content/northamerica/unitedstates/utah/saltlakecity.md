Salt Lake City is the capital of Utah and the largest city in the Mountain West between Denver and the Pacific coast. The city was founded by Mormon pioneers who arrived in 1847 and proceeded to build an entire civilisation from scratch in a desert valley — and the traces of that founding are everywhere, from the six spires of the [Salt Lake Temple](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/salt_lake_temple) to the grid of streets measured in blocks from [Temple Square](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/temple_square).

The mountains are the other defining fact of the city. The Wasatch Range rises directly from the eastern edge of the urban area, and within 45 minutes of the airport you can be skiing at [Snowbird](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/snowbird_ski_resort), [Alta](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/alta_ski_resort), Brighton, or Solitude — some of the best powder ski terrain in the world. This proximity to the slopes was the decisive reason the 2002 Winter Olympics were held here; the [Olympic Cauldron Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/olympic_cauldron_park) on the University of Utah campus commemorates the games.

Beyond skiing and LDS history, Salt Lake City has a thriving food and arts scene that has developed rapidly since the 2000s. The [9th and 9th neighbourhood](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/ninth_and_ninth) is excellent for independent restaurants, bars, and bookshops. The [Natural History Museum of Utah](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/natural_history_museum) has one of the best dinosaur collections in the American West. Day trips to [Antelope Island](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/antelope_island), the [Bonneville Salt Flats](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/bonneville_salt_flats), and the [Red Butte Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/red_butte_garden_a) round out the options for anyone spending more than a weekend.

The [Great Salt Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/saltlakecity/great_salt_lake) to the west is eight times saltier than the ocean and shrinking rapidly due to water diversions — a environmental crisis playing out in slow motion that is worth understanding before you visit.
