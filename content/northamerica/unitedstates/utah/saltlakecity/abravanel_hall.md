Abravanel Hall on West Temple is the home of the Utah Symphony — a modern concert hall opened in 1979 and regarded as one of the acoustically finest in the American West. The hall is named after Maurice Abravanel, who conducted the Utah Symphony for 32 years and transformed it from a part-time regional orchestra into a nationally respected ensemble.

The Utah Symphony performs a full season from September through May. Tickets are reasonably priced by major-city standards, and the hall is pleasant to visit even if classical music is not usually your first choice.
