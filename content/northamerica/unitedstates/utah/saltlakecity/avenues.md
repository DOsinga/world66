The Avenues is the hilly residential neighbourhood north of downtown and the State Capitol, occupying the lower slopes of the Wasatch foothills. The streets run alphabetically (A Street, B Street, C Street...) and the Victorian and Craftsman houses on them form the most architecturally coherent historic neighbourhood in Salt Lake City.

The neighbourhood is primarily residential with few visitor-specific attractions, but it is the best area for a walking tour of late 19th-century domestic architecture. City Creek Canyon is accessible from the upper end of the Avenues. Several good neighbourhood restaurants and cafes are scattered along the lower streets.
