---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Utah's liquor laws, once the most restrictive in the US, have relaxed significantly since the 2000s. Salt Lake City now has a genuine bar scene centred on the downtown entertainment district around Main Street and the 9th and 9th neighbourhood. The craft beer scene is particularly strong — a legacy of the home-brewing culture that developed when commercial strong beer was harder to obtain.
