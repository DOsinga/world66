The Bonneville Salt Flats are about 90 miles west of Salt Lake City on I-80, the remains of the vast ancient Lake Bonneville that once covered most of Utah. The flats stretch for miles in every direction, absolutely flat, white, and surreal. You can walk out onto them from a roadside pullout; the surface feels like packed plaster.

Every August the Bonneville Speedway hosts Speed Week, when hundreds of home-built and professional land-speed vehicles attempt records on the marked course. Outside Speed Week the flats are open and empty — bring sunglasses, sunscreen, and something to drink.
