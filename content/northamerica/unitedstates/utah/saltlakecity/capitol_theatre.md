The Capitol Theatre on 200 South is a beautifully preserved 1913 Baroque Revival theatre that serves as the main performing arts venue for Ballet West, the Ririe-Woodbury Dance Company, and touring Broadway productions. The interior is particularly fine: plaster ornamentation, a painted ceiling, and opera boxes that give it the atmosphere of a proper European theatre.

Check the schedule before your visit — the theatre's programming is substantial and covers opera, ballet, and dance throughout the year. Tours of the building are occasionally offered.
