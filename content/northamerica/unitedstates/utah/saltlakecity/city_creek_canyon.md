City Creek Canyon begins just north of the State Capitol and offers a paved running and cycling trail through a narrow canyon with year-round water, cottonwoods, and wildlife. It is one of the few places you can walk from downtown Salt Lake City into genuine mountain terrain within 20 minutes.

The canyon road alternates between pedestrians-only and cyclists-allowed on different days (check the schedule). At the upper end, the trail connects with the Wasatch Mountain trail network. In spring and early summer the creek runs fast with snowmelt; in autumn the canyon turns gold.
