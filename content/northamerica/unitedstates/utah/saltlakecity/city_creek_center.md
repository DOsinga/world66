City Creek Center is a large upscale shopping mall opened in 2012 directly across from Temple Square, built on the former site of the old Crossroads and ZCMI malls. It houses Nordstrom and Macy's as anchors, along with over 100 specialty retailers on two levels connected by a retractable glass roof.

The mall is notable for its stream — City Creek runs through the centre in a channelled waterway — and for the quality of its construction and materials, which reflect the LDS Church's investment (the mall was developed by Property Reserve Inc., the Church's real estate arm). Luxury shopping by Salt Lake City standards.
