The LDS Conference Center, completed in 2000, is one of the largest single-room auditoriums in the world, seating 21,000 people for the Church's twice-yearly General Conference broadcasts. The building occupies an entire city block north of Temple Square and was designed to blend into the streetscape despite its enormous scale.

Free tours of the interior run daily and include access to the main hall, which is impressive for its sheer scale and the engineering required to create unobstructed sightlines for 21,000 seats. The rooftop garden, planted with native Utah species including a small waterfall feature, is open to the public and offers one of the best views of the Temple below — well worth the elevator ride.
