The Westerner on 3360 South is Salt Lake City's best country dance bar and one of the most unpretentious venues in the city. It has a large wooden dance floor, a mechanical bull, and live country music most nights. The crowd is genuinely local — this is not a tourist-oriented version of country culture but the real thing.

Lessons are available on weeknight evenings for those who want to learn two-step or line dancing before hitting the floor. It is loud, smoky in the best way, and a completely different Salt Lake City from the Temple Square blocks downtown.
