Big Cottonwood and Little Cottonwood Canyons cut into the Wasatch Mountains directly south of Salt Lake City. Together they contain four world-class ski resorts — Brighton, Solitude, Alta, and Snowbird — all within 45 minutes of downtown. The canyons are equally good in summer for hiking, with trailheads at every pullout giving access to high-altitude alpine terrain.

Little Cottonwood Canyon also contains the Snowflake cavern where the LDS Church stores its genealogical records in granite vaults — an oddly appropriate location for the world's most comprehensive archive of human lineage.
