Exchange Place is a two-block Historic District on 400 South containing the best early 20th-century commercial architecture in Salt Lake City. The buildings were developed between 1905 and 1920 by non-Mormon businessmen as an alternative financial centre to the LDS-aligned blocks downtown. The result is a coherent set of Chicago-style office buildings that now contain restaurants, galleries, and offices.

The district is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Walk through on a weekday when the ground-floor businesses are open; on weekends it is quiet.
