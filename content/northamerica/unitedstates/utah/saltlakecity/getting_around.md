---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Salt Lake City's street grid is one of the most logical in the United States: every address is given as a distance in blocks from Temple Square (e.g., "400 South 200 East" means four blocks south and two blocks east of the Temple). Once you grasp the system, navigation is easy.

The TRAX light rail system covers downtown, the University of Utah, and the airport, and connects to the FrontRunner commuter rail that runs north to Ogden and south to Provo. Buses fill in the gaps. For skiing, the ski buses running up the Cottonwood Canyons from downtown are far more convenient than driving, especially on powder days when traffic on the canyon roads is chaotic.

Cycling is possible on the flat valley streets, and the city has an improving network of bike lanes. The mountains are car-dependent unless you use the canyon buses.
