The Heber Valley Historic Railroad operates vintage steam and diesel trains on a scenic route through Heber Valley and Provo Canyon, about 45 minutes southeast of Salt Lake City. The line passes through mountain meadows, along the Provo River, and through Bridal Veil Falls canyon, with views of the Wasatch Mountains throughout.

Round trips from Heber City take two to three hours depending on the route. Special event trains run seasonally — the Halloween ghost train and the Christmas polar express are extremely popular and sell out well in advance. A worthwhile excursion for anyone with children or an interest in historic railway travel.
