The International Peace Gardens are a set of themed national gardens within Jordan Park. Each section was created with contributions from a different country, often using sculpture, planting, or small architectural features to represent that culture.

The gardens are best in warm months, when the plantings are full and the paths are pleasant for a slow walk. They are a good low-key stop away from the downtown temple and museum circuit.
