Liberty Park is Salt Lake City's largest urban park, a 100-acre rectangle in the central residential neighbourhoods south of downtown. It has a small amusement area, tennis courts, a pond with paddle boats, a spray ground for children, and an aviary with birds of prey. The park is the main outdoor gathering spot for the city's diverse south-side communities and is notably less LDS-dominated in character than the downtown blocks.

The Chase Home Museum of Utah Folk Arts, in a historic house at the park's northern edge, has a small but good collection of Utah folk art, quilts, and crafts. Admission is free.
