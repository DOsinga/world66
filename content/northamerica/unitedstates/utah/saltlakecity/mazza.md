Mazza on 9th South is a Lebanese-Middle Eastern restaurant with two Salt Lake locations that has been a local favourite for years. The mezze are the reason to go: hummus, muhammara, fattoush, and a long list of cold starters. The grilled meats are also good. It's the kind of restaurant where you order too many small plates and then realise you're not going to manage the main course.

The 9th and 9th location is the original and has the better neighbourhood atmosphere. Reservations recommended for Friday and Saturday evenings.
