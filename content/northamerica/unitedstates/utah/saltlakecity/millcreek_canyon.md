Millcreek Canyon cuts into the Wasatch Mountains east of Salt Lake City and is one of the area's reliable escapes for hiking, cycling, picnicking, and winter recreation. It feels close to the city but quickly becomes mountain country.

Trails range from short walks to longer climbs, and conditions change sharply by season. Check road access, snow, and dog rules before heading up, especially outside summer.
