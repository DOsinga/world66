The Museum of Church History and Art occupies a large building just west of Temple Square and houses the LDS Church's collection of artefacts, art, and documents relating to Mormon history from the church's founding in 1830 to the present. Admission is free.

The early history galleries are the most compelling: original manuscripts, pioneer artefacts, and material relating to the westward migration. The art galleries show LDS-themed work from the 19th century to the present, including some striking monumental paintings of frontier Utah. The museum is professionally run and intellectually honest about Mormon history, including some of its more complex episodes.
