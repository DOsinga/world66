The Downtown Farmers Market at Pioneer Park runs every Saturday morning from June through October. It is one of the best farmers markets in the Mountain West, with local farms selling vegetables, fruit, cheese, bread, honey, prepared food, and crafts. The market typically has over 150 vendors and draws significant crowds on good weather weekends.

Arrive early for the best produce selection; by 10am the most popular stalls have sold out of their best items. The market starts at 8am.
