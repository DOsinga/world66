Olympic Cauldron Park on the University of Utah campus preserves the flame cauldron from the 2002 Winter Olympics. The cauldron itself stands in a landscaped plaza and can be visited any time; interpretive panels tell the story of Salt Lake City's Olympic bid, the games themselves, and the doping scandal that preceded them.

The Rice-Eccles Stadium next door hosted the opening and closing ceremonies. The broader University of Utah campus is pleasant to walk through, and the park offers views toward the Wasatch Mountains. Free admission.
