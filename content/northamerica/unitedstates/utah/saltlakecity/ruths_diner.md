Ruth's Diner has been operating in Emigration Canyon since 1930, making it one of the oldest restaurants in Utah. The original restaurant was established by Ruth Evans in a converted trolley car at the mouth of the canyon; the current building has expanded considerably but retains the cosy canyon-side setting with a patio overlooking the creek.

The menu is American comfort food — eggs and pancakes in the morning, burgers and sandwiches through the day, heavier mains in the evening. The mile-high biscuits are the signature item and consistently well-regarded. The location makes it a natural stop before or after a cycle ride or hike up the canyon.
