The City and County Building at Washington Square is Salt Lake City's seat of municipal government and one of its finest pieces of architecture. Completed in 1894 in Romanesque Revival style, it sits in a landscaped park on 400 South — a deliberate statement of civic authority distinct from the LDS Temple Square blocks north of it.

The building is fully restored and open to the public during business hours. The main hall features marble floors, carved stonework, and stained glass. The surrounding Washington Square park is a popular gathering spot in summer, with occasional outdoor events and concerts.
