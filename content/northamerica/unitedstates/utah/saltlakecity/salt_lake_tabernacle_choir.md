The Mormon Tabernacle Choir and Orchestra at Temple Square performs a live radio and television broadcast every Sunday morning from the Tabernacle on Temple Square. The broadcast, called Music and the Spoken Word, has been running since 1929 — making it the longest-running continuous network broadcast in American radio history.

The public is welcome to attend. Doors open at 9am for the 9:30am broadcast; latecomers are not admitted after the broadcast begins. The choir has around 360 volunteer members and performs with a full professional orchestra. No tickets are required, but arrive early to get a seat.
