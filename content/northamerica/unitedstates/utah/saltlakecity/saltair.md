Saltair is a concert venue and ghost resort about 17 miles west of downtown on the south shore of the Great Salt Lake. The current building, a Moorish-style structure that replaced earlier resort facilities destroyed by fire and flooding, now operates primarily as a music venue hosting mid-size rock and alternative shows.

The real interest is the setting: the wide, flat expanse of the lake stretching to the horizon, the bizarre saltscapes at the water's edge, and the awareness that you are on the shore of a sea that has been retreating for decades. When driving out on I-80, the smell hits well before you arrive. Worth a stop if you are already heading toward the Bonneville Salt Flats or the airport.
