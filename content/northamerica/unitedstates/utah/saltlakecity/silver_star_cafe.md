Silver Star Cafe on South Temple is a local breakfast and lunch institution in a historic building near the Capitol. It draws a morning crowd of state workers, neighbourhood regulars, and visitors looking for straightforward American diner food done well: fluffy pancakes, proper eggs, good coffee. Nothing fancy, consistently good.

Arrive before 9am on weekdays to get a seat without waiting. Closed in the evenings.
