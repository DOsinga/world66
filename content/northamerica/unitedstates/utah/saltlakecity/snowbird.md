Snowbird is one of the defining ski areas of the Wasatch, tucked high in Little Cottonwood Canyon within easy reach of Salt Lake City. It is known for steep terrain, deep snow, the Aerial Tram, and a season that often runs longer than many other Utah resorts. For experienced skiers and snowboarders, it is one of the strongest mountain days available from the city.

The resort is not only a winter stop. Summer brings tram rides, mountain views, hiking access, and canyon scenery, though skiing is the reason most travelers know the name. Canyon traffic and weather closures can affect winter plans, so check conditions and allow more time than the map suggests after a storm.
