Snowbird is the most demanding of the four ski resorts in the Cottonwood Canyons, 25 miles southeast of Salt Lake City. Its combination of challenging terrain, deep powder snow, and proximity to the city has made it one of the best-known resorts in the American West. The vertical drop is 3,240 feet and the average snowfall is over 500 inches per year.

In summer the resort transforms into a mountain hiking and mountain biking destination, with a gondola providing uplift to the high terrain. The Snowbird tram runs year-round and the views from Hidden Peak are among the best accessible by cable car in Utah.
