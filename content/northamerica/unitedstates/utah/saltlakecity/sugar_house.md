Sugar House is the most established of Salt Lake City's residential neighbourhoods outside downtown — a former independent town absorbed by the city in the late 19th century. The commercial strip along 2100 South has a good selection of restaurants, coffee shops, and bars, along with the large Sugar House Park, which has a lake and trails popular with joggers and dog walkers.

The neighbourhood is popular with young professionals and university students and has a more mixed, secular character than some other parts of the city. Sugar House Brewing is a well-regarded local brewery here.
