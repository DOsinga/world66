Sugar House Park is a large open park in the Sugar House neighbourhood, built on the site of a former state prison. It has a small lake, walking and running paths, a skateboard park, and open grass fields used for soccer, frisbee, and summer concerts. The park is one of the main outdoor recreational spaces for the south side of the city.

The park's slight elevation gives reasonable views of both the Wasatch Mountains to the east and the Oquirrh Mountains to the west — on a clear day you can see the entirety of the Salt Lake Valley from the central rise.
