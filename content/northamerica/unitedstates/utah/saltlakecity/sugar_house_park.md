Sugar House Park is one of Salt Lake City's better everyday parks, spread across rolling ground below the Wasatch Mountains. Locals come for running loops, picnics, dog walks, and wide-open views that remind you how close the city sits to the foothills.

It is especially good if you are staying east or south of downtown and want a low-key outdoor stop. In winter the park's slopes become a popular sledding ground; in warmer months it is more about shade, grass, and a slower neighbourhood rhythm.
