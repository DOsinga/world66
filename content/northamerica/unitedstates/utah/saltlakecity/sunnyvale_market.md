Sunnyvale Market is a community market in Salt Lake City where refugee farmers sell produce, including vegetables and herbs that can be hard to find elsewhere in the city. It is run with support from the International Rescue Committee and has a stronger local character than a routine grocery stop.

It is most worthwhile for visitors who like markets, cooking, or immigrant food cultures. Check the current schedule before going, as market days and seasons can change.
