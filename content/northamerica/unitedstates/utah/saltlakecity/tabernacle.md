The Salt Lake Tabernacle is the large domed building on Temple Square that serves as the broadcast home of the Mormon Tabernacle Choir. Built between 1864 and 1867, it is an architectural achievement: a 250-foot elliptical roof supported by 44 sandstone buttresses, spanning a room that holds 6,000 people with no interior columns obstructing the view.

The acoustics are extraordinary — a hallmark of the building that has been demonstrated for visitors for over a century. Free public organ recitals run at lunchtime on most weekdays. The Tabernacle Choir and Orchestra at Temple Square performs Sunday morning broadcasts that the public can attend; tickets are not required but seats fill early.
