Takashi on Pierpont Avenue is consistently rated the best sushi restaurant in Utah and has been for two decades. Chef Takashi Gibo focuses on traditional nigiri and omakase — the fish is flown in from Japanese and American coastal markets and the selection changes with availability. It is not cheap, but it is a genuine Japanese sushi counter in a state more famous for ski resorts than food.

Reservations are recommended for dinner. The bar seats are available for walk-ins and give a view of the chefs working. Lunch is considerably less expensive and a good time to try the restaurant without a dinner bill.
