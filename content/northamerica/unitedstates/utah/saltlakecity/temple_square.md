Temple Square is the ten-acre walled complex at the heart of downtown Salt Lake City, containing the Salt Lake Temple, the Tabernacle, two visitor centres, and a range of historic buildings. It is the world headquarters of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and the most visited tourist attraction in Utah.

The Gothic-style temple with its distinctive six spires dominates the skyline for blocks around. Non-members cannot enter the temple itself, but the grounds are freely open, and the visitor centres offer an extensive introduction to Mormon history and belief. Volunteer guides — almost invariably young women from around the world — will offer a free tour; they are knowledgeable and unfailingly welcoming. Even visitors with no interest in religion will find the architecture and the sheer scale of the place worth an hour of their time.
