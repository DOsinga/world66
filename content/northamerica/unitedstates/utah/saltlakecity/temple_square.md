Temple Square is the 10-acre block at the centre of Salt Lake City that contains the Salt Lake Temple, the Tabernacle, the Assembly Hall, two visitor centres, and several historic monuments. It is the headquarters and spiritual home of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints and one of the most visited tourist sites in the American West.

The Salt Lake Temple itself is open only to LDS members, but the grounds, the Tabernacle, and the visitor centres are open to everyone and free. The Tabernacle is worth entering for the architecture alone: an enormous elliptical roof supported without interior columns, designed with acoustics so precise that a pin dropped at the pulpit is audible in the back row. The famous Mormon Tabernacle Choir rehearses here and broadcasts weekly — open sessions are popular.
