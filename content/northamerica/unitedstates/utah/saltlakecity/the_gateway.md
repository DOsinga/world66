The Gateway is an open-air shopping and entertainment district in the western part of downtown, developed on the site of the former Union Pacific Railroad yards. The complex opened in 2001 and includes retail, restaurants, and Clark Planetarium, centred on an outdoor promenade with a dancing fountain popular with families in summer.

The district has seen some retail turnover over the years but remains a lively part of downtown, particularly in the evenings. The Olympic Legacy Plaza at the centre commemorates the 2002 Winter Olympics with the original Olympic cauldron on display. The area connects easily to the Gateway TRAX light rail station.
