title: The Deuel Log Home
type: poi
---

Built in 1847, the Deuel Log Home is one of the first log homes constructed in the Salt Lake Valley after the Mormon pioneers arrived. It now stands in a small landscaped area across from Temple Square, between the Museum of Church History and Art and the Family History Library.

The home is compact and modest — one room, chinked logs, a low doorway — and gives a concrete sense of the conditions in which the first settlers lived. Standing next to the towers and spires of the Temple Square complex, the contrast is striking.
