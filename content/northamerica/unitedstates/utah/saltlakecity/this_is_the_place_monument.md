The This Is The Place Monument stands at the mouth of Emigration Canyon, on the spot where Brigham Young and the first group of Mormon pioneers emerged from the canyon and first saw the Salt Lake Valley in July 1847. The 60-foot bronze monument, erected in 1947 for the centennial of the pioneer arrival, depicts Young, Heber C. Kimball, and Wilford Woodruff looking out over the valley they chose to settle.

The surrounding Heritage Park contains a collection of reconstructed pioneer-era buildings staffed by costumed interpreters during the summer months, along with a small train and children's activities. The monument itself is free to view from the parking area; the heritage village charges admission. The setting at the canyon entrance, with the mountains rising behind and the city spread out below, is genuinely dramatic.
