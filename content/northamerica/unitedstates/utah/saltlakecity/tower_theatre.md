The Tower Theatre is Salt Lake City's oldest independent cinema, operating since 1928 in a 1920s-era building in the 9th and 9th neighbourhood. The theatre shows independent, foreign, and documentary films alongside repertory screenings, and has survived as an independent operation through decades when most of its counterparts closed.

The building retains much of its original interior character, and the surrounding 9th and 9th neighbourhood — with its independent restaurants, coffee shops, and bookstores — makes it a pleasant evening destination. The theatre is a short walk or bus ride from downtown.
