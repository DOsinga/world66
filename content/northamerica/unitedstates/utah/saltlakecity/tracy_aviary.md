Tracy Aviary is a bird-focused zoo and conservation centre inside Liberty Park, with exhibits ranging from local species to tropical and wetland birds. Its scale is manageable, and the setting makes it easy to combine with a walk through the park.

It is one of Salt Lake City's better family attractions away from Temple Square. Bird shows, keeper talks and shaded paths make a visit more engaging than simply moving from enclosure to enclosure.
