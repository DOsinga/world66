Tracy Aviary in Liberty Park is one of the oldest and largest free-standing aviaries in the United States, with over 400 birds representing 135 species. The collection includes flamingos, bald eagles, hornbills, parrots, condors, and dozens of other species in naturalistic outdoor enclosures.

A modest admission is charged. The aviary is well maintained and the animals are in good condition. It is a genuine ornithological resource rather than a mere attraction — the aviary participates in species conservation programmes for endangered birds. A good option for families or anyone with a serious interest in birds.
