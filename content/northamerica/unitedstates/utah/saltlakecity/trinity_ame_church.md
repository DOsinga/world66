Trinity A.M.E. Church at 239 East 600 South is a historic African Methodist Episcopal congregation in a red-brick building listed on the National Register of Historic Places. The church was founded in 1869 by Black soldiers garrisoned at Fort Douglas and has served the African American community in Salt Lake City ever since.

The building is an active church, not a museum, but it is an important landmark in a city whose history is overwhelmingly told through a Mormon lens. The church is open to visitors who contact them in advance, and Sunday services are public.
