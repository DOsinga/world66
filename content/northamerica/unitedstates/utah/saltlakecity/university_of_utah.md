The University of Utah campus sits on a bench of land at the eastern edge of the city, with the Wasatch Mountains rising directly behind it. It is the oldest university in Utah, founded in 1850, and the largest in the state with about 33,000 students. The campus hosts several good museums, Red Butte Garden, the Olympic Cauldron, and the stadium that hosted the 2002 Winter Olympics opening and closing ceremonies.

A pleasant area to spend a morning: the museums are excellent, the architecture is varied (from 19th-century sandstone to postmodern glass), and the views from the upper campus are among the best in the city.
