The Delta Center is the home arena of the Utah Jazz NBA basketball team and the main concert and event venue in Salt Lake City. It seats over 18,000 and hosts 41 Jazz home games each season plus major touring concerts. If you are in the city during basketball season (October through April), catching a Jazz game is a genuinely good evening out — the team has a strong fan base and the arena atmosphere is lively.

The arena is in the western downtown, close to TRAX connections and several pre-game bars and restaurants. Tickets are available on the team website or at the box office.
