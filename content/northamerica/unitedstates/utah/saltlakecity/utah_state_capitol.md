The Utah State Capitol sits on a prominence north of Temple Square, its Corinthian-columned facade and copper-clad dome visible from much of the city. Completed in 1916, it is one of the best-preserved Beaux-Arts capitols in the American West. Free guided tours run daily and cover the rotunda, the legislative chambers, and the restored murals.

The grounds around the Capitol offer good views over the city toward the Wasatch Mountains. The building is particularly striking at night when lit from below. The nearby Capitol Visitor Center provides maps and information.
