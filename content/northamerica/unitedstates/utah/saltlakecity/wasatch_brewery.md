Wasatch Brewery, founded in 1986, is one of Utah's oldest craft breweries, known as much for its irreverent attitude toward the state's liquor laws as for its beer. The State Street brewpub is the flagship location, serving a full range of beers including the flagship Polygamy Porter and Devastator double bock, along with a standard pub menu.

Utah's quirky liquor regulations have historically limited beer to 3.2% ABV in supermarkets and restaurants, making full-strength craft beer available only through licensed bars and state liquor stores. Wasatch has leaned into this with deliberate mockery on its labels and marketing, and the brewpub has become a gathering point for locals who appreciate both the beer and the attitude.
