Weller Book Works at 607 Trolley Square is the best independent bookshop in Salt Lake City, with an excellent general stock covering literature, history, science, and local interest. The staff are knowledgeable and the selection is curated rather than exhaustive — you will find things you want to buy rather than an overwhelming warehouse of books.

The shop has been operating in various forms for decades and is a genuine community institution. A good place to pick up books on Utah and Great Basin history, or to find out what local writers are producing.
