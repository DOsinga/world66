Jordan Landing in West Jordan, about 20 minutes south of downtown Salt Lake City, is one of the largest open-air retail centres in Utah, anchored by a cinema, major chain stores, and a good selection of restaurants. It draws shoppers from across the south valley.

For visitors the main interest is the practical combination of stores in a single location, and the proximity to Gardner Village and Wheeler Farm if you are making a day of the south valley. The area is car-dependent but easily reached via State Route 154 from I-15.
