Wheeler Historic Farm in the southern Salt Lake Valley is a 75-acre working farm preserved as a living history museum. The farm dates from the 1890s and demonstrates late 19th-century agricultural life in Utah: animals, farm equipment, historic buildings, and staff in period dress. It is run by Salt Lake County and admission is free; individual activities like horse-drawn wagon rides charge separately.

A low-key, unpretentious destination that works well for families with young children. The farm holds seasonal events including a well-regarded Halloween celebration and a fall harvest festival.
