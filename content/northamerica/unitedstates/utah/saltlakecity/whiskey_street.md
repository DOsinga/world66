Whiskey Street on Main Street is one of downtown Salt Lake City's most popular bars, with a long selection of American whiskeys, craft beers, and cocktails. The bar occupies a large space in an old commercial building and draws a mixed crowd of locals, visitors, and off-duty state workers.

Utah liquor laws are less restrictive than they once were — the "Zion curtain" rule requiring bartenders to mix drinks out of sight was repealed in 2017 — but some quirks remain, including restrictions on how spirits are sold in some establishments. Whiskey Street navigates these without difficulty and is a good starting point for an evening in the downtown entertainment district.
