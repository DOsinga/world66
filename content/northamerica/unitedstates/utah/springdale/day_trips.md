[Bryce Canyon](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/bryce_canyon) is the obvious extension to a Zion trip — an hour and a half east through the Mount Carmel Tunnel, with a wildly different landscape of pink-and-white hoodoos. Plan a long day or, better, an overnight. [Kanab](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/kanab) is sixty miles east, smaller and quieter than Springdale, and the base for permits to the Wave at Coyote Buttes. [St George](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/st_george) is the nearest sizeable city, useful for resupplies and a different look at the southern Utah landscape on the way to or from Las Vegas.
