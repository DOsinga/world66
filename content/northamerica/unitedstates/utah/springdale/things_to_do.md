---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Springdale exists for Zion. The park entrance is a five-minute walk from the centre of town and the shuttle bus that ferries visitors up the canyon stops every few hundred metres along the main street. Plan at least two full days; the famous hikes — Angels Landing, the Narrows, Observation Point — each take a hard half-day at minimum. The town itself has a small art scene worth a stroll between hikes.
