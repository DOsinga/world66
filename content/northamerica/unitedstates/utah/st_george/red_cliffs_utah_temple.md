Red Cliffs Utah Temple is the second Latter-day Saint temple in St. George, standing as a bright landmark on the eastern side of the city. The temple itself is reserved for church members with a temple recommend, but visitors can still walk the landscaped grounds.

It is best treated as a short architectural and cultural stop, especially for travellers interested in the role of the LDS Church in southern Utah. The visitor-facing part is the setting and exterior rather than the worship spaces.
