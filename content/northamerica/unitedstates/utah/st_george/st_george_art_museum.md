St. George Art Museum is a small downtown museum housed in a sturdy former sugar beet seed storage
building. Its exhibitions usually focus on regional artists, Utah subjects, and rotating shows
rather than a permanent blockbuster collection.

It is a pleasant indoor stop when desert heat or weather makes outdoor plans less appealing. Pair it
with the historic downtown, Brigham Young Winter Home, or nearby cafes for a slower look at St.
George beyond red-rock scenery.
