The St. George Dinosaur Discovery Site is one of the city’s strongest family and science stops. Its centrepiece is a set of Jurassic dinosaur tracks preserved where they were found, turning the museum into both an exhibit hall and a protected fossil site.

The displays explain the ancient lakeshore environment that left the tracks behind, with enough hands-on material to keep children engaged. It is a good reminder that southern Utah’s deep-time story is not only in its cliffs and canyons, but also under ordinary-looking ground at the edge of town.
