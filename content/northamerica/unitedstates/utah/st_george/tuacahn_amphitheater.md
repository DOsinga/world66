Tuacahn Amphitheater turns southern Utah scenery into part of the performance. The stage sits against red cliffs near Snow Canyon, so a musical or concert here begins with the setting long before the lights come up.

It is one of the better evening plans around St. George, especially in the warmer months when the outdoor season is running. Book ahead for popular shows and bring a layer; the desert can cool quickly after sunset.
