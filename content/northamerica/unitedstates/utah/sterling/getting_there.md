---
title: Getting there
type: section
---

Sterling sits on **US-89** in central Utah, roughly 160km south of Provo and 250km south of Salt Lake City. The drive from Salt Lake City takes about two and a half hours on I-15 south to Nephi, then US-89 south through the valley.

There is no public transport to Sterling or anywhere nearby. A car is essential; there is simply no other practical way to reach or leave the village.

The nearest commercial airport is **Salt Lake City International (SLC)**, approximately 2.5 hours north by car. Provo Airport (PVU) is closer at around 90 minutes but has very limited commercial service.

Fuel and basic services are available in **Manti**, 10km north on US-89. There is nothing to buy in Sterling itself.
