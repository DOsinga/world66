Torrey is a tiny town at the western entrance to [Capitol Reef National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/torrey/capitol_reef_national_park), one of Utah's least-visited but most rewarding national parks. The setting is spectacular — red rock cliffs rise directly behind the town, and the Waterpocket Fold stretches for a hundred miles to the south.

The town itself is just a handful of lodges, a couple of restaurants, and a gas station, but that is part of the appeal. Torrey is the quiet alternative to the more developed gateway towns around Zion and Arches. From here you can drive the [Capitol Reef Scenic Drive](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/torrey/capitol_reef_scenic_drive), hike to [Hickman Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/torrey/hickman_bridge) natural arch, pick fruit at the [Gifford Homestead](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/torrey/gifford_homestead) orchard, or head south on the [Burr Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/torrey/burr_trail) for some of the most dramatic desert scenery in the American West. The strange moonscape of [Factory Butte](/northamerica/unitedstates/utah/torrey/factory_butte) is 30 miles to the north-east and worth a detour for its sheer otherworldliness.

Torrey sits at 1,900 metres and has a dark-sky designation — light pollution is minimal and the Milky Way is visible on clear nights. The Heritage Starfest, held each September, brings astronomers and telescopes to the area for a weekend of public stargazing events.
