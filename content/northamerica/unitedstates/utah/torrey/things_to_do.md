---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Almost everything of note near Torrey sits inside Capitol Reef National Park, 11 miles east via Highway 24. The town itself is a handful of buildings, but it provides the services — gas, food, lodging — that make an extended stay in this remote corner of Utah practical. The area rewards those who stay longer: Capitol Reef is large, varied, and far less crowded than Zion or Arches.
