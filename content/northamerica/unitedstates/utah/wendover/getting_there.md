title: Getting There
type: section
---

Wendover is 120 miles west of Salt Lake City on Interstate 80, about 90 minutes by car. There is no public transport. The drive crosses the full width of the Bonneville Salt Flats; watch for the Tree of Utah on the right side about 25 miles before town.

West Wendover airport (ENV) has scheduled services from Salt Lake City with smaller regional carriers, though flights are infrequent. Most visitors drive.
