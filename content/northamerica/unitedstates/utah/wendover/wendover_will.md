Wendover Will is a giant neon cowboy sign that has become the most recognizable roadside landmark of
Wendover and West Wendover. Built as casino-era advertising and later preserved as a civic symbol,
it captures the border-town mix of highway travel, gambling, salt-flat distance, and mid-century
showmanship.

It is a quick stop, but a memorable one: the kind of oversized western sign that makes most sense
after hours of desert driving. Photographers should try to see it after dark, when the neon gives
the figure its full personality.
