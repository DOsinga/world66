Angels Landing is Zion's celebrity hike, and for once the fame is justified. The route climbs from the Grotto up Walter's Wiggles to Scout Lookout, then follows a narrow sandstone fin with fixed chains and sheer drops on both sides.

It is spectacular, exposed, and not something to attempt casually. A permit is required for the chained section, and the best decision some visitors make is to stop at Scout Lookout, enjoy the view, and leave the final ridge to those who are steady with heights.
