The Amtrak station is a small red-brick building on Depot Street, still serving the daily Vermonter on its run between Washington, DC and St Albans. It is in many ways the village's anchor — most visitors arrive on the train.

In the warmer months, the Green Mountain Railroad runs the **Green Mountain Flyer**, an excursion train, from the same platform north to Chester through some of the prettiest farmland in southern Vermont. The two-and-a-half-hour round trip uses restored 1930s coaches. Schedules vary by season; the railroad's website has current departures.
