---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Bellows Falls is unusual for a Vermont village in that it is easy to reach without a car. **Amtrak's Vermonter** stops at the downtown station once a day in each direction, connecting Bellows Falls to New York, New Haven, Springfield, Hartford and Brattleboro to the south and to Montpelier and Burlington to the north.

By road, **Interstate 91** passes a mile west of the village; the closest exits are 5 and 6. From [Brattleboro](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/brattleboro) it is a 30-minute drive north up Route 5 along the Connecticut River. Boston is two and a half hours; New York City is four.
