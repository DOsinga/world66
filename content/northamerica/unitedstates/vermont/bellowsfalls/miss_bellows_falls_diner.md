A genuine 1940s railcar diner parked at 90 Rockingham Street, Miss Bellows Falls is on the National Register and still serves breakfast and lunch six days a week. The interior is the original wood, marble, and tile, with a counter, stools, and a row of booths.

The food is short-order American — eggs, hash, burgers, the daily blue-plate special — and the value is honest. Coffee is bottomless and the staff have seen everything. Cash is appreciated.
