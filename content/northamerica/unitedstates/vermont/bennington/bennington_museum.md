Bennington Museum is the town's best indoor stop, with collections that range across Vermont art, furniture, pottery, and local history. Its best-known strength is Grandma Moses, the folk painter who lived nearby and turned rural memory into bright, deceptively simple scenes.

The museum is also useful because it grounds Bennington's attractive streets in a deeper regional story. You leave with a better sense of the area's Revolutionary War politics, industrial craft, and the stubborn local identity that still marks southern Vermont.
