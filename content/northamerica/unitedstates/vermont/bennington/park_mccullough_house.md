Park-McCullough House is a 35-room Victorian mansion in North Bennington, built in the 1860s for a family connected to railroads, politics, and the wider industrial wealth of the era. It is grand without being anonymous, full of the kind of domestic detail that makes historic houses worth touring.

The setting is part of the appeal: lawns, porches, and outbuildings give the place a slower rhythm than downtown Bennington. Check seasonal hours before going; this is not a museum you can assume will be open every day.
