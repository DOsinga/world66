The Robert Frost Stone House Museum sits north of Bennington in Shaftsbury, close enough to make an easy literary detour. Frost lived here in the 1920s, kept orchards, and wrote work that helped fix his public image as the poet of rural New England.

The house is modest, which helps. Rather than turning Frost into a statue, it shows the landscape, rooms, and working farm context behind the poems. The grounds are worth a walk even if you are only mildly literary.
