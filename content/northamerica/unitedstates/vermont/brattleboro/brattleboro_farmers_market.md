The Brattleboro Farmers' Market is more than a few tables of vegetables; in season it becomes a Saturday gathering place for Windham County produce, prepared food, crafts, flowers, and musicians. Its West Brattleboro site on Western Avenue makes it easy to combine with a slow morning rather than a rushed errand.

It is most useful for travellers renting a place with a kitchen, but even day visitors can make a meal from bakery stalls, snacks, and seasonal fruit. Check the season before going: the outdoor market is a warm-month fixture, and winter arrangements may differ.
