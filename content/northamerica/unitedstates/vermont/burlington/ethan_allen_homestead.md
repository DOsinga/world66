The Ethan Allen Homestead preserves the final home of Vermont Revolutionary War figure Ethan Allen in Burlington's Intervale. Guided visits focus on Allen's life, the early settlement of Vermont, and the farm landscape around the Winooski River, making it a useful historic stop beyond the downtown waterfront.
