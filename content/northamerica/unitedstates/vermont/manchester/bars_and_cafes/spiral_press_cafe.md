Spiral Press is the closest thing Manchester has to a proper independent coffee shop — a bright, relaxed spot in Manchester Center with decent espresso, locally baked pastries, and the kind of comfortable seating that encourages lingering. It doubles as a bookshop, stocking a thoughtful selection of Vermont-focused titles and general interest books alongside the usual café fare. A good place to start the morning before heading to Hildene or out on the trails.
