A casual sports bar in Manchester Center that serves as the town's closest equivalent to a local pub. The beer selection runs to Vermont craft ales and national standards on tap, the food is straightforward bar fare, and the televisions are always on. It attracts a younger, more local crowd than the hotel bars. Good for a post-hike beer or a low-key evening when the fancier restaurants feel like too much.
