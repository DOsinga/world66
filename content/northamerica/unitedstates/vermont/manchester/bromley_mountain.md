Bromley Mountain is one of the main outdoor draws near Manchester, with skiing in winter and mountain rides, alpine slides, and family activities in warmer months. It is smaller and easier-going than some Vermont resorts, which is often exactly the attraction.

For travellers based in Manchester, Bromley is a practical mountain day without a long drive. In winter it suits families and mixed-ability groups; in summer it gives children and casual adventurers something more active than outlet shopping and historic-house touring.
