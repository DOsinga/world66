---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Manchester eats well for a small Vermont town. The restaurant scene clusters around Manchester Center and Route 7A, with a mix of New England tavern food, upscale farm-to-table dining, and casual spots that cater to the hiking and ski crowd. Locally sourced ingredients — Vermont cheese, maple syrup, local beef — turn up throughout. Book ahead for dinner on autumn weekends and around peak ski season.
