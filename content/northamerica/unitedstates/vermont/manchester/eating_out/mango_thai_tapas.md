Mango Thai is a reliable mid-range option in Manchester Center that stands out from the town's heavily American-focused restaurant scene. The menu runs to curries, noodle dishes, and small plates with enough heat for those who want it, and the kitchen handles takeaway orders well for picnic lunches to take up the mountain. It tends to fill up on busy weekends — arrive early or phone ahead.
