A Manchester institution with a name that raises eyebrows but earns its place with consistent, generous cooking. The menu leans eclectic — Vermont comfort food sitting alongside more ambitious dishes — and the prices are reasonable by Manchester standards. The tavern side is livelier and more casual than the main dining room, and the local crowd keeps things grounded. Good for a relaxed dinner without the formality or price tag of the Reluctant Panther.
