The Museum of the Creative Process is a small art museum and research center devoted to creativity, psychology, and the way artists develop ideas. Its collection includes contemporary and international art, with exhibits that connect the works to broader questions about imagination and problem-solving.

It is a quieter stop than Manchester's larger historic attractions, but a useful one for visitors interested in art, creative practice, or unusual private collections.
