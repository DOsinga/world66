Orvis, the fly fishing and sporting goods company, has been headquartered in Manchester since 1856, and their flagship store and fly fishing school are a destination in themselves. The school runs casting lessons and guided fishing trips on the Battenkill River — lessons suit complete beginners as well as experienced anglers looking to refine technique. Half-day and full-day programmes are available; book well ahead in summer.

The flagship store on Route 7A stocks the full Orvis line alongside a serious selection of fishing equipment, outdoor clothing, and dog accessories. Even if you're not buying, it's worth stepping in to see the range. The Battenkill out back — one of Vermont's most beautiful rivers — is visible from the store's rear windows and accessible via a short path. The river still holds wild brown trout, and catch-and-release fishing is permitted on certain stretches.
