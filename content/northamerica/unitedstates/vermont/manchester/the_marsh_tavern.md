The Marsh Tavern is the main dining room at the Equinox Resort and the most accessible of the hotel's restaurants — a wood-panelled New England tavern serving straightforward American food: chowder, burgers, steaks, and good Vermont cheddar on everything. The atmosphere is warm and unhurried, the portions are generous, and the setting inside the historic Equinox building gives it a sense of occasion that the menu alone wouldn't justify. A solid choice for lunch after a morning at Hildene or the fly fishing museum.
