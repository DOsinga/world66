---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Manchester's main draws are Hildene — the Lincoln family summer home, one of the finest historic house museums in New England — and the Green Mountain landscape that surrounds the town. The outlet shopping along Route 7A is a draw for many visitors, but the town repays those who look beyond the boutiques. The American Museum of Fly Fishing is one of the quirkier pleasures, and the walk up Equinox Mountain on a clear day offers views over four states.
