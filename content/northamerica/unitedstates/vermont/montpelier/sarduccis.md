Sarducci's is the long-running Italian restaurant on the riverside end of Main Street, with a wood-fired oven turning out genuinely good pizza and a respectable list of regional Italian wines. The dining room overlooks the Winooski; in summer the terrace is the prime outdoor table in town.

Pizza, pasta, and a chalkboard of nightly specials. Family-run, reliable, not glamorous. Book ahead at weekends.
