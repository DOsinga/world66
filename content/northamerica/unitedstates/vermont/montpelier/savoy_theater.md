The Savoy is Montpelier's small independent cinema, with a 130-seat room and a programme that leans toward foreign, alternative, and art-house films. It is also tied into the Green Mountain Film Festival, which gives the place a bigger cultural role than its size suggests.

Come here when you want an evening indoors that still feels local. The scale is modest, but that is part of the charm.
