Reading is a small Vermont town with a population of around 700, situated in the eastern central part of the state on Route 106 between Woodstock and Springfield. It is quiet and agricultural, with the unhurried feel of rural southern Vermont.

The main point of interest is [Jenne Farm](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/reading/jenne_farm), which sits on a hillside outside town and is said to be among the most photographed farms in New England. The classic red barns set against rolling hills and autumn foliage draw photographers and painters, particularly in October. The [South Reading Schoolhouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/reading/south_reading_schoolhouse), a well-preserved 19th-century one-room school, represents the kind of rural New England heritage that Reading preserves quietly and without fuss.
