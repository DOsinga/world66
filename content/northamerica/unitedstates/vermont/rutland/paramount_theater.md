The Paramount Theatre is Rutland's cultural anchor: a restored 1914 movie palace on Merchants Row that hosts live music, comedy, film screenings, and community events through the year. The interior was restored with care in the early 2000s and retains its atmospheric gilded plasterwork and the character of a small-city venue that people actually use rather than simply preserve.

Check the schedule before visiting — the building is worth seeing for its architecture but the experience is better with a show. The theatre seats around 800 and programmes range from national touring acts to local theatre groups to classic film screenings. The downtown location makes it easy to combine with dinner at one of the nearby restaurants.
