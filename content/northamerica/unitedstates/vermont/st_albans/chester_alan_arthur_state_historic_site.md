This state historic site sits east of St. Albans in Fairfield, where Chester A. Arthur, the 21st president of the United States, was born in 1829. The main structure is a 1950s reconstruction based on an old photograph of the family house, with a monument marking the traditional birthplace site.

It is a modest stop, but an interesting one if you like presidential history away from the usual big-city houses. The surrounding countryside also gives a good feel for rural Franklin County.
