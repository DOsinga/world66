---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Stowe has activities for every season. In winter, [Stowe Mountain Resort](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/stowe/stowe_mountain_resort) on Mount Mansfield dominates, but the [Trapp Family Lodge](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/stowe/trapp_family_lodge) cross-country trail network is a quieter alternative. In summer and fall, hiking on [Mount Mansfield](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/stowe/mount_mansfield) is the main draw, along with the [Stowe Recreation Path](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/stowe/stowe_recreation_path) that follows the river through the village. The [Vermont Ski and Snowboard Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/stowe/vermont_ski_museum) in the village is worth an hour any time of year. The [Gold Brook Covered Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/stowe/emilys_bridge) — also known as Emily's Bridge — is a short drive from the village and one of the most photographed covered bridges in New England.
