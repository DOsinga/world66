Artisans' Gallery is a good Waitsfield stop because it reflects the valley's creative side without feeling precious. The cooperative represents more than 170 Vermont artists, with ceramics, jewellery, prints, woodwork, textiles, and small pieces that travel better than most souvenirs.

It sits in an 1830s storefront in the historic village, so it pairs naturally with the covered bridge and a short wander around Bridge Street.
