The Waitsfield Farmers Market gathers local farms, bakers, makers, and prepared-food stalls on the Mad River Green. It runs on Saturdays from late spring into autumn, and it is one of the easiest ways to see the valley's weekend rhythm.

Come hungry and unhurried. The market is as much a social ritual as a place to buy vegetables, with music, coffee, cheese, baked goods, and plenty of people who clearly know each other.
