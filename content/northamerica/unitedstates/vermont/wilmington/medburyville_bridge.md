About five kilometres west of the village on Route 9, the Medburyville Bridge is a historic steel through-truss spanning the Deerfield River — one of the older surviving bridges of its type in southern Vermont, listed on the National Register. Photogenic in any season, especially with autumn foliage reflecting in the river below.

Pull off into the small picnic area on the south side for the best view.
