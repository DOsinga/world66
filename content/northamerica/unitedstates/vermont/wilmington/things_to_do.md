---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The Deerfield Valley around Wilmington is mostly about the outdoors — skiing at Mount Snow, hiking in the Green Mountain National Forest, paddling on Lake Whitingham, and driving the foliage roads in October. In town, the historic village centre has a National Register-listed core of 19th-century buildings, several covered bridges within a few miles, and Vermont's small but engaging Southern Vermont Natural History Museum on Hogback Mountain.
