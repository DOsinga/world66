Woodstock is one of Vermont's prettiest villages, and it knows it. The green, covered bridge, white steeples, old inns, and tidy brick storefronts can feel almost too composed, but the surrounding hills keep the place from becoming a stage set.

The best reason to linger is that Woodstock puts town and countryside unusually close together. You can walk from the village to [Marsh-Billings-Rockefeller National Historical Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/woodstock/marsh_billings_rockefeller), climb [Mount Tom](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/woodstock/mount_tom), tour [Billings Farm](/northamerica/unitedstates/vermont/woodstock/billings_farm), and be back on the green for coffee without getting in the car.

It is not cheap, especially during fall foliage weekends, and it can feel crowded for such a small place. Come midweek if you can. Woodstock rewards slow walking, back roads, and early mornings before the day-trippers arrive from Route 4.
