Jones Point Park is a riverside park at the south end of Old Town Alexandria, with views of the Woodrow Wilson Bridge and the Potomac. Its small lighthouse and boundary marker for the District of Columbia add a layer of local history.

The park is also a good place to pause on a waterfront walk. Displays explain the shipyard history of the site, including its role during World War I.
