The National Inventors Hall of Fame and Museum is a free museum inside the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office headquarters. Exhibits introduce hundreds of inductees whose inventions shaped everyday life, industry, medicine, and communications.

It is a compact stop rather than a full afternoon museum, but it pairs well with Old Town Alexandria or nearby patent office business. The best reason to visit is the focus on invention as a human story, not just as a list of patents.
