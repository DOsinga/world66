The Old Presbyterian Meeting House is one of Old Town Alexandria's important colonial-era religious sites. Its sanctuary and churchyard connect the city's eighteenth-century mercantile community with the Revolution-era and early national history that still shapes the surrounding streets.

It is a compact stop, but a worthwhile one on a self-guided walk through Old Town. The churchyard, brick architecture, and proximity to other historic houses make it easy to fold into a broader Alexandria history route.
