River Farm is a historic Potomac-side estate now used as the headquarters of the American Horticultural Society. Visitors come mainly for the public gardens and river views rather than the house itself.

It works well as a quiet garden stop near Mount Vernon and Alexandria, especially when flowers are in season. Check current opening hours before making a special trip.
