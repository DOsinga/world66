Tall Ship Providence brings a bit of Revolutionary War theatre to Alexandria's waterfront. The vessel is a replica of the sloop Providence, associated with John Paul Jones, and it sits close enough to Old Town's brick warehouses and riverfront walk to feel part of the historic harbour story.

Visits depend on the season and programme, with dockside interpretation, ticketed sails, and special events at different times of year. It is most appealing if you are already walking the waterfront and want a hands-on counterpoint to Alexandria's house museums and colonial streets.
