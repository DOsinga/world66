The Torpedo Factory Art Center turns a former munitions depot on Alexandria's waterfront into a cluster of working studios and galleries. Visitors can walk through the building, see local artists at work, and browse painting, ceramics, photography, jewelry, and other media in a setting that still carries traces of its industrial past.
