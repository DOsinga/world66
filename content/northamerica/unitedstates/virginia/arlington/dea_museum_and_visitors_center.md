The DEA Museum and Visitors Center explains the work of the Drug Enforcement Administration and the history of illegal drug trafficking, law enforcement, and drug policy in the United States. It is a niche museum, but a real one, and it gives context to a subject that usually appears only in headlines.

It sits near Pentagon City, so it is easiest to combine with the Pentagon Memorial or other Arlington stops. Expect a compact visit rather than a full museum afternoon.
