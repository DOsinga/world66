The Lyric Theatre is Blacksburg's old downtown cinema, restored by the community after years of decline. Its marquee on College Avenue is one of the town's familiar landmarks, and the programming mixes independent films, classic screenings, concerts, and local events.

It is especially useful after a day outside. Check the schedule before you go; if there is something on, the Lyric gives Blacksburg the kind of evening anchor many small towns lack.
