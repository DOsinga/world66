---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Blacksburg's best sights split neatly between Virginia Tech and the hills around town. Start on campus, where the Drillfield, Burruss Hall, the Pylons, and the Hokie Stone buildings give the town its look. Then choose between gardens, local history, and easy outdoor escapes toward Pandapas Pond or the Huckleberry Trail.
