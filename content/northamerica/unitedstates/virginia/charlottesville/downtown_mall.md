A 7-block pedestrianised street at the heart of downtown Charlottesville, one of the longest pedestrian malls in the United States. The street runs east to west with restaurants, independent shops, two music venues (the Jefferson and the Paramount), and a public skating rink in winter. Most activity starts in the late afternoon and evening; daytime is quieter. Parking garages are located on either end of the mall.
