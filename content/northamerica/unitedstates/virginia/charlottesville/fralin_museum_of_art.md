---

The University of Virginia's main art museum, located on the western edge of the UVA campus. The permanent collection covers American art, African sculpture, ancient works, and prints and drawings. Admission is free. The connected Kluge-Ruhe Aboriginal Art Collection, 3 kilometres away at 400 Worrell Drive, is the only museum outside Australia dedicated to Aboriginal Australian art; also free and open to the public.
