The Skyline Drive is 169 kilometres of ridge-top road with pull-outs every few miles; it is genuinely beautiful but should not be confused with serious wilderness access. The best hikes — Old Rag Mountain, Whiteoak Canyon, the Dark Hollow Falls — require leaving the car and walking into the forest, where the crowds thin significantly even on busy days."

A 800-square-kilometre national park in the Blue Ridge Mountains, 45 kilometres north of Charlottesville. The Skyline Drive runs the length of the park along the ridge; entry is by vehicle permit (annual pass or fee per car). Hikes range from short overlook walks to challenging full-day routes. Old Rag Mountain and Whiteoak Canyon are the classic day hikes from the Charlottesville side of the park. The visitor centre at Byrd is near the park's centre; the closest entrance to Charlottesville is Swift Run Gap on US-33.
