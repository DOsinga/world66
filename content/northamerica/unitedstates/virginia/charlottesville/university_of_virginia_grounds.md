The historic core of the University of Virginia is Thomas Jefferson's great architectural project, centred on the Rotunda, the Lawn, and the pavilions he designed as an "Academical Village." It is part of the same UNESCO World Heritage listing as Monticello and is the best walk in Charlottesville for anyone interested in early American architecture, campus planning, or Jefferson's complicated legacy.
