Chincoteague is the Virginia Eastern Shore's soft-edged island town, all marsh light, seafood shacks, pony lore, and bicycles heading toward Assateague. The town itself is low-key, but it sits beside one of the best wildlife-and-beach combinations on the Mid-Atlantic coast.

The real pull is across the causeway at [Chincoteague National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/chincoteague/chincoteague_national_wildlife_refuge), where beach, dunes, marsh, birds, and the famous ponies share the southern end of Assateague Island. [Assateague Light](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/chincoteague/assateague_light) gives the classic view, while the [Museum of Chincoteague Island](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/chincoteague/museum_of_chincoteague_island) explains why a pony swim became a civic ritual.

Come outside high summer if you can. Spring and autumn are quieter, the birding is better, and the island feels less like a beach queue and more like a working edge of the Atlantic.
