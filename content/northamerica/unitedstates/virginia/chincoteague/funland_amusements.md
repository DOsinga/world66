Funland Amusements is Chincoteague's small family fun park, with go-karts, mini golf, bumper boats, an arcade, and a few other easygoing attractions. It is not the reason to come to the island, but it is useful when children need a break from beaches, ponies, and wildlife drives.

The appeal is old-fashioned and practical: an hour or two of uncomplicated fun after dinner or on a cloudy afternoon.
