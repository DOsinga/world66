Island Creamery is the local sweet stop that has escaped purely local fame. It is still just an ice cream shop, but after a hot refuge day that can be exactly the right level of ambition.

Expect lines in summer and do not overthink the order. The pleasure is sitting outside with something cold while the island traffic rolls back from Assateague.
