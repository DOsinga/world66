The Danville Riverwalk Trail follows the Dan River through parks, old industrial ground, and the edges of downtown. It is the easiest way to see how the city has turned a working riverfront into public space.

Walk a short section if you are passing through, or bring a bike if you want to cover more ground. The trail is not dramatic, but it gives Danville breathing room and links the river back to the city after decades of industrial use.
