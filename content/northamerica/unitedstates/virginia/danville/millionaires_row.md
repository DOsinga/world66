Millionaires' Row is the showpiece stretch of Danville's Old West End, where tobacco wealth left behind large 19th- and early 20th-century houses. The architecture ranges from Italianate and Queen Anne to Colonial Revival, with porches, towers, ironwork, and big lawns on the slope above downtown.

It is best approached as a walk rather than a checklist. The houses explain Danville's boom years more clearly than any plaque: this was a small river city where tobacco auctions and textile mills made real fortunes.
