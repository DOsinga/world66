Fredericksburg's historic district is compact enough to cover on foot. The colonial house museums — [Rising Sun Tavern](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/rising_sun_tavern), [Hugh Mercer Apothecary](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/hugh_mercer_apothecary), [James Monroe Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/james_monroe_museum), [Mary Washington House](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/mary_washington_house) — are clustered within a few blocks on Caroline and Charles Streets. [Kenmore](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/kenmore) is a 10-minute walk from the commercial center.

The Civil War battlefields require more planning. The [Fredericksburg and Spotsylvania National Military Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/fredericksburg_spotsylvania_park) manages four separate sites; the Fredericksburg battlefield is walkable from downtown, but Chancellorsville, the Wilderness, and Spotsylvania require a car. [Chatham Manor](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/chatham_manor), across the river, is a short drive or a long walk.

[Ferry Farm](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/ferry_farm) and [Gari Melchers Home](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/gari_melchers_home) are both a few miles from downtown and require a car or a rideshare. The [Fredericksburg Area Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/fredericksburg/fredericksburg_area_museum) in the old Town Hall is the logical starting point for a first visit.
