The Belle Boyd Cottage is part of the Warren Heritage Society's small museum complex near Main Street. Boyd, a Confederate spy, lived in Front Royal during part of the Civil War and became one of the town's best-known historical figures after passing information to Stonewall Jackson's forces in 1862.

This is a compact local-history stop rather than a major museum. It pairs best with a walk through the old centre, especially if you want the town's Civil War story before heading back to the river or the mountains.
