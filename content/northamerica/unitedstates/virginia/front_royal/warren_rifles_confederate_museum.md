The Warren Rifles Confederate Museum is a small Front Royal museum focused on Civil War relics, local records, and material connected with the Shenandoah Valley's wartime history. It is a compact stop rather than a major museum, but it adds useful local context to a visit built around the valley, Belle Boyd Cottage, or nearby battle sites.

Hours are limited and seasonal, so check before making a special trip.
