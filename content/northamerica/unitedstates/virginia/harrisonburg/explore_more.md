Explore More is Harrisonburg's children's discovery museum, occupying a restored building downtown with hands-on exhibits covering science, local history, culture, and creativity. It is the best children's attraction in the city and well-maintained.

The museum is aimed at children up to around 10 years old, with exhibits that include a miniature grocery store, a construction zone, a water table, and rotating special exhibitions. It is a good half-day activity for families visiting Harrisonburg with young children.
