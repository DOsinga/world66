type: poi

The Harrisonburg Farmers' Market is held every Saturday morning at the Turner Pavilion downtown, and is one of the more complete markets in the Shenandoah Valley — produce from the valley's Mennonite and Amish farms, fresh meat, cheese, baked goods, crafts, and prepared food. The market reflects Harrisonburg's unusual demographics: alongside the conventional Appalachian produce, you will find Vietnamese, Somali, and Latino vendors.

The market runs year-round, outdoor in summer, covered in winter. It is busy on Saturday mornings and worth arriving early for the best selection. The surrounding blocks of downtown are pleasant for a post-market walk.
