---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Jamestown sits at the western end of the Colonial Parkway, about a fifteen-minute drive from [Williamsburg](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/williamsburg). The nearest major airports are Norfolk and Richmond, each about an hour away by car. Newport News–Williamsburg International, twenty minutes east, has limited service.

Most visitors arrive by car: there is no public transit between Jamestown and the rest of the Historic Triangle. Free parking is plentiful at both Historic Jamestowne and Jamestown Settlement.
