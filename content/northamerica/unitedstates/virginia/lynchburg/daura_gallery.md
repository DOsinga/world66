Daura Gallery is Randolph College's art gallery, a small but worthwhile stop for visitors who like campus museums and rotating exhibitions. Its collection and programming add a quieter cultural option to Lynchburg's better-known historic houses and riverfront walks.

Check hours before going, because college galleries often follow academic calendars rather than tourist rhythms. It works best as a short, focused visit, especially if you are already near the college or interested in regional arts programming.
