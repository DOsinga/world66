Hill City AquaZoo is a small aquarium and animal attraction in Lynchburg, with fish, stingrays, turtles, snakes, birds, and small mammals. Some animal encounters are available for an extra fee.

It is a compact family stop rather than a major aquarium, best suited to travelers with children or anyone looking for an indoor break.
