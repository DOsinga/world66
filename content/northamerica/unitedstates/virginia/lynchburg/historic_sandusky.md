Historic Sandusky is a preserved house on Lynchburg's west side that served as Union headquarters during the Civil War Battle of Lynchburg. The house museum explains the battle, the families connected to the property, and the wider wartime history of the city.

Visits are by guided tour, so check opening arrangements before making the trip out from downtown.
