Liberty Mountain Snowflex Centre is a year-round synthetic slope for skiing, snowboarding, and
tubing on the ridge above Lynchburg. Its Snowflex surface makes winter-style recreation possible
even when Virginia weather is far from snowy, which gives it a novelty value beyond a standard
sports centre.

The attraction is most useful for families, beginners, or visitors already on Liberty Mountain, but
the views and unusual surface make it a distinctive Lynchburg activity. It is not a substitute for a
mountain resort, but it is a memorable local oddity and an easy active break from museums and
historic districts.
