The Red Fox Inn & Tavern is Middleburg's landmark building, a stone inn on Washington Street with roots in the eighteenth-century turnpike era. It has been a tavern, inn, stagecoach stop, and social address for a town that still trades heavily on history.

You do not need to sleep here for it to matter. A meal or drink is enough to feel how the place holds the old town together, though the prices match Middleburg's modern weekend clientele.
