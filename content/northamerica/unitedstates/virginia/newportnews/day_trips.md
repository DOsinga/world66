Newport News sits at the southern end of the Virginia Peninsula and has some of the most rewarding day-trip territory in the United States within easy reach. [Colonial Williamsburg](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/williamsburg) is 20 minutes north — a remarkable living-history reconstruction of the 18th-century capital of Virginia — and the original site of [Jamestown](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/jamestown), the first permanent English settlement in North America, sits beside it on the river. Together with Yorktown they form the Historic Triangle.

South across Hampton Roads are [Norfolk](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/norfolk) (the world's largest naval base, plus the Chrysler Museum) and [Virginia Beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/virginiabeach) (boardwalk and ocean), both reachable by car via the bridge-tunnel or — more pleasantly — by the cross-harbour ferry from downtown.
