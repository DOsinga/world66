---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**Newport News/Williamsburg International Airport** (PHF) sits at the north end of the city and has limited but useful direct flights to Atlanta, Charlotte, Chicago and a handful of Florida cities, mostly on regional carriers. Most visitors flying in from further afield use Norfolk International (ORF), 25 miles south across Hampton Roads.

Newport News is on the **Amtrak** Northeast Regional, with through trains from Washington DC, Baltimore, New York and Boston pulling in at the small downtown station near the shipyard. Driving is straightforward — Interstate 64 runs the length of the Peninsula from Williamsburg through Hampton, with the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel onwards to Norfolk and Virginia Beach.
