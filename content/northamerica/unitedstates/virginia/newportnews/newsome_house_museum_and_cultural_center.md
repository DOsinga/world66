The Newsome House Museum preserves the home of J. Thomas Newsome, a prominent African American attorney, journalist, and civic leader in early 20th-century Newport News. The house gives the city’s Black professional and political history a specific address rather than leaving it as background context.

Visits are intimate and history-focused. This is not a large museum, but it is a valuable counterpoint to Newport News’ military, maritime, and plantation-era sites. Check hours before going, as small house museums often keep limited schedules.
