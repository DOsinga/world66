Norfolk is the cultural centre of the Hampton Roads region in southeastern Virginia, a naval city with a revitalised downtown waterfront. The world's largest naval base, Naval Station Norfolk, dominates the northern end of the city, and the military presence is woven into the fabric of the place — you will see grey hulls on the horizon from almost any waterfront vantage point.

The highlight for visitors is the [Chrysler Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/norfolk/chrysler_museum_of_art), one of the best mid-size art museums in the country, with a particularly strong glass collection and free admission. Moored at the downtown waterfront is the [USS Wisconsin](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/norfolk/uss_wisconsin), an Iowa-class battleship you can tour, next to the free [Hampton Roads Naval Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/norfolk/hampton_roads_naval_museum). The [MacArthur Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/norfolk/macarthur_memorial), in the restored old city hall, is another free highlight.

The [NEON District](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/norfolk/neon_district), a former warehouse area northeast of downtown, is the city's arts and nightlife hub, while [Ghent](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/norfolk/ghent) — walkable, full of independent restaurants, and home to the Chrysler Museum — is the neighbourhood worth spending time in. The [Norfolk Botanical Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/norfolk/norfolk_botanical_garden), 175 acres near the airport, is outstanding in spring.
