Fort Norfolk is a rare surviving early American harbour fort on the Elizabeth River. Its brick walls, magazine, and compact grounds recall the period when Norfolk’s waterfront was defended by a chain of small coastal fortifications.

The site is especially interesting because it sits close to the modern city and naval landscape. Opening arrangements can be limited, so check before going; even a short visit helps connect Norfolk’s maritime present with its older military geography.
