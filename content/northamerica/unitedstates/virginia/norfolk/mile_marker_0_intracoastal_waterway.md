Mile Marker 0 marks the northern starting point of the Intracoastal Waterway mileage system, a small but satisfying stop for anyone interested in boats, charts and the working geography of the American coast. In Norfolk, where naval history and harbour traffic are never far away, the marker fits naturally into the waterfront story.

It is not a destination on its own, but it is worth noticing while walking near the harbour, Nauticus or the Elizabeth River. The appeal is conceptual: from this point, thousands of waterway miles lead south through canals, sounds and sheltered channels toward Florida and beyond.
