Virginia Zoo is one of Norfolk's best family-friendly attractions, with walkable grounds, animal habitats, gardens, and a setting close to the Lafayette River. It offers a slower, greener break from the naval and waterfront sights elsewhere in the city.

The zoo is large enough for a half-day visit but not so large that it overwhelms a city itinerary. It is especially useful for families or anyone spending several days in Norfolk and wanting an easy outdoor activity.
