The National Museum of the United States Army is a large modern museum near Fort Belvoir, south of Alexandria. Its galleries trace the Army from the Revolutionary War through modern conflicts, with uniforms, vehicles, weapons, personal stories, and changing exhibits.

It is most rewarding if you have an interest in military history, but the presentation is broad enough for a general visit. Allow a couple of hours, and check entry requirements before heading out.
