One of the oldest continuously operating markets in America (on this site since 1737), the 17th Street Farmers Market runs Saturday mornings in Shockoe Bottom and brings together local produce, meat, cheese, and artisan vendors. The setting — cobblestone streets, cast-iron market sheds, the old city centre around it — is as good as the goods on offer.
