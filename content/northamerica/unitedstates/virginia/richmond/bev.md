An Austrian-alpine themed bar and restaurant in Scott's Addition with a remarkable collection of schnapps and spirits, a devoted following for its Glühwein in winter, and a covered outdoor terrace year-round. The drinks programme is the serious centre of the place, but the food — fondue, raclette, charcuterie, alpine staples — is genuinely good. One of the more original concepts in a district full of original concepts.
