A neighbourhood bar on the edge of Carytown with a good whiskey selection, comfortable leather seats, and the kind of low-key atmosphere that makes it possible to have an actual conversation. It's not a craft cocktail destination or a scene bar — it's a bar that takes its drinks seriously and its customers' comfort seriously, which is a combination rarer than it should be. Particularly good on winter evenings when the rest of Carytown goes quiet.
