Richmond's best independent bookshop, tucked into a small space on the edge of Carytown with a carefully curated selection of new and used books, a strong local authors section, and occasional reading events. The shop reflects the literary tastes of the Fan/Carytown neighbourhood — eclectic, well-read, with opinions. A proper bookshop in the old tradition.
