The Children's Museum of Richmond is a hands-on museum built around play, experiments, and creative problem-solving for younger children. Exhibits are designed for climbing, building, pretending, and testing ideas rather than quiet looking.

It is best for families with small children who need a break from Richmond's more traditional historic sites and art museums. The museum sits near the Science Museum of Virginia and the Museum District, so it is easy to combine with other stops.
