Richmond's best rooftop bar, on the top floor of a downtown hotel with views over Capitol Square and the skyline. The cocktail list is intelligent and seasonal; the food is better than hotel rooftop bars usually manage. Come for sunset and the view; the atmosphere is dressy and slightly more expensive than the neighbourhood average.
