The museum dedicated to America's master of gothic horror occupies Richmond's oldest surviving building, a plain stone house from the 1730s in the Shockoe area. The collection includes Poe memorabilia, manuscripts, and first editions, along with the reconstructed study where Poe wrote. Poe grew up in Richmond and the city has a genuine claim on him — he spent most of his formative years here before his erratic adult life took him to Philadelphia, New York, and Baltimore.

The enchanted garden behind the museum, with its moody plantings and stone walls, is the perfect complement to the exhibits inside. Small but well-curated; allow about an hour.
