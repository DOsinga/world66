One of the most ambitious restaurants in Richmond, Saison occupies a converted fire station on Church Hill and serves a tightly focused seasonal menu that changes frequently. The cooking is confident and ingredient-driven — Virginia oysters, heritage pork, produce from nearby farms — and the execution is better than in most restaurants twice the size in cities twice the population. Reservations essential; not cheap.
