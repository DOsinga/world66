Richmond's most sought-after craft brewery, The Veil has developed a national reputation among beer enthusiasts for its hazy IPAs and sour beers. Release days bring queues around the block; the taproom on weekdays is calmer and gives you time to work through the draft selection at your own pace. The kitchen produces genuinely good food — in particular the smash burgers, which have attracted their own following. Expect to see out-of-town visitors; this is on the beer pilgrim circuit.
