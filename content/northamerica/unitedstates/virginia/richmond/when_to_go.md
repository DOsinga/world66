---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Richmond has a mid-Atlantic climate with four distinct seasons. **Spring** (March–May) is the most appealing: mild temperatures, dogwood and cherry blossoms throughout the parks, and the James River at a lively level for kayaking. **Summer** is hot and humid — July highs around 32°C (90°F) — but the city remains lively and the James River swimming holes are in full use. **Fall** (September–November) brings cool days and warm light; the outdoor farmers markets are at their best and the university population is back in town. **Winter** is cold but rarely severe; the city sees occasional snow but nothing like Washington, D.C. just 90 minutes north.

The **Monument Avenue 10K** road race in April is one of the city's biggest events. The **Richmond Folk Festival** in October brings free outdoor music to the James River waterfront for three days and is one of the best music festivals in the region. The **French Film Festival**, held in March at the Byrd Theatre, is the longest-running French film festival in the United States.
