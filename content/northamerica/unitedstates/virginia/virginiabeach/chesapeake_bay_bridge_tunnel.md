The Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel is an 18-mile bridge and tunnel crossing between Virginia Beach and the Eastern Shore. It is both a major transport link and an engineering landmark, with long views over the Chesapeake Bay.

The scenic overlook near the southern end is the main reason to stop, giving a close look at the crossing and the open water around it.
