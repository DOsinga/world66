The Naval Air Station Oceana Viewing Area is a public spot set aside for watching fighter jets take off and land at one of the U.S. Navy's major East Coast air stations. It is simple rather than scenic, but aviation fans can get close views of aircraft operations when the base is active.

Bring hearing protection if you are sensitive to noise, and do not expect a museum or visitor centre. The appeal is the chance to watch the flight line from a legal, designated viewing point.
