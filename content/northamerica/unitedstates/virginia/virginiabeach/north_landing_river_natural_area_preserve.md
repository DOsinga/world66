North Landing River Natural Area Preserve protects one of the quieter natural landscapes around Virginia Beach: marshes, swamp forest, and river-edge habitat rather than boardwalk sand. It is valuable precisely because it shows a side of the area that beach-focused visitors often miss.

Access is more sensitive than at a city park, so check current Virginia DCR guidance before going. For nature-minded travelers, the preserve is best approached as a low-impact birding or ecology stop, with patience and respect for seasonal closures or restricted areas.
