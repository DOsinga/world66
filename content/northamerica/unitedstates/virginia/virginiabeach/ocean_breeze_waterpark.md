Ocean Breeze Waterpark is a large seasonal water park near the Virginia Aquarium, with slides, tube rides, and a wave pool spread across a compact resort-style site. It is aimed squarely at families and groups looking for a full day away from the beach.

The park is most useful in summer, when the resort strip is busy and children need a change from the ocean. It pairs naturally with the aquarium area, but it is an attraction to plan around rather than something to drop into briefly.
