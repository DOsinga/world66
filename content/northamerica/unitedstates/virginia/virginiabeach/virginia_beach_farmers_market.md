Virginia Beach Farmers Market is a year-round, country-style market with local produce, dairy, meat, organic groceries, jams, gifts, and places to eat. It gives visitors a small taste of the rural side of Virginia Beach, away from the oceanfront strip.

It is most useful if you are self-catering, looking for local food gifts, or passing through the inland side of the city. Special events are held through the year, so check the schedule if you want more than a quick shopping stop.
