The Capitol in Colonial Williamsburg marks the seat of colonial Virginia's government. It was here that Virginia voted for independence on May 15, 1776, making the building one of the key political sites in the restored town.

Visit it as part of a wider Colonial Williamsburg walk, especially if you want the political story behind the taverns, houses, and trades.
