The Colonial Parkway is a 23-mile scenic road built and maintained by the National Park Service, connecting the three points of the Historic Triangle: Williamsburg, Jamestown Island, and Yorktown. The road runs through mature forest along the York River and the James River, with no commercial development or signage permitted along its length.

Driving the full parkway in a single afternoon — stopping at Jamestown Island (where the first permanent English settlement in America was established in 1607) and at Yorktown (where Cornwallis surrendered in 1781, effectively ending the Revolution) — gives a condensed version of the founding and independence narrative in a few hours. The route is also pleasant for cycling or walking sections; the park service maintains bicycle access on parts of the route.
