The most natural day trip from Williamsburg follows the [Colonial Parkway](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/colonial_parkway), the 23-mile National Park Service road that connects Williamsburg to [Jamestown](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/jamestown) and [Yorktown](/northamerica/unitedstates/virginia/yorktown) without a single billboard or petrol station in sight. You can complete the full Historic Triangle loop — 1607 at Jamestown, the colonial capital here, the 1781 surrender at Yorktown — in a single unhurried afternoon, returning to Williamsburg for dinner.
