Peyton Randolph House is an interpreted historic house in Colonial Williamsburg. Randolph was president of the First Continental Congress, and the site presents both the life of an elite colonial household and the lives of the enslaved people who worked there.

The contrast between the main house and the work spaces behind it makes this one of Williamsburg's stronger house visits.
