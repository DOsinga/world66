R. Charlton's Coffeehouse is a reconstructed Colonial Williamsburg stop focused on 18th-century coffeehouse culture. Visitors can sample coffee, tea or drinking chocolate prepared in period style.

It works best as part of a wider Colonial Williamsburg visit rather than as a stand-alone sight.
