R. Charlton's Coffeehouse recreates the kind of social room where 18th-century Williamsburg's merchants, officials, and gossips came for coffee, tea, and chocolate. It is a small stop, but a useful one because it turns Colonial Williamsburg's political history into something more domestic and immediate.
