Raleigh Tavern was one of the political centers of colonial Williamsburg, where Virginia patriots met in defiance of royal authority before the American Revolution. The reconstructed tavern is part of Colonial Williamsburg and is used for interpretation, discussions, music, and comedy performances that help bring the revolutionary town to life.
