The Thomas Everard House is an original 18th-century building in Colonial Williamsburg, restored to show the home of a planter and civic leader who rose from orphaned immigrant to one of the town's wealthy residents. The interiors use period antiques to give a clear sense of upper-class domestic life in colonial Virginia.
