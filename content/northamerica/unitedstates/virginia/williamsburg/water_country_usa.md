Water Country USA is a large seasonal water park east of Williamsburg, run by the same broader resort ecosystem as Busch Gardens. It trades colonial history for slides, wave pools, lazy rivers, and family-friendly summer cooling.

It is a practical POI for families spending several days in the Historic Triangle, especially in hot weather. Check seasonal dates and ticket options before going, because it is not open year-round and can consume most of a day.
