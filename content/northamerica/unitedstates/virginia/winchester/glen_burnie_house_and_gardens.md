Glen Burnie is the historic house at the heart of the Museum of the Shenandoah Valley, with a layered story that reaches back to Winchester's early settlement and the Wood-Glass family. The house is furnished and interpreted as part of the museum campus rather than as an isolated old home.

The gardens are the real pleasure for many visitors: formal rooms, sculpture, water, and seasonal planting set against the soft edges of the Shenandoah landscape. It is an easy addition to Winchester's Civil War and old-town sights, especially when you want a quieter, more domestic view of the valley's history.
