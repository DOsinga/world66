Marker-Miller Orchards is a Shenandoah Valley farm stop west of Winchester, known for seasonal fruit, a farm market, and pick-your-own visits. It fits Winchester's apple-country identity better than a generic roadside shop would.

The orchard is most worthwhile in season, especially around apple harvest or fall travel. Check what is being picked before going, and treat it as a relaxed countryside outing rather than a formal historic sight.
