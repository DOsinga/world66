Winchester sits at the northern end of the Shenandoah Valley, and the valley stretching south is one of the more rewarding day-trip corridors on the East Coast. The drive south on US 340 or US 11 passes through small towns — Front Royal, Luray, Harrisonburg, Staunton — each with its own historic center and surrounding orchards and vineyards. Luray Caverns, about 50 miles south, is among the largest and most dramatic cave systems in the eastern United States, with rooms large enough to contain a concert hall and formations that took millions of years to develop.

Shenandoah National Park begins roughly 20 miles south of Winchester and extends for 80 miles along the Blue Ridge. The Skyline Drive runs the spine of the ridge and offers views across the valley on both sides; in fall, the foliage is exceptional. The park has good hiking ranging from easy walks to strenuous ridge climbs. A full day gets you to the park and back comfortably. Two days allows time to explore the valley floor and the ridge. Camping is available inside the park if you want to stay longer.
