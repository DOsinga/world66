Star Fort is a preserved Civil War earthwork connected to the repeated campaigns around Winchester. The site is subtle compared with a stone fort, but it helps make the Shenandoah Valley's military geography visible.

Bring a little background or use interpretive material on site, because the value is in understanding the terrain and defensive lines. It pairs well with Winchester's other Civil War and old-town stops.
