Winchester punches well above its weight for a city of 28,000. The Civil War passed through here so many times — the city changed hands roughly 70 times over four years — that history is embedded in nearly every street. George Washington was here before the war, surveying land and building forts. Patsy Cline was born and raised here. The walking distances are short and the historic core is compact.
