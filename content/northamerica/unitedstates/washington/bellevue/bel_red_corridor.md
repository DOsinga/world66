The Bel-Red corridor between Bellevue and Redmond is Bellevue's most rapidly changing neighbourhood — a former strip of light industrial businesses and auto dealers that is being rebuilt around two new light rail stations on the East Link extension. When the line opened in 2023, it catalysed exactly the transit-oriented development that planners had hoped for: mixed-use apartment buildings, ground-floor retail, and a more walkable street grid replacing the sprawling surface lots.

It is not yet a finished neighbourhood, but it is an interesting one to visit if you are curious about how American cities rebuild around transit. The Overlake area nearby, centred on the Microsoft campus in Redmond, has several good restaurants that have colonised the new apartment buildings, and the 130th Street station area is seeing the fastest pace of change.
