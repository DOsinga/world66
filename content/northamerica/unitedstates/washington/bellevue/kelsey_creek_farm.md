Kelsey Creek Farm is a low-key farm park in Bellevue, with barns, pastures, animals, and walking paths set surprisingly close to the city's office towers and shopping areas. It is especially useful for families who need a short outdoor break that is not another mall or playground.

The farm is not a regional headline attraction, but it gives Bellevue a softer, local side. Pair it with the surrounding park trails or a visit to Bellevue Botanical Garden if you want a half-day away from the downtown core.
