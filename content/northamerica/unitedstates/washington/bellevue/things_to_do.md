title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bellevue's main attractions lean toward parks and gardens, with the [Bellevue Botanical Garden](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/bellevue/bellevue_botanical_garden) as the standout — 53 free acres of cultivated beds and wetland trails east of downtown. The city also has a growing arts scene centred around the [Bellevue Arts Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/bellevue/bellevue_arts_museum), which focuses on craft and design rather than fine art and sits in a striking Steven Holl building.

For outdoor space in the urban core, [Bellevue Downtown Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/bellevue/bellevue_downtown_park) provides 20 acres of lawns and a reflecting pool a short walk from the transit centre. South of downtown, [Mercer Slough Nature Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/bellevue/mercer_slough_nature_park) is Lake Washington's largest surviving wetland and one of the best urban nature walks in the Eastside. The [Lake Washington waterfront](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/bellevue/lake_washington_waterfront) has public beaches at Newcastle and Chism parks, and the SR 520 bridge trail connects cyclists and pedestrians to Seattle.

Bellevue's neighbourhoods reflect its diversity. [Crossroads](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/bellevue/crossroads_bellevue) is the most culturally varied — a strip of East Asian restaurants and markets that is arguably the best eating area in the city. [Old Bellevue](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/bellevue/old_bellevue) along Main Street has the most pleasant walkable streetscape, with independent restaurants and cafés in a low-rise setting near the lake.
