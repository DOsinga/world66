Lake Padden is one of Bellingham's most useful outdoor escapes, a wooded lake park with a loop trail, swimming areas, picnic spots, and enough space to feel away from town. It is local, but in Bellingham local nature is part of the travel appeal.

The loop is easy and satisfying in almost any season. Go for a morning walk, a summer swim, or a low-effort reset between Fairhaven, downtown, and the Chuckanut coast.
