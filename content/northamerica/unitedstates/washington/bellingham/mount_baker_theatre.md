Mount Baker Theatre is a restored 1927 performing arts venue in downtown Bellingham. Its Moorish Revival details and atmospheric interior make it worth noticing even if you are not catching a show.

The programme ranges from touring theatre and concerts to family performances. It sits close to the Whatcom Museum and SPARK Museum, making it easy to include on a downtown walk.
