The Skagit-Squatch Museum is Burlington's most unusual attraction — a serious-minded collection devoted entirely to Sasquatch and other cryptids. The exhibits trace the long history of Bigfoot sightings in the Pacific Northwest, mixing Indigenous oral traditions, cast footprints, hair samples sent for analysis, and accounts from local witnesses.

It isn't a joke shop or a novelty store. The museum treats the subject with genuine curiosity: why do so many people report seeing something in these dense forests, and what does that tell us about the relationship between humans and wild places? Whether you're a true believer or a committed skeptic, it's a thought-provoking hour.
