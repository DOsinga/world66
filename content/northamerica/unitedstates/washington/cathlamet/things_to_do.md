Cathlamet punches above its weight for a town of 560 people. The [Julia Butler Hansen National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/cathlamet/things_to_do/julia_butler_hansen_refuge) protects one of the most endangered deer subspecies in North America, with sightings almost guaranteed along the river road. The [Wahkiakum County Historical Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/cathlamet/things_to_do/wahkiakum_county_historical_museum) covers the logging, fishing, and Indigenous history of this remote corner of Washington. The [Pioneer Church](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/cathlamet/things_to_do/pioneer_church), on the National Register of Historic Places since 1973, still stands on Columbia Street. And [Puget Island](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/cathlamet/things_to_do/puget_island), just across a short bridge, is one of the more pleasant flat bike loops in southwest Washington.
