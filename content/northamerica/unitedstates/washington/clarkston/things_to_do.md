---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Almost everything worth doing in Clarkston is on or near the river. The town is the standard launching point for jet-boat trips up the Snake River into [Hells Canyon](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/clarkston/hells_canyon) — the deepest river gorge in North America — and the marina is the main embarkation point. The [Snake River Confluence](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/clarkston/snake_river_confluence) with the Clearwater is at the centre of the town. The [Lewis and Clark Discovery Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/clarkston/lewis_clark_discovery_center) interprets the expedition's arrival here in 1805. Inland, the [Clarkston Public Library](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/clarkston/clarkston_public_library) is a small but charming Carnegie building from 1913. The [Asotin County Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/clarkston/asotin_county_museum) is a 15-minute drive south in the much older town of Asotin.
