Fort Ebey State Park covers a former Second World War coastal defense site on Whidbey Island, with blufftop views over the Strait of Juan de Fuca. The old fortifications are part of the appeal, but the park is just as useful for shoreline walks, forest trails, and broad water views.

The park has miles of hiking and biking trails, a freshwater lake, and access to a rugged stretch of coast. It pairs naturally with nearby Ebey's Landing and Fort Casey for a full day of Whidbey Island history and scenery.
