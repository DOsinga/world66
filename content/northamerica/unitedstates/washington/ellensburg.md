The small city of Ellensburg is often overlooked by travellers in Washington State, which is a shame. Just two hours over Snoqualmie Pass on I-90 from Seattle, nestled on the floor of the picturesque Kittitas Valley, it has an appealing character that comes from the unlikely mix of a stalwart farming community and the lively student population of [Central Washington University](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/ellensburg/central_washington_university).

Unlike Leavenworth's Bavarian theme or Wenatchee's fruit-trade bustle, Ellensburg is unpretentious — an eccentrically country college town that was once a contender to become the state capital. The [historic downtown](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/ellensburg/historic_downtown) was rebuilt in brick after a fire in 1889 and is largely intact. The dining is surprisingly good: the [Yellow Church Cafe](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/ellensburg/yellow_church_cafe) serves eggs Benedict with a hint of curry, [El Caporal](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/ellensburg/el_caporal) does an excellent carne asada plate, and Chinese, Thai, Mexican, and barbecue options are all within walking distance of the university grounds. The outsider-art property at [Dick and Jane's Spot](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/ellensburg/dick_and_janes_spot) is worth the short walk from downtown.

If you spend one weekend of the year here, make it Labor Day. The [Ellensburg Rodeo](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/ellensburg/ellensburg_rodeo), the Rodeo Parade, and the Kittitas County Fair draw thousands of visitors. The town comes alive in a way that perfectly captures its character.
