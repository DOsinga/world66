Olmstead Place State Park preserves a pioneer farmstead east of Ellensburg, with farmhouses, barns, and outbuildings that show Kittitas Valley farm life in the 19th century. It is a quiet stop, but a useful one if you want the valley's agricultural history alongside the town's rodeo and university character.
