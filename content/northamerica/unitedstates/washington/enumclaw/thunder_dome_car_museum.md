An unusual private car museum in downtown Enumclaw, the Thunder Dome displays a rotating collection of classic, exotic, and custom automobiles in a purpose-built sunlit facility. The collection tilts toward American muscle and European sports cars, with occasional curveballs that keep regular visitors coming back.

The museum is a welcome surprise in a town that doesn't otherwise have much in the way of conventional attractions. It's informal and enthusiast-run, with knowledgeable staff who are happy to talk about the cars. Check hours before visiting — it's not always open during the week.
