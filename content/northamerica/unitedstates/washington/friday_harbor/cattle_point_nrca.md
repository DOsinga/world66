Cattle Point Natural Resources Conservation Area protects one of San Juan Island's most open, wind-brushed landscapes: prairie, headlands, beaches, and wide views across the Strait of Juan de Fuca. It feels very different from the wooded lanes around Friday Harbor, and that contrast is part of the appeal.

Come for walking, birding, photography, and the sense of standing at the island's edge. It pairs naturally with American Camp, so give yourself enough time to explore both rather than rushing down only for a quick photo.
