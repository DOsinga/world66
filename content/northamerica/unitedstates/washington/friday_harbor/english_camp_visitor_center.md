English Camp Visitor Center is the best starting point for the northern half of San Juan Island National Historical Park. Exhibits and a park film explain the Pig War, the long joint military occupation of the island, and the natural history of the surrounding shore and woodland.

Several surviving camp buildings stand nearby, including the commissary, barracks, blockhouse, and hospital. The Union Jack is still raised and lowered here by park rangers, a small ritual that makes the site's layered history feel unusually present.
