The San Juan Islands Museum of Art gives Friday Harbor a cultural stop that works well between ferries, whale-watching trips, and waterfront walks. The museum presents rotating exhibitions rather than a large permanent collection, with a strong interest in contemporary work and Pacific Northwest connections.

Its scale is part of the appeal. You can see an exhibition without losing half a day, then step back into the island's harbour streets. Check the current show before going; when the programming is strong, it adds welcome depth to a San Juan visit.
