Gilman Town Hall Museum tells Issaquah's local story through photographs, artifacts, and hands-on exhibits about mining, logging, early water systems, and everyday life in the Squak Valley.

It is a small stop, but it pairs well with the nearby Issaquah Depot Museum and salmon hatchery. Go if you want a compact local-history museum that gives the old town centre more context.
