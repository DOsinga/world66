Poo Poo Point is one of Issaquah's classic day hikes, a cleared launch area high on Tiger Mountain with broad views over town, Lake Sammamish, and the Cascades on a clear day. The name is silly, but the view earns the walk.

The trail is popular with hikers and paragliders, so expect company on fair weekends. Start early if you want a quieter climb, bring water, and treat the summit as a viewpoint rather than a wilderness escape: it is close to Seattle, and that convenience is exactly why locals use it so much.
