---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Downtown Kirkland and the waterfront are walkable from each other — the core area is compact. Parking is available in several public garages near the downtown core and marina. The Burke-Gilman Trail runs through town and connects cyclists north and south along the lake. For visits to outlying areas like Juanita or Houghton, a car is more convenient. Ride-sharing (Uber, Lyft) works reliably throughout the city.
