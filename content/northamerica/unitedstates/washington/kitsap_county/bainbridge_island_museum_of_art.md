Bainbridge Island Museum of Art is a free contemporary art museum a short walk from the Bainbridge ferry terminal. The focus is art and craft from the Puget Sound region, so it feels closely tied to the islands, forests, and small communities around Seattle.

It is an easy first or last stop on a Bainbridge day trip. The scale is manageable, the building is bright, and the location makes it simple to combine with Winslow's shops, cafes, and waterfront walks.
