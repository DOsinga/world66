Foulweather Bluff is a 110-acre nature preserve at the north tip of the Kitsap Peninsula, with forest, wetlands, tidelands, and a long stretch of Puget Sound shoreline. The short walk to the beach is quiet and good for birdwatching, driftwood, and broad water views.

The preserve is managed for wildlife habitat, so keep to marked paths and leave pets behind.
