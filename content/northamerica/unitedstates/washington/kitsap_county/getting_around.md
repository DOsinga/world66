---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

A car is the only practical way to cover the peninsula. Distances are short — Bremerton to Poulsbo is twenty minutes, Bremerton to Port Gamble forty — but the road network is two-lane rural through forest and farmland, and traffic outside the few towns is light.

**Kitsap Transit** runs local buses linking the main settlements (Bremerton, Silverdale, Poulsbo, Port Orchard, Kingston) and connecting with the ferry terminals; useful for staying put in one town and visiting a second, but not for a touring trip. The **Bremerton–Port Orchard foot ferry** is run by Kitsap Transit and crosses Sinclair Inlet every half hour for the price of a coffee — the quickest way between the two seats of the peninsula.
