Green Mountain State Forest is a large working forest west of Bremerton with trails for hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding, and other outdoor use. The summit area has views that can reach across Puget Sound, Hood Canal, Mount Rainier, Mount Baker, and the Seattle skyline.

It is a practical escape from the built-up Kitsap Peninsula rather than a manicured park. Check access before going, since some roads and facilities have limited opening times.
