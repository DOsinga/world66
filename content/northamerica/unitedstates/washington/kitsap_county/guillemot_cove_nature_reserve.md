Guillemot Cove Nature Reserve protects forest, creek, and shoreline habitat on the west side of Kitsap County. Trails lead through mature woodland to beach access, with a few old structures still visible on the property.

It is best for a quiet walk and wildlife watching. There are no restroom facilities, so plan it as a simple nature stop rather than a full-service park visit.
