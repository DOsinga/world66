The Kitsap Historical Society and Museum preserves the county's local history, with exhibits built around photographs, objects, and stories from communities across the Kitsap Peninsula.

It is a useful stop in downtown Bremerton if you want context beyond the naval sights. The collection is especially good for understanding how logging, ferries, shipbuilding, and small towns shaped the county.
