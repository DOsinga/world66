Old Man House Park marks the site of a major Suquamish winter village and longhouse used for at least two thousand years. Chief Seattle lived here before the longhouse was dismantled in the 19th century, so the quiet waterfront park is most meaningful as a cultural and historical stop, especially when paired with the nearby Suquamish Museum.
