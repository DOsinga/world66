Point No Point Lighthouse and Park sits at the northeastern tip of the Kitsap Peninsula, where open water, beach, and maritime history meet. The lighthouse marks one of the oldest light stations on Puget Sound, with keeper buildings nearby and seasonal tours when volunteers are present.

Come for the lighthouse, then stay for the shoreline views and birding. The park is undeveloped enough to feel quieter than many Puget Sound waterfront stops.
