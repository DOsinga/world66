Veterans Living History Museum is a tiny downtown Port Orchard museum built around a large private collection of military memorabilia. The displays are compact, but the personal scale gives it more character than a standard civic exhibit.

It works best as a short stop while walking the downtown waterfront and nearby historic blocks.
