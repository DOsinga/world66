Tucked behind the Sunnyslope Plant Nursery on Bethel Road south of Port Orchard sits a small, hand-built turf-roofed cottage modelled — never officially, but unmistakably — on a hobbit-hole from Tolkien's Shire. The round door, the wooden window, the moss and the planted roof are all the work of the nursery's owner; you can wander up to it and peer through the windows.

It is free and there is no schedule — just turn up during nursery hours. It is the sort of small, surprising stop that pairs nicely with a meander around the Elandan Gardens up the road, and very little else makes the trip out to Bethel Road worthwhile.
