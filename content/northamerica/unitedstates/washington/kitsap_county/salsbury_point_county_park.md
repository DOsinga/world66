Salsbury Point County Park is a small saltwater park near Port Gamble, with beach access, a boat launch, fishing pier, restrooms, and picnic areas. The main draw is the easy shoreline stop on the north Kitsap coast, with views across the water and a quieter feel than the larger state parks nearby.

It is best as a short picnic, launch, or beach-walk stop while exploring Port Gamble and the Hood Canal side of Kitsap County.
