Sinclair Inlet Wildlife Viewing Area is a small, rough-edged stop for watching birds and shoreline life near Gorst. A viewing platform looks over the inlet, and a steep path gives access closer to the water.

The area is left fairly wild, which is part of the appeal. Winter is the best season for birdwatching, when several sea bird species use the inlet as nesting and feeding ground.
