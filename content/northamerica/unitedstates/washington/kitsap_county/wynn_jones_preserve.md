Wynn-Jones Preserve protects 54 acres of shoreline, woodland, and trail access near Manchester. The short walks lead to views over Puget Sound, with ferries, naval traffic, and the Seattle skyline sometimes visible across the water.

It is a modest stop rather than a full-day destination, but it works well if you are exploring the east side of Kitsap County and want a quiet walk with saltwater views.
