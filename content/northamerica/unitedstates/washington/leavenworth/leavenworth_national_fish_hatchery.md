Leavenworth National Fish Hatchery explains the salmon life cycle in a setting that still feels tied to the mountains and Icicle Creek. The best time to visit is during the salmon run, when the biology is not just on panels but moving through the water in front of you.

The grounds also include a wildlife trail and pollinator garden, making it a low-key stop before or after hikes on the Icicle Creek side of town.
