Leavenworth National Fish Hatchery is a visitor-friendly hatchery where you can learn about salmon life cycles and, in season, see fish returning upstream. The grounds also include a wildlife trail and pollinator garden.

It is a worthwhile short stop for families or anyone using Leavenworth as a base for Icicle Creek and nearby mountain scenery.
