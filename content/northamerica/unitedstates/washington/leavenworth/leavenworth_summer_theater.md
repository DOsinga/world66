Leavenworth Summer Theater stages open-air musicals in the warm months, using the town's mountain setting as part of the show. The Sound of Music is the classic fit here, and the alpine backdrop makes the choice feel almost inevitable.

It is not an everyday sightseeing stop; it is worth planning around if you are in town during the season. Tickets for the best-known productions can sell out, especially on summer weekends.
