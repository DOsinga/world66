The Cranberry Museum explains one of the Long Beach Peninsula's defining crops. Inside are displays on cranberry farming equipment and local growing history; outside, a demonstration bog shows what the harvest landscape actually looks like.

It is a modest stop, but a useful one if you want to understand why cranberry fields are part of the peninsula's scenery. It pairs easily with the beaches, Oysterville, or the wildlife refuge.
