---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The Long Beach Peninsula's appeal is almost entirely elemental: the ocean, the bay, the beach, and the food that comes out of the water. Walking or driving the hard-packed sand at low tide, digging razor clams, watching storms roll in from the Pacific, and eating oysters straight from Willapa Bay are the core experiences. The historic district of [Oysterville](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/long_beach_peninsula/oysterville) at the north end is worth a drive for its Victorian calm. The [Willapa National Wildlife Refuge](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/long_beach_peninsula/willapa_wildlife_refuge) protects the bay's extraordinary bird habitat.
