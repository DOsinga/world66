type: section
---

Moclips is a one-activity destination: the [beach](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/moclips/moclips_beach). The broad Pacific strand runs for miles and at low tide reveals ancient drowned forest stumps — remnants of a 1700 Cascadia earthquake event. Razor clamming is the main seasonal activity (check state regulations). Beachcombing can turn up glass floats, agates, and interesting driftwood.

The [Ocean Crest Resort](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/moclips/ocean_crest_resort) on the bluff has the best food and views in the area. Day trips reach the Quinault Rain Forest (less than an hour inland) or [Olympic Peninsula](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/olympicpeninsula) destinations.
