The Carbon River Rain Forest Nature Trail shows a side of Mount Rainier that feels different from the open meadows around Paradise and Sunrise. The Carbon River valley is low, wet, and densely green, with moss, ferns, huge trees, and the soft light of a temperate rain forest.

The loop is short and easy, making it a useful first stop if you enter the park from the northwest. It is also a good choice in cloudy weather, when higher mountain views may be hidden but the forest is at its best. Expect damp ground and bring insect repellent in warm months.
