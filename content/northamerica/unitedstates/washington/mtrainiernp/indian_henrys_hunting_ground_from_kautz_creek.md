The Kautz Creek route to Indian Henry's Hunting Ground is one of Mount Rainier's serious southwest-side hikes. It climbs from forest into open subalpine meadows where wildflowers, old patrol-cabin history, and views toward the mountain make the effort worthwhile.

This is not a casual nature trail. Expect a long, steep day, check snow and trail conditions before setting out, and carry proper mountain-weather gear. In the right season, the meadows are among the park's classic rewards.
