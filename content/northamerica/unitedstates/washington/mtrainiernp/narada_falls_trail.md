Narada Falls is one of Mount Rainier's easiest waterfall stops, just below the road to Paradise. The trail drops from the parking area to a viewpoint where the Paradise River spreads over a broad lip and falls in a white sheet into the forested gorge below.

It is short, but the path can be wet, icy, or slick depending on season, so treat it as a real trail rather than a roadside photo stop. In late spring and early summer the water volume is especially strong; later in the season the falls are still handsome and much easier to combine with longer Paradise-area hikes.
