Skyline Trail is the signature hike from Paradise and one of Mount Rainier's clearest statements of purpose. The loop climbs through flower meadows and open slopes toward Panorama Point, where the mountain feels close enough to dominate every pause, photograph, and weather decision.

It is popular, exposed, and often snow-affected well into summer, so check conditions before treating it as a simple walk. In good weather, though, this is the Rainier trail many visitors are imagining before they arrive: big views, alpine air, and a route that earns its fame.
