Diablo Lake Overlook is one of the easiest big-payoff stops on the North Cascades Highway. The view drops over the reservoir's bright turquoise water, a colour made by fine glacial flour suspended in the lake.

It is a quick roadside stop rather than a hike, but the setting is pure North Cascades: steep forested slopes, sharp peaks, and water that looks almost unreal on a clear day.
