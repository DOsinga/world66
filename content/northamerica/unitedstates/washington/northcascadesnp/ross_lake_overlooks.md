The Ross Lake overlooks give broad views over the long reservoir, the Canadian border country, and the sharp peaks around Hozomeen Mountain. Interpretive panels add context on the geology and writers associated with the North Cascades, making this an easy stop along the highway rather than just a roadside pullout.
