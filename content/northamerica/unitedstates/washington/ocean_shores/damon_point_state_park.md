Damon Point is the ragged southern tip of the Ocean Shores peninsula, a low gravel spit extending into the mouth of Grays Harbor. The landscape is raw and exposed — driftwood piles, beach grass, and an unobstructed view across the harbor to the open Pacific. It is one of the better birding spots in Grays Harbor County, particularly for shorebirds and raptors.

The point is accessible by a short trail from the parking area at the end of Marine View Drive. The walk takes about 20 minutes one way and is not difficult, though the gravel is rough underfoot and there is no shade. On clear days the view extends north to the Olympic Mountains. Harbor seals haul out on the gravel bars near the tip, and brown pelicans are a reliable summer presence offshore.
