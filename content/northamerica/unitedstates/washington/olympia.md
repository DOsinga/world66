Olympia is the capital of Washington State, sitting at the southern tip of Puget Sound. It is a small city — around 55,000 people — with a college-town vibe driven by the presence of Evergreen State College and a strong independent streak in its politics, art, and food scenes.

The domed [State Capitol](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/olympia/state_capitol) campus is worth a walk even if you have no interest in government — the grounds look out over Capitol Lake and the Olympic Mountains beyond. Downtown centres on a compact grid of local shops, coffee roasters, and restaurants. The [Olympia Farmers Market](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/olympia/olympia_farmers_market) runs from April through December on the waterfront and is one of the best in the Pacific Northwest.

Olympia makes a good base for exploring the southern reaches of the Olympic Peninsula. [Olympic National Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/olympicnationalpark) is about two hours' drive northwest. For a quick local curiosity, the [Mima Mounds](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/olympia/mima_mounds) southwest of the city are one of the Pacific Northwest's enduring geological mysteries.
