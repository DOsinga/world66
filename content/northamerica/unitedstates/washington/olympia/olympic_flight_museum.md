Olympic Flight Museum is a compact aviation collection at Olympia Regional Airport, with aircraft and displays tied to military and civilian flight. It is a good rainy-day option and a natural fit for travellers interested in machines and restoration work.

The museum is small enough for a focused visit, but special events and fly days can make it much more compelling. Check the calendar before going, especially if you hope to see aircraft outside static display mode.
