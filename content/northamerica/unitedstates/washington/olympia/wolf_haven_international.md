Wolf Haven International is a wolf sanctuary south of Olympia that cares for rescued wolves and wolfdogs. Visits are by guided tour, with a focus on the animals, their habitats, and wolf conservation.

It is a good short trip for wildlife-minded visitors, especially families who want a structured stop outside the city.
