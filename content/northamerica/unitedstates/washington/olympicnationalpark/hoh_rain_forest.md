The Hoh Rain Forest is Olympic's signature green cathedral: Sitka spruce, western hemlock, mossy maples, nurse logs, ferns, and a soft filtered light that makes the whole valley feel underwater.

The Hall of Mosses loop is short and deservedly famous, but the Hoh River Trail lets you keep walking deeper into the valley. Expect mud, crowds in peak season, and a landscape that makes both worthwhile.
