North Fork Skokomish River Trail is one of the main routes into Olympic National Park from the Staircase area. It follows river and forest into increasingly quiet country, with mossy lowland woods, bridges, gravel bars, and options for anything from a short walk to a serious backcountry trip.

The first miles are the most useful for casual visitors, especially if you want an Olympic forest hike without committing to the coast or high ridges. Conditions can be wet, bridges and trail sections can change, and longer itineraries need normal wilderness planning.
