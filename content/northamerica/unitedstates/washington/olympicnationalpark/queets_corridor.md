The Queets Corridor is one of Olympic National Park's quieter rain-forest approaches,
following the Queets River through huge trees, wet understory, and a sense of distance
from the park's busier loops. It is less convenient than Hoh or Quinault, which is
exactly why some travelers prefer it.

Conditions matter here: road washouts, river levels, and trail closures can change
plans. Check park information before driving in, and be prepared for mud and slow
travel. The reward is a wilder-feeling corner of the Olympic rain forest.
