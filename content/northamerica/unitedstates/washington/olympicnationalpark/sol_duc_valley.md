Sol Duc Valley is one of Olympic National Park's classic interior valleys, with old-growth forest, salmon streams, subalpine lakes, snowy peaks, and access to hot springs. It is a strong base for day hikes, especially if you want the park's deep green forest mood without heading all the way out to the coast.
