Ediz Hook is the long spit sheltering Port Angeles Harbor, with water on both sides and views across the Strait of Juan de Fuca toward Canada. It is a good low-key walk for beachcombing, birdwatching, and watching ships move in and out of the harbour.

The hook is especially pleasant when the Olympic Mountains are clear behind Port Angeles. Bring a wind layer: the same open water that gives the view can make the walk feel colder than town.
