Feiro Marine Life Center is a small marine science centre on the Port Angeles waterfront, with aquariums and touch tanks focused on local and Pacific marine life. It is a useful family stop before or after exploring tide pools, beaches, and the Olympic Peninsula coast.

The centre is compact, so plan it as a short hands-on visit rather than a full museum day. Its strength is close contact with local sea creatures and simple interpretation of the Strait of Juan de Fuca ecosystem.
