Forever Twilight in Forks Collection is the town's most direct nod to the books and films that made Forks famous far beyond the Olympic Peninsula. It sits in the Rainforest Arts Center and gathers costumes, props, signed pieces, and fan material in a free gallery.

Even if Twilight is not your thing, the collection is useful because it shows how completely the story changed the town's visitor economy. Fans should check dates for the annual Forever Twilight in Forks festival.
