John's Beachcombing Museum is exactly what the name promises, only larger and odder: decades of Pacific drift, from glass floats and bottles to buoys, nets, toys, and objects carried across the ocean by current and storm.

It works because it is personal rather than polished. The collection turns the wild coast near Forks into a story of tides, shipping, storms, and patient looking.
