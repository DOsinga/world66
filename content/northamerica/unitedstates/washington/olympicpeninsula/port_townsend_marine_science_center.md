The Port Townsend Marine Science Center is a small museum and aquarium focused on the marine and shoreline environment of Puget Sound and the Olympic Peninsula. Its exhibits sit by the water, with aquarium displays on the pier and natural-history interpretation on shore.

It is especially useful for families and visitors who want local context before a beach walk, wildlife cruise, or tidepool stop.
