The Puget Sound Coast Artillery Museum explains the military history of Fort Worden and the coastal defenses built to guard Puget Sound. Exhibits cover the artillery batteries, the soldiers stationed here, and the wider network of forts around the entrance to the sound.

It is a focused stop, best paired with a walk through Fort Worden State Park and its old batteries, parade grounds, and views across Admiralty Inlet.
