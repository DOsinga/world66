The Downtown Redmond Station opened in 2024 as the terminus of Sound Transit's 2 Line, the light rail extension that finally connects Redmond directly to Bellevue, Mercer Island, and Seattle. The station is adjacent to Redmond Town Center and effectively becomes the gateway to downtown Redmond for visitors arriving from the rest of the Seattle metro area.

The station is in an elevated design with good views over the Town Center area. For travellers, it removes the need for a car to reach Redmond from Seattle — the journey takes around 45 minutes from downtown Seattle, with transfers possible at Bellevue. The adjacent Marymoor Village Station, a short distance south, serves the park end of the city. Both stations are served by the same 2 Line and operate on the same fare system as the broader Link light rail network.
