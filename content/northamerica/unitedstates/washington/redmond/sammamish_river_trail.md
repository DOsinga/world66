The Sammamish River Trail is the most used recreational corridor in Redmond, popular with cyclists, runners, and walkers year-round. The paved surface is wide enough for both directions of travel without conflict, and the riverside setting — willow trees, waterfowl, and the quiet of the river — feels surprisingly removed from the suburban development on either side.

Bike rentals are available near the Marymoor Park entrance, and the trail is flat enough to be accessible to casual cyclists. The stretch between Marymoor and downtown Redmond is the most scenic, running along open meadows and the river's edge. In summer the river itself is shallow enough for wading in several spots, and great blue herons are a constant presence along the banks.
