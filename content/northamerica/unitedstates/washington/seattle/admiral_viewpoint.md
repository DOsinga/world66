Admiral Viewpoint is a small West Seattle overlook with a clean angle back toward the downtown skyline. It is the kind of place you visit for ten quiet minutes rather than a full outing, but the view over Elliott Bay is strong.

Bring a camera if the weather is clear. It pairs well with a drive or walk through Admiral and Alki, especially near sunset.
