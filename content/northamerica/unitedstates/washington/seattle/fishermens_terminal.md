Fishermen's Terminal is a working Seattle waterfront stop where visitors can walk the docks carefully and see commercial fishing boats, including parts of the Alaska fleet in the off-season. In season, some boats sell crab or fish directly, and the Fishermen's Memorial honors those lost at sea.
