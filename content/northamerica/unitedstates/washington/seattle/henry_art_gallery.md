The Henry Art Gallery sits on the University of Washington campus and focuses on modern and contemporary visual art. Its programme mixes work by established artists with changing exhibitions, installations, and shows connected to the university's art community.

It is a good reason to linger in the University District if your Seattle trip leans toward galleries rather than skyline views. Pair it with a walk through the campus, especially in cherry blossom season.
