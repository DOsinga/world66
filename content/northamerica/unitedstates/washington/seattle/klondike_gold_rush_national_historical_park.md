The Seattle unit of Klondike Gold Rush National Historical Park explains how the city became the main supply gateway for prospectors heading to the Yukon in 1897 and 1898. The free museum has photographs, artifacts, films, and ranger interpretation that connect the gold rush to Seattle's rapid growth.

It sits near Pioneer Square and works well before or after an underground tour, especially in summer when rangers may lead walks through the historic district.
