Lake Union Park gives Seattle a public edge on the south shore of the lake, with lawns, paths, boat activity, and skyline views. It is also the setting for the Center for Wooden Boats and the Museum of History and Industry, so it works well as a relaxed stop between museum time and a waterfront walk.
