Lakeview Cemetery is a hillside cemetery on Capitol Hill, incorporated in 1872 and laid out with views toward Lake Union and the mountains. Many early Seattle families are buried here.

The cemetery is also known for the graves of Bruce Lee and Brandon Lee, which draw a steady stream of visitors.
