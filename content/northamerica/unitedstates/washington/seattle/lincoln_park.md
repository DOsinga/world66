Lincoln Park is one of West Seattle's best green spaces, with wooded trails, picnic areas, and a pebbly beach facing Puget Sound. It sits beside the Fauntleroy ferry dock, but the park itself feels surprisingly wild once you are among the trees.

The shoreline is full of driftwood and views toward Vashon Island, while the upper paths are good for an easy walk without leaving the city. It is a strong choice when you want Seattle's water and forest in the same outing.
