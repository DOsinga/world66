Madison Park is a Lake Washington neighbourhood beach with lawns, swimming, and broad views toward the floating bridge and the Cascades. In summer it feels more like a local hangout than a formal sight, with families using the bathhouse and lifeguarded beach.

It is a good warm-weather stop if you want a quieter lakeside afternoon without leaving the city. The small commercial strip nearby makes it easy to pair the beach with coffee or a casual meal.
