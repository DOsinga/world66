The Museum of History and Industry, usually shortened to MOHAI, tells the story of Seattle and the wider Puget Sound region. Exhibits cover the city's maritime past, industrial growth, technology boom, and changing civic identity, with rotating shows alongside the permanent collection. Its South Lake Union location makes it easy to combine with a waterfront walk or other central Seattle sights.
