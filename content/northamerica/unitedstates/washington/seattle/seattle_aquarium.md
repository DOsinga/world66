Seattle Aquarium focuses on Puget Sound and Pacific Northwest marine life, with touch tanks, native fish, octopuses, seals, otters, and an underwater dome that puts visitors close to the animals of Elliott Bay. Its waterfront location makes it an easy pairing with Pike Place Market and the piers.

It is strongest for families and for travellers who want a better sense of the inland sea just below the city.
