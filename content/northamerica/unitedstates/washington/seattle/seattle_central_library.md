Seattle Central Library is a sharp glass-and-steel downtown landmark designed by Rem Koolhaas and OMA. It is still a working public library, but the building has become a destination for its angled exterior, high reading rooms, and spiralling internal layout.

Ride up, look back through the structure, then walk down through the Book Spiral. It is one of Seattle's best free architecture stops.
