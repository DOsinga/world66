The Seattle Great Wheel rises above Pier 57 on the central waterfront, putting Elliott Bay, the downtown towers, ferries, and the Olympic Mountains into one easy view. It is a touristy stop, but the ride is short and the setting does most of the work.

Go near sunset if the weather is clear. The gondolas are enclosed, which makes it a reasonable option even when Seattle is being Seattle.
