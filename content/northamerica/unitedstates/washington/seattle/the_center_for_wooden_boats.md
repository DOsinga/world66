The Center for Wooden Boats is a hands-on maritime museum on Lake Union, with classic wooden craft, workshops, and exhibits on small-boat culture. It is especially good if you want Seattle's watery side without just looking at it from shore: some programs put visitors close to the craft of building, repairing, and sailing traditional boats.
