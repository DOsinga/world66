Volunteer Park is Capitol Hill's main historic park, with Olmsted-designed grounds, a conservatory, and the Seattle Asian Art Museum. It is more than a neighborhood lawn: the park works as a combined garden, architecture, and museum stop.

Climb the nearby water tower if it is open, then pair the visit with a walk through Capitol Hill or the graves and views at Lake View Cemetery.
