Waterfall Garden is a pocket park in Pioneer Square, built around a 22-foot artificial waterfall pouring over granite boulders. It is small, shaded, and surprisingly calm for a downtown corner.

The park marks the site where James Casey and Claude Ryan started a messenger service in 1907, the business that later became UPS. It is best treated as a short pause while walking through the old commercial streets south of downtown.
