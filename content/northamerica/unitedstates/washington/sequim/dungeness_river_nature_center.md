The Dungeness River Nature Center sits at Railroad Bridge Park, where the Olympic Discovery Trail crosses the river on a restored trestle. It is a useful stop for birding, short walks, and understanding the watershed that made Sequim's farms possible.

It is not a headline sight like the spit, but it is a good change of pace between lavender farms and the coast, especially if you are cycling the trail.
