Olympic Game Farm is a drive-through animal park on the north side of Sequim. It has an unusual local history tied to trained film animals, and today visitors loop past bison, deer, llamas, bears, and other animals from their cars.

This is a selective stop. Families often enjoy it, but wildlife purists will prefer Dungeness or the Olympic trails.
