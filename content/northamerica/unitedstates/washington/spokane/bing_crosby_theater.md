The Bing Crosby Theater is a 1915 performing arts venue in downtown Spokane, renamed for the city's most famous son in 2006. The theater presents a varied program of music, comedy, and spoken word performances, with an intimate seated capacity of around 750. The original design by theater architect Edwin Houghton has been well preserved, with a warm interior well suited to amplified music.

Spokane's connection to Bing Crosby is something the city wears with pride. He was born in Tacoma but grew up here, and several local institutions keep the memory alive. If you're in town around Christmas, local radio stations still play his version of "White Christmas" with particular frequency.
