Cat Tales Wildlife Center is a small wildlife and education facility north of Spokane, best known for big cats and close-up animal viewing. It is more specific than a generic roadside zoo, and it can be a useful family-oriented stop for travellers with a car.

The centre is not a core Spokane landmark, so it should be chosen deliberately by visitors interested in wildlife education. Check hours and animal-care messaging before going, and treat it as a short outing rather than a full-day attraction.
