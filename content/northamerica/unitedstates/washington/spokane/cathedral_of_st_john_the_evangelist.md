The Cathedral of St. John the Evangelist stands above downtown Spokane on the South Hill, a serious Gothic Revival building in rough stone. Its scale and craftsmanship make it one of the city’s strongest architectural stops, especially if your Spokane visit has otherwise centred on the river and downtown.

Inside, look for the stained glass, carved details, and the way the nave gathers light. It is an active church, so visit respectfully and check for services or events. The cathedral pairs well with nearby Manito Park for a slower South Hill circuit.
