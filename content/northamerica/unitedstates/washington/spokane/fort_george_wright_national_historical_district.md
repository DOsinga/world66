Fort George Wright National Historical District preserves part of Spokane's former military post, later reused by educational institutions. The district's brick buildings, parade-ground layout, and layered campus history make it a worthwhile architectural detour west of downtown.

It is not a conventional attraction with a single ticketed entrance, so approach it as a self-guided walk and history stop. Travelers interested in Spokane's growth beyond the riverfront will find it adds useful context.
