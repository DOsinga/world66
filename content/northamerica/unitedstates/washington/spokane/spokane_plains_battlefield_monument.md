The Spokane Plains Battlefield Monument marks the site of the 1858 battle in which U.S. troops defeated a confederation of Spokane, Coeur d'Alene, Yakama, Palouse, and other Native groups. It is a modest stop, but a meaningful one for visitors tracing the region's military and Indigenous history.
