Fort Nisqually is the most atmospheric history stop in Tacoma: a recreated 19th-century Hudson Bay Company post set among the trees of Point Defiance Park. Costumed interpreters, working shops, and timber buildings make it easier to understand the fur-trade and farming world that existed here before Tacoma became an industrial port.

It is especially good for families, but not only for them. The site gives Tacoma a longer story than glass art and waterfront redevelopment, with enough hands-on detail to reward an hour or two before or after walking the park trails. Check the calendar for blacksmithing, cooking, and living-history days.
