The Museum of Glass is Tacoma's most visited attraction and one of the better purpose-built art museums in the Pacific Northwest. Arthur Erickson's tilted steel cone dominates the waterfront and contains the Hot Shop, a working glassblowing facility where artists demonstrate their craft — this is not a recorded demonstration but live work by resident and visiting glass artists, changed on a rotating schedule.

The galleries beyond the Hot Shop cover glass art from contemporary studio work to historical objects, with a collection that takes the medium seriously as both art and craft. Temporary exhibitions rotate throughout the year. The museum sits at the end of the Bridge of Glass, a pedestrian footbridge designed by Chihuly that connects the waterfront district to downtown — making the walk between the museum and Union Station one of the more visually dramatic short strolls in any American city.
