The Tacoma Art Museum (TAM) occupies a prominent corner near the Museum of Glass and Union Station, part of the concentration of cultural institutions that defines Tacoma's waterfront district. The collection spans American art with a deliberate emphasis on the Pacific Northwest: the holdings include Northwest Coast Indigenous objects, early 20th-century regional painting, and a significant collection of glass art that contextualises Tacoma's place in the studio glass movement.

The museum's temporary exhibition programme is active and well-curated. Combined with the Museum of Glass a few blocks south, TAM gives visitors a thorough day in Tacoma's arts district. Admission is moderate by urban museum standards; check for free days if budget is a concern.
