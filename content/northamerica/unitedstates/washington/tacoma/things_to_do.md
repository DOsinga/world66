title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tacoma's cultural scene punches above its weight, anchored by a cluster of museums on the waterfront and a lively gallery district downtown. The city's connection to glass art — courtesy of native son Dale Chihuly — runs deep, but there is plenty beyond blown glass to keep you occupied.

The [Museum of Glass](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/tacoma/museum_of_glass) is the flagship, with its working Hot Shop and major Chihuly installations. The [Bridge of Glass](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/tacoma/bridge_of_glass) connects it to [Tacoma Union Station](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/tacoma/union_station), another Chihuly site inside a Beaux-Arts federal courthouse. The [Tacoma Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/tacoma/tacoma_art_museum) and [Washington State History Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/tacoma/washington_state_history_museum) complete the downtown cultural district.

Further afield, [Point Defiance Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/tacoma/point_defiance_park) is one of the country's great urban parks — old-growth forest, zoo, historic fort, and Puget Sound waterfront in a single 700-acre peninsula. The [Dune Peninsula](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/tacoma/dune_peninsula) nearby is a remarkable environmental reclamation story. The [Tacoma Narrows Bridge](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington/tacoma/tacoma_narrows_bridge) is visible from Point Defiance and carries the story of the original 1940 collapse — one of the most consequential structural failures in engineering history.
