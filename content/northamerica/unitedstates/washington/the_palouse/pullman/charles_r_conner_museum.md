The Charles R. Conner Museum at Washington State University is a natural-history museum with a large collection of taxidermied birds and mammals from the Pacific Northwest and beyond. The displays range from small mammals to bison, making it an easy family-friendly stop on campus.

It pairs well with the nearby Jordan Schnitzer Museum of Art if you are exploring Pullman beyond the main university landmarks.
