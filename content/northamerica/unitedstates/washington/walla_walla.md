Walla Walla sits in southeastern [Washington](/northamerica/unitedstates/washington), close to the Oregon border and surrounded by wheat fields, vineyards, and low Blue Mountain foothills. It is famous for sweet onions, but the modern draw is wine: tasting rooms, vineyard drives, and a downtown that has grown into one of eastern Washington's more appealing small-city centres.

This is also a college town. Whitman College gives Walla Walla a leafy, walkable campus and a cultural life that feels larger than the population suggests. The city is relaxed but not sleepy, with restaurants, galleries, old brick storefronts, and enough wine traffic to keep weekends busy.

There is serious history here too. Fort Walla Walla and Whitman Mission point to the fur trade, missionary years, and violent colonial encounters that shaped the Columbia Plateau. A good visit balances the pleasant downtown with that harder regional story.
