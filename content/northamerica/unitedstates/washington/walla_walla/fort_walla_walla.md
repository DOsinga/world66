Fort Walla Walla is the city's main regional history museum, covering the military post, pioneer settlement, farming, and everyday life in the valley. It is the best single stop for understanding how Walla Walla became more than a wine-country name.

The museum is especially useful before driving around the valley. The landscape reads differently once you have a feel for the fur-trade routes, wagon roads, farms, and conflicts that shaped it.
