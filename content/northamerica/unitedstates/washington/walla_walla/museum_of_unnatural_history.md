The Museum of Unnatural History is a small Walla Walla gallery focused on Dadaist and Surrealist art, with unusual and sometimes unsettling objects mixed into the displays. It is a niche stop, but a memorable one for visitors who enjoy offbeat collections.

Some material may not suit children, so check before bringing younger visitors.
