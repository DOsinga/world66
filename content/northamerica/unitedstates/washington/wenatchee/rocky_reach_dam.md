Rocky Reach Dam is a working Columbia River hydroelectric project north of Wenatchee. Its discovery center explains the river, the dam, and the power system that serves much of the Pacific Northwest.

The most memorable part is the fish viewing room, where visitors can watch salmon moving through the passage system. It is an easy stop by car and works well with Ohme Gardens.
