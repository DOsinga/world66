---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Woodinville is about 30 km northeast of downtown Seattle, roughly a 30-minute drive when traffic cooperates. Highway 522 is the main route from Seattle; from Redmond and the Eastside, NE 124th Street connects to the area quickly.

By public transport, King County Metro Route 311 connects downtown Seattle to Woodinville. Sound Transit also provides service from the Eastside. Most visitors arrive by car, particularly for wine country touring where groups are common — several Seattle-based tour operators run weekend wine country excursions to Woodinville.
