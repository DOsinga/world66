The Central Washington Agricultural Museum in Union Gap is a good fit for Yakima because agriculture is the point of the valley. The displays include historic machines and buildings tied to farm work in Central Washington.

It is most rewarding if you like old equipment or want the practical side of the region's fruit, hop, and wine economy.
