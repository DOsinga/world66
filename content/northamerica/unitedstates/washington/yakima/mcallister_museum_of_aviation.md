McAllister Museum of Aviation grew out of a flight school opened in 1926 by Charlie and Alister McAllister. Wikivoyage notes that it became one of the longest-running flight schools in the Northwest before becoming a museum.

It is a specialist stop, but a good one if aircraft history interests you or if you are already near the airport.
