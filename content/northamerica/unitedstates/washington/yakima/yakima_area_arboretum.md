Yakima Area Arboretum is a living museum of trees, shrubs, grasses, herbs, and display gardens. In a valley known for dry heat and irrigated agriculture, the shade and plant collections are a welcome change of pace.

It is a gentle stop rather than a grand garden. Go for a quiet walk, especially if you have spent the day driving through open country.
