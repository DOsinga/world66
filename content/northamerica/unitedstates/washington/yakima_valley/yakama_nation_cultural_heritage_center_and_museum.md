The Yakama Nation Cultural Heritage Center in Toppenish is the valley's strongest introduction to Yakama history and culture. The museum focuses on the Plateau peoples, with exhibits on traditional life, treaty history, art, and the Yakama Nation today.

The wider complex includes a theatre, library, winter lodge, gift shop, and restaurant. It is the right first stop before exploring Toppenish's murals or the lower Yakima Valley.
