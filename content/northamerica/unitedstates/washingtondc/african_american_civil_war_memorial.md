The African American Civil War Memorial honours the United States Colored Troops who fought for the Union during the Civil War. The plaza is centred on Ed Hamilton's bronze sculpture, *The Spirit of Freedom*, and is ringed by stainless-steel plaques listing more than 200,000 soldiers and officers. It is a compact but meaningful stop near the U Street corridor.
