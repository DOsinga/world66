Arlington Memorial Bridge links the Lincoln Memorial with Arlington National Cemetery across the Potomac. Completed in 1932, it was designed as a symbolic line between North and South after the Civil War.

The bridge is most useful for visitors on foot or by bike moving between the National Mall and Arlington's memorials, with broad river views along the way.
