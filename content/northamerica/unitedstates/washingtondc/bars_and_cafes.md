---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Washington's bar scene is concentrated in a handful of neighbourhoods. Adams Morgan's 18th Street strip is the most reliably active on weekends. Dupont Circle has bars ranging from the neighbourhood standby [Fox and Hounds](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/foxandhounds) to more polished cocktail bars on P Street. U Street Corridor — historically significant as the centre of DC's Black music scene — has a mix of live music venues and craft beer bars that have transformed since the neighbourhood's renovation in the 1990s.

Georgetown's M Street and Wisconsin Avenue have bars that skew toward Georgetown University students and young professionals. Capitol Hill's Pennsylvania Avenue SE has a more neighbourhood feel — quieter, with a regular clientele of Hill staffers and local residents. [Kramerbooks](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/kramerbooks) in Dupont Circle stays open late and functions as much as a bar as a bookshop after 10pm.

For live music, the 9:30 Club (815 V Street NW) is the city's premier mid-size venue. The Kennedy Center has classical, jazz, and popular music in multiple halls.
