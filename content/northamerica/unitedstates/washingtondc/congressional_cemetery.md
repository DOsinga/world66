Congressional Cemetery is one of Washington's most interesting historic burial grounds, with graves and memorials tied to figures from American politics, music, journalism, and civic life. Its paths are open to visitors, making it a quieter East Capitol Hill stop beyond the Capitol and Library of Congress.
