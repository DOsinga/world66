D.A.R. Constitution Hall is a 1929 National Historic Landmark designed by John Russell Pope, near the White House and the National Mall. It was built for the Daughters of the American Revolution's annual convention and has also served as a major concert and broadcast venue.

The building is most useful to visitors as a historic architecture stop or when combined with the D.A.R. Museum, which displays decorative arts, quilts, ceramics, and period rooms.
