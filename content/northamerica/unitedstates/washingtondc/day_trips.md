Washington's position makes day trips easy. **Charlottesville, Virginia** (about 2 hours by Amtrak or car) holds Monticello, Jefferson's house, and the University of Virginia campus — two of the country's finest examples of Palladian-influenced American architecture. **Annapolis, Maryland** (45 minutes by car) is the state capital and a handsome colonial port town with the US Naval Academy and good seafood. **Mount Vernon**, George Washington's plantation estate on the Potomac south of the city, is accessible by boat from the southwest waterfront or by car and makes a worthwhile half-day trip.
