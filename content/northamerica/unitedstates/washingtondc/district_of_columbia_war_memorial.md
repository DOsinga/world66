The District of Columbia War Memorial is a small open-air Doric temple on the National Mall, dedicated to Washington, D.C. residents who served in World War I. It is quieter than the Mall's larger national memorials, which makes it a reflective stop near the Lincoln Memorial and Tidal Basin.
