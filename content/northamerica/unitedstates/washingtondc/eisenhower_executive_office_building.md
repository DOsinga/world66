The Eisenhower Executive Office Building stands beside the White House and is one of Washington's most elaborate 19th-century government buildings. Completed for the State, War, and Navy Departments, it is a massive Second Empire landmark with granite walls, mansard roofs, and heavy ornament.

The building is not a casual interior visit, but the exterior is worth pausing for while walking around the White House area.
