The Exorcist Steps are the steep staircase between Prospect Street and M Street in Georgetown, made famous by the 1973 film The Exorcist. They are also a popular local workout spot and a quick detour near the Key Bridge.

The stop is brief, but film fans will want the photo, and the stairs fit easily into a Georgetown walk. The best context is the surrounding streets, canal, and waterfront rather than the staircase alone.
