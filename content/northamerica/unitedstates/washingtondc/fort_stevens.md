Fort Stevens preserves the site of the 1864 Civil War battle at the northern edge of Washington, D.C., where Confederate forces under Jubal Early tested the capital's defenses. Earthworks, interpretive markers, and the small battlefield cemetery make it a worthwhile stop for Civil War history, especially when paired with nearby Rock Creek Park or Takoma.
