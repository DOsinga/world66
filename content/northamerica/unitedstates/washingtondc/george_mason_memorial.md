The George Mason Memorial is a quiet National Mall stop near the Tidal Basin, honoring the Virginia statesman whose ideas shaped the Bill of Rights. It is easy to miss, which is part of its appeal: a shaded bench, a reflective pool, and a slower moment between the Jefferson and Martin Luther King Jr. memorials.
