The George Washington University Museum and Textile Museum is one of Washington's best specialist museums, with strong collections of carpets, fabrics, and textile arts from around the world. The exhibitions are carefully presented and reward close looking.

It is a strong Foggy Bottom stop for visitors interested in design, craft, and material culture, and it pairs well with nearby memorials and university-area walks.
