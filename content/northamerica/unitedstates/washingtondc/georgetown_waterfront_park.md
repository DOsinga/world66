Georgetown Waterfront Park follows the Potomac River between Key Bridge and Washington Harbour. Lawns, water steps, an interactive fountain, and riverside paths make it one of Georgetown's easiest places to slow down between shopping streets and the canal.

The park is most useful as part of a Georgetown walk, with views toward Rosslyn and easy access to the riverside cycling path.
