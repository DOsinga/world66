The Kreeger Museum is a private house-museum with a strong collection of Impressionist and early modern paintings. The building itself is part of the draw, designed by Philip Johnson and Richard Foster as a modernist residence.

It is quieter than Washington's major Smithsonian museums and usually appeals most to visitors interested in art, architecture, or smaller collections.
