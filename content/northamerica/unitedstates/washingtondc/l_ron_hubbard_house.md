The L. Ron Hubbard House is a carefully maintained Dupont Circle townhouse linked to the founder of Scientology. Guided tours focus on Hubbard's biography and the church's preferred version of its own history, so it is best approached with a critical ear. It is a niche stop, but an unusually specific one for visitors interested in American religious movements.
