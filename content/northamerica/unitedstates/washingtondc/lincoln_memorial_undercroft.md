Beneath the Lincoln Memorial is a basement that most visitors never see. The undercroft was never intended to be part of the memorial experience — it served as a maintenance and storage space — but the NPS has opened it to tours as part of an ongoing effort to reveal the monument's hidden infrastructure.

The space is raw and unexpectedly atmospheric: concrete pillars, exposed stone, and the geological formations that grew in the damp interior over ninety years. Tours must be booked through the NPS in advance; they are free and limited in number. The undercroft tour adds a dimension to the Lincoln Memorial visit that the polished upstairs chamber does not provide — a reminder that these civic monuments are also physical structures with basements, drainage systems, and unexpected histories.
