The Franciscan Monastery of the Holy Land is a quiet Brookland landmark with an Italianate church, formal gardens, and a rosary portico lined with chapels. Its replica grottoes and holy sites can feel a little theatrical, but the grounds are peaceful and the church is genuinely handsome.
