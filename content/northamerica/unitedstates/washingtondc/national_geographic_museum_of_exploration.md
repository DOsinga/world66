The National Geographic Museum of Exploration brings the society's photography, field science, and exploration stories into a public museum near Dupont Circle. Expect immersive exhibits, big images, and a polished presentation aimed at curious adults as much as families.

It is not one of Washington's free Smithsonian museums, but it fills a different niche: less national collection, more storytelling about the planet and the people documenting it.
