The National Postal Museum is the Smithsonian's museum of mail, stamps, and communication. Its collections cover rare philatelic material, the history of mail delivery, and the ways postal systems shaped everyday life across the United States and beyond.

It sits by Union Station, making it an easy museum to add before or after a Capitol Hill visit.
