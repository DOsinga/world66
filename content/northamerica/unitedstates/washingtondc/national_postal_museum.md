The National Postal Museum is the Smithsonian's deep dive into mail, stamps, and the systems that moved messages across the country and the world. The rare-stamp collection is serious, but the museum is broader than philately: exhibits cover mail trains, postal routes, delivery technology, and the way mail shaped American life.

It sits beside Union Station, so it is easy to fit in before a train or on a Capitol Hill walk. The subject sounds narrow, but the best displays turn it into a surprisingly good history museum.
