Washington's National Theatre opened in 1835 and has long been one of the city's main stages for major touring productions. Presidents and well-known performers have passed through over the years, and today it is a regular host for Broadway shows, musicals, and other large visiting acts.

It is not a museum-style stop, but it is useful for visitors looking for an evening performance near the White House and the East End. Check the programme before planning around it.
