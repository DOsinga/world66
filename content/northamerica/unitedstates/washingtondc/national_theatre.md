Washington's National Theatre has been presenting performances since 1835, with presidents, touring stars, and big Broadway productions all passing through its auditorium. It is one of the city's main commercial stages rather than a museum piece.

Check the schedule if you want a theatre night close to the White House and downtown hotels. The building's long history gives it extra interest, but the reason to go is still the show on the night.
