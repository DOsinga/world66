These linked historic cemeteries in Georgetown are among the oldest surviving Black burial grounds in Washington, DC. They preserve the memory of enslaved Africans, their descendants, and free Black Washingtonians, and are a quiet but important stop for understanding the city's history beyond its monuments and federal buildings.
