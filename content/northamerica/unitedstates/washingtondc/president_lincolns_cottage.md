President Lincoln's Cottage is the former summer retreat where Abraham Lincoln and his family stayed from 1862 to 1864. Lincoln used the cottage as a refuge from the White House and drafted part of the Emancipation Proclamation here.

The house is now a museum focused on Lincoln's presidency, the Civil War, and the moral stakes of emancipation. It is away from the National Mall, but important enough to justify the detour for visitors interested in American history.
