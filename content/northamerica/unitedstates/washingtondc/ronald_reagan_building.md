The Ronald Reagan Building and International Trade Center is a large federal complex between Pennsylvania Avenue and the National Mall. Its public interior spaces, broad atrium, and location make it a useful architectural stop while walking between the museums and downtown.

It is not a museum in itself, but visitors can enter public areas, use the food court, and see the scale of one of Washington's biggest modern government buildings. Security screening and access rules can change.
