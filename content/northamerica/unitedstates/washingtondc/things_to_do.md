title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Washington's sights divide between the free national institutions of the [National Mall](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/the_national_mall) and the city's own neighbourhoods and history. Allow at least three to four days to do justice to both.

The Mall's free Smithsonian museums include the [National Gallery of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/national_gallery_of_art), the [National Air and Space Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/national_air_and_s), the [National Museum of Natural History](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/smithsonian_natural_history), the [National Museum of the American Indian](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/national_museum_of), the [Hirshhorn Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/hirshhorn_museum_a), the [Freer and Sackler Galleries](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/freer_sackler), the [National Museum of African American History and Culture](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/african_american_history_museum), and others.

The memorials and monuments — all free and open 24 hours — include the [Lincoln Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/lincoln_memorial), [Vietnam Veterans Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/vietnam_veterans_memorial), [Washington Monument](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/george_washington), [Jefferson Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/jefferson_memorial), [Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/martin_luther_king_memorial), [Korean War Veterans Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/korean_war_memorial), and the [World War II Memorial](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/world_war_2_memorial).

Beyond the Mall: [Georgetown](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/georgetown) for walking and the C&O Canal; [Dupont Circle](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/dupont_circle) for the [Phillips Collection](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/phillips_collection) and bookshops; [Capitol Hill](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/capitol_hill) for the [Eastern Market](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/eastern_market), [Library of Congress](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/library_of_congress), and Victorian streetscapes; [Adams Morgan](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/adams_morgan) for Ethiopian food and neighbourhood vitality. The [US National Arboretum](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/national_arboretum) and [Rock Creek Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/rock_creek_park) offer green space outside the formal monument circuit.

Across the Potomac in Virginia: [Arlington National Cemetery](/northamerica/unitedstates/washingtondc/arlington_cemetery) for the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier and Kennedy graves.
