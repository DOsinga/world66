The Titanic Memorial is a 1934 waterfront statue dedicated to the men who died on the Titanic so that women and children could escape in the lifeboats. The figure's outstretched arms make the memorial instantly recognisable, and the riverside setting gives it a quiet mood away from the busier monuments on the Mall.

It is a short stop rather than a destination by itself, but it fits naturally into a walk along the Southwest Waterfront or around The Wharf.
