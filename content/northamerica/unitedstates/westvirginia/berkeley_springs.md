Berkeley Springs is a small West Virginia town with an unusually clear identity: warm mineral water in the middle of town, old spa rituals, and Appalachian ridges rising close enough to make the streets feel tucked away. It has drawn bathers for centuries, including colonial visitors who helped turn the springs into one of the older resort traditions in the United States.

[Berkeley Springs State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/berkeley_springs/berkeley_springs_state_park) is the heart of town, with open spring pools, a Roman bath house, and the small [Museum of the Berkeley Springs](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/berkeley_springs/museum_of_the_berkeley_springs) upstairs. A few blocks away, the [Ice House](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/berkeley_springs/ice_house) and [Star Theatre](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/berkeley_springs/star_theatre) keep the town from feeling like a spa brochure.

The setting matters as much as the water. [Prospect Overlook](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/berkeley_springs/prospect_overlook) gives a fast view across three states, and [Cacapon Resort State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/berkeley_springs/cacapon_resort_state_park) turns Berkeley Springs into a good base for a day of walking, lake time, or ridge views.
