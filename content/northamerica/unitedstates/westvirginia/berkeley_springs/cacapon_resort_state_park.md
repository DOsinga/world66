Cacapon Resort State Park is the easiest way to add real outdoor time to a Berkeley Springs visit. The park covers more than 6,000 acres below Cacapon Mountain, with wooded trails, a lake, golf, and broad ridge scenery.

The resort label can make it sound more packaged than it is. Treat it as a mountain park with amenities: pick a trail, aim for a view, and let the forest reset the day after the compact spa town.
