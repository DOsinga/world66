---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Berkeley Springs sits on US 522 in West Virginia's Eastern Panhandle, about two hours by car from Washington, DC or Baltimore in normal traffic. The town is much easier with a car, especially if you plan to visit Cacapon Resort State Park or Prospect Overlook.

The nearest larger rail and air connections are outside town, so most visitors arrive by road from Maryland, Virginia, Pennsylvania, or the Shenandoah Valley.
