The Ice House is Berkeley Springs' main arts centre, run by the Morgan Arts Council in a former industrial building near the middle of town. It hosts galleries, concerts, classes, theatre, and a co-op shop, depending on the week.

It is worth checking even if you are mainly here for the springs. Small spa towns can become a little too polished; the Ice House gives Berkeley Springs a working local culture.
