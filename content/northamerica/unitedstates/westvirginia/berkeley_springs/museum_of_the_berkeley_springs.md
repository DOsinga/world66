The Museum of the Berkeley Springs sits above the Roman Bath House, which is exactly where it should be. Its exhibits explain why the springs mattered, how the town grew around them, and why people kept coming for the water long after medical fashion changed.

It is a compact stop, but it gives useful depth to the park below. Visit before or after a bath so the town feels less like a pretty spa stop and more like a place with a long memory.
