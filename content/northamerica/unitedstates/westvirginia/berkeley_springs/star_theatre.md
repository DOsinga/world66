The Star Theatre is a small downtown movie house with more character than its size suggests. It is part of West Virginia's Historic Theatre Trail and still operates as a local cinema, which makes it more useful than a preserved facade.

Check the schedule if you are staying overnight. A film here is a good low-key counterpoint to the baths, galleries, and mountain views.
