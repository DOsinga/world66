The Granada Theater is Bluefield's restored 1920s picture palace, a Commerce Street landmark that reopened after decades of decline. It began life as a vaudeville and movie house, later hosted major touring performers, and still carries the decorative confidence of the era when small Appalachian cities built theatres with real ambition.

Today it functions as a cinema and live-event venue, but the building itself is the reason to notice it. If you are walking Bluefield's historic downtown, the Granada gives the district a focal point and a reminder that coal-country prosperity once paid for more than banks and office blocks.
