---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Bluefield is a half-day place: a walk through the downtown commercial district, the East River Mountain overlook, and one or two of the surrounding curiosities — Gary Bowling's House of Art, the Bramwell millionaires' homes — and you've seen it. Combine it with a drive on the Coal Heritage Trail or a visit to Pipestem State Park for a more substantial stop.
