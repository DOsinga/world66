J.Q. Dickinson Salt-Works revives one of the Kanawha Valley's oldest industries on a family property southeast of Charleston. Brine from an ancient underground sea is brought to the surface and evaporated into small-batch salt, turning a once-industrial Appalachian staple into a modern food stop.

Tours and tastings make the visit more interesting than simply buying a jar. You learn why salt mattered so much to the valley's early economy, how brine shaped settlement and transport, and why chefs now care about the mineral character of a local salt. It is a good fit for travellers who like food with a sense of place.
