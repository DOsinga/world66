Wine Cellar Park is a small but characterful wooded park near Dunbar, west of Charleston. Its name comes from old stone cellar remains tucked into the landscape, giving the short trails a more memorable hook than a standard neighborhood green space.

It is a modest outing, best for travellers who want an easy walk, a local picnic stop, or a break from driving through the Kanawha Valley. Do not expect a major attraction, but the combination of woods and ruins just clears the bar for a worthwhile local detour.
