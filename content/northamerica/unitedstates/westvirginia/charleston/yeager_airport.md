Yeager Airport (CRW) sits on a flattened hilltop about three miles north-east of downtown Charleston — its short, plateau-edge runways are famous among pilots for the unusual approaches required. Daily commercial flights connect Charleston with Atlanta, Charlotte, Chicago, Washington (Dulles), Houston, and a handful of other Eastern hubs.

A taxi to downtown takes about 10 minutes and costs around $20–25; Uber and Lyft both serve the airport. There is no public bus.
