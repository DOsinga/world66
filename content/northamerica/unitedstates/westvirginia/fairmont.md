Fairmont sits at the confluence of the Monongahela, Tygart Valley, and West Fork rivers in the rolling hills of north-central West Virginia. It is the county seat of Marion County and a small, pleasant town of about 20,000 people with an outsized contribution to American food history.

The area is known for its Italian-American heritage — the pepperoni roll, West Virginia's official state food, was invented here by Italian immigrant Giuseppe Argiro in 1927. [Country Club Bakery](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/fairmont/country_club_bakery) still makes them to the original recipe, and they are exactly as good as anything that has been made since. [Prickett's Fort State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/fairmont/pricketts_fort), a few kilometres north of town, is a full-scale reconstruction of an 18th-century frontier fort and a genuinely good state park. The [Mon River Rail-Trail](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/fairmont/mon_river_trail) follows the Monongahela through town and connects to a longer network of river trails that cyclists find worthwhile.
