---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Fayetteville is a small town but the rafting and climbing economy keeps a few decent bars open all season. Most close earlier on weeknights and outside summer. **Freefolk Brewery** out on Route 16 brews on site (the saisons and IPAs are notably good) and runs a taproom with food trucks. **The Station**, in a converted gas station downtown, is a reliable late-night spot for cocktails and shared plates.

In winter, the social centre of town effectively becomes the Cathedral Café and the bars inside Secret Sandwich Society and Pies & Pints, which all stay open year-round.
