---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Huntington has a culinary culture that punches above its weight for a city of its size. The hot dog is a local obsession — the West Virginia-style hot dog with slaw and mustard is a regional staple, and the annual Hot Dog Festival draws visitors from across the state. Beyond that, there are proper steakhouses, long-established diners, and a few surprises.
