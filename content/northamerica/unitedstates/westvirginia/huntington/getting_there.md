---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Huntington sits on I-64, about 80 kilometres west of [Charleston](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/charleston) and roughly 150 kilometres from Lexington, Kentucky. By road it is accessible but the drive from any major city involves at least two hours on the highway.

Tri-State Airport (HTS) serves Huntington with limited commercial flights, primarily regional connections to Charlotte and Washington Dulles. Amtrak's Cardinal line stops at Heritage Station three times a week in each direction, with service to Chicago and Washington DC. The train journey to Washington takes around nine hours — slow but scenic through the Appalachian mountains.
