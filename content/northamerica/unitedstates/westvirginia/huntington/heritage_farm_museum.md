Heritage Farm Museum and Village, located a few kilometres east of downtown, recreates Appalachian rural life from the 19th and early 20th centuries. The outdoor complex includes a working blacksmith shop, a gristmill, a one-room schoolhouse, a country store, and several historic cabins — all staffed by interpreters who demonstrate period crafts and skills.

The indoor museum holds an impressive collection of farm equipment, folk art, and everyday objects from the region's past, including an extensive gallery devoted to early automobiles and motorcycles. It is the kind of place that appeals to children and adults equally — specific enough to be interesting, informal enough to be enjoyable. Allow at least two hours.
