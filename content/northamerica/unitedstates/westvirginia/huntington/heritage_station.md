Heritage Station is the restored Baltimore and Ohio Railroad depot in downtown Huntington, now converted into a mixed-use complex of restaurants, shops, and event spaces. The building dates to 1913 and is a handsome piece of Beaux-Arts civic architecture — red brick with limestone trim and a grand waiting hall interior.

The Amtrak station adjacent to Heritage Station still receives the Cardinal line three days a week, connecting Huntington to Washington DC and Chicago. Even if you are not catching a train, the building and its surrounding plaza make for a pleasant stop on any walk through downtown.
