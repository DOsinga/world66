The Huntington Museum of Art is the city's strongest cultural attraction and one of West Virginia's major art museums. Its collections include American and European art, decorative arts, glass, and rotating exhibitions, with enough variety to reward visitors who are not specialists.

The hilltop setting adds to the visit: trails, sculpture, and a plant conservatory make the museum feel connected to the surrounding landscape rather than isolated in a gallery district. It is a clear half-day stop for anyone passing through Huntington.
