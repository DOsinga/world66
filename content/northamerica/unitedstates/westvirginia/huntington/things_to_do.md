---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Huntington's attractions lean toward the authentic and the offbeat. The downtown area has a cluster of historic buildings, a beloved old theatre, and a restored train depot. A vintage amusement park, a sunken garden carved from a former quarry, and a museum of radio technology offer the kind of experiences that are harder to find in larger cities. The Ohio River waterfront is worth a walk at any time of year.
