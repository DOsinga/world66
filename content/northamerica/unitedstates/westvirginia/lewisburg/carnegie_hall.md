Lewisburg's Carnegie Hall is a regional arts centre in a 1902 Carnegie building, with galleries, concerts, theatre, classes, and film events. It gives the town a cultural weight that is unusual for a place this small.

Check the programme before you arrive. Even if there is no performance, the galleries and building are worth folding into a downtown walk.
