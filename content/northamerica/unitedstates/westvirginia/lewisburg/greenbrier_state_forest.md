Greenbrier State Forest protects more than 5,000 acres of wooded hills near Lewisburg and White Sulphur Springs. Come for hiking, picnics, a summer swim, or the mountain air around Kate's Mountain; it works best as a half-day outdoors break from the Greenbrier Valley towns.
