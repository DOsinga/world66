West Virginia University's Creative Arts Center is the campus hub for theatre, music, lectures, and art exhibitions. With several theatres and a performance hall, it gives Morgantown a steady cultural calendar beyond the bars and football weekends.

Check what is on before you go. Many exhibitions, lectures, and receptions are free and open to the public.
