Dorsey's Knob Park sits above Morgantown and gives the city one of its easiest hilltop views. Trails, open fields, and the Sky Rock overlook make it a useful outdoor break close to town.

It is not as wild as Coopers Rock, but it is far simpler to fit into a short stay. Go for sunset views, a quick walk, or a bit of space after time around the university and downtown.
