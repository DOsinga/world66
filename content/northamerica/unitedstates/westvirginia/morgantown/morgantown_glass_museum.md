Morgantown Glass Museum focuses on the glass industry that once helped define the city and the Monongahela Valley. Its collection includes thousands of pieces made by local firms such as Morgantown Glass Company, Seneca Glass, and Gentile Glass Company, with work dating from the late 19th century into the late 20th.

It is a useful stop for travellers who want a local story beyond the university and riverfront. The appeal is in the craft, patterns, and industrial history, so allow time to look closely rather than treating it as a quick display room.
