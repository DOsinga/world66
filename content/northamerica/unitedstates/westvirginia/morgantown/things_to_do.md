Morgantown's identity is inseparable from West Virginia University. The downtown campus is a handsome collection of late-Victorian brick buildings centered on Woodburn Circle, while the separate Evansdale campus upriver handles engineering, agriculture, and the health sciences. Between them, the city's unusual [PRT](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/morgantown/prt) system runs on elevated guideways — a working relic of 1970s transit experimentation that still carries students and curious visitors daily.

The [Monongahela River waterfront](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/morgantown/monongahela_waterfront) anchors the south edge of downtown, and the preserved [Old Stone House](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/morgantown/old_stone_house) near the river is one of the oldest standing structures in the state. On fall Saturdays, everything stops for [WVU football](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/morgantown/wvu_football) — game days are a civic spectacle worth experiencing even for those with no interest in American football.

[Coopers Rock State Forest](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/morgantown/coopers_rock) is the outdoors escape closest to the city, a 13-mile drive east on I-68 with dramatic canyon views and solid rock climbing.
