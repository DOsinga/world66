The Watts Museum at West Virginia University tells the story of the state's extractive industries through mining equipment, petroleum history, and engineering displays. It is a focused museum, but the subject is central to understanding Morgantown and West Virginia.

It is a practical campus-area stop for travellers interested in industry, labor history, or geology. Pair it with a walk around WVU, and check public hours because university museums can be closed during breaks.
