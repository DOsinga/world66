---
title: 'Getting There'
type: section
---

Parkersburg sits at the junction of **Interstate 77** and **US 50**, about 90 minutes north of [Charleston](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/charleston) and roughly the same south of Wheeling. Both are easy drives and most visitors arrive by car.

The nearest commercial airport is **Mid-Ohio Valley Regional Airport** (PKB) at Williamstown across the Ohio River, with a small daily service to Charlotte; **Pittsburgh** and **Columbus** are each about a two-hour drive and have far more flights. Greyhound runs intercity buses through Parkersburg on the Charleston–Pittsburgh corridor, with stops downtown.
