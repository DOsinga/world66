Snowshoe is a mountaintop resort area in the Allegheny Mountains of eastern West Virginia. It is not a traditional town, and that matters: come expecting a compact resort village, lift-served slopes, and a remote mountain setting rather than courthouse-square street life.

The place still earns a W66 page because the activities are strong. [Snowshoe Mountain Resort](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/snowshoe/snowshoe_mountain_resort) is one of the main ski addresses in the Southeast and Mid-Atlantic. In summer, the [Snowshoe Bike Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/snowshoe/snowshoe_bike_park) keeps the lifts working for downhill riders. Nearby, [Cass Scenic Railroad](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/snowshoe/cass_scenic_railroad) and [Green Bank Observatory](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/snowshoe/green_bank_observatory) make the wider Pocahontas County trip much more than a resort stay.

Snowshoe is seasonal and isolated. Bring offline directions, check conditions before driving up the mountain, and remember that mobile coverage in this part of West Virginia can disappear for long stretches.
