Green Bank Observatory is one of the strangest and best side trips in the Snowshoe area: a major radio astronomy site set in a landscape where radio interference is tightly controlled. The huge telescopes rise above quiet Pocahontas County fields, and tours explain why this remote valley matters to science.

The quiet-zone rules are part of the experience. Expect restrictions on phones and digital devices near sensitive equipment, and check tour availability before driving over.
