Kumbrabow State Forest sits high on Rich Mountain, with cool hardwood forest, picnic areas, fishing, and a small network of hiking trails. Mill Creek Falls is the easiest target, but the real reason to come is the quiet mountain-forest setting, especially in summer when the higher elevation takes the edge off the heat.
