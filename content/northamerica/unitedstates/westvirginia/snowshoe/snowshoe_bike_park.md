Snowshoe Bike Park is why the mountain stays interesting after the ski season. The resort runs lift-served downhill trails with a serious reputation, including race events and enough terrain for both cautious first-timers and riders who came for rocks, roots, and speed.

It is a real bike park, not just a few paths through the woods. Rent proper gear, take a lesson if you are new to downhill riding, and expect weather to change quickly at elevation.
