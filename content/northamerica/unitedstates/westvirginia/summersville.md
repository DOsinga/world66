Summersville is a small town on US-19 in central [West Virginia](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia), useful at first glance and better than that once you get off the highway. Its real claim is the water and rock just south of town: [Summersville Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/summersville/summersville_lake), the state's largest lake, backed by cliffs and the Gauley River gorge.

The town works as a one-day base for a very West Virginia mix of lake swimming, sandstone climbing, whitewater, and Civil War history. [Carnifex Ferry Battlefield State Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/summersville/carnifex_ferry_battlefield) sits on the rim of the Gauley canyon, while the [Gauley River National Recreation Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/summersville/gauley_river_national_recreation_area) starts below Summersville Dam.

Summersville itself is not a polished mountain resort. It has highway services, a courthouse square, and practical places to eat. But if you are already crossing West Virginia and have a spare day, the lake and gorge make stopping here a much better choice than driving straight through.
