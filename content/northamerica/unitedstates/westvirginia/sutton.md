Sutton is a small Braxton County town just off I-79 in central [West Virginia](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia). It can look like a highway stop at first glance, but the old courthouse town, nearby lake, Civil War ground, and local UFO legend give it enough odd texture for a spare day.

Start in the [Sutton Downtown Historic District](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/sutton/sutton_downtown_historic_district), where brick storefronts and county buildings still hold the main street together. Around the corner, the [Flatwoods Monster Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/sutton/flatwoods_monster_museum) turns one of West Virginia's strangest 1950s stories into a small but memorable stop.

The outdoor reason to come is [Sutton Lake](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/sutton/sutton_lake), an Elk River reservoir with boating, fishing, and wooded coves just outside town. If you are interested in Civil War history, [Bulltown Historic Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/westvirginia/sutton/bulltown_historic_area) adds preserved earthworks and a battlefield trail a short drive away. Sutton is not polished, but it rewards travelers who like Appalachian county seats with a few unusual stories attached.
