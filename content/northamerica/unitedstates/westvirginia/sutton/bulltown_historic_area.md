Bulltown Historic Area preserves the site of an 1863 Civil War battle in the hills north of Sutton. The stop includes battlefield ground, earthworks, historic structures, and a trail that explains why this quiet valley mattered during the war.

It is a short drive rather than a walk from Sutton, but it pairs well with Sutton Lake or Burnsville Lake if you are making a full Braxton County day. Check seasonal opening details before going, as visitor facilities are not always open year-round.
