Landmark Studio for the Arts is Sutton's small community arts centre, housed in a converted Victorian-era church near the historic downtown. Its programme mixes plays, musicals, dinner theatre, readings, and occasional exhibits.

Check the schedule before making a special trip. If something is on while you are in town, it is one of the better ways to see Sutton as a living community rather than just a preserved courthouse square.
