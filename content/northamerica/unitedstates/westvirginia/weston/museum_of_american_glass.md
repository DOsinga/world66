The Museum of American Glass is a small-town museum with a specialist's obsession. Its rooms cover American glass from factory tableware to decorative pieces, with special attention to West Virginia's own glass industry. If you have ever wondered why pressed glass, tumblers, paperweights, and advertising pieces matter to collectors, this is the place that makes the case.

It is not a slick museum, and that helps. Cases are packed, labels can feel old-fashioned, and the pleasure is in slowing down enough to notice patterns, colours, makers, and oddities. Pair it with the asylum and Weston becomes more than a one-building stop.
