Stonewall Jackson Lake gives Weston a soft landing after the asylum's heavy history. The lake sits a short drive south of town, with wooded shorelines, marina access, trails, fishing, and the broad, quiet water that north-central West Virginia does well.

It is most useful if you have a car and a loose afternoon. You do not need to stay at the resort to appreciate the landscape; even a short drive around the lake or a walk near the state park changes the mood of a Weston visit.
