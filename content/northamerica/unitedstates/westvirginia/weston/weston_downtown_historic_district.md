Weston's downtown historic district is the useful pause between the town's two museums. It is a compact run of brick storefronts, civic buildings, and side streets that still reads as a county-seat centre rather than a highway strip. Walk it slowly and the town feels less like a single-attraction stop.

The architecture is modest but coherent: commercial blocks, older houses, and courthouse-town details set into the hills of Lewis County. It is best treated as a short wander before lunch or after the asylum tour, not as a destination on its own.
