Eckhart House is a restored Victorian house in Wheeling, a city whose older neighborhoods still show traces of its 19th-century prosperity. The building's domestic scale makes the history feel personal: parlors, woodwork, and decorative detail rather than grand civic architecture.

It is a gentle stop for travelers interested in old houses or Wheeling's heritage. Opening arrangements can be limited, so check ahead, but the house adds a human layer to a city often seen through bridges, riverfronts, and industrial history.
