The Oglebay Institute Glass Museum tells Wheeling's glassmaking story through thousands of pieces made between the early 19th and mid-20th centuries. The showpiece is the enormous Sweeney Punch Bowl, a five-foot cut-crystal bowl that feels half artwork, half industrial boast. It pairs well with a visit to Oglebay Park.
