The Toy and Plastic Brick Museum is a specialist collection near Wheeling built around construction toys, plastic bricks, models, and playful display worlds. It is niche, but that specificity is exactly the appeal for families, collectors, and anyone travelling with children who need a break from historic houses.

Treat it as an offbeat add-on rather than a core Wheeling landmark. If you enjoy model layouts and brick-built creations, it can be more memorable than its modest exterior suggests.
