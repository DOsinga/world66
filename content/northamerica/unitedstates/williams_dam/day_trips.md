---
title: Day Trips
type: section
---

Williams Dam makes a quiet base from which to explore the karst country of southern Indiana. **Spring Mill State Park** at Mitchell, 15 minutes south, has a restored pioneer village around a working water-powered grist mill, several show caves of its own, and a museum to astronaut Gus Grissom — Mitchell's most famous son. **Bedford**, 15 minutes east, sits at the heart of the limestone-quarrying district that supplied the stone for the Empire State Building and the Pentagon. **Bloomington**, 45 minutes north, is the university town and the natural cultural anchor for the region.

The **Hoosier National Forest**, much of which lies within a half-hour drive, has hundreds of miles of trails for hiking, mountain biking, and horseback riding.
