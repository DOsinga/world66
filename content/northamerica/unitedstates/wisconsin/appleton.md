Appleton sits on the Fox River in the heart of northeast Wisconsin's Fox Cities, a cluster of communities that together make up one of the state's most livable metro areas. [Lawrence University](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/appleton/lawrence_university), a small liberal arts college with a well-regarded conservatory of music, gives the downtown an energy and cultural life you might not expect from a city this size. Appleton is also the birthplace of Harry Houdini, and the [History Museum at the Castle](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/appleton/history_museum_at_the_castle) keeps that connection very much alive with a substantial permanent exhibition.

The revitalized College Avenue downtown has a good mix of local restaurants, coffee shops, and the [Fox Cities Performing Arts Center](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/appleton/fox_cities_performing_arts_center). The Fox River runs through the middle of everything, providing trails and parkland. Appleton also sits on a genuinely interesting piece of technological history: the [Vulcan Street Plant](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/appleton/vulcan_street_plant) here was the world's first hydroelectric central power station, and the [Hearthstone Historic House](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/appleton/hearthstone_historic_house) it powered was the first home in the world lit by central hydroelectric electricity — both in 1882. The [Gordon Bubolz Nature Preserve](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/appleton/gordon_bubolz_nature_preserve) on the city's northern edge offers nearly 800 acres of trails and wetlands.
