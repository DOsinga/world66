The Paper Discovery Center is a waterfront museum in an old paper mill, focused on the industry that shaped Appleton and the Fox River Valley. The exhibits explain paper-making in a hands-on way, including the chance to make a sheet yourself.

It is a good family stop and a useful piece of local context: paper is not just a historical footnote here, but one of the reasons Appleton grew into a manufacturing town.
