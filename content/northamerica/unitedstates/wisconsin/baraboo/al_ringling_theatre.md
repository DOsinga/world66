Al. Ringling Theatre is the handsome downtown counterpoint to Circus World. Built in 1915 by Albert Ringling, it still anchors the town square with a grand interior more ornate than you expect in a small Wisconsin town.

Check the calendar before you go. A performance or tour gives the building more life than a quick look at the facade.
