Circus World is Baraboo's signature sight. It occupies part of the Ringling Brothers' former winter quarters, where the circus grew from a small wagon show into a national touring machine.

The museum is at its best when you slow down: painted parade wagons, old posters, Ringling-era buildings, and summer big-top performances make the place feel like a working memory rather than a generic collection.
