The International Crane Foundation is a quiet but memorable stop north of town. Its Baraboo headquarters focuses on crane conservation and gives visitors the rare chance to see all 15 crane species in one place.

The grounds are laid out for slow walking rather than spectacle. It pairs well with Devil's Lake if you want the day to lean toward wildlife and landscape rather than circus history.
