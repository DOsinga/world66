Ochsner Park & Zoo is a small family stop in Baraboo with prairie dogs, capuchin monkeys, llamas, black bears, white-tailed deer, and birds. It is not a big-city zoo, but it makes an easy, low-key break if you are travelling with children or already visiting Baraboo.
