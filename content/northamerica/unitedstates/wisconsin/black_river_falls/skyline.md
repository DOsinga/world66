An 18-hole championship course open to the public, halfway between Minneapolis and Madison along I-94. The clubhouse and pro shop stay open year-round, and the course is set up to handle groups of all sizes. Nothing fancy, but a solid round in pleasant Wisconsin hill country.

The course plays through rolling wooded terrain typical of this part of west-central Wisconsin. Cart rentals are available and the facility is accessible to beginners and casual players as much as serious golfers. Tee times can be booked in advance by phone or walk-on during quieter periods.
