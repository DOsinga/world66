Paul Bunyan Logging Camp, now tied to the Wisconsin Logging Museum, is an outdoor history stop in Carson Park. Its cabins, tools, and camp buildings turn Eau Claire’s lumber-era past into something more tangible than a paragraph in a local-history display.

The Paul Bunyan name adds a bit of roadside Americana, but the better reason to go is the regional story. Eau Claire grew around timber, rivers, and rail connections, and the camp gives families and history-minded travellers a compact way to see that working world.
