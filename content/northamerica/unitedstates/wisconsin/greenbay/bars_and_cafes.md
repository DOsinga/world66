---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Green Bay's bar scene runs heavily on the Wisconsin staples: the Brandy Old Fashioned, beer (local and craft), and the Friday fish fry which crosses over from restaurant to bar territory. The Broadway District downtown has the main concentration of bars, and the riverfront sees action in summer. On Packers game days, the whole city seems to transform into one enormous tailgate party.
