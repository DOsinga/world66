Green Bay sits at the base of the Door Peninsula, one of Wisconsin's most scenic areas, and makes a good base for exploring it. The peninsula extends 70 miles northeast into Lake Michigan, flanked on the west by the bay and on the east by the open lake, with a series of small towns, state parks, orchards, and wineries along both coasts. [Sturgeon Bay](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/sturgeon_bay) is the nearest Door County town, about 45 minutes north. Peninsula State Park, near the village of Fish Creek further up the peninsula, is a 3,776-acre park with limestone bluffs, bike trails, and camping.

The Oneida Reservation, immediately west of Green Bay, is worth a half-day visit for its casino complex (if that's your thing), the Oneida Nation Museum, and the community's farm markets.
