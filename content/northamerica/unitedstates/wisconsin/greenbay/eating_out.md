---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Green Bay has a surprisingly strong food scene for a mid-sized Midwest city, with a cluster of good restaurants in the Broadway District downtown and newer spots along the riverfront. The local specialty to try is the Friday night fish fry — a Wisconsin institution — typically featuring beer-battered cod or perch. Brandy Old Fashioneds (Wisconsin's unofficial state cocktail) are on every bar menu.
