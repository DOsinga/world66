Hazelwood Historic House Museum is an 1830s Greek Revival house that helps tell Green Bay's early settlement story. Period rooms, local collections, and riverside history make it a quieter complement to the city's sports and railroad attractions.

The visit is modest in scale, but worthwhile if you like historic houses or want a more local view of Green Bay. It works best as part of a short circuit with downtown and the Fox River rather than a stand-alone excursion.
