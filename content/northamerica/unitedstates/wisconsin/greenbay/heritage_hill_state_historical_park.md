Heritage Hill State Historical Park is an outdoor living history museum on the south side of Green Bay, bringing together 26 historic buildings from across Wisconsin into a single walkable site. The structures represent four periods: Native American, early colonial French, frontier, and turn-of-the-century agricultural life. Staff in period clothing demonstrate crafts and skills, and the collections include everyday objects from each era.

The fur trade era exhibits are particularly strong, reflecting the Fox River valley's central role as a highway for the French and then British fur trade from the 1600s through the early 1800s. Green Bay (originally called La Baye or Fort Howard) was one of the oldest European settlements in Wisconsin, and the French colonial context here is part of the city's story that often gets overshadowed by the football narrative. Heritage Hill takes it seriously.
