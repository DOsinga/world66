The NEW Zoo gives Green Bay a solid family stop away from the football and riverfront sights. It is set within the Brown County Reforestation Camp, which makes the grounds feel more open and wooded than a purely urban zoo.

The collection is broad enough for a half-day visit, with North American animals, big cats, primates, and kid-friendly exhibits. It is north of the city, so it pairs naturally with outdoor time in the surrounding recreation area rather than a tight downtown walking route.
