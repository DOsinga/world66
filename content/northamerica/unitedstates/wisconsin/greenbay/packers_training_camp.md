Packers Training Camp is one of Green Bay's most distinctive seasonal rituals. Practices draw fans to watch the team work, and the surrounding traditions make it feel less like a private sports session than a civic gathering.

It only matters if you are in town at the right time, usually in summer before the NFL season. Check the official schedule, arrive early for popular sessions, and treat it as a way to understand how deeply the Packers are woven into Green Bay's identity.
