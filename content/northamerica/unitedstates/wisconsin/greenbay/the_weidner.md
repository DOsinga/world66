The Weidner is Green Bay's main performing-arts center, located on the University of Wisconsin-Green Bay campus. It gives the city a formal venue for concerts, touring shows, lectures, dance, and community performances beyond the Packers-focused visitor circuit.

Travellers should check the calendar rather than visit for the building alone. When there is a worthwhile performance, The Weidner can turn a Green Bay stay into an evening cultural outing.
