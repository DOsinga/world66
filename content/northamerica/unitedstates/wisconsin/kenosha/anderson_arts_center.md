Anderson Arts Center is a small art museum on the Kemper Center campus, focused on local and regional artists. The building itself, completed in 1931, is part of the draw, with French Renaissance Revival details that give the visit more character than a simple gallery stop.

It is best for visitors already exploring Kenosha's lakefront and museum cluster, or anyone looking for a quieter art stop away from the main downtown museums.
