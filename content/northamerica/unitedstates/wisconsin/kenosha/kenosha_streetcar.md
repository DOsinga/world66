Kenosha's streetcar is a short downtown loop, but it gives the lakefront a bit of old-city charm that the surrounding suburbia lacks. Restored electric cars circle between the Metra station, HarborPark, and the museum district, making it the easiest way to stitch the main sights together.

Ride it for the experience rather than speed. The route is only about two miles, and the best use is to hop off for the museums, the harbour walk, and downtown cafes.
