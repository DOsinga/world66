---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Kewaskum is a base for the surrounding Kettle Moraine landscape — kettle lakes, glacial ridges, and the Ice Age Trail that runs the length of the moraine. The village itself is small (around 4,000 people), with a quiet main street and a couple of family-run restaurants, but the outdoor offer in every direction is the reason most people stop.
