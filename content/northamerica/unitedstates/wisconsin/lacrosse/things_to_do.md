---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

La Crosse packs a lot into a small city: bluff hikes with sweeping Mississippi views, a genuine Oktoberfest with real German heritage behind it, and a riverfront that invites walking and cycling. The oddball attractions — a 25-foot statue of Hiawatha, the world's largest six-pack — sit alongside serious natural scenery in the bluffs.
