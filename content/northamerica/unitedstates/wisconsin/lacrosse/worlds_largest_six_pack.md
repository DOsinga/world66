Six giant beer storage tanks painted to look like Old Style cans tower over the City Brewing Company complex on the south side of downtown. At a combined capacity of 688,200 gallons, they hold the Guinness World Record as the world's largest six-pack. The claim is legitimate — the math has been verified.

City Brewing occupies the historic Heileman brewery complex, a reminder that La Crosse was once a significant player in American brewing. The facility is one of the largest contract brewing operations in the country now, producing beers for dozens of brands. You can't tour the facility, but the outside view of the giant cans along South Third Street is free and genuinely impressive in a deeply American roadside-attraction way. It's the kind of sight that makes you glad someone thought to do it.
