Madison is the capital of Wisconsin and the most pleasant small city in the upper Midwest — a university town of about 270,000 people squeezed onto a narrow **isthmus** between two lakes, with the [Wisconsin State Capitol](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/madison/wisconsin_state_capitol) at one end and the University of Wisconsin at the other. The whole of downtown fits on the isthmus, which makes it unusually walkable, and [State Street](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/madison/state_street), the pedestrianised mile that links the Capitol to the University, is one of the nicest downtown streets in America — a continuous run of bookshops, coffee places, student bars, ice-cream parlours and live-music venues.

The **Wisconsin State Capitol**, a National Historic Landmark, is modelled on the U.S. Capitol in Washington and is the only state capitol in the country built of granite. Its enormous dome is the fifth-largest in the world and the only one topped by a figure (*Wisconsin*, the gilded bronze at the summit). Free tours run throughout the day, and the building is genuinely impressive inside. The [Monona Terrace](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/madison/monona_terrace), the convention centre at the south edge of downtown overlooking Lake Monona, was designed by **Frank Lloyd Wright** in 1938 and finally built in 1997; its rooftop garden is a particularly good free view. On Saturday mornings from spring to autumn, the [Dane County Farmers' Market](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/madison/dane_county_farmers_market) — one of the largest producer-only farmers' markets in the country — encircles the Capitol and is a ritual for much of the city.

The University of Wisconsin campus spreads along the south shore of **Lake Mendota**, with the [Memorial Union Terrace](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/madison/memorial_union_terrace) (sunburst-yellow and orange deckchairs facing the water) one of the most beloved public spaces in the state on a summer evening. The free [Chazen Museum of Art](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/madison/chazen_museum_of_art) and the [UW-Madison Geology Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/madison/geology_museum) are both on campus. Madison is a notoriously bikeable city, with over 200 km of bike paths, and a reliably good food scene that punches well above its size — the cheese, obviously, but also farm-to-table places, lively Southeast Asian and Mexican neighbourhoods, and some of the best craft beer in the Midwest. The [Henry Vilas Zoo](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/madison/henry_vilas_zoo) and the [University of Wisconsin Arboretum](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/madison/uw_arboretum) give the south side of the city its own draw. Not a destination most foreign travellers go out of their way for, but one of the great small American cities if you find yourself nearby.
