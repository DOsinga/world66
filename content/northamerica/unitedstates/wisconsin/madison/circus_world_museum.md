Circus World Museum is in Baraboo, the historic home base of the Ringling Brothers, and makes a worthwhile excursion from Madison for travellers interested in American popular entertainment. Its collection of circus wagons, posters, costumes, and performance material gives the place a stronger identity than a generic small-town museum.

The museum works best as a deliberate day trip, especially for families or anyone following Wisconsin's offbeat history. Seasonal programming can change the feel of a visit, but the permanent circus heritage is the reason it clears the travel threshold.
