The L. R. Ingersoll Physics Museum is a hands-on science museum on the University of Wisconsin-Madison campus. Its exhibits demonstrate basic ideas in mechanics, waves, electricity, magnetism, and atomic physics, with a few historical instruments mixed in.

It is a good short stop for families and science-minded visitors already exploring the campus area near the Chazen Museum of Art and Memorial Union.
