Overture Center is Madison's main performing arts complex, home to ballet, opera, symphony, repertory theatre, and touring shows. It is also worth a look between performances for its galleries, the attached Madison Museum of Contemporary Art, and a rooftop garden that adds a bright stop along State Street.
