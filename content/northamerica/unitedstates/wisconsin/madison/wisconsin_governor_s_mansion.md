The Wisconsin Governor's Mansion sits on Lake Mendota in Maple Bluff, just outside central Madison. The residence is an official working house, but it also opens for tours that show the public rooms, state furnishings, and a quieter side of Wisconsin political life.

It is best for travelers who enjoy civic architecture or state history. The lakeside setting is handsome, and the visit pairs naturally with other Madison stops around the Capitol, university, and the lakefront.
