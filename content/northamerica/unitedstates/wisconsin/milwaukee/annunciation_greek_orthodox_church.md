Annunciation Greek Orthodox Church is one of Milwaukee's most distinctive modern religious buildings. Designed by Frank Lloyd Wright and listed on the National Register of Historic Places, it is worth seeking out for its low dome, circular plan, and unusual late-Wright interpretation of Byzantine church forms.
