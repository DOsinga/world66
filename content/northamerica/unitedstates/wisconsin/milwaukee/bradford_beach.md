Bradford Beach is Milwaukee's classic city beach: broad sand on Lake Michigan, volleyball courts, concessions in season, and a crowd that makes it feel more urban festival ground than remote shoreline. It is one of the easiest ways to understand how much Milwaukee turns toward the lake in summer.

The water can be cold even when the beach is lively, so many visitors come as much for sun, people-watching, and the lakefront walk as for swimming. Combine it with Lake Park or the East Side if you want a fuller afternoon outdoors.
