Bradley Symphony Center is the home of the Milwaukee Symphony Orchestra, housed in a restored Art Deco movie palace. The renovation kept the building's historic theatre character while adapting the hall for orchestral concerts and other performances.

It is most worthwhile if you can attend a concert, but architecture fans may also want to look for tour opportunities.
