The Charles Allis Art Museum occupies a Tudor-style mansion and combines art, decorative objects, and domestic architecture. The building itself is a major part of the visit, showing the taste and collecting habits of an early twentieth-century Milwaukee family.

It is smaller and quieter than Milwaukee's main art museum, which is part of its appeal. Visitors interested in historic houses, design, or neighborhood culture will get the most from it.
