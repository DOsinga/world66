Lake Park is Milwaukee's great bluff-top park, designed with the broad, civic confidence associated with Frederick Law Olmsted's firm. It sits above Lake Michigan with lawns, wooded ravines, bridges, walking paths, and views that make the city feel more coastal than many visitors expect.

Do not treat it only as green space. The North Point Lighthouse, old stonework, and curving drives give the park architectural presence, while the ravines make even a short walk feel pleasantly removed from the city grid. It is one of Milwaukee's best free breaks between museums, breweries, and lakefront sightseeing.
