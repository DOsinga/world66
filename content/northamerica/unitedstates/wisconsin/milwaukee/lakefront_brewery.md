Lakefront Brewery is a modern Milwaukee beer stop with enough personality to sit beside the city's older brewing history. Founded in Riverwest in 1987, it helped push Milwaukee beyond its big-brewery reputation and into the craft era while keeping the mood unpretentious.

The tour is the draw: part brewery explanation, part local comedy routine, with samples and a beer hall to linger in afterwards. It is still a commercial brewery, of course, but for visitors trying to understand Milwaukee through beer, Lakefront feels like a live chapter rather than a museum label.
