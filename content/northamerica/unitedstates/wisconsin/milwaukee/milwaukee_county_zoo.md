Milwaukee County Zoo is a large, long-established zoo west of downtown, with broad animal collections and enough ground for a half-day family outing. It is one of the city's major child-friendly attractions outside the lakefront museum cluster.

The zoo is easiest by car and best when you are not rushing between central sights. Check seasonal hours and special exhibits, since the experience varies with weather.
