The Mitchell Park Domes are among Milwaukee's most recognizable modern landmarks: three
glass conservatory domes containing desert, tropical, and seasonal plant worlds. The
architecture is part of the attraction, giving the gardens a retro-futurist profile
unlike a conventional greenhouse.

They are useful year-round, especially in winter when warm, humid plant rooms feel like
a small escape. Pair the Domes with other west-side or downtown Milwaukee stops, and
check current restoration or access notes before going.
