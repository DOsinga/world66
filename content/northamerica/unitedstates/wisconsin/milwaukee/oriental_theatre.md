Milwaukee's Oriental Theatre is one of the city's great surviving movie palaces, famous for its ornate interior and atmospheric design. It remains a working cinema, which makes the architecture feel lived-in rather than fossilized.

The best way to see it is to attend a film, but architecture fans can still appreciate the facade and lobby when access is available. It is a strong East Side cultural landmark and a good evening option.
