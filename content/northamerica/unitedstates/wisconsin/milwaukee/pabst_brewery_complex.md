The Pabst Brewery Complex is a substantial remnant of the brewing industry that shaped Milwaukee's identity. Its brick brewhouses, offices, and converted industrial buildings sit close to downtown, giving the area a different texture from the lakefront museums and riverwalk.

Even if you are not taking a beer tour, the complex is worth a look for its architecture and urban history. It shows how brewing once operated at city scale, and several buildings have been adapted for new uses without erasing the industrial character that made the district distinctive.
