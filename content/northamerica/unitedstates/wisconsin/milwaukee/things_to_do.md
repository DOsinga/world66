title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Milwaukee's standout attraction is the [Milwaukee Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/milwaukee/milwaukee_art_museum), whose Calatrava-designed Brise Soleil — a set of movable white wings that open and close over the lakefront — is one of the most striking pieces of architecture in the Midwest. The [Milwaukee Public Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/milwaukee/milwaukee_public_museum) is a dense, old-school natural history museum with the best display of its kind in Wisconsin. On the north side, the [Pabst Mansion](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/milwaukee/pabst_mansion) and the [Pabst Theater](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/milwaukee/pabst_theater) are monuments to the beer-baron era that shaped the city. The [Milwaukee Riverwalk](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/milwaukee/milwaukee_riverwalk) follows the river through downtown and is the best way to see the city on foot. Out in [Bay View](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/milwaukee/bayview), the neighbourhood on the Lake Michigan shore south of downtown, you will find the city's liveliest concentration of independent bars and restaurants.
