Veterans Park is part of Milwaukee's lakefront chain, a wide open space between the city and Lake Michigan. It works because it is simple: lawns, paths, water, skyline views, and easy access from the museum district.

Come here to decompress after the art museum or to follow the shoreline north and south. In good weather the park fills with kites, bikes, and picnic blankets, giving Milwaukee's lake edge its everyday civic life.
