Villa Terrace Decorative Arts Museum is one of Milwaukee's most atmospheric house museums, set in an Italian Renaissance-style villa overlooking Lake Michigan. The architecture, terraces, and gardens are as important as the decorative arts collection inside.

It is a strong East Side stop for visitors who enjoy historic houses, design, and lakefront neighborhoods. Pair it with nearby Lake Park, the Milwaukee Art Museum, or a walk through the city's older residential streets.
