Oshkosh sits on the west shore of Lake Winnebago, but its name travels much farther than the lake. To pilots, homebuilders, and anyone who loves aircraft, "Oshkosh" usually means [EAA AirVenture](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/oshkosh/eaa_airventure), the huge summer fly-in that briefly turns Wittman Regional Airport into one of the busiest places in aviation.

The aviation pull is not just one week a year. The [EAA Aviation Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/oshkosh/eaa_aviation_museum) is a strong year-round anchor, with historic, experimental, military, and homebuilt aircraft that explain why this city matters to flying culture. Away from the airport, Oshkosh has a quieter Fox River and Lake Winnebago side, with parks, old industrial buildings, and a downtown that feels more local than polished.

The best spare-day version of Oshkosh combines the aviation museum with the [Paine Art Center and Gardens](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/oshkosh/paine_art_center_and_gardens), a Tudor-style estate with art galleries and formal gardens. Add the [Oshkosh Public Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/oshkosh/oshkosh_public_museum) or a walk in [Menominee Park](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/oshkosh/menominee_park), and the city easily clears the bar for travelers already moving through northeast Wisconsin.
