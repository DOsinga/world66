EAA AirVenture is the reason Oshkosh is known in hangars around the world. For one week in late July, the airport fills with aircraft, campers, builders, pilots, mechanics, and spectators, and the daily airshow becomes only part of the point.

Come for the scale: warbirds, homebuilt aircraft, vintage planes, forums, evening talks, and long rows of machines you may never see together anywhere else. If aviation is even a passing interest, AirVenture can turn Oshkosh from a stop into the destination.
