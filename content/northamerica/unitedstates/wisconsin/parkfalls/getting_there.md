---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Park Falls is in northern Wisconsin, about 120 miles southeast of Duluth, Minnesota, and 260 miles north of Milwaukee. There is no commercial air service; the closest airports with regular flights are Rhinelander-Oneida County Airport (about 60 miles east) and Duluth International Airport. Most visitors drive, taking US-8 from the east or the various state highways from the south.

The town has a small municipal airport (KPKF) for private and charter aircraft.
