The Smith Rapids Covered Bridge spans the South Fork of the Flambeau River near Fifield, about 10 miles south of Park Falls. It's a handsome structure in a beautiful setting — a dark wooden tunnel over fast-moving water, surrounded by forest. The bridge is open to traffic and you can also walk across it; the river below is clear and cold, popular with canoeists.

The surrounding area is part of the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest. A short trail leads from the bridge along the river bank, and there is a small picnic area. It makes a good stop on any drive south from Park Falls toward Medford or Phillips.
