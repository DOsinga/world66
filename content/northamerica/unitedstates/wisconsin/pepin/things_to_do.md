type: section
---

Pepin's appeal is built on two things: the water and Laura Ingalls Wilder. Lake Pepin offers sailing, kayaking, and fishing in summer; the Laura Ingalls Wilder Museum and the cabin site north of town draw literary pilgrims year-round. The [Great River Road](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/pepin/great_river_road) runs right through town, making Pepin a natural stop on a longer Mississippi River drive.
