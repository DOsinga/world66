Racine sits on Lake Michigan between Milwaukee and Chicago, with enough of its own identity to reward a stop. The city mixes industrial history, lakefront parks, a working marina, and one exceptional architectural reason to come: Frank Lloyd Wright's Johnson Wax Headquarters.

The best day starts with a reserved tour of [Johnson Wax Headquarters](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/racine/johnson_wax_headquarters), then moves downtown to the [Racine Art Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/racine/racine_art_museum) and the harbour. If you have more time, [Wind Point Lighthouse](/northamerica/unitedstates/wisconsin/racine/wind_point_lighthouse) gives the shoreline a proper landmark.

Racine is not just a service city between bigger names. It has a compact set of sights that make sense together, plus a local food obsession with kringle that gives even a short visit a clear taste of place.
