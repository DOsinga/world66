Johnson Wax Headquarters is Racine's standout sight. Frank Lloyd Wright designed the complex for S.C. Johnson, and the result is one of the great pieces of 20th-century corporate architecture in the United States.

Tours are not casual walk-ins, so reserve ahead and check the schedule. If you can make the timing work, this is the anchor that turns Racine from a pleasant lake city into a real destination.
