Reefpoint Marina is where Racine opens up to the water. The riverwalk, boats, and shoreline views make it an easy place to understand the city's Lake Michigan setting.

In summer the nearby Festival Hall area often adds music, events, and fireworks. Even without an event, it is a good reset between downtown sights.
