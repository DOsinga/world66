---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Sheboygan is about 60 miles north of Milwaukee on I-43 — roughly an hour's drive. From Chicago it's a little over two hours. There is no passenger train service to Sheboygan; a car is the practical necessity.

Milwaukee Mitchell International Airport is the closest commercial airport, with connections across the Midwest and to major hub cities. Green Bay's Austin Straubel International Airport is an alternative from the north.
