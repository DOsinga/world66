Ben Bikin' is the large unicyclist-style penny-farthing rider greeting visitors near Water Street and Highway 16. It is pure roadside theatre, but it neatly sums up Sparta's claim to be the Bicycling Capital of America.

You will not spend long here. Stop for the photo, then head for the trail.
