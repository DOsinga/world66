American Players Theatre stages classical drama — heavy on Shakespeare, but also Molière, Chekhov, Wilde and contemporary work — in two purpose-built theatres in the wooded hills south of Spring Green. The original Up-the-Hill amphitheatre seats around 1,100 under the open sky; the newer indoor Touchstone Theatre seats 200 for more intimate productions.

The season runs roughly from June through early October. Bring layers — Wisconsin summer nights cool off quickly — and arrive at least an hour before curtain to enjoy the pre-show picnic atmosphere. Tickets sell out for popular plays; book a month or more in advance for weekend performances.
