Unity Chapel sits beside a small country cemetery on the gentle ridge just south-east of Taliesin. It is a tiny shingled Gothic-Revival building, austere outside and unexpectedly warm inside with a panelled ceiling and oak benches. The exterior is by Silsbee; the interior is Wright's earliest credited architectural work.

The chapel can be visited as part of certain Taliesin estate tours, and the surrounding Wright-Lloyd Jones family cemetery contains the headstones of generations of the family, including the marker (now empty) where Wright was originally interred. A quiet, atmospheric ten-minute stop on a longer Wright pilgrimage day.
