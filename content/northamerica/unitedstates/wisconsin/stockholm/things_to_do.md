---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Stockholm punches well above its weight for a village of a hundred people. The Great River Road runs right through, bringing cyclists and road-trippers who stop for the views over Lake Pepin, the galleries clustered along Main Street, and the short hike up to Maiden Rock Bluff. Most visitors spend a few hours; those who linger a day or two find a surprising amount here.
