Peninsula State Park is one of Door County's big outdoor draws, spread across a wooded peninsula that pushes into Green Bay. Come for lake views, beaches, steep bluffs, trails, campgrounds, an amphitheater, and the observation tower, which gives the park its easiest wide-angle view.
