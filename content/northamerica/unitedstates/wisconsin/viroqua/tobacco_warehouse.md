A large brick warehouse on Decker Street, built in the early 20th century by the Northern Wisconsin Cooperative Tobacco Pool. For decades, Vernon County's small organic-style tobacco farmers brought their cured leaf here to be graded, baled and shipped to cigar-makers on the east coast. The building is on the National Register and a tangible reminder that this was once Wisconsin's tobacco country, before the crop disappeared in the late 20th century.
