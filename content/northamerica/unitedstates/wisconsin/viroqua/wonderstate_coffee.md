Wonderstate (formerly Kickapoo Coffee) was founded in Viroqua and roasts beans here for cafes across the Midwest. The Main Street flagship is a bright, modern cafe with very good espresso, batch brew, pastries from local bakers, and a few seats outside in summer. It is the obvious morning coffee stop and a reliable wifi hangout the rest of the day.
