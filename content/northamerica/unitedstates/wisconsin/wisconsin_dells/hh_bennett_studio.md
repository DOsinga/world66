H.H. Bennett Studio is the quiet counterweight to the water parks. Bennett's 19th-century photographs of the river gorge helped turn the Dells from a lumber town into a tourist destination.

The restored studio covers his life, his images, and the early machinery of photography, including the story of the revolving solar printing house he designed to follow the sun.
