Mt. Olympus is one of the big family draws in Wisconsin Dells, mixing water slides, wave pools, go-karts, roller coasters, and Greek-themed kitsch. It is a full-day stop rather than a quick ride, and it fits best if your trip is built around the Dells' water-park scene.
