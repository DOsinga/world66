Noah's Ark is the best-known water park in Wisconsin Dells and a useful shorthand for what the town has become: big, loud, family-oriented, and proudly excessive.

It covers more than 70 acres, with slides, pools, and lazy rivers. If you are coming for the water-park side of the Dells, this is the obvious place to start.
