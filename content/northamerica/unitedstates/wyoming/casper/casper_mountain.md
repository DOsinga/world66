A long, forested ridge rising 2,500 feet directly above town — the visual backdrop to every street
in Casper. The drive up Casper Mountain Road takes about 25 minutes and opens onto a small ski hill
(Hogadon), looping campgrounds, and a network of trails through pine and aspen.

The Bridle Trail loops 4.5 miles across the upper mountain, easy and shaded. In winter the
cross-country ski trails are groomed; in summer the mountain is up to 10°C cooler than the city
below, which is why every Casper family has at least one barbecue scheduled up here on the
hottest weekends.
