A locally owned steakhouse on the east side, doing solid wood-fired ribeyes, prime rib, and bison
burgers. The dining room is dark and a little cavernous in the American chophouse style, but the
beef is good and the local-flavour touches (Wyoming lamb, native trout) are well executed.

This is a sensible upgrade from the downtown bar-and-burger options when you want a real dinner.
Book ahead on Friday and Saturday; lunch is much easier.
