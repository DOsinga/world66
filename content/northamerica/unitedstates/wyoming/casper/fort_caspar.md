A reconstructed army fort and museum on the south bank of the North Platte, on the spot where the
old emigrant trails crossed the river. The original log buildings are gone, but careful 1936
recreations stand on the original footings — soldiers' quarters, stables, telegraph office, and
the Mormon Ferry boat.

The adjoining Fort Caspar Museum covers prehistoric peoples, the trails era, the 1860s army post,
and Casper's later oil-town history. Inexpensive admission, mostly outdoor, a good two hours.
Especially worth the visit if travelling with kids.
