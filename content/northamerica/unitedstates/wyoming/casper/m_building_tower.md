Two oddities side by side downtown: a bulbous reflective-glass office tower the locals call the
Onion, and a squat lattice-frame building everyone calls the Egg Beater. Built in the 1980s as part
of an ambitious downtown redevelopment, they have remained beloved or hated depending on whom you
ask.

Worth a quick look in passing for the contrast with the brick storefronts of the historic
district one block over. The plaza between the two buildings is a reasonable spot to sit with a
takeaway coffee.
