A modern art museum housed in a converted 1920s power-station building two blocks off the river.
The Nic, as everyone calls it, focuses on contemporary regional artists with rotating shows in
five gallery spaces. The permanent collection covers 20th-century western American art at a level
serious enough to surprise out-of-state visitors.

The Discovery Center upstairs is one of the better hands-on art spaces for kids in the region —
a working pottery wheel, costume area, and changing themed installations. Inexpensive admission,
closed Mondays.
