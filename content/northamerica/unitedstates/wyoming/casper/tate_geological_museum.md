The Tate Geological Museum is a compact but rewarding stop on the Casper College campus. Its collections focus on Wyoming geology, fossils, and minerals, with enough local material to make the region’s deep time feel close rather than abstract.

The star is Dee the Mammoth, but the museum is also useful before heading out toward Independence Rock, Hell’s Half Acre, or other landscapes around Casper. It gives you the vocabulary for what you are seeing: ancient seas, uplift, erosion, and the fossil record under the high plains.
