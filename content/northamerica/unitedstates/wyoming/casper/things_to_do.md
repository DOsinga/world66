---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The North Platte River anchors the city — fly fishing in the cool months, paddling and trail
running in summer. Above town, Casper Mountain offers cross-country skiing in winter and shaded
ridge walks in summer. Downtown holds a small but interesting set of historic buildings, and the
National Historic Trails Interpretive Center on the north edge of town is the headline museum.
