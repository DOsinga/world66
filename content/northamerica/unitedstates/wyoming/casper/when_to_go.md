---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Summer (June through August) is the easiest visit — long days, warm but rarely humid afternoons,
and the river and Casper Mountain at their best. Wildflowers peak in June. July can be very hot
in the city; head up the mountain to escape.

September brings cooler evenings, golden aspen, and the best stretch of trout fishing on the
North Platte. Winters are long and seriously cold — January highs hover around -3°C and the wind
makes the rest feel worse — but the cross-country skiing on Casper Mountain is excellent. The
city briefly drew worldwide attention on 21 August 2017 when it sat directly in the path of a
total solar eclipse; the next chance won't come around for centuries.
