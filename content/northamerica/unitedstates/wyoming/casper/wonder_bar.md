A no-nonsense downtown saloon that has been pouring drinks since the 1930s. Pool tables, juke
box, and an easy local crowd at the bar. The food side is workmanlike steakhouse — the steak
sandwich is the order — and the drink list is uncomplicated beer and whiskey.

It is the obvious first stop on any night out downtown. Live music several nights a week, often
country or rockabilly, and a happy hour that fills the place by 5pm.
