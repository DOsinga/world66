Centennial is a tiny mountain town west of [Laramie](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/laramie), sitting where the plains start climbing into the Snowy Range. The town itself is only a handful of streets, but the setting is strong enough to carry it.

In summer and early autumn, the reason to come is the [Snowy Range Scenic Byway](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/centennial/snowy_range_scenic_byway), which climbs past lakes, trailheads, and high granite views toward Medicine Bow Peak. In winter, [Snowy Range Ski Area](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/centennial/snowy_range_ski_area) keeps the town useful after the high road closes.

Centennial is not a resort town in the polished sense. It is a base camp with a few places to eat, a small museum in the old depot, and immediate access to [Medicine Bow National Forest](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/centennial/medicine_bow_national_forest). If you have a spare day in Laramie, this is one of the easiest ways to trade cafe time for mountains.
