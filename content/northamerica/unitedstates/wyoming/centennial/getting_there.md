title: Getting There
type: section
---

Centennial is on Wyoming Highway 130, about 30 miles west of [Laramie](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/laramie). The road from Laramie to Centennial is open year-round, but the high Snowy Range section west toward Saratoga usually closes in winter.

You need a car. This is a mountain gateway town, and the best sights sit along the highway and forest roads beyond the centre.
