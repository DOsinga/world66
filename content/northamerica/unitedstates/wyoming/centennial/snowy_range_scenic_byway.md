The Snowy Range Scenic Byway is Centennial's great asset. West of town, Highway 130 climbs quickly from foothills into Medicine Bow National Forest, passing lakes, picnic areas, trailheads, and views toward the pale granite of the Snowy Range.

The drive is short enough to treat as an outing from Laramie, but it deserves stops. In good weather, build in time for Lake Marie, Libby Flats, and at least one short walk. The high section is seasonal, so check road conditions if you are travelling outside summer.
