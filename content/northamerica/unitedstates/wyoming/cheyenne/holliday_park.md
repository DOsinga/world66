Holliday Park, on the east side of town, contains one of the last surviving Union Pacific Big Boy steam locomotives — number 4004, retired in 1962 and parked here as a memorial in 1963. It is a magnificent and mostly unguarded thing — you can walk straight up to the wheels, taller than a person, and read the maker's plate on the side. The cab is locked but a ladder lets you peer in.

The rest of the park is a flat, lake-sided municipal green space with a children's playground and the city's main outdoor pool. A short drive or 30-minute walk east of downtown. Free; always open.
