type: section
---

Cody lives in Buffalo Bill's shadow — he founded the town, named the hotel after his daughter, and remains its most bankable identity — but there is more here than the mythology. The Buffalo Bill Center of the West is a genuinely serious museum complex. The Cody Nite Rodeo is a real competitive event. The drive to Yellowstone through the Wapiti Valley is one of the best approaches to any national park in the country.
