The Tower Trail Walk circles the base of Devils Tower and gives the monument its best close-up. The loop is short and paved, with changing views of the fluted rock columns as you move through the ponderosa pines and boulder fields.

Interpretive signs explain the geology and the continuing cultural importance of the tower to Native nations. It is the essential walk here, and it is worth taking slowly rather than treating it as a quick photo stop.
