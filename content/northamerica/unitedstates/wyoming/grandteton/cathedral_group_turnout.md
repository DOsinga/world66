Cathedral Group Turnout is a roadside viewpoint with a direct look at the central Teton skyline. The view takes in Teewinot Mountain, Grand Teton, and Mount Owen, the trio that gives the Cathedral Group its name.

It is a short stop rather than a hike, but it is useful for orientation and photography, especially early or late in the day when the light gives the peaks more shape.
