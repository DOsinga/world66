Chapel of the Sacred Heart is a small Catholic chapel near Jackson Lake Junction, built in a rustic log style that suits the national park setting. It is simple and quiet, with more charm than grandeur.

Stop if you are already driving the Jackson Lake area. The chapel makes a peaceful pause between viewpoints, especially when the park roads are busy.
