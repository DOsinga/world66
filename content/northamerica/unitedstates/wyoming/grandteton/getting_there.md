---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Jackson Hole Airport sits inside the valley just south of the main park roads, making Grand Teton unusually easy to reach by air. Many visitors also arrive by car from Yellowstone, Idaho's Teton Valley, or Salt Lake City.

US-26/89/191 runs through the valley. In summer, expect traffic near Jenny Lake and the main viewpoints; in winter, road access is more limited.
