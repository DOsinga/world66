Hidden Falls is a popular waterfall above the west shore of Jenny Lake. The route is short if you use the boat shuttle and more satisfying if you walk the lake trail.

It is not hidden in the sense of being uncrowded, but the cascade is handsome and the approach gives a quick taste of the mountain forest. Continue toward Inspiration Point if you want a broader view.
