Jenny Lake is the park's most beloved lake, and for good reason. The water sits tight against the mountains, with trails, boat shuttles, and views that make the Tetons feel close enough to touch.

Walk part of the lake loop, take the shuttle boat across for a shorter hike to Hidden Falls, or linger near the shore early in the morning. Midday summer crowds are heavy, but the setting survives them.
