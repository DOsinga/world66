Paintbrush Canyon is one of Grand Teton's classic canyon hikes, usually approached from the String Lake trailhead north of Jenny Lake. The route climbs through forest and open alpine terrain toward Holly Lake, with big views and a much wilder feel than the easier lake walks.

The out-and-back to Holly Lake is about 12.4 miles round trip with substantial elevation gain, so it is a real day hike rather than a casual stroll. Start early, carry layers and water, and expect the final miles to feel strenuous.
