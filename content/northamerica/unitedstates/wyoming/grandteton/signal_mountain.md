Signal Mountain is one of Grand Teton's easiest high viewpoints. A narrow paved road climbs through the forest to a parking area near the summit, where a short walk opens up a broad view over Jackson Hole, Jackson Lake, and the Teton Range.

The drive is slow and winding, but that is part of the appeal. Go early or late for cleaner light and less traffic, and allow time to stop at the overlooks rather than treating the summit as a quick photo errand.
