---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Jackson has by far the best restaurant scene in Wyoming — a resort town with a wealthy clientele demands good food, and the town delivers. The range runs from truck-stop bison burgers to sophisticated Rocky Mountain cuisine, with several restaurants genuinely worth seeking out.

**The Handle Bar** at the Four Seasons in Teton Village serves bison short ribs and elk tenderloin in a rustic-luxe ski lodge setting — expensive but the quality is consistent. **Local Restaurant & Bar** on North Cache is a reliable mid-range option with a strong focus on locally sourced beef, game, and trout. For a classic Wyoming experience, the **Cadillac Grille** on the town square has been a local institution for decades with an eclectic menu and an unmissable neon sign. Breakfast in Jackson is taken seriously — the **Jedediah's House of Sourdough** has been serving enormous sourdough pancakes since 1967.

Note that most restaurants are closed or reduced-hours in the shoulder seasons (May and November). Reservations are essential in summer and ski season.
