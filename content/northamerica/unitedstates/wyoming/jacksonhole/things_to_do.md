---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Jackson Hole is primarily an outdoor destination, and the landscape is the main event in every season. In summer, Grand Teton National Park offers world-class hiking, climbing, and wildlife viewing — moose, bears, and wolves are regularly spotted along the Snake River and in the Oxbow Bend area. In winter, Jackson Hole Mountain Resort draws serious skiers for some of the steepest lift-served terrain in North America. The town of Jackson provides food, gear, and culture on the days when the weather grounds you.
