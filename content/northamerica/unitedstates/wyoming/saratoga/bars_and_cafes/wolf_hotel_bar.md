---
title: Wolf Hotel Bar
type: poi
---

The Wolf Hotel bar occupies the ground floor of one of Wyoming's best-preserved Victorian buildings, and it has been serving cold beer and whiskey since the 1890s. Saloon-style, unhurried, and genuinely old — not a recreation. The bar is the social centre of a small ranch town in the evenings, and the building itself is worth seeing even if you just stop in for one drink.
