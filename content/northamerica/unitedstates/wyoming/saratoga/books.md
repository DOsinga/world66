**Close Range: Wyoming Stories** by Annie Proulx — the definitive literary voice of this landscape. Hard, spare short fiction set in Wyoming ranch country; some of the best American writing of the past thirty years. Read it before you come and the sagebrush flats will look different.

**Rising from the Plains** by John McPhee — superb long-form journalism about Wyoming geology and the ranch families who have worked this land for generations. McPhee makes the geology feel like drama. Quiet and essential.

**Teewinot: A Season in the Grand Tetons** by Jack Turner — Wyoming mountain writing at its best. Turner spent decades as a climbing guide in the Tetons and writes about the high country with precision and feeling.

**Wyoming Fishing Guide** by Ken Retallic — the practical companion for anyone planning serious time on the North Platte or other Wyoming waters. River by river, season by season.
