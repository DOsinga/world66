---
title: The Wolf Hotel
type: poi
---

The Wolf Hotel is a Victorian-era building that has been at the centre of Saratoga since 1893. The dining room serves Wyoming standards — steaks, burgers, and local trout — in a setting that has fed travellers and ranch hands for well over a century. The food is unfussy and the atmosphere is genuinely historic rather than decoratively so. A town landmark worth having a meal in regardless of where you are staying.
