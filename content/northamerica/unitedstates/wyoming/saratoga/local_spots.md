A few small restaurants and diners around town serve Wyoming comfort food — chicken-fried steak, green chilli, trout pulled from the North Platte. Unpretentious and filling, the kind of places where regulars outnumber visitors and the coffee is always on. Good for breakfast before a day on the river or a warm meal after a winter soak in the Hot Pool.
