title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Sheridan's appeal divides neatly between town and mountains. Downtown has a well-preserved Main Street with some genuinely characterful stops: the [Sheridan Inn](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/sheridan/sheridan_inn), a National Historic Landmark where Buffalo Bill once held cattle auditions on the veranda; [King's Saddlery and Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/sheridan/kings_saddlery), a working saddlery with a legendary collection of Western artefacts; and the [Mint Bar](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/sheridan/mint_bar), Wyoming's most famous saloon, open since 1907.

The [Trail End State Historic Site](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/sheridan/trail_end), a Flemish Revival mansion built by cattle rancher turned Governor John Kendrick, gives a vivid picture of the Gilded Age Wyoming ranching elite. The [Brinton Museum](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/sheridan/brinton_museum) in nearby Big Horn — a short drive south — is an outstanding collection of Western and Native American art in a handsome ranch setting.

For outdoor activity, the [Bighorn National Forest](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/sheridan/bighorn_national_forest) rises directly to the west, with the [Tongue River Canyon](/northamerica/unitedstates/wyoming/sheridan/tongue_river_canyon) trail offering spectacular limestone canyon hiking just a short drive from town.
