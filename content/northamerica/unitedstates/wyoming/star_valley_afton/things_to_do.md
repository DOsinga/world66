---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Star Valley's main draws are natural and unhurried. The Periodic Spring in Swift Creek Canyon is the headline attraction — genuinely odd and worth the short hike to reach it. Afton town centre has a walkable main street and the famous elk antler arch. The surrounding mountains offer fishing, hiking, and in winter, snowmobiling on hundreds of kilometres of groomed trails.
