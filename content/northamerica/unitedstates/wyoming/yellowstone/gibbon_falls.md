Gibbon Falls drops about 84 feet over the rim of the Yellowstone Caldera, beside the road between Madison Junction and Norris. It is one of the park's easiest waterfall stops: a short pullout and viewing area give a clear look at the cascade and the volcanic rock wall across the road.
