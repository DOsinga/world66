Heart Lake lies in the southern part of Yellowstone, west of Lewis Lake and south of Yellowstone Lake. It is a backcountry destination in the Snake River watershed, with mountain scenery and a quieter feel than the park's roadside basins.

Reaching it takes planning and a proper hike, but it rewards visitors looking for a less crowded Yellowstone lake and wilderness setting.
