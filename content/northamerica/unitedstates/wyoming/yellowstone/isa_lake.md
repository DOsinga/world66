Isa Lake sits on the Continental Divide at Craig Pass in Yellowstone. Its odd claim to fame is that it drains naturally toward two oceans in the opposite direction from what you might expect: the east side toward the Pacific and the west side toward the Atlantic.

It is a quick roadside stop rather than a long hike, but the hydrological quirk makes it more interesting than its small size suggests.
