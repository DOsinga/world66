Lake Ranger Station is a rustic 1923 National Park Service building near Yellowstone Lake. Its trapper-cabin style was meant to blend with the park landscape, and the octagonal community room reflects the early years of park interpretation.

It is best treated as a short historic stop while exploring the Lake area, not as a destination that justifies crossing the park on its own.
