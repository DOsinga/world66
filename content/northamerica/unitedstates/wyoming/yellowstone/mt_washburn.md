Mt. Washburn is the main summit of the Washburn Range, rising to 10,243 feet above the west side of the Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone. The trail to the lookout tower is one of the park's classic high-country hikes, with broad views, summer wildflowers, and a good chance of seeing bighorn sheep.
