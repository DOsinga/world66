Virginia Cascades is a 60-foot waterfall on the Gibbon River, reached along a three-mile stretch of old road in Yellowstone. It is a quieter stop than the park's headline geysers and canyons, but the drive and falls make a good short detour when you want moving water and forest instead of crowds.
