Frederiksted Pier is one of St. Croix's best-known shore dives, with marine life gathered around the pilings close to town. Even non-divers can use the waterfront as a simple orientation point for Frederiksted, but the real draw is underwater: eels, crabs, lobster, seahorses, and other small reef life are regularly seen here.
