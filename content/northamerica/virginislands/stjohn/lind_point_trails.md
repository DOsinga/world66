The Lind Point Trails start near the Virgin Islands National Park visitor center in Cruz Bay and lead toward Honeymoon Bay and Solomon Bay. Spurs climb to overlooks where benches face the water and passing boats.

They are among the easiest rewarding walks on St. John, useful for visitors who want a short hike without leaving the Cruz Bay area for long.
