Bahía Blanca is a major port city in southern Buenos Aires province, the gateway to Patagonia and the largest urban centre between [Buenos Aires](/southamerica/argentina/buenosaires) and the south. It is a working city rather than a tourist destination, but it has a lively university atmosphere thanks to the [Universidad Nacional del Sur](/southamerica/argentina/bahiablanca/universidad_nacional_del_sur), and serves as a practical base for exploring the surrounding coast and mountains.

The city has a pleasant central area around [Plaza Rivadavia](/southamerica/argentina/bahiablanca/plaza_rivadavia), flanked by the [Bahía Blanca Cathedral](/southamerica/argentina/bahiablanca/bahia_blanca_cathedral) and the handsome Municipal Palace. The [Mercado Municipal](/southamerica/argentina/bahiablanca/mercado_municipal) is a fine early 20th-century market building, and the [Teatro Municipal](/southamerica/argentina/bahiablanca/teatro_municipal) runs an active cultural programme. The [Museo Municipal de Bellas Artes](/southamerica/argentina/bahiablanca/museo_arte) has a good collection of Argentine painting.

The port area of [Ingeniero White](/southamerica/argentina/bahiablanca/ingeniero_white), a few kilometres south, has a different character entirely — a working-class neighbourhood that gave Argentina some of its finest tango musicians, with a cluster of seafood restaurants along the waterfront and the fascinating [Museo del Puerto](/southamerica/argentina/bahiablanca/museo_del_puerto). The nearby Monte Hermoso beach resort draws summer visitors from across the region.
