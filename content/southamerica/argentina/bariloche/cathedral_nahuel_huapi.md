The Neo-Gothic cathedral of Bariloche dominates the lakefront a couple of blocks east of the Centro Civico. Designed by architect Alejandro Bustillo — the same architect who shaped the Civic Centre and the Llao Llao Hotel — it was completed in 1947 in a local stone that ages a beautiful grey-pink. The pointed spire rises above the surrounding low buildings and is visible across the bay.

Inside, the cathedral is quieter than the lakefront would suggest. Stained-glass windows tell the story of the Mapuche people and the early missionaries to Patagonia. Step in for the cool air on a hot summer day, or for the view across Nahuel Huapi to the snow-capped Andes through the open doors.
