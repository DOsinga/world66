Tronador is the giant of the region — a 3,491 metre extinct volcano on the Chilean border, with three peaks and eight glaciers tumbling off its slopes. It is far enough from Bariloche (90 km on a winding gravel road) that the full-day trip is a serious excursion, but the views along the way and at the end are exceptional.

Most visitors stop at **Pampa Linda**, a clearing with a refugio and the trailheads for longer treks, before continuing to the **Ventisquero Negro** viewpoint and the base of the **Garganta del Diablo** waterfall. Tour operators in Bariloche run daily summer buses; the road is one-way uphill in the morning and downhill in the afternoon to manage the traffic.
