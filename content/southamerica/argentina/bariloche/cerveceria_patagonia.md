Patagonia is the big commercial brewery — owned now by AB InBev — but the flagship taproom on Bustillo Km 24.7 is the place every visitor ends up at least once. The wooden deck overlooks Nahuel Huapi with the Andes behind, the standard beers are reliable, and the food (pork ribs, smoked-trout pizza, Patagonian lamb sandwiches) is much better than typical brewpub fare.

In summer the deck is packed by sunset; arrive early or expect to wait. In winter the indoor bar with its big stone fireplace is the draw.
