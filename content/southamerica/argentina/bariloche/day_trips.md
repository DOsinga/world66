Bariloche is the natural base for trips along the **Seven Lakes Road** (Ruta de los Siete Lagos) north toward [Junín de los Andes](/southamerica/argentina/junindelosandes), or further south to [Esquel](/southamerica/argentina/esquel) and [Los Alerces National Park](/southamerica/argentina/losalercesnationalpark). Most are too long for a true day trip but make excellent two-night extensions of a Bariloche stay.

For a day from Bariloche itself, the **Cerro Tronador** loop, the **Cerro Catedral / Refugio Frey** hike, the **Isla Victoria and Arrayanes** boat trip, and the **Circuito Chico** all qualify and are described under Things to Do.
