The Huemul Project Site is the strange remnant of Argentina's early-1950s attempt to build a fusion power plant on an island in Lago Nahuel Huapi. The project was backed by President Juan Peron, but collapsed when scientists found that Ronald Richter's claims did not stand up.

The ruins are not a polished museum, but the story is memorable: high politics, Cold War science, and a mountain lake setting all in one place. It is best treated as a short historical stop as part of a lake trip from Bariloche.
