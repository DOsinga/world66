A central in-town brewpub a short walk from the Centro Civico, Manush brews about ten styles on the premises — the porter and the IPA are the strongest. The kitchen turns out solid pub food, the music gets louder after 11pm, and the crowd is a mix of locals and travellers. A good place to start an evening before moving on to one of the bigger places out on Bustillo.
