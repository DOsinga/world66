---

Bariloche shopping is dominated by chocolate. [Mamuschka](/southamerica/argentina/bariloche/mamuschka), Rapanui, Del Turista, and Fenoglio all have shops on or just off Calle Mitre and provide an embarrassing amount of free sampling. Prices for boxes are about 30% above what you would pay in Buenos Aires for the same brand — the premium for the **Bariloche** label.

Outdoor gear shops cluster on Mitre and Quaglia: the **Patagonia** flagship, Columbia, and a handful of Argentine outdoor brands. Genuine Mapuche silver and weavings are sold at the Sunday craft market in [Colonia Suiza](/southamerica/argentina/bariloche/colonia_suiza) and at the Centro Civico's stalls in summer. **Cuero**-leather goods, hand-made knives, and yerba mate gear are everywhere.
