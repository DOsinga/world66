A classic bodegón (traditional Argentine restaurant-bar) on the corner of Peru and Calvo in San Telmo, open since 1864 and largely unchanged. The decor — pressed tin ceiling, wooden bar, old advertisements painted directly onto the walls — is original rather than reconstructed. The menu is traditional Argentine: *milanesas*, *puchero*, *cazuela de mondongo* (tripe stew), and good wine by the carafe.

El Federal is what a neighbourhood corner bar looked like in Buenos Aires before the city became a tourist destination. It is still primarily used by local residents, which becomes clear at lunchtime on weekdays. The *café con leche* and *medialunas* are reliable for breakfast; the lunch menu is hearty and cheap.
