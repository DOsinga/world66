Museo del Automovil looks at Argentina's motoring history through more than a hundred cars, commercial vehicles, and historic motorcycles. The displays include period fuel-station details, drive-in material, and advertising from the early years of car culture.

It is a specialist museum, but a worthwhile stop for visitors interested in classic cars, design, or Buenos Aires beyond the usual central circuit.
