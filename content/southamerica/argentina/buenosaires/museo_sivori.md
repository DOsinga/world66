Museo de Artes Plásticas Eduardo Sívori is an art museum in Palermo's park district, near the Rosedal and the lakes of the Bosques de Palermo. Its collection focuses on Argentine art, with paintings, sculpture, prints, and works on paper.

The museum is a good cultural stop if you are already spending time in the Palermo parks. Temporary exhibitions and the quiet setting make it a useful counterpoint to the larger museums in Recoleta and the city centre.
