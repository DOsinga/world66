Parroquia Nuestra Señora de Balvanera is the historic parish church that gave the Balvanera district its name. Built between the late 18th and mid-19th centuries, it remains a busy local devotional site.

The church is especially associated with San Expedito, drawing worshippers from across Buenos Aires. It is a worthwhile pause if you are exploring the Congreso and Balvanera area.
