---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Cafayate divides its appeal between wine, landscape, and a small but genuinely engaging set of cultural sites. The bodegas are the main event, but the Quebrada de Cafayate to the north and the quieter roads south through the Calchaquí Valley are as important as any tasting room. The town plaza is the social hub and the natural starting point for any visit.
