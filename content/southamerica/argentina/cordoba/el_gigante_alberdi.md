The home stadium of Club Atlético Belgrano, built tight against the houses of barrio Alberdi and famous for its atmosphere. Capacity is about 30,000 and the steep stands put the crowd very close to the pitch.

Match days transform the neighbourhood. Tickets for Belgrano matches in the Argentine Primera División are inexpensive and the experience is essential for anyone interested in South American football. Avoid wearing rival colours.
