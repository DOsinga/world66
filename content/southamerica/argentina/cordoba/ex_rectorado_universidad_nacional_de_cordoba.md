The Ex Rectorado of the National University of Córdoba is an 18th-century Baroque colonial building with a cloister and museum space open to visitors. It is part of the city's historic university landscape and sits close to the Jesuit Block.

The building still belongs to the university, so some areas are academic rather than touristic. The public cloister and tours are the reason to come.
