Córdoba's artisan and antique neighbourhood, just west of the centre across La Cañada. The streets are narrower and older than the colonial grid, with low single-storey houses, ironwork balconies, and overgrown courtyards.

The weekend Mercado de Artesanos on Calle Belgrano fills several blocks with stalls selling leather, ceramics, mate gourds, and silverwork. Antique shops, design studios, and small restaurants occupy the surrounding streets. Friday and Saturday nights are particularly lively.
