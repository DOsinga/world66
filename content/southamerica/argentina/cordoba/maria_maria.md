A small craft cocktail bar in Güemes, dim and intimate, with a short rotating menu and a serious bartender behind the counter. Local gins and obscure vermouths feature; the negronis and clarified milk punches are the strong suit.

Limited seating means you may have to queue or come early. Cash or card.
