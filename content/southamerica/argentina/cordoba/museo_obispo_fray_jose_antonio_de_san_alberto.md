The Museo Obispo Fray José Antonio de San Alberto is housed in a building that has shifted roles several times: private house, church residence, school, and Carmelite convent. That layered history is the main reason to visit.

Visits are by guided tour, which suits the place better than a quick wander. Inside, the museum gives a closer look at colonial Córdoba's religious and domestic architecture, away from the city's larger and busier landmarks.
