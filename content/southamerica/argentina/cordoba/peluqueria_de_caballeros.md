A speakeasy-style cocktail bar in a former barbershop on the edge of Güemes, with the original chairs and mirrors still in place and a hidden door to a back room. Cocktails are inventive without being silly; the bartenders take their time.

No reservations, no sign on the door — look for the unmarked entrance off Calle Belgrano. Closed Mondays.
