---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Córdoba's most distinctive shopping is the weekend artisan market in Güemes, which fills several blocks of Calle Belgrano on Saturday and Sunday afternoons with stalls of leather, ceramics, silverwork, and mate gear. The Mercado Norte and Mercado Sur — both old indoor food markets in the centre — are worth visiting for cheeses, dulce de leche, alfajores, and the local fernet that fuels the city. For mainstream shopping, the Patio Olmos mall in a restored historic building and the Nuevocentro mall to the south cover the chain stores.
