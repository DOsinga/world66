Centro de Arte Contemporáneo Chateau Carreras is a small contemporary art centre in a 19th-century castle-like building on the western side of Córdoba. The patio and historic setting are part of the draw, especially if you want a quieter art stop away from the city centre.

Exhibitions focus on modern and contemporary art, so it works best for travellers with a specific interest in Córdoba's cultural scene rather than as a first stop in the city.
