The former rectorate of the National University of Córdoba is one of the city's handsome colonial university buildings. Its Baroque facade and quiet cloister belong to the same old academic world as the Jesuit Block, though the building now serves the law faculty.

Tours are the best way to see more than the public cloister. Even without one, it is worth a short detour while walking the historic centre, especially if you are tracing Córdoba's long university history.
