La Perla is a memorial site in a former clandestine detention centre used during Argentina's military dictatorship from 1976 to 1983. It is a difficult but important place, preserving the memory of the people imprisoned, tortured, and disappeared here.

The site sits outside central Cordoba, on the road toward Villa Carlos Paz. Give it time and do not treat it as a quick photo stop; this is one of the region's most serious memory sites.
