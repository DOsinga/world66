Museo Municipal de Bellas Artes Dr. Genaro Perez is Cordoba's municipal fine arts museum, with a mix of permanent displays and changing exhibitions. It is smaller and less polished than the city's headline museums, but often more local in feel.

The museum is a good central stop for visitors interested in Argentine art, temporary shows, or a quieter gallery visit between the historic core and the city's larger cultural sights.
