Museo Historico de la Universidad tells the story of Cordoba's university, one of the oldest in the Americas. It is housed in the former rectorate, now part of the law faculty, close to the city's Jesuit heritage core.

The museum is best for visitors already interested in Cordoba's colonial and academic history, especially when combined with the Jesuit Block and nearby churches.
