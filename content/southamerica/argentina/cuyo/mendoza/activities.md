title: Activities
type: section
---

Mendoza's activities sort into three places: the Río Mendoza at Potrerillos, an hour west, where the rafting and kayaking are; the high Andes beyond it, where Aconcagua and the Cordón del Plata draw climbers from all over the world; and the wine country of Maipú and Luján de Cuyo, which is flat enough to do by bicycle. Everyone below advertises WhatsApp for bookings, which in Argentina is how most of this gets arranged anyway.
