Portal del Atuel is the one-stop option at Valle Grande: rafting on the river, a catamaran out on the reservoir, zip lines, abseiling, 4x4 runs, and shaded beaches with canoes for anyone who would rather do nothing. For a family with mixed appetites for adventure, it is the easiest place to spend a whole day.

The catamaran is the thing the others do not have — the Valle Grande reservoir sits between red canyon walls, and seeing it from the water is a different experience from seeing it from the road.

Their published address is km 35 of Ruta 173; the pin marks the reservoir where the boat trips run. Book by WhatsApp.
