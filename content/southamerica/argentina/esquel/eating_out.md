---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Esquel's restaurant scene is small but solid. **Don Chiquino** is the long-running pasta house (chef-owner Maurizio's pasta is hand-rolled and the lamb ravioli excellent), **La Tour d'Argent** serves Patagonian lamb and trout, and **La Espana** on the main square does respectable Spanish-style tapas and steaks. For straightforward Argentine asado, **La Esquina** by the bus terminal is the locals' choice. Welsh tea houses (casas de té) in nearby [Trevelin](/southamerica/argentina/esquel/trevelin) serve a substantial afternoon spread of cakes, scones and dense Welsh fruit cake.
