---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Esquel has a small airport with daily flights to Buenos Aires (about two hours) on Aerolineas Argentinas; it is by far the easiest way in. Onward connections to El Calafate and Ushuaia exist but are infrequent.

By road, Esquel is roughly halfway between [Bariloche](/southamerica/argentina/bariloche) (3 hours, 280km north on the spectacular Ruta 40 / Camino de los Siete Lagos route) and [Comodoro Rivadavia](/southamerica/argentina/comodororivadavia) on the Atlantic. Long-distance buses run daily to and from both, and to Buenos Aires (a 24-hour overnight). The bus terminal is in the centre of Esquel, walking distance from most hotels.
