---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Esquel itself is small and unhurried — a couple of streets of low buildings, a small square, a stone-and-wood Patagonian architecture, an old Welsh church. The real attractions are outside town: the ancient alerce forest in **Los Alerces National Park** to the west, the steam railway **La Trochita** that runs from the station east across the steppe, and the **La Hoya** ski resort just north of town. Allow at least two or three days to see the area properly; longer if you want to fish, ride or trek.
