---

Wildlife watching is the reason to come to Iberá, and the range of experiences here is exceptional. [Boat tours on Laguna Iberá](/southamerica/argentina/iberawetlands/boat_tours) are the most accessible activity, delivering close encounters with [capybara, caimans](/southamerica/argentina/iberawetlands/capybara_caiman), and an outstanding variety of waterbirds. The [birdwatching](/southamerica/argentina/iberawetlands/birdwatching) is world-class, with over 360 species including the globally vulnerable strange-tailed tyrant. [Horseback riding](/southamerica/argentina/iberawetlands/horseback_riding) opens up the savannah and wetland edges that boats cannot reach.

The big-ticket experiences are the rewilded megafauna. [Giant anteaters](/southamerica/argentina/iberawetlands/giant_anteater) are now regularly seen on vehicle tours, while [jaguar tracking](/southamerica/argentina/iberawetlands/jaguar_trail) offers the possibility — not guarantee — of seeing the most powerful predator in the Americas, reintroduced after a 70-year absence. The base for most visitors is [Colonia Carlos Pellegrini](/southamerica/argentina/iberawetlands/colonia_carlos_pellegrini), the small village on the lagoon shore within the [Iberá National Park](/southamerica/argentina/iberawetlands/ibera_national_park) protected area.
