---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The falls run year-round but the flow varies enormously. The Argentinian summer (December to March) brings the highest water — sometimes too high, with the catwalks to the Devil's Throat and the boat trips to San Martín Island closed for days or weeks at a time after exceptional storms. It is also hot, humid and crowded.

The best window is April to early June, and again from August to October — the river is still substantial, temperatures sit in the 20s, the worst of the rains have passed, and the crowds thin out noticeably. July is the local school holidays and visitor numbers spike again.

Avoid the long Argentinian weekends if you can: Semana Santa (Easter), the second weekend of August, and the first weekend of October all bring large domestic crowds and a corresponding scramble for tickets and trains inside the park. Check timeanddate.com for current holiday dates.
