Tilcara's main square is not grand, but it is where the village loosens up. Craft sellers set out textiles and ceramics, musicians drift between restaurants, and travellers compare plans for the next day's quebrada trip. Come back after dark, when the air cools and the plaza feels less like a stop on a route and more like the centre of town.
