The Museum of Contemporary Latin American Art gives La Plata an important modern-art counterpoint to its better-known natural history museum. Its exhibitions focus on contemporary and Latin American work, often with a regional and experimental edge.

It is a strong addition for travelers who want La Plata to be more than architecture and fossils. The museum is central enough to combine with the city's plazas, civic buildings, and other cultural stops.
