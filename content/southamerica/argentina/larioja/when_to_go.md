---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) are the best times to visit. Temperatures are comfortable — typically 20–28°C — and the national parks are at their most accessible. The light in autumn is particularly good for photography in Talampaya.

Winters (June–August) are mild and dry, with daytime temperatures around 15–20°C and cold nights. This is perfectly pleasant and increasingly popular with visitors who want to be sure of cool walking conditions in the parks.

Summer (December–March) is difficult. Temperatures regularly exceed 40°C, and the heat in the desert parks can be dangerous. If you come in summer, plan any outdoor activity for the early morning hours and treat the midday as a forced siesta. The city more or less shuts down between noon and 5 pm regardless of season, but this is especially true in summer.
