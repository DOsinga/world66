Plaza 25 de Mayo is Corrientes' main civic square, the kind of place where the city introduces itself without much ceremony. Palms, monuments, benches, and government buildings frame a space used for passing through, meeting friends, and watching the day cool down.

It is not a spectacular plaza, but it is a necessary one if you want to understand central Corrientes. Come in the evening, when the heat eases and the square becomes more social than architectural.
