Playas del Thompson is one of Paraná's river-beach areas, giving visitors a direct way to experience the city's relationship with the Paraná River. It is a local leisure spot, but the river setting makes it more relevant than an ordinary municipal park.

It is best in warm weather, when the beach, water, and nearby services are active. Treat it as a casual seasonal stop rather than a year-round headline attraction.
