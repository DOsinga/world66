A riding school on the road to the falls that takes visitors into the forest on horseback, on circuits of half an hour, one hour or two. The two-hour ride passes the caiman lagoon and visits the Guaraní villages, whose crafts are for sale.

The WhatsApp is the number Puerto Iguazú's municipal tourism board lists for them. They are at kilometre 4.5 of Ruta 12, on the way to the falls; the pin marks the town rather than the stables.
