---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Reconquista is not a sightseeing town. A morning around Plaza 25 de Mayo and the cathedral covers the centre; the two local museums fill another hour each. The real pleasure here is sitting in a plaza watching small-town Argentine life unfold — students cruising the centre in their cars, families walking the Costanera in the evening, men playing chess outside the kiosks. Time it for late afternoon when the heat eases.
