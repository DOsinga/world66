---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Reconquista has a subtropical climate — long, very hot, humid summers and mild winters. December to February is uncomfortable: highs around 33 °C with stifling humidity, and occasional violent thunderstorms. Many locals retreat indoors in the afternoon.

The best months to visit are **April to June** and **September to November**, when daytime temperatures sit in the low 20s and the air dries out. The biggest local event is the **Fiesta Nacional del Surubí**, a river-fishing competition held on the Paraná in late April that draws competitors from across Argentina.
