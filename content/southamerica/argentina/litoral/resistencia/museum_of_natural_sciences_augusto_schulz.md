The Museum of Natural Sciences Augusto Schulz gives Resistencia a compact natural-history counterpoint to its better-known public sculpture scene. Its collections focus on the Chaco region's animals, fossils, plants, and environmental setting, which helps visitors understand the landscape beyond the city streets.

It is a modest museum rather than a blockbuster, but it is useful for travelers with an interest in ecology, families looking for an indoor stop, or anyone continuing into the wetlands and forests of northeastern Argentina. Check current hours before going, as smaller provincial museums can change schedules.
