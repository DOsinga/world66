---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Cafayate eats well for a town of its size. The emphasis is on straightforward Argentine food — empanadas, grilled meats, locro (a hearty Andean stew) — accompanied by the local wine. The restaurants around the plaza are the obvious choices for dinner; most are reliably good and affordable.

**El Rancho**: A long-established restaurant near the plaza serving hearty Andean cooking — goat stew, locro, humita — alongside grilled meats. The wine list is local and well-priced. Outdoor tables when the evenings are warm.

**Chato's Wine Bar**: A relaxed bar and restaurant combining local Torrontés and Malbec with tapas and snacks. Good for an afternoon tasting session that extends into dinner.

**Empanadas**: Every corner shop and lunch counter in Cafayate sells empanadas. The Salta-style empanadas here — fried, filled with seasoned beef, onion, and hard-boiled egg — are excellent and cost almost nothing. The market on the edge of town is the best place for them in the morning.
