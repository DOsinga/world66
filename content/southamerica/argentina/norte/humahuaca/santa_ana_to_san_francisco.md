This high-country trek links the adobe village of Santa Ana with the tiny settlement of San Francisco through dramatic mountain country above Humahuaca. The route is long, remote, and affected by altitude, so it is best treated as a serious outing rather than a casual walk. The reward is a quiet slice of Jujuy, with big views and hot springs near the far end.
