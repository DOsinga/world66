Pajcha is a small museum of folk and indigenous art that gives Salta a more intimate cultural stop than its grander central museums. Textiles, masks, ceramics, ritual objects, and craft traditions connect the city to the wider Andean world.

It is a good place to slow down and look closely at materials and patterns before heading into the valleys or puna. The museum is modest, but its focus fits Salta beautifully.
