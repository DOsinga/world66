---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Tucumán is a university town, and its bar and cafe culture reflects that. The streets around the **Universidad Nacional de Tucumán** and the cafes along **calle 25 de Mayo** fill with students in the late afternoon and stay busy until midnight or beyond. **Calle Lillo**, near the law faculty, is a particular hub — a row of bars and outdoor terraces that turns into a casual street party on warm Friday evenings.

For something more sedate, the classic **confiterías** around Plaza Independencia serve coffee, alfajores, and afternoon submarinos (a glass of warm milk into which you drop a slab of dark chocolate). Many have been in business for decades.
