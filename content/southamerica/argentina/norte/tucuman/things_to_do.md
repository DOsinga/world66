---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The core sights cluster around Plaza Independencia and along the parallel pedestrian streets a few blocks south. Casa Histórica de la Independencia — the small colonial house where Argentine independence was declared in 1816 — is the obvious starting point. From there, walk through the Cathedral, Iglesia San Francisco, and the Casa Padilla on the plaza itself. Parque 9 de Julio, east of the centre, is the city's lung and contains several museums and the racetrack.
