Draghi is both a workshop and a museum, and that is the point. The display cases hold 19th-century and contemporary criollo silverwork: knives, mate gourds, belt pieces, spurs, bridles, and the elaborate fittings that turned useful ranch objects into status symbols.

It is close to the main square, so it fits easily into a walk around town. The best part is seeing that this is not just heritage under glass; the craft is still being practised.
