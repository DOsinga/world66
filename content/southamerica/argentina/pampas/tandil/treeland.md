An aerial adventure park on the edge of town: twenty rope bridges at two levels, ziplines of up to 60 metres, a nine-metre climbing and rappelling wall, and a low course for children under eight. You can make a day of it and bring a picnic.

The WhatsApp is the one Tandil's municipal tourism site gives for them.
