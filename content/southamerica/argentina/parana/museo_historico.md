A small history museum in a 19th-century townhouse, focused on the Entre Ríos province and on the years (1854–1861) when Paraná served as capital of the Argentine Confederation. Period furniture, documents, weapons, and portraits. Of most interest to those curious about Argentina's slow and contested process of national unification — a story that runs through every room. Free entry.
