The 2.4-km road tunnel under the Paraná River, linking Paraná with Santa Fe on the opposite bank. It is the only direct route between the two provincial capitals, and an unusual engineering feat for the era — a series of pre-fabricated concrete tubes sunk to the river bed and joined together. There is a small museum about the tunnel's construction at the eastern entrance, useful only as a curiosity. The drive through — twenty minutes either way with traffic — is the experience.
