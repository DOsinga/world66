---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The river-valley climate is humid subtropical. Summers (December to March) are hot and sticky, with afternoon temperatures regularly above 32°C and frequent thunderstorms; the Costanera and the river beaches are at their best in this season but the city itself can be uncomfortable. Winters (June to August) are mild — 16°C days, cool nights — and rarely freeze; this is the easiest season for walking the centre and the park.

**Spring (September to November)** is the most pleasant overall: warm but not yet humid, the jacarandas in bloom around the plaza, river levels usually good for boat trips.

The big festival is **Carnival**, in February — quieter and more provincial than Gualeguaychú further south, but with parades along the Costanera.
