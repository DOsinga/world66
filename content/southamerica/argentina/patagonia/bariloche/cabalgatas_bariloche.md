Horse riding is the classic way into the country behind Bariloche, and this outfit runs rides through the forest and up to viewpoints over the lakes, pitched at people who have never ridden as much as at those who have. They also run gentle family kayak trips on Lago Moreno, one of the sheltered lakes west of town.

Bookings go through WhatsApp, where they route you to an adviser who confirms a place on the spot; they answer every day from nine in the morning to nine at night. The office is on Mitre, the main street.
