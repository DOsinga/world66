Cerro Lopez is one of Bariloche's classic mountain hikes, with a trail climbing toward the mountain shelter above Colonia Suiza. The route is tiring enough to feel like a real day out, but the payoff is a broad view over the lakes and peaks west of the city. Go in the warmer months and carry water.
