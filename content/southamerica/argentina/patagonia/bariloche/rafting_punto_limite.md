Punto Límite run rafting on the Río Manso, south-west of Bariloche, where the river cuts through forest towards the Chilean border. They combine the rafting with a horse ride for a full day in the valley, and run longer expeditions for people who want more river.

The Manso is the region's main white-water river, and it is cold — it is glacier-fed and runs through the Andes — so wetsuits are part of the deal.

They publish no base address; the pin marks the Río Manso, where the trips run. Book by WhatsApp.
