The most memorable way to experience Perito Moreno is to walk on it. Two guided options operate from the park: Mini Trekking (about 1.5 hours on the ice, suitable for most fitness levels) and Big Ice (a longer, more demanding route that takes you deeper into the glacier's crevasses and seracs). Both begin with a short boat transfer to the glacier's edge, where guides fit you with crampons before leading the walk. The ice underfoot is a startling deep blue in places, riddled with meltwater channels and sculpted forms. Book well in advance in high season — both tours fill quickly.
