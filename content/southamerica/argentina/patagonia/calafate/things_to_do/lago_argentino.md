The largest lake in Argentina and the body of water into which Perito Moreno calves. Its shores and shallow lagoons around El Calafate attract flamingos — the Chilean flamingo is a regular visitor — and the lake itself is a milky turquoise colour from glacial flour suspended in the water. The waterfront in town is pleasant for an evening walk. If you have time, the drive along the lake's southern shore towards the park offers sweeping views of the Andes and the surrounding steppe.
