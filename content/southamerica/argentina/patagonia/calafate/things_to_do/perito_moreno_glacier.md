  Perito Moreno is one of the only glaciers in the world that is not retreating — it is still

The undisputed star of Los Glaciares National Park and one of the most awe-inspiring natural sights in South America. The glacier's face stretches roughly 5 km wide and stands 60 m above the waterline, a sheer wall of fractured blue-white ice. A system of elevated boardwalks and viewing platforms puts you directly opposite the face, close enough to hear the deep groaning from inside the ice and, if you wait, to watch enormous chunks calve and crash into the turquoise water below. Plan at least three to four hours here. The drive from El Calafate takes about 80 km and 1.5 hours.
