Full-day boat excursions depart from Puerto Bajo de las Sombras (about 60 km from El Calafate) and cruise into the upper arms of Lago Argentino, weaving through icebergs to reach the Upsala and Spegazzini glaciers. Upsala was once the largest glacier in South America by area; it has retreated significantly in recent decades, but the calving front and the iceberg-choked lake before it remain remarkable. Spegazzini is taller than Perito Moreno — its face rises up to 135 m above the water. The tours typically stop on an island for a walk before returning to port. A full day out.
