title: Cathedral of Comodoro Rivadavia
type: poi
---

The Cathedral of Comodoro Rivadavia stands at the centre of the city, a mid-20th-century building that reflects the optimism and functionalism of postwar Argentine architecture. It is not a landmark of great architectural significance, but the interior has good proportions, decent natural light, and some regional devotional art that gives a sense of the religious culture of Patagonian immigrant communities — Croatians, Italians, and Spaniards who came to work the oil fields and built churches in their new towns.

A visit takes fifteen minutes. It is a convenient stop while exploring the city centre, and the plaza around it is the social hub of the commercial district.
