Chorrillo del Salto is a small waterfall a few kilometres north of El Chaltén, accessible in about 45 minutes on foot from the village. It is not one of Patagonia's dramatic sights, but it makes for an easy warm-up hike on arrival day while you are sorting out gear and waiting for the weather to clear. The falls drop into a pool of brilliantly clear glacial water surrounded by lenga beech forest.

The trail to the falls continues north up the Río de las Vueltas valley, where the Mirador Río de las Vueltas offers broad views of the valley and the mountains around El Chaltén. This section is flat and easy, and birds — particularly Andean condors, which are regularly seen riding thermals above the valley — are the reward. The round trip to the falls and the mirador takes about 2–3 hours at an easy pace.
