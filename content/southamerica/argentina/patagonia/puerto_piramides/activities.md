Whale watching from Puerto Pirámides is a closed shop, deliberately: the province licenses six companies to take boats among the southern right whales of the Golfo Nuevo, and the municipality publishes the list. All six are below, each with the WhatsApp number that list gives.

The season runs from about June to December, with the most whales in September and October. Trips last about ninety minutes, and the boats are launched from the beach by tractor.
