A family company that has been taking boats out to the southern right whales since 1980. The trips last about 90 minutes and leave several times a day in season, June to December; the fleet runs from a two-deck observation boat to a small rigid inflatable, and they arrange photography and private trips.

The WhatsApp is the one the Puerto Pirámides municipality lists for them, and it is also the number of their office in Puerto Madryn, on Avenida Roca. The pin marks the village beach, where the boats are launched.
