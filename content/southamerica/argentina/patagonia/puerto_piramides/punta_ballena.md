The company that started commercial whale watching here, in 1971, in launches that took eight people. Whale season runs from mid-June to mid-December; in summer, when the whales have gone, they switch to boat trips and snorkelling in the bay.

The WhatsApp is the one the Puerto Pirámides municipality lists for them, and it is on their own site. The office is on the second beach access road. The pin marks the village beach, where the boats are launched.
