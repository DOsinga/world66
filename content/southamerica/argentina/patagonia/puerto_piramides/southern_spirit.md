Southern Spirit runs the usual boat trips out to the whales, and one that nobody else does: the Yellow Submarine, a semi-submersible whose passengers sit below the waterline and watch the whales through windows.

The WhatsApp is the one the Puerto Pirámides municipality lists for them, and it is on their own site. The pin marks the village beach, where the boats are launched.
