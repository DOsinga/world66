Puerto Piramides is the only village on Peninsula Valdes and the place most boat trips use for whale watching. It is small, sandy, and surrounded by pale cliffs that make the bay feel like a natural amphitheatre.

You can visit as part of a peninsula circuit or stay overnight for a slower rhythm. The village is most useful in whale season, but even outside it the landscape feels wonderfully remote after the bustle of Puerto Madryn.
