The Pioneer Museum preserves Rio Gallegos' oldest standing house, furnished with domestic objects from the late 19th and early 20th centuries. It is a small stop, but useful if you want a feel for early settler life in southern Patagonia.

Pair it with the larger regional museum nearby for a fuller picture of the city and Santa Cruz province.
