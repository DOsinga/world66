La Pastera is a small museum dedicated to Ernesto "Che" Guevara, set in a former National Parks storehouse where he stayed during his youthful journey through South America. It is modest, but it adds a political and literary footnote to a town otherwise dominated by outdoor pursuits.

The museum is close to the centre and easy to combine with a walk around town. It will not occupy long, but for anyone interested in Latin American history it is worth the detour.
