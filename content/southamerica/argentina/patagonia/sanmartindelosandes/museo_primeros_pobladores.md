Museo Primeros Pobladores is a small municipal museum near the centre of San Martín. Its exhibits cover the area's early inhabitants, Mapuche culture, pioneers, and the civic beginnings of the town.

It is not flashy, and that is fine. Visit when the weather closes in or when you want a little context before heading into a landscape that can otherwise feel like pure scenery.
