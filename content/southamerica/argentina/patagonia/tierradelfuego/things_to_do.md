---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The island demands to be explored outdoors. Tierra del Fuego National Park is the centrepiece, but there are boat trips on the Beagle Channel, ski runs above Ushuaia in winter, and the extraordinary End of the World Train winding through the Pipo River valley. Wildlife is everywhere — sea lions, Magellanic penguins, condors, and the giant Magellanic woodpecker that hammers through the lenga beech forests.
