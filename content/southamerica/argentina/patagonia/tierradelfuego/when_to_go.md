---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The main tourist season runs from October to April, when the weather is mild, the days are extraordinarily long, and the national park is fully accessible. December and January have the longest daylight — up to seventeen hours — and the warmest temperatures, hovering around 12–15°C. This is also when the Magellanic penguin colonies at Martillo Island are most active.

May through September is winter. Ushuaia operates as a ski resort, with good snow on the runs above town. Fewer tourists mean lower prices and a more local atmosphere, but some boat excursions don't operate and the national park can be difficult to access after heavy snow. The Antarctic cruise season ends by April — anyone hoping to join a last-minute discounted departure should target March or early April.
