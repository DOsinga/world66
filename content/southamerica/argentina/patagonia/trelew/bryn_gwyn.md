Bryn Gwyn Geopark sits on pale bluffs south of Gaiman, about 25km from Trelew. The trail climbs through exposed geological layers, with fossil replicas and interpretation that connect the valley landscape to the deep-time story told at MEF.

It is best paired with the museum rather than treated as a standalone attraction. Wear sun protection and decent shoes; the valley can feel gentle from town, but the open slopes are hot, dry, and exposed.
