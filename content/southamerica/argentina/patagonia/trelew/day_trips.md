[Gaiman](/southamerica/argentina/patagonia/gaiman) is the obvious half-day trip, especially if you want Welsh tea and the Chubut Valley story after the dinosaur museum. [Puerto Madryn](/southamerica/argentina/patagonia/puertomadryn) is close enough for the coast, though it deserves more time in whale season.
