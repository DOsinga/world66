Laguna Chiquichano is Trelew's easiest breathing space: a small urban lagoon with paths, birds, and open sky close to the centre. It will not compete with Patagonia's wild landscapes, but it is a pleasant place to stretch your legs.

Use it between the museum and dinner, or when you have an hour before a bus. In a city that can feel hard-edged, the lagoon gives Trelew a softer local rhythm.
