Plaza Independencia is Trelew's central square and a useful orientation point rather than a major sight. The blocks around it hold civic buildings, shops, cafes, and the everyday movement of the city.

Stop if you are already downtown for MEF or the bus offices. It is the kind of square that helps you understand Trelew as a working valley city, not just a transfer point.
