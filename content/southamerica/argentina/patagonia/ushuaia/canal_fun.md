Canal's trip worth singling out is the canoe run down the Río Lasifashaj to its mouth on the Beagle Channel, with a walk on Isla Gable and a navigation to the Harberton penguin colony. They also run sea kayaking, national-park walks with a canoe leg, and off-road days to Lago Escondido and Fagnano.

The WhatsApp is the floating button on their site. The office is on Pasaje Beban, a short lane off the centre; the pin marks the lane.
