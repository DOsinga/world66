Cerro Castor is Ushuaia's main ski area and one of the southernmost developed ski resorts in the world. In winter it turns the mountains east of town into a proper snow-sports destination rather than just a backdrop for boat trips and national-park visits.

Conditions and transport matter, so check the season, snow report, and shuttle options before committing. Outside ski season, the area can still be useful for mountain scenery, but winter is when it justifies the detour.
