Faro Les Éclaireurs is the small red-and-white lighthouse often seen on Beagle Channel boat trips from Ushuaia. Built in 1920, it stands on a rocky islet east of town and is widely marketed as a "lighthouse at the end of the world."

You cannot usually land at the lighthouse, but the close pass by boat gives good views of the structure, the channel, and the surrounding seabird and sea-lion colonies.
