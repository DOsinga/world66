Glaciar Martial is the mountain outing Ushuaia keeps almost on its doorstep. The trail climbs above town toward the small glacier and, more importantly for most visitors, opens broad views over the Beagle Channel, the city, and the dark ridges around it.

Conditions change quickly, so treat it like a real Patagonian hike even though it starts close to hotels and taxis. On a clear day it is one of the easiest ways to understand Ushuaia's setting: squeezed between cold water and steep mountains at the end of the continent.
