Laguna Esmeralda is the classic half-day hike from Ushuaia: a wet, often muddy trail through Fuegian forest and peat bog to a small green lake backed by sharp mountains. It gives a strong taste of Tierra del Fuego without requiring a technical expedition.

Waterproof boots matter more than speed, and weather can change quickly even on this popular route. On clear days the final view is superb; on misty days the bog, lenga forest, and glacial water still make the walk feel properly southern.
