Les Eclaireurs Lighthouse stands on a small island in the Beagle Channel and is one of the classic sights reached by short boat trips from Ushuaia. It is often marketed as the "Lighthouse at the End of the World", though the lighthouse from Jules Verne's novel is farther east on Isla de los Estados.

You cannot usually land at the lighthouse, but boats pass close enough for good views and photographs. The trip pairs naturally with sea lion and seabird colonies in the channel.
