Mastil de General Belgrano is a remote memorial west of Ushuaia, on Isla Redonda near the edge-of-the-world post office circuit. The stop is small, but the setting gives it weight: wind, water, mountains, and a tribute to Manuel Belgrano and to the Argentine cruiser sunk during the Falklands War.
