Onashaga is one of the five operators on the Ushuaia municipality's official list of penguin-colony providers. Its navigation leaves daily in the afternoon and takes about five hours along the Beagle Channel to Isla Martillo, where Magellanic penguins nest alongside a handful of gentoos — one of very few mixed colonies anywhere.

The number below is the one the municipality lists for them, and it is also the WhatsApp link on their own site. Office on Avenida Maipú, the waterfront road; the pin marks that street. The colony is occupied from October to April.
