Plaza Malvinas is one of Ushuaia's most visible civic memorials, set near the waterfront where the mountains, wind, and Beagle Channel give the site a stark backdrop. The memorial commemorates Argentine soldiers who died in the 1982 South Atlantic war, with names, flags, and symbolic elements arranged around the square.

It is a short stop rather than a museum, but it matters for understanding Ushuaia's sense of national frontier and memory. Visit while walking the waterfront promenade; the tone is solemn, so it is worth slowing down rather than treating it as just another photo point.
