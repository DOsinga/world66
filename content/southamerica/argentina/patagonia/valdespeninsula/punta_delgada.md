Punta Delgada is one of the remote coastal stops on Peninsula Valdes, known for cliffs, open sea views, and a sea lion colony below the bluff. Access to some areas is controlled by commercial tours, but you can usually make a short scenic walk to the panoramic point without joining the full visit.

Ask clearly what is open before setting out, and do not expect a free wander down to the beach. The reward is the wide Patagonian coast, with wildlife below and very little human clutter in the view.
