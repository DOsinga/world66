Los Arrayanes protects the Quetrihue Peninsula and its rare forest of arrayan trees, whose smooth bark turns copper and cinnamon in the filtered light. Argentina's national parks service notes specimens more than 650 years old, which explains why the forest feels less like a normal lakeside walk and more like a small, strange room in the Patagonian woods.

You can reach the forest by boat, on foot, or by mountain bike from Villa La Angostura. Walking the full peninsula is the most satisfying option if you have the time and weather.
