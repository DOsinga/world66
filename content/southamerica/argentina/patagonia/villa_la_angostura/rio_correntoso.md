The Correntoso River runs only a very short distance between Lago Correntoso and Lago Nahuel Huapi, but it is one of the town's classic views: fast, clear water, forested banks, and cold mountain light. Anglers come for trout; everyone else comes for the bridge, the river mouth, and the sense of how much water is moving through this small corner of Patagonia.

It works well as a gentle half-day with nearby lake beaches, especially when the weather is too uncertain for a longer mountain walk.
