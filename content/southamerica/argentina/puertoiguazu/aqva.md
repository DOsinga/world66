A regional-fish-and-grill restaurant in the centre of Puerto Iguazú that has become the most reliable upmarket choice in town. The kitchen specialises in the big river fish of the Paraná — **surubí** (tiger catfish), **pacú**, **dorado** — cooked simply on the grill with regional sides like *mandioca frita* and *pollo al chimichurri*.

The dining room is on a covered terrace with palm trees and ferns; the wine list emphasises Mendoza Malbecs and a few good local craft beers. Prices are middle-range by international standards, high by Argentine ones. Worth booking on weekends.
