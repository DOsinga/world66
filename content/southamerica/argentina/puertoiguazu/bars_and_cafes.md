---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

The nightlife in Puerto Iguazú is unambitious — it is a tourist town that empties out fairly early — but there are enough bars and cafes for an evening drink. The main concentration is along **Avenida Brasil** and around the central plaza; the rooftops of a few hotels do good sundowner drinks.

Argentine cafe culture is alive and well: serious espresso, *medialunas* (croissants) for breakfast, and the universal afternoon ritual of *mate*. Coffee is generally good and cheap.
