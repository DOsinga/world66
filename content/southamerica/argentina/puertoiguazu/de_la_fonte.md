A small Italian-Argentine restaurant on Avenida 1° de Mayo, run by an Argentine family of Italian descent and one of the better dinner options in central Puerto Iguazú. The menu is short and home-style — fresh pasta, gnocchi, eggplant parmigiana, a couple of meat mains, tiramisu — and the wine list is well-priced.

The interior is small (perhaps twelve tables) and the atmosphere is more like someone's dining room than a restaurant. Reservations recommended in peak season. A useful change of pace after several grill meals.
