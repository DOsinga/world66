The single most spectacular point of the Iguazú Falls: a horseshoe-shaped cataract 82 metres high and 150 metres wide where the Iguazú River drops in a continuous roaring curtain into a chasm so deep and so full of spray that the bottom is often invisible. The Argentine viewing platform stands directly over the lip of the falls — close enough that you can feel the river accelerating beneath your feet before it disappears into white water.

The platform is reached by an 1.1 km elevated walkway that snakes across the river above what is otherwise calm water. From the visitor centre take the free **Tren Ecológico de la Selva** (rainforest train) to the Garganta del Diablo station. Get there for the first or last train of the day for the smallest crowds. The walkway and platform get hit by a constant breeze of spray; wear a poncho or expect to get soaked.
