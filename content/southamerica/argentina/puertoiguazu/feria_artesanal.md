The town's main craft market, on a half-square block in the centre of Puerto Iguazú between Avenida Brasil and Avenida Misiones. The stalls sell carvings in *palo santo* and *guayaibí* wood, *mate* gourds and silver-rimmed straws, Guaraní bows and arrows, leather goods, and shelves of *yerba mate* from the local plantations.

Prices are not especially cheap by Argentine standards but are mostly fair — the Indigenous craft cooperatives that supply many of the stalls have driven up quality and consistency. Bargain politely. Best in the late afternoon when the heat eases.
