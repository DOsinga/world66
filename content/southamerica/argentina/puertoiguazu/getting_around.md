---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The town is small enough to walk across in 20 minutes. To the **falls**, public buses leave the bus terminal every 20 minutes (look for "Cataratas") and take about 30 minutes — pay in cash. To **Foz do Iguaçu** on the Brazilian side, frequent international buses leave from the same terminal and take about 45 minutes to an hour with the border crossing; immigration is handled on the bridge itself and is usually quick.

**Taxis** are cheap and metered. The most useful one-day option for the over-busy is to hire a remise (private car) for the day to do both sides of the falls — typically around US$80 for the car, plus park entrance fees. The town has no Uber, but the app-based **DiDi** works.
