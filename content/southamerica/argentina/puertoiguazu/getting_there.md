---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**Cataratas del Iguazú International Airport** (IGR), 25 km southeast of Puerto Iguazú on the road to the falls, has daily flights to Buenos Aires (Aeroparque and Ezeiza) on Aerolíneas Argentinas, JetSMART, and FlyBondi, plus a handful of regional connections. A shuttle bus runs from the airport to the bus terminal in town. From the airport you can also take a taxi straight to the falls — convenient if you only have a day.

Across the river, **Foz do Iguaçu International Airport** (IGU) in Brazil is larger and often cheaper — it has direct flights to São Paulo, Rio, Lima, and a few European cities. Border-crossing buses connect the two cities, but be aware that Brazil and Argentina have different visa requirements depending on your passport, and the customs queues at the bridges can be slow.

**By bus:** the **Iguazú bus terminal** on Avenida Córdoba has services to Buenos Aires (16–19 hours, several companies), Posadas (5 hours), and Asunción (Paraguay, 7 hours via the Tancredo Neves bridge). Sleeper-class buses to Buenos Aires are far more comfortable than the price suggests.
