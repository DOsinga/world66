The most popular paid add-on at the Argentine park: a 12-minute speedboat ride up the lower Iguazú River and **directly into the spray** of two of the larger cataracts. You will get completely, comprehensively soaked. Phones and cameras should be in dry bags or left behind.

Trips run from a small dock at the end of the Lower Circuit (a separate kiosk takes bookings — buy a ticket on arrival, or book ahead online if you are visiting in peak season). It's not cheap — typically around US$70 — but for many people it is the most vivid memory of the visit. Bring towels, a change of clothes, and waterproofs for everything you carry.
