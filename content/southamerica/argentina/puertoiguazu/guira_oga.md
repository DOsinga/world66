A wildlife rescue and rehabilitation centre on the road from Puerto Iguazú to the falls, run as a small not-for-profit. **Güira Oga** ("house of birds" in Guaraní) cares for around 200 birds, mammals, and reptiles confiscated from traffickers or injured in the wild — jaguars, ocelots, tapirs, harpy eagles, capuchin monkeys, and a variety of macaws.

The site is a short loop walk with a guide; about 90 minutes. It is the best place near Puerto Iguazú to see the larger jungle animals at close range, and the proceeds genuinely support rehabilitation work. Located 5 km east of the town centre; combine with a visit to the falls on the same road.
