The point where Argentina, Brazil, and Paraguay meet — at the confluence of the **Iguazú** and **Paraná** rivers, a few kilometres north of Puerto Iguazú town. The Argentine side has a small landscaped plaza with a blue, white, and gold concrete obelisk, a viewing platform over the rivers, a few craft stalls, and increasingly an evening light-and-sound show.

It is a quick stop — 30 minutes is enough — but the view is unique: the only place in the Americas where you can see three countries at once. The yellow Brazilian and red-and-blue Paraguayan obelisks are clearly visible on the opposite banks. Tour buses go there as part of city-tour packages; a taxi is cheap.
