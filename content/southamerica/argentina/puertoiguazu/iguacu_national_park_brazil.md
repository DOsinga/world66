The Brazilian side of the falls is faster, smaller, and gives you the postcard panoramic view that the Argentine side cannot. **Parque Nacional do Iguaçu** is essentially a single 1.2 km walkway running along the cliff opposite the main line of cascades, ending at an elevated platform pushed out into the spray of the Devil's Throat from below.

Allow about half a day. From Puerto Iguazú the bus journey across the **Tancredo Neves bridge** and through Foz do Iguaçu takes about 90 minutes; from inside the Brazilian park double-decker shuttle buses take you from the visitor centre to the walkway and back. The famous **Belmond Hotel das Cataratas** — the only hotel inside the park — sits on the cliff above the falls and has a terrace where non-guests can have a drink with the best view in the system. Pay the park admission fee in Brazilian reais.
