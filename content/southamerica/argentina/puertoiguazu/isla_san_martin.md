A small island in the middle of the Iguazú River, just below the main line of cascades. **Isla San Martín** is accessible by a free, frequent ferry from the dock at the end of the Lower Circuit — but only when the river is at moderate flow, which means it is closed for weeks at a time after heavy rain.

On the island a series of staircases climbs to viewpoints that look back at the **Salto San Martín** (the second-largest individual fall after the Devil's Throat) and across to the Brazilian side. The view from the highest point is unlike any other in the park: you are essentially inside the falls. Check on arrival in the park whether the ferry is running; if it is, do it. Allow an hour.
