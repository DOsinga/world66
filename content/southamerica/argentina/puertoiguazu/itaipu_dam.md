The world's second-largest hydroelectric dam (and for thirty years the largest), straddling the Paraná River between Brazil and Paraguay 30 km north of Foz do Iguaçu. **Itaipú** generates about 10% of Brazil's electricity and nearly 90% of Paraguay's. It is a useful piece of geography to combine with the falls — both are spectacles of South American water, and the contrast between the natural and the engineered is the point.

Tours leave from the visitor centre on the Brazilian side. The "panoramic" tour (about 90 minutes) takes a bus along the top of the dam; the "special" tour goes inside the turbine hall. Book ahead; bring your passport. From Puerto Iguazú a half-day excursion is straightforward.
