A small private garden in central Puerto Iguazú where the owners have set up dozens of sugar-water feeders and a covered viewing terrace from which you can sit and watch hummingbirds — sometimes 10–15 species at a time, often a hundred birds at a feeder. Tour groups stop here for 30 minutes; if you arrive independently you can stay as long as you like for a small donation.

The garden is on a quiet residential street about a 10-minute walk from the bus terminal. Photography is excellent; bring a long lens if you have one. Cool drinks are sold inside. Open mornings only — go early.
