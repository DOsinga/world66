A small, well-loved restaurant on Calle Curupí that does a thoughtful contemporary take on regional Misiones cuisine — surubí with cassava purée, beef cheeks slow-cooked with local beer, pacú escabeche, and a short menu of vegetable mains that is unusually good for a town heavy on grills. The cocktail list uses local ingredients (yerba mate, sugar cane).

The dining room is compact (perhaps fifteen tables) and books out in peak season — reserve a couple of days ahead. The chef-owner is often in the dining room and happy to talk through the menu.
