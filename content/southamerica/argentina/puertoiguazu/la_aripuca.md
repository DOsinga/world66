A timber-frame cultural centre off Ruta 12 on the road to the falls. **La Aripuca** is a wood-construction monument modelled on a Guaraní bird trap (the *aripuca*), assembled from 29 enormous tree trunks rescued from the deforestation of the surrounding rainforest — each tree species, each piece of timber, identified with a plaque.

The site doubles as a small museum on the regional ecology, a craft shop selling Indigenous wood and ceramic work, and a café serving local *yerba mate* and *chipá*. It is firmly in the "tour-bus stop" category but the underlying environmental story — the destruction of the Atlantic Forest, of which less than 10% remains — is serious. Allow 45 minutes.
