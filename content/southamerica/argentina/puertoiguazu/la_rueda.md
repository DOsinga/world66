A long-standing Argentine grill in the centre of town, in business since (as the name suggests) 1975. **La Rueda** does the classic Misiones combination of *parrilla* and river fish: bife de chorizo, *bondiola* (pork shoulder), grilled surubí, *pacú* fillets, plus generous salads and locally brewed beer.

The dining room is cavernous and busy most nights with a mix of tour groups and locals. Service is unfussy and quick. Portions are large; share if you can. One of the safer bets in town for a classic asado without surprises.
