A 1.4 km loop of stairways and walkways that descends below the rim of the falls, giving you the perspective from underneath. This is the more physically demanding of the two main circuits — many stairs, several hundred steps in total — but easily the most rewarding for photographs. Several of the cataracts are visible from directly beneath, and the path winds within a few metres of the curtain of water at the **Salto Bossetti**.

The Lower Circuit ends at the small boat dock on the Río Iguazú from which you can take a free ferry to **Isla San Martín** (when river conditions allow) and the **Gran Aventura** speedboat trips depart. Allow 2 hours for the circuit; longer if you do the island.
