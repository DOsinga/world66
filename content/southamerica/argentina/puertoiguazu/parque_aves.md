A 16-hectare private bird sanctuary in Brazil, just outside the entrance to the Iguaçu National Park. Parque das Aves houses more than 1,000 birds of 150 species, most of them rescued or born in captivity, in a series of walk-through aviaries large enough to feel almost like the surrounding rainforest.

The macaws are the highlight: vivid blue, scarlet, and green pairs flying between perches a metre above your head. There is also a toucan aviary (where they will steal anything shiny you are holding), a harpy eagle enclosure, several butterfly houses, and an excellent breeding programme for endangered species. Allow 2 hours; easily combined with a half-day at the Brazilian side of the falls.
