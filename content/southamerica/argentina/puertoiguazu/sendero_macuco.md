The longest hiking trail in the Argentine park: 7 km return through Atlantic rainforest, ending at the **Salto Arrechea** waterfall (small by Iguazú standards) and a swimming hole. The trail is the only way to see deep rainforest in the park — capuchin and howler monkeys, toucans, hundreds of butterflies, the occasional ocelot for the very lucky.

The trailhead is near the visitor centre, on the road that branches off opposite the train station. The path is flat and well-marked. Allow 3–4 hours including a swim. Go early — wildlife is most active in the cooler morning hours, and the trail is closed by 3pm to ensure walkers are out before sunset. Bring water and insect repellent.
