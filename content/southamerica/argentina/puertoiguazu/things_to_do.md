---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

There is essentially one reason to come to Puerto Iguazú — the falls — and everything else is a way of filling the time between visits. Allow at least one full day for the Argentine side, half a day for the Brazilian side, and consider an extra day for the **Triple Frontier**, the **Hito Tres Fronteras** viewpoint, and a wildlife centre or two on the way.

The falls are best in the **morning**, when the sun is behind you for photographs and the coatis have not yet eaten everyone's lunch.
