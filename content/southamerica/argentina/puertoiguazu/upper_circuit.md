A 1.7 km loop of elevated metal walkways along the top of the Argentine falls, with views down the lip of the major cascades — Bossetti, Bernabé Méndez, Adam and Eve. The walk is flat and accessible (no stairs) and takes about an hour at a normal pace, longer if you stop at every viewpoint.

The Upper Circuit is the best place to understand the geography of the falls system: dozens of separate cataracts spread along nearly 3 km of rim, separated by small jungle-covered islands. Mid-morning sun makes the rainbows; late afternoon backlights the spray. Combine with the Lower Circuit, which goes underneath several of the same falls.
