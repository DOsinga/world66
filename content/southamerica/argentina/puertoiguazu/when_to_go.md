---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Iguazú is subtropical: hot and humid most of the year, and the falls run year-round but vary substantially in volume. **August to November** is the best general window — moderate temperatures, less rain, manageable humidity, and the falls at solid (but not flood-stage) flow.

**December to February** is the local summer: 35°C+ heat, intense humidity, full hotels, and the falls at their fullest after the summer rains. This is the wettest period and the best time to see the Devil's Throat in full roar — but it is also when the spray is so heavy that you can sometimes barely see the falls from the closest walkways. **June and July** are the coolest months (15–22°C); pleasant for walking, fewer mosquitoes, but the river is at its lowest and the falls can look a little thin. Avoid Brazilian school holidays (mid-December to early February, July) and the Argentine winter break (mid-July) if you can — these are when the park is most crowded.
