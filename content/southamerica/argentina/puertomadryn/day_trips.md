[Trelew](/southamerica/argentina/trelew) and [Gaiman](/southamerica/argentina/gaiman) make an easy inland pair: dinosaurs in the morning, Welsh tea in the afternoon. Peninsula Valdes and Punta Tombo are longer wildlife days and are listed here as POIs because they are usually visited from Madryn rather than as separate overnight bases.
