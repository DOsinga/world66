Playa Parana is a useful escape from the busier central waterfront. The beach sits southeast of Puerto Madryn on the road toward Punta Loma, with open views, a rougher edge, and fewer people once you leave the city behind.

Divers know the area for nearby wrecks and clear-enough water on good days. For everyone else it is a simple place for a walk, a picnic, or a pause on the way to the sea-lion reserve.
