Punta Cuevas is where the Welsh story of Chubut begins. The first settlers arrived in 1865 and sheltered in rough caves near this headland before moving inland toward the Chubut Valley, where [Gaiman](/southamerica/argentina/gaiman) still keeps much of that heritage alive.

Today it is a low-key stop near Ecocentro rather than a grand monument. The reason to go is the combination of history, sea air, and bay views, especially if you are walking the coast south from the city centre.
