---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Resistencia eats simply and well. The Chaco produces good beef and *yacaré* (caiman), and is close enough to Paraguay that *chipá* (cassava cheese bread), *sopa paraguaya* (a savoury cornbread) and grilled river fish — *dorado*, *surubí* — turn up on most menus. Expect Argentine *parrilla* (mixed grill) restaurants on every other block, plus a handful of pizzerias and a few good cafés.

The streets around the Plaza 25 de Mayo have the most restaurants; **Charlie** and **Coco Vino** are the standard reliable upmarket grills, both with the standard *bife de chorizo* and *provoleta*. For an unfussy lunch, the market stalls behind the Mercado Central serve *empanadas* and *milanesas* for less than five dollars.
