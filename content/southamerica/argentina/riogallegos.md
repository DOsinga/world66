Rio Gallegos is the capital of Santa Cruz province in Patagonia, sitting at the mouth of the Gallegos River where it meets the Strait of Magellan. It is primarily a transit city — most travellers pass through on their way to El Calafate and the Perito Moreno glacier, or continuing south to Tierra del Fuego. That said, it's a perfectly functional place to spend a night and has a certain austral character to it.

The city has a frontier feel: wide streets, low buildings, and strong winds that sweep in off the Atlantic. The main square, [Plaza San Martín](/southamerica/argentina/riogallegos/plaza_san_martin), is tidy and pleasant, flanked by the [cathedral](/southamerica/argentina/riogallegos/cathedral_of_our_lady_of_lujan) and the provincial government buildings. The [Museo Regional Provincial](/southamerica/argentina/riogallegos/museo_regional_provincial) has a decent collection of Patagonian flora and fauna, including guanaco and rhea specimens. For those with time, the coast road out toward the [Cabo Vírgenes lighthouse](/southamerica/argentina/riogallegos/cabo_virgenes) offers dramatic views and a large Magellanic penguin colony.
