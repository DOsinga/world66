The seat of the Diocese of Rio Gallegos faces the Plaza San Martín on its south side. The current building dates from the 1950s, replacing an earlier wooden chapel — a typical Patagonian story, since most of the region's older buildings were either too flimsy or too remote to survive. The blue-tiled exterior is its most distinctive feature.

The interior is functional rather than grand. Worth a quick look if you are already on the square; not worth a detour otherwise.
