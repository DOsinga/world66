The mausoleum of former Argentine president Néstor Kirchner, a Rio Gallegos native, sits in the city's main cemetery about two kilometres south of the centre. The structure is modern, sober, and architecturally serious — a stone block with a glass-walled chamber containing the tomb.

Kirchner is a polarising figure in Argentine politics and the mausoleum reflects both sides: respected as a pilgrimage site by his supporters, ignored by his opponents. Whatever your politics, it's a striking piece of contemporary funerary architecture and worth half an hour if you're passing.
