Museo Las Lilas is a small, polished museum devoted to criollo art, especially the work of Florencio Molina Campos. His humorous, affectionate images of gauchos, horses, ranch life, and country types shaped how urban Argentina pictured the pampa.

The collection is useful even if you know nothing about Argentine folk art: it shows the difference between gaucho life as a working culture and gaucho life as national myth.
