---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Tucumán has a humid subtropical climate, hot and wet in summer (December to March) with afternoon thunderstorms common, and mild and dry in winter (June to August). The best months for visitors are **April, May, September, and October**, when temperatures are warm but not punishing and rainfall is low.

The big annual event is **9 July**, Argentine Independence Day, when Tucumán is the country's symbolic capital. The president traditionally addresses the nation from the Casa Histórica, parades fill the streets, and hotel prices spike — book well in advance if you want to be in the city. **Carnival** in February is more low-key here than in northeastern Argentina but still worth catching.
