[Junín de los Andes](/southamerica/argentina/junindelosandes) is an easy day for fishing culture, the Chimehuin River, and access toward Lanin volcano. [Bariloche](/southamerica/argentina/bariloche) is better as an onward stop than a true day trip, but the Seven Lakes road between the two is one of Argentina's great drives.
