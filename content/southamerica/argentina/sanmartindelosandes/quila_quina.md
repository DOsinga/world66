Quila Quina is one of the loveliest easy excursions from San Martín: a small settlement and beach on the south side of Lago Lacar, inside Lanin National Park. The water is cold, the setting is sheltered, and the forest comes right down toward the shore.

In summer, boats and road transfers make it straightforward. Out of season, check transport before committing; the gravel road and limited services are part of the charm only if you are prepared.
