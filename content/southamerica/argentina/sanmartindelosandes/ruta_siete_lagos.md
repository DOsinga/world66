The Seven Lakes route is the classic road trip of the northern Argentine Lake District. It leaves San Martín and winds south through forest, viewpoints, beaches, and cold blue lakes toward Villa La Angostura and eventually Bariloche.

You can do it by bus, tour, bike, or car, but a car gives the best freedom to stop. Do not rush it as a transfer if the weather is clear; this is one of those roads where the journey is the main attraction.
