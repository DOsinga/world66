Ave Caesar is one of the more reliably lively spots in the city centre — a café-bar that functions as a coffee stop in the mornings and drifts into wine and small plates as the afternoon goes on. The crowd is a mix of locals and the weekend visitors from Buenos Aires who've discovered it, and the atmosphere is easy and unhurried in the way that Tandil itself is. It's a good place to start an evening before moving on, or to sit and do nothing productive for an hour with a Malbec and whatever the kitchen is sending out.
