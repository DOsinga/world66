---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By bus**

The main way to reach Tandil from Buenos Aires is by long-distance coach from **Retiro bus terminal**. Several companies — including El Rápido and Flecha Bus — run frequent services covering the roughly 350 kilometres in **five to six hours**, depending on stops. Buses are comfortable, with semi-cama (reclining seat) and cama ejecutivo (near-flat) options on the longer services; book online at Plataforma 10 or directly with the bus company. Tandil's bus terminal is a short taxi or remis ride from the city centre.

**By car**

The drive from Buenos Aires takes around three and a half hours via the **Ruta Nacional 3** south to Azul, then cutting east on Ruta Provincial 74 into Tandil. The road is straightforward and in good condition. Having your own car is useful in Tandil — some of the best walking trails and viewpoints are a few kilometres outside the city centre, and the hills are easier to explore at your own pace.

**By air**

Tandil has a small airport (**Aeródromo Tandil**) with limited regional services. Most visitors arrive overland from Buenos Aires.
