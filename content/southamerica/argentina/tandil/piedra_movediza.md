The Piedra Movediza — the "moving stone" — is Tandil's defining landmark, and the hill park that bears its name is the first place most visitors head. The original granite boulder, which balanced improbably on the hilltop and rocked visibly in strong winds, fell in 1912; the replica installed in 2007 faithfully recreates the shape but is, predictably, bolted down. The site is worth visiting regardless: the hilltop gives panoramic views over the city and the surrounding pampa, and the approach through the park is a pleasant walk. There are picnic areas and a small café at the base.
