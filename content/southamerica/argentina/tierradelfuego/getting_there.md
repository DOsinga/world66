---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Ushuaia's Malvinas Argentinas International Airport (USH) has several daily flights from Buenos Aires (Aerolineas Argentinas and LATAM), with the flight taking about three and a half hours. During high season, book well in advance — seats on the Buenos Aires route fill quickly. There are also connections from Bariloche and El Calafate.

There is no land border crossing into the Argentine side of Tierra del Fuego without going through Chilean territory — National Route 3 runs through Chile on its way south to Ushuaia. Overland travellers typically cross from the Argentine mainland near Río Gallegos, transit through Chilean Tierra del Fuego, and cross back into Argentina via the ferry at Punta Delgada. Allow a full day for the overland journey from El Calafate.
