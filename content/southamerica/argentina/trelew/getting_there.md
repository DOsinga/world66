---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Almirante Marcos A. Zar Airport is just outside Trelew and is the main air gateway for both Trelew and [Puerto Madryn](/southamerica/argentina/puertomadryn). Taxis, remises, and rental cars connect the airport with the city and the coast.

Long-distance buses link Trelew with Puerto Madryn, [Gaiman](/southamerica/argentina/gaiman), Rawson, Comodoro Rivadavia, and Buenos Aires. If your real goal is Madryn's wildlife coast, check transfer times before assuming Trelew is only a quick stop.
