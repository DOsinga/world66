The single most important building in Argentine national memory: this is the colonial house where the congress of delegates from the former United Provinces of the Río de la Plata declared independence from Spain on 9 July 1816. The original house was largely demolished in 1903 — only the famous Salón de la Jura, where the signing took place, survived — and the building was then reconstructed in the 1940s.

The museum inside displays the original document of the declaration, period furniture, and portraits of the signatories. A sound-and-light show in the rear patio runs in the evenings.
