The classic loop heads west into the Sierra del Aconquija via the Garganta del Diablo road to Tafí del Valle, a high green valley at 2,000 m used for cattle and cheese production. From Tafí the road climbs further to the 3,000 m Abra del Infiernillo pass and drops into the rugged Calchaquí Valleys, ending at the pre-Inca ruins of Quilmes — the largest archaeological site in Argentina, abandoned in 1667 after the Spanish forcibly relocated the surviving population to Buenos Aires.

A full circuit through Cafayate to Salta makes one of South America's great drives but needs two or three days; it is doable as a long single-day round trip to Tafí and Quilmes.
