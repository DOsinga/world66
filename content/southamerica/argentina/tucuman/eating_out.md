---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Tucumán is famous for empanadas — the regional version is baked rather than fried, with a juicy beef filling that includes diced potato and a touch of cumin. Locals are vocal about which empanadería is best. The traditional time to eat them is at lunch with a glass of red wine from the nearby Valles Calchaquíes. Other regional dishes worth trying are tamales, locro, and humita, all rooted in the Andean north.
