---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Teniente General Benjamín Matienzo International Airport (TUC) is 8 km east of the centre, with daily flights to Buenos Aires (about 1h 45m) and seasonal regional connections.

The main bus terminal — Estación Terminal de Ómnibus Brigido Terán — is one of the largest in northern Argentina, with frequent overnight services to Buenos Aires (15–17 hours), Salta (4–5 hours), Córdoba (8 hours), and onward to La Quiaca on the Bolivian border. The Mitre train line operates a slow but cheap weekly sleeper service between Tucumán and Buenos Aires from the Estación Mitre, taking around 26 hours.
