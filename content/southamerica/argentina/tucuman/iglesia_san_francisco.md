A baroque Franciscan church on the southwest corner of Plaza Independencia, with a richly decorated late-colonial facade in cream and dark red. The Franciscans were the first religious order to settle in Tucumán in the late 16th century and the order's cloister behind the church is one of the oldest standing structures in the city.

Inside, the gilded altarpiece and a small museum of religious art make a quick stop on a walk around the plaza.
