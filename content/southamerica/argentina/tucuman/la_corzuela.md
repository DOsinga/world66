A more upmarket parrilla and regional kitchen, set in a converted house with a leafy outdoor patio. The asado is the obvious draw — lomo, bife de chorizo, mollejas — but the kitchen also turns out solid versions of the regional empanadas and tamales.

A safe choice for a serious dinner.
