A small folk museum housed in another well-preserved colonial-era townhouse, displaying textiles, musical instruments, mate gourds, and ceramics from across northwest Argentina. The collection focuses on the rural traditions of the Calchaquí Valleys and the Andean foothills.

A good complement to the Sugar Museum for understanding the cultural depth of the region beyond the city's national-history reputation. Free admission.
