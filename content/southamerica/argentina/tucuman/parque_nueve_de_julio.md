The city's largest public park, a 400-hectare green expanse of lakes, gardens, and sports facilities east of the centre, designed by the French landscape architect Charles Thays in the early 20th century. Families and joggers fill it on weekends.

Inside the park are a small zoo, the racetrack, the football stadium of Atlético Tucumán, the Casa del Obispo Colombres (now the Sugar Museum), and a Japanese garden donated by the local Japanese community. The northern lake is a popular spot for paddle boats.
