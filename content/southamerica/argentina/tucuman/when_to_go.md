---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The subtropical climate makes summer (December to March) hot, humid, and prone to afternoon thunderstorms. Winter (June to August) is mild in the city and pleasant for sightseeing, though nights can be cold and the surrounding mountains see snow.

Spring (September to November) is the most comfortable. Independence Day on 9 July fills the centre with celebrations and is worth timing a visit around. The provincial Día de la Independencia commemorations centre on the Casa Histórica.
