Museo de Arte Contemporaneo Conrad Meier is a small regional art museum with drawings, paintings, photography, and a handful of modern sculptures. It is a modest stop rather than a headline sight, but it adds a bit of indoor culture to a town better known for lake views and mountain walks.

Drop in if you have a spare hour in the centre or if bad weather has pushed you off the trails. The scale is manageable, and the focus on contemporary work gives it a different feel from the usual Patagonian history displays.
