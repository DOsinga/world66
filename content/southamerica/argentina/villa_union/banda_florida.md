Banda Florida is a small village a few kilometres from Villa Unión, sitting against low red hills with adobe houses, vineyards, and fruit trees. It is modest, but it gives a closer look at the Bermejo Valley than a quick stop in town.

Use it as a short warm-up or wind-down around bigger excursions. The appeal is the setting and the slow rural texture, not a checklist of monuments.
