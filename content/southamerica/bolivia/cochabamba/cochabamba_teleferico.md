Cochabamba's teleferico climbs from the city toward the hill of Cristo de la Concordia, turning a steep approach into a short aerial ride. The cars give you quick views over the valley, the city grid, and the mountains that frame Cochabamba's famously mild climate.

It is both transport and attraction. Ride it for the view even if you are not especially focused on the statue, and try to go in clear weather before haze softens the edges of the valley.
