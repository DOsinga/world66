---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Yungas have two seasons. The **dry season (May to October)** has clearer skies, easier hiking, more reliable mountain biking on the Death Road, and the famous views over the valleys. Nights are cool (10–15°C) but days are warm and sunny. This is by far the better season to visit and the period when most travellers arrive.

The **rainy season (November to April)** is hot, humid, and frequently cloudy. Trails turn to mud, landslides are common, and the views vanish behind cloud for days at a time. It is still beautiful — flowers, butterflies, dramatic clouds in the valleys — but the practical drawbacks are real. April is a good shoulder month: the rains are easing and the forest is at its lushest.

The town celebrates its **patronal festival** at the end of October with three days of dancing, brass bands, and parades. Worth timing a visit around if you can.
