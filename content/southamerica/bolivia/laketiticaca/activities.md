The Bolivian side of Titicaca is quieter than the Peruvian: Copacabana on its bay, the Isla del Sol an hour out by boat, and the reed islands further north.

The two operators below run the hydrofoil and catamaran services that have worked this lake for decades — a different proposition from the public launches, and the only way to cross to Puno on the Peruvian side without a bus.
