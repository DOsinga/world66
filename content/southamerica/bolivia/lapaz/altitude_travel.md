An agency working out of the Wild Rover hostel on Comercio, which tells you most of what you need to know about the clientele, and which is also the reason they are open until ten at night when everyone else on Sagárnaga shut at six.

The list is genuinely national: Rurrenabaque jungle and pampas, Huayna Potosí, the Choro and Takesi treks, Condoriri, Sajama, Copacabana and the Isla del Sol, the Potosí mines, Sucre, the Jesuit missions, cholitas wrestling and a La Paz walking tour. The Death Road they sell under a separate brand, One Death Road, at $70 on a Scott or $80 on a full-suspension Transition — half what Gravity charges, on a road where the difference between operators is the brakes and the guide-to-rider ratio. They publish theirs as one to five.

Open 09:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and until 21:30 at weekends. Three phones, of which the first is the WhatsApp; the third is marked for last-minute problems only.
