"Bolivia en Ascenso, la montaña nos une" — a Spanish-first agency working the Cordillera Real, with named guides on the site, a gallery, a blog and a published calendar of upcoming departures, which is more than most La Paz climbing agencies manage.

Illimani and Huayna Potosí are the two they lead with, and their own pages on them are written by somebody who has been up: the note that Huayna Potosí's glaciers have retreated far enough that what was an easy ascent a decade ago is now genuinely technical is the kind of thing a sales page leaves out.

They publish no office address — one mobile, answered on WhatsApp, and an email.
