The deepest list of any climbing agency in La Paz, and the one that reads as though it were written by people who go up rather than people who sell. Fifteen summits from Huayna Potosí and Pequeño Alpamayo to Illampu, Ancohuma and Chaupi Orco on the Peruvian border, seventeen treks including a fourteen-day Transcordillera and three Apolobamba circuits, and a mountaineering course for people who have not done this before.

The guide's leader, Julio Choque, holds IFMGA certification — the international qualification, not a national one — and the agency says several of its guides sit on the national mountain rescue team. On fixed group departures they run one guide to every two clients, minimum two people and maximum ten, and they will run a scheduled date with a single participant rather than cancel it.

They publish no office address and no prices: contact is a mobile and an email in Eduardo Mamani's name.
