Museo San Francisco occupies part of La Paz's restored San Francisco religious complex. Exhibits explain the church's role in Bolivian history, including events tied to the 1809 independence movement, and displays are presented in Spanish and English.

The tower climb is the best reason to add the museum to a visit to the basilica, with views over the indigenous and mestizo quarters around the old center.
