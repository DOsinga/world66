The Musical Instrument Museum holds a wide collection of sound-producing instruments from Bolivia and beyond. It was founded by charango player and instrument maker Ernesto Cavour, whose experimental creations are among the most memorable displays. Some exhibits are hands-on, which makes the small museum more engaging than a simple cabinet collection.
