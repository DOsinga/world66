The Cardozo Velásquez House Museum is the former home of Bolivian sculptor Víctor Cárdenas Velásquez, who turned his house into an artwork over the course of his lifetime. Velásquez was known for his work in stone, particularly spheres — he produced thousands of them in various sizes — and embedded his sculptures throughout every surface of his home, creating a labyrinthine accumulation of carved stone, folk imagery, and personal mythology that is unlike anything else in Bolivia.

The house is now preserved as a museum and visited mostly by locals curious about the eccentric genius who lived here. It is not polished or well-promoted, which adds to its appeal — this is what it looks like when an artist devotes an entire life to a single obsession. Ring the bell at the gate; hours are irregular.
