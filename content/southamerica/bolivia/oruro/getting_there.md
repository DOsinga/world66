---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Oruro has an airport (Juan Mendoza Airport) with limited domestic service, mostly to La Paz and Santa Cruz. Most travellers arrive by bus or train from La Paz (about 3–4 hours by bus) or Cochabamba (4–5 hours). The bus terminal is on the eastern edge of the city centre.

The train connection is one of the better overland options in Bolivia. Ferroviaria Andina runs services south from Oruro to Uyuni (the jumping-off point for the Salar de Uyuni salt flat) and onward to Villazón on the Argentine border — a spectacular journey across the altiplano. Check current schedules before arriving, as services are sometimes suspended.
