Rurrenabaque is the gateway to the Bolivian Amazon and the reason most people fly rather than drive: the road from La Paz is long and the flight is under an hour.

Two quite different trips leave from here. The **pampas** are flooded grassland up the Río Yacuma — motorised canoes, caiman, capybara and pink river dolphins, and the wildlife is easy to see. The **jungle** is Madidi National Park, denser, harder to see anything in, and far more interesting for it. Madidi is one of the most biodiverse protected areas on earth, and the lodges inside it are community-owned.
