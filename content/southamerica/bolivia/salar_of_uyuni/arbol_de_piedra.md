Árbol de Piedra is the famous stone tree of the Siloli Desert, a volcanic rock formation narrowed at its base by windblown sand. It looks almost too deliberate, as if someone balanced the top on a pedestal and then walked away into the desert.

The stop is popular because it gives a single, memorable shape to the strangeness of this part of Bolivia. Keep some distance when taking photos; the rock is fragile, and the best view is often from a few paces back, where the tree sits among other eroded stones and the empty altiplano.
