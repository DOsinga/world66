Isla del Pescado is one of the strange rocky islands that break the white geometry of the Salar de Uyuni. In the dry season it feels like a low, cactus-covered ship run aground in salt; from the top, the flats stretch away in bright polygons toward the volcanoes.

Most tours stop here or at nearby Incahuasi when the surface is firm enough to drive across. Take sunglasses, water, and a little patience for the crowds: the view is still extraordinary, especially when the scale of the salar finally becomes obvious from above.
