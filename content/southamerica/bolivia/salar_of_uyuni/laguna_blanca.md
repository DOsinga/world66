Laguna Blanca is one of the first great colour changes on the southwestern Bolivia circuit: a pale, mineral-rich lake lying below the cone of Licancabur, close to the Chilean border. After hours of salt, rock, and brown altiplano, the water has a washed, milky brightness that makes the volcano look even more severe.

Most travellers see it on the long jeep route between Salar de Uyuni and San Pedro de Atacama, often paired with nearby Laguna Verde. The stop is brief but worthwhile, especially in clear weather, when the wind carves hard ripples across the surface and the border mountains feel very close.
