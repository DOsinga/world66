Laguna Colorada is one of the great moments of the Uyuni circuit. The lake can look rust-red, copper, or almost purple depending on the season and light, with white borax deposits breaking the surface and flamingos feeding in the shallows.

The altitude and wind make it feel raw, not postcard-pretty, and that is part of the appeal. Arrive with warm layers even in strong sun, and linger long enough to see how quickly the colour changes as clouds move across the water.
