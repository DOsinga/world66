Laguna Verde is a high-altitude lake near Licancabur volcano, close to the Chilean border. Minerals in the water can give it a vivid green color, especially when wind stirs the surface, though the color is not guaranteed every morning.

Most visitors see it as part of a multi-day Salar de Uyuni and Eduardo Avaroa reserve route. The landscape is stark, windy, and very exposed, so bring warm layers even on clear days.
