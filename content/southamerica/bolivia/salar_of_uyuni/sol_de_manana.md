Sol de Mañana is the geothermal field that makes the Uyuni circuit feel suddenly alive underfoot. Steam vents, sulphur smells, and bubbling mud pools break through a cold, treeless plateau at roughly 4,800 metres.

Many tours arrive around dawn, when the steam shows strongly in the freezing air. Stay on the obvious paths and listen to local guidance: there are no elaborate barriers, and the ground around the vents can be dangerous as well as dramatic.
