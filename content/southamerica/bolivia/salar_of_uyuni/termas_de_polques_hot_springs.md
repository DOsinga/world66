Termas de Polques is a simple but memorable hot-spring stop on the southern Uyuni route. The pools are not fancy, but soaking in warm mineral water while the altiplano wind moves over the empty plain is a hard experience to improve.

Most tours arrive early, when the air is cold and the contrast is at its best. Bring a towel you can reach quickly, sandals for the rough ground, and realistic expectations: the facilities are basic, but the setting is the point.
