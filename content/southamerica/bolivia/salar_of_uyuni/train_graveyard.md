The Train Graveyard sits just outside Uyuni, where abandoned locomotives and wagons have been left to rust in the dry altiplano air. The engines are relics of the railways built to move minerals toward Chilean ports, and their stripped frames now make one of the strangest introductions to the Salar de Uyuni circuit.

Most salt-flat tours stop here briefly before heading onto the open salar. It is at its best early or late in the day, when the metal throws long shadows and the place feels less like a photo stop and more like an industrial ruin slowly being reclaimed by wind and salt.
