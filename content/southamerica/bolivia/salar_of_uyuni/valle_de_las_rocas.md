Valle de las Rocas is a spread of eroded volcanic formations on the highland route connected with Salar de Uyuni tours. The rocks rise from the plain in odd towers, blocks, and animal-like shapes, all sharpened by wind and sand.

It is less iconic than Árbol de Piedra, but it gives a better sense of the whole landscape rather than one famous formation. Walk carefully around the outcrops and watch the light; in late afternoon the rocks take on warmer colours and the valley feels much larger than it first appears from the road.
