A small ecotourism operator in Samaipata, the colonial village below the pre-Inca rock carvings of El Fuerte, run by Michael Blendinger and Gaby Cunningham with a team of local scouts. What they sell is nature interpretation rather than transport: trekking into Amboró, horseback rides, and birdwatching in the parks and the remote spots on the Andean–Amazonian edge, which is one of the richest bird transitions on the continent.

Their website is ancient and their contact page lists four numbers and two email addresses. None of them is advertised as a WhatsApp line — write, or ring.
