AFASI is a Bolivian foundation running a 44-hectare protected park outside Santa Cruz, where rescued and endangered lowland wildlife is given habitat rather than enclosures. The reserve is open to visitors, and the foundation takes volunteers.

It sits in the forest-and-savannah country around the city — Nick's Adventures, who tip it, put it at about 45 minutes out. AFASI's own site is emphatic about one thing on the way in: use the tarmac road, the one marked blue on their map, rather than whichever shortcut a navigation app offers.

A caution on the pin here. The only coordinate AFASI publishes is the point on its website's map, which falls inside Santa Cruz itself and cannot be the reserve; it is the foundation's own marker rather than the park gate. Check the map on afasi.org before setting off, and confirm opening arrangements by phone — a 44-hectare private reserve is not somewhere to turn up unannounced.
