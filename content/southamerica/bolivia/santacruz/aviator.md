Wings, burgers and beer, out towards Equipetrol on Avenida Marcelo Terceros. It trades as Aviator Wings & Beer and is the sort of place that fills up late and stays loud.

Worth knowing as the counterweight to the churrasquerías and the traditional lunch places: when you have eaten beef and yuca for four days running, this is where the city goes for something else.
