---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Santa Cruz works best as a base. The city itself is short on sights, but within a few hours' drive lie some of Bolivia's most spectacular landscapes and cultural sites.

The Lomas de Arena dunes are the closest excursion — a half-day with a 4x4. Amboró National Park, the cloud forest of Samaipata and the pre-Inca rock of El Fuerte, and Biocentro Güembé all make full-day or one-night trips. The Jesuit Missions of Chiquitos need at least four days to do properly. Further afield, multi-day tours run into the Pantanal wetlands toward the Brazilian border, and to the Beni jungle around Trinidad and Rurrenabaque.
