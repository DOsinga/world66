A Spanish-language Bolivian operator with an unusually complete national list: the Death Road and Coroico, Copacabana and the islands, Tiwanaku, Sajama, the Potosí mines, four different Uyuni formats including a two-to-ten-day photography trip with indigenous communities, Sucre's dinosaur tracks, Samaipata, Rurrenabaque and Madidi.

Their own speciality is the **Chiquitania Jesuit missions** circuit east of Santa Cruz — the wooden baroque churches that are a UNESCO site and one of the strangest, best things in South America. They describe it as a standard part of any Santa Cruz visit, which it should be and rarely is.

The website publishes no address, no phone and no email: a contact form and a WhatsApp button.
