The National Military History Museum has a broad collection of Bolivian and international military objects, including artillery, aircraft, engines, typewriters, uniforms, and small arms. The strongest rooms are those that connect the collection to Bolivia's own conflicts, especially the Pacific War and the country's loss of its coast.

Explanations are mostly in Spanish, so it is most rewarding if you can read some Spanish or already have an interest in military history.
