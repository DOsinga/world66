---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tarija's main pleasures are slow ones: sitting in the main square, visiting one of the local bodegas, wandering the colonial streets in the mild afternoon sun. The city is small and human-scaled, the sort of place where you walk everywhere and find unexpected things.

The [Valle de la Concepción](/southamerica/bolivia/tarija/valle_de_la_concepcion), 15 km south of the city, is the heartland of Bolivian wine production and the main attraction for visitors with more than a day. The [Catedral de San Bernardo](/southamerica/bolivia/tarija/catedral_san_bernardo) anchors the main plaza and is one of the better colonial churches in the region. The [Casa Dorada](/southamerica/bolivia/tarija/casa_dorada) is the city's most ornate building and houses a small museum of local history. The Chapaco festival culture is worth timing a visit around if you can — Tarija has more festivals than any other Bolivian city relative to its size.
