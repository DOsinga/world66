---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Tupiza sits at 2,950 metres in a valley that is much milder than the high altiplano. The best time to visit is the dry season from **May to October**, when days are sunny and pleasantly warm and nights are cold and clear. June and July nights can drop below freezing — bring a sleeping bag if you are camping.

The rainy season from December to March brings dramatic skies and green canyons, but the dirt roads to San Vicente and the Salar can become impassable, and horseback trips are sometimes cancelled. April and November are good shoulder months.
