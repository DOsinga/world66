The only real day trip from Uyuni is the [Salar de Uyuni](/southamerica/bolivia/salar_of_uyuni) itself, but that single trip is the entire reason most people make the journey to this corner of the Altiplano. Standard one-day tours leave at dawn for the train cemetery, the salt village of Colchani, Incahuasi Island in the middle of the flat, and back by sunset. Multi-day tours continue south into the Eduardo Avaroa Reserve.
