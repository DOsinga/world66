---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air:** Joya Andina Airport (UYU) has daily flights from La Paz with Boliviana de Aviación, taking around an hour. The terminal is basic but functional, and a short taxi ride from town.

**By train:** The Expreso del Sur and Wara Wara del Sur trains run between Oruro and Uyuni several times a week, continuing south to Tupiza and Villazón on the Argentine border. The trip from Oruro takes around seven hours and is one of the few comfortable long-distance train rides left in South America. Tickets sell out — book ahead at the station or through a travel agency.

**By bus:** Overnight buses connect Uyuni with La Paz (10–12 hours), [Sucre](/southamerica/bolivia/sucre) (8 hours), [Potosí](/southamerica/bolivia/potosi) (4–5 hours), and [Tupiza](/southamerica/bolivia/tupiza) (5 hours). The road is now paved most of the way to Potosí, though the Altiplano nights can be brutally cold.

**From Chile:** Many travellers reach Uyuni at the end of a multi-day jeep tour starting from San Pedro de Atacama, crossing into Bolivia at Hito Cajón near Laguna Verde.
