Águas de Lindóia is a small spa town in the hills of São Paulo state, part of the Circuito das Águas Paulista. Its identity rests on mineral water: drinking fountains, baths, old resort habits, and the idea that a short stay should be good for the body as well as the mood.

The town is modest, but it has a clear shape for a relaxed day. Start at the [Balneário Municipal](/southamerica/brazil/aguas_de_lindoia/balneario_municipal), walk through [Praça Adhemar de Barros](/southamerica/brazil/aguas_de_lindoia/praca_adhemar_de_barros), then head toward [Morro Pelado](/southamerica/brazil/aguas_de_lindoia/morro_pelado) for broader views of the green hills around the state line with Minas Gerais.

This is not a place for urban drama or nightlife. It is for warm water, slow walks, families, older travellers, and anyone who likes Brazil's inland spa towns with a little kitsch left intact.
