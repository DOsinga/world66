The Balneário Municipal is the classic Águas de Lindóia stop: a public spa complex built around the town's mineral waters. Treatments and access can change with renovations and season, but the place explains why the town exists.

Even if you are not booking a bath or massage, it is worth seeing as part of Brazil's old hydro-mineral resort tradition.
