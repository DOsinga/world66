Praça Adhemar de Barros is the town's outdoor living room, with a lake, bridges, lawns, benches, and the mountains close behind. It is not a grand urban square; it is the place for a slow circuit, a photo, and a pause between spa treatments and hill viewpoints.

The park's design is often linked locally to Roberto Burle Marx. Go early or late if you want softer light and fewer parked cars around the edges.
