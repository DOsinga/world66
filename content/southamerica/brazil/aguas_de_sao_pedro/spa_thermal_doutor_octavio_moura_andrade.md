The municipal spa is the reason to take Águas de São Pedro seriously. It offers mineral-water baths and treatments using the town's famous sulphurous water, drawn from a deep artesian source first linked to old oil exploration.

You do not need to believe every medicinal claim to enjoy the ritual. The building, the smell of sulphur, and the slow pace put you squarely inside Brazil's old hydromineral-resort tradition.
