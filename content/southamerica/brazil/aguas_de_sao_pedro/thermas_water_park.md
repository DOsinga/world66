Thermas Water Park sits outside Águas de São Pedro in neighbouring São Pedro, but it is part of the same visitor circuit. It is the practical choice if you are travelling with children or want a whole day of pools and slides rather than a short spa stop.

This is leisure rather than local history, and weekends can be busy. Still, it gives the town's water identity a louder, more modern version and helps make the area worth a day trip.
