---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Angra dos Reis is busy year-round, but the rhythm of the place changes with the seasons. The hottest months — December through February — coincide with Brazilian summer holidays, when wealthy Cariocas and Paulistas descend on the bay with their boats and the islands become crowded. Accommodation prices spike at Carnival, New Year, and throughout the summer school break.

The shoulder months of April, May, September, and October offer a good balance: warm enough to swim, quieter on the water, and cheaper to sleep. The Atlantic Forest on Ilha Grande is at its greenest after the rains.

July brings a second wave of domestic tourists for the winter school holiday — still busy, but nothing like summer. The wettest months are December through March; rain comes as heavy afternoon thunderstorms that clear quickly rather than all-day drizzle.
