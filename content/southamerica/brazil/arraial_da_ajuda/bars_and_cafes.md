---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The [Rua do Mucugê](/southamerica/brazil/arraial_da_ajuda/rua_do_mucuge) is the centre of Arraial's nightlife, lined with bars that start quiet and build through the evening. [La Morocha](/southamerica/brazil/arraial_da_ajuda/la_morocha) is the liveliest spot — flaming cocktails, live music, and multilingual bartenders who keep the crowd entertained. The village also has regular beach parties, full moon parties, and forró nights, especially during high season and festivals.
