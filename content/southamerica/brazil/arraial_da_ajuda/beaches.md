---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Arraial's coastline stretches south from the village along a series of beaches backed by low red-brown cliffs. [Mucugê](/southamerica/brazil/arraial_da_ajuda/praia_do_mucuge), [Parracho](/southamerica/brazil/arraial_da_ajuda/praia_do_parracho), and [Pitinga](/southamerica/brazil/arraial_da_ajuda/praia_da_pitinga) are the most popular — Mucugê and Parracho have beach bars and music, while Pitinga has dramatic clay cliffs and more space. Further south, [Taipe](/southamerica/brazil/arraial_da_ajuda/praia_de_taipe) and Rio da Barra are quieter and emptier. [Araçaipe](/southamerica/brazil/arraial_da_ajuda/praia_do_aracaipe), near the ferry crossing, is close to town and good for sunset. All the beaches have warm water year-round.
