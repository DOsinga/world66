---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Arraial has a surprising number of restaurants for a small village, ranging from Italian to sushi. For Brazilian food, [Paulinho Pescador](/southamerica/brazil/arraial_da_ajuda/paulinho_pescador) serves good seafood at reasonable prices. São João, on the square in front of the church, is another local favourite. The best value is [A Portinha](/southamerica/brazil/arraial_da_ajuda/a_portinha), a kilo restaurant (you pay by weight) with a huge variety of dishes and desserts that are hard to find anywhere else in the region.
