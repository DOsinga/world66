---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Arraial is a beach village first, so most days revolve around picking a stretch of sand, watching the sun, and stopping at a *barraca* for grilled fish. The hilltop **Igreja Nossa Senhora d'Ajuda** is the only old building of note: walk up the steep cobbled lane to see the view down to the river and across to Porto Seguro. The square in front — the **Largo da Igreja** — is the heart of the village and the best place to watch evening light catch the whitewashed church facade.

For something more active, the **Eco Parque** offers waterslides and a forest setting, and **Pitinga's** clay cliffs are good for an afternoon of scrambling and photo-taking. Walking south along the beach you reach progressively quieter sands; with a half day you can make it as far as **Taipe** and back. The village is small enough that almost everything worth seeing is between the church square and the shoreline.
