Atins is a small fishing village on the edge of Lençóis Maranhenses National Park, where the Preguiças River meets the Atlantic. It feels remote in a very physical way: sandy streets, scattered houses, weak mobile signal, and no easy arrival by ordinary road.

That roughness is part of the draw. Atins gives you access to the quieter eastern side of the dunes, with lagoon trips that see fewer vehicles than the standard routes from Barreirinhas. It is also one of Brazil's better kitesurfing bases, with steady wind from July to December and a broad river-mouth lagoon for beginners.

The village itself is simple. Days are built around wind, sand, boats, and meals of shrimp or fish. Walks are tiring because the streets are soft sand, so distances feel longer than they look on a map.

Atins is worth the effort if you want Lençóis Maranhenses with a slower edge. It is less convenient than Barreirinhas, but the combination of dunes, kitesurfing, red ibis trips, and a real sandy-village identity gives it enough character for a stay.
