Atins is small, but the sand makes it slow. Walking is fine for short hops in the morning or evening, while midday sand can be hot and exhausting.

For lagoon tours and transfers to the port, use local 4x4s, quadbikes, boats, or arranged pickups. If you are staying outside the village centre, confirm transport before you arrive.
