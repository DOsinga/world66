Cristo Luz looks over Balneário Camboriú from the hills behind the high-rise beachfront, a lit statue of Christ that changes colour at night and turns into one of the city's easiest landmarks. The statue itself is less subtle than scenic, but the view explains why people come: the curve of the bay, the dense wall of towers, and the Atlantic beyond.

Go near sunset if the weather is clear. By day it is a viewpoint; after dark it becomes part of the resort city's neon personality. It pairs well with the cable car and Barra Sul waterfront, giving you a second high angle on a city that is often best understood from above.
