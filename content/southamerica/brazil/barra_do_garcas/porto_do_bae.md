Porto do Baé is Barra do Garças' riverfront living room. It sits by the Araguaia, with views toward the meeting of the rivers, boat ramps, event space, and a cluster of bars and restaurants.

Come late in the day when the heat drops. In the dry season, the wider Araguaia river-beach culture is one of the city's main pleasures.
