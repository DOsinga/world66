Vale dos Vinhedos is the reason Bento Gonçalves earns a place on the map. The valley holds Brazil's first DOC wine appellation, and the hills are lined with wineries ranging from large producers to family cellars pouring sparkling wine, Chardonnay, Merlot, grape juice, and the occasional rougher country red.

This is a good place to be unhurried. Book one proper cellar tour, stop at a smaller tasting room, and leave time for lunch among the vines. The wine is strongest when you do not expect Bordeaux or Tuscany; it is southern Brazilian, Italian-descended, and improving fast.
