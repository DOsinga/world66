The bars along Rua Coronel Pilad Rebuá serve caipirinhas — Brazil's national cocktail — alongside local Pantanal craft spirits and cold beer. In peak season the street is lively well into the night, with music spilling out of several venues. Outside of July and the summer holidays, things wind down earlier and options are fewer. A pleasant spot to compare notes with other travellers after a day on the water.
