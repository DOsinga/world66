---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

For a small town, Bonito has a solid restaurant scene shaped by the ecotourism trade and the local river fish. The star ingredients are freshwater fish from the rivers of Mato Grosso do Sul — pintado, piraputanga, and pacu appear on most menus, grilled or fried. Prices are higher than you might expect given the town's size, reflecting the volume of domestic tourists passing through.
