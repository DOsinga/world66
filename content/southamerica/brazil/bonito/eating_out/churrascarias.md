Traditional Brazilian churrascarias — open-fire barbecue restaurants — are well represented in Bonito. The regional twist is that grilled river fish sits alongside beef and pork on the menu; pintado and pacu cooked over charcoal are the highlight and rarely disappoints. Rodízio (all-you-can-eat) format is common. A good option after a long day on the river.
