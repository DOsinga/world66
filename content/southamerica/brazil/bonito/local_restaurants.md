Bonito's main street has several restaurants serving the regional Mato Grosso do Sul kitchen: grilled pintado (spotted catfish), piraputanga, and pacu are the local fish to try, typically served simply with rice, beans, and farofa. Sobá — a cold noodle soup brought to the region by Japanese-Brazilian immigrants and now a Pantanal staple — appears on many menus and is worth ordering. Most places are casual and family-friendly.
