---
title: "Things To Do"
type: section
---

Bonito's ecotourism attractions are managed through a visitor quota system — all activities must be booked in advance through a local licensed agency. You cannot buy tickets independently at the gate. Agencies handle logistics, guides, and transport to the reserves, which is straightforward and keeps crowds manageable. Book as far ahead as possible, especially for July school holidays and January–February high season.
