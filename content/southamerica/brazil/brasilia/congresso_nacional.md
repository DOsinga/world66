Congresso Nacional is one of Brasilia's defining modernist landmarks, with Oscar Niemeyer's twin towers and paired bowl-like chambers anchoring the eastern end of the Monumental Axis.

Guided visits usually explain both the architecture and the working chambers of Brazil's legislature. Security rules can change, so bring identification and expect access limits during official sessions.
