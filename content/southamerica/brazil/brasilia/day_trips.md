**Chapada dos Veadeiros** (about 220 km north) is one of Brazil's most beautiful national parks — a high plateau of waterfalls, canyons, and clear pools where you can swim — but it is more of a long weekend than a true day trip. **Pirenópolis** (150 km west) is a colonial gold-rush town with cobbled streets and a fine cathedral, three hours by car.

Closer to hand, the **Águas Emendadas Ecological Station** an hour northeast is one of the few places on Earth where two major watersheds (Amazon and Plata) meet from a single spring. The **Catedral Rainha da Paz** in Sobradinho, with a 32-metre fibreglass dome, is another half-day's outing.
