The Feira da Torre runs every weekend at the base of the [TV Tower](/southamerica/brazil/brasilia/brasilia_tv_tower) — about 600 stalls of handmade jewellery, leather, hammocks, ceramics, indigenous craft, and a long food court of regional dishes from across Brazil. It is the most popular weekend market in the city and one of the few places where Brasília feels like a Latin American capital rather than a planned utopia.

Open Saturday and Sunday 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., busiest in the afternoon. Bring cash for the smaller stalls.
