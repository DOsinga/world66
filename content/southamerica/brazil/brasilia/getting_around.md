Brasília was built for cars, and you will need either a car, a ride-share app, or a lot of patience for the bus system. Distances are deceptive — what looks close on the map can be a long walk in 35°C heat with little shade and few pedestrian crossings. Uber and 99 work reliably and are reasonably cheap.

A small **metro** runs between the central station and the southwest suburbs but is not useful for sightseeing. The cheap and frequent **Rodoviária do Plano Piloto** is the city's main bus hub, just below the platform between the two axes. For the monumental axis itself, several bus circuits start from there — buses 108 and 131 cover the main sights.

If you visit on a Sunday, the Eixo Monumental is partially closed to cars and turns into a long urban park — perfect for cycling end-to-end.
