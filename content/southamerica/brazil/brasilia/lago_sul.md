Lago Sul wraps the south shore of Lake Paranoá: low-rise mansions on tree-shaded plots, the Embassy district, the [Palácio da Alvorada](/southamerica/brazil/brasilia/palacio_da_alvorada), and the most expensive houses in Brasília. The original plan reserved this zone exclusively for single-family villas (Asa Sul and Asa Norte had collective apartment blocks), and the difference is immediate — bigger gardens, longer driveways, lower density.

For visitors, the main attractions are the embassies (some open for events) and the [Pontão do Lago Sul](/southamerica/brazil/brasilia/pontao_lago_sul) restaurant strip on the lakeshore. It is also where the [Dom Bosco Sanctuary](/southamerica/brazil/brasilia/dom_bosco_sanctuary) sits.
