The Pontão is a 6-hectare lakeside leisure complex on the south shore of Lake Paranoá, with about twenty restaurants and bars facing west across the water. On a clear evening it is the most reliably picturesque place to be in Brasília: families on the grass, joggers on the lakeside boardwalk, and the silhouette of the [JK Bridge](/southamerica/brazil/brasilia/jk_bridge) framing the sunset.

Cheaper than its position suggests — many of the restaurants are mid-priced. The Mormaii Surf Bar and Belini Cafe are popular for sundowners.
