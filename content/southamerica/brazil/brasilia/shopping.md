Brasília's shopping happens in malls — the city was designed without a true street-level retail tradition, so the *shopping centres* fill the gap. **Iguatemi** in Lago Norte is the most upmarket, with international luxury brands. **Park Shopping** in the south is the largest. **[Conjunto Nacional](/southamerica/brazil/brasilia/conjunto_nacional)** above the bus station is the most central and most useful for visitors.

For Brazilian crafts, the weekend **Feira da Torre** at the base of the TV Tower is excellent: handmade jewellery, woven hammocks, leather, and good cheap food. The **Feira dos Importados** in SIA (south-west) sells (often dubious) electronics and household goods at low prices. The Museu de Valores at the Central Bank also has a small numismatic shop with collectible coins and notes.
