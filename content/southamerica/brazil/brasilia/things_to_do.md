Brasília's main attraction is the city itself — Lúcio Costa's Pilot Plan and Oscar Niemeyer's procession of white concrete monuments along the **Eixo Monumental** are best seen across a single long day, ideally a Sunday when traffic eases and several of the federal buildings open for tours. Start at the [TV Tower](/europe/netherlands/groningen/brasilia_tv_tower) viewing platform for the bird's-eye orientation, then walk the Esplanada from the [Cathedral](/southamerica/brazil/brasilia/cathedral) east to the [National Congress](/southamerica/brazil/brasilia/national_congress) and the [Three Powers Plaza](/southamerica/brazil/brasilia/three_powers_plaza).
