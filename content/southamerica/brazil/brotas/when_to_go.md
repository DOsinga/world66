---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season from April to October is the best time for outdoor activities. Water levels on the Jacaré-Pepira are manageable, trails are dry, and the heat is less punishing — temperatures in the low to mid 20s Celsius make for comfortable days in the river or on a zip line.

December through February brings the summer rains. The heat climbs and afternoon downpours are frequent, but the rivers run fuller and more powerfully — experienced rafters sometimes prefer this season for the added intensity. Note that some operators scale back or close during heavy flooding.

Weekends year-round bring visitors from São Paulo, so activity slots and accommodation fill up fast. If you can, come midweek for a quieter experience and easier bookings.
