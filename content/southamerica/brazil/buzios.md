Armação dos Búzios sits on a rocky peninsula about 170 km east of Rio de Janeiro, and it has been Brazil's most fashionable beach resort since Brigitte Bardot put it on the map in 1964. A bronze statue of her still stands on the [Orla Bardot](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/orla_bardot) boardwalk, a reminder of the moment this quiet fishing village turned into something altogether more stylish.

The peninsula has more than 20 beaches, each with its own character. [João Fernandes](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/joao_fernandes_beach) is the social hub — calm, clear water, a line of colourful fishing boats, and vendors weaving through the crowd. [Ferradura](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/ferradura_beach) is sheltered and popular with families. [Geribá](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/geriba_beach) faces the open Atlantic and draws surfers. The quieter beaches on the northern side, like [Tartaruga](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/tartaruga_beach), are where you go to see sea turtles and snorkel without crowds.

The town centre, Vila de Búzios, has the cobblestone [Rua das Pedras](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/rua_das_pedras) running through it — a strip of open-air restaurants, boutiques, and bars that gets lively after dark. The food leans heavily on seafood: grilled fish, prawns, and moquecas made with produce landed that morning. [Satyricon](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/satyricon) is the most famous table on the street; [Chez Michou](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/chez_michou) is the most beloved. Caipirinhas flow freely at [Anexo Bar](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/anexo_bar) and [Pier 54](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/pier_54) on the waterfront.

Despite the gloss, Búzios is genuinely beautiful. The light in the late afternoon, the fishing boats coming in against a green-forested hillside, the warmth of the water — it earns its reputation. The crowds can be intense in January and February; April and May are quieter, cooler, and better for actually enjoying the place. A [schooner tour](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/schooner_tour) of the outer beaches gives you views you can't get any other way, and the [diving](/southamerica/brazil/buzios/diving) around the peninsula's rocky outcrops is among the best in Rio de Janeiro state.
