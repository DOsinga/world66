Chez Michou is an institution on Rua das Pedras — a crêperie that has been feeding Búzios visitors since the 1980s. It occupies a brightly coloured space that is hard to miss, and at almost any hour of the day or evening there are people eating crepes at the outdoor tables.

The menu covers sweet and savoury versions, from the simple (cheese and ham, Nutella and banana) to more elaborate local combinations. Prices are modest by Rua das Pedras standards and the portions are generous. The whole operation has a cheerful, casual energy that suits a beach town well.

It also functions as something of a social hub — the owner is a fixture, the place is always lively, and it stays open late enough to absorb the post-bar crowd.
