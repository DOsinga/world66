---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Búzios sits about 170 km east of Rio de Janeiro and is most often reached by road. The drive from Rio takes roughly two and a half hours outside peak traffic, longer on holiday weekends when the road from Cabo Frio fills up.

Regular bus services run from Rio's Novo Rio bus terminal to Búzios (operated by Auto Viação 1001). The journey takes around three hours and costs a modest amount compared to a car hire or taxi. From Rio Galeão International Airport, the most practical approach is to take a bus or taxi to Novo Rio terminal and connect from there.

There is no train or air service to Búzios itself. The nearest significant airport is Rio Galeão (GIG); Aeroporto Internacional de Cabo Frio (CFB), about 25 km away, has some seasonal charter and domestic flights.
