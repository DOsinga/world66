The Orla Bardot is Búzios's seafront promenade, running along the inner bay of the Armação peninsula. It's a pleasant walk at any time, but it comes into its own in the late afternoon when the light turns golden and the boats knock against their moorings.

The promenade's landmark is a bronze statue of Brigitte Bardot in her 1960s prime — windswept, confident, and positioned so she looks out over the water. It is one of the most photographed spots in Búzios. A few metres away stands a second statue, of Bob Zagury, the man who brought her here. The juxtaposition is diplomatically ambiguous: Zagury slightly smaller, slightly behind.

From the Orla you can watch the schooner boats return in the late afternoon. Food and drink stalls dot the waterfront; it's a good place for a cold beer and some people-watching as the day cools down.
