Two small beaches separated by a rocky point, accessible on foot from Praia dos Ossos via a short trail. Azeda and Azedinha are among the clearest-water beaches in Búzios: the cove is sheltered, the bottom is sandy, and the water is shallow enough to see your feet at considerable depth. Snorkelling is excellent around the rocks.

The beaches are small enough that they fill up quickly on busy weekends, but because they are only reachable on foot (no vehicle access), the crowd here is more intentional than at the more developed beaches. Bring water and snacks — there are no kiosks. The walk from Praia dos Ossos takes ten minutes on a well-marked path through light vegetation.
