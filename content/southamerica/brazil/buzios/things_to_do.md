---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Búzios has more than 20 beaches, each with a distinct character, and the peninsula's compact size means you can hop between them easily. Beyond the beaches, the cobblestone old town offers good food, craft shopping, and a bronze statue that has become one of Brazil's most photographed landmarks. Water sports — snorkelling, diving, sailing, and windsurfing — are the main activities, though simply walking the Orla Bardot at sunset holds its own.
