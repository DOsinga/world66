Caldas Novas is Brazil's big inland hot-water resort, a Goiás city that grew around thermal springs rather than beaches. It is busy, family-oriented, and often more practical than pretty, but the identity is real: warm mineral water is the reason people come.

Caldas Novas makes sense only if you accept it on its own terms. The most typical day is not a museum crawl; it is soaking, sliding, and watching Brazilians turn a hot-spring aquifer into a holiday industry. [Lagoa Termas Parque](/southamerica/brazil/caldas_novas/lagoa_termas_parque) is the clearest version of that experience.

For something less resort-like, go to [Parque Estadual da Serra de Caldas Novas](/southamerica/brazil/caldas_novas/parque_estadual_da_serra_de_caldas_novas). The park protects the cerrado ridge that helps recharge the region's warm-water system, and its trails and lookouts give the place a better frame than hotel pools alone.
