---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

June is the unmissable month — the entire city is given over to the Festa Junina and the atmosphere is extraordinary. Book accommodation well in advance if coming for the festival; the city fills up completely. Outside June, Campina Grande's altitude (around 550m) keeps temperatures relatively mild compared to the coast — typically 20–28°C. The rainy season runs from March to July; the rest of the year is dry, and the dry season (August to February) can be dusty and very hot.

Carnival (February or March) is celebrated here but on a smaller scale than in coastal cities. The city's student population keeps it lively year-round.
