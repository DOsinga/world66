Amantikir Park is the most polished attraction in Campos do Jordão: a large landscaped garden complex with themed sections, lawns, ponds, and viewpoints across the Mantiqueira. It is orderly, photogenic, and very popular with Brazilian visitors.

This is not wild nature, and it does not pretend to be. Go for a relaxed garden walk, the mountain backdrop, and a break from the bustle of Capivari.
