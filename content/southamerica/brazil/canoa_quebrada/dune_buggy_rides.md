Buggy trips are the standard way to cover the dunes and coast around Canoa Quebrada without turning the day into a long hike. Drivers know the sandy tracks, viewpoints, and beach stretches that are worth the time.

It is touristy, but the landscape suits it. Go for the scenery rather than the speed, and leave room in the day for a slower walk afterward.
