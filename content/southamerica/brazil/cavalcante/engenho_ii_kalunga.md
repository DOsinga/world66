Engenho II is the most accessible village of the **Kalunga quilombola territory**, one of the largest surviving quilombo communities in Brazil. It serves as the entry point for visits to the spectacular **Cachoeira Santa Bárbara** and the surrounding network of waterfalls and pools, and is itself worth visiting for the sense of an old, self-contained Afro-Brazilian rural culture.

The community runs a visitor centre, sells simple food and crafts, and organises guides for the waterfall trails. Cultural festivals — particularly the **Festa do Império** in August — bring drumming, food, and the local *sussa* dance. Stay overnight in a community-run pousada or campsite for a better feel of the place than a day-trip will give.
