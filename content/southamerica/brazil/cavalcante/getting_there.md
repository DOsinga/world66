---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Cavalcante is remote. The nearest international airport is **Brasília**, about 470 km south on a good federal highway (BR-020 and then GO-118). The drive takes six to seven hours and the final stretch — from Alto Paraíso to Cavalcante — is paved but slow, with potholes that demand attention.

There is no airport at Cavalcante itself. **Real Expresso** runs daily buses from Brasília to Alto Paraíso de Goiás, with onward connections two or three times a week to Cavalcante. Most travellers rent a car in Brasília or join a tour package.

Once in Cavalcante, a four-wheel-drive is essential for the dirt roads to Engenho II, Santa Bárbara, and most of the waterfalls.
