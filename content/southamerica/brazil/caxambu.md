Caxambu is one of the old hydromineral spa towns of southern Minas Gerais, in the same water-circuit tradition as [São Lourenço](/southamerica/brazil/sao_lourenco). Its rhythm is gentle: people come to walk under trees, taste mineral waters, ride the chairlift, and enjoy a mountain-town pace that belongs more to health resorts than to beach Brazil.

The centre of gravity is [Parque das Águas](/southamerica/brazil/caxambu/parque_das_aguas), a large green park with a dozen mineral springs, each with its own chemical character. From inside the park, the [Morro de Caxambu and Cable Car](/southamerica/brazil/caxambu/morro_de_caxambu_and_cable_car) lift you to a viewpoint over the town and the Mantiqueira hills.

Caxambu is not dramatic, but it has a clear identity. Add the [Church of Santa Isabel of Hungary](/southamerica/brazil/caxambu/church_of_santa_isabel_of_hungary), built from a story tied to Princess Isabel and the town's healing waters, and the place becomes a pleasant one-day stop for travellers already exploring the spa towns of Minas.
