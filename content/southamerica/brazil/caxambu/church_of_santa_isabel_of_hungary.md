The Church of Santa Isabel of Hungary is Caxambu's main historic church, a neo-Gothic landmark on a rise above the centre. Local tradition ties its construction to Princess Isabel, who ordered the church after treatment with the town's mineral waters.

That story matters because it links the building to Caxambu's spa identity rather than leaving it as just another pretty church.
