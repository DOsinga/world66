The Eberle Palace (Palacete Eberle) on Sinimbu Street is one of the most architecturally distinguished buildings in Caxias do Sul — a late 19th-century mansion built for the Eberle family, who were among the most successful of the Italian immigrant entrepreneurs in the Serra Gaúcha. The building reflects the ambitions of the immigrant community at the turn of the century: the desire to build something permanent and European-looking in their new home, and to signal their commercial success in brick and stone.

The building is listed as a historic landmark and has been used for various cultural and institutional purposes over the years. The elaborate facade, with its arched windows and decorative stonework, stands out in the mixed urban streetscape of the city centre. Worth seeing as part of a walk through the historic centre, particularly in combination with the nearby Dante Alighieri Square and the Municipal Museum.
