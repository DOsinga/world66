Discoporto is exactly what it sounds like: a UFO landing pad in Serra Azul State Park. It grew out of local stories about strange lights, Serra do Roncador mysteries, and Barra do Garças' habit of leaning into its extraterrestrial reputation.

The site is free and odd rather than grand. It is worth adding if you are already heading into the park, if only because few places make their folklore this literal.
