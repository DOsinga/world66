Parque das Águas Quentes is the easy recovery stop after a hot walk in Serra Azul. The municipal park has warm thermal pools, simple facilities, and a green cerrado setting about 7 km from the centre.

It is more local leisure park than spa retreat. Go for a relaxed soak, not luxury.
