The Anthropology Museum of the Federal University of Goias is one of Goiania's more thoughtful cultural stops. Its collections and exhibitions focus on Indigenous peoples, archaeology, and the human history of central Brazil, adding depth to a city often read mainly through modern planning and parks.

University museums can be quieter and more academic than tourist institutions, but that is part of the appeal here. Go when you want context rather than spectacle, and check current exhibition hours before crossing town.
