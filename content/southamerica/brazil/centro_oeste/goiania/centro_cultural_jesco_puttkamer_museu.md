Centro Cultural Jesco Puttkamer/Museu is connected with the Pontifical Catholic University of Goias and focuses on Indigenous Brazilian cultures. Exhibits cover daily life, history, legends, and cultural traditions, making it one of Goiania's more specific museum stops.

It is most useful for visitors who want cultural context beyond the city's parks and shopping districts. Allow time to check current opening hours, as university-linked museums can have limited schedules.
