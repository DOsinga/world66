Museu Pedro Ludovico Teixeira occupies the former home of Pedro Ludovico Teixeira, the founder of Goiania and a former governor of Goias. The rooms preserve furniture, personal objects, and memorabilia from his life, including his 1953 Chevrolet truck, giving a compact look at the political story behind the planned city.
