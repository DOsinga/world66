The Boa Morte Sacred Art Museum is housed in the old Church of the Good Death and holds religious sculpture and church objects from the region. Its strongest pieces are associated with Veiga Valle, the nineteenth-century sacred artist born in Goiás.

It is a compact stop, best paired with the surrounding streets and the nearby civic buildings.
