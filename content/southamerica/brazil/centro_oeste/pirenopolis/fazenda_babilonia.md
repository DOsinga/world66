Fazenda Babilônia is one of the best rural excursions from Pirenópolis if you want to understand the old sugar-and-cattle economy behind Goiás's colonial towns. The farm dates from the end of the 18th century and preserves thick stone walls, broad wooden structures, and the atmosphere of a working estate that once depended on enslaved labour.

Visits usually revolve around a guided look at the house and farm history, followed by a table of traditional food that draws on old rural recipes. It is not a quick urban sight; the appeal is the slower shift from cobbled Pirenópolis to cerrado farmland, where architecture, food, and uncomfortable history sit together in one place.
