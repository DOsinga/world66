---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**From Belo Horizonte:** Regular buses run from the rodoviária in Belo Horizonte to Congonhas, taking about 90 minutes on the BR-040. The trip works well as a day excursion combined with [Ouro Preto](/southamerica/brazil/ouropreto).

**From Ouro Preto:** Buses run several times a day, taking about 90 minutes via Conselheiro Lafaiete. The road winds through typical Minas Gerais mining country.

**By car:** Congonhas is on the BR-040 between Belo Horizonte and Rio de Janeiro, with a clearly signposted exit. Parking is straightforward at the sanctuary.
