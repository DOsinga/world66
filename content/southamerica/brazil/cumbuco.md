Cumbuco is a small fishing village 25 kilometres north of [Fortaleza](/southamerica/brazil/fortaleza), and one of the best kitesurfing destinations in [Brazil](/southamerica/brazil). The setting is dramatic: a long sandy beach with blue sea on one side and towering sand dunes on the other, with palm trees, a small town square, and a football pitch in between.

The wind blows reliably year-round, making Cumbuco a magnet for [kitesurfers](/southamerica/brazil/cumbuco/kitesurfing). The best months are August to December, when 10–14m kites handle the conditions well. Six kilometres downwind, the [Lagoa de Cauipe](/southamerica/brazil/cumbuco/lagoa_de_cauipe) is a flat freshwater lagoon reached by a 45-minute downwinder — also a good family destination for calm swimming. For wave riding, Paracuru is an hour north — best for intermediate and advanced kiters.

Beyond kitesurfing, [dune buggy tours](/southamerica/brazil/cumbuco/dune_buggy_tours) over the coastal dunes are the other classic activity. The dunes here are high enough to be genuinely exciting, and the drivers know how to use them. The village itself is tiny — about 1,500 residents — and nightlife is limited to cold beers on the town square. For anything more, head to Fortaleza, where the clubs and live music venues run every night of the week. The water temperature is 22–25°C year-round.
