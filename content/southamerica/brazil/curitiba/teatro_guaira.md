The Teatro Guaíra is Paraná's main state theatre and one of the most important performing arts venues in southern Brazil. The building, completed in 1974, is a bold piece of Brazilian modernist architecture — all angles and concrete, with a dramatic entrance hall and two main performance spaces.

The theatre hosts opera, ballet, orchestral performances, and theatre year-round. The main auditorium, the Guairão, seats close to 2,000 people. Even if you do not attend a performance, the building's public areas are worth visiting during the day — the architecture is distinctive, and a short walk from the historic centre.

**Location:** Rua XV de Novembro, Centro.
