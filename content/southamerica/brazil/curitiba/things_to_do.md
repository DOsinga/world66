---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Curitiba rewards slow exploration. The city's parks, cultural institutions, and historic centre are spread across distinct neighbourhoods — the circular bus system makes getting between them straightforward. The Oscar Niemeyer Museum and the Botanical Garden are the top two draws, but the historic quarter around Largo da Ordem has its own particular charm, and the parks reveal a city that takes public space seriously.
