Erechim is a planned city in the north of Rio Grande do Sul, far from Brazil's beach-and-carnival image. It was laid out in the early 20th century with broad avenues and a radial street plan, and grew with waves of European immigration, especially Italian, German, Polish, and Jewish families.

The city is a regional centre rather than a classic sightseeing stop. It has industry, universities, football, and a practical downtown, with enough green space and local history to make a short pause worthwhile if you are crossing the interior between Passo Fundo, Chapecó, and the Serra Gaúcha.

Start in the centre around [Praça da Bandeira](/southamerica/brazil/erechim/praca_da_bandeira), then look for the old municipal building known as [Castelinho](/southamerica/brazil/erechim/castelinho). [Longines Malinowski Park](/southamerica/brazil/erechim/longines_malinowski_park) is the best place to see the araucaria trees that once dominated much of the southern Brazilian highlands.
