Castelinho was Erechim's first municipal building, built between 1912 and 1915, when the planned town was still taking shape. Its small scale and old-fashioned civic style make it stand out among the modern shops and traffic of the centre.

Today the building is used for exhibitions and events tied to local tradition. Even when nothing is on, it is worth a look while walking around the central square.
