Longines Malinowski Park is Erechim's most useful patch of green, a 24-hectare park close to the centre. Its araucaria trees are the highlight, a reminder of the highland forests that once covered large areas of southern Brazil.

Come for a walk rather than a formal attraction. It is a good place to break up a day of driving or to get a quieter view of the city away from the radial avenues.
