Praça da Bandeira is Erechim's central square and the easiest place to understand the city's planned layout. Several main avenues radiate from around this part of town, giving the centre a more deliberate shape than many Brazilian interior cities.

It is a simple civic square, not a destination in itself, but it anchors a short walk through downtown and pairs naturally with nearby Castelinho.
