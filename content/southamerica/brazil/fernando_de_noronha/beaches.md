---
title: Beaches
type: section
---

Fernando de Noronha has about 16 beaches, and most of them are genuinely outstanding. The main distinction is between the northern coast (Mar de Fora — outer sea) and the southern coast (Mar de Dentro — inner sea). The northern beaches face the open Atlantic with strong surf; the southern beaches are sheltered and calm. 

**Praia da Cacimba do Padre** is a long, curved beach below the Dois Irmãos peaks — the best surf break on the island, with consistent waves especially between December and March. **Praia do Leão** is the island's largest beach and an important sea turtle nesting ground, closed to visitors at night during nesting season. **Praia do Atalaia** on the southern coast is accessible only at low tide and requires a timed permit; the tidal pool here is one of the best natural aquariums you'll find anywhere. **Praia do Boldró** is sheltered, easy to reach, and known for underwater tunnels and sea turtles that approach with alarming nonchalance.
