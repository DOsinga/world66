---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

Fernando de Noronha is tiny and the main road is a single loop around the island. The most enjoyable way to get around is by rented buggy — four-wheel beach buggies are available from several operators near the main village. Bicycles work well for shorter distances in the flat central area. Taxis (mainly more buggies) are available for longer journeys.

Cars are restricted in volume to protect the environment, which means renting early in your stay is advisable. Much of the southern coast is accessible only on foot — there are no roads to the best beaches, which is part of what keeps them clean.

Everything on the main island is within comfortable walking distance in the early morning or late afternoon when the heat eases. The distances sound manageable on a map, but the tracks can be rough and the sun intense; start early.
