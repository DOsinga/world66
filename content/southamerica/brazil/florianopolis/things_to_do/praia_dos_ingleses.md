---
title: "Praia dos Ingleses"
type: poi
latitude: -27.437
longitude: -48.380
---

The largest beach in the north of the island, with a sheltered bay that keeps the water calm and ideal for swimming year-round. Praia dos Ingleses has long been popular with Argentine visitors — many return every summer — and with Brazilian families looking for a gentler alternative to the surf beaches. The waterfront is lined with restaurants and kiosks serving grilled fish, cold beer, and fresh coconut water. It's busiest in January and February but holds its appeal in the quieter shoulder months too.
