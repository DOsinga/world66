Formosa is a Goiás city northeast of Brasília, useful not because the centre is charming, but because the surrounding cerrado is unusually rich. Within a day trip are tall waterfalls, clear pools, sinkholes, and prehistoric rock markings that make the plateau feel older and wilder than the highway approach suggests.

The headline sight is [Itiquira Falls](/southamerica/brazil/formosa/itiquira_falls), a 168-metre drop in a protected municipal park. The Bezerra district adds the [Bisnau Archaeological Site and Waterfall](/southamerica/brazil/formosa/bisnau_archaeological_site_and_waterfall), where rock carvings sit close to swimming water, and [Buraco das Araras](/southamerica/brazil/formosa/buraco_das_araras), a huge quartzite sinkhole with forest inside it.

Formosa is not a polished resort town, and you need a car or arranged transport for the best places. But if you are in Brasília and want a day with real landscape rather than another cafe or mall, it earns the trip.
