Fortaleza is the capital of Ceará state and one of Brazil's largest cities, with about 2.5 million people, on the Atlantic coast in the northeast. It is primarily a beach city: the urban beaches of [Praia de Iracema](/southamerica/brazil/fortaleza/praia_de_iracema) and [Meireles](/southamerica/brazil/fortaleza/meireles) line the Avenida Beira Mar, which comes alive every evening with food stalls, craft markets, and street performers. The ocean breeze keeps temperatures bearable year-round, and the trade winds make the northeast coast ideal for kitesurfing — especially at [Cumbuco](/southamerica/brazil/fortaleza/cumbuco), 40 km west.

The city has a well-earned reputation for nightlife — the New York Times once called Monday night in Fortaleza the liveliest in the world — and there is indeed something happening every night of the week. The [Mercado Central](/southamerica/brazil/fortaleza/mercado_central), a four-storey market near the centre, is the best place to buy Ceará's distinctive crafts: lacework, leather goods, and hand-printed hammocks. The [Dragão do Mar Centre of Art and Culture](/southamerica/brazil/fortaleza/dragao_do_mar) in Iracema anchors the cultural life of the city. The beaches east of the city along the Costa do Sol — particularly [Canoa Quebrada](/southamerica/brazil/fortaleza/canoa_quebrada) with its rust-red cliffs — are among the most striking in Brazil.
