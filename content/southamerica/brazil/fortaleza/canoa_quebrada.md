Canoa Quebrada, about 160 km east of Fortaleza along the CE-040 coastal highway, is the most famous beach destination in Ceará: a village perched on rust-red sandstone cliffs above a broad arc of white sand, with dunes rolling away behind and the sea a deep blue below. The main street (Rua Broadway) is lined with bars, pousadas, and craft shops, and gets lively in the evenings with live forró and capoeira.

The beach below the cliffs is reached by a steep sandy path. Buggy tours are the most popular way to explore the dunes and lagoons further east along the coast. The village has been discovered — it is busy at peak season — but the landscape is genuinely extraordinary and the cliffs at sunset are reliably spectacular. Best reached by the direct bus from Fortaleza's rodoviária (about 3.5 hours) or by hired car.
