Mercado dos Pinhões is a restored iron market hall in Fortaleza, valued as much for its atmosphere as for its architecture. The structure gives the surrounding streets a historic anchor, while food stalls and cultural events keep it from feeling like a static monument.

It is best visited when something is happening, especially in the evening or during food-focused events. Even on a quieter day, it makes a useful stop for seeing a smaller-scale local market space beyond the larger Mercado Central.
