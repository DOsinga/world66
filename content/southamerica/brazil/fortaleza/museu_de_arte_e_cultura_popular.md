The Museu de Arte e Cultura Popular occupies part of Fortaleza's old prison complex, now used as the Centro de Turismo. Its exhibits cover folk art, boats, craft traditions, and everyday cultural objects from Ceará and the wider northeast.

The museum is small, but it gives useful context before browsing the handicraft stalls in the same complex. It is easy to combine with the Central Market, the cathedral, and the historic centre.
