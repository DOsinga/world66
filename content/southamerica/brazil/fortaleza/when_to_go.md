---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Fortaleza has a tropical semi-arid climate with year-round heat and sunshine. The trade winds from the northeast keep the city from becoming unbearable even in the hottest months. The **rainy season** runs February to June, with January and February the wettest months; the **dry season** runs July to January. For beach tourism, July to January is ideal — lower humidity, stronger winds (ideal for kitesurfing at Cumbuco), and reliably clear skies.

Avoid Carnival (February or March) unless you are there for the party specifically — prices double and accommodation books out months in advance. The July high season brings Brazilian tourists from the south; prices are higher but the beaches are at their most animated.
