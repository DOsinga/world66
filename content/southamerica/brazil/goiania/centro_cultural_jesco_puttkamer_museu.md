Centro Cultural Jesco Puttkamer is a small museum linked to PUC Goiás, with exhibitions on Indigenous peoples in Brazil and the cultures of the central plateau. It is a quieter, more academic stop than Goiânia's civic museums, but useful if you want the city to connect to the wider history of Goiás.

The displays focus on daily life, material culture, stories, and identity rather than spectacle. Pair it with the city's other museums if you are trying to understand Goiânia beyond its parks and Art Deco centre.
