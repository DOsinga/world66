The Instituto Histórico e Geográfico de Goiás keeps a collection of documents and historical material from Goiás, including records from the Portuguese colonial period. One highlight is a group of original letters written by Princess Isabel to Dom Pedro II.

It is best for travellers who like archives, regional history, and the paper trail behind Brazil's inland cities.
