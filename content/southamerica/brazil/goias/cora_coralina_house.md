The Cora Coralina House preserves the home of Ana Lins dos Guimarães Peixoto Bretas, the Goiás poet better known as Cora Coralina. She published her first book late in life and is now one of the town's best loved figures.

The house is as important as the exhibits: an old riverside building with domestic rooms, personal objects, and a sense of the town that shaped her writing. It is one of the more human stops in the historic centre.
