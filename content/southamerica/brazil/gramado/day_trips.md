---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The neighbouring town of Canela, ten minutes' drive away, is effectively part of the Gramado experience and is where the Cascata do Caracol, Parque da Ferradura, and most of the canyon viewpoints sit. Beyond Canela, the wider Serra Gaúcha is full of vineyards (Vale dos Vinhedos around Bento Gonçalves), cheese producers, and dramatic canyons.

The Aparados da Serra National Park, around three hours east, contains the spectacular Itaimbezinho canyon — a 700-metre-deep gorge that is one of the most impressive in Brazil. It is a long day from Gramado but doable; many visitors stay overnight in the village of Cambará do Sul instead.
