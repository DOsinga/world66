A horseshoe-shaped canyon carved into the basalt rim of the Serra Gaúcha, 12 kilometres from Canela. The viewpoint stands more than 400 metres above the river Caí, and the panorama down into the gorge is one of the best in the region. A trail descends through Atlantic forest to a small waterfall and pool inside the canyon.

Best in the dry months; the trails get muddy after rain. The park has zip lines and other adventure activities for those who want them. A car or taxi is needed to get there from Gramado — around 30 minutes.
