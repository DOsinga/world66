Guaratuba is a small coastal town in Paraná state, about 90 minutes from [Curitiba](/southamerica/brazil/curitiba) by car. It sits on the edge of a wide bay backed by well-preserved Atlantic Forest, making it a popular weekend escape for Curitibanos who want beach, boat rides, and quiet.

The town has 22 km of beaches along the open ocean, but the bay is the real draw — calm water, mangroves, and good fishing. [Boat trips](/southamerica/brazil/guratuba/bay_boat_trips) across the bay and up the estuary are easy to arrange. The surrounding hills are part of the [Saint-Hilaire/Lange National Park](/southamerica/brazil/guratuba/saint_hilaire_lange_park), with trails through dense subtropical forest.

In town, the walk up to the [Cristo statue](/southamerica/brazil/guratuba/cristo_statue) (200 steps) rewards you with a panoramic view over the bay and coastline. The [central beach](/southamerica/brazil/guratuba/central_beach) stretches 2.5 km along the waterfront. At the far end, the [Nossa Senhora de Lourdes grotto](/southamerica/brazil/guratuba/nossa_senhora_lourdes_grotto) is a local pilgrimage spot with a freshwater spring. The [main church](/southamerica/brazil/guratuba/matriz_church) dates from the 19th century and faces a pleasant town square.

Guaratuba is also known for its [oyster farms](/southamerica/brazil/guratuba/cabaraquara_oysters) in the Cabaraquara area, on the bay's southern shore inside the protected zone. Small restaurants there serve fresh oysters and seafood straight from the water.
