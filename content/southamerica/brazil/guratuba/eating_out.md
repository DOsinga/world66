---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The food here is shaped by the bay — oysters from Cabaraquara, blue crab in the mangrove season, fresh fish from the small boats that work the bar — and by the steady inflow of Curitibanos who expect their summer beach town to feed them well. The best restaurants are not in the town centre but out at Cabaraquara on the south side of the bay.
