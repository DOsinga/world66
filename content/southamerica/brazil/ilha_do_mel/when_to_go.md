---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Brazilian summer — **December to February** — is the busiest time on Ilha do Mel. Warm days, regular thunderstorms, and a small holiday crowd from Curitiba and São Paulo. The peak is between Christmas and Carnival; prices roughly double and pousadas can fill up.

**March to May** is the best time to visit: temperatures are still warm, the rain has eased, and the beaches and trails are largely empty. **September to November** is similar but cooler.

**Winter (June–August)** is mild — Paraná state is not tropical — and the sea is too cool for swimming. Many pousadas and restaurants close. Worth it if you want solitude.
