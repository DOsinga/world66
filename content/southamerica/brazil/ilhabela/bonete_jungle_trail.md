The Bonete Jungle Trail is one of Ilhabela's classic adventure routes, crossing Atlantic forest toward the remote beach village of Bonete. The hike is long, humid, and beautiful, with forest shade, river crossings, waterfalls, and the slow satisfaction of reaching the island's wilder south coast on foot.

This is not a casual beach stroll. Start early, carry water, expect mud, and check local advice on trail conditions before setting out. Many hikers arrange a boat return from Bonete when seas allow, which turns the day into a proper island journey rather than an out-and-back slog.
