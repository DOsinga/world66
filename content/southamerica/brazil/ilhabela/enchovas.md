Enchovas is one of Ilhabela's wilder beach spots, with yellow sand, rocks, and a river meeting the sea. It sits on the quieter side of the island, so getting there is part of the appeal.

Come for the natural setting rather than facilities. It is best suited to travellers already exploring Ilhabela's trails and remote beaches.
