Pedra do Sino is one of Ilhabela's better-known beaches, named for rock formations said to make a bell-like sound when struck together by the sea. The setting is classic north-coast Ilhabela: pale sand, coconut palms, and calm, shallow water.

It is a good choice when you want an easier swimming beach rather than a wild trail-access cove. The unusual rocks give it just enough character to stand out from the island's many beaches.
