A two-and-a-half-hour walk south from Abraão across the spine of the island, the small fishing village of Dois Rios sits on the wild Atlantic coast where two rivers meet the sea. The beach here is wide, open, and almost always empty — the surf is heavier than at Lopes Mendes, and there are no facilities to speak of.

The village existed mainly to service the old prison; the families who lived here worked as guards, cooks, and laundry hands. Since the prison closed, the population has dwindled to a few dozen. Walk the beach, swim if the surf is manageable, eat at one of the two simple restaurants, and head back over the ridge in the afternoon.
