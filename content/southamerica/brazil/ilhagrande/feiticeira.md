A small waterfall about 90 minutes' walk from Abraão along the western trail. The water falls 15 metres into a clear, cold pool that is deep enough to swim in and just wide enough to feel like a private hideout. The forest around the pool is dense and shady, with the constant noise of insects.

The walk in is not difficult, though it climbs steadily. Continue past the falls and the trail leads to the small beach of Praia da Feiticeira; a longer day can combine the waterfall, the beach, and a swim before walking back to Abraão. Bring a swimsuit and water shoes — the rocks around the pool are slippery.
