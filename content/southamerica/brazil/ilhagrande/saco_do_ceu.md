The "Bay of Heaven" is a long, deep inlet on the north coast of the island that is so calm and protected that boats moor here for the night and the water lies perfectly flat well into the morning. Steep forested hills come down to the shore on all sides; there is no road in and only a few houses around the edge.

It is one of the standard stops on the round-the-island boat tour from Abraão, and a fine place for a swim or a snorkel. The water is darker than at Lagoa Azul — the bay is deeper — but the shelter makes it ideal for paddleboards and kayaks, which some boats carry. A handful of floating restaurants serve grilled fish and caipirinhas to passing boats.
