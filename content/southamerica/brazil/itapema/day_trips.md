Itapema's position on the central Santa Catarina coast makes it a good base. **Balneário Camboriú** is the high-rise resort just to the north — the closest thing southern Brazil has to a Copacabana skyline. **Florianópolis** is a longer hop south, where the island's beaches, the central lagoon, and the historic centre all reward a full day. Up the coast, **Porto Belo** runs boat trips out to **Ilha de Porto Belo** and the Reserva Biológica Marinha do Arvoredo offshore — a protected dive site with the clearest water on this stretch of coast.
