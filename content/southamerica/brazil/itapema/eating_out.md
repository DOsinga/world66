---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Itapema is a beach town and most of its eating happens at the kiosks along Meia Praia: fried fish, isca de peixe, fresh oysters from Florianópolis, and the inevitable rodízio de espetinhos. The proper restaurants line **Avenida Nereu Ramos** and the cross streets behind it, with the densest concentration roughly between the Hotel Plaza Itapema and the Morro da Sentinela.

The Azorean heritage shows up in the seafood: **anchova grelhada** (grilled bluefish), **tainha assada na telha** (mullet roasted on a clay tile, in season), and a hearty **caldeirada** of mixed shellfish and fish. The fish markets at Canto da Praia sell the morning catch direct, and several family-run restaurants nearby will cook anything you buy.
