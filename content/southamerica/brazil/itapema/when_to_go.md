---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Itapema's summer is **December through February** — hot, sticky, and packed solid with Brazilians on holiday from Santa Catarina, Paraná, Rio Grande do Sul, and increasingly Argentina. Prices double, beaches fill, and traffic on the BR-101 backs up for kilometres on weekend afternoons. The town is at its most fun then if you do not mind crowds.

**March, April, and November** are the local sweet spot: the water is still warm, the beaches are quiet, and rates drop sharply. **May to September** is winter — cool, frequently rainy, and the sea is too cold for most swimmers, but this is when the tainha (mullet) run brings the fishing colony alive in late May and June. The **Festa da Tainha**, held annually in early June, is the calendar's biggest local event outside the summer season.
