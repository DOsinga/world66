João Pessoa sits at the easternmost point of the Americas — [Cabo Branco](/southamerica/brazil/joaopessoa/cabo_branco_lighthouse), at longitude W34°44'55", is where the continent's land runs out. That geography alone makes it worth a stop on any northeast Brazil itinerary, but there's more to it than a bragging right. It's often cited as Brazil's second-greenest city, and the pace is noticeably slower than Recife or Natal.

The old centre rewards an afternoon on foot. The [Igreja São Francisco](/southamerica/brazil/joaopessoa/igreja_sao_francisco) is one of the finest baroque churches in the country — look up at the ceiling and you'll find a trompe-l'œil panel in perspective said to be one of the largest of its kind in the world. The [Lagoa park](/southamerica/brazil/joaopessoa/lagoa_park) is a good place to recover afterwards, shaded and calm, and it's also where buses leave for [Tambaú](/southamerica/brazil/joaopessoa/praia_de_tambau), the main beach strip just east of the centre.

Beyond Tambaú, the coast opens up. [Praia Cabo Branco](/southamerica/brazil/joaopessoa/praia_de_cabo_branco) is cleaner and less built-up, while [Tambaba](/southamerica/brazil/joaopessoa/praia_de_tambaba) to the south is one of Brazil's few official nudist beaches. João Pessoa sits between [Recife](/southamerica/brazil/recife) and [Natal](/southamerica/brazil/natal) on the coastal route, and [Pipa](/southamerica/brazil/pipa) is an easy day trip north.
