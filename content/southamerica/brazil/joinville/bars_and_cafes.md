---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

Joinville nightlife is centred on the streets around the Praça Nereu Ramos and along Rua Visconde de Taunay. A wave of craft breweries, mostly German-Brazilian, has opened in the last decade and given the city a more interesting drinking scene than it once had. Coffee culture is solid — Santa Catarina sits next to one of the best coffee-growing regions in Brazil.
