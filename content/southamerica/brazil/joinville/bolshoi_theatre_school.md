The Russian ballet masters live in Joinville and teach in a mixture of Russian and Portuguese."

The Escola do Teatro Bolshoi no Brasil is the only branch of the Moscow Bolshoi Theatre outside Russia, and one of the most selective ballet schools in Latin America. The school occupies a striking modernist building in the centre of the city and runs free public performances throughout the year — these are well worth catching, since the level is genuinely high.

The school also organises guided tours during the daytime, which include a look at the practice studios and the costume workshops. Check the website for the performance and tour schedule before you visit.
