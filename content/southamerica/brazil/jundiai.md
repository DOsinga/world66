Jundiaí is more than a middle city between São Paulo and Campinas. Its older identity is agricultural: fruit farms, Italian immigrant families, vineyards and rural restaurants on the edge of the city. Its wilder identity is the [Serra do Japi](/southamerica/brazil/jundiai/serra_do_japi), a protected Atlantic Forest mountain range that gives Jundiaí a green backdrop unusual for an industrial city.

The urban centre has enough to fill a slow afternoon. [Nossa Senhora do Desterro Cathedral](/southamerica/brazil/jundiai/nossa_senhora_do_desterro_cathedral) anchors the old town, while [Theatro Polytheama](/southamerica/brazil/jundiai/theatro_polytheama) keeps a bit of early 20th-century civic pride alive. The railway station and rail heritage matter too: Jundiaí grew with the 19th-century lines that carried coffee, workers and immigrants through São Paulo state.

The best reason to stay a full day is the contrast. You can start with the [Botanical Garden](/southamerica/brazil/jundiai/botanical_garden), head into the centre, then spend the afternoon on the rural wine and fruit roads or near the Serra do Japi. It is not a showpiece city, but it has a clear local flavour.
