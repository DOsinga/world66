Lauro Müller's main draw is the Serra do Rio do Rastro road, which is worth the detour from anywhere in southern Santa Catarina. The Castelo de Laje in town is an unusual piece of industrial-era architecture. For geology and mining history, the Geological Museum offers context on why this corner of Brazil was once economically significant.
