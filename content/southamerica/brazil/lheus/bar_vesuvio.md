Bar Vesúvio is the most famous landmark in the Bloco Jorge Amado, restored to something close to its early-20th-century appearance and preserved as part of the Jorge Amado Heritage Circuit. The bar appears prominently in *Gabriela, Cravo e Canela* (1958), Amado's most commercially successful novel, which was set in Ilhéus during the 1920s cacao boom.

You can eat and drink here — the food is straightforward Bahian, heavy on moqueca and grilled fish. The historic setting is the main draw, but it is a functioning bar and restaurant, not a pure museum piece. Sit outside on the terrace and watch the square.
