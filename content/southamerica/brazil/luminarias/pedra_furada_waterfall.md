Pedra Furada Waterfall is one of the named natural sights closest to Luminarias. The draw is the mix of falling water, rock, and the pierced-stone formation that gives the place its name.

It is a good first choice if you want a clear, specific target without committing to a full day far from town. As with most of the area's waterfall stops, check local conditions before swimming or scrambling around wet rock.
