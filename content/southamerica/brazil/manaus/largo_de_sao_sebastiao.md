The Largo de São Sebastião is the small but elegant square in front of the Teatro Amazonas. The pretty white-and-yellow Igreja de São Sebastião (1888) sits on one side, while the centre is occupied by the Monumento à Abertura dos Portos (1900) — four bronze figures representing the Amazon and the continents of trade. Cafes and souvenir stalls line the edges.

This is where Manaus comes after dark on warm evenings: street performers, ice-cream vendors, families and the occasional outdoor concert. The black-and-white wave paving is one of the most photographed surfaces in the city.
