Seminário Maior São José is a neo-colonial seminary building in Mariana. The architecture is the main reason to notice it, and the chapel has mosaics that make it more than a simple institutional stop.

Visits need to be arranged in advance, so it is not a casual drop-in sight. It is most useful for travellers with a specific interest in Mariana's religious architecture and historic institutions.
