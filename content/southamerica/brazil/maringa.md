Maringá is a planned city in northern Paraná, laid out with broad avenues and large pockets of green space rather than the denser colonial pattern found in older Brazilian towns. It is not a place to cross the country for, but if you are already moving through the north of the state it has enough character for a good day.

The city landmark is the [Cathedral of Maringá](/southamerica/brazil/maringa/cathedral_of_maringa), a tall concrete cone inspired by the Sputnik era and visible from much of the centre. Nearby, [Parque do Ingá](/southamerica/brazil/maringa/parque_do_inga) preserves a piece of urban forest around a lake, while [Parque do Japão](/southamerica/brazil/maringa/parque_do_japao) points to the strong Japanese-Brazilian community that helped shape the city.

Maringá works best as a relaxed urban stop: the cathedral in the morning, a walk under the trees in Ingá Park, lunch in the centre, then the Japanese gardens or the high point around [Praça Pio XII](/southamerica/brazil/maringa/praca_pio_xii) before sunset. The appeal is modest but real, especially compared with using the city only as a bus or airport connection.
