---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Maringá's best sights are spread through its planned urban landscape rather than packed into an old centre. Start with the [Cathedral of Maringá](/southamerica/brazil/maringa/cathedral_of_maringa), the city's unmistakable concrete spire, then walk to [Parque do Ingá](/southamerica/brazil/maringa/parque_do_inga) for shade and lake views. [Parque do Japão](/southamerica/brazil/maringa/parque_do_japao) is the most explicit sign of the city's Japanese-Brazilian identity, and [Praça Pio XII](/southamerica/brazil/maringa/praca_pio_xii) gives a small but useful sense of the city's topography.
