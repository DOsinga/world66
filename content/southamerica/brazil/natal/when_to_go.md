---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Natal has one of the sunniest climates in Brazil. The dry season runs from roughly September through January, when the northeast trade winds blow reliably and the sky stays clear. This is the best time for beach activities and buggy tours. The rainy season peaks between April and July, but even then the rain is usually concentrated in brief afternoon downpours rather than all-day grey skies.

The city is busiest in July (school holidays) and around Carnival in February or March. If you want warm weather, good surf, and smaller crowds, September and October are ideal.
