The Nordeste is where Brazil began, and where most Brazilians go on holiday. Nine states curve around the country's north-eastern shoulder, from Maranhão to Bahia, with a coast of reefs, palms, dunes and fishing villages, a sun that barely takes a day off, and the deepest roots of the country's African and Portuguese culture.

[Salvador](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/salvador), the first capital, is the heart of Afro-Brazilian Brazil: the Pelourinho's painted colonial streets, capoeira, candomblé and the best food in the country. South of it runs the Bahian coast — [Morro de São Paulo](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/morro_de_sao_paulo) and [Boipeba](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/boipeba), [Itacaré](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/itacare), and the Discovery Coast around [Porto Seguro](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/portoseguro), [Trancoso](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/trancoso) and [Caraíva](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/caraiva). Inland, the [Chapada Diamantina](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/chapadadiamantina), reached from [Lençóis](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/lencois), has some of the best hiking in Brazil.

Further north, [Recife](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/recife) and its baroque twin [Olinda](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/olinda) hold one of Brazil's great carnivals, and the reef pools of [Porto de Galinhas](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/porto_de_galinhas), [Maragogi](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/maragogi) and [São Miguel dos Milagres](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/sao_miguel_dos_milagres) line the coast either side. Offshore, [Fernando de Noronha](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/fernando_de_noronha) is the finest marine park in the South Atlantic.

The far north is dune country: [Jericoacoara](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/jericoacoara) and the kitesurf beaches of Ceará, the [Parnaíba delta](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/delta_do_parnaiba), and the [Lençóis Maranhenses](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/lencois_maranhenses), whose rain-filled lagoons are one of the great sights of South America. In the dry interior, the [Serra da Capivara](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/serra_da_capivara) holds the richest rock art in the Americas.

The coast is warm all year. Along much of it the heaviest rain falls from April to July; in Maranhão and Ceará the rains come earlier, from January to May, and fill the dune lagoons.
