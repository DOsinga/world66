The dunes behind Canoa are the easy sunset viewpoint. Go when the beach starts to fall into shade and the town quiets down a little from the heat of the day.

It is a simple place, not a formal attraction, but it gives Canoa its best angle: red cliff, pale dune, and a long sweep of Atlantic beach.
