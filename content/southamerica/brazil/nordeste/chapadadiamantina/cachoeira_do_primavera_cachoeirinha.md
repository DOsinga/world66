Cachoeira do Primavera and Cachoeirinha are small waterfalls reached on foot from Lencois, usually as a short half-day outing into the rocky streams above town. The route climbs past the water-treatment area and follows the river upstream, with natural pools and falls along the way.

They are not the biggest waterfalls in Chapada Diamantina, but they make a good close-to-town walk when there is not enough time for one of the longer park trips.
