Gruta da Fumaça is a cave in the Chapada Diamantina region known for its crystalline formations. It is smaller and quieter than the area's better-known cave stops, which is part of the appeal.

The cave works best as part of a wider day exploring the northern side of the park rather than as a standalone trip.
