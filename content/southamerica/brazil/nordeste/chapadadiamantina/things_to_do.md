Chapada Diamantina rewards active visitors. The easiest and most rewarding day trips from Lençóis include [Ribeirão do Meio](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/chapadadiamantina/ribeirao_do_meio) for natural waterslides and swimming, [Morro do Pai Inácio](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/chapadadiamantina/morro_pai_inacio) for the classic panoramic view at sunset, and the [Gruta do Lapão](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/chapadadiamantina/gruta_do_lapao) cave system. Longer day trips reach [Poço Azul](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/chapadadiamantina/poco_azul) and [Poço Encantado](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/chapadadiamantina/poco_encantado), the extraordinary blue-lit caves in the eastern Chapada. The two-to-three day trek to [Cachoeira da Fumaça](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/chapadadiamantina/cachoeira_fumaca) — approaching from the top of the plateau for the full 340-metre plunge view, or from [Vale do Capão](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/chapadadiamantina/vale_do_capao) below — is the defining experience of the park.
