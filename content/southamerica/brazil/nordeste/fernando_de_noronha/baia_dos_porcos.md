Baía dos Porcos, the Bay of Pigs, is one of the small places that does a lot of work in Fernando de Noronha's mythology. The cove is tucked near Cacimba do Padre and looks out toward the twin humps of Morro Dois Irmãos, with dark volcanic rock, clear water, and natural pools doing the rest.

Access depends on tides and conditions, and the final approach over rock can be slippery, so this is not the beach for an idle flip-flop stroll. Come prepared to move carefully, and resist treating the pools like a private aquarium; this fragile corner is most rewarding when visited slowly and lightly.
