Capim-Acu is one of Fernando de Noronha's more demanding walking routes, crossing rough ground and native vegetation toward wilder coastal scenery. It is a good choice if you want the island beyond the easy beaches and viewpoints.

Because the route lies within the national park system, rules, access, and guide requirements can change. Check locally before setting out, carry water, and take the trail seriously; the reward is a less polished, more physical encounter with Noronha's volcanic landscape.
