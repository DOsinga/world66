---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Fernando de Noronha is reached by air from Recife or Natal on the Brazilian mainland. Both LATAM and Gol operate regular flights; the journey takes about an hour. The small airport — Aeroporto de Fernando de Noronha (FEN) — is on the main island and handles regular aircraft without incident.

All visitors must register online before arrival and pay the environmental preservation tax (TPA), which increases with each day of stay up to a maximum of around 15 days (the maximum permitted stay). Visitors arriving without having paid face delays at the airport.

There is no ferry service to Fernando de Noronha from the mainland.
