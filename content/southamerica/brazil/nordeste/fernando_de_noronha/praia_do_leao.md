Praia do Leão is one of Fernando de Noronha's wilder beaches, a broad sweep of sand facing open water on the island's south-east side. The name comes from the offshore rock formation, but the stronger impression is of space: cliffs, surf, turtle nesting areas, and the feeling that the national park is in charge here.

Rules can change with turtle protection, shark activity, and sea conditions, so treat posted guidance seriously. Even when swimming is restricted, the beach is worth the trip for the viewpoint and the raw Noronha scenery.
