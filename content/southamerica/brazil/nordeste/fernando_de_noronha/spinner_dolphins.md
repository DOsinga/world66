  Fernando de Noronha hosts one of the largest resident populations of spinner dolphins in the world — several hundred individuals that use the Bay of Dolphins as a resting area every morning. Every morning, a large pod of spinner dolphins enters the Bay of Dolphins to rest after a night of feeding in the open ocean. They arrive reliably between 6am and 7am, and the bay holds them through the mid-morning hours before they disperse back to sea in the afternoon. The Brazilian government prohibits boats from entering the bay and swimming within it during these hours, protecting the dolphins' rest period.

The best way to watch is from the Mirante dos Golfinhos (Dolphin Viewpoint) above the bay, where the pod is visible from the clifftop. You can count hundreds of animals in the water below, their dorsal fins cutting through the calm surface in rhythmic rows. It is one of the finest dolphin viewing experiences anywhere in the world.
