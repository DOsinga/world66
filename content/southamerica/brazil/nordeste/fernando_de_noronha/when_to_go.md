---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The best time to visit Fernando de Noronha depends on what you want. The dry season (August to February) offers the calmest sea conditions for diving and snorkelling, with visibility at its peak between September and March. These months also coincide with the turtle nesting season (December to May), when visitors can observe nesting females and hatchlings.

The rainy season (March to July) brings larger swells to the northern beaches — excellent for surfing, particularly around Cacimba do Padre — and slightly lower visibility in the water. However, the island is visibly greener and lusher, the crowds are smaller, and accommodation prices drop.

High season in Brazil (December–January and July school holidays) sees the island at its most expensive and most heavily booked. The strict visitor limits mean accommodation sells out months in advance during these periods.
