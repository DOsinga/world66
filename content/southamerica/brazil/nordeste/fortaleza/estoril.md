Estoril is one of Praia de Iracema's more characterful old buildings. Built in 1925 as Vila Morena, it later passed through spells as a casino, restaurant, and political bar before being restored for public cultural use.

Its appeal is as much local history as architecture. Stop by while walking the beachfront and the Dragao do Mar area to see a rare survivor from Fortaleza's early 20th-century seaside life.
