Museu do Ceará presents the history of Ceará and Fortaleza in a late 19th-century former seat of state government. The collection gives useful context for the city beyond the beach districts, with exhibits on politics, culture, and daily life in the state. It is a good central stop when exploring Fortaleza's older civic core.
