---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

The [Duna do Pôr do Sol](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/jericoacoara/duna_por_do_sol) is the daily ritual — climb the dune and watch the sunset with the whole village. [Pedra Furada](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/jericoacoara/pedra_furada) is the landmark rock arch on the beach to the east. [Lagoa do Paraíso](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/jericoacoara/lagoa_do_paraiso) is the stunning freshwater lagoon accessible by buggy, with floating restaurants in the water. [Lagoa Azul](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/jericoacoara/lagoa_azul) is a smaller, quieter lagoon walkable from the village. The [main beach](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/jericoacoara/main_beach) is the centre of village life. [Kitesurfing](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/jericoacoara/kitesurfing) dominates from August to January when the trade winds blow. The whole area sits within the [Jericoacoara National Park](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/jericoacoara/jericoacoara_national_park).
