The natural pools are Porto de Galinhas' signature sight. At low tide, gaps in the reef trap clear seawater and small fish, and local *jangada* rafts run short trips from the beach. It is simple rather than wild snorkelling, but the setting is lovely and the timing gives the outing a small sense of occasion.

Go only around low tide. Outside that narrow window the reefs disappear under the water and the trip loses most of its point.
