Toquinho Beach is a quiet southern beach in Ipojuca, near the mouth of the Sirinhaem River. It is more remote than Porto de Galinhas, reached by dirt roads and bordered by summer houses rather than resort crowds.

The ocean side has stronger waves, while the river mouth offers calmer water and a more sheltered landscape. It suits visitors looking for a less built-up beach stop south of the better-known Porto de Galinhas beaches.
