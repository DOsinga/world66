---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Bahian food is one of the great regional cuisines of Brazil and Porto Seguro is a good place to try it. The staples are *moqueca* — fish or seafood stewed in coconut milk, *dendê* palm oil, and coriander — and *acarajé*, deep-fried black-eyed-pea fritters served by *baianas* in white lace from street carts, usually filled with *vatapá* and dried shrimp. Most restaurants in town also serve standard Brazilian beach fare: grilled fish, rice, beans, chips, and the inevitable *caipirinha*.

The strip along the Passarela do Álcool concentrates the tourist-facing restaurants. For better cooking, look at the smaller streets behind, or eat at the *barracas* on the northern beaches at lunch — many of them have a surprisingly good kitchen.
