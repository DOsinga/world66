The Abolition Museum occupies a historic house in Recife and deals with one of Brazil's hardest subjects: slavery, emancipation, and the long afterlife of both in Brazilian society. Its exhibitions are strongest when they connect national history to Pernambuco's plantation economy and Afro-Brazilian culture.

This is a small museum, but it adds necessary weight to a Recife itinerary otherwise dominated by churches, beaches, and colonial streets. Give it time; the visit is less about polished spectacle than about understanding the forces that shaped the region.
