Benfica Cultural Center focuses on the Armorial Movement, the Pernambuco-rooted arts movement associated with Ariano Suassuna. Its collection also includes works from Recife's former Fine Arts School, giving it a useful place in the city's art circuit.

It is a specialist stop rather than a broad survey museum, best for visitors interested in regional art, literature, and the cultural history of Pernambuco.
