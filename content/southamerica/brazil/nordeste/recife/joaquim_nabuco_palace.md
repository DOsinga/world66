Joaquim Nabuco Palace is the seat of Pernambucos legislature and one of the grand civic buildings around Recifes old centre. Its formal facade and ceremonial interiors speak to the period when provincial politics, sugar wealth, and urban ambition shaped the citys public architecture.

You will usually see it from outside while walking between the Santa Isabel Theater, Marco Zero, and the riverfront. Even without a tour, it helps explain why this part of Recife feels like a capital rather than just a beach city.
