Museu Franciscano de Arte Sacra is a small sacred-art museum in Recife's historic centre. Its collection includes religious vestments, statues, chandeliers, and other objects connected with Franciscan worship and church life.

It works best as part of a walk through Santo Antônio's churches and colonial buildings, rather than as a standalone museum trip.
