Museu Murillo La Greca is a small art museum in Recife dedicated to the Pernambuco-born painter Murillo La Greca. The collection and building offer a quieter, more local counterpoint to the city's larger cultural institutions and colonial churches.

It is not a blockbuster museum, but it can be worthwhile for travelers interested in Brazilian modern and regional art. Check current opening hours before making a special trip, as smaller municipal museums can have changing schedules.
