The Museum of Natural History Louis Jacques Brunet is one of Latin America's older natural history museums, founded in 1861 and housed at Ginasio Pernambucano. Its collection covers archaeology, botany, geology, and zoology.

It is a compact, specialist stop near central Recife, useful for visitors who want a different kind of museum between the city's churches, markets, and colonial-era sights.
