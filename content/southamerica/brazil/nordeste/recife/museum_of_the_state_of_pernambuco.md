The Museum of the State of Pernambuco is housed in a grand nineteenth-century mansion in Recife and gives a broad introduction to the state's history, art, and elite domestic life. Its collection ranges from furniture and religious art to paintings, photographs, and objects tied to Pernambuco's political and cultural identity.

It is especially useful if your Recife visit is otherwise focused on beaches, food, and the old centre. The museum slows the city down and puts the sugar economy, regional aristocracy, and North-eastern artistic traditions into a more tangible setting.
