The Pernambuco Image and Sound Museum documents the state's culture through film, records, photographs, postcards, and other audiovisual material. Its collection runs to thousands of items, so it is strongest for travellers interested in music, media, and local memory.

It is a good secondary stop in central Recife, especially if you want context beyond churches, bridges, and colonial architecture.
