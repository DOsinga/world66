A working-class quarter just south of Santo Antônio, anchored by the Mercado de São José and a knot of narrow streets selling everything from religious statues to dried beans. The pace is faster and the surfaces grittier than in the more polished historic streets to the north.

The neighbourhood is at its best in the morning, when the market is full and the bars on the side streets serve cheap fish stews. By late afternoon it empties out and is not a place to linger after dark.
