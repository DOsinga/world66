---
title: Bars and Cafes
type: section
---

São Luís is famous throughout Brazil for its reggae bars. On weekends, the sound systems — *radiolas* — set up in the streets of Praia Grande and the surrounding neighbourhoods, and the city dances to a slower, rootsier version of Jamaican reggae. The bar scene in the historic centre is a mix of colonial-building restaurants that stay open late and dedicated reggae venues. Start in Praia Grande and follow the music.
