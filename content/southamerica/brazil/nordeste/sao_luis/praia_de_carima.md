Praia de Carima is a quiet dune-and-beach outing near Raposa, north-east of Sao Luis. Access can depend on tides and local boat connections, so treat it as a small adventure rather than a simple city beach. The payoff is a wilder stretch of sand than the urban beaches closer to the historic centre.
