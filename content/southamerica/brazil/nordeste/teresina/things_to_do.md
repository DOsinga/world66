title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Teresina's sights are modest but distinct. Start in the centre with the [Museu do Piauí](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/teresina/museu_do_piaui), [Palácio de Karnak](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/teresina/palacio_de_karnak), and [Igreja São Benedito](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/teresina/igreja_sao_benedito), then use the river as your guide. The [Ponte Estaiada viewpoint](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/teresina/ponte_estaiada_viewpoint) gives the easiest city panorama, while the [Encontro dos Rios](/southamerica/brazil/nordeste/teresina/encontro_dos_rios) is the place where Teresina's geography becomes visible.
