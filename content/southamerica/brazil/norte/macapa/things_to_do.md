---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Macapá's sights divide neatly between river history and Equator theatre. Start at the Fortaleza de São José and the riverfront, then use a taxi or ride-hailing app for Marco Zero and the Zerão stadium. Museu Sacaca adds the best introduction to Amapá's river communities, forest knowledge, and everyday Amazon culture.
