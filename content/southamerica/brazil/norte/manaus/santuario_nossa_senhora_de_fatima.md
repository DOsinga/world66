Santuário Nossa Senhora de Fátima is a distinctive cylindrical church with a local following in Manaus. It is a modest stop rather than a major monument, but the shape makes it stand out from the city's more familiar colonial and theatre-era landmarks.

It is best visited while already moving through central Manaus or nearby museum streets, not as a long detour on its own.
