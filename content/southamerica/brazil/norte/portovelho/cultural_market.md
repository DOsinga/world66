The Cultural Market is a central stop for a simple Porto Velho walk. It is the place to look for local snacks, music, and a bit of evening life close to the old railway sights.

Treat it as atmosphere rather than a major attraction. It works best combined with the Three Water Boxes, the cathedral, and the riverfront.
