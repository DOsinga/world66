Sacred Heart Cathedral is Porto Velho's main church, a plain but useful landmark in the centre. It is not an architectural showpiece, but it helps orient a walk through the old civic core.

Stop in if you are already nearby. The cathedral pairs naturally with the market, water towers, and railway complex rather than justifying a special trip on its own.
