The Three Water Boxes are the postcard image of Porto Velho: three metal water towers standing together near the old railway area. They were tied to the railway era and are now the easiest landmark to recognise in the centre.

There is not much to do here beyond looking and taking a photo, but the towers make a useful stop between the railway complex, the cathedral, and the cultural market.
