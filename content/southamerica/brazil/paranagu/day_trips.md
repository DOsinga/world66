The famous train ride up the Serra do Mar back to [Curitiba](/southamerica/brazil/curitiba) is a perfectly viable day trip in itself — go up by bus, descend by train, or vice versa.

**Ilha do Mel** is the standard escape. The car-free island lies just outside Paranaguá Bay; boats leave from a terminal a few minutes outside the centre. There are two settlements, both very small, two long beaches, and an 18th-century Portuguese lighthouse. Possible as a long day trip but better with a night.

**Antonina**, a smaller and arguably more atmospheric colonial town, is 40 km away on the bay and reachable by bus.

The **Marumbi State Park**, on the railway line back to Curitiba, has hiking trails up to peaks above the rainforest. You can board or alight at the small station mid-route — confirm with the train operator before booking.
