---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Paranaguá sits at the end of the line from [Curitiba](/southamerica/brazil/curitiba), 90 km inland. The journey through the Serra do Mar is one of the great train rides of South America, and the main reason many travellers come down to the coast.

**By train.** The Serra Verde Express runs daily from Curitiba's Estação Ferroviária, descending nearly a thousand metres through tunnels and over viaducts in the Atlantic rainforest. The full route takes about three hours one way. Sit on the right-hand side going down for the best views. Tickets sell out — book in advance, particularly at weekends.

**By bus.** Frequent buses run from Curitiba's main rodoviária throughout the day; the trip takes about 90 minutes on the BR-277 highway. Cheaper than the train and faster, but no scenery.

**By car.** The same BR-277 is well paved and easy to drive. The descent through the mountains is steep but uneventful.

**By air.** Paranaguá has a small airport (PNG) used mainly for general aviation. The nearest commercial airport is Afonso Pena in Curitiba.
