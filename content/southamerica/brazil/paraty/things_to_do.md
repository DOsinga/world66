---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Paraty's historic centre is compact enough to walk in an afternoon, but the surrounding coast and mountains reward several days of exploration. The cobblestone streets flood at high tide — a quirk of the 17th-century town's design — and the best way to see them is simply to wander at your own pace, ducking into the white-walled churches and small squares as you go.
