Pequena Finlândia is the tourist heart of Penedo: wooden storefronts, bridges, chocolate shops, cafes, and a Santa Claus house wrapped in Finnish-colony theming. It is commercial, but it is also the easiest place to feel the village's strange Brazil-meets-Finland identity.
