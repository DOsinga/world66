Cervejaria Bohemia turns one of Brazil's best-known beer names into a visitor experience in Petrópolis, where the brand's roots go back to the 19th century. The tour mixes brewing history, process displays and tastings inside a handsome central building.

It is a good change of pace from imperial palaces and churches, especially on a rainy mountain afternoon. Even non-specialists get a useful look at how Petrópolis' European immigrant history fed into Brazil's brewing culture.
