Rio Negro Palace is one of the official residences of the President of Brazil and one of the historic mansions in central Petropolis. Its formal architecture fits naturally into a walk between the cathedral, the Imperial Museum, and the Crystal Palace.

Access can vary, so treat it as a worthwhile architectural stop and check locally if interiors are open during your visit.
