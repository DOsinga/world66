---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Pipa's main street, **Avenida Baía dos Golfinhos**, is a tight strip of restaurants, bars, and small shops that comes alive at dusk. Seafood dominates — *moqueca* (Bahian-style fish stew), grilled fish of the day, garlic prawns, and *casquinha de siri* (stuffed crab shell) — but the village is cosmopolitan enough to offer competent Italian, sushi, and Argentinian steakhouses too.

**Cruzeiro do Pescador**, **Camarão & Cia**, and **Cumadre** are reliable for traditional seafood; **Tapas**, a small Spanish-style place on the main strip, has been packed every night for a decade. Cheaper, local eating is at the *barracas* on Praia do Centro — grilled fish, rice, beans, and a beer for less than half the main-street prices. The strip also hosts a string of açaí bowls and tapioca crepe stands open from breakfast.
