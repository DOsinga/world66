---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pipa is a beach village, and most of what there is to do takes you to the sand. Four main beaches sit within walking distance of the village centre, each with a different character: the central **Praia do Centro** is the all-purpose town beach; **Praia do Amor**, reached down a steep set of wooden stairs, is the surf beach; **Baía dos Golfinhos** is famous for resident dolphins; and **Praia das Minas** stretches empty and red-cliffed to the south. Surfing, swimming, body-boarding, and long walks at low tide along the base of the cliffs are the main pastimes.

Inland from the village, the protected **Mata Atlântica** rainforest at the Santuário Ecológico has trails through the last patches of coastal forest. A boat trip out to swim with the dolphins is a near-compulsory ritual; sea-kayaking and stand-up paddleboarding are easy to arrange on the central beach.
