---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Pipa is good year-round but the dry season — roughly **September to March** — has the most reliable sunshine, the warmest sea, and the busiest scene. December to February is Brazilian high summer and the village is at capacity; prices double, the strip is shoulder-to-shoulder at night, and accommodation must be booked well in advance.

The rainy months **April to July** bring short, heavy tropical downpours rather than continuous rain — most days still have plenty of beach hours — but humidity rises, and some of the smaller hostels and shops shut up for repairs. The shoulder months **September and October** are ideal: warm sea, low rain, and a fraction of the December crowds.
