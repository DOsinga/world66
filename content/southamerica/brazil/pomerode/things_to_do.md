---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Pomerode's main draw is its exceptionally well-preserved German heritage, concentrated in the old town centre along Rua 15 de Novembro. The [Pomerode Zoological Garden](/southamerica/brazil/pomerode/pomerode_zoo) is one of the oldest zoos in Brazil and sits right in the heart of town. The [Museu Pomerano](/southamerica/brazil/pomerode/museu_pomerano) covers the history of the Pomeranian immigration wave, while the [Carl Weege Immigrant House](/southamerica/brazil/pomerode/carl_weege_house) preserves the original home of one of the first settlers. Walking the [enxaimel architecture trail](/southamerica/brazil/pomerode/enxaimel_architecture) through the old centre is the most rewarding way to spend a morning here.
