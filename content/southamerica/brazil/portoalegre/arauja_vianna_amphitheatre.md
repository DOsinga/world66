The Anfiteatro Pôr do Sol — Sunset Amphitheatre — in Parque Farroupilha (Redenção), more often called the Araújo Vianna after the older venue it replaced, is Porto Alegre's main outdoor concert stage. It holds around 8,000 people and hosts everything from classical concerts and MPB shows to rock gigs and electronic music events throughout the year. Many of the performances are free; the paid shows are typically very reasonably priced.

The setting is one of the best for any outdoor concert in Brazil: the stage faces west, so evening concerts often have the Guaíba sunset as a backdrop through the trees, and the audience sits or stands on the park's gently sloping lawn with the old trees of Redenção overhead. Summer evenings (December through March) bring the most programming; the park's natural shading makes afternoon shows comfortable even in the heat. Check the city's cultural calendar — available at the tourism office or online — for what is on during your visit. Even without a specific event, the amphitheatre area is worth walking through on a summer evening when musicians practice and small informal performances happen around its edges.
