The Brique da Redenção is Porto Alegre's great weekly market, held every Sunday morning along the northern edge of Parque Farroupilha (Redenção) from about 8am to 2pm. It is not merely a craft market — though there are craft stalls — but a full market ecosystem: antiques dealers with old gaúcho silver and 20th-century furniture, vintage clothing sellers with genuinely good finds, jewellery makers, local food producers with cheese and cured meats and infused cachaça, book dealers, artists selling original work, children's toy vendors, and a dozen different food stalls doing their best business of the week.

The atmosphere is as important as the shopping. By 10am the market is dense with people: families out for their Sunday morning ritual, students nursing hangovers with mate, elderly portoalegrenses who have been coming for decades, tourists with cameras. Live music is almost always happening — usually a gaúcho folk duo or a small jazz ensemble at the café kiosk near the main entrance. Come hungry: the **pastel** (fried dough filled with cheese, meat, or heart of palm) and grilled sausage sandwiches sold at the market are excellent. The best strategy is to arrive at 8am when the antique dealers are still willing to negotiate, fill a bag with food from the stalls, and spend the morning walking slowly through the whole length of the market.
