A morning walking Porto Alegre's Centro Histórico covers an amount of history, architecture, and urban texture that would take a week to fully absorb. The compact area between the Praça da Matriz, the Praça da Alfândega, and the Cais Mauá waterfront holds the city's most important buildings within easy walking distance of each other, and the streets between them have some of the best-preserved 19th- and early 20th-century commercial architecture in southern Brazil.

Start at **Praça da Matriz** — the cathedral, palace, theatre, and assembly all visible from one spot. Walk south to the **Basílica Nossa Senhora das Dores** for the oldest church. Back north through the pedestrianised **Rua dos Andradas** (Rua da Praia) past the **Casa de Cultura Mário Quintana** and the **Clube do Comércio** to the **Praça da Alfândega** — the MARGS and Santander Cultural here. Continue to the **Farol Santander** for the rooftop view. End at the **Mercado Público** for lunch. The whole route takes about two to three hours at a leisurely pace with stops. Do it on a Tuesday through Saturday: Monday many things are closed; Sunday the market is shut. The **tourist office** near the Mercado Público stocks a free walking map that adds context to the route.
