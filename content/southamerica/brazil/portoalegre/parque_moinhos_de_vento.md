Parque Moinhos de Vento is smaller and quieter than Redenção but in some ways more charming: a well-maintained urban park in the upscale neighbourhood of the same name, with a lake, a good playground, jogging paths, and several café kiosks. It functions as the neighbourhood's living room, and at any time of day you will find a representative cross-section of Moinhos de Vento life: professional joggers in the early morning, mothers with strollers at mid-morning, dog-walkers at all hours, and the regular afternoon gathering of older residents who come to sit and talk.

The park is small enough to walk across in fifteen minutes but designed well enough that it does not feel cramped. The lake has paddle boats for rent on weekends. The café kiosks serve decent coffee and the inevitable chimarrão. Along the southern edge of the park, a weekend artisan market sets up most Saturdays, smaller than the Brique da Redenção but worth a look for local crafts and food products. The surrounding streets — particularly Rua Padre Chagas — are Porto Alegre's best café strip, so the park makes a natural anchor for a morning spent between coffee shops. If you are staying in Moinhos de Vento or Bom Fim, this is where you end up for your daily run.
