The Santander Cultural occupies the former Banco Mercantil building on the Praça da Alfândega, a magnificent example of early 20th-century Brazilian banking architecture: marble columns, ornate bronze fittings, a soaring main hall with a glass ceiling that floods the space with diffused light. Santander acquired the building and converted it into a cultural centre that now hosts some of the best contemporary art exhibitions in Porto Alegre. Entry is free.

The main banking hall is used as the primary exhibition space, and the combination of the original architecture and contemporary art works surprisingly well — the grandeur of the setting gives even modest shows a certain authority. The upper galleries, in what were once the bank's offices, are used for smaller exhibitions and project spaces. The programme tends toward Brazilian contemporary art with an emphasis on work from the south, which is underrepresented in the São Paulo and Rio-centric national art world. The building is right on the Praça da Alfândega, steps from the MARGS, making the two natural partners for a single cultural morning. The Santander Cultural bookshop stocks a good selection of Brazilian art books and publications.
