The Teatro São Pedro is Porto Alegre's oldest and most beautiful performing arts venue, a stately Italian-neoclassical building facing Praça da Matriz that has anchored the city's cultural life since 1858. The main auditorium seats around 700 people in a horseshoe of red-velvet boxes and balconies; the acoustics are excellent and the sight lines were designed by someone who understood that seeing the stage matters as much as hearing the music. The theatre hosts opera, classical music, ballet, and theatre from local companies and occasional international visitors.

It is worth going to a performance here rather than just admiring it from the outside. The programming tends toward the classical: symphony concerts, chamber music, and occasional opera productions that punch above their weight given the venue's size and budget. Check the schedule at teatrosaopedro.com.br — tickets are very reasonably priced by any international standard and the experience of sitting in this room is part of what Porto Alegre is. Even if you cannot get to a show, the theatre sometimes offers guided visits to the interior, including backstage areas and the restored dressing rooms.
