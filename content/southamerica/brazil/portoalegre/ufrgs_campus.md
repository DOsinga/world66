The Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS) is one of the best universities in Brazil and its main campus, spread across several blocks between the city centre and Bom Fim, is one of Porto Alegre's most pleasant urban spaces. The campus architecture mixes late 19th-century institutional buildings — the old faculties of medicine and law in serious neoclassical style — with modernist additions from the mid-20th century, and the whole is generously planted with trees that make it agreeable to walk through even in summer heat.

The university runs several museums open to the general public: the **Museu da UFRGS** (natural history, ethnography, and science collections), the university art gallery with regular exhibitions, and the **Planetário da UFRGS** with public shows on weekend evenings. The main campus also has good cafeterias and coffee kiosks where you can eat well at student prices. Wandering through the campus during a weekday when the students are about gives you a sense of Porto Alegre's intellectual life — UFRGS has produced a remarkable proportion of Brazil's scientists, politicians, and artists, and the campus has the confident atmosphere of an institution that knows it matters. The edge of the campus along Avenida Paulo Gama is particularly handsome, with a long tree-lined walk past the older faculty buildings.
