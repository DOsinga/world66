Vila Flores is one of Porto Alegre's best creative spaces: a complex of early 20th-century former industrial buildings in the Floresta neighbourhood, north of the city centre, that has been converted into a hub for studios, small businesses, cultural events, and some of the city's most interesting food and coffee. The buildings retain their industrial character — exposed brick, high ceilings, original timber frames — while hosting a constantly evolving mix of tenants that includes architects, designers, craft producers, music studios, and a very good café.

The complex hosts regular weekend markets, concerts, film screenings, and cultural events that draw a young, creative Porto Alegre crowd. Even without a specific event, a visit during the week to have coffee in the courtyard and walk through the buildings is worthwhile: the spaces have been adapted with genuine care and the atmosphere is collaborative rather than commercial. It is in a part of the city that visitors rarely reach — the Floresta neighbourhood is north of the centre, not on the tourist circuit — but that is part of its appeal. The surrounding streets have a few other interesting spots: studios and small creative businesses that have followed Vila Flores into the neighbourhood.
