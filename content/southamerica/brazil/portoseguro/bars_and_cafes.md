---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Porto Seguro's nightlife is one of the loudest in Bahia, and it concentrates on the Passarela do Álcool — the waterfront strip where *cachaça* bars, live-music stages, and capoeira circles keep going until the early hours in season. The music is *axé* and forró, the drinks are sweet fruit *cachaças* in plastic cups, and the atmosphere is resolutely Brazilian beach-holiday. Arrive after 10pm; the strip empties before midnight in the off-season.

Away from the main strip, there are quieter bars around the Praça dos Pataxós and a handful of seafront esplanade cafes that work well for a drink at sunset. The *barracas* on the northern beaches double as daytime bars: cold beer, coconut water, and grilled fish served from plastic tables at the water's edge.
