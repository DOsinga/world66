The easiest and most rewarding day trip is across the river to **Arraial d'Ajuda** — a quieter clifftop village reached by a five-minute car ferry, with its own beaches and a cluster of restaurants around the church square. South from Arraial the coast continues to **Trancoso**, a once-bohemian village turned upmarket retreat, and beyond that to the still-rustic riverside village of **Caraíva**.

North of Porto Seguro, **Santa Cruz Cabrália** is a small fishing town at the mouth of the João de Tiba river with a quieter colonial plaza of its own and good beaches at **Coroa Vermelha** — the actual landing site of Cabral's fleet in 1500.
