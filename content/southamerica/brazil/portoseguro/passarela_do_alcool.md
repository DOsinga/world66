The waterfront promenade between the historic centre and the river mouth, lined for several hundred metres with bars, restaurants, craft stalls, and *cachaça* vendors. By day it is a quiet seafront walk; by night, in season, it is the loudest single block in the south of Bahia. Live bands play on raised stages, capoeira circles form on the pavement, and the *cachaça* stalls hand out free shots in coloured plastic cups.

The energy is what people come to Porto Seguro for. It is not subtle and not for everyone — the volume is high, the drinks are sweet, the prices climb in peak season. But for a single noisy night out it delivers exactly what it promises. Most bars don't fill until after 10pm; the strip thins out around 2am.
