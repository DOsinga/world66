A 700-hectare Atlantic Forest reserve managed by the Pataxó indigenous community, about ten kilometres inland from Porto Seguro. Guided visits introduce traditional plant use, archery, body painting with *jenipapo* and *urucum*, and the Pataxó language; a meal of fish or chicken cooked in banana leaves and *beiju* (cassava flatbread) is part of the standard tour.

The visit lasts most of half a day and is run entirely by the community — visitor fees go directly to the reserve. It is one of the better organised indigenous cultural tourism experiences in Brazil and gives a sense of the people whose ancestors met Cabral's fleet here in 1500. Book through a Porto Seguro travel agency or directly with the reserve; transport is usually included.
