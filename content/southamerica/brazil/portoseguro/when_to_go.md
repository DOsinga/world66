---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Porto Seguro is tropical and warm year-round, with daytime temperatures of 26–32°C across all seasons. The high season runs from December through Carnival (usually February), when Brazilians take their summer holidays and the town fills with families and party crowds. Prices double, beaches and *barracas* are packed, and the Passarela do Álcool runs at full volume every night. Book accommodation well in advance.

The shoulder season — March to May and September to November — is the best time to visit if you want warm water and open beaches without the crush. Rain is more frequent (especially April–June) but rarely lasts a whole day. July and August are dry and cooler, with strong winds — pleasant but the sea can be choppy. Carnival itself is one of the biggest in Brazil outside Salvador and Rio; expect everything booked solid for a week.
