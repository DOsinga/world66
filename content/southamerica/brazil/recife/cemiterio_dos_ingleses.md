---
title: Cemitério dos Ingleses
type: poi
latitude: -8.04722
longitude: -34.87639
tags: [sight]
---

A small walled cemetery dating from 1814, established for the British merchants who settled in Recife in the early 19th century under the trade treaties between Britain and the Portuguese crown. The headstones — many in English, a few in German — are an unusual window onto an Atlantic merchant community that has otherwise vanished from the city.

The cemetery is gated and access is irregular. Easiest to combine with a walk around Santo Amaro.
