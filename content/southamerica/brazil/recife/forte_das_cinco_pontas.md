A five-pointed star-shaped fort just south of the historic centre, now home to the Museu da Cidade do Recife. The building is the more impressive part of the visit: thick stone walls, original gun emplacements, and a generous courtyard.

The city museum displays inside are mixed — old maps, historic photographs, a few good period engravings of the colonial city — and the labelling is mostly Portuguese-only. Worth half an hour for the building.
