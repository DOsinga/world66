The faux-medieval Tudor castle at the centre of the Ricardo Brennand Institute compound. The building was constructed entirely in the 1990s but uses traditional stone, oak, and stained glass to convincing effect. It serves as the main display space for the institute's arms and armour collection.

The castle is included in the standard Ricardo Brennand admission, and most visitors spend at least as much time inside the building as in the surrounding park.
