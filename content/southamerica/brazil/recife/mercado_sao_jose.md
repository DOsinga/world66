A cast-iron market hall from 1875 at the centre of the São José district, and the best place in the city to see the everyday commercial life of northeast Brazil. Inside and around it traders sell tropical fruit, dried meat, regional sweets, herbal medicines, religious figurines, and rough leather goods.

Mornings are when it is at full pitch; by mid-afternoon the food stalls dominate. Worth combining with a visit to the nearby Basilica and Convent of Nossa Senhora do Carmo and the Co-Cathedral.
