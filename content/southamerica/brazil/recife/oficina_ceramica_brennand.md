The working ceramic studios attached to the main Brennand sculpture park, where the artist developed and produced his pieces over decades. Original kilns, moulds, and tools are still in place, and on most days a number of works in various stages of firing are on view.

Combine with the larger sculpture park and the Ricardo Brennand Institute for a full day in the western suburbs.
