Parque 13 de Maio is Recife's old central park, a green pocket between the busy streets and civic buildings of Boa Vista. Its ponds, mature trees, bridges, and shaded paths make it one of the easier places in the centre to pause without leaving the city fabric behind.

It is not Recife's main reason to visit, but it helps explain daily life away from the waterfront and colonial showpieces. Go in daylight, combine it with nearby museums or churches, and treat it as a short urban breather rather than a destination for a whole afternoon.
