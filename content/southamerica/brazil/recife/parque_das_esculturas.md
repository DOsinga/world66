A sculpture park on a natural reef off Marco Zero, with around ninety ceramic columns by Francisco Brennand emerging from the water. From the shore the towers look like a small fleet of bishops; up close they are densely worked, slightly grotesque, totemic figures.

The only practical way to visit is by catamaran or small boat from the Marco Zero steps. Trips are short and frequent and a popular sunset activity.
