A private museum complex built around a faux-medieval castle in the Várzea district, on the western edge of the city. The displays are eclectic and rich: thousands of pieces of European armour and edged weapons, paintings by the 17th-century Dutch artist Frans Post, tapestries, rare books, and a manicured garden.

Allow two to three hours. The collection is much larger than first impressions suggest and the explanatory panels are detailed. Closed Mondays. A taxi from the centre takes around twenty minutes and is the easiest way to arrive.
