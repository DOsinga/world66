On the south side of [Rio de Janeiro](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro), Barra da Tijuca has wide beaches, a quieter atmosphere, and modern shopping malls. It feels more like a Miami suburb than the rest of Rio — all wide avenues, gated condominiums, and beachfront high-rises. The [beach](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/barra_beach) runs for 18 kilometres along the Atlantic coast, making it the longest in Rio, less crowded than Copacabana or Ipanema and with better surf.

The [Cidade das Artes](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/cidade_das_artes), opened in 2013, is the home of the Brazilian Symphony Orchestra and the largest concert hall in South America — a striking piece of architecture by Christian de Portzamparc, elevated on stilts above the main avenue. Further west, [Prainha Beach](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/prainha) is one of the finest and most protected beaches near Rio, tucked into a nature reserve about 20 kilometres from the main Barra strip.
