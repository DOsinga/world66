The Carmen Miranda Museum holds photographs, posters, recordings, costumes, and the fruit-topped hats linked to the Brazilian singer and film star. The collection is small, but it is a lively stop for visitors interested in Brazilian popular culture and mid-century cinema.

It sits by Parque do Flamengo, so it is easiest to include while exploring the waterfront park and nearby Flamengo sights.
