Instituto Moreira Salles is one of Rio's better cultural stops for photography, film, and Brazilian visual history. Exhibitions change, but the focus is usually thoughtful and well presented rather than blockbuster.

The setting helps: a modernist house tucked into leafy Gavea, away from the beach traffic. Come when you want a quieter Rio afternoon, especially if the current photography show fits your interests.
