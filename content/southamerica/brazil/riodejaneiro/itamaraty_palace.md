Itamaraty Palace in Rio de Janeiro is a former presidential palace and foreign ministry building. It now houses a museum of South American diplomacy, a library and United Nations offices in Brazil.

The palace is a useful Centro stop for visitors interested in Brazil's republican history and diplomatic architecture. It fits naturally with nearby cultural sights such as the Royal Portuguese Reading Room, CCBB and the museums around the port area.
