Morro Dois Irmãos is one of Rio's great short hikes, rising above Leblon with open views across the South Zone. The trail has several viewpoints before the summit: Rocinha and Pedra da Gávea appear first, then Gávea, Corcovado, Pedra Bonita, Rodrigo de Freitas Lagoon, and the beaches from Leblon toward Botafogo.

The walk is steep but not long, and the reward is a perspective on Rio that feels less polished than the classic Sugarloaf and Christ the Redeemer views. Go early for cooler weather and clearer light.
