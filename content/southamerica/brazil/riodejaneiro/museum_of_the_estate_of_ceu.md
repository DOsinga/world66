The Museum of the Estate of Ceu is an art and antique museum in Santa Teresa, set in a hillside house with views over central Rio. Its collection includes works by modern masters, decorative objects, and period pieces that give the place the feel of a private collection rather than a large institutional museum.

It pairs well with a walk through Santa Teresa and the nearby Selaron Steps. Check current opening details before going, as smaller Rio museums can keep irregular hours.
