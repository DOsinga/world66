Oi Futuro is a cultural centre and gallery in a former telephone museum. Its exhibitions often focus on digital, media and interactive art, making it a different kind of stop from Rio's older museum collections.

It is most useful to visitors who want a small, contemporary gallery visit around Flamengo or Catete.
