The most dramatic of Rio's granite peaks — an 844-metre massif that drops sheer to the Atlantic on one side. The hike from the Tijuca Forest takes three to four hours and involves some easy scrambling; go with a guide as the trail is not always marked. The summit view is exceptional: the beach curve of Barra da Tijuca to the south, the city stretching north, and the sea below. Start early to avoid the afternoon heat and return before storms roll in.
