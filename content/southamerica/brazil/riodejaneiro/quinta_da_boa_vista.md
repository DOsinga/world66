Quinta da Boa Vista is a large public park in the São Cristóvão neighbourhood of the North Zone, formerly the estate of the Brazilian imperial family. The neoclassical São Cristóvão Palace at its centre housed the National Museum of Brazil — the fire of 2018 destroyed the building and most of its collection. The palace shell is being slowly rebuilt; the museum's remaining collection is gradually being restored and rehoused.

The park itself remains open and is one of the best green spaces in the North Zone — large, shaded by enormous tropical trees, and popular with local families on weekends. The Rio Zoo (Jardim Zoológico) occupies the southern section and is home to a good collection of South American wildlife including jaguars, anacondas, tapirs, and the full range of Brazilian macaws. A working-class neighbourhood with an imperial past, and now a place of gradual rebuilding.
