St. John the Baptist Cemetery is the burial place of many major Brazilian figures, including former presidents, musicians, writers, and actors. Visitors come for its funerary art and for graves associated with names such as Tom Jobim, Machado de Assis, Jose de Alencar, and Carmen Miranda.
