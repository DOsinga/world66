The 396-metre granite plug at the entrance to Guanabara Bay is Rio's most distinctive landmark. Two cable-car stages: first to Morro da Urca (halfway), then to the summit. The view from the top takes in the bay, the beaches of Flamengo and Botafogo, Corcovado with the Christ statue, and the mountains beyond. Sunset is the obvious time — the queue is long but the light is worth it. Come early on a clear day to maximise the view; cloud rolls in from the sea by mid-morning.
