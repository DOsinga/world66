title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Rio is a city where the outdoors is the main attraction. Beyond the famous beaches, you can hike through the world's largest urban rainforest in the [Tijuca Forest](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/floresta_da_tijuca), ride the cable car up [Sugarloaf Mountain](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/sugarloaf), take in the panorama from [Christ the Redeemer](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/corcovado), or stroll through the 19th-century [Botanical Garden](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/botanicalgarden). The more adventurous can [hang glide](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/hang_glide_in_rio) off Pedra Bonita and land on Pepino beach, or take a sailing trip around Guanabara Bay.

The city has strong cultural depth. The [Banco do Brasil Cultural Center](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/banco_do_brasil_cu) in Centro hosts excellent exhibitions, and the [Modern Art Museum](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/modernartmuseum) sits on the waterfront at Aterro do Flamengo. The [Real Gabinete Português de Leitura](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/royal_reading_room) — the Royal Portuguese Reading Room — is one of the great library interiors in the world. For a quieter afternoon, [Parque Lage](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/parque_lage) offers lush gardens at the foot of Corcovado.

Don't miss the [Selarón Steps](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/selaron_steps) connecting Lapa to [Santa Teresa](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/santa_teresa), the [Sambadrome](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/sambadrome) (best visited during a rehearsal rather than the main Carnival event), and the hidden colonial square of [Largo do Boticário](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/largo_do_boticario). Football fans must visit the [Maracanã](/southamerica/brazil/riodejaneiro/maracana) — if possible for an actual match.
