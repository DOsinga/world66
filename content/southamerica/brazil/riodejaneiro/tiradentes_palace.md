Tiradentes Palace is the seat of Rio de Janeiro's Legislative Assembly and one of Centro's notable civic buildings. Visitors can walk the first-floor corridors, where displays explain the history of the assembly and the building.

The palace is most useful as part of a wider walk through Rio's historic centre around Praca XV, the cultural centres, and the old port streets. Access to some areas may depend on guided visits or assembly business.
