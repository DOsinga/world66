Valongo Wharf is the archaeological remains of the landing place where hundreds of thousands of enslaved Africans were brought into Brazil in the early nineteenth century. Rediscovered during works in the old port area, it is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Rio's most important places for understanding the Atlantic slave trade and Afro-Brazilian history.
