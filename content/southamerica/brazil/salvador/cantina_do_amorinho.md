A small Italian restaurant on the Barra peninsula, owned by an Italian who migrated to Bahia in the 1980s and never left. The pasta is made on the premises, the seafood is local, and the wine list — heavy on Italian imports — is one of the better-curated in the city.

A useful break from Bahian cooking after a few days of palm oil. Book; the dining room is small.
