The Canaã Valley is the landscape most often used to represent Santa Maria de Jetibá: steep green slopes, farmhouses, patches of forest, and cultivated fields in the folds of the mountains.

It is not a single fenced attraction. Treat it as a scenic rural drive or guided local outing, with stops for viewpoints, churches, and farm produce when available.
