Pedra do Garrafão is one of the main natural landmarks of Santa Maria de Jetibá municipality, rising in the rural hills west of town. The hike is short on paper but steep enough to feel like a real outing.

Go for the view over the green Espírito Santo mountains and small farming communities below. Paths and access can change, so ask locally before setting out, especially after rain.
