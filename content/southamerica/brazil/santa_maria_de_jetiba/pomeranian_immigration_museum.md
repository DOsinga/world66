The Pomeranian Immigration Museum is the best first stop in Santa Maria de Jetibá. Its collection explains how Pomeranian families moved inland from Vitória in the 19th century and built a farming culture that kept its language, faith, food, and building habits.

The museum is small, but the context matters. Once you have seen the old tools, household objects, photos, and documents, the surrounding town makes more sense.
