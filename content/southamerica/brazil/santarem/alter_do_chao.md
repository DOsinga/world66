Alter do Chão is a village 37 kilometres south of Santarém on the Tapajós, surrounded by white-sand beaches that emerge from the river during the dry season (July to November). On a clear afternoon, with the water bright blue and the sand white and fine, it looks nothing like the muddy Amazon most people imagine.

The annual Çairé festival in October draws large crowds and is one of the most colourful indigenous and folkloric celebrations in the Brazilian Amazon. The rest of the year the village is quiet, with a few good restaurants, guesthouses, and boat operators who can take you into the surrounding flooded forest and Lake Verde. Getting there from Santarém takes about 45 minutes by bus along a well-maintained road.
