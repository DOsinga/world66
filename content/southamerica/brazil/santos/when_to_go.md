---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Santos lies on the Tropic of Capricorn and has a humid subtropical climate. December to March is hot, humid, and very busy with São Paulo holidaymakers; expect afternoon storms and the occasional flooded street. June to August is cool by Brazilian standards (15–22 °C), often grey but with the calmest seas and fewest crowds.

The best months are April, May, September, and October — warm but not exhausting, mostly dry, and quieter than peak summer. Carnival in February is a big local affair, with samba schools parading along the seafront, though it is overshadowed by Rio and Salvador.
