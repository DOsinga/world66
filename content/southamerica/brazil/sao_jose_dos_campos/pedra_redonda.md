Pedra Redonda is a lower and easier mountain outing than Pico do Selado, though still far enough from the main city to need planning. The route is about 5 km and reaches a rounded summit near the Minas Gerais border.

The reward is the view: ridges, forest, and, in good weather, the wider São José dos Campos area far below.
