Forte Santo Antonio da Barra is a fort replica and small historical museum interpreting the island of Saint Louis as it passed from French to Portuguese and then Brazilian control. The stop is useful for visitors who want more context for Sao Luis beyond the colonial centre.

Treat it as a focused history stop rather than a major fortress complex. Its value is the local story: why this Atlantic island mattered, and how its identity was shaped by competing colonial powers.
