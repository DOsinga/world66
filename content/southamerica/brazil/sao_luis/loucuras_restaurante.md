Loucuras do Sul is a well-regarded restaurant in the historic centre serving a combination of Maranhão regional food and churrasco (Brazilian barbecue). The regional dishes are the real draw: *torta de camarão* (shrimp tart), *vatapá*, crab rice, and the ubiquitous *arroz de cuxá*. Portions are large and prices are reasonable.

The restaurant occupies a restored colonial building with azulejo-tiled walls and an inner courtyard. It's popular with local business lunchers as well as tourists, which is usually a good sign. In the evenings, it sometimes has live music — regional rhythms including *bumba-meu-boi* — though the schedule varies and it's worth checking ahead.
