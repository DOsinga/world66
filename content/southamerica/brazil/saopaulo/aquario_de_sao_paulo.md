Aquário de São Paulo is a large aquarium and family attraction in the southeast of the city, with marine tanks and animal exhibits spread across a substantial indoor route. It is often described as the largest aquarium in South America.

The appeal is straightforward: fish, aquatic animals, and family-friendly exhibits under one roof. It is most useful as a half-day stop with children or on a rainy day.
