Auditorio Ibirapuera is Oscar Niemeyer's concert hall in Ibirapuera Park, designed as part of the park's original 1950s plan and completed decades later. Its sharp white form and red canopy make it one of the park's clearest architectural landmarks. The rear stage door can open toward the lawn for large outdoor performances.
