---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

São Paulo's drinking culture is built around the **boteco** — the corner bar, half-open to the street, serving cold beer in stubby glasses, salgadinhos, and grilled meats. Every neighbourhood has its classic botecos and most are worth a visit. The city has also gone deep on cocktails over the last decade; the bar scene from Vila Madalena through Pinheiros and into Jardins is as serious as anywhere in Latin America.

For coffee, **specialty cafes** are now everywhere in Pinheiros, Vila Madalena, and Vila Mariana. Brazil is the world's largest coffee producer and São Paulo is where the good beans get drunk — Coffee Lab, Octavio Café, and Suplicy are the names to look for. For nightlife, the area around **Rua Augusta** below Paulista is the city's main night-out strip; **Vila Olímpia** further south is the upmarket club zone.
