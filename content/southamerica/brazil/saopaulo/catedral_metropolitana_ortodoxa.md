Catedral Metropolitana Ortodoxa is the main Antiochian Orthodox cathedral in Sao Paulo and one of Brazil's notable examples of Byzantine-inspired church architecture. Its domed interior and iconography make it a distinctive religious stop away from the city's better-known Catholic landmarks.
