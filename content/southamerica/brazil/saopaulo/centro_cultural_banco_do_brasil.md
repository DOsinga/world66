Centro Cultural Banco do Brasil occupies the bank's former Sao Paulo headquarters in the historic center. The building now hosts art exhibitions, film, theater, music, and other cultural programming, usually at accessible prices.

It is a useful anchor for a daytime walk through Centro, where several restored cultural buildings sit close together. Check the current program before going, as the visit depends heavily on what is showing.
