Centro Cultural São Paulo is a large public cultural centre near Vergueiro metro station, close to Liberdade and Bela Vista. It hosts exhibitions, performances, a library, and other public programmes.

It is best treated as a flexible cultural stop: check what is on before going, or use it as a quieter break between central São Paulo neighbourhoods.
