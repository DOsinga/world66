Cinemateca Brasileira is Brazil's national film archive and a good stop for travellers interested in cinema beyond the multiplex. Screenings often lean toward classic, cult, and Brazilian films, while the institution preserves a huge part of the country's film history.

It is worth checking the programme before you go, since the appeal depends on what is showing. When the schedule lines up, it gives São Paulo a thoughtful, local cultural night out.
