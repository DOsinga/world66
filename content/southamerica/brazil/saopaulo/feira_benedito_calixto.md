Feira Benedito Calixto is a Saturday market on Praça Benedito Calixto, known for antiques, records, crafts, collectibles, and a lively food-and-music scene. It is one of São Paulo's more enjoyable weekend markets, with traditional choro music often adding to the atmosphere around the square.

Come with time to browse rather than a fixed shopping list. The surrounding bars and restaurants make it easy to turn the market into a long lunch stop.
