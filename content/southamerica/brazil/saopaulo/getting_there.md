---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

São Paulo has two main airports. **Guarulhos International (GRU)** is 25 km northeast of the centre and handles almost all international flights. The Airport Express bus to the city centre takes about 40 minutes off-peak and well over an hour in traffic; a taxi or Uber is 80–120 reais depending on traffic. The **Airport Train** to Engenheiro Goulart connects to the suburban rail network and is the cheapest way in.

**Congonhas (CGH)**, much closer to the centre, handles the busy shuttle to Rio and other domestic flights. A taxi to the south-zone neighbourhoods (Vila Olímpia, Itaim, Jardins) takes 20 to 40 minutes depending on traffic.

The **Tietê Rodoviária** is the main intercity bus station, with services across Brazil and into neighbouring countries. The Rio shuttle by bus takes about six hours; the air shuttle takes 45 minutes plus airport time.
