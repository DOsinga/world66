Igreja da Consolação is a neo-Gothic church from 1909, built when this part of São Paulo was still semi-rural and poor. Its pointed arches and vertical lines stand out among the denser streets around Consolação.

Inside, look for paintings by Benedito Calixto and Oscar Pereira da Silva. It is a modest but worthwhile stop if you are already exploring central São Paulo's churches, theatres, and old civic buildings.
