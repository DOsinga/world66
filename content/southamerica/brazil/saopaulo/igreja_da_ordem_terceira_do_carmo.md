Igreja da Ordem Terceira do Carmo is a colonial-period church in central Sao Paulo. The present building was finished in 1758 on the site of an earlier church dating to 1592, and it contains a notable collection of colonial religious art.

It is an easy historic stop near other sights in the old centre.
