Igreja de Santa Ifigenia is a central Sao Paulo church built in a neo-Romanesque style with Gothic details. It served as the city's temporary cathedral from 1930 to 1954.

The church is worth a look for its northern European feel and for interior paintings by Henri Bernard, Benedito Calixto, and Carlos Oswald.
