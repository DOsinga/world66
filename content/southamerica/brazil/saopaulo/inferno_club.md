Inferno Club is the Baixo Augusta institution for guitar music — a dark, sweaty two-floor club on the lower stretch of Rua Augusta that has been spinning rock, punk, metal, and indie since the strip first turned bohemian. The crowd is young, pierced, and unpretentious, the drinks are cheap, and the night runs long past sunrise on weekends.

It is the kind of place that defines this end of Consolação: no dress code, no velvet rope, just a queue on the sidewalk and a wall of noise inside.
