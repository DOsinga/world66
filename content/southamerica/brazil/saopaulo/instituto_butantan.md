Instituto Butantan is one of Sao Paulo's major science attractions, best known for its work with snakes, venom, vaccines, and infectious disease. Visitor areas include the Biology Museum, History Museum, Serpentarium, Primatarium, and Microbiology Museum.

The animal exhibits and shaded grounds make it more approachable than a conventional research institute, while still giving real context to the scientific work done here.
