Instituto Tomie Ohtake is one of Sao Paulo's most recognisable contemporary cultural centres, set in a bold, brightly coloured building in Pinheiros. Its galleries focus on modern and contemporary art, with changing exhibitions rather than a fixed collection.

The building itself is part of the appeal, and the institute is easy to combine with Vila Madalena's galleries, cafes, and street art.
