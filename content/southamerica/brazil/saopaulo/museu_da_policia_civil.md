Museu da Polícia Civil is a small museum about the history of São Paulo's civil police and the criminal cases they investigated. Its subject matter is unusual for the city, covering both policing and crime rather than a standard art or history collection.

It is most useful for visitors with a special interest in local institutions, forensic history, or offbeat museums.
