Museu de Arqueologia e Etnologia is the University of Sao Paulo's archaeology and ethnology museum. Its collections cover Indigenous cultures in Brazil as well as archaeological material from other parts of the world.

It is most useful for visitors already heading to the USP campus or those with a specific interest in archaeology and anthropology.
