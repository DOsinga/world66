The Museu de Arte Contemporanea is one of Sao Paulo's key art museums, with a large collection of Brazilian and international work from the 20th century onward. Its holdings run from major modernist names to changing contemporary exhibitions, so it is worth checking what is on before you go.

The museum is especially useful for visitors who want Sao Paulo beyond Avenida Paulista and Ibirapuera. Give it time; the collection is big enough that a quick look can feel rushed.
