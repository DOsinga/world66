The Museu de Arte Moderna is one of Sao Paulo's main modern art stops, with rotating exhibitions and a strong cultural program. Its setting in Ibirapuera Park makes it easy to combine with the park's other museums, gardens, and Oscar Niemeyer buildings.

Check the current exhibition schedule before going, as the museum is most rewarding when a major show or film program is running.
