The Museu de Arte Sacra holds one of Sao Paulo's main collections of religious art, with sculpture, paintings, devotional objects, and nativity scenes displayed in a historic Carmelite convent complex near Luz station.

It is a quieter counterpoint to the nearby Pinacoteca. The museum is especially worthwhile for visitors interested in colonial Brazilian art, Catholic imagery, and the way religious institutions shaped the early city.
