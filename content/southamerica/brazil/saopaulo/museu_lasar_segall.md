Museu Lasar Segall is devoted to the Lithuanian-born modernist painter and printmaker who became an important figure in Brazilian art. The museum is modest in scale, but it gives useful context for Segall's work and often hosts temporary exhibitions and courses.

It is a good choice if you want a quieter art stop away from Sao Paulo's biggest museum circuit.
