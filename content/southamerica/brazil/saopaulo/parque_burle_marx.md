Parque Burle Marx protects a pocket of Atlantic forest in the Panamby area, with walking paths, lawns, water features, and traces of a 19th-century estate. It is not as central as Ibirapuera, but it is one of São Paulo's better-designed green escapes and a good stop if you are exploring the south-western side of the city.
