Parque Burle Marx is a landscaped park in Panamby with a surviving patch of Atlantic rainforest. Its 138,000 square metres include gardens, walking paths and a 19th-century mansion once used by Francisco Matarazzo Pignatari.

It is a calmer green stop in the south-west of Sao Paulo, especially useful if you are already nearby.
