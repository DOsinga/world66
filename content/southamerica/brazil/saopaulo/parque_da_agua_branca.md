Parque da Agua Branca is a historic public park that began in the early 1900s as a centre for agricultural education and trade. Its old pavilions, shaded paths, small animal areas, and weekend food and produce stalls give it a different feel from Sao Paulo's larger landscape parks.

It is most rewarding as a local morning stop, especially when events or markets are running.
