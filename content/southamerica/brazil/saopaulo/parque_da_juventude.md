Parque da Juventude occupies the former site of the Carandiru Penitentiary Complex, once one of Brazil's most notorious prisons. Most of the prison has gone, but one building and some ruins remain among lawns, paths, and sports areas. It is more interesting for its layers of recent history than for the landscaping alone.
