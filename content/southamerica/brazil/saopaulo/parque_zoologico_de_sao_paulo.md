Parque Zoologico de Sao Paulo is Brazil's largest zoo, with a large collection of animals spread across a green site south of the city center. It is most useful for families or visitors who want a slower day away from central Sao Paulo's museums and architecture.

The scale is the main draw: the grounds are big enough that you should allow several hours, especially if visiting with children.
