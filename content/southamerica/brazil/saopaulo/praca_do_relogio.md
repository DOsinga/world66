Praça do Relógio is the clock-tower square at the University of São Paulo, a broad green space laid out with plantings that echo the ecosystems of São Paulo state. The 50-metre tower is the marker to look for: its panels represent the university's different fields of study, and the open campus setting makes it a calm break from the busier city.
