---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

**Rua Oscar Freire** in the Jardins is São Paulo's luxury strip, with the international fashion houses and the better Brazilian designers; **Alameda Lorena** and **Alameda Itu** are the surrounding side streets. **Shopping Iguatemi** (the original Brazilian shopping mall, opened 1966) sits at the south end of Faria Lima and is the high-end mall. **Shopping JK Iguatemi** in Itaim and **Cidade Jardim** further south are newer and even more upmarket.

For markets and street shopping, the **25 de Março** district downtown is the wholesale and bargain quarter — chaotic, crowded, every kind of cheap goods in the world. Saturday morning at the **Praça Benedito Calixto** in Pinheiros is the antiques and curio market, with live chorinho music in the afternoon.
