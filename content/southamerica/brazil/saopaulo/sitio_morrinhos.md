Sitio Morrinhos is a rural property dating from 1702, now surrounded by the modern city. It preserves a rare piece of early colonial Sao Paulo, when the area still had farms and small settlements rather than dense urban neighbourhoods.

The site is most useful for visitors who want to understand Sao Paulo before the coffee boom and skyscrapers, as it shows a much older layer of the city's Portuguese colonial history.
