Vale do Anhangabaú is a broad public space in central São Paulo, lined by some of the historic centre's major office buildings and civic landmarks. The valley gives good views of downtown architecture, including the towers around the old commercial core.

It is not a quiet park, but it is a useful place to read the scale and shape of central São Paulo while walking between nearby sights.
