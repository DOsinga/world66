---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

São Paulo sits on a 760-metre plateau, which keeps it cooler than the Brazilian coast. **April to October** is the best stretch: dry, sunny days in the mid-twenties, cool evenings. **November to March** is the wet season, with hot, humid afternoons that often end in spectacular thunderstorms; the city can flood and traffic can be miserable.

The **São Paulo Carnival** in February or early March is smaller and more local than Rio's, but the *blocos* (street parades) have grown over the last decade and the Anhembi Sambódromo runs serious parade nights. The **Virada Cultural** in late May is a 24-hour citywide cultural marathon with free concerts and performances on every plaza. The **São Paulo Bienal** runs from September to December in even-numbered years — the second-oldest contemporary-art biennial in the world after Venice.

Hotel rates are highest during Formula One weekend (November) and the big international art and design fairs.
