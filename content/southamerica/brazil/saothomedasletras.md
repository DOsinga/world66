São Thomé das Letras sits high in the quartzite hills of southern Minas Gerais, a small stone-built town with a reputation much larger than its size. It mixes old Mineiro religion, waterfall weekends, rough-edged backpacker energy, and a long-running taste for mysticism.

The centre is compact and odd in the best way. Start at the [Gruta São Thomé](/southamerica/brazil/saothomedasletras/gruta_sao_thome), where the town's founding legend is rooted, then walk up to the [Piramide](/southamerica/brazil/saothomedasletras/piramide) for sunset over the Serra da Mantiqueira. The [Toca do Picu e Pedra da Bruxa](/southamerica/brazil/saothomedasletras/pedra_da_bruxa) gives the town its more playful rock-and-myth character.

The waterfalls are the other reason to come. [Cachoeira Véu de Noiva](/southamerica/brazil/saothomedasletras/cachoeira_veu_de_noiva), [Cachoeira Paraíso](/southamerica/brazil/saothomedasletras/cachoeira_paraiso), and [Cachoeira da Lua](/southamerica/brazil/saothomedasletras/cachoeira_da_lua) make a good warm-weather circuit if you have your own transport or arrange a local ride. São Thomé is not polished, and that is part of the point: come for a day of stone lanes, open views, cold water, and local stories that nobody tells quite the same way twice.
