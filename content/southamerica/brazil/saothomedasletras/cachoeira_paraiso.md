Cachoeira Paraíso sits close enough to Véu de Noiva to make the two an easy pair. It is a simple waterfall stop, but the setting has the cool water and rocky Minas countryside that people come to São Thomé for.

Do not expect wilderness solitude on a sunny weekend. Come early or late, bring water, and treat the outing as a slow rural excursion rather than a checklist of named falls.
