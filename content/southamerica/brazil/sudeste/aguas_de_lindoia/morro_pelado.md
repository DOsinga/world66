Morro Pelado gives Águas de Lindóia its easiest countryside escape. The hill area has viewpoints over town and the surrounding green ridges, with rural leisure businesses offering rides, short walks, and family activities.

Come for the view rather than for wilderness. It is a polished countryside outing, but it rounds out the town's spa-and-park character nicely.
