Mini Horto is a small planted reserve rather than a major park, but it suits Águas de São Pedro's mood. It gives you shade, water, birds, and a break from the paved centre.

Pair it with the spa and fountain house rather than making a special trip. In a town this small, that is exactly the point: the good bits are close together and unhurried.
