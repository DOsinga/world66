Museu Historico Abilio Barreto tells the story of Belo Horizonte from inside the last surviving farmhouse of the old Curral d'El Rey settlement, the rural community replaced by the planned city. The contrast between the farmhouse and the modern city around it is the point.

The museum is useful early in a visit, since it gives context for Belo Horizonte's planned streets, growth, and Minas Gerais identity before you explore the city centre and Pampulha.
