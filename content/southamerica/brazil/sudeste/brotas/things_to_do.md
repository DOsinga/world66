---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Adventure sports are the reason most people come to Brotas. The Jacaré-Pepira river provides the backbone — rafting, canyoning, and kayaking on its rapids — while the surrounding cerrado hills open up for zip lines, mountain biking, and horse trekking. Most operators in town sell packages combining multiple activities, so it is easy to fill a weekend without having to plan too far ahead.
