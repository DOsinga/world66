The Metropolitan Cathedral of Our Lady of the Conception is the architectural centrepiece of downtown Campinas. Its long 19th-century construction history shows in the building's mix of ambition, local craft, and urban presence.

The interior is the reason to step inside. Rich carved woodwork softens the scale of the nave and gives the cathedral a warmth that many stone churches lack. It is an easy anchor for a walk around the old centre and nearby markets.
