Pico do Itapeva is a high viewpoint outside Campos do Jordão, looking out over the Paraíba Valley when the weather cooperates. The drive is part of the appeal, climbing through the cooler, sharper landscape around the town.

Cloud can wipe out the view, so do not make it your only plan for the day. On a clear morning it is one of the easiest ways to understand the altitude that gives Campos its climate.
