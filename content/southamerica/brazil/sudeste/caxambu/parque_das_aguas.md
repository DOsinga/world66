Parque das Águas is Caxambu's reason for being. The park gathers twelve mineral springs, each traditionally understood to have a different character, among shaded paths, old spa buildings, gardens, and water features.

It is the kind of place to take slowly. Taste the waters, watch local visitors fill bottles, and treat the park less as a theme attraction than as the ritual centre of an old spa town.
