A short, narrow strip of sand on the southeast coast that is the most common boat drop-off for Lopes Mendes. From the beach, a marked trail crosses the wooded ridge for about fifteen minutes and emerges on the much longer Lopes Mendes bay. Many people stop here on the way back to swim or have a snack at one of the two small kiosks under the trees.

Palmas itself is pretty enough — calm, clear water and a backdrop of forest — but it is short and busy at peak times. Use it as a way station.
