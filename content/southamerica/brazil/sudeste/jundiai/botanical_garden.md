Jundiaí's Botanical Garden is an easy green stop inside the city. Paths, lawns, lakes and themed plantings make it useful for a relaxed walk before heading into the old centre or out toward the rural roads.

The garden also frames the city well, with the Serra do Japi in the distance. It is not wild nature, but it gives you a gentle introduction to the landscape Jundiaí likes to claim as its own.
