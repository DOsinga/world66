Serra do Japi is the landscape that gives Jundiaí real travel weight. The forested hills west of the city preserve a rich patch of Atlantic Forest, with trails, viewpoints, streams and a much cooler feel than the urban corridor below.

Access can be controlled, and some trails need local guidance or prior arrangement, so check current rules before setting out. Even a partial visit changes how you read Jundiaí: the city is not just industry and highways, but a gateway to a serious green range.
