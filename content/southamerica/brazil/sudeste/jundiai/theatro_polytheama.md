Theatro Polytheama opened in 1911 and still carries the civic pride of that period. After decades of decline and closure, it was restored and returned to use, making it one of the better reasons to look beyond Jundiaí's newer commercial face.

Check the cultural agenda if you are staying overnight. Even without a performance, the theatre is worth including on a short walk through the centre.
