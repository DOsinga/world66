Casa Guignard is a small museum devoted to Alberto da Veiga Guignard, an important Brazilian painter who spent his later years in Ouro Preto. The collection gives a focused look at his connection with the city and its colonial landscape.

It is close to the historic centre, so it works well as a short art stop between Ouro Preto's churches and larger museums.
