---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Ouro Preto is a good place to eat *comida mineira* — the rich, conservative cuisine of inland Minas Gerais, built on pork, beans, cabbage, and corn. Look for *tutu à mineira* (mashed beans with bacon and farofa), *frango com quiabo* (chicken with okra), and *feijão tropeiro* (beans with sausage and toasted manioc flour). Most restaurants charge by the kilo at lunch, *por quilo* — load a plate at the buffet, weigh it at the counter.

Evenings are quieter than you'd expect for a town this famous. The student bars below Praça Tiradentes are the most reliable spots for a late meal; restaurants further from the square close by 22:00.
