Cachoeira de Deus is one of Penedo's easiest waterfall outings. The fall sits on the Rio das Pedras in Alto Penedo, reached by a short trail of about 300 metres, and drops into a natural pool. Go early on weekends if you want the place to feel more like forest than picnic spot.
