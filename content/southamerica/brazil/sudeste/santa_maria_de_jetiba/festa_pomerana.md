Festa Pomerana is Santa Maria de Jetibá's main cultural event, usually held in May. It brings out traditional music, dance, dress, food, floats, and rural games connected to the town's Pomeranian heritage.

The festival is the easiest time for visitors to see that heritage in public rather than just read about it. Book transport and rooms early if you plan to come during the event.
