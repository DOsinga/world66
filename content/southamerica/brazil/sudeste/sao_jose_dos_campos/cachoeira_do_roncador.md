Cachoeira do Roncador is one of the higher waterfalls around São Francisco Xavier, dropping about 45 metres on private land. Access is by rural road, and opening hours and fees can change, so check before setting out.

It is a better fit for travellers with a car and a full day in the district than for someone trying to squeeze nature into a short city stop.
