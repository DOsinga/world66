Cachoeira Pedro David is a 15-metre waterfall close to São Francisco Xavier village. It is easier to reach than many of the area's more remote falls, with basic visitor infrastructure and a straightforward approach.

For a one-day escape from São José dos Campos, this is one of the simplest ways to add rainforest and cold water to the itinerary.
