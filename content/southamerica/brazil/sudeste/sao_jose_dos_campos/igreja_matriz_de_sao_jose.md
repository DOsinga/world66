The Igreja Matriz de São José stands on the traditional founding site of São José dos Campos. The present church dates from 1934, replacing an earlier building that had collapsed in the nineteenth century.

It is not a major architectural monument, but it anchors the old centre and makes a sensible stop while walking between the Banhado and the central streets.
