The Memorial Aeroespacial Brasileiro is the clearest place to understand why São José dos Campos matters. Its collection focuses on aircraft, rockets, military technology, and Brazilian aerospace research, including the Bandeirante aircraft that helped launch Embraer.

The displays are technical, but even casual visitors get the point quickly: this city is one of the main places where Brazil learned to design and build aircraft.
