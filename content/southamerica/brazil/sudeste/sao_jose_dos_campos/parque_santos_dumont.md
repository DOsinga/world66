Parque Santos Dumont is an easy urban park with a strong aviation theme. It displays a Bandeirante prototype and a replica of Santos Dumont's 14-bis, alongside sports areas and a small Japanese garden.

It pairs naturally with the aerospace memorial. One is a proper museum; this is the relaxed city-park version.
