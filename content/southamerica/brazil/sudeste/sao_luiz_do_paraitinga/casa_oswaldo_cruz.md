Casa Oswaldo Cruz is one of the historic centre's more specific stops: a rammed-earth house associated with Oswaldo Cruz, the physician and public-health figure who became one of Brazil's best-known scientists.

It is not a large museum, but it gives the town a human anchor beyond pretty streets and churches. Check opening hours locally before planning around it.
