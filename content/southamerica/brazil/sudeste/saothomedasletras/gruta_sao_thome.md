Gruta São Thomé is the small cave beside the town's main church and the place where local legend locates the discovery that gave São Thomé das Letras its name. It is not a grand cave system; its importance is symbolic, religious, and local.

Visit it together with the church and the central square. The attraction makes most sense as the beginning of a walk through town, before climbing toward the viewpoints above the stone roofs.
