The Museu Monteiro Lobato occupies the farm house where the writer spent part of his childhood. Lobato's stories about the Sítio do Picapau Amarelo are part of Brazilian childhood for generations, and the museum leans into that mix of literature, play, and memory.

The house has family material, a children's library, green space, and activities aimed at younger visitors. It is the most distinctive stop in Taubaté and the one that best explains the city's claim as a capital of children's literature.
