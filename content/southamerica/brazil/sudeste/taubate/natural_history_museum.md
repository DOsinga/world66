The Natural History Museum of Taubaté is a compact science museum with fossils, animals, minerals, and earth-science displays. It is especially useful for families or anyone spending a rainy afternoon in the city.

The museum sits near other municipal cultural spaces, so it pairs easily with the historical archive or the nearby garden. Check current hours before crossing town; Taubaté's smaller museums can keep public-sector schedules.
