Our Lady of Rosary Church was built in 1861 by the brotherhood of Black Catholics in Taubate. Its Baroque style and social history make it a more specific stop than a routine parish church.

The church has been undergoing restoration, so access and appearance can vary, but it remains a useful piece of the city's religious and architectural history.
