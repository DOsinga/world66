Vale do Itaim Municipal Park is a large family park with lakes, trails, playgrounds, a theatre, and a recreation of the Sítio do Pica Pau Amarelo farmhouse from Monteiro Lobato's children's books. It works best as an easy outdoor stop, especially with children.
