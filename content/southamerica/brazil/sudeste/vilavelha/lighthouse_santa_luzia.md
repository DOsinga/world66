Santa Luzia Lighthouse is a compact maritime landmark on Vila Velha's coast, marking the entrance area between the open Atlantic and Vitória Bay. Its white tower and rocky setting make it a simple but worthwhile coastal stop.

The visit is short, so combine it with nearby beaches or the Convento da Penha rather than planning around it alone. Clear days give the best views back toward the water and the urban shoreline.
