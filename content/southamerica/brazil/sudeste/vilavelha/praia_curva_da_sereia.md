Praia Curva da Sereia is a small, curving beach near the rocky end of Vila Velha's coast. It is more intimate than Praia da Costa, with a sheltered feel and a rocky point called Pedra de Sereia at its southern end.

It can get crowded in good weather, but it is a pleasant stop if you are already exploring the coast around Morro do Moreno and Santa Luzia Lighthouse.
