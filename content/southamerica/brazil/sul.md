The Sul — Paraná, Santa Catarina and Rio Grande do Sul — is the Brazil that surprises people. It has four seasons, frost in winter and snow some years in the highlands, and it was settled in the nineteenth century largely by Germans, Italians, Poles and Azoreans, whose towns and food still set it apart from the rest of the country.

Its greatest sight is at its western edge: [Iguaçu Falls](/southamerica/brazil/sul/iguacufalls), reached from [Foz do Iguaçu](/southamerica/brazil/sul/fozdoiguacu), the most spectacular waterfalls on the continent. From [Curitiba](/southamerica/brazil/sul/curitiba), the train down the Serra do Mar to [Morretes](/southamerica/brazil/sul/morretes) is one of the great rail journeys in Brazil, with [Paranaguá](/southamerica/brazil/sul/paranagua) and the car-free [Ilha do Mel](/southamerica/brazil/sul/ilha_do_mel) at the bottom.

Santa Catarina has the south's best coast: the island of [Florianópolis](/southamerica/brazil/sul/florianopolis), with more than forty beaches, and to its south the surf and whale bays of [Garopaba](/southamerica/brazil/sul/garopaba) and [Praia do Rosa](/southamerica/brazil/sul/praia_do_rosa). Inland, German towns such as [Blumenau](/southamerica/brazil/sul/blumenau) and [Pomerode](/southamerica/brazil/sul/pomerode) keep half-timbered houses and an Oktoberfest, and the highland road of the [Serra do Rio do Rastro](/southamerica/brazil/sul/serra_do_rio_do_rastro) climbs to cold [Urubici](/southamerica/brazil/sul/urubici) and [São Joaquim](/southamerica/brazil/sul/sao_joaquim).

In Rio Grande do Sul, the canyons of [Aparados da Serra](/southamerica/brazil/sul/aparados_da_serra), reached from [Cambará do Sul](/southamerica/brazil/sul/cambara_do_sul), drop hundreds of metres off the plateau, and the Serra Gaúcha around [Gramado](/southamerica/brazil/sul/gramado), [Bento Gonçalves](/southamerica/brazil/sul/bento_goncalves) and the [Vale dos Vinhedos](/southamerica/brazil/sul/vale_dos_vinhedos) is Brazil's wine country. In the far west, the Jesuit mission ruins of [São Miguel das Missões](/southamerica/brazil/sul/sao_miguel_das_missoes) and the gaúcho ranching culture of the pampas continue into Uruguay and Argentina.

Summer, December to March, is the beach season; winter, June to August, is cold enough for fondue in Gramado and frost on the canyon rims.
