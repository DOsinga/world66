The Aristides Carlos Rodrigues Museum is the reason Candelária belongs on a travel map at all. Its paleontology exhibits connect the town to the wider Paleorrota, where Triassic fossils have helped researchers understand early dinosaurs and the transition toward mammals.

It is a local museum, not a blockbuster institution, but the subject is exceptional. The best way to read it is as a window into the landscape around town: those ordinary-looking red rocks have scientific weight.
