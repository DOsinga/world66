Cascata da Ferradura is a 52-metre waterfall in the rural Roncador area, reached by a short trail. The fall drops into a natural pool, and the walk in makes it feel more like an outing than a roadside photo stop.

It is best treated as a countryside excursion. Check local conditions after rain, wear shoes with grip, and do not expect the infrastructure of a heavily managed park.
