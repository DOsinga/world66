Caxias do Sul Cathedral stands on the city's central square, a Gothic-inspired church whose design looks back to northern Italy. The interior is arranged around ten altars, with the main altar and side chapels giving the building more depth than its busy downtown setting suggests.

It is an easy stop while walking the centre, especially combined with Dante Alighieri Square and the nearby municipal museum.
