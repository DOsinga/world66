Bosque de Portugal is one of Curitiba's immigrant and cultural memory parks, this time dedicated to Portuguese language and heritage. It is smaller and quieter than the city's headline parks, but its tree cover and literary panels give it a distinct identity.

The park is most interesting as part of Curitiba's wider pattern of neighbourhood green spaces, each tied to a different community or theme. It suits a short stop for travellers exploring the northern districts or comparing the city's many themed parks beyond the Botanical Garden and Bosque Alemao.
