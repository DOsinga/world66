Curitiba’s Natural History Museum sits in Capão da Imbuia, away from the city’s best-known parks but closely tied to its environmental identity. The exhibits focus on regional fauna, ecosystems, and the natural history of Paraná, giving context to a city that often presents itself through green planning.

The museum is modest, but the forested setting helps. It is best for travelers with a specific interest in nature or families looking for a quieter educational stop. Pair it with Curitiba’s larger parks if you want to see how the city’s environmental story moves between display, planning, and actual habitat.
