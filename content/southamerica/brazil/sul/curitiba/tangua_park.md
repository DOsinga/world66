Tangua Park is one of Curitiba's most photogenic green spaces, built around former quarry terrain on the city's north side. Lakes, cliffs, gardens, a tunnel, and a high viewpoint give it more drama than an ordinary urban park.

It is especially good late in the day, when locals come for the view and the old quarry walls catch softer light. Curitiba is famous for planned parks, and Tangua shows why: it turns damaged industrial ground into a public landscape that feels both designed and genuinely loved.
