---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Florianópolis rewards exploration in every direction. The island's coastline ranges from sheltered, calm bays in the north — ideal for swimming and families — to powerful Atlantic surf breaks on the east coast that draw serious surfers from across South America. Inland, the Lagoa da Conceição is the social heart of the island: a large lagoon ringed by hills, popular for watersports and with a lively bar and restaurant scene. The historic colonial villages on the west coast, especially Santo António de Lisboa, offer a quieter, more contemplative Floripa — fishing boats, oyster restaurants, and 18th-century Portuguese architecture.
