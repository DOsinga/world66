Sebuí is a private reserve reached by boat from Guaraqueçaba, set among forested rivers and mangroves. Visits usually focus on short trails, waterfalls, canoeing or kayaking, and the slow shift from bay water into dense Atlantic Forest.

It is more effort than a town stroll, and that is exactly why it belongs here. Guaraqueçaba's best days are the ones that leave the road behind.
