---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Itapema sits on the **BR-101**, the main coastal highway running the length of southern Brazil, and is straightforward to reach by road. From **Florianópolis** the drive is about an hour to the south; from **Curitiba** five hours to the north. Long-distance buses on the BR-101 stop at the Itapema rodoviária, with frequent services to Balneário Camboriú, Florianópolis, Joinville, Curitiba, and São Paulo.

The nearest airport is **Navegantes (NVT)**, around 30 km north, with daily flights from São Paulo, Rio, Brasília, and Porto Alegre. **Florianópolis (FLN)** offers more routes and is roughly 75 km south. Taxis and rideshares run from both. There is no train.
