---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Itapema is a beach town first and everything else after. Most days fold into a pattern of beach, lunch, beach, sunset, dinner, and bar — and you can do worse than that in January with the South Atlantic at the door. The town stretches along **Meia Praia** for several kilometres and adds a couple of smaller coves at the southern end where the fishing colony at **Canto da Praia** still works the boats at dawn.

Above the beach, the **Morro do Cabeço** and **Morro da Sentinela** both reward a short, steep walk with a view over the whole bay. The historic centre is small but worth a wander for the Azorean stone church and the few surviving colonial houses. For a more demanding outing, the trails into the Serra do Mar behind the town climb to waterfalls and forest viewpoints — best in cooler months when the mosquitoes and humidity are tolerable.
