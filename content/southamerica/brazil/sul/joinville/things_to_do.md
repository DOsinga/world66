title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Joinville is built around its German colonial heritage and its modern reputation as Brazil's capital of dance. The two together make a half-decent itinerary — a morning at the immigration museum, an afternoon ride out into the Atlantic Forest along the old colonial road, and an evening at a Bolshoi ballet performance. Add the Babitonga Bay boat trips and a day in São Francisco do Sul for a proper long weekend.
