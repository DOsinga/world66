The Fragments of Time Museum adds another layer to Mata's fossil circuit. It is best understood as a companion stop: smaller and more local in feel, but useful for seeing how the town has turned scattered remnants of deep time into a public story.

Do not expect a polished metropolitan museum. The appeal is the specificity of the place, and the way several small collections together make Mata more than just a dot on the road.
