The Museu de Ciencias e Tecnologia da PUCRS is Porto Alegre's big hands-on science museum, set on the Pontifical Catholic University campus. It is strongest for curious children and adults who like experiments, models, and displays that invite interaction rather than quiet glass-case viewing.

It sits outside the historic centre, so it takes a little intention to visit. That effort makes sense for families, rainy days, or anyone who wants to see a different side of Porto Alegre from markets, churches, and Guaiba sunsets.
