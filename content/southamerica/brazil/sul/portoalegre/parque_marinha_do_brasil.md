Parque Marinha do Brasil is a long green strip near the Guaiba waterfront, less famous than Redencao but useful when you want open space without leaving the city. Locals come for jogging, skateboarding, informal sports, and weekend walks.

The park also has a scattered collection of modern sculptures by Mercosur artists, which gives it a little more character than a routine recreation ground. Late afternoon is the best time to come, when the heat drops and the waterfront starts to fill.
