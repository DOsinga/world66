Parque Maurício Sirotsky Sobrinho, better known locally as Parque Harmonia, spreads along the Guaíba side of Porto Alegre with open lawns, event spaces, and a strong gaucho-culture identity. It is especially lively when the city gathers for fairs and September traditions.

On ordinary days it works as a breathing space near the centre and waterfront. Pair it with a walk toward the Guaíba sunset, when Porto Alegre's river edge is at its most persuasive.
