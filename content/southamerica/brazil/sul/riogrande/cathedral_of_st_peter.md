The Cathedral of St Peter is one of the old-centre buildings that keeps Rio Grande from feeling like just another port. The church dates from the colonial period and still sits close to the civic core, where the city's early political and commercial life gathered.

Come for the setting as much as the interior. A short walk around the nearby streets gives a compact view of the city's older public buildings before the port and beach pull you outward.
