Rota Germânica do Rio Pardinho follows the road between Santa Cruz do Sul and Sinimbu through a German-settled farming district. The route is best explored by car, with rural churches, local shops, and traces of the area's immigrant history along the way.

Treat it as a slow countryside drive rather than a single sight. It works well for visitors who want to see the region beyond the city center.
