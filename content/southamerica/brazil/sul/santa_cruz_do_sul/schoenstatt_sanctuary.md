The Schoenstatt Sanctuary in Santa Cruz do Sul is a small Marian shrine run by the Schoenstatt Sisters of Mary. Opened in 1977, it follows the design of the movement's original shrine in Germany and is a quiet stop for visitors interested in the city's religious life.

The site is modest, but its setting and devotional use make it more than a routine church visit.
