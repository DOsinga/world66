The Museum of Image and Sound is a small Taubaté museum focused on local film, photography, and music history. Its collection includes props, historical city images, and musical objects connected with old local cinemas.

It is a modest stop rather than a major museum, but it adds useful local colour if you are already visiting Taubaté's cluster of small museums.
