Santa Clara Convent is a hilltop religious site founded in 1673. The original building was destroyed by fire in the 19th century and rebuilt in Baroque style in 1891.

It is a compact historic stop in Taubaté, best suited to visitors interested in old churches, colonial-era religious architecture, and the city's older layers.
