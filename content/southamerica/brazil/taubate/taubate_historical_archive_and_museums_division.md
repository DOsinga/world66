The Taubaté Historical Archive and Museums Division brings several small collections together in one place: the city historical museum, a historical library and archive, an art gallery, and a building with old cars.

It is most useful if you want local context before visiting Taubaté's better-known museums. The mix is eclectic, but it gives a compact look at the city's civic memory and everyday history.
