Vale do Itaim Municipal Park is Taubaté's big family park, with lakes, trails, playgrounds, theatre space, and a recreated Sítio do Picapau Amarelo farmhouse tied to Monteiro Lobato's children's books. It is a good open-air stop if you want something greener and less formal than the city's museums.
