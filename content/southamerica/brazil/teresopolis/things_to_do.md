---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Teresópolis's main draw is the [Parque Nacional da Serra dos Órgãos](/southamerica/brazil/teresopolis/parque_nacional_serra_dos_orgaos), whose entrance is at the edge of town. The park's rock spires — particularly the [Dedo de Deus](/southamerica/brazil/teresopolis/dedo_de_deus) — are visible from most of the town and give the place its distinctive silhouette. Hiking ranges from easy Atlantic Forest walks to serious multi-day routes, including the classic [Travessia Teresópolis–Petrópolis](/southamerica/brazil/teresopolis/travessia_petropolis).

Away from the park, the town has a pleasant [Praça Olímpica](/southamerica/brazil/teresopolis/praca_olimpica) area for eating and wandering, and the surrounding countryside is dotted with craft breweries and farm stays (pousadas) that have proliferated in recent years.
