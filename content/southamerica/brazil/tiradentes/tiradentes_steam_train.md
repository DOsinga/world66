The maria-fumaca steam train links Tiradentes with nearby Sao Joao del Rei on a preserved narrow-gauge line. It is a short ride, but the old locomotive, mountain-edge views, and slow approach into town make it more than transport.

Check running days before planning around it. Service is usually aimed at visitors, especially on weekends and holidays.
