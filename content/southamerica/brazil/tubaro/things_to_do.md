title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tubarão's main attractions reflect the city's two defining characteristics: its railway heritage and its artistic tradition.

The [Museu Ferroviário](/southamerica/brazil/tubaro/museu_ferroviario) (Railway Museum) is the starting point for the famous [Maria Fumaça](/southamerica/brazil/tubaro/maria_fumaca) steam train excursions — one of the last operational steam railways in southern Brazil, running through coastal towns and farmland on weekend departures. The [Museu Willy Zumblick](/southamerica/brazil/tubaro/museu_willy_zumblick) houses the most comprehensive collection of works by the city's most celebrated painter, whose canvases document 70 years of Tubarão life and history.

The [Catedral de Nossa Senhora da Piedade](/southamerica/brazil/tubaro/catedral_nossa_senhora_da_piedade) is the architectural centrepiece of the city centre, with stained glass and a neo-Gothic tower that gives the main square its focal point. Nearby, the Tubarão River provides pleasant walking along its banks.

For day trips, the thermal springs at Gravatal (30 km) and the beaches of Laguna (45 km) are both within easy reach. The Serra do Rio do Rastro mountain road to the northeast is one of the most dramatic drives in southern Brazil.
