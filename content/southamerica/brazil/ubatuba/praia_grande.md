Praia Grande is Ubatuba's busiest urban beach, a long strip of sand with strong waves that attract surfers. Its easy access and steady line of kiosks make it popular with younger crowds and families who want a lively beach day close to town.

Come outside peak holiday periods if you want the sand and surf with a little more space.
