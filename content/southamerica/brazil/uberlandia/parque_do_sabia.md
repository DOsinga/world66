Parque do Sabiá is Uberlândia's big breathing space, a large urban park with lakes, woods, sports facilities, a running track, and the city zoo. It is busiest on weekends, when families use it less like a formal attraction and more like a second living room.

For a visitor, that is part of the point. The park shows the city at leisure and gives you a more relaxed view of Uberlândia than the traffic-heavy avenues in the centre.
