Praia Barra do Jucu sits south of Vila Velha's more central beaches, where the city starts to feel looser and more village-like. The area is known for surf, fishing traditions, and a beach culture that is less polished than Praia da Costa.

It is a good choice if you want a wider look at Vila Velha's coast rather than staying on the main urban strip. Conditions can be rough, so treat it as a place for walking, watching surfers, and eating nearby as much as for swimming.
