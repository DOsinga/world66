The Salar is best explored as a chain of stops rather than as one viewpoint. Flamingo reserves, salt lagoons, oasis villages, and high-altitude lakes all show a different side of the same desert water system.

Two of the stops below — the hidden lagoons at Baltinache and the orchard gorge behind Toconao — come from the hundred-item inventory kept by Vulcano Expediciones, a San Pedro guiding outfit we list in [6 Blogs for San Pedro and the Atacama](/southamerica/chile/sanpedrodeatacama/blogs_for_san_pedro_and_the_atacama).
