---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tours leave San Pedro de Atacama most afternoons and usually finish after sunset. Biking is possible for fit travellers with enough water, but the combination of heat, traffic, sand, and return darkness is not for everyone.

If you self-drive, check current entry rules and closing times before setting out. Access arrangements in the Atacama can change, and the protected area is more regulated than it once was.
