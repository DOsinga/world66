---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Viña del Mar's beaches are the reason most Chileans come here, and they range from the lively and social to the quietly pleasant. The water is cold year-round — the Humboldt Current doesn't warm up for anyone — but that doesn't stop crowds from packing the sand in December and January. Go in the shoulder months (November or March) and you'll have the beach in far better company.

Reñaca, just north of the city, is the most famous stretch and gets extremely crowded in high summer. The main city beach is more central and easier to reach on foot. Playa de los Artistas, south of the centre, is calmer and draws a slightly older crowd looking for somewhere to read rather than a party.
