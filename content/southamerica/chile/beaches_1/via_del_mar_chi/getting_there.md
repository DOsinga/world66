---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Most visitors arrive from [Santiago](/southamerica/chile/santiago), about 120 km to the east. Turbus and Pullman Bus run frequent, comfortable services from the Alameda terminal — the journey takes around ninety minutes to two hours depending on traffic, and buses run throughout the day and into the evening. You can also take the commuter train (Merval) from Limache, which connects to Santiago's Metro system at Estación Alameda, though the combined journey is slower than the direct bus.

The nearest airport is Arturo Merino Benítez International (SCL) in Santiago. There is no airport in Viña del Mar itself; from SCL, the easiest option is a direct bus or a transfer by taxi or private car. [Valparaíso](/southamerica/chile/valparaiso) is immediately adjacent — the two cities blend into each other — and frequent local buses and shared taxis (colectivos) run between them constantly.
