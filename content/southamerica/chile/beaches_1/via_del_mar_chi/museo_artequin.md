Museo Artequin is a small, approachable art museum on the edge of Quinta Vergara. Its collection is built around reproductions rather than priceless originals, which sounds modest but makes the place unusually relaxed: the emphasis is on learning how to look at art, not shuffling past guarded canvases.

It is especially useful for families or anyone travelling with children in Viña del Mar. Workshops and guided activities change through the year, and the museum pairs naturally with a walk through the surrounding park or a visit to the nearby amphitheatre and palace grounds.
