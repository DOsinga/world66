Quinta Vergara is Viña del Mar's finest park: a large botanical garden set on a hillside, dense with palms, native Chilean trees, and ornamental plantings accumulated over more than a century. At the centre stands the Venetian-style Palacio Vergara, a pale neoclassical building that now houses the Municipal Museum of Fine Arts. You can spend a couple of hours here without hurrying, working up through the terraced gardens and looking out over the city.

The amphitheatre at the upper end of the park is the venue for the Festival de la Canción de Viña del Mar, held every February — the largest popular music event in South America, with a tradition of crowning new stars and bringing in international names. Even outside festival season, the tiered seating and hillside setting make it worth walking up to see.
