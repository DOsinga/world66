title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Viña del Mar rewards exploration beyond the beach. The city's parks are exceptional — Quinta Vergara alone is worth a morning — and the cluster of Belle Époque castles and the iconic flower clock give the place a genteel, slightly nostalgic character you won't find in workaday Chilean cities. The casino has been pulling in visitors since 1930, the horse racing at the Sporting Club runs most weekends, and the Fonck Museum is the best introduction to Easter Island culture on the mainland.
