---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The best time to visit Viña del Mar is December through March, when the Pacific coast has warm, dry weather and long evenings. January and February are the peak months — beaches fill up, restaurants are busy, and prices rise accordingly. This is also when the Festival de la Canción takes place (usually the second week of February), which packs the city and requires booking accommodation well in advance.

For a more relaxed visit, November and March offer similar weather with fewer crowds. Viña is mild even in winter (June–August) — temperatures rarely drop below 10°C — but the beach is deserted and many seasonal businesses close. It's a pleasant time to walk the parks and visit the museums without company.
