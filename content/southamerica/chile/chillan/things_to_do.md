---

Chillán has more to it than most travellers expect from a transit city. The [Feria de Chillán](/southamerica/chile/chillan/feria_de_chillan) is one of the best outdoor markets in central Chile and reason enough to stop. The [Escuela México](/southamerica/chile/chillan/escuela_mexico) contains murals by David Alfaro Siqueiros and Xavier Guerrero — a genuinely surprising piece of Mexican muralism deep in the Chilean countryside. The [Museo Claudio Arrau León](/southamerica/chile/chillan/museo_claudio_arrau) commemorates the great Chilean pianist born here in 1903.

The main reason visitors come to the Chillán area is the [Termas de Chillán](/southamerica/chile/chillan/termas_de_chillan), 80 kilometres east in the Andes. This resort complex combines volcanic hot pools, one of Chile's best ski areas (operating June–September), and spectacular mountain scenery. [Volcán Chillán](/southamerica/chile/chillan/volcan_chillan), an active volcanic complex near the resort, offers dramatic hiking in summer. The [Cathedral of Chillán](/southamerica/chile/chillan/cathedral_of_chillan), a striking modernist structure built after the 1939 earthquake, is worth a look in the city centre.
