The obvious first call on the island, and the one operator here with an external credential worth quoting: their own site leads with being named **Best Adventure Tourism Company 2025 by FEDETUR**, the Chilean tourism federation.

What they actually sell is the whole archipelago rather than one product: **Parque Tantauco** in the far south, the **penguin colony at Puñihuil**, **Dalcahue and Isla Quinchao** for the wooden churches, **Isla Lemuy**, the east-coast villages, sea kayaking, birding, navigation into the fjords, horseback riding, Chilote cooking with curanto, and — a genuinely odd one that is on their menu — **knife forging**. They also run multi-day programmes and something they label scientific tourism.

The founder, Cyril Christensen, is named in their own testimonials as the guide; the site is bilingual and the English is native. Note the **US and Canada toll-free number** — this is an operator set up to take North American bookings, which is rare on the island.

One number for phone and WhatsApp, advertised in prose in the footer, matching four live SERNATUR registrations.
