Fuerte San Antonio is one of Ancud's old Spanish defensive sites, looking out over the bay from a grassy bluff. Cannons, low walls, and sea views tell the story without needing much theatrical reconstruction.

Chiloé was one of the last royalist strongholds in Chile, and the forts around Ancud make that history visible. Come for context as much as for the view.
