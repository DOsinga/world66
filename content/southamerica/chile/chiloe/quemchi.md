Quemchi is a quiet northern Chilote town associated with writer Francisco Coloane, whose sea stories helped define Patagonia in Chilean literature. It sits among channels, small islands, and fishing boats rather than big-ticket sights.

Stop here if you like literary traces and small ports. The town is modest, but the surrounding water and island views have the right melancholy.
