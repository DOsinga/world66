The Church of Nercón is one of the finest of Chiloé's UNESCO World Heritage churches, located about 6 km south of Castro in a small village. The church dates from the late 18th century in its current form and is built in the characteristic Chiloé style: a timber frame, shingled exterior, a single-nave interior with a painted wooden barrel-vaulted ceiling, and a tower at the west end.

The interior is remarkable. The ceiling is painted in geometric patterns in deep reds and blues, typical of the Chiloé Jesuits who employed local craftsmen to adapt European decorative traditions using native materials. The church serves a small farming parish and is kept immaculate. The surrounding village and farmland are as quiet and green as anywhere in Chiloé. The church is part of the standard circuit of UNESCO churches and most guided tours include it; independent visitors simply ring the bell to request entry.
