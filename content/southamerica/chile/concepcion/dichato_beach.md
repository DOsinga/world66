Dichato is the most popular beach escape from Concepción, a long curve of dark sand on a sheltered bay about 60 km north of the city. The town behind it was almost entirely destroyed by the tsunami that followed the 2010 earthquake, and has been rebuilt as a low-rise resort that fills with Chileans every January and February.

The water is cold but the bay is calm and good for swimming. There are seafood restaurants in old wooden houses along the shore, and small marisquerías on the beach itself. From Concepción, take a bus from the Collao terminal — the trip takes about 90 minutes.
