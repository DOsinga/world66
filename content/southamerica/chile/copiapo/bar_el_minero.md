Bar El Minero is a no-frills watering hole in the city centre that has been serving Copiapó's mining workforce for years. The beer is cold, the pisco sours are strong, and nobody pays much attention to décor. On Friday and Saturday nights it gets genuinely lively, with a mix of miners, university students, and workers from the surrounding camps. Not the place for a quiet drink; very much the place for understanding the social texture of a copper-mining city.
