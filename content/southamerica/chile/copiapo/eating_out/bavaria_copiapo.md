Bavaria is a long-established Chilean restaurant chain with a branch in central Copiapó, and it has earned its place in the city. The menu covers the basics of Chilean cooking well: cazuela de vacuno, pastel de choclo, porotos granados, and grilled meats. The portions are large and the prices fair. It is not a destination restaurant, but it is exactly what you want when you come in from a long drive across the desert and need a solid meal. Reliable for lunch and dinner.
