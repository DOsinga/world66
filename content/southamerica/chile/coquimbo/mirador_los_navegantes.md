Mirador Los Navegantes is one of Coquimbo's small coastal viewpoints, looking over the port, the bay, and the dry hills behind the city. It is a simple stop, but the view helps orient you in a city stretched between harbour, beaches, and the great Cross of the Third Millennium.

Come for a quick look rather than a long visit. It works well as part of a circuit through Barrio Inglés, the waterfront, and the hilltop viewpoints that show how closely Coquimbo's identity is tied to the sea.
