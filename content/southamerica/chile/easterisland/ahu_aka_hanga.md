Ahu Aka Hanga is an unrestored ceremonial platform on Rapa Nui's south coast, with fallen moai, stonework, and a rougher feeling than the island's reconstructed showpieces. That rawness is the reason to stop: it shows the archaeological landscape before everything is tidied for photographs.

Local tradition sometimes links the area with the burial place of Hotu Matu'a, the island's founding ancestor, though stories vary. Walk carefully and stay off the stonework; the power of the place is in seeing the platform, statues, and coast as one fragile historical setting.
