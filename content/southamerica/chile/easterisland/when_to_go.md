---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The island has a subtropical climate — warm year-round, with average highs between 22°C and 28°C and lows that rarely fall below 15°C. Rain is possible in any month and the weather changes fast in any season. The driest period runs roughly from September to December.

The peak tourist months are January and February, which coincide with the Tapati Rapa Nui festival in the first two weeks of February — a fortnight of traditional dance, singing, body-painting contests, horse races, and a banana-trunk sled race down the side of a volcano. It is the best time to be on the island but accommodation books out a year ahead. The shoulder seasons of March-April and October-November are quieter and cheaper.
