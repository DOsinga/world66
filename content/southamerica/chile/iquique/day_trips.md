---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The best day trip from Iquique is to the UNESCO-listed nitrate ghost towns of Humberstone and Santa Laura, an hour inland — these are covered as a POI in things to do. Beyond that, the desert north and east of Iquique hides a surprising amount: the geoglyphs of Cerro Pintados (a few hours by road, hundreds of ancient line-figures etched into the desert hillside), the oasis village of Pica with its sweet citrus farms and a thermal swimming pool, and the colonial church at Mamiña hot springs.

Tour agencies along the Cavancha seafront combine several of these into half- or full-day excursions. For more remote desert sites, a 4WD rental is the better option.
