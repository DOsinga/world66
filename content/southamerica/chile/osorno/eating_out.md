---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Osorno's food reflects its agricultural surroundings: good beef, fresh dairy, and the German-Chilean tradition of kuchen and smoked meats. The Mercado Municipal is the best place for an affordable lunch; the downtown streets around the Plaza de Armas have a reasonable selection of restaurants and parrillas. Don't expect sophistication — this is a practical regional city with practical regional food.
