Agustín Ross Park is the most visible remnant of Pichilemu's old resort era. Its palms, lawns, terraces, and former casino were part of Agustín Ross Edwards' plan to turn the town into a refined Pacific retreat.

The former casino now works as a cultural centre, while the park gives an easy walk above the main beach. It is not grand in a European-spa-town way, but it gives Pichilemu a layer of history beyond surfboards and summer rentals.
