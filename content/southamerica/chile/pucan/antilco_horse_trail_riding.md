A riding centre on the far side of the Trancura from Pucón, at Quelhue, running since 1997. The short rides are the ordinary business — two hours at CLP 45,000, a full day at CLP 160,000 — but what Antilco is actually for is the long ones. Four days on the Río Blanco trail, six through glaciers and hot springs, and a nine- to eleven-day crossing of the Andes into Argentina, camping or moving cabin to cabin, finishing at the crater of the Sollipulli volcano.

They publish a full price list in pesos, euros and dollars, valid to May 2027, which almost nobody in Chilean adventure tourism does. The Andes crossing is CLP 4,680,000 a head for nine days. That is a serious holiday and priced like one.

They also run Spanish lessons alongside the riding, and farmstays. There is no WhatsApp: one mobile, one email address, and a contact form.
