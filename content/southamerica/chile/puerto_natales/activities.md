Puerto Natales is the staging post for Torres del Paine, and almost everything sold here is a version of the same decision: the W in four or five days, the full O circuit in eight, or a day trip in and out.

The park's refugios and campsites are booked separately from any guide, and in season they sell out months ahead — which is the single most important thing to know before planning anything else.
