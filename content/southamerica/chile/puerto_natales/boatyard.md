South of the Navimag pier, wooden fishing boats are hauled ashore for painting, repair, or slow decay. It is a working corner rather than a curated attraction, which is exactly the point.

The boatyard is best folded into a waterfront walk. Bring a camera, keep out of anyone's way, and watch how quickly the light changes on the hulls and the sound.
