If you walk the W from east to west you sleep in this company's buildings. Las Torres Patagonia, which traded as Fantástico Sur until recently, holds the concession on the Estancia Cerro Paine side of Torres del Paine: the refugios at Central, Chileno and Los Cuernos, the campsites, and the hotel at the trailhead. The booking office is on Bulnes in Puerto Natales, which is where you go when your plan has fallen apart.

The all-inclusive W is five days and four nights and starts at CLP 1,080,000 a head, sharing a six- or eight-bed room, with the bus from Puerto Natales, all meals and a guide. That is a lot of money, and it is also roughly what the park costs if you are not carrying a tent and a stove. They also sell the O circuit, day tours, horse riding and a fully independent booking where you take the beds and nothing else.

Book early. Torres del Paine beds sell out months ahead for January and February, and the park's rules require you to hold a confirmed reservation for every night before CONAF will let you on the circuit at all.

WhatsApp is their stated message channel; the footer says plainly that the number does not take calls.
