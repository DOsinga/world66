---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**El Tepual Airport (PMC)**, sixteen kilometres west of the centre, is the second-busiest airport in Chile after Santiago. Direct flights from Santiago run almost hourly with LATAM, Sky, and JetSmart and take just under two hours.

**Long-distance buses** run from Santiago (about 12 hours), Bariloche in Argentina (about 6 hours via the Cardenal Samoré pass), and every town in southern Chile. The bus terminal is on the Costanera near Angelmó.

**Ferries** leave Puerto Montt heading south to Chaitén, Puerto Chacabuco, and Puerto Natales — Navimag's 3-day sailing through the Patagonian fjords to Puerto Natales is one of the great cruises of the southern hemisphere.
