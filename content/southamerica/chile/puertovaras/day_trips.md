Puerto Varas is the best base in Chile's Lake District. Apart from the park excursions described above, several day trips are easy. The German-heritage village of **Frutillar**, half an hour north on the lake, has a fine open-air museum of nineteenth-century settler buildings and one of the best concert halls in South America — the Teatro del Lago, built on stilts over the water. **Puerto Montt**, half an hour south, is the regional capital and the main embarkation point for ferries to Chiloé and Patagonia. The fishing village of **Angelmó** on its outskirts has the best seafood market in the country.

The island of **Chiloé**, with its wooden churches and curanto, can be done as a long day trip but is better with at least one overnight. **Cochamó valley**, two hours southeast, is the Yosemite of Chilean Patagonia — granite walls, waterfalls, and serious trekking — and is the obvious overnight from Puerto Varas in good weather.
