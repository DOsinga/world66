---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air**: the closest airport is Tepual (PMC), 25 kilometres south near Puerto Montt. Daily flights connect to Santiago in a little under two hours; less frequent flights to Punta Arenas. From the airport, taxis and minibuses to Puerto Varas cost around US$25.

**By bus**: long-distance buses from Santiago take 12 to 14 hours overnight; from Puerto Montt, frequent local buses take half an hour. The bus terminal is on the lakefront, a short walk from the centre.

**By car**: Ruta 5 (the Pan-American Highway) runs straight through the region. Puerto Varas is signposted just before Puerto Montt coming from the north.

**By boat**: the Cruce Andino lake-crossing route arrives by ferry from Bariloche in Argentina, an iconic day's journey via three lakes and three short bus rides.
