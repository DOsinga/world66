The classic city viewpoint, a short and steep walk up from the Plaza along Calle Fagnano. From the small terrace by the cross you get the whole geography of Punta Arenas in a single sweep — the corrugated-iron roofs in blue, green and red stretching down to the harbour; the long grey line of the **Strait of Magellan**; and, on a clear day, the silhouette of **Tierra del Fuego** across the water.

It's most rewarding in the late afternoon, when the light is low and the wind sometimes drops. Lovers come up here at sunset, and there are usually a few stray dogs and a couple of school kids playing football. Bring a hat: even in summer this is a windy spot.
