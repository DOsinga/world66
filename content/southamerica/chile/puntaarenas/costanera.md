The **Costanera** is the seafront promenade running along Avenida Costanera del Estrecho on the eastern edge of the city. It is not pretty in any conventional sense — concrete, wind-bent flagpoles, a few faded murals — but the view across the **Strait of Magellan** is the whole reason to come to Punta Arenas. Cargo ships, fishing boats, Antarctic research vessels and the occasional cruise liner pass at all hours.

On a clear day you can see across to **Tierra del Fuego**, the long flat island that Magellan named after the campfires of the Selk'nam he glimpsed from his ship. Walk a kilometre south and you reach the wreck of the **Lord Lonsdale**; walk north along the harbour and you pass the fish market and the ferries to Porvenir.
