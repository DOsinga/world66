A friendly pub-style bar on Calle Errázuriz with wood panelling, a long counter, and a small kitchen that turns out decent burgers and pizzas. **Jekus** is where the slightly younger Punta Arenas crowd gathers on weekends — students, sailors on leave, occasional Antarctic researchers.

The beer list runs to a handful of Patagonian craft brews alongside the usual mainstream lagers. Live music some Saturdays; otherwise the soundtrack is reasonably loud rock.
