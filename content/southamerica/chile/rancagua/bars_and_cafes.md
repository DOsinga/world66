---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Café culture in Rancagua centres on the plaza. **Café Mokka** and **Café Carmelitas** serve respectable coffee, sandwiches, and pastries through the morning. For evening drinks, **Estadio Español** is the long-established choice for a pisco sour or a Pacific seafood empanada with a beer — a Spanish-Chilean social club open to the public for dining. **Mister Beer** on Cuevas is the closest the city has to a craft-beer pub, with a rotating tap list and a young crowd. Beyond that, weekend nightlife is small — most younger residents drive to Santiago when they want a real night out.
