---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Rancagua is a good base for some of central Chile's better day trips. **Sewell** and the **El Teniente** mine to the east are the headline excursions, both accessed only on licensed tours. The **Cachapoal Valley** wineries lie immediately south, with cellar doors open at Casa Silva, Anakena, and Viu Manent (technically just over the line in Colchagua). For nature, the **Reserva Nacional Río de los Cipreses** preserves a stretch of cypress-clad Andean canyon south of town, with hiking trails into condor country.
