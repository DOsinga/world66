---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Rancagua's restaurant scene is functional rather than exciting — central Chilean food, served fast and cheap. The best meals tend to be either at the **Mercado Central** lunch stalls or in the wine country just outside town.

In the centre, **Bavaria** on the plaza is a long-running steak and German-Chilean restaurant — solid roast meats, good pisco sours, generous portions. **El Sayonara** is an unlikely but established Japanese-Chilean hybrid serving sushi alongside Chilean classics. For breakfast or a quick lunch, **Café Mokka** and **Café Carmelitas** are the two reliable cafés around the plaza.

For something more memorable, drive 30 minutes into the Cachapoal Valley vineyards. **Casa Silva** at the entrance to the valley has one of the best restaurant terraces in the central valley, overlooking horses grazing in the polo paddock.
