The Estadio El Teniente, also known as the Estadio Codelco El Teniente, is Rancagua's main football stadium and the home of **O'Higgins FC**. It was rebuilt for the 2015 Copa América, when it hosted group games, and now holds about 14,000 spectators in an entirely roofed bowl.

O'Higgins is one of the better-supported provincial clubs in Chile — they won the Chilean Primera in 2013–14 — and home games against Colo-Colo or Universidad de Chile are loud, partisan affairs. Match days are the obvious time to come, but the stadium is also worth a look from the outside for the gleaming new architecture in a city that otherwise reads as colonial-and-functional.
