---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Rancagua's main draws are its compact colonial core around the Plaza de los Héroes and the day trips it offers into copper-mining and wine country. The 1814 battle that made the city famous is commemorated in the central plaza, and the surrounding streets retain a handful of colonial-era houses and churches. Most visitors come for what lies outside town: **Sewell**, the abandoned mining camp clinging to the Andes; **El Teniente**, the world's largest underground copper mine; and the Cachapoal Valley vineyards. Save half a day for the city itself and use the rest as a base.
