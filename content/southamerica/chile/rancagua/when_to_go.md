---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The central valley has a Mediterranean climate — dry, hot summers and cool, wet winters. The best months are **October to December** and **March to April**, when the days are warm without being punishing and the vineyards either flower or harvest. Summer (January and February) is hot and dusty; locals flee to the coast.

If you are interested in **chilean rodeo**, the National Championship is in late March or early April — book accommodation well in advance. **Grape harvest** in the Cachapoal Valley is from late February to April, when vineyards welcome visitors for tastings and lunches.

Winter (June to August) brings snow at altitude — sometimes enough to close the road to Sewell — but the valley itself is grey and damp rather than properly cold.
