Wood-fired pizza in the middle of Caracoles, Uruguayan-run, and the most reliable cheap dinner in town. The oven is genuine and the bases come out properly blistered; the toppings run from plain margherita to llama and rocket, which is more successful than it sounds.

It also functions as the kitchen for [Chela Cabur](/southamerica/chile/sanpedrodeatacama/chela_cabur) next door, which serves beer and no food — order a pizza there and it arrives from here. That arrangement has been running for years and is a good illustration of how small this town actually is.

Expect to wait at peak times; there are only so many pizzas an oven this size produces in an hour. Takeaway is quicker.
