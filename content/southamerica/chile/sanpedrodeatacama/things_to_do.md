The main business of San Pedro de Atacama is exploring the desert. Most sights require transport — tour operators in town run excursions to all the major sites, or you can rent a bicycle for the closer ones. Altitude awareness is important: many sites are above 4,000 metres.

The list below goes well beyond the standard circuit, because the standard circuit is only a fraction of what is out here. Several of these pages exist because Vulcano Expediciones, a local mountain-guide outfit, sat down and counted a hundred things to do in and around San Pedro — the ignimbrite columns on the Tara road, the cactus canyon at Guatín, the petroglyph fields north of town. Their inventory is one of the six sites in [6 Blogs for San Pedro and the Atacama](/southamerica/chile/sanpedrodeatacama/blogs_for_san_pedro_and_the_atacama).
