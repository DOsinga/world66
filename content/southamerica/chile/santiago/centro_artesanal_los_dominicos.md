Centro Artesanal Los Dominicos is a craft village in Las Condes, laid out with narrow lanes, adobe-style workshops, and thatched roofs to evoke a traditional Chilean settlement. Artisans sell ceramics, textiles, jewellery, leatherwork, woodwork, and other crafts directly from small studios.

It is a better stop than a standard souvenir market because many stalls are working spaces rather than simple retail counters. The complex also hosts cultural events and is protected as a national monument, preserving a pocket of older Las Condes beside the modern eastern suburbs.
