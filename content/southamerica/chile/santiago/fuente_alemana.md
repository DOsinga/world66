Fuente Alemana is one of the oldest snack bars in Santiago, operating on the Alameda since the 1950s. It is a quick-service counter restaurant focused on one thing: the *Completo* — the Chilean hot dog. The Chilean version is loaded with avocado, tomato, sauerkraut, and mayonnaise, and Fuente Alemana's are considered among the best in the city. The *Italiano* (avocado, tomato, mayonnaise — the colours of the Italian flag) is the classic order.

It is not a sophisticated dining experience, but it is an entirely authentic one: office workers, students, and construction workers all eat here together. Very cheap and very fast. A good introduction to Chile's street food culture without having to find a street cart.
