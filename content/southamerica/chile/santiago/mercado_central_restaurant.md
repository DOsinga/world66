Donde Augusto is consistently recommended as the best of the seafood restaurants inside the Mercado Central's main hall. It is not a fine-dining establishment — the decor is market tables and white tablecloths, the service is quick and efficient — but the seafood is as fresh as it gets in Santiago and the cooks know what they are doing with it. Order the *paila marina* (a mixed seafood broth of extraordinary richness), the *congrio frito*, or whatever the fishmongers are recommending that day.

The key is to sit in the central hall at Donde Augusto rather than at the tourist-facing restaurants around the perimeter, which serve average food at inflated prices to people who don't know the difference. Lunch only; go before 1 p.m. on weekdays to get a table without a long wait.
