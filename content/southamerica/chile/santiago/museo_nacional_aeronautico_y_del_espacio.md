Museo Nacional Aeronautico y del Espacio is Santiago's aviation and space museum. Its collection focuses on Chilean aircraft and flight history, with planes, technical displays, and exhibits that work well for families or anyone interested in aviation.

It sits away from the central museum cluster, so it is best visited as a planned stop on the west or south side of the city.
