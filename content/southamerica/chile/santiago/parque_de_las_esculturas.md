Parque de las Esculturas is an open-air sculpture park in Providencia, set beside the Mapocho River. It mixes modern Chilean sculpture with lawns, paths, and views toward Santiago's high-rise financial district.

It is not a large museum-style visit, but it is a pleasant stop while walking through Providencia or crossing between the riverfront, Costanera Center, and nearby neighbourhood streets.
