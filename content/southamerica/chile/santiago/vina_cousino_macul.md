Vina Cousino Macul is one of the classic winery visits within easy reach of central Santiago, with vineyards pressed up against the city's eastern edge and the Andes behind them. The estate has enough history and working-winery detail to feel more substantial than a simple tasting room.

Tours usually focus on the cellars, old equipment, and the Cousino family's role in Chilean wine. It is a good choice if you want a wine outing without spending the whole day driving into the Maipo Valley.
