---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Santiago's climate is Mediterranean — warm, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. The best time to visit for clear Andes views is winter (June–August): cold fronts regularly clear the smog that settles in the basin in spring and autumn, and the mountains appear in sharp relief against the blue sky. It is also ski season at the nearby resorts.

Summer (December–February) is hot and popular for outdoor activities, though smog and heat can both be uncomfortable in January. Spring (September–November) is pleasant for walking the city, with the jacarandas flowering purple in November. The wine harvest (vendimia) runs March–April in the Maipo and Casablanca valleys.

Santiago's wettest months are June and July, but "wet" in Santiago means a few days of rain rather than tropical downpours — the city averages about 300 mm of rain per year, almost all falling in winter.
