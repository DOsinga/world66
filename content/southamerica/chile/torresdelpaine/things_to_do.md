---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Torres del Paine is a hiking destination above all else — the trails, the geology, and the wildlife are the reason people come. The W Trek and the full Circuit are the two main routes, but day hikes to the towers viewpoint or to the Grey Glacier are excellent options for those without time for the multi-day routes. Guanacos and condors are visible without any special effort almost anywhere in the park.
