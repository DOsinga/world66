German-Chilean kuchen — a range of open-faced fruit tarts, cream cakes, and streusel pastries — is one of the most distinctive culinary contributions of the German immigrant culture in southern Chile, and Valdivia is the best city in the country to eat it. Café Hausmann, one of the old-established German-style cafes in the city centre, is the most reliable place to try it alongside a proper coffee.

The menu at these German-style cafes runs to both sweet and savoury: kuchen with local berries (murta, calafate, and the imported strawberries that grow well in the Los Ríos climate), sandwiches, and hearty stews for a more substantial meal. The afternoon tea (onces) tradition, brought from Germany and now thoroughly Chilean, means these cafes fill between 4 and 7pm as much as at lunchtime. The apple, cherry, and raspberry kuchens are all excellent.
