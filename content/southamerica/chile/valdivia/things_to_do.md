---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Valdivia's main attractions are arranged around its rivers and its German heritage. The waterfront market is the social heart of the city, the museums on Teja Island give depth to the region's history, and the Kunstmann brewery south of town is the best place to understand the German immigrant contribution to Chilean culture. The colonial forts at Niebla and Corral, guarding the river mouth, are a half-day boat trip away.
