Three hours under the Elqui sky for CLP 22,000, leaving the office on San Martín at 19:45. The observing site is seven kilometres out of Vicuña — dark enough, and near enough that the minibus is only CLP 3,000 more. You get a big motorised telescope, a guide who works in Spanish, English or French, a pisco sour, and a photograph of yourself under the Milky Way. Minimum four people, and it runs all year, weather allowing.

The other half of the business is bicycles. They hire them out, and they run downhill descents from Pisco Elqui and from Alcohuaz, which is the pleasant way to see the upper valley: a van takes you and the bike up, and you roll back down past the pisco distilleries.

Book through the website form or the WhatsApp button. One warning for anyone following links: their `/contacto/` page is a 404 that looks like a real page. Use `/contactanos/`.
