A small outfit on Arturo Prat in Vicuña that does two quite different things well. One is the Titiritour, a walk around the Plaza de Armas told through Gabriela Mistral's eyes with puppets and live storytelling — genuinely aimed at families and school groups rather than a gimmick bolted on to a city tour, and CLP 5,000 to 15,000 depending on numbers.

The other is hill walking. Easy routes up Cerro El Molle and Cerro Portillo, to the Gualliguaica waterfall and along the Elqui, from CLP 10,000 a head in a group of three or more. Harder ones — Mamalluca, Cerro Peralillo, La Ventana, the hot springs at Las Hediondas — from CLP 25,000, and they ask for previous experience. Guides are registered and carry radios, a satellite communicator and a group first-aid kit, which they list out in full on the website.

They publish no phone number and no email at all. WhatsApp is the only way in.
