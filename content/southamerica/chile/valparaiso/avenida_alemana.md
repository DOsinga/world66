Avenida Alemana is one of Valparaiso's best long walks, curling along the hills above the port with repeated views over the city, harbour, and Pacific coast. It links a run of cerros, stairways, and lookout points, so the pleasure is in wandering rather than aiming for one monument.

Start with daylight to spare and expect steep side streets if you drop down toward the centre. The route is a good way to understand why Valparaiso is built as much vertically as horizontally.
