Ecomapu have been working Valparaíso for more than twenty years and organise the city by theme rather than by neighbourhood: the funiculars, the street art, the La Matriz quarter, a cooking tour, a tipsy tour, and a heritage walk that starts before the Spanish. They also run a free tour.

The pitch is community tourism — routes built with the people who live on the hills, with the fee meant to stay in the barrio. That is a claim a lot of operators make; what is checkable is that they belong to AVALPO and CHILESERTUR and print their SERNATUR registration number, 3099, on the page, which not many do.

The website is in Spanish, English and French. Booking is by WhatsApp or the form. **Their site has a broken WhatsApp link with a Brazilian country code on it** — use the number in the text.
