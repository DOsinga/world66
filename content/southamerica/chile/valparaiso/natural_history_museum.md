The Natural History Museum of Valparaiso covers Chile's landscapes, geology, and biodiversity, with exhibits linking the Pacific coast, Juan Fernandez, Valparaiso, and the Andes. It is a useful indoor stop when you want context for the region beyond the port city's street art and funiculars.

The displays are strongest on natural history and geography. Allow about an hour unless a temporary exhibition catches your attention.
