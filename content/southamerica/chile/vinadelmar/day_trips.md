The obvious day trip from Viña is its neighbour [Valparaíso](/southamerica/chile/valparaiso), twenty minutes south by metro. The two cities are so close that most people stay in one and visit the other on a half-day. Valpo's tangle of painted hills, ascensores, and bohemian quarters makes the perfect counterpoint to Viña's manicured order.

Further afield, the **Casablanca Valley** wineries are an easy half-day inland — most cellars are signposted off Route 68 and several offer tastings without an appointment. The fishing village of **Horcón** and the long beach at **Maitencillo**, both about an hour's drive north, are popular weekend escapes for people in Viña.
