Viña del Mar trades on its parks, its beaches, and a string of early twentieth-century mansions left from the city's heyday as a summer retreat for Santiago's wealthy. The historic core is small enough to walk: from the **Reloj de Flores** at the south end of the seafront, along the coast past the **Castillo Wulff** and **Sausalito**, up through the **Quinta Vergara** gardens, and back through the centre.

For a half-day trip, combine the museums of the Quinta Vergara with the beaches and a meal at a seafront restaurant. The cliffside palaces above the bay are best seen on foot from the coast road.
