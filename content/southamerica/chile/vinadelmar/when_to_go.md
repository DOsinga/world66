---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Viña's high season runs from December to early March, when Santiago empties out to the coast and the city is at its liveliest. Summer days are warm rather than hot — typically 22–25°C / 72–77°F — and the Pacific stays cold year round thanks to the Humboldt Current. Out of season, autumn (April and early May) is pleasant and much quieter; winter is wet, grey, and best avoided unless you have a specific reason to come.

The single fixed point in the calendar is the **Festival Internacional de la Canción** in the third week of February. The town packs out, hotel prices climb, and every café has the live broadcast on. If you want crowds and atmosphere, this is the week; if you want a calm seaside holiday, time your visit for before or after.
