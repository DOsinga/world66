Cocora Valley is one of Colombia's signature landscapes, a high Andean valley near Salento where wax palms rise improbably above green pastures and cloud-forest slopes. Even though it is outside Armenia itself, the city is a common gateway for travellers exploring Quindio and the Coffee Cultural Landscape.

The valley is worth a full outing for walking, photography, and a sense of the region's scale beyond coffee towns. Weather changes quickly, so bring layers and start early if you plan to hike rather than only visit the viewpoints.
