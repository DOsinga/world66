A small village about an hour south of Armenia, tucked into a coffee-growing valley below the Cordillera. Pijao earned international notice in 2014 when it became the first Latin American town to join the Cittaslow movement — a global network of small towns committed to slower, more local rhythms of life. Whether or not that designation means much in practice, the village has stayed beautifully unrushed.

A pastel main street climbs to a small church, a couple of cafés serve carefully made local coffee, and the surrounding hills are lined with coffee farms that take visitors. It works as a day trip but rewards an overnight, especially if you want to experience a coffee village without Salento's noise.
