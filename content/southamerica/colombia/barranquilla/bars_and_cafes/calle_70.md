Barranquilla's main nightlife strip, running through the upscale Zona Norte district. On weekend evenings the pavement fills with people moving between bars, clubs, and outdoor tables, with cumbia and vallenato drifting from open doorways. The street has enough variety to suit most tastes — lively dive bars alongside more polished cocktail spots — and it stays busy until at least 2am on Fridays and Saturdays. Most venues don't charge cover except during special events.
