A reliable mid-range restaurant in the Zona Norte known for its seafood. The ceviche and fried fish platters are the things to order, along with coconut rice and fried plantain on the side. Open-air patio seating makes it bearable in the heat, and it's busy at lunch on weekdays — a sign that the kitchen is doing something right. Carrera 53 area, Zona Norte.
