---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Barranquilla's highlights range from world-class cultural institutions to the raw spectacle of carnival season. The Parque Cultural del Caribe anchors the cultural heart of the city, while the Malecón del Río offers a breezy escape to the riverfront. Outside February's carnival frenzy, the city moves at a relaxed pace that rewards wandering.
