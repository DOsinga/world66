Barranquilla’s zoo is a leafy, family-oriented stop that gives the city a wildlife attraction beyond carnival and the riverfront. The collection focuses strongly on Colombian and tropical species, so visitors can see animals tied to the Caribbean coast and the wider country without leaving the city.

It is a practical choice if you are travelling with children or have a spare half-day in the north of Barranquilla. Serious wildlife travellers should still prioritise national parks and reserves, but the zoo is accessible, shaded, and locally popular, which makes it a useful break from traffic and heat.
