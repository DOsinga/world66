Bogotá's tour scene grew up around two things that are genuinely worth doing: the graffiti in La Candelaria, which is a real civic argument rendered on walls rather than a decorative attraction, and the food.

Several of the operators below are also the booking office for trips much further out — Zipaquirá's salt cathedral, the Chicaque cloud forest, and further afield still.
