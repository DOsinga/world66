A free-walking-tour company that has done the unusual thing of registering properly in every city it works in: Bogotá, Cartagena, Medellín and Cali all hold their own active RNT under one tax number, and they run Santa Marta and the Cocora valley too.

Beyond the free walks there is a long private-tour catalogue, and it is more thought-through than most: downtown Cali with a *caleña* salsa lesson, Cartagena's walls plus the Palace of the Inquisition, Medellín's centre with Comuna 13, Cocora with a coffee farm and lunch.

**Because they operate in five of our locations, this should be one POI on Bogotá, named from the others' `activities.md` with a `providers:` note** — not five copies.
