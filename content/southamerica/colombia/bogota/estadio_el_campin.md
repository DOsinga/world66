Estadio Nemesio Camacho El Campín, to give it its full name, holds around 45,000 and is home to Bogota's two main football clubs — Millonarios and Independiente Santa Fe. On a match day the atmosphere is electric; the derby between the two clubs is one of the loudest events in the city. The stadium was built in 1938 and significantly expanded over the decades.

Tickets are available at the stadium box office and through official channels. Millonarios wear blue and white; Santa Fe wear red and white. Either game against provincial rivals is worth attending if the fixture falls during your visit. Located at Calle 57 near Carrera 30 — easily reached by TransMilenio.
