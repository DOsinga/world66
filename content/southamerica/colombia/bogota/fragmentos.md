Fragmentos is an art space and memorial connected to Colombia's peace process. After FARC laid down its arms, metal from the weapons was melted and hammered into sheets used for the flooring of the site.

The space shows videos and installations focused on victims of the Colombian conflict and on peace work in other countries. It is a sober, worthwhile stop in La Candelaria, close to the political and colonial heart of Bogotá.
