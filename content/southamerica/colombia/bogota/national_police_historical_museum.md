The National Police Historical Museum covers the history of Colombia's police forces, with its most visited rooms focused on the pursuit of Pablo Escobar. Guided tours are commonly offered and give more context than the displays alone.

The museum sits in La Candelaria, so it is easy to combine with the historic centre's churches, plazas, and government buildings.
