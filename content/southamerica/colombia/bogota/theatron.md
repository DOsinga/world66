Set in an old cinema, Theatron is the biggest gay nightclub in Latin America — more than a dozen rooms across several floors, each with its own music, and an open-bar ticket that keeps the place roaring until dawn at weekends. The anchor of Chapinero's nightlife and a Bogotá institution.
