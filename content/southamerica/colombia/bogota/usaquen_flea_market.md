Every Sunday, the streets around the colonial square in Usaquén fill with stalls selling artisan crafts, antiques, vintage clothing, handmade jewellery, and street food. It is one of the best markets in Bogota and draws a mix of locals and visitors who browse for a few hours before settling into one of the nearby restaurants for lunch.

The quality of crafts is notably better than the tourist-trap stalls in La Candelaria — look for handmade leatherwork, regional textiles, and ceramics from potters who actually made what they're selling. Live music plays most Sundays. The market runs roughly 9am–7pm. Get there before noon for the best selection.
