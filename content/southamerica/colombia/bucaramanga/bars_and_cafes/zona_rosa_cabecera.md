The cluster of bars and restaurants on Carrera 33 and surrounding streets in the Cabecera del Llano neighbourhood is the city's main nightlife corridor. Bars range from sports-focused venues with enormous screens to smaller craft-beer spots and rooftop bars with views over the city lights. Thursday is the big student night; Friday and Saturday draw a broader crowd. Most venues don't charge cover unless there's a live act. The area is easy to navigate on foot and the streets are well-lit and busy enough to feel safe late into the evening.
