Cali's main covered market, overflowing with tropical fruit, fresh fish from the Pacific coast, dried herbs and every cut of meat imaginable. Come here for breakfast: the comedores inside serve cholado, empanadas, changua (milk and egg soup) and the strongest tintos in the city. The fish stalls are the best source of fresh pargo and bocachico to take back to a hostel kitchen.
