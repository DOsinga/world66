One of the most respected traditional restaurants in Cali, operating in Granada for decades. Specialises in the dishes of the Pacific coast and the Cauca Valley — fresh river fish with patacones, chontaduro (peach palm) salads, and a sancocho that takes three hours to make. The dining room is open-air and leafy; book ahead on weekend evenings.
