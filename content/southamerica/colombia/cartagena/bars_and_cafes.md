---
title: Bars and Cafes in Cartagena
type: section
---

Cartagena has a strong drinking culture spread across several distinct zones. [Café del Mar](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/cafe_del_mar) on the city walls is overpriced but unbeatable for sunset views over the Caribbean. [Café Havana](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/cafe_havana) in Getsemaní is the city's best live salsa venue. The [Plaza de la Trinidad](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/plaza_de_trinidad) in Getsemaní has a spontaneous, local bar scene that puts the tourist-facing old city to shame. For a quiet coffee and bookshop atmosphere, [Ábaco Libros y Café](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/abaco_libros) is the place.
