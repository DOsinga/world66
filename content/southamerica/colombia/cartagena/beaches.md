---
title: Beaches in Cartagena
type: section
---

Cartagena's beaches range from the convenient but busy [Playa de Bocagrande](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/playa_bocagrande) within the city, to the local-feeling [Playa Marbella](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/playa_marbella) just north of the walls, to the excellent [Playa Blanca](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/playa_blanca) on Isla Barú, which is the best beach within easy reach. The [Islas del Rosario](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/islas_del_rosario) archipelago offers snorkelling and smaller, quieter beaches on coral islands 45km from the city.

[La Boquilla](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/la_boquilla), a fishing village north of the city, has a calmer beach than Bocagrande and good fish restaurants. The water around Bocagrande can be murky; for cleaner swimming go further from the city. Boat trips to the islands depart every morning from the Muelle Turistico near the old city.
