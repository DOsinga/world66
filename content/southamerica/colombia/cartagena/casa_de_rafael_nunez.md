Casa de Rafael Nunez is a quieter Cartagena stop, set in a wooden Caribbean house outside the busiest walled-city circuit. It remembers Rafael Nunez, the Cartagena-born politician who helped shape Colombia's 1886 constitution and wrote the words that became the national anthem.

The house is worth seeing for its domestic scale as much as for the biography. After the fortresses, plazas, and convents of Cartagena, its verandas and rooms give a more intimate view of elite 19th-century life on the coast.
