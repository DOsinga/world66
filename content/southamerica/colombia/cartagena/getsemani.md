The neighbourhood immediately outside the walled city that once housed dockworkers and enslaved Africans, now home to Cartagena's most vibrant street art and nightlife. The murals here are not decorative — they engage with the neighbourhood's history of resistance and its ongoing gentrification. Bars and restaurants are cheaper and less staged than inside the walls. La Trinidad square on weekend evenings fills with locals and cumbia music. Walk here in the late afternoon, stay for dinner, and don't leave before midnight.
