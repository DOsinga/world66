Monumento a la India Catalina stands just outside Cartagena's old walled core, honouring the Indigenous woman who became an interpreter in the Spanish conquest of the region. The statue is also the symbol of Cartagena's film festival awards, so its image appears widely in the city.

The monument is a short stop, but the story behind it is complicated and worth considering before entering the postcard version of Cartagena. It works well with a walk through nearby plazas, Getsemaní, and the old-city gates.
