Plaza de la Aduana is one of the grand civic spaces inside Cartagena's old walls, a triangular square framed by arcaded colonial buildings. It was tied to customs and administration in the Spanish period, and still feels more formal than the smaller, looser plazas nearby.

You will almost certainly pass through it while walking between the Clock Tower, San Pedro Claver, and the waterfront walls. Pause long enough to look at the scale of the buildings; this was Cartagena showing its official face.
