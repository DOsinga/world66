---
title: Shopping in Cartagena
type: section
---

[Las Bóvedas](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/lasbovedas), the vaulted chambers built into the city walls, houses a long arcade of craft shops selling hammocks, emeralds, mochilas (woven bags), and Colombian souvenirs. Prices are tourist-level but the setting is worth seeing. [Compendium](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/compendium) on Plaza Bolívar has a better-curated selection of artesanías. [Ábaco Libros y Café](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/abaco_libros) is the place for Colombian and Latin American books.

For emeralds, the [Museo de la Esmeralda](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/museo_de_la_esmera) has a small museum explaining what you're buying before you enter the adjacent showroom. The [Mercado de Bazurto](/southamerica/colombia/cartagena/mercado_de_bazurto) is where locals shop and is not aimed at tourists — but it's the most authentic market experience in the city.
