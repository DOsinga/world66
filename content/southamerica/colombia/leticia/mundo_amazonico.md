Mundo Amazonico is a guided ethno-botanical centre on the road out of Leticia. Tours focus on Amazon plants, medicinal uses, ecology, recycling, and local culture, making it one of the safest and easiest introductions to the forest around town.

It is especially good if you do not have time for a multi-day jungle trip or are travelling with children.
