The Yahuarcaca lakes sit close enough to Leticia for a short outing but feel much softer and greener than the town. Kayak trips and small boat tours look for birds, aquatic plants, and river life in the flooded landscape.

Conditions change with water levels, so ask locally before setting your expectations too firmly.
