Manizales sits in the middle of Colombia's eje cafetero and the surrounding country is full of easy day trips.

**Los Nevados National Park** (44 km south) is the headline trip — volcanic páramo and the snow-capped Nevado del Ruiz. Allow a full day with a tour operator.

**Salento and the Cocora Valley** (about 2.5 hours south) — the picture-book Andean town and the famous valley of giant wax palms. Easily a long day from Manizales, but better as an overnight.

**Pereira** (1.5 hours south) — the larger sister city, with a modern centre and the Matecaña Zoo.

**Chinchiná and the coffee farms** — the heart of working coffee country, with multiple haciendas accepting visitors.
