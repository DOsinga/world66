A public square in Medellín's civic centre, facing the Museum of Antioquia, with 23 bronze sculptures by Fernando Botero — the city's most famous son, known for his voluptuously exaggerated figures. The sculptures are free to walk among and touch; several have been worn shiny by years of hands. The adjacent Museum of Antioquia holds Botero's personal art collection, including paintings he donated to the city. The square is one of the safest and most relaxed spots in central Medellín, busy at all hours with families and vendors.
