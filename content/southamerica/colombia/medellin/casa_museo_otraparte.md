The home of Fernando González (1895–1964), the eccentric paisa philosopher and writer who lived in this house in Envigado for over forty years. The garden and ground floor have been preserved as he left them, with his books, manuscripts, and reading chair in place. Free admission; lectures and small concerts on weekends. A pleasant, contemplative stop and one of the few literary museums in the country.
