The 80-metre hill in the south of central Medellin, topped by the [Pueblito Paisa](/southamerica/colombia/medellin/pueblito_paisa) replica village and an open-air sculpture park. Reach the summit by a winding road, by a stepped footpath up the southwest face, or by car park if you must. The view runs north up the entire Aburrá Valley and is at its best at sunset — pale concrete towers, the Metro line snaking south, the lights coming on across the hillside barrios.
