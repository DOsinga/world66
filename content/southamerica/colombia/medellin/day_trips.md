---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The valley around Medellin holds a string of small Antioquian towns that work well as day trips. **Guatapé**, two hours east, has a polychrome painted village and the climb up El Peñol — 740 steps to the top of a granite plug with one of South America's great views. **Santa Fe de Antioquia**, an hour and a half northwest, is the old colonial capital, drowsy and white-walled, with the famous **Puente de Occidente** suspension bridge nearby. **Jardín**, three hours south, is a coffee-country mountain town built around a hand-painted central plaza. **Jericó**, on the same southern route, is smaller and quieter. **Carmen de Viboral** is the ceramics village, an hour east. **San Antonio de Pereira** is the dessert town, halfway to Guatapé.

For nature without the drive: **Parque Arvi** at the top of the Metrocable, **Cerro El Volador** within city limits, and the cloud-forest reserves on the western ridge.
