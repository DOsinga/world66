The city's headline annual festival, ten days at the start of August. Concerts, fireworks, classic-car processions, a horse parade through downtown, and the climactic **Desfile de Silleteros** on the closing day — farmers from the hill town of Santa Elena carrying elaborate wooden flower arrangements (silletas), some weighing seventy kilograms, the length of the parade route. If you can plan your trip around any single Medellin event, this is the one.
