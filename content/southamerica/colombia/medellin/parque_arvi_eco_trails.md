Several well-marked walking trails within the [Parque Arví](/southamerica/colombia/medellin/parque_arvi) reserve, ranging from forty-five minutes to most of a day. Two are notable: the **Camino Prehispánico**, which follows the line of a pre-Columbian stone path through the woods, and the **Sendero de los Cipreses**, a longer loop through cloud-forest oak. Guides at the entrance — a paid local guide for the longer routes is worth it for both safety and interpretation.
