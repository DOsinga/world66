A small triangular park at the heart of El Poblado, ringed on every side by bars, restaurants, hostels, and clubs that pour onto the surrounding streets every night of the week. The scene is loud, international, and unapologetic; not subtle, but the engine of Medellin nightlife for most travellers. Locals will tell you to go to Provenza for somewhere a little less raucous, and they are right. Still: at midnight on a Friday, this is where the city congregates.
