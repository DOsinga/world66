The city planetarium, set in a low circular concrete building on the [Parque de los Deseos](/southamerica/colombia/medellin/parque_de_los_deseos). Permanent exhibits on astronomy and the solar system, daily shows in the 100-seat dome, telescopic public observation nights on clear evenings. Combine with a free outdoor film in the plaza outside on a Tuesday evening.
