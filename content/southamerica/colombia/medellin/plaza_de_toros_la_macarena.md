A 1945 Spanish-style bullring south of downtown with a striking polychrome tile facade in the Mudejar revival style. Bullfighting is largely defunct in Colombia and the ring is now used mostly for concerts and other events. The building itself, especially the brick-and-tile exterior, is one of the more interesting twentieth-century landmarks in this part of the city.
