The few blocks immediately north of Parque Lleras, a tight cluster of cafes, cocktail bars, third-wave coffee roasters, and small designer shops. Quieter than Parque Lleras and considerably more polished — Pergamino for coffee, Salón Málaga for cocktails, La Causa for ceviche. The streets are unevenly paved and lined with mature flowering trees. By night it stays out late.
