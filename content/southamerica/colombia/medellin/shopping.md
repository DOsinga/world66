---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

Medellin was the heart of Colombia's textile industry through the twentieth century and still produces a steady flow of clothing and leather at prices well below European or US equivalents. The main shopping ground for visitors is **El Centro** — Pasaje Junín and the streets around Botero Plaza are wall-to-wall with cheap clothing stores, jewellery stalls, and leather workshops where you can have a belt or jacket made in a day.

For the air-conditioned mall version, **Centro Comercial El Tesoro** (in the hills above Poblado) and **Santa Fe** (in Itagüí) are the big anchors, both with the usual Colombian and international brands. The **Mercado de San Alejo** craft market on Parque Bolívar happens the first Saturday of each month — local makers, art, handicrafts. For food: **Plaza Minorista** José María Villa is the city's main fresh market, a covered hall of fruit, fish, meat, and ground-floor lunch stalls.
