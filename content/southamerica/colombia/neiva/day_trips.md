The landscapes and archaeological sites surrounding Neiva are the main reasons to be in this part of Colombia. The Tatacoa Desert to the north and the thermal springs at Rivera are easy half-day excursions; San Agustín, with its UNESCO-listed pre-Columbian statues, justifies a longer stay and is best treated as an overnight trip.
