Huila Afrika Salvaje is an odd theme park outside Neiva, mixing African motifs, Christian imagery, animals, a lagoon, and outdoor activities. Its most memorable feature is a huge crucifixion statue laid out across the ground.

It is not subtle, but it is distinctive. Families may get the most out of it, especially if they want an easy outing beyond the city centre.
