Jardín Botánico de Neiva Bertha Hernández de Ospina is a wetland-style botanical garden with lagoons, boardwalks, birds, aquatic plants, and plenty of insects. It is a quiet nature stop for travellers who want a slower break from the city.

The setting is swampy, so bring repellent and shoes that can handle damp paths. Photographers and birdwatchers will get the most out of it.
