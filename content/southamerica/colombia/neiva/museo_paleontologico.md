Museo Paleontologico displays fossils and other finds from the Colombian Andes and the Tatacoa Desert area. Its small collection is divided between reptiles, mammals, and local environments, with giant sloth remains among the highlights.

It works best as background for travelers using Neiva as a base for Tatacoa.
