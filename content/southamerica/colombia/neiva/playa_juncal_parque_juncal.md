Playa Juncal & Parque Juncal is a family recreation park outside Neiva, with pools, water-park facilities, ziplines, a ropes course, climbing wall, animal exhibits, and dinosaur figures.

It is not a cultural sight, but it is a practical day out for families looking for structured activities and a break from the heat around the Magdalena valley.
