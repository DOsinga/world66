Palmira is a mid-sized city in the Valle del Cauca, just 25 km south of [Cali](/southamerica/colombia/cali) and easily reached by frequent buses. Known as the agricultural capital of Colombia, it sits in the heart of sugar cane country — the flat, fertile valley floor stretches out to the Andes on both sides.

The city is pleasant and walkable, with a lively central plaza and the Parque del Azúcar reflecting its sugar heritage. Most travellers pass through on the way to or from Cali, but the [Jardín Botánico de Cali](/southamerica/colombia/palmira/botanical_garden) — which is actually in Palmira despite its name — is worth a stop, with an excellent collection of tropical plants and orchids. The [Hacienda Piedechinche](/southamerica/colombia/palmira/hacienda_piedechinche) outside town is a well-preserved colonial sugar estate that tells the story of the industry that built the entire valley.
