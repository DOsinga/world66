The Cathedral of Our Lady of the Rosary is Palmira's central landmark, the kind of church that helps orient both the map and the mood of a Colombian city. Its brick towers and broad facade face the main square, where daily life carries on around benches, vendors, traffic, and shade.

The cathedral is best visited as part of a walk through the centre rather than as a stand-alone sight. Step inside if it is open, then linger outside long enough to see how the plaza works: church, municipal life, errands, and conversation all sharing the same public room.
