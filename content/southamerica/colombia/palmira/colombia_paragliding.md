Long one of the best-known paragliding schools in Colombia, and worth flagging that it is no longer where most write-ups say it is: the address and the tourism registration are both now at km 31 of the old Palmira road, in El Cerrito, above the Cauca valley.

Tandem flights and a full pilot course, which is what they are really set up for — people come and stay a fortnight to get licensed.

They publish one mobile and two email addresses and no WhatsApp line. Write or call.
