---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Palmira is served by the Alfonso Bonilla Aragón International Airport, located between Palmira and Cali roughly 10 km from Palmira's centre. Despite its "International" designation, most flights here are domestic — the airport is Cali's main airport and handles connections to Bogotá, Medellín, Cartagena, and other Colombian cities. International connections to Miami and Panama City also operate from here.

From the airport to Palmira centre, taxis are the practical option. Frequent buses connect Palmira to Cali (about 45 minutes, leaving from the Terminal de Transportes in each city). The journey costs very little and buses run throughout the day and into the evening.
