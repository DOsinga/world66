type: section
---

Nariño cuisine reflects the cold Andean climate: hearty soups, stewed meats, and dishes built around potatoes, corn, and beans. Cuy (guinea pig), introduced to the cooking from neighbouring Ecuador, is on many menus. Lighter Pastuso staples include lapingachos, hornado, and frito pastuso (chunks of pork belly with potato and chocolate).
