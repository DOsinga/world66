---

The most lavishly decorated of Pasto's colonial churches, built by the Jesuits in the early 20th century but housing a remarkable collection of older religious art rescued from the city's earlier churches. The interior is a riot of gilded woodwork, carved saints, and painted ceilings.

Look out especially for the painted retable behind the main altar and the small side chapels with their bleeding Christs in glass cases — characteristic of the Pasto religious aesthetic.
