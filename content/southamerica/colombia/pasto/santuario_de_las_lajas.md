A neo-Gothic basilica built across the Guáitara canyon, 7 km from the Ecuadorian border at Ipiales and about 90 km south of Pasto. The structure spans the gorge on a stone bridge, with the church itself rising from the cliff on the far side. The current building was completed in 1949, replacing an 18th-century pilgrim chapel.

Among the most photographed religious sites in Colombia and an essential day trip from Pasto. Take warm clothing — the canyon is windy and the altitude is over 2,600 m.
