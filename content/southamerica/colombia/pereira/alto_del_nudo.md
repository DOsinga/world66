Alto del Nudo is an eco-park on the hills above Pereira. The reward is a broad 360-degree view over the city and the coffee-growing valleys around it.

It is best on a clear day, when the ridges east, north, and south of Pereira are visible. Treat it as a short nature-and-viewpoint trip rather than a major hike.
