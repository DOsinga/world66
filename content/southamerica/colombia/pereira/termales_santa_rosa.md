A complex of natural hot-spring pools below a tall waterfall, about an hour east of Pereira on the road into the Cordillera Central. The water emerges from the ground at over 70°C and is cooled into a series of pools at different temperatures, ranging from a comfortable 38°C up to lobster-pot levels.

There are two main resorts: Termales Santa Rosa (the original, larger and more touristy) and the smaller, more atmospheric Termales San Vicente, deeper in the valley. Both are popular weekend trips for Pereirans, so come midweek if you want quiet. Many Pereira hotels organise day trips that include transport and a swimsuit-friendly buffet lunch.
