---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The most rewarding day trip is to **Silvia**, a small market town 60 km northeast of Popayán where the Guambiano (Misak) indigenous community gathers on Tuesdays. The market is one of the most visually striking in Colombia — the Guambiano still wear their traditional blue-and-fuchsia ponchos and bowler hats — and is a working market for the community, not a tourist set-piece.

Further south, **Coconuco** has hot springs in a high-altitude landscape that is genuinely Andean, and the slopes of the **Puracé volcano** offer good day hikes for fit walkers. Reach all three by colectivo from Popayán's bus terminal.

The road east into Tierradentro and the famous underground tombs is technically a day trip but is better tackled with at least one overnight, given how long it takes to get in and out of the valleys.
