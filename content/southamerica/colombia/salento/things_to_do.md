---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Salento's main draw is the Valle de Cocora hike — one of the most iconic walks in Colombia. The town itself rewards a half day of wandering, coffee tasting, and exploring the craft shops. Coffee farm tours are abundant in the surrounding area, and the viewpoint at the end of Calle Real is worth the short climb. Trout fishing in the streams is popular with locals.
