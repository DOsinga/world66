The Fonce River is San Gil's standard first rafting run. It is close to town, easy to arrange, and lively enough to explain why San Gil became an adventure base without demanding the commitment of the rougher Suarez River.

Tour companies tend to offer similar half-day trips. Ask about guide certification, safety kayaks, water levels, and what happens if the river is running too high; the best operators answer those questions clearly.
