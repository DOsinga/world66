---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

San Agustín sits at around 1,800 metres elevation in a zone of frequent rainfall. There are two main dry seasons: December to February, and June to August. These are the best times to visit — the archaeological sites can be muddy and difficult in heavy rain, and the horseback excursions to the more remote groups are considerably less enjoyable when the tracks turn to mud.

That said, the highland landscape is lush precisely because of the rainfall, and even in the wet seasons there are usually several dry hours each day. The town is quiet year-round; there is no real peak tourist season by Colombian standards, and you are unlikely to find the sites crowded at any time of year.
