title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Providencia's main draw is its reef — one of the most extensive barrier reefs in the Americas — and the snorkelling and diving are genuinely exceptional. [Crab Cay](/southamerica/colombia/sanandresandprovidencia/providencia/crab_cay) is the most popular snorkelling spot: a small rocky islet just offshore surrounded by shallow coral gardens teeming with fish. The [Old McBean Lagoon](/southamerica/colombia/sanandresandprovidencia/providencia/old_mcbean_lagoon) national park area protects the best stretches of reef.

On land, the hike up to [El Pico](/southamerica/colombia/sanandresandprovidencia/providencia/el_pico) (360 metres) is the most rewarding thing to do — a steep but well-marked trail through tropical forest that delivers panoramic views over the sea of seven colours. [Fort Warwick](/southamerica/colombia/sanandresandprovidencia/providencia/fort_warwick) on the hill above Santa Isabel is a small 18th-century English fortification with good views and a slice of the island's complicated colonial history.

The island's beaches are beautiful but not the primary attraction — the water is where Providencia shines.
