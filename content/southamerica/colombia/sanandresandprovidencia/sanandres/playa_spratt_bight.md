The main town beach stretches along the northern shore near the hotels and duty-free strip. The water is calm and brilliantly clear, and the beach fills with Colombian tourists in high season. It is the most convenient beach on the island, with sunbed rental, snack stalls, and easy access to boat trips to Johnny Cay and El Acuario just offshore.
