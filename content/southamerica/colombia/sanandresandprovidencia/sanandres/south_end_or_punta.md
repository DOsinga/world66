A cluster of small open-air seafood restaurants at the southern tip of the island, right by the blowhole. The speciality is Caribbean crab, grilled or in coconut sauce, caught the same morning from the surrounding reef. Take a mototaxi or rent a bicycle — it is a 20-minute ride from the main town and worth every minute.
