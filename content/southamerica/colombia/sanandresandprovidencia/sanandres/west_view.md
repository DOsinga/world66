A sheltered coral pool on the less-developed west coast where you can swim among sea cucumbers, sea urchins, and a dazzling variety of reef fish. Local operators here offer snorkelling kits and guided three-hour trips for a fraction of what you would pay near the main hotels. The current around the outer reef edge can be strong — stick to the rope lines if you are not a confident swimmer.
