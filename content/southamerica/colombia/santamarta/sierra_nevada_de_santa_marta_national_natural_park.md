Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta National Natural Park protects the extraordinary mountain
massif that rises almost straight from Colombia's Caribbean coast. Snow peaks, cloud
forest, rivers, and Indigenous territories sit behind Santa Marta, making the city a
gateway to one of South America's most unusual natural regions.

The park is not a casual drop-in attraction; access depends on route, permissions,
guides, and the specific area you hope to visit. Treat it with more respect than a day
hike. For many travelers, the practical entry point is an organized trek or community-
guided visit that acknowledges the cultural as well as ecological importance of the
mountains.
