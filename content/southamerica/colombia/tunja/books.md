title: "Books"
type: section
---

**The Liberator** by Eric Beerman covers Simón Bolívar's military campaign and the 1819 Battle of Boyacá fought just south of Tunja — the decisive engagement that broke Spanish rule in northern South America. Useful background for visiting the Puente de Boyacá.

**El Carnero** by Juan Rodríguez Freyle, written in the 1630s, is a gossipy chronicle of the colonial city of New Granada in its first century — full of duels, scandal, witchcraft, and political infighting. Some of the most memorable stories are set in Tunja. Hard to find in English; the Spanish original is widely available in Colombian bookshops.

**Tunja: Capital de un Antiguo Reino** by Eduardo Pinzón Cerón is the standard local history in Spanish, covering the Muisca origins, the Spanish conquest, and the colonial centuries in detail. Often available at the museum bookshops.

**Affirmation of a Diocese: Tunja in the Sixteenth and Seventeenth Centuries** by Jorge Tovar Pinzón is a scholarly study (English-language) of the early Catholic Church in Tunja, useful background for anyone who wants more depth than the church plaques provide.
