Catedral Basilica Metropolitana Santiago de Tunja anchors Plaza de Bolivar and is one of the city's most important churches. Built from the 16th century onward, it blends Isabelline, Renaissance, and neoclassical elements.

It is a natural first stop in Tunja's historic center, especially when paired with the nearby colonial houses, convents, and churches.
