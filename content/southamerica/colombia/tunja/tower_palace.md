Tower Palace is a colonial mansion in central Tunja, built in the early Spanish period and later given a neoclassical appearance. It now holds portraits and canvases connected with the governors of Boyaca, making it a compact stop for visitors already exploring Tunja's churches and old houses.

The building is more interesting as part of a walk through the historic centre than as a stand-alone museum. Pair it with nearby colonial houses and the churches around Plaza de Bolivar.
