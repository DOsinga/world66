---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The Plaza Mayor and the streets around it are where Villa de Leyva's cafe life concentrates. The town is primarily a daytime destination — most places open early for breakfast and coffee — but on Friday and Saturday evenings in holiday season the square comes alive. [Pastelería Francesa](/southamerica/colombia/villadeleyva/pasteleria_francesa), two blocks from the square, is the best breakfast spot. [La Terraza](/southamerica/colombia/villadeleyva/la_terraza), on the northern edge of the Plaza Mayor, has the finest position in town for a sunset drink. [El Cortijo](/southamerica/colombia/villadeleyva/el_cortijo), a few blocks away, is the locals' choice for live music and cold beer in the evening.
