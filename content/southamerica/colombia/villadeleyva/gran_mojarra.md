La Gran Mojarra is one of the most popular restaurants in Villa de Leyva with locals and regular visitors alike. The speciality is grilled mojarra — a freshwater fish served with fried plantains, papaya salad, and rice. It is a simple, honest Colombian meal done well and at fair prices. The portions are generous.

The restaurant is near the town centre, within easy walking distance of the Plaza Mayor. Unlike some of the more tourist-oriented establishments around the square, it maintains a local clientele and doesn't inflate its prices accordingly. Order the sangrita — a Colombian mix of orange juice, chili, onion, and salt — as the traditional accompaniment.
