title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Villa de Leyva's attractions divide between the town itself and the extraordinary concentration of archaeological, palaeontological, and religious sites in the surrounding countryside. The [Plaza Mayor](/southamerica/colombia/villadeleyva/plaza_mayor), the house museums, and [Casa Terracota](/southamerica/colombia/villadeleyva/casa_terracota) are all compact and close together — a morning covers them comfortably. But the countryside is what makes a longer stay worthwhile.

West of town, a single road connects three remarkable sites: [El Fósil](/southamerica/colombia/villadeleyva/el_fosil) (a 120-million-year-old Kronosaurus skeleton still in the earth where it was found), [El Infiernito](/southamerica/colombia/villadeleyva/el_infiernito) (a Muisca astronomical site of stone monoliths), and the [Convent of El Santo Ecce Homo](/southamerica/colombia/villadeleyva/convento_ecce_homo) (a 17th-century Dominican monastery with marine fossils embedded in its floor). A half-day circuit covers all three.

The [Santuario de Flora y Fauna Iguaque](/southamerica/colombia/villadeleyva/santuario_iguaque) deserves a full day — it is a serious hike to a sacred Muisca lagoon through cloud forest and páramo, and one of the finest natural experiences in Boyacá.
