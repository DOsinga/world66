---
title: "Tours and Excursions"
type: section
---

Most visitors to Zipaquirá come as part of an organised day trip from Bogotá, and this is a perfectly good way to see the Salt Cathedral. Bogotá-based tour operators run half-day and full-day excursions that include transportation, a guided tour of the cathedral, and often the colonial town centre. Groups tend to visit in the late morning and early afternoon; arriving independently in the early morning gives you the cathedral with fewer crowds.

The [Tren de la Sabana](/southamerica/colombia/zipaquira/tren_de_la_sabana) tourist train from Bogotá is one of the more pleasant ways to reach the town and effectively functions as a guided day trip with the train journey as the experience. It runs Saturday and Sunday and includes stops at Cajicá, where traditional chicha and fresh strawberries are sold on the platform.

For those wanting to combine Zipaquirá with other destinations, the town sits on the same route as Guatavita (site of the original El Dorado legend, 35 km east) and Nemocón (another salt mine, 18 km north) — both can be reached by bus from Zipaquirá and make for a full day's circuit from Bogotá.
