The Ecuadorian Amazon is reached in one of two directions. Cuyabeno, in the north-east, is lagoon country — flooded forest, canoes, pink dolphins — and is the cheaper and easier of the two. Yasuní is bigger, wilder and further, and its lodges are community-owned in a way Cuyabeno's largely are not.

Neither has a booking office in the forest. Both operators below sell from Quito and fly you to Lago Agrio or Coca, and the price always includes the transfer, because there is no other way to arrive.
