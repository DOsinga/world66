Cuyabeno is the flooded forest reserve in the far north-east, all blackwater lagoons and paddling under closed canopy, and this lodge claims to have been first in and to have taken the best spot. Pink river dolphins in the lagoon, caiman at night, and a Siona community whose programme they fold into the tour.

The rate card is fully published, which is unusual for an Amazon lodge: four days from $279 in the dormitory up to $499 in a suite, per person sharing. Transport from Quito is included either by road or by the Coca flight.

The booking office is on Leonidas Plaza in Quito's Mariscal, next to the Petroecuador building.
