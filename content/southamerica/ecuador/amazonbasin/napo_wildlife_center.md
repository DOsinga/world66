Inside Yasuní national park on the Añangu creek, reached by motor canoe from Coca and then two hours of paddling up a blackwater creek because outboards are not allowed on the last stretch. That last hour, in silence, is the best part of getting there.

The distinctive thing is the ownership: the tourism ministry registers it not as a lodge but as a *centro de turismo comunitario*, the Añangu Kichwa community's own operation. The guides are from the community. Two canopy towers, the parrot clay licks, and a bird list long enough that they sell dedicated four, five and six-day birding itineraries.

Flights Quito–Coca–Coca–Quito are extra at $256 an adult.
