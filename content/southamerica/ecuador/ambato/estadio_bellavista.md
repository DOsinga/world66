Estadio Bellavista is home to Deportivo Ambato, the city's football club, and sits in a fine location above the city with views toward Tungurahua on clear days. The stadium holds around 18,000 spectators and comes alive on match days with the particular intensity that highland football generates in Ecuador.

For visitors who enjoy football, catching a match here is a much more authentic experience than attending a game in Quito or Guayaquil. The stadium is close to the city centre, prices are low, and the atmosphere is local rather than touristic.

Check the Liga Pro schedule for match dates. Kick-off times are typically Saturday afternoons. Tickets are available at the ground on match day.
