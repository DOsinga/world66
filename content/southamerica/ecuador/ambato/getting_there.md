---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Ambato has no commercial airport. The nearest airports are at [Quito](/southamerica/ecuador/quito) (about 130 km to the north) and at [Guayaquil](/southamerica/ecuador/guayaquil) (about 250 km to the southwest).

By bus, Ambato is around two and a half hours from Quito on the Pan-American Highway, and connections run frequently from Quito's Quitumbe terminal throughout the day. From [Riobamba](/southamerica/ecuador/riobamba) to the south it is about an hour, and from [Baños](/southamerica/ecuador/banos) around 45 minutes. The main bus terminal (Terminal Terrestre) is on the north side of the city.

The Pan-American Highway runs directly through Ambato, making it a natural stop on overland journeys between Quito and the southern highlands.
