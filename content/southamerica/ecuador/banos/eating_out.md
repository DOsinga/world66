---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Baños has a good range of restaurants given its size, catering to the international backpacker crowd as well as Ecuadorian weekenders. Set lunches (almuerzo) are the best value: soup, a main of chicken, fish or meat, and juice for a few dollars. The main streets around the plaza have the highest concentration of options, ranging from local Ecuadorian kitchens to traveller-friendly international food.
