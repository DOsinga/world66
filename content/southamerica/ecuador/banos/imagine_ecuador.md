An agency two blocks off the main drag that does the ordinary Baños work — canyoning, the waterfall route, the bridge — and then does something less ordinary: it sells Galápagos yacht berths, sorted on the website by luxury, first class, midrange, budget and economy class.

That combination is unusual. Most Baños agencies stop at the edge of Tungurahua province. If you are already in Baños and have decided late that you want a boat in the islands, this is a place to ask.

They publish one mobile for both calls and WhatsApp and a QR code to start the chat.
