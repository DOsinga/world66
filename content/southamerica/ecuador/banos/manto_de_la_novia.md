Manto de la Novia is one of the named waterfalls on the Ruta de las Cascadas east of Banos. Its thin white drop gives the falls their bridal-veil name, and the surrounding gorge scenery is the real reason to stop.

Many travellers see it by bike, bus, or short tour while following the waterfall road toward Pailon del Diablo. Conditions change with rain and river levels, so keep plans flexible and leave time for the viewpoints rather than rushing the route.
