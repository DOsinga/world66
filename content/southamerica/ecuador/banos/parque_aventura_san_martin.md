Parque Aventura San Martin fits Banos's identity as Ecuador's easy-access adventure town. The park uses the canyon landscape for ziplines, suspended bridges, climbing routes, and other guided activities, giving visitors a controlled way to play with the steep terrain around town.

It is best for travellers who want more structure than simply renting a bike for the waterfall road. Check current operators, safety equipment, and weather before booking, and skip it if you are uncomfortable with heights.
