Three kilometres up the Runtún road above the town, which is the side of Baños where the adrenaline operators actually have land rather than just a shopfront. Puntzan runs zipline circuits — superman and bat position — Tibetan and vertical bridges, rappel, a bungee jump and canyoning, and throws in sport fishing for people who have had enough.

Prices are published, which almost nobody in Baños does: half a day of canopy is $20 to $25 a head, canyoning $30, the combination packages $25. Open Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 19:00.
