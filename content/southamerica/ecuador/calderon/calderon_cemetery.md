The municipal cemetery at the edge of Calderón is a striking example of indigenous Andean burial tradition. Family tombs are painted in bright primary colours — pinks, blues, yellows — and decorated with photographs, plastic flowers, and small offerings.

It comes alive on **Día de los Difuntos**, 2 November, when families clean and repaint the graves, lay out food and drink, and spend the day among their dead. Outside that single day it is quiet and worth a calm wander, though as in any cemetery be respectful with photographs.
