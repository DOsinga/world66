---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Calderón is essentially a northern suburb of [Quito](/southamerica/ecuador/quito), about 12 kilometres from the historic centre. Regular city buses leave from the **Río Coca** terminal on Quito's eco-via and run to the main square in around 30 minutes — longer in traffic. A taxi is around 8–10 USD.

If you are driving north on the **Panamericana** towards Cayambe or Otavalo, Calderón is a 15-minute detour off the highway and an easy first stop.
