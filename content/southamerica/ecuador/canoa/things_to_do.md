---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Canoa's pleasures are mostly outdoor and low-key. The beach is long enough to walk for an hour in either direction without running out of sand, and the surf is gentle enough for beginners most of the year. Paragliding from the cliffs above town is one of the best spots for the sport in Ecuador. For something more organised, the Rio Muchacho organic farm a few kilometres inland offers day tours, and the ancient stone fish traps at Monkey Beach to the north are a worthwhile low-tide walk.
