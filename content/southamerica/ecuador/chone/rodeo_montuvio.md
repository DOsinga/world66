The rodeo montuvio is the cultural event that most distinguishes Chone from other Manabí towns of similar size. Rooted in the traditions of the montuvio people — the rural mestizo communities of coastal Ecuador — it combines equestrian skills competitions, bull-riding, and traditional music into a festival that is entirely local in character and not designed for outside consumption.

The main events are held during the **Fiestas de Cantonización** in August, centred around the parque central and the nearby streets that are temporarily converted into event space. Smaller competitions take place at various points during the year. If you are in Chone during a rodeo event, the atmosphere is genuinely festive — the crowds are local, the food stalls are everywhere, and the competitions themselves are serious business for participants who have trained for months.

The event area is in and around the central square during fiesta season.
