The string of resort and surf beaches south of Esmeraldas city makes the most natural day trip — or a longer stay. [Atacames](/southamerica/ecuador/atacames) is the loud and busy one; Tonsupa just north of it is similar but quieter; Súa and Same are smaller and more relaxed. [Mompiche](/southamerica/ecuador/mompiche), further south, has the best surf and the longest stretches of empty sand.

Inland, the river runs up into the Chocó rainforest. **Bilsa Biological Reserve** and the **Mache-Chindul Ecological Reserve** are reachable from Esmeraldas with a guide and a sturdy vehicle, and offer some of the most biodiverse forest left on the Pacific coast.
