---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Esmeraldan cooking is closer to Caribbean and West African food than to anything you find inland in Ecuador. The defining ingredient is **coconut milk** (*leche de coco*), and the regional specialities all use it: **encocado** (fish or seafood in coconut sauce, served over rice and patacones), **tapao** (steamed fish with green plantain), and **cazuelas** (a heavier seafood stew thickened with peanut). Local plantain dishes — *bolones*, *patacones*, and **bala** (a sweet-savoury plantain dumpling) — are everywhere.

The best places to try them are the small *cevicherías* and family restaurants in Las Palmas and along the malecón. Markets serve the cheapest and often the best food: ask for **encocado de pescado** or **encocado de camarón**.

Coconut sweets and tropical fruits — *guanábana*, *zapote*, *borojó* — are sold from carts everywhere.
