---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

**By air.** Esmeraldas has a small airport, **Carlos Concha Torres (ESM)**, with daily flights from [Quito](/southamerica/ecuador/quito) (about 30 minutes). It is the easiest way to reach the coast from the highlands.

**By road.** Buses from Quito take about 6–7 hours via Calacalí and the road that drops down through cloud forest. From [Guayaquil](/southamerica/ecuador/guayaquil) the coastal route via Manta and Pedernales is about 9–10 hours. From [Cali](/southamerica/colombia/cali) and the Colombian border at San Lorenzo, regular buses run south down the coast.

The bus terminal is in the southern part of the city; the city centre and Las Palmas are about 15 minutes by taxi.

**Safety note.** The province has had problems with organised crime in recent years, particularly along the Colombian border. Travel between cities is generally fine during the day; ask locally before night moves.
