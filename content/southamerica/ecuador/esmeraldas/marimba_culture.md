Esmeraldas is the heart of Afro-Ecuadorian culture, and the strongest expression of it is the music: bamboo **marimbas** played in pairs, **bombos** and **cununos** (the drums brought from the African coast), and the call-and-response chants of *arrullos* and *currulaos*. The tradition descends from enslaved Africans who arrived on this coast in 1553 — a Spanish slave ship wrecked off the coast and the survivors established free Maroon communities in the forest.

The musicians, *grupos de marimba*, perform at cultural centres around the city and at festivals through the year. The **Centro Cultural Afroecuatoriano** in Esmeraldas runs regular rehearsals and shows that are open to visitors. Outside Esmeraldas, the small town of **San Lorenzo** further north is the other main centre of the tradition.
