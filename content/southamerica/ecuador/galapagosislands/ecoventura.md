Twenty guests, seven nights, and a published rate card — which in the Galápagos cruise market is genuinely unusual, since almost everyone quotes through an agent. A week sharing a double starts at $10,950 per person for 2026 departures and climbs each year after.

Read the exclusions before the inclusions. The national park entrance is $200 on top, the transit card another $20, and they suggest $400 a week in crew gratuities. Whole-boat charter is $219,000.

The head office is in Samborondón outside Guayaquil, not in the islands. They run a 24-hour line that is WhatsApp only, and say so on the page.
