Galakiwi's argument is that you do not have to sleep on a boat to see the Galápagos. They base people in the towns and run day boats out, which costs a fraction of a cruise and means you can change your mind about tomorrow.

It is a real alternative rather than a compromise — San Cristóbal, Santa Cruz and Isabela are all inhabited, all have day-trip fleets, and the wildlife does not care where you slept. What you give up is the outer islands that only liveaboards reach.

Based on the Charles Darwin avenue in Puerto Baquerizo Moreno, on San Cristóbal.
