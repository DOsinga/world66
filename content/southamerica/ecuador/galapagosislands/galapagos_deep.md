A dive and day-tour operation with feet on two islands — Santa Cruz and San Cristóbal — under one RUC, selling Kicker Rock, Bartolomé, Seymour, the Plazas, Floreana and the experimental sport-fishing trips that Galápagos licenses to a handful of boats.

The website is rough and the company publishes no address and no email, only a WhatsApp number. That is not unusual in Puerto Ayora, where the shopfront is on the avenue and the booking happens on a phone.
