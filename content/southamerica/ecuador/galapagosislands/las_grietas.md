Las Grietas is a narrow volcanic crack filled with clear green water, reached from Puerto Ayora by water taxi and a walk past German's Beach, salt pans, and lava rock. It is one of the easiest places on Santa Cruz to swim in a setting that feels distinctly Galapagos without needing a boat tour.

The water is a mix of sea and freshwater, so visibility can be excellent, though the snorkelling is more about the dramatic crevice than abundant wildlife. Access rules have changed over time and guided visits may be required, so check locally before setting out.
