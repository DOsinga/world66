A two-boat fleet of sailing catamarans working the islands, booked from an office in Quito. The distinction worth caring about is that they are catamarans under sail rather than motor yachts with a mast — the crossings are quieter and, when the wind is right, slower in the good way.

Both vessels are registered individually with the tourism ministry as marine tourist transport, which is the licence that matters in the archipelago.
