Playa Carola sits just beyond the Interpretation Centre, at the end of the same path that leads to Frigatebird Hill. It is a small, sheltered cove with calm water suitable for snorkelling — sea turtles are often seen here too, feeding on the algae in the shallows rather than nesting. Marine iguanas are a reliable presence on the rocks.

The beach is within walking distance of town — around 25 minutes from the main waterfront. No facilities, no entry fee. It makes a natural extension to a morning visit to the Interpretation Centre and Frigatebird Hill, turning what could be a two-hour trip into a very full half-day without needing any transport.
