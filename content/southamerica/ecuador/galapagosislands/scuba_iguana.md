One of the established dive operations on Santa Cruz, running daily boats to Gordon Rocks, Seymour, Mosquera, Floreana and the rest, with snorkelling trips and land day tours for people who are not diving.

They sell liveaboards too, and hiking on the Sierra Negra volcano over on Isabela — the useful thing about a Puerto Ayora dive shop is that it can put the whole week together without you moving agency.

They publish a Santa Cruz landline and, separately, a mobile behind their WhatsApp button. The two are not the same number, and the page's layout invites you to confuse them.
