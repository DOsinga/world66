The Charles Darwin Research Station, just outside Puerto Ayora on Santa Cruz, explains the conservation work behind the Galapagos National Park. Its tortoise breeding area, iguana exhibits, and natural history displays make it one of the easiest places in the islands to understand the wildlife recovery programs that visitors see across the archipelago.
