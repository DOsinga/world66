El Chato Tortoise Reserve is a private reserve in the Santa Cruz highlands where giant tortoises roam through damp pasture and native vegetation. It is one of the easiest places in the Galapagos to see tortoises outside a breeding centre.

The visit usually includes a walk through lava tunnels as well as time on the reserve trails. Wear shoes that can handle mud, especially after rain.
