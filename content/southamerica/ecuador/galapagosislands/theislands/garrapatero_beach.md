Garrapatero Beach is a white-sand beach on Santa Cruz reached by road from Puerto Ayora and a short trail through coastal vegetation. The beach is good for swimming and snorkeling, and the surrounding mangroves and lagoon often bring pelicans, marine iguanas, ducks, and flamingos into view.

It is quieter than the beaches closest to town, but still easy enough for an independent half-day trip. Camping may be possible with advance permission from the national park.
