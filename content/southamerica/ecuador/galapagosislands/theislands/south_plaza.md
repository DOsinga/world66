South Plaza is a small Galapagos landing site known for close wildlife encounters. A dry landing on a concrete pontoon leads along a rocky shore with sea lions, marine iguanas, Sally Lightfoot crabs, and lava herons. A short path passes prickly pear cactus and land iguanas before reaching cliffs where swallow-tailed gulls wheel overhead.
