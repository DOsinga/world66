Lake Cochas is a light local outing about 10 km from Guaranda. It is not a wild Andean lake, but a managed recreation spot with boats, gardens, and food stalls.

Use it as an easy breather if you have extra time or are travelling with family. Serious scenery seekers should save their energy for Chimborazo or the higher roads.
