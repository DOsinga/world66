El Lago Park is a wetland-style nature stop west of Guayaquil, with birds and other wildlife around the water. It is not a polished city park, but it gives visitors a look at the lowland ecology beyond the urban centre.

Go for a quiet walk and birdwatching rather than formal exhibits. It works best as a half-day nature break if you have transport.
