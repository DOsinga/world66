El Lago Park is a wetland park west of Guayaquil, built around a broad reservoir with trails, picnic areas, and good chances of seeing birds. It is less polished than the central waterfront sights, but it gives you a quick look at the marshy landscapes around the lower Guayas basin.

Come early for cooler weather and better wildlife activity. It makes the most sense if you have your own transport or are already heading toward the coast.
