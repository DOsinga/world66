Guayaquil's Patrimonial Cemetery is one of the city's more atmospheric historical sites, a formal necropolis of marble tombs, family mausoleums, and funerary sculpture north of the centre. It tells a civic history that is easy to miss in the modern city: politicians, merchants, artists, and old Guayaquil families are remembered here in stone.

Visit in daylight and treat it as a quiet walk rather than a spectacle. The cemetery is still a place of mourning, but for travellers interested in architecture and local history it gives a different, more reflective view of Guayaquil than the riverfront promenades and regenerated public spaces.
