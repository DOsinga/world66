---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Loja's pleasures are quiet ones — walking the colonial streets, attending a live music performance in one of the plazas, and using the city as a base for the extraordinary natural landscapes that surround it. Podocarpus National Park to the southeast is the main draw for nature-minded visitors, while the Valley of Vilcabamba to the south is one of the more pleasant detours in all of Ecuador.
