Centro Civico Ciudad Alfaro stands above Montecristi and combines a museum, civic space, and memorial to Eloy Alfaro, the liberal president born in the town. The exhibits cover Alfaro, Indigenous Ecuador, and the country's political history, while the hilltop position gives broad views back over Montecristi and toward Manta.
