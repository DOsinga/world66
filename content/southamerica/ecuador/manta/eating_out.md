---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

The local cuisine is the centre of Manta's appeal. Ceviche de pescado, encocados (seafood in coconut sauce), corviche (plantain-and-fish dumplings), and the daily catch served grilled or fried with patacones and rice. The Malecón is full of seafood restaurants; the best sit slightly back from the prime tourist strip.
