The local football stadium, home of Manta FC. Capacity around 17,000, named for the pre-Columbian settlement that gave Manta its older name. Match days, mostly weekend afternoons, are the best (and only good) time to visit — the atmosphere is loud, the beer is cold, and a ticket costs almost nothing.
