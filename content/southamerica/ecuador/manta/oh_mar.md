A long-running mid-range seafood restaurant on the Malecón, popular with families. The corvina al ajillo (sea bass with garlic) and the encocado de camarón (shrimp in coconut sauce) are reliable picks. Service is unhurried, the tables out front have a sea breeze, and the bill stays modest.
