---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The Manabí coast has two seasons, but neither is severe.

**December to May** is the warm wet season — high humidity, hot days (28–32°C), short heavy downpours in the afternoon. The sea is warmest and the sky is most often blue between rain bursts; this is the local high season for beach travel.

**June to November** is the cool dry season — air temperatures 22–26°C, the sky often a flat grey from the Humboldt-current marine layer, the sea cooler and choppier. Fewer crowds, lower hotel prices, better for whale-watching: humpbacks migrate up this coast from late June to October and are routinely seen offshore from Puerto López, two hours south.

**Carnival** (February or early March) is the biggest event of the year, with three days of beach parties and water fights along the Malecón. Hotels book out weeks ahead.
