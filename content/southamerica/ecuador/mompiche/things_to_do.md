---
title: 'Things to Do'
type: section
---

Mompiche is not a place with a list of attractions. You come here for the surf, the beach, and the slow pace. The main break is a long left-hand point that works best from December to April, and the black-sand beach stretches far enough that you can always find a quiet spot. Beyond that, there are boat trips to nearby islands and a lot of hammock time.
