---
title: 'When to Go'
type: section
---

Mompiche has a tropical climate with two loose seasons. December to April is the wet season — hotter, more humid, and with regular afternoon downpours, but this is also when the surf is best. May to November is drier and slightly cooler, with smaller waves and fewer visitors. The village is busiest during Ecuadorian holiday weekends (particularly Carnival in February and Semana Santa) when prices rise and the quiet atmosphere changes considerably. For the best balance of good surf and low crowds, January is hard to beat.
