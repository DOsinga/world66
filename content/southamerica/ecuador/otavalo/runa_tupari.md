"Runa tupari" is Kichwa for meeting indigenous people, and that is what the agency sells: homestays and day trips in the villages around Cotacachi, plus the Intag cloud-forest valley on the western slope, which almost nobody visits.

It is the alternative to the standard Otavalo circuit of market, Peguche waterfall and lunch. Registered in Cotacachi canton, with the office in Otavalo on 10 de Agosto.

Their site is in Spanish only. The number in the header is the WhatsApp; the one in the footer is the same number written with a trunk zero.
