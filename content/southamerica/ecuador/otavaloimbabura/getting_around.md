---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The town centre is compact and easy to walk. The Plaza de los Ponchos market is a few blocks from the main square, and taxis within town cost a dollar or two. For the surrounding lakes and villages, taxis are the most practical option — agree a price before you leave. Local buses run from the main terminal to nearby towns like Peguche and San Pablo del Lago, but the frequency is limited outside market days.

Renting a bicycle is an option for the lake at San Pablo, which is largely flat; the road to Laguna Cuicocha is a much longer climb and better done by taxi or on a tour.
