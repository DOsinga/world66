---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Otavalo is about 110 kilometres north of Quito by the Pan-American Highway — roughly two to two and a half hours by bus. Buses run frequently from Quito's Terminal Terrestre Carcelén to Otavalo throughout the day; the fare is around $2.50. Shared taxis and private transfers are also easy to arrange from Quito. There is no commercial airport.

Coming from the Colombian border, Otavalo is about 100 km south of Tulcán, with frequent buses via Ibarra. Ibarra itself is the regional hub, just 20 km north of Otavalo, with connections to more distant destinations.
