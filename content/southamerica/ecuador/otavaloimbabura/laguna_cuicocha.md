A volcanic crater lake sitting at 3,068 metres in the shadow of Cotacachi volcano, Laguna Cuicocha is one of the most dramatic landscapes in the Imbabura region. The water is a deep blue-green, the rim walk takes about four to five hours, and the views of Cotacachi and, on clear days, Cayambe and Imbabura volcanoes are exceptional.

The lake is about 14 km west of Otavalo, accessible by taxi in around 30 minutes. Boats run from the small dock to the two central islands, though swimming is not permitted — the lake releases sulphurous gases. The trail around the rim is clearly marked, rises to around 3,500 metres at the highest point, and requires basic fitness rather than technical skills. Take water, sunscreen, and a layer for the wind.
