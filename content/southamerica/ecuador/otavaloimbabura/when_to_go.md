---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Imbabura region sits near the equator at around 2,500 metres, which keeps temperatures moderate year-round — warm days and cool nights, rarely above 22°C or below 8°C. The driest months are June through September, making this the most comfortable time to walk the lakeside trails and hike to Laguna Cuicocha.

The Saturday market is the biggest draw, and it runs every week without exception. The Inti Raymi festival in late June is the most important celebration in the indigenous calendar — a week of music, dancing, and ceremonies that has its roots in pre-Inca harvest traditions. If you can time a visit around it, do.
