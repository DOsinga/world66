Portoviejo is a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy (designated 2019) and takes that distinction seriously. Manabí cuisine is built around peanuts, plantains, fresh fish, yucca and corn — a pre-Columbian larder that has been cooking for centuries. The dishes to seek out: **corviche** (deep-fried green plantain balls stuffed with fish and ground peanuts, served with peanut sauce), **viche** (a thick, complex peanut and fish soup with plantains and vegetables), **encocado de camarón** (prawns in coconut sauce), and **ceviche** made from fresh shellfish caught along the Manabí coast. The city's market stalls and mid-range restaurants serve these more reliably than the tourist-facing places.
