A relaxed spot in the city centre serving Manabí tapas and traditional dishes: corviches, green empanadas, fish buns (bolones de verde rellenos). The setting is rustic — tiled floors, wooden tables, a covered terrace — and the prices are modest. Good for an informal lunch or an early dinner sampling several small dishes rather than committing to a single main.
