---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The post-earthquake city centre is compact and pleasant to walk. The main sights — the cathedral, the municipal museum, Parque Las Vegas, the Casa de la Cultura — are within fifteen minutes of each other on foot, and the pedestrian priority redesign of the centre means the streets are less chaotic than many Ecuadorian cities of similar size.

**Taxis:** Abundant and cheap. Short rides within the city cost $1.50–$3. Use InDriver or negotiate the fare before getting in.

**City buses:** The urban bus network is extensive and covers outer districts including Picoazá and the university campuses. Fares are a few cents. For the Mamey Ecological Park, the park is directly adjacent to the main bus terminal.

**To coastal towns:** Shared buses and minivans run frequently to Manta (45 min), Crucita (30–40 min), and San Jacinto (1 hour) from the main terminal and from stops along Avenida Universitaria.
