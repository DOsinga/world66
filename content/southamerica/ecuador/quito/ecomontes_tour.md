A Juan León Mera agency that publishes what its day trips cost, which in Quito is almost eccentric. A shared full day to Otavalo is $65, the same day privately is $100, and Mindo is priced identically. The four-day Napo Wildlife Center package is $1,700.

Their RUC begins 1091, an Imbabura number, so the roots are in Otavalo country even though the registration and the office are in Quito. They print their MINTUR registro in the footer, and it matches the national catastro.

Three named staff answer three different WhatsApp lines, split by what you are asking about — high mountain, Galápagos, or inbound groups.
