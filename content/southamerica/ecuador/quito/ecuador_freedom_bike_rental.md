Founded in 2009 by Court and Sylvain, and still running out of Calle Finlandia with its own maintenance workshop in Quito, which matters more than it sounds when you are 4,000 metres up a dirt road on somebody else's bike.

Two products: rent a motorcycle or a 4x4 and ride a self-guided route with the GPS tracks loaded and the hotels booked, or take a guided tour with a local rider in front. The routes go Andes, Amazon and coast, and they have been riding the same ones long enough to have opinions about them.

Open 09:00 to 18:00 daily, year round. They answer WhatsApp on a number they print in plain text beside it.
