If you want a Galápagos ship with a naturalist guide and a fixed itinerary approved by the national park, this is the company the rest of the industry buys from. Office hours 08:00 to 17:00 Ecuador time.

They run two WhatsApp lines and label them clearly: one for people calling from inside Ecuador, one for everybody else.
