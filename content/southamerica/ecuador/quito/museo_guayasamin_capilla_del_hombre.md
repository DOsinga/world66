The Guayasamin museum complex is one of Quito's strongest cultural visits, joining the
artist's former home, collections, studio atmosphere, and the monumental Capilla del
Hombre. Oswaldo Guayasamin's work turns Ecuadorian and Latin American history into
faces, hands, grief, anger, and dignity on a scale that is hard to ignore.

Set aside enough time for both the intimate and ceremonial sides of the site. The
hilltop location adds views across Quito, but the real reason to come is the emotional
force of the paintings and the way the complex frames art as memory, protest, and
national identity.
