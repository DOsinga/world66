---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Quito's Historic Centre is one of the best-preserved colonial cities in the Americas and the reason most travellers come here. Start with the [Plaza Grande](/southamerica/ecuador/quito/plaza_grande), the main square, and work outward through the extraordinary concentration of churches, convents, and plazas that UNESCO designated a World Heritage Site in 1978. The [Church of La Compañía](/southamerica/ecuador/quito/church_la_compania) is the most dazzling interior in the city; the [Basilica and Convent of San Francisco](/southamerica/ecuador/quito/basilica_san_francisco) is the largest and most impressive colonial complex. Up on the hill, the [Basílica del Voto Nacional](/southamerica/ecuador/quito/basilica_del_voto_nacional) offers the best views in the city and a genuinely vertiginous tower climb.

Beyond the Historic Centre, the [El Panecillo](/southamerica/ecuador/quito/el_panecillo) volcanic hill with its giant aluminium Virgin statue provides panoramic views, and the [Teleférico](/southamerica/ecuador/quito/teleferico) cable car rides up to the flanks of Pichincha volcano for even more dramatic perspectives. For museums, the [Casa del Alabado](/southamerica/ecuador/quito/casa_del_alabado) has the finest pre-Columbian collection in the country.

The [Centro Histórico](/southamerica/ecuador/quito/centro_historico) neighbourhood and the modern [La Mariscal](/southamerica/ecuador/quito/la_mariscal) district each have their own character and are worth exploring separately.
