Tena sits where the Andes drop into the Amazon basin, which gives it the best whitewater in Ecuador and a long season: the Jatunyacu runs at a friendly Class III, the Jondachi and the Upper Misahuallí at Class IV and up.

The operators below are rafting companies first. All three also run jungle trips into the upper Napo, which is the other reason people come.
