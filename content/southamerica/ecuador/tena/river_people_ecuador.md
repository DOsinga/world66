A Tena rafting and kayaking operation that publishes prices for everything it sells, which is rare enough in Ecuador to be a reason to start here. A full day on the water is $75 or $85 depending on the river, two days is $190 to $220, and the advanced Class III+ to IV+ kayak week runs to $2,155.

They also sell the standard Quito day trips — Cotopaxi at $115, Quilotoa at $125 — which is how a lot of Tena agencies fill the shoulder season.

The base is out on the Inchillaqui road rather than in the middle of town. Note that their WhatsApp line is not their phone number, and the two are only a few digits apart.
