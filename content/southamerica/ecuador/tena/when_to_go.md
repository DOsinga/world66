---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Tena is in the Amazon basin and it rains year-round — there is no truly dry season. That said, the drier months run roughly from October to February, when rainfall eases up and trails are less muddy. March through June tends to be the wettest period, with heavy afternoon downpours most days.

For rafting, the rivers are runnable year-round, but higher water levels during the rainy season make the rapids more challenging. If you are a beginner, the drier months offer more manageable conditions. Temperatures hover around 25-30°C throughout the year — it is always warm, always humid.
