The Port Howard Museum is small, direct, and unusually local. Set in a semi-circular corrugated hut beside the lodge, it holds artefacts from the 1982 war, including Argentine equipment, ejector seats, weapons, and a large parachute. It works best as a reminder that the Falklands War was not only fought around Stanley.
