The Warrah River is one of West Falkland's better-known fishing rivers, valued for the size and quality of its sea trout more than sheer numbers. It runs north from the hills behind Mount Maria toward Keppel Sound, with Green Hill Stream as its main tributary. Access is normally arranged through Port Howard or nearby farms.
