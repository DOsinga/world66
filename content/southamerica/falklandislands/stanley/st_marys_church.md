St Mary's Church is the Catholic church in Stanley, a modest wooden building dating from 1899. It is notable as the only Catholic church in the Falkland Islands and for the oil murals by local artist James Peck on the western wall.

It is a short stop in the upper part of town, best combined with Christ Church Cathedral, the waterfront, and the Falkland Islands Museum.
