The turtle beach is run by the Réserve Naturelle de l'Amana, whose information house is in the village: night visits are guided, with rules about torches and how close you may go, and they are free of any operator.

What is bookable here is the village's own tourism — craft, plants and food with a Kali'na family at Ayawandé.
