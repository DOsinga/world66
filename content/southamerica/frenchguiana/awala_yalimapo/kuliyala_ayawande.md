Kuliyala Ayawandé is a Kali'na operation at the village of Ayawandé, on the Coswine creek behind the turtle beaches, showing the crafts of the coast — pottery, basketry, carving, painting — and the plants they come from. Fifty euros upwards for adults, twelve for children.

Visits are arranged in advance. This is the community's own tourism rather than somebody else's tour of it.
