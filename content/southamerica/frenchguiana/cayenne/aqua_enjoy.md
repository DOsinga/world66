Aqua Enjoy operates from the Terre Rouge water base on the Montsinéry river, with paddleboards, paddle-bikes and four-seat pedalos, some of them fitted with slides. It is a family afternoon rather than an expedition, on a quiet stretch of river with forest on both banks, from thirty euros.
