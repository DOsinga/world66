---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

The Cayenne coast and immediate hinterland have several worthwhile day-trip destinations. The **Centre Spatial Guyanais** at Kourou, 60km west, is one of the world's busiest spaceports — Ariane and Vega rockets launch over the rainforest and free guided tours run on weekdays. From Kourou you can also take a boat to the **Îles du Salut**, the offshore islands that housed France's most notorious penal colony, including Devil's Island.

Inland, the Hmong agricultural village of **Cacao** holds an excellent Sunday market — pak choi, lemongrass, fresh pho — about 75 km southwest of the city. North along the coast, the **Marais de Kaw** is a vast wetland with caimans, jaguars and one of the largest populations of scarlet ibis in South America.
