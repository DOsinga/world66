Expéditions Aïmara runs long fishing trips — six to twelve days — after the aïmara, the wolf fish of the Guianese rivers, with everything from the airport pick-up onwards arranged. This is the specialist end of the market: anglers fly in for it, and the itinerary is built around where the fish are rather than around a fixed programme.

The operator publishes no business premises, so this pin is Montsinéry, where the company is registered.
