Guya Fishing Amazonie is a sport-fishing outfit based in the Galmot industrial zone in Cayenne, fishing the coast, the Îlets de Rémire and the rivers inland. The species list is the draw: tarpon and jacks in the estuaries, and aïmara — the wolf fish that South American anglers travel for — up the creeks.

Days are run as guided outings with the boat and tackle provided, and they also put together longer trips that combine fishing with time in the forest.
