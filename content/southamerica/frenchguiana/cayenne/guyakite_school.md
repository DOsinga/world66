Guya'Kite is an association that runs both a kitesurf school and a club for people who already ride, teaching on the beaches east of Cayenne when the trade wind is up — roughly December to April, the same months that bring the driest weather.

They teach beginners from scratch and run tandem sessions for riders with disabilities, which is unusual anywhere and rare in the Guianas.
