Kano Dilo is a qualified canoe guide working out of Macouria, west of Cayenne, who both hires boats out and takes people along the creeks himself — the Montsinéry and Tonnégrande rivers, the marshes, and the forest edges where the wildlife is.

Guided days start around eighty euros. Hire is cheaper, but you collect the boats from his base and get yourself to the water.
