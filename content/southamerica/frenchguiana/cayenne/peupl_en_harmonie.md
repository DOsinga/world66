Peupl'en Harmonie is an association that arranges day visits inside villages across the territory, hosted by the communities themselves — cassava and Arawak land at Sainte-Rose-de-Lima, Kali'na plants and pottery at Espérance and Bellevue, Bushinengue cooking and medicine near Mana, Palikur country in the east, and the Hmong village at Cacao.

Groups are small, usually three to six people, and the day belongs to whoever is hosting it. It is the most straightforward way to visit a village without turning up uninvited.
