Plage de Montjoly is the long sandy beach just east of Cayenne, the closest swimmable beach to the city. The water is brown — the Amazon dumps so much silt into the Atlantic that the entire Guianan coast looks more like coffee than ocean — but it's safe to swim, and the beach is a popular weekend retreat for *cayennais*.

The real draw is sea turtles. From April to July, leatherback, green and olive ridley turtles come ashore at night to nest, and conservation NGOs run guided turtle-watching tours from the beach. By day, walk the long flat sand and watch the frigatebirds dive offshore.
