ULM Équateur flies microlight floatplanes from the water at Montsinéry, on circuits over the forest, the savannas and the coast. Flights start around fifty euros and last long enough to see how abruptly the forest begins once the last houses end.

The company's old web address has lapsed and now serves unrelated content, so book by telephone or e-mail.
