---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Cayenne is hot and humid year-round, at around 27°C in town and warmer in the interior. There are two seasons. The **dry season** runs roughly from August to mid-November and again in March — these are the best months for travel, with reliable sunshine and easier road access into the rainforest.

The **wet season** runs from December through July with a short break in March; expect heavy daily downpours, swollen rivers and tougher overland travel. Even in the wet season, mornings are usually dry. Sea-turtle nesting peaks April-July, which adds a reason to come even in the rains.
