There is no operator on the islands: everything leaves from [Kourou](/southamerica/frenchguiana/kourou), twenty minutes away across the water, and the choice is between the scheduled ferry, a catamaran under sail, and a chartered fast boat.

The ferry is the cheapest and the most reliable; the catamarans make more of the crossing itself and usually include a circuit of Île Royale with the history told on the way. Whichever you take, the day is fixed by the boat — out in the morning, back in the late afternoon — unless you are staying the night on Royale.
