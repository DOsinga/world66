The only restaurant on the islands, set in the former guards' buildings on Ile Royale with a terrace looking out over the sea and towards the unreachable Ile du Diable. The menu is straightforward: fish, grilled chicken, rice and vegetables, with a few local touches. The food is decent rather than remarkable, but the setting makes everything taste better. Cold drinks — beer, soft drinks, bottled water — are available throughout the day.

Book ahead if you plan to eat lunch here on a day trip, as numbers are limited and they can fill up during high season. The same management runs the dormitory and bungalow accommodation if you want to stay overnight.
