---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Beyond the auberge bar there is essentially nothing. The islands have no shops, no kiosks, and no street food. If you want anything other than what the auberge provides, bring it with you from Kourou. A cold beer from the bar after a day of exploring ruins in the tropical heat is, however, one of the better small pleasures the islands offer.
