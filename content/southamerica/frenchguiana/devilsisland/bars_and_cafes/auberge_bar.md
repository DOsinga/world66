The small bar at the Auberge des Iles du Salut is the social centre of the islands — which is to say, the only option. Cold Desperados and soft drinks are the staples, along with whatever rum or wine the auberge has in stock. It tends to come alive in the late afternoon as day-trippers gather before the return boat and overnight guests swap stories about the ruins. A congenial spot given the setting: prison walls, jungle, and sea.
