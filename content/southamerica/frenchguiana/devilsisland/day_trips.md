**[Kourou](/southamerica/frenchguiana/kourou)** — The town from which you reach the islands is itself worth a few hours. The Guiana Space Centre (Centre Spatial Guyanais) is one of the world's primary rocket launch sites, operated by ESA and the French space agency CNES. Tours of the facility run on most weekdays and can be booked in advance; the visitor centre has exhibits on the Ariane, Soyuz, and Vega programmes. A launch, if you happen to be in French Guiana when one is scheduled, is an unforgettable spectacle visible from the town and the surrounding coast.

**[Cayenne](/southamerica/frenchguiana/cayenne)** — The capital of French Guiana, an hour's drive from Kourou, has a covered market full of Creole, Hmong, and Amerindian produce, colonial-era architecture around Place des Palmistes, and the best range of restaurants in the territory. The city has a distinctive character unlike anywhere else in South America — a creolised French Caribbean culture that takes some time to get a feel for.

**Sinnamary and Iracoubo** — Heading north from Kourou along the coast road, the small town of Sinnamary sits at the mouth of the Sinnamary river and has good birdwatching in the mangrove lagoons. A further 30 kilometres inland, the church at Iracoubo contains extraordinary murals painted in the late nineteenth century by a convict transported from the penal colony — a strange and vivid piece of art hidden in an unlikely corner of the territory.
