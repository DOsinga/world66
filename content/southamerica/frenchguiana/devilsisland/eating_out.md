---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

The Auberge des Iles du Salut on Ile Royale is the only place to eat on the islands. It serves a simple menu of French Guianese food — rice, fish, grilled meats — and cold drinks, which are very welcome after a morning of walking in the heat. Lunch tends to be the main service.

It is advisable to book ahead, especially in the dry season and during school holidays when the islands can be busy. If you are making a day trip, confirm your lunch reservation when you book your boat ticket. Bring snacks and water regardless — portions are generous but the restaurant is not always open between meals.
