---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Ile Royale is entirely walkable. A network of stone and dirt paths connects the dock, the auberge, the museum, the chapel, the cemetery, and the various prison buildings. A leisurely circuit of the island takes two to three hours, longer if you linger at the ruins or wait for the monkeys to come close.

A small boat shuttles between Ile Royale and Ile Saint-Joseph; the fare is paid separately from your main crossing ticket. The inter-island crossing is short — ten minutes or so — but can be choppy. Ile du Diable, the island where Dreyfus was actually held, is not accessible to visitors.

Allow a full day if you want to see both main islands properly. The afternoon return boat waits for no one, so keep an eye on the time.
