---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Boats to the Iles du Salut leave from Kourou's Vieux Bourg port, at the end of Avenue Charles de Gaulle. Departures are in the morning, typically around 8am, with the crossing to Ile Royale taking about 30 minutes. Return boats leave in the late afternoon. Schedules vary by season and operator; check locally for current timings.

Book ahead during the July–November dry season and during French school holidays — the boats fill up and last-minute tickets can be hard to come by. In the low season you may be able to turn up on the day, but calling ahead is still sensible.

Occasional day-trip tours operate from Cayenne, combining road transfer to Kourou with the boat crossing. These can be convenient if you are based in the capital. There is no overnight ferry service; the only way to stay on the islands is to be on one of the morning boats and pre-book accommodation at the auberge.
