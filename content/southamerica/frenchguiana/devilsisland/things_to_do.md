title: "Things To Do"
type: section
---

The Iles du Salut offer a singular experience: wandering through the crumbling ruins of one of the world's most notorious penal colonies, surrounded by dense jungle, howler monkeys, and nesting seabirds. There are no beaches to lounge on and no tourist infrastructure beyond the auberge — the draw is entirely the atmosphere and history.

Ile Royale is the hub, with the largest concentration of ruins, the small museum, and the auberge. Ile Saint-Joseph is the more unsettling island, its solitary confinement cells half-flooded and overtaken by roots and vines. Budget a full day for both.
