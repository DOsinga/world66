---
title: "Ile Royale"
type: poi
latitude: 5.289
longitude: -52.584
---

The largest and most accessible of the three islands, Ile Royale is where boats dock and most visitors spend the majority of their time. The ruins here are extensive: a church, administrative buildings, the guards' quarters, a hospital, and the convict cemetery all survive in various states of decay. A small museum in the former wardens' building explains the history of the penal colony with photographs, documents, and artefacts.

The island is home to a large population of howler monkeys, who are remarkably unbothered by visitors, and enormous iguanas that sun themselves on the stone walls. The auberge provides accommodation and the only restaurant on the islands. Well-worn paths connect all the main sights and can be walked in a couple of hours, though the heat and humidity make a slow pace sensible.
