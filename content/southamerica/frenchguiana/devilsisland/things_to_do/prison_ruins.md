title: "Prison Ruins"
type: poi

Scattered across Ile Royale are the administrative buildings that kept the penal colony functioning: the hospital where prisoners and guards alike succumbed to tropical disease, the bakery that supplied bread to the entire archipelago, the guards' quarters, and the chapel with its faded murals still visible on the interior walls. None of it has been restored — the French authorities have left the buildings in a dignified state of managed decay.

Walking through them, it is easy to lose track of time. Vegetation pushes through walls and floors, and the sea wind carries the sounds of howler monkeys and seabirds. Signs explain what each building was used for, in French and increasingly in English too.
