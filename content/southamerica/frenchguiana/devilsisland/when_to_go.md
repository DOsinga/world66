---
title: "When To Go"
type: section
---

The dry season, roughly July to November, brings calmer seas and makes the crossing from Kourou considerably more reliable and comfortable. This is the recommended period for a visit, though it coincides with French school holidays, which means the islands can be busy and accommodation at the auberge fills up quickly — book well in advance.

The rainy season (December to June) brings rougher seas and the boat crossings can be unpleasant or cancelled altogether if conditions are bad. On the other hand, visitor numbers drop sharply and the islands feel more remote and atmospheric. If you have flexibility in your schedule and are comfortable with the possibility of a cancelled crossing, an out-of-season visit has its rewards.

Sea conditions can cancel boats at any time of year. Always have a contingency plan for your onward travel from Kourou, and do not rely on catching the return boat on a fixed deadline.
