Nobody visits the Kaw marshes without a boat and a guide, and the operators below are the ones who have them. The standard trip is an afternoon out, a night on a carbet, and a run after dark with a torch for the black caiman, followed by the birds at first light.

Tig Dilo does it by kayak, which is slower, quieter and closer to the water. Le Morpho's boat is built for the marsh and carries more people. Both start from the landing at the village of Kaw, two hours from Cayenne by way of [Roura](/southamerica/frenchguiana/roura) and the mountain road.
