Kourou's restaurant scene reflects its mixed population: Creole, French, Brazilian, and Chinese kitchens all have a presence. Prices are high by regional standards — this is metropolitan France in terms of cost of living, and a sit-down meal will usually run to European prices. The fish market in Vieux Bourg is the place to buy ingredients if you have access to a kitchen.
