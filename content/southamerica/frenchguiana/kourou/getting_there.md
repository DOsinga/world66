---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Kourou has its own small airport (Kourou Airport, ICAO: SOOK), but scheduled commercial services are limited — the main point of entry for French Guiana is **Félix Éboué International Airport** near Cayenne, about 65 km southeast. Direct flights connect Cayenne with Paris (Air France, roughly 9 hours), as well as Martinique, Guadeloupe, Fort-de-France, and a handful of other Caribbean and South American destinations.

From Cayenne airport the most common route to Kourou is by shared taxi (*taxi collectif*), which departs when full from the area outside arrivals. The journey takes about an hour. Private taxis are faster but significantly more expensive, as with everything in French Guiana. A rental car from Cayenne airport is a practical option if you plan to explore more of the territory.

There is no railway. Long-distance buses connect Kourou with Cayenne and Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni to the west, but frequency is low and schedules are irregular.
