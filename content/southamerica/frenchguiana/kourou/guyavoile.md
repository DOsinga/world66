Guyavoile sails a catamaran out to the Îles du Salut: meet at a quarter to eight at the Kourou maritime station, sail out in an hour and a half when the wind cooperates, round the three islands, land on Île Royale, and sail back. Fifty euros upwards.

They also take people out along the coast to look for turtles in season.
