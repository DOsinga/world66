KTM is a Kourou maritime transport company that runs the crossing to the Îles du Salut fast — a fraction of the ferry's time — and also works the rivers and the coast. The boat is chartered rather than sold by the seat, from around four hundred and fifty euros, which makes sense for a group and not for two people.
