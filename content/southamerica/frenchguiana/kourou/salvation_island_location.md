Salvation Island Location hires motorboats at the Pariacabo port in Kourou without a skipper — you need the coastal licence — and also runs guided sport-fishing days for people who would rather someone else found the fish. Hire starts around two hundred and sixty euros, and they rent towable rings and boards with the boat.

The two numbers on their site belong to the two men who run it; the WhatsApp button points at the first.
