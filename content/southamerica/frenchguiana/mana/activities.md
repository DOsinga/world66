Mana's two operators fly and walk: floatplane circuits over the estuary and the turtle beaches, and birdwatching along the Acarouany near the Hmong village at Javouhey.

The turtle watching itself is at [Awala-Yalimapo](/southamerica/frenchguiana/awala_yalimapo), twenty minutes west, where the reserve runs the beach.
