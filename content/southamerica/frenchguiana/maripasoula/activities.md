There is no road to Maripasoula: you arrive by plane or by pirogue, and once here the pirogue is the transport. The operators below run the upper Maroni — the Lawa — to the falls, the fishing and the Wayana and Aluku villages upstream, and the kayak club puts you on the river for thirty euros if all you want is the water.

Trips into the [Guiana Amazonian Park](/southamerica/frenchguiana/guiana_amazonian_park) beyond here need permission as well as a boat; the operators arrange it.
