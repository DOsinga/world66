Envie d'Aventures Amazoniennes runs pirogue expeditions on the upper Maroni out of Maripasoula, combining river days with the forest, the falls and sport fishing. The guides speak Dutch and English as well as French, which on this river — Suriname is the far bank — is more useful than it sounds.
