The kayak club at Maripasoula runs canoe trips on the Lawa — the upper Maroni — any day of the week from two people, at thirty euros upwards. It puts you on the river that this village exists for, without the cost of chartering a pirogue.

The club is at the Amerindian landing in the bourg.
