Rémire-Montjoly has the marina, and the marina has the boats. The Îlets de Rémire lie a few kilometres offshore — seabirds, ruins, clear water — and the Îlet la Mère beyond them keeps a population of squirrel monkeys left behind by the Pasteur Institute. Both are half-day trips from Dégrad des Cannes.

The other thing sold here is fishing, offshore at the Connétable banks, which is some of the best ground on the Guianese coast.
