ASPAG — the Association de Sports en Plein Air en Guyane — takes people out by canoe, kayak and pirogue along the coast, up the rivers, into the marshes and across to the Îlets de Rémire. It is a sports club rather than a tour company, which shows in the prices: outings from about thirty euros.

Trips are led, and the longer ones camp.
