Guyane Pêche Sportive fishes the sea off Rémire: the Îlets de Rémire close in, and the Battures du Connétable further out under the bird island, which is some of the best ground in the territory. Tarpon, red drum and giant grouper are what people come for, with a qualified instructor-guide aboard.

Days start around a hundred and ten euros a head.
