Sotalie runs eco-tourism excursions and charters out of the Dégrad des Cannes marina, mostly to the Îlets de Rémire — the small islands off the coast, with their nesting seabirds and the ruins of the leper colony on Le Père — and along the shore where the turtles come in. The name is the local dolphin.

The boat goes out for the day from about three hundred and twenty euros, which is the charter rather than the seat.
