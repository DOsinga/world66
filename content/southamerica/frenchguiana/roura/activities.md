---

Roura is the base for the rivers. The water base in the village hires canoes and motor boats; the guides here work the Comté and the Oyack, the forest behind, and the Kaw road that climbs south from the village.

Prices are the lowest in the territory for this kind of thing, because these are one- and two-person operations rather than agencies, and most of them will meet you at the landing.
