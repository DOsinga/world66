L'Oxygen runs a floating carbet — a covered raft — on the Comté, boarding at eight at the Dégrad de la Comté. There are two cruises: a half day upriver towards the village, and a longer day on the water. It is the slow version of a river trip, with lunch aboard rather than a schedule.
