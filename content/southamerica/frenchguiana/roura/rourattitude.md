Rour'attitude — trading as Amazonie Découverte — runs the water base at Roura, hiring out canoes, kayaks and motorised boats and organising river journeys of a day or longer on the Oyack, the Comté and the Orapu. They also let lodges on the river bank.

It is the most straightforward way to get onto the rivers inland from Cayenne without owning a boat.
