GBT works the Oyapock — river transport and guiding, on foot and by pirogue — in the forest, the savannas and the falls around Saint-Georges, and across to the Brazilian bank at Oiapoque. The border here is a river people cross constantly, and a boatman who knows both sides is the practical way to see it.
