Niak Rafting is the only whitewater operator in French Guiana, running the Saut Maripa rapids on the Oyapock upstream of Saint-Georges in rafts and on hydrospeed boards, with walking trips as well. From fifty-three euros.

The Maripa falls are a serious set of rapids in a remote place, and the company is the reason it is possible to run them at all.
