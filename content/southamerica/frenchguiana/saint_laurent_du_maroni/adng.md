ADNG — Association pour la Découverte de la Nature Guyanaise — is an environmental education association on the Mana road out of Saint-Laurent, running kayak trips, walks, birdwatching, botany and insect outings. Its purpose is teaching rather than selling trips, and the outings are priced accordingly.
