Nature de Guyane runs walks and circuits on foot and by pirogue in the forest and savanna around Saint-Laurent, from a day out to trips of several days sleeping in carbets. Sixty euros upwards.

They are one of the few operators here that will take you walking rather than boating, which in this territory is the harder sell and the better way to see the forest floor.
