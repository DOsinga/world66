A microlight and gyrocopter school at Saint-Laurent that takes passengers on first flights over the river and the forest, from fifty euros. The Maroni from the air explains the geography of this coast better than any map: the river braided round its islands, Suriname on the far bank, and forest to the horizon in every other direction.
