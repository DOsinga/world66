Annai is a base rather than an attraction: a place to sleep, and a starting point for the North Rupununi villages, the Pakaraima foothills and the Iwokrama canopy walkway an hour north.

Connections are by the Georgetown–Lethem road or the airstrip, and both are slow. Allow more time than the map suggests.
