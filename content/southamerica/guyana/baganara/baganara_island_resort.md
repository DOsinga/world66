One hundred and eighty-seven acres of private island in the Essequibo, about five miles from [Bartica](/southamerica/guyana/bartica), with a white sand beach that is unusual on this river.

It works two ways: as an overnight stay — Evergreen sells one- and two-night escapes at USD 625 and USD 750 — or as the lunch stop on an Essequibo day tour, which is how most visitors experience it. The booking office is at Ogle airport rather than on the island.

The site lists the office landline and, separately, a mobile marked "WhatsApp available" — the latter is the one to message.
