Five acres on an island in the Lower Mazaruni just off Bartica, marketed as Guyana's only island resort. Kayaking, a water trampoline, beach volleyball, a spa, a lifeguarded pool, a restaurant and river lounges.

Rooms are priced in Guyanese dollars: superior suites from GYD 46,200 a night, a junior suite from 61,300, and the presidential "Yarapima" suite from 115,000.

It is also the lunch stop on several Essequibo day tours sold out of Georgetown, so you can see it without staying. No WhatsApp.
