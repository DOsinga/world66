A small resort on an island at Makouria in the Essequibo, whose pitch is that you do not have to go looking: howler monkeys, parrots and a long bird list are around the buildings themselves.

It doubles as the lunch stop on the Essequibo river day tour run out of Georgetown, which is how most people first see it.

Bookings share an office with Dagron Tours. Two numbers are advertised for calls and WhatsApp — +592 654-1974 and 650-4758.
