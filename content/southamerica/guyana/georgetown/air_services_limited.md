The country's long-standing domestic charter airline, which also runs its own Kaieteur day tour every Saturday and Sunday, roughly 11:30 to 5, at GYD 54,000 — about USD 270 — including the park pass and a snack. That makes it usually the cheapest scheduled way to the falls.

They also sell a Georgetown city tour from about USD 40, and charter across the interior airstrips, which is how most people reach the southern lodges.

Licensed by the Guyana Tourism Authority. Bookings by phone or email; no WhatsApp.
