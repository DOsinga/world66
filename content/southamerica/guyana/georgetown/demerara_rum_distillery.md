Demerara Rum Distillery gives Georgetown visitors a way into one of Guyana's defining exports. Demerara rum is tied to the sugar estates along the river, and a distillery visit makes that history more concrete than a bottle label ever can.

Tours and visitor arrangements should be confirmed in advance, since industrial sites do not always operate like museums. If you can arrange a visit, it pairs well with Georgetown's markets and colonial-era sights as a fuller look at the city's trade history.
