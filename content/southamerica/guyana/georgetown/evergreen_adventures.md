An Ogle-based operator that publishes what things cost, which is rarer here than it should be. The Kaieteur day tour is USD 350 for about five hours in a twelve-seat aircraft; adding Orinduik makes it USD 440 and six hours. Beyond the falls they run the Essequibo River day tour at USD 150, a Georgetown city tour at USD 130, the Mahaica River at USD 125, and overnight escapes to [Baganara](/southamerica/guyana/baganara) from USD 625.

Flights need a minimum of twelve passengers, so weekends fill first and midweek departures depend on numbers. Airport transfers cost extra, and alcohol is not permitted on the Kaieteur flight.

Licensed by the Guyana Tourism Authority. They answer WhatsApp on a number separate from the office switchboard.
