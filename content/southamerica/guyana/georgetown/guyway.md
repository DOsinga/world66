Launched in 2025 and the most substantial new thing in Georgetown transport: an all-electric ride-hailing app taking card, MMG, Apple Pay and Google Pay, with drivers vetted in person and no surge pricing.

The rates are published, which is rare here. A city ride is 2,000 Guyana dollars for the first three kilometres and 375 a kilometre after that; the run out to Cheddi Jagan is around 20,500 and takes 45 to 55 minutes off peak; Ogle is on the city rate, fifteen to twenty-five minutes.

There is no airport desk - the driver meets you at the terminal entrance with a PIN. You can sign up with a foreign mobile number, which not every local app allows.
