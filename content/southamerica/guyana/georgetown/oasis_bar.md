A cricket-themed sports bar in central Georgetown, cheerfully decorated with bats, photographs, and signed shirts from the West Indies cricket era. Big screens cover all the major matches and the place fills up whenever a West Indies game is on. Cold beer, basic bar food, and an enthusiastic local crowd.

Not a place for a quiet conversation, but if you want to watch cricket among Guyanese fans, it is the most reliable spot in the city. Especially good during ICC tournaments.
