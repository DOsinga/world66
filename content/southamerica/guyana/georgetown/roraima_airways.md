Founded in 1992 by Captain Gerry Gouveia, a former Guyana Defence Force chief pilot, and still family-run. The day trips pair Kaieteur either with Orinduik or with their own Arrowpoint Nature Resort up the Kamuni Creek. Prices are not published; third-party quotes put the Kaieteur trip near USD 280.

The group also owns the Roraima Residence Inn and the Duke Lodge, so they can package the ground end of a trip as well as the flying.

Note their website carries a WhatsApp link that belongs to the web design agency credited in the footer, not to the airline — book by phone or email.
