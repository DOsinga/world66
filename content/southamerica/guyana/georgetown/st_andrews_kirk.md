St. Andrew's Kirk is one of Georgetown's older church landmarks, a Presbyterian building whose timber construction fits the city's distinctive wooden architectural tradition. It is quieter than St. George's Cathedral, but it adds another layer to the capital's colonial and religious map.

Visit it as part of a central Georgetown walk rather than as a standalone expedition. The interest lies in the building's age, craft, and survival in a city where climate and fire have been hard on historic wooden structures.
