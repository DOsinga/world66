Founded in 1994 and now a full destination-management company for Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana and the Caribbean — which in practice means most multi-stop Rupununi itineraries are put together by them, whoever you booked through.

Their own Kaieteur day trip is USD 360, weekends only, six hours, with the flight from Ogle and guided walks to the three viewpoints. They are also joint operators of Atta Rainforest Lodge and the [Iwokrama canopy walkway](/southamerica/guyana/iwokrama/atta_rainforest_lodge), and work with Surama village and the Iwokrama Centre.

There is a duty-manager mobile for travellers already in the country: +592 624-2225. Licensed by the Guyana Tourism Authority.
