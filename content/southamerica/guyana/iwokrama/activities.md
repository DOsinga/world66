Visiting Iwokrama means staying in it: either the [river lodge](/southamerica/guyana/iwokrama/iwokrama_river_lodge) at Kurupukari on the Essequibo, or [Atta](/southamerica/guyana/iwokrama/atta_rainforest_lodge) in the forest beside the canopy walkway. Both are usually part of a longer itinerary running south to the Rupununi rather than a destination on their own.

The reserve publishes its rates, which is unusual here — and worth reading before you commit, because the transport costs more than the beds. The Georgetown minivan is about USD 180 each way; a chartered flight runs into the thousands.
