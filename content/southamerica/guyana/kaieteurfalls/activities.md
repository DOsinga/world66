Kaieteur is a single drop of 226 metres — about five times Niagara — falling off a sandstone plateau into a gorge with almost nobody watching. There is no road. Everyone arrives the same two ways: a day flight from Ogle, or the five-day overland hike in.

The flights are the ordinary choice and cost USD 270 to 375 depending on the operator, typically five to six hours door to door with an hour or two at the falls and guided walks to Boy Scout View, Rainbow View and Johnson's View. Aircraft are small and need to fill, so departures cluster at weekends.

Most operators will pair the trip with [Orinduik](/southamerica/guyana/orinduik_falls) for a few hundred dollars more, which is worth it — Kaieteur you can only look at, Orinduik you can swim in.
