Almost everything bookable in Lethem is either a border crossing or the rodeo. The Rupununi Rodeo at Easter fills the town well beyond its capacity and needs booking months out; the rest of the year Lethem is a staging post for the Kanukus, the southern ranches and the road into Brazil.
