A Lethem operator with its own accommodation at both ends of its range: Hotel Toucanna in Lethem, looking out at the Kanuku Mountains from Tabatinga, and Piraiba Lodge on the eastern bank of the Essequibo.

Trips are nature, adventure and fishing, and they also run the cross-border journeys that Lethem makes possible — into Brazil, and onward to Suriname, French Guiana and Venezuela.

Licensed by the Guyana Tourism Authority. Prices on enquiry; phone or email, no WhatsApp.
