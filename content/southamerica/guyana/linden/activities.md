Linden's tourism is small and mostly on the water — kayaking on Blue Lake, a flooded bauxite pit with a startling colour, and on Kara Kara Creek. Prices are in Guyanese dollars and a fraction of what the interior costs, which makes it a cheap day out from Georgetown.
