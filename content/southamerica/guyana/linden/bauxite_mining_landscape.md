  Linden was for most of the 20th century the centre of one of the world's most important bauxite industries. The ore deposits here were worked by the Demba company (later nationalised) and at their peak supplied aluminium to aircraft manufacturers during World War II.
---

The industrial infrastructure around Linden — conveyor towers, processing sheds, ore stockpiles — is visible from many parts of town and gives the place its distinctive working-class character. The reddish soil across the landscape is laterite enriched with bauxite, and the colour becomes particularly vivid after rain.

The mines themselves are not open to visitors, but the scale of the operation is apparent from the roads leading south and east out of town. If you ask around, locals can point you toward overlooks where the open-cast excavations are visible from a distance.
