  The Demerara River takes its name from an Arawak word meaning "land of the ripe fruit of the hard wood tree." For over a century, it carried bauxite barges from Linden north to the Atlantic coast — a journey of roughly 100km through some of the most remote rainforest in South America.
---

The waterfront is the most pleasant part of Linden for a walk or a sit-down. The river here is broad and calm, with fishing boats moored along the bank and the occasional larger vessel heading downstream toward Georgetown. In the late afternoon the light turns the reddish laterite banks an even deeper shade of orange.

Across the river you can see the Wismar district, once a separate town, connected to Linden by a small ferry crossing. The two banks together form the wider urban area, and most of the river traffic moves between them throughout the day.
