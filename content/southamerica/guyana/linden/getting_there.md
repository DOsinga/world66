---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Linden is about 100km south of [Georgetown](/southamerica/guyana/georgetown) on the Linden Highway, a well-maintained paved road that takes about two hours by minibus or shared taxi. Minibuses depart from the stabroek area of Georgetown throughout the day; the fare is modest.

There is no train service and no scheduled flights into Linden itself. If you are heading deeper into the interior toward the Rupununi or the Brazilian border, Linden is the last significant town on the paved road — from here the Linden-Lethem Highway runs south through forest and savannah for around 600km to [Lethem](/southamerica/guyana/lethem) on the Brazilian border.
