Mackenzie High School on Purple Heart Street is one of the oldest secondary schools in the Guyanese interior, its name preserving the colonial-era identity of the Mackenzie district before it was amalgamated with Linden. The school is a point of local pride and produced several Guyanese politicians and public figures.

For visitors, it is mainly of interest as an example of mid-century colonial institutional architecture and as a reminder of how the bauxite towns were carefully planned and provisioned by mining companies to attract and retain labour. The broader Mackenzie district has a more ordered, less improvised layout than Georgetown's older neighbourhoods.
