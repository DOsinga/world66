A family business started in 2021 by Dillon and Stephanie Ross, and the only Guyana Tourism Authority-licensed operator in Region Six — which is the whole reason Berbice is doable as anything other than a drive-through.

Half and full days on the Berbice River and Canje Creek: caiman spotting after dark, fishing, tubing, birding, overnight camping, and a historical run up to Fort Nassau, the old Dutch seat of Berbice now mostly swallowed by forest. Boats are licensed and insured.

They will also run trips on the Corentyne, at Rockstone and on the Essequibo. Their own wording is "Call or WhatsApp".
