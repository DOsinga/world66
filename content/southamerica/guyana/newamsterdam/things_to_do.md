---
title: Things to Do in New Amsterdam
type: section
---

New Amsterdam is primarily a transit stop and regional centre rather than a tourist destination, but it has some colonial-era wooden architecture worth a look. The [Mission Chapel](/southamerica/guyana/newamsterdam/mission_chapel) on Main Street is one of the oldest surviving buildings in Guyana. The [Berbice River](/southamerica/guyana/newamsterdam/berbice_river) is the defining feature of the town, and the ferry crossing from Rosignol is an experience in itself. The [Berbice Bridge](/southamerica/guyana/newamsterdam/berbice_bridge) a few kilometres north is the only fixed crossing of the river for those travelling by road.
