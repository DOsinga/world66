Orinduik is almost always sold as the second half of a Kaieteur flight rather than on its own: the aircraft continues 25 minutes west to the Ireng, you get an hour or so to swim in the pools between the jasper terraces, and then fly back to Georgetown.

Expect to pay roughly USD 90 to 100 on top of the Kaieteur-only fare. There is nothing at the falls — no lodge, no shop, no road — so what you have is the aircraft's schedule.
