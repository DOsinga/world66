The Rupununi is where Guyana stops being a coastal country. Savannah, gallery forest and the Kanuku mountains, a scatter of Amerindian villages, and lodges that are mostly owned by those villages rather than by anyone in Georgetown.

The four below are the ones worth knowing, and they are genuinely different from each other: Surama for the Makushi village experience, Rewa for arapaima on a fly rod, Caiman House to work a night shift with the caiman researchers, and Guyana Truly Wild for camps with nothing around them at all.

Nothing here is quick. Reckon on flying in to Annai or Lethem, or two days on the road from Georgetown.
