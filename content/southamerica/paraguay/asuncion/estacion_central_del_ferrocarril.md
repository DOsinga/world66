Estacion Central del Ferrocarril is Asuncion's old central railway station, a reminder that Paraguay was the first country in South America to run a steam-powered train. The station now houses a railway museum, with historic wagons and exhibits on the country's rail history.

The building itself is a major part of the visit. Its old platforms, carriages, and station architecture make it one of the more atmospheric heritage stops in central Asuncion.
