Manzana de la Rivera is a cultural complex occupying a full block of restored colonial houses opposite the Palacio de los López. Exhibitions, small museums, and events make it one of the easiest places to connect Asuncion's older architecture with its current arts scene.

The outdoor cafe is also a good reason to pause here, with one of the better close-up views of the presidential palace.
