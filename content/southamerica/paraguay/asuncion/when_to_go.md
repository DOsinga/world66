---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Asuncion is hot for most of the year. The coolest months are June and July, when temperatures drop to 15–18°C at night and the days are bearable — this is the best time to visit. From November to March, temperatures regularly reach 35–40°C with high humidity, making sightseeing genuinely uncomfortable. Rain falls year-round but is heaviest in March and October. There are no major festivals that should drive your timing, though the Holy Week processions in April are worth seeing if you happen to be there.
