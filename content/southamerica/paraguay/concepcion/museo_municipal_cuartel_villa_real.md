Museo Municipal del Cuartel de la Villa Real occupies a restored 19th-century building that once served as barracks and a command post for Mariscal Lopez's army. The colonial-era structure is part of the appeal, with thick walls and a strong sense of local history.

Inside, the museum covers Concepcion's military and civic past. It is a useful stop for understanding why this river city mattered in Paraguay's north.
