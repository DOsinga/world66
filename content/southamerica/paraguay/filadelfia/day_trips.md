title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

**Loma Plata**, the slightly older Menno-colony town 25 km east, is the centre of the Trébol dairy industry and has its own colony museum — a useful complement to Filadelfia's. **Neuland**, the third Mennonite colony, lies a similar distance to the south.

**Defensores del Chaco National Park**, 3–4 hours' drive north, is the largest protected area in Paraguay and one of the most isolated places on the continent. Visiting really needs a full-day or multi-day trip with a local guide and a 4WD; the rewards are jaguars, giant armadillos, and a landscape that feels genuinely untouched.

The indigenous villages of the **Enlhet** and **Nivaclé** communities surround the Mennonite colonies; some can be visited with local NGO guides who can also explain the complex history of the relationship between the colonies and the original inhabitants.
