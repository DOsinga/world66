**Read this before using it.** World66 deliberately does not do hotels, and Hotel Florida is first and foremost a hotel. It earns a place here only because it hires bicycles and arranges horse riding, because it is owned by the Fernheim co-operative rather than by anyone selling you a tour, and because it is the most reliable point of contact in Filadelfia for a traveller who has arrived without a plan. If that is not enough, leave it out — it is the weakest provider in this report on the "is it bookable" test.

What it is: a low, shaded building on Avenida Hindenburg in the middle of Filadelfia, with wide rooms, cool galleries, a garden, a pool and a restaurant that does both Paraguayan and international food. Their own "Turismo" page is a decent short briefing on the Chaco — the salt lagoons at Campo María, the palm stands and the thorn forest, the three Filadelfia museums (Koloniehaus, Knelsen-Haus and the Jakob Unger natural-history collection), and the Chaco War sites at Fortín Boquerón, Isla Poí and Toledo.

For actual guiding they send you across the road: the tourist office sits opposite the hotel in the Menno Simons Hof park, and they publish a downloadable town map. Their events calendar is worth knowing if you are timing a trip — the Expo Pioneros del Chaco and the Neuland rodeo in May, the Trébol rodeo in August.
