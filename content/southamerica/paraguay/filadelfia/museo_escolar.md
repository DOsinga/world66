Museo Escolar preserves the old school building that opened in 1936 in Colonia Fernheim. Its exhibits show what early education looked like in the Chaco, with old furniture, books, classroom materials, photographs, and records from nearby settlements.

The museum is a small but useful companion to Filadelfia's other Mennonite history museums, especially for understanding how the colony built everyday institutions in a remote environment.
