---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Filadelfia is small and the main interest is the place itself — a slice of Plautdietsch-speaking northern Europe transplanted into the South American thornscrub. Spend a morning at the museum complex, walk the main avenue, drop into the Fernheim cooperative supermarket for a look at the dairy aisle, and use the town as a base for wildlife trips into the surrounding Chaco.
