Laguna Blanca is 147 hectares of spring-fed water lying on white calcified sand, clear enough to see the bottom at seven metres, which is not a thing that happens anywhere else in Paraguay. Ecotur Laguna Blanca is the operation that sells access to it: camping under the stars, cabins, a booking form that asks how many adults, how many children between six and eleven, and how many under five, and then hands the whole thing to WhatsApp to finish.

They describe themselves as an ecological destination in the heart of San Pedro sitting on the largest recharge zone of the Guaraní aquifer, and they are open that the form is a request and not a confirmation. Write to `+595 976 401 792` for a booking or `+595 983 381 656` for questions; there is no telephone number on the site, only those two.

Two caveats. Their certificate expired in March 2026, so your browser will refuse the site until they renew it — the WhatsApp numbers above are the way in. And they publish no street address and no coordinates, so any pin on a world66 page is the lake, not their gate; the road in from Santa Rosa del Aguaray is unpaved and slow after rain.
