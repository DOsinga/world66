This is the most useful thing to have appeared in the Paraguayan missions in years, and it is four rooms. A couple who spent a working life in international tourism moved to San Cosme, bought a house on the corner of the Marina road and calle San Francisco, and now run it as a lodge with a restaurant, a pool, a jacuzzi, a library, satellite internet off Starlink and solar power. They speak English and Spanish and say they are working on Guaraní.

What matters for a guide is the list of things they will arrange, because nobody else in these villages arranges anything. An English-speaking local guide. Private transfers. Excursions to the San Cosme mission next door, to Trinidad and Jesús up the road, and across the border to the Esteros del Iberá. A visit to the Mbya Guaraní community beside the village, which is the kind of thing that needs somebody with standing locally to set up. A trip down the Paraná on a working barge. All of it is priced separately and all of it needs asking for in advance.

Rooms were Gs 430.000 in 2026, Gs 90.000 for an extra bed, and there are only four of them, so this is not a drop-in. The number on the site takes messages only — WhatsApp it.

Worth saying plainly, since the report has been hard on Paraguayan websites: this one works, it is current, it prices its rooms, it marks its paid extras with asterisks and it explains what it is. That is rarer here than it should be.
