**Read the caveat first.** World66 does not do hotels, and this is a hotel. It is here because of what it sells besides beds: a guided tour of the entire Jesuit-Guaraní circuit, run by the people who live in one of the reductions, and because in a village of 3,000 people there is no other contact point. If you would rather not carry a hotel into `content/`, the tour is the thing to keep and the tariff is the thing to drop.

Santa María de Fe is the quietest of the mission towns and has the best of the Guaraní baroque museums, a couple of dozen polychrome saints in a building on the plaza. The hotel is on the corner of that plaza, between the church and the museum, and its whole proposition is that you can walk out of the door into a reduction that is still a working village rather than an archaeological site.

The tour is the reason this entry exists. Fourteen days, roughly £1,000 or $1,700 a head not counting flights, and they will meet you at Asunción and put you on the plane at Foz do Iguaçu at the other end. In between: Yaguarón, the only complete church left standing from the reduction period; a week in Santa María itself with the campesino co-operatives and the local social projects; the four baroque museums at Santa María, San Ignacio, Santa Rosa and Santiago; San Cosme y Damián with its observatory; the ruins at Jesús and Trinidad including the illuminated night walk; San Ignacio Miní across the Argentine border with its *son et lumière*; and a day at Iguazú. Shorter visits are arranged too — their Tours page says plainly "come for a day".

Practical: one direct bus a day from Asunción (the Mariscal López, 11:45), or any Ruta 1 bus to San Ignacio and fifteen minutes by taxi from there; they will meet you in San Ignacio if you ask. Rooms in 2025 were Gs 180.000 single to Gs 440.000 for five. Everything is arranged by WhatsApp or email with Milciades Mancuello — there is no booking engine, and the site has not been restyled since 2017.
