The modest white church on the plaza is one of the older buildings in Santaní and the clear focal point of the town's spiritual and ceremonial life. Inside, the whitewashed walls and painted wooden ceiling create a simple, cool interior that is a welcome relief on hot afternoons. The church is dedicated to Saint Stanislaus of Kraków, a legacy of the Jesuit mission circuit that once stretched through eastern Paraguay and into what is now Brazil and Argentina.

Mass is held daily, and the church comes fully alive during the Fiesta Patronal de San Estanislao in May. If you are here around that time, expect processions, music, food stalls spilling out from the plaza, and the kind of communal enthusiasm that otherwise only surfaces at football matches.
