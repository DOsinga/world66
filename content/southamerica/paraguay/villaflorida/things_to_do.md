---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Villa Florida is essentially a one-river town. The Tebicuary River is the reason to stop — for sport fishing in season (August to November, dorado and surubí the main quarry), for the broad sandy beach that forms along the western bank in the dry months, or simply for the view from the Ruta 1 bridge. The November Festival del Pacú is the town's main annual event. Outside fishing season there is very little to do beyond a lunch stop on the way south.
