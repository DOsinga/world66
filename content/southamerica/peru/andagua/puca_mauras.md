Puca Mauras is one of the named cones in the Valley of the Volcanoes, rising northeast of Andagua in the black volcanic landscape that makes the area worth the long trip. It is not a casual town stroll, but it gives the valley a clear destination beyond the viewpoints.

Ask locally before setting out. Trails, transport, and conditions are not packaged for quick tourism, and altitude makes even moderate distances feel longer.
