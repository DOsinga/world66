---
title: 'Bars and Cafes'
type: section
---

Arequipa's drinking culture is mellow by Peruvian standards. The cafe scene is strong — the south Peruvian coffee industry has had a quiet renaissance over the past decade — and there are good specialty coffee bars around the Plaza de Armas. Pisco is the spirit to drink, and the classic Arequipa cocktail is the **chilcano de pisco** (pisco, ginger beer, lime). For something more local, ask for **chicha de jora**, the fermented corn beer that has been brewed in the region since pre-Columbian times.

The nightlife concentrates on Calle San Francisco, immediately north of the plaza, and the side streets running east — Calle Santa Catalina and Calle Zela are the densest. Live música criolla turns up at half a dozen restaurants on weekend evenings.
