Mansion del Fundador is a country house on the outskirts of Arequipa, associated with the colonial landholding world that grew around the city. Its rooms, courtyards, and rural setting offer a different historical mood from the white-stone churches and monasteries downtown.

It is best visited with transport or as part of a countryside circuit, since it sits outside the compact historic center. The appeal is strongest for travellers who want to understand Arequipa's colonial society beyond the main plaza.
