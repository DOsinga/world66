The Señor de Burgos Church stands on the Plaza de la Independencia in the La Laguna quarter of Chachapoyas, a few blocks from the main plaza. Built in the 17th century, it is one of the city's older colonial structures — an adobe church with a modest facade that opens into an interior containing colonial-era religious paintings and carvings that have received little academic attention despite their age and quality.

The church serves the La Laguna neighbourhood and remains an active parish. The surrounding quarter — slightly off the tourist circuit that concentrates around the Plaza de Armas — retains a more everyday character, with residents going about their business around a working neighbourhood church rather than a heritage display. Combined with a walk to the Pozo de Yanayacu a few blocks away, the La Laguna quarter makes for a pleasant alternative half-hour to the main plaza circuit.
