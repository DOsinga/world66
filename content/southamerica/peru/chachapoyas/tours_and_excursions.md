---
title: "Tours and Excursions"
type: section
---

Most travellers to Chachapoyas join guided tours for the major sites — the distances and terrain make independent visits to Kuélap, Karajía, and Gocta harder to manage without local transport knowledge.

Local tour agencies on and around the Plaza de Armas in Chachapoyas offer day and half-day tours to all the main sites. The typical packages:

- **Kuélap day trip**: Bus or van to the cable car, guided visit to the fortress. Half-day in terms of site time, full day with travel. About 4–5 hours in total from Chachapoyas.
- **Karajía and Gocta**: Often combined in a single day — the sarcophagi in the morning, the waterfall hike in the afternoon. Requires an early start.
- **Revash funerary houses**: A lesser-visited but rewarding site — small mausolea built into cliff faces south of Chachapoyas, dating from the same culture as Karajía.

Expect to pay USD 25–50 per person for day tours from town. A private vehicle with a driver-guide gives more flexibility, especially if you want to combine multiple sites or spend more time at each.
