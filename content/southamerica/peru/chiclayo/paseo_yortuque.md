Paseo Yortuque is a long landscaped walk lined with sculptures inspired by Moche and Lambayeque mythology. The canal, trees, and figures make it one of Chiclayo's more distinctive public spaces.

It is best treated as a short walk rather than a major monument. Come in daylight, when the sculptures are easier to read and the path is livelier.
