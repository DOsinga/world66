One of the largest operators on the SERNANP licensed list, and the one most travellers end up with. The classic Inca Trail runs four days and three nights, in groups or private; there is a two-day version with either camping or a hotel night in Aguas Calientes; and a women-only departure led by female guides and porters, which is unusual here.

Beyond the Trail they run Salkantay with sky domes, Lares, Ausangate and Rainbow Mountain, Choquequirao and Huchuy Qosqo. The porter-welfare pitch is not decoration — weight limits, proper kit and pay are what the company is known for locally.

Licensed by SERNANP for the Inca Trail and registered with MINCETUR at the Heladeros address.
