The outfit to talk to if you would rather not walk the Inca Trail with everyone else. Rafting on the Apurímac and Urubamba, mountain and e-biking in the Sacred Valley, kayaking and paddleboarding, and genuinely worked-out family itineraries.

They are a long-standing SERNANP-licensed operator, MINCETUR-registered on Collasuyo, and sell into the UK and US markets — there is a North American toll-free line as well as the Cusco office.

An emergency mobile is published separately for people already out on a trip.
