A Cusco-based Inca Trail operator running the classic four-day and the short two-day routes, plus Salkantay and the Sacred Valley. Like Alpaca, the porter story is the differentiator — they were among the earlier companies here to publish what their crews are paid and carry.

Booking is by WhatsApp as much as email; the number is advertised as answered 24/7, which in a town where treks fill and drop at short notice is worth something.
