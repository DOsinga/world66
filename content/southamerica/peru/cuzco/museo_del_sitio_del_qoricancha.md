Museo del Sitio del Qoricancha is a small underground museum beside Qorikancha, with displays on pre-Columbian cultures and Inca ceramics, textiles, mummies, and cranial modification.

It is compact and the English explanations are limited, but it adds useful context before or after visiting Qorikancha itself.
