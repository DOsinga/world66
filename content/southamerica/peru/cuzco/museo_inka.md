Museo Inka has a broad collection of pre-Inca, Inca, and colonial objects, making it one of Cuzco's stronger museums for historical context. It is especially useful before or after visiting the city's major Inca and colonial sites.

Labels are in Spanish and English, though the English text may not always match the Spanish descriptions exactly.
