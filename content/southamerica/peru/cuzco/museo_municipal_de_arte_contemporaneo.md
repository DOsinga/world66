The Museo Municipal de Arte Contemporáneo sits in the Municipal Palace on Plaza Regocijo, close to Cuzco's main tourist circuit. Its changing exhibitions are a good counterpoint to the city's colonial churches and Inca stonework, especially if you are using the tourist ticket and want a short indoor stop near the centre.
