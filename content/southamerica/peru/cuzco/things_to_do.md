---
title: "Things to Do in Cuzco"
type: section
---

Cuzco's core sights divide between the [Inca ruins](/southamerica/peru/cuzco/sacsayhuaman) on the hills above town and the colonial buildings built directly on Inca foundations in the city centre. The [Qorikancha](/southamerica/peru/cuzco/coricancha) is the must-see combination: Inca stonework with a Spanish monastery on top. The [Cusco Cathedral](/southamerica/peru/cuzco/cathedral) dominates the Plaza de Armas. Further out, [Tambomachay](/southamerica/peru/cuzco/tambomachay) and [Q'enqo](/southamerica/peru/cuzco/qenqo) are stops on the circuit of Inca sites ringing the city.
