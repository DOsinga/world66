Huancabamba is one of the most important centres of curanderismo in South America, a syncretic healing tradition that blends indigenous Andean plant medicine with Catholic symbolism and pre-Columbian ritual. The most respected healers — known as curanderos or maestros — live in the mountain villages above Huancabamba rather than in the town itself, and work primarily at night at the sacred lakes.

Clients arrive from across Peru, Ecuador, and further afield seeking cures for physical illness, emotional problems, bad luck, and spiritual afflictions. A session typically involves ritual bathing in a sacred lake, the ingestion of San Pedro cactus (a mescaline-containing plant used medicinally for millennia), and a ceremony in which the curandero works with a mesa — an altar of ritual objects specific to each healer. Arranging access requires time and local contacts; asking at your accommodation in Huancabamba is the most practical starting point. Treat the tradition with respect — this is an active spiritual and medical practice, not a tourist attraction.
