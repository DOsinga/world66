---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**From Piura:** Buses run daily from Piura's bus terminals to Huancabamba. The journey takes 4–6 hours on a mountain road in variable condition. Departures are typically in the early morning and late afternoon; check locally for current operators and times. Several companies serve the route.

**There is no airport.** Huancabamba is accessible only by road. The only connection is to Piura; continuing east toward Jaen involves additional rough road travel.

**Note:** Road conditions can be affected by rain and landslides, particularly between December and April. Confirm current conditions before travelling, especially in the rainy season.
