---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

The dry season (May to October) is the best time to visit. Roads are more reliable, the mountain hike to the lagunas is less hazardous, and the views are clearer. The wet season (November to April) brings rain that can make the road from Piura impassable for periods and the high-altitude laguna trail very muddy.

The lagunas and curandero ceremonies occur year-round, as pilgrims come regardless of season, but dry-season conditions make the visit far more practical.
