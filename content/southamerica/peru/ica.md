Ica sits in the middle of [Peru's](/southamerica/peru) wine country, and this is where Pisco — the grape brandy behind the Pisco Sour — is produced. Several bodegas can be visited, and a tour of the wineries like [Bodega Tacama](/southamerica/peru/ica/bodega_tacama), [Vista Alegre](/southamerica/peru/ica/bodega_vista_alegre), or [El Catador](/southamerica/peru/ica/bodega_el_catador) makes a pleasant half-day excursion.

The [Museo Regional](/southamerica/peru/ica/museo_regional) has mummies, ceramics, and elongated skulls from the Paracas, Nazca, and Inca cultures, though as usual the best pieces have been shipped off to [Lima](/southamerica/peru/lima). The strange [Cabrera Museum](/southamerica/peru/ica/cabrera_museum) houses thousands of carved Ica Stones, and the [Santuario del Señor de Luren](/southamerica/peru/ica/santuario_senor_de_luren) is the city's pilgrimage church.

The real draw is just outside town: the [Huacachina Oasis](/southamerica/peru/ica/huacachina_oasis), a lagoon ringed by enormous sand dunes where you can go [sandboarding](/southamerica/peru/ica/sandboarding) and take dune buggy rides. [Pisco](/southamerica/peru/pisco) is about an hour away by bus, and Lima is a four-hour ride north.
