La Isla de los Monos is a wildlife stop on the Amazon near Iquitos, known for rescued monkeys that move freely around the forested island. Visitors may also see sloths, coatis, toucans, and macaws, depending on current rescue and rehabilitation work.

It is usually visited by boat as part of a day trip from Iquitos or as an add-on to a lodge itinerary. Keep glasses, loose bags, and food secure, since the monkeys are used to people and can be bold.
