---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

There are three ways to reach Kuelap from the valley below:

The teleférico (cable car), opened in 2017, is the most convenient option. It runs from Nuevo Tingo and takes about 20 minutes, offering spectacular views over the cloud forest and valley on the way up. This is the option most visitors now use. Check locally for current operating hours and ticket prices, as these change.

A bus service runs from Tingo up the steep dirt road to the site, taking around two hours. The road is narrow and the drops are considerable — not for the faint-hearted, but the views are extraordinary. This option is more weather-dependent than the cable car.

You can also hike from Tingo, a climb of three to six hours depending on your pace and fitness. The trail is well-established. It is hard work but genuinely rewarding, and gives you the site to yourself before the day visitors arrive.

Most visitors reach Kuelap as a day trip from Chachapoyas, which is two to three hours away by road and serves as the main base for the region. Chachapoyas is connected to the coast by bus from Chiclayo (around ten hours) and to the south by bus from Cajamarca. Organised tours from Chachapoyas to Kuelap are widely available and take the guesswork out of the logistics.
