A basic café and restaurant operates near the site entrance, serving set lunches and simple Peruvian dishes — rice, beans, grilled chicken, that sort of thing. It fills up quickly when tour groups arrive around midday, so eat early or late if you want a seat. Prices are reasonable; quality is functional rather than memorable. Most visitors treat it as a fallback rather than a destination.
