Ducha del Diablo is one of the waterfall stops used by local tours from La Merced. It is less immediate than Catarata del Tirol, but it fits well into a day of Chanchamayo scenery if you have transport.

Expect wet paths, changing water levels, and basic facilities. The appeal is the lush setting rather than any formal visitor infrastructure.
