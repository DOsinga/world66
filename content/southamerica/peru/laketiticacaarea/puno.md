Puno sits at 3,827 metres above sea level on the western shore of Lake Titicaca, the world's highest navigable lake, on the border between Peru and Bolivia. It is a fairly unremarkable Andean city in its own right — functional, concrete, and somewhat battered around the edges — but it is the undisputed gateway to one of South America's most extraordinary landscapes, and travellers who give it more than a single night tend to be rewarded.

The altitude hits most visitors hard. Headaches, fatigue, and breathlessness are common for the first day or two. The local remedy is coca tea (mate de coca), available everywhere and genuinely helpful, though what it really takes is rest and patience. Give yourself a day to acclimatise before heading out onto the lake, and consider arriving by bus from Cusco rather than flying directly, to give your body time to adjust.

The city's main draw is the lake itself. [Uros Islands](/southamerica/peru/laketiticacaarea/sights/uros_islands) — floating reed platforms inhabited by the Uros people — are the most visited attraction, touristy but genuinely unusual. Further out, [Taquile Island](/southamerica/peru/laketiticacaarea/sights/taquili_island) offers a glimpse of Andean community life that is largely unchanged by tourism, with textiles made by hand using pre-Columbian techniques. The [Yavari](/southamerica/peru/laketiticacaarea/puno/yavari) — a Victorian-era iron steamship still moored on the lake — is one of the more unexpected sights in South America.

The city centre has a few worthwhile stops: the [Cathedral of Puno](/southamerica/peru/laketiticacaarea/puno/cathedral_puno), built in the Spanish Baroque style, faces a pleasant main square. The [Arco Deustua](/southamerica/peru/laketiticacaarea/puno/arco_deustua) commemorates the battles of Junín and Ayacucho. And the [Sillustani funerary towers](/southamerica/peru/laketiticacaarea/sights/sillustani_funerar), an hour outside the city, are among the most atmospheric pre-Columbian ruins in Peru, set on a peninsula above a small lake with a dramatic Andean backdrop.
