---
title: Getting Around
type: section
---

The city centre is walkable. The main pedestrian street, Jirón Lima, runs from the Arco Deustua down towards the port, and most of the key sights are within a 20-minute walk of each other. The altitude means that even short uphill walks can feel more demanding than expected.

Taxis (both metered and negotiated) are plentiful and cheap within the city. Mototaxis (three-wheeled motorcycle taxis) are everywhere and cost even less, though they are slower and offer less protection in rain.

**Lake transport:** Boats to the Uros Islands, Taquile, and Amantani leave from the Puno Port (Muelle de Puno) at the end of Avenida El Puerto. Tour operators in the city can arrange day trips or multi-day tours. Independent travellers can also negotiate directly with boatmen at the port, though tour operators often provide useful guides for the islands.

For Sillustani, taxis or tour agency minibuses make the 35 km journey in about 45 minutes. A shared taxi from the centre costs around 30–40 soles.
