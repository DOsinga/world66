---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

**By bus:** The most common approach is the bus from Cusco (about 6–7 hours) or Arequipa (about 6 hours). Several companies run the Cusco route with tourist-class buses, and this is the recommended approach if you are coming from high altitude anyway — it allows gradual acclimatisation as you cross the Altiplano. The bus passes through Raqchi, Sicuani, and La Raya Pass (4,335m) before descending to the lake.

**By rail:** The PeruRail service between Cusco and Puno is a scenic option, covering the journey in about 10 hours with a panoramic route across the Altiplano. It runs a few times weekly and is considerably more expensive than the bus, but the views are outstanding.

**From Bolivia:** The border crossing at Desaguadero connects Puno to La Paz by bus (about 4 hours total, with a stop for border formalities). Alternatively, boats cross the lake from Copacabana, Bolivia, with bus connections. Some travellers choose the lake route to absorb the landscape gradually.

**By air:** The nearest airport is in Juliaca, 44 km north of Puno. Flights connect to Lima and occasionally Cusco and Arequipa. Taxis and collectivos run between Juliaca airport and Puno city centre.
