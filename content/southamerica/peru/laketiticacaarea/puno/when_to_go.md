---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Puno's altitude (3,827m) means temperatures are cool year-round — expect cold nights even in the height of summer. The dry season (May to October) brings clearer skies and colder nights; the wet season (November to April) brings afternoon rain and the risk of flooding on the lake's access roads.

**Candelaria Festival (February):** The Festival of the Virgin of Candelaria is one of the largest in South America and fills the city with elaborate costumed dances, brass bands, and processions for two weeks around 2 February. It is extraordinary to witness — 200 or more dance troupes representing different communities, each with different costumes, music, and traditions. Book accommodation months in advance and expect prices to triple.

**Dry season (May–October)** is the most comfortable time for outdoor activities and lake excursions. June and July bring Peruvian school holidays with more domestic tourism. August sees the city's civic celebrations (anniversary of Puno's founding).

The wet season isn't a disaster — the lake is at its highest and most dramatic, and tourist numbers are lower — but some roads become impassable and the cold, damp weather can be genuinely punishing.
