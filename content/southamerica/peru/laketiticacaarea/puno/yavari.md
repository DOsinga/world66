The SS Yavari is a Victorian-era iron-hulled steamship that has been moored on Lake Titicaca since 1870 and is now preserved as a floating museum. It is one of the most improbable sights in the Andes: a full-sized 19th-century gunboat, with brass fittings, a small steam engine, and a painted hull, sitting on the world's highest navigable lake in the middle of the South American continent.

The ship operated as a Peruvian Navy gunboat for many years and later as a passenger ferry. It was decommissioned and left to rust before being rescued by a British charity in the 1980s. The restoration has been slow but the vessel is genuinely seaworthy — it was even sailed briefly for a circumnavigation of Lake Titicaca in more recent years. Visitors can tour the ship's cramped interiors, see the engine room, and walk the deck. The views across the lake are excellent.
