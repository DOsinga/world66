Lambayeque is a small town 11km north of [Chiclayo](/southamerica/peru/chiclayo), and the reason to come here is its outstanding museums. Most visitors treat it as a half-day trip from Chiclayo — regular minivans make the short journey.

The [Royal Tombs of Sipán Museum](/southamerica/peru/lambayeque/royal_tombs_of_sip) is the star attraction, housing spectacular gold, silver, and turquoise treasures excavated from the nearby tomb of the Lord of Sipán, a Moche ruler buried around 250 AD. The collection is one of the finest pre-Columbian archaeological finds in South America. The older [Brüning Museum](/southamerica/peru/lambayeque/bruning_museum), a short walk away, covers the full sweep of the region's pre-Columbian cultures from Chavín through Moche, Sicán, and Chimú.

The [Plaza de Armas](/southamerica/peru/lambayeque/plaza_de_armas_lambayeque) in the centre of town is worth a look for the Casa de la Logia, whose 64-metre colonial balcony is one of the longest in Peru. For those with more time, the [Chotuna-Chornancap Archaeological Complex](/southamerica/peru/lambayeque/chotuna_chornancap) 12km northwest adds a rewarding detour tied to the founding legend of the Lambayeque civilisation.
