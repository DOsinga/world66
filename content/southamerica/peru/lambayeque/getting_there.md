---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Lambayeque has no airport or train service. The usual way to get there is by combi (shared minivan) from Chiclayo. These depart frequently from the corner of Avenida Balta and Vicente de la Vega in Chiclayo, take around 20 minutes, and cost very little. Taxis from Chiclayo take the same time and cost more but are more convenient if you are coming from the airport or with luggage.

Chiclayo is the regional hub and the practical base. The city is served by José Abelardo Quiñones Gonzales Airport with regular flights from Lima.
