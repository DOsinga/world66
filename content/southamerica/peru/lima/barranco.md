**Barranco** sits between Miraflores and the Pacific, two kilometres south of the centre of Lima nightlife. Built as a 19th-century seaside summer colony, it preserves a row of pastel-coloured **casas-quinta** — Italian-influenced villas with verandas and palm gardens — that have been converted into restaurants, boutique hotels, and galleries. The famous **Puente de los Suspiros** ("Bridge of Sighs"), a small wooden footbridge over a ravine, is the symbolic heart of the district.

Barranco is the right place to spend an evening. Cocktail bars (**Ayahuasca**, **Carnaval**), restaurants (**Isolina**, **Cosme**, **Central** and **Kjölle**, which are just over the line), galleries (**MAC Lima** sits on the clifftop nearby) and live music. By day it is quieter than Miraflores — strollers between the Plaza Municipal and the bridge.
