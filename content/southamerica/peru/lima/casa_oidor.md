The **Casa del Oidor** is a corner building on the Plaza Mayor that preserves the best surviving example of Lima's distinctive carved-wood "Moorish" closed balconies — the dark green latticed *miradores* that once allowed women of the upper classes to look out onto the street without being seen. The building was the home of one of the senior magistrates (*oidores*) of the colonial *Audiencia*.

It is not generally open to visitors but is one of the most photographed buildings on the plaza. The balcony detail and the heavy stone door surround give a sense of what most of the centre's buildings looked like before earthquake reconstructions and 20th-century alterations.
