The **Fortaleza del Real Felipe**, at the port of **Callao** about half an hour west of central Lima, was the largest Spanish military installation in the Americas. Begun in 1747 after a tsunami destroyed the previous Callao defenses, it is a vast pentagonal stone fortress with five bastions, designed to mount nearly 200 cannons against pirate and naval attacks.

The fortress has been continuously garrisoned since it opened. Today, **Peruvian Army museum tours** wind through casemates, the **Torreón del Rey** central keep, and the chambers where royalist forces made their final stand in 1826. The Callao district outside the walls can be dangerous; visit by taxi or organised tour and do not wander.
