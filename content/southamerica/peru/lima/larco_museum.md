The finest pre-Columbian collection in the Americas, housed in a baroque 18th-century mansion in the Pueblo Libre district. The Larco collection spans 5,000 years: Mochica, Inca, Nazca, and Chimú artefacts including extraordinary gold work. The open-access storage rooms hold 45,000 items on open shelves. The museum's most notorious section is the erotic pottery gallery — Mochica ceramics depicting sexual acts with remarkable directness. The garden café is one of the best lunch spots in Lima.
