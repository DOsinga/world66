Luis Miro Quesada Garland Hall is a municipal exhibition space in the centre of Miraflores, close to Parque Kennedy. The galleries show mainly Peruvian contemporary art, with occasional international work, and the programme changes regularly.

It is an easy cultural stop while walking Miraflores. Check what is on before making a special trip, since the appeal depends heavily on the current exhibition.
