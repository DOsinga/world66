**MATE** is the personal museum of **Mario Testino**, the Peruvian fashion photographer best known internationally for his portraits of celebrities and the British royal family. Housed in a restored 19th-century *casa-quinta* in Barranco, the small but well-presented galleries include rotating exhibitions of fashion photography (Testino's and others'), a permanent space of his Peruvian "Alta Moda" portraits of Andean women in traditional dress, and the famous **Diana Room** of his 1997 Princess Diana shoot.

The shop and café in the courtyard are popular meeting spots — and a useful place to interrupt a walk through Barranco. Closed Mondays.
