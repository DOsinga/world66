The **Mercado de Surquillo** (Mercado N° 1) is the working market that supplies most of Lima's top restaurants — Gastón Acurio, Virgilio Martínez, and the rest send buyers here at dawn. Stalls heap up Peruvian potatoes (the country has over 4,000 varieties), Andean grains (*quinoa*, *kiwicha*, *kañiwa*), jungle fruit, fresh ají chillies in a dozen varieties, and an entire wall of fish.

The eastern half is dry goods and household; the western half is fresh produce. A few **picanterías** at the back of the building serve cheap, excellent lunches of *jalea* and *ceviche* to porters and shoppers. Most visitors come on a food tour, but a solo walk-through is rewarding too. Quietest after 11am.
