The Museo de Sitio Naval Submarino Abtao lets you go inside a real Cold War-era submarine moored in Callao. Built in 1952 by the American Electric Boat Company and bought by Peru two years later, the Abtao is compact, metal, and claustrophobic in the way a good submarine visit should be.
