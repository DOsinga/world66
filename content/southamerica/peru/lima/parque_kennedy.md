**Parque Kennedy** — named for the American president after his 1962 visit — is the social centre of Miraflores, a triangle of flower beds, palm trees, and benches between the **Iglesia La Virgen Milagrosa** and the main commercial streets. The cats are the most famous residents: dozens of them, looked after by neighbours and an animal-welfare group, lounge on the lawns and benches and largely ignore tourists.

In the evenings the southern half of the park hosts open-air **artisan markets**, **chess players**, occasional live bands, and a steady drift of strollers between cafés. The **Pasaje Cavenecio** street art alley runs off one corner. Easy to reach from anywhere in Miraflores and a useful navigation point.
