The **tomb of Francisco Pizarro** sits in a glass-fronted side chapel near the cathedral's main entrance, holding the embalmed remains and a lead casket. The display was reconfigured after a 1977 forensic study established that the body shown to visitors for centuries was not Pizarro's at all — the real remains, identified by injuries consistent with his 1541 assassination by political rivals, were found in a sealed lead box in the crypt below.

The chapel is small and easily overlooked; the painted ceiling above shows scenes from Pizarro's life and conquest of the Inca. Information panels (in Spanish and English) cover the dramatic story of the mistaken identity.
