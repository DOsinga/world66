On the north bank of the Rímac river, across the colonial **Puente de Piedra**, the **Plaza de Toros de Acho** opened in 1766 and is the oldest bullring in the Americas — the second-oldest in the world, after Seville. Built by Viceroy Manuel de Amat y Junyent, it holds about 13,000 spectators in a deep, narrow octagonal ring of red-painted wooden seats.

The main **Feria del Señor de los Milagros** runs through October each year, with fights every Sunday. Outside the bullfighting season, the small **Museo Taurino** on the site displays costumes, posters, and Goya prints connected to Peruvian bullfighting. The surrounding **Rímac** district is rough — go and return by taxi.
