**Pueblo Libre** is a low-key residential district halfway between the centre and Miraflores. Most visitors come for the **Museo Larco**, the great pre-Columbian collection housed in an old viceregal mansion on a quiet side street. The neighbourhood has its own small **Plaza Bolívar**, the **Museo Nacional de Arqueología, Antropología e Historia** (covering 13,000 years of Peruvian history), and a couple of old-fashioned *picanterías* serving regional creole food.

The historic **Antigua Taberna Queirolo** on the plaza has been pouring *pisco* and *chichas* since 1880 — a good lunch stop after Larco. The neighbourhood is residential and safe and easily reached by taxi in 15 minutes from Miraflores.
