There is no road to Machu Picchu. You arrive by train to Aguas Calientes and then by bus up the switchbacks, or you walk in — the Inca Trail from km 82, or the cheap route along the railway from the Hidroeléctrica station.

Entry is by timed ticket on a fixed circuit and sells out in high season, so this is one of the few places in Peru where booking ahead is not optional. Several of the operators here bundle the ticket with the train, which is the simplest way to do it.

The trekking companies that bring people in over the passes are based up in [Cusco](/southamerica/peru/cuzco).
