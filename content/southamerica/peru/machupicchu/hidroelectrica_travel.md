The cheap route to Machu Picchu, and a good day out in its own right: a long minibus ride over the pass and down to the Hidroeléctrica station, then about two and a half hours on foot along the railway line beside the Urubamba to Aguas Calientes. Cloud forest, orchids, and the first sight of Huayna Picchu above you.

It is not faster and not more comfortable than the train. It is a fraction of the price, and the walk is the point.
