There is no road to Machu Picchu, so the train is the journey: Inca Rail runs from Ollantaytambo down the Urubamba gorge to Aguas Calientes in a couple of hours, with the valley narrowing and the vegetation thickening as you drop.

Four services — The Voyager, The 360° with its observatory car, The Prime and The First Class — plus a private train. Some fares bundle the Machu Picchu entry ticket, which saves a separate scramble for a timed slot.

Ticket offices at Ollantaytambo station and on the Plaza de Armas in Cusco. The booking number takes WhatsApp.
