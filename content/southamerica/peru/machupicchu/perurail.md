The larger of the two operators on the line, running everything from the workaday Expedition to the Vistadome with its panoramic roof, and at the top the Hiram Bingham — a Belmond train with dinner, a bar car and a price to match.

Departures run from Ollantaytambo and, in the wet season when the track allows, from Poroy near Cusco.

Booking is by phone or email; there is a WhatsApp number inside their own contact widget.
