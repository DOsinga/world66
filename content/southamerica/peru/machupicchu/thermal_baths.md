The thermal baths above Aguas Calientes are the town's namesake hot springs and a common post-trek soak for travelers coming off the Inca Trail or a long day at Machu Picchu. The pools are reached by walking uphill through town.

Do not expect a quiet spa. The baths can feel more like a public pool and are often crowded, but tired legs may still appreciate the hot water.
