The Lines only resolve from the air, so this is a town organised around a small aerodrome. The standard flight is about thirty minutes over a dozen figures, banking steeply so both sides of the aircraft get each one; longer routes add Palpa or run to 23 figures.

Book with an operator that publishes its aircraft and its prices, fly early before the thermals build, and eat lightly — the turns are tight and the queasiness rate is high.

Flights also run from Pisco and Ica, which saves the drive south but adds air time.
