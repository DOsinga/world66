The Lines only make sense from the air: thirty minutes in a small aircraft over the hummingbird, the monkey, the spider and the condor, banking hard each way so both sides of the cabin see each figure. It is not a smooth ride and the airline will tell you to eat lightly.

They fly the classic circuit and a longer Nazca-plus-Palpa route, and sell the ground tours as well — the Cantalloc aqueducts, the Cahuachi pyramids, the Chauchilla cemetery with its mummies still in situ.

MINCETUR-registered at the aerodrome, with the website named in the register. The booking WhatsApp sits behind their short links; the number printed as "telephone" on the page is the same one.
