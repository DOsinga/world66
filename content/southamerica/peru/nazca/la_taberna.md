One of Nazca's most popular restaurants, known for grilled meats, seafood, and Peruvian staples at reasonable prices. La Taberna has a reliable set lunch that is a good deal — soup, a main of fish or chicken, and a drink. The walls are decorated with the signatures and comments of passing travellers, a decades-long tradition. Located near the Plaza de Armas.
