A metal tower on the Panamericana highway, about 20 km north of Nazca, that gives a ground-level view of three of the Nazca geoglyphs: the Hands, the Lizard, and the Tree. This is the only way to see the lines without taking a flight, though the perspective is necessarily limited — the figures are so large that from ground level you cannot make out their shapes, only the lines that compose them.

The tower is free to approach and is a standard stop for buses on the Lima–Nazca route, which means it can be crowded. The nearby Museo Maria Reiche is just a few kilometres south. For travellers who cannot afford the overflight or cannot stomach the turbulent planes, the tower at least provides some sense of what the lines look like up close and in their actual desert context.
