Jaén is a hot, busy market town in the far north of Peru, most useful to travellers as a staging post between the Pacific coast and the cloud-forest country around Chachapoyas and the Kuélap ruins. It has a new archaeology museum and easy access to riverside spots like [Buena Esperanza Beach](/southamerica/peru/northern_pacific_t/jan/buena_esperanza_be), but most visitors pass through rather than linger.
