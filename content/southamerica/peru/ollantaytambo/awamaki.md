Not a tour agency but a non-profit working with women's weaving co-operatives in the Patacancha valley above Ollantaytambo. The visits are the co-operatives' own income: a day learning to spin and dye with plants and cochineal, an Andean overnight in a family home, an earth-oven pachamanca lunch, cooking and Quechua classes.

This is the antidote to the weaving demonstrations laid on for coach parties in the Sacred Valley — the money goes to the weavers, and the day runs at their pace.

The published phone is Spanish-speaking only; email is the practical route for most visitors. No WhatsApp.
