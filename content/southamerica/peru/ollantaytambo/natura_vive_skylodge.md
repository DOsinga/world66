The most photographed bed in Peru: glass-and-aluminium capsules hanging off a cliff face above the Urubamba, reached either by climbing the via ferrata or by zipline, and left by the other. You sleep with the valley below you and the stars through the roof.

They also sell the via ferrata and the zipline circuit as day activities without the overnight, and lunch at the lodge.

MINCETUR-registered at Pachar. No WhatsApp — checked by hand; the contact page carries phones and a form.
