  Máncora's reputation as Peru's best surf town rests on a long, consistent left-hand point break that holds shape from about March to December. The wave breaks over a sandy bottom and runs for 200 metres on good days — one of the longest rideable lefts on the South American Pacific coast.

About 180 kilometres north of Piura along the Panamericana, Máncora is the country's busiest beach resort and the only Peruvian beach with a real surf scene. The town itself is small and lively, packed with hostels, beach bars, ceviche restaurants, and yoga retreats. The sand is wide, the water is warmer than anywhere else in Peru, and there are humpback whales offshore from July to October.

It is too far from Piura for an easy day trip — count on three hours each way — but it works well as a stopover for a couple of nights either side of a Piura visit. Direct buses run several times a day.
