---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

Pucallpa sits in the wet tropics: it is hot year-round (highs in the low 30s Celsius) and rains in earnest from **November to April**. The dry season — **May to October** — is the easier window for visiting: river levels are lower, the mosquitoes are slightly less terrible, and the unpaved tracks around Yarinacocha are usable.

July and August are the coolest and driest months; expect afternoon highs around 30°C and pleasant nights. The Ucayali rises through January and February and can flood the lower parts of the malecón in big years. For boat trips on Yarinacocha, any month works — the lake itself is stable — but the surrounding flooded forest is more reachable in the wet season.
