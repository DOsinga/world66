A small island in the bay just north of the Puno harbour, connected to the mainland by a short causeway. Esteves is occupied entirely by a large lakeside hotel and a few public walkways; non-guests can walk out onto the island via the causeway and along the lakeside paths for views back over the city.

The position gives some of the best near-shore views of the lake — open water in one direction, the long curve of Puno spread along the hillside in the other. Worth a half-hour walk if you have an afternoon to fill and want to escape the central streets.
