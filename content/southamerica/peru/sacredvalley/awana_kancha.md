A working camelid breeding centre and weaving cooperative on the road between Cuzco and Pisac, around 25 kilometres from Cuzco. Awana Kancha keeps a small herd of llamas, alpacas, vicuñas, and guanacos in pens beside the road; visitors can feed them, see the differences between the four species, and watch the wool being spun and dyed using traditional natural-dye techniques.

There is a substantial shop selling textiles produced by an associated cooperative of weavers from a dozen villages in the surrounding hills, with prices and quality much higher than the typical tourist-market output. Entry is free; the place runs on textile sales. A useful stop on the way down into the valley, especially if you have an interest in Andean weaving.
