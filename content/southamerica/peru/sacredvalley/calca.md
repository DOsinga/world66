A medium-sized market town in the middle of the Sacred Valley, about 50 kilometres from Cuzco. Calca is more workaday than Pisac or Ollantaytambo and largely off the tourist radar — most of its population works in agriculture in the surrounding valley.

The Sunday market is genuinely local, with little tourist-oriented production on sale, and the central plaza has a small 18th-century church. The main reason for a visit is the hot springs at Machacancha and Minasmoqo, a few kilometres up the side valley to the south — small, basic, and authentically local, with bathing pools fed by sulphurous thermal water.
