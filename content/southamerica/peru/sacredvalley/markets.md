The Sacred Valley still keeps the old Andean market system, with major villages taking turns through the week. The most famous is the Sunday market at Pisac — large, colourful, and the standard stop on a packaged valley tour. Quality varies and prices for tourists are noticeably higher than for locals, but the textile and pottery selection is genuinely good.

The Sunday morning market at Chinchero is smaller and more locally oriented, with fewer trinkets and more agricultural produce. The Saturday market at Urubamba is the workhorse where Quechua-speaking families from the surrounding hills actually shop — vegetables, freeze-dried potato, coca leaves, dried meats. The Wednesday market at Pisac (smaller version of Sunday's) is a good middle option for travellers who want the buzz without the worst of the crowds.

Bargaining is expected but should be friendly rather than aggressive. A good final price is usually 60 to 80 per cent of the opening ask.
