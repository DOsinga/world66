A row of restored Inca granaries built high into the cliff above Ollantaytambo, directly opposite the main fortress. Pinkuylluna is reached by a steep but short walking path that climbs from a quiet street in the upper town — well signposted and free to enter, unlike the main ruins across the valley.

The climb takes about 45 minutes to an hour, with the granaries themselves perched on natural ledges several hundred metres above the town. The views back across to the main complex, the Urubamba river, and the surrounding peaks are exceptional. A good morning's walk if you are spending a night in Ollantaytambo; bring water, take it slowly at altitude, and start early before the heat builds.
