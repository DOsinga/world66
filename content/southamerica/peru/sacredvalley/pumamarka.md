A small Inca complex on a ridge about 5 kilometres above Ollantaytambo, reached by a moderately strenuous half-day hike up a side valley. The ruins are far less visited than the main Ollantaytambo fortress — most days you will have them to yourself — and the setting, high on the ridge with views across to the snow peaks of the Vilcabamba range, is exceptional.

The hike up takes around two to three hours from Ollantaytambo through agricultural terraces and Quechua hamlets. Take water, sun protection, and rain cover; the path is well-trodden but easy to lose at junctions. A guide is not essential but local farmers may show you the way for a small fee.
