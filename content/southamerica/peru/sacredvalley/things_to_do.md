The standout sights are the Inca terraces and citadels — Pisac, Ollantaytambo, Moray — and the geometric salt pans at Maras. The valley's weekly markets (Pisac on Sunday, Chinchero on Sunday morning, Urubamba on Saturday) are the other reliable attraction.
