A small village a few kilometres east of Urubamba along the valley floor. Yucay was the favourite country estate of Huayna Capac, the last Inca emperor before the Spanish conquest, and the village still has fragments of Inca terraces and walls scattered through it.

The shaded central plaza is one of the prettiest in the valley, with a fine 16th-century church (San Pedro y San Pablo) on one side. A handful of restored colonial haciendas in the village now operate as upmarket hotels — La Casona de Yucay, the Sonesta Posada del Inca — and are good options for travellers who want to base themselves in the valley but escape the bustle of Urubamba.
