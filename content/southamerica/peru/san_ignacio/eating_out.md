---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

San Ignacio is small enough that you will eat in the same handful of places. The food is broad-Peruvian — *arroz con pollo*, *lomo saltado*, soups, plenty of yucca and plantain alongside the rice. The local distinction is the coffee: locally grown arabica is sold by weight in several roasters, and the small cafés around the Plaza de Armas serve it properly.

**Restaurante Yuradi** does a reliable *menú del día* — three courses for around 10 soles. **La Casona** is a step up in price and quality, with a longer carta of grilled meats and fish. For breakfast, the small cafés around the plaza serve *huevos con jamón* alongside fresh juice (pineapple, papaya, maracuyá) and good coffee from Industrias Cafenor.

Local sweets are worth trying: **chancaca** (raw cane sugar fudge) and **bocadillos** (peanut bars) are the regional snacks, sold by weight from small shops on the main streets.
