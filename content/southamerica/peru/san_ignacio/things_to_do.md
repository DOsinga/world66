---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

San Ignacio rewards a day or two of slow exploration. The town itself is pretty and worth walking. A mototaxi ride to the [Cerro Campano](/southamerica/peru/san_ignacio/cerro_campano) viewpoint takes about 20 minutes and gives sweeping views over the whole province. The [Pinturas Rupestres de Faical](/southamerica/peru/san_ignacio/pinturas_rupestres) — pre-Columbian rock art — are 20 minutes by road and are worth seeing while they last. As a coffee capital, stop at [Industrias Cafenor](/southamerica/peru/san_ignacio/industrias_cafenor) to taste locally roasted coffee and coffee liquors. The [Plaza de Armas](/southamerica/peru/san_ignacio/plaza_de_armas) is the gentle social heart of the town. For a longer trip out, the [Santuario Nacional Tabaconas Namballe](/southamerica/peru/san_ignacio/santuario_nacional_tabaconas_namballe) protected area is a few hours west.
