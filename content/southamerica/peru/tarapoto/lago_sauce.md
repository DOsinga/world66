Two hours south of Tarapoto along the Huallaga river crossing at Cumbaza, **Laguna Azul** — the locals' name for **Lago Sauce** — is a roughly oval lake of about 350 hectares, ringed by low jungle hills and a string of small lakeside lodges. The water is clear and warm and you can swim straight from the dock at any of the lodges. Kayaks, paddle boards, and *peque-peque* boats are available; an hour's loop along the shore takes you past a few small villages and beaches.

The road down is a steep, twisting descent on the last stretch, and most visitors stay one or two nights rather than try to do it in a day. Combi vans and cars-for-hire leave from Tarapoto's centre; alternatively, organised tours include the boat crossing of the Huallaga river. The best time is the drier months from May to September.
