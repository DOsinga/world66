---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tarapoto is more a base than a destination — most of the highlights are in the cloud forest and lakes within an hour or two of town. In the city itself, the **Plaza de Armas** is the social heart, busiest at dusk when the day's heat eases and the *triciclo* drivers and food carts come out. The **Mercado Central** a few blocks east is good for jungle fruit — *aguaje*, *cocona*, *camu camu* — and a sense of the Amazon edge that Tarapoto sits on.

For nature, the **Catarata de Ahuashiyacu** is the closest waterfall (14 km), reached in 25 minutes by *mototaxi*. The **Lamas** community an hour northwest preserves Quechua-Lamista traditions in a colourful hillside village. **Laguna Azul (Lago Sauce)** is the standard overnight: a clear-water lake two hours south, where the surrounding hills hide jungle lodges. The **Cordillera Escalera Regional Conservation Area** wraps around the city — a cloud forest reserve with trails, viewpoints, and several waterfalls.
