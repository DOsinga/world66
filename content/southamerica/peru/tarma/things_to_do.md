---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Tarma's best sights are split between the town and the nearby valley. Start at the [Plaza de Armas and Santa Ana Cathedral](/southamerica/peru/tarma/plaza_de_armas_and_santa_ana_cathedral), then use the town as a base for [Tarmatambo](/southamerica/peru/tarma/tarmatambo), the Inca-period site above the valley. The deeper excursion is [Gruta de Huagapo](/southamerica/peru/tarma/gruta_de_huagapo), a cave near Palcamayo, while the [Sanctuary of the Lord of Muruhuay](/southamerica/peru/tarma/sanctuary_of_the_lord_of_muruhuay) gives the area its strongest living pilgrimage tradition.
