---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Tingo is not easy to reach, which is part of why it remains quiet. The nearest large hub is Chachapoyas (the regional capital), from which all routes into the Utcubamba valley pass.

**From Chachapoyas** — Combis (shared minibuses) run regularly between Chachapoyas and Tingo, taking roughly two hours along a winding mountain road. Departures are most frequent in the morning. The road descends steeply into the valley before following the Utcubamba river south to Tingo.

**From Celendin or Cajamarca** — Buses from Cajamarca to Chachapoyas pass through Celendin and Leimebamba before continuing north; some travellers approach from this direction as part of a longer circuit. The journey from Cajamarca to Chachapoyas takes around 10–12 hours on unpaved mountain roads.

**From Chiclayo or Lima** — Chiclayo on the coast is the most common entry point for travellers arriving by air or overnight bus. From Chiclayo, direct buses to Chachapoyas take around 9–11 hours (many depart in the evening). From Lima, the journey is substantially longer; flying to Chiclayo and busing from there is the most practical option.

The road from Nuevo Tingo up to the cable car terminal is paved; the old track from Old Tingo to Kuélap is not.
