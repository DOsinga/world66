The Huacas de Moche Site Museum sits beside one of northern Peru's key archaeological landscapes, the Moche complex of Huaca de la Luna and Huaca del Sol. Its displays give essential context before or after seeing the adobe pyramids, murals, and excavated ceremonial spaces.

This is one of the strongest short trips from Trujillo for anyone interested in pre-Inca history. The museum makes the site easier to read, especially because the surrounding desert and monumental platforms can otherwise feel hard to interpret at first glance.
