---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Trujillo is a strong eating city by Peruvian standards. The northern coast has its own culinary tradition, heavier on seafood and slightly different in seasoning from Lima. Ceviche, sudado de pescado, and seco de cabrito (braised goat) are the regional staples. Huanchaco, the beach town 12 km away, is the better place for fresh seafood.

Downtown restaurants cluster around the Plaza de Armas and along Pizarro and Independencia streets. El Mochica on Bolívar is the most established traditional restaurant in the centre, reliable for regional dishes. For cheaper eating, the covered markets serve caldo de gallina and rice dishes from early morning.
