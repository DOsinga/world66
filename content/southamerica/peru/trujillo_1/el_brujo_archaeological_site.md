El Brujo is one of the strongest archaeological day trips from Trujillo, a coastal Moche complex where adobe pyramids, painted reliefs, and museum interpretation make the ancient north coast feel immediate. The site's best-known figure is the Lady of Cao, whose discovery changed assumptions about power and gender in Moche society.

The trip takes effort, but the reward is a site that is less crowded than Chan Chan and more tightly focused. Give time to both the exposed huaca structures and the museum, where the preserved burial context helps turn the ruins from impressive walls into a human story.
