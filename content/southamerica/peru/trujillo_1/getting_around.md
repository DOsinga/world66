---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

Trujillo's colonial centre is walkable. Taxis are cheap and plentiful for getting to the archaeological sites — agree a price before you get in. Combis (shared minibuses) run to Huanchaco and to the entrance roads near Chan Chan frequently and cheaply.

Hiring a taxi for a day to visit Chan Chan, the Huacas del Sol y la Luna, and possibly Huaca El Brujo is the most efficient way to cover the region's major sites without a tour. Drivers typically charge around US$40–60 for a full day. Tours are available from the main agencies on or near the Plaza de Armas.
