---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Trujillo is 560 km north of Lima. The most practical way to get there from Lima is by overnight bus — a journey of about eight hours. Multiple companies run comfortable double-decker coaches with reclining seats; Cruz del Sur, Oltursa, and Línea are the main operators. There are also flights from Lima to Trujillo's Capitán FAP Carlos Martínez de Pinillos Airport, a journey of about an hour.

From Trujillo, shared taxis and combis run to Huanchaco (30 minutes) and to the archaeological sites throughout the day.
