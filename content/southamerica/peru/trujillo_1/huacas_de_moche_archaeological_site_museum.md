The Huacas de Moche museum is the interpretive half of a visit to the great adobe temple complex south of Trujillo. Its ceramics, models, and excavated objects help make sense of the Moche world before you stand in front of the massive Huaca del Sol and the painted walls of Huaca de la Luna.

Do not skip it just because the ruins are the obvious draw. The museum gives the site human scale: ritual vessels, iconography, and everyday objects show how sophisticated the Moche were, and they make the sun-baked pyramids outside feel less like anonymous mounds of earth.
