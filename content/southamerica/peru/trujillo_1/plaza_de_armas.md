Trujillo's colonial centre is one of the finest in northern Peru, and the Plaza de Armas is its heart. The square is large and well-maintained, flanked by brightly painted mansions and the yellow-fronted cathedral. A central monument to Liberty, erected in 1820 following Trujillo's declaration of independence, stands in the middle.

The surrounding streets hold a number of colonial mansions open to visitors: the Casa Urquiaga (now owned by the Banco Central de Reserva), the Casa de la Emancipación, and the Casa Orbegoso are all within a few blocks. Their interiors contain period furniture and exhibits relating to Trujillo's republican history. The square itself is a pleasant place to sit in the evening when it fills with locals.
