Bigi Pan is a broad, shallow wetland behind Suriname's western coast. Lagoons, mudflats, mangroves, and flooded grassland attract scarlet ibis, herons, spoonbills, birds of prey, and large numbers of migrants. Flamingos appear too, but they move with feeding conditions and are never guaranteed.

Trips start near [Nieuw Nickerie](/southamerica/suriname/nieuw_nickerie) and enter through the Jamaer canal by shallow boat. The ride through narrow vegetation is part of the experience; once the water opens out, the scale of the wetland becomes clear. Wind, water level, and tide affect routes, so local boat knowledge matters.

Bigi Pan is a Multiple Use Management Area rather than an untouched sanctuary. Fishing and other livelihoods continue alongside conservation, and the wetland is important both to coastal communities and migratory birds. Arrange an experienced local boat, bring sun and rain protection, and ask what the trip includes before leaving town.
