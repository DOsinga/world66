Bigi Pan is a shallow brackish lagoon behind the Nickerie rice fields, and one of the best bird sites in the Guianas — scarlet ibis, flamingos, herons, and something over 120 recorded species. It is also five hours from Paramaribo, so it is nearly always sold as a two-day trip with a night in a stilt lodge over the water.

Boats go out at dawn and again before sunset, which is when the ibis come in to roost and the whole thing turns red.
