Brownsberg is the easiest serious rainforest trip from [Paramaribo](/southamerica/suriname/paramaribo). The park rises above the Brokopondo reservoir, and short trails leave the plateau for viewpoints and waterfalls. You are more likely to hear red howler monkeys before seeing them; birds and smaller primates reward an early start.

It can be done as a long day trip, but staying longer gives the forest time to quiet down after daytime visitors leave. Do not expect polished facilities. Check current accommodation, trail, water, and road conditions with STINASU before departure, and bring food, drinking water, insect repellent, and cash unless your operator says otherwise.

The view over the reservoir needs context. The Afobaka dam flooded a vast forest area in the 1960s and forced Saamaka communities from their villages; dead treetops still break the water in places. A good guide should explain that history as well as identify wildlife.
