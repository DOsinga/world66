Brownsberg sits on a plateau above the Brokopondo reservoir, close enough to Paramaribo to be the country's standard day trip into rainforest — waterfalls, howler monkeys, and a view over the drowned forest of the lake. Most operators pair it with a boat trip out to one of the islands in the reservoir.

It can be done in a long day from the city, or as an overnight if you want the dawn.
