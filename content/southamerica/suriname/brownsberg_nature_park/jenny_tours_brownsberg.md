Brownsberg as a day trip from Paramaribo: up onto the plateau, the walk down to the Leo and Irene falls, howler monkeys most mornings, and the view out over the Brokopondo reservoir with the tops of drowned trees still showing.

The two-day version adds Ston Island on the lake, with time for swimming and piranha fishing, which is more fun than it sounds.

Around €95 for the day tour and €175 for the two-day, with departures guaranteed from three people.
