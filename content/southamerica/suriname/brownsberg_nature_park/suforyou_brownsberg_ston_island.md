SUforYou's version pairs the Brownsberg plateau with Ston Island out on the Brokopondo reservoir — forest walks and waterfalls on the first day, the lake and its drowned forest on the second.

Fixed departures run on set days of the week rather than on demand, so the date usually decides the operator here rather than the other way round.

Priced per person on a sliding scale by group size; ask on WhatsApp for the current departures.
