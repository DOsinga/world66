Colakreek is a forest swimming place near Zanderij, south of Paramaribo. Tannins from fallen leaves colour the water a deep tea brown; the colour is natural, and the creek can feel surprisingly cool beneath the trees.

This is a local recreation area rather than wilderness. Open shelters, changing facilities, and picnic space make it busiest on weekends and holidays, when families come for the day. Weekdays are usually quieter. Bring food, drinking water, insect repellent, and whatever you need for swimming.

The drive from Paramaribo takes roughly an hour and is easiest by car or taxi because public transport is of little use for a day visit. Opening arrangements and entry charges can change, so confirm them before setting out. Combine it with another Para stop only if you have your own transport; Colakreek works best as an unhurried swim, not a hurried checklist item.
