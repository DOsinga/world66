The Commewijne district across the river from Paramaribo is a half-day trip: the old coffee and cacao plantations at Frederiksdorp and Rust en Werk, the fort at Nieuw Amsterdam, and — at the confluence where the Commewijne meets the Suriname — a resident group of Guiana river dolphins.

Boats leave from Leonsberg on the Paramaribo side, and the dolphin trips are timed for late afternoon when the animals are feeding and the light is worth having.
