Guiana river dolphins — *profosu* in Sranan — live in small groups where the Commewijne meets the Suriname River, and the trip out to them is timed for late afternoon when they are feeding and the light is low.

It is normally sold together with the plantation circuit, making a full day from Paramaribo: forts, estates, a swim, and the dolphins on the way back.

Sightings are good but not guaranteed, which any honest operator will tell you before you pay.
