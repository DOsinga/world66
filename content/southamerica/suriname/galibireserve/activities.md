Between February and August, leatherback and green turtles come ashore at Galibi to nest, and the reserve is the reason most people make the long trip east to Albina. There is no way to arrange this on arrival: the beach is an hour by korjaal from Albina, the sleeping is basic, and the timing depends on tides and the season.

Every operator here runs essentially the same two- or three-day trip, usually combined with the Marowijne river crossing and a village visit.
