The Galibi trip in its standard form: the long drive east to Albina, an hour by korjaal across the Marowijne, a night in the Carib village, and a boat out after dark to Baboensanti where the leatherbacks come ashore.

The turtles are seasonal — roughly February to August, with the peak in April and May — and the beach visit is at the mercy of tide and weather. Sleeping is basic, in hammocks or simple huts.

Can be extended by a day into French Guiana across the river. Book well ahead in season; the village can only take so many people at once.
