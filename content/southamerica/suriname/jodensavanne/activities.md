Jodensavanne is the ruin of a seventeenth-century Jewish settlement in the savannah south of Paramaribo — a synagogue foundation and a cemetery in the sand, reached by road or, better, by boat up the Suriname River. It is usually sold as a day trip paired with a swim at Overbridge or Cola Creek on the way back.
