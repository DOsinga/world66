A guided day trip south from Paramaribo to the ruins of Jodensavanne — the foundations of the 1685 synagogue and the cemetery in the savannah sand, one of the oldest Jewish sites in the Americas.

The return usually breaks at Overbridge River Resort on the Suriname River for a swim and lunch, which is how nearly every operator runs it.

Book by WhatsApp, or arrange a video call first if you want to fold it into a longer route.
