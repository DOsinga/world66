A Paramaribo agency on the Kleine Waterstraat with a second office in the Netherlands, which makes it a common choice for Dutch visitors arranging things before they fly. Trips are sold either as one or two destinations or as full round trips, including the Jodensavanne bus tour and the Palumeu and Awarradam jungle lodges.

They take messages on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook and will do a video consultation if you want to talk a route through before committing.

Open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm.
