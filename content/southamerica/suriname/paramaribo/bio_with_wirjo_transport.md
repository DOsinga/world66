A Surinamese operator whose transport arm is worth knowing because it publishes a price list, which almost nobody here does. One way, per vehicle: the airport 50 euro, Paramaribo to Albina 150, Moengo 100, Groningen 50, and Leonsberg or Peperpot 10 each.

Those are vehicle prices rather than per seat, so they make sense for three or four people and much less so alone.

They say they answer WhatsApp immediately but do not publish which of their numbers it is, so the phone line is listed here instead. No street address published; the pin is central Paramaribo.
