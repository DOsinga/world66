Blauwgrond began as a Javanese village north of the old city and is now part of Paramaribo's urban spread. Its draw is food rather than monuments: simple warungs cluster around Josephine Samson Greenstraat and serve Javanese-Surinamese dishes such as saoto, bami, nasi, satay, and fried snacks.

Come in the evening, when more kitchens are open, and choose by what looks busy. [Mirosso](/southamerica/suriname/paramaribo/mirosso) offers a more comfortable introduction, while [Warung Renah](/southamerica/suriname/paramaribo/warung_renah) keeps the small-warung feel. Blauwgrond is well outside the walkable historic centre, so use a taxi and arrange the return rather than hoping to find one late.
