An established Paramaribo agency whose real usefulness is the interior: they book and bundle the jungle lodges most travellers cannot easily reach on their own, including Danpaati and Anaula on the Upper Suriname River, and Palumeu, Awarradam and Kasikasima further south.

Closer to town they run the standard day tours — the plantation bike trip, dolphin spotting, Jodensavanne, Brownsberg — plus multi-day expeditions to Bigi Pan, Galibi and Raleighvallen, and fourteen-day round trips for people doing the whole country.

The office on the Cornelis Jongbawstraat is open six days a week, and the mobile number doubles as their WhatsApp.
