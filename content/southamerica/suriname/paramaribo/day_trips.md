---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Paramaribo is an excellent base for exploring the immediate hinterland. The most popular day trip is the boat journey along the Commewijne River, visiting plantation ruins, the restored Fort Nieuw Amsterdam, and looking for pink river dolphins at the confluence. Most tour operators on the Waterkant offer this trip.

For those interested in Jewish history, Jodensavanne — the ruined 17th-century Jewish settlement upriver — is one of the most historically significant sites in the Western Hemisphere and can be combined with a visit to a Maroon community. The Brownsberg Nature Reserve, about 130 km south, requires a longer day or an overnight; it is the most accessible introduction to Surinamese rainforest and has excellent wildlife and waterfall walks.
