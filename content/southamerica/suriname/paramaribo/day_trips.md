The easiest trip is [Commewijne](/southamerica/suriname/commewijne), reached by road or boat for Fort Nieuw Amsterdam, Peperpot Nature Park, plantation history, and a chance of seeing Guiana dolphins. Choose a trip that explains the hard history rather than treating old estates as decoration.

[Jodensavanne](/southamerica/suriname/jodensavanne) is a longer historical outing to the UNESCO-listed synagogue ruins and cemeteries. [Brownsberg Nature Park](/southamerica/suriname/brownsberg_nature_park) is the ambitious option: possible in a long day, better overnight, and dependent on current road and park conditions.
