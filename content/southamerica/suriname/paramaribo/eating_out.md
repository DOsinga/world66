---
title: Eating Out
type: section
---

Paramaribo is one of the few capitals where eating is as important as sightseeing. Start with roti, seek out a Javanese warung for saoto or nasi, and try Creole dishes such as pom and moksi alesi. [Blauwgrond](/southamerica/suriname/paramaribo/blauwgrond) is the classic evening destination for Javanese food; the centre is easier for roti shops and market snacks. Carry cash and do not assume every place accepts foreign cards.
