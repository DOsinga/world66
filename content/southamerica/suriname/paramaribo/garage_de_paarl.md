The most formal of the Paramaribo operators: airport service to and from Johan Adolf Pengel with drivers in branded red or white polo shirts carrying badges, a limousine service, and car rental, all from the Kankantriestraat.

Booking goes through a form that comes back with an estimate rather than a published rate. The office keeps business hours - weekdays to half past four, Saturday mornings - even though the driving does not.

The WhatsApp number is labelled as such on their contact page, separate from the office lines.
