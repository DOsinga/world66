Paramaribo's historic centre is a UNESCO-listed district of colonial wooden buildings, broad streets, and riverside landmarks. The area is easy to explore on foot around Onafhankelijkheidsplein, Waterkant, Fort Zeelandia, and the surrounding old streets.
