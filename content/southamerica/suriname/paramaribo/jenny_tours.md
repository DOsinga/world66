A Paramaribo travel organisation with about fifteen years behind it, running the full spread of Surinamese trips: Brownsberg and Jodensavanne as day tours, Galibi for the turtles, Nickerie and Bigi Pan for the birds, Raleighvallen and Voltzberg as a three-day expedition, and multi-day trips up the Suriname River. They also run transfers and cross-border trips into French Guiana and Guyana.

Departures are generally guaranteed from three people, prices are published per person in euros, and you can pay online — which is less common here than you might expect.

The office is on the Waterkant in the middle of town, but the practical way to reach them is WhatsApp, which they answer 24 hours a day for questions or bookings.
