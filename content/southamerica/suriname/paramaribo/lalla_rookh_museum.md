Named for the ship that brought the first group of indentured workers from British India in 1873, the Lalla Rookh Museum traces Indo-Surinamese history from recruitment and plantation labour through farming, family life, religion, and the present.

It is outside the historic centre and has limited public hours. Confirm before going; groups may need an appointment. The collection fills an essential gap in a city tour otherwise dominated by Dutch colonial buildings.
