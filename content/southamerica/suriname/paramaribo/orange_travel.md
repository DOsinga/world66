One of the larger Surinamese operators, with offices in Paramaribo, The Hague and Florida, selling day tours, round trips and tailor-made itineraries — the Jodensavanne river cruise and the Danpaati River Lodge among them.

Departures for the river trips leave from the Marinetrap on the Paramaribo waterfront, usually between 8 and 9 in the morning.

They say they answer WhatsApp but do not publish which of their two Surinamese numbers it is, so the office line is listed here instead.
