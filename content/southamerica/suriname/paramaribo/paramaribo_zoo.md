Paramaribo Zoo is a modest, old-fashioned city zoo rather than a polished wildlife park. Its value for travellers is the chance to see some animals associated with Suriname and the Guianas without heading immediately into the rainforest.

Standards and displays may feel uneven compared with major international zoos, so go with measured expectations. It works best as a short family stop or a gentle break from the heat and traffic of central Paramaribo.
