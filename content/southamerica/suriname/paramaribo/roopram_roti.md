Roopram is a reliable first stop for Surinamese roti. Choose a curry, potato, and long beans, tear off pieces of the flatbread, and use them to pick up the rest. The Zwartenhovenbrugstraat branch is central and functional rather than atmospheric, which is exactly the point.
