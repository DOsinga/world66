One caution on reaching them: the single number on their site is printed in a trunk-zero form that makes their own WhatsApp link unusable. The number here is that number written correctly for international dialling, but contact them by phone rather than assuming the WhatsApp works. They publish no street address, so the pin is central Paramaribo.
