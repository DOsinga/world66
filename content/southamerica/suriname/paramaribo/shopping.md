---
title: "Shopping"
type: section
---

The Central Market is the most rewarding place to browse, especially early in the day when produce, fish, spices, and prepared food fill the aisles. The nearby Maroon Market is more specialised, selling herbs, roots, oils, and ritual ingredients brought from the interior. Ask before photographing vendors or their goods.

For contemporary art, Readytex Art Gallery on Steenbakkerijstraat represents Surinamese artists and sells original work. Crafts vary widely in origin and quality; ask who made an item rather than assuming every carving or textile is local.
