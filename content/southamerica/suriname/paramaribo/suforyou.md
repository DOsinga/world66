Running since 2013 out of the Henck Arronstraat, SUforYou sells day excursions and longer expeditions across the country — Brownsberg and Ston Island, the Commewijne plantation boat tour, dolphin spotting, Bigi Pan, and expeditions up the Suriname River to Danpaati, Gunsi and Knini Paati. There are also cooking, cacao and coffee workshops in the city, which is unusual.

They make a point of the local communities visited sharing in the money, which in the interior generally means the village rather than an operator in town.

Quick questions and bookings go through WhatsApp on the same number as the office line.
