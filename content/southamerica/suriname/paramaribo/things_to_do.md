---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Paramaribo's UNESCO-listed centre rewards slow exploration on foot. Start at Fort Zeelandia, follow the Waterkant, then turn inland toward the cathedral, Keizerstraat, and the markets. The Koto and Lalla Rookh museums add voices that the colonial buildings cannot supply. Check museum hours directly: small institutions may open only on limited days or by appointment.
