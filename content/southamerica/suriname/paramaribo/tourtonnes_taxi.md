The largest and longest-running of the Paramaribo taxi centrals: more than twenty-five years in business, a fleet in the hundreds, vetted drivers, twenty-four hours a day. From a Surinamese phone you dial the four-digit 1690; from abroad it is the full number.

Besides metered city work they sell a booked airport service, where you give the flight number and they meet it, and three fixed sightseeing runs: the historic centre, the Commewijne plantations, and Para with its creeks and villages.

No prices published, and unusually for this market, no WhatsApp at all. Phone or the booking form on the site.
