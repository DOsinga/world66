Raleighvallen, deep inside the Central Suriname Nature Reserve, is the serious end of the country's rainforest: three days minimum, a boat up the Coppename, and a hard climb up the Voltzberg granite dome for the view over unbroken canopy. Cock-of-the-rock nest on the way up.

This is not a trip you improvise. It is sold as a fixed multi-day expedition, and the operators here are the ones who run it.
