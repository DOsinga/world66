The three-day Raleighvallen trip, and the reason to make it: a boat up the Coppename into the Central Suriname Nature Reserve, nights at the Foengoe Island camp, and an early climb up the bare granite of the Voltzberg for sunrise over unbroken canopy in every direction.

The walk in to the dome passes a cock-of-the-rock lek, where the birds are absurdly orange and entirely unbothered.

This is a fixed expedition with set departure dates — there is no infrastructure out here to improvise with.
