A jungle and river lodge in Sipaliwini, about 200 km from Paramaribo and reached by korjaal from Atjoni, notable for being run by Saramaccan Maroon management rather than an operator in the capital.

Six cabins sleeping up to five each, a restaurant and bar looking out over the water, and a pool. Trips out from the lodge cover the villages, the forest and fishing on the river.

You can book it direct — phone or WhatsApp on the same number — which is not true of every lodge up here.
