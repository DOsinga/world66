Expeditions up the Suriname River to the island lodge at Danpaati, the village of Gunsi and the resort at Knini Paati — three days at the shortest, and as much about the Saramaccan villages along the water as about the forest.

The bundled transport is the point: getting to Atjoni and onward by korjaal is the awkward part of doing this independently, and the road is long.

Ask on WhatsApp which departures still have space; the lodges are small.
