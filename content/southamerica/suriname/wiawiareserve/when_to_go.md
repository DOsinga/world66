---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

There is no dependable visitor season while tourism facilities are closed. The wider coast receives migratory shorebirds mainly during the northern winter, and marine-turtle activity falls broadly in the first half of the year, but both habitat and animal use shift along this dynamic shoreline.

Contact STINASU before making any plan. A seasonal wildlife calendar does not imply that landing, overnight stays, or guided visits are permitted.
