---
title: When to Go
type: section
---

Two wildlife seasons overlap at Wia Wia. The leatherback turtles nest at night from March through July, with peak laying in April and May. Green turtles come ashore on a similar but slightly later schedule. Hatching runs from May into August.

For migratory shorebirds, September to April is the window. Tens of thousands of birds from North America winter along this coast, joining the resident scarlet ibis, herons, and spoonbills. April is the sweet spot if you want both turtles and birds in the same trip.

Avoid the long rainy season from late April through early August if you do not want soaked trails and biting insects at full strength. The short dry season in February and March is the most comfortable for general visits.
