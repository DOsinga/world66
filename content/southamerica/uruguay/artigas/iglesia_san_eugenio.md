Iglesia San Eugenio del Cuareim faces Plaza Artigas and is one of the useful stops for reading the older town centre. Its value is quiet rather than spectacular: a parish church, a small historic collection, and a link to the settlement's early civic life.

Visit it with the plaza and the Jefatura de Policia rather than making a separate trip across town.
