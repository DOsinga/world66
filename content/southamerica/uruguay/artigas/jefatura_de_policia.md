The departmental police headquarters is the building in Artigas that makes visitors stop twice. With towers, battlements, and a stern late-19th-century silhouette, it looks more like a small medieval castle than a civic office in northern Uruguay.

You do not need long here. See it as part of a walk around Plaza Artigas, where the building gives the town centre its most distinctive profile.
