Plaza Jose Batlle y Ordonez is one of the easiest places to pause while walking central Artigas. It is not a grand square, but it helps orient the town: commercial streets, free shops, and everyday border-city life sit around it.

Use it as a practical stop between the busier downtown blocks and the older civic buildings near Plaza Artigas.
