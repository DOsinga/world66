---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Chuy sits on Ruta 9, roughly 340 km northeast of [Montevideo](/southamerica/uruguay/montevideo). Long-distance buses connect it with Montevideo, [Rocha](/southamerica/uruguay/rocha), [Punta del Este](/southamerica/uruguay/puntadeleste), and Brazilian border services.

For [Parque Nacional Santa Teresa](/southamerica/uruguay/parquesantateresa), Chuy is the practical northern access town. Local buses and taxis run toward La Coronilla, Barra del Chuy, and the fort, but a car makes the coast and protected areas much easier.
