Minas is the capital of Lavalleja Department and the most useful town in Uruguay's low hill country. It is not dramatic by Andean standards, but in a country of broad plains and beach towns the surrounding sierras feel genuinely different: rocky ridges, eucalyptus groves, mineral springs, and small roads that curl through green valleys.

The centre is compact and provincial, with [Plaza Libertad](/southamerica/uruguay/minas/plaza_libertad), the [Cathedral of Minas](/southamerica/uruguay/minas/cathedral_of_minas), and [Teatro Lavalleja](/southamerica/uruguay/minas/teatro_lavalleja) within an easy walk. Minas is also the practical base for [Cerro del Verdún](/southamerica/uruguay/minas/cerro_del_verdun), [Parque Salus](/southamerica/uruguay/minas/parque_salus), and the waterfall at [Salto del Penitente](/southamerica/uruguay/minas/salto_del_penitente).

Most foreign travellers know Minas only because the road to [Villa Serrana](/southamerica/uruguay/villaserrana) passes through it. That is fair enough, but the town rewards a stop if you want to see Uruguay away from the coast: more mate thermoses than beach bars, more Sunday pilgrimage than resort polish, and some of the country's best small-scale landscapes close at hand.
