Salto del Penitente is a compact waterfall park east of Minas, reached by a scenic rural road off Ruta 8. The fall itself is not huge, but Uruguay has few waterfalls of this kind, and the rocky setting makes it feel like a proper hill-country excursion.

Trails, viewpoints, and picnic areas make it easy to spend a half day here. It combines well with a drive toward Villa Serrana, though you will want your own transport or a local tour; public connections are thin.
