A hundred years of Uruguayan winemaking, and the bodega says so on every page of its site — "no somos apenas una bodega, somos parte viva de la historia vitivinícola del Uruguay". It is a working family operation in Montevideo that welcomes visitors, with tastings arranged in advance.

The curiosity here is a line of table wines made for diabetics, which they have been producing for years and which you will not find at the other bodegas. Whether it is any good is a matter for your own palate, but it is a genuinely unusual thing to be shown round.

We hold no street-level position for them: the address on their Ministry of Tourism registration is Camino Hudson 5415, and the pin sits at the Montevideo city coordinate rather than the gate. Message them for directions before setting out.
