The Obelisk of Montevideo rises where Avenida 18 de Julio opens toward Parque Batlle, a strong urban marker in a city that often prefers low, horizontal lines. José Luis Zorrilla de San Martín's granite and bronze monument commemorates Uruguay's first constitution.

It is worth seeing less as a standalone stop than as part of Montevideo's civic geography. Walk or ride past it on the way between Centro, Tres Cruces, and the stadium district, and it helps explain how the capital stages its public memory along broad avenues and open spaces.
