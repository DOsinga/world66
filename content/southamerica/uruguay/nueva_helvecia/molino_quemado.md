Molino Quemado is the remains of a steam mill built in 1876, a reminder that Nueva Helvecia's Swiss identity was tied to agriculture from the beginning. The town became one of Uruguay's important farming districts, and early mechanisation mattered here.

The mill is not polished into a major attraction, but it gives the town's rural immigrant story some weight beyond crests and pretty details.
