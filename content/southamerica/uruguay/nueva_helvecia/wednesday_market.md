Nueva Helvecia's traditional market is held on Wednesday mornings. It is the best time to catch the town acting like a local service centre rather than a heritage stop, with produce, daily errands, and small-town conversation around the centre.

For travellers passing through Colonia Department, the market is worth timing if the schedule fits. It pairs naturally with a walk to the square and churches.
