The first bungee operation in Uruguay, jumping from Cerro San Antonio, the hill immediately behind Piriápolis with the chairlift and the view down over the bay. It is a short country with not much to jump off, which makes this more or less the only game of its kind.

They run roped jumps and abseiling from the same site, so there is a less committing option if the full drop is not what you came for. The location does most of the work: you are looking at the Río de la Plata opening into the Atlantic as you go.

Seasonal, weather-dependent, and busiest in January. Message ahead on WhatsApp rather than turning up.
