Castillo de Piria is the most revealing building in Piriapolis. Francisco Piria built it as his residence in the 1890s, when he was turning fields, hills, and beach into a planned resort town.

The castle mixes Renaissance and medieval gestures with the confidence of someone selling a dream. It now works as a municipal museum, and even a quick visit helps explain why Piriapolis feels more deliberate than most beach towns.
