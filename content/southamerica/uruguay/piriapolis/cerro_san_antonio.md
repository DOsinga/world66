Cerro San Antonio is the short, satisfying climb above Piriapolis. From the top you see the curve of the bay, the harbour, the town grid, and the low hills that make this stretch of coast more interesting than a simple beach strip.

There is a small chapel and religious statuary at the summit, but the view is the reason to come. You can drive up or walk if the weather is mild.
