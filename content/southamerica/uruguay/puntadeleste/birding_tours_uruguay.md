Uruguay is small, flat and quietly excellent for birds: the coastal lagoons of Rocha, the palm savannah at Butiá, the wetlands along the Río Negro, and a list that runs past four hundred species without anyone having to travel far. Birding Tours Uruguay work the whole country from a base at La Barra, just over the bridge from Punta del Este.

Trips run from a morning in the lagoons east of here to multi-day circuits taking in Rocha and the interior. The draw for visiting birders is the mix of Atlantic coast and pampas in one short country — you can see black-necked swans and greater rheas in the same day.
