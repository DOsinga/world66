Rocha is the inland capital of Uruguay's most appealing coastal department. The city itself is quiet, workaday, and short on spectacle, but it is useful: buses converge here, shops stay open when beach villages go sleepy, and the Atlantic coast is close enough for day trips.

The centre has the familiar rhythm of a Uruguayan departmental capital, with [Plaza Independencia](/southamerica/uruguay/rocha/plaza_independencia), the [Church of Nuestra Señora de los Remedios](/southamerica/uruguay/rocha/church_nuestra_senora_remedios), and [Teatro 25 de Mayo](/southamerica/uruguay/rocha/teatro_25_de_mayo) close together. It is a place for errands, coffee, and orientation rather than grand sightseeing.

Rocha's real value is strategic. To the east are [Parque Nacional Santa Teresa](/southamerica/uruguay/parquesantateresa), [Chuy](/southamerica/uruguay/chuy), and the Brazilian border; to the south are lagoons, fishing villages, and long beaches. [Laguna de Rocha](/southamerica/uruguay/rocha/laguna_de_rocha), just outside the city, gives the best quick taste of the wetlands and birdlife that make this department different from Uruguay's more polished resort coast.
