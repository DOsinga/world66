Rocha is a practical inland base for several parts of the eastern coast. [Parque Nacional Santa Teresa](/southamerica/uruguay/parquesantateresa) and [Chuy](/southamerica/uruguay/chuy) make the strongest eastbound trips, while [Punta del Este](/southamerica/uruguay/puntadeleste) is reachable to the west if you want the polished resort contrast.
