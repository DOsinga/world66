Plaza Independencia is Rocha's main square, the place where the town shows its civic face. Palms, benches, low municipal buildings, and the church nearby make it a straightforward but pleasant starting point.

Come here to understand the scale of the city. Rocha is not trying to dazzle; the square is useful precisely because it feels lived-in, with local errands and conversations doing more work than monuments.
