Casa Horacio Quiroga connects Salto with one of Uruguay's most influential writers. Quiroga was born in the city and became famous for intense short stories shaped by jungle life, illness, accident, and psychological unease. The house museum gives that literary biography a local anchor.

Do not expect a large institution. The appeal is modest and specific: rooms, objects, and context for travellers who like literature or want to understand why Quiroga matters in the Río de la Plata tradition. Pair it with the Museo del Hombre y la Tecnología and a walk along the costanera for a fuller sense of Salto beyond the thermal baths.
