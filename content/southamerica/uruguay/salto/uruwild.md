Uruwild run fishing trips and river tourism on the Río Uruguay at Salto, out on Ruta 31. The fish everyone comes for is the dorado — golden, aggressive, and the reason Argentine and Brazilian anglers cross the border for a weekend — with surubí and other river species alongside it.

They also do non-fishing river trips for people who would rather look at the water than pull things out of it, which pairs well with a stay at the Daymán hot springs down the road.

The Salto Grande dam shapes everything here: the reservoir above it and the tailwater below fish quite differently, and knowing which to be on is the whole value of a guide. Book on WhatsApp.
