---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The Sierra de Carapé is accessible year-round, but spring (September–November) and autumn (March–May) are the best times to visit. In spring the hills are green from winter rains, wildflowers are blooming, and the temperatures are mild — ideal for hiking. Autumn has clear skies and cooling nights after the summer heat.

Summer (December–February) is popular with Uruguayan campers and domestic tourists, and the village gets busy on long weekends. If you want quiet, come mid-week. Winters are mild by most standards but can be wet and grey. The forest is at its most atmospheric in winter rain.
