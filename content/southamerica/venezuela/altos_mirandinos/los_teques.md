Los Teques is the capital of Miranda state, sitting in the highlands about 30 kilometres southwest of Caracas at around 1,200 metres. With over 400,000 inhabitants, it has grown rapidly and somewhat chaotically — what was once a cooler retreat from the capital is now effectively part of the Caracas metropolitan sprawl.

The altitude gives Los Teques a noticeably milder climate than the lowlands. The town itself is not a tourist destination in the traditional sense, but it serves as a gateway to [Parque Nacional Macarao](/southamerica/venezuela/altos_mirandinos/los_teques/parque_nacional_macarao) and the cooler hill towns of the Altos Mirandinos. The Metro de Los Teques — a convenient extension of the Caracas metro — makes it easy to visit as a half-day trip from the capital or to use as a base for exploring the surrounding cloud forest.
