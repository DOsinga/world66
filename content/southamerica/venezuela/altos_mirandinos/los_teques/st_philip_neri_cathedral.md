St. Philip Neri Cathedral is the main historic church in Los Teques, standing near Bolivar Square in the old part of the city. Its roots go back to the late 18th century, and the building remains a well-kept landmark in the centre.

It is a natural stop while walking the historic core rather than a long standalone visit.
