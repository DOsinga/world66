Basilica de Santa Rosa is one of the key religious sites in the Barquisimeto area, tied to the devotion to the Divina Pastora. The basilica stands in Santa Rosa, a short trip from the city centre, and becomes especially important around the huge annual procession.

Outside festival time it is a calmer visit, useful for understanding why Barquisimeto's religious identity reaches beyond its modern cathedral. Dress respectfully and expect a working pilgrimage church rather than a tourist-only monument.
