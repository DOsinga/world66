---
title: "Getting Around"
type: section
---

The historic centre is laid out on a regular north-south, east-west grid of carreras and calles, and walking is the most practical way to cover the cathedral, Plaza Bolívar, and the museum — everything is within twenty minutes on foot. The blocks are long and the sun is strong, so a hat and water help.

For longer hops — out to the Obelisco, the Flor de Venezuela, or the airport — taxis are cheap and easy to flag, but always agree the fare before getting in. Local buses (*por puestos*) run on fixed routes, useful if you understand the system but rarely worth the effort for short stays. The **TransBarca** trunk bus system runs along the main axis of the city but coverage and reliability have been uneven in recent years.
