Monumento Manto de María Divina Pastora is one of Barquisimeto’s most visible modern landmarks, a huge Marian structure tied to the city’s devotion to the Divina Pastora. Its scale is the first thing you notice: part religious symbol, part lookout, part civic statement.

The monument is best visited for the view and for what it says about local identity. Barquisimeto’s annual Divina Pastora procession is one of Venezuela’s largest religious events, and this site gives that devotion a permanent, monumental form.
