Museo de Ciencias Naturales La Salle is a compact natural history museum with archaeology, paleontology, and science displays. The collection includes fossils, ammonites, mastodon remains, and other material that helps explain the natural history of the Barquisimeto region.

It is a low-key but worthwhile indoor stop, especially if you are interested in fossils or travelling with curious children.
