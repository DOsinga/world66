Centro de Arte La Estancia is one of eastern Caracas's calmer cultural spaces, set in a restored former coffee estate with gardens and exhibition rooms. The programming changes, but the appeal is steady: art, music, heritage architecture, and a patch of green in a city that can feel hard-edged.

It is a good daytime stop if you are staying or moving around Altamira and Chacao. Check current conditions and opening information locally, as Caracas logistics can change quickly, but keep it on the list when you want culture without the weight of a major museum.
