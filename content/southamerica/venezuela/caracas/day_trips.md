The nearest worthwhile day trip is **Colonia Tovar**, the German mountain village an hour and a half west of the city, founded by Black Forest immigrants in 1843 and still recognisable as such — half-timbered houses, sausages, strudel. The road climbs through cloud forest and is best on a clear morning.

The **Ávila** mountain itself is a day trip from anywhere in the city: hike up from Sabas Nieves to Pico Naiguatá, or ride the *teleférico* from Maripérez to the old Hotel Humboldt at the summit for the view over the city to the south and the Caribbean to the north.

The **Caribbean coast** is 30 minutes north over the mountain — Catia La Mar, La Guaira, Macuto, Naiguatá. Beach quality is variable and parts of the coast have safety issues; Naiguatá and the islands east are generally considered better than the corridor closer to the airport. **Los Roques**, the offshore archipelago that is Venezuela's finest beach destination, is a short flight from Caracas and is best done as an overnight rather than a day trip.
