Museo de Bellas Artes de Caracas is one of Venezuela's major art museums, set around the Museum Square area of Los Caobos Park. Its collection is especially strong on European art, with works by artists such as Picasso and Botero alongside Venezuelan holdings.

The neoclassical building gives the museum a formal, old-Caracas feel and pairs well with the nearby contemporary art and national gallery collections.
