---
title: Shopping
type: section
---

Shopping in Caracas centres on the malls of the eastern districts. **Centro Sambil** in Chacao is the largest and best-known — six floors of stores, a cinema multiplex, and a food court, busy at weekends. **Centro San Ignacio**, also in Chacao, is the city's most fashionable mall, with international labels, design boutiques, and the bars and restaurants that fill its terraces at night. **CCCT** in Chuao and **Sambil La Candelaria** are alternative options.

For something more local, the **Boulevard of Sabana Grande** is a long pedestrian street between Plaza Venezuela and Chacaíto, lined with mid-range clothing shops, electronics, and street vendors — a slice of the old commercial Caracas. **El Hatillo**, the colonial village, has the best concentration of craft and folk-art shops in the metropolitan area, selling work from across Venezuela.

Premium **chocolate** from Venezuelan cacao (Franceschi, Krön, Mantuano) makes the best souvenir; pick it up at the airport, at any decent supermarket, or at the brand boutiques in Las Mercedes and Altamira. Local **rum** — Diplomático Reserva Exclusiva, Pampero Aniversario, Santa Teresa 1796 — is similarly world-class and widely available.
