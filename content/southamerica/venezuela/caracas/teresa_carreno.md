The Teresa Carreño Theatre is the largest performing arts venue in Latin America — a vast concrete-and-glass complex completed in 1983 between Parque Central and the Parque Los Caobos. Architects Jesús Sandoval and Tomás Lugo designed it as a flowing sequence of cantilevered concrete planes and brise-soleils, with a 2,400-seat main auditorium (the Ríos Reyna hall), a smaller chamber theatre, and large public foyers that double as exhibition spaces.

The Venezuelan Symphony Orchestra and the National Philharmonic perform here regularly; ballet, opera, and large international productions take the main stage. Even when no performance is on, the building rewards a walk-through — the concrete sculpture and play of light through the foyers is a high-water mark of Venezuelan late modernism.
