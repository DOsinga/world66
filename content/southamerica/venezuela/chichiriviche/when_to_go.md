---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season runs roughly from December to April and gives you the most reliable swimming weather — calm seas, fewer mosquitoes in the mangroves, and water clear enough for snorkelling. This is also when Venezuelan holidaymakers flood in: Christmas–New Year, Carnaval (February), and Semana Santa (Easter week) see the cays packed and the town fully booked.

May to November is wetter. Brief afternoon storms are common but they pass quickly; mornings are usually fine for boat trips. The plus side is that prices drop and you may have entire cays to yourself on a weekday. Whatever the season, take serious sun protection — there is essentially no shade on most of the cays and the equatorial sun is unforgiving.
