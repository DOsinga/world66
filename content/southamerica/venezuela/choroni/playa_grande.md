Playa Grande is the main beach at Puerto Colombia, a broad crescent of dark sand backed by coconut palms and the green slopes of Henri Pittier National Park. The water is warm, clear, and calm enough for swimming most of the year, protected by a shallow bay. Fishing boats are beached at the northern end; the southern section is where families and weekend visitors spread out. On weekends, especially in high season, Playa Grande fills up with Venezuelans from Caracas and Maracay, with music, food stalls, and a lively atmosphere. Arrive early if you want space and quiet.

During the week you may have significant stretches to yourself. The beach vendors serve cold drinks and grilled fish, and the coconut palms provide natural shade in the late afternoon. At the northern end, a small reef area is popular for snorkelling. Boat trips along the coast to smaller, more isolated beaches depart from the Puerto Colombia beach in the morning; arrange them with local fishermen directly.
