Choroni's pleasures are simple: the beach, the colonial streets, the national park, and the Afro-Venezuelan music that fills the main square in Puerto Colombia on weekend evenings. The tambor drumming sessions at the Plaza de Bolívar in Puerto Colombia are a genuine cultural event, spontaneous and participatory, not staged for tourists. They typically start after dark on Friday and Saturday nights and can continue well past midnight.

The surrounding [Henri Pittier National Park](/southamerica/venezuela/choroni/henri_pittier_national_park) protects one of the most biodiverse regions in Venezuela, and the drive through it from Maracay is itself an experience — the road climbs through cloud forest before descending dramatically to the sea. Birdwatchers come from around the world for the park; over 500 species have been recorded here, including the colourful cock-of-the-rock.
