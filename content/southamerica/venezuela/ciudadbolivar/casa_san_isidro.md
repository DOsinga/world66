Casa San Isidro is the house where Simón Bolívar stayed during the Congress of Angostura. The building and its tropical garden add a quieter historical stop to Ciudad Bolívar's old centre.

It is close enough to combine with the city's Bolivar-era houses, cathedral, and riverfront sights.
