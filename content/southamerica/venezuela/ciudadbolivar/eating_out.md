---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Eating in Ciudad Bolívar is a straightforward affair. Riverside places along the Paseo Orinoco do grilled freshwater fish (look for **pavón** — peacock bass — and **lau-lau**, a giant catfish), often served with arepas and yuca. Traditional Venezuelan stews like **pisillo de chigüire** (shredded capybara, a regional specialty) and **carne en vara** (skewered beef roasted over open fire) are common in the smaller comedores around the centre.

Prices and availability fluctuate with the broader economic situation; carry cash, and confirm with your accommodation which places are currently operating.
