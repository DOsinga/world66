---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

The dry season — roughly December through April — is the easiest time to visit, with reliable light-aircraft flights to Canaima and stable conditions in the Gran Sabana. The rainy season (May to November) is dramatic in its own way: the Orinoco rises by 10–15 metres, the falls in Canaima run at full flow, and the Gran Sabana turns vivid green — but flights and overland trips are more often cancelled.

Angel Falls itself is best in the late wet season (August–October), when the volume of water is at its peak. Visit Ciudad Bolívar at any time of year for the colonial centre; the dry months give the most consistent weather along the river.
