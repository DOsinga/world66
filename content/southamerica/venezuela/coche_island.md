Coche is a small, flat island in the Caribbean Sea, part of Venezuela's Nueva Esparta state along with Margarita and Cubagua. It is quieter and less developed than Margarita, with a handful of resort hotels along the southern coast and a fishing village at [San Pedro de Coche](/southamerica/venezuela/coche_island/san_pedro_de_coche), the island's capital and ferry port.

San Pedro is a colourful settlement with a character all its own. Along the waterfront you can watch fishermen at work and fish drying in the sun. The beaches are calm and uncrowded, and the steady trade winds make Coche a popular spot for kitesurfing and windsurfing — [Playa El Amor](/southamerica/venezuela/coche_island/playa_el_amor) on the southern coast is the main centre. The interior of the island is given over to the [salt flats](/southamerica/venezuela/coche_island/salinas_coche) that have been worked since pre-colonial times.
