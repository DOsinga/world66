The Cervecería Tovar is the town's own brewery, founded by descendants of the original settlers and producing a small range of German-style lagers — chiefly a Pilsner and a Munich-style dunkel, with seasonal bocks. The beer is served on draft at the brewery's own bierhalle in the centre and at most other restaurants in the colony.

The bierhalle has long tables under timber beams, schnitzel and sausage plates from the kitchen, and a small terrace looking down into the valley. It is the closest thing in Venezuela to a Black Forest beer garden and is busiest at weekends.
