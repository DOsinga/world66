---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Coro's main attraction is the old town itself — a tight grid of colonial streets best wandered in the cool early morning before the sun bakes the walls. Beyond the historic centre, the Médanos de Coro dunes and the wide curve of the Caribbean coast give the city its second identity as a desert-edge town. Plan a morning for the centre, an afternoon at the dunes, and an evening on the malecón at La Vela.
