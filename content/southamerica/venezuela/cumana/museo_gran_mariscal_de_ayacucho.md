Museo Gran Mariscal de Ayacucho is the birthplace museum of Antonio Jose de Sucre, one of the central figures in Venezuela's independence struggle and the first president of Bolivia.

The museum adds useful historical context to Cumaná's colonial churches and forts, especially for visitors interested in the independence era.
