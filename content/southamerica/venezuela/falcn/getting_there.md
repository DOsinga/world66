---
title: Getting There
type: section
---

Coro is served by José Leonardo Chirinos Airport (IATA: CZE), about 4 km northeast of the city centre. There are scheduled flights to and from Caracas (Simon Bolívar International Airport) operated by Venezuelan carriers, though schedules and reliability have been erratic in recent years. Check the current status of domestic air services before planning around a flight connection — services have historically been suspended or cut back without much notice.

By road, Coro is around 460 km from Caracas, roughly a four to five hour drive on the main highway (Route 3). Direct buses connect Coro to Caracas and other major Venezuelan cities, departing from the Terminal de Pasajeros de Coro. The road passes through Barquisimeto and is generally in reasonable condition, though road conditions and fuel availability can vary. Travellers arriving from Colombia cross at the border at Paraguaita or elsewhere in Zulia state and can reach Falcón from the west.

Travel within Venezuela currently requires careful planning. Fuel shortages are common and can significantly affect road travel. It is advisable to have a clear plan for fuel stops and to carry cash in US dollars for flexibility. The security situation on intercity roads should be checked against current advisories before travel.
