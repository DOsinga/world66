---
title: Things to Do
type: section
---

Falcón state's attractions divide neatly between the colonial city of [Coro](/southamerica/venezuela/falcn/coro_colonial_city) and the natural landscape around it. The UNESCO-listed historic centre of Coro rewards a half-day walk through its pastel streets. The [Médanos de Coro](/southamerica/venezuela/falcn/medanos_de_coro) dune field just outside the city is one of Venezuela's most striking natural sights. The peninsula's coast — particularly around Adicora — draws kitesurfers and windsurfers year-round for its reliable trade winds, while [Chichiriviche](/southamerica/venezuela/chichiriviche) on the southern coast offers snorkelling among mangroves and offshore islands.
