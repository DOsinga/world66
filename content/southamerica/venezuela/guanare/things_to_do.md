title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Guanare's identity is inseparable from its role as Venezuela's spiritual capital. The city receives pilgrims year-round, with the largest crowds arriving on September 8th, the feast day of the Virgin of Coromoto. The cathedral on the main plaza and the National Sanctuary outside town are the two principal sites.

For travellers not specifically drawn by religion, Guanare makes a serviceable stop when passing through the western llanos. The town has the services of a provincial capital, the surrounding plains offer wildlife, and the journey into or out of the city crosses some of the most characteristic Venezuelan landscape — flat, open savannah with waterfowl-filled wetlands in the wet season.
