The Jardín Botánico de Maracaibo protects a large green tract on the edge of the city, with native dry-forest vegetation, walking areas, and plant collections that show a different side of a place better known for heat, traffic, and oil-industry history. It is one of the few substantial nature breaks within reach of Maracaibo.

Conditions and access can vary, so verify current opening information before travelling across town. When open, it is most rewarding early in the day, when temperatures are lower and birds and shade make the garden feel less exposed.
