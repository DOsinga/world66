The restaurants along the Juan Griego waterfront serve fresh fish and seafood at prices well below the south coast resorts. The standard offering is fish of the day — typically pargo (red snapper), mero (grouper), or whatever the boats brought in — grilled or fried and served with rice, plantains, and salad. Langostinos and calamari are also common.

The tables face the bay and the sunset, which is the real selling point. On a calm evening with a beer and a grilled fish and the sun dropping into the water in front of you, it is difficult to think of many better places to spend two hours. The restaurants are informal — no reservations needed, no dress code, and the prices reflect a local economy rather than a tourist one.
