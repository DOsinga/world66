---
title: "Day Trips"
type: section
---

Pampatar makes a good base for the eastern half of Margarita: the colonial capital of La Asunci**ó**n, the fortress of Santa Rosa, and the basilica at El Valle del Esp**í**ritu Santo are all within twenty minutes by car.

**La Asunci**ó**n** — the colonial capital of the island, with its 16th-century cathedral, the Santa Rosa de la Eminencia castle on the hill behind, and a quieter atmosphere than the seaside resort towns. The Matasiete battlefield, where the patriots stopped a royalist advance in 1817, is nearby.

**El Valle del Esp**í**ritu Santo** — home to the Basilica of Our Lady of the Valley, the patron of the eastern Caribbean fishermen and the destination of the largest religious pilgrimage on the island each September.

**Cerro El Copey National Park** — the island's highest mountain (985 m) and a national park of cloud forest reachable by a short paved road from El Valle. A clear day gives views all the way to the mainland.
