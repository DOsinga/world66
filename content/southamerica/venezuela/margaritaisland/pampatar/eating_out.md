---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Pampatar's restaurants line the malec**ó**n along the bay and run the local Caribbean spread: grilled fish, fried plantain, arepas, and rum. Lunch is the main meal; many places close in the early evening.

**El Punto Cri**oll**o** — straightforward Margarite**ñ**o food on the waterfront, fresh fish (often pargo or mero) grilled or stewed with coconut, generous portions.

**Cocina Caribe**ñ**a** — slightly more upmarket, with a terrace over the bay; the seafood platter is the dish to order, two people minimum.

Around the central square, several casual places serve **empanadas de cazón** (the local shark turnover) and breakfast arepas; they are cheap, fast, and the best meal of the day for a lot of visitors.
