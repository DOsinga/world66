Heladería Coromoto near Plaza Bolívar holds the Guinness World Record for the most ice cream flavours offered — currently more than 900, ranging from straightforward mango and coconut to bizarre experiments like smoked trout, garlic, avocado soup, and spaghetti. Not all flavours are available every day; the menu rotates.

The shop is a genuine Mérida institution, packed with locals and visitors alike. The unusual flavours are not a gimmick: many are surprisingly good, and trying the stranger ones is part of the experience. Go with an open mind and low expectations for any single flavour — the pleasure is in the variety and the spectacle of the menu itself.
