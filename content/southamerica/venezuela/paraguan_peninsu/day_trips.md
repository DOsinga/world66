The colonial city of Coro, at the base of the peninsula, is the most rewarding day trip from Paraguaná. A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Coro was one of the first European settlements in South America and retains a compact historic center of pastel adobe buildings, cobblestone streets, and Dutch-influenced colonial architecture. The Cathedral Basilica and the Coro Synagogue — the oldest surviving synagogue in the Americas — are the standout monuments. Coro is less than two hours from Punto Fijo by road.

Just outside Coro, the [Médanos de Coro](/southamerica/venezuela/paraguan_peninsu/things_to_do/medanos_de_coro) sand dunes can be visited on the same day.
