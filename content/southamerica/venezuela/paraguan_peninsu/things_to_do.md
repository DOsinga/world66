The Paraguaná Peninsula rewards visitors who embrace its austere character. The terrain is flat, dry, and wind-scoured — but that wind is the point, making Adicora one of the best kitesurf spots on the Caribbean coast. Cerro Santa Ana punctuates the plain with unexpected altitude and cloud forest, and the surrounding interior villages have an unhurried quality rare in Venezuela's coastal towns.
