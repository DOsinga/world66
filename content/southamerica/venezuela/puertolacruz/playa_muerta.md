Playa Muerta has a grim name, but it is a popular beach near Puerto La Cruz and one of the better local places to watch the sunset. Come for a simple coastal stop rather than a polished resort scene.

It works best as part of a relaxed afternoon around the waterfront, especially if you want a quieter beach option close to town.
