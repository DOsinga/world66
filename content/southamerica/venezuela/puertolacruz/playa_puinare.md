Playa Puinare is a quiet beach on Chimana Grande Island, reached by boat from the Puerto La Cruz area. Its appeal is the island setting: clear water, sand, and a slower day away from the busier mainland waterfront.

Go with enough time to arrange return boat transport, and treat it as a half-day beach outing rather than a quick stop.
