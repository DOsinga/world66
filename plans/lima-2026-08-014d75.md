## Lima | 1–4 August 2026
- Arrival, Miraflores/Barranco, food scene, museums. Sea-level start.


## Cuzco | 4–6 August 2026
- Acclimatize to 3,400m. Coca tea, light walking, no exertion.


## Sacred Valley | 6–9 August 2026
- Pisac, Ollantaytambo, Maras/Moray. Lower altitude (~2,800m). Rainbow Mountain day trip.


## Salkantay Trek | 9–13 August 2026
- 5-day Salkantay trek ending at Machu Picchu.
