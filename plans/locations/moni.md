Moni is a small village in central Flores, Indonesia, best known as the base for hiking to the tri-coloured crater lakes of Kelimutu. The lakes — each a different colour due to volcanic minerals — are one of the most striking natural sights in the archipelago. Most travellers arrive on an overnight bus from Labuan Bajo or Maumere.
