## Auckland | 6–7 December 2026
- Arrival + recover from jet lag. Quick city taste before flying south.

## Christchurch | 7–9 December 2026
- Fly in from Auckland, pick up rental car. Light start — explore the rebuilt city.

## Kaikoura | 9–11 December 2026
- Whale watching, swim with dolphins, fresh crayfish from the coastal caravans.
- ~locations/kaikoura

## Canterbury | 11–12 December 2026
- Bungy on the way (Thrillseekers Canyon), then thermal pools to recover.

## Christchurch | 12–14 December 2026
- Drive across the Southern Alps. Day hikes (Avalanche Peak, Devils Punchbowl). Kea country.

## Franz Josef | 14–16 December 2026
- Heli-hike the glacier — the must-do here. Glacier hot pools in the evening.

## Wanaka | 16–19 December 2026
- Roy's Peak at sunrise, skydive over the lake (most scenic drop in the world), Rob Roy Glacier walk.

## Queenstown | 19–23 December 2026
- Adventure capital. Nevis bungy (134m), Shotover jet, paragliding, canyon swing. Day trip to Glenorchy.

## Milford Sound | 23–26 December 2026
- Base for Milford Sound (overnight cruise) and Doubtful Sound. Day-hike sections of the Kepler Track.

## Southland | 26–28 December 2026
- Ferry from Bluff. Kiwi spotting at night, untouched bush. Skip if you'd rather slow down.

## Dunedin | 28–30 December 2026
- Otago Peninsula wildlife (albatross, sea lions, yellow-eyed penguins). Scottish-tinged city.

##  Mount Cook | 30 December – 1 January 2027
- Hooker Valley Track, Tasman Glacier boat, dark-sky stargazing. NYE under the stars.

## Christchurch | 1–2 January 2027
- Drop the car, fly home.
