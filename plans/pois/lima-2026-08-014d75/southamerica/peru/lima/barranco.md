Barranco is Lima's bohemian quarter, a neighbourhood of crumbling Republican-era mansions, street murals, and the best bar scene in the city. It sits just south of Miraflores and feels entirely different — slower, more creative, more alive after dark. The main plaza is lined with trees and surrounded by restaurants and galleries. The famous Puente de los Suspiros (Bridge of Sighs) is here, a wooden footbridge leading down to the coast.

During the day, Barranco rewards wandering. You will find contemporary art galleries, vintage shops, and excellent coffee. At night, the streets fill with locals heading to bars along Avenida Grau and Calle Domeyer. The neighbourhood is also home to some of Lima's best restaurants — Isolina, a classic criollo tavern, is on Avenida San Martín and should not be missed.
