Posada Amazonas is the closest of the Rainforest Expeditions lodges to Puerto Maldonado, about a 45-minute boat ride upriver, and it is the right pick if you have only two or three days for the jungle or you are travelling with kids or older parents. The lodge is co-owned and co-managed with the Infierno community, a local Ese'eja indigenous group, which gives the visit a different flavor — guides are often from the community and the cultural component feels real rather than performed.

Rooms are the same open-walled style as the other lodges in the family, with mosquito nets and decent bathrooms, and there is a 30-meter canopy tower on the property. The wildlife here is good but not exceptional — you are still in secondary forest near a populated river, so do not expect jaguars or tapirs. What you will see is plenty of monkeys, macaws over the canopy, caimans on a night boat ride, and giant otters on a day trip to nearby Lake Tres Chimbadas.

The value is excellent for the price, and the short transfer means you do not lose half a day in each direction on a boat. If you have more time and budget, push deeper to Refugio or TRC instead.
