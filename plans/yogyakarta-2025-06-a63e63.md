## Rantepao | 5–10 June 2025
- Tana Toraja base. Funeral ceremonies, cliff graves at Lemo, tongkonan villages, Kete Kesu, Batutumonga rice terraces.

## Waikabubak | 10–14 June 2025
- West Sumba: megalithic villages (Praijing, Tarung, Ratenggaro), ikat weaving, Pasola festival if Feb–Mar.

## Waingapu | 14–17 June 2025
- East Sumba: ikat villages (Pau, Prailiu, Rende), wild beaches, royal tombs.
